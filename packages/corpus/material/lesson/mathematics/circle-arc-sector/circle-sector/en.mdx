## Definition of Circle Sector

A circle sector is the region of a disk bounded by two radii and the arc between them. A slice cut radially from a round cake or an orange wedge has roughly the same shape.

A sector consists of three main components:

- Two radii that meet at the center of the circle
- One arc that connects the ends of both radii

The two radii form a central angle at the center of the circle. For a fixed radius, this angle determines what fraction of the disk the sector occupies.

## Relationship Between Central Angle and Sector Area

For a fixed radius, sector area is proportional to the central angle. Doubling the angle doubles the area of the corresponding sector.

The relationship between central angle and sector area can be expressed as a ratio. If the central angle is <InlineMath math="\alpha" /> degrees, then the sector area is <InlineMath math="\frac{\alpha}{360^\circ}" /> part of the total circle area.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Sector Area} = \frac{\alpha}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2" />
</MathContainer>

For a fixed radius, sector area is proportional to the central angle. An angle of <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> covers one-quarter of the circle, while <InlineMath math="180^\circ" /> covers one-half.

## Calculating Sector Area

To calculate the sector area, we need two important pieces of information: the circle radius and the central angle size. The calculation process is quite simple using the formula we have learned.

Consider a circle with radius <InlineMath math="14 \text{ cm}" /> and a sector with central angle <InlineMath math="60^\circ" />. Substitution gives:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Sector Area} = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \pi \times 14^2" />
<BlockMath math="= \frac{1}{6} \times \pi \times 196" />
<BlockMath math="= \frac{196\pi}{6}" />
<BlockMath math="= \frac{98\pi}{3} \text{ cm}^2" />
<BlockMath math="\approx 102.63 \text{ cm}^2" />
</MathContainer>

The exact sector area is <InlineMath math="\frac{98\pi}{3} \text{ cm}^2" />, approximately <InlineMath math="102.63 \text{ cm}^2" />.

## Practice Problems

1. A circle has a radius of <InlineMath math="21 \text{ cm}" />. Determine the sector area formed by a central angle of <InlineMath math="120^\circ" />.

2. A sector has area <InlineMath math="49\pi \text{ cm}^2" /> in a circle of radius <InlineMath math="14 \text{ cm}" />. What is its central angle?

3. A round flatbread with diameter <InlineMath math="28 \text{ cm}" /> is cut radially through its center into <InlineMath math="8" /> equal sectors. Determine the area of each piece.

### Worked Solutions

1. **Solution Steps:**

   Given: <InlineMath math="r = 21 \text{ cm}" />, <InlineMath math="\alpha = 120^\circ" />

   <MathContainer>
   <BlockMath math="\text{Sector Area} = \frac{\alpha}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2" />
   <BlockMath math="= \frac{120^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \pi \times 21^2" />
   <BlockMath math="= \frac{1}{3} \times \pi \times 441" />
   <BlockMath math="= \frac{441\pi}{3}" />
   <BlockMath math="= 147\pi \text{ cm}^2" />
   <BlockMath math="\approx 461.81 \text{ cm}^2" />
   </MathContainer>

2. **Solution Steps:**

   Given: sector area = <InlineMath math="49\pi \text{ cm}^2" />, <InlineMath math="r = 14 \text{ cm}" />

   <MathContainer>
   <BlockMath math="49\pi = \frac{\alpha}{360^\circ} \times \pi \times 14^2" />
   <BlockMath math="49\pi = \frac{\alpha}{360^\circ} \times 196\pi" />
   <BlockMath math="49 = \frac{\alpha}{360^\circ} \times 196" />
   <BlockMath math="\frac{\alpha}{360^\circ} = \frac{49}{196}" />
   <BlockMath math="\frac{\alpha}{360^\circ} = \frac{1}{4}" />
   <BlockMath math="\alpha = \frac{1}{4} \times 360^\circ" />
   <BlockMath math="\alpha = 90^\circ" />
   </MathContainer>

3. **Solution Steps:**

   Given: diameter = <InlineMath math="28 \text{ cm}" />, so <InlineMath math="r = 14 \text{ cm}" />
   The flatbread is cut radially into <InlineMath math="8" /> equal sectors, so the central angle of each piece is <InlineMath math="\frac{360^\circ}{8} = 45^\circ" />

   <MathContainer>
   <BlockMath math="\text{Area of each piece} = \frac{45^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \pi \times 14^2" />
   <BlockMath math="= \frac{1}{8} \times \pi \times 196" />
   <BlockMath math="= \frac{196\pi}{8}" />
   <BlockMath math="= \frac{49\pi}{2} \text{ cm}^2" />
   <BlockMath math="\approx 76.97 \text{ cm}^2" />
   </MathContainer>
