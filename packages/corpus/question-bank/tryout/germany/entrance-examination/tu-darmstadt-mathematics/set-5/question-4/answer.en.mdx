The task asks for the *Scheitelpunkt* (vertex) of the parabola. A parabola is **symmetric about the vertical line through its vertex**. The two zeros are mirror images about that axis, so the vertex lies exactly halfway between them.

<BlockMath math="x_S=\frac{-1+5}{2}=2" />

For the <InlineMath math="y" />-coordinate you need the equation. With the zeros <InlineMath math="-1" /> and <InlineMath math="5" /> and the coefficient <InlineMath math="1" /> in front of <InlineMath math="x^2" />, its factored form is <InlineMath math="y=(x+1)(x-5)" />. Substitute <InlineMath math="x_S=2" />.

<BlockMath math="y_S=(2+1)(2-5)=3\cdot(-3)=-9" />

As a check, expand to <InlineMath math="y=x^2-4x-5" /> and complete the square: <InlineMath math="x^2-4x-5=(x-2)^2-4-5=(x-2)^2-9" />. This also shows the vertex <InlineMath math="(2\mid-9)" />.

So the vertex of the parabola is <InlineMath math="(2\mid-9)" />.

- <InlineMath math="(2\mid9)" /> has the wrong sign for <InlineMath math="y" />. A parabola that opens upwards and has two zeros has its vertex below the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis.
