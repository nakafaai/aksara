# Track the amount of alcohol

At the start the barrel holds <InlineMath math="0.3\cdot50=15" /> litres of alcohol. Pouring off <InlineMath math="x" /> litres of mixture removes more than water: **<InlineMath math="30\,\%" /> of it is alcohol too**, so <InlineMath math="0.3x" /> litres. Then <InlineMath math="x" /> litres of pure alcohol go in. The volume is back to <InlineMath math="50" /> litres:

<BlockMath math="\text{alcohol at the end}=15-0.3x+x=15+0.7x" />

# Set the new share equal

At the end, <InlineMath math="44\,\%" /> of <InlineMath math="50" /> litres is alcohol, which is <InlineMath math="22" /> litres:

<BlockMath math="15+0.7x=22\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 0.7x=7\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=10" />

# Why the other answers are wrong

- <InlineMath math="x=7" /> forgets that the poured-off mixture **takes alcohol with it**: <InlineMath math="15+x=22" />.
- <InlineMath math="x=\frac{70}{3}" /> uses <InlineMath math="0.3x" /> instead of <InlineMath math="0.7x" /> as the gain: <InlineMath math="15+0.3x=22" />.
- <InlineMath math="x=14" /> reads the <InlineMath math="14" /> percentage points from <InlineMath math="30\,\%" /> to <InlineMath math="44\,\%" /> as litres.
- <InlineMath math="x=12.5" /> pours nothing off and only adds alcohol: <InlineMath math="\frac{15+x}{50+x}=0.44" />.
- <InlineMath math="x=22" /> is the amount of alcohol at the end, not the amount exchanged.
