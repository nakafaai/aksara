# Domain and the first squaring

Both roots must be defined: <InlineMath math="3x+1\ge0" /> and <InlineMath math="x-1\ge0" />, so together <InlineMath math="x\ge1" />. Move one root to the other side before squaring, so that **only one root is left**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{3x+1}&=2+\sqrt{x-1} \\
3x+1&=4+4\sqrt{x-1}+x-1 \\
2x-2&=4\sqrt{x-1} \\
x-1&=2\sqrt{x-1}
\end{aligned}" />

# Don't divide by the root

It's tempting to divide both sides by <InlineMath math="\sqrt{x-1}" />. But for <InlineMath math="x=1" /> that number is <InlineMath math="0" />, and **you can't divide by <InlineMath math="0" />**. Instead set <InlineMath math="u=\sqrt{x-1}\ge0" />. Then <InlineMath math="x-1=u^2" />:

<BlockMath math="u^2=2u\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad u(u-2)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad u=0\ \text{or}\ u=2" />

From <InlineMath math="u=0" /> you get <InlineMath math="x=1" />, and from <InlineMath math="u=2" /> you get <InlineMath math="x-1=4" />, so <InlineMath math="x=5" />.

# Check both candidates

Squaring can create false solutions, so **substitute both into the original equation**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
x=1:&\quad\sqrt4-\sqrt0=2 \\
x=5:&\quad\sqrt{16}-\sqrt4=4-2=2
\end{aligned}" />

The solution set is <InlineMath math="\mathbb{L}=\{1;\,5\}" />.

# Why the other answers are wrong

- <InlineMath math="\mathbb{L}=\{5\}" /> comes from dividing by <InlineMath math="\sqrt{x-1}" />. That way **the solution <InlineMath math="x=1" /> gets lost**.
- <InlineMath math="\mathbb{L}=\{1\}" /> comes from squaring the left side term by term: <InlineMath math="(3x+1)-(x-1)=4" />. But <InlineMath math="(a-b)^2\ne a^2-b^2" />.
- <InlineMath math="\mathbb{L}=\{1;\,3\}" /> comes from not squaring the factor <InlineMath math="2" /> in <InlineMath math="x-1=2\sqrt{x-1}" />: <InlineMath math="(x-1)^2=2(x-1)" />.
- <InlineMath math="\mathbb{L}=\{3+2\sqrt3\}" /> comes from a sign slip when expanding: <InlineMath math="x^2-2x+1=4x+4" />.
- <InlineMath math="\mathbb{L}=\{\}" /> overlooks that both checks work out.
