The task says *Fassen Sie den Term zusammen* (simplify the expression). You need two **power rules for equal bases** here. Raising a power to a power multiplies the exponents, and dividing subtracts them.

<BlockMath math="\left(a^m\right)^n=a^{m\cdot n}\qquad\frac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}" />

First square the bracket in the numerator. Every factor inside the bracket is squared, so both exponents double.

<BlockMath math="\left(x^{3}y^{-2}\right)^{2}=x^{6}y^{-4}" />

Then divide base by base, <InlineMath math="x" /> and <InlineMath math="y" /> separately. The exponent of <InlineMath math="y" /> has two minus signs in a row, and two minus signs make a plus.

<BlockMath math="\frac{x^{6}y^{-4}}{x^{4}y^{-5}}=x^{6-4}\cdot y^{-4-(-5)}=x^{2}\cdot y^{1}=x^{2}y" />

As a check, substitute <InlineMath math="x=1" /> and <InlineMath math="y=2" />. The original expression gives <InlineMath math="\frac{\left(2^{-2}\right)^{2}}{2^{-5}}=\frac{2^{-4}}{2^{-5}}=2" />, and <InlineMath math="x^{2}y" /> also gives <InlineMath math="1\cdot2=2" />.

So the expression simplifies to <InlineMath math="x^{2}y" />.

- <InlineMath math="\frac{x^2}{y}" /> comes from subtracting the exponents of <InlineMath math="y" /> in the wrong order, <InlineMath math="-5-(-4)=-1" /> instead of <InlineMath math="-4-(-5)=1" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{x^{10}}{y^9}" /> comes from adding the exponents instead of subtracting them when dividing: <InlineMath math="6+4=10" /> and <InlineMath math="-4+(-5)=-9" />.
- <InlineMath math="x^2y^3" /> comes from squaring only <InlineMath math="x^3" /> and leaving <InlineMath math="y^{-2}" /> unchanged. That gives <InlineMath math="y^{-2-(-5)}=y^3" />.
- <InlineMath math="xy^5" /> comes from adding the <InlineMath math="2" /> to the exponents instead of multiplying by it. The numerator then becomes <InlineMath math="x^5y^0" />, and dividing gives <InlineMath math="x^{5-4}y^{0+5}" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{x^2}{y^9}" /> comes from the sign error <InlineMath math="-4-5=-9" />. The minus in front of <InlineMath math="(-5)" /> turns the subtraction into an addition.
