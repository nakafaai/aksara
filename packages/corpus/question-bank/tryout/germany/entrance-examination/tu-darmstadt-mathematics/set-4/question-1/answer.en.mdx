# Given

We are given a **telescoping sum** (*Teleskopsumme*) of <InlineMath math="15" /> radical fractions.

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{1 + \sqrt{2}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{4}} + \ldots + \frac{1}{\sqrt{15} + \sqrt{16}}" />

# Asked

Compute the exact value of this sum of <InlineMath math="15" /> fractions.

# Solution

Picture laying fifteen ribbon strips end-to-end without gaps along a ruler, starting at mark <InlineMath math="1" /> and finishing at mark <InlineMath math="4" />. You never need to measure each tiny strip individually to find their total length: simply **subtract the starting mark from the ending mark**!

> Rationalizing each denominator turns a daunting chain of fractions into a telescoping sum where every interior term cancels and only the first and last endpoints remain.

Rationalize the general <InlineMath math="k" />-th term by multiplying top and bottom by its conjugate so the denominator collapses to <InlineMath math="1" />.

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{\sqrt{k} + \sqrt{k + 1}} = \frac{\sqrt{k + 1} - \sqrt{k}}{(k + 1) - k} = \sqrt{k + 1} - \sqrt{k}" />

## Collapsing the telescoping sum

Replace all <InlineMath math="15" /> fractions with consecutive square-root differences so that **every middle root from <InlineMath math="\sqrt{2}" /> through <InlineMath math="\sqrt{15}" /> cancels out**.

<BlockMath math="\left(\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{1}\right) + \left(\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}\right) + \ldots + \left(\sqrt{16} - \sqrt{15}\right) = \sqrt{16} - \sqrt{1} = 4 - 1 = 3" />

## Sanity check and wrong choices

Quick check: every fraction is positive and smaller than <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{2}" />, so **a positive sum of <InlineMath math="3" /> fits <InlineMath math="15" /> terms naturally**.

- <InlineMath math="-3" /> multiplies by <InlineMath math="\sqrt{k} - \sqrt{k + 1}" /> yet wrongly keeps <InlineMath math="+1" /> in the denominator, even though a sum of positive fractions cannot be negative.
- <InlineMath math="4 - \sqrt{2}" /> drops the first fraction so the chain starts late at <InlineMath math="\sqrt{2}" />.
- <InlineMath math="\sqrt{17} - 1" /> counts one extra fraction past the end up to <InlineMath math="\sqrt{17}" />.
- <InlineMath math="4" /> forgets to subtract the starting term <InlineMath math="1" />.
- <InlineMath math="15" /> is just the number of fractions, not their sum.

# Answer

The exact value of the sum is **<InlineMath math="3" />**.
