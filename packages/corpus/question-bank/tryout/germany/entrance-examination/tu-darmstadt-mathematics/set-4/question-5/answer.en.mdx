The task asks for the height of one side face of the tent, from the middle of a base edge to the apex. That segment is the hypotenuse of a **right triangle inside the pyramid**. Its corners are the apex, the centre of the base, and the middle of a base edge.

One leg is the height of the pyramid, <InlineMath math="h=4\text{ m}" />. The other runs from the centre of the base to the middle of a base edge. It is half a base edge long, so <InlineMath math="3\text{ m}" />. The right angle is at the centre of the base, because the height is perpendicular to the base.

<BlockMath math="h_s^2=4^2+3^2=16+9=25\quad\Rightarrow\quad h_s=5" />

As a check, compare with the lateral edge <InlineMath math="s" />, which runs from a corner to the apex. Its horizontal leg is half the diagonal, <InlineMath math="3\sqrt2\text{ m}" />, so <InlineMath math="s=\sqrt{16+18}=\sqrt{34}\approx5.8" />. The height of a side face must be shorter than the lateral edge, and <InlineMath math="5<5.8" /> fits.

So the height of a side face is <InlineMath math="5\text{ m}" />.
