# The height of a side face

Each side face is a triangle with base <InlineMath math="6\text{ cm}" />. Its area gives its height <InlineMath math="h_s" />, the **height of the side face**, not the height of the pyramid:

<BlockMath math="\frac12\cdot6\cdot h_s=15\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_s=5\text{ cm}" />

# The volume

<BlockMath math="V=\frac13\cdot G\cdot h=\frac13\cdot6^2\cdot4=48\text{ cm}^3" />

The pyramid has **a volume of <InlineMath math="48\text{ cm}^3" />**.

# Why the other answers are wrong

- <InlineMath math="144\text{ cm}^3" /> **forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\frac13" />** in the pyramid volume.
- <InlineMath math="60\text{ cm}^3" /> uses the slant height <InlineMath math="5" /> as the pyramid's height: <InlineMath math="\frac13\cdot36\cdot5" />.
- <InlineMath math="12\sqrt7\text{ cm}^3" /> takes <InlineMath math="5\text{ cm}" /> as the length of a side edge and works with half the diagonal, <InlineMath math="3\sqrt2" />: <InlineMath math="h=\sqrt{25-18}=\sqrt7" />.
- <InlineMath math="72\text{ cm}^3" /> uses <InlineMath math="\frac12" /> instead of <InlineMath math="\frac13" />.
- <InlineMath math="180\text{ cm}^3" /> makes two mistakes: slant height as the height and no factor <InlineMath math="\frac13" />.
