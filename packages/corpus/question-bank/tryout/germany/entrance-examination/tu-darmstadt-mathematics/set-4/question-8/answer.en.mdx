# Given

A **right square pyramid** (*gerade Pyramide*) has a square base of side <InlineMath math="6\text{ cm}" /> with its apex directly above the center of the base. Each of the four triangular lateral faces has area <InlineMath math="15\text{ cm}^2" />.

# Asked

Find the volume of this pyramid.

# Solution

Picture a pyramid-shaped camping tent: the slanted fabric wall is always longer than the vertical center pole! The triangular face area first gives **the slant height** (*Seitenhöhe*) <InlineMath math="h_s" />, and slicing the tent down the middle reveals the true vertical height <InlineMath math="h" />.

> In a right square pyramid, half the base side, the vertical height, and the slant height always form a right triangle with the slant height as hypotenuse.

First extract the slant height <InlineMath math="h_s" /> from one triangular face of base <InlineMath math="6\text{ cm}" />.

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot h_s = 15 \implies h_s = 5\text{ cm}" />

## Finding the vertical height from the central slice

Slice the pyramid vertically through the apex <InlineMath math="S" /> and the midpoints of two opposite base edges. Inside the cross-section sits **a classic <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="5" /> right triangle**.

Applying the Pythagorean theorem to this right triangle gives the vertical height and volume immediately.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
h &= \sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = \sqrt{16} = 4\text{ cm} \\
V &= \frac{1}{3} \cdot G \cdot h = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 6^2 \cdot 4 = 48\text{ cm}^3
\end{aligned}" />

## Sanity check and wrong choices

Quick check: a prism with the same base and height <InlineMath math="4\text{ cm}" /> has volume <InlineMath math="144\text{ cm}^3" />, and **a pyramid always holds exactly one third of that, or <InlineMath math="48\text{ cm}^3" />**.

- <InlineMath math="144\text{ cm}^3" /> omits the <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{3}" /> factor in the pyramid volume formula.
- <InlineMath math="60\text{ cm}^3" /> plugs the slant height <InlineMath math="5\text{ cm}" /> directly in place of the vertical height.
- <InlineMath math="12\sqrt{7}\text{ cm}^3" /> mistakes <InlineMath math="5\text{ cm}" /> for a lateral edge and pairs it with the half-diagonal <InlineMath math="3\sqrt{2}" />.
- <InlineMath math="72\text{ cm}^3" /> uses <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{2}" /> instead of <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{3}" />.
- <InlineMath math="180\text{ cm}^3" /> makes both mistakes at once: using the slant height <InlineMath math="5\text{ cm}" /> and dropping the <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{3}" /> factor.

# Answer

The volume of the pyramid is <InlineMath math="48\text{ cm}^3" />.
