The task asks for the values of <InlineMath math="a" /> with *genau einen gemeinsamen Punkt* (exactly one common point). You find common points of a parabola and a line by **setting the two function terms equal**. Every solution of the resulting equation gives one common point.

<BlockMath math="x^2=ax-1\quad\Leftrightarrow\quad x^2-ax+1=0" />

This is a quadratic equation. The discriminant <InlineMath math="D=p^2-4q" /> of the form <InlineMath math="x^2+px+q=0" /> decides how many solutions it has. Here <InlineMath math="p=-a" /> and <InlineMath math="q=1" />.

<BlockMath math="D=(-a)^2-4\cdot1=a^2-4" />

There is exactly one solution when <InlineMath math="D=0" />. From <InlineMath math="a^2-4=0" /> you get <InlineMath math="a^2=4" />, and this equation has two solutions, <InlineMath math="a=2" /> and <InlineMath math="a=-2" />.

As a check, substitute both values. For <InlineMath math="a=2" /> the equation becomes <InlineMath math="x^2-2x+1=(x-1)^2=0" />, so the line touches the parabola only at <InlineMath math="(1\mid1)" />. For <InlineMath math="a=-2" /> you get <InlineMath math="(x+1)^2=0" /> and the point of contact <InlineMath math="(-1\mid1)" />.

So there is exactly one common point for <InlineMath math="a=-2" /> and for <InlineMath math="a=2" />.

- Only <InlineMath math="a=2" /> misses the negative solution of <InlineMath math="a^2=4" />. Also <InlineMath math="(-2)^2=4" /> makes the discriminant zero.
- For <InlineMath math="-2<a<2" />, <InlineMath math="D<0" />. Then the parabola and the line have no common point at all.
- <InlineMath math="a=-1" /> and <InlineMath math="a=1" /> come from dropping the factor <InlineMath math="4" /> in the discriminant, that is, solving <InlineMath math="a^2-1=0" />.
- The option *für keinen Wert* (for no value) comes from the sign error <InlineMath math="D=a^2+4" />. That expression is never zero, but the equation has <InlineMath math="+1" />, so <InlineMath math="D" /> contains <InlineMath math="-4\cdot1" />.
- For <InlineMath math="a<-2" /> or <InlineMath math="a>2" />, <InlineMath math="D>0" />. Then the line cuts the parabola in two points.
