The task asks for the area of the path, *exakt* (exactly). The path is an **annulus**, a ring: the large circle up to the outer edge of the path minus the small circle of the pond. So you need both radii.

The inner radius is the radius of the pond, <InlineMath math="r=6\text{ m}" />. The outer radius adds the width of the path, <InlineMath math="R=6\text{ m}+2\text{ m}=8\text{ m}" />. With <InlineMath math="A=\pi r^2" /> for each circle, take the difference.

<BlockMath math="A_{\text{path}}=\pi R^2-\pi r^2=\pi\cdot8^2-\pi\cdot6^2=64\pi-36\pi=28\pi" />

As a check, estimate. The path is a strip of width <InlineMath math="2\text{ m}" />. Its length lies between the inner circumference <InlineMath math="12\pi\text{ m}" /> and the outer circumference <InlineMath math="16\pi\text{ m}" />, so in the middle at <InlineMath math="14\pi\text{ m}" />. That gives <InlineMath math="14\pi\cdot2=28\pi" />, which matches the calculation.

So the path has an area of <InlineMath math="28\pi\text{ m}^2" />, which is about <InlineMath math="88\text{ m}^2" />.
