The task asks how long the edge band (*Kantenband*) around the table top is, exactly. The edge band runs along the **outer edge of the whole figure**. The two short sides of the rectangle lie inside, where the semicircles are attached. So they are not part of the edge.

The edge has two parts. One part is the two long sides of the rectangle, together <InlineMath math="2\cdot100\text{ cm}=200\text{ cm}" />. The other is the two semicircular arcs. Two semicircles with the same diameter together make the circumference of one full circle, <InlineMath math="C=\pi d" />.

<BlockMath math="C_{\text{arcs}}=\pi\cdot60\text{ cm}=60\pi\text{ cm}\qquad C_{\text{band}}=200\text{ cm}+60\pi\text{ cm}" />

As a check, estimate with <InlineMath math="\pi\approx3.14" />: <InlineMath math="60\pi\approx188" />, so the band is about <InlineMath math="388\text{ cm}" /> long. That is more than the perimeter of the rectangle alone, <InlineMath math="320\text{ cm}" />, as it should be, because each arc is longer than the straight side it replaces.

So the edge band is <InlineMath math="(200+60\pi)\text{ cm}" /> long.
