# Given

For a positive real number <InlineMath math="x > 0" />, we are given a **three-layer nested radical** (*Term*):

<BlockMath math="\sqrt{x \cdot \sqrt[3]{x^2 \cdot \sqrt{x}}}" />

# Asked

Write this nested radical as **one single power** of <InlineMath math="x" />.

# Solution

Nested roots work like nesting dolls: the cleanest approach is to **unwrap them from the innermost layer outward**. Multiplying powers of <InlineMath math="x" /> adds exponents, while taking an <InlineMath math="n" />-th root divides the exponent by <InlineMath math="n" />. Simply track the exponent rising and falling until you clear the outer radical.

> For nested radicals with the same base, work from inside out: multiplication adds exponents and taking a root divides the exponent.

## Tracking the exponent from inside out

Apply fractional-exponent rules step by step starting at **the innermost root <InlineMath math="\sqrt{x}" />**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
x^2 \cdot \sqrt{x} &= x^2 \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{5}{2}} \\
\sqrt[3]{x^{\frac{5}{2}}} &= \left(x^{\frac{5}{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} = x^{\frac{5}{6}} \\
x \cdot x^{\frac{5}{6}} &= x^{1 + \frac{5}{6}} = x^{\frac{11}{6}} \\
\sqrt{x^{\frac{11}{6}}} &= \left(x^{\frac{11}{6}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{11}{12}}
\end{aligned}" />

Quick check with <InlineMath math="x = 2^{12}" />: the innermost root gives <InlineMath math="2^6" />, multiplying by <InlineMath math="2^{24}" /> gives <InlineMath math="2^{30}" />, the cube root gives <InlineMath math="2^{10}" />, multiplying by <InlineMath math="2^{12}" /> gives <InlineMath math="2^{22}" />, and the outer square root gives <InlineMath math="2^{11}" />, matching <InlineMath math="\frac{11}{12}" /> exactly.

## Checking the distractors

Notice which layer was **skipped or misread** in each wrong choice:

| Option | Why it is wrong |
| --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{11}{6}}" /> | Forgets to take the outer square root on the final step. |
| <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{5}{6}}" /> | Stops after the cube root, omitting the outer <InlineMath math="x" /> and outer square root. |
| <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{3}{4}}" /> | Drops <InlineMath math="x^2" /> and the cube root, evaluating only <InlineMath math="\sqrt{x\sqrt{x}}" />. |
| <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{7}{12}}" /> | Wrongly places the outer <InlineMath math="x" /> inside the cube root as well. |
| <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{13}{12}}" /> | Misreads the innermost <InlineMath math="\sqrt{x}" /> as <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{3}{2}}" /> instead of <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{1}{2}}" />. |

# Answer

Written as a single power of <InlineMath math="x" />, the expression equals **<InlineMath math="x^{\frac{11}{12}}" />**.
