# Work from the inside out

Every root is a power with a fractional exponent: <InlineMath math="\sqrt{u}=u^{\frac12}" /> and <InlineMath math="\sqrt[3]{u}=u^{\frac13}" />. When you multiply, **the exponents add**, and when you take a root, they are divided. So work from the innermost root outwards:

| Step | Expression | as a power |
| --- | --- | --- |
| innermost root | <InlineMath math="\sqrt{x}" /> | <InlineMath math="x^{\frac12}" /> |
| times <InlineMath math="x^2" /> | <InlineMath math="x^2\cdot\sqrt{x}" /> | <InlineMath math="x^{\frac52}" /> |
| cube root | <InlineMath math="\sqrt[3]{x^2\sqrt x}" /> | <InlineMath math="x^{\frac56}" /> |
| times <InlineMath math="x" /> | <InlineMath math="x\cdot\sqrt[3]{x^2\sqrt x}" /> | <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{11}{6}}" /> |
| outer root | <InlineMath math="\sqrt{x\cdot\sqrt[3]{x^2\sqrt x}}" /> | <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{11}{12}}" /> |

# The calculation step by step

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
x^2\cdot x^{\frac12}&=x^{2+\frac12}=x^{\frac52} \\
\left(x^{\frac52}\right)^{\frac13}&=x^{\frac56} \\
x\cdot x^{\frac56}&=x^{\frac{11}{6}} \\
\left(x^{\frac{11}{6}}\right)^{\frac12}&=x^{\frac{11}{12}}
\end{aligned}" />

Check with <InlineMath math="x=2^{12}=4096" />: the innermost root is <InlineMath math="2^6" />, then <InlineMath math="2^{24}\cdot2^6=2^{30}" />, its cube root is <InlineMath math="2^{10}" />, times <InlineMath math="2^{12}" /> gives <InlineMath math="2^{22}" />, and its square root is <InlineMath math="2^{11}=\left(2^{12}\right)^{\frac{11}{12}}" />. The expression **equals <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{11}{12}}" />**.

# Why the other answers are wrong

- <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{11}{6}}" /> **forgets the outer root**.
- <InlineMath math="x^{\frac56}" /> stops at the cube root and leaves out the <InlineMath math="x" /> in front and the outer root.
- <InlineMath math="x^{\frac34}" /> drops the factor <InlineMath math="x^2" /> and the cube root and works out only <InlineMath math="\sqrt{x\cdot\sqrt x}=x^{\frac34}" />.
- <InlineMath math="x^{\frac7{12}}" /> applies the cube root to the outer <InlineMath math="x" /> as well: <InlineMath math="\left(x\cdot x^{\frac52}\right)^{\frac13}=x^{\frac76}" />, and the square root gives <InlineMath math="x^{\frac7{12}}" />.
- <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{13}{12}}" /> reads <InlineMath math="\sqrt x" /> as <InlineMath math="x^{\frac32}" />: the inside becomes <InlineMath math="x^{\frac72}" />, the cube root <InlineMath math="x^{\frac76}" />, times <InlineMath math="x" /> gives <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{13}{6}}" />, and the outer root <InlineMath math="x^{\frac{13}{12}}" />.
