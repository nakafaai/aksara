The task asks for the largest number of crates the person may take. The total load of person and crates must not exceed <InlineMath math="630\text{ kg}" />. With <InlineMath math="n" /> crates this gives a **linear inequality**, whose solution you then restrict to whole numbers.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
90+24n&\le630 \\
24n&\le540 \\
n&\le22.5
\end{aligned}" />

The number of crates must be a whole number. The largest whole number with <InlineMath math="n\le22.5" /> is <InlineMath math="22" />.

As a check, test both neighbours. With <InlineMath math="22" /> crates the load is <InlineMath math="90+528=618\text{ kg}" />, which is allowed. With <InlineMath math="23" /> crates it would be <InlineMath math="90+552=642\text{ kg}" />, which is too much.

So the person may take at most <InlineMath math="22" /> crates.
