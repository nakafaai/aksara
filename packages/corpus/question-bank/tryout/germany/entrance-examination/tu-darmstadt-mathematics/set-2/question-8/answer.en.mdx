No side of the triangle is parallel to an axis. So **put an axis-parallel rectangle around the triangle** and subtract the three right triangles left over. Work in coordinate units first and convert to square metres only at the end. The German notation <InlineMath math="A(x\mid y)" /> separates the two coordinates with a vertical bar.

The rectangle runs from <InlineMath math="x=-4" /> to <InlineMath math="x=6" /> and from <InlineMath math="y=-1" /> to <InlineMath math="y=7" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A_{\text{rectangle}}&=10\cdot8=80 \\
\text{corner at }AB&:\ \tfrac12\cdot10\cdot2=10 \\
\text{corner at }BC&:\ \tfrac12\cdot4\cdot6=12 \\
\text{corner at }CA&:\ \tfrac12\cdot6\cdot8=24 \\
A_{ABC}&=80-10-12-24=34
\end{aligned}" />

One unit is <InlineMath math="10\text{ m}" />, so one square unit is <InlineMath math="10\cdot10=100\text{ m}^2" />. The area is therefore <InlineMath math="34\cdot100=3{,}400\text{ m}^2" />.

As a check with the determinant formula, <InlineMath math="\frac12\,|10\cdot8-6\cdot2|=\frac12\cdot68=34" />.

So the swimming zone has an area of <InlineMath math="3{,}400\text{ m}^2" />.

- <InlineMath math="340\text{ m}^2" /> converts the square unit with the factor <InlineMath math="10" /> instead of <InlineMath math="100" />.
- <InlineMath math="4{,}000\text{ m}^2" /> takes half of the surrounding rectangle. That holds only when a side of the triangle is a side of the rectangle.
- <InlineMath math="4{,}400\text{ m}^2" /> subtracts only two of the three corner triangles and forgets the one at <InlineMath math="AB" />.
- <InlineMath math="6{,}800\text{ m}^2" /> forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\frac12" /> in the determinant formula.
- <InlineMath math="8{,}000\text{ m}^2" /> is the whole rectangle.
