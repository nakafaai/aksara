No side of the triangle is parallel to an axis. So **put an axis-parallel rectangle around the triangle** and subtract the three right triangles left over. Work in coordinate units first and convert to square metres only at the end. The German notation <InlineMath math="A(x\mid y)" /> separates the two coordinates with a vertical bar.

The rectangle runs from <InlineMath math="x=-10" /> to <InlineMath math="x=13" /> and from <InlineMath math="y=-11" /> to <InlineMath math="y=14" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A_{\text{rectangle}}&=23\cdot25=575 \\
\text{corner at }AB&:\ \tfrac12\cdot23\cdot1=11.5 \\
\text{corner at }BC&:\ \tfrac12\cdot22\cdot25=275 \\
\text{corner at }CA&:\ \tfrac12\cdot1\cdot24=12 \\
A_{ABC}&=575-11.5-275-12=276.5
\end{aligned}" />

One unit is <InlineMath math="10\text{ m}" />, so one square unit is <InlineMath math="10\cdot10=100\text{ m}^2" />. The area is therefore <InlineMath math="276.5\cdot100=27{,}650\text{ m}^2" />.

As a check with the determinant formula, <InlineMath math="\frac12\,|23\cdot24-1\cdot(-1)|=\frac12\cdot553=276.5" />.

So the swimming zone has an area of <InlineMath math="27{,}650\text{ m}^2" />.
