# Given

In **triangle** (*Dreieck*) <InlineMath math="ABC" />, side <InlineMath math="\overline{AB}" /> has length <InlineMath math="6" />. Two segments parallel to <InlineMath math="\overline{BC}" /> split the triangle into three regions of equal area (*gleichem Flächeninhalt*), cutting <InlineMath math="\overline{AB}" /> at <InlineMath math="P" /> and <InlineMath math="Q" /> with <InlineMath math="P" /> closer to <InlineMath math="A" />.

# Asked

Find the length of segment <InlineMath math="\overline{PQ}" />.

# Solution

Picture a triangular slice of cake that gets wider toward edge <InlineMath math="BC" />. Because the tip near <InlineMath math="A" /> is narrow, you need **a long cut to claim one third of the cake**. Closer to <InlineMath math="BC" />, the cake is wide so a thinner strip holds the same area.

> For similar figures, the area ratio equals the square of the length ratio, so the length scale factor is always the square root of the area ratio.

See how the parallel cuts through <InlineMath math="P" /> and <InlineMath math="Q" /> divide side <InlineMath math="AB" /> into three segments that shrink as they approach <InlineMath math="B" />.

Segment <InlineMath math="AP" /> is much longer than <InlineMath math="PQ" />, and <InlineMath math="QB" /> is the shortest piece of the three.

## Locating points P and Q

The small triangle bounded by the cut through <InlineMath math="P" /> holds <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{3}" /> of the total area, while the triangle up to <InlineMath math="Q" /> holds <InlineMath math="\frac{2}{3}" />. **Take the square root of each area ratio** and multiply by <InlineMath math="6" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
AP &= 6\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{3}} = 2\sqrt{3} \\
AQ &= 6\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}} = \frac{6\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}} = 2\sqrt{6}
\end{aligned}" />

Subtract to get the exact length of <InlineMath math="PQ" />.

<BlockMath math="PQ = AQ - AP = 2\sqrt{6} - 2\sqrt{3} \approx 4.899 - 3.464 = 1.435" />

## Sanity check and wrong choices

The three pieces along <InlineMath math="AB" /> measure roughly <InlineMath math="3.464" />, <InlineMath math="1.435" /> and <InlineMath math="1.101" />, summing to <InlineMath math="6" /> and **shrinking steadily toward <InlineMath math="B" />**.

- <InlineMath math="2" /> wrongly splits <InlineMath math="AB" /> into three equal linear parts.
- <InlineMath math="2\sqrt{3}" /> is the first segment <InlineMath math="AP" />.
- <InlineMath math="6 - 2\sqrt{6}" /> is the final segment <InlineMath math="QB" />.
- <InlineMath math="2\sqrt{6}" /> is the full distance <InlineMath math="AQ" /> from <InlineMath math="A" />.
- <InlineMath math="6 - 2\sqrt{3}" /> is the combined segment <InlineMath math="PB" />.

# Answer

The length of segment <InlineMath math="\overline{PQ}" /> is <InlineMath math="2\sqrt{6} - 2\sqrt{3}" />.
