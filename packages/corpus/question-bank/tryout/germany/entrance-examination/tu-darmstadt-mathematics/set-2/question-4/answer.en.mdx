The diagonal needs both side lengths <InlineMath math="a" /> and <InlineMath math="b" />. The perimeter (*Umfang*) gives their **sum** and the area gives their **product**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2(a+b)&=34 &&\Rightarrow\ a+b=17 \\
ab&=60
\end{aligned}" />

We need two numbers with sum <InlineMath math="17" /> and product <InlineMath math="60" />. They are <InlineMath math="12" /> and <InlineMath math="5" />, because <InlineMath math="12+5=17" /> and <InlineMath math="12\cdot5=60" />. The diagonal is the hypotenuse of the right triangle with these sides.

<BlockMath math="d=\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=\sqrt{144+25}=\sqrt{169}=13\text{ cm}" />

It also works without finding the sides: <InlineMath math="d^2=a^2+b^2=(a+b)^2-2ab=289-120=169" />. Both routes give the same value.

So the diagonal is <InlineMath math="13\text{ cm}" /> long.
