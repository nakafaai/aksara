<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
a(a+7)&=120 \\
a^2+7a-120&=0 \\
(a-8)(a+15)&=0
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="8\cdot15=120" /> and <InlineMath math="15-8=7" />.
