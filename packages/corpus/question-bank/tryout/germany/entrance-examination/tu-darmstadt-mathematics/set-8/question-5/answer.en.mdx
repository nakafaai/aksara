# Given

An isosceles trapezoid (*gleichschenkliges Trapez*) has:

- Parallel bases of lengths <InlineMath math="8\text{ cm}" /> and <InlineMath math="2\text{ cm}" />.
- An **incircle** (*Inkreis*) tangent to all four sides from inside.

# Asked

What is the area (*Flächeninhalt*) of the trapezoid?

# Solution

Picture a coin wedged snugly inside a trapezoidal frame touching all four edges. From each corner, the two tangent segments to the coin are equal in length. Adding those pairs around all four corners gives a neat rule: **opposite sides always sum to the same total**, revealing the slanted leg length immediately.

> In any quadrilateral with an incircle, the two pairs of opposite sides have equal sums, so the bases of an isosceles trapezoid immediately determine its legs.

## Finding the leg length from the incircle property

Let <InlineMath math="s" /> be the length of each slanted leg. Equating the sums of opposite sides gives:

<BlockMath math="8 + 2 = s + s \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad 2s = 10 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad s = 5\text{ cm}" />

So **each slanted leg measures exactly <InlineMath math="5\text{ cm}" />**.

## Computing the height with a right triangle

Drop an altitude straight down from one top corner to the bottom base. By symmetry, the overhang at each bottom corner is half the difference of the bases, <InlineMath math="3\text{ cm}" />.

That forms a **right triangle** with hypotenuse <InlineMath math="5" /> and base <InlineMath math="3" />:

<BlockMath math="h = \sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = \sqrt{25 - 9} = 4\text{ cm}" />

Look at the inscribed circle of radius <InlineMath math="r = 2\text{ cm}" />, whose diameter matches the trapezoid height exactly.

Now apply the trapezoid area formula:

<BlockMath math="A = \frac{8 + 2}{2} \cdot 4 = 5 \cdot 4 = 20\text{ cm}^2" />

Cross-check via the incircle area rule: radius <InlineMath math="r = 2\text{ cm}" /> times semiperimeter <InlineMath math="10\text{ cm}" /> also yields <InlineMath math="2 \cdot 10 = 20\text{ cm}^2" />.

# Answer

The area of the trapezoid is **<InlineMath math="20\text{ cm}^2" />**.
