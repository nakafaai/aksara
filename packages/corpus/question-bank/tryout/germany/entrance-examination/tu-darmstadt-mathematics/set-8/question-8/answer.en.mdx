<BlockMath math="\left(0.2\right)^{-3}=\left(5^{-1}\right)^{-3}=5^{(-1)\cdot(-3)}=5^{3}=125" />

<BlockMath math="\frac{5^3}{5^2}=5^{3-2}=5" />
