The task asks for the area of the octagon (*Achteck*). The octagon is **the square without the four cut-off triangles**. So subtract the area of the four corners from the area of the square.

Each triangle is right-angled with legs <InlineMath math="2\text{ cm}" /> and <InlineMath math="2\text{ cm}" />. Its area is half the product of the legs.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A_{\text{triangle}}&=\frac12\cdot2\cdot2=2\text{ cm}^2 \\
A_{\text{octagon}}&=8^2-4\cdot2=64-8=56\text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, imagine pushing two corners together. Two such triangles make a square of <InlineMath math="2\text{ cm}" /> by <InlineMath math="2\text{ cm}" />, which is <InlineMath math="4\text{ cm}^2" />. All four corners together are then <InlineMath math="8\text{ cm}^2" />.

So the octagon has an area of <InlineMath math="56\text{ cm}^2" />.

- <InlineMath math="48\text{ cm}^2" /> subtracts a whole square of <InlineMath math="4\text{ cm}^2" /> at each corner. The triangle is only half of that.
- <InlineMath math="60\text{ cm}^2" /> takes the triangle's area as <InlineMath math="\frac12\cdot2=1" /> and forgets the second leg.
- <InlineMath math="62\text{ cm}^2" /> subtracts only one corner.
- <InlineMath math="64\text{ cm}^2" /> is the whole square, with nothing cut off.
- <InlineMath math="58\text{ cm}^2" /> subtracts only three of the four corners.
