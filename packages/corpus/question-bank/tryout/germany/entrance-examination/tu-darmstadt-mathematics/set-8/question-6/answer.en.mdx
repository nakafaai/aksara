A linear function <InlineMath math="f(x)=mx+n" /> is strictly decreasing exactly when **its slope <InlineMath math="m" /> is negative**. The intercept <InlineMath math="n=1" /> plays no role.

Here the slope is <InlineMath math="m=2-k" />. Solve the inequality <InlineMath math="m<0" />.

<BlockMath math="2-k<0\quad\Rightarrow\quad2<k\quad\Rightarrow\quad k>2" />
