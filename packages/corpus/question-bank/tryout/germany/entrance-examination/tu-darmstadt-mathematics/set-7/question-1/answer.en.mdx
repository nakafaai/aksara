The task asks you to rewrite the fraction so that no root remains in the denominator. You remove a difference of roots from a denominator by **expanding with the sum of the same roots**. The denominator then becomes a difference of squares, and the squares of the roots are whole numbers.

<BlockMath math="\frac{2}{\sqrt5-\sqrt3}\cdot\frac{\sqrt5+\sqrt3}{\sqrt5+\sqrt3}=\frac{2\left(\sqrt5+\sqrt3\right)}{\left(\sqrt5\right)^2-\left(\sqrt3\right)^2}" />

The denominator is <InlineMath math="5-3=2" />. This <InlineMath math="2" /> cancels against the factor <InlineMath math="2" /> in the numerator.

<BlockMath math="\frac{2\left(\sqrt5+\sqrt3\right)}{2}=\sqrt5+\sqrt3" />

As a check, multiply the result by the original denominator. You get <InlineMath math="\left(\sqrt5+\sqrt3\right)\left(\sqrt5-\sqrt3\right)=5-3=2" />, exactly the original numerator.

So the fraction equals <InlineMath math="\sqrt5+\sqrt3" />.
