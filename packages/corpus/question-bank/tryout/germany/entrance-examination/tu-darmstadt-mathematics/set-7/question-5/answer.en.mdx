# Given

A person <InlineMath math="1.8\text{ m}" /> tall stands on flat ground near a vertical streetlamp with a light at its top.

- At the first spot, **their shadow is <InlineMath math="2\text{ m}" /> long**.
- After walking <InlineMath math="3\text{ m}" /> farther straight away from the base of the pole, **their shadow grows to <InlineMath math="3.5\text{ m}" />**.

# Asked

How high above the ground is the lamp?

# Solution

Have you noticed your shadow stretching longer as you walk away from a streetlamp at night? The lamp, the top of your head, and the tip of your shadow always lie on **one straight ray of light**. Because both you and the pole stand upright, every spot creates two similar right triangles.

> At every standing position, the light ray creates two similar right triangles, keeping the ratio of height to base identical.

## Setting up two equations from similar triangles

Let <InlineMath math="H" /> be the lamp height and <InlineMath math="d" /> the initial distance from the pole. By similar triangles (*Strahlensatz*, intercept theorem), any distance <InlineMath math="d" /> and shadow <InlineMath math="s" /> satisfy **one clean relation**:

<BlockMath math="\frac{1.8}{s} = \frac{H}{d + s} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad H s = 1.8(d + s)" />

Apply this relation at the first position (<InlineMath math="s = 2" />) and the second position (<InlineMath math="d + 3" /> and <InlineMath math="s = 3.5" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2H &= 1.8(d + 2) \\
3.5H &= 1.8(d + 3 + 3.5) = 1.8(d + 6.5)
\end{aligned}" />

## Eliminating the unknown distance to find the lamp height

You do not even need to find <InlineMath math="d" /> first. Subtract the first equation from the second so **the variable <InlineMath math="d" /> cancels cleanly**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(3.5 - 2)H &= 1.8\bigl((d + 6.5) - (d + 2)\bigr) \\
1.5H &= 1.8 \cdot 4.5 = 8.1 \\
H &= \frac{8.1}{1.5} = 5.4
\end{aligned}" />

Reverse check: the first equation gives <InlineMath math="d = 4\text{ m}" />. At <InlineMath math="7\text{ m}" /> from the pole, the total base is <InlineMath math="10.5\text{ m}" />, and <InlineMath math="5.4 / 10.5" /> matches <InlineMath math="1.8 / 3.5" /> exactly.

# Answer

The lamp is **<InlineMath math="5.4\text{ m}" />** above the ground.
