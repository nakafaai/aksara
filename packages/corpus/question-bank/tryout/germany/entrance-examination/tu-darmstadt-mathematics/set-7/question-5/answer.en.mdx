The task asks how tall the image (*Bild*) of the person is on the back wall of the pinhole camera. The light rays from the person's head and feet pass straight through the hole. They cross there like two lines, and the person and the image are parallel. That is the figure of the **intercept theorem with the hole as its vertex**.

The lengths are in the same ratio as the distances from the hole. First convert both distances to the same unit: <InlineMath math="20\text{ cm}=0.2\text{ m}" />.

<BlockMath math="\frac{b}{1.8}=\frac{0.2}{6}\quad\Rightarrow\quad b=1.8\cdot\frac{0.2}{6}=\frac{0.36}{6}=0.06" />

So the image is <InlineMath math="0.06\text{ m}=6\text{ cm}" /> tall. As a check, look at the scale. The person stands <InlineMath math="6:0.2=30" /> times as far from the hole as the back wall, so the image is <InlineMath math="30" /> times smaller: <InlineMath math="180\text{ cm}:30=6\text{ cm}" />.

So the image of the person is <InlineMath math="6\text{ cm}" /> tall.
