The distance from <InlineMath math="S" /> to <InlineMath math="B" /> comes from the two given parts: <InlineMath math="\overline{SB}=\overline{SA}+\overline{AB}=4+6=10\text{ cm}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{\overline{BB'}}{\overline{AA'}}&=\frac{\overline{SB}}{\overline{SA}} \\
\overline{BB'}&=3\cdot\frac{10}{4}=7.5\text{ cm}
\end{aligned}" />

So the segment <InlineMath math="\overline{BB'}" /> is <InlineMath math="7.5\text{ cm}" /> long.
