# Three conditions at once

"Two different real solutions that are both positive" is really three conditions. **You need all three, not just one**:

- there are two different real solutions, so the discriminant is positive
- the sum of the solutions is positive
- the product of the solutions is positive

Two real numbers are both positive exactly when their sum and their product are both positive. A positive product means they have the same sign, and a positive sum then makes that sign positive.

# Working out each condition

For <InlineMath math="x^2-2ax+(a+6)=0" />, **the sum and product rules** give <InlineMath math="x_1+x_2=2a" /> and <InlineMath math="x_1x_2=a+6" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
D=4a^2-4(a+6)=4(a-3)(a+2)>0\quad&\Rightarrow\quad a<-2\ \text{or}\ a>3 \\
x_1+x_2=2a>0\quad&\Rightarrow\quad a>0 \\
x_1x_2=a+6>0\quad&\Rightarrow\quad a>-6
\end{aligned}" />

# Where all three overlap

<InlineMath math="a" /> has to satisfy all three. The part <InlineMath math="a<-2" /> fails <InlineMath math="a>0" />, so **only <InlineMath math="a>3" /> is left**.

Check two examples. For <InlineMath math="a=4" />: <InlineMath math="x^2-8x+10=0" /> has solutions <InlineMath math="4\pm\sqrt6" />, both positive. For <InlineMath math="a=-3" />: <InlineMath math="x^2+6x+3=0" /> has solutions adding up to <InlineMath math="-6" />, so both are negative.

So the equation has two different positive solutions exactly for <InlineMath math="a>3" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="a<-2" /> or <InlineMath math="a>3" /> only uses the discriminant. For <InlineMath math="a<-2" /> both solutions are real but not positive.
- <InlineMath math="a>0" /> only uses the sum. For <InlineMath math="a=1" />, the discriminant is <InlineMath math="4(-2)(3)<0" />, so there's no real solution.
- <InlineMath math="-6<a<-2" /> or <InlineMath math="a>3" /> **forgets the sum condition**. The example <InlineMath math="a=-3" /> above gives two negative solutions.
- <InlineMath math="a\ge3" /> includes <InlineMath math="a=3" />. There <InlineMath math="x^2-6x+9=0" /> has only one solution, <InlineMath math="x=3" />.
- <InlineMath math="-2<a<3" /> is where the discriminant is negative, so its sign was read the wrong way round.
