# Draw the left side first

The number of solutions of <InlineMath math="|x^2-4x|=a" /> equals **the number of points where the graph of <InlineMath math="y=|x^2-4x|" /> meets the horizontal line <InlineMath math="y=a" />**. So draw the left side first.

Without the absolute value, <InlineMath math="x^2-4x=(x-2)^2-4" /> is an upward parabola with vertex <InlineMath math="(2\mid-4)" /> and zeros at <InlineMath math="x=0" /> and <InlineMath math="x=4" />. The absolute value flips the negative part, between <InlineMath math="0" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />, above the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis. The vertex <InlineMath math="(2\mid-4)" /> becomes a hill with its top at <InlineMath math="(2\mid4)" />.

# Counting meeting points for each value of a

Slide the line <InlineMath math="y=a" /> from the bottom up and count where it meets the graph:

| Value of <InlineMath math="a" /> | Meeting points |
| :---: | :---: |
| <InlineMath math="a<0" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |
| <InlineMath math="a=0" /> | <InlineMath math="2" /> |
| <InlineMath math="0<a<4" /> | <InlineMath math="4" /> |
| <InlineMath math="a=4" /> | <InlineMath math="3" /> |
| <InlineMath math="a>4" /> | <InlineMath math="2" /> |

**Exactly three meeting points only happen when the line touches the top of the hill**, at <InlineMath math="a=4" />.

# Checking the three solutions

For <InlineMath math="a=4" />, the hill gives <InlineMath math="-(x^2-4x)=4" />, that is <InlineMath math="(x-2)^2=0" />, so <InlineMath math="x=2" />. The outer arms give <InlineMath math="x^2-4x=4" />, that is <InlineMath math="(x-2)^2=8" />, so <InlineMath math="x=2\pm2\sqrt2" />. **There are exactly three different solutions**.

So the equation has exactly three solutions for <InlineMath math="a=4" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="a=0" /> only gives <InlineMath math="x=0" /> and <InlineMath math="x=4" />, two solutions.
- <InlineMath math="0<a<4" /> gives four solutions: two on the hill and two on the outer arms. One example is <InlineMath math="a=2" />.
- <InlineMath math="a>4" /> is above the hill, so the line only crosses the two outer arms.
- <InlineMath math="a=-4" /> is the vertex value of the parabola without the absolute value. **An absolute value is never negative**, so there's no solution.
