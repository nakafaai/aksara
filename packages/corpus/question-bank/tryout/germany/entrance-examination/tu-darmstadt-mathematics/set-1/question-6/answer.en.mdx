The path is a diagonal of the rectangle. It splits the rectangle into two right triangles whose legs are the sides <InlineMath math="21\text{ m}" /> and <InlineMath math="20\text{ m}" />. The diagonal is the **hypotenuse**, so the Pythagorean theorem applies.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
d^2&=21^2+20^2=441+400=841 \\
d&=\sqrt{841}=29\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="29^2=841" />, and the hypotenuse is longer than each leg but shorter than their sum <InlineMath math="41\text{ m}" />.

So the path is <InlineMath math="29\text{ m}" /> long.

- <InlineMath math="\sqrt{41}\text{ m}" /> comes from <InlineMath math="21^2-20^2=41" />. For the hypotenuse, the squares of the legs are added.
- <InlineMath math="21\text{ m}" /> is only the longer side. A diagonal is always longer than each side.
- <InlineMath math="25\text{ m}" /> and <InlineMath math="27\text{ m}" /> do not fit, because <InlineMath math="25^2=625" /> and <InlineMath math="27^2=729" /> are less than <InlineMath math="841" />.
- <InlineMath math="41\text{ m}" /> is the sum of the sides, the way along two edges instead of straight across the plot.
