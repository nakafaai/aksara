# Given

A **right prism** (*gerades Prisma*) has <InlineMath math="30" /> edges (*Kanten*). A **space diagonal** (*Raumdiagonale*) connects two vertices that do not share any face of the prism.

# Asked

Find the total number of space diagonals in this prism.

# Solution

Picture a tower with identical top and bottom floors. Each base corner contributes three edges: one on the bottom, one on the top, and one vertical pillar. With <InlineMath math="30" /> edges total, the base must be a **decagon**.

<BlockMath math="3n = 30 \implies n = 10" />

So the prism has <InlineMath math="20" /> vertices in all: <InlineMath math="10" /> on the top face and <InlineMath math="10" /> on the bottom face.

> Every space diagonal must bridge one top vertex to one bottom vertex that is neither directly beneath it nor one of its two side-face neighbors.

Look at this top view of the ten-sided base: from one top corner <InlineMath math="A_0" />, three bottom positions are ruled out (the point directly below plus its two adjacent corners), leaving seven valid targets.

Because every space diagonal has one top endpoint and one bottom endpoint, counting from the ten top vertices counts each diagonal once with zero overlap.

## Total count and verification

Multiply the <InlineMath math="10" /> top vertices by the <InlineMath math="7" /> valid bottom vertices.

<BlockMath math="10 \times 7 = 70" />

Cross-check by subtraction: out of <InlineMath math="\binom{20}{2} = 190" /> vertex pairs, remove <InlineMath math="30" /> edges, <InlineMath math="70" /> base diagonals and <InlineMath math="20" /> rectangular side-face diagonals.

<BlockMath math="190 - 30 - 70 - 20 = 70" />

**Both counts match at <InlineMath math="70" />**.

## Why other choices fail

- Getting <InlineMath math="90" /> **removes only the vertex directly below** and forgets the two neighbors on shared side faces.
- Getting <InlineMath math="35" /> divides <InlineMath math="70" /> by two unnecessarily, even though top-to-bottom counting never double-counts.
- Getting <InlineMath math="80" /> excludes only one bottom neighbor instead of both.
- Getting <InlineMath math="140" /> forgets to subtract the <InlineMath math="70" /> base diagonals from all vertex pairs.
- Getting <InlineMath math="160" /> subtracts only the <InlineMath math="30" /> edges and keeps all face diagonals.

# Answer

A right prism with **<InlineMath math="30" />** edges has exactly **<InlineMath math="70" />** space diagonals.
