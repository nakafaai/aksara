- the whole long side at the bottom, <InlineMath math="12\text{ cm}" />,
- the rest of the short side on the right, <InlineMath math="8-4=4\text{ cm}" />,
- the cut line,
- the rest of the long side at the top, <InlineMath math="12-3=9\text{ cm}" />,
- the whole short side on the left, <InlineMath math="8\text{ cm}" />.

The cut line is the hypotenuse of the removed right triangle with legs <InlineMath math="3\text{ cm}" /> and <InlineMath math="4\text{ cm}" />.
