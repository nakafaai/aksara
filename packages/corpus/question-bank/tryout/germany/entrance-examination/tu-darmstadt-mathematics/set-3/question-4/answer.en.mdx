# Put the triangle into coordinates

Place the right angle at the origin <InlineMath math="C(0\mid0)" /> and the legs on the axes: <InlineMath math="A(8\mid0)" /> and <InlineMath math="B(0\mid6)" />. Then **both centres become points with coordinates**. The hypotenuse is <InlineMath math="\overline{AB}=\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=10" />.

# Find the incentre

The incircle touches both legs, so **<InlineMath math="I" /> is at distance <InlineMath math="r" /> from both axes**: <InlineMath math="I(r\mid r)" />. The area gives the radius. Split the triangle at <InlineMath math="I" /> into three smaller triangles whose heights are all <InlineMath math="r" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac12\cdot8\cdot6&=\frac12\,r\,(8+6+10) \\
24&=12r \\
r&=2
\end{aligned}" />

So <InlineMath math="I(2\mid2)" />.

# Find the circumcentre

In a right triangle **the circumcentre is the midpoint of the hypotenuse** (Thales' theorem). The midpoint of <InlineMath math="A(8\mid0)" /> and <InlineMath math="B(0\mid6)" /> is <InlineMath math="U(4\mid3)" />, and the radius is <InlineMath math="5" />.

# Work out the distance

<BlockMath math="\overline{IU}=\sqrt{(4-2)^2+(3-2)^2}=\sqrt{4+1}=\sqrt5" />

The two centres are **<InlineMath math="\sqrt5" /> apart**.

# Why the other answers are wrong

- <InlineMath math="\sqrt{13}" /> comes from <InlineMath math="r=1" />: the area was divided by **the full perimeter <InlineMath math="24" /> instead of half of it**, <InlineMath math="12" />, and <InlineMath math="I(1\mid1)" /> is <InlineMath math="\sqrt{9+4}" /> away from <InlineMath math="U" />.
- <InlineMath math="3" /> is the difference of the radii, <InlineMath math="5-2" />. But the two centres don't lie on one line with a point where the circles touch.
- <InlineMath math="7" /> is the sum of the radii, <InlineMath math="5+2" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac23" /> comes from mixing up <InlineMath math="U" /> with the centroid <InlineMath math="\left(\frac83\mid2\right)" />. The centroid isn't the circumcentre.
- <InlineMath math="2\sqrt2" /> is the distance from <InlineMath math="I" /> to the corner <InlineMath math="C" />, not to <InlineMath math="U" />.
