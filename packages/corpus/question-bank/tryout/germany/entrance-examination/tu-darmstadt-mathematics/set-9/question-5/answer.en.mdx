# Given

Point <InlineMath math="P(5 \mid 0)" /> is **reflected (*gespiegelt*) across the straight line** <InlineMath math="g" /> through the origin given by:

<BlockMath math="y = 2x" />

The reflection produces the image point (*Bildpunkt*) <InlineMath math="P'" />.

# Asked

What are the coordinates of the image point <InlineMath math="P'" />?

# Solution

When you stand in front of a flat mirror, the line from your eyes to your reflection always **meets the glass at a right angle**, and the mirror sits dead in the **midpoint** between you and your reflection. So drop a perpendicular from <InlineMath math="P" /> to the mirror line <InlineMath math="g" />, find the foot <InlineMath math="F" />, and step the same distance straight past <InlineMath math="F" />.

> The foot of the perpendicular from a point to a mirror line is always the exact midpoint between the original point and its reflection.

## Finding the perpendicular line and mirror foot

The mirror line <InlineMath math="g" /> has slope <InlineMath math="2" />, so any perpendicular line has **negative reciprocal slope <InlineMath math="-\frac{1}{2}" />**. Passing through <InlineMath math="P(5 \mid 0)" />, its equation is:

<BlockMath math="y = -\frac{1}{2}(x - 5)" />

Intersect it with the mirror <InlineMath math="y = 2x" /> to locate the foot <InlineMath math="F" />:

<BlockMath math="2x = -\frac{1}{2}(x - 5) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad 4x = -x + 5 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad x = 1, \quad y = 2" />

So the foot on the mirror is <InlineMath math="F(1 \mid 2)" />.

## Stepping equally far past the midpoint

Because <InlineMath math="F(1 \mid 2)" /> is the midpoint of <InlineMath math="\overline{PP'}" />, the image follows from **the midpoint rule <InlineMath math="P' = 2F - P" />**:

<BlockMath math="P' = \bigl(2 \cdot 1 - 5 \ \big|\  2 \cdot 2 - 0\bigr) = (-3 \mid 4)" />

## Checking the distractors

See how each wrong option **stops halfway or reflects across the wrong axis**:

| <InlineMath math="P'(0 \mid 5)" /> | Reflects across <InlineMath math="y = x" /> instead of <InlineMath math="y = 2x" />. |

| <InlineMath math="P'(-4 \mid 3)" /> | Swaps the <InlineMath math="x" /> and <InlineMath math="y" /> coordinates of the true reflection. |

# Answer

The coordinates of the image point are **<InlineMath math="P'(-3 \mid 4)" />**.
