# Given

**A pond starts with** <InlineMath math="800" /> rudd and <InlineMath math="100" /> sunfish. Rudd halve exponentially every <InlineMath math="2" /> months while sunfish double exponentially every <InlineMath math="2" /> months. Later, the total fish count hits <InlineMath math="600" /> for the second time.

# Asked

How many sunfish live in the pond at that second moment?

# Solution

> Because one species doubles on the exact same clock that halves the other, **naming the doubling factor <InlineMath math="u" /> turns the whole sum into a simple quadratic** with two clean roots.

## Two species on the same clock

One balloon loses half its air every two months, another doubles every two months. Both changes tick with the same clock, the power <InlineMath math="2^{t/2}" /> with <InlineMath math="t" /> in months. **The rudd shrink with <InlineMath math="2^{-t/2}" />, the sunfish grow with <InlineMath math="2^{t/2}" />**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
R(t)&=800\cdot2^{-t/2}\\
S(t)&=100\cdot2^{t/2}
\end{aligned}" />

## One new letter makes a quadratic

Since <InlineMath math="2^{-t/2}" />, everything depends on the single number <InlineMath math="u=2^{t/2}" />, which is always positive. **Write <InlineMath math="R+S=600" /> in <InlineMath math="u" /> and multiply by <InlineMath math="u" />**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{800}{u}+100u&=600\\
100u^2-600u+800&=0\\
u^2-6u+8&=0\\
(u-2)(u-4)&=0
\end{aligned}" />

Both roots are positive, so both are real moments. Back from <InlineMath math="u" /> to months:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2^{t/2}=2&\ \Rightarrow\ t=2\\
2^{t/2}=4&\ \Rightarrow\ t=4\\
S(4)&=100\cdot4=400
\end{aligned}" />

## The second moment rather than the first

After <InlineMath math="2" /> months there are <InlineMath math="400" /> rudd and <InlineMath math="200" /> sunfish, the total is still falling. Between the two moments it dips below <InlineMath math="600" />, and **after <InlineMath math="4" /> months it climbs back to <InlineMath math="600" /> with <InlineMath math="200" /> rudd and <InlineMath math="400" /> sunfish**. Taking the first moment would give <InlineMath math="200" /> sunfish.

# Answer

There are <InlineMath math="400" /> sunfish in the pond at that time.
