# The cable as a parabola

Hold a skipping rope at both ends and it sags into a gentle U. Put the origin on the deck right under the lowest point of the cable. **Then the vertex is <InlineMath math="(0\mid2)" /> and each pylon top is <InlineMath math="80\text{ m}" /> to the side at height <InlineMath math="42" />, which fixes the parabola**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
y&=2+ax^2\\
42&=2+a\cdot80^2\\
a&=\frac{40}{6400}=\frac{1}{160}
\end{aligned}" />

A hanger at position <InlineMath math="x" /> reaches from the deck up to the cable, so it is <InlineMath math="2+\frac{x^2}{160}" /> metres long.

# Which positions get a real hanger

The plan lists <InlineMath math="x=0,\pm10,\dots,\pm70" />, fifteen spots, because the next step <InlineMath math="\pm80" /> is a pylon. **The clamp rule then removes every spot where the cable is less than <InlineMath math="3\text{ m}" /> high**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2+\frac{x^2}{160}&<3\\
x^2&<160
\end{aligned}" />

Since <InlineMath math="10^2=100" /> is below <InlineMath math="160" /> and <InlineMath math="20^2=400" /> is above it, only <InlineMath math="x=0" /> and <InlineMath math="x=\pm10" /> get clamps. Twelve hangers remain, from <InlineMath math="\pm20" /> to <InlineMath math="\pm70" />.

# Adding by mirror pairs

Left and right of the middle the hangers come in equal pairs, so add one side and double it. **Writing <InlineMath math="x=10k" /> turns the squares into <InlineMath math="100k^2" />, and <InlineMath math="2^2+3^2+\dots+7^2=139" />**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L&=2\sum_{k=2}^{7}\left(2+\frac{(10k)^2}{160}\right)\\
&=2\left(12+\frac{100}{160}\cdot139\right)\\
&=2\left(12+86.875\right)=197.75\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

Taking all fifteen planned spots gives <InlineMath math="205\text{ m}" />, and the clamps save exactly <InlineMath math="2+2\cdot2.625=7.25\text{ m}" /> of that.
