# What box B tells you about the moved resistor

If you drop one unknown coin from your left pocket into your right pocket and then pull two gold coins out of the right pocket, it becomes much more likely that you moved a gold coin. **Let <InlineMath math="H_{100}" /> and <InlineMath math="H_{200}" /> be the two transfer cases and <InlineMath math="E" /> the event that both resistors drawn from B are <InlineMath math="200\,\Omega" />**. Before looking at B, <InlineMath math="P(H_{100})=\tfrac13" /> and <InlineMath math="P(H_{200})=\tfrac23" />.

After the transfer box B holds five resistors: two <InlineMath math="100\,\Omega" /> and three <InlineMath math="200\,\Omega" /> under <InlineMath math="H_{100}" />, or one <InlineMath math="100\,\Omega" /> and four <InlineMath math="200\,\Omega" /> under <InlineMath math="H_{200}" />. **Drawing two <InlineMath math="200\,\Omega" /> resistors without replacement doubles the likelihood under <InlineMath math="H_{200}" />, so Bayes' rule shifts the weights to <InlineMath math="20\,\%" /> and <InlineMath math="80\,\%" />**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(H_{100}\cap E)=\tfrac26\cdot\tfrac35\cdot\tfrac24=\tfrac1{10},\qquad P(H_{200}\cap E)=\tfrac46\cdot\tfrac45\cdot\tfrac34=\tfrac4{10}\\
P(E)=\tfrac1{10}+\tfrac4{10}=\tfrac12,\qquad P(H_{100}\mid E)=\tfrac15=20\,\%,\qquad P(H_{200}\mid E)=\tfrac45=80\,\%
\end{aligned}" />

# What is left inside box A in each case

Now move back to box A, which holds five resistors, and write <InlineMath math="G" /> for drawing two resistors of the same value. **Here sits the trap: under <InlineMath math="H_{100}" /> only one <InlineMath math="100\,\Omega" /> resistor stays in A, so a <InlineMath math="100\,\Omega" /> pair is impossible, whereas under <InlineMath math="H_{200}" /> both <InlineMath math="200\,\Omega" /> pairs and <InlineMath math="100\,\Omega" /> pairs count**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(G\mid H_{100})=\tfrac45\cdot\tfrac34+0=\tfrac35=60\,\%\\
P(G\mid H_{200})=\tfrac35\cdot\tfrac24+\tfrac25\cdot\tfrac14=\tfrac3{10}+\tfrac1{10}=\tfrac25=40\,\%\\
P(G\mid E)=0.20\cdot0.60+0.80\cdot0.40=0.12+0.32=0.44=44\,\%
\end{aligned}" />
