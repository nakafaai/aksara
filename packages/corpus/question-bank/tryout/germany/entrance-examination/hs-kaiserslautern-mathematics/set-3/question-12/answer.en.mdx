# Finding the wind speed from the test flight

An east-west round trip feels the wind straight behind on the way out and straight against on the way back. **Combine both legs of the <InlineMath math="7\text{ km}" /> test flight to pull the unknown wind speed <InlineMath math="w" /> directly from the <InlineMath math="48" />-minute total**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{7}{40+w}+\frac{7}{40-w}=\frac{48}{60}=\frac45\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \frac{560}{1600-w^2}=\frac45\\
1600-w^2=700\ \Longrightarrow\ w^2=900\ \Longrightarrow\ w=30\,\tfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}
\end{aligned}" />

# Splitting the oblique wind along and across the corridor

**Here hides the key trap: because the corridor runs along <InlineMath math="(3\mid4)" />, the east wind has both an along-track part and a crosswind part, and you must spend airspeed cancelling the crosswind first**. Along the unit vector <InlineMath math="(\tfrac35\mid\tfrac45)" /> the crosswind is <InlineMath math="24\,\tfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}" />, cutting the forward airspeed component from <InlineMath math="40" /> down to <InlineMath math="32\,\tfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
w_\parallel=30\cdot\tfrac35=18\,\tfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}},\qquad w_\perp=30\cdot\tfrac45=24\,\tfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}\\
v_\parallel=\sqrt{40^2-24^2}=\sqrt{1024}=32\,\tfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}\\
v_{\text{out}}=32+18=50\,\tfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}},\qquad v_{\text{back}}=32-18=14\,\tfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}
\end{aligned}" />

Forgetting the crosswind compensation and taking <InlineMath math="40\pm18" /> would grossly overestimate the range at <InlineMath math="25.52\text{ km}" /> instead of <InlineMath math="17.5\text{ km}" />.

# Computing the maximum distance from usable flight time

**Keep <InlineMath math="20\,\%" /> battery reserve and subtract the <InlineMath math="4" />-minute hover, leaving <InlineMath math="96" /> minutes (<InlineMath math="\tfrac85\text{ h}" />) of flying time**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
t_{\text{fly}}=0.8\cdot125-4=96\text{ min}=\tfrac85\text{ h}\\
\frac{d}{50}+\frac{d}{14}=\frac{32d}{350}=\frac{16d}{175}=\frac85\ \Longrightarrow\ d=17.5\text{ km}
\end{aligned}" />

Check both legs at <InlineMath math="17.5\text{ km}" />: <InlineMath math="21" /> minutes out plus <InlineMath math="75" /> minutes back equals <InlineMath math="96" /> minutes. **The customer may live at most <InlineMath math="17.5\text{ km}" /> away**.
