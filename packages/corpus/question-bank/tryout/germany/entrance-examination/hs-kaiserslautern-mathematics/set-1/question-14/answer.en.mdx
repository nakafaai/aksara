# Two timings, two unknowns

Picture the train as a long snake going through a pipe. To get all the way through, its head travels the tunnel length plus its own length. To be completely inside, the head only travels the tunnel length minus its own length. **So <InlineMath math="\frac{L+300}{v}=50" /> and <InlineMath math="\frac{L-300}{v}=30" />**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L+300&=50v\\
L-300&=30v\\
600&=20v\ \Rightarrow\ v=30\,\tfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}},\ L=1200\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

# Each train's window

Start the clock when the freight train is fully inside. **It stays fully inside for its <InlineMath math="30\text{ s}" />, until <InlineMath math="t=30" />**. The passenger train's head enters at <InlineMath math="t=0" />, but its tail follows <InlineMath math="\frac{200}{20}=10\text{ s}" /> later. It is fully inside from <InlineMath math="t=10" /> until its head reaches the far end at <InlineMath math="\frac{1200}{20}=60\text{ s}" />.

# The overlap

Both windows are open from <InlineMath math="t=10" /> to <InlineMath math="t=30" />. **That is <InlineMath math="20" /> seconds**. Letting the passenger train count as inside the moment its head enters would give <InlineMath math="30\text{ s}" />.
