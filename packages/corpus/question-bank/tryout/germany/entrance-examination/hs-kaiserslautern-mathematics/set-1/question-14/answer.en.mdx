# Given

A <InlineMath math="300\text{ m}" /> **freight train** takes <InlineMath math="50\text{ s}" /> from nose entering to tail leaving a straight tunnel and stays completely inside for <InlineMath math="30\text{ s}" />. The instant it is fully inside, a <InlineMath math="200\text{ m}" /> passenger train enters from the opposite end at <InlineMath math="20\,\tfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}" />.

# Asked

For how many seconds are both trains completely inside the tunnel at the same time?

# Solution

> Comparing total passage time with fully-inside time cancels the tunnel length and leaves **the exact time the train needs to cover twice its own length**.

## Two timings two unknowns

Picture the train as a long snake going through a pipe. To get all the way through, its head travels the tunnel length plus its own length. To be completely inside, the head only travels the tunnel length minus its own length. **So <InlineMath math="(L+300)/v = 50" /> and <InlineMath math="(L-300)/v = 30" />**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L+300&=50v\\
L-300&=30v\\
600&=20v\ \Rightarrow\ v=30\,\tfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}},\ L=1200\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

## Each trains window

Start the clock when the freight train is fully inside. **It stays fully inside for its <InlineMath math="30\text{ s}" />, until <InlineMath math="t=30" />**. The passenger train's head enters at <InlineMath math="t=0" />, but its tail follows <InlineMath math="10\text{ s}" /> later. It is fully inside from <InlineMath math="t=10" /> until its head reaches the far end at <InlineMath math="60\text{ s}" />.

Comparing both interval bars makes **the overlap from <InlineMath math="10\text{ s}" /> to <InlineMath math="30\text{ s}" />** immediately visible, giving exactly <InlineMath math="20\text{ s}" />.

## The overlap

Both windows are open from <InlineMath math="t=10" /> to <InlineMath math="t=30" />. **That is <InlineMath math="20" /> seconds**. Letting the passenger train count as inside the moment its head enters would give <InlineMath math="30\text{ s}" />.

# Answer

Both trains are completely inside the tunnel simultaneously for <InlineMath math="20\text{ s}" />.
