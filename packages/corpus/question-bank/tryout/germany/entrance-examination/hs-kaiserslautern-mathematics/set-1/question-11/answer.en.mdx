# Given

**A bacterial culture** starts at 8:00 and grows exponentially, reaching <InlineMath math="5000" /> at 9:30 and <InlineMath math="40000" /> at 11:00. At 12:00 an agent is added that alone cuts the count to one-sixteenth per hour while the original growth continues.

# Asked

How many minutes after the agent is added does the population return to its 8:00 starting size?

# Solution

> When exponential growth and decay act together, **the combined hourly factor is simply the product of the growth factor and the decay factor**.

## How fast the culture doubles

Think of a pile of coins that doubles on a fixed beat. Between the two counts the number of bacteria becomes eight times as large in <InlineMath math="90" /> minutes. **Eight is three doublings, so the culture doubles every <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes and grows fourfold each hour**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{40000}{5000}&=8=2^3\\
T_2&=\frac{90\text{ min}}{3}=30\text{ min}
\end{aligned}" />

## Back to the start and on to noon

Going back in time is halving instead of doubling. **The culture was set up <InlineMath math="90" /> minutes before the first count, three halvings back, and the agent comes one hour after the second count, two doublings on**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
N_0&=\frac{5000}{2^3}=625\\
N_{12}&=40000\cdot2^2=160000
\end{aligned}" />

## Two effects at the same time

Imagine a bathtub with the tap running and the plug pulled out. What is left after an hour depends on both. **The agent alone would leave <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{16}" /> each hour, but in that same hour the bacteria multiply by <InlineMath math="4" />, so the net factor is <InlineMath math="1/4" /> per hour**. Using only <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{16}" /> forgets the growth and gives <InlineMath math="120" /> minutes. Starting from <InlineMath math="40000" /> instead of <InlineMath math="160000" /> forgets the last hour of growth and gives <InlineMath math="180" /> minutes.

## Back to the starting count

Now count how many quarterings bring <InlineMath math="160000" /> down to <InlineMath math="625" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
160000\cdot\left(\tfrac14\right)^s&=625\\
4^s&=\frac{160000}{625}=256=4^4\\
s&=4\text{ h}=240\text{ min}
\end{aligned}" />

**The count is back at its starting value <InlineMath math="240" /> minutes after the agent is added**, at 16:00.

# Answer

The culture returns to its starting size after **<InlineMath math="240\text{ min}" />**.
