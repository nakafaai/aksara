# Given

**A hill profile** follows <InlineMath math="f(x) = 40 - x^2/40" /> for <InlineMath math="[-40, 40]" /> above flat ground <InlineMath math="y=0" />. An observer lies at <InlineMath math="B(50\mid0)" /> and a vertical radio tower stands at <InlineMath math="x=-60" />.

# Asked

What is the minimum height of the tower in metres so the observer can just see its tip over the hill?

# Solution

> The lowest line of sight that clears the hill is **the tangent line from the observer that just grazes the parabolic slope**.

## The line that just grazes

Lie on the ground behind a hill and slide a ruler from your eye until it just touches the hilltop curve: anything above the ruler you can see. **That ruler is the tangent from <InlineMath math="B(50\mid0)" /> to the hill**. It touches at some <InlineMath math="x=a" /> with slope <InlineMath math="-a/20" /> and must pass through <InlineMath math="B" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
f(a)+f'(a)\,(50-a)&=0\\
40-\tfrac{a^2}{40}-\tfrac{a}{20}(50-a)&=0\\
a^2-100a+1600&=0\\
(a-20)(a-80)&=0
\end{aligned}" />

## Choosing the right touch point

The hill only runs from <InlineMath math="x=-40" /> to <InlineMath math="x=40" />, so <InlineMath math="a=80" /> touches the parabola where there is no hill. **The real touch point is <InlineMath math="P(20\mid30)" />, where the slope is <InlineMath math="-1" />**, so the sight line is <InlineMath math="y = -x + 50" />.

The tangent line grazing the hill **meets the vertical tower line at <InlineMath math="110\text{ m}" />**, giving the exact minimum visible height.

## Up to the tower

At <InlineMath math="x=-60" /> the sight line is <InlineMath math="60+50=110\text{ m}" /> high. **The tower must be at least <InlineMath math="110\text{ m}" /> tall**. Aiming at the hilltop <InlineMath math="(0\mid40)" /> gives <InlineMath math="88\text{ m}" />, but that line passes below the hill at <InlineMath math="x=20" />, where it is only <InlineMath math="24\text{ m}" /> high against the hill's <InlineMath math="30\text{ m}" />.

# Answer

The radio tower must be at least **<InlineMath math="110\text{ m}" />** tall.
