# Given

At <InlineMath math="12\text{ €}" /> **per day**, <InlineMath math="40" /> bicycles are rented, changing linearly by <InlineMath math="4" /> bicycles for every <InlineMath math="1\text{ €}" /> price shift. Each rented bicycle costs <InlineMath math="2\text{ €}" /> to service, and the shop owns only <InlineMath math="36" /> bicycles.

# Asked

What is the highest daily profit in euros the rental shop can achieve?

# Solution

> Always check whether **the vertex of the profit parabola stays inside the physical stock cap**, otherwise the best feasible point sits right on the cap.

## Price and bikes on one seesaw

Price and demand sit on a seesaw: push the price up by <InlineMath math="1\text{ €}" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> customers walk away. With price <InlineMath math="p" /> the shop rents <InlineMath math="n = 88 - 4p" /> bikes, and **each bike earns <InlineMath math="p-2" /> euros after cleaning**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
G(p)&=(p-2)(88-4p)\\
&=-4p^2+96p-176
\end{aligned}" />

## The top of the parabola is out of reach

The parabola opens downwards, and its top sits halfway between its zeros <InlineMath math="p=2" /> and <InlineMath math="p=22" />, at <InlineMath math="p=12" /> with <InlineMath math="G=400\text{ €}" />. **But at <InlineMath math="12\text{ €}" /> the shop would need <InlineMath math="40" /> bikes, and it owns only <InlineMath math="36" />**. So <InlineMath math="88-4p\le36" /> forces <InlineMath math="p\ge13" />.

The parabolic peak sits outside the feasible stock region, so **the highest reachable profit lies right on the boundary at <InlineMath math="13\text{ €}" />**.

## The best allowed price

Right of its top the parabola only falls, so on <InlineMath math="p\ge13" /> the best price is the edge itself. **At <InlineMath math="13\text{ €}" /> all <InlineMath math="36" /> bikes go out and <InlineMath math="396\text{ €}" />**. The answer <InlineMath math="400\text{ €}" /> uses four bikes the shop does not have.

# Answer

The maximum daily profit is **<InlineMath math="396\text{ €}" />**.
