All three sides are known, so Heron’s formula gives the area. The semiperimeter is <InlineMath math="s=\frac{13+14+15}{2}=21" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A^2&=s(s-13) \\
&\quad\cdot(s-14)(s-15) \\
&=21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6 \\
&=7{,}056 \\
A&=\sqrt{7{,}056}=84
\end{aligned}" />

The incenter is a distance <InlineMath math="r" /> from every side. Three smaller triangles of height <InlineMath math="r" /> fill the original triangle, so:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&=\frac12r(13+14+15)=rs \\
r&=\frac{84}{21}=4
\end{aligned}" />

The inradius is <InlineMath math="4" /> length units.
