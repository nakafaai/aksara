The required region is the sector minus the triangle formed by the two radii. The sector has area

<BlockMath math="\frac{120}{360}\pi(12^2)=48\pi" />

and the triangle has area

<BlockMath math="\frac12(12)(12)\sin120^\circ=36\sqrt3" />

Therefore, the shaded area is <InlineMath math="48\pi-36\sqrt3" /> cm². Using the sector area alone would include the triangle between the two radii.
