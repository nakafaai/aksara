The logarithm base requires <InlineMath math="x>0" /> and <InlineMath math="x\neq1" />. Its second argument is positive throughout this domain because

<BlockMath math="2x-4+\frac{4}{x}=\frac{2\left((x-1)^2+1\right)}{x}>0" />

Combine the logarithms and use <InlineMath math="1=\log_x x" />.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\log_x 3 - \log_x\left(2x - 4 + \frac{4}{x}\right) = 1" />
<BlockMath math="\log_x \frac{3}{2x - 4 + \frac{4}{x}} = \log_x x" />
<BlockMath math="\frac{3}{2x - 4 + \frac{4}{x}} = x" />
<BlockMath math="\left(2x - 4 + \frac{4}{x}\right)x = 3" />
<BlockMath math="2x^2 - 4x + 4 = 3" />
<BlockMath math="2x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0" />
</MathContainer>

Both roots <InlineMath math="1\pm\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}" /> are positive and different from <InlineMath math="1" />, so both belong to the original domain. Vieta's formula now gives

<BlockMath math="\alpha + \beta = \frac{-b}{a} = \frac{-(-4)}{2} = 2" />
