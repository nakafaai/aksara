Completing the square gives <InlineMath math="f(x)=(x-2)^2-1" />. Thus the vertex is <InlineMath math="(2,-1)" />, the range is <InlineMath math="[-1,\infty)" />, and the function decreases up to <InlineMath math="x=2" />. It is not one-to-one on all of <InlineMath math="\mathbb R" />. On <InlineMath math="[2,\infty)" />, its inverse is <InlineMath math="f^{-1}(y)=2+\sqrt{y+1}" />.
