For positive numbers, a fractional power takes a root and then raises it to a power. Write each base as a perfect power.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
81^{3/4}&=(3^4)^{3/4}=27 \\
32^{2/5}&=(2^5)^{2/5}=4 \\
27^{1/3}&=(3^3)^{1/3}=3
\end{aligned}" />

Substitute these three values, then evaluate the requested expression.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
s&=\frac{27+4}{3}=\frac{31}{3} \\
3s-1&=3\left(\frac{31}{3}\right)-1=30
\end{aligned}" />
