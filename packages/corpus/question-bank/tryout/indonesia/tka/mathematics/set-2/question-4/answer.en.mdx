The right side must be nonnegative, so <InlineMath math="x\ge-4" />. If <InlineMath math="2x-3\ge0" />, then <InlineMath math="2x-3=x+4" />, giving <InlineMath math="x=7" />. If <InlineMath math="2x-3<0" />, then <InlineMath math="3-2x=x+4" />, giving <InlineMath math="x=-1/3" />. Their product is <InlineMath math="-7/3" />.
