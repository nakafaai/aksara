<InlineMath math="\\sqrt{4^2}=|4|=4" /> is true because <InlineMath math="4>0" />. Squaring a negative number gives the same square, and two factors with exponent <InlineMath math="\\frac12" /> combine to exponent <InlineMath math="1" />. However, <InlineMath math="4^{-1}=\\frac1{4}" />, not <InlineMath math="-4" />.
