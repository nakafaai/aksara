Reflection in the x-axis maps <InlineMath math="(x,y)" /> to <InlineMath math="(x,-y)" />. Rotating the result <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> counterclockwise gives <InlineMath math="T(x,y)=(y,x)" />. Thus <InlineMath math="T(2,-3)=(-3,2)" />, <InlineMath math="T^2" /> is the identity, and the fixed points lie on <InlineMath math="y=x" />. Since T is a reflection, it reverses orientation.
