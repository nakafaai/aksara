Given the matrices

<BlockMath math="\begin{bmatrix} \tan x & 1 \\ 1 & \tan x \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \cos^2 x \\ \sin x \cos x \end{bmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix}" />

#### Multiply the matrices

This is matrix multiplication, so

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \tan x \cdot \cos^2 x + \sin x \cdot \cos x \\ \cos^2 x + \tan x \cdot \sin x \cos x \end{bmatrix}" />

Change <InlineMath math="\tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}" />, to

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \left(\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}\right) \cdot \cos^2 x + \sin x \cdot \cos x \\ \cos^2 x + \left(\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}\right) \cdot \sin x \cos x \end{bmatrix}" />

Simplify to

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \sin x \cdot \cos x + \sin x \cdot \cos x \\ \cos^2 x + \sin^2 x \end{bmatrix}" />
<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \sin 2x \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}" />
</MathContainer>

Thus <InlineMath math="a=2\sin 2x" /> and <InlineMath math="b=2" />. The tangent in the original matrix also requires <InlineMath math="\cos x\neq0" />.

#### Substitute into the given condition

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="2=2(2\sin 2x)" />
<BlockMath math="\sin 2x=\frac{1}{2}" />
</MathContainer>

The values of <InlineMath math="2x" /> that satisfy are

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="2x_1 = \frac{\pi}{6} + k \cdot 2\pi \quad \text{or} \quad 2x_2 = \frac{5\pi}{6} + k \cdot 2\pi" />
<BlockMath math="x_1 = \frac{\pi}{12} + k\pi \quad \text{or} \quad x_2 = \frac{5\pi}{12} + k\pi" />
</MathContainer>

The values of <InlineMath math="x" /> that satisfy are <InlineMath math="\frac{\pi}{12}" /> or <InlineMath math="\frac{5\pi}{12}" />.

Therefore, the correct statements are the second and fourth.
