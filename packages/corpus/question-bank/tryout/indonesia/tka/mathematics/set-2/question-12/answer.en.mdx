Set <InlineMath math="t=\frac1h" />. As <InlineMath math="h\to\infty" />, we have <InlineMath math="t\to0^+" /> and <InlineMath math="h=\frac1t" />. The given limit is therefore the definition of the derivative of <InlineMath math="f'" />.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\lim_{h \to \infty} h \left[f'\left(x + \frac{1}{h}\right) - f'(x)\right]" />
<BlockMath math="=\lim_{t\to0^+}\frac{f'(x+t)-f'(x)}{t}" />
<BlockMath math="=f''(x)" />
</MathContainer>

Differentiate <InlineMath math="f(x)=\sin^2x" /> twice.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="f(x) = \sin^2 x" />
<BlockMath math="f'(x)=2\sin x\cos x=\sin 2x" />
<BlockMath math="f''(x) = 2 \cos 2x" />
</MathContainer>
