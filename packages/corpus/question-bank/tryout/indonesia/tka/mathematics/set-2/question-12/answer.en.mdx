When the lateral surface is unrolled, half the circumference between the opposite points becomes a horizontal distance of <InlineMath math="3\pi" />. The vertical distance is <InlineMath math="8" />. Therefore, the square of the shortest path is <InlineMath math="8^2+(3\pi)^2=64+9\pi^2" />.
