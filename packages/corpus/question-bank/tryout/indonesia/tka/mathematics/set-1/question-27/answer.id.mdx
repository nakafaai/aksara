Gunakan dua identitas berikut:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="1-\cos(2x)=2\sin^2x" />
<BlockMath math="\tan(2x)=\frac{\sin(2x)}{\cos(2x)}=\frac{2\sin x\cos x}{\cos(2x)}" />
</MathContainer>

Substitusi kedua identitas tersebut menghasilkan

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\frac{1-\cos(2x)}{x\tan(2x)}=\frac{2\sin^2x}{x}\cdot\frac{\cos(2x)}{2\sin x\cos x}" />
<BlockMath math="=\frac{\sin x}{x}\cdot\frac{\cos(2x)}{\cos x}" />
</MathContainer>

Sekarang hitung limit kedua faktornya:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin x}{x}=1" />
<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\cos(2x)}{\cos x}=\frac{1}{1}=1" />
</MathContainer>

Jadi,

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to0}\frac{1-\cos(2x)}{x\tan(2x)}=1" />
