Sunlight makes the same angle for both shadows. The ratio of height to shadow length is therefore the same. Let <InlineMath math="H" /> be the combined height of the tree and platform.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{H}{8}&=\frac{1.8}{1.2} \\
H&=8(1.5)=12
\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the platform height to find the tree height.

<BlockMath math="h=12-2=10\,\mathrm{m}" />
