<InlineMath math="\\sqrt{3^2}=|3|=3" /> is true because <InlineMath math="3>0" />. Squaring a negative number gives the same square, and two factors with exponent <InlineMath math="\\frac12" /> combine to exponent <InlineMath math="1" />. However, <InlineMath math="3^{-1}=\\frac1{3}" />, not <InlineMath math="-3" />.
