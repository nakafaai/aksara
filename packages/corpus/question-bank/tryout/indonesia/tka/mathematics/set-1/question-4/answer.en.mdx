The denominators require <InlineMath math="x\ne1" /> and <InlineMath math="x\ne-1" />. Multiply both sides by <InlineMath math="2(x-1)(x+1)" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
&4(x+1)+6(x-1) \\
&\quad=5(x^2-1) \\
&10x-2=5x^2-5 \\
&5x^2-10x-3=0
\end{aligned}" />

Its discriminant is <InlineMath math="(-10)^2-4(5)(-3)=160>0" />, so there are two real roots. Substituting <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="-1" /> into the quadratic gives <InlineMath math="-8" /> and <InlineMath math="12" />, so neither is a root. Vieta’s formula gives the sum of the valid roots.
