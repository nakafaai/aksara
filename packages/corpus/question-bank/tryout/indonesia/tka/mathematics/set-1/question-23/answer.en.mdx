#### Find the horizontal projection

Let <InlineMath math="O" /> be the center of the square base. Its diagonal is

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="TR=\sqrt{3^2+3^2}=3\sqrt{2}" />
<BlockMath math="TO=\frac{TR}{2}=\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}" />
</MathContainer>

The segment <InlineMath math="TO" /> is the orthogonal projection of <InlineMath math="PT" /> onto the base. Therefore, the angle between <InlineMath math="PT" /> and the base is <InlineMath math="\angle PTO" />.

#### Find the pyramid height

Triangle <InlineMath math="PTO" /> is right-angled at <InlineMath math="O" />. Using <InlineMath math="PT=3\sqrt{2}" />,

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="PO=\sqrt{PT^2-TO^2}" />
<BlockMath math="PO=\sqrt{(3\sqrt{2})^2-\left(\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^2}" />
<BlockMath math="PO=\sqrt{18-\frac{9}{2}}=\frac{3\sqrt{6}}{2}" />
</MathContainer>

#### Calculate the tangent

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\tan\alpha=\frac{PO}{TO}" />
<BlockMath math="\tan\alpha=\frac{\frac{3\sqrt{6}}{2}}{\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}}=\sqrt{3}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the answer is <InlineMath math="\sqrt{3}" />.
