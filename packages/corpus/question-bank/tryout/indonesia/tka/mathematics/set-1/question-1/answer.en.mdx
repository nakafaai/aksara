Extract the perfect-square factors and rewrite the negative power as a reciprocal:

<BlockMath math="\frac{\sqrt{50}-\sqrt{8}}{\sqrt{18}}+2^{-2}=\frac{5\sqrt2-2\sqrt2}{3\sqrt2}+\frac14=\frac54" />

The question then asks for the square of this value:

<BlockMath math="s=\frac54\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s^2=\frac{25}{16}" />
