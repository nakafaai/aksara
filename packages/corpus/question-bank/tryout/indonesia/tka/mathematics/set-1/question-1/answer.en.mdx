Extract the perfect-square factors from each radical. The negative exponent gives <InlineMath math="2^{-2}=\frac14" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
s&=\frac{5\sqrt2-2\sqrt2}{3\sqrt2}+\frac14 \\
&=1+\frac14=\frac54
\end{aligned}" />

Square this value to obtain the requested result.

<BlockMath math="s^2=\left(\frac54\right)^2=\frac{25}{16}" />
