#### Assign coordinates

Place the cube in a coordinate system with

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="B=(12,0,0),\quad C=(12,12,0)" />
<BlockMath math="G=(12,12,12),\quad H=(0,12,12)" />
</MathContainer>

Since <InlineMath math="P" /> is the midpoint of <InlineMath math="CG" />, and the cube center <InlineMath math="O" /> is the midpoint of the space diagonal <InlineMath math="HB" />,

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="P=(12,12,6)" />
<BlockMath math="O=(6,6,6)" />
</MathContainer>

#### Identify the perpendicular segment

A direction vector of <InlineMath math="HB" /> and the vector from <InlineMath math="O" /> to <InlineMath math="P" /> are

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\overrightarrow{HB}=B-H=(12,-12,-12)" />
<BlockMath math="\overrightarrow{OP}=P-O=(6,6,0)" />
</MathContainer>

Their dot product is zero:

<BlockMath math="\overrightarrow{HB}\cdot\overrightarrow{OP}=12(6)-12(6)-12(0)=0" />

Thus, <InlineMath math="OP" /> is perpendicular to <InlineMath math="HB" />, so the length of <InlineMath math="OP" /> is the required distance.

<BlockMath math="OP=\sqrt{6^2+6^2+0^2}=6\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}" />

Therefore, the answer is <InlineMath math="6\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}" />.
