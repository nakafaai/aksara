The order of matrix multiplication matters. Starting from <InlineMath math="AC=B" />, multiply both sides on the left by <InlineMath math="A^{-1}" />:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="A^{-1}AC=A^{-1}B" />
<BlockMath math="C=A^{-1}B" />
</MathContainer>

#### Find the inverse of <InlineMath math="A" />

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\det(A)=1(3)-2(1)=1" />
<BlockMath math="A^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}3&-2\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}" />
</MathContainer>

Now calculate <InlineMath math="C" /> in the correct order:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="C=\begin{pmatrix}3&-2\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\1&3\end{pmatrix}" />
<BlockMath math="C=\begin{pmatrix}10&-3\\-3&2\end{pmatrix}" />
</MathContainer>

Its determinant is

<BlockMath math="\det(C)=10(2)-(-3)(-3)=20-9=11" />

Therefore, the answer is <InlineMath math="11" />.
