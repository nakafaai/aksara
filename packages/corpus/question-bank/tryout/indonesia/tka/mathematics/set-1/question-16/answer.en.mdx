The distances form an arithmetic sequence:

<BlockMath math="10,18,26,34,\ldots" />

Its first term is <InlineMath math="a=10" />, its common difference is <InlineMath math="d=8" />, and it has <InlineMath math="n=10" /> terms.

#### Find the final distance

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="U_n=a+(n-1)d" />
<BlockMath math="U_{10}=10+(10-1)(8)=82" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, <InlineMath math="B_{10}" /> alone is <InlineMath math="82\text{ m}" /> from the box. The question asks for the sum of all ten distances. The final distance alone does not answer it.

#### Add all ten distances

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="S_n=\frac{n}{2}(a+U_n)" />
<BlockMath math="S_{10}=\frac{10}{2}(10+82)" />
<BlockMath math="S_{10}=5(92)=460" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the sum is <InlineMath math="460\text{ m}" />.
