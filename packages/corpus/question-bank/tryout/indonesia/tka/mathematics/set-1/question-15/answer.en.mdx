Dilating by factor <InlineMath math="2" /> and then rotating <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> counterclockwise gives <InlineMath math="T(x,y)=(-2y,2x)" />. Thus <InlineMath math="T(1,-2)=(4,2)" />. Lengths double, areas quadruple, and orientation is preserved. Applying the transformation twice gives <InlineMath math="T^2(x,y)=(-4x,-4y)" />.
