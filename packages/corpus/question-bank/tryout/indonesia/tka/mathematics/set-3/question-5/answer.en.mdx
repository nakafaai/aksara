Let <InlineMath math="r" /> and <InlineMath math="e" /> be the standard and express prices in thousands of rupiah. The two bundles give this system.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
4r+e&=245 \\
2r+3e&=185
\end{aligned}" />

Multiply the first equation by <InlineMath math="3" /> and subtract the second to eliminate <InlineMath math="e" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
12r+3e&=735 \\
10r&=735-185=550 \\
r&=55 \\
e&=245-4(55)=25
\end{aligned}" />

The ratio of standard to express price is:

<BlockMath math="r:e=55:25=11:5" />
