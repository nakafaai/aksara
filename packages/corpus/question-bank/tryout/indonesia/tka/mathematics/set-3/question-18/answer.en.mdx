The area enclosed by the inner edge of the path must equal the combined garden and pool area:

<BlockMath math="(40-2x)(30-2x)=744+12\cdot6=816" />

This equation gives <InlineMath math="x=3" /> or <InlineMath math="x=32" />. A <InlineMath math="32" /> m path cannot fit inside a courtyard only <InlineMath math="30" /> m wide, so <InlineMath math="x=3" /> m. The inner edge of the path measures

<BlockMath math="(40-2\cdot3)\times(30-2\cdot3)=34\times24" />

The fence length comes from three boundaries:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L
&=2(40+30)+\bigl(2(34+24)-4\bigr)+2(12+6)\\
&=140+112+36\\
&=288.
\end{aligned}" />

The <InlineMath math="4" /> metre opening applies only to the inner edge of the path. The total fence length is <InlineMath math="288" /> m.
