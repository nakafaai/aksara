The path reduces both ends of each dimension. The inner rectangle’s area equals the garden area plus the pool area.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="744+12\cdot6=816" />

  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(40-2x)(30-2x)&=816 \\
1{,}200-140x+4x^2&=816 \\
4x^2-140x+384&=0 \\
x^2-35x+96&=0 \\
(x-3)(x-32)&=0
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

The candidate widths are <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="32" />. The width must satisfy <InlineMath math="0<x<15" /> so the shorter inner side remains positive. Thus <InlineMath math="x=3\,\mathrm{m}" />.

The inner rectangle measures <InlineMath math="34\,\mathrm{m}" /> by <InlineMath math="24\,\mathrm{m}" />. Calculate the three perimeters in metres.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L_o&=2(40+30)=140 \\
L_i&=2(34+24)=116 \\
L_p&=2(12+6)=36
\end{aligned}" />

Only the fence on the inner path edge has an opening.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L&=L_o+(L_i-4)+L_p \\
&=140+112+36=288
\end{aligned}" />

The total fence length is <InlineMath math="288\,\mathrm{m}" />.
