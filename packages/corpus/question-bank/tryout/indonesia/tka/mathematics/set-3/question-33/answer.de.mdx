Eliminieren Sie beide Gleichungen

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2a + b &= 4 \quad \ldots (2) \\
a + b &= 3 \quad \ldots (1) \\
\hline
(2) - (1): \quad (2a + b) - (a + b) &= 4 - 3 \\
a &= 1
\end{aligned}" />

Der Wert <InlineMath math="a + b = 3" />, dann <InlineMath math="b = 3 - a = 3 - 1 = 2" />.
