Polynomial long division, also known as *porogapit*, makes the remainder visible. Divide the leading terms, multiply the divisor by the resulting quotient term, subtract, and repeat:

<BlockMath math="\begin{array}{c|l}
& 5x^2 + 23 \\
\hline
x^2 - 5 & 5x^4 + 0x^3 - 2x^2 + 7x + 9 \\
& \underline{5x^4 + 0x^3 - 25x^2 \phantom{+ 7x + 9}} \; - \\
& \phantom{5x^4 + 0x^3} \; 23x^2 + 7x + 9 \\
& \phantom{5x^4 + 0x^3} \; \underline{23x^2 + 0x - 115} \; - \\
& \phantom{5x^4 + 0x^3 - 2x^2} \; 7x + 124
\end{array}" />

The polynomial left below the final subtraction is the remainder, so <InlineMath math="h(x) = 7x + 124" />. Substitute <InlineMath math="x = 1" /> to obtain the requested value:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="h(x) = 7x + 124" />
<BlockMath math="h(1) = 7(1) + 124 = 131" />
</MathContainer>
