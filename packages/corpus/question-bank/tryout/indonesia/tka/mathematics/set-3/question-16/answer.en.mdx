The two semicircles on each boundary form one complete circle. The outer and inner stadium areas are

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="A_{\text{outer}}=20(12)+\pi(6)^2=240+36\pi" />
  <BlockMath math="A_{\text{inner}}=20(8)+\pi(4)^2=160+16\pi" />
</MathContainer>

The inward offset reduces the radius from <InlineMath math="6" /> to <InlineMath math="4" /> metres, while the two straight sections remain <InlineMath math="20" /> metres long. The track area is

<BlockMath math="A_{\text{outer}}-A_{\text{inner}}=80+20\pi" />

Thus, the track has area <InlineMath math="80+20\pi" /> m².
