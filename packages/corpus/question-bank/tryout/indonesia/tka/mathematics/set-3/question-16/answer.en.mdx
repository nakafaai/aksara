The two outer semicircles have the combined area of a circle of radius <InlineMath math="6\,\mathrm{m}" />. Let <InlineMath math="A_o" /> be the full area inside the outer boundary.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A_o&=20(12)+\pi(6)^2 \\
&=240+36\pi
\end{aligned}" />

The inner boundary is offset inward by <InlineMath math="2\,\mathrm{m}" />. Its radius becomes <InlineMath math="4\,\mathrm{m}" /> and its width <InlineMath math="8\,\mathrm{m}" />. The semicircle centers stay fixed, so the straight sections remain <InlineMath math="20\,\mathrm{m}" /> long.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A_i&=20(8)+\pi(4)^2 \\
&=160+16\pi
\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the inner area from the full outer area.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&=A_o-A_i \\
&=240+36\pi-160-16\pi \\
&=(240-160)+(36-16)\pi \\
&=80+20\pi
\end{aligned}" />

The track area is <InlineMath math="(80+20\pi)\,\mathrm{m}^2" />.
