The foot’s distance from the wall changes from <InlineMath math="7\,\mathrm{m}" /> to <InlineMath math="15\,\mathrm{m}" />. The ladder length is fixed, so Pythagoras gives these two heights.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
h_0&=\sqrt{25^2-7^2}=24 \\
h_1&=\sqrt{25^2-15^2}=20
\end{aligned}" />

The sine of the angle with the ground is the height divided by the ladder length.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\sin\alpha&=\frac{24}{25} \\
\sin\beta&=\frac{20}{25} \\
\sin\alpha-\sin\beta&=\frac4{25}
\end{aligned}" />

Divide the decrease by the initial sine value.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{\sin\alpha-\sin\beta}{\sin\alpha}&=\frac4{25}\div\frac{24}{25} \\
&=\frac4{24}=\frac16
\end{aligned}" />
