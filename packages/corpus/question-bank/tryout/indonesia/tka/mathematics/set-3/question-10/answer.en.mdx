Since <InlineMath math="P" /> lies on <InlineMath math="y=x+1" />, write <InlineMath math="P=(t,t+1)" />. Use the equidistance condition with squared distances:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
PA^2&=(t+2)^2+(t-3)^2,\\
PB^2&=(t-6)^2+(t+1)^2.
\end{aligned}" />

Equating the two expressions gives

<BlockMath math="2t^2-2t+13=2t^2-10t+37\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=3" />

Thus, <InlineMath math="P=(3,4)" /> and

<BlockMath math="OP^2=3^2+4^2=25" />
