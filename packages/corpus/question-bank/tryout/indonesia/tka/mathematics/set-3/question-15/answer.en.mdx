Reflecting across <InlineMath math="y=x" /> and then translating by <InlineMath math="(2,-1)" /> gives <InlineMath math="M(x,y)=(y+2,x-1)" />. Hence <InlineMath math="M(3,-2)=(0,2)" /> and <InlineMath math="M^2(x,y)=(x+1,y+1)" />. As a glide reflection, M preserves distance and reverses orientation. The fixed-point equations <InlineMath math="x=y+2" /> and <InlineMath math="y=x-1" /> are inconsistent, so M has no fixed point.
