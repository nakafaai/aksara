Apply the tangent-secant theorem from the external point <InlineMath math="P" />:

<BlockMath math="PA^2=PB\cdot PC" />

Let <InlineMath math="PB=x" />. Since <InlineMath math="BC=10" />, the whole secant has length <InlineMath math="PC=x+10" />. Therefore,

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
12^2&=x(x+10),\\
x^2+10x-144&=0,\\
(x-8)(x+18)&=0.
\end{aligned}" />

A length must be positive, so <InlineMath math="x=8" /> and <InlineMath math="PC=8+10=18" /> cm.
