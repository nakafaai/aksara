The denominators require <InlineMath math="x\ne-2" /> and <InlineMath math="x\ne1" />. Multiply by <InlineMath math="(x+2)(x-1)" /> to clear the denominators.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
&4(x-1)+(x+2) \\
&\quad=2(x+2)(x-1) \\
&5x-2=2x^2+2x-4 \\
&2x^2-3x-2=0 \\
&(2x+1)(x-2)=0
\end{aligned}" />

Both roots, <InlineMath math="-\frac12" /> and <InlineMath math="2" />, are in the domain and are nonzero. Vieta’s formulas give their sum <InlineMath math="\frac32" /> and product <InlineMath math="-1" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac1{x_1}+\frac1{x_2}&=\frac{x_1+x_2}{x_1x_2} \\
&=\frac{3/2}{-1}=-\frac32
\end{aligned}" />

Direct substitution also gives <InlineMath math="\frac12-2=-\frac32" />.
