<InlineMath math="\\sqrt{5^2}=|5|=5" /> is true because <InlineMath math="5>0" />. Squaring a negative number gives the same square, and two factors with exponent <InlineMath math="\\frac12" /> combine to exponent <InlineMath math="1" />. However, <InlineMath math="5^{-1}=\\frac1{5}" />, not <InlineMath math="-5" />.
