Rationalize both denominators using conjugates. The product of the two denominators is <InlineMath math="3-2=1" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
s&=\frac{\sqrt3+\sqrt2}{3-2}-\frac{\sqrt3-\sqrt2}{3-2} \\
&=(\sqrt3+\sqrt2)-(\sqrt3-\sqrt2) \\
&=2\sqrt2
\end{aligned}" />

Substitute this value of <InlineMath math="s" /> into the requested expression.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
s^2+s\sqrt2&=(2\sqrt2)^2 \\
&\quad+(2\sqrt2)(\sqrt2) \\
&=8+4=12
\end{aligned}" />
