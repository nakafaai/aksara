Start with the first equation and express <InlineMath math="y" /> in terms of <InlineMath math="x" />.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="-2x+y=a^2-1" />
<BlockMath math="y=2x+a^2-1" />
</MathContainer>

Substitute this expression into the second equation.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="3x+2(2x+a^2-1)=2a^2+7a+5" />
<BlockMath math="7x=7a+7" />
<BlockMath math="x=a+1" />
<BlockMath math="y=2(a+1)+a^2-1=(a+1)^2" />
</MathContainer>

Now substitute <InlineMath math="x=a+1" /> and <InlineMath math="y=(a+1)^2" /> into the required inequality. The principal square root satisfies <InlineMath math="\sqrt{u^2}=|u|" />, so

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="x\sqrt{y}+3>0" />
<BlockMath math="(a+1)\sqrt{(a+1)^2}+3>0" />
<BlockMath math="(a+1)|a+1|+3>0" />
</MathContainer>

#### Case <InlineMath math="a\geq-1" />

Here <InlineMath math="|a+1|=a+1" />. Therefore,

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="(a+1)^2+3>0" />
<BlockMath math="a\geq-1" />
</MathContainer>

The first inequality is always true, so every <InlineMath math="a\geq-1" /> satisfies the condition.

#### Case <InlineMath math="a<-1" />

Now <InlineMath math="|a+1|=-(a+1)" />. Therefore,

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="-(a+1)^2+3>0" />
<BlockMath math="(a+1)^2<3" />
<BlockMath math="-\sqrt3<a+1<0" />
<BlockMath math="-1-\sqrt3<a<-1" />
</MathContainer>

Combining both cases gives

<BlockMath math="(-1-\sqrt3,-1)\cup[-1,\infty)=(-1-\sqrt3,\infty)" />

Therefore, <InlineMath math="a>-1-\sqrt3" />.
