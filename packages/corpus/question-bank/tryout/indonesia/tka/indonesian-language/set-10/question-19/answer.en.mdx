# Given

At the end of the **lost child at the market** story, Mbak Sari falls silent after hearing Nisa's mother's account, places the largest slice of cake on Arif's plate, and says quietly, **"Maaf, ya. Kakak tidak tahu"** (I'm sorry, I didn't know).

# Asked

The event most likely to happen after the story ends.

# Solution

Once you realize you scolded someone unfairly simply because you **did not know the reason**, the clearest lesson you carry forward is to **ask first before getting upset**. That natural growth follows straight from Mbak Sari's apology.

> A character's honest admission of a mistake at the ending signals how their future behavior is most likely to improve.

## Drawing the lesson from the apology

Mbak Sari's closing words pinpoint her exact mistake: <Highlight>Maaf, ya. Kakak tidak tahu</Highlight> (sorry, I did not know). She realizes she **scolded Arif before ever asking why he was late**.

Therefore, the most plausible sequel is that **Mbak Sari will ask for the reason first if Arif ever comes home late again**.

## Why the other four continuations fail

- **Arif stops helping bake cakes** clashes with his loyalty, since he **stayed up late stirring batter** even right after being scolded.
- **Arif forbids Nisa from visiting** makes no sense after such a warm reunion and thank-you visit.
- **Mbak Sari never sends Arif to the market again** misreads the problem, which was **her misunderstanding**, not Arif's shopping.
- **Nisa's mother reports Arif to security** contradicts her grateful visit bearing **fried bananas**.

# Answer

The event most likely to follow is that **Mbak Sari asks for the reason first if Arif ever returns home late again**.
