# Given

In paragraph three of the **school mini-bank** passage, many students **only save early in the semester**, and deposits **drop by nearly half near the end of the semester**. Paragraph four opens with **"Untuk mengatasinya"** (to address this) and introduces the **"save one thousand a day"** program with certificates for saving at least <InlineMath math="20" /> times per semester.

# Asked

The relationship between falling deposits at semester's end and the "save one thousand a day" program.

# Solution

If someone exercises intensely in week one and quits for the rest of the month, a smart plan shifts to **small daily workouts** to build steadiness. Likewise, saving a small amount daily with a <InlineMath math="20" />-deposit goal encourages **consistent saving all semester long**.

> Opening a paragraph with *Untuk mengatasinya* (to solve this) directly connects the problem just reported to the program designed to fix it.

## Connecting the semester slump to the daily saving program

Paragraph three names the uneven habit: <Highlight>banyak siswa hanya menabung pada awal semester. Pada bulan-bulan akhir semester, jumlah setoran turun hampir setengahnya</Highlight> (many students save only at the start of the semester, and deposits drop almost by half near the end).

Paragraph four answers it directly: <Highlight>Untuk mengatasinya, pengurus bank mini membuat program "menabung seribu sehari". Siswa yang menabung paling sedikit <InlineMath math="20" /> kali dalam satu semester mendapat sertifikat</Highlight>. Small regular deposits and a frequency milestone aim to **keep students saving steadily throughout the semester**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Raising the minimum deposit to <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" />** confuses <InlineMath math="20" /> **visits** with money, while the program actually promotes a small daily sum.
- **Because savers dropped in year two** is false because savers **rose from <InlineMath math="210" /> to <InlineMath math="360" />**.
- **Replacing passbooks** invents a change never mentioned in the text.
- **Unrelated to falling deposits** ignores the explicit connector *Untuk mengatasinya*.

# Answer

The relationship is that **the program was created to encourage students to save regularly throughout the semester, not only at the start**.
