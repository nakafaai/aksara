# Given

At the end of paragraph three in the **loud exhaust** passage, officers point out that **Saturday night, when the street is much busier, has not been measured yet**. In the final sentence, **residents ask officers to repeat the measurement on Saturday night**.

# Asked

The relationship between the officers' note about Saturday night and the residents' closing request.

# Solution

If you check traffic only on quiet afternoons and someone notes **rush hour is much busier and still unchecked**, the logical next step is to **measure again during rush hour**. Here the officers' caveat directly prompts the residents' request.

> A note pointing out an unmeasured peak period directly causes a follow-up request to collect data during that exact window.

## Linking the officers caveat to the residents request

Paragraph three highlights a clear data gap: <Highlight>Petugas mengingatkan bahwa malam Minggu, ketika jalan itu jauh lebih ramai, belum diukur</Highlight> (officers noted that Saturday night, when the road is far busier, had not been measured yet).

Paragraph four answers that gap directly: <Highlight>Warga juga meminta petugas mengulang pengukuran pada malam Minggu</Highlight> (residents also asked officers to repeat measurements on Saturday night). Thus **because the far busier night was unmeasured, residents want testing repeated on Saturday night**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Because the noise meter broke** invents an equipment fault absent from the passage.
- **Canceled the reminder banner** contradicts the residents' agreement to **install the banner**.
- **Unrelated to the officers' note** misses the exact match on **Saturday night testing**.
- **Proves no motorcycles were loud** contradicts the **<InlineMath math="18" /> motorcycles** exceeding <InlineMath math="90\text{ dB}" />.

# Answer

The relationship is that **because the much busier night had not yet been measured, residents want the measurement repeated on Saturday night**.
