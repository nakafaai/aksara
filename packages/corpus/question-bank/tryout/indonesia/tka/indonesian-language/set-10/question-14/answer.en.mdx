# Given

Paragraph three of the **school mini-bank** passage reports that savers grew from **<InlineMath math="210" /> to <InlineMath math="360" /> students** in year two. After the word **Namun** (however), it adds that many students **only save early in the semester**, causing deposits to **drop almost by half** near the end.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph three.

# Solution

When a paragraph opens with good news and pivots on **However** to a drawback, its main idea must **hold both halves together**. Participation grew, yet regular saving across the whole semester is still missing.

> A mid-paragraph pivot such as *Namun* (however) signals that the main idea combines the positive trend with its accompanying limitation.

## Combining both halves of paragraph three

The opening reports **growth** from <InlineMath math="210" /> to <InlineMath math="360" /> savers. After **Namun**, the text pinpoints the **gap**: <Highlight>banyak siswa hanya menabung pada awal semester. Pada bulan-bulan akhir semester, jumlah setoran turun hampir setengahnya</Highlight> (many save only early on, and deposits drop almost half late in the semester).

Uniting both halves gives the complete main idea: **the number of savers rose, yet saving habits remain irregular across the semester**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Every student has opened an account** is false since only **<InlineMath math="360" /> of <InlineMath math="600" />** signed up.
- **Allowances rise at semester's end** is false since allowance money gets **spent on school events and field trips**.
- **Field trips were canceled** adds a detail absent from the passage.
- **Savers declined in year two** flips the actual **increase**.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph three is that **the number of savers increased, but saving habits are not yet regular throughout the semester**.
