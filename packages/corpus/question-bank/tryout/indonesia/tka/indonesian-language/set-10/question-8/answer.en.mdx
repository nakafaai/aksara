# Given

After Grandmother passed away, her batik-covered recipe book stayed untouched in a kitchen drawer. On Sinta's fourth attempt, the *nastar* comes out **golden brown with a buttery aroma filling the house**. Her mother eats one, **sits quiet for a long moment, and smiles with wet eyes**.

# Asked

The most fitting emotional response to Sinta's mother smiling with wet eyes after tasting the tart.

# Solution

A familiar homemade scent and flavor from a loved one who passed away can instantly **bring warm memories rushing back**. Seeing Mother pause and **smile through tears** clearly conveys **tender emotion**, making **feeling moved** the natural response.

> A quiet pause followed by a tearful smile over a family heirloom dish signals deep fondness and poignant remembrance.

## Reading Mother pause and tearful smile

Paragraph one establishes that no one dared open the recipe book after Grandmother's death. Paragraph four closes the emotional circle: <Highlight>Ibu memakan satu, terdiam lama, lalu tersenyum dengan mata basah</Highlight> (Mother ate one, fell silent for a long while, then smiled with wet eyes).

The taste and buttery scent **rekindled cherished memories of Grandmother**, so the fitting emotional response is **feeling deeply moved**.

## Why the other four responses fail

- **Annoyed because the tarts are too hard** refers to the failed second and third batches, whereas batch four **succeeded**.
- **Amused because Mother burned her mouth** invents a detail never mentioned in the story.
- **Worried because Mother is ill** misreads **tears of emotion** as physical sickness.
- **Disappointed that it tastes nothing like Grandmother's** contradicts **Mother's warm smile**.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is **feeling moved because the taste of the *nastar* brings back memories of Grandmother**.
