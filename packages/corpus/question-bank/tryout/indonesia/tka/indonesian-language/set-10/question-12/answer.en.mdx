# Given

# Asked

The correct category for each of the four statements according to its role in the text.

# Solution

The passage follows three tidy sections: **paragraph one** explains how the mini-bank operates, **paragraph three** reports both rising membership and the late-semester slump, and **paragraph four** presents improvement measures. Mapping each statement to its home paragraph solves all four cleanly.

> Noticing that paragraph three combines growth statistics and the semester-end dip places both statements accurately in the same category.
