# Given

In the **grandmother's recipe book** story, Sinta tries baking pineapple tarts (*nastar*) from her late grandmother's handwritten notes. From four choices, we must pick **all details showing Sinta's difficulty understanding the recipe**.

# Asked

Every detail that genuinely reflects why Sinta struggled to follow her grandmother's recipe.

# Solution

Trying to bake from notes that omit **exact ingredient weights** and **oven temperature** leaves you guessing by trial and error. Checking paragraph two quickly separates the two genuine recipe obstacles from unrelated background details.

> Evidence of a character's obstacle comes directly from what blocked the task, not from decorative descriptions or the happy ending.

## Checking the recipe obstacles in paragraph two

Paragraph two lists the exact difficulties: <Highlight>Masalahnya, takarannya aneh: "tepung secukupnya", "mentega sebesar telur bebek", dan "panggang sampai harum". Tidak ada ukuran gram, tidak ada suhu oven</Highlight> (vague measures like "enough flour" and no gram amounts or oven temperature).

Both **vague flour amounts** and **missing oven temperature** directly caused Sinta's struggle, whereas the batik cloth cover is merely how the book looks, and her mother's smile comes only after Sinta succeeds.

## Verdict on all four options

- **The recipe book has a batik cover**: **Incorrect**, only describing the book's appearance.
- **Flour is listed as "enough"**: **Correct**, giving no exact weight and causing three failed batches.
- **Her mother eats a tart and smiles**: **Incorrect**, happening after the fourth batch succeeds.
- **No oven temperature is listed**: **Correct**, leaving Sinta without a clear baking setting.

# Answer

The details showing Sinta's difficulty understanding the recipe are:
- **The flour measurement is written as "enough" (*secukupnya*)**.
- **The recipe does not list an oven temperature**.
