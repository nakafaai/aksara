# Given

The evening before, Mbak Sari scolded Arif for getting home at dusk. The next morning, **Nisa's mother visits and tells her what Arif did at the market**. After the guest leaves, **Mbak Sari places the biggest slice of cake on Arif's plate and says softly, "Maaf, ya. Kakak tidak tahu"** (I'm sorry, I didn't know).

# Asked

The strongest evidence that Mbak Sari regrets scolding Arif.

# Solution

Regret never shows up while someone is still angry, but in their **apology and gentle gesture once they learn the truth**. Serving **the biggest slice of cake** while saying **sorry** proves her remorse beyond doubt.

> Strong evidence of regret appears after the truth emerges, expressed through an apology or a caring act to make amends.

## Pinpointing the proof of regret in the final paragraph

The ending spells out her remorse clearly: <Highlight>ia menaruh potongan kue yang paling besar di piring Arif dan berkata pelan, "Maaf, ya. Kakak tidak tahu."</Highlight> (she placed the largest piece of cake on Arif's plate and softly apologized for not knowing).

Both **the generous slice** and **the direct apology** decisively prove Mbak Sari regrets scolding him.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Telling Arif to come straight home** happened **before** anything went wrong.
- **Greeting him with a sour face** shows **frustration**, not regret.
- **Delivering the cake order the next morning** only explains why she felt rushed.
- **Nisa's mother bringing fried bananas** shows **Nisa's mother's gratitude**, not Mbak Sari's feelings.

# Answer

The strongest evidence is that **Mbak Sari put the largest slice of cake on Arif's plate and apologized.**
