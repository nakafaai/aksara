Sinta adds **clear measurements**: grams of flour, how the dough should feel, temperature, and time. That makes the recipe **easier to follow** for whoever comes next. But she writes it **small, in the margin**, *agar tidak menutupi tulisan Nenek* (so as not to cover Grandma's writing). So Sinta completes the recipe while still honoring her grandmother's handwriting.

- She doesn't replace the recipe. Grandma's writing stays visible.
- Nothing suggests she's embarrassed. The note records what she learned.
- She erases nothing. She only adds.
- Mother isn't angry. She smiles with wet eyes.
