# Given

The **loud exhaust** passage uses four loanwords: *desibel*, *pabrik*, *spanduk*, and *konsentrasi*. From four statements, we must choose **all accurate meanings matching the passage's context**.

# Asked

Every accurate contextual meaning among the four loanword statements.

# Solution

Test each proposed meaning by plugging it straight back into its sentence. When it matches **what the word actually does in the sentence**, keep it. When it swaps concepts completely, eliminate it.

> Substituting a proposed definition back into its original sentence immediately exposes mismatched meanings.

## Testing all four loanwords in context

Paragraph two states <Highlight>batas kebisingan kendaraan bermotor yang diukur dalam satuan desibel (dB)</Highlight> (vehicle noise limits measured in decibels), confirming ***desibel* means the unit for measuring sound loudness**.

Paragraph four notes <Highlight>memasang spanduk imbauan di ujung jalan</Highlight> (putting up a reminder banner at the end of the street), confirming ***spanduk* means a stretched cloth banner carrying a public message**.

By contrast, **pabrik** (*knalpot bawaan pabrik*, factory-stock exhaust) means a **large-scale manufacturing plant**, not a small repair shop, and **konsentrasi** disrupted by noise means **mental focus**, not engine sound.

## Verdict on all four statements

- ***Desibel* = unit for measuring sound loudness**: **Correct**, stated explicitly as the noise measurement unit.
- ***Pabrik* = small shop for swapping exhausts**: **Incorrect**, as a factory builds vehicles at scale.
- ***Spanduk* = stretched banner with a public message**: **Correct**, hung by residents at the end of the street.
- ***Konsentrasi* = very loud engine noise**: **Incorrect**, as concentration means focused attention.

# Answer

The accurate statements about the loanwords in context are:
- ***Desibel* means a unit for measuring the loudness of sound**.
- ***Spanduk* means a stretched cloth banner displaying a message in a public place**.
