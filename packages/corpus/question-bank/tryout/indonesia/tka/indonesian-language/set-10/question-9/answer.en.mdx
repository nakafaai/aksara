# Given

After succeeding on her fourth batch, Sinta **writes her proven measurements in the margin of the recipe book**: roughly <InlineMath math="450" /> grams of flour, dough neither sticky nor cracked, <InlineMath math="150" /> degrees for <InlineMath math="25" /> minutes.

# Asked

The event most likely to happen on Eid (*Lebaran*) the following year.

# Solution

Why write down the exact winning numbers right inside a family cookbook? So that **next year you can bake the same treat smoothly without three failed tries**. Sinta's pencil notes directly prepare for next Eid.

> Practical notes carefully saved by a protagonist at a story's close point directly toward repeating that success in the future.

## Reading how Sinta notes prepare for next year

The closing line shows Sinta preserving every exact detail: <Highlight>kira-kira <InlineMath math="450" /> gram tepung, adonan tidak lengket dan tidak retak, <InlineMath math="150" /> derajat selama <InlineMath math="25" /> menit</Highlight> (about <InlineMath math="450" /> grams of flour, dough neither sticky nor cracking, <InlineMath math="150" /> degrees for <InlineMath math="25" /> minutes).

With Grandmother's recipe now clear and warmly approved by her mother, the most likely outcome next Eid is that **Sinta bakes *nastar* again using the margin notes she added**.

## Why the other four events fail

- **Mother locks the book away again** ignores that the book brought comforting memories rather than grief alone.
- **Sinta buys store-bought *nastar*** makes no sense once she has **mastered and recorded the recipe**.
- **Mother's sibling throws the book out** clashes with their wish to keep Grandmother's Eid tarts alive.
- **Sinta erases her notes** contradicts her careful effort to preserve them neatly for future use.

# Answer

The event most likely to occur next Eid is that **Sinta makes *nastar* again with the help of the notes she added to the recipe book**.
