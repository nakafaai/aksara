# Given

In paragraph three of the **butterfly garden** passage, students notice caterpillars chewing holes in citrus leaves. Some parents suggest **spraying the caterpillars with *pestisida***, and **students refuse** because caterpillars are the exact butterfly stage they have been waiting for.

# Asked

The contextual meaning of the loanword *pestisida* in the sentence about spraying caterpillars.

# Solution

Watch how the students react: they **immediately reject the spray** to protect the caterpillars they want to grow into butterflies. A harmless or healing liquid would never draw that refusal, so *pestisida* clearly means **a chemical used to kill pests**.

> How characters react against an action provides a direct clue to the effect and meaning of an unfamiliar loanword.

## Reading the clue in the students refusal

The passage pairs the proposal directly with the refusal: <Highlight>Sebagian orang tua sempat mengusulkan agar ulat itu disemprot pestisida. Siswa menolak dengan menjelaskan bahwa ulat adalah tahap hidup kupu-kupu yang justru mereka tunggu</Highlight> (some parents suggested spraying the caterpillars with pesticide, and students refused because caterpillars are the life stage they awaited).

Parents viewed leaf-eating caterpillars as **pests to eliminate**, while students knew the spray would **kill them**. Thus *pestisida* means **a chemical substance for exterminating pests**.

## Why the other four meanings fail

- **Fertilizer to enrich plants** nourishes soil and roots rather than targeting caterpillars.
- **Water for daily watering** would not harm caterpillars or prompt student objections.
- **A spraying device** names the tool rather than the **liquid sprayed onto leaves**.
- **Medicine to cure sick caterpillars** would help the caterpillars survive rather than threatening them.

# Answer

In context, the loanword *pestisida* means **a chemical substance used to eliminate pests**.
