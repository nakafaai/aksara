# Given

The **loud exhaust on Jalan Cendana** passage unfolds across four paragraphs: **resident complaints about modified exhausts**, **health impacts of noise**, **official sound measurements**, and **collective community steps**.

# Asked

The main idea of the entire passage.

# Solution

A full-text main idea needs to **cover all four paragraphs under one roof**, not just one narrow claim. Here the passage traces **the disturbance caused by modified exhausts** and **how residents respond through testing and joint action**.

> The main idea of an informational passage combines the core issue introduced up front with the measurements and solutions developed afterward.

## Bringing all four paragraphs together

Paragraphs one and two present **modified exhaust noise** and its harm to sleep, energy, and focus. Paragraphs three and four cover the response: <Highlight>petugas mengukur suara sepeda motor yang melintas</Highlight> (officers measured passing motorcycles), followed by **residents agreeing on banners, exhaust changes, and weekend re-testing**.

Combining the problem and the organized community response captures the whole passage.

## Why the other four options fail

- **All motorcycles broke noise limits** is false because only **<InlineMath math="18" /> of <InlineMath math="80" />** exceeded <InlineMath math="90\text{ dB}" />.
- **Officers banned night motorcycles** invents a ban when officers only **measured sound levels**.
- **Noise only causes mild discomfort** contradicts paragraph two stating it is *not just discomfort*.
- **Police fined <InlineMath math="18" /> riders** adds police and tickets never mentioned in the text.

# Answer

The main idea of the passage is that **noise from modified exhausts disturbs residents, and residents respond with measurements and collective action**.
