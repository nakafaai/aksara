# Given

In paragraph three of the **loud exhaust** passage, environmental officers took readings on **two weekday nights**. Out of **<InlineMath math="80" /> passing motorcycles**, **<InlineMath math="18" />** produced noise above **<InlineMath math="90\text{ dB}" />**, most of the rest stayed below <InlineMath math="80\text{ dB}" />, and **Saturday night was not measured yet**.

# Asked

The most accurate sentence for reporting the officers' measurement results.

# Solution

A trustworthy data report states both the **exact numbers** and **when the readings were taken**. Naming two weekday nights, <InlineMath math="18" /> out of <InlineMath math="80" /> motorcycles, and the threshold above <InlineMath math="90\text{ dB}" /> reports the evidence faithfully with zero exaggeration.

> A precise report states exact counts, units, and sampling conditions without stretching conclusions to unmeasured periods.

## Checking the counts and timeframe in paragraph three

Paragraph three states the record verbatim: <Highlight>Pada dua malam hari kerja, petugas mengukur suara sepeda motor yang melintas dengan alat ukur kebisingan. Dari <InlineMath math="80" /> sepeda motor yang lewat, <InlineMath math="18" /> menghasilkan suara di atas <InlineMath math="90" /> dB</Highlight> (on two weekday nights, <InlineMath math="18" /> of <InlineMath math="80" /> passing motorcycles exceeded <InlineMath math="90\text{ dB}" />).

Reporting **On two weekday nights, <InlineMath math="18" /> of <InlineMath math="80" /> motorcycles measured by officers produced noise above <InlineMath math="90\text{ dB}" />** matches every number and scope limit cleanly.

## Why the other four sentences fail

- **Every motorcycle on Jalan Cendana is too loud** overstates <InlineMath math="18" /> out of <InlineMath math="80" />.
- **<InlineMath math="62" /> motorcycles exceeded <InlineMath math="90\text{ dB}" />** flips the count, since <InlineMath math="80 - 18 = 62" /> is the **remaining group**, mostly under <InlineMath math="80\text{ dB}" />.
- **Proves every night is equally loud** ignores that **busier Saturday nights remain unmeasured**.
- **Residents cannot sleep any night** exaggerates the text saying noise **can disturb sleep**.

# Answer

The most accurate reporting sentence is **On two weekday nights, <InlineMath math="18" /> of <InlineMath math="80" /> motorcycles measured by officers produced sound above <InlineMath math="90\text{ dB}" />**.
