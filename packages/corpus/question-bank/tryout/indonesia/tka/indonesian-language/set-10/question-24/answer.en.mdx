A report sentence has to **match the data** and **be honest about its limits**. The officials measured on **two weeknights**, and **18 of 80** motorbikes were above 90 dB. The sentence that gives that figure along with when it was measured is the most accurate.

- Only 18 of 80 measured very loud, not all of them.
- 62 is the rest, since <InlineMath math="80 - 18 = 62" />, and most of those were below 80 dB.
- Saturday nights weren't measured, so you can't conclude every night is equally loud.
- The text says noise can disturb sleep, not that residents can't sleep every night.
