# Given

In the **lost child at the market** story, Mbak Sari asks Arif to hurry home with sugar and eggs. At the market, Arif stays nearly an hour to **help a lost little girl named Nisa**, causing him to **arrive at dusk and get scolded** without arguing back until the truth comes out the next morning.

# Asked

The main idea of the story.

# Solution

Sum up the entire arc in one breath: Arif **gives up his time to keep a lost child safe**, quietly bears his sister's scolding for being late, and is appreciated once the truth comes to light. That complete arc is the main idea.

> A narrative's main idea connects the protagonist's key choice with the cost or consequence they endure along the way.

## Summarizing Arif sacrifice and its consequence

Arif stays with Nisa at the **security post for nearly an hour** until her mother arrives. Because of that delay, the story records: <Highlight>Mbak Sari menyambutnya dengan wajah masam. "Kamu ke mana saja? Kuenya jadi terlambat!"</Highlight> (Mbak Sari met him with a sour face and scolded him for delaying the cake).

The next morning Nisa's mother reveals his kindness, proving the main idea: **Arif gave up his time to help a lost child even though he ended up scolded by his sister**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Arif forgot to buy sugar and eggs** is false because he **handed over the groceries** upon arriving home.
- **Mbak Sari refused the cake order** contradicts her rushing to **bake that very order**.
- **Nisa's mother scolded Arif** clashes with her visiting with **fried bananas** to thank him.
- **Arif got lost at the market** swaps roles, since **Nisa** was the lost child.

# Answer

The main idea of the story is that **Arif gave up his time to help a lost child, even though it resulted in getting scolded by his sister**.
