# Given

At the market, Arif spots Nisa crying alone after losing her mother while passersby walk past. From four options, we must select **all true details about how Arif helped Nisa**.

# Asked

Every accurate statement describing what Arif actually did to help Nisa.

# Solution

Check each choice directly against **Arif's real actions in paragraph three** and cross out anything invented. Watch out for the word *sandal*, which appears in Nisa's description even though Arif never buys her new ones.

> A familiar noun from the passage can still be a trap if the story never shows the character performing that action.

## Checking every action Arif took in paragraph three

Paragraph three lists three clear acts: <Highlight>Ia menggandeng Nisa ke sana</Highlight> (to the security post near the entrance) and <Highlight>Ia duduk menemaninya dan membagi roti yang tadinya untuk bekalnya sendiri</Highlight> (staying beside her and sharing his own bread). He also waited until Nisa's mother hugged her **right in front of Arif**.

Although paragraph two mentions **Nisa had only one sandal left**, the story never says Arif bought her new sandals.

## Verdict on all four options

- **Arif bought Nisa new sandals**: **Incorrect**, never stated in the text.
- **Arif led Nisa to the security post near the entrance**: **Correct**, stated directly in paragraph three.
- **Arif shared the bread meant for his own snack**: **Correct**, stated explicitly while they waited.
- **Arif stayed with Nisa until her mother arrived**: **Correct**, staying nearly an hour until the reunion right in front of him.

# Answer

All true details about how Arif helped Nisa are:
- **Arif held Nisa's hand and brought her to the security post near the market entrance.**
- **Arif shared the bread that had been meant as his own snack.**
- **Arif stayed with Nisa until her mother arrived.**
