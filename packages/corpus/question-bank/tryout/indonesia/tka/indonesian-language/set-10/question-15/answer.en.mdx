*Saving a thousand a day* responds to students who **save a lot early in the semester and then stop**. The idea is **small but regular**. The best everyday application is setting aside a little money regularly, like every day from pocket money.

- Saving only when you have a lot is close to the habit the program wants to change.
- Spending all your pocket money goes against saving.
- Borrowing money to save isn't good money management.
- The certificate is an incentive, not the goal, and stopping afterward repeats the old problem.
