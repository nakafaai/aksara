# Given

The previous evening, Arif was scolded without defending himself even though he was late from helping lost Nisa. The next morning Nisa's mother tells the full story, and Mbak Sari places **the biggest slice of cake on Arif's plate** while saying gently, **"Maaf, ya. Kakak tidak tahu"** (I'm sorry, I didn't know).

# Asked

The most fitting emotional response when Mbak Sari softly says, "I'm sorry, I didn't know."

# Solution

Imagine being unfairly blamed for doing something kind, and the very next morning the truth comes out and the person apologizes sincerely. That heavy knot of misunderstanding dissolves into **relief and being deeply moved**.

> When a silent good deed is finally understood and warmly acknowledged, the natural emotional response is relief paired with being moved.

## Reading the warmth after the misunderstanding clears

The night before, Arif **handed over the groceries without explaining a word**. Now the full story reaches his sister: <Highlight>ia menaruh potongan kue yang paling besar di piring Arif dan berkata pelan, "Maaf, ya. Kakak tidak tahu."</Highlight> (she put the largest cake slice on Arif's plate and softly apologized).

Because **Arif's quiet kindness is finally known and valued by his sister**, the most fitting response is **relieved and moved**.

## Why the other four responses fail

- **Annoyed that Mbak Sari keeps scolding Arif** clashes with her **apologizing gently**.
- **Afraid Nisa's mother will scold Arif** contradicts her **grateful visit**.
- **Confused because Arif never went to the market** ignores that he clearly shopped and brought the groceries home.
- **Sad because the cake failed** contradicts the fact that Arif helped stir the batter and **received the largest slice**.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is **feeling relieved and moved because Arif's kindness is finally known and appreciated by his sister**.
