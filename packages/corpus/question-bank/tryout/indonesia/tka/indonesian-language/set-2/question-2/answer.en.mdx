# Given

The SMA Tunas Repair Clinic trains students to **diagnose faults**, verify safety limits at table one, test likely causes at table two, and weigh repair decisions at table three.

# Asked

What role the loanword *mendiagnosis* (diagnosing) serves in the clinic's workflow.

# Solution

Think of visiting **a doctor when you have a fever**. The doctor does not hand out random medicine first. She asks your symptoms and checks your temperature to uncover the cause. Repair diagnosis follows that exact order.

> Diagnosing means tracking down the root cause through symptoms and structured checks before picking any repair action.

## Tracing diagnosis across the clinic tables

Paragraph one frames the class around <Highlight>mendiagnosis kerusakan, menentukan batas aman, dan memutuskan kelayakan perbaikan</Highlight> (diagnosing damage, setting safety limits, and judging repairability). Owners describe symptoms at table one, and students <Highlight>membuat dugaan penyebab lalu memilih satu pengujian yang paling aman</Highlight> (hypothesize causes and run the safest test) at table two before deciding at table three.

The term therefore **marks the stage of finding causes from symptoms and testing before choosing a repair action**.

## Why the other options fail

- **Equating initial inspection with replacing every part** skips targeted tests and cost review.
- **Guaranteeing repair success beforehand** clashes with the <InlineMath math="9" /> items that could not be repaired.
- **Selling unpriced items** never occurs, and **skipping symptom notes** breaks table one's core rule.

# Answer

**The word *mendiagnosis* marks the stage of identifying causes from symptoms and testing before selecting a repair action**.
