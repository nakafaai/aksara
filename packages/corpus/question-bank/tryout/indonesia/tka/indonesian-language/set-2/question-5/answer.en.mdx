The figure 28 is a **count of reused objects**, not a percentage. The total number reported is:

<BlockMath math="28+11+9=48" />

The proportion returned to use in this activity is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{28}{48}\times100\%\approx58.3\%" />

Thus, 28 does not mean 28 percent. Even 58.3 percent describes only the objects brought to this clinic. Owners chose those objects, whose faults may not represent all household faults. **Selection of participants and objects** limits generalization without making the activity's results useless.
