# Given

In the **Rain Garden** text, the table shows puddle duration dropping from <InlineMath math="46" /> and <InlineMath math="51" /> minutes to <InlineMath math="24" /> and <InlineMath math="28" /> minutes, and the very next paragraph lists three confounding factors that were not separated.

# Asked

How the table and the paragraph right after it are related.

# Solution

Imagine a scoreboard showing **your team scored twice as many goals**, followed by a note explaining the wind favored your side and the opponent rested starters. The table displays a clear pattern, and the paragraph explains the limits on interpreting it.

> The link between a data table and its follow-up paragraph comes from their roles, showing an observed pattern versus clarifying limits on what that pattern proves.

## Contrasting the table with the next paragraph

The table displays shorter puddle durations after construction (storms C and D versus A and B).

The next paragraph qualifies that pattern by noting <Highlight>Intensitas hujan tidak diukur, selokan dibersihkan pada minggu yang sama, dan tanah sebelum hujan mungkin memiliki kadar air berbeda</Highlight> (rainfall intensity was unmeasured, the drain was cleaned, and prior soil moisture may have differed).

Thus **the table shows a pattern, and the paragraph explains the limitations of interpreting it**.

## Why the other options fail

- **Isolating the garden's effect** is the opposite of what the paragraph says.
- **Dismissing the table as not useful** clashes with the school keeping the garden on practical grounds.
- **Summarizing plant species** misstates the table's contents, and **claiming longer puddles** reverses the numbers.

# Answer

**The table shows a pattern, while the following paragraph explains the limitations in interpreting it**.
