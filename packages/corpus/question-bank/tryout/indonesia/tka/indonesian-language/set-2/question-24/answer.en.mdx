The next design addresses two weaknesses already identified. Splitting participants between two condition orders prevents practice from always favoring the break session. Testing the question sets on another group also helps compare their difficulty. Select:

- Splits participants into **two condition orders**.
- Checks **question-set comparability** on another group.

The group will still record performance in the final section, and participants will no longer all begin without a break.
