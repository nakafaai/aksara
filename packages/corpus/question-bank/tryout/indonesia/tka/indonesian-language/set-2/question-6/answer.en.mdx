# Given

At the end of **Last Broadcast**, the principal hands over the archive cabinet key so tapes can move to the history teacher's dry room for gradual digitization, and Dara has already taped a provisional contents list onto the cabinet door.

# Asked

What Dara is most likely to do after the story ends.

# Solution

Imagine organizing **boxes of old school photos** and taping a starter index to the lid right before scanning begins. The most natural next step is continuing to catalog each box so nothing gets skipped or lost during scanning.

> The most plausible continuation extends the concrete responsibility the protagonist has already begun in the final scene.

## Reading the clues in the closing paragraphs

Paragraph four notes the decision that <Highlight>arsip suara akan dipindahkan dan didigitalkan secara bertahap</Highlight> (the audio archive will be moved and digitized gradually).

Paragraph five shows Dara <Highlight>menempelkan daftar isi sementara di pintu lemari</Highlight> (taping a provisional contents list to the cabinet door) so others know which tapes moved.

The natural next step is **helping catalog the recordings before digitization**.

## Why the other predictions clash with the story

- **Replacing date labels** destroys the dates Dara carefully announced after every clip.
- **Keeping tapes in the broadcast room** or **taking over the language lab** breaks the accepted plan to build the lab.
- **Forcing fragile cassettes to play** ignores Bimo's warning that Dara already respected.

# Answer

After the story ends, Dara is most likely to help catalog the recordings before digitization.
