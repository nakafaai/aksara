# Given

In **paragraph two of the Repair Clinic**, every item begins at table one where the owner describes symptoms without opening the device, and a facilitator inspects cords, burnt smells, cracks, and severe hazards before deciding whether students may handle it or refer it to a technician.

# Asked

Which statement best summarizes paragraph two.

# Solution

Think of **an amusement park attendant** checking height limits and harness latches before anyone boards a ride. That gate check is not the ride itself, it decides whether proceeding is safe. Table one performs that exact screening.

> An accurate paragraph summary captures the main purpose linking every detail together, here screening for safety before any disassembly.

## Pulling together table one details

Paragraph two specifies that owners <Highlight>menjelaskan gejala tanpa membuka alat</Highlight> (explain symptoms without opening the device), facilitators <Highlight>memeriksa kabel, bau terbakar, retakan, dan tanda bahaya lain</Highlight> (inspect cables, burnt smells, cracks, and other hazards), and high-risk devices <Highlight>tidak dibongkar oleh siswa</Highlight> (are not opened by students) but referred to qualified technicians.

Together, these steps mean **initial inspection determines whether an item is safe to handle**.

## Why the other options fail

- **Opening every item immediately** violates the explicit ban on disassembling devices at table one or high-risk tools.
- **Assigning referrals without inspection** reverses the workflow, since referrals depend on spotting hazards first.
- **Testing swollen batteries** and **recording symptoms only after testing** directly contradict paragraph two.

# Answer

The best summary of paragraph two is that **initial inspection determines whether an item is safe to handle**.
