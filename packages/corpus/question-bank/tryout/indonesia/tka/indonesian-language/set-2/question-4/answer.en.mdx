# Given

The **SMA Tunas Repair Clinic** splits troubleshooting across **three sequential tables**, moving from safety screening at table one, to safe testing at table two, to cost and feasibility decisions at table three.

# Asked

How to match three clinic tasks to their proper table (Table <InlineMath math="1" />, Table <InlineMath math="2" />, or Table <InlineMath math="3" />).

# Solution

Picture **three stations at a school fair**: station one checks safety rules, station two runs a safe test, and station three decides whether to proceed. Each task belongs to one clear stop in order.

> In procedural matrix questions, match each activity directly to the explicit responsibility stated for that stage in the text.

## Matching each task to its table

The text assigns exact duties: table one logs symptoms and <Highlight>tanda bahaya lain</Highlight> (other hazard signs), table two has students <Highlight>memilih satu pengujian yang paling aman</Highlight> (choose the safest single test), and table three compares <Highlight>biaya perbaikan dan umur pakai yang mungkin diperoleh</Highlight> (repair cost and expected service life).

| Activity | Table | Direct passage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recording symptoms and hazard signs | Table <InlineMath math="1" /> | Owner describes symptoms while facilitator checks cords, smell, and cracks. |
| Selecting a safe test | Table <InlineMath math="2" /> | Students form a hypothesis and pick one safest test in sequence. |
| Comparing cost and likely service life | Table <InlineMath math="3" /> | Owner weighs repair cost against expected remaining lifespan. |

## Verifying the sequential logic

Safety always precedes testing, and testing always precedes cost decisions, confirming the exact order **Table <InlineMath math="1" />, Table <InlineMath math="2" />, Table <InlineMath math="3" />**.

# Answer

Recording symptoms and hazards belongs to Table **<InlineMath math="1" />**, choosing a safe test to Table **<InlineMath math="2" />**, and comparing cost and service life to Table **<InlineMath math="3" />**.
