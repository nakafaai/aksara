The stations separate **safety checks, testing, and decision-making**.

| Activity | Category | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recording symptoms and hazards | Meja 1 | Establishes safety before opening an object. |
| Choosing a safe test | Meja 2 | Investigates a possible cause. |
| Comparing cost and useful life | Meja 3 | Informs the owner’s decision. |
