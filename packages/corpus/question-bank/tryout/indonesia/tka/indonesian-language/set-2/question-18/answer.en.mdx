The final four taps are a **symbolic echo** of the first inspection. Initially, Kakek taps the bamboo strips, and one short, heavy sound identifies a crack. At the end, he taps the **table**, rather than carrying out another test of the frame.

The now-clear rhythm recalls both the repaired frame and Nara's understanding. The flight test supports the repair's success, while Nara's explanation to the judge supports the learning. The table taps symbolically bring both developments to a close.
