# Given

In paragraph two, Grandfather Jati taps four bamboo slats: **three ring clear and one sounds dull and heavy** because it is cracked. In the final sentence, right after hearing Nara's reply to the judge, Grandfather taps the table with **four beats that all ring clear**.

# Asked

The symbolic meaning of the four clear taps at the end of the story.

# Solution

Imagine a music teacher tapping **four glass chimes**: at the start one chimes dull because of a crack, and at the end all four ring pure and clear. That clean sound signals both a mended frame and a lesson fully grasped.

> A motif repeated at the start and close of a story reveals its meaning through what changed between the two moments.

## Comparing the opening taps and closing taps

In paragraph two, <Highlight>Tiga ketukan berbunyi nyaring, satu terdengar pendek dan berat</Highlight> (three taps rang clear, one sounded dull) exposed the cracked bamboo.

At the close, after Nara answers the judge, Grandfather <Highlight>mengetuk meja dengan irama empat ketukan yang kini semuanya terdengar nyaring</Highlight> (taps four beats that all ring clear).

Those four clear beats silently affirm that **the frame is whole again and Nara has understood Grandfather's lesson**.

## Why the other readings fail

- **Stopping the contest**, **buying a brand-new frame**, and **judges picking the prettiest decoration** clash with Grandfather's quiet pride.
- **Other kites having unrepaired frames** is never mentioned in the story.

# Answer

**The four clear taps mean that the frame has been restored and Nara has understood Grandfather's lesson**.
