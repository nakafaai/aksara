# Given

Across **The Kite Frame**, Nara's way of judging a kite evolves step by step, from wanting shiny paper in paragraph one to proudly leaving the repair seam visible before the judges on contest day.

# Asked

All statements that accurately show how Nara's judgment of kites changes.

# Solution

Think of someone who first picks **running shoes solely for glittery paint**, then learns to test sole grip, and finally takes pride in the stitched reinforcement that carries her through the race. Nara walks through all three stages.

> In multi-select questions on character change, verify every statement against the starting attitude, learning stage, and final outlook.

## Verifying the three supported statements

- **At first she prioritized shiny paper** is true. Paragraph one says she hoped Grandfather would replace all the paper <Highlight>dengan bahan baru yang berkilau</Highlight> (with shiny new material).
- **She learned to check frame strength** is true. She puts away the silver paint after checking that <Highlight>sambungan bambu tetap kokoh ketika benang ditarik dari dua arah</Highlight> (the bamboo splice stayed firm when pulled from two directions).
- **She came to view repair marks as proof of learning** is true. She tells the judge the seam <Highlight>menunjukkan tempat ia belajar mendengar rangka</Highlight> (marks where she learned to listen to the frame).

## Why the remaining option fails

- **Still thinking repair marks must be hidden** contradicts her decision to cancel painting over them with silver paint the night before the contest.

# Answer

**The three correct statements are that she first prioritized shiny paper, learned to check frame strength, and finally saw the repair line as proof of learning**.
