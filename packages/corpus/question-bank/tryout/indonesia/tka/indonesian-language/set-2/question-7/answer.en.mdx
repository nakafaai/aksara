# Given

In **paragraph three of Last Broadcast**, Dara skips a long farewell speech on Friday afternoon and instead plays clips from three eras, naming the date, speaker, and importance after each recording.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph three.

# Solution

Imagine sharing **three old school recordings** with friends and explaining after each clip who spoke, when it happened, and why it mattered. The heart of the moment is playing old clips together with the story behind them.

> A paragraph's main idea combines the protagonist's primary action with the essential method that gives it meaning.

## Combining the two key actions in paragraph three

First, Dara broadcasts <Highlight>potongan suara dari tiga masa</Highlight> (sound clips from three eras: cheering crowds, the caretaker's tree story, and flood guidance). Second, <Highlight>Setelah setiap potongan, Dara menyebut tanggal, pembicara, dan alasan rekaman itu penting</Highlight> (after each clip, Dara names the date, speaker, and why the recording matters).

Together, the paragraph centers on one clear point: **Dara presents old recordings along with their context**.

## Why the other options fail

- **Replacing alumni broadcasts** gets the timeline backward, as alumni only call in paragraph four.
- **Bimo moving moldy cassettes** contradicts their decision to avoid fragile, moldy tapes.
- **Canceling the language lab** is false, and **the caretaker planting a tree live** mistakes an old recording for a live event.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph three is that Dara presents old recordings along with their context.
