# Given

At the end of **Last Broadcast**, the "on air" light switches off at five as antenna broadcasts end, yet the archive gains dry storage, gradual digitization, backup copies, and new listeners among alumni and teachers.

# Asked

Which emotional response best fits the ending of the story.

# Solution

Imagine **your school's paper bulletin board retiring**, while every classic issue gets scanned onto a lively school website that alumni excitedly read. Instead of grief, you feel **optimistic** that the stories live on in a stronger form.

> A fitting emotional response aligns with the hopeful, forward-looking tone crafted in the final paragraph.

## Reading the hopeful note at the close

The closing paragraph voices Dara's quiet confidence: <Highlight>ia tidak merasa radio itu lenyap</Highlight> (she did not feel the radio had vanished) because <Highlight>suara yang hampir hilang kini memiliki pendengar baru dan tempat yang lebih aman untuk menunggu giliran didengar kembali</Highlight> (voices nearly lost now have new listeners and a safer home waiting to be heard again).

The most fitting reaction is feeling **optimistic because a medium can change form without losing its memory**.

## Why the other responses fail

- **Disappointment that Dara destroyed the archive** reverses her careful preservation onto two backup devices.
- **Anger that the language lab was canceled** misreads the text, the lab is still being built.
- **Anxiety or doubt about losing context** overlooks Dara's catalog list and the peaceful, hopeful closing tone.

# Answer

The most fitting response is feeling optimistic because a medium can change form without losing its memory.
