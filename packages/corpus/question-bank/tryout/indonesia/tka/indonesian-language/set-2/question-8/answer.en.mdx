Two listed details support the shift from radio broadcasting to preserving the sound archive:

- **Ruang radio akan dipakai sebagai laboratorium bahasa.** The room's former function is ending.
- **Guru sejarah menawarkan penyimpanan kering.** This offer provides a new place for the sound archive.

The tapes are inspected before playback because mold and fragility could damage them. Dara and Bimo select only three tapes in the best condition. The claim that every tape is played immediately therefore conflicts with the passage.
