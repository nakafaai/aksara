Two opening actions show **calm persistence**. Dara accepts the decision without arguing, but asks for an additional hour for a farewell programme. She still seeks an opportunity to act within that decision.

She discovers the tapes only afterward, while inspecting the archive. She then uses the final broadcast to demonstrate their value. The initial request for an hour therefore cannot already be attributed to a plan to rescue an archive she had not yet discovered.
