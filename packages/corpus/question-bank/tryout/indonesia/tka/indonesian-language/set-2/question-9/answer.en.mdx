# Given

When the principal announces that school radio will close, **Dara**, the last broadcaster, avoids heated arguments yet stays determined to honor the station's voice archive in a final broadcast.

# Asked

Which part of the story best proves that Dara is both calm and persistent.

# Solution

Imagine your soccer field is closing for renovations. A kid who is **calm yet persistent** does not yell at the coach, she accepts the decision smoothly and politely asks for one well-planned farewell match.

> The best evidence for a paired character trait captures both qualities acting together in one concrete scene.

## Seeing calm and persistence side by side in paragraph one

Paragraph one pairs both qualities immediately: <Highlight>Dara, penyiar terakhir, menerima keputusan itu tanpa berdebat</Highlight> (Dara accepted the decision without arguing, showing **calm**) followed by <Highlight>Ia hanya meminta satu jam tambahan untuk membuat acara perpisahan</Highlight> (she asked only for one extra hour to produce a farewell show, showing **persistence**).

Therefore, **accepting the decision and asking for one hour for a farewell show** proves both traits together.

## Why the other options fail

- **Walking out before the final show** and **switching off the on-air light early in protest** invert the story, as Dara broadcasts cleanly until five o'clock.
- **Cleaning moldy tapes to overturn the closure** and **demanding phone calls before accepting** never happen in the passage.

# Answer

**The strongest proof is that Dara accepts the decision and asks for one hour to hold a farewell broadcast**.
