A careful report states what was observed without turning an early pattern into proof of causation. **Pada dua hujan yang diamati sesudah pembangunan, genangan surut lebih cepat daripada dua pengamatan sebelumnya.** does exactly that: it keeps the small number of observations visible and makes no claim that the garden was the sole cause.
