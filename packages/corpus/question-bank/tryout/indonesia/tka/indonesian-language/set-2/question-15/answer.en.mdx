Distinguish **initial data** from **planned measurements**.

| Data | Category | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waterlogging duration | Sudah dicatat | The table already contains four duration values. |
| Rainfall intensity | Akan dicatat | The rain gauge will record rainfall amount with duration. |
| Soil moisture before rain | Akan dicatat | Introduced as a measurement for the next observations. |
