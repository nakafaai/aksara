# Given

During the initial **Rain Garden** survey, the team measured only **puddle duration** across four rainstorms, then planned to add a rain gauge for rainfall intensity and measure prior soil moisture in the next observation round.

# Asked

How to classify three data items as either already recorded or to be recorded in future observations.

# Solution

Think of a grocery list where one item is **already sitting in your basket** and two items are **planned for the next aisle**. Sorting them simply checks what already appears in the initial table versus what belongs to the follow-up plan.

> In timeline classification matrices, match each variable to whether it already has values in the initial survey or only appears in the follow-up plan.

## Checking the status of all three variables

Paragraph two's table already lists <Highlight>Lama genangan di gerbang</Highlight> (puddle duration at the gate). Meanwhile, paragraph three notes <Highlight>Intensitas hujan tidak diukur</Highlight> (rainfall intensity was not measured), and paragraph four plans to log <Highlight>banyaknya hujan beserta lama turunnya</Highlight> (rainfall amount and duration) plus <Highlight>mengukur kelembapan tanah sebelum hujan</Highlight> (measure soil moisture before rain).

| Data item | Category | Passage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Puddle duration | Already recorded | Fully tabulated for storms A through D. |
| Rainfall intensity | To be recorded | Unmeasured initially, planned via a rain gauge next. |
| Soil moisture before rain | To be recorded | Scheduled for the next observation round in paragraph four. |

## Confirming the cutoff between rounds

Only **puddle duration** has measured figures in the initial table, while both rainfall intensity and pre-rain soil moisture belong strictly to the upcoming phase.

# Answer

Puddle duration is already recorded, whereas rainfall intensity and soil moisture before rain are to be recorded next.
