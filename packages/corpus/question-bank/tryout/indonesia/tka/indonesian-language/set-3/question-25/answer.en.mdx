The record separates **the search result** from **the unknown survival status**.

| Question | Available evidence | Supported conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location of seedling and marker | Neither found after searching the surrounding area. | Not found. |
| Seedling survival | No evidence yet establishing life or death. | Cannot yet be determined. |
| Reason for disappearance | No evidence that the current carried the seedling away. | Do not assign a cause. |

Thus, the appropriate assessed wording is “Bibit tidak ditemukan. Status hidup bibit belum dapat dipastikan”.
