# Given

When Ayu arrives with stiff fingers and falters on her first run, **Rafi holds his breath**, counts four steady beats, and lets her find her tempo while the whole orchestra protects the rest without adding a single extra note.

# Asked

What the figurative sentence "that empty measure felt full" means in paragraph four.

# Solution

Picture an entire classroom sitting in **warm, encouraging silence** while a nervous classmate catches her breath before reciting a poem. Not a sound is spoken, yet the room overflows with quiet care and patience.

> The paradox of "empty yet full" means the measure has zero written notes while brimming with shared focus and support.

## Reading the direct explanation inside the same sentence

Paragraph four explains the metaphor right inside the sentence itself: <Highlight>Birama kosong itu terasa penuh oleh perhatian para pemain yang menunggu bersama</Highlight> (the empty measure felt full with the attention of the players waiting together) as they <Highlight>menjaga jeda itu seperti menjaga pintu tetap terbuka bagi Ayu</Highlight> (kept the pause open like a door for Ayu).

It means **silence is filled with attentiveness and willingness to wait**.

## Why the other four readings fail

- **Rafi secretly scribbling new notes** contradicts his holding back and leaving the measure untouched.
- **Audience filling the concert hall** confuses rehearsal with the later concert.
- **Ayu stopping cello** and **the conductor removing rests** clash with her calm second take.

# Answer

The sentence means that silence is filled with attentiveness and willingness to wait.
