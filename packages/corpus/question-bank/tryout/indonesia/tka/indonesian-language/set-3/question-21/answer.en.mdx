Follow where and when each activity occurs. New leaves are recorded while seedlings remain in nursery bags, bamboo supports are added on planting day, and missing markers are searched for after planting. The classification is:

- **Mencatat munculnya daun baru**: Persemaian
- **Mengikat bibit pada penyangga bambu**: Penanaman
- **Mencari penanda yang terbawa arus**: Pemantauan
