# Given

The **Mangrove Nursery** workflow unfolds across **three sequential phases**: raising seedlings in polybags during nursery care, transplanting them to the shore during planting, and inspecting survival afterward during monitoring.

# Asked

How to assign three activities to the nursery, planting, or monitoring phase.

# Solution

Think of growing **sunflowers from seed**: first you watch tiny leaves sprout in starter cups (nursery), next you move stems into the garden tied to stakes (planting), and later you check weekly to see how they survived storms (monitoring).

> In sequential procedure matrices, match each concrete action directly to the paragraph describing that stage of the workflow.

## Assigning each activity to its phase

Paragraph two (nursery) says volunteers <Highlight>mencatat apakah buah tetap tegak, muncul daun baru, atau membusuk</Highlight> (log whether propagules stand upright, sprout new leaves, or rot). Paragraph four (planting) notes <Highlight>setiap bibit diikat longgar pada penyangga bambu</Highlight> (each seedling is tied loosely to a bamboo stake). Paragraph five (monitoring) notes <Highlight>Tim mencari penanda</Highlight> (the team searches for markers washed away).

| Activity | Phase | Direct passage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recording new leaves appearing | Nursery | Done during the first two weeks in polybags on low racks. |
| Tying seedlings to bamboo stakes | Planting | Done on planting day at the coastal site. |
| Searching for markers swept by currents | Monitoring | Done at one-week, one-month, and three-month post-planting checks. |

## Confirming chronological order

All three activities fall neatly along the project timeline from start to finish: **nursery, planting, then monitoring**.

# Answer

**Recording new leaves belongs to the nursery phase, tying seedlings to bamboo stakes to the planting phase, and searching for swept-away markers to the monitoring phase**.
