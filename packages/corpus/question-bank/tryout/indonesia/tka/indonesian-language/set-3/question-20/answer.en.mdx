# Given

Across **The Empty Measure**, Rafi progresses from penciling extra notes into a rest to protecting quiet space for Ayu's cello melody and distilling his lesson into one single margin word.

# Asked

# Solution

Think of three snapshots of learning to swim: the first shows **clinging tightly to the edge**, the second shows **steadying your breathing in the water**, and the third shows **jotting down a reminder for next time**. Together they trace real growth from start to finish.

> In multi-select questions on character arc, include the starting point, turning-point action, and closing milestone, while rejecting anything contradicting the resolved character.

## Checking the three verified milestones

- **He initially added notes to the empty measure** is true as his starting point: <Highlight>menulis deretan not kecil dengan pensil</Highlight> (wrote a row of small notes in pencil) in paragraph one.
- **He held himself back so Ayu could find her tempo** is true as his turning point: <Highlight>Ia menahan napas, menghitung empat ketukan, dan membiarkan Ayu menemukan tempo</Highlight> (he held his breath, counted four beats, and let Ayu find the tempo).
- **He wrote the word listen on the score** is true as his closing realization: <Highlight>menulis satu kata di tepinya: "dengarkan"</Highlight> (wrote one word in the margin: "listen").

## Why the remaining option fails

- **Still filling Ayu's melody space when his clarinet part feels short** directly contradicts his restraint in paragraphs four and five.

# Answer

The three pieces of evidence are that he initially added notes to the empty measure, held back so Ayu could find her tempo, and wrote the word **"dengarkan"** (listen) on the score.
