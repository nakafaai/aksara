Rafi's change becomes clear when the beginning and ending are read together. At first, he fills the empty measure because he thinks silence has no value. At the later rehearsal, he waits while Ayu finds the tempo. After the concert, he writes “dengarkan” in the score. Select:

- **Ia mula-mula menambah not pada birama kosong.**
- **Ia menahan diri agar Ayu menemukan tempo.**
- **Ia menulis kata ‘dengarkan’ pada partitur.**

The claim that he still fills Ayu's melodic space contradicts his action during the later rehearsal.
