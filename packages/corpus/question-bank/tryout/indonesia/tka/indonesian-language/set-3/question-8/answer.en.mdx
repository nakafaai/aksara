# Given

At the end of **Mapmaker's Ink**, after hearing Laras at the village meeting, residents agree to inspect every remaining route before posting the map, checking paths for the first time with footsteps, wheels, and questions.

# Asked

Which event is most likely to happen after the story closes.

# Solution

Imagine a **neighborhood checking** a blocked safety path and testing every lane together with walkers, strollers, and wheelchairs. Since fences and bridges change over time, the most natural habit going forward is rechecking routes regularly with all kinds of users.

> A grounded prediction extends the shared checking habit and inclusive standard established in the closing scene.

## Reading the village agreement in paragraph five

The final paragraph records two commitments: <Highlight>Warga sepakat meninjau semua jalur lain sebelum peta dipasang</Highlight> (residents agreed to inspect all other routes before posting the map) and <Highlight>setiap jalurnya diperiksa dengan langkah kaki, roda, dan pertanyaan</Highlight> (every path was checked with footsteps, wheels, and questions).

The most likely continuation is that **residents periodically recheck routes while involving diverse users**.

## Why the other four predictions fail

- **Replacing field notes with paper copies** flips the lesson that ground truth guides the map.
- **Posting the old map unchanged** and **declaring a broken bridge safe** directly violate the village decision.
- **Delaying route fixes until a brand-new reprint** ignores that Laras already patched the sheet and residents inspect routes before posting.

# Answer

The most likely continuation is that **residents periodically recheck the routes while involving diverse users**.
