# Given

In **paragraph three of Mapmaker's Ink**, Laras walks the northern route she previously knew only as lines on paper. She spots a broken bamboo bridge, slippery planks, and a new blocking fence, then asks garden owners until she finds a wider lane fit for wheelchairs and carts.

# Asked

Which statement best summarizes paragraph three.

# Solution

Imagine checking **a shortcut to the park** on foot, finding a broken bridge and locked gate, and asking neighbors until they show you a smooth lane wide enough for strollers and wheelchairs. Your whole trip comes down to spotting obstacles and discovering an accessible detour.

> A complete narrative summary combines the concrete obstacles discovered on site with the workable solution the character uncovers.

## Combining the field obstacles and new route

Paragraph three lists real hazards: <Highlight>jembatan bambu patah</Highlight> (broken bamboo bridge), slippery narrow planks, and <Highlight>pagar baru menutup jalan</Highlight> (a new fence blocking the road). Laras then asks local garden owners and finds <Highlight>lorong yang sedikit lebih panjang, tetapi cukup lebar untuk kursi roda dan gerobak</Highlight> (a slightly longer lane wide enough for wheelchairs and carts).

Thus **Laras checks the obstacles and finds a more accessible alternative route**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Buying red ink without checking** and **moving the field on the map** ignore her actual walk along the route.
- **Printing extra copies** contradicts paragraph two stating only one large sheet remained.
- **Recording old paths without verification** reverses her thorough on-foot inspection.

# Answer

The best summary of paragraph three is that **Laras examines the obstacles and finds a more accessible alternative route**.
