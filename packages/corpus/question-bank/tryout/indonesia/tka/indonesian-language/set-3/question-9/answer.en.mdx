The stain does not repair the map by itself. It **prompts an inspection** that changes Laras’s attitude.

| Stage | Attitude or action | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginning | Irritated and wanting a reprint. | Treats the stain as an appearance problem. |
| Inspection | Finds a broken bridge, slippery board, and fence. | Learns that a line on a map does not ensure access. |
| Ending | Revises the route and leaves the correction visible. | Values careful checking and openness. |
