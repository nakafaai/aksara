Look at the phrase following *program*: *peminjaman botol*. The context is neither software nor an event schedule. The student council is organizing a service so students without a suitable bottle can still use the refill station. Here, *program* means **rangkaian kegiatan terencana yang menyediakan botol bagi siswa yang membutuhkan**.
