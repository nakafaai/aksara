# Given

In paragraph four of **Refill Station**, student council responds to students who forget or lack leakproof bottles by providing drying racks and a **bottle-lending program** (*program peminjaman botol*) using student ID cards.

# Asked

The most accurate meaning of the loanword *program* inside the phrase *program peminjaman botol*.

# Solution

Think of a school launching an **umbrella-lending program** on rainy days using student cards. Here *program* does not mean computer software or an event timetable, it means an **organized service** supplying what students need.

> A loanword like *program* shifts meaning by context, ranging from computer software to an organized plan of services.

## Checking the service context in paragraph four

Paragraph four states <Highlight>OSIS menyediakan rak pengering dan program peminjaman botol dengan kartu siswa</Highlight> (student council provided drying racks and a bottle-lending program using student IDs) right after learning <InlineMath math="35" /> surveyed students lacked a bottle on hand.

In this context, *program* means **a planned set of activities supplying bottles to students who need them**.

## Why the other four meanings miss the context

- **Software on student IDs** forces a computing definition absent from the text.
- **An announcement agenda** and **tank refill schedule** refer to entirely different routines.
- **A mandatory roster of students** turns a helpful loan service into a compulsory list.

# Answer

In this phrase, *program* means a planned set of activities that provides bottles for students who need them.
