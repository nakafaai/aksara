The transcript is an access feature, not one of the variables being compared. Every participant may still use it. Earphone access differs between groups, while the new audio titles appear on every device. Therefore, **karena transkrip tetap dapat diakses semua kelompok, sedangkan pemberian earphone dibedakan dengan penamaan audio yang dibuat sama** is the supported answer.
