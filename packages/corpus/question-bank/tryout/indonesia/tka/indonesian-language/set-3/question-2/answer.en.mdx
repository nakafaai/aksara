The transcript is an access feature, not one of the variables being compared. Every participant may still use it. Earphone access differs between groups, while the new audio titles appear on every device. Therefore, **karena transkrip dipertahankan sebagai akses dasar bagi semua kelompok, sedangkan earphone dan penamaanlah yang dibedakan untuk diuji** is the supported answer.
