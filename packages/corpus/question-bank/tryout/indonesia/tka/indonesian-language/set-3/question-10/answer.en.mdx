# Given

While patching the evacuation map, **Laras refuses white paint** from her mother so the faint trace of the correction remains readable. At the village meeting, she openly explains that the old map nearly directed residents toward a broken bridge and blocked fence.

# Asked

Which emotional response best fits the moment Laras reveals why she kept the stain mark visible.

# Solution

Imagine a classmate standing before the room to **honestly admit a mistake on a safety chart** and share the real-world fix she verified on foot. Rather than scolding her, you naturally **appreciate** her honesty and care for everyone's safety.

> A fitting emotional reaction to honest transparency honors a character who puts community safety above flawless appearances.

## Reading open honesty before the village

Paragraph four notes she declined white paint because she <Highlight>ingin bekas koreksi tetap dapat dibaca jelas</Highlight> (wanted the correction mark to remain clearly readable). In paragraph five she openly warns residents that <Highlight>peta lama hampir mengarahkan orang ke jembatan rusak dan pagar tertutup</Highlight> (the old map almost sent people to a broken bridge and closed fence).

This courage inspires **appreciation for Laras admitting a mistake and fixing it through real-world inspection**.

## Why the other four emotions fail

- **Annoyance at carelessness** ignores how thoroughly she verified the ground route.
- **Doubt that a corrected map is unsafe** misses that the revised lane actually fits wheelchairs and carts.
- **Pride that the stain became a new assembly point** misreads a correction mark as a new landmark.
- **Worry that the path is unreadable** overlooks that she pasted clean paper over the smudge and redrew the route clearly.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is **appreciating Laras's courage to admit a mistake and fix it through real inspection**.
