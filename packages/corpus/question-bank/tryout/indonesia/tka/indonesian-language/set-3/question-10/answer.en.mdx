The stain does not remain because the map is impossible to clean. Laras deliberately refuses white paint after the accident leads her to inspect the route. As an aesthetic detail, the mark preserves the map's revision history and **menjadi pengingat bahwa peta diperbaiki melalui pemeriksaan nyata**.
