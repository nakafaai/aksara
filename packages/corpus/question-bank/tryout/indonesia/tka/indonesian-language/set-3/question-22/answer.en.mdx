# Given

In the **final paragraph of Mangrove Nursery**, the team inspects seedlings at one week, one month, and three months after planting. Missing seedlings are never automatically counted dead, staff search for markers and log "not found" when evidence is insufficient so mortality counts are not overstated.

# Asked

Which statement best summarizes the final paragraph.

# Solution

Imagine taking **attendance on a zoo field trip**. If one classmate is not standing by the elephant pen, you do not immediately mark him gone home before checking nearby. You record only what your evidence actually proves.

> A strong summary of a monitoring paragraph captures both the recording rule and the scientific caution behind it.

## Pairing the recording rule with its reason

Paragraph five states that staff <Highlight>mencatat status "tidak ditemukan" jika bukti tidak cukup</Highlight> (record the status "not found" when evidence is insufficient) in order to <Highlight>mencegah angka kematian dibuat lebih pasti daripada pengamatan yang tersedia</Highlight> (prevent mortality numbers from sounding more certain than available observations allow).

Hence **seedling status is recorded according to the strength of evidence so results are not overstated as overly certain**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Marking missing seedlings dead to keep totals neat** is the exact mistake the team avoids.
- **Inspecting only once** contradicts three scheduled checks (one week, one month, three months).
- **Deciding status from old notes without checking** contradicts searching the surrounding area.
- **Claiming currents do not affect planting sites** clashes directly with paragraph three.

# Answer

The best summary of the final paragraph is that seedling status is recorded according to the strength of evidence so results are not made more certain than observations warrant.
