# Given

# Solution

Imagine picking **the right corner of a yard for a plant**. You check how long water pools there, how strong runoff flows, and which plants already thrive naturally in that soil, never the color of the plastic cup.

> In explicit multi-select items, verify each choice directly against the exact list stated in the matching paragraph.

## Verifying the three factors in paragraph three

Paragraph three lists all three criteria in one clear sentence: <Highlight>Mereka memeriksa lama genangan, kekuatan arus, dan jenis mangrove yang tumbuh alami di dekatnya</Highlight> (they inspect flooding duration, current strength, and mangrove species growing naturally nearby).

- **Flooding duration** is true.
- **Current strength** is true.
- **Natural mangrove species nearby** is true.

## Why the fourth option fails

- **The brightest polybag color** never appears in the text and has nothing to do with coastal habitat suitability.

# Answer

**The three factors considered when choosing a planting site are flooding duration, current strength, and naturally occurring mangrove species nearby**.
