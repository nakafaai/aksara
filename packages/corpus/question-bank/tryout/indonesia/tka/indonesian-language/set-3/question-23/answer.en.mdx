Before choosing a site, the team checks whether a seedling matches local conditions. The passage names three factors:

- **Inundation duration**.
- **Current strength**.
- **Naturally occurring mangrove species** nearby.

The color of a nursery bag says nothing about whether the planting site is suitable.
