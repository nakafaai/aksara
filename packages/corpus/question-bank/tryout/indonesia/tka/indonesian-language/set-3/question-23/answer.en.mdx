A seedling that cannot be seen is not necessarily dead. Its marker may have been carried away, so the team searches the surrounding area before recording “tidak ditemukan”. This procedure means **status bibit dicatat sesuai kekuatan bukti agar hasil tidak terlalu pasti**.
