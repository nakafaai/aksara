# Given

The **Refill Station** report presents a three-week table of **dispensed water and visible bottles**, then notes in paragraph three outside influences including the cafeteria dropping one bottled brand and higher air temperatures in week three.

# Asked

How to classify three items as either primary table data or an alternative explanation.

# Solution

Think of a lemonade stand notebook: **cups sold each week in the table** are your primary data, while **a heat wave** or **a rival stand closing** are alternative explanations that could shift those numbers.

> Primary table data record the core variables tracked each week, whereas alternative explanations are outside conditions that may also move the figures.
