Distinguish **the main measured values** from **other events that may affect them**.

| Element | Category | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 438 liters in week three | Data utama pada tabel | A directly recorded volume. |
| One water brand no longer sold | Penjelasan alternatif | May change the number of packaged bottles used. |
| Rising temperature | Penjelasan alternatif | May change drinking needs and refill volume. |
