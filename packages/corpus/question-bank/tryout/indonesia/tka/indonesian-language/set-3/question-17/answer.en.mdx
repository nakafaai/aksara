# Given

In paragraph two of **The Empty Measure**, Rafi nods at the conductor's words yet still feels silence means doing nothing. The next day, **Ayu arrives late** with a slipped bike chain, so the orchestra rehearses part three **without the cello**.

# Asked

What purpose the rehearsal without Ayu serves in the plot.

# Solution

Imagine thinking **a picture frame does nothing** until the photo is pulled out and you stare at an empty square. Suddenly you feel how the frame existed to hold the picture. Rehearsing without the cello gives Rafi that exact realization.

> In narrative structure, removing a counterpart lets the protagonist feel firsthand a truth they previously only heard as advice.

## Reading the realization in paragraph three

Paragraph three pinpoints the insight triggered by Ayu's absence: <Highlight>Birama kosong Rafi kini terdengar benar-benar hampa</Highlight> (Rafi's empty measure now sounded truly hollow), and <Highlight>Ia baru menyadari bahwa diamnya klarinet semula membentuk bingkai bagi melodi Ayu</Highlight> (he finally realized his clarinet's silence had framed Ayu's melody).

Thus the rehearsal without Ayu serves **to let Rafi experience the purpose of silence through the absence of the cello melody**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Replacing the cello with silence** reverses his discovery, because without the cello the rest loses direction.
- **Postponing the cello part**, **turning a clarinet into a string instrument**, and **ending the concert** never occur.

# Answer

**The rehearsal without Ayu lets Rafi feel the true function of silence through the absence of the cello melody**.
