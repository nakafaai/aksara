When Ayu is present, the clarinet's silence creates room for her cello solo. Rafi fully understands that function only when she is late: the empty measure remains, but it feels directionless without the melody it normally frames. Her absence **membuat Rafi merasakan fungsi diam melalui ketiadaan melodi cello**.
