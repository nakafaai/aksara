Rehearsing without Ayu reveals **the rest’s function through contrast**.

| Situation | What is heard | Rafi’s understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ayu present | The clarinet rests while the cello plays a solo. | The rest gives another melody room. |
| Ayu absent | The clarinet rest remains without the cello melody. | It feels empty because the framed melody is missing. |
