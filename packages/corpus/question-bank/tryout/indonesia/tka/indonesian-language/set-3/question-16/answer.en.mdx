# Given

At the close of **The Empty Measure**, Rafi feels no disappointment when an audience member praises only Ayu's cello solo. He circles the empty measure and writes "listen" in the margin, and as the orchestra bows, **Ayu points at that circle and smiles**.

# Asked

Which emotional response best fits Ayu's smile at the end of the story.

# Solution

Imagine quietly holding back so **your friend can shine during her solo**, and while bowing afterward she spots your tiny note in the margin and smiles without a word. You feel **warm** because both of you share an unspoken bond.

> An emotional response to a silent closing gesture reflects the quiet mutual understanding built through the story.

## Reading the unspoken bond in paragraph five

The closing paragraph captures that quiet recognition: Rafi writes <Highlight>dengarkan</Highlight> (listen) beside the circled rest, and <Highlight>Ketika orkes membungkuk, Ayu menunjuk lingkaran itu lalu tersenyum</Highlight> (as the orchestra bowed, Ayu pointed to that circle and smiled).

Ayu silently honors the space Rafi protected for her solo, evoking **warmth because both understand an unspoken form of support**.

## Why the other four emotions fail

- **Anger over Ayu taking the score**, **fear over a canceled concert**, **disappointment over a broken clarinet**, and **confusion because the orchestra never bowed** all invent events absent from the text.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response to Ayu's smile is **warmth because both share an unspoken understanding of support**.
