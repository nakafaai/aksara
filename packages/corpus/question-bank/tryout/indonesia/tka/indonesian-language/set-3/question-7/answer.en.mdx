# Given

On the draft map, the northern route looked like **a plain narrow road to the field** that Laras had never walked. Once she walks it in paragraph three, physical reality differs sharply from the drawn lines.

# Asked

All details showing that the actual northern path differs from its initial depiction on the map.

# Solution

Imagine a paper map showing **a clean straight path to the park**, yet when you walk there you hit a broken bridge, slippery boards, and a locked fence. Those three physical surprises prove the paper lines did not match the real ground.

> In multi-select evidence questions, select every physical fact directly encountered onstage and reject false assumptions about the map.

## Verifying the three field details in paragraph three

Paragraph three reports three real barriers explicitly:

- **The bamboo bridge is broken** is true, matching <Highlight>ia menemukan jembatan bambu patah</Highlight> (she found the bamboo bridge broken).
- **The crossing planks are slippery** is true, matching <Highlight>papan itu licin setelah hujan</Highlight> (those planks were slippery after rain).
- **A new fence blocks the path** is true, matching <Highlight>sebuah pagar baru menutup jalan menuju lapangan</Highlight> (a new fence blocked the road to the field).

## Why the fourth option fails

- **Assuming line width on the map predicts wheelchair access** contradicts the whole lesson, since the paper map hid a broken bridge, narrow planks, and a blocking fence.

# Answer

**The three correct details are that the bamboo bridge is broken, the crossing planks are slippery, and a new fence blocks the road**.
