# Given

The **pencak silat club** passage provides four statements to sort into three roles: **Club growth** (*Perkembangan ekstrakurikuler*), **Emerging problem** (*Masalah yang muncul*), or **School response** (*Langkah sekolah*).

# Asked

The right category for each of the four statements according to its role in the passage.

# Solution

The passage moves through three clear stages: **paragraph one** reports club growth, **paragraph three** lists the problems caused by that growth, and **paragraph four** details how the school responded. Matching each item to its stage sorts all four cleanly.

> Structural signposts like *sempat menimbulkan masalah* (created problems) separate growth facts from obstacles and solutions immediately.

## Matching the four statements to their source paragraphs

Paragraph one documents **rapid membership growth**: members rose from <InlineMath math="25" /> to <InlineMath math="70" /> students in two years. Statement one therefore belongs to **Perkembangan ekstrakurikuler**.

Paragraph three signals obstacles with <Highlight>Kenaikan jumlah anggota itu sempat menimbulkan masalah</Highlight> (that rise in members caused problems). It lists both **a field holding only about <InlineMath math="40" /> students** and **newcomers quitting after a month**, putting statements two and four in **Masalah yang muncul**.

Paragraph four explains the school's remedies, including **training two senior members as assistant coaches**. Statement three therefore fits **Langkah sekolah**.

# Answer

The correct mapping is:
1. Membership rising from <InlineMath math="25" /> to <InlineMath math="70" /> students in two years: **Perkembangan ekstrakurikuler** (Club growth)
2. Practice field holding only about <InlineMath math="40" /> students: **Masalah yang muncul** (Emerging problem)
3. Training two senior members as assistant coaches: **Langkah sekolah** (School response)
4. Several new students quitting after a month: **Masalah yang muncul** (Emerging problem)
