# Given

In paragraph three of the **refill water depot** passage, some residents spread a social media post claiming **"refill depot water is dangerous and should not be drunk at all"**. The clinic head immediately calls the post **misleading** because **most tested depots met safety standards** and the issue is preventable with proper maintenance.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph three.

# Solution

When a paragraph quotes a sweeping viral rumor and immediately presents an official rebuttal backed by evidence, the main idea is **the rebuttal and its core reason**, not the rumor itself. Here the clinic head calls the claim **misleading** because most depots passed.

> In a rumor-and-rebuttal paragraph, the main idea centers on the authoritative evaluation and the evidence supporting it.

## Checking the viral message and the official rebuttal

Paragraph three directly answers the online claim: <Highlight>Kepala puskesmas menyebut pesan itu menyesatkan. Menurutnya, sebagian besar depot yang diperiksa justru memenuhi syarat, dan masalah dapat dicegah jika depot merawat alatnya dengan benar</Highlight> (the clinic head called the message misleading, noting that most tested depots met standards and problems can be prevented through proper equipment care).

That captures the entire paragraph's core point: **the claim that all depot water is dangerous is considered misleading because most depots meet safety requirements**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Proven dangerous for all residents** clashes with <InlineMath math="16" /> out of <InlineMath math="20" /> depots passing inspection.
- **Closing every depot in the district** invents a shutdown never mentioned, since failing depots will be **re-inspected after one month**.
- **Sharing accurate test results** reverses the clinic head's judgment calling the post **misleading**.
- **Banning residents from buying depot water** ignores that residents are merely **advised to check gallon hygiene**, not forbidden from buying.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph three is that **the message claiming all refill depot water is dangerous is judged misleading because most depots meet safety standards**.
