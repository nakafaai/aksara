# Given

In the story about a **hand-me-down school uniform**, Damar starts high school wearing his older brother Galih's faded shirt with an ink stain. He initially **feels embarrassed**, then **Pak Sunu reveals that Galih won a provincial physics olympiad**, and that night **Damar drops his plan to ask for a new uniform** and opens Galih's physics notebook.

# Asked

The main idea of the story.

# Solution

A story's main idea is the **single thread connecting the opening to the ending**, not one isolated moment. Here Damar's feelings travel cleanly from **hiding his stained shirt in shame** to **feeling proud of his brother's hard work**.

> The main idea of a narrative captures the protagonist's full arc from initial tension through the turning point to the closing change.

## Tracking Damar from beginning to end

At first, Damar **covers the faint ink stain near his pocket** and nods while **holding back embarrassment**. The turning point arrives when Pak Sunu explains: <Highlight>Kakakmu dulu juara olimpiade fisika provinsi. Noda tinta itu pasti dari malam-malam ia belajar rumus</Highlight> (your brother won the provincial physics olympiad, and that ink stain surely came from late nights studying formulas).

By the ending, **Damar chooses not to ask for a new uniform** and opens **Galih's old physics notes**. Together, these moments show shame turning into pride once he learns his brother's story.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Damar asks his mother for a new uniform** contradicts the ending, where he cancels that plan.
- **Pak Sunu scolds Damar** clashes with the scene, since Pak Sunu **smiles warmly** and praises Galih.
- **Galih returns to teach physics** misreads the characters because Galih graduated three years earlier and **Pak Sunu** is the physics teacher.
- **Classmates mock Damar** adds an event never shown in the text, since Damar's shame is purely internal.

# Answer

The main idea of the story is that **Damar's embarrassment about his hand-me-down uniform turns into pride after learning his brother's story**.
