# Given

In paragraph three of the **pencak silat club** passage, several new students quit after a month because they **thought pencak silat was only about competing**. In paragraph four, Pak Rahmat now **explains at the first meeting of every semester that competitions are only a small part of the activity**.

# Asked

The relationship between several new students quitting and Pak Rahmat's explanation at the start of every semester.

# Solution

If newcomers quit a gardening club because they expected only flower contests, and the leader begins explaining on day one that contests are only a tiny slice of the work, that talk clearly aims to **clear up the misconception early**.

> A problem-solution link across paragraphs appears when an action in the later paragraph targets the exact cause named in the earlier paragraph.

## Linking the dropout cause to the semester opening talk

Paragraph three identifies the exact misconception behind students leaving: <Highlight>Beberapa siswa baru juga keluar setelah sebulan karena mengira pencak silat hanya tentang bertanding</Highlight> (several new students quit after a month because they thought pencak silat was only about fighting matches).

Paragraph four introduces the targeted remedy: <Highlight>Pada pertemuan pertama setiap semester, Pak Rahmat juga menjelaskan bahwa pertandingan hanya sebagian kecil dari kegiatan</Highlight> (at the first meeting each semester, Pak Rahmat explains that matches are only a small part of the club). His briefing directly **corrects that false expectation**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **To reduce sign-ups** misstates the goal, which is **setting accurate expectations**, not turning students away.
- **Because Pak Rahmat banned competitions** contradicts the text, which still includes matches as a **small part** of club activities.
- **Unrelated** ignores the direct match between the misconception and Pak Rahmat's clarification.
- **Because practice moved to Thursday** confuses the dropout issue with splitting sessions into Tuesday and Thursday to solve **field crowding**.

# Answer

The relationship is that **the explanation aims to correct the misconception that pencak silat is only about competing**.
