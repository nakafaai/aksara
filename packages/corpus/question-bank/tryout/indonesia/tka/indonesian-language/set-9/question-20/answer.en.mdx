# Given

In paragraph three of the **lost cat** story, a vegetable vendor calls after spotting a cat resembling Puspa, and Nayla cycles there after school. We must select **all true details about the place where Puspa was found**.

# Asked

Every correct statement describing the location where Puspa was found.

# Solution

For multi-select questions, the safest strategy is to **underline every place clue in paragraph three** and check each option one by one. Be careful not to mix up **Bu Wening's porch** with the **spot where Puspa was discovered**.

> Always verify setting details against the exact paragraph where the event occurs so two different locations in the story never get swapped.

## Checking every location detail in paragraph three

Paragraph three gives three clear facts about the discovery site: <Highlight>di gudang beras dekat pasar, dua kilometer dari rumah</Highlight> (at a rice warehouse near the market, two kilometers from home) and <Highlight>Di sudut gudang, di antara tumpukan karung, ia menemukan Puspa</Highlight> (in a corner of the warehouse, among stacks of sacks, she found Puspa).

By contrast, **Bu Wening's porch** is where Bu Wening called for Puspa each afternoon and where the box was set down after the rescue, not where Puspa was found.

## Verdict on all four options

- **Found at a rice warehouse near the market**: **Correct**, directly reported by the vegetable vendor.
- **Found under Bu Wening's porch**: **Incorrect**, as the porch is where Bu Wening waited and later received the box.
- **Two kilometers away from home**: **Correct**, matching the exact distance stated in paragraph three.
- **Among stacks of sacks**: **Correct**, matching the exact corner where Nayla spotted Puspa.

# Answer

All correct details about where Puspa was found are:
- **Puspa was found at a rice warehouse near the market**.
- **The place was two kilometers from home**.
- **Puspa was among stacks of sacks**.
