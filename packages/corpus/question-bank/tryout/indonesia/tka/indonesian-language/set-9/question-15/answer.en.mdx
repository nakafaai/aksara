Pak Rahmat calls **respect and self-control** the heart of pencak silat. Training opens with a respectful greeting **to the coach and the opponent**, and students hold back their strength so they don't hurt a partner. Those values work in daily life, like staying polite and keeping your temper in a contest or debate. So that's the most applicable value.

- The text says matches are only a small part of the activity.
- Students learn to **control** their strength, not to use all of it.
- The text presents regular weekly training as a good thing.
- Training happens every Tuesday and Thursday, not only before matches.
