# Given

The **refill water depot** passage provides four statements to sort into three roles: **How depots operate** (*Cara kerja depot*), **Inspection findings** (*Hasil pemeriksaan*), or **Clinic follow-up** (*Tindak lanjut puskesmas*).

# Asked

The correct category for each of the four statements according to its role in the text.

# Solution

The passage divides neatly into three jobs: **paragraph one** describes how depots treat water, **paragraph two** reports the July test results and causes found, and **paragraph four** details the clinic's follow-up actions. Matching each sentence to its paragraph sorts all four accurately.

> Separating general workflow, inspection findings, and post-inspection actions prevents mixing up what inspectors found with what the clinic did next.

## Matching the four statements to their source paragraphs

Paragraph one outlines **water treatment**: <Highlight>air biasanya disaring berlapis lalu disinari lampu ultraviolet untuk mematikan kuman</Highlight> (water is layered-filtered and exposed to ultraviolet light to kill germs). Statement two fits **Cara kerja depot**.

Paragraph two presents the **July inspection findings**: <InlineMath math="4" /> depots exceeded bacteria limits, and inspectors found those depots **rarely replaced filters**. Both statements three and four belong to **Hasil pemeriksaan**.

Paragraph four states the clinic's follow-up: <Highlight>Puskesmas kini memasang stiker hasil pemeriksaan di setiap depot yang lolos uji</Highlight> (the clinic now places inspection stickers on every passing depot). Statement one therefore fits **Tindak lanjut puskesmas**.

# Answer

The correct mapping is:
1. Stickers placed on every passing depot: **Tindak lanjut puskesmas** (Clinic follow-up)
2. Water treated with ultraviolet light to kill germs: **Cara kerja depot** (How depots operate)
3. Water from <InlineMath math="4" /> depots containing excess bacteria: **Hasil pemeriksaan** (Inspection findings)
4. Inspectors finding failing depots rarely changed filters: **Hasil pemeriksaan** (Inspection findings)
