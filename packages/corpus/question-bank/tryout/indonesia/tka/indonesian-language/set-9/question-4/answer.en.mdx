The officer says there's been **no measurement of the kualitas of the river water before and after**. That measurement would show whether the river is still polluted with oil. What's being judged is **how good or bad** the water is, not how much there is. So *kualitas* here means the quality, how good or bad the river water is.

- An amount of water is *kuantitas* (quantity), not quality.
- Color is just one sign, while quality testing covers pollutants.
- Current speed has nothing to do with oil pollution.
- The river's name isn't mentioned.
