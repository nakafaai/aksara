# Given

In the **hand-me-down uniform** story, Damar first planned to ask for a new shirt because he was embarrassed by the faded fabric and ink stain. After hearing Pak Sunu's story about Galih, **that night Damar did not ask for a new uniform as he had originally planned**.

# Asked

The strongest evidence that Damar's attitude toward his hand-me-down uniform has changed.

# Solution

To prove someone **changed their mind**, describing the object or their old habit is not enough. The strongest proof is a **new choice that cancels an earlier plan**: he meant to replace the shirt, and then he chose to keep it.

> Strong evidence of a shift in attitude directly contrasts an earlier intention with a new decision made after the turning point.

## Pinpointing the change in the final paragraph

The closing paragraph states the reversal explicitly: <Highlight>Malam itu, Damar tidak meminta seragam baru seperti rencananya semula</Highlight> (that night, Damar did not ask for a new uniform as he had originally planned). The phrase **as he had originally planned** confirms his earlier wish to replace it and proves **he now accepts the uniform**.

No other detail captures the before-and-after shift inside Damar as directly as this sentence.

## Why the other four options fail

- **The white fabric is less bright** describes the **condition of the shirt**, not Damar's change of heart.
- **Trying to hide the ink stain** shows his **earlier embarrassment** before his attitude shifted.
- **Pak Sunu reading the collar name** is **Pak Sunu's action**, not Damar's changed stance.
- **His mother sewing the loose buttons** is **his mother's effort**, not evidence of Damar's shift.

# Answer

The strongest evidence is that **Damar decided not to ask for a new uniform as he had originally planned**.
