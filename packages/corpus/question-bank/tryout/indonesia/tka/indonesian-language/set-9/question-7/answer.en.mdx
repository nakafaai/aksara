# Given

On his first day of high school, Damar wears his brother's faded uniform with a faint ink stain near the pocket. Four statements are listed, and we must select **every detail showing Damar's embarrassment on the first day of school**.

# Asked

All details that genuinely show Damar's embarrassment on his first day of school.

# Solution

When someone feels embarrassed in a new room, they try to **hide what makes them self-conscious** and **escape attention as quickly as possible**. Checking each option against Damar's own classroom behavior separates the two true details from the two traps.

> Evidence of a character's feeling must come from that character's own actions at the specified moment, not another person's task or a later change of heart.

## Checking each of the four options

Paragraph two directly describes Damar in class: <Highlight>Damar duduk dengan tangan terlipat di atas meja, berusaha menutupi noda tinta samar di dekat saku. Ketika guru meminta siswa memperkenalkan diri, ia bicara cepat lalu langsung duduk</Highlight>.

Folding his arms to hide the stain and rushing through his introduction plainly show **first-day embarrassment**. By contrast, altering the waist was **his mother's preparation before school**, and opening Galih's notebook happens **that night after his shame fades**.

## Verdict on all four options

- **His mother altered the waist** -> **Incorrect**, as that is his mother's action before school.
- **Damar folded his arms to hide the ink stain** -> **Correct**, directly showing he wanted to conceal the mark.
- **Damar took Galih's physics notebook** -> **Incorrect**, as this occurred at night after he grew proud.
- **Damar spoke quickly and sat right down** -> **Correct**, showing his rush to get out of the spotlight.

# Answer

The details showing Damar's embarrassment on his first day of school are:
- **Damar folded his arms on the desk to cover the ink stain.**
- **Damar spoke quickly and sat right back down when introducing himself.**
