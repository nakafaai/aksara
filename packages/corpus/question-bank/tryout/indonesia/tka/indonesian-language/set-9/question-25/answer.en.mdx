# Given

In paragraph two of the **refill water depot** passage, inspectors found that the four failing depots **rarely replaced filters and did not wash buyers' gallon containers before refilling**. In the final paragraph, **residents are advised to check whether their containers are washed and sealed tightly before bringing them home**.

# Asked

The relationship between the inspectors' finding on failing depots and the advice given to residents in the last paragraph.

# Solution

If health inspectors discover contamination because a stall skipped washing cups, and customers are then advised to check for clean cups before taking their drink, that advice **directly answers the exact lapse inspectors found**.

> Practical advice for readers at the end of an informational text often targets a specific preventable cause identified earlier in the inspection findings.

## Linking the container finding to the advice for residents

Paragraph two pinpoints a concrete hygiene failure: <Highlight>tidak mencuci galon pembeli sebelum diisi</Highlight> (not washing buyers' gallon containers before filling).

Paragraph four directly empowers buyers to guard against that exact risk: <Highlight>Warga juga dianjurkan memperhatikan apakah galon dicuci dan ditutup rapat sebelum dibawa pulang</Highlight> (residents are advised to check whether containers are washed and tightly sealed before going home). Thus **the advice to watch container washing responds directly to the finding that failing depots skipped washing buyers' containers**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Telling residents to stop buying depot water** clashes with the clinic head's point that most depots are safe.
- **Unrelated to the inspectors' finding** misses the exact overlap on **washing buyers' containers**.
- **Prompted by rising depot prices** invents a price hike never mentioned in the text.
- **Asking residents to replace depot filters themselves** confuses **depot maintenance duties** with what buyers can observe.

# Answer

The relationship is that **the advice to check container washing responds to the finding that failing depots did not wash buyers' gallon containers**.
