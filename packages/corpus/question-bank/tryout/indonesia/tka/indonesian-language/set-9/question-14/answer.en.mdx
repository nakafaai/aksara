# Given

In paragraph one of the **pencak silat club** passage, the text notes that at a suburban junior high school, **club membership rose from <InlineMath math="25" /> to <InlineMath math="70" /> students in two years**.

# Asked

The most accurate sentence for reporting the growth of the pencak silat club at that school.

# Solution

A reliable report works like an **honest scale**: it never exaggerates, never flips a trend, and keeps numbers exact. Matching the starting count of <InlineMath math="25" />, the ending count of <InlineMath math="70" />, and the two-year span picks the right sentence immediately.

> An accurate factual report preserves exact figures, direction of change, and true scope without inserting sweeping opinions.

## Checking the exact numbers and timeframe

Paragraph one gives the precise figures: <Highlight>jumlah anggotanya naik dari <InlineMath math="25" /> menjadi <InlineMath math="70" /> siswa dalam dua tahun</Highlight> (membership rose from <InlineMath math="25" /> to <InlineMath math="70" /> students in two years). That represents a net gain of <InlineMath math="70 - 25 = 45" /> students, not an addition of <InlineMath math="70" />.

Stating **Within two years, pencak silat club membership rose from <InlineMath math="25" /> to <InlineMath math="70" /> students** captures every core fact neutrally and accurately.

## Why the other four sentences fail

- **Every student in the school is now a martial artist** exaggerates <InlineMath math="70" /> members into the entire student body.
- **Increased by <InlineMath math="70" /> students** confuses the **final total** with the actual increase of <InlineMath math="45" /> students.
- **The best sport in the world** adds an **unsupported personal superlative**.
- **Membership dropped due to a small field** reverses the trend, since enrollment **increased** and crowded the field.

# Answer

The most accurate reporting sentence is **Within two years, pencak silat club membership rose from <InlineMath math="25" /> to <InlineMath math="70" /> students**.
