# Given

Nayla found thin Puspa in a quiet corner of a rice warehouse near the market **nursing three kittens**. Once the box is placed on the porch and Bu Wening looks at the three kittens, she says, **"Pantas kamu pergi. Kamu mencari tempat yang tenang"** (no wonder you left, you were looking for a quiet place).

# Asked

The meaning of Bu Wening's remark, "No wonder you left. You were looking for a quiet place."

# Solution

A mother cat about to give birth naturally slips away to a secluded corner so **her newborns stay safe and undisturbed**. Saying *pantas* (no wonder) as soon as she sees the kittens shows Bu Wening **finally understands why Puspa vanished**.

> When a character says *no wonder* immediately upon seeing new evidence, the line expresses sudden understanding of a previously mysterious action.

## Linking her words to the three kittens

Right before speaking, Bu Wening **gazes at the three kittens**, who had been found **nursing among rice sacks in a warehouse corner**. She then says: <Highlight>Pantas kamu pergi. Kamu mencari tempat yang tenang</Highlight> (no wonder you left, you were seeking a quiet place).

In context, her remark means **Bu Wening realizes Puspa left in order to give birth and care for her kittens**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Accusing Nayla of hiding Puspa** misidentifies the listener, since *kamu* (you) addresses **Puspa**, while Nayla rescued her.
- **Bu Wening wanting to move house** misreads *kamu* as referring to Bu Wening instead of Puspa.
- **Annoyed that her house is too noisy** clashes with the opening fact that Bu Wening **lives alone**.
- **Planning to chase Puspa away** contradicts her affectionate petting and tears of joy.

# Answer

Her remark means that **Bu Wening understands Puspa left to give birth and care for her kittens**.
