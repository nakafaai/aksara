Paragraph two opens with its topic sentence, *Proses pembuatannya memerlukan kehati-hatian* (making it takes care). The rest lists the stages: **straining**, **soaking with charcoal**, **mixing with lye** while wearing protective gear, **stirring and molding**, then **leaving it about four weeks**. So the main idea is that making soap from used oil takes several careful stages.

- The oil has to be strained, soaked, and mixed with lye first.
- The soap rests about **four weeks** before it's safe to use.
- Charcoal **reduces the smell**, it doesn't harden the soap.
- The text says the lye solution can injure skin.
