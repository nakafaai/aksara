# Given

Paragraph two of the **waste oil soap** passage opens with **"Proses pembuatannya memerlukan kehati-hatian"** (the manufacturing process requires care). Subsequent sentences list **filtering**, **soaking with charcoal for a day**, **gradually dissolving caustic soda**, **wearing safety gear**, **stirring and molding**, and **curing for four weeks**.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph two.

# Solution

Picture a main idea as an umbrella that covers every sentence underneath it. Here the opening sentence states the **need for care**, and every sentence after it walks through **each careful step in the recipe**.

> A paragraph's main idea unites its topic sentence with all supporting steps rather than repeating one isolated detail or contradicting a safety rule.

## Tracing the topic sentence and supporting steps

The topic sentence appears right at the start: <Highlight>Proses pembuatannya memerlukan kehati-hatian</Highlight> (the production process requires care). Every following sentence adds a **concrete stage**, from straining food scraps and soaking with charcoal to adding caustic soda slowly into water, wearing protective gear, molding, and waiting about four weeks.

Putting the topic sentence and steps together gives one clear main idea: **making soap from waste oil takes several steps that require careful handling**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Used directly without processing** ignores filtering, charcoal soaking, caustic soda mixing, and curing.
- **Ready to use after one day** mixes up **one-day charcoal soaking** with the **four-week curing time** needed before safe use.
- **Charcoal hardens the soap** misstates its purpose, which is **reducing odor**.
- **Caustic soda is safe to touch** directly contradicts the warning that it is **hot and can injure skin**.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph two is that **making soap from used cooking oil involves several steps that require careful handling**.
