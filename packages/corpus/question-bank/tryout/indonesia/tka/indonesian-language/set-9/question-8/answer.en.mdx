# Given

At the end of the **hand-me-down uniform** story, Damar decides not to ask for a new shirt. Instead, he **takes Galih's old physics notebook out of the cupboard, blows off the dust, and opens the first page**.

# Asked

The event most likely to happen after the story ends.

# Solution

If someone pulls an old soccer ball from storage, wipes off the dust, and laces up their cleats, you immediately expect them to **go practice**. Opening page one of Galih's physics notebook signals that Damar is **ready to start studying from his brother's notes**.

> A solid prediction extends the character's closing action and new motivation instead of reversing the growth just achieved.

## Reading the closing clue in the final paragraph

The final sentence points straight ahead: <Highlight>Ia mengambil buku catatan fisika lama milik Galih dari lemari, meniup debunya, dan membuka halaman pertama</Highlight> (he took Galih's old physics notebook from the cupboard, blew off the dust, and opened the first page). This happens right after learning **Galih won a provincial physics olympiad**.

The natural next step is that **Damar begins studying his brother's physics notes earnestly**.

## Why the other four continuations fail

- **Throwing the uniform in the trash** clashes with his decision to **cancel his plan for a new shirt**.
- **Transferring schools to hide his identity** reverses his shift from shame to **pride in being Galih's brother**.
- **Pak Sunu telling Damar to buy a new uniform** contradicts Pak Sunu's warm smile and admiration for Galih.
- **Removing Galih's stitched name** ignores that the collar name has become **a source of pride**.

# Answer

The event most likely to follow is that **Damar begins studying his brother's physics notes earnestly**.
