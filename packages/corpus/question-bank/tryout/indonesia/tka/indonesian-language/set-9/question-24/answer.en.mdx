# Given

Clinic testing showed that <InlineMath math="16" /> of <InlineMath math="20" /> refill depots **met safety standards**, while only <InlineMath math="4" /> failed because they **rarely changed filters and did not wash buyers' containers**. On social media, a post claimed **"refill depot water is dangerous and should not be drunk at all"**.

# Asked

The most accurate evaluation of that social media message based on the passage's data.

# Solution

If <InlineMath math="16" /> out of <InlineMath math="20" /> restaurants on a street pass inspection cleanly and only <InlineMath math="4" /> skip washing dishes, declaring **every restaurant unsafe** is clearly **an exaggeration**. Only a minority failed, and proper care fixes the cause.

> Condemning an entire group on the basis of a preventable minority of failures is an overgeneralization unsupported by the evidence.

## Comparing the viral claim with the inspection numbers

Paragraph two reports the exact split: <Highlight>air dari <InlineMath math="16" /> depot memenuhi syarat, sedangkan air dari <InlineMath math="4" /> depot mengandung bakteri melebihi batas</Highlight> (<InlineMath math="16" /> depots met standards while <InlineMath math="4" /> exceeded bacteria limits). Only <InlineMath math="4" /> of <InlineMath math="20" /> failed.

Paragraph three adds that **the issue can be prevented if depots maintain their equipment properly**. Telling residents never to drink depot water at all is therefore **exaggerated**.

## Why the other four evaluations fail

- **Accurate because some depots failed** stretches <InlineMath math="4" /> failures into a blanket verdict over <InlineMath math="16" /> safe depots.
- **Accurate because bottled water costs more** confuses **price** with drinking-water safety.
- **False because every depot passed** ignores the <InlineMath math="4" /> depots that **did fail**.
- **Unrelated to the inspection** ignores that the post reacted directly to the clinic report and drew the clinic head's response.

# Answer

The best evaluation is that **the message is exaggerated because only <InlineMath math="4" /> of <InlineMath math="20" /> depots failed and the issue can be prevented through proper equipment maintenance**.
