# Given

The **refill water depot** passage uses four loanwords: *stiker*, *bakteri*, *galon*, and *ultraviolet*. We must select **all accurate statements explaining their meanings in context**.

# Asked

Every accurate contextual definition among the four loanword statements.

# Solution

To test each loanword definition, picture how the word behaves inside its sentence. If the proposed meaning fits **the exact job the word performs**, keep it. If it swaps the object for something else, cross it out.

> A valid contextual definition slots cleanly into the original sentence without distorting what happens in the passage.

## Testing all four loanwords against their sentences

The passage mentions water **containing *bakteri* above the safety limit**, explaining why those depots failed inspection. Thus ***bakteri* rightly means microscopic living organisms that can cause disease**.

It also writes <Highlight>dimasukkan ke galon pembeli</Highlight> (poured into the buyer's gallon container) and advises checking whether it is **washed and sealed tightly before taking it home**. Thus ***galon* rightly means the large water container buyers carry home**.

By contrast, a **stiker** is an **adhesive inspection label** posted on passing depots rather than a mailed business license, and **ultraviolet** is a **type of germ-killing light**, not filtered water.

## Verdict on all four statements

- ***Stiker* = business license mailed home**: **Incorrect**, as it is a posted inspection sticker at the depot.
- ***Bakteri* = tiny living organisms that can cause disease**: **Correct**, matching why high bacteria fails water safety tests.
- ***Galon* = large container for taking drinking water home**: **Correct**, matching how buyers fill and carry water home.
- ***Ultraviolet* = water filtered in layers**: **Incorrect**, as ultraviolet is a type of lamp light that kills germs.

# Answer

The accurate statements about the loanwords in context are:
- ***Bakteri* means tiny living organisms that can cause disease.**
- ***Galon* means a large container used by buyers to carry drinking water home.**
