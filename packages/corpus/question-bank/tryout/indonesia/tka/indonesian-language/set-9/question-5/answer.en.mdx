# Given

The passage on **soap from used cooking oil** provides four statements to sort into three roles: **Waste oil problem** (*Masalah jelantah*), **Soap-making process** (*Proses pembuatan sabun*), or **Group plan** (*Rencana kelompok*).

# Asked

The correct category for each of the four statements according to its role in the passage.

# Solution

The passage unfolds like three neat boxes: **paragraph one** explains the waste-oil problem, **paragraph two** walks through the soap-making process, and **paragraph four** announces future plans. Matching each statement to its home paragraph sorts all four immediately.

> Identifying each paragraph's structural role first prevents confusing an existing production step with a future plan.

## Matching the four statements to each paragraph

Paragraph one introduces the **problem of dumping oil**: <Highlight>minyak yang mengeras di dalam pipa dapat menyumbat saluran</Highlight> (hardened oil in pipes can clog drains). That places statement one in **Masalah jelantah**.

Paragraph two details the **production steps**, covering both soaking oil with charcoal for a day and letting molded soap cure for four weeks. Statements two and four therefore belong to **Proses pembuatan sabun**.

Paragraph four signals future intent with <Highlight>Kelompok itu berencana memasang jeriken penampung jelantah di setiap RT</Highlight> (the group plans to place collection jerrycans in every neighborhood unit). Statement three therefore fits **Rencana kelompok**.

## Summary table of all four statements

| Statement | Category | Textual basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardened oil clogs pipes | **Masalah jelantah** | Harm of oil disposal in paragraph one |
| Soaked with charcoal for a day | **Proses pembuatan sabun** | Odor-reduction step in paragraph two |
| Jerrycans placed in every RT | **Rencana kelompok** | Marked by *berencana* (plans) in paragraph four |
| Soap left for four weeks | **Proses pembuatan sabun** | Final curing stage in paragraph two |

# Answer

The correct mapping is:
1. Hardened oil clogging pipes -> **Masalah jelantah** (Waste oil problem)
2. Soaking waste oil with charcoal for a day -> **Proses pembuatan sabun** (Soap-making process)
3. Installing collection jerrycans in every RT -> **Rencana kelompok** (Group plan)
4. Letting the soap sit for about four weeks -> **Proses pembuatan sabun** (Soap-making process)
