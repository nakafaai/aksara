# Given

During break time, **Pak Sunu smiles** upon noticing Galih's stitched name on Damar's collar and says, **"Kakakmu dulu juara olimpiade fisika provinsi. Noda tinta itu pasti dari malam-malam ia belajar rumus"** (your brother won the provincial physics olympiad, so that ink stain surely came from nights spent studying formulas). Damar then remembers Galih falling asleep at his study desk still holding a pen.

# Asked

The most fitting emotional response to Pak Sunu's remark about the ink stain on Damar's uniform.

# Solution

Imagine feeling ashamed of scuffs on your older sibling's running shoes, only for a coach to smile and call them **proof of winning a championship**. Shame instantly melts into **feeling deeply moved** once a flaw becomes a badge of hard work.

> An appropriate emotional response matches both the speaker's warm tone and the uplifting reinterpretation offered in the scene.

## Reading the tone and meaning of the remark

Pak Sunu speaks gently with a smile and reframes the mark: <Highlight>Noda tinta itu pasti dari malam-malam ia belajar rumus</Highlight> (that ink stain must be from late nights studying formulas). What had been **Damar's source of shame** turns into **a visible sign of his brother's dedication**.

Damar's fond memory of Galih falling asleep with a pen in hand confirms that the fitting response is **feeling moved**.

## Why the other four responses fail

- **Annoyed for exposing a secret before the class** fails because Pak Sunu speaks **kindly during break**, not to humiliate him publicly.
- **Amused at a misread name** fails because Pak Sunu reads *Galih Pratama* **accurately**.
- **Afraid of punishment** fails because Pak Sunu **smiles and praises** Galih with zero hint of discipline.
- **Jealous of Galih's intelligence** fails because Damar feels inspired to study rather than envious.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is **feeling moved because Pak Sunu turns an embarrassing stain into a sign of hard work**.
