# Given

In the **lost cat** story, Bu Wening lives alone and calls out for Puspa every afternoon **in a voice growing quieter each day**. When Nayla brings Puspa home with **three kittens** in a box, Bu Wening stays quiet for a long moment, strokes Puspa's head, and then **laughs while crying**.

# Asked

The most fitting emotional response to Bu Wening laughing while crying.

# Solution

Think of losing a beloved pet for days and suddenly getting it back safe alongside a tiny surprise family. Pent-up worry breaks open into **tears of relief mixed with joyful laughter**, naturally making the reader **feel moved alongside her**.

> Laughing through tears after anxious waiting signals sorrow suddenly giving way to overwhelming relief and unexpected joy.

## Reading the emotional contrast before and after Puspa returns

For three days, Bu Wening suffered lonely worry as she **lived alone** and called Puspa in an **ever softer voice**. Then comes the joyful twist: <Highlight>Ia mengelus kepala Puspa, lalu menatap tiga anak kucing itu dan tertawa sambil menangis</Highlight> (she stroked Puspa's head, gazed at the three kittens, and laughed while crying).

That blend of laughter and tears shows **grief transforming into unexpected happiness**, inviting the reader to **feel deeply moved**.

## Why the other four responses fail

- **Confused because Bu Wening is angry** contradicts her gentle petting and understanding words.
- **Annoyed that she does not thank Nayla** misreads **speechless emotion** as ingratitude.
- **Afraid the kittens will be abandoned** clashes with her tenderness and Nayla's hope to adopt one.
- **Amused that she mistook another cat** is false since she immediately recognizes and strokes Puspa.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is **feeling moved alongside her because Bu Wening's sadness turns into unexpected happiness**.
