# Given

When Raras falls twice and scrapes her knee in paragraph four, Arga **washes her scrape with water from his bottle without a word**, then **lifts the bicycle and holds the saddle again** so she can keep practicing.

# Asked

The reader's most fitting emotional response to Arga's behavior in that moment.

# Solution

Imagine falling while learning to skate, and your older brother quietly rinses your knee without scolding or laughing, then steadies your hand so you can try again. Calm care paired with quiet faith naturally inspires **admiration**.

> A reader's emotional response must match the character's actual deeds and tone in the scene.

## Examining the actions of Arga in paragraph four

The story highlights two steady gestures: <Highlight>Arga membersihkan lukanya dengan air dari botol minum tanpa berkata apa-apa</Highlight> (Arga rinsed her scrape silently with bottle water) and <Highlight>Arga hanya mengangkat sepeda dan memegang sadelnya lagi</Highlight> (he simply lifted the bike and held the saddle again). He tends to her injury while keeping her courage alive.

Therefore, the fitting response is **admiration because Arga calmly cares for Raras's scrape and keeps guiding her practice**.

## Why the other four feelings fail

- **Annoyed at indifference** ignores his immediate care for her knee.
- **Amused at laughter** invents mocking that never happens.
- **Afraid of coercion** misreads silent readiness as force.
- **Sad over scolding** directly contradicts *tanpa berkata apa-apa* (without saying a word).

# Answer

The most fitting response is **admiration because Arga calmly cleans Raras's scrape and continues supporting her practice**.
