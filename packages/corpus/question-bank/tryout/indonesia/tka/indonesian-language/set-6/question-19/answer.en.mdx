# Given

In the final paragraph, Bu Lastri slips in a friendly note in big letters: **"Next time write the number in words too, okay. Eighty-five."** Tika **smiles and sticks the note onto her wardrobe door**.

# Asked

The reader's most fitting emotional response to Tika taping Bu Lastri's note onto her wardrobe door.

# Solution

Imagine getting a warm, funny reminder slip from a kind mentor, smiling, and taping it where you see it every day. Seeing friendly advice welcomed so warmly naturally makes the reader **feel glad along with her**.

> A fitting emotional response mirrors the scene's warm mood shown through the character's smile and appreciative action.

## Reading the reaction of Tika in paragraph five

The closing line makes her delight unmistakable: <Highlight>Tika tersenyum dan menempelkan kertas itu di pintu lemarinya</Highlight> (Tika smiled and taped the paper onto her wardrobe door). Placing <Highlight>Lain kali tulis angkanya pakai huruf juga, ya</Highlight> in plain sight shows she happily takes the tip to heart.

Therefore, the fitting response is **feeling happy alongside her because she gladly accepts the advice and wants to remember it**.

## Why the other four feelings miss

- **Upset over humiliation** contradicts her bright smile.
- **Sad and afraid to return** has zero basis in the story.
- **Worried the trousers are still too long** ignores that the hem already fits neatly at her ankles.
- **Puzzled by irrelevance** misses that writing numbers in words directly prevents another misread digit.

# Answer

The most fitting response is **feeling glad along with Tika because she gladly accepts the advice and wants to keep remembering it**.
