# Given

At eleven years old, Raras feels **too embarrassed to admit she cannot ride** a bicycle and always claims she prefers walking. In paragraph two, Arga takes deliberate steps to shield her from onlookers while she starts learning.

# Asked

All details showing how Arga prevents Raras from feeling embarrassed during practice.

# Solution

Imagine feeling shy about practicing a speech. The two things that melt embarrassment right away are **picking an empty room** and **promising zero audience**. Separate shame-shielding steps from riding mechanics or first aid.

> In multi-select evidence items, keep only the details that directly address the target problem of being watched.

## Checking all four details against the embarrassment

- **Taking her to a quiet morning volleyball court** is **correct**, matching paragraph two: <Highlight>lapangan voli di belakang masjid yang selalu sepi pada pagi hari</Highlight> (a volleyball court behind the mosque that is always empty in the morning).
- **Saying there are no spectators either** is **correct**, matching Arga's reassurance: <Highlight>tapi juga tidak ada penonton</Highlight> (and there are no spectators either).
- **Raising the saddle slightly the next week** is **incorrect** because that is a pedaling step in paragraph four.
- **Cleaning her knee with bottle water** is **incorrect** because that is post-fall care rather than avoiding embarrassment.

## Confirming the two matching details

Both **choosing an empty morning court** and **assuring her there are no spectators** directly remove the fear of being watched.

# Answer

The two correct details are that **Arga brings Raras to a volleyball court that is always empty in the morning** and **tells her there are no spectators either**.
