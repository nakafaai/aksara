# Given

Paragraph three's footage shows **two opposite trends**: red-light runners fell from <InlineMath math="46" /> to <InlineMath math="31" />, yet late-green speeders climbed from <InlineMath math="20" /> to <InlineMath math="34" />. Paragraph four summarizes those opposing effects, then uses **Karena itu** (because of that) before stating that the office holds off on citywide rollout and tests again.

# Asked

The relationship between paragraph three's recording data and paragraph four's policy decision.

# Solution

Imagine a medicine test where fever drops yet drowsiness spikes. **Because the outcomes pull in opposite directions**, doctors pause mass release and run another trial. That direct **cause-and-effect bridge** is signaled explicitly by *Karena itu*.

> A causal connector like *karena itu* (therefore) right after summarizing test results proves that the subsequent decision flows directly from those mixed findings.

## Tracing cause and effect from paragraph three to four

Paragraph four names the cause: <Highlight>Hasil itu menunjukkan bahwa satu alat dapat menimbulkan dua efek yang berlawanan</Highlight> (the results show one device creates two opposite effects). Then comes the consequence: <Highlight>Karena itu, dinas tidak langsung memasang penghitung di semua persimpangan</Highlight> (therefore, the office does not immediately install timers everywhere) and tests another crossing.

Hence, **because the data reveals opposite effects, the office chooses to test further before rolling the timers out everywhere**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Immediate citywide installation** directly reverses *tidak langsung memasang* (does not immediately install).
- **Uninfluenced by the data** contradicts *Hasil itu* and *Karena itu*.
- **All violations rising** ignores red-light running dropping from <InlineMath math="46" /> to <InlineMath math="31" />.
- **Stopping due to broken cameras** contradicts paragraph four, which installs new cameras.

# Answer

The link is that **because the data shows opposite effects, the office chooses to test further before installing the timers at every intersection**.
