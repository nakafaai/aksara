# Given

Transport staff in Kota Purwaraja filmed **one intersection** for **two weeks before and two weeks after** installation (four weeks total). The data revealed two real but conflicting shifts, prompting tests at a busier pedestrian crossing before wider rollout.

# Asked

The fairest evaluation of the transport office's recording data.

# Solution

Imagine tasting **one spoonful from a single pot**: it gives real, useful information about that pot, yet it cannot guarantee every pot in the kitchen tastes the same. Likewise, four weeks at one crossing is **useful, yet too narrow to generalize to every intersection**.

> Balanced evaluation of empirical data acknowledges what the sample genuinely reveals while respecting its geographic and time limits.

## Weighing both the value and the scope limit of the data

The footage is genuinely informative, documenting two real shifts: <Highlight>turun dari rata-rata <InlineMath math="46" /> menjadi <InlineMath math="31" /> per hari</Highlight> (down from <InlineMath math="46" /> to <InlineMath math="31" />) alongside <Highlight>naik dari <InlineMath math="20" /> menjadi <InlineMath math="34" /> per hari</Highlight> (up from <InlineMath math="20" /> to <InlineMath math="34" />). Yet the sample covers only <Highlight>satu persimpangan selama dua minggu sebelum dan dua minggu sesudah</Highlight> (one junction for <InlineMath math="2 + 2 = 4" /> weeks).

Thus, **the data is useful, yet coming from just one intersection over four weeks, it cannot yet apply to every intersection**.

## Why the other four judgments fail

- **Proving roads are safer** overlooks the conflicting surge in late-green speeding.
- **Completely useless** goes too far since officials actively use it to plan the next trial.
- **All drivers speeding** inflates a daily average of <InlineMath math="34" /> into everyone.
- **Proving pedestrian safety** fails because pedestrian-heavy junctions have not been tested yet.

# Answer

The fairest judgment is that **the data is useful, yet because it covers only one intersection for four weeks, it cannot yet generalize to all intersections**.
