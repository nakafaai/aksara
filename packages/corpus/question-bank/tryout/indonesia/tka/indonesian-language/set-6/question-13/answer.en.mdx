# Given

The **Student Eye Screening** passage organizes its details into **three sequential parts**: doctor screening results (paragraph two), reasons glasses remain unused (paragraph three), and follow-up actions by the clinic, foundation, and teachers (paragraph four).

# Asked

How to assign four statements to **Hasil pemeriksaan** (screening result), **Penyebab kacamata belum dipakai** (reason glasses are unused), or **Tindak lanjut** (follow-up).

# Solution

Picture a **school clinic card**: what the doctor finds is the *screening result*, why students skip wearing glasses is the *cause*, and handing out free glasses afterward is the *follow-up*. Match each line to its source paragraph.

> Paragraph sequence and signposts such as *alasan* (reasons) or *menanggapi temuan* (responding to findings) lock each statement into its exact column.

## Mapping all four statements to their paragraphs

Paragraph two reports the clinical finding: <Highlight>Setelah diperiksa dokter, <InlineMath math="120" /> di antaranya membutuhkan kacamata</Highlight> (<InlineMath math="120" /> need glasses). Paragraph three lists the barriers: <Highlight>menganggap harga kacamata terlalu mahal</Highlight> (cost seen as too high) and <Highlight>malu diejek teman</Highlight> (ashamed of teasing).

Paragraph four marks the response: <Highlight>Untuk menanggapi temuan itu, puskesmas bekerja sama dengan sebuah yayasan untuk memberikan kacamata gratis</Highlight> (a foundation provides free glasses).

The <InlineMath math="120" /> figure is **Hasil pemeriksaan**, both teasing and cost barriers are **Penyebab kacamata belum dipakai**, and free glasses are **Tindak lanjut**.
