# Given

Paragraph three reports helmet use climbing to **<InlineMath math="700" /> of <InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> children**, a community leader claiming the program saves lives, and a health worker countering with **Namun** (however) that observers only counted helmets worn rather than accidents or injuries.

# Asked

The main idea capturing the entire third paragraph.

# Solution

Imagine an umbrella seller cheering, **"Sales doubled, so nobody got rained on!"**, while a nurse reminds everyone, **"We only counted umbrellas carried, not people staying dry."** The complete **main idea** joins both halves: helmet wear rose, yet actual safety impact remains unmeasured.

> When a paragraph pivots on a contrast marker like *namun* (however), the main idea must unite both the gain and the limitation.

## Uniting the rising count with the caution

The opening reports higher use: <Highlight><InlineMath math="700" /> dari <InlineMath math="1{.}200" /> anak yang dibonceng memakai helm</Highlight> (<InlineMath math="700" /> of <InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> children wore helmets). Then the health worker sets the evidence limit: <Highlight>pengamatan itu hanya menghitung helm yang dipakai, bukan jumlah kecelakaan atau cedera</Highlight> (the count tracked only helmets worn, not accidents or injuries).

Therefore, the main idea is that **helmet use increased, yet the data does not yet show fewer accidents or injuries**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Proven to save lives** repeats the exact claim questioned two lines later.
- **Rejecting the loan program** misreads a methodological caution as opposition.
- **More children riding motorcycles** reverses the drop from <InlineMath math="1{,}250" /> to <InlineMath math="1{,}200" />.
- **All chin straps fastened** contradicts the note that some straps remained loose.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph three is that **helmet use rose, but the data does not yet show a drop in accidents or injuries**.
