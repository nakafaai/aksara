# Given

The phrase *sebuah komunitas relawan keselamatan jalan* (a road-safety volunteer community) appears in paragraph two for **road-safety volunteers** who observe eight elementary school gates, partner with schools, and plan follow-up checks.

# Asked

The contextual meaning of the loanword *komunitas* in the passage.

# Solution

Picture neighbors who **share a passion for clean rivers** and team up every weekend without being a government office or factory. A group of people united by a shared purpose or interest is exactly what **komunitas** means.

> Context clues beside a loanword and the actions tied to it lock down its exact meaning.

## Reading the clues around komunitas

The text pairs the word directly with *relawan* (volunteers): <Highlight>Sebuah komunitas relawan keselamatan jalan mengamati gerbang delapan SD</Highlight> (a community of road-safety volunteers observed eight primary school gates) and <Highlight>bekerja sama dengan sekolah</Highlight> (partnered with schools). These are citizens organized around a shared mission.

Thus, *komunitas* means **a group of people gathered around a shared goal or interest**.

## Why the other four meanings fail

- **Government road office** clashes with *relawan* (volunteers).
- **Elementary schools** are the partners they collaborate with, not the community itself.
- **Helmet factories** and **parent meetings** never appear in the passage.

# Answer

In context, *komunitas* means **a group of people brought together by a shared goal or interest**.
