# Given

In paragraph three, the health worker cautions that observations **counted only helmets worn, not accidents or injuries**. In paragraph four, the community plans to request **child accident data from local police** so the program's impact can be judged more accurately.

# Asked

The logical link between the plan to request police accident data and the health worker's note.

# Solution

Imagine a coach saying, **"You timed how fast players laced their shoes, not how fast they ran the lap."** The next day you bring a stopwatch for the track. Your new step directly **fills the gap** the coach pointed out.

> When a closing plan adds the exact data type flagged as missing earlier, the plan serves as a direct response to that critique.

## Linking paragraph three to paragraph four

The health worker flags the missing metric: <Highlight>hanya menghitung helm yang dipakai, bukan jumlah kecelakaan atau cedera</Highlight> (only counted helmets worn, not accidents or injuries). Paragraph four fills that exact gap: <Highlight>meminta data kecelakaan anak dari kepolisian setempat agar dampak program dapat dinilai lebih tepat</Highlight> (requesting child accident data from local police to assess impact accurately).

Hence, **the plan answers the note that observations had not yet measured accidents or injuries**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Proving the health worker wrong** flips acceptance into denial.
- **Adding more helmets at schools** is unrelated to police accident statistics.
- **Parents refusing helmets** is never linked to requesting police data.
- **Replacing chin-strap checks** contradicts paragraph four, which explicitly adds strap checks in August too.

# Answer

The link is that **the plan directly answers the health worker's note that observations had not yet measured accidents or injuries**.
