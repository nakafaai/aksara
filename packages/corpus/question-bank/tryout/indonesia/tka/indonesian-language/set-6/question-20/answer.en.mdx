# Given

Bu Lastri folds the extra ten centimeters inward and tells Tika: **"If you grow taller next year, we just open the stitching."** Tika realizes those extra ten centimeters are now **a reserve for next year**.

# Asked

The event most likely to happen one year later.

# Solution

Imagine keeping a built-in **spare hem** tucked inside your uniform specifically for next year's growth spurt. When you grow taller a year later, the most natural **next step** is taking the trousers back to Bu Lastri to let the hem down.

> Long-range predictions in fiction follow the exact preparation the characters intentionally put in place earlier.

## Connecting the tailor plan with the growth reserve

Paragraph four sets up the future scene explicitly: <Highlight>Kalau tahun depan kamu bertambah tinggi, jahitannya tinggal dibuka</Highlight> (if you grow taller next year, the seam can simply be opened), and Tika notes <Highlight>kelebihan sepuluh sentimeter itu kini menjadi cadangan untuk tahun depan</Highlight> (the extra ten centimeters are now reserve for next year).

Therefore, a year later **Tika will bring the trousers back to Bu Lastri to let out the hem after growing taller**.

## Why the other four continuations fail

- **Throwing the trousers away** ignores the ten-centimeter reserve saved precisely for growth.
- **Writing only slanted digits again** contradicts Tika taping the reminder note onto her wardrobe door.
- **Bu Lastri refusing orders** and **Tika leaving in disappointment** clash with their warm, smiling relationship.

# Answer

A year later, the most likely event is that **Tika brings the trousers to Bu Lastri to let out the hem once she grows taller**.
