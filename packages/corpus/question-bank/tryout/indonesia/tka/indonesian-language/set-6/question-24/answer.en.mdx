# Given

The **Countdown Traffic Lights** passage separates views cleanly by paragraph: **supporters' arguments** in paragraph two, **opponents' concerns** in paragraph three, and **the transport office's actions** in paragraph four.

# Asked

How to assign four statements to **Pendapat pendukung** (supporters' view), **Pendapat penentang** (opponents' view), or **Langkah dinas** (office's step).

# Solution

Picture a classroom debate board: benefits go under **supporters**, safety worries go under **opponents**, and the official trial decision goes under **office steps**. Each paragraph opener tells you the exact column.

> Attribution openers such as *pendukung berpendapat* (supporters argue), *penentangnya* (opponents), and *dinas memilih* (the office chooses) map each statement unambiguously.

## Mapping all four statements to their paragraphs

Paragraph two lists two pro points: <Highlight>Pendukung penghitung waktu mundur berpendapat bahwa pengendara menjadi lebih sabar</Highlight> (drivers grow more patient) and <Highlight>antrean bergerak lebih cepat</Highlight> (queues move faster). Paragraph three states the counter-worry: <Highlight>sebagian pengendara justru mempercepat laju agar tidak tertahan</Highlight> (some drivers speed up near the end of green).

Paragraph four gives the official step: <Highlight>Dinas memilih menguji alat itu di persimpangan lain yang lebih ramai pejalan kaki</Highlight> (testing at a busier pedestrian intersection).

| Statement | Category | Paragraph evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drivers grow more patient knowing the wait | Pendapat pendukung | First supporter claim in paragraph two. |
| Some drivers speed up as green nearly ends | Pendapat penentang | Opponent concern in paragraph three. |
| Device tested at a busier pedestrian crossing | Langkah dinas | Follow-up decision by the office in paragraph four. |
| Queues move faster as drivers prepare to go | Pendapat pendukung | Second supporter claim in paragraph two. |

## Confirming all four placements

Both patience and faster queues belong to **Pendapat pendukung**, late-green speeding belongs to **Pendapat penentang**, and pedestrian-junction testing belongs to **Langkah dinas**.

# Answer

More patient drivers and faster-moving queues belong to **Pendapat pendukung**, speeding up as green ends belongs to **Pendapat penentang**, and testing at a busier pedestrian intersection belongs to **Langkah dinas**.
