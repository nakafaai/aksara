# Given

Paragraph one notes that many students struggling to read the board **do not realize their vision is impaired** and are mislabeled as lazy. Paragraph two reports screening <InlineMath math="900" /> middle schoolers: **<InlineMath math="120" /> need glasses**, yet only <InlineMath math="45" /> already own them.

# Asked

The relationship between paragraph one's general statement and paragraph two's screening data.

# Solution

Imagine a teacher suspecting sleepy students are not lazy but short on sleep, followed by a school survey showing dozens stay up past midnight. The second paragraph **backs up the opening observation with concrete numbers**.

> When paragraph one states a general pattern and paragraph two supplies matching field statistics, the second paragraph substantiates the first with evidence.

## Matching the opening claim to the screening numbers

Paragraph one introduces the hidden issue: <Highlight>Banyak siswa yang kesulitan membaca tulisan di papan tidak menyadari bahwa penglihatannya bermasalah</Highlight> (many students struggling with the board do not realize they have vision problems). Paragraph two confirms it: out of <InlineMath math="900" /> students, <Highlight><InlineMath math="120" /> di antaranya membutuhkan kacamata. Namun, baru <InlineMath math="45" /> siswa yang sudah memilikinya</Highlight> (<InlineMath math="120" /> need glasses, yet only <InlineMath math="45" /> own them, leaving <InlineMath math="75" /> untreated).

Thus, **the screening results show that unnoticed vision problems really do affect many students**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Proving students are lazy** flips the point, since vision trouble explains copying errors.
- **Refuting board-reading difficulty** misreads supporting data as contradiction.
- **All students needing glasses** overstates <InlineMath math="120" /> out of <InlineMath math="900" />.
- **Explaining how glasses are made** never appears in the text.

# Answer

The link is that **the screening results show that often unnoticed vision problems truly affect many students**.
