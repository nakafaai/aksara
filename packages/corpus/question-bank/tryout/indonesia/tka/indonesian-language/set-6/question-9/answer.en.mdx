# Given

Paragraph three describes Arga's teaching sequence: he **does not ask Raras to pedal right away**, lowers the saddle so her feet touch the ground, has her glide by pushing off, and explains, **"Learn balance first. Pedaling comes later."**

# Asked

The main idea summarizing paragraph three as a whole.

# Solution

Picture a swimming coach teaching you to **float calmly before swinging your arms**. Lowering the saddle, gliding like a scooter, and coasting a few meters all serve one central **main idea**: mastering balance first before pedaling.

> A narrative paragraph's main idea captures the core action or method unifying every supporting detail inside it.

## Connecting every sentence in paragraph three

The opening line sets the method: <Highlight>Arga tidak langsung menyuruh Raras mengayuh</Highlight> (Arga did not tell Raras to pedal right away). His own rule sums it up: <Highlight>Belajar seimbang dulu. Mengayuh belakangan</Highlight> (learn to balance first, pedal later), leading to her gliding without putting her feet down.

Hence, the main idea is that **Arga trains Raras's balance first before teaching her to pedal**.

## Why the other four options miss

- **Bicycle too small** misreads why she felt silly gliding like a little kid.
- **Making the bike go faster** flips the true reason for lowering the saddle so her feet could touch down.
- **Riding a scooter for half an hour** takes a simile literally.
- **Pedaling without falling on day one** contradicts paragraph four, where pedaling begins the next week with two falls.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph three is that **Arga trains Raras to balance first before teaching her to pedal**.
