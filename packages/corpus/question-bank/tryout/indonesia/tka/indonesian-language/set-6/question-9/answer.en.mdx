Paragraph three opens with *Arga tidak langsung menyuruh Raras mengayuh* (Arga didn't make Raras pedal right away). He lowers the saddle so her feet touch the ground, has her glide by pushing off with both feet, and says *Belajar seimbang dulu. Mengayuh belakangan* (balance first, pedaling later). As a result, she can glide without putting her feet down. The idea tying it all together is that **Arga trains her balance first** before teaching her to pedal.

- Raras feels silly because she's like a little kid on a scooter, not because the bike is too small.
- The saddle goes down so her feet reach the ground, not to make the bike faster.
- The scooter is only a comparison for how she feels. She doesn't actually ride one.
- She doesn't pedal at all in this paragraph. She starts the next week and falls twice.
