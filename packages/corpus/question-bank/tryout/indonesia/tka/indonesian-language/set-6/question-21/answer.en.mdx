# Given

The **Countdown Traffic Lights** passage moves from an ongoing safety debate (paragraph one) through supporters' claims (paragraph two), opponents' concerns plus mixed data from Kota Purwaraja (paragraph three), to the transport office deciding on further testing due to two opposite effects (paragraph four).

# Asked

The main idea covering the entire passage.

# Solution

Picture a **countdown clock at an intersection**: fewer drivers jump red lights, yet more drivers gun the throttle as green ticks down. Because one device reduces one violation while sparking a new risk, officials hold off and test further. That full arc is the **main idea**.

> A whole-passage main idea must weave together both contrasting findings and the resulting policy decision rather than spotlighting only one side.

## Synthesizing all four paragraphs into one main idea

Paragraph three shows opposing shifts: red-light runners dropped from <InlineMath math="46" /> to <InlineMath math="31" />, while late-green speeders climbed from <InlineMath math="20" /> to <InlineMath math="34" />. Paragraph four sums up the takeaway: <Highlight>satu alat dapat menimbulkan dua efek yang berlawanan</Highlight> (one device can produce two opposite effects), so <Highlight>dinas tidak langsung memasang penghitung di semua persimpangan</Highlight> (the office does not install them everywhere immediately) and runs more tests.

Hence, the main idea is that **countdown timers can curb one violation yet trigger another risky behavior, so further testing is needed**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **All drivers becoming calmer** captures only supporters' hopes and ignores surging late-green speeding.
- **Removing traffic lights** is never proposed anywhere.
- **More red-light runners** reverses the drop from <InlineMath math="46" /> to <InlineMath math="31" />.
- **Camera prices** are never mentioned in the passage.

# Answer

The main idea of the passage is that **countdown timers can reduce one kind of violation while triggering another risky behavior, so further testing is required**.
