# Given

The passage on the **Child Helmet Loan Program** unfolds across **three clear stages**: the baseline February count (early paragraph two), the helmet-loan rollout (late paragraph two), and the upcoming August follow-up (paragraph four).

# Asked

How to assign four statements to **Keadaan awal** (initial state), **Pelaksanaan program** (program implementation), or **Rencana berikutnya** (next plan).

# Solution

Picture a **school cleanup board**: the messy room before work begins is the *initial state*, sweeping and placing bins is *program implementation*, and next month's inspection is the *next plan*. Every fact slots cleanly into one stage.

> In text-role tables, separate baseline observations before action, active program measures, and future steps marked by planning verbs.

## Matching all four statements to their source lines

Paragraph two sets the baseline before the program: <Highlight>Dari <InlineMath math="1{.}250" /> anak yang dibonceng sepeda motor, hanya <InlineMath math="300" /> yang memakai helm</Highlight> (only <InlineMath math="300" /> of <InlineMath math="1{,}250" /> children wore helmets). It then lists two program actions: <Highlight>rak berisi helm anak berbagai ukuran yang dapat dipinjam</Highlight> (borrowable helmet racks) and <Highlight>Guru juga mengajarkan cara mengencangkan tali helm</Highlight> (teachers showing how to fasten chin straps).

Paragraph four marks a future step: <Highlight>Mereka juga akan meminta data kecelakaan anak dari kepolisian setempat</Highlight> (they will also request child accident data from local police).

| Statement | Category | Passage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schools provide borrowable child helmet racks | Pelaksanaan program | Core part of the loan rollout in paragraph two. |
| Only <InlineMath math="300" /> of <InlineMath math="1{,}250" /> children wore helmets | Keadaan awal | February baseline count before the program started. |
| Teachers show how to fasten chin straps | Pelaksanaan program | School training activity inside paragraph two. |
| Accident data will be requested from police | Rencana berikutnya | Future plan marked by *akan* in paragraph four. |

## Checking each column assignment

Both active school measures belong to **Pelaksanaan program**, the February figure is **Keadaan awal**, and the police data request is **Rencana berikutnya**.

# Answer

Borrowable helmet racks and chin-strap lessons belong to **Pelaksanaan program**, <InlineMath math="300" /> of <InlineMath math="1{,}250" /> children belongs to **Keadaan awal**, and requesting police accident data belongs to **Rencana berikutnya**.
