# Given

Paragraph three of **Student Eye Screening** opens with **Petugas menemukan beberapa alasan** (officers found several reasons), then lists three factors: parents did not know, glasses seemed too expensive, and students feared teasing.

# Asked

The main idea summarizing paragraph three as a whole.

# Solution

Picture a list titled **"Why bikes stay parked"**, followed by three points: flat tire, rain, and no lock. Every bullet supports one umbrella **main idea**: there are multiple reasons students who need glasses are not wearing them yet.

> In a deductive paragraph, the opening topic sentence announces the main idea while the following sentences unpack its examples.

## Reading the topic sentence and its three supporting reasons

The opening line states the frame: <Highlight>Petugas menemukan beberapa alasan</Highlight> (officers found several reasons). The next sentences list all three: <Highlight>Sebagian orang tua tidak tahu</Highlight> (some parents did not know), <Highlight>harga kacamata terlalu mahal</Highlight> (cost seen as too high), and <Highlight>malu diejek teman</Highlight> (ashamed of teasing).

Therefore, the main idea is that **there are several reasons why students who need glasses are not yet wearing them**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **All parents refusing** overgeneralizes *sebagian* (some) parents and drops the other factors.
- **Glasses always causing teasing** exaggerates one factor into a universal rule.
- **Doctor errors** never appear in the passage.
- **Sitting in the back row** contradicts paragraph four's recommendation to seat them in front.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph three is that **there are several reasons why students who need glasses have not yet worn them**.
