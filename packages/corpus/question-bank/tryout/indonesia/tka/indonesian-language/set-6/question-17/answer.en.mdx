The evidence has to be about **the future**. Bu Lastri says, *Kalau kupotong, kainnya tidak bisa kembali ... Kalau tahun depan kamu bertambah tinggi, jahitannya tinggal dibuka* (if I cut it, the fabric can't come back ... if you grow taller next year, we just undo the seam). She deliberately **folds the fabric in instead of cutting it**, so the trousers can be lengthened later. That's the strongest evidence.
