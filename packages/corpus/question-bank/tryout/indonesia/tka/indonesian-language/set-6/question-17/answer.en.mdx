# Given

Instead of cutting off the extra ten centimeters from Tika's scout trousers, Bu Lastri deliberately **folds the extra fabric inward**, explaining that when Tika grows taller next year, the stitching can simply be let out.

# Asked

The strongest evidence in the story that Bu Lastri thinks ahead about Tika's future needs.

# Solution

Imagine a carpenter leaving a hidden extension leaf inside a table because a child's family will grow. Choosing **not to cut the fabric so the trousers can grow with Tika next year** is the clearest proof of long-term foresight.

> Evidence of forward-looking care must point to a deliberate choice built for a future moment rather than just today's fix.

## Reading the explicit reasoning in paragraph four

Bu Lastri voices her long-term thought directly: <Highlight>Kalau kupotong, kainnya tidak bisa kembali</Highlight> (if I cut it, the cloth cannot come back) and <Highlight>Kalau tahun depan kamu bertambah tinggi, jahitannya tinggal dibuka</Highlight> (if you grow taller next year, we just open the seam).

Thus, the strongest proof is that **Bu Lastri folds the hem inward instead of cutting it so the seam can be let out when Tika grows taller**.

## Why the other four options fall short

- **Working late with the radio** shows diligence on the current order.
- **Rereading the order slip** merely locates how the number was misread.
- **Tapping her forehead and chuckling** shows good humor, not future planning.
- **Placing pins one by one** is a routine sewing motion rather than foresight.

# Answer

The strongest evidence is that **Bu Lastri folds the trouser hem inward rather than cutting it so the stitching can be opened when Tika grows taller**.
