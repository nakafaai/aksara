# Given

In the story on **Training Wheels**, Raras has just pedaled all the way across the field without Arga holding the saddle. On **Monday morning**, as her father waits by the house holding motorcycle keys, Raras **shakes her head gently and wheels the blue bike out through the gate**.

# Asked

The event most likely to happen right after the story ends.

# Solution

Imagine a kid who just learned to tie her shoes yesterday, then shakes her head when Dad reaches down and walks proudly out the door. The clearest **next step** is that she uses her brand-new skill herself.

> Predicting what happens next in fiction depends on the skill a character just mastered plus the concrete cue in the closing lines.

## Reading the two closing clues on Monday morning

Paragraph five confirms her new mastery: <Highlight>ia terus mengayuh sampai ke ujung lapangan</Highlight> (she kept pedaling to the far end of the field).

Paragraph six gives her Monday choice: <Highlight>ayahnya menunggu di depan rumah dengan kunci motor di tangan. Raras menggeleng pelan, lalu menuntun sepeda biru itu keluar dari pagar</Highlight> (her father waited with keys, and Raras shook her head and wheeled the bike out).

Therefore, **Raras will cycle to school without being driven by her father**.

## Why the other four continuations fail

- **Reattaching training wheels** ignores that she already rides unassisted.
- **Father forbidding cycling** never occurs because he simply waits to offer his usual ride.
- **Walking instead** contradicts her wheeling the bike out the gate.
- **Arga selling the bike** clashes with Raras actively riding it to school.

# Answer

The most likely next event is that **Raras rides her bicycle to school without her father driving her**.
