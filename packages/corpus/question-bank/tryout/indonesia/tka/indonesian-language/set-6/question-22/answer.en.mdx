# Given

The passage uses four key terms in clear sentences: **efek** (*dua efek yang berlawanan*), **dinas** (*petugas dinas perhubungan*), **kamera** (*memasang kamera untuk mencatat kecepatan kendaraan*), and **lampu** (*lampu lalu lintas* changing color beside the digits).

# Asked

All statements that accurately define these loanwords in context.

# Solution

Match each word to the exact **job it performs in its sentence**: *efek* names the outcome produced and *kamera* records vehicle speed, while *dinas* is a government department (not a uniform) and *lampu* is a traffic signal light (not paint on asphalt).

> Every loanword definition must fit the exact grammatical and practical role the word plays in its original sentence.

## Testing all four definitions against the passage

- **Efek means an effect or consequence** is **correct**, matching <Highlight>satu alat dapat menimbulkan dua efek yang berlawanan</Highlight> (one device produces two opposite outcomes: fewer red-light runners, more speeders).
- **Kamera means an image-recording device tracking speed** is **correct**, matching <Highlight>memasang kamera untuk mencatat kecepatan kendaraan</Highlight> (installing cameras to record vehicle speeds).
- **Dinas means an officer's uniform** is **incorrect** because <Highlight>petugas dinas perhubungan</Highlight> refers to the government transport department.
- **Lampu means numbers painted on asphalt** is **incorrect** because the lights glow and change color beside the countdown digits.

## Confirming the two accurate definitions

Only the definitions of **efek** and **kamera** match their contextual roles.

# Answer

The two accurate statements are that ***Efek* means an effect or consequence produced by something** and ***Kamera* means an image-recording device used to log vehicle speeds**.
