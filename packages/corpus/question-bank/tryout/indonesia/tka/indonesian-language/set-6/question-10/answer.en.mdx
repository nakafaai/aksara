# Given

Early in the story, Raras feels ashamed of not knowing how to ride and always takes motorcycle rides from her father. At the story's close on Monday morning, when her father waits with his keys, Raras **shakes her head gently and wheels the blue bicycle out through the gate**.

# Asked

The strongest evidence that Raras feels confident riding a bicycle by the end of the story.

# Solution

Imagine someone who used to fear speaking aloud finally **waving off a helper and stepping up to the microphone alone**. Choosing to ride out into real street traffic on a school morning is the clearest **proof of confidence**.

> Evidence of a character's final growth must come from the closing scene and show independent choice rather than early practice steps.

## Reading the closing scene in paragraph six

On Monday morning, the passage records: <Highlight>Raras menggeleng pelan, lalu menuntun sepeda biru itu keluar dari pagar</Highlight> (Raras shook her head and wheeled the blue bike out) while <Highlight>ayahnya menunggu di depan rumah dengan kunci motor di tangan</Highlight> (her father waited with keys).

She is ready for the real road to school, not just an empty court.

Thus, the strongest evidence is that **Raras shakes her head when her father waits with motorcycle keys and wheels her bike out the gate**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Gliding a few meters** belongs to her very first balance drill.
- **Falling twice** shows early persistence rather than end-of-story confidence.
- **Claiming she prefers walking** is her opening excuse to hide embarrassment.
- **Nearly wobbling in surprise** shows startlement on the practice court rather than her final school-day choice.

# Answer

The strongest evidence is that **Raras shakes her head at her father waiting with motorcycle keys and wheels the bicycle out through the gate**.
