# Given

The passage uses four transport loanwords in unmistakable context: **rute** (*rute sepanjang <InlineMath math="18" /> kilometer*), **baterai** (*perlu diganti setelah beberapa tahun*), **halte** (*tidak mengeluarkan asap di halte*), and **terminal** (*mengisi daya di terminal setiap malam*).

# Asked

All statements that accurately define these loanwords in context.

# Solution

Test each definition against **its exact sentence in the passage**: *baterai* stores electricity and *terminal* is where buses depart and charge nightly, whereas *rute* is a travel route (not ticket fare) and *halte* is a passenger stop (not a repair garage).

> Transport loanword meanings are fixed directly by the measurements, actions, and people surrounding them in the sentence.

## Testing all four definitions against the passage

- **Baterai means a power-storage unit needing replacement after several years** is **correct**, matching <Highlight>Baterainya juga perlu diganti setelah beberapa tahun</Highlight>.
- **Terminal means where buses depart and recharge nightly** is **correct**, matching <Highlight>dari terminal ke kawasan industri</Highlight> and <Highlight>mengisi daya di terminal setiap malam</Highlight>.
- **Rute means ticket fare** is **incorrect** because <Highlight>rute sepanjang <InlineMath math="18" /> kilometer</Highlight> measures a route length.
- **Halte means a bus repair shop** is **incorrect** because passengers wait and board at a <Highlight>halte</Highlight> (bus stop).

## Confirming the two accurate definitions

Only the definitions of **baterai** and **terminal** fit the text accurately.

# Answer

The two accurate statements are that ***Baterai* means an electricity-storage device needing replacement after several years** and ***Terminal* means the hub where buses depart and charge each night**.
