The heart of sasi is **a temporary ban**, agreed together, so natural resources can **grow and breed** before being harvested again at buka sasi. The same principle works in daily life, like resting a grass field so it grows back or not picking unripe fruit. So the most applicable principle is giving a resource time to recover.

- Taking as much as possible goes against the purpose of sasi.
- The sasi period is *disepakati bersama* (agreed together) by the villagers.
- Penalties exist to protect the rule.
- The proceeds go to divers' families and the village fund, not all sold to outsiders.
