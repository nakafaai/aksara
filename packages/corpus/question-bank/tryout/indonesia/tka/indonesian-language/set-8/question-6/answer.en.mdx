# Given

In **Lost Vote**, Bagas wins the student-council election with <InlineMath math="312" /> votes to Kirana's <InlineMath math="287" />. Paragraph three depicts the **disappointed mood on Kirana's side** right after the field announcement.

# Asked

All details showing the **mood of disappointment on Kirana's side** after the election results are announced.

# Solution

Picture a losing campaign team returning to class: everyone sits **hushed in disappointment** while someone **tears down the campaign poster**. Keep only details reflecting the losing team's gloom, not the winner's cheers or the warm parking-lot talk afterward.

> For multi-select mood questions, match both the exact group named (Kirana's side) and the exact emotion (disappointment).

## Checking all four details against disappointment on Kirana side

- **Kirana's campaign teammates sat silent in class** is **correct**, matching paragraph three: <Highlight>Di kelas, teman-teman tim kampanyenya diam</Highlight> (her campaign teammates were silent).
- **Someone tore Kirana's portrait poster off the wall** is **correct**, matching the next sentence: <Highlight>Seseorang menyobek poster bergambar wajah Kirana dari dinding dan membuangnya ke tempat sampah</Highlight>.
- **Loud applause from twelfth graders** is **incorrect** because that celebrates Bagas's win.
- **Bagas approaching Kirana at the bike rack** is **incorrect** because that scene lifts the mood and brings new hope.

## Confirming the two matching details

Only **the campaign team's silence in class** and **tearing down Kirana's poster** capture disappointment on her side.

# Answer

The two correct details are that **Kirana's campaign teammates were silent in class** and **someone tore Kirana's portrait poster off the wall**.
