# Given

# Asked

How to assign four statements to **Syarat dan prosedur** (requirements and procedure), **Hasil kegiatan** (activity results), or **Saran petugas** (officer advice).

# Solution

Think of a **blood-drive checklist**: age, weight, and pre-check steps are *requirements and procedure*, total bags collected is the *activity result*, and reminders to sleep well and eat breakfast are *officer advice*.

> Separate pre-event requirements and steps, final numerical outcomes, and forward-looking suggestions to map every row accurately.
