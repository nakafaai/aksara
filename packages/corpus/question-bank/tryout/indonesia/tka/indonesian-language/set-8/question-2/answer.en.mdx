# Given

At the *buka sasi* harvest in Negeri Waimahu, residents gather **lola sea snails much larger than before the ban**. Village elders explain that **the closed period gives the snails time to grow and reproduce**.

# Asked

The relationship between the closed ban period and the size of harvested lola snails.

# Solution

Imagine leaving green mangoes **unpicked on the tree for months**: untouched fruit gets the time it needs to grow full and large. In the exact same way, the two-year ban **directly causes** the snails to grow much bigger before harvest.

> When a passage explicitly explains why an outcome improved, the earlier restriction functions as the direct cause of the better harvest.

## Tracing cause and effect in paragraph three

Paragraph three states the harvest gain: <Highlight>warga memanen lola yang ukurannya jauh lebih besar daripada sebelum sasi</Highlight> (residents harvested lola far larger than before sasi). The next line explains why: <Highlight>masa larangan memberi kesempatan siput tumbuh dan berkembang biak</Highlight> (the ban gives snails a chance to grow and breed).

Therefore, **the ban gives the snails time to grow so the harvested lola are larger**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Large size extending the ban** reverses cause and effect and invents an extension.
- **Making snails smaller** directly contradicts *jauh lebih besar* (much larger).
- **No relationship** clashes with the elders' explicit explanation.
- **Outside boats staying away** reverses paragraph four, where outside boats are a problem rather than the reason snails grew.

# Answer

The link is that **the ban period gives the snails time to grow, making the harvested lola much larger**.
