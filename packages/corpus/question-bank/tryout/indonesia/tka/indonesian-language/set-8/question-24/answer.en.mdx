# Given

Paragraph three opens with **Meski begitu** (even so) right after listing advantages, then enumerates three concrete drawbacks: **nearly double the purchase price**, **costly battery replacement**, and **range dropping by a fifth** on hot days due to air conditioning.

# Asked

The main idea summarizing paragraph three as a whole.

# Solution

When a paragraph starts with **"Even so..."** and lists three downsides one after another (high price, costly battery swap, shorter hot-day range), the **main idea** gathers all three drawbacks into one clear statement.

> In a list-style paragraph detailing drawbacks without a separate summary line, the main idea synthesizes all three limitations together.

## Combining all three drawbacks in paragraph three

All three points appear back-to-back: <Highlight>harga satu bus listrik hampir dua kali lipat harga bus solar</Highlight> (nearly double diesel price), <Highlight>Baterainya juga perlu diganti setelah beberapa tahun dengan biaya besar</Highlight> (costly battery replacement), and <Highlight>pendingin udara yang terus menyala membuat jarak tempuh berkurang sekitar seperlima</Highlight> (AC cuts range by one-fifth).

Hence, **electric buses have drawbacks including high price, battery replacement, and reduced range in hot weather**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Cheaper in every way** quotes the mayor's disputed claim from paragraph four.
- **AC making buses faster** distorts range loss into speed gain.
- **Batteries never needing replacement** directly contradicts the paragraph.
- **Diesel buses costing double** reverses the price comparison.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph three is that **electric buses have drawbacks in high purchase price, battery replacement costs, and shorter range during hot weather**.
