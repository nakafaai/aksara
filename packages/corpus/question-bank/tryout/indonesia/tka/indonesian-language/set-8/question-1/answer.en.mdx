The source word behind *sanksi* can also mean approval, so check the context. The sentence says *warga yang melanggar dikenai sanksi* (villagers who break the rule receive sanksi), then gives examples: **paying a fine** or **cleaning the village road**. Both are penalties. So *sanksi* here means a penalty for breaking the rules.

- The sense of approval doesn't fit, since sanksi is given to people who break rules.
- The ceremony that ends the ban is called *buka sasi*.
- The group of guards is called *kewang*.
