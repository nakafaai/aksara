# Given

When the video call connects, Laras first sees only the shop ceiling. Laras laughs affectionately and says, **"Grandma, the camera is flipped!"** Nenek Sumi taps Arif's two-arrow icon right away, her smiling face appears in her best kebaya, and they chat for nearly an hour.

# Asked

The reader's most fitting emotional response when Laras laughs and says, "Grandma, the camera is flipped!"

# Solution

Picture a first-time video call starting on the ceiling, a granddaughter chuckling warmly, and Grandma smoothly flipping the view before an hour-long chat. A harmless slip solved in seconds inside such warmth naturally makes readers **smile along**.

> A lighthearted minor slip in a loving family moment that is solved right away invites a warm smile rather than anger, worry, or sadness.

## Reading the warm mood in paragraph five

The moment stays affectionate and resolves smoothly: <Highlight>Laras tertawa. Nenek Sumi menekan tombol yang digambar Arif dengan dua panah melingkar. Wajahnya pun muncul di layar</Highlight> (Laras laughed, Nenek Sumi tapped the two-arrow icon and her face appeared), followed by nearly an hour of conversation.

Thus, the fitting response is **smiling along because the tiny slip happens in a warm atmosphere and is fixed immediately**.

## Why the other four feelings fail

- **Angry at mocking** misreads warm affection as cruelty.
- **Sad at failure** ignores the successful hour-long call.
- **Anxious the phone is broken** forgets the camera only needed flipping.
- **Disappointed in Arif** reverses the fact that Arif's drawing immediately fixed the camera.

# Answer

The most fitting response is **smiling along because the small slip occurs in a warm atmosphere and gets resolved right away**.
