# Given

When Bagas offers her the cleanliness coordinator role in paragraph five, Kirana looks at her printed platform and recalls the **genuine motivation** behind the late nights she spent drafting her canteen plan.

# Asked

The strongest evidence that Kirana wrote her program not merely to win the election.

# Solution

Imagine drafting a school-garden plan not to chase votes, but because bare dirt in the yard truly bothered you. The sentence directly stating **her inner reason for writing the plan** is the strongest proof.

> Evidence of a character's deeper motive must cite the stated reason behind creating the idea rather than campaign effort or election numbers.

## Reading inner motivation in paragraph five

Paragraph five states her true motive explicitly: <Highlight>Ia ingat malam-malam ketika menyusun program itu, bukan untuk menang, melainkan karena ia benar-benar kesal melihat sampah di bawah meja kantin</Highlight> (not to win, but because trash under canteen tables genuinely upset her).

Thus, **Kirana remembering she wrote the program because trash under canteen tables genuinely bothered her** is the strongest proof.

## Why the other four options fall short

- **Campaigning for two weeks** shows effort to win votes, not why she cared about the issue.
- **Receiving <InlineMath math="287" /> votes** is simply the vote count.
- **Joining the applause** reflects sportsmanship, not program motive.
- **Gripping her skirt hem** shows nervousness before results.

# Answer

The strongest evidence is that **Kirana remembers drafting the program because she was genuinely upset by trash under the canteen tables**.
