# Given

The **Sea Sasi** passage organizes its facts across **three clear parts**: sasi rules and penalties (paragraphs one and two), *buka sasi* harvest outcomes (paragraph three), and outside challenges with official responses (paragraph four).

# Asked

How to sort four statements into **Aturan sasi** (sasi rules), **Hasil buka sasi** (harvest results), or **Tantangan dan tanggapan** (challenges and response).

# Solution

Think of a **village orchard handbook**: harvest bans and fines belong to *sasi rules*, sharing harvest proceeds belongs to *harvest results*, and midnight intruders belong to *challenges and response*. Each row maps directly to its paragraph.

> Separating rules and sanctions, harvest benefits, and external threats locks every statement into its exact column.

## Mapping all four statements to their paragraphs

Paragraph two lists the rule and sanction: <Highlight>sasi laut untuk siput lola berlangsung dua tahun</Highlight> (a two-year ban) and violators <Highlight>membayar denda kepada negeri</Highlight> (pay fines to the village). Paragraph three states the harvest split: <Highlight>Hasil penjualannya dibagi untuk keluarga penyelam dan untuk kas negeri</Highlight> (shared among diver families and community funds).

Paragraph four gives the threat: <Highlight>Perahu bermotor dari luar daerah kadang datang pada malam hari</Highlight> (outside motorboats arriving at night).

| Statement | Category | Paragraph evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residents banned from harvesting lola for two years | Aturan sasi | Ban duration stated in paragraph two. |
| Sales proceeds split between diver families and village funds | Hasil buka sasi | Harvest distribution in paragraph three. |
| Motorboats from outside arrive at night | Tantangan dan tanggapan | New external challenge in paragraph four. |
| Violators pay fines to the village | Aturan sasi | Customary penalty in paragraph two. |

## Checking all column placements

The two-year ban and fines belong to **Aturan sasi**, sales distribution belongs to **Hasil buka sasi**, and outside motorboats belong to **Tantangan dan tanggapan**.

# Answer

The two-year harvesting ban and fines belong to **Aturan sasi**, sharing sales proceeds belongs to **Hasil buka sasi**, and nighttime outside motorboats belong to **Tantangan dan tanggapan**.
