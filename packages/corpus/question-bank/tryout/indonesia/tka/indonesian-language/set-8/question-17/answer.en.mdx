# Given

When Nenek Sumi taps the two circular arrows to flip the camera in paragraph five, **her face appears onscreen with freshly combed silver hair and her finest kebaya**.

# Asked

The strongest evidence that Nenek Sumi treats the video call as deeply special.

# Solution

Imagine combing your hair neatly and putting on your very best outfit just to greet someone through a phone screen at home. **Dressing in her finest clothes as if for a festive occasion** is the strongest proof of how special the call feels to her.

> Evidence of how deeply a character treasures an occasion shines through deliberate personal care like wearing their finest outfit.

## Reading the telling detail in paragraph five

As soon as the camera flips, the story reveals: <Highlight>dengan uban yang baru disisir dan kebaya terbaiknya</Highlight> (with freshly combed silver hair and her best kebaya). Wearing her best outfit in her own shop shows how precious seeing Laras is.

Thus, the strongest evidence is that **Nenek Sumi combs her silver hair and wears her finest kebaya**.

## Why the other four options fall short

- **Phone gifted by her child** only tells where the phone came from.
- **Arif buying crackers regularly** merely sets up how they know each other.
- **Only knowing the green button** reflects her starting skill gap.
- **A different ringtone** is a technical feature of video calls, not her feelings.

# Answer

The strongest evidence is that **Nenek Sumi combs her silver hair and wears her best kebaya**.
