# Given

Kirana accepts Bagas's offer to be cleanliness coordinator with one clear condition: **"Next week we start right away with sorted trash bins in the canteen."** Bagas laughs warmly in agreement, and Kirana laughs along in relief.

# Asked

The event most likely to happen the following week.

# Solution

Picture two new student officers shaking hands and laughing after agreeing: **"Next week we start with sorted bins!"** The clearest **next week's outcome** is carrying out that exact canteen bin project together.

> Predicting what comes next follows the concrete commitment both characters just agreed to at the story's close.

## Reading the concrete commitment in paragraphs six and seven

Kirana names the immediate first step: <Highlight>Tapi minggu depan kita langsung mulai dengan tempat sampah terpilah di kantin</Highlight> (next week we start immediately with sorted trash bins in the canteen). Bagas jokes warmly (<Highlight>Baru juga diangkat, sudah memberi perintah</Highlight>) and welcomes her initiative.

Therefore, next week **Kirana and the student council will set up sorted trash bins in the canteen**.

## Why the other four continuations fail

- **Kirana resigning** contradicts her eager acceptance and renewed joy.
- **Reposting for a rerun vote** has zero basis in the text.
- **Bagas withdrawing his offer** misreads friendly laughter as rejection.
- **Her team refusing to talk to her** never appears anywhere in the story.

# Answer

Next week, **Kirana and the student council board will most likely install sorted trash bins in the canteen**.
