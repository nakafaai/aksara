# Given

After losing the student-council vote, someone tears Kirana's campaign poster off the wall and drops it in the trash. Without anger, Kirana **picks up the poster, folds it neatly, and puts it inside her bag**.

# Asked

The reader's most fitting emotional response to Kirana picking up, folding, and saving her poster.

# Solution

Imagine pouring two weeks into a project, losing by a hair, finding your poster tossed in the bin, and quietly folding it away with dignity instead of lashing out. That quiet **respect for her own hard work** naturally makes a reader feel **moved**.

> A character's calm dignity in honoring their own effort after defeat evokes empathy and feeling moved rather than anger or amusement.

## Reading quiet dignity in paragraph three

Paragraph three captures her composed grace: <Highlight>Kirana memungut poster itu, melipatnya, lalu memasukkannya ke dalam tas</Highlight> (Kirana picked up the poster, folded it, and placed it in her bag). She cherishes her two weeks of effort without lashing out.

Thus, the fitting response is **feeling moved because Kirana still values her effort even in defeat**.

## Why the other four feelings fail

- **Annoyed at littering** reverses reality since she neatly packs the poster away.
- **Amused by her portrait** clashes with the quiet grief of losing.
- **Afraid of revenge** invents hostility absent from her calm silence.
- **Puzzled about a rerun vote** adds an event never suggested because she accepts the outcome.

# Answer

The most fitting response is **feeling moved because Kirana continues to value her hard work even after losing**.
