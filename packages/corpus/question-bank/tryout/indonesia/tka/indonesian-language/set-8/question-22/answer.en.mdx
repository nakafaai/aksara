**Pasti** (surely) and **dalam segala hal** (in every way) make the mayor's claim very broad. The text shows electric buses do use cheaper energy, but the analyst raises three points: a **purchase price** almost twice as high, costly **battery replacement**, and **four-hour charging** that calls for spare buses. So the claim is overstated.

- Lower energy cost is only one part of the total cost.
- Electric buses actually use less energy money, about Rp1,200 versus Rp4,000 per km.
- Passengers' comfort doesn't prove the bus is cheaper or more practical in every way.
- The claim interprets the trial results, so it's directly related.
