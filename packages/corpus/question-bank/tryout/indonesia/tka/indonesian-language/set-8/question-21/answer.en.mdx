# Given

The **Kota Sentana Electric Bus Trial** passage sorts its facts across **three clear sections**: electric bus advantages (paragraph two), drawbacks introduced by *Meski begitu* (paragraph three), and next year's city plans (paragraph five).

# Asked

How to assign four statements to **Kelebihan bus listrik** (advantages), **Kekurangan bus listrik** (drawbacks), or **Rencana pemerintah kota** (city plan).

# Solution

Think of an **electric-bike comparison sheet**: lower running cost is an *advantage*, double sticker price and shrinking range are *drawbacks*, and building fast chargers next year is a *city plan*.

> Transition cues like *lebih rendah* (lower), *meski begitu* (even so), and *berencana* (plans to) slot every row cleanly.

## Mapping all four statements to their paragraphs

Paragraph two highlights energy savings: <Highlight>biaya energi bus listrik sekitar <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{.}200" /> per kilometer</Highlight> (far cheaper than diesel's <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{.}000" />). Paragraph three lists two drawbacks: <Highlight>harga satu bus listrik hampir dua kali lipat harga bus solar</Highlight> (nearly double the price) and <Highlight>pendingin udara yang terus menyala membuat jarak tempuh berkurang sekitar seperlima</Highlight> (air conditioning cuts range by a fifth).

Paragraph five gives the city's plan: <Highlight>membangun dua titik pengisian cepat di kawasan industri</Highlight> (two fast-charging points in the industrial zone).

| Statement | Category | Paragraph evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase price is nearly double a diesel bus | Kekurangan bus listrik | High upfront cost in paragraph three. |
| Two fast-charging stations built in the industrial zone | Rencana pemerintah kota | Planned expansion in paragraph five. |
| Energy cost is about <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{.}200" /> per kilometer | Kelebihan bus listrik | Lower running cost in paragraph two. |
| Range drops when air conditioning runs continuously | Kekurangan bus listrik | One-fifth range loss on hot days in paragraph three. |

## Checking all column placements

Low energy cost belongs to **Kelebihan bus listrik**, double price and reduced range belong to **Kekurangan bus listrik**, and fast-charging stations belong to **Rencana pemerintah kota**.

# Answer

Energy cost of around <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{.}200" /> per km belongs to **Kelebihan bus listrik**, nearly double price and reduced range belong to **Kekurangan bus listrik**, and building two fast-charging points belongs to **Rencana pemerintah kota**.
