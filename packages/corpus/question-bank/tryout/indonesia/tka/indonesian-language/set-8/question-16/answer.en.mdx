# Given

In **Nenek Sumi's Video Call**, thanks to lessons from fifth-grader Arif, Nenek Sumi enjoys an hour-long video call with her granddaughter Laras. Right afterward, she **sets aside ten crackers in a plastic bag** labeled **"For Teacher Arif."**

# Asked

The event most likely to happen right after the story ends.

# Solution

Imagine packing a bag of snacks and writing **"For Teacher Arif"** right after a friend helped you succeed. The obvious **next moment** is handing Arif that bag of crackers as a heartfelt thank-you gift.

> Predicting what happens next follows the concrete gift or action the character just prepared in the closing sentence.

## Reading the closing clue in paragraph six

The ending points straight ahead: <Highlight>menyisihkan sepuluh kerupuk ke dalam kantong plastik</Highlight> (set aside ten crackers in a plastic bag) and labeled it <Highlight>Untuk Pak Guru Arif</Highlight> (For Teacher Arif).

Thus, **Nenek Sumi will give the bag of crackers to Arif as a thank-you gift**.

## Why the other four continuations fail

- **Selling her phone** ignores her joyful hour-long success.
- **Asking Laras to stop video calls** clashes with her dressing up excitedly to see her granddaughter.
- **Arif demanding high payment** contradicts a spontaneous gift from Nenek Sumi.
- **Throwing away Arif's button diagram** ignores how helpful it just proved to be.

# Answer

The most likely next event is that **Nenek Sumi gives the bag of crackers to Arif as a thank-you gift**.
