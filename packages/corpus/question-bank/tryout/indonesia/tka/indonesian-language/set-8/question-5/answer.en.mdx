# Given

Paragraph four notes a **new challenge from outside motorboats** harvesting marine life at night, where customary sanctions are hard to enforce on non-residents. Consequently, the village government **partners with water police** and **codifies sasi into formal village regulations**.

# Asked

The main idea summarizing paragraph four as a whole.

# Solution

Imagine outsiders sneaking into a protected village orchard at night, prompting village leaders to team up with local officers and put the rules into formal written law. Joining **the outside threat and the formal reinforcement** gives paragraph four's **main idea**.

> In a problem-and-response paragraph, the main idea pairs the emerging challenge with the corrective action introduced by *karena itu* (therefore).

## Combining the outside threat with formal reinforcement

The paragraph first states the threat: <Highlight>Perahu bermotor dari luar daerah kadang datang pada malam hari</Highlight> (outside motorboats come at night) while <Highlight>sanksi adat sulit dikenakan kepada orang yang bukan warga negeri</Highlight> (customary penalties rarely reach non-residents).

Then comes the fix: <Highlight>bekerja sama dengan polisi air dan menuliskan aturan sasi dalam peraturan negeri</Highlight> (working with water police and codifying sasi).

Thus, **sasi faces outside violations, so the village strengthens it with written regulations and water-police cooperation**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Abolishing sasi** reverses the village's move to strengthen it.
- **Local residents violating at night** mistakes outside boats for villagers.
- **Water police banning buka sasi** turns a protective partnership into a prohibition.
- **Repairing houses of worship and schools** belongs to paragraph three.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph four is that **sasi faces violations from outside the area, so the village reinforces it with written regulations and cooperation with water police**.
