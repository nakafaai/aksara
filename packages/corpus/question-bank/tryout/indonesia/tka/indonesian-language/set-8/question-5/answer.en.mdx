Paragraph four opens with *sasi menghadapi tantangan baru* (sasi faces new challenges). The challenge: **boats from outside** take sea produce at night, while **customary penalties are hard to apply** to non-villagers. The last sentence starts with *Karena itu* (that's why): the village **works with the water police** and **writes the sasi rules down**. The main idea combines the challenge and the response.

- Sasi isn't abolished. The village strengthens it.
- The night boats come from **outside the area**, not from the village.
- The water police are partners, not banning the ceremony.
