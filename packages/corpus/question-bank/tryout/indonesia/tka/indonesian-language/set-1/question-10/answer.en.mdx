# Given

Across the Harbor Lamp story, the role of the pier lamp **shifts in meaning** from beginning to end, moving from Raka's solitary evening habit to a shared coastal safety effort with village teenagers.

# Asked

All statements that accurately describe how the lamp's role changes for Raka.

# Solution

Picture **a little bell on your porch** that you first rang only to call your own cat home, and later the whole street uses to warn neighbors about rising floodwater. Its original personal memory stays, while its purpose expands to protect everyone.

> In multi-select questions, test every statement individually against the exact start, middle, and ending of the story.

## Checking the correct statements

- **At first the lamp preserved the memory of his father** is true. Paragraph three quotes Raka explaining that the old lamp exists <Highlight>agar aku tidak melupakan cara Ayah memperhatikan laut</Highlight> (so I will not forget how Father watched over the sea).
- **The personal habit grew into shared care** is true. Paragraph four states <Highlight>Kebiasaan kecil itu kini membantu semua perahu menemukan jalan pulang</Highlight> (that small habit now helps every boat find its way home) alongside teenagers building emergency signs.

## Why the other two statements fail

- **Waiting without signaling during the rain** reverses the storm scene, where Raka actively runs for the mirror and flashes light out to sea.
- **Turning the lamp into a reason to sell the pier** invents an event, because only his father's boat had been sold two years earlier, never the pier.

# Answer

The correct statements are that the lamp initially preserved the memory of his father, and by the end the personal habit grew into shared care.
