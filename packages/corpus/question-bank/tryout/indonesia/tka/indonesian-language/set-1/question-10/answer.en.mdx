Two listed statements describe how the lamp's meaning changes for Raka:

- **Awalnya lampu mempertahankan ingatan tentang ayahnya.** Raka lights it each afternoon to preserve his memory of his father.
- **Pada akhir cerita, kebiasaan pribadi berkembang menjadi kepedulian bersama.** Other teenagers help build a shared emergency system on the pier.

During the rain, Raka actively reflects the lamp with a mirror to create a visible signal. The option claiming that he waits without signalling reverses that event. The passage also never says that the lamp becomes a reason to sell the pier.
