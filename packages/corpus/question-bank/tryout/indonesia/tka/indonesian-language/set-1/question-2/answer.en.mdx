# Given

One **borrowing cycle** at the Seed Cabinet begins with the seed stock ready before pickup, continues through planting and drying harvested seeds, and ends when seeds and the history card return to the cabinet.

# Asked

How to classify three items in that single cycle as input, process, or output.

# Solution

Think of **making fresh orange juice in a kitchen**. The whole oranges waiting on the counter are the input, squeezing them is the process, and the poured glass of juice is the output.

> An input is already ready when a cycle begins, a process is an action that transforms material, and an output is the finished result returned at the end.

## Mapping each part of the cycle

The passage explains that residents take seeds, then <Highlight>menyisihkan sebagian benih yang sehat, mengeringkannya, lalu mengembalikannya</Highlight> (set aside healthy seeds, dry them, and return them) along with a filled history card.

| Part | Category | Evidence in one cycle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seed stock before borrowing | Input | Already waiting inside the cabinet when the cycle starts. |
| Drying seeds after harvest | Process | A physical step that prepares harvested seeds for return. |
| Completed history card | Output | Finished record handed back to the cabinet at cycle end. |

## Checking the cycle boundary

Returned seeds feed the next cycle later, yet the prompt fixes **one single cycle** starting before pickup. Inside that boundary, the waiting seed stock is strictly the starting input.

# Answer

**Seed stock in the cabinet before borrowing is an input, drying seeds after harvest is a process, and the completed history card is an output**.
