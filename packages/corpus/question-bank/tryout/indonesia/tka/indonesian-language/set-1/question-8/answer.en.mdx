# Given

On the **final evening** of the story, village teenagers arrive with paint, batteries, and wooden boards to install emergency signs along the pier and note the coast guard number, after a younger child has already asked to help light the new lamp in bad weather.

# Asked

Which event is most likely to happen after the story ends.

# Solution

Imagine classmates suddenly bringing **brooms, cloths, and a roster** to fix up the class reading corner together. The most natural next step is taking turns caring for that corner, not abandoning it overnight.

> A reasonable story prediction extends the habits, teamwork, and momentum already established in the closing scene.

## Tracing the natural next step from the ending

In paragraph three a child asks to help when storms hit, and in paragraph four <Highlight>Beberapa remaja datang membawa cat, baterai, dan papan kayu</Highlight> (several teenagers arrive carrying paint, batteries, and wooden boards) to <Highlight>memasang tanda darurat di sepanjang dermaga</Highlight> (install emergency signs along the pier).

Safety at the pier has clearly grown into shared neighborhood stewardship, making it most likely that **village teenagers take turns maintaining the emergency signs**.

## Why the other predictions break from the text

- **Raka stepping away completely** or **dropping the emergency system** ignores how eagerly he teaches another child to check the wick, oil, and emergency number.
- **The coast guard restricting pier access** has zero textual support.
- **Residents banning the new lamp** clashes directly with the village chief gifting it in praise.

# Answer

The most likely event after the story ends is that **the village teenagers maintain the emergency signs in turns**.
