# Given

In paragraph two of the Heat Map report, sentence one states the team's early claim that trees always cool better than buildings, **sentence two** says their advisor asked them to reconsider that conclusion, and **sentence three** notes the reading room was cooler than the garden yet used a fan.

# Asked

The logical relationship between sentence two and sentence three in the paragraph starting with "Tim awalnya menyimpulkan".

# Solution

Imagine a teacher saying, "Double-check your conclusion," and immediately pointing out, "Look, that cooler room had a fan running." The second sentence gives a **concrete reason** why the earlier conclusion needs another look.

> To identify how two neighboring sentences connect, ask what claim or prompt the first sentence makes and how the next sentence backs it up.

## Reading sentences two and three together

Sentence two states <Highlight>Guru pembimbing meminta mereka meninjau simpulan itu</Highlight> (the advisor asked them to review that conclusion). Sentence three follows with <Highlight>Ruang baca ternyata sedikit lebih sejuk daripada kebun, tetapi ruang tersebut memakai kipas</Highlight> (the reading room was slightly cooler than the garden at <InlineMath math="29.9\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}" /> versus <InlineMath math="30.4\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}" />, yet it used a fan).

Thus sentence three supplies **an example showing why the initial conclusion is not strong enough**.

## Why the other options fail

- **Denying temperature differences** is false because the report explicitly acknowledges differences across sites.
- **Sentence two explaining a procedure** misreads an advisor's request to reconsider.
- **Claiming location no longer affects temperature** and **claiming the sentences are unrelated** both ignore the direct supporting link.

# Answer

Sentence three gives an example explaining why the initial conclusion is not yet strong.
