# Given

Raka has carried **personal grief** since his father's boat was sold two years ago, including keeping the dull mirror his father used while fixing engines. When a tourist boat drifts lost in the rain, Raka grabs that mirror to flash signals until rescue arrives.

# Asked

Which story detail best proves that Raka turns personal loss into helpful action.

# Solution

Imagine missing a grandparent who loved gardening, then using **their old hand trowel** to plant vegetables for neighbors in need. The strongest proof that grief became service is the exact moment that keepsake actively helps someone else.

> The strongest textual proof directly connects both the reminder of loss and the concrete action that helps others.

## Linking the keepsake of loss to the rescue

Paragraph two joins both halves in one scene. The dull mirror is the tool <Highlight>yang dahulu dipakai ayahnya memperbaiki mesin</Highlight> (his father once used to repair engines), and <Highlight>Dengan cermin itu, ia memantulkan cahaya lampu ke arah laut berulang kali</Highlight> (with that mirror, he bounced the lamp's light toward the sea repeatedly) until the coast guard sent help.

Therefore, **reflecting light with the mirror until help arrives** directly transforms loss into lifesaving action.

## Why the other details are weaker proof

- **People assuming he keeps an old habit** only reflects outside guesses before the storm.
- **The sea line growing dark** is merely evening setting description.
- **The village chief's praise** is someone else's reaction afterward, not Raka's own deed.
- **Placing the old lamp on the shelf** preserves memory without directly rescuing anyone.

# Answer

**The strongest proof is that he reflects light with the mirror until help arrives**.
