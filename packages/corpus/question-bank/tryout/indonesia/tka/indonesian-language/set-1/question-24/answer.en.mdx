The procedure separates plant scraps from liquids, oil, animal remains, and packaging before processing. If a similar set of containers and labels were used at home, the most direct benefit would be **easier separation of suitable scraps** by family members.

The passage does not establish that most household waste will disappear. Processing still restricts the materials used and requires regular checks.
