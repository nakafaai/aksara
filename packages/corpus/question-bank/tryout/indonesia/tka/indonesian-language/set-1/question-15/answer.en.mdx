# Given

The initial SMA Nusa survey ran only **for five sunny days at <InlineMath math="12.30" />**, and by the end of the text the team explicitly narrows its report claims so they stay careful and grounded in evidence.

# Asked

Which additional sentence best strengthens the cautious tone of the team's report.

# Solution

Imagine seeing **clear blue skies for five days in a row** during summer. You would never claim it never rains in your town all year. Careful researchers always point out the time and weather window their data actually covered.

> A cautious scientific statement explicitly states where the evidence stops so readers do not overgeneralize to untested conditions.

## Matching the actual boundary of the survey

Paragraph one specifies the team measured <Highlight>Selama lima hari cerah</Highlight> (over five sunny days) at one midday hour. Five uniform sunny days do not sample cloudy weather, rain, or different seasons.

Adding that **five days of measurement do not represent all seasons and weather conditions** directly reinforces the report's disciplined scope.

## Why the other sentences weaken caution

- **Calling the back garden lowest in temperature** is factually wrong (the reading room was lower) and overly sweeping.
- **Claiming the benches already improved comfort** asserts a follow-up result never measured.
- **Declaring trees the main cause** and **saying the table needs no follow-up** both repeat the hasty overclaim the advisor corrected.

# Answer

The best sentence to add is that **five days of measurement do not yet represent all seasons and weather conditions**.
