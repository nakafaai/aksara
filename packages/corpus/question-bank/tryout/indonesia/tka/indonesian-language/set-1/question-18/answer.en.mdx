# Given

Pak Damar tells Mira that seat seven <Highlight>memberi tempat bagi harapanmu tanpa menghalangi langkahmu</Highlight> (gives your hope a place without blocking your steps), and right afterward Mira limits her wait to the afternoon train before leaving to rehearse.

# Asked

What Pak Damar's statement means for Mira.

# Solution

Imagine waiting for **rain to clear before heading to the park**, yet spending the wait drawing and tidying your room instead of freezing by the window. Healthy hope neither gives up wishing nor stops you from living your day.

> Figurative advice in fiction becomes crystal clear when read alongside the exact habit the character adopts right after hearing it.

## Pairing the advice with daily habit

His sentence balances two halves: <Highlight>memberi tempat bagi harapanmu</Highlight> (keep room for hope) and <Highlight>tanpa menghalangi langkahmu</Highlight> (do not let it halt your own steps).

The very next line puts it into action: <Highlight>setiap Jumat Mira menunggu hanya sampai kereta sore berangkat</Highlight> (every Friday she waited only until the afternoon train left) before going to rehearse. She **may keep hoping while continuing her own activities**.

## Why the other readings fail

- **Waiting at the station all day** lets hope block her steps.
- **Giving up hope for Seno's return** erases the first half of Pak Damar's reassurance.
- **Making the empty chair more important than the festival** and **Pak Damar playing Seno's violin part** contradict the story.

# Answer

**Pak Damar's statement means that Mira may keep hoping while still carrying on with her activities**.
