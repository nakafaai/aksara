# Given

At the close of **Seat Seven**, Seno still has not returned home, yet his voice reaches the hall through Pak Damar's phone. Mira then walks onstage and plays both melody lines alone to completion.

# Asked

Which emotional response best fits the ending of the story.

# Solution

Picture a younger sister singing **a duet all by herself at a school concert** after hearing her distant brother cheer her on by phone. You do not feel disappointed, and instead feel deeply **moved** by her quiet courage.

> A well-grounded emotional response honors the exact reality of the ending without inventing a happy guarantee the text never gives.

## Matching emotion to the final line

The closing sentence captures the story's bittersweet triumph: <Highlight>Kursi kosong itu tidak berubah menjadi kepulangan, tetapi penantian Mira berubah menjadi pertunjukan yang dapat ia selesaikan</Highlight> (the empty chair did not turn into a homecoming, yet Mira's waiting turned into a performance she could finish).

Because she transforms longing into strength onstage, the most fitting response is feeling **moved that Mira turns hope for Seno's return into strength to keep performing**.

## Why the other emotional responses miss the mark

- **Relief that her persistence guarantees Seno's return** goes beyond the text, since the chair remains empty.
- **Disappointment that a solo performance lacks meaning** ignores how deeply meaningful her two-part violin solo is.
- **Anger at Pak Damar** misreads his kindness in protecting Mira's hope and bringing Seno's call.
- **Anxiety that Mira stopped practicing** contradicts her confident performance onstage.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is feeling moved because Mira turns hope of a homecoming into strength to perform.
