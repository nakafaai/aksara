Paper straws were often placed with plant scraps because students noticed the base material but did not understand the effect of adhesive or protective coatings. The team clarified the guidance in two ways:

- Adds **real examples** to the container lids. Students could compare the item in their hand with a visible example.
- Explains the problem of **adhesive or protective coatings**. This reason explains why some plant-based objects decompose poorly.

The text reports neither mixing unidentified scraps nor removing volunteers. Neither is a change reported after week two.
