# Given

In **week two** of SMA Arunika's cafeteria trial, many paper straws ended up in the plant-scrap bin because students assumed anything plant-based could be composted. The team then improved its bin instructions before week three.

# Asked

All instruction improvements the team carried out after spotting the week-two errors.

# Solution

Imagine helping a friend tell **a real apple from a wax apple**. Rather than leaving a bare sign, you place one real apple on the lid as a model and briefly explain why wax does not belong in a snack bowl. The cafeteria team made those exact two upgrades.

> In multi-select questions tied to a specific stage, verify each option directly against the paragraph describing that exact week.

## Verifying both improvements after week two

Paragraph three states both actions explicitly:

- **Attaching real examples to the bin lids** is true, matching <Highlight>Tim kemudian memasang contoh nyata pada tutup wadah</Highlight> (the team then attached real examples to the bin lids).
- **Explaining the problem of glue or protective coatings** is true, matching <Highlight>menambahkan penjelasan bahwa lapisan perekat atau pelindung dapat menghambat penguraian</Highlight> (adding an explanation that adhesive or protective layers slow breakdown).

## Why the other two options fail

- **Merging unidentified scraps for staff to sort later** reverses the entire point of student sorting and never occurs in the passage.
- **Removing volunteers from the tray-return table** contradicts the passage, since volunteers continue guiding hesitant students and photographing daily sorting patterns.

# Answer

The two improvements made after week two are **attaching real examples to the bin lids and explaining the issue of adhesive or protective coatings**.
