Seat seven helps Mira in three ways:

- Protects Mira from **repeated questions**. Pak Damar's sign explains why the seat is empty.
- Gives her hope a **visible form**. The reserved seat gives her waiting for Seno a visible place.
- Reminds Mira to **continue rehearsing**. She always leaves for rehearsal after the afternoon train departs.

Pak Damar explicitly says the seat guarantees nobody’s arrival. Its symbolic role is therefore not a promise that Seno will return.
