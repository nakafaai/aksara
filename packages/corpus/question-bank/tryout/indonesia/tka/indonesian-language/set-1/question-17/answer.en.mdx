# Given

Pak Damar deliberately leaves **seat number seven** empty with a note reading "Reserved for someone who has not arrived yet", while clearly reminding Mira that the seat guarantees no one's arrival.

# Asked

All statements that accurately match the functions of seat number seven in the story.

# Solution

Picture **a small shelf** set aside for postcards from a distant friend. It gives your longing a visible home, spares you constant questions from visitors, and still leaves your desk clear so you keep doing your own work.

> For multi-select items, verify every candidate function directly against explicit lines of dialogue and action in the passage.

## Verifying the three supported functions

- **Protecting Mira from repeated questions** is true. Paragraph three explains Pak Damar wrote the note <Highlight>agar Mira tidak perlu menjelaskan penantiannya kepada setiap orang</Highlight> (so Mira would not have to explain her waiting to everyone).
- **Giving shape to Mira's hope** is true. Pak Damar tells her the seat <Highlight>memberi tempat bagi harapanmu</Highlight> (gives your hope a place).
- **Reminding Mira to keep practicing** is true. The reminder <Highlight>harapan tidak boleh menghalangi langkah</Highlight> (hope must not block your steps) leads her to rehearse after the train leaves and finish her performance.

## Why the remaining option fails

- **Guaranteeing Seno will return every Friday** flatly contradicts Pak Damar's warning: <Highlight>Kursi ini tidak menjamin siapa pun datang</Highlight> (this seat does not guarantee anyone's arrival).

# Answer

**The three correct statements are protecting Mira from repeated questions, giving shape to Mira's hope, and reminding Mira to continue practicing**.
