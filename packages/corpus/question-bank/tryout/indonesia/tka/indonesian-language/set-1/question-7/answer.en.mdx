When the lamp cannot reach the boat, Raka immediately looks for another method. He takes a mirror from the shed, reflects the light, and produces a signal the coastguard can see.

That sequence shows **tanggap memanfaatkan alat yang tersedia saat rencana pertama gagal**. His loyalty to an old routine appears elsewhere in the passage, but his action during the rain specifically shows that he can recognise a failed plan and adapt it.
