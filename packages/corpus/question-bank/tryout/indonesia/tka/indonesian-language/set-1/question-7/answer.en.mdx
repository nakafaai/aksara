# Given

On a **sudden rainy night**, a tourist boat loses direction and Raka's small lamp shines too low to be seen. Raka runs to the shed for his father's dull mirror and flashes light repeatedly toward the sea until the coast guard spots it.

# Asked

Which character trait of Raka stands out most clearly from his actions during the rain.

# Solution

Imagine you are painting and **your blue brush snaps**. Instead of freezing up, you quickly spot a sponge nearby and finish the sky anyway. Raka responds with that same calm resourcefulness when his first attempt falls short.

> A character trait is best proven by the specific decisions and actions taken during the exact scene named in the question.

## Tracing the quick adaptation in the rain

The second paragraph shows his sequence: <Highlight>cahayanya terlalu rendah</Highlight> (its light was too low), then <Highlight>Ia berlari ke gudang dan menemukan cermin kusam</Highlight> (he ran to the shed and found a dull mirror), then <Highlight>memantulkan cahaya lampu ke arah laut berulang kali</Highlight> (bounced the lamp's light toward the sea repeatedly).

He recognizes immediately that lifting the lamp alone fails and shows he is **resourceful with available tools when his first plan fails**.

## Why the other traits do not fit this scene

- **Taking reckless risks without checking consequences** misreads a safe, practical signaling effort.
- **Loyalty to old habits** fits the opening paragraph, not the emergency problem-solving during the storm.
- **Relying on adults to start the rescue** is false, since Raka initiates the signal himself.
- **Stubbornly refusing new equipment** contradicts paragraph three, where he accepts the village chief's larger lamp.

# Answer

The strongest trait shown during the rain is that Raka is quick to use available tools when his first plan fails.
