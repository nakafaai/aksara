# Given

On **festival day**, seat seven remains empty and Mira almost puts her violin away. After hearing Seno's voice over the speakerphone and remembering the note on the chair, she steps onstage and plays both melody lines herself to the end.

# Asked

Which detail best proves that Mira grows as a character.

# Solution

Picture a child who used to **fear riding a bike without an older sibling holding the seat**. When the sibling cannot come, she hesitates for a second, takes a deep breath, and pedals all the way to the finish line alone. Pushing past that hesitation is real growth.

> Character growth is proven by a decisive shift toward greater courage or maturity compared with how the character felt earlier.

## Spotting the peak proof of character growth

Mira nearly gives up when the chair stays empty. Then paragraph four marks her turning point: <Highlight>Mira naik ke panggung</Highlight> (Mira walked onstage), <Highlight>memainkan dua garis melodi secara bergantian</Highlight> (played both melody lines in turn), until <Highlight>penantian Mira berubah menjadi pertunjukan yang dapat ia selesaikan</Highlight> (her waiting turned into a performance she could finish).

The clearest evidence of growth is that **she manages to complete the performance even though Seno has not returned**.

## Why the other options fall short

- **Keeping seat seven empty** was Pak Damar's routine at the station, not a personal transformation by Mira.
- **Nearly putting away her violin** shows the moment of doubt before she grows past it.
- **Seno's phone going offline** and **Pak Damar standing at the door** are external events, not Mira's own inner change.

# Answer

**The strongest proof of Mira's character growth is that she manages to complete the performance even though Seno has not returned**.
