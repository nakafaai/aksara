The cards record residents' observations and help people choose seeds. The managers also warn that growth can differ from one garden to another. The supported assessment is **useful guidance without a guarantee** of identical results.

Useful notes need not wait for laboratory testing, but observations from one garden do not establish germination performance in every garden.
