Use **the materials permitted by this passage’s procedure**, rather than assuming every kind of waste is processed alike.

| Material | Category | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vegetable pieces | Boleh | Plant scraps are added in small portions. |
| Oily broth | Tidak boleh | Oil and broth are explicitly excluded. |
| Dry leaves | Boleh | Cover the scraps and are added if the contents are too wet. |
