The correct classification is:

- **Potongan sayur**: Boleh
- **Kuah berminyak**: Tidak boleh
- **Daun kering**: Boleh

The procedure puts plant scraps into the biopore hole and covers them with dry leaves. It explicitly excludes oil and broth, so vegetable pieces and dry leaves are allowed, while oily broth is not.
