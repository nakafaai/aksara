# Given

The Sukamaju neighborhood library runs **Lemari Benih** (the Seed Cabinet), where residents borrow a packet of local vegetable seeds, grow them, dry healthy harvested seeds, and return part of the harvest to the cabinet.

# Asked

Why the word *sirkulasi* (circulation) describes how the Seed Cabinet works better than *penyimpanan* (storage).

# Solution

Picture **passing a ball around a circle** of friends in a yard. The ball never sits still in one box. It leaves, travels to another player, and comes right back so the next person can play.

> Circulation means a repeating loop that leaves and returns, whereas storage only keeps items sitting in one place.

## Passage evidence for a repeating loop

The first paragraph explains <Highlight>persediaan bergerak dari lemari ke kebun warga dan kembali lagi ke lemari</Highlight> (the stock moves from the cabinet to residents' gardens and back to the cabinet again). Growers borrow seeds, harvest healthy ones, and bring them back with notes for the next grower.

That outgoing and returning movement creates a **cycle of exchange** for both seeds and growing experience.

## Why the other options fail

- **Keeping every seed inside the cabinet** cancels the borrowing and planting that define the service.
- **Testing every seed in a laboratory** clashes directly with the final paragraph, which says the records <Highlight>belum diuji di laboratorium</Highlight> (have not been tested in a laboratory).
- **Replacing all old varieties** and **guaranteeing identical harvests** are never claimed, and managers explicitly warn that growth varies across gardens.

# Answer

The word *sirkulasi* is more accurate because seeds move from the cabinet to gardens and part of the harvest returns, creating a cycle of exchange.
