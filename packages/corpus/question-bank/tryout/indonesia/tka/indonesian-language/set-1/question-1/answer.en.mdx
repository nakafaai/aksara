The correct classification is:

- **Amplop benih yang dibawa pulang warga**: Keluaran
- **Pengeringan benih setelah panen**: Proses
- **Kartu riwayat yang telah diisi**: Keluaran

The envelope taken home is an **output of the lending service**, drying is a process step, and the completed history card is a recorded output. Seed held in the cabinet could be an input, but the assessed statement specifically describes an envelope after a resident has taken it home.
