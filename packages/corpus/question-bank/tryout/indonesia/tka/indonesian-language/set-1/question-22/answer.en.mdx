The label needs to name the accepted material clearly. This container takes clean plant scraps, while coated or adhesive packaging can hinder decomposition. **Sisa Nabati Bersih Tanpa Kemasan** expresses both conditions.

A paper straw remains a nonfood object, not leftover vegetables. A plant-based raw material alone does not make an object the plant scraps accepted by this procedure.
