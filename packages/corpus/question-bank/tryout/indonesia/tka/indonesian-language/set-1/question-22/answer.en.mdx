Paper straws were often placed with plant scraps because students noticed the base material but did not understand the effect of adhesive or protective coatings. The team clarified the guidance in two ways:

- **Memasang contoh nyata pada tutup wadah.** Students could compare the item in their hand with a visible example.
- **Menjelaskan masalah lapisan perekat atau pelindung.** This reason explains why some plant-based objects decompose poorly.
