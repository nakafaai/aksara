# Given

In **The Old Letter**, Wulan discovers Hasan's <InlineMath math="1987" /> letter inside an old atlas advising readers to learn countries through their neighbors on the map. Wulan adopts his method, reaches the finals, and slips in her own note "Tried it, worked. Pass it on" for the next borrower.

# Asked

The main idea of the entire story.

# Solution

Picture **a relay baton of kindness across decades**: practical study advice left by an older student changes how a new learner studies, and she passes that exact baton forward to whoever borrows the atlas next.

> A story's central idea links the advice discovered, the change in the protagonist's method, and her choice to pass it along.

## Tracing the full relay from letter to letter

Wulan switches from memorizing phone lists to following Hasan's tip <Highlight>Negara lebih mudah diingat kalau kamu tahu tetangganya</Highlight> (countries are easier to recall when you know their neighbors). After reaching the finals, she adds <Highlight>Sudah dicoba, berhasil. Teruskan</Highlight> (tried it, worked, pass it on) behind his letter.

Thus **advice in an old letter transforms how Wulan studies, and Wulan passes it on to the next borrower**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Wulan winning the contest** contradicts paragraph four stating <Highlight>Ia tidak menang</Highlight> (she did not win).
- **Hasan winning in <InlineMath math="1987" />** is false because he lost in round one.
- **Bu Rina banning notes** never happens, and **memorizing phone lists being best** is the exact old habit Wulan left behind.

# Answer

The main idea is that advice in an old letter changes how Wulan studies, and she passes it along to the next borrower.
