# Given

Before handing the old atlas back to Bu Rina, **Wulan slips a small sheet** reading "Tried it, worked. Pass it on." right **behind Hasan's letter**, leaving Hasan's original letter inside the book.

# Asked

What the next borrower of the atlas is most likely to experience.

# Solution

Imagine tucking **a short reply right behind an old postcard** inside a library book before returning it to the shelf. Whoever checks out that book next is bound to discover both pieces waiting together inside.

> A grounded continuation follows directly from the physical state of the object left at the end of the final paragraph.

## Checking what stays inside the atlas at the close

Paragraph five specifies Wulan's exact move: <Highlight>Wulan mengambil secarik kertas, menulis satu kalimat, dan menyelipkannya di belakang surat Hasan</Highlight> (Wulan wrote one sentence on a slip of paper and tucked it behind Hasan's letter) before returning the atlas.

Hence the next borrower will most likely **find Hasan's letter together with Wulan's single-sentence slip tucked inside the atlas**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Getting a new atlas because the old one was thrown away** contradicts returning it intact.
- **Bu Rina asking to return the letter to Hasan** fails because Bu Rina never saw the letter.
- **Finding no letter** contradicts tucking both inside, and **reading a long essay on Wulan's contest** contradicts her writing just one short line.

# Answer

**The next borrower is most likely to find Hasan's letter together with Wulan's short note tucked inside the atlas**.
