# Given

**Low-Cost Air Sensors** sorts its facts into **three clear categories**: strengths of cheap sensors (paragraphs one and four), causes of misreadings (paragraph two), and researchers' usage guidelines (paragraph three).

# Asked

How to assign four statements to advantages, causes of inaccurate readings, or usage recommendations.

# Solution

Think of a user guide for **an inexpensive room thermometer**: putting one in every room is an advantage, placing it right beside a stove causes false readings, and reading daily averages is a usage tip. Every detail fits cleanly into one column.

> In text-role matrices, separate device benefits, sources of measurement distortion, and recommended correction practices.

## Mapping all four statements to their source paragraphs

Paragraph two lists both distortion sources: <Highlight>butir air yang sangat kecil ikut terhitung sebagai partikel</Highlight> (tiny water droplets counted as particles) and sensors <Highlight>dipasang dekat dapur atau tempat parkir</Highlight> (placed near kitchens or parking lots).

Paragraph three advises <Highlight>hasil dibaca sebagai rata-rata harian</Highlight> (read daily averages). Paragraph four highlights the key benefit: <Highlight>sensor murah dapat dipasang di banyak sekolah</Highlight> (can be installed at many schools).

| Statement | Role | Passage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water droplets in humid air counted as particles | Cause of misreading | Explained for foggy or rainy mornings in paragraph two. |
| Results read as daily averages | Usage recommendation | Second researcher guideline in paragraph three. |
| Sensors can be installed across many schools | Advantage of low-cost sensors | Broad neighborhood coverage highlighted in paragraph four. |
| Sensor placed too close to a kitchen or parking lot | Cause of misreading | Triggers unrepresentative local spikes in paragraph two. |

## Verifying both distortion sources

Both **humid-air droplets** and **proximity to kitchens or parking** sit in paragraph two as **causes of inaccurate readings**.

# Answer

**Installing across many schools is an advantage, water droplets in humid air and placement near kitchens or parking lots are causes of misreading, and reading daily averages is a usage recommendation**.
