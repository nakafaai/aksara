# Given

When Gilang first joins the **bass section** of three quiet seniors, his voice is barely audible on day one. On day three, right when the bass enters on time, **one of those seniors taps Gilang's shoulder**, sparking his enthusiasm to memorize the score on the bus and hum at home.

# Asked

Which emotional response best fits the senior tapping Gilang on the shoulder.

# Solution

Imagine joining **a quiet varsity team as a nervous newcomer**, landing your pass right on cue, and feeling a teammate give your shoulder a brief reassuring pat. That tiny wordless nod makes you feel instantly **warm** and welcomed.

> An emotional response to a quiet gesture in fiction follows the boost in belonging and confidence it sparks in the protagonist.

## Seeing the welcoming effect in paragraph five

Paragraph five links the gesture straight to Gilang's turnaround: <Highlight>salah satu dari mereka menepuk bahunya ketika bagian bas masuk tepat waktu</Highlight> (one of them patted his shoulder when the bass came in right on cue), and right away he starts practicing everywhere.

The fitting response is **feeling warm because that small encouragement welcomes Gilang into his new section**.

## Why the other four emotions misread the moment

- **Annoyance at belittling**, **pity over mockery**, **anxiety about scolding**, and **amusement at a mistake** all reverse the scene, since the pat rewards entering *on time* and lifts his spirit.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is feeling warm because that small gesture of support makes Gilang feel accepted in his new section.
