# Given

In **Cracked Voice**, Gilang's high note suddenly turns hoarse during choir rehearsal while teammates behind him hold back laughter. Paragraphs one and two capture **Gilang's embarrassment** right afterward.

# Asked

All details showing Gilang's embarrassment after his voice cracks.

# Solution

Imagine tripping in front of class while classmates stifle giggles. **Clear signs of embarrassment** show when your face flushes, you stare at the floor, and next day you sneak in last by the door. Separate those early signs of shame from later rehearsal drive or stage nerves.

> In multi-select questions about a specific emotion, select details from the scene where that emotion occurs rather than later scenes of confidence or performance nerves.

## Checking the two embarrassment details in paragraphs one and two

- **Gilang blushing and looking down until rehearsal ends** is true, matching paragraph one: <Highlight>Gilang memerah dan menunduk sampai latihan selesai</Highlight> (Gilang blushed and kept his head down until rehearsal ended).
- **Gilang arriving last and sitting near the door** is true, matching paragraph two: <Highlight>ia datang ke ruang musik paling akhir dan duduk di dekat pintu</Highlight> (he entered the music room last and sat near the door) ready to quit.

## Why the other two options belong to different feelings

- **Sweaty palms under stage spotlights** shows normal competition-day stage jitters in the final paragraph.
- **Memorizing sheet music on the bus** shows newfound dedication in the bass section in paragraph five.

# Answer

The two details showing embarrassment are that Gilang blushed and looked down until rehearsal ended, and arrived last to sit near the door.
