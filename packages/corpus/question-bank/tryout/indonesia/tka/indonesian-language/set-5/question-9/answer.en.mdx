# Given

On competition day at the end of **Cracked Voice**, Gilang stops chasing high notes and anchors the song with steady low tones, hearing the sopranos float above him like a roof resting on solid pillars.

# Asked

What is most likely to happen after the choir competition ends.

# Solution

Imagine thriving on game day in **a brand-new position as goalkeeper**, realizing your saves hold the whole defense together. After the match, the most natural next step is continuing to practice in that new role with growing confidence.

> The most plausible continuation builds directly on the confidence and sense of purpose the protagonist gains in the climax.

## Reading the pride of Gilang in his new role

The closing paragraph highlights his solid footing onstage: <Highlight>Ia menyanyikan nada rendah yang menjadi dasar seluruh lagu</Highlight> (he sang the low notes forming the foundation of the whole song) with sopranos <Highlight>seperti atap yang bertumpu pada tiang</Highlight> (like a roof resting on pillars).

Most likely, **Gilang continues practicing in the bass section with greater confidence**.

## Why the other four continuations fail

- **Asking to return to high voices** ignores that his voice naturally shifted lower.
- **Quitting out of shame** and **Bu Sekar dropping him** clash with her support and his onstage success.
- **Being laughed at again** ignores that his low voice now sounds full and anchors the choir.

# Answer

After the competition, Gilang is most likely to keep practicing in the bass section with greater confidence.
