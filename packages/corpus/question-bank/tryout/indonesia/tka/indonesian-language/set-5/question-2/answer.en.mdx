# Given

In **Sukamaju Clinic Queue**, patients without smartphones rely on desk staff to register, and paragraphs three and four call this **manual registration** (*pendaftaran manual*) in direct contrast to online queuing by app or text message.

# Asked

The most accurate meaning of the loanword *manual* in the phrase *pendaftaran manual*.

# Solution

Think of borrowing a library book: you can scan it on a phone app (**online**), or walk up to the counter and have the librarian enter it by hand (**manual**). Here *manual* contrasts in-person staff desk registration against app booking.

> Contextual contrast in a passage (online via phone app versus manual at a staffed desk) locks in the exact sense of a loanword.

## Checking the contrast in paragraphs two and three

Paragraph two notes elderly patients <Highlight>harus dibantu mendaftar oleh petugas</Highlight> (must be helped to register by staff) at the counter. Paragraph three adds <Highlight>Sebagian pasien lansia tidak memiliki ponsel pintar</Highlight> (some lack smartphones), placing them in <Highlight>pendaftaran manual</Highlight> (manual registration).

Thus *manual* means **done directly at the counter with staff assistance, without an app**.

## Why the other four meanings miss the context

- **An instruction handbook** is a different noun sense of *manual* not used here.
- **Automatic without staff** is the exact opposite of manual.
- **Only for patients who forgot cards** confuses manual sign-in with the separate line it was unfortunately merged into.
- **Before clinic opening hours** never appears in the text.

# Answer

**In *pendaftaran manual*, *manual* means carried out directly at the counter with staff help and without an app**.
