The word *manual* has several meanings, so check the context. The text contrasts taking a number **through the app or a text message** with the registration older patients go through, where they **have to be helped by staff**. The clinic then opens a special counter for manual registration. So *pendaftaran manual* means registering in person at the counter with staff help, without the app.

- The sense of an instruction booklet exists, as in *buku manual*, but it doesn't fit registration at a counter.
- *Otomatis* (automatic) is the opposite of manual.
- Patients who forgot their card only share the queue. That isn't what the word means.
- The text doesn't link manual registration to the hours before opening.
