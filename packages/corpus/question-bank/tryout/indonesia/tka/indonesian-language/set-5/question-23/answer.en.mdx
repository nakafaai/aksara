# Given

**Sea Turtle Nests** uses four common Indonesian loanwords tied to biology and fieldwork: *embrio*, *area*, *termometer*, and *patroli*.

# Asked

All statements that accurately define these loanwords in the passage's context.

# Solution

Imagine compiling **a quick field glossary** with sea-turtle volunteers: *embrio* develops inside the egg, *termometer* measures sand temperature, and *patroli* means walking the beach at night searching for tracks. Watch out for one option swapping a place word into a time word.

> Context confirms each loanword's exact category so any mismatch (such as defining a place word as a span of time) stands out right away.

## Checking the three accurate definitions

- ***Embrio* means the developing organism inside an egg** is true, matching <Highlight>embrio di dalamnya bisa mati</Highlight> (the embryo inside can die) in paragraph two.
- ***Termometer* means a device for measuring temperature** is true, matching <Highlight>relawan memasang termometer di area penetasan</Highlight> (volunteers install thermometers in the hatchery) to monitor sand temperature.
- ***Patroli* means going around to guard or search for something** is true, matching <Highlight>berpatroli dari pukul sembilan malam sampai pukul tiga pagi</Highlight> (patrolling from nine at night until three in the morning) looking for tracks.

## Why the remaining option fails

- ***Area* is not hatching time** because it refers to a *zone or place*, as in *area penetasan berpagar* (fenced hatchery area).

# Answer

The three accurate statements define *embrio* (developing organism inside the egg), *termometer* (tool for measuring temperature), and *patroli* (patrolling around to monitor or search).
