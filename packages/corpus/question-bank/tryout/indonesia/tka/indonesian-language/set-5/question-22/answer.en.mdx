# Given

**Sea Turtle Nests** organizes facts into **three clear roles**: why eggs are relocated from open beaches (paragraph one), volunteers' technical procedures (paragraphs two and three), and rules for visitors during hatchling release (paragraph four).

# Asked

How to assign four statements to reasons for relocating eggs, volunteer methods, or visitor rules.

# Solution

Think of a **wildlife sanctuary guide**: predators explain why nests move, care techniques describe volunteer work, and no-flash photography sets the rule for guests. Each statement maps directly to one column.

> In text-role matrices, separate conservation motives, staff protocols, and public visitor restrictions cleanly.

## Mapping all four statements to their paragraphs

Paragraph one gives the reason for moving eggs: <Highlight>sarang di pantai terbuka sering digali anjing liar</Highlight> (open nests are dug up by stray dogs). Paragraphs two and three describe volunteer work: <Highlight>memindahkannya dalam waktu enam jam</Highlight> (relocate within six hours) and <Highlight>menaungi sebagian sarang dengan jaring paranet</Highlight> (shade some nests with shade netting).

Paragraph four states the visitor rule: <Highlight>dilarang memotret dengan lampu kilat</Highlight> (flash photography is forbidden).

| Statement | Role | Passage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open beach nests are often dug up by stray dogs | Reason for moving eggs | Threat explaining relocation in paragraph one. |
| Some nests are shaded with paranet netting | Volunteer method | Temperature management by volunteers in paragraph three. |
| Flash photography is forbidden | Visitor rule | Restriction for release spectators in paragraph four. |
| Eggs are moved within six hours | Volunteer method | Handling time limit for volunteers in paragraph two. |

## Confirming both volunteer tasks together

Both **shading with paranet** and **moving eggs within six hours** describe technical **volunteer methods**.

# Answer

**Stray dogs digging nests is a reason for moving eggs, shading with paranet and moving within six hours are volunteer methods, and banning flash photography is a visitor rule**.
