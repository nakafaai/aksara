# Given

**Paragraph two of Sea Turtle Nests** insists moving eggs is never casual: volunteers must relocate them **within six hours**, **keep each egg's orientation unchanged** so embryos survive, and match **nest depth at about sixty centimeters**.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph two.

# Solution

Imagine moving **a delicate glass terrarium** to a safer shelf: you follow a time limit, never tilt it sideways, and set it at the same light level so the plant survives. Those three strict handling rules to protect the embryo define paragraph two.

> The main idea of a procedural paragraph unites every required rule with the survival goal it protects.

## Combining all three rules in paragraph two

Paragraph two lists three strict rules for relocation: <Highlight>dalam waktu enam jam</Highlight> (within six hours), <Highlight>menjaga posisi telur tetap seperti semula</Highlight> (keeping orientation unchanged so embryos do not die), and <Highlight>Kedalaman sarang buatan juga dibuat sama dengan sarang asli</Highlight> (matching artificial nest depth to natural depth).

Therefore, **relocating eggs must follow rules on timing, orientation, and depth so embryos stay alive**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Night patrols** and **stray dogs digging nests** belong to paragraph one.
- **Turning eggs for even growth** reverses the warning that rotating eggs kills embryos.
- **Sand temperature shaping sex ratios** belongs to paragraph three.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph two is that egg relocation must follow strict rules on timing, orientation, and depth so embryos survive.
