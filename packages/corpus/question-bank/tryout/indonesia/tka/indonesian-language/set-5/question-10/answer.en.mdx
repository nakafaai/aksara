# Given

After moving to the bass section and receiving a reassuring tap on the shoulder on day three, **Gilang takes personal initiative** to practice beyond regular choir rehearsal hours.

# Asked

Which detail most strongly proves that Gilang is taking his new bass role seriously.

# Solution

Imagine a student who does not stop practicing when club hour ends, and instead **studies notes on the bus ride and hums lines at home**. Voluntary practice outside required hours is the clearest proof of real dedication.

> The strongest textual proof of dedication is voluntary extra effort taken outside mandatory practice time.

## Spotting voluntary practice in paragraph five

Paragraph five captures his new drive right after the shoulder tap: <Highlight>Gilang mulai menghafal partitur di dalam bus dan bersenandung pelan di kamar mandi sampai adiknya mengetuk pintu</Highlight> (Gilang started memorizing the score on the bus and humming softly in the bathroom until his sibling knocked).

Therefore, **Gilang memorizing the score on the bus and humming in the bathroom** most clearly proves his seriousness.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Sitting near the door** showed his earlier shame when planning to quit.
- **Full, calm low notes** showed natural vocal tone during Bu Sekar's test, not sustained practice effort.
- **Three seniors in the bass row** is background info, and **sweaty palms** simply show stage nerves.

# Answer

The strongest proof is that Gilang memorizes the sheet music on the bus and hums in the bathroom.
