# Given

Wulan has never met Hasan, who wrote his note back in <InlineMath math="1987" />, yet after benefiting from his advice she repays that stranger's kindness by slipping in **"Tried it, worked. Pass it on."** for future borrowers.

# Asked

Which emotional response best fits Wulan's closing sentence inside the atlas.

# Solution

Imagine borrowing **a stranger's umbrella** left at a bus stop with a note saying "Use when it rains," then returning it dry with "Used it, worked great. Pass it on." That quiet chain of generosity between strangers naturally leaves you feeling **moved**.

> A fitting emotional response to a chain of quiet kindness highlights warmth and gratitude for paying help forward.

## Reading the chain of kindness in paragraph five

Hasan's <InlineMath math="1987" /> note reached out <Highlight>Untuk siapa pun yang meminjam buku ini sesudah aku</Highlight> (to whoever borrows this book after me). Wulan honors that generosity with <Highlight>Sudah dicoba, berhasil. Teruskan</Highlight> (tried it, worked, pass it on) and returns the atlas smiling.

Therefore, the fitting response is feeling **moved because Wulan repays a stranger's advice by passing it forward**.

## Why the other four emotions miss the mark

- **Disappointment she did not mention winning** forgets she did not win and focuses on sharing a helpful study tip.
- **Annoyance at damaging the book** is wrong because she only tucked a loose slip inside.
- **Worry about confiscation** and **confusion over no link** miss how directly her note echoes Hasan's gift.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is feeling moved because Wulan repays a stranger's advice by passing it along.
