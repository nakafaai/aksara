# Given

Hasan's core tip is studying map positions and **learning countries together with their neighbors** instead of rote capital lists. After practicing that tip, Wulan reaches the finals and nails the tough question naming Switzerland's bordering countries.

# Asked

Which detail most strongly proves that Hasan's advice helped Wulan.

# Solution

Imagine a coach teaching you **how to curve a corner kick**, and during the real match you score with that exact kick when everyone else misses. Succeeding on the exact challenge that tests the new method is the strongest possible proof.

> The clearest evidence that a study method works is concrete success on a difficult test item that directly relies on that exact strategy.

## Linking the neighbor strategy to the Switzerland question

Hasan advised <Highlight>Negara lebih mudah diingat kalau kamu tahu tetangganya</Highlight> (countries stick better when you know their neighbors). Paragraph four delivers the exact payoff: Wulan <Highlight>bisa menjawab soal yang membuat banyak peserta lain ragu, yaitu negara mana saja yang berbatasan dengan Swiss</Highlight> (answered the question that stumped many contestants about which countries border Switzerland).

Thus **answering the Switzerland-border question while others hesitated** proves Hasan's tip worked directly.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Opening the envelope under the table**, **the <InlineMath math="1987" /> date**, and **Bu Rina's puzzled look** show zero learning outcome.
- **Three nights memorizing capitals on her phone** describes her old method *before* reading Hasan's letter.

# Answer

The strongest proof is that Wulan answers the question about countries bordering Switzerland that left many other contestants uncertain.
