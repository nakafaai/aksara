The clinic measures waiting time **from when a number is taken to when the patient is called**. Taking a number isn't the same as arriving. Patients who take a number from home aren't at the clinic yet, while older patients have already queued a long time before getting one. The clinic itself plans to **record arrival time at the entrance**. So the old method falls short because it doesn't record when patients actually arrive.

- Data from an app can still mislead if it measures the wrong thing.
- Older patients' counter time isn't recorded, so their wait looks **shorter**, not longer.
- The special counter handles the queue but doesn't fix the measuring. That's why a new method is planned.
- The text gives no data showing waits went up. It only shows the figure is incomplete.
