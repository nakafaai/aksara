*Pindah rumah* (moving house) is a figure of speech. Bu Sekar first tests Gilang on a **low key**, and his voice comes out *round and calm*. Then she says *Suaramu tidak rusak* (your voice isn't broken) and moves him to the **bass section**. So his voice is shifting to a lower range, not breaking down.

- *Moving house* isn't literal. Nobody talks about changing schools.
- Bu Sekar doesn't predict his voice will go back up. She moves him to the basses.
- The image is about his voice, not where he practises.
- Bu Sekar says clearly that his voice isn't broken.
