# Given

When Bu Sekar presses low piano keys, Gilang's voice emerges full and steady with zero hoarseness. Bu Sekar smiles and tells him, **"Your voice is not broken. Your voice is moving to a new home. Starting today you stand in the bass section."**

# Asked

What Bu Sekar's figurative phrase "Your voice is moving to a new home" means.

# Solution

Imagine outgrowing your favorite sneakers and hearing a parent say, "Your feet are not hurt, they just need a bigger size." **Moving to a new home** means Gilang's growing voice is shifting into a deeper pitch range rather than breaking.

> Figurative dialogue in fiction is unlocked directly by the literal sentences spoken immediately before and after it.

## Reading the surrounding lines in paragraph four

Bu Sekar defines her metaphor on both sides: <Highlight>Suaramu tidak rusak</Highlight> (your voice is not broken) and <Highlight>Mulai hari ini kamu berdiri di barisan bas</Highlight> (starting today you stand in the bass row), right after his low notes ring steady.

It means **Gilang's voice is shifting into a lower pitch range rather than being damaged**.

## Why the other four readings fail

- **Transferring schools** and **practicing at home** misread *home* literally instead of figuratively.
- **Returning to high notes after the contest** is never suggested.
- **Damaged and needing rest** directly contradicts <Highlight>Suaramu tidak rusak</Highlight> (your voice is not broken).

# Answer

**Bu Sekar's words mean that Gilang's voice is moving into a lower vocal range, not that it is damaged**.
