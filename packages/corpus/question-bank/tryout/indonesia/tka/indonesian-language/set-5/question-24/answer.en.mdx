A report sentence has to **match the numbers** and **not exaggerate**. Paragraph four says 3,200 hatchlings came from 4,000 moved eggs. Work out the percentage:

<BlockMath math="\frac{3200}{4000} \times 100\% = 80\%" />

The sentence naming 3,200 out of 4,000 moved eggs, or 80 percent, is the accurate report.

- *Almost all turtle eggs on the beach* is too broad. The data covers only moved eggs, and 20 percent didn't hatch.
- 4,000 is the number of eggs, not hatchlings.
- 800 is the number of eggs that **didn't** hatch, since <InlineMath math="4000 - 3200 = 800" />.
- 75 percent is wrong, because the calculation gives 80 percent.
