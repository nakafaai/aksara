# Given

The closing paragraph of **Sea Turtle Nests** reports that last year volunteers hatched **<InlineMath math="3{,}200" /> hatchlings from <InlineMath math="4{,}000" /> relocated eggs**.

# Asked

Which report sentence states last year's hatching outcome accurately in both numbers and scope.

# Solution

Imagine checking how many **cupcakes baked cleanly** out of ten: eight out of ten equals eighty percent. Computing the turtle hatching rate follows the exact same quick division.

> An accurate quantitative sentence gets the arithmetic right and keeps the exact scope stated in the source passage.

## Computing the exact hatching percentage

The text records <Highlight>relawan menetaskan <InlineMath math="3{.}200" /> tukik dari <InlineMath math="4{.}000" /> telur yang dipindahkan</Highlight> (volunteers hatched <InlineMath math="3{,}200" /> hatchlings from <InlineMath math="4{,}000" /> moved eggs):

<BlockMath math="\frac{3{,}200}{4{,}000} \times 100\% = \frac{32}{40} \times 100\% = 80\%" />

Therefore, **<InlineMath math="3{,}200" /> of <InlineMath math="4{,}000" /> relocated eggs, or <InlineMath math="80" /> percent, successfully hatched** matches both calculation and scope exactly.

## Why the other four sentences fail

- **Almost all eggs on the beach saved** stretches beyond *relocated* eggs to the entire coast.
- **Hatching <InlineMath math="4{,}000" /> hatchlings** swaps total eggs with successful hatchlings.
- **<InlineMath math="800" /> eggs hatching** gives the unhatched remainder (<InlineMath math="4{,}000 - 3{,}200 = 800" />), and **<InlineMath math="75" /> percent** miscalculates the ratio.

# Answer

The most accurate sentence is that <InlineMath math="3{,}200" /> out of <InlineMath math="4{,}000" /> relocated eggs, or <InlineMath math="80" /> percent, hatched successfully.
