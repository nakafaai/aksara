# Given

Wulan finds a <InlineMath math="1987" /> yellow envelope containing a handwritten letter from **Hasan**, who recounts losing in round one of a geography contest and urges future readers to study maps patiently.

# Asked

All statements that match the contents of Hasan's envelope and letter.

# Solution

Imagine reading **Hasan's letter line by line** with a highlighter, checking off each detail directly written on the envelope and sheet while crossing out a victory he never won.

> In literal multi-select questions, every true option maps directly to explicit lines in the quoted letter.

## Checking the three explicit facts from the letter of Hasan

- **Addressed to whoever borrows the atlas after Hasan** is true, matching paragraph one: <Highlight>Untuk siapa pun yang meminjam buku ini sesudah aku</Highlight> (to whoever borrows this book after me).
- **Hasan admits he nearly threw the atlas away** is true, matching paragraph two: <Highlight>Aku hampir membuang atlas ini ke tempat sampah</Highlight> (I nearly threw this atlas in the trash).
- **Hasan advises readers to study the map for a long time** is true, matching paragraph two: <Highlight>Kalau kamu sedang belajar, lihatlah petanya lama-lama</Highlight> (if you are studying, look at the map closely and patiently).

## Why the fourth option fails

- **Hasan winning the final round** directly contradicts paragraph two stating he <Highlight>gagal di babak pertama</Highlight> (lost in the first round).

# Answer

The three matching statements are that the envelope is addressed to future borrowers, Hasan admits he almost threw the atlas away, and Hasan advises looking closely at the map.
