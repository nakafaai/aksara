# Given

The news article on **SMA Harapan Bangsa's Honesty Canteen** follows **three clear sections**: past background behind the first closure (paragraph one), changes made for the July reopening (paragraph two), and the October stock-logging plan (final paragraph).

# Asked

How to assign four statements to background problem, changes implemented, or upcoming plan.

# Solution

Think of a news story on **reopening a school library**: why it closed before (background), what changed on reopening day (changes made), and what starts next month (next plan). Each time marker places the statement cleanly.

> In news-structure matrices, time references separate past background, current reforms, and future plans cleanly.

## Mapping all four statements to their sections

Paragraph one cites past background: <Highlight>selisih antara nilai barang yang diambil dan uang di kotak pernah mencapai lebih dari sepertiga</Highlight> (shortfalls once exceeded one-third). Paragraph two lists July reforms: <Highlight>Harga dibulatkan ke kelipatan seribu rupiah</Highlight> (rounded prices) and <Highlight>pembayaran dengan kode QR disediakan</Highlight> (digital scan payment).

Paragraph five sets the future plan: <Highlight>Mulai Oktober, koperasi berencana mencatat stok dua kali sehari</Highlight> (twice-daily stock logs from October).

| Statement | News section | Textual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QR code payment provided | Changes implemented | Rolled out for July reopening in paragraph two. |
| First canteen shortfall exceeded one-third | Background problem | Reason the earlier canteen closed in paragraph one. |
| Stock to be logged twice daily from October | Upcoming plan | Future plan starting October in paragraph five. |
| Prices rounded to thousand-rupiah increments | Changes implemented | Reopening change introduced in paragraph two. |

## Confirming all three timeframes

Both **payment upgrades** (QR codes and rounded prices) belong to changes already implemented, the one-third shortfall is **background**, and October counting is the **upcoming plan**.

# Answer

The shortfall exceeding one-third is background problem, digital scan payment and rounded prices are changes implemented, and twice-daily stock logging starting October is the upcoming plan.
