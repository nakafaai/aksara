# Given

Nisa's change-counting mistake happens on **Monday morning** (paragraph two) when the motorcycle-taxi rider pays with a fifty-thousand bill while a noisy queue of high school students presses behind him.

# Asked

All details that describe the stall at the exact moment Nisa miscounts the change.

# Solution

Imagine pausing **a movie right on the Monday morning scene**, ignoring what happens after school or on Tuesday morning. Only details taking place inside that exact rushed moment qualify.

> In multi-select questions anchored to a specific scene, keep only events happening during that moment and exclude later fixes or next-day scenes.

## Checking the two Monday morning details

Paragraph two sets the rushed scene explicitly:

- **Four uniformed high school students shouting orders while holding out coins** is true, matching <Highlight>empat anak berseragam putih abu-abu berteriak memesan sambil menyodorkan uang receh</Highlight> (four high school students shouted orders while holding out small change).
- **Nisa hurrying to serve the students after handing back change** is true, matching <Highlight>Nisa menyerahkan kembalian, lalu buru-buru melayani anak-anak itu</Highlight> (Nisa handed over the change, then rushed to serve those kids).

## Why the other two details belong to later scenes

- **Sorting coins into plastic cups** happens later after school in paragraph three.
- **The rider pointing at the cardboard sign** happens the next day, Tuesday morning, in paragraph four.

# Answer

The two correct details are that **four uniformed students shouted orders while holding out coins, and Nisa rushed to serve them right after handing back the change**.
