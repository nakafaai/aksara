# Given

In the **Tanjung Rejo Solar Streetlamp** report, an oral survey of <InlineMath math="60" /> residents finds **<InlineMath math="47" /> feel safer** walking at night, yet the same installers who mounted the lamps asked the questions face to face and several residents admitted feeling hesitant to complain.

# Asked

Which evaluation best judges the survey finding of <InlineMath math="47" /> out of <InlineMath math="60" /> residents.

# Solution

Imagine cooking a pot of soup and **asking your guests face to face**, "My soup tastes great, right?" Polite friends may nod yes even if it needs salt. The feedback sounds encouraging, yet who asks the question can tilt the answers.

> Surveys run face to face by project installers carry courtesy bias, so positive impressions must be read with caution.

## Balancing the finding and its bias in paragraph four

Paragraph four reports both facts side by side: <Highlight><InlineMath math="47" /> orang merasa lebih aman berjalan pada malam hari</Highlight> (<InlineMath math="47" /> felt safer walking at night), while <Highlight>survei dilakukan secara lisan oleh petugas yang juga memasang lampu, dan beberapa warga mengaku segan menyampaikan keluhan secara langsung</Highlight> (the installers ran the survey orally and some residents felt shy about complaining).

Hence **the data show a positive impression, yet answers may be influenced because residents were asked directly by the installers**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Proving crime fell** leaps far beyond perceived safety.
- **Unusable because the group is tiny** overstates the flaw, <InlineMath math="60" /> responses still give an initial impression.
- **Saying <InlineMath math="13" /> residents felt less safe** misreads the remainder, who could simply feel unchanged.
- **Showing nightly lamp outages** confuses resident polling with technician outage logs.

# Answer

The best evaluation is that **the data show a positive impression, yet responses may be skewed because residents were interviewed directly by the lamp installers**.
