# Given

The **Solar Streetlamps** report moves chronologically from **problems discovered** in April (paragraph two), to **actions already taken** in May (paragraph three), to **next-stage plans** in paragraph five.

# Asked

How to assign four report statements to the exact report section where each belongs.

# Solution

Think of fixing **a school garden**: what broke (problem), what you already fixed yesterday (completed action), and what you will add tomorrow (next plan). Time clues in each sentence separate all three cleanly.

> In report-structure matrices, tense and time markers distinguish discovered problems, completed actions, and upcoming plans cleanly.

## Mapping all four statements to their paragraphs

Paragraph two reports <Highlight>panel di bawah pohon ketapang tertutup daun dan debu</Highlight> (panels covered with leaves and dust), and paragraph three logs <Highlight>warga membersihkan panel setiap hari Jumat</Highlight> (Friday panel cleaning).

Paragraph five plans <Highlight>akan mencatat curah hujan setiap hari</Highlight> (daily rainfall logging) and <Highlight>Kotak saran tanpa nama juga akan dipasang</Highlight> (an anonymous suggestion box).

| Statement | Report section | Textual clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anonymous suggestion box will be installed | Next-stage plan | Future marker *will be installed* in paragraph five. |
| Panels under ketapang trees covered in leaves and dust | Problem found | Outage cause discovered in paragraph two. |
| Residents clean panels every Friday | Action already taken | Maintenance performed during May in paragraph three. |
| Rainfall will be logged daily | Next-stage plan | Future marker *will record* in paragraph five. |

## Confirming time markers across sections

Both **future-tense** items belong to the next-stage plan, leaves and dust name the **problem**, and May Friday cleaning names the **completed action**.

# Answer

**Panels covered in leaves and dust belong to problems found, Friday panel cleaning belongs to actions already taken, and both the anonymous suggestion box and daily rainfall logging belong to next-stage plans**.
