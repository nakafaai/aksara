# Given

**Paragraph two of Solar Streetlamps** logs nightly outages before 05.00 climbing from two in March to five in April after consecutive rainy days, and reports the technician finding panels under ketapang trees covered by leaves and dust so batteries failed to charge fully.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph two.

# Solution

Imagine a note about **your bike suddenly pedaling harder in week two**, followed by inspecting the chain and finding it caked in mud. The paragraph's heart pairs the worsening problem with the physical cause found on site.

> The main idea of a field report paragraph joins the observed trend with the direct physical finding uncovered by inspection.

## Pairing the outage spike with the field discovery

Paragraph two presents both points back to back: <Highlight>Pada bulan April, angkanya naik menjadi lima</Highlight> (in April, the figure rose to five outages per night), and <Highlight>Teknisi menemukan bahwa panel di bawah pohon ketapang tertutup daun dan debu sehingga baterai tidak terisi penuh</Highlight> (the technician found shaded panels covered in leaves and dust, keeping batteries from charging fully).

Together, the main idea is that **nightly outages rose in April, and the technician found obstructed panels preventing full battery charging**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Recording exactly at 05.00** misreads *before* 05.00 and misses the core point.
- **Cutting down ketapang trees** is never proposed, villagers move two poles and clean panels instead.
- **Working worse than grid lamps** and **rain damaging batteries inside metal boxes** are never claimed in the text.

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph two is that lamp outages rose in April, and the technician discovered obstructed panels keeping batteries from charging fully.
