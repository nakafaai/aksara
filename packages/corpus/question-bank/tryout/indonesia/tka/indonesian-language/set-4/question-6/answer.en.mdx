# Given

In **Nisa's Change Coins**, Nisa accidentally gives ten thousand rupiah too much change during a rush, upgrades her counter setup with a sign and sorted coin cups, receives the extra bill back from the honest rider the next morning, and gets a warm hug plus a thank-you line from her mother.

# Asked

The main idea of the whole story.

# Solution

Imagine summing up **a short movie in one sentence** for a friend. You cannot stop at the opening mistake, you connect the mistake, the protagonist's smart fix, and the heartwarming resolution at the close.

> A story's central idea links its triggering problem, the character's constructive response, and the value affirmed by the ending.

## Connecting the full story arc

First, Nisa discovers <Highlight>kaleng uangnya kurang sepuluh ribu</Highlight> (her cash tin is short ten thousand) and writes <Highlight>Kembalian dihitung dua kali. Mohon sabar sebentar</Highlight> (change is counted twice) while sorting coins.

Next, the honest rider returns the bill and her mother adds <Highlight>Terima kasih untuk pembeli yang jujur</Highlight> (thank you to honest buyers).

Together, **a change-counting mistake pushes Nisa to improve her workflow and leads to honesty being appreciated**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **A buyer deliberately cheating her** reverses the rider's honesty.
- **Her mother scolding her** contradicts <Highlight>Ibunya tidak memarahinya. Ia memeluk Nisa</Highlight> (her mother did not scold her and hugged her).
- **The stall closing** and **Nisa quitting to study** never occur in the story.

# Answer

The main idea is that a change-counting mistake motivates Nisa to improve how she works and culminates in honesty being valued.
