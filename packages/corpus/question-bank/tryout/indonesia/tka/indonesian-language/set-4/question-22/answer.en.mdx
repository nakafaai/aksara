# Given

The **paragraph right after the Honesty Canteen table** contrasts two interpretations of shortfalls dropping from <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}180{.}000" /> to <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}56{.}000" />: cooperative chair Dimas credits growing honesty, while advisor Bu Ratna cautions about stock-counting errors and adjustment to new payment rules.

# Asked

The main idea of the paragraph immediately following the table.

# Solution

Imagine two people staring at **the exact same improving score chart**: one cheers "everyone got more honest," while the other says "hold on, counting errors and getting used to the rules could also explain it." The paragraph's heart is contrasting those two interpretations.

> The main idea of a contrast paragraph captures how two perspectives interpret the same evidence differently.

## Placing Dimas and Bu Ratna side by side

Paragraph four contrasts both views cleanly: Dimas asserts <Highlight>Selisihnya turun terus. Teman-teman makin jujur</Highlight> (the gap keeps shrinking, friends are more honest), whereas <Highlight>Guru pembina koperasi, Bu Ratna, lebih berhati-hati</Highlight> (advisor Bu Ratna is more cautious) and notes possible stock-counting errors plus growing familiarity with payment methods.

Thus **the shrinking shortfall is interpreted differently by the cooperative chair and the faculty advisor**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Dimas proving every student paid** fails because September still had a <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}56{.}000" /> gap.
- **Bu Ratna opposing reopening** is false, she only urges careful interpretation.
- **September shortfall rising** reverses the falling table figures.
- **Stock miscounts as the sole cause** exaggerates Bu Ratna saying *some* of the gap might come from miscounts.

# Answer

The main idea is that the declining shortfall is interpreted differently by the cooperative chair and the faculty advisor.
