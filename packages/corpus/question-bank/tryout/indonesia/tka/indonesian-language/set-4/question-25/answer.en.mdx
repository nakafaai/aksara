# Given

In paragraph four of **Honesty Canteen**, Bu Ratna warns that part of the shortfall may arise from **errors when logging stock**. In paragraph five, the cooperative plans starting October to **log stock twice daily** (before opening and after closing) to separate recording mistakes from unpaid items.

# Asked

How the twice-daily stock-logging plan relates to Bu Ratna's view.

# Solution

Imagine **a teammate warning**, "Maybe apples seem missing because we miscounted before opening." Starting tomorrow, you count every morning and afternoon. Your new routine directly tests and answers your teammate's concern.

> A follow-up measurement plan directly addresses and tests the methodological doubt raised in the preceding paragraph.

## Linking the caution to the October plan

Paragraph four notes Bu Ratna's worry about <Highlight>salah hitung saat stok dicatat</Highlight> (miscounts while logging stock). Paragraph five answers it directly: twice-daily logs aim to <Highlight>membedakan selisih karena kesalahan pencatatan dari selisih karena barang yang tidak dibayar</Highlight> (distinguish recording errors from unpaid goods).

Thus **the plan responds to Bu Ratna's doubt about possible stock-counting mistakes**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Proving Bu Ratna wrong** misreads an objective test designed to separate two causes fairly.
- **Eliminating cash**, **raising prices**, and **Dimas rejecting QR codes** never appear in the article.
