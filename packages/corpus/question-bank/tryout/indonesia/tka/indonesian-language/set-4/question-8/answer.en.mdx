# Given

At the close of **Nisa's Change Coins**, her sign reading "Change is counted twice. Please be patient" wins praise from a customer who agrees to wait patiently, and her mother adds a thank-you sentence right below Nisa's writing.

# Asked

Which event is most likely to happen at Nisa's stall after the story ends.

# Solution

Imagine posting **a neat counter rule** that immediately makes lines calmer, earns praise from customers, and gets backed by your parent. Naturally, you keep practicing that steady habit whenever the line grows long.

> The most plausible continuation sustains the constructive habit reinforced by other characters at the close of the story.

## Seeing the new habit reinforced at the ending

The rider praises the sign directly: <Highlight>Tulisannya bagus. Saya juga mau antre lebih sabar</Highlight> (great sign, I want to queue more patiently too). Her mother reinforces it by <Highlight>menambahkan satu kalimat kecil di bawah tulisan Nisa</Highlight> (adding a short sentence beneath Nisa's writing) so the sign stays up.

The most likely continuation is that **Nisa keeps counting change twice even when the queue grows long**.

## Why the other four predictions fail

- **Taking the sign down** clashes with her mother adding onto it to stay posted.
- **Refusing large bills**, **her mother banning her from the stall**, and **raising cake prices** all clash with her mother's supportive hug and the ten thousand rupiah already returned in full.

# Answer

Most likely, Nisa continues counting change twice even when the queue grows long.
