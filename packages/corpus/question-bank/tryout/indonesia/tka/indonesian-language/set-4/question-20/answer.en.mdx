# Given

At the end of paragraph two of **The Chess Draw**, Grandfather finally gives up his rook on move twenty. Paragraph three then narrates everything on the board until the game finishes in a draw.

# Asked

All statements that match how the match unfolds after Grandfather sacrifices his rook.

# Solution

Imagine replaying **the board moves from move twenty to the finish**: Bima attacks eagerly, Grandfather's pawns creep forward on the left, and Bima scrambles back to block promotion until only two kings remain in a draw, never a surrender.

> In chronological multi-select items, verify each choice strictly against the paragraph covering that exact phase of events.

## Checking the three verified events in paragraph three

Paragraph three states all three developments explicitly:

- **Bima keeps attacking eagerly** is true, matching <Highlight>Bima makin bersemangat dan terus menyerang</Highlight> (Bima grew more eager and kept attacking).
- **Grandfather's small pawns advance slowly on the left side** is true, matching <Highlight>bidak-bidak kecil kakek yang perlahan maju di sisi kiri papan</Highlight> (Grandfather's pawns slowly advancing on the left side of the board).
- **Bima pulls his pieces back to block the pawn near promotion** is true, matching <Highlight>Bima terpaksa menarik pasukannya mundur untuk menghadang</Highlight> (Bima had to pull his pieces back to block it).

## Why the fourth option fails

- **Grandfather admitting defeat** flatly contradicts the draw: only two kings remained and <Highlight>Tidak ada yang bisa menang. Remis</Highlight> (neither could win, a draw).

# Answer

**The three matching statements are that Bima kept attacking eagerly, Grandfather's pawns advanced slowly on the left, and Bima pulled back his pieces to block the pawn about to promote**.
