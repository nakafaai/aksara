# Given

**Classroom Noise** organizes its facts into **three clear roles**: acoustic causes of echo in paragraph one, low-cost damping steps in paragraph three, and measurement limits in paragraph four.

# Asked

How to assign four statements to cause of the problem, improvement effort, or measurement limitation.

# Solution

Think of fixing **a glaring sunny room**: bare glass is the cause, hanging fabric curtains is the fix, and checking brightness with a phone camera is a measurement limit. Every statement slots neatly into one of those three roles.

> In text-role matrices, match each statement directly to the function of the paragraph that introduces it.

## Mapping all four statements to their roles

Paragraph one notes <Highlight>lantai keramik, dan langit-langit beton memantulkan sebagian besar bunyi</Highlight> (floors and ceilings reflect sound), while paragraph three notes <Highlight>memasang tirai tebal di jendela yang menghadap jalan</Highlight> (thick curtains on road windows).

Paragraph four flags <Highlight>Aplikasi ponsel tidak seteliti alat ukur akustik, dan pengukuran dilakukan di kelas kosong</Highlight> (phone-app limits and empty-room testing).

| Information | Role in text | Passage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measured in an empty classroom | Measurement limitation | Differs from a room filled with <InlineMath math="30" /> students in paragraph four. |
| Ceramic floor and concrete ceiling reflect sound | Cause of the problem | Acoustic source of echo in paragraph one. |
| Thick curtains hung on road-facing windows | Improvement effort | Low-cost sound-damping measure in paragraph three. |
| Phone app less accurate than acoustic meter | Measurement limitation | Instrument precision caveat in paragraph four. |

## Checking both measurement caveats

Both **testing in an empty room** and **phone-app precision** sit together in paragraph four as **measurement limitations**.

# Answer

**Ceramic floors and concrete ceilings are a cause of the problem, hanging thick curtains is an improvement effort, and both testing in an empty room and limited phone-app accuracy are measurement limitations**.
