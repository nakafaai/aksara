# Given

In **The Chess Draw**, Bima feels disappointed after almost winning before Grandfather's pawn forces a draw. Grandfather smiles and says, **"You watched your knight, yet forgot to watch the whole board,"** then turns the board around to coach Bima openly.

# Asked

Which emotional response best fits Grandfather's remark.

# Solution

Imagine a coach smiling and saying, "You watched your own dribble, yet forgot the open teammate," before walking you through the play together. Honest feedback given warmly stings gently at first, then sparks **motivation** to grow.

> A fitting emotional reaction to warm, accurate coaching blends mild self-awareness with fresh motivation to improve.

## Reading both accuracy and warmth in the advice

Paragraph five highlights the tone: <Highlight>jawab Kakek sambil tersenyum</Highlight> (Grandfather answered with a smile) while pinpointing Bima's blind spot: <Highlight>Kamu melihat kudamu, tapi lupa melihat seluruh papan</Highlight> (you watched your knight, yet forgot the whole board). Right afterward he turns the board to coach aloud, and Bima treasures the lesson.

Thus the fitting response is feeling **gently stung yet motivated because the critique is accurate and delivered warmly**.

## Why the other four emotions miss the tone

- **Feeling mocked or belittled** contradicts Grandfather's warm smile and patient review.
- **Satisfied that he won** is factually false because the match ended in a draw.
- **Afraid Grandfather will quit playing** and **confused as unrelated** clash with their immediate replay from move twenty.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is feeling gently stung yet motivated because the critique is accurate and delivered warmly.
