# Given

After Monday's miscount, **Nisa posts a cardboard sign** reading "Change is counted twice. Please be patient" and organizes coin cups. The next morning, the motorcycle-taxi rider reads that sign at the front of the stall.

# Asked

Which detail most strongly proves that Nisa's action influences another person's attitude.

# Solution

Imagine hanging **a friendly reminder to queue calmly** outside a shop, and a customer points at it saying, "Nice sign, I want to wait more patiently too." A direct pledge sparked by your action is the clearest proof of influence.

> The strongest proof that a character influences others is a direct response or new commitment triggered right after seeing the character's action.

## Spotting the direct reaction to the sign

Paragraph four captures that direct link: the rider points at Nisa's cardboard sign and says, <Highlight>Tulisannya bagus. Saya juga mau antre lebih sabar</Highlight> (great sign, I also want to queue more patiently).

Thus **the rider saying he wants to queue more patiently after reading Nisa's sign** directly proves her action shaped someone else's attitude.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Nisa losing focus in class** and **sorting coins into cups** concern only Nisa herself.
- **Noticing extra change back at the stand** happened on Monday before the sign even existed.
- **Four students shouting orders** happened before Nisa changed anything.

# Answer

**The strongest proof is that the motorcycle-taxi rider says he also wants to queue more patiently after reading Nisa's sign**.
