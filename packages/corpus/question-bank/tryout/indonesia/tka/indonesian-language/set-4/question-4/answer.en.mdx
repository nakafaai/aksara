# Given

Paragraph one of **Solar Streetlamps** notes each lamp carries a **panel at the top of the pole** and a battery inside a metal box, and paragraph two explains that when leaves and dust cover the panel, the battery cannot charge fully during the day.

# Asked

The most accurate meaning of the loanword *panel* in this passage.

# Solution

Picture **the dark flat strip at the top of a solar calculator**. On a solar streetlamp, the flat piece mounted atop the pole that collects daylight to charge the battery is the *panel*.

> A multi-meaning loanword like *panel* is defined by the physical position and working function given in the surrounding sentences.

## Checking physical position and function in the text

Paragraph one places it clearly: <Highlight>Setiap lampu memiliki panel di puncak tiang dan baterai di dalam kotak logam</Highlight> (each lamp has a panel atop the pole and a battery in a metal box). Paragraph two links it to charging: covered by leaves, <Highlight>baterai tidak terisi penuh pada siang hari</Highlight> (the battery does not charge fully during the day).

Thus *panel* means **a flat plate capturing sunlight mounted at the top of the pole**.

## Why the other four meanings miss the context

- **An expert review group** and **a village noticeboard** are unrelated senses of the word *panel*.
- **The metal battery box** is explicitly named as a separate component right beside the panel.
- **A timer switch** never appears in the passage.

# Answer

In this context, *panel* means the flat sunlight-capturing plate mounted at the top of the pole.
