# Given

In the final paragraph of **Nisa's Change Coins**, Nisa honestly tells her mother everything after fearing a sigh or scolding. Instead, **her mother does not scold her**, hugs her warmly, and adds "Thank you to honest customers" beneath Nisa's sign.

# Asked

Which emotional response best fits Nisa's mother's reaction at the end of the story.

# Solution

Imagine bracing for a scolding after admitting a slip, only to receive **a calm hug** and watch your parent write a thank-you note to the honest stranger who helped. Your heart naturally feels deeply **touched**.

> A well-grounded emotional response echoes the warmth, calm support, and gratitude displayed at the story's close.

## Reading the gentle response in paragraph five

The closing paragraph highlights both gestures: <Highlight>Ibunya tidak memarahinya. Ia memeluk Nisa</Highlight> (her mother did not scold her and hugged Nisa) and wrote <Highlight>Terima kasih untuk pembeli yang jujur</Highlight> (thank you for honest customers).

Her calm grace evokes feeling **touched because the mother responds calmly to Nisa's confession and honors the buyer's honesty**.

## Why the other four emotions miss the mark

- **Disappointment over no punishment** overlooks that Nisa had already learned and fixed her system.
- **Amusement treating it as a joke** clashes with the tender hug and sincere gratitude.
- **Worry that the stall will close** and **anger at the rider** contradict the heartwarming ending.

# Answer

The most fitting emotional response is feeling touched because the mother responds calmly to Nisa's confession and appreciates the customer's honesty.
