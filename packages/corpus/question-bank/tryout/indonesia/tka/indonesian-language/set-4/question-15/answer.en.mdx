The text's closing idea is that simple materials placed **on the main reflecting surfaces** can already reduce reverberation. Paragraph one also explains that hard, smooth surfaces bounce sound back. The application that stays true to that idea is putting **soft materials** like cloth or cork on the hall walls that reflect the most sound.

- Banning talking goes against the text's point that the room itself shapes the noise.
- A louder speaker adds even more sound to bounce around.
- Glossy paint makes walls reflect more, the opposite of the goal.
