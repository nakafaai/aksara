# Given

The **Honesty Canteen** news report uses four everyday Indonesian loanwords in a school context: *koperasi*, *stok*, *kantin*, and *informasi*.

# Asked

All statements that accurately define these loanwords according to their context in the passage.

# Solution

Imagine writing **a mini pocket glossary** using the clues around each word in the article. *Stok* is counted morning and evening as merchandise inventory, while *informasi* appears on the Friday noticeboard. Be careful not to swap a word with an object sitting beside it.

> Context defines a loanword through the verbs and phrases attached directly to it in the passage.

## Verifying the two accurate definitions

- ***Stok* means the supply of goods sold at the canteen** is true, matching <Highlight>mencatat stok dua kali sehari</Highlight> (recording stock twice daily before opening and after closing).
- ***Informasi* means details or announcements shared with students** is true, matching sales and shortfalls being <Highlight>diumumkan di papan informasi</Highlight> (announced on the information board every Friday).

## Why the other two definitions fail

- ***Koperasi* is not a scan payment room**, it is the school cooperative organization managing the canteen.
- ***Kantin* is not the cash box**, it is the snack shop itself, whereas the money box is called *kotak bayar*.

# Answer

**The two correct statements are that *stok* means the inventory of goods sold in the canteen and *informasi* means announcements or details shared with students**.
