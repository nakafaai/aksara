# Given

Pak Darmo shares that **his wife planted the mango tree thirty years ago**. She **always shared its mangoes with every neighbor**, and since she passed away, he did not know whom to give them to anymore.

# Asked

The reader's most fitting emotional response to Pak Darmo's story about his wife.

# Solution

Imagine a quiet older neighbor sharing a tender memory of his late wife's generosity and hoping her kindness can live on. Hearing love and loss woven into a single fruit tree naturally makes a reader feel **deeply moved**.

> A heartfelt memory of a late loved one's kindness invites empathy and feeling moved rather than suspicion, fear, or amusement.

## Reading the emotion in paragraph four

Pak Darmo's words carry gentle grief and enduring love: <Highlight>Pohon itu ditanam istri saya tiga puluh tahun lalu</Highlight> (his wife planted that tree thirty years ago) and <Highlight>Dulu ia selalu membagikan mangganya kepada semua tetangga</Highlight> (she always shared with neighbors).

Since she passed away, he no longer knew whom to give the fruit to.

Therefore, the fitting response is **feeling moved because the tree preserves his wife's memory and habit of sharing**.

## Why the other four feelings fail

- **Annoyed at blaming Ayu** invents blame where he actually welcomes her.
- **Afraid of being scolded** misreads his gentle nostalgia as anger.
- **Amused** clashes with grief over a late spouse.
- **Suspicious of lies** has zero support since his generous actions match his story.

# Answer

The most fitting response is **feeling deeply moved because the mango tree holds his late wife's memory and generous spirit**.
