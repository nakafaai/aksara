# Given

The passage features four loanwords in clear context: **poster** (at the neighborhood hall), **saset** (tiny single-use pouch), **plastik** (nearly unrecyclable packaging layer), and **aluminium** (lightweight metal forming one packaging layer).

# Asked

All statements that accurately define these loanwords in context.

# Solution

Check each definition directly against **its sentence in the text**: *saset* is a small single-use packet and *aluminium* is a lightweight metal layer, whereas a *poster* is displayed in a public hall (not mailed home) and *plastik* is synthetic packaging (not banana leaf).

> Accurate loanword definitions match the exact material, use, and setting described in the passage.

## Testing all four definitions against the passage

- **Saset means a small single-use packet** is **correct**, matching <Highlight>kemasan saset kecil</Highlight> and <Highlight>isinya pas untuk sekali pakai</Highlight> (sized for one use).
- **Aluminium means a lightweight metal forming one packaging layer** is **correct**, matching <Highlight>beberapa lapis plastik dan aluminium yang direkatkan menjadi satu</Highlight>.
- **Poster means an official letter sent to every house** is **incorrect** because <Highlight>poster di balai kelurahan</Highlight> is a public notice posted at the community hall.
- **Plastik means natural banana-leaf material** is **incorrect** because the text describes synthetic layers that can barely be recycled.

## Confirming the two accurate statements

Only the definitions of **saset** and **aluminium** match the passage.

# Answer

The two accurate statements are that ***Saset* means a small single-use packet** and ***Aluminium* means a lightweight metal used as one layer of the packaging**.
