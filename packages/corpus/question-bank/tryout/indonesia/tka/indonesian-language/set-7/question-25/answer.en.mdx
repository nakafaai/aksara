# Given

In paragraph two, the waste bank reports sachets make up only **about <InlineMath math="8" /> percent by weight** of plastic waste yet **more than <InlineMath math="40" /> percent by piece count**. The next sentence adds that **because they are light and thin**, wind and water easily carry them into drains.

# Asked

The connection between the waste-bank figures and the lightweight nature of sachets in paragraph two.

# Solution

Compare a sack of **feathers** to a sack of stones: tiny total weight yet countless loose pieces that scatter in every breeze. Having only <InlineMath math="8" /> percent of the weight across over <InlineMath math="40" /> percent of all pieces **explains how feather-light sachets drift so easily and clog drains**.

> Pairing a low weight share with a huge item share shows each piece is ultra-light, directly explaining why it scatters into waterways.

## Linking the statistics to drain clogging

Sentence one gives the contrast: <Highlight>sekitar <InlineMath math="8" /> persen dari beratnya, tetapi lebih dari <InlineMath math="40" /> persen dari jumlah lembarnya</Highlight> (around <InlineMath math="8" /> percent of weight, over <InlineMath math="40" /> percent of sheets). Sentence two builds directly on that lightness: <Highlight>Karena ringan dan tipis, saset juga mudah terbawa angin dan air hingga menyumbat selokan</Highlight> (light and thin, so easily carried to clog drains).

Thus, **sachets account for little weight yet many pieces, and that lightness lets them scatter easily and clog drains**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Heavy and sinking** directly reverses *ringan dan tipis* (light and thin).
- **<InlineMath math="40" /> percent of total weight** swaps piece share (<InlineMath math="40" /> percent) with weight share (<InlineMath math="8" /> percent).
- **Easy to recycle** contradicts paragraph one stating they can barely be recycled.
- **Found only at the waste bank** ignores wind and water carrying them into drains.

# Answer

The link is that **sachets make up little weight yet many pieces, and because they are so light, they scatter easily and clog drains**.
