# Given

Monthly library loans at **SMP Cahaya Bangsa** jumped from an average of <InlineMath math="120" /> to <InlineMath math="310" /> books. The principal concludes **"This program made our students love reading"**, while Bu Anisa points out that students are **required to bring a book every morning**, so many borrow purely to follow the rule.

# Asked

The fairest evaluation of the principal's claim that the program made students love reading.

# Solution

Imagine a school requiring every student to **carry a water bottle** each morning, causing bottle sales to double. Does that prove kids suddenly love drinking water? Not necessarily, because compliance alone drives purchases. Hence, the principal's conclusion is **too hasty**.

> A jump in activity caused by a mandatory rule cannot by itself prove genuine enthusiasm without independent behavioral evidence.

## Testing the principal claim against the teacher point

The principal equates loans rising from <InlineMath math="120" /> to <InlineMath math="310" /> with a love of reading. Bu Anisa pinpoints the flaw: <Highlight>sebagian kenaikan terjadi karena siswa wajib membawa buku setiap pagi sehingga banyak yang meminjam buku hanya untuk memenuhi aturan</Highlight> (many borrow simply to meet the daily rule).

Therefore, **the claim is too hasty because higher borrowing may stem from the requirement to bring a book**.

## Why the other four evaluations miss

- **Proven because loans more than doubled** (<InlineMath math="310 \div 120 \approx 2.6" />) still mixes rule-following with true enjoyment.
- **Wrong because loans dropped** reverses the rise from <InlineMath math="120" /> to <InlineMath math="310" />.
- **Right because <InlineMath math="25" /> students read at home** lacks pre-program baseline data and covers only two classes.
- **Irrelevant because teachers read too** confuses program design with evidence validity.

# Answer

The best evaluation is that **the claim is premature because increased borrowing may simply reflect the requirement to bring a book**.
