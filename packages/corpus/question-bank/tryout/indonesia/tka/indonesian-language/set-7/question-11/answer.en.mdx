The principal uses **borrowing figures** to conclude that students **love reading**. Bu Anisa points out the gap: students *wajib membawa buku setiap pagi* (must bring a book every morning), so many borrow **just to follow the rule**. Borrowing a book isn't the same as loving to read. So the claim is too hasty.

- Borrowing did more than double, since <InlineMath math="310 \div 120 \approx 2.6" />, but that number doesn't separate keen readers from students just following the rule.
- Borrowing went up from 120 to 310, not down.
- The 25 students can't be compared with before the program, and they come from just two classes.
- Teachers reading along is part of how the program runs. It doesn't make the claim true or false.
