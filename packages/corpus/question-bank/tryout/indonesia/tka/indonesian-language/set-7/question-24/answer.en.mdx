# Given

The **Sachet Waste** passage organizes its content into **three clear stages**: the sachet waste problem (paragraphs one and two), the bulk-selling trial and its hurdles (paragraph three), and the waste bank's response to the poster (paragraph four).

# Asked

How to sort four statements into **Masalah sampah saset** (sachet waste problem), **Uji coba penjualan curah** (bulk-selling trial), or **Tanggapan terhadap poster** (response to the poster).

# Solution

Follow the **problem, trial, evaluation** structure of the essay: clogged drains belong to *the sachet waste problem*, bringing containers and hygiene doubts belong to *the bulk-selling trial*, and calling the poster overblown belongs to *response to the poster*.

> Each paragraph in a problem-solution report cleanly separates environmental damage, trial operations and friction, and closing evaluation.

## Mapping all four statements to their paragraphs

Paragraph two details the hazard: <Highlight>mudah terbawa angin dan air hingga menyumbat selokan</Highlight> (easily carried by wind and water to clog drains). Paragraph three covers bulk-trial mechanics and hesitation: <Highlight>Pembeli membawa wadah sendiri</Highlight> (bringing own containers) and <Highlight>sebagian lain ragu akan kebersihan barang curah</Highlight> (doubting bulk hygiene).

Paragraph four gives the poster critique: <Highlight>Pengurus bank sampah menilai poster itu terlalu jauh</Highlight> (organizers judged the poster too extreme).

| Statement | Category | Paragraph evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sachets wash away easily and clog drains | Masalah sampah saset | Consequence of light sachets in paragraph two. |
| Shoppers bring their own containers | Uji coba penjualan curah | Bulk-refill practice in paragraph three. |
| Waste-bank organizers call the poster too extreme | Tanggapan terhadap poster | Evaluation of the poster in paragraph four. |
| Some buyers doubt the hygiene of bulk goods | Uji coba penjualan curah | Buyer concern during the trial in paragraph three. |

## Confirming all four placements

Drain clogging belongs to **Masalah sampah saset**, own containers and hygiene doubts belong to **Uji coba penjualan curah**, and the poster judgment belongs to **Tanggapan terhadap poster**.

# Answer

Clogging drains belongs to **Masalah sampah saset**, bringing own containers and doubting bulk hygiene belong to **Uji coba penjualan curah**, and calling the poster too extreme belongs to **Tanggapan terhadap poster**.
