# Given

The **Fifteen-Minute Reading** passage divides its information into **three clear roles**: daily program execution (paragraph one), reported numerical data from the library and survey (paragraphs two and four), and the school's follow-up action (paragraph five).

# Asked

How to sort four statements into **Pelaksanaan program** (program execution), **Data yang dilaporkan** (reported data), or **Tindak lanjut sekolah** (school follow-up).

# Solution

Think of a **morning fitness report**: what students do every morning is *program execution*, recorded counts and survey numbers are *reported data*, and adding new equipment afterward is *school follow-up*. Match each item directly to its paragraph.

> Separate daily routine rules, numerical findings, and closing school decisions so every row lands in the exact right column.

## Mapping all four statements to their source paragraphs

Paragraph one describes the routine: <Highlight>Setiap pagi, siswa membaca buku pilihan mereka sendiri</Highlight> (students read self-chosen books each morning). Paragraphs two and four report two datasets: <Highlight>peminjaman buku naik dari rata-rata <InlineMath math="120" /> menjadi <InlineMath math="310" /> buku per bulan</Highlight> (<InlineMath math="310" /> loans per month) and <Highlight>Dari <InlineMath math="64" /> siswa, <InlineMath math="25" /> orang mengaku membaca di rumah setidaknya dua kali seminggu</Highlight> (<InlineMath math="25" /> of <InlineMath math="64" /> reading at home).

Paragraph five adds the follow-up: <Highlight>menambahkan pojok baca di setiap kelas</Highlight> (adding reading corners).

| Statement | Category | Paragraph evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loans rose to an average of <InlineMath math="310" /> books per month | Data yang dilaporkan | Library count reported in paragraph two. |
| Reading corners added in every classroom | Tindak lanjut sekolah | School's follow-up decision in paragraph five. |
| Students read self-chosen books every morning | Pelaksanaan program | Daily program routine in paragraph one. |
| <InlineMath math="25" /> of <InlineMath math="64" /> students read at home twice a week | Data yang dilaporkan | Survey finding in paragraph four. |

## Checking all column assignments

Morning reading fits **Pelaksanaan program**, both statistics fit **Data yang dilaporkan**, and classroom reading corners fit **Tindak lanjut sekolah**.

# Answer

Reading self-chosen books every morning belongs to **Pelaksanaan program**, <InlineMath math="310" /> monthly loans and <InlineMath math="25" /> of <InlineMath math="64" /> home readers belong to **Data yang dilaporkan**, and classroom reading corners belong to **Tindak lanjut sekolah**.
