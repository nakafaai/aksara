A good report sentence has to **match the data** and **not exaggerate**. The trial involved **six participants**, and the average time fell from **eleven minutes** to **about four** after some signs were moved. The sentence naming the number of participants, both figures, and the cause of the change is the most accurate.

- *All blind people* is far too broad. The trial involved six people at one terminal.
- Paragraph four says *masih ada masalah* (there are still problems), so not every problem is gone.
- The numbers are reversed. Time fell **from** eleven minutes, by <InlineMath math="11 - 4 = 7" /> minutes, to about four.
- The text doesn't compare this terminal with others in Indonesia.
