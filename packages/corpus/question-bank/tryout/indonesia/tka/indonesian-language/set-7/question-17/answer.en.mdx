# Given

On the dark, slick stage, the dancers match the slowed gamelan by **lowering their stance, widening their steps, and slowing their turns**. After extended applause, Bu Ratih hugs each dancer and says, **"We did not dance the way we rehearsed. We danced the way tonight needed."**

# Asked

What Bu Ratih means by "We danced the way tonight needed."

# Solution

Imagine a soccer team rehearsing fast passes on dry grass, then shifting to steady, safe plays in pouring rain and winning cheers. Their coach hugging them and praising how they played means **adapting to the moment was the exact right choice**.

> A closing line's true meaning comes from pairing its words with the speaker's proud hug and the audience's warm applause.

## Reading the closing praise alongside the performance

After longer-than-usual applause, <Highlight>Bu Ratih memeluk mereka satu per satu</Highlight> (Bu Ratih hugged them one by one) and remarked <Highlight>Kita tidak menari seperti yang kita latih</Highlight> followed by <Highlight>Kita menari seperti yang malam ini butuhkan</Highlight> (we danced as tonight required). Slowing down kept them safe and deeply moving.

Her words mean **the dancers adapted their dance to the night's conditions, and that adaptation was right**.

## Why the other four readings fail

- **Forgetting rehearsed moves** ignores that they deliberately adapted on Bu Ratih's cue.
- **Repeating the show another night** never appears because the show succeeded wonderfully.
- **Bu Ratih feeling disappointed** directly contradicts her warm hugs.
- **Audience asking for faster dancing** flips the deliberate slowdown.

# Answer

Bu Ratih's words mean that **the dancers adjusted their choreography to fit the night's conditions, and that adjustment was just right**.
