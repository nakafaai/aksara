# Given

When pouring rain causes **the spotlights to flicker and go out**, several spectators under the tent **switch on their phone flashlights and aim them at the stage**, casting gentle light across the dancers until the song ends.

# Asked

The reader's most fitting emotional response to spectators turning on their phone flashlights.

# Solution

Imagine performing onstage when the power dies, and suddenly the audience raises dozens of phone lights so you can keep going safely. That spontaneous gesture of **shared solidarity** naturally leaves a reader feeling **touched**.

> A spontaneous act of community support keeping a dark performance alive evokes warmth and feeling touched rather than annoyance or disappointment.

## Reading audience support in paragraph four

The passage paints a tender moment: <Highlight>Dari bawah tenda, beberapa penonton menyalakan senter ponsel dan mengarahkannya ke panggung</Highlight> (spectators under the tent turned on phone flashlights and aimed them at the stage), while <Highlight>Cahaya-cahaya kecil itu bergoyang di atas genangan air</Highlight> (little lights shimmered on the puddles).

Thus, the fitting response is **feeling touched because the audience helps keep the performance going when the lights fail**.

## Why the other four feelings fail

- **Annoyed at distraction** forgets that the spotlights had gone completely dark.
- **Amused at bad aim** contradicts the text stating they aimed right at the stage.
- **Worried lights make the floor slick** confuses rain with flashlight beams.
- **Disappointed at staying back** ignores the heavy rain driving them under shelter while they still cheered warmly.

# Answer

The most fitting response is **feeling touched because the spectators pitch in to keep the performance alive when the stage lights go out**.
