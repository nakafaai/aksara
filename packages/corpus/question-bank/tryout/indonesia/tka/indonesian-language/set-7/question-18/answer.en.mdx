The spotlights went out in the rain. The audience, sheltering under the tent, **switched on their phone lights and pointed them at the stage**, so small lights swayed over the puddles. That way they **helped light** the dancers and kept the show going. The natural response is to feel **touched**.

- The light helped rather than disturbed, since the stage lights were out.
- The lights were pointed at the stage, so they weren't misdirected.
- Phone lights don't make a stage slippery. The rain did.
- The audience sheltered from heavy rain but still watched and clapped.
