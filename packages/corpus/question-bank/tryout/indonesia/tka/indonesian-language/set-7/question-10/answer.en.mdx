A good continuation has to follow **the change at the end**. Pak Darmo wants the mangoes shared *dari Bu Darmo* (from Mrs. Darmo), carrying on his wife's habit. That afternoon he sits on the porch pointing out ripe fruit, and the kids see him **laugh for the first time**. So next season, Ayu and the kids will most likely **pick and share mangoes with him again**.

- Fencing the tree goes against his wish to share the mangoes.
- Ayu now knows the mangoes can be shared, so there's no reason to throw them away.
- The kids have picked mangoes with him and seen him laugh, so their fear has faded.
- Ayu's mother only asked for the mangoes to be returned. She never objected to the tree.
