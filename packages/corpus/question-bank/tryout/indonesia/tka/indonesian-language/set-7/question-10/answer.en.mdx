# Given

At the end of **Mango Tree**, Pak Darmo revives his wife's tradition by saying **"Just tell them it is from Bu Darmo."** That afternoon, Ayu and three neighbor kids pick fruit together as Pak Darmo guides them from the porch, and **for the first time the alley kids see him laugh**.

# Asked

The event most likely to happen during the next mango season.

# Solution

Picture a joyful neighborhood tradition just brought back to life, melting years of distance into laughter. When the next harvest arrives, the clearest **next chapter** is Ayu and the neighborhood kids picking and sharing mangoes together with Pak Darmo again.

> A sound fiction prediction continues the warm relationship and shared habit established in the closing scene.

## Following the closing transformation in paragraphs five and six

Pak Darmo restarts his wife's legacy: <Highlight>Bagikan juga kepada anak-anak di gang. Bilang saja dari Bu Darmo</Highlight> (share with the alley kids from Bu Darmo).

The ending seals their new bond: <Highlight>Ayu dan tiga anak tetangga memetik mangga bersama</Highlight> and <Highlight>Untuk pertama kalinya, anak-anak di gang melihatnya tertawa</Highlight> (for the first time, the kids saw him laugh).

Thus, next season **Ayu and the alley children will pick and share mangoes together with Pak Darmo again**.

## Why the other four predictions fail

- **Fencing off the tree** contradicts his wish to share its fruit.
- **Ayu throwing fallen mangoes away** ignores that sharing them is now warmly welcomed.
- **Children staying afraid** ignores that they just harvested happily with him laughing.
- **Ayu's mother demanding the tree be cut** never appears because she only taught honesty.

# Answer

Next mango season, **Ayu and the neighborhood children will most likely pick and share mangoes together with Pak Darmo again**.
