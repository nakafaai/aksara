# Given

In paragraph two, Ayu's mother tells her to return the mangoes, **yet Ayu hesitates** to knock on Pak Darmo's door. Right after *Namun, Ayu ragu* (however, Ayu hesitated), the story lists two things about Pak Darmo that make her reluctant.

# Asked

All details showing why Ayu hesitates to return the mangoes to Pak Darmo.

# Solution

Imagine hesitating before knocking on a new neighbor's door: what holds you back are **his quiet manner** and **rumors that he is stern**, not your mother's rule or a full bucket. Separate what makes Ayu hesitant from what pushes her to act.

> For multi-select cause questions, select only the reasons directly creating hesitation rather than prompts that move the action forward.

## Checking all four details against the hesitation

- **Pak Darmo is known to be quiet and rarely returns greetings** is **correct**, appearing right after her hesitation: <Highlight>Pak Darmo terkenal pendiam dan jarang membalas sapaan</Highlight>.
- **Alley children call Pak Darmo stern** is **correct**, matching the very next line: <Highlight>Anak-anak di gang menyebutnya galak</Highlight> (kids called him harsh).
- **Ayu's mother forbade eating the mangoes** is **incorrect** because that prompted her to return them rather than making her hesitate.
- **The bucket was full on day three** is **incorrect** because that finally gave her the push to knock.

## Confirming both causes of hesitation

Ayu holds back because **Pak Darmo rarely returns greetings** and **neighborhood kids call him stern**.

# Answer

The two correct details are that **Pak Darmo is known to be quiet and rarely returns greetings** and **the neighborhood children call him stern**.
