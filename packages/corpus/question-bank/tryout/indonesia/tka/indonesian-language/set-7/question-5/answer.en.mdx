# Given

In paragraph one, **yellow tactile guide tiles** with raised lines and dots run from the entrance to the waiting hall to guide blind passengers. In paragraph four, **some staff do not yet know the fungsi of those tiles**, allowing vendors to stack merchandise on top of them.

# Asked

The contextual meaning of the loanword *fungsi* in "sebagian petugas belum tahu fungsi ubin itu".

# Solution

Imagine someone stacking boxes in front of an emergency exit because they **do not know what the door is for**. Inside a bus terminal, *fungsi* plainly means the **purpose or role** of the guide tiles for passengers, not a math formula or tile color.

> When a loanword carries technical meanings in other subjects, context inside the passage locks down the everyday sense intended.

## Checking the context of fungsi in paragraph four

Paragraph four describes the breakdown: <Highlight>Beberapa pedagang meletakkan dagangan di atas ubin pemandu, dan sebagian petugas belum tahu fungsi ubin itu</Highlight> (vendors placed goods on the guide tiles, and some staff did not know their function). Because those tiles guide passengers from entrance to waiting hall, *fungsi* means **the use or role of those tiles for passengers**.

## Why the other four meanings fail

- **Relation between two sets of numbers** is the mathematical sense of *function*, off-topic here.
- **Purchase price** and **installation schedule** never appear in the passage.
- **Color and shape** are already visible, while what staff lack is understanding of what the tiles do.

# Answer

In this sentence, *fungsi* means **the purpose or role of the guide tiles for passengers**.
