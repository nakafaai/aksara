*Fungsi* has more than one meaning, so check the context. Paragraph one describes guide tiles with raised lines and dots laid **from the entrance to the waiting room**, to guide blind passengers. Staff who *belum tahu fungsi ubin itu* (don't yet know the tiles' fungsi) don't know **what the tiles are for**, so they let goods cover them. So *fungsi* here means the tiles' purpose or role.

- The sense of a relation between two sets exists in mathematics, but it doesn't fit tiles at a bus terminal.
- The tiles' price isn't discussed.
- The yellow color and raised shape are visible. The problem is that staff don't know their use.
- An installation schedule has nothing to do with goods left on the tiles.
