The main idea has to cover the whole story. Ayu **returns the mangoes** that fell into her yard, even though she's nervous because Pak Darmo seems grumpy. He turns out to talk about his late wife and invites Ayu and the kids to **pick and share the mangoes**. At the end, the kids see him laugh. So the main idea is that Ayu's honesty opens a warm connection with that neighbor.

- Ayu doesn't eat the mangoes. Her mother says no, and she returns them.
- The tree isn't cut down. Its mangoes are picked together.
- The fence-climbing ban is only background to the kids' view of him.
- Ayu's mother and Pak Darmo never argue.
