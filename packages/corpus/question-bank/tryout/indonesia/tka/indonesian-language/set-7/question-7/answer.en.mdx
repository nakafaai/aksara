# Given

In the **Mango Tree** story, Ayu listens to her mother and **returns the mangoes that fell into her yard** despite fearing Pak Darmo's strict reputation. Her honesty unlocks memories of late Bu Darmo, ending with neighborhood kids harvesting fruit together and **seeing Pak Darmo laugh**.

# Asked

The main idea capturing the story from beginning to end.

# Solution

Imagine honestly returning a stray ball to a feared neighbor, only for that simple kindness to break the ice and turn the yard into a place of laughter. **Honesty opening a warm bond with a misunderstood neighbor** is the core arc of the whole story.

> A story's main idea weaves together the opening action, the turning point, and the closing transformation.

## Tracing the full arc from start to finish

Ayu acts honestly despite her nerves: <Highlight>Ayu menjelaskan bahwa mangga-mangga itu jatuh ke halamannya</Highlight> (Ayu explained the mangoes had fallen into her yard). That gesture invites everyone to pick and share fruit, culminating in warmth: <Highlight>Untuk pertama kalinya, anak-anak di gang melihatnya tertawa</Highlight> (for the first time, the alley kids saw him laugh).

Thus, the main idea is that **Ayu's honesty in returning the mangoes opens a warm relationship with a neighbor thought to be stern**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Ayu secretly eating mangoes** contradicts her returning the full bucket.
- **Cutting down the tree** never happens because they harvest its fruit together.
- **Forbidding fence climbing** is only minor backstory.
- **Ayu's mother arguing** never occurs in the story.

# Answer

The main idea of the story is that **Ayu's honesty in returning the mangoes opens a warm bond with a neighbor once thought to be harsh**.
