# Given

The **Arunika Terminal Braille Signs** passage arranges its facts across **three clear stages**: trial timing results (paragraph two), participant suggestions on sign wording and placement (paragraph three), and management's upcoming plans (paragraph four).

# Asked

How to sort four statements into **Hasil uji coba** (trial result), **Masukan peserta** (participant feedback), or **Rencana pengelola** (management plan).

# Solution

Think of a **playground test log**: measured timing goes under *trial results*, changes requested by testers go under *participant feedback*, and scheduled staff training goes under *management plans*. Match each sentence to its source paragraph.

> Action cues such as *waktunya turun* (time dropped), *peserta meminta* (participants requested), and *pengelola berencana* (management plans) assign every row accurately.

## Mapping all four statements to their paragraphs

Paragraph two reports the timing outcome: <Highlight>waktunya turun menjadi sekitar empat menit</Highlight> (time dropped to around four minutes). Paragraph three lists two participant requests: <Highlight>Peserta meminta nama kota tujuan ditambahkan</Highlight> (add destination city names) and <Highlight>meminta semua papan dipasang di sisi kanan pintu setiap jalur</Highlight> (place all signs on the same side).

Paragraph four gives management's plan: <Highlight>Pengelola berencana memberikan pelatihan singkat kepada petugas dan pedagang</Highlight> (short training for staff and vendors).

| Statement | Category | Paragraph evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participants asked for destination city names | Masukan peserta | Content suggestion in paragraph three. |
| Search time dropped to about four minutes | Hasil uji coba | Trial outcome reported in paragraph two. |
| Staff and vendors will receive brief training | Rencana pengelola | Planned action marked by *berencana* in paragraph four. |
| All signs requested on the same side of every lane | Masukan peserta | Placement suggestion in paragraph three. |

## Checking all column placements

The four-minute result fits **Hasil uji coba**, both sign requests fit **Masukan peserta**, and brief training fits **Rencana pengelola**.

# Answer

Search time dropping to about four minutes belongs to **Hasil uji coba**, adding city names and matching sign placement belong to **Masukan peserta**, and brief staff and vendor training belongs to **Rencana pengelola**.
