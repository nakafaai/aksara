# Given

The story establishes **consistent natural patterns and explicit instructions**: Pak Hasan says old teak always talks after sunset, wind through an unlatched shutter rattled the shuttle, jasmine drops pollen naturally at dawn, and Bibi Ratih ends with **"Tell no one, and keep opening the shutters"**.

# Asked

Based on the story, which numbered predictions are reasonable.

# Solution

A reasonable prediction extends **a stated physical cause or an explicit instruction the narrator follows**. Reject any prediction that **disobeys Bibi Ratih's direct order** or **links a natural plant cycle to her hospital stay**.

> Good predictions follow established character habits and stated natural causes without contradicting direct closing instructions.

## Checking each prediction

| Prediction | Reasonable? | Basis in the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Pak Hasan states old teak <Highlight>always talks after sunset</Highlight>, so he would repeat that explanation for another night creak. |
| II | **Yes** | An unlatched shutter let wind rattle the shuttle once, so repeating the slip can repeat the rattle. |
| III | No | Directly contradicts Bibi Ratih's final command: <Highlight>Tell no one</Highlight>. |
| IV | No | Jasmine drops pollen at dawn by natural habit, completely unrelated to Bibi Ratih's return. |
| V | **Yes** | The dutiful narrator has obeyed throughout, and Bibi Ratih reinforces <Highlight>keep opening the shutters</Highlight>. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III** ignore the clear command *Tell no one*.
- **Options including IV** connect jasmine pollen to Bibi Ratih despite its natural cause.
- **I and V only** or **II and V only** drops a well-grounded causal prediction (II or I).

# Answer

The reasonable predictions based on the story are **I, II, and V only**.
