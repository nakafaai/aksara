# Solution

Notice the clean match: the remaining summit stretch is the **exact same <InlineMath math="2" /> km distance** as the forest-to-ridge stretch. Simply **add <InlineMath math="3" /> hours <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes to 11:50**, then compare that arrival time with 13:15.

> When two stretches share the exact same distance, maintaining the same pace means the travel time is identical too.

## Finding summit arrival time and gap after the cloud

The text reports <Highlight>The <InlineMath math="2" /> kilometres from the edge of the forest to a flat shoulder of rock we called the ridge took us three hours and twenty minutes, stops included</Highlight>.

- **Summit arrival**: leaving at 11:50 plus **<InlineMath math="3" /> hours <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes** reaches the summit at **15:10**.
- **Comparison with 13:15**: from 13:15 to 15:10 is **<InlineMath math="1" /> hour <InlineMath math="55" /> minutes after** the cloud reached the ridge.

- **About <InlineMath math="25" /> minutes before** uses Raka's optimistic <InlineMath math="1" />-hour estimate (12:50) rather than the group's real pace.
- **About <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes before** uses the faster pine-forest pace (<InlineMath math="72" /> minutes for <InlineMath math="2" /> km, around 13:02).
- **About <InlineMath math="25" /> minutes after** averages the entire <InlineMath math="7" /> km ascent (around 13:39).
- **About <InlineMath math="2" /> hours <InlineMath math="5" /> minutes after** starts counting from 12:00 (15:20) instead of 11:50 as stated.
