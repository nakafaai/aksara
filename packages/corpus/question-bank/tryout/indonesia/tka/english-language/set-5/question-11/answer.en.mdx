# Given

Clues across the four paragraphs of the songket story link directly together:
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />: Bibi Ratih hands the narrator **the only key** to the weaving room.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" />: Dewi is in Medan and **will not be home for a month**, and the cloth measures **<InlineMath math="2" /> metres** on the first night.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="3" />: the gold dust on the sill is **jasmine pollen**.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="4" />: on the seventh morning (about a week later), the cloth measures **<InlineMath math="2.4" /> metres** while the narrator keeps the key in a pocket.

# Asked

Putting together details from different parts of the story, which numbered statements the reader can conclude.

# Solution

Join the cross-paragraph clues like a careful investigator. Each valid conclusion links **two verified facts across the text** (single key, month-long exam stay, and two cloth measurements), while eliminating the already-explained gold dust.

> Cross-paragraph synthesis pairs early baseline facts with later events to test whether a conclusion truly follows.

## Computing cloth growth and checking each statement

**The cloth's growth from the first night to the seventh morning** is:

<BlockMath math="2.4\text{ m} - 2\text{ m} = 0.4\text{ m} = 40\text{ cm}" />

| Statement | Concluded? | Cross-paragraph evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Bibi Ratih gave <Highlight>the only key to her weaving room</Highlight> and the narrator held it, so nobody else could enter with a key. |
| II | **Yes** | Dewi <Highlight>would not be home for a month</Highlight>. The seventh morning is only about a week in, leaving roughly three weeks before she was due home. |
| III | No | The narrator traced the dust to the garden: <Highlight>the jasmine below the window drops its pollen at dawn</Highlight>. |
| IV | **Yes** | Growing from <InlineMath math="2" /> metres to <InlineMath math="2.4" /> metres equals exactly <InlineMath math="40" /> centimetres. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III** treat the explained jasmine pollen as part of the songket mystery.
- **I and IV only** or **II and IV only** drops either Dewi's month-long absence (II) or the narrator's sole key (I).

# Answer

The reader can conclude **I, II, and IV only**.
