# Given

In this year's evacuation drill, **Staircase B is blocked**, forcing all first-floor classes (**11A, 11B, 11C, 11D**) and second-floor classes (**12A, 12B, 12C**) onto **Staircase A**.

Key timing rules:
- Classes sharing a staircase descend one at a time in alphabetical order, **<InlineMath math="2" /> minutes** each, entering only after the previous class has completely cleared it.
- Walking from the foot of either staircase to the sports field takes **<InlineMath math="2" /> minutes**.

# Asked

Counting from the end of the bell, how many minutes pass before the last staircase-using class reaches the field.

# Solution

Picture all seven upper-floor classes queued neatly at Staircase A: **11A, 11B, 11C, 11D, 12A, 12B, 12C**. Multiply **<InlineMath math="7" /> classes by <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes** to clear the staircase, then **add the <InlineMath math="2" />-minute walk** to the field for the final class.

> The last class in a single-file staircase queue arrives at the field after total staircase clearance time plus one walk to the field.

## Calculating staircase time and arrival at the field

Rules <InlineMath math="3, 4" />, and <InlineMath math="5" /> state <Highlight>If a staircase is blocked, its classes use the other one</Highlight>, <Highlight>Each class takes <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes to clear a staircase</Highlight>, and <Highlight>the walk to the field takes <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes</Highlight>.

All **<InlineMath math="7" /> classes** clear Staircase A in <InlineMath math="14" /> minutes, and 12C then walks <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes to the field:

<BlockMath math="7 \times 2 + 2 = 14 + 2 = 16\text{ minutes}" />

## Why the other options fail

- **Option <InlineMath math="14" />** forgets the <InlineMath math="2" />-minute walk from the staircase to the field.
- **Option <InlineMath math="10" />** calculates the time with both staircases open (<InlineMath math="4" /> classes on Staircase A plus walk).
- **Option <InlineMath math="8" />** counts only the <InlineMath math="4" /> first-floor classes on the stairs with no walk.
- **Option <InlineMath math="18" />** adds the <InlineMath math="2" />-minute walk twice.

# Answer

The last class using a staircase reached the field **<InlineMath math="16" />** minutes after the bell ended.
