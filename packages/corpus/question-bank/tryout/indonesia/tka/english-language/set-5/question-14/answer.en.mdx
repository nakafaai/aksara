# Given

In paragraph three, **all three initial oddities in the weaving room** (night creaks, gold dust on the sill, rattling shuttle) **receive grounded everyday explanations**, right before the seventh morning when the songket grows by <InlineMath math="40" /> cm without explanation.

# Asked

What role the explained noises and gold dust in paragraph three play in the story.

# Solution

Think of paragraph three as a **deliberate contrast**: by proving the narrator actively hunts for rational causes, it makes **the one event that refuses every ordinary explanation (the growing cloth) stand out sharply**.

> Explaining the first three oddities logically does not prove everything is ordinary, it sharpens the contrast around the single true marvel.

## Checking each proposed role

| Statement | Correct? | Narrative function and text evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | The narrator tracks down or accepts ordinary causes for every oddity: cooling teak, <Highlight>the jasmine below the window drops its pollen at dawn</Highlight>, and an unlatched shutter. |
| II | **Yes** | Because everything else is neatly explained, the unexplained <InlineMath math="40" /> cm of new weaving stands out unmistakably. |
| III | No | Paragraph <InlineMath math="3" /> moves in the opposite direction by giving natural causes, not proving a haunting. |
| IV | No | A single slip (<Highlight>a shutter I had forgotten to latch</Highlight>) is accidental forgetfulness, not ignoring Bibi Ratih's two instructions. |
| V | No | Overgeneralisation: three ordinary explanations do not mean *everything* is ordinary, since the cloth's growth receives no ordinary cause. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including V** stretch three ordinary explanations to cover the unexplained growth of the cloth.
- **Options including IV** mistake a minor slip for ignoring instructions.
- **I only** overlooks the structural contrast with the growing cloth in II.

# Answer

The explained noises and gold dust in paragraph three serve **I and II only**.
