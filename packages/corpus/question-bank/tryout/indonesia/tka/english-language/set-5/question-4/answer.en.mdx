# Given

Four **cause statements** explain aspects of Balai Surya's solar system: switching to the grid at <InlineMath math="4" /> kWh, blocking the pump during projector use, daytime battery charging, and rainy-month grid purchases.

# Asked

Which numbered statements give a cause **stated or clearly implied by the text**.

# Solution

Match each proposed reason strictly against **the reason actually given in the passage**. Reject any plausible-sounding guess ("battery too small", "bigger events") when the text states a different cause or gives no such reason.

> Only causes explicitly stated or directly entailed by the passage count, never outside guesses that sound plausible.

## Checking each cause statement

| Statement | Supported cause? | Direct evidence in the text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | A fifth of <InlineMath math="20" /> kWh is <InlineMath math="4" /> kWh, kept <Highlight>because draining it completely would shorten its life</Highlight>, after which the hall switches to the grid. |
| II | No | The text explicitly says the rule <Highlight>comes from the wiring rather than the battery</Highlight> (shared circuit), ruling out battery size. |
| III | **Yes** | <Highlight>During the day the panels power the hall directly and send only the surplus to the battery</Highlight>, meaning the battery gets only unused daytime power. |
| IV | No | The text never claims events are bigger between December and February. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including II** replace the stated wiring-circuit cause with battery size, which the text explicitly denies.
- **Options including IV** invent "bigger events" that appear nowhere in the passage.
- **I only** overlooks statement III, which follows directly from sending only surplus daytime energy to the battery.

# Answer

The statements giving a cause stated or clearly implied by the text are **I and III only**.
