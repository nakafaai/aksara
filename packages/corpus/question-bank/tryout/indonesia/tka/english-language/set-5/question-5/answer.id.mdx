> Ingat selalu dua angka pengunci: baterai penuh hanya memberi <InlineMath math="16" /> kWh siap pakai, dan harga panel surya sama sekali tidak disebut di teks.

# Jawaban

Pernyataan yang didukung oleh teks adalah:
- **On an overcast day, the panels produce less than half of what they produce on a clear day**.
- **Starting full, with everything else switched off (the refrigerator too), the battery can run the fans for <InlineMath math="20" /> hours before the hall switches to the grid**.
- **In one day, the refrigerator uses more energy than the projector uses on a typical event evening**.
