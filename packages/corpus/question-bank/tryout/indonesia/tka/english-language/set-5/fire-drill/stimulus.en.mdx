**Evacuation drill: SMA Tunas Bangsa**

When the long bell rings, every class leaves its room and goes to its marked place on the sports field.

| Floor | Classes |
| --- | --- |
| Ground floor | 10A, 10B, 10C |
| First floor | 11A, 11B, 11C, 11D |
| Second floor | 12A, 12B, 12C |

1. The teacher takes the class register and leaves the room last, closing the door but not locking it.
2. Ground-floor classes leave through the nearest outside door and do not use the stairs. They reach the field in under <InlineMath math="4" /> minutes.
3. First-floor classes use Staircase A, and second-floor classes use Staircase B. If a staircase is blocked, its classes use the other one, and the teacher reports the change at the field.
4. Classes on the same staircase go down in alphabetical order of class name, and a class may not enter a staircase until the class ahead of it has completely left it. Each class takes <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes to clear a staircase, and the first class enters as soon as the bell ends.
5. From the bottom of either staircase, the walk to the field takes <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes.
6. At the field, the teacher counts the class against the register and raises a green card if everyone is present, or a red card if anyone is missing. A student who was in the toilet or the library when the bell rang goes straight to the field and joins the class there. The teacher counts that student only when the student arrives.
7. The drill ends when every class shows a green card. The school's target is to finish within <InlineMath math="8" /> minutes of the bell.
