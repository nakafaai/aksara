# Given

The climb's timeline runs clearly: start at **5:30**, reach the pine-forest edge <InlineMath math="3" /> hours later (**8:30**), Pak Bayu puts Laras in front above the forest, reach the ridge at **11:50**, Raka speaks there, **ten minutes later (12:00 sharp)** Laras turns the group around, and the cloud hits the ridge at **13:15**.

# Asked

Which numbered events happened **before 12:00**.

# Solution

Place all five events on the morning clock from 5:30 to 13:15. Catch the precision trap in V: **12:00 sharp is not before 12:00**!

> "Before 12:00" strictly excludes an event that takes place at 12:00 itself.

## Placing each event on the clock

| Event | Clock time | Before 12:00? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I (Pak Bayu puts Laras at the front) | Above the pine forest, between 8:30 and 11:50 | **Yes** |
| II (Raka says they can reach the summit in an hour) | At the ridge, between 11:50 and 12:00 | **Yes** |
| III (The cloud reaches the ridge) | 13:15 (<Highlight>The cloud reached the ridge at 13:15</Highlight>) | No |
| IV (Group reaches the edge of the pine forest) | 8:30 (5:30 plus three hours) | **Yes** |
| V (Laras turns the group around) | **12:00 exactly** (<Highlight>Ten minutes later, Laras, still in front, turned us around</Highlight> after 11:50) | No (at 12:00, not before) |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including V** mistakenly count an event at 12:00 sharp as happening before 12:00.
- **Options including III** ignore the 13:15 timestamp for the cloud.
- **I and IV only** leaves out Raka's comment (II), spoken between 11:50 and 12:00.

# Answer

The events that happened before 12:00 are **I, II, and IV only**.
