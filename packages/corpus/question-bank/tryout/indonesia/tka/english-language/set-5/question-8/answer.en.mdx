Place each event on the morning's clock. The group left at 5:30, reached the forest edge three hours later (8:30), reached the ridge at 11:50, and turned back "Ten minutes later", which is 12:00.

- **I happened before 12:00**. Pak Bayu put Laras at the front above the forest, after 8:30 and before 11:50.
- **II happened before 12:00**. Raka spoke at the ridge, between 11:50 and 12:00.
- III happened after. The cloud reached the ridge at 13:15.
- **IV happened before 12:00**. The forest edge came at 8:30.
- V didn't happen before 12:00. **Laras turned the group around at 12:00 exactly**, ten minutes after 11:50.

So the events before 12:00 are I, II and IV only.
