A fact can be checked, while an opinion is a **personal judgment**. *I also think the museum was the most important part* uses *I think* and rates the museum as *the most important* part. Someone else could judge differently, and the writer even chose the darkness as the most memorable moment earlier. So this sentence is an opinion.

- The departure and arrival times can be checked, so that's a fact.
- What the museum displays can be checked too.
- Pak Darto asking everyone to switch off their lamps is something that happened.
- The teacher's task on the bus is also an event, not a judgment.
