# Given

The **[GAP]** in paragraph three sits directly after noting that **the first <InlineMath math="5" /> km through the pine forest took <InlineMath math="3" /> hours, including two rests of a quarter of an hour each**. Earlier we know the hike began at **5:30** on a **<InlineMath math="9" /> km** trail.

# Asked

Which numbered sentences could fill **[GAP]** without contradicting anything in the account.

# Solution

Test all four sentences with **three quick checks** from the figures right around the gap: 5:30 plus <InlineMath math="3" /> hours, <InlineMath math="5" /> km out of <InlineMath math="9" /> km, and two <InlineMath math="15" />-minute rests combined.

> Check every gap-fill candidate against the start time, total distance, and rest durations given in the surrounding sentences.

## Checking each candidate sentence

The sentence just before **[GAP]** says <Highlight>The first <InlineMath math="5" /> kilometres, through the pine forest, took us three hours, including two rests of a quarter of an hour each</Highlight>.

| Sentence | Verdict | Check against text figures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Fits | Starting at 5:30 and walking <InlineMath math="3" /> hours reaches the forest edge at **8:30** (*half past eight*). |
| II | Contradicts | Half past nine (9:30) would mean <InlineMath math="4" /> hours, not <InlineMath math="3" /> hours. |
| III | Fits | <InlineMath math="5" /> km out of the <InlineMath math="9" /> km trail is **more than half** (<InlineMath math="4.5" /> km). |
| IV | Contradicts | Two rests of a quarter hour (<InlineMath math="15" /> minutes each) total **<InlineMath math="30" /> minutes**, not a full hour. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including II or IV** clash with the <InlineMath math="3" />-hour forest walk or the <InlineMath math="30" />-minute combined rest time.
- **I only** omits sentence III, which accurately compares <InlineMath math="5" /> km against the <InlineMath math="9" /> km trail.

# Answer

The sentences that can fill **[GAP]** without contradicting the account are **I and III only**.
