# Given

**Five sunset event plans** run with the **refrigerator (<InlineMath math="0.15" /> kW) on throughout**. Two rules control the outcome:
- **Wiring rule**: the water pump may not run while the projector or sound system is on.
- **Battery rule**: usable energy equals the sunset battery charge **minus the <InlineMath math="4" /> kWh floor**.

# Asked

Whether each plan runs on **the battery alone**, **needs the grid**, or is **not allowed as planned**.

# Solution

Check every plan in two tidy steps: **test the wiring rule first** (does the water pump overlap with the projector or sound system?), then **compare total kWh needed against usable charge above the <InlineMath math="4" /> kWh floor**.

> Never compare energy demand against the raw sunset battery level: subtract the protected <InlineMath math="4" /> kWh floor first.
