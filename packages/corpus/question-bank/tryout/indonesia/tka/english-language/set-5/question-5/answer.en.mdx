A good closing sentence has to continue the paragraph's topic. Paragraph two describes the **three lanes**, and its last sentence covers the west lane, which is *kept clear of stalls* for strollers and wheelchairs. A sentence about yellow tape marking the edges of this open lane still describes the west lane, so it fits best as the ending.

- The band's new song is news about the show, and the show belongs to paragraph three.
- The history of night markets in China wanders away from this market.
- Advice to readers turns a description into a recommendation.
- The benches in front of the stage belong to paragraph three, not to the lane layout.
