A realistic story contains events that **could happen** in the real world. Borrowing a film camera, losing a lens cap, winning second place in a photo contest, and buying a new cap are all possible. There's no magic and nothing breaks the laws of nature. So the story is realistic.

- The gull *frozen in the air* describes the photo, not a bird that actually froze. A photo really does capture motion in a single instant.
- A roll of film really does hold a limited number of pictures, such as 24.
- The contest prize at the end is ordinary, nothing magical.
- A lesson doesn't make a story a fairy tale. Realistic stories can teach something too.
