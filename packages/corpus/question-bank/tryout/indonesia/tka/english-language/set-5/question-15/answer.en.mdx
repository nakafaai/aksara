# Given

**Five events** are judged for real-world possibility: (I) cooling wood creaking, (II) gold dust on a sill, (III) a songket growing <InlineMath math="40" /> cm in a locked room while its weaver is in hospital, (IV) wind rattling a shuttle, and (V) a songket growing <InlineMath math="40" /> cm while its weaver works on it.

# Asked

Which numbered events **could happen in the real world**.

# Solution

Compare III and V side by side! Both mention **<InlineMath math="40" /> centimetres**, yet the crucial detail changes everything: **cloth cannot weave itself in an empty locked room (III)**, whereas **a living weaver adding <InlineMath math="40" /> cm in a week (V) is completely ordinary**.

> Judge the whole situation, not just a familiar number: the same <InlineMath math="40" /> cm is impossible without a weaver and completely ordinary with one.

## Checking all five events against reality

| Event | Possible in reality? | Reasoning and text link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Wood contracts as night temperatures drop and creaks (<Highlight>old teak shrinks as it cools</Highlight>). |
| II | **Yes** | Pollen from flowering plants like jasmine settles easily on a windowsill each morning. |
| III | **No** | Cloth cannot weave itself inside a locked room with nobody at the loom. |
| IV | **Yes** | A breeze blowing through an unlatched shutter can easily rattle a loose shuttle. |
| V | **Yes** | A weaver actively working on a loom can normally weave <InlineMath math="40" /> cm in a week. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options omitting V** (such as I, II, and IV only) react blindly to the number <InlineMath math="40" /> cm while overlooking that the weaver is working on it.
- **Options including III** accept the one supernatural event with nobody at the loom.

# Answer

The events that could happen in the real world are **I, II, IV, and V only**.
