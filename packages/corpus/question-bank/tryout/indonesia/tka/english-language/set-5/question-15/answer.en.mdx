Judge each event on its own, without the story around it.

- **I could happen**. Wood shrinks as it cools and creaks, just as Pak Hasan says.
- **II could happen**. Pollen from a flowering plant can settle on a sill every morning.
- III couldn't happen. Cloth doesn't weave itself: with the weaver in hospital and the room locked, nobody is at the loom.
- **IV could happen**. Wind through an open shutter can rattle a loose shuttle.
- **V could happen**. A weaver adding <InlineMath math="40" /> centimetres in a week is ordinary work. The same length is strange in the story only because nobody was weaving.

The options without V react to the number instead of the situation. The option with III accepts the one event that needs magic.

So the events that could happen are I, II, IV and V only.
