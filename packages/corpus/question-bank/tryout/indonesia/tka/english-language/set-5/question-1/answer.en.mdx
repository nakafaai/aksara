# Given

At sunset on an event evening, **Balai Surya's <InlineMath math="20" /> kWh battery is full**, yet its controller **never lets it drop below one fifth (<InlineMath math="4" /> kWh)** before switching to the village grid.

Equipment running during the <InlineMath math="5" />-hour window:
- Ceiling lights (<InlineMath math="0.6" /> kW) for <InlineMath math="5" /> hours.
- All eight fans (<InlineMath math="0.8" /> kW) for <InlineMath math="5" /> hours.
- Sound system (<InlineMath math="0.5" /> kW) for <InlineMath math="4" /> hours.
- Refrigerator (<InlineMath math="0.15" /> kW) running as always for all <InlineMath math="5" /> hours.

# Asked

At the end of those five hours, how much more energy the hall can draw from the battery before switching to the grid.

# Solution

Picture a <InlineMath math="20" />-litre water tank whose bottom <InlineMath math="4" /> litres must stay untouched. Only **<InlineMath math="16" /> kWh is actually usable**. Subtract the five-hour total of all four appliances (including the refrigerator) from <InlineMath math="16" /> kWh.

> Subtract the battery's protected floor first, and never forget continuous loads like the refrigerator during the event window.

## Computing energy used and remaining usable charge

The text specifies <Highlight>The battery holds <InlineMath math="20" /> kWh, but its controller never lets it fall below a fifth of that</Highlight>. With a <InlineMath math="4" /> kWh floor, **usable charge, evening consumption, and remaining energy** are:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Usable charge: } 20 - 4 = 16\text{ kWh}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{Used: } 0.6 \times 5 + 0.8 \times 5 + 0.5 \times 4 + 0.15 \times 5 = 3 + 4 + 2 + 0.75 = 9.75\text{ kWh}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{Remaining above floor: } 16 - 9.75 = 6.25\text{ kWh}" />
</MathContainer>

## Why the other options fail

- **Option <InlineMath math="10.25" /> kWh** subtracts from the full <InlineMath math="20" /> kWh, forgetting the <InlineMath math="4" /> kWh floor.
- **Option <InlineMath math="7" /> kWh** omits the refrigerator (<InlineMath math="0.75" /> kWh over five hours).
- **Option <InlineMath math="3.4" /> kWh** charges a full <InlineMath math="24" />-hour day of refrigerator use (<InlineMath math="3.6" /> kWh) instead of five hours.
- **Option <InlineMath math="7.4" /> kWh** makes both errors together (full-day refrigerator and no floor).

# Answer

The hall can still draw **<InlineMath math="6.25" /> kWh** from the battery before switching to the grid.
