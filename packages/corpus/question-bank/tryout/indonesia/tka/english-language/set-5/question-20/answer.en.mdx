# Given

After the drill, a teacher explains that **everyone reached the field except Bima, who had been in the library**, so the teacher **raised a red card and went back inside to look for him**.

Rules <InlineMath math="6" /> and <InlineMath math="7" /> require a red card whenever anyone is missing, send library students straight to the field, count them only on arrival, and end the drill only when every class holds up a green card.

# Asked

Which numbered replies to the teacher are supported by the procedure.

# Solution

Check both parts of the teacher's behaviour against **Rules <InlineMath math="6" /> and <InlineMath math="7" />**: raising the red card was **right**, re-entering the building had **no basis in the procedure** because Bima heads straight to the field, and one red card keeps the entire drill running.

> A green card certifies physical arrival at the field, never an assumption that a missing student is safe indoors.

## Checking each reply against the procedure

| Reply | Supported? | Direct evidence in the procedure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Rule <InlineMath math="6" /> demands <Highlight>a red card if anyone is missing</Highlight> and counts an away student <Highlight>only when the student arrives</Highlight>. |
| II | **Yes** | Rule <InlineMath math="6" /> instructs that a library student <Highlight>goes straight to the field and joins the class there</Highlight>, giving no reason to search inside. |
| III | No | Green cards apply only <Highlight>if everyone is present</Highlight> on the field, and Bima had not arrived yet. |
| IV | **Yes** | Rule <InlineMath math="7" /> states <Highlight>The drill ends when every class shows a green card</Highlight>, so the drill waits until Bima arrives. |

## Why the other options fail

- **I, II, III, and IV** allows a green card for a student not physically present on the field.
- **I and II only**, **II and IV only**, and **I and IV only** drop a clear requirement from Rule <InlineMath math="6" /> or Rule <InlineMath math="7" />.

# Answer

The replies supported by the procedure are **I, II, and IV only**.
