Wilting means the seedlings lost more water than they took up. A step explains it only if skipping it leaves them shorter of water.

- **I can explain it**. Without the watering first, the roots come out without their soil and take up water poorly.
- **II can explain it**. With only <InlineMath math="250" /> ml in the dry season, each seedling gets half what it needs.
- **III can explain it**. Without shade for the first days, the sun dries the young plants.
- IV can't. The distance between rows doesn't change how much water a seedling gets.
- V can't. Where the first seedling stands doesn't matter for water.

The option without I misses what happens to the roots. The options with IV or V count spacing rules as causes of wilting.

So the steps that could explain it are I, II and III only.
