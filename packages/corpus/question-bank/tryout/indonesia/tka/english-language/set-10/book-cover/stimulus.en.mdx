How to Cover a Textbook with Clear Plastic

A clear plastic cover keeps a textbook clean and dry for the whole school year, and you can still read the title through it. At the end of the year, the plastic can be peeled off and the book returned in good condition. Covering one book takes about ten minutes.

You will need a roll of clear plastic cover, scissors, a ruler, a pencil, and clear tape.

1. Open the book and lay it flat on the plastic, with the covers facing down.
2. Using the ruler and pencil, mark a line on the plastic 5 centimeters outside the book on every side. Cut along the line.
3. At the top and bottom of the spine, cut a narrow V shape out of the plastic, from the edge of the plastic to the spine.
4. Fold the plastic along the top and bottom edges over the inside of the front and back covers.
5. Fold the side flaps over the inside of the front and back covers, so that they hold the folded top and bottom edges in place.
6. Tape each corner of the flaps to the plastic, not to the pages of the book.
7. Open and close the book a few times. If the cover feels tight, loosen the side flaps a little.

Tip: Write your name on a sticker and put it on the outside of the plastic. In my opinion, a book you have covered yourself is a book you take better care of.
