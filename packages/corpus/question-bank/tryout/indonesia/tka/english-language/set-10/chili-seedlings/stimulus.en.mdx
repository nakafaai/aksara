**Planting out chili seedlings**

1. Water the seedling tray well <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes before you start, so that the roots come out with their soil.
2. Plant out only seedlings with at least <InlineMath math="4" /> true leaves. Seedlings with fewer stay in the tray for another week.
3. Lay out the bed in rows. Put the first row along one long edge of the bed, then another row every <InlineMath math="70" /> cm, as long as it fits inside the bed, the far edge included.
4. Along each row, put the first seedling at one end and then one every <InlineMath math="50" /> cm, the far end included.
5. Water each seedling with <InlineMath math="250" /> ml every morning. In the dry season, give <InlineMath math="500" /> ml.
6. Shade the bed with palm leaves for the first <InlineMath math="3" /> days.
