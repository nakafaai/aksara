**Text 1: a letter from the environment club of SMA Pelita**

SMA Pelita should build <InlineMath math="60" /> bike racks and stop students riding motorbikes to school. Most of our students are under <InlineMath math="17" /> and should not be riding motorbikes at all. Last year, SMA Harapan built bike racks, and the number of students cycling to school rose from <InlineMath math="40" /> to <InlineMath math="110" />, while late arrivals fell. Cycling is simply the best start to a school day, and no other change could do so much for so little.

**Text 2: a reply from Pak Yudi, vice principal of SMA Pelita**

I agree with the club that too many of our students ride motorbikes without a licence, and the cycling figures from SMA Harapan are striking. But SMA Harapan's racks were not the only change. In the same term, the town opened a cycle lane on the main road to SMA Harapan, and fuel prices rose. Nobody kept lateness figures there for earlier years, so we cannot say lateness fell. And a third of our students live more than <InlineMath math="10" /> km from school. I propose that we build <InlineMath math="30" /> racks first, keep the motorbike park for now, and count cyclists every month, comparing each month with the same month last year.
