# Given

Summary of the library loan table and rules:
- **Book**: <InlineMath math="14" /> days plus up to <InlineMath math="2 \times 7" /> days = **<InlineMath math="28" /> days**, fine <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500" />/day.
- **Magazine**: **<InlineMath math="7" /> days**, no renewals, fine <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}300" />/day.
- **DVD**: <InlineMath math="3" /> days plus <InlineMath math="1 \times 3" /> days = **<InlineMath math="6" /> days**, fine <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" />/day.
- Members may hold up to <InlineMath math="6" /> items (**at most <InlineMath math="2" /> DVDs**), and members under <InlineMath math="12" /> pay half the fine.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral comparison statements are supported by the text.

# Solution

Compute each item's **maximum loan span** including renewals, compare the exact fine totals, and check the DVD cap directly against the rules.

> Careful arithmetic on maximum durations and halved fines immediately separates the two supported comparisons from the three numerical traps.

## Testing each comparison against the table

Maximum durations and the fine comparison in statement II give:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Max book: } 14 + 7 + 7 = 28\text{ days}, \qquad \text{Max DVD: } 3 + 3 = 6\text{ days} \\
\text{DVD 2 days: } 2 \times \text{Rp}2{,}000 = \text{Rp}4{,}000 > \text{Book 7 days: } 7 \times \text{Rp}500 = \text{Rp}3{,}500
\end{aligned}" />

| Statement | Supported? | Evidence from table and rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | A book (<InlineMath math="28" /> days) beats both a magazine (<InlineMath math="7" /> days) and a renewed DVD (<InlineMath math="6" /> days). |
| II | **Yes** | Two late DVD days (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000" />) cost more than seven late book days (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}500" />). |
| III | No | Half the DVD fine is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" />/day, double the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500" />/day adult book fine. |
| IV | No | Members may hold <Highlight>no more than <InlineMath math="2" /> of them DVDs</Highlight>, not <InlineMath math="3" />. |
| V | No | A renewed DVD lasts <InlineMath math="6" /> days, one day shorter than a magazine's <InlineMath math="7" /> days. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with III** miscalculate half of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> as <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500" />.
- **Options with V** equate a <InlineMath math="6" />-day renewed DVD with a <InlineMath math="7" />-day magazine.

# Answer

The supported comparison statements are **I and II only**.
