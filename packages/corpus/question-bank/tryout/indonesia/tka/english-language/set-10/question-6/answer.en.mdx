# Asked

# Solution

Verify the total marks, true laps, how Bu Sinta uncovered the error, and the **exact refund scope** while rejecting details the account never reports.

> Marking the starting line inflated each runner's tally by one, turning twenty-nine real laps into thirty-three recorded marks.

## Checking each summary statement

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with III** misread one lap per runner as one lap in total.
- **Options with IV** invent an after-event detail absent from the story.
- **Options without I** omit the core tallying error.

# Answer

The statements that belong in an accurate summary are **I, II, and V only**.
