# Given

Five statements test due dates on a renewed book, a Sunday-due DVD, a child's DVD fine, a magazine renewal, and the maximum item limit.

# Asked

All statements supported by the library table and rules (select every correct option).

# Solution

Test each statement independently using day <InlineMath math="0" /> for borrowing day, the renewal column, the under-<InlineMath math="12" /> half-fine rule, and the <InlineMath math="6" />-item borrowing cap.

> Three options satisfy every rule exactly, while the remaining two violate the no-renewal rule for magazines and the six-item borrowing limit.

## Testing all five statements against the rules

| Statement | Supported? | Calculation and textual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book borrowed Saturday and renewed once is due Saturday three weeks later | **Yes** | Total loan is <InlineMath math="14 + 7 = 21" /> days, exactly <InlineMath math="3" /> weeks from day <InlineMath math="0" />. |
| DVD borrowed Thursday falls due Sunday and may return Monday fine-free | **Yes** | Thursday plus <InlineMath math="3" /> days is Sunday, and <Highlight>An item that falls due on a Sunday may be returned on the Monday without a fine</Highlight>. |
| Member under <InlineMath math="12" /> returning a DVD <InlineMath math="3" /> days late (no Sunday) pays <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" /> | **Yes** | Half the DVD fine is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" />/day, giving <InlineMath math="3 \times \text{Rp}1{,}000 = \text{Rp}3{,}000" />. |
| Magazine borrowed Monday may be renewed next Monday | No | The table lists **none** for magazine renewals, and renewals must occur before the due date anyway. |
| Member holding <InlineMath math="4" /> books and <InlineMath math="2" /> DVDs may also borrow a magazine | No | Adding a magazine makes <InlineMath math="7" /> items, exceeding <Highlight>hold up to <InlineMath math="6" /> items at a time</Highlight>. |

## Why the other two options fail

- **Renewing a magazine** directly breaks the zero-renewal rule for magazines.
- **Borrowing a seventh item** breaks the hard limit of <InlineMath math="6" /> items held at once.

# Answer

The supported statements are **A book borrowed on a Saturday and renewed once is due on the Saturday three weeks later.**, **A DVD borrowed on a Thursday falls due on the Sunday, so it may come back on the Monday without a fine.**, and **A member under <InlineMath math="12" /> who returns a DVD <InlineMath math="3" /> days late, with no Sunday among them, pays <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" />.**.
