# Given

Six gardening actions must be classified against the chili-seedling guide:
- First row along one long edge, seedlings every <InlineMath math="50" /> cm including both ends, watering <InlineMath math="250" /> ml per seedling (<InlineMath math="500" /> ml in the dry season), and **palm-leaf shade** for the first <InlineMath math="3" /> days.

# Asked

Whether each action **Follows the instructions**, **Breaks the instructions**, or is **Not covered**.

# Solution

Match each action to its exact step and avoid treating silence on an extra practice (such as rice straw mulch) as a **rule violation**.

> Outside the dry season seedlings need half the dry-season water, and silence on rice straw means not covered rather than forbidden.

## Classifying all six planting actions

| Statement | Category | Evidence from guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Putting the first row <InlineMath math="35" /> cm in from the long edge | **Breaks** | Step <InlineMath math="3" /> directs <Highlight>Put the first row along one long edge of the bed</Highlight>. |
| Giving <InlineMath math="36" /> seedlings <InlineMath math="18" /> litres each morning in the dry season | **Follows** | At <InlineMath math="500" /> ml each in the dry season, <InlineMath math="36 \times 0.5 = 18" /> litres. |
| Putting <InlineMath math="9" /> seedlings along a <InlineMath math="4" /> m row | **Follows** | Every <InlineMath math="50" /> cm including both ends gives <InlineMath math="400 \div 50 + 1 = 9" /> seedlings. |
| Covering the soil with rice straw | **Not covered** | The instructions mention nothing about rice straw. |
| Giving <InlineMath math="36" /> seedlings <InlineMath math="18" /> litres outside the dry season | **Breaks** | Outside the dry season the rule is <InlineMath math="250" /> ml each (<InlineMath math="36 \times 0.25 = 9" /> litres, not <InlineMath math="18" />). |
| Taking palm-leaf shade off on the fourth morning | **Follows** | Step <InlineMath math="6" /> keeps shade <Highlight>for the first <InlineMath math="3" /> days</Highlight>, so removing it on morning four matches. |

## Common pitfalls to avoid

- Applying the dry-season <InlineMath math="18" />-litre amount year-round misclassifies statement 5.
- **Treating silence on rice straw as a ban** misclassifies statement 4.

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="6" />** **Follow the instructions**, statements **<InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="5" />** **Break the instructions**, and statement **<InlineMath math="4" />** is **Not covered**.
