# Given

A neighbour says: *"Fifty centimetres between plants wastes space. I plant mine <InlineMath math="25" /> cm apart and get twice as many plants in a row."*

Relevant guide rules:
- Step <InlineMath math="4" />: put one seedling every **<InlineMath math="50" /> cm** along each row.
- Step <InlineMath math="5" />: water **each seedling** with <InlineMath math="250" /> ml every morning (<InlineMath math="500" /> ml in the dry season).

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral replies to the neighbour are supported by the instructions.

# Solution

Check which replies reflect step four's **within-row spacing rule** and step five's per-seedling watering rule, rejecting invented exceptions about row spacing or seasons.

> Doubling the number of seedlings in a row both breaks the fifty-centimetre spacing rule and doubles morning water demand because watering is measured per seedling.

## Evaluating each reply against the guide

| Statement | Supported? | Evidence from guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Step <InlineMath math="4" /> sets one seedling <Highlight>every <InlineMath math="50" /> cm</Highlight> along each row. |
| II | **Yes** | Because step <InlineMath math="5" /> waters <Highlight>each seedling</Highlight> individually (<InlineMath math="250" /> ml or <InlineMath math="500" /> ml), twice as many plants need twice as much water every morning. |
| III | No | Keeping rows <InlineMath math="70" /> cm apart never waives the <InlineMath math="50" /> cm spacing rule inside each row. |
| IV | No | Spacing rules apply year-round, not only in the dry season. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Option I only** leaves out the direct watering consequence from step five.
- **Options with III** approve the neighbour's denser planting under a condition absent from the guide.
- **Options with IV** limit spacing rules to the dry season without textual basis.

# Answer

The supported replies are **I and II only**.
