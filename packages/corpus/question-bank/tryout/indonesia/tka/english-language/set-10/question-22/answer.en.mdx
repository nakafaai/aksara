# Given

Text 2 is Vice Principal Pak Yudi's reply to the environment club:
- He agrees too many students ride motorbikes without a licence and calls SMA Harapan's cycling figures striking.
- He names two rival explanations (a new cycle lane and higher fuel prices), points out that a third of students live more than <InlineMath math="10" /> km away, and proposes building <InlineMath math="30" /> racks first while keeping the motorbike park for now.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral argumentative moves Text 2 makes.

# Solution

Check each candidate move directly against Pak Yudi's reply, separating his partial agreement and phased <InlineMath math="30" />-rack proposal from outright rejection of racks or numbers.

> Pak Yudi neither disputes SMA Harapan's cycling numbers nor rejects bike racks altogether, proposing thirty racks first alongside monthly tracking.

| Statement | Made by Text 2? | Evidence from Text 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with III** confuse questioning the cause with rejecting the cycling numbers.
- **Options with V** ignore that Pak Yudi explicitly proposes building <InlineMath math="30" /> racks as a first step.

# Answer

Text 2 makes moves **I, II, and IV only**.
