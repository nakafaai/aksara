# Asked

# Solution

Recognise the mistake as a **constant offset of plus one mark** per runner: identical in size, identical in money cost, and completely preserving the ranking of runners.

> A fixed one-mark error adds the same two thousand rupiah to every runner regardless of distance and leaves their distance ranking untouched.
