# Given

Two texts debate building <InlineMath math="60" /> bike racks and stopping motorbike riding at SMA Pelita:
- **Text 1 (environment club)** judges cycling the best start to a school day, argues students should not ride motorbikes at all, and cites SMA Harapan.
- **Text 2 (Pak Yudi)** notes a new cycle lane and rising fuel prices in that same term at SMA Harapan, plus the lack of earlier lateness records at SMA Harapan.

# Asked

Whether each statement is **Stated as a fact**, **Stated as an opinion**, or **Not stated in either text**.

# Solution

Separate **verifiable events** reported in the texts from value judgements and unstated claims, watching carefully for causal leaps and swapped school names.

> Naming rising fuel prices as a confounding factor is not claiming they caused the rise, and lateness is discussed only for SMA Harapan, never SMA Pelita.

## Sorting facts opinions and unstated claims

| Statement | Classification | Evidence from the texts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cycling is simply the best start to a school day | **Opinion** | Club value judgement: <Highlight>Cycling is simply the best start to a school day</Highlight>. |
| Fuel prices rose in the same term as SMA Harapan built racks | **Fact** | Reported factually by Pak Yudi: <Highlight>In the same term, the town opened a cycle lane on the main road to SMA Harapan, and fuel prices rose</Highlight>. |
| Rising fuel prices caused the rise in cycling at SMA Harapan | **Not stated** | Pak Yudi raises fuel prices as an alternative factor without claiming they caused the increase. |
| Nobody kept lateness figures at SMA Harapan for earlier years | **Fact** | Stated factually by Pak Yudi: <Highlight>Nobody kept lateness figures there for earlier years</Highlight>. |
| SMA Pelita's students should not be riding motorbikes at all | **Opinion** | Normative view from the club: <Highlight>should not be riding motorbikes at all</Highlight>. |
| Late arrivals at SMA Pelita fell last year | **Not stated** | The only lateness claim in either text concerns SMA Harapan, not SMA Pelita. |

## Common traps to watch for

- Marking statement 3 as stated turns a **possible rival factor** into a causal claim Pak Yudi never makes.
- Marking statement 6 as stated transfers SMA Harapan's lateness claim onto SMA Pelita.

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />** are **Stated as a fact**, statements **<InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **Stated as an opinion**, and statements **<InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="6" />** are **Not stated in either text**.
