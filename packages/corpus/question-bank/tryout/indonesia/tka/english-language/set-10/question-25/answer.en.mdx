# Given

Positions of the two writers:
- **Environment club (Text 1)** wants <InlineMath math="60" /> bike racks and an end to students riding motorbikes, citing SMA Harapan.
- **Pak Yudi (Text 2)** accepts the cycling figures, notes other changes happened at SMA Harapan that term, and proposes building <InlineMath math="30" /> racks, keeping the motorbike park for now, and comparing monthly cyclist counts with the same month last year.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral statements belong in an accurate summary of the two texts.

# Solution

Keep every proposal matched to the right writer and avoid turning Pak Yudi's caution about confounding factors into proof that racks had no effect.

> An accurate summary preserves both attribution and restraint: Pak Yudi raises alternative factors without proving the racks failed, and keeping the motorbike park is his proposal rather than the club's.

## Checking each summary statement

| Statement | Belongs? | Evidence from both texts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | The club proposes to <Highlight>build <InlineMath math="60" /> bike racks and stop students riding motorbikes to school</Highlight>, citing SMA Harapan. |
| II | **Yes** | Pak Yudi calls the cycling figures striking yet cautions <Highlight>SMA Harapan's racks were not the only change</Highlight>. |
| III | No | He lists other possible causes without ever proving that the racks failed to raise cycling. |
| IV | **Yes** | Matches his exact plan to <Highlight>build <InlineMath math="30" /> racks first, keep the motorbike park for now</Highlight>, and compare monthly counts with the same month last year. |
| V | No | Keeping the motorbike park is Pak Yudi's proposal, whereas the club wants motorbike riding stopped. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with III** exaggerate Pak Yudi's doubt into negative proof he never offers.
- **Options with V** attribute Pak Yudi's compromise plan to the environment club.
- **Options without II** leave out Pak Yudi's main objection to the SMA Harapan comparison.

# Answer

The statements that belong in an accurate summary are **I, II, and IV only**.
