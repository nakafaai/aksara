A realistic story has events that **could happen** in real life. Boarding a bus with a similar number, asking the driver, and arriving late is an everyday experience. There's no magic. So the story is realistic.

- Bus 7B passes rice fields because its route is different, not by magic.
- The story doesn't explain how the bus got its name.
- The driver is a kind person, not an animal.
- A nearly dead phone battery is completely ordinary.
