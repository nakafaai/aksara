Judge each event on its own, without the story around it.

- **I could happen**. Seed kept watered and damp for a week can sprout, and fresh wax can hide that the jar was opened.
- **II could happen**. A cloth can be pressed and smoothed back without breaking the wax.
- III couldn't happen. Seed needs water to sprout, and a finger's length takes days, not one dry night.
- **IV could happen**. A second key opens a lock while the first stays on its nail.
- **V could happen**. Geckos live in roof beams and rustle at night.

Leaving out I or IV rejects an event only because the story says nobody opened the jar, or that there is only one key, not because the event is impossible.

So the events that could happen are I, II, IV and V only.
