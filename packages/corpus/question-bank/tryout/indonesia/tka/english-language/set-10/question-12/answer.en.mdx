A narrative is built from **orientation, complication, and resolution**. The orientation introduces Nadia's first solo trip and her mother's route card. The complication starts when Nadia **looks up and sees rice fields**, then realizes the sign says **7B**. That's where the problem begins.

- Her mother writing the route is orientation.
- Reaching the bakery is the resolution.
- The aunt's note closes the story.
- Checking the sign twice is part of solving the problem.
