Each statement joins details from different paragraphs.

- **I can be concluded**. Kakek hangs "the only key on a nail above his bed", it hung there "All night", and the narrator slept beside him.
- **II can be concluded**. "The wax seal was whole" until Kakek "cut the wax".
- III can't be concluded. Dimas only pressed the cloth to test the wax on another morning.
- **IV can be concluded**. "The rice was as dry as sand and no water had been near it."

The options with III carry Dimas's test over to the sprouting. The options without I or II leave out what makes the morning hard to explain.

So the reader can conclude I, II and IV only.
