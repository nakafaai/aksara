Look at what Nadia does after realizing she's on the wrong bus. Her phone is nearly dead, so she **asks the driver**, follows his directions, and **checks the bus sign twice** at the market. She gets to the bakery, late but on her own. That kind of problem-solving is best described as **resourceful**.

- She doesn't lose the card. She grips it tightly, and her aunt writes on the back.
- She asks in a small voice. She doesn't argue.
- She walks to the bakery as the card says.
- She's clearly worried, since *Her stomach dropped*.
