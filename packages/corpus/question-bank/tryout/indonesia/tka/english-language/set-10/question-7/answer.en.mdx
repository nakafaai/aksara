# Given

Fun-run pledge figures for the four runners:
- Total recorded marks across all four runners (<InlineMath math="9" />, <InlineMath math="7" />, <InlineMath math="12" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />): **<InlineMath math="33" /> marks**.
- Pledge rate: **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> per lap**.
- Pak Guntur refunded **one lap per runner** across all four runners.

# Asked

How much money Pak Guntur refunded in all, and how much the sponsors paid in the end.

# Solution

Multiply the <InlineMath math="33" /> recorded marks by <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> to get the **initial collection**, subtract <InlineMath math="4" /> refunded laps (one per runner), and verify the final total equals <InlineMath math="29" /> real laps.

> Refunding one lap for each of the four runners returns four times two thousand rupiah out of the initial thirty-three-mark collection.

## Calculating refund and final payment

The account sets the rate at <Highlight><InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> for every lap</Highlight> and reports returning <Highlight>the money for one lap per runner</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Collected: } 33 \times \text{Rp}2{,}000 = \text{Rp}66{,}000, \qquad \text{Refunded: } 4 \times \text{Rp}2{,}000 = \text{Rp}8{,}000 \\
\text{Paid in the end: Rp}66{,}000 - \text{Rp}8{,}000 = \text{Rp}58{,}000 \quad (29 \times \text{Rp}2{,}000)
\end{aligned}" />

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> back, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}64{,}000" /> paid** refunds only one lap total instead of one per runner.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}8{,}000" /> back, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}66{,}000" /> paid** forgets to subtract the refund from the initial collection.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}16{,}000" /> back, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}50{,}000" /> paid** refunds two laps per runner.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}8{,}000" /> back, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}74{,}000" /> paid** adds the refund instead of subtracting it.

# Answer

Pak Guntur gave **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}8{,}000" /> back, and <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}58{,}000" /> paid in the end**.
