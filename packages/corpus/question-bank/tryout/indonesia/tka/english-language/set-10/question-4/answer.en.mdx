A cause-and-effect statement is supported only if the text gives both the effect and the link.

- **I is supported**. Day <InlineMath math="0" /> is the day of borrowing, so <InlineMath math="14" /> days later is the same weekday.
- **II is supported**. The Sunday is free, and the Monday is the one late day.
- **III is supported**. A renewal "runs from the old due date", so the remaining days aren't lost.
- IV isn't supported. Items can be renewed "online", so the closed library doesn't stop a Sunday renewal.
- V isn't supported. The limit of <InlineMath math="6" /> items is the same for everyone.

The options with IV miss the online route. The option with V links two rules the text keeps apart.

So the supported statements are I, II and III only.
