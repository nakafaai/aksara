Pak Yudi doubts the racks because a cycle lane opened and fuel prices rose in the same term. A fact weakens his doubt if it makes the racks a more likely cause, or the lane and the prices less likely ones.

- **I weakens it**. The same rise at a school with racks and no new lane points to the racks.
- II doesn't. A rise that began only after the lane opened points to the lane, which strengthens his doubt.
- III doesn't. New cyclists naming the lane strengthens his doubt too.
- **IV weakens it**. Most new cyclists live far from the lane, so the lane is unlikely to have drawn most of them.

The options with II or III count facts that point to the lane. The option with only I and II misses the fact about where the cyclists live.

So the facts that weaken his doubt are I and IV only.
