The town library lends three kinds of item.

| Item | Loan period | Renewals | Fine for each day late |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Book | <InlineMath math="14" /> days | up to <InlineMath math="2" />, of <InlineMath math="7" /> days each | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500" /> |
| Magazine | <InlineMath math="7" /> days | none | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}300" /> |
| DVD | <InlineMath math="3" /> days | <InlineMath math="1" />, of <InlineMath math="3" /> days | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> |

A loan period counts the day of borrowing as day <InlineMath math="0" />, so a book borrowed on a Monday is due on the Monday two weeks later. The library is closed on Sundays, and no fine is charged for a Sunday. An item that falls due on a Sunday may be returned on the Monday without a fine.

An item can be renewed only before its due date, online or at the desk, and each renewal runs from the old due date. A member may hold up to <InlineMath math="6" /> items at a time, no more than <InlineMath math="2" /> of them DVDs. Members under <InlineMath math="12" /> pay half the fine.
