# Given

In a month starting on **Monday the <InlineMath math="1" />st**, Sundays fall on **<InlineMath math="7" />, <InlineMath math="14" />, <InlineMath math="21" />, and <InlineMath math="28" />**.

Library due-date rules:
- Borrowing day is day <InlineMath math="0" /> (Book <InlineMath math="14" /> days plus up to <InlineMath math="2" /> renewals of <InlineMath math="7" /> days, Magazine <InlineMath math="7" /> days with no renewal, DVD <InlineMath math="3" /> days).
- An item falling due on a **Sunday** may be returned on **Monday without a fine**.

# Asked

Whether each member pays **No fine** or is **Fined**.

# Solution

Add the **loan period and any renewals** directly to the borrowing date since borrowing day is day <InlineMath math="0" />, then check whether a Sunday due date shifts the deadline to Monday.

> The Monday grace rule protects only items actually due on Sunday, whereas an item due on Saturday still incurs a Monday fine even though Sunday itself is free.

## Common pitfalls to avoid

- Counting borrowing day as day <InlineMath math="1" /> **falsely marks** statement 1 as fined.
- Forgetting the Sunday-to-Monday rule falsely marks statement 3 as fined.

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="3" />** pay **No fine**, while statements **<InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **Fined**.
