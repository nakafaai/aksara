# Given

Repeatable patterns established in the story:
- Nighttime rustling came from a gecko living in the storeroom roof beams.
- After every storm the storeroom smells of wet earth because rain gets in under the tiles.
- Pressing the cloth on the jar made it look loose when Dimas tested the wax.
- By contrast, the overnight dry-rice sprouting is a single unexplained event, and the aunt's soaking theory clashes with the dry rice and whole seal.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral predictions are reasonable based on the story.

# Solution

Keep predictions that follow repeatable physical causes established in paragraph two, and reject predictions that expect the lone unexplained event to repeat or the aunt's flawed guess to be confirmed.

> Reasonable predictions extend established everyday causes in the storeroom rather than assuming a one-off mystery will repeat.

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with II** turn the single unexplained mystery into a predictable seasonal pattern.

- **Options without I** omit the gecko explanation already confirmed by Kakek.

# Answer

The reasonable predictions are **I, III, and IV only**.
