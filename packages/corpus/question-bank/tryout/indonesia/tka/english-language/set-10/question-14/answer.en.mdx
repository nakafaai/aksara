A reasonable prediction follows from a pattern or a fact the story sets up.

- **I is reasonable**. The rustling came from "a gecko living in the roof beams".
- II isn't reasonable. The sprouting is the one event nothing explains. Nothing says it will happen again.
- **III is reasonable**. The storeroom smells of wet earth "after every storm, because rain gets in under the tiles".
- **IV is reasonable**. Last time the cloth "looked loose" after Dimas pressed it.
- V isn't reasonable. The rice was dry and the seal whole, which goes against the aunt's idea.

The options with II turn the one unexplained event into a pattern. The option with V backs the explanation the story undercuts.

So the reasonable predictions are I, III and IV only.
