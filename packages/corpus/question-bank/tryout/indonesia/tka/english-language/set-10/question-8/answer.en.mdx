- **Q comes next**. The fifth mark comes at the end of lap <InlineMath math="4" />.

- **T comes "a week later"**, after Bu Sinta compared the sheet with her times.

The order with P before Q treats the fifth mark as the fifth lap. The order starting with Q puts the fifth mark before the first, which was made at the start. The order with S before P has Bu Sinta write the finishing time before Citra finished her fifth lap. The order ending with S puts the refund before the time it was based on.

So the order is R, Q, P, S, T.
