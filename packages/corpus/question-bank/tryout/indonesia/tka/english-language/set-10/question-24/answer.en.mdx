# Given

The environment club (Text 1) and Pak Yudi (Text 2) share **two points of agreement** while disagreeing over causality, closing the motorbike park, and lateness claims.

# Asked

All points accepted by **both writers** (select every correct option).

# Solution

Verify each claim in **both** Text 1 and Text 2, checking the arithmetic for SMA Harapan's cycling rise (<InlineMath math="110 - 40 = 70" />) and excluding every point Pak Yudi disputes.

> Pak Yudi agrees on unlicensed motorbike riding and the seventy-student jump in cycling at SMA Harapan, while rejecting the lateness claim and keeping the motorbike park open for now.

## Checking shared ground between both texts

The increase in cyclists at SMA Harapan accepted by both writers is:

| Too many students ride motorbikes without a licence | **Yes** | The club warns most are under <InlineMath math="17" />, and Pak Yudi replies <Highlight>I agree with the club that too many of our students ride motorbikes without a licence</Highlight>. |

## Why the other three options fail

- Causality of the racks, closing the motorbike park, and falling lateness are each **directly challenged or rejected** by Pak Yudi in Text 2.

# Answer
