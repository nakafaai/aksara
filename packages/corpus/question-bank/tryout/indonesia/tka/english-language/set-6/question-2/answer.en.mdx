# Given

Usage is **<InlineMath math="60" /> litres/person/day**, one tank holds **<InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> litres**, and any part of a day counts as a whole day. # Asked

Whether each household's stored water **Lasts** or **Runs short** before its block's next turn.

# Solution

Watch **Block A's two unequal gaps**: after day four's afternoon turn (17:00 to 5:00) the wait is only <InlineMath math="12" /> hours (**<InlineMath math="1" /> day**), yet after day one's morning turn (9:00 to 15:00 on day four) the wait is <InlineMath math="3" /> days <InlineMath math="6" /> hours, which still rounds up to **<InlineMath math="4" /> whole days**.

> Having two turns does not protect a large Block A household after its morning turn, because a <InlineMath math="3" />-day <InlineMath math="6" />-hour wait still counts as <InlineMath math="4" /> days.
