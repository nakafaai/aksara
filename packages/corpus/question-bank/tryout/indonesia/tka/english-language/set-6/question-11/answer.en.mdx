Each statement joins details from different paragraphs.

- **I can be concluded**. The chain went "through the frame and the back wheel", and that morning it was "still wrapped around the gatepost, the padlock still closed". A frame with a closed chain through it can't be lifted free.
- **II can be concluded**. The narrator "wore the only key on a string", and that morning "the key was still on its string around my neck".
- III can't be concluded. The sister borrowed the bicycle on another occasion, when she pumped the tyres. Nothing ties her to the hundredth morning.
- **IV can be concluded**. The bicycle was at Kakek's grave, and "The cemetery is twelve kilometres from our yard."

The options with III carry the sister's earlier borrowing over to a different morning. The options without I or II leave out the details that make the morning impossible to explain.

So the reader can conclude I, II and IV only.
