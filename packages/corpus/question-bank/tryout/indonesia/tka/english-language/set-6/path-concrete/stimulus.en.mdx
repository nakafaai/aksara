**Mixing concrete for a garden path**

These instructions make concrete for a path <InlineMath math="8" /> centimetres thick. All the amounts are measured by volume, with the same bucket for everything.

1. Mix <InlineMath math="1" /> bucket of cement, <InlineMath math="2" /> buckets of sand and <InlineMath math="3" /> buckets of gravel while they are dry, until the colour is even.
2. Add water slowly, about half a bucket for every bucket of cement, and stop as soon as the mix holds its shape when you squeeze a handful. Too much water makes weak concrete that cracks.
3. Pour within <InlineMath math="90" /> minutes of adding the water. A mix that has started to set must be thrown away, not softened with more water.
4. Cover the poured path with damp sacks and keep them damp for <InlineMath math="7" /> days. Concrete that dries too quickly loses up to a third of its strength.
5. Nobody walks on the path for <InlineMath math="3" /> days, and no cart or motorbike goes on it for <InlineMath math="7" /> days.

One <InlineMath math="40" />-kilogram bag of cement fills about <InlineMath math="2.5" /> buckets. Each full batch of <InlineMath math="1" /> bucket of cement, <InlineMath math="2" /> of sand and <InlineMath math="3" /> of gravel makes enough concrete for about <InlineMath math="0.5" /> square metres of path. The dry materials shrink when they are mixed, so a batch never fills as many buckets as went into it.
