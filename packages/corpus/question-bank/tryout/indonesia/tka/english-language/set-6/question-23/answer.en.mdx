Pak Teguh doubts the start time because two schools also opened a canteen breakfast. A fact weakens his doubt if it makes the start time a more likely cause, or the breakfast or another change a less likely one.

- **I weakens it**. The school without a breakfast saw the same fall, so the fall happened without one.
- **II weakens it**. Lateness elsewhere in the district stayed the same, so the fall wasn't something every school saw that semester.
- III doesn't. A change to the bus routes is one more change in the same semester, which strengthens the doubt.
- **IV weakens it**. A breakfast that began in the last month couldn't explain a fall across the semester.

The options with III treat another change as support for the start time, although it's one more rival cause. The option with only I misses that ruling out other causes also weakens the doubt.

So the facts that weaken his doubt are I, II and IV only.
