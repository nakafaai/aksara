# Asked

# Solution

Verify **three quick ratios** (fill time, five-person supply duration, price per litre) while spotting two wording traps: **"exactly four days"** confuses the <InlineMath math="96" />-hour cycle with the <InlineMath math="92" />-hour gap between turns, and **"most houses"** contradicts "a few houses".

> Separate cycle length (four days from start to start) from the dry wait between the end of one turn and the start of the next.

## Running the figures on all five statements

**The three core calculations** settle statements <InlineMath math="1, 4" />, and <InlineMath math="5" /> directly:

- **A <InlineMath math="1" />-cubic-metre tank that is empty can be filled in under an hour of supply**.
