Line up the events: meeting at half past six, being divided into groups, **receiving sacks**, working near the river mouth and **counting until nine**, **weighing the sacks at ten**, and finally going home. The event between receiving sacks and weighing them is the group counting wrappers, straws, and bottles.

- Meeting at the parking area comes first, before sacks are handed out.
- Forming groups happens before or along with getting the sacks, not after.
- Thinking about the wrappers happens on the way home, after weighing.
- Kak Dewi explains the river link *after* the sacks are weighed.
