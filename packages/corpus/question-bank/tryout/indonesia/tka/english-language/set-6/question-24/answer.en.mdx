# Given

The parents' association (Text <InlineMath math="1" />) and Pak Teguh (Text <InlineMath math="2" />) **disagree over first-lesson marks and an 8:00 start**, yet **share three clear points**: 6:45 is hard on teenagers, lateness dropped <InlineMath math="8" /> percentage points (<InlineMath math="14\%" /> to <InlineMath math="6\%" />), and the three schools did move their start time.

# Asked

# Solution

Test every option against both texts side by side. The key trap is **first-lesson marks**: Pak Teguh accepts the lateness figures as "striking", yet **refuses to accept the <InlineMath math="5" />-point mark gain** without earlier years' baselines.

# Answer

The points accepted by both writers are:
- **A 6:45 start is hard on teenagers**.
- **The share of students arriving late at the three schools fell by <InlineMath math="8" /> percentage points**.
- **The three schools moved their start time**.
