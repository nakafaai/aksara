A narrative is built from **orientation, complication, and resolution**. The orientation introduces Maya and her role. The complication comes when Maya **forgets her lines** in front of the audience. The resolution is when that problem is solved: Maya answers Tomi's question and *the rest of her speech came back*. So the resolution is Maya answering Tomi and her speech returning.

- Practicing every night is part of the orientation.
- Forgetting her lines is the complication, not the solution.
- The cough is just a detail that builds tension during the complication.
- Standing behind the curtain comes before the problem appears.
