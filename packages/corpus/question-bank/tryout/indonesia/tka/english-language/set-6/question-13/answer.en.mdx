Judge each statement by what the narrator does and says.

- **I is supported**. Kakek said "a lock is worth nothing if its key hangs where anyone can reach it", and the narrator wears the only key on a string around the neck.
- II isn't supported. When the uncle explains, "My father nodded. I didn't say anything." Silence isn't saying yes.
- **III is supported**. The narrator "lay awake until my father showed me" why the bell rang.
- **IV is supported**. The narrator chains it again "the chain through the frame and the back wheel, as before".
- **V is supported**. The narrator stays silent, and has reason to doubt: a bicycle with a closed chain through its frame can't be lifted over a gate.

The option with every statement adds II, which turns silence into agreement. The options without I or V miss what the key and the silence show about the narrator.

So the supported statements are I, III, IV and V only.
