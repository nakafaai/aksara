A summary keeps what the account actually reports.

- **I belongs**. It's the float method the group used.
- **II belongs**. They "crossed out" the third and fifth runs, the slowest and the fastest.
- **III belongs**. The stakes were <InlineMath math="9" /> metres apart, not <InlineMath math="10" />. A shorter distance over the same times gives a lower speed, so the first answer was too high.
- IV doesn't belong. "We kept the times and changed only the distance."

The options with IV invent a repeat of the runs, which the last sentence rules out. The options without I or III leave out the method or the mistake that the whole account is about.

So the summary should contain I, II and III only.
