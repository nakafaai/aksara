# Given

Rina mixes dry ingredients for **<InlineMath math="4" /> full batches at once** (<InlineMath math="4" /> buckets of cement), then pours in **<InlineMath math="3" /> buckets of water all at once** because she is in a hurry.

The guide specifies **about half a bucket of water per bucket of cement**, added **slowly** until a squeezed handful holds its shape, warns that too much water makes weak concrete that cracks, and states each full batch covers about **<InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" />**.

# Asked

Which numbered statements about Rina's mix are supported by the instructions.

# Solution

Compare Rina's mix against **recommended water** (<InlineMath math="4 \times 0.5 = 2" /> buckets vs. her <InlineMath math="3" />), **pouring method** (slowly vs. all at once), and **coverage of <InlineMath math="4" /> full batches** (<InlineMath math="4 \times 0.5 = 2\text{ m}^2" />).

> Extra water weakens the cured concrete without shrinking the area covered by four full batches of dry ingredients.

## Checking each statement about Rina mix

**Expected water and batch coverage** for <InlineMath math="4" /> full batches:

<BlockMath math="\text{Recommended water: } 4 \times 0.5 = 2\text{ buckets} < 3\text{ buckets}, \qquad \text{Coverage: } 4 \times 0.5 = 2\text{ m}^2" />

| Statement | Supported? | Direct evidence in the guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | <Highlight>about half a bucket for every bucket of cement</Highlight> calls for roughly <InlineMath math="2" /> buckets, so <InlineMath math="3" /> buckets exceeds the guide. |
| II | **Yes** | Step <InlineMath math="2" /> warns directly: <Highlight>Too much water makes weak concrete that cracks</Highlight>. |
| III | No | Step <InlineMath math="2" /> instructs <Highlight>Add water slowly ... and stop as soon as the mix holds its shape</Highlight>, so pouring method matters too. |
| IV | **Yes** | Four full batches cover roughly <InlineMath math="2\text{ m}^2" />, excess water weakens the mix rather than reducing its coverage. |

## Why the other options fail

- **I, II, III, and IV** ignores the instruction to pour *slowly* and stop as soon as the mix holds its shape.
- **I and II only** wrongly assumes extra water makes four batches cover less than <InlineMath math="2\text{ m}^2" />.

# Answer

The supported statements about Rina's mix are **I, II, and IV only**.
