# Given

In every four-day cycle, **Block A** receives two turns (day <InlineMath math="1" /> 5:00 to 9:00 and day <InlineMath math="4" /> 15:00 to 17:00) because it sits highest on the hill with the weakest pressure, whereas **Blocks B, C, and D** each get one morning turn from 5:00 to 9:00.

# Asked

Which numbered statements comparing Block A with the other blocks are supported by the text.

# Solution

Check four exact dimensions: **total supply hours**, **longest gap measured in hours** (not rounded days), **flow rate per hour**, and **the reason for two turns**. Never confuse more total hours with more litres per hour.

> Measuring gaps in exact hours (<InlineMath math="78" /> hours vs. <InlineMath math="92" /> hours) reveals Block A's shorter longest gap even though both round up to <InlineMath math="4" /> days.

## Comparing hour gaps and checking each statement

**Block A's longest gap** (day <InlineMath math="1" /> 9:00 to day <InlineMath math="4" /> 15:00) versus **other blocks** (9:00 to 5:00 four days later):

<BlockMath math="\text{Block A: } 3 \times 24 + 6 = 78\text{ hours}, \qquad \text{Blocks B, C, D: } 3 \times 24 + 20 = 92\text{ hours}" />

| Statement | Supported? | Evidence from table and text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Block A gets <InlineMath math="4" /> morning plus <InlineMath math="2" /> afternoon hours (<InlineMath math="6" /> total), more than the <InlineMath math="4" /> hours of every other block. |
| II | **Yes** | Measured in hours, Block A's longest gap (<InlineMath math="78" /> hours) is shorter than the <InlineMath math="92" />-hour gap of other blocks. |
| III | No | Pipes deliver <Highlight>about <InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> litres an hour to each house</Highlight> across blocks, and Block A's morning water actually <Highlight>ran thin</Highlight>. |
| IV | **Yes** | Stated directly: <Highlight>Block A has two turns in each four-day cycle because it sits highest on the hill, where the pressure is weakest</Highlight>. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III** confuse more supply hours with a higher flow rate per hour.
- **I and IV only** misses the exact-hours comparison (<InlineMath math="78" /> vs. <InlineMath math="92" />) in II.

# Answer

The supported comparison statements are **I, II, and IV only**.
