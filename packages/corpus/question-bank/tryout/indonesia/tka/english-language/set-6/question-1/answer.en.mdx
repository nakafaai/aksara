# Given

A **five-person household** in **Block C** has one **<InlineMath math="1" />-cubic-metre (<InlineMath math="1{,}000" />-litre) tank** that is full when supply stops at 9:00 on day three.

Bu Narti's figures and rules:
- Block C's next turn starts at 5:00 on day three of the next cycle (<InlineMath math="3" /> days <InlineMath math="20" /> hours later), **counting any part of a day as a whole day**.
- Usage is **<InlineMath math="60" /> litres per person per day**.
- Cart water costs **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> per <InlineMath math="20" />-litre container**.

# Asked

How much cart water the household must buy before its next turn and what it will cost.

# Solution

Work through three tidy steps: **round the wait to whole days**, **find the shortfall above the <InlineMath math="1{,}000" />-litre tank**, and **convert litres into <InlineMath math="20" />-litre containers** to price them.

> Counting any part of a day as a whole day turns a <InlineMath math="3" />-day <InlineMath math="20" />-hour wait into <InlineMath math="4" /> full days.

## Calculating water shortfall and cart cost

Bu Narti's rules specify <Highlight>counting any part of a day as a whole day</Highlight>, <Highlight><InlineMath math="60" /> litres per person per day</Highlight>, and <Highlight><InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> for every <InlineMath math="20" />-litre container</Highlight>.

Rounding <InlineMath math="3" /> days <InlineMath math="20" /> hours up to **<InlineMath math="4" /> days**, **the demand, shortfall, and cart cost** are:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Needed: } 5 \times 60 \times 4 = 1{,}200\text{ L}, \qquad \text{Shortfall: } 1{,}200 - 1{,}000 = 200\text{ L}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{Cost: } \frac{200}{20} \times \text{Rp}5{,}000 = 10 \times \text{Rp}5{,}000 = \text{Rp}50{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

## Why the other options fail

- **None** assumes one tank covers four days, yet five people need <InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> litres.
- **<InlineMath math="150" /> litres costing <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}37{,}500" />** uses exact fractional hours (about <InlineMath math="3.8" /> days) instead of whole days.
- **<InlineMath math="200" /> litres costing <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" />** treats a container as holding <InlineMath math="100" /> litres.
- **<InlineMath math="200" /> litres costing <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000{,}000" />** charges <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> per litre rather than per <InlineMath math="20" /> litres.

# Answer

The household must buy **<InlineMath math="200" /> litres, costing <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}50{,}000" />**.
