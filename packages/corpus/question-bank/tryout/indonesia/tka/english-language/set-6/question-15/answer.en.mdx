- **II could happen**. Someone can pump tyres without telling the owner.
- III couldn't happen. A closed chain through the frame holds the bicycle to the post, so the bicycle can't travel anywhere while it stays locked.
- **IV could happen**. A truck can carry a bicycle.
- **V could happen**. Once the chain is cut, a bicycle can be ridden twelve kilometres. It's strange in the story only because the chain there was never cut.

The options without V judge the event by the story, where the chain stayed whole. The option with III accepts the one event that breaks the physical facts.

So the events that could happen are I, II, IV and V only.
