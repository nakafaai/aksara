# Given

**Five cause-and-effect statements** cover Kampung Sumber's water rota: why blocks take turns, why Block A gets <InlineMath math="6" /> hours, cart-water cost for <InlineMath math="200" /> litres, a claimed cut to Block A's morning turn in <InlineMath math="2024" />, and whether a <InlineMath math="1{,}000" />-litre tank covers five people.

# Asked

Which numbered cause-and-effect statements are supported by the text.

# Solution

Verify both **the stated cause** and **the resulting effect or arithmetic**. Reject IV (which invents a morning cut contradicted by the table) and V (which skips the four-day water calculation).

> A cause-and-effect claim stands only when both its stated reason and its factual or numerical consequence hold in the text.

## Checking each cause and effect statement

| Statement | Supported? | Text evidence and arithmetic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Matches paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />: <Highlight>the reservoir cannot supply all four blocks at once, so each block receives water for one fixed stretch of time</Highlight>. |
| II | **Yes** | Weakest pressure gives Block A two turns (<InlineMath math="4" /> plus <InlineMath math="2" /> equals <InlineMath math="6" /> hours, <InlineMath math="2" /> more than any other block). |
| III | **Yes** | At <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> per <InlineMath math="20" /> litres, <InlineMath math="200" /> litres (<InlineMath math="10" /> containers) costs exactly <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}50{,}000" />. |
| IV | No | The afternoon turn was added in <InlineMath math="2024" />, yet the table still lists Block A's morning turn as 5:00 to 9:00, never shortened. |
| V | No | Five people use <InlineMath math="300" /> litres a day, needing <InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> litres over four days, which exceeds one <InlineMath math="1{,}000" />-litre tank. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including IV** invent a trade-off contradicted by the timetable.
- **Options including V** fail to check <InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> litres against <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> litres.
- **I and II only** or **II and III only** drops a verified statement (III or I).

# Answer

The supported cause-and-effect statements are **I, II, and III only**.
