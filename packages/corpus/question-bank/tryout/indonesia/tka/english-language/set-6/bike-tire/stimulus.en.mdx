How to Fix a Flat Bicycle Tire

You will need two tire levers, a patch kit with sandpaper, glue, and patches, a pump, and a bowl of water.

1. Turn the bicycle upside down so that it rests on its seat and handlebars.
2. Open the brake if necessary, and remove the wheel from the frame.
3. Let out any remaining air. Push one tire lever under the edge of the tire and hook it onto a spoke. Then slide the second lever around the rim until one side of the tire comes off.
4. Pull out the inner tube.
5. Pump a little air into the tube and hold it under water. Bubbles will show where the hole is. Mark the spot.
6. Dry the tube. Rub the area around the hole with sandpaper so that the glue can hold.
7. Spread a thin layer of glue, wait about three minutes until it is no longer wet, and then press the patch firmly onto it.
8. Before putting the tube back, run your fingers carefully around the inside of the tire to find whatever caused the puncture, such as a thorn or a small piece of glass. Remove it.
9. Put the tube back, push the tire onto the rim with your thumbs, and pump the tire up.

Tip: Do not use a screwdriver instead of tire levers. Its sharp edge can tear the tube and make a new hole.
