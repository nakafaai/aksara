# Given

We want to calculate **how many <InlineMath math="40" />-kilogram bags of cement** a garden path of a given area requires, selecting from **five numbered details** (I through V) in the concrete guide.

# Asked

Which numbered details are **needed to compute the number of cement bags** for a given path area.

# Solution

Write the unit-conversion chain from area to bags and keep **only the three conversion factors that enter the formula**: area per batch (<InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" />), cement per batch (<InlineMath math="1" /> bucket), and buckets per bag (<InlineMath math="2.5" /> buckets). Sand never enters the cement count, and the <InlineMath math="8" />-cm thickness is already baked into <InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" /> per batch.

> A conversion figure like "<InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" /> per batch" already incorporates the path's fixed <InlineMath math="8" />-cm thickness, so thickness is not needed separately.

## Tracing every factor in the cement bag formula

**The chain from path area to cement bags** uses exactly three figures (<Highlight><InlineMath math="0.5" /> square metres of path</Highlight>, <Highlight><InlineMath math="1" /> bucket of cement</Highlight>, and <Highlight>fills about <InlineMath math="2.5" /> buckets</Highlight>):

<BlockMath math="\text{Cement bags} = \frac{\text{Area} \div 0.5 \times 1}{2.5}" />

| Detail | Needed? | Role in the calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I (<InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" /> per full batch) | **Yes** | Converts path area into number of full batches. |
| II (<InlineMath math="2.5" /> buckets per <InlineMath math="40" />-kg bag) | **Yes** | Converts buckets of cement into cement bags. |
| III (<InlineMath math="1" /> bucket of cement per batch) | **Yes** | Converts full batches into buckets of cement. |
| IV (<InlineMath math="2" /> buckets of sand per batch) | No | Sand quantity does not change the cement-bag count. |
| V (<InlineMath math="8" />-cm path thickness) | No | Already built into the <InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" /> coverage per batch. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options without III** jump from batches to bags without knowing how much cement each batch uses.
- **Options including IV** include sand, which plays no part in finding cement bags.

# Answer

The details needed to work out the bags of cement are **I, II, and III only**.
