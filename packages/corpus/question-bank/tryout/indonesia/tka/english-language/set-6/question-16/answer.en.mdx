# Given

Pak Joko wants a garden path **<InlineMath math="1" /> metre wide and <InlineMath math="6" /> metres long** (<InlineMath math="6\text{ m}^2" />) using full batches from the guide:
- Each full batch covers about **<InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" />** and uses **<InlineMath math="1" /> bucket of cement**.
- One <InlineMath math="40" />-kg bag of cement fills about **<InlineMath math="2.5" /> buckets**.

# Asked

How many <InlineMath math="40" />-kilogram bags of cement Pak Joko must buy.

# Solution

Follow the unit chain straight across: **path area** to **full batches**, batches to **buckets of cement**, buckets to **bags**, then **round up** because you cannot buy a fraction of a bag.

> Whenever materials are bought in whole bags, any fractional requirement like <InlineMath math="4.8" /> bags must be rounded up to <InlineMath math="5" /> so the job does not run short.

## Calculating batches and bags of cement

The guide states <Highlight>One <InlineMath math="40" />-kilogram bag of cement fills about <InlineMath math="2.5" /> buckets</Highlight> and <Highlight>Each full batch of <InlineMath math="1" /> bucket of cement ... makes enough concrete for about <InlineMath math="0.5" /> square metres of path</Highlight>. **Step by step**:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Area: } 1 \times 6 = 6\text{ m}^2, \qquad \text{Batches: } \frac{6}{0.5} = 12\text{ batches (12 buckets of cement)}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{Bags: } \frac{12}{2.5} = 4.8\text{ bags} \implies \text{must buy } 5\text{ bags}" />
</MathContainer>

## Why the other options fail

- **Option <InlineMath math="12" />** treats <InlineMath math="12" /> buckets as <InlineMath math="12" /> bags.
- **Option <InlineMath math="4" />** rounds <InlineMath math="4.8" /> down, leaving Pak Joko short of cement.
- **Option <InlineMath math="3" />** skips the <InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" /> batch size (<InlineMath math="6" /> divided by <InlineMath math="2.5" />, rounded up).
- **Option <InlineMath math="30" />** multiplies <InlineMath math="12" /> by <InlineMath math="2.5" /> instead of dividing.

# Answer

Pak Joko must buy **<InlineMath math="5" />** bags of cement.
