# Given

**Six statements** from the SMA Cahaya debate (Text <InlineMath math="1" /> from the parents' association and Text <InlineMath math="2" /> from deputy head Pak Teguh) must be sorted into **Stated as a fact**, **Stated as an opinion**, or **Not stated in either text**.

# Asked

Whether either text presents each statement as a fact, as an opinion, or does not state it at all.

# Solution

Check **whether either text actually states the claim** before deciding between **verifiable fact** and **value judgement**. Catch two sharp traps: statement <InlineMath math="3" /> sneaks in the word "alone", and statement <InlineMath math="6" /> shifts the three other schools' <InlineMath math="14\%" /> onto SMA Cahaya.

> Adding "alone" to a correlation or transferring another school's statistic onto SMA Cahaya creates a claim neither text ever makes.

## Sorting all six statements

| Statement | Classification | Direct evidence in Text 1 or Text 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" /> (Two of the three schools opened a canteen breakfast) | **Stated as a fact** | Reported directly by Pak Teguh in Text <InlineMath math="2" />: <Highlight>Two of them also opened a canteen breakfast in the same semester</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="2" /> (Thinking before seven wastes the first lesson) | **Stated as an opinion** | A value judgement in Text <InlineMath math="1" /> (<Highlight>wastes the first lesson of every day</Highlight>). |
| <InlineMath math="3" /> (The later start alone cut lateness) | **Not stated in either text** | Text <InlineMath math="1" /> never says "alone", and Text <InlineMath math="2" /> explicitly doubts it. |
| <InlineMath math="4" /> (7:30 is the latest start time the buses allow) | **Stated as a fact** | Stated as a checkable timetable constraint in Text <InlineMath math="2" /> (<Highlight>the latest time the buses allow</Highlight>). |
| <InlineMath math="5" /> (No other change could do as much for so little cost) | **Stated as an opinion** | The parents' persuasive judgement closing Text <InlineMath math="1" /> (<Highlight>No other change could do as much for so little cost</Highlight>). |
| <InlineMath math="6" /> (More than <InlineMath math="14\%" /> of SMA Cahaya's students arrive late) | **Not stated in either text** | The <InlineMath math="14\%" /> belongs to the three other schools, neither text gives SMA Cahaya's own lateness rate. |

# Answer

- **Stated as a fact**: statements **<InlineMath math="1" />** and **<InlineMath math="4" />**.
- **Stated as an opinion**: statements **<InlineMath math="2" />** and **<InlineMath math="5" />**.
- **Not stated in either text**: statements **<InlineMath math="3" />** and **<InlineMath math="6" />**.
