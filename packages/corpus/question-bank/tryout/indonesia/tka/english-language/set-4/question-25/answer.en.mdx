# Given

Five candidate summary statements cover the exchange between the student council (**Text <InlineMath math="1" />**) and Bu Anjani (**Text <InlineMath math="2" />**).

# Asked

Which numbered statements belong in an accurate summary of the two texts.

# Solution

A faithful two-text summary captures **Text <InlineMath math="1" />'s request and survey**, **Text <InlineMath math="2" />'s concession**, and **Text <InlineMath math="2" />'s counterproposal**, without overstating what Bu Anjani proved or inventing a fallback promise she never makes.

> Watch for two classic summary traps: turning a caveat into proof of a smaller figure, and inventing a future concession the writer never offers.

## Checking each summary statement

| Statement | Verdict | Direct evidence in both texts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Belongs | Captures Text <InlineMath math="1" />'s core request (<Highlight>cap homework at <InlineMath math="60" /> minutes a school day</Highlight>) and its <InlineMath math="400" />-student survey. |
| II | Belongs | Captures Bu Anjani's opening concession: <Highlight>The council is right that many students are too tired</Highlight>. |
| III | Excluded | Overreaches: she notes phone time at a desk is not all homework, yet never shows how much time students truly spend. Her own test even showed students reporting more time (<InlineMath math="75" /> vs. <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes). |
| IV | Belongs | Captures Text <InlineMath math="2" />'s alternative: <Highlight>What we need first is a shared calendar</Highlight>. |
| V | Excluded | Nothing in Text <InlineMath math="2" /> promises she will accept a cap if the calendar fails. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III** claim Bu Anjani proved a lower homework total she never established.
- **Options including V** invent a fallback agreement absent from Text <InlineMath math="2" />.
- **I and IV only** omits her important agreement on student tiredness (II).

# Answer

The statements that belong in an accurate summary of the two texts are **I, II, and IV only**.
