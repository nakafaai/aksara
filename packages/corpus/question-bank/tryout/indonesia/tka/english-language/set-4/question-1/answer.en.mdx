# Given

A **monthly pass holder** rides an **e-bike** for **<InlineMath math="1" /> hour <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes** (<InlineMath math="80" /> minutes) and ends the ride properly in a dock.

From the monthly-pass column of the fare table:
- First <InlineMath math="45" /> minutes are free, then <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" /> for each started <InlineMath math="30" />-minute block after that.
- An e-bike adds <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> per ride.

# Asked

How much the ride costs.

# Solution

Picture the meter starting only after the <InlineMath math="45" /> free minutes run out. Simply **subtract the free period**, count **how many <InlineMath math="30" />-minute blocks are started**, and add **the pass-holder e-bike surcharge**.

> Even a few minutes past a boundary starts a brand-new half-hour block that is billed in full.

## Finding chargeable time and total fare

The text states <Highlight>A started half hour is charged in full</Highlight>, and the pass column sets <Highlight>First <InlineMath math="45" /> minutes free, then <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" /> for each started <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes after that</Highlight> plus <Highlight><InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> per ride</Highlight>.

An **<InlineMath math="80" />-minute ride** runs **<InlineMath math="35" /> minutes** past the free window (one full half hour plus <InlineMath math="5" /> minutes of a second), counting as **<InlineMath math="2" /> started half hours**:

<BlockMath math="80 - 45 = 35\text{ minutes} \implies 2\text{ half hours}, \qquad 2 \times \text{Rp}3{,}000 + \text{Rp}2{,}000 = \text{Rp}8{,}000" />

## Why the other options fail

- **Option <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" />** charges <InlineMath math="35" /> minutes as just <InlineMath math="1" /> half hour instead of <InlineMath math="2" />.
- **Option <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}11{,}000" />** ignores the <InlineMath math="45" /> free minutes and bills all <InlineMath math="80" /> minutes as <InlineMath math="3" /> half hours.
- **Option <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" />** applies the non-pass e-bike surcharge (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000" />) instead of the pass surcharge (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" />).
- **Option <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}6{,}000" />** leaves out the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> e-bike surcharge.

# Answer

The ride costs **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}8{,}000" />**.
