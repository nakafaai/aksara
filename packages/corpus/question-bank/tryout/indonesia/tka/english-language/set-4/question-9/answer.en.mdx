# Given

In the final paragraph, the narrator writes a letter to the editor responding to the headline *"Students' mangroves: only <InlineMath math="64\%" /> survive"* (<InlineMath math="385" /> divided by <InlineMath math="600" />), **explicitly stating both what was not argued and what was argued**.

# Asked

Which numbered points are part of the narrator's objection to the newspaper piece.

# Solution

Highlight the closing two sentences with two colours: **what the narrator refuses to argue** (higher or lower) versus **the exact two flaws the letter attacks** (treating uncertain seedlings as dead, and omitting the flood that explains Zone C's losses).

> Always separate a writer's explicit disclaimer ("I did not argue...") from the actual thesis ("I argued that...").

## Checking each point against the letter

| Point | Part of objection? | Direct evidence in paragraph five |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Yes | First point of the letter: <Highlight>a figure which treats the uncertain seedlings as dead is not a survival rate at all</Highlight>. |
| II | No | Flatly disclaimed in <Highlight>I did not argue that the true figure was higher or lower</Highlight>. |
| III | Yes | Second point of the letter: <Highlight>the piece never mentioned the flood</Highlight>. |
| IV | No | The paper divided <InlineMath math="385" /> by <InlineMath math="600" /> (March seedlings), leaving the <InlineMath math="50" /> replanted seedlings out completely. |
| V | Yes | Directly tied to point III: the flood <Highlight>explains most of the losses in one zone and says nothing about the planting</Highlight>. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including II** contradict the narrator's explicit refusal to argue that the rate was higher.
- **Options including IV** wrongly claim the newspaper counted the <InlineMath math="50" /> May seedlings.
- **I and III only** drops the causal point (V) explaining why omitting the flood distorted the story.

# Answer

The points that form part of the narrator's objection are **I, III, and V only**.
