The gap sits right before "Stations in the busiest districts then stood empty by mid-morning, because bikes that should have come back after one trip stayed out for hours." Whatever fills it must agree with that sentence.

- **I fits**. Riders keeping a bike all morning is exactly why bikes "stayed out for hours" and stations emptied by mid-morning.
- II doesn't fit. If riders hardly used the extra time, bikes wouldn't have stayed out for hours.
- **III fits**. Taking a bike at breakfast and keeping it until lunch is another way of keeping it out for hours, and it would empty the stations in the morning.
- IV doesn't fit. Stations that "overflowed with returned bikes" are the opposite of stations that "stood empty".

So the sentences that could fill the gap are I and III only.
