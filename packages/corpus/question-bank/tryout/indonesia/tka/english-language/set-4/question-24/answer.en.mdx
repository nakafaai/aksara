# Given

Text <InlineMath math="1" /> (student council) and Text <InlineMath math="2" /> (Bu Anjani) **disagree on what the survey proves and on the flat cap**, yet share **explicit common ground** at the start of Bu Anjani's reply.

# Asked

Which points **both writers accept** (choose all that apply).

# Solution

Picture a Venn diagram of Text <InlineMath math="1" /> and Text <InlineMath math="2" />. A point belongs in the overlap only when **Text <InlineMath math="1" /> advances it and Text <InlineMath math="2" /> clearly concedes it**.

> Shared ground requires agreement from both sides: a point held by only one writer, or disputed by the other, never belongs in the overlap.

## Checking all five points across both texts

| Point | Accepted by both? | Evidence from Text 1 and Text 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Many students at the school are too tired | **Yes** | Text <InlineMath math="1" /> cites <InlineMath math="58\%" /> sleeping under seven hours, and Text <InlineMath math="2" /> opens <Highlight>The council is right that many students are too tired</Highlight>. |
| Homework is part of the reason students are tired | **Yes** | Text <InlineMath math="1" /> says homework eats sleep, and Text <InlineMath math="2" /> agrees they are <Highlight>right that the problem starts with homework</Highlight>. |
| Students average <InlineMath math="2.1" /> hours of homework set by teachers | **No** | Implied only in Text <InlineMath math="1" />. Text <InlineMath math="2" /> disputes it directly ("time spent, not time set"). |
| A <InlineMath math="60" />-minute daily cap would solve the problem | **No** | Claimed only by Text <InlineMath math="1" />. Text <InlineMath math="2" /> rejects a flat cap. |
| Teachers often misjudge how long tasks take | **No** | Only Bu Anjani mentions personal <Highlight>my misjudgement</Highlight> in Text <InlineMath math="2" />. Text <InlineMath math="1" /> says teachers think their subject deserves an extra half hour, not that they misjudge duration. |

# Answer

The points accepted by both writers are:
- **Many students at the school are too tired**.
- **Homework is part of the reason students are tired**.
