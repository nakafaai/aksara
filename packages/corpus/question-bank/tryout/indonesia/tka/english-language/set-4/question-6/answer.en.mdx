# Given

The Muara Asri mangrove recount covers **<InlineMath math="600" /> seedlings planted in March** across three zones, a **May flood hitting Zone C**, a June count showing **<InlineMath math="385" /> surviving** (<InlineMath math="160" /> dead or missing, <InlineMath math="55" /> uncertain), and the narrator's letter answering a newspaper headline.

# Asked

Which numbered statements belong in an accurate summary of the account.

# Solution

A strong summary must be **true to the table totals and faithful to the narrator's actual argument**. Summing the table rules out "most died", and reading the final paragraph catches the subtle trap about arguing for a higher rate.

> Check summary choices against both the column totals in the table and any explicit disclaimer the narrator makes at the end.

## Checking the table totals and each statement

**Summing the June count table** gives <InlineMath math="385" /> surviving seedlings and only <InlineMath math="160" /> dead or missing:

<BlockMath math="\text{Surviving: } 150 + 175 + 60 = 385, \qquad \text{Dead or missing: } 30 + 50 + 80 = 160" />

| Statement | Verdict | Direct evidence in the account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Belongs | Captures the <InlineMath math="600" /> seedlings in three zones and the May flood that <Highlight>tore most of the stakes out of Zone C</Highlight>. |
| II | Belongs | The surviving column adds up to exactly <InlineMath math="385" /> March seedlings. |
| III | Excluded | False: only <InlineMath math="160" /> of <InlineMath math="600" /> died or went missing, far less than half. |
| IV | Excluded | Flatly denied by <Highlight>I did not argue that the true figure was higher or lower</Highlight>. |
| V | Belongs | States the heart of the letter: the figure <Highlight>treats the uncertain seedlings as dead</Highlight> and the article <Highlight>never mentioned the flood</Highlight>. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III or IV** contradict the table total (<InlineMath math="160" />) or the narrator's explicit disclaimer.
- **I and V only** leaves out the <InlineMath math="385" /> surviving count that sparked the newspaper's <InlineMath math="64\%" /> headline.

# Answer

The statements belonging in an accurate summary are **I, II, and V only**.
