# Given

The cycled tank is ready for a planned total of **<InlineMath math="9" /> fish**. The club adds **<InlineMath math="3" /> fish** on Monday (week <InlineMath math="1" />), and on Wednesday ammonia reads **<InlineMath math="0.5" /> ppm** (above <InlineMath math="0.25" /> ppm). Every later test is fine.

# Asked

What the club should do now and the earliest week all <InlineMath math="9" /> fish can be in the tank (counting the first week as week <InlineMath math="1" />).

# Solution

Step <InlineMath math="6" /> supplies **two rules that work together**: an immediate response whenever ammonia exceeds <InlineMath math="0.25" /> ppm, and a weekly pace limit of **at most one third of the planned fish**.

> Even when every later water test passes, the one-third weekly cap fixes the minimum number of weeks needed to stock the whole tank.

## Finding the immediate action and earliest week

Step <InlineMath math="6" /> sets <Highlight>no more than a third of the planned number of fish in any one week</Highlight> and, if ammonia exceeds <InlineMath math="0.25" /> ppm, instructs to <Highlight>add no more fish that week and change a quarter of the water</Highlight>.

- **Immediate action**: since <InlineMath math="0.5" /> ppm exceeds <InlineMath math="0.25" /> ppm, the club must **change a quarter of the water and add no more fish this week**.
- **Earliest week**: one third of <InlineMath math="9" /> is at most **<InlineMath math="3" /> fish per week**, giving:

<BlockMath math="\text{Week } 1: 3 \text{ fish}, \qquad \text{Week } 2: 6 \text{ fish}, \qquad \text{Week } 3: 9 \text{ fish}" />

## Why the other options fail

- **Adding <InlineMath math="3" /> more fish now (week <InlineMath math="2" />)** breaks both the high-ammonia pause and the <InlineMath math="3" />-fish weekly cap.
- **Changing a quarter of the water with all <InlineMath math="9" /> in week <InlineMath math="2" />** would require adding <InlineMath math="6" /> fish in one week, double the cap.
- **Changing all the water and re-cycling (week <InlineMath math="4" />)** goes far beyond the guide's one-quarter water change.
- **Adding no more fish without changing water (week <InlineMath math="3" />)** skips the required quarter water change.

# Answer

The club should **change a quarter of the water and add no more fish this week, so all <InlineMath math="9" /> fish could be in during week <InlineMath math="3" />**.
