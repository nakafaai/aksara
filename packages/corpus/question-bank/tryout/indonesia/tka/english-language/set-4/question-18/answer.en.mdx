The opening claim comes with its own reason: "New fish die in new tanks because the water has none of the bacteria that clean it." A detail supports the claim if it shows how that missing cleaning harms fish.

- **I supports it**. It's the reason the guide gives for the claim.
- **II supports it**. Fish waste produces ammonia, the harmful substance the bacteria would otherwise deal with.
- III doesn't. It explains why nitrate, the end of the chain, is **safe**. It says nothing about why new fish die.
- IV doesn't. The heater setting is an instruction, not part of the danger.
- **V supports it**. A sudden load of fish makes more ammonia than the bacteria can handle, and a new tank has hardly any bacteria at all.

So the supporting details are I, II and V only.
