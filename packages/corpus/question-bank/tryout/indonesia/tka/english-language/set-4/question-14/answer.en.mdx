Two parts of the story give the rules for these predictions: Grandfather's lesson, "A kite always flies downwind of the hand that holds it", and his explanation of the cliff, "Every afternoon ... the sea breeze runs up the face of the cliff".

- **I is reasonable**. Wind from the sea blows towards the land, so downwind is over the land behind the flier, exactly as Grandfather says.
- **II is reasonable**. Wind from the land blows towards the sea, so downwind is out over the water.
- **III is reasonable**. The sea breeze on the cliff comes every afternoon, while the village keeps "its own calm", which is what happened on the narrator's first afternoon.
- IV isn't. Over the water would be upwind of a wind from the sea. **Only the red kite did that**, and it's the one event the story leaves unexplained.

So the reasonable predictions are I, II and III only.
