A started half hour is charged in full, so a <InlineMath math="31" />-minute ride without a pass costs the same as a <InlineMath math="60" />-minute one. If the rider reaches a station and finds every dock full, a "full dock" button on the station screen adds <InlineMath math="15" /> free minutes to the ride, enough to reach the next station. A bike left outside a dock is charged a recovery fee of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}25{,}000" /> on top of the ride, because a van has to fetch it.

The free period for pass holders was the subject of a long argument. The transport office first tried <InlineMath math="90" /> free minutes. [GAP] Stations in the busiest districts then stood empty by mid-morning, because bikes that should have come back after one trip stayed out for hours. The office cut the period to <InlineMath math="45" /> minutes because, in its trip records, most rides between neighbouring districts take <InlineMath math="25" /> to <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes, so the shorter period still covers the trips the scheme was built for.
