Decide each statement by finding the sentence that **states the fact directly**. A statement is true only if every part of it matches the recount, so one wrong detail makes it false.

| Statement | Category | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before the first rehearsal, the writer practiced every evening on a saron at home. | False | The writer practiced "by tapping it on a book". The recount never mentions a saron at home. |
| The group had to stop twice because the writer's speed did not match theirs. | True | "Twice the whole group had to stop because of me." The writer was ahead when the music sped up and still rushing when it slowed down. |
| Bu Ratna told the writer to count the beats more carefully in the next piece. | False | She told the writer "to play the next piece without counting at all". |

The statement about practicing on a saron is the easiest one to get wrong, because the writer really did practice every evening. Only the instrument is wrong, since the practice was tapping on a book. The statement about counting more carefully reverses the teacher's advice. So the statements about the saron at home and about counting more carefully are false, and only the statement about the two stops is true.
