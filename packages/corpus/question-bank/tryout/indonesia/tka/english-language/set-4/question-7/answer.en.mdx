# Given

From the June count table:
- **Zone A**: <InlineMath math="200" /> planted, <InlineMath math="150" /> surviving, <InlineMath math="20" /> uncertain, <InlineMath math="30" /> dead or missing.
- **Zone C**: <InlineMath math="150" /> planted, <InlineMath math="60" /> surviving, <InlineMath math="10" /> uncertain, <InlineMath math="80" /> dead or missing.

By Bu Fitri's method, survival rate equals **surviving seedlings divided by seedlings with a known fate** (excluding uncertain seedlings from both numerator and denominator).

# Asked

How many percentage points higher Zone A's survival rate was than Zone C's using Bu Fitri's method.

# Solution

Set aside the "uncertain" seedlings completely so only seedlings with a settled fate enter the fraction. **Compute Zone A's and Zone C's survival rates separately**, then **subtract the two percentages** to get percentage points.

> Percentage points measure the direct difference between two percentages, found by subtracting one rate from the other.

## Computing both survival rates and their difference

The text defines Bu Fitri's formula as <Highlight>the surviving seedlings divided by the seedlings whose fate is known, so the uncertain ones are left out of the bottom of the fraction as well as the top</Highlight>. **Applying that denominator rule to both zones** gives:

<BlockMath math="\text{Zone A: } \frac{150}{200 - 20} = \frac{150}{180} \approx 83.3\%, \qquad \text{Zone C: } \frac{60}{150 - 10} = \frac{60}{140} \approx 42.9\%" />

<BlockMath math="83.3 - 42.9 = 40.4 \approx 40 \text{ percentage points}" />

## Why the other options fail

- **Option <InlineMath math="35" /> percentage points** divides by all planted seedlings (<InlineMath math="75\%" /> minus <InlineMath math="40\%" />), ignoring Bu Fitri's rule.
- **Option about <InlineMath math="38" /> percentage points** counts uncertain seedlings as surviving (<InlineMath math="85\%" /> minus <InlineMath math="46.7\%" />).
- **Option about <InlineMath math="43" /> percentage points** applies Bu Fitri's rule to Zone A (<InlineMath math="83.3\%" />) but forgets it on Zone C (<InlineMath math="40\%" />).
- **Option about <InlineMath math="6" /> percentage points** compares Zone A with Zone B (<InlineMath math="83.3\%" /> vs. <InlineMath math="77.8\%" />) instead of Zone C.

# Answer

Zone A's survival rate was **about <InlineMath math="40" /> percentage points** higher than Zone C's.
