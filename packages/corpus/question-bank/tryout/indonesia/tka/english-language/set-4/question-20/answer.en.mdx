# Given

A club member argues that since ammonia and nitrite read **<InlineMath math="0" /> on the last three tests**, the club can put **all nine fish** into the tank tomorrow.

# Asked

Which numbered replies to the member's plan are supported by the guide.

# Solution

The member's shortcut skips **three consecutive safety checks** in the guide: verifying nitrate above zero in Step <InlineMath math="4" />, checking the <InlineMath math="40" /> ppm nitrate cap in Step <InlineMath math="5" />, and keeping weekly additions to one third in Step <InlineMath math="6" />.

> Check a proposed shortcut step by step against the guide so you catch every skipped safeguard while rejecting invented extra rules.

## Checking each reply against the guide

| Reply | Verdict | Direct evidence in the guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Supported | Step <InlineMath math="4" /> requires that <Highlight>nitrate reads above <InlineMath math="0" /> ppm on the last of them</Highlight>, which the member omitted. |
| II | Supported | Restates Step <InlineMath math="5" /> directly: <Highlight>If nitrate reads above <InlineMath math="40" /> ppm on that last test, change a quarter of the water before adding any fish</Highlight>. |
| III | Supported | Step <InlineMath math="6" /> caps each week at <Highlight>no more than a third of the planned number of fish in any one week</Highlight>, meaning at most <InlineMath math="3" /> of <InlineMath math="9" /> fish. |
| IV | Not supported | Step <InlineMath math="4" /> asks for three consecutive zero readings, never five. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including IV** invent a five-test rule absent from the guide.
- **I and III only** or **III only** omits either the <InlineMath math="40" /> ppm nitrate check (II) or the nitrate-above-zero test (I).

# Answer

The replies supported by the guide are **I, II, and III only**.
