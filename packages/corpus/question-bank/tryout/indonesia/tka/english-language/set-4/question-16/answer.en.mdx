# Given

The science club's tank holds **<InlineMath math="120" /> litres**. The guide requires **<InlineMath math="5\text{ mL}" /> of dechlorinator per <InlineMath math="10" /> litres of new tap water**, and never for water already in the tank.

In one week, the club tops up **<InlineMath math="10" /> litres** (dosed properly) and later changes **a quarter of the tank** (<InlineMath math="30" /> litres), where a member mistakenly doses all **<InlineMath math="120" /> litres**.

# Asked

How many millilitres more dechlorinator the club added that week than the guide asks for.

# Solution

Compare **what the guide prescribes** side by side with **what the club actually poured in**. Because the <InlineMath math="10" />-litre top-up was dosed accurately, the entire excess comes from dosing <InlineMath math="120" /> litres instead of <InlineMath math="30" /> litres at the water change.

> Subtract the prescribed weekly total from the actual weekly total so you answer "how much more", not the wrong dose itself.

## Calculating prescribed dose and excess

Step <InlineMath math="1" /> states <Highlight>Add dechlorinator to every litre of new tap water that goes in, at <InlineMath math="5" /> millilitres for every <InlineMath math="10" /> litres, and never to water that is already in the tank</Highlight>.

**A quarter of <InlineMath math="120" /> litres** equals **<InlineMath math="30" /> litres**. Computing both weekly totals and their difference gives:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Guide: } 5 + \frac{30}{10} \times 5 = 20\text{ mL}, \qquad \text{Club: } 5 + \frac{120}{10} \times 5 = 65\text{ mL}" />
<BlockMath math="65 - 20 = 45\text{ mL extra}" />
</MathContainer>

## Why the other options fail

- **Option <InlineMath math="40" /> millilitres** subtracts the guide's full-week total (<InlineMath math="20" />) from the water-change error alone (<InlineMath math="60" />).
- **Option <InlineMath math="60" /> millilitres** is the erroneous water-change dose itself, not the excess.
- **Option <InlineMath math="65" /> millilitres** is the club's entire weekly total, not the extra amount.
- **Option <InlineMath math="15" /> millilitres** is the correct water-change dose, not the excess.

# Answer

The club added **<InlineMath math="45" /> millilitres** more dechlorinator than the guide asks for.
