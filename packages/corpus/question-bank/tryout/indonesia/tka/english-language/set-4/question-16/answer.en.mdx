The three categories are **stages of the procedure**, and the text gives each stage its own paragraph. The second paragraph covers making the projector, the third covers setting it up outside, and the fourth covers adjusting the image. Classify each action by the paragraph in which it appears and by what it does.

| Statement | Category | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Holding the foil card above your shoulder | Setting it up | "Hold the foil card above your shoulder so that sunlight falls on the foil." This puts the finished projector in position. |
| Moving the screen farther from the foil | Adjusting the image | "To make the image larger, move the screen farther from the foil." The image already exists and is being changed. |
| Pushing a pin through the center of the foil | Making the projector | "Then push the pin through the center of the foil once to make a small, round hole." This happens while the projector is being built. |

The likeliest confusion is between setting up and adjusting, because both involve holding the cards. Setting up comes first and makes the image appear. Adjusting changes an image that is already on the screen, so moving the screen farther away belongs to adjusting. Holding the card above your shoulder therefore belongs to setting it up, moving the screen belongs to adjusting the image, and the pin belongs to making the projector.
