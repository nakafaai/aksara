# Given

Five practice tanks (**Tanks P through T**) report their latest water tests in ppm, oldest first.

Two rules from the guide govern what happens next:
- **Step <InlineMath math="4" />** (cycled): both ammonia and nitrite read **<InlineMath math="0" /> ppm on three tests in a row**, and **nitrate reads above <InlineMath math="0" /> ppm** on the last test.
- **Step <InlineMath math="5" />**: if nitrate reads **above <InlineMath math="40" /> ppm** on that last test, change a quarter of the water before adding fish.

# Asked

What each tank needs now (**Ready for fish**, **Keep cycling**, or **Change a quarter of the water first**).

# Solution

Pass each tank through **two gates in order**: **Gate <InlineMath math="1" />** checks if cycling is complete (three consecutive zeros for ammonia and nitrite, plus nitrate above zero). Only tanks clearing Gate <InlineMath math="1" /> reach **Gate <InlineMath math="2" />**, which checks whether nitrate exceeds <InlineMath math="40" /> ppm.

> A nitrate reading of <InlineMath math="0" /> means cycling is unfinished, whereas an older non-zero ammonia reading does not disqualify a tank whose last three readings are all <InlineMath math="0" />.

- **Ready for fish**: **Tank P** and **Tank T**.
- **Keep cycling**: **Tank Q** and **Tank S**.
- **Change a quarter of the water first**: **Tank R**.
