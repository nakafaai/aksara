# Solution

Catch two sharp traps: **equal is not more** (statement <InlineMath math="1" />), and a general warning that **many uncertain seedlings die later** does not tell us what happened specifically in Zone B (statement <InlineMath math="5" />).

> Equal totals contradict a claim of "more", while a general trend leaves the fate of a specific subset Not stated.
