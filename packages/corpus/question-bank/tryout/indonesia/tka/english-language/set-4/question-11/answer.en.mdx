This question asks you to **join two parts of the story**: how Bayu feels when the lights go out, and what he does after his grandmother wakes up. Read each part first, then look for the option that connects them correctly.

At the start, Bayu is frightened. He pulls his knees up onto the sofa, decides to stay there until the power comes back, and the narrator says: "Nothing could make him cross that room." Then his grandmother calls, "Bayu? Where are you?", and he remembers that she cannot see well and that her stick is at the other end of the house. The next paragraph opens with "He stood up before he could think about the tall man."

Put the two parts together. The fear keeps him still, and **his grandmother's need** makes him move. The option in which his fear keeps him on the sofa but her need for help gets him moving joins both parts in the right order.

Check the order of events as well. Bayu crosses the house before the candle is lit and long before the power returns, so the change comes from his grandmother's call.

Bayu's fear keeps him on the sofa, but his grandmother's need for help gets him moving.

- "He is calm from the start, so he goes straight to the kitchen for candles." This ignores the first part, in which he is too frightened to cross the room.
- "He is afraid of the storm, so he stays on the sofa until his parents come home." He leaves the sofa soon after his grandmother calls, and his parents are not expected home before midnight.
- "He forgets his fear once the power returns, and then he looks for her stick." He fetches the stick before the power returns.
- "His grandmother is frightened too, so she asks him to stay where he is." She only asks where he is. She never asks him to stay.
