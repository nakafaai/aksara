Put the rule next to the last paragraph. The rule: "A kite always flies downwind of the hand that holds it." The last afternoon: "The wind came off the sea from the west", the flags "streamed towards the village", and the red kite "hung far out over the water to the west".

- **I is true**. The wind came "from the west".
- **II is true**. Downwind of a west wind is east, over the land and the village behind them.
- **III is true**. The red kite hung "far out over the water to the west".
- IV is false. The narrator only guesses that "The wind must have turned", and the next sentence rules it out: the flags "were still streaming towards the village".
- V is false. West of Grandfather, with the wind coming from the west, the kite was **upwind**, the opposite of what the rule allows.

So the true statements are I, II and III only.
