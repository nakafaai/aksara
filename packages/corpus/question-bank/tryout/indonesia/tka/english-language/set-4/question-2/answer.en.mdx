This question tests a detail that the text states directly, so find the sentences about the logbook and compare every part of each option with them. They come right after the description of the painted board: "Visitors write down each bird they identify, the time, and how many they counted. Every Monday, a ranger copies the new entries into the reserve's records."

The second sentence answers the question. It names **who handles the entries** (a ranger), what happens to them (they are copied into the reserve's records), and how often (every Monday). An option is correct only if all three parts match. A ranger who copies the entries every Monday does it once a week, so the option about a ranger adding them to the records once a week matches on every point.

Check the time words in each option with care. One tempting option uses the same words as the text but changes how often the copying happens.

The text says that a ranger adds the visitors' entries to the reserve's records once a week.

- "Visitors copy them onto the painted board beside the door." The board shows bird outlines that are already painted on it. Nothing is copied onto it.
- "A ranger reads them aloud to visitors on the upper platform." The text never mentions reading entries aloud, and the platform appears only in another paragraph.
- "Visitors check them against the bird outlines before they leave." The board helps visitors identify birds, but the text describes no check of the logbook.
- "A ranger copies them into the reserve's records every evening." The person and the action are right, but the time is wrong. The text gives the time as "Every Monday", which is once a week.
