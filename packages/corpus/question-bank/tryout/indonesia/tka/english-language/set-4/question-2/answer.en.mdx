# Given

For a **Gowes Kota monthly pass holder**, pedal-bike rides get <InlineMath math="45" /> free minutes, the "full dock" button adds <InlineMath math="15" /> free minutes at a full station, e-bikes always charge <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" />, and bikes left outside a dock incur a <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}25{,}000" /> recovery fee.

# Asked

Which rides cost a pass holder **nothing beyond the monthly pass** (choose all that apply).

# Solution

Check every ride against **three strict conditions**: it must use a **pedal bike (never an e-bike)**, finish **inside the free window** (<InlineMath math="45" /> minutes, or <InlineMath math="60" /> minutes with the full-dock button), and **click into a dock**.

> Failing even one condition (wrong bike type, one minute over time, or leaving the bike undocked) triggers an extra charge.

## Checking all five rides

The text states that <Highlight>a "full dock" button on the station screen adds <InlineMath math="15" /> free minutes to the ride</Highlight> and <Highlight>A bike left outside a dock is charged a recovery fee of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}25{,}000" /></Highlight>.

| Ride | Free? | Rule check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="40" />-minute pedal-bike ride ending in a dock | **Yes** | Within <InlineMath math="45" /> minutes, pedal bike, properly docked. |
| <InlineMath math="40" />-minute e-bike ride ending in a dock | **No** | Time is fine, yet an e-bike still adds <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> per ride. |

| <InlineMath math="30" />-minute pedal ride left leaning against a full station | **No** | Never clicked into a dock, triggering the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}25{,}000" /> recovery fee while the timer keeps running. |
| <InlineMath math="46" />-minute pedal-bike ride ending in a dock | **No** | One minute past <InlineMath math="45" /> starts a paid half hour (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" />). |

# Answer

The rides costing nothing beyond the monthly pass are:
- **A <InlineMath math="40" />-minute pedal-bike ride that ends in a dock**.
- **A <InlineMath math="44" />-minute pedal-bike ride to a full station, a press of the full-dock button, and <InlineMath math="12" /> more minutes to a free dock at the next station**.
