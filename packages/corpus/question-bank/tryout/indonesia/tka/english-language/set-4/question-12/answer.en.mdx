A trait counts only if **an action in the story shows it**. Judge each option by its evidence, and reject any trait that no action supports.

| Option | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He stays practical when he has to act in the dark. | Select | He keeps "one hand on the wall", finds the stick, and goes straight to the drawer "where his mother kept candles and matches". |
| He is annoyed that his grandmother needs his help. | Do not select | Nothing in the story shows annoyance. He stands up as soon as she calls, before he can even think about his fear. |
| He is able to joke about his own fear afterwards. | Select | When he sees the coat stand, "Bayu almost laughed", and later he tells her about the "tall man" so dramatically that she laughs. |
| He tries to make his grandmother feel at ease. | Select | He gives her the stick, sits beside her, and turns his fright into a funny story that makes her laugh. |

The option about annoyance is the one to watch. Bayu was afraid, but fear and annoyance are different feelings, and his quick reaction to her call shows care. The traits that his actions show are staying practical in the dark, joking about his own fear afterwards, and trying to make his grandmother feel at ease.
