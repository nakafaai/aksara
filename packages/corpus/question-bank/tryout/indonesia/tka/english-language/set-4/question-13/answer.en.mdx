Judge each description against what Grandfather does in the story.

- **Practical decisions from nature: supported**. He brings the kites in because the swallows fly low, and he knows the sea breeze runs up the cliff every afternoon.
- Exact time of the rain: not supported. He only tells the narrator to carry the kites inside. The rain comes "An hour later", but he never names a time.
- **Lesson in words first: supported**. "On my first day he gave me a lesson before he gave me a kite."
- **His brother's kite as something precious: supported**. His brother "had been dead for many years", yet Grandfather still has the kite, takes it down on the last afternoon and winds the string in "very slowly".
- Explains the red kite as a wind change: not supported. **That's the narrator's idea**, "The wind must have turned", and Grandfather "did not say a word".

So the supported descriptions are the practical decisions from nature, the lesson in words first, and the brother's kite treated as precious.
