# Given

The narrative portrays **Grandfather** through his choices: giving an oral lesson before handing over a kite, reading sea breezes and low-flying swallows before a storm, and flying then slowly winding in his late brother's red kite.

# Asked

Which descriptions of Grandfather are supported by the story (choose all that apply).

# Solution

Match each option strictly against **what Grandfather himself says and does**. Reject any claim that **invents a precise weather forecast he never gives** or **credits him with the narrator's private guess**.

> Always verify character descriptions directly against that character's own actions and speech, keeping the narrator's observations separate.

## Checking all five descriptions of Grandfather

| Description | Supported? | Direct evidence in the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Makes practical decisions from what he sees in nature | **Yes** | Brings drying kites indoors when swallows fly low and uses the daily afternoon sea breeze on the cliff. |
| Tells the narrator exactly when rain will start | **No** | He only says to carry the kites inside. Rain arrives an hour later, yet he never states a time. |
| Gives his lesson in words before letting the narrator try | **Yes** | Stated directly: <Highlight>On my first day he gave me a lesson before he gave me a kite</Highlight>. |
| Treats his late brother's kite as something precious | **Yes** | Kept it for many years after his brother's death, flew it silently on the last afternoon, and <Highlight>wound in the string very slowly</Highlight>. |
| Explains the red kite's flight as a wind change | **No** | That excuse is what the narrator <Highlight>told myself</Highlight>, whereas <Highlight>Grandfather did not say a word</Highlight>. |

# Answer

The supported descriptions of Grandfather are:
- **He makes practical decisions from what he sees in nature**.
- **He gives his lesson in words before he lets the narrator try**.
- **He treats his late brother's kite as something precious**.
