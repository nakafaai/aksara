The prompt names one function: keeping visitors **out of the birds' sight**. Several features in the text protect the birds, but only some of them do it by hiding people. Sort each option by the reason the text gives for it.

| Option | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The sign asking visitors to stop talking | Do not select | The sign is there "so that their voices do not disturb the birds". It deals with sound, and a silent visitor can still be seen. |
| The reed screens along the end of the boardwalk | Select | The screens are placed "so people walking toward the hide cannot be seen from the water". |
| The wooden flaps over the viewing slits | Select | Visitors lower a flap before they stand up "because birds on the water can notice movement through an open slit". |
| The waist-high rail on the upper platform | Do not select | The platform has "no walls, only a waist-high rail", and the text adds that birds "can easily see anyone standing up there". The rail hides no one. |

The likeliest mistake is selecting the sign, because silence also protects the birds. The prompt, however, asks about being seen, and a rule about voices does not change what the birds can see. The features that keep visitors out of sight are the reed screens and the wooden flaps.
