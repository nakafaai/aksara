# Given

Five **cause-and-effect statements** test Gowes Kota's rules: the van recovery fee, the <InlineMath math="45" />-minute pass limit, the full-dock extension, the overnight e-bike battery swap (23:00 to 5:00), and why busy stations emptied by mid-morning.

# Asked

Which numbered cause-and-effect statements are true according to the text.

# Solution

Test both halves of every claim like a two-part lock: **both the cause and the effect must match the text**. Watch especially for **swapping the old <InlineMath math="90" />-minute experiment with its <InlineMath math="45" />-minute fix**.

> A classic cause-and-effect trap blames the remedy (the <InlineMath math="45" />-minute cap) for the problem caused by the earlier policy (<InlineMath math="90" /> free minutes).

## Checking each cause and effect statement

| Statement | Verdict | Direct evidence and check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | True | Matches <Highlight>A bike left outside a dock is charged a recovery fee of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}25{,}000" /> on top of the ride, because a van has to fetch it</Highlight>. |
| II | True | A <InlineMath math="50" />-minute ride runs <InlineMath math="5" /> minutes past <InlineMath math="45" />, starting one paid half hour at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" />. |
| III | False | Adding <InlineMath math="15" /> free minutes raises the free period to <InlineMath math="60" /> minutes. A <InlineMath math="70" />-minute ride is still <InlineMath math="10" /> minutes over (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" />), not free. |
| IV | True | The text states <Highlight>E-bikes cannot be unlocked between 23:00 and 5:00, when the batteries are swapped</Highlight>, which includes midnight. |
| V | False | Stations stood empty when the office first tried **<InlineMath math="90" /> free minutes**. Cutting it to <InlineMath math="45" /> minutes was the fix, not the cause. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III** forget that <InlineMath math="60" /> free minutes still leaves <InlineMath math="10" /> chargeable minutes in a <InlineMath math="70" />-minute ride.
- **Options including V** confuse the <InlineMath math="90" />-minute trial with the <InlineMath math="45" />-minute fix.
- **I and IV only** omits the verified <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" /> calculation in II.

# Answer

The true cause-and-effect statements according to the text are **I, II, and IV only**.
