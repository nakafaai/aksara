Each strange event in the story either gets an ordinary explanation or breaks a rule the story itself has set up.

- **I could happen**. Grandfather explains it: the sea breeze runs up the cliff while houses and palms shelter the village.
- **II could happen**. Swallows "follow the insects, and the insects come down before a storm", so watching them can warn of rain.
- III couldn't happen. The flags show the wind blowing from the sea towards the village, and Grandfather's rule says "A kite always flies downwind of the hand that holds it". A kite hanging "far out over the water to the west" would be upwind. The narrator's excuse, "The wind must have turned", is **ruled out by the next sentence**: the flags "were still streaming towards the village".
- **IV could happen**. A kite with a snapped string drifts off and lands somewhere. Finding it in a tree, untorn, is lucky but ordinary.

So the events that could happen in the real world are I, II and IV only.
