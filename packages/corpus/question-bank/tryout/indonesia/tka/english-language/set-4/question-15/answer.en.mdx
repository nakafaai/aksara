# Given

**Four events from the cliff story** are listed: (I) a kite lifting on the cliff while village washing hangs still, (II) Grandfather bringing kites inside after watching swallows before heavy rain, (III) the red kite hanging over the sea while boat flags stream towards the village, and (IV) a snapped-string kite landing undamaged in a mango tree.

# Asked

Which numbered events in the story **could happen in the real world**.

# Solution

Watch the wording carefully: the question asks which events **COULD happen in reality**, not which one is impossible! Three events have **ordinary natural causes or everyday luck**, while only one defies wind physics.

> Check whether a realism-vs-fantasy item asks for possible events or impossible events before selecting your combination.

## Checking each event against reality

| Event | Possible in reality? | Evidence and explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | A coastal updraft lifts kites on an exposed cliff while buildings shelter the village (<Highlight>the sea breeze runs up the face of the cliff</Highlight>). |
| II | **Yes** | Swallows chasing low-flying insects before a storm is a real weather clue (<Highlight>They follow the insects, and the insects come down before a storm</Highlight>). |
| III | **No** | Boat flags streaming inland (<Highlight>were still streaming towards the village</Highlight>) prove a steady sea wind, making a kite hovering upwind over the sea physically impossible. |
| IV | **Yes** | A loose kite drifting into a mango tree without tearing is lucky yet completely ordinary. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III** include the sole supernatural moment that breaks wind physics.
- **I and IV only** or **II and IV only** leave out a completely realistic natural event explained by Grandfather.

# Answer

The events that could happen in the real world are **I, II, and IV only**.
