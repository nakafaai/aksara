# Given

The relay recount tracks a **<InlineMath math="70" />-lap boast** (over <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000{,}000" />), **<InlineMath math="50" /> laps ridden** (<InlineMath math="46" /> counted), a <InlineMath math="20" />-minute rain delay on an unstopped clock, and the narrator's closing insight about the stop rule.

# Asked

Which numbered statements belong in an accurate summary of the account.

# Solution

An accurate summary statement must be both **factually right and central to the story**. Statement I sounds tempting at first glance, yet **a quick calculation disproves its word "mainly"** by comparing the two shortfalls.

> Whenever a summary claim says "mainly because", compare the size of the competing causes using the passage's own numbers.

## Comparing the two causes of the shortfall

**Missing <InlineMath math="20" /> planned laps** (<InlineMath math="70" /> minus <InlineMath math="50" />) cost five times as much as losing <InlineMath math="4" /> laps to stops:

<BlockMath math="20 \times 1.5 \times \text{Rp}5{,}000 = \text{Rp}150{,}000 \quad \text{vs.} \quad 4 \times 1.5 \times \text{Rp}5{,}000 = \text{Rp}30{,}000" />

## Checking each summary statement

| Statement | Verdict | Direct evidence in the account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Excluded | The main reason they missed the boast was **riding <InlineMath math="20" /> fewer laps** (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}150{,}000" />), not the <InlineMath math="4" /> uncounted laps (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}30{,}000" />). |
| II | Belongs | They waited <Highlight>under the bandstand for twenty minutes</Highlight>, and <Highlight>The relay was four hours on the clock, whatever happened</Highlight>, so lost time was never made up. |
| III | Belongs | The table totals <InlineMath math="50" /> laps ridden and <InlineMath math="46" /> counted, meaning all but four counted. |
| IV | Belongs | The final sentence recognises the rule as <Highlight>the reason the sponsors could trust the number at all</Highlight>. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including I** misattribute the main shortfall to the <InlineMath math="4" /> uncounted laps.
- **II and III only** or **III and IV only** leave out a core pillar of the recount (the rain pause or the narrator's final realisation).

# Answer

The statements that belong in an accurate summary are **II, III, and IV only**.
