# Given

A reader reports a heap centre of **<InlineMath math="30^\circ\text{C}" /> on two checks in a row**, air at **<InlineMath math="27^\circ\text{C}" />**, and an **earthy smell**, concluding it must be ready. Crucially, the reader **never states how old the heap is**.

# Asked

Which numbered replies to the reader are supported by the guide.

# Solution

Think of the ready test as a three-box checklist. The reader checked off **temperature and smell**, yet skipped **the eight-week age box**. Because the age is unknown, a conditional rule for a young heap also follows directly from the text.

> When a rule requires all three criteria, missing one criterion leaves readiness unproven and keeps earlier-stage rules conditionally relevant.

## Checking the temperature difference

On both consecutive checks, **the centre-to-air temperature gap** is:

<BlockMath math="30^\circ\text{C} - 27^\circ\text{C} = 3^\circ\text{C} \le 5^\circ\text{C}" />

## Checking each reply

| Reply | Verdict | Direct evidence in the guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Supported | Ready condition <InlineMath math="1" /> demands <Highlight>At least eight weeks have passed since the heap was built</Highlight>, which the reader never mentions. |
| II | Supported | A <InlineMath math="3^\circ\text{C}" /> gap on both checks satisfies <Highlight>within <InlineMath math="5^\circ\text{C}" /> of the air temperature on two checks in a row</Highlight>. |
| III | Not supported | The guide permits a <InlineMath math="5^\circ\text{C}" /> margin and never demands an exact temperature match. |
| IV | Supported | The guide instructs <Highlight>During the first six weeks, turn the heap whenever the centre falls below <InlineMath math="45^\circ\text{C}" /></Highlight>. If the heap is under six weeks old, <InlineMath math="30^\circ\text{C}" /> triggers a turn. |

## Why the other options fail

- **I, II, III, and IV** invents an exact-match temperature rule not in the guide.
- **I and II only**, **II and IV only**, and **I and IV only** drop a valid point established directly by the guide's rules.

# Answer

The replies supported by the guide are **I, II, and IV only**.
