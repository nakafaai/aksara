The procedure gives the facilitator two responsibilities at once: widen access to the discussion and preserve each person's right to pass. The supported action is **Respect the pass, return to the focused question, and invite another perspective.** Forcing the third student would violate the stated right, while returning control to the same pair would leave the imbalance unchanged.
