Some of these are stated outright, and one needs two parts of the guide put together.

- **Statement 1: yes**. It joins two places. The guide says "a ratio by weight puts in far too few greens", and the problem table says a heap with "Too few greens" "Stays cold in the first week". So a heap mixed by weight is likely to stay cold.
- **Statement 2: yes**. The table gives "Smells of ammonia" with the cause "Too many greens".
- Statement 3: no. The guide says "These fixes apply at any stage", and a turn made to fix a problem is "the one exception to the six-week rule". A sour heap can be turned at eight weeks.
- Statement 4: no. "A working heap reaches <InlineMath math="55" /> to <InlineMath math="65^\circ\text{C}" /> within its first week", so <InlineMath math="60^\circ\text{C}" /> is a sign of a healthy heap, not a wet one.
- **Statement 5: yes**. A heap that stays cold in its first week and is damp with no smell has "Too few greens", and the fix is "Add greens". Water is the fix only for a heap that is dry.

So statements 1, 2 and 5 can be worked out.
