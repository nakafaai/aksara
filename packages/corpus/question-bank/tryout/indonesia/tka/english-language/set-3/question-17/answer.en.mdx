# Given

The garden log lists **five compost heaps (A through E)** with no smell problem. The guide sets three age-and-temperature rules:
- First <InlineMath math="6" /> weeks: turn whenever the centre falls below <InlineMath math="45^\circ\text{C}" />.
- After <InlineMath math="6" /> weeks: stop turning and let the heap cool.
- Ready to use only when **all three conditions hold**: at least <InlineMath math="8" /> weeks old, within <InlineMath math="5^\circ\text{C}" /> of air temperature on **two checks in a row**, and earthy-smelling.

# Asked

What each heap needs today (**Ready to use**, **Turn it now**, or **Leave it as it is**).

# Solution

Picture each heap passing through an **age gate first**: heaps up to <InlineMath math="6" /> weeks follow the turning rule, heaps past <InlineMath math="6" /> weeks stop turning, and heaps aged <InlineMath math="8" /> weeks or more face the three-part ready test.

> Today's reading alone is never enough for the ready test: the centre must sit within <InlineMath math="5^\circ\text{C}" /> of the air on two consecutive checks.
