The outline must preserve the **order and purpose** of the stages.

| Stage | Action from the passage |
| --- | --- |
| Focus and prepare | Display one question, define terms, and allow quiet writing. |
| Exchange and restate | Restate the previous idea accurately before adding a contribution. |
| Widen participation | Invite other voices while respecting the right to pass. |
| Revise | Write whether a view changed, strengthened, or needs more information. |
| Review the process | Check accuracy, evidence, and fair invitations. |

Ranking speakers or choosing a winner changes the goal. The passage protects access to discussion rather than rewarding the fastest response.
