The original gap lets damp air move behind the rack. Pushing it against the wall removes that path while leaving the awning and clips unchanged. The most direct consequence is **less air circulation behind the prints**. The bridges, cooking lamps, and central drain are in other parts of the market and are unaffected by this local change.
