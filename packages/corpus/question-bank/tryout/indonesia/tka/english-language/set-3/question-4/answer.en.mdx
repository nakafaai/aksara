# Given

Last year's survey of **<InlineMath math="210" /> members** found that <InlineMath math="64\%" /> had put off buying a tool because they could borrow it. The closing sentence adds that the survey **did not ask which tools, or whether those members bought them later anyway**.

# Asked

Which numbered conclusions are supported by the survey result as reported in the text.

# Solution

Think of a survey result like a photograph showing one slice of a room. You may conclude **what is inside the frame** (including direct arithmetic), but never invent details the survey explicitly left unasked.

> Good survey reading respects three strict boundaries: the exact sample surveyed, the exact action asked about, and every gap the author flags as unasked.

## Checking the member count

To test statement II, **compute the exact share** <InlineMath math="64\%" /> of the <InlineMath math="210" /> surveyed members:

<BlockMath math="0.64 \times 210 = 134.4 \approx 134 \text{ members}" />

## Checking each conclusion

| Conclusion | Verdict | Direct evidence from the text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Supported | <Highlight><InlineMath math="64\%" /> had put off buying a tool because they could borrow it</Highlight> is more than half of the surveyed group, so "most" holds. |
| II | Supported | As computed above, <InlineMath math="64\%" /> of <InlineMath math="210" /> equals <InlineMath math="134.4" />, or about <InlineMath math="134" /> surveyed members. |
| III | Not supported | Fails twice: the survey covered <InlineMath math="210" /> respondents, not all library members, and <Highlight>The survey did not ask which tools, or whether those members bought them later anyway</Highlight>, so "never bought" overreaches. |
| IV | Not supported | The survey asked only about delaying a purchase and never mentions money saved. |

## Why the other options fail

- **I only** misses the accurate count of about <InlineMath math="134" /> members in II.
- **Options including III or IV** turn "put off" into "never bought" or invent financial savings not reported by the survey.

# Answer

The conclusions supported by the survey result are **I and II only**.
