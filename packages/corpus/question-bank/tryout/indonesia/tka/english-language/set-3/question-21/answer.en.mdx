A single broken part may need repair. Records of repeated failures across identical items also help the purchasing team compare the reliability of designs for its next purchase. The supporting statement is **Repeated failures across identical items provide evidence for choosing a better design.** The pattern informs evaluation. The pattern does not prove that design caused every failure.
