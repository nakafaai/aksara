Every strange event in the story gets an ordinary explanation except one, and the details that rule that one out are in the first paragraph.

- I could happen. Uncle shows a wire "whose end had worked loose", touching only when the set was tipped forward, and lifting the radio tipped it.
- II could happen. Belang "simply heard the engine at the corner, long before we could".
- **III couldn't happen**. Grandmother's radio "ran only on mains power, and that morning he had pulled its plug from the wall", yet the song "was coming from the top shelf" and the speaker cloth "trembled under my fingers". **An unplugged mains radio can't play**.
- IV could happen. Uncle "set the wall clock ten minutes fast".

So the only event that couldn't happen in the real world is III.
