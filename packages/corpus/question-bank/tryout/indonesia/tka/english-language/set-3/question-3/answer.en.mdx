# Given

Dimas returns a green-band hedge cutter **<InlineMath math="12" /> days late** (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" />/day), and Sari returns a yellow-band sander **<InlineMath math="4" /> days late** (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" />/day). Both tools are clean, working, and have no deposit, under a ten-day late-fee cap.

# Asked

Which numbered statements about Dimas's and Sari's late fees are true.

# Solution

Think of the ten-day limit as a ceiling on **billable days**, not on the total rupiah owed. Calculate **each person's capped fee first**, then check the four statements directly against the rules.

> The library's ten-day cap limits the number of charged days, so a higher daily rate still yields a higher maximum fine.

## Computing both late fees

The passage sets a firm cap: <Highlight>No loan is ever charged more than ten days of late fees</Highlight>. Dimas's <InlineMath math="12" /> late days are capped at <InlineMath math="10" />, while Sari pays for all <InlineMath math="4" /> days:

<BlockMath math="\text{Dimas: } 10 \times \text{Rp}2{,}000 = \text{Rp}20{,}000, \qquad \text{Sari: } 4 \times \text{Rp}5{,}000 = \text{Rp}20{,}000" />

Checking the two hypothetical scenarios in statements III and IV gives:

<BlockMath math="\text{Dimas uncapped: } 12 \times \text{Rp}2{,}000 = \text{Rp}24{,}000, \qquad \text{Sari at 12 days (capped at 10): } 10 \times \text{Rp}5{,}000 = \text{Rp}50{,}000" />

## Checking each statement

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence and calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | True | Both owe exactly <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" />. |
| II | True | Neither green nor yellow bands require a deposit, and for non-red tools fees <Highlight>are added to the member's account</Highlight>. |
| III | True | Without the cap, Dimas would owe <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}24{,}000" />, which exceeds Sari's <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" />. |
| IV | False | At <InlineMath math="12" /> days late, Sari's capped fine is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}50{,}000" />, which is more than Dimas's <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" />, not less. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including IV** invert the comparison between <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}50{,}000" /> and <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" />.
- **I and II only** or **I and III only** drop a verified truth (the non-red account rule in II or the uncapped <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}24{,}000" /> comparison in III).

# Answer

The true statements about their late fees are **I, II, and III only**.
