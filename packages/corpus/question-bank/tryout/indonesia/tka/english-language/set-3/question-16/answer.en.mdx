The guide's ratio is "about three parts browns ... to one part greens", measured "by the bucketful, not by weight". First sort what's already in the heap.

**Browns**. Shredded cardboard and dry leaves are both browns: <InlineMath math="9 + 2 = 11" /> buckets.

**Greens**. Fresh grass is a green: <InlineMath math="4" /> buckets.

**What the ratio needs**. Four buckets of greens need three times as many browns:

<BlockMath math="4 \times 3 = 12 \text{ buckets of browns}, \quad 12 - 11 = 1 \text{ more bucket}" />

The heap is already **one bucket of browns short**, so adding scraps, which are greens, would push it further off.

- <InlineMath math="3" /> more buckets of browns forgets that the dry leaves are browns too.
- <InlineMath math="9" /> more buckets of browns counts the dry leaves as greens: <InlineMath math="6" /> greens would need <InlineMath math="18" /> browns, and <InlineMath math="18 - 9 = 9" />.
- <InlineMath math="3" /> buckets of scraps treats the heap as if it held only the cardboard, and forgets the grass is already a green.
- <InlineMath math="3" /> kilograms makes the same mistake and also measures by weight, which the guide rules out.

So Raka should add <InlineMath math="1" /> more bucket of browns and none of the scraps.
