# Given

Raka's new compost heap already holds **<InlineMath math="9" /> buckets of shredded cardboard**, **<InlineMath math="4" /> buckets of fresh grass**, and **<InlineMath math="2" /> buckets of dry leaves**, plus he has a <InlineMath math="12\text{-kg}" /> sack of vegetable scraps on hand.

The Sido Rukun guide calls for **<InlineMath math="3" /> parts browns** (dry leaves, shredded cardboard) to **<InlineMath math="1" /> part greens** (vegetable scraps, fresh grass), measured **by the bucketful, not by weight**.

# Asked

What Raka should add to bring his heap to the guide's ratio.

# Solution

Think of following a recipe with measuring cups instead of a kitchen scale. First **sort everything already inside the heap into browns and greens**, then calculate how many buckets are missing for a <InlineMath math="3 : 1" /> ratio.

> Group every listed ingredient into browns or greens first, and measure strictly by the unit the guide requires (buckets, never kilograms).

## Sorting current ingredients and finding the gap

The guide instructs: <Highlight>Mix about three parts browns (dry leaves, shredded cardboard) to one part greens (vegetable scraps, fresh grass). Measure by the bucketful, not by weight</Highlight>.

- **Browns** (shredded cardboard plus dry leaves): <InlineMath math="9" /> buckets plus <InlineMath math="2" /> buckets gives **<InlineMath math="11" /> buckets**.
- **Greens** (fresh grass): **<InlineMath math="4" /> buckets**.

To balance <InlineMath math="4" /> buckets of greens at a <InlineMath math="3 : 1" /> ratio, Raka needs:

<BlockMath math="4 \times 3 = 12 \text{ buckets of browns}, \qquad 12 - 11 = 1 \text{ more bucket}" />

Because the heap is already **short by <InlineMath math="1" /> bucket of browns**, adding vegetable scraps (which are greens) would push it further off ratio.

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="3" /> more bucketfuls of browns** forgets that the <InlineMath math="2" /> buckets of dry leaves are browns too.
- **<InlineMath math="9" /> more bucketfuls of browns** misclassifies dry leaves as greens (<InlineMath math="6" /> greens needing <InlineMath math="18" /> browns).
- **<InlineMath math="3" /> bucketfuls of the scraps** treats the heap as holding only cardboard and ignores the <InlineMath math="4" /> buckets of fresh grass.
- **<InlineMath math="3" /> kilograms of the scraps** makes the same error and violates the no-weight rule.

# Answer

Raka should add **<InlineMath math="1" /> more bucketful of browns, and none of the scraps**.
