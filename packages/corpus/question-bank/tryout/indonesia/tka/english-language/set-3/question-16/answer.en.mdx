Fairness is checked through **three observable features**: accurate restatements, evidence connected to claims, and invitations that preserve the right to pass. These are the criteria named in the final review.

A dominant speaker who invites others only at the end does not establish fair access. Keeping an original view is not itself a failure, because the procedure allows participants to retain a view for stronger reasons. Speed and confidence do not replace evidence.
