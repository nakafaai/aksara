Check each statement against what Uncle Bonar says and does.

- **I is supported**. He finds the loose wire and says, "Magic is just a fault you haven't found yet."
- **II is supported**. He sets the clock fast "so that he would never keep Aunt Mira waiting at dinner".
- III isn't. He "waved away her money", and the cake is a joke he makes "every time". The story never shows him taking cake instead.
- IV isn't. The idea that the song came "through the wall from next door" is what the narrator tells herself. Uncle says nothing.
- **V is supported**. When the lullaby plays, he "stood very still", then smiled, lifted the radio down, dusted it and set it where customers would see it, **all without a word**.

So the supported statements are I, II and V only.
