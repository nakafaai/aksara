Teks menunjukkan bahwa **preparation time, invitations, and the right to pass protect different ways of participating**. Karena itu, jawaban yang tepat adalah:

- **Provide quiet writing time.**
- **Invite someone who has not spoken.**
- **Allow an invited person to pass.**
