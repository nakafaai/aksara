# Given

The relay ran **from <InlineMath math="6:00" /> to <InlineMath math="10:00" />** (<InlineMath math="4" /> straight hours on the clock). At **<InlineMath math="8:40" />**, rain forced the team under the bandstand for **twenty minutes**. The **[GAP]** sits right after that pause and right before the sentence noting that Dimas's interrupted lap did not count.

# Asked

Which numbered sentences could fill **[GAP]** in paragraph four without contradicting any fact in the account.

# Solution

Think of **[GAP]** as a jigsaw piece that must lock cleanly into every surrounding edge. Test each sentence against **four fixed facts**: the <InlineMath math="6:00" /> start, the <InlineMath math="20" />-minute wait from <InlineMath math="8:40" />, the unstoppable clock, and Dimas's uncounted lap.

> A gap-fill sentence works only when it respects every timeline calculation and rule stated both before and after the gap.

## Checking the clock when the rain eased

Adding <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes to <InlineMath math="8:40" /> brings the clock to **<InlineMath math="9:00" />**, which is **<InlineMath math="3" /> hours** into the <InlineMath math="4" />-hour relay.

Adding <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes to 8:40 reaches 9:00, which means <InlineMath math="3" /> of the <InlineMath math="4" /> relay hours have passed since 6:00.

## Checking each candidate sentence

| Sentence | Verdict | Fit with surrounding facts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Fits | <InlineMath math="8:40" /> plus twenty minutes is <InlineMath math="9:00" />, and because <Highlight>The relay was four hours on the clock, whatever happened</Highlight>, the lost minutes could not be ridden back. |
| II | Contradicts | Stopping the watch directly violates the unbroken four-hour clock rule. |
| III | Fits | From <InlineMath math="6:00" /> to <InlineMath math="9:00" /> is exactly <InlineMath math="3" /> hours, so three of the four hours had passed. |
| IV | Contradicts | The sentence right after the gap states <Highlight>Dimas finished his interrupted lap, but it did not count</Highlight>, matching his <InlineMath math="5" /> of <InlineMath math="6" /> counted laps in the table. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including II** contradict the non-stop four-hour clock rule.
- **Options including IV** contradict both the next sentence and the rider table.
- **I only** misses that sentence III accurately computes the <InlineMath math="3" /> hours elapsed between <InlineMath math="6:00" /> and <InlineMath math="9:00" />.

# Answer

The sentences that can fill **[GAP]** without contradicting the account are **I and III only**.
