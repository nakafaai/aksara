The distinction is the **source of each claim**. The visual description records what can be seen in the photograph. The name and approximate year come from the visitor's memory.

Keeping both sources identifiable lets later researchers check new evidence without erasing the earlier record. It neither makes the approximate year final nor gives memory automatic priority. Waiting for complete certainty would also contradict the archive's practice of recording useful, limited evidence.
