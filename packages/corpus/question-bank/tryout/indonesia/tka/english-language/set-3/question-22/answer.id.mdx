Teks menunjukkan bahwa **safety, expected life, alternatives, and a recorded reason make the trade-off visible**. Karena itu, jawaban yang tepat adalah:

- **Assess safety.**
- **Estimate additional useful life.**
- **Record the reason for the final choice.**
