The proposed check assesses safety, estimates the additional useful life after repair, and records the reason for the final choice. The supported actions are:

- Assess **safety**.
- Estimate **additional useful life**.
- Record the **reason for the final choice**.

Ignoring replacement contradicts the decision record, which explicitly includes a replacement option. Repair-first means checking repair, not choosing it regardless of the evidence.
