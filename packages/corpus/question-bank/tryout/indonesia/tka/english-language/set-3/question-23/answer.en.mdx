Distinguish a **targeted repair benefit** from a problem that justifies **considering replacement**.

| Item | Category | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replacing only a worn wheel | Repair benefit | Avoids replacing the entire device for one damaged part. |
| Requiring an unapproved battery | Replacement reason | Raises a safety concern named in the passage. |
| Failing to meet accessibility needs | Replacement reason | Continued use does not meet users' needs. |

These categories do not make repair automatic. The decision must still consider safety, support, useful life, and cost.
