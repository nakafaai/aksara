# Given

The writer's safety claim rests on **comparing recorded incidents in dimmed Sukamaju against bright Sukajadi**: burglaries (<InlineMath math="18" /> to <InlineMath math="15" />) and road crashes (<InlineMath math="6" /> to <InlineMath math="5" />) did not worsen.

# Asked

Which numbered findings, if true, would **weaken the support** that the trial gives the writer's safety claim.

# Solution

Picture the trial as a controlled experiment comparing two sets of streets. Its support weakens whenever **the recorded counts stop reflecting reality** or **an extra safety factor protects only the dimmed group**.

> To weaken experimental evidence, look for reporting bias that distorts the counts or a confounding factor applied unevenly between the two groups.

## Testing each finding against the safety claim

The writer argues <Highlight>the city's own trial suggests that it would not make those streets less safe</Highlight> because <Highlight>nothing in the trial got worse</Highlight>.

| Finding | Weakens claim? | Effect on the trial's safety evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Weakens** | If dimmed-street residents reported far fewer burglaries in <InlineMath math="2025" />, the recorded drop from <InlineMath math="18" /> to <InlineMath math="15" /> could mask an actual rise. |
| II | Does not weaken | Showing complaints came from people never out past midnight discounts the complaints, which **helps** the writer. |
| III | **Weakens** | Nightly police patrols on dimmed streets alone introduce a confounding variable: patrols, not safe dimming, could explain why incidents stayed low. |
| IV | Does not weaken | Higher electricity prices affect cost savings, not the trial's safety counts. |

## Why the other options fail

- **I only** or **III and IV only** drops a genuine flaw in the trial or brings in irrelevant electricity prices.
- **Options including II** reverse the direction of the evidence, since II strengthens the writer's dismissal of complaints.

# Answer

The findings that would weaken the trial's support for the safety claim are **I and III only**.
