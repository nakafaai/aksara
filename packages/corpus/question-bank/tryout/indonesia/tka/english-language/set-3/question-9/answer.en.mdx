# Given

Eight riders took turns **one lap each in a fixed order**: (<InlineMath math="1" />) Rina, (<InlineMath math="2" />) Yusuf, (<InlineMath math="3" />) Dimas, (<InlineMath math="4" />) Ayu, (<InlineMath math="5" />) Bagas, (<InlineMath math="6" />) Fitri, (<InlineMath math="7" />) Hendra, and (<InlineMath math="8" />) the narrator. Rain stopped play at <InlineMath math="8:40" /> when Dimas was **halfway through his fifth lap**.

# Asked

Which listed events happened **before the rain** stopped the relay (choose all that apply).

# Solution

Because the passage gives no clock times for most incidents, picture numbering every lap from **<InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="50" />**. Each rider's turns sit exactly <InlineMath math="8" /> laps apart, so comparing lap numbers immediately reveals what happened before the rain.

> In a fixed <InlineMath math="8" />-rider rotation, rider <InlineMath math="r" />'s <InlineMath math="k" />-th lap is always lap number <InlineMath math="r + 8(k - 1)" />.

## Finding the lap when the rain arrived

Dimas rides <InlineMath math="3" />rd and was <Highlight>halfway round his fifth lap</Highlight> when the sky opened. His fifth lap is:

<BlockMath math="3 + 8 \times (5 - 1) = 35" />

So the rain arrived **midway through lap <InlineMath math="35" />**. Any event occurring earlier happened before the rain.

## Checking every listed event

| Event | Lap number | Before the rain? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rina's chain came off (her <InlineMath math="3" />rd lap, position <InlineMath math="1" />) | <InlineMath math="17" /> (<InlineMath math="1, 9, 17" />) | **Yes**, well before lap <InlineMath math="35" />. |
| Bagas stopped for a cat (his last lap, <InlineMath math="6" />th, position <InlineMath math="5" />) | <InlineMath math="45" /> (<InlineMath math="5, 13, 21, 29, 37, 45" />) | **No**, after lap <InlineMath math="35" />. |
| The narrator put a foot down (<Highlight>my very first lap</Highlight>, position <InlineMath math="8" />) | <InlineMath math="8" /> | **Yes**, early in the relay before lap <InlineMath math="35" />. |
| Yusuf rode his <InlineMath math="7" />th lap (position <InlineMath math="2" />) | <InlineMath math="50" /> (final lap of the day) | **No**, after lap <InlineMath math="35" />. |
| Dimas began the uncounted lap | Start of lap <InlineMath math="35" /> | **Yes**, since he was already <Highlight>halfway round</Highlight> when the rain hit. |

# Answer

The events that happened before the rain stopped the relay are:
- **Rina's chain came off**.
- **The narrator put a foot down at the corner by the fountain**.
- **Dimas began the lap that did not count**.
