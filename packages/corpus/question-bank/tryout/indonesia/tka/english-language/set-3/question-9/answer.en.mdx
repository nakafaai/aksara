The account never gives a clock time for most of these events, so you have to number the laps. The riders kept "a fixed order, one lap each": Rina, Yusuf, Dimas, Ayu, Bagas, Fitri, Hendra and the narrator. Each rider's laps are therefore <InlineMath math="8" /> apart.

**When did the rain come**? At 8:40, "with Dimas halfway round his fifth lap". Dimas rides laps <InlineMath math="3, 11, 19, 27, 35" />, so the rain came during lap <InlineMath math="35" />.

- Rina's chain came off on her third lap. Rina rides laps <InlineMath math="1, 9, 17" />, so this was lap <InlineMath math="17" />, **before** the rain.
- Bagas stopped on his last lap. The table shows he rode <InlineMath math="6" /> laps: <InlineMath math="5, 13, 21, 29, 37, 45" />. His last was lap <InlineMath math="45" />, after the rain.
- The narrator put a foot down on "my very first lap", lap <InlineMath math="8" />, before the rain.
- Yusuf's seventh lap is lap <InlineMath math="2 + 6 \times 8 = 50" />, the very last lap of the day, after the rain.
- Dimas's uncounted lap is the one the rain interrupted. He was "halfway round" it at 8:40, so it began before the rain.

So the events before the rain are Rina's chain coming off, the narrator's foot down at the fountain, and the start of Dimas's uncounted lap.
