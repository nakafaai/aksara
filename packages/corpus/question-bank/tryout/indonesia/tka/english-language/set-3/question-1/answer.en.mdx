# Given

A member borrows a red-band pressure washer on **Friday** and returns it on the **following Wednesday**, working properly but covered in mud.

From the library table and rules:
- Red-band tools have a <InlineMath math="3" />-day loan period, a <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}150{,}000" /> deposit, and a late fee of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" /> per day.
- Day one is the day the tool leaves, every calendar day counts, the library closes on Mondays, and a dirty tool incurs a flat <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" /> cleaning fee.

# Asked

How much of the deposit the member receives back.

# Solution

Picture renting a tool for three days starting Friday. Work through the problem in three clear steps: **find the due day**, **count the late days**, and **subtract every fee from the deposit**.

> The Monday grace rule protects only tools that fall due on a Monday, never tools that already fell due on Sunday.

## Finding the due day and late days

The passage states that the loan period counts <Highlight>every calendar day, and the day a tool leaves the library is day one</Highlight>. Counting <InlineMath math="3" /> days from Friday gives **Friday (day <InlineMath math="1" />), Saturday (day <InlineMath math="2" />), and Sunday (day <InlineMath math="3" />)**, so the washer is due on Sunday.

Returning it on Wednesday makes **Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday** late (<InlineMath math="3" /> days). The Monday exception does not apply because it covers only <Highlight>a tool that falls due on a Monday</Highlight>, whereas this tool fell due on Sunday.

## Calculating the returned deposit

For red-band tools, both the cleaning charge and any late fee <Highlight>are taken from the deposit</Highlight>. Subtracting three late days and the mud-cleaning fee leaves:

<BlockMath math="\text{Rp}150{,}000 - 3 \times \text{Rp}20{,}000 - \text{Rp}10{,}000 = \text{Rp}80{,}000" />

## Why the other options fail

- **Option <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}100{,}000" />** skips Monday because the library is closed, counting only <InlineMath math="2" /> late days.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}60{,}000" />** mistakenly counts Sunday (the due day itself) as late, charging <InlineMath math="4" /> late days.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}120{,}000" />** starts counting on Saturday, pushing the due day to Monday and charging only Wednesday.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}90{,}000" />** forgets the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" /> cleaning fee for the mud.

# Answer

The member gets **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}80{,}000" />** of the deposit back.
