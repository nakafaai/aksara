Connect **each protective feature to its function** in the craft area.

| Feature | Rain-related problem addressed |
| --- | --- |
| Vertical racks beneath deep awnings | Keep paper prints under shelter. |
| A gap between rack and wall | Lets damp air circulate behind the prints. |
| Clips on the lower corners | Keep the prints in place when wind enters. |

The correct option combines all three details. Fruit covers, bridge edges, cooking lamps, and the drain belong to other parts of the market.
