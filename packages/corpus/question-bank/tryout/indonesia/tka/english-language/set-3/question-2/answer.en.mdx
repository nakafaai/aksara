Check each loan against every rule: the number of tools allowed, the due day (counted from the day the tool leaves, as in "taken out on the 1st is due back on the 14th"), the Monday rule, and the cleaning charge.

- **I is free**. A green-band tool out on a Tuesday is due on day <InlineMath math="14" />, a Monday. The Tuesday two weeks later is the next day, and "a tool that falls due on a Monday may come back on the Tuesday without a late fee".
- II isn't. A yellow-band tool out on a Sunday is due on the Saturday, its seventh day. The next Sunday is one day late: <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" />.
- **III is free**. A red-band tool has <InlineMath math="3" /> days: Saturday, Sunday and Monday. It **falls due on a Monday**, so the Tuesday is allowed, and because it comes back clean and working, the whole deposit comes back too.
- IV isn't. Dried paint makes the sander dirty, so the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" /> cleaning charge is added to the account.
- V isn't. Out on a Thursday, the sander is due on the Wednesday, its seventh day, so the next Thursday is one day late.

So the loans with no charge are I and III only.
