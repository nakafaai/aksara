# Given

Five borrowing scenarios (I through V) are tested against the Selaras Tool Library rules: **green <InlineMath math="14" /> days**, **yellow <InlineMath math="7" /> days**, **red <InlineMath math="3" /> days**, inclusive day-one counting, Monday-to-Tuesday grace rule, and a <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" /> fee for dirty returns.

# Asked

Which loans follow every library rule and finish with zero charge for the member.

# Solution

Picture marking day one on a wall calendar the day a tool leaves. Because that first day counts, **a <InlineMath math="7" />-day loan is due the day before the same weekday next week**, and only a Monday due date earns a free Tuesday return.

> Watch the inclusive count: a <InlineMath math="7" />-day or <InlineMath math="14" />-day loan taken out on a given weekday falls due on the weekday right before it, not on the same weekday.

## Checking every loan against the rules

| Loan | Verdict | Due-day count and condition check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Free | Green (<InlineMath math="14" /> days) out Tuesday is due on day <InlineMath math="14" />, a Monday. By the rule <Highlight>a tool that falls due on a Monday may come back on the Tuesday without a late fee</Highlight>, returning it clean on Tuesday costs nothing. |
| II | Charged | Yellow (<InlineMath math="7" /> days) out Sunday is due Saturday (day <InlineMath math="7" />). Returning Sunday is <InlineMath math="1" /> day late (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" />). |
| III | Free | Red (<InlineMath math="3" /> days) out Saturday counts Saturday, Sunday, Monday. It falls due on Monday, so clean and working on Tuesday earns no fee and returns the full deposit. |
| IV | Charged | Dried paint means the sander is dirty, triggering the cleaning charge of <Highlight>a flat <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" /></Highlight>. |
| V | Charged | Yellow (<InlineMath math="7" /> days) out Thursday is due Wednesday (day <InlineMath math="7" />), making the next Thursday <InlineMath math="1" /> day late. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including II or V** forget that the day a tool leaves is already day <InlineMath math="1" />.
- **III only** overlooks that loan I also falls due on a Monday (<InlineMath math="14" /> days from Tuesday), making the Tuesday return free.

# Answer

The loans that follow every rule with no charge at all are **I and III only**.
