A summary statement must be true and must matter to the whole story.

- **I belongs**. The student's radio (a loose wire), Belang at the door (she hears the engine) and the fast clock (set on purpose) are odd events, and each gets an ordinary explanation.
- **II belongs**. The radio "ran only on mains power", and Uncle "had pulled its plug from the wall" that morning. This is the event the story turns on.
- III doesn't belong. The narrator tells herself the song "must be coming through the wall from next door" and goes on sweeping. She tells Uncle **nothing**.
- **IV belongs**. Uncle "lifted the radio down, dusted it and set it on the counter, where every customer would see it".
- V doesn't belong. Uncle says nothing about the song. The idea of next door is the narrator's.

So the accurate summary contains I, II and IV only.
