# Given

Six statements about the Selaras Tool Library must be sorted into **Supported** (stated or directly entailed by the numbers), **Contradicted** (directly opposed by the text), or **Not stated** (the text does not settle the claim).

# Asked

Whether the text supports, contradicts, or gives insufficient information to decide each statement.

# Solution

Picture sitting at a fact-checking desk with three stamps. Keep **Contradicted** (the passage states the opposite) strictly separate from **Not stated** (the passage stays silent or notes that data was never collected).

> A high deposit may hint that a tool is pricey, yet an unstated price remains Not stated until the text actually reports replacement costs.
