Use the **main purpose** of each feature, rather than its color or position.

| Feature | Category | Supporting detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yellow-edged wooden bridges | Safe movement | Mark safe crossing points over the channels. |
| Vertical racks beneath deep awnings | Product protection | Shelter fabric bags and paper prints. |
| A blue circle kept clear of chairs | Drainage | Keeps the central drain clear for flowing water. |
