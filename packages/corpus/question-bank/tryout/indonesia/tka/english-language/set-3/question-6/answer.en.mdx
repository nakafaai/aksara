# Given

Eight Class <InlineMath math="11\text{C}" /> riders cycled a relay on a **<InlineMath math="1.5\text{-km}" /> loop**. Per-kilometre sponsors pledged **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> per kilometre**, and fixed sponsors pledged **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500{,}000" />** regardless of distance.

From the table, the team completed **<InlineMath math="50" /> laps ridden** and **<InlineMath math="46" /> laps counted**, yielding <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}845{,}000" /> in the envelope.

# Asked

What total the envelope would have shown if every lap ridden had counted.

# Solution

Picture the envelope holding two pools of money: a fixed pool (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500{,}000" />) and a distance pool (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> per kilometre). **Verify the formula on the real <InlineMath math="46" /> counted laps first**, then apply the exact same formula to all <InlineMath math="50" /> laps ridden.

> Watch the billing unit carefully: sponsors paid per kilometre (<InlineMath math="1.5\text{ km}" /> per lap), not per lap.

## Verifying the formula against the actual envelope

The text states that sponsors promised <Highlight><InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> for every kilometre the team rode</Highlight> plus <Highlight><InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500{,}000" /> between them, however far we went</Highlight>. Checking the <InlineMath math="46" /> counted laps gives:

<BlockMath math="46 \times 1.5 = 69\text{ km}, \qquad 69 \times \text{Rp}5{,}000 + \text{Rp}500{,}000 = \text{Rp}845{,}000" />

That **matches the actual envelope**, confirming our formula.

## Calculating the total for all ridden laps

Adding all eight rows of the *Laps ridden* column gives **<InlineMath math="50" /> laps**:

<BlockMath math="50 \times 1.5 = 75\text{ km}, \qquad 75 \times \text{Rp}5{,}000 + \text{Rp}500{,}000 = \text{Rp}875{,}000" />

## Why the other options fail

- **Option <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}865{,}000" />** adds <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> per extra lap instead of per kilometre for the <InlineMath math="4" /> uncounted laps.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}750{,}000" />** makes the same per-lap mistake across all <InlineMath math="50" /> laps.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}025{,}000" />** computes the Friday boast of <InlineMath math="70" /> laps rather than the laps actually ridden.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}375{,}000" />** drops the fixed sponsors' <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500{,}000" />.

# Answer

If every ridden lap had counted, the envelope would have shown **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}875{,}000" />**.
