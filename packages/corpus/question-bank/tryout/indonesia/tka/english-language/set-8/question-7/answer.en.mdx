# Given

In the **egg incubator** table: **<InlineMath math="15" />** eggs started, **<InlineMath math="12" />** were still developing at candling on **day <InlineMath math="7" />**, **<InlineMath math="10" />** were still developing at candling on **day <InlineMath math="14" />**, and **<InlineMath math="9" />** hatched.

# Asked

The share **of the eggs that never hatched** that was removed at the candling on day <InlineMath math="14" />.

# Solution

Read the denominator carefully: the question asks for a share **of the eggs that never hatched** (<InlineMath math="6" /> eggs), not of all <InlineMath math="15" /> eggs. Comparing day <InlineMath math="7" /> (<InlineMath math="12" />) with day <InlineMath math="14" /> (<InlineMath math="10" />) shows **<InlineMath math="2" /> eggs** were removed on day <InlineMath math="14" />.

> Always verify which subgroup forms the denominator before simplifying a fraction.

## Computing the denominator and day fourteen removals

The text explains <Highlight>At each candling we held the eggs up to a torch and took out the ones that had stopped developing</Highlight>. Computing **total unhatched, day-<InlineMath math="14" /> removals, and the share**:

<BlockMath math="\text{Never hatched} = 15 - 9 = 6, \qquad \text{Removed on day 14} = 12 - 10 = 2, \qquad \text{Share} = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}" />

## Why the other four fractions fail

- **One half** (<InlineMath math="\frac{3}{6}" />) counts the <InlineMath math="3" /> eggs removed on day <InlineMath math="7" />.
- **One sixth** (<InlineMath math="\frac{1}{6}" />) counts the <InlineMath math="1" /> egg remaining after day <InlineMath math="14" /> that failed to hatch.
- **Two fifteenths** (<InlineMath math="\frac{2}{15}" />) uses all <InlineMath math="15" /> eggs as the denominator instead of the <InlineMath math="6" /> unhatched ones.
- **One fifth** (<InlineMath math="\frac{2}{10}" />) divides by the <InlineMath math="10" /> remaining eggs instead of the <InlineMath math="6" /> unhatched ones.

# Answer

**One third** (<InlineMath math="\frac{1}{3}" />) of the eggs that never hatched were taken out on day <InlineMath math="14" />.
