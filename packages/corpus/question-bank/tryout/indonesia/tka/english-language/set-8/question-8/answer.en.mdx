# Given

On Bu Ratna's chart, the first Monday is **day <InlineMath math="0" />**, candlings happen on **day <InlineMath math="7" />** and **day <InlineMath math="14" />**, turning lasts until **day <InlineMath math="18" />**, and chicks hatch on **day <InlineMath math="21" />**.

Because the narrator counted Monday as day <InlineMath math="1" />, turning stopped **one day early** (day <InlineMath math="17" /> on the chart), and on Saturday (**day <InlineMath math="19" />**) the narrator promised chicks for Sunday.

# Asked

The chronological order of events P, Q, R, S, and T.

# Solution

Convert every single event onto **one single calendar** (Bu Ratna's chart days). Because the narrator stopped turning **a day early**, **P** (day <InlineMath math="17" />) happened right **before** **S** (day <InlineMath math="18" />).

> Converting two shifted numbering systems onto one shared calendar reveals the exact sequence immediately.

## Mapping all five events onto Bu Ratna chart

The narrator writes <Highlight>So I stopped the turning one day early, and on the Saturday after the candling on day <InlineMath math="14" />, I told the whole class that the chicks would come the next morning</Highlight>. Placing **P through T on the chart calendar**:

- **R (day <InlineMath math="14" />)**: Second candling with a torch.
- **P (day <InlineMath math="17" />)**: Narrator stops turning one day early.
- **S (day <InlineMath math="18" />)**: Official last turning day on Bu Ratna's chart.
- **Q (day <InlineMath math="19" />, Saturday)**: Narrator tells the class chicks will hatch Sunday morning.
- **T (day <InlineMath math="21" />, Monday)**: First chick breaks through its shell.

## Why the other four orders fail

- Orders placing **S before P** forget that the narrator stopped turning *before* day <InlineMath math="18" />.
- **P, R, S, Q, T** puts stopping turning before the day-<InlineMath math="14" /> candling.
- **R, P, Q, S, T** places Saturday's announcement before day <InlineMath math="18" />.

# Answer

The events happened in the order **R, P, S, Q, T**.
