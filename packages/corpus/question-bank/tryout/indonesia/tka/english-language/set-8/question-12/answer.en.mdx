A narrative is built from **orientation, complication, and resolution**. The problem appears when Rani and her grandmother reach the harbor and **the ferry is already full**, with her cousin's wedding the next morning. That's the complication.

- Carrying the basket and rice sack is part of the orientation.
- The grandmother dozing on the boat happens once the problem is solved.
- Reaching the island before midnight is the resolution.
- The lighter basket is Rani's reflection at the end.
