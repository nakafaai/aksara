# Given

In the **class egg incubator** recount, **<InlineMath math="15" /> eggs** were set on Monday and **<InlineMath math="9" /> hatched** (<InlineMath math="12" /> left on day <InlineMath math="7" />, <InlineMath math="10" /> left on day <InlineMath math="14" />).

Because the narrator numbered Monday as **day <InlineMath math="1" />** instead of **day <InlineMath math="0" />**, turning stopped a day early and the class expected hatching on Sunday instead of Monday. Bu Ratna said stopping turning a day early **does little harm** and pinned the record up **with the first page corrected**.

# Asked

Which of statements I through V belong in an accurate summary of the account.

# Solution

Keep the true facts and reject two traps: **five of the six unhatched eggs were removed before turning even stopped**, and on the Monday the first chick hatched it was **day <InlineMath math="21" /> by Bu Ratna's chart**, which was **day <InlineMath math="22" />** in the narrator's off-by-one numbering.

> Track which numbering system (*Bu Ratna's day <InlineMath math="0" />* versus *the narrator's day <InlineMath math="1" />*) each statement refers to.

## Checking statements I through V for the summary

The account records <Highlight>Bu Ratna said that stopping the turning a day early does little harm, and she pinned my record on the wall with the first page corrected</Highlight>. Checking **all five statements**:

- **I (belongs)**: Fifteen eggs were placed in the incubator and nine hatched.
- **II (belongs)**: Starting at day <InlineMath math="1" /> shifted every expected milestone one day early.
- **III (false)**: Three eggs failed by day <InlineMath math="7" /> and two more by day <InlineMath math="14" />, long before turning stopped, and Bu Ratna said stopping early did little harm.
- **IV (false)**: Monday was day <InlineMath math="21" /> on Bu Ratna's chart, making it **day <InlineMath math="22" />** in the narrator's numbering.
- **V (belongs)**: Bu Ratna corrected page one before pinning the record to the wall.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I and II only** omits Bu Ratna's correction in V.
- **I, II, and IV only** confuses Bu Ratna's day <InlineMath math="21" /> with the narrator's day <InlineMath math="22" />.
- Options with **III** blame stopping turning early for losses that already happened during candling.

# Answer

An accurate summary should contain **I, II, and V only**.
