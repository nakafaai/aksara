**Puskesmas Kenanga: general clinic**

The general clinic is open Monday to Saturday. Queue numbers are handed out at the gate from 7:00 to 11:00, and the doctors start at 7:30.

On weekdays two doctors work side by side; on Saturdays there is one. Each doctor sees one patient every <InlineMath math="12" /> minutes. On weekdays the two doctors call their patients together, so numbers go in two at a time: numbers <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="2" /> at 7:30, numbers <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> at 7:42, and so on. A patient whose turn has not begun by 12:00 is given a ticket for the first turn the next morning.

Children under <InlineMath math="5" /> and patients over <InlineMath math="60" /> do not take a queue number. They go to the priority counter, where a nurse sees them first and calls a doctor if needed.

Registration costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" />. Patients with a national health card pay nothing.
