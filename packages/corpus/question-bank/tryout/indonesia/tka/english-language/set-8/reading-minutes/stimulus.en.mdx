**Text 1: a proposal from the literacy team of SMP Harapan**

Every class should begin the day with <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes of silent reading. Last semester, two classes tried it. Together, the books they borrowed from the library rose from <InlineMath math="120" /> in the semester before to <InlineMath math="300" />, and their average score on the reading test rose by <InlineMath math="8" /> points. Nothing builds readers like daily practice, and <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes a day is a small price for it.

**Text 2: a reply from Bu Yuni, mathematics teacher**

I agree with the literacy team that our students should read more, and the borrowing figures are striking. But the two pilot classes were the ones whose teachers volunteered, and both teachers already ran book clubs. The library also received two hundred new books that semester, and the reading test changed its format, so last semester's scores cannot be compared with the year before. And the <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes would come out of the first lesson, which is mathematics on four days of the week. I propose that every class in Year 8 read for <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes at the start of the long break for one semester, and that we compare their borrowing with Year 7's.
