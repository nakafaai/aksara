# Given

At **Puskesmas Kenanga**, doctors start at **7:30** with **<InlineMath math="12" />-minute** slots, and any patient whose turn has **not begun by 12:00** receives a ticket for the next morning. Weekdays call **two numbers per slot** and Saturdays call **one number per slot**.

# Asked

Whether each of the five patients is **Seen that day** or **Given a ticket for the next morning**.

# Solution

First find the **last valid start time before 12:00**: from 7:30 to 11:54 spans <InlineMath math="264" /> minutes (<InlineMath math="22" /> steps after 7:30), giving **<InlineMath math="23" /> turns** total. That means up to **number <InlineMath math="46" />** on weekdays and **number <InlineMath math="23" />** on Saturdays are seen that day.

> Include the opening 7:30 turn when counting slots up to 11:54, since <InlineMath math="22" /> intervals create <InlineMath math="23" /> turns.

## Finding the cutoff numbers for weekdays and Saturdays

The rule states <Highlight>A patient whose turn has not begun by 12:00 is given a ticket for the first turn the next morning</Highlight>. Computing **the cutoff slot**:

<BlockMath math="7{:}30 + 22 \times 12\text{ min} = 11{:}54 \implies 23\text{ turns} \implies \text{weekday max } 46, \quad \text{Saturday max } 23" />

Slot <InlineMath math="24" /> would start at **12:06**, past 12:00. Checking **all five patients**:

| Patient | Day limit | Start time | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Number <InlineMath math="44" /> on Wednesday | Up to <InlineMath math="46" /> | 11:42 | **Seen that day** |
| Number <InlineMath math="24" /> on Saturday | Up to <InlineMath math="23" /> | 12:06 | **Given a ticket for the next morning** |
| Number <InlineMath math="47" /> on Monday | Up to <InlineMath math="46" /> | 12:06 | **Given a ticket for the next morning** |
| Number <InlineMath math="23" /> on Saturday | Up to <InlineMath math="23" /> | 11:54 | **Seen that day** |
| Number <InlineMath math="45" /> on Friday | Up to <InlineMath math="46" /> | 11:54 | **Seen that day** |

# Answer

Numbers **<InlineMath math="44" /> (Wednesday)**, **<InlineMath math="23" /> (Saturday)**, and **<InlineMath math="45" /> (Friday)** are **Seen that day**, while **<InlineMath math="24" /> (Saturday)** and **<InlineMath math="47" /> (Monday)** are **Given a ticket for the next morning**.
