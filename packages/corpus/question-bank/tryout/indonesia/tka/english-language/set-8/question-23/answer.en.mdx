Bu Yuni doubts the reading time because the pilot teachers ran book clubs and the library got new books. A fact weakens her doubt if it makes the reading time a more likely cause, or the clubs and books less likely ones.

- **I weakens it**. Classes without book clubs saw the same rise, so the clubs can't explain it.
- **II weakens it**. Books that arrived in the last week can't explain a rise across the semester.
- III doesn't. If book-club classes borrowed more without reading time, the clubs look like the cause, which strengthens her doubt.
- IV doesn't. It's about test scores, not borrowing, and it strengthens her doubt about the scores.

The options with III or IV count facts that support her doubt. The option with only I leaves out the late books.

So the facts that weaken her doubt are I and II only.
