Judge each statement by what Pak Udin does and says.

- **I is supported**. Once a month he lowers a rope "and writes "dry" in his book".
- II isn't supported. His book says the well was dry, and the neighbour says the water came from someone's home. The two fit together.
- **III is supported**. He "said nothing" and then gave everyone a cupful, without saying where he thinks the water came from.
- IV isn't supported. He starts "with the oldest", not with the narrator.
- **V is supported**. He looks at the bucket and says nothing, so he neither repeats nor rejects the neighbour's idea.

The options with II see a clash between two claims that can both be true. The option with IV moves the first cup to the narrator.

So the supported statements are I, III and V only.
