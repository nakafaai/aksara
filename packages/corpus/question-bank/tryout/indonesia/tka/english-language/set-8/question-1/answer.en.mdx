# Given

At **Puskesmas Kenanga**, doctors start at **7:30** and see one patient every **<InlineMath math="12" /> minutes**. On **weekdays**, two doctors call patients **in pairs** (<InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="2" /> at 7:30, <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> at 7:42), whereas on **Saturdays**, one doctor calls patients **one at a time**.

Pak Ali holds number **<InlineMath math="31" />** on Tuesday, and his wife holds number **<InlineMath math="16" />** on Saturday.

# Asked

How the start time of his wife's Saturday turn compares with the start time of Pak Ali's Tuesday turn.

# Solution

Think of Tuesday as a **two-lane highway** and Saturday as a **one-lane road**. Even though Pak Ali's number (<InlineMath math="31" />) looks almost double hers (<InlineMath math="16" />), two lanes move twice as fast, so **both wait behind exactly <InlineMath math="15" /> turns**.

> Always count how many complete turns come *before* a patient's own slot, using pairs on weekdays and single slots on Saturdays.

## Computing both start times

The clinic notes <Highlight>On weekdays two doctors work side by side</Highlight> and <Highlight>on Saturdays there is one. Each doctor sees one patient every <InlineMath math="12" /> minutes</Highlight>.

On Tuesday, numbers <InlineMath math="31" /> and <InlineMath math="32" /> form the **<InlineMath math="16" />th pair**, so **<InlineMath math="15" /> pairs** precede Pak Ali. On Saturday, **<InlineMath math="15" /> single patients** precede number <InlineMath math="16" />. **Both wait durations** are identical:

<BlockMath math="\text{Wait} = 15 \times 12 = 180\text{ min} = 3\text{ h}" />

Adding <InlineMath math="3" /> hours to 07:30 places her consultation at **10:30**.

## Why the other four comparisons fail

- **<InlineMath math="96" /> minutes earlier** mistakenly treats Saturday as paired (<InlineMath math="84" /> min instead of <InlineMath math="180" /> min).
- **<InlineMath math="12" /> minutes later** counts her own slot (<InlineMath math="16" />) instead of the <InlineMath math="15" /> slots ahead of her.
- **<InlineMath math="180" /> minutes earlier** wrongly treats Tuesday as a single-doctor day.
- **<InlineMath math="6" /> minutes earlier** divides <InlineMath math="31" /> by <InlineMath math="2" /> without pairing odd and even numbers.

# Answer

Her turn **begins at the same time of the morning** as his (both start at **10:30**).
