# Given

# Asked

Whether each of the six actions **Follows the instructions**, **Breaks the instructions**, or is **Not covered**.

# Solution

Check boundary words tightly: **"at least as far"** includes standing **exactly <InlineMath math="5" /> m** from a **<InlineMath math="5" /> m** tree, while standing <InlineMath math="3" /> m from a <InlineMath math="4" /> m wall falls short. Silence on paint colour means **Not covered**, never a ban.

> Phrases like *at least as far* allow equality, whereas an unmentioned detail belongs in *Not covered*.
