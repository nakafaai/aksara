Simple Fried Rice for Two

This recipe works best with rice that was cooked the day before and kept in the fridge. Freshly cooked rice is soft and sticky, so it tends to turn mushy in the pan.

Ingredients:

- 2 plates of cold cooked rice
- 2 eggs
- 2 cloves of garlic and 3 shallots, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon of sweet soy sauce
- a pinch of salt
- 2 tablespoons of cooking oil
- sliced cucumber and fried shallots for serving

Steps:

1. Break up any lumps in the cold rice with your fingers.
2. Heat the oil in a wok over medium heat. Fry the garlic and shallots until they smell fragrant and turn golden, but not brown.
3. Push them to one side, crack the eggs into the empty space, and stir until the eggs are almost set.
4. Add the rice and turn up the heat. Stir and toss for about three minutes so that every grain is coated in oil.
5. Add the sweet soy sauce and salt, and stir until the color is even.
6. Taste the rice, and add a little more salt if needed.
7. Serve hot with cucumber slices and a sprinkle of fried shallots.

Be careful when you add the rice. If the wok is very hot, the oil may spit.
