# Given

In **The Dry Well**, Pak Udin showed that an **old drain pipe carries sound from the staff room**, the well-house door is **padlocked with its only key on Pak Udin's belt**, and the well has been **dry for three years** (including the day before).

When the **water tanker broke down**, families relied only on what was left in their drums. The text never says what the class-two boy did after being caught leaving a coin.

# Asked

Which predictions among I through V are reasonable based on the story.

# Solution

Reasonable predictions follow **solid physical setups and recurring patterns**, not wishes or unstated character changes. The drain pipe, the padlocked door, and the village's dependence on the tanker all hold firm, whereas expecting a three-year-dry well to suddenly hold water or guessing the boy's future habit lacks support.

> Ground every prediction in an explicit mechanism or vulnerability already shown in the story.

## Testing all five predictions against the story

The text states <Highlight>an old drain pipe carried the sound from the staff room</Highlight> and the tanker breakdown left families with <Highlight>only what was left in its drums</Highlight>. Checking **predictions I through V**:

- **I (reasonable)**: The drain pipe already links the staff room to the well-house acoustically.
- **II (reasonable)**: The bucket hangs inside a padlocked door whose single key stays on Pak Udin's belt.
- **III (unreasonable)**: Three straight years of dryness (including the day before) give no reason to expect water next month.
- **IV (reasonable)**: Without the tanker, households rapidly depend on limited drum reserves.
- **V (unreasonable)**: The story never hints whether the boy stopped leaving coins after being caught.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I and II only** and **II and IV only** drop one directly grounded prediction.
- **I, II, III, and IV only** treats the mysterious bucket as proof the dry well refilled.
- **I, II, IV, and V only** guesses a behaviour change for the boy that the text never mentions.

# Answer

The reasonable predictions are **I, II, and IV only**.
