A reasonable prediction follows from a pattern or a fact the story sets up.

- **I is reasonable**. Pak Udin showed that "an old drain pipe carried the sound from the staff room".
- **II is reasonable**. The door is padlocked, and "the only key hangs on his belt".
- III isn't reasonable. The well "has been dry for three years", and his book said "dry" the day before.
- **IV is reasonable**. The last breakdown left families with "only what was left in its drums".
- V isn't reasonable. The story never says what the boy does next.

The options with III read the full bucket as a sign that the well has water again. The options with V predict a change the story gives no reason for.

So the reasonable predictions are I, II and IV only.
