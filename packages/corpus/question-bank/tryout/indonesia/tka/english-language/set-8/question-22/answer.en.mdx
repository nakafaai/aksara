# Given

In Text 2 of **SMP Harapan**, Bu Yuni calls the borrowing figures **striking** while noting **volunteer teachers with book clubs**, **two hundred new books**, and a **changed reading test format**.

She notes **<InlineMath math="15" /> minutes would come out of mathematics four days a week**, and proposes **Year 8 read at the start of the long break** compared against **Year 7**.

# Asked

Which argumentative moves among I through V Text 2 makes.

# Solution

Read Bu Yuni's exact boundary on each point: she **keeps the <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes by moving it to break** (**IV**) and uses **Year 7 as the non-reading comparison group** (ruling out V). She also notes lost maths time without ever claiming maths test scores would fall (ruling out III).

> Never stretch a concern about *losing lesson time* into an unstated claim about *lower exam results*.

## Checking all five moves in Bu Yuni reply

Text 2 writes <Highlight>the borrowing figures are striking</Highlight>, <Highlight>the reading test changed its format, so last semester's scores cannot be compared</Highlight>, and proposes <Highlight>every class in Year 8 read for <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes at the start of the long break</Highlight>. Testing **moves I through V**:

- **I (made)**: Accepts the striking borrowing numbers while questioning what caused them.
- **II (made)**: Challenges the <InlineMath math="8" />-point test gain because the test format changed.
- **III (not made)**: Notes <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes come out of maths class, without claiming maths results would drop.
- **IV (made)**: Shifts reading time to the start of the long break rather than cancelling reading.
- **V (not made)**: Only Year 8 reads during break so Year 7 serves as the comparison group.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I and II only** omits her alternative timing proposal in IV.
- **I, II, III, and IV only** inflates lost class minutes into falling maths scores.
- Options with **V** give reading time to Year 7 instead of keeping it as the control group.

# Answer

Text 2 makes argumentative moves **I, II, and IV only**.
