# Given

In the **SMP Harapan** pair of texts, the literacy team proposes daily silent reading and reports borrowing jumping from **<InlineMath math="120" /> to <InlineMath math="300" />** books plus an <InlineMath math="8" />-point test gain across **two pilot classes**.

Bu Yuni replies that **students should read more** and calls the **borrowing figures striking**, rejects comparing test scores, and proposes **long break** instead of the first lesson.

# Asked

All points among the five statements that **both writers** accept.

# Solution

Apply the **two-signature rule**: select only points both the literacy team and Bu Yuni agree on. Notice the clear split: Bu Yuni accepts **more reading** and the **<InlineMath math="180" />-book jump** (<InlineMath math="300 - 120" />), while rejecting the test scores and rejecting taking first-lesson time.

> Separate numbers both sides treat as real from claims one writer disputes or neither writer states.

## Checking all five statements for shared agreement

Bu Yuni begins with <Highlight>I agree with the literacy team that our students should read more, and the borrowing figures are striking</Highlight>. The increase from <InlineMath math="120" /> to <InlineMath math="300" /> is **<InlineMath math="180" /> more books**:

<BlockMath math="300 - 120 = 180\text{ more books}" />

- **Students should read more**: **Both accept**, proposed in Text 1 and agreed to in Text 2.
- **Reading time raised test scores by <InlineMath math="8" /> points**: **Literacy team only**, rejected by Bu Yuni due to format change.
- **The two pilot classes borrowed <InlineMath math="180" /> more books**: **Both accept**, reported in Text 1 and called striking by Bu Yuni.
- **The <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes should come from the first lesson**: **Neither accepts** as stated (team only says "begin the day", Bu Yuni opposes cutting first lesson).
- **Reading time was tried in every class**: **Neither accepts**, as both texts state only **two classes** tried it.

# Answer

The points accepted by both writers are:
- **Students should read more.**
- **The two pilot classes together borrowed <InlineMath math="180" /> more books than in the semester before.**
