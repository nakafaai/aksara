Judge each event on its own, without the story around it.

- **I could happen**. Pipes carry sound from room to room.
- **II could happen**. Deep stone stays cool when the air above is hot.
- III couldn't happen. With the padlock closed, the only key never leaving its owner and nobody opening the door, nothing can bring the bucket out.
- **IV could happen**. A caretaker can make a mistake or write something untrue.
- **V could happen**. Someone can pour water from a drum into a bucket.

The options without IV judge the caretaker by the story, where his book is trusted, instead of by what is possible. The option with III accepts the one event that needs magic.

So the events that could happen are I, II, IV and V only.
