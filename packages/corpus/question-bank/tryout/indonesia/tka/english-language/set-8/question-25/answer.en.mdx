# Given

In the **SMP Harapan** exchange, the literacy team proposes starting each day with <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes of silent reading and cites borrowing rising from **<InlineMath math="120" /> to <InlineMath math="300" />** in two pilot classes.

Bu Yuni agrees **students should read more**, doubts the pilot proves causation (book clubs, new books, changed test), and proposes **Year 8 read at the start of the long break** compared with **Year 7**.

# Asked

Which of statements I through V belong in an accurate summary of the two texts.

# Solution

Keep each side's **exact claims and attribution** crisp. Bu Yuni never links the new library books to **test scores** (**III**), and it was **Bu Yuni**, not the literacy team, who pointed out the time would come out of mathematics (**V**).

> An accurate two-text summary preserves who introduced each consequence and never mixes up two separate critiques.

## Checking statements I through V against both texts

Text 1 reports borrowing rose <Highlight>from <InlineMath math="120" /> in the semester before to <InlineMath math="300" /></Highlight> and Text 2 proposes <Highlight>every class in Year 8 read for <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes at the start of the long break for one semester, and that we compare their borrowing with Year 7's</Highlight>. Checking **all five statements**:

- **I (belongs)**: Accurately summarizes the literacy team's <InlineMath math="120" />-to-<InlineMath math="300" /> borrowing report.
- **II (belongs)**: Accurately captures Bu Yuni's agreement on reading more alongside her pilot doubts.
- **III (false)**: Bu Yuni mentions new books regarding borrowing and changed format regarding test scores, never linking new books to test scores.
- **IV (belongs)**: Matches Bu Yuni's Year 8 long-break trial compared with Year 7.
- **V (false)**: The literacy team only proposed beginning the day, while **Bu Yuni** raised the impact on mathematics.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I and IV only** omits Bu Yuni's core critique in II.
- **I, II, III, and IV only** invents a link between new books and test scores in III.
- Options with **V** attribute Bu Yuni's maths observation to the literacy team.

# Answer

An accurate summary of the two texts should contain **I, II, and IV only**.
