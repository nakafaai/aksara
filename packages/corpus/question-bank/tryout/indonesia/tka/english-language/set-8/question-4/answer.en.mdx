# Given

At **Puskesmas Kenanga**, queue numbers open at **7:00**, doctors start at **7:30** (<InlineMath math="12" /> minutes per patient, no turn beginning after 12:00), two weekday doctors **call patients together in pairs**, and at the priority counter a nurse sees patients first and **calls a doctor if needed**.

# Asked

Which cause-and-effect statements among I through V are supported by the text.

# Solution

Test both parts of every *because* claim: the result must be true AND the text must support the link. Watch out for **overstating "if needed" as "every"** (IV) and turning two neighboring opening times into a causal claim (V).

> Two facts placed side by side do not form a cause-and-effect link unless the text explicitly connects them.

## Checking all five cause and effect statements

The clinic states <Highlight>On weekdays the two doctors call their patients together, so numbers go in two at a time</Highlight> and the nurse <Highlight>calls a doctor if needed</Highlight>. Testing **statements I through V**:

- **I (supported)**: Two doctors working together directly causes pairs to enter two at a time.
- **II (supported)**: <InlineMath math="12" />-minute turns from 7:30 up to 11:54 give <InlineMath math="23" /> weekday pairs, ending at **number <InlineMath math="46" />**.
- **III (supported)**: Consecutive pairs (<InlineMath math="1" />-<InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="3" />-<InlineMath math="4" />, ..., <InlineMath math="29" />-<InlineMath math="30" />) mean <InlineMath math="29" /> and <InlineMath math="30" /> enter together.
- **IV (false)**: Priority patients see a doctor only **if needed**, not every time.
- **V (false)**: The text lists 7:00 and 7:30 without claiming one caused the other.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I and II only** and **II and III only** each drop a directly supported link.
- **I, II, III, and IV only** changes *if needed* into *every patient*.
- **I, II, and V only** invents a causal link between 7:00 and 7:30.

# Answer

The supported cause-and-effect statements are **I, II, and III only**.
