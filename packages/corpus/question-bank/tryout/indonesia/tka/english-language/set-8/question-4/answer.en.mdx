A cause-and-effect statement is supported only if the text gives both the effect and the link.

- **I is supported**. Because two doctors "call their patients together", numbers go in two at a time.

- IV isn't supported. The nurse "calls a doctor if needed", so not every priority patient sees one.
- V isn't supported. The text gives both times but never says one causes the other.
