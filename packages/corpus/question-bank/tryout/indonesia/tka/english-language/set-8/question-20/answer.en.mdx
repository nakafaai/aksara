An extra step has to **fit the order** and **not contradict** the recipe. In step 3 the eggs are stirred *until almost set*, and in step 4 the rice goes in. Scrambling the eggs together with the garlic and shallots pushed to the side makes sense in between, since they're ready to mix with the rice.

- The recipe wants rice cooked *the day before*, not fresh rice.
- The eggs stay in the wok and mix with the rice, not served separately.
- The cucumber is a side for serving, not cooked in the wok.
- Rinsing the wok mid-cooking would wash away the garlic and eggs.
