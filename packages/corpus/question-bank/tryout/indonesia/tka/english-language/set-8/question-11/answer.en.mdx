Each statement joins details from different paragraphs.

- **I can be concluded**. "The only key hangs on his belt", and that morning Pak Udin "said the key had not left his belt, not even at night".
- **II can be concluded**. "His book, written the day before, said "dry"."
- III can't be concluded. A coin appeared every Monday, and one boy was caught leaving one because his sister had dared him. Nothing ties him to the bucket.
- **IV can be concluded**. The bucket turned up "on the sixth morning" of the six days without the tanker.

The options with III carry the coin dare over to a different event. The options without I or II leave out the details that make the bucket hard to explain.

So the reader can conclude I, II and IV only.
