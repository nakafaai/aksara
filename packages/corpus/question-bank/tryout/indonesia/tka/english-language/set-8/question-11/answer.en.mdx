# Given

In **The Dry Well**, the **only key** to the padlocked well-house hangs on **Pak Udin's belt**, and in September the village's **water tanker broke down for six days**.

On the **sixth morning**, the well-house bucket stood brimming with cold water outside the closed padlock, while Pak Udin said **the key had not left his belt, not even at night**, and **his book written the day before said "dry"**.

# Asked

Which of statements I through IV the reader can conclude by combining details across the story.

# Solution

Link the details across paragraphs one and three cleanly: **only key on belt all night** (**I**), **book wrote "dry" the day before** (**II**), and **sixth morning of the six-day tanker breakdown** (**IV**). The class-two boy was only caught leaving **Monday coins** on a dare, with zero tie to the bucket (**III**).

> Cross-paragraph synthesis joins factual clues from the setup and climax without blaming an earlier prankster for a separate mystery.

## Combining details across paragraphs for I through IV

Paragraph three records <Highlight>Pak Udin said the key had not left his belt, not even at night. His book, written the day before, said "dry"</Highlight> on the **sixth morning** without tanker water. Checking **all four conclusions**:

- **I (concluded)**: Combining "only key hangs on his belt" with "had not left his belt, not even at night" confirms the key stayed with him.
- **II (concluded)**: His logbook recorded "dry" the very day before the full bucket appeared.
- **III (unsupported)**: The class-two boy left Monday coins on his sister's dare, never a water bucket.
- **IV (concluded)**: The bucket appeared on the sixth morning of the six-day tanker breakdown.

## Why the other four options fail

- Options including **III** stretch the Monday coin dare onto the water bucket without evidence.
- **I and II only** and **II and IV only** leave out one confirmed element of the sixth morning.

# Answer

By combining details from different parts of the story, the reader can conclude **I, II, and IV only**.
