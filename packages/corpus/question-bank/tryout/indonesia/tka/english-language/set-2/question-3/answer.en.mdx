# Given

Under the hypothetical scenario, the squall stops counting for only **<InlineMath math="10" /> minutes** while every table figure stays identical. The three complete hours already total **<InlineMath math="778" /> birds**, and the third hour holds <InlineMath math="690" /> southbound and <InlineMath math="25" /> northbound birds.

# Asked

What total the club would have sent to the regional survey under its rules.

# Solution

Picture the third hour as a jar of tokens that had been set aside only because the break ran too long. Shortening the stop to <InlineMath math="10" /> minutes **brings that jar back into the total**, while still requiring the northbound subtraction.

> Two rules act together: a <InlineMath math="10" />-minute stop stays inside the <Highlight>more than <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes</Highlight> cutoff in paragraph two, and paragraph five still subtracts northbound birds.

## Computing the updated total

Add the third hour's net count (<InlineMath math="690 - 25 = 665" />) to the existing three-hour total (<InlineMath math="778" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Third-hour net} &= 690 - 25 = 665 \\
\text{New total} &= 778 + 665 = 1{,}443
\end{aligned}" />

Cross-checking all four rows directly confirms the exact same result:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Southbound total} &= 146 + 512 + 690 + 233 = 1{,}581 \\
\text{Northbound total} &= 12 + 40 + 25 + 61 = 138 \\
\text{Net count} &= 1{,}581 - 138 = 1{,}443
\end{aligned}" />

The submitted figure is therefore **<InlineMath math="1{,}443" /> birds**.

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="1{,}468" /> birds** adds all <InlineMath math="690" /> southbound birds without subtracting the third hour's <InlineMath math="25" /> northbound birds.
- **<InlineMath math="1{,}581" /> birds** totals all southbound birds and skips northbound subtraction completely.
- **<InlineMath math="1{,}158" /> birds** adds the <InlineMath math="380" /> sandpipers onto <InlineMath math="778" /> even though they were already counted in hour two.
- **<InlineMath math="665" /> birds** reports only the third hour's net count without the other three hours.

# Answer

Following the club's rules with a <InlineMath math="10" />-minute stop, the club would have sent **<InlineMath math="1{,}443" /> birds** to the survey.
