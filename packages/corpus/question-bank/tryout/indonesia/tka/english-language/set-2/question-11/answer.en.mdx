# Solution

Think of grading a <InlineMath math="50" />-question quiz. Add packet B's seven-day total, **include the three late sprouts**, convert the fraction out of <InlineMath math="50" /> into a percentage, and read the matching band from paragraph five.

> Paragraph five sets the middle band as <Highlight>From <InlineMath math="50\%" /> up to <InlineMath math="80\%" />, sow two seeds in every hole instead of one</Highlight>, showing how three late seeds tip packet B across the <InlineMath math="50\%" /> cutoff.

## Computing the adjusted germination rate

Sum packet B's seven-day counts, add the three day-<InlineMath math="8" /> sprouts, and divide by the <InlineMath math="50" /> tested seeds:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Seven days} &= 1 + 5 + 8 + 6 + 0 + 4 = 24 \\
\text{With day } 8 &= 24 + 3 = 27 \\
\text{Rate} &= \frac{27}{50} \times 100\% = 54\%
\end{aligned}" />

Because **<InlineMath math="54\%" />** sits between <InlineMath math="50\%" /> and <InlineMath math="80\%" />, the rule tells the club to **sow two seeds in every hole**.
