# Given

Four numbered statements test **explicit character, place, and day details** about the four people who held the yellow umbrella (Bu Sari, Raka, Pak Darmo, and Wulan).

# Asked

Which numbered statements about the people who held the yellow umbrella are stated in the story.

# Solution

Picture two locations (the clinic and the market) and two back-to-back nights (Monday and Tuesday). Two distractors swap **one location and one night**, so check every statement's exact who, where, and when against the paragraphs.

> Explicit-detail questions in multi-day stories test whether you verify all three parts together: who acted, where, and on which day.

## Checking each detail statement

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence and location in the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Stated | Paragraph two reports Raka was <Highlight>running to a mathematics exam with no coat</Highlight> when he found the umbrella. |
| II | Not stated | Swaps locations: Raka lent it to Pak Darmo <Highlight>outside the clinic</Highlight> on Wednesday (paragraph three), while Pak Darmo handed it to Wulan <Highlight>at the market</Highlight> on Thursday (paragraph four). |
| III | Stated | Paragraph four introduces Wulan as the girl <Highlight>whose own umbrella had blown inside out</Highlight>. |
| IV | Not stated | Swaps nights: paragraph one puts Bu Sari drying it by her stove on Monday night, whereas on Tuesday she left it at the shelter. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Any option including II or IV** moves a real event onto the wrong place (clinic instead of market) or the wrong night (Tuesday instead of Monday).
- **I only** omits statement III, which paragraph four states word for word.

# Answer

The statements stated in the story are **I and III only**.
