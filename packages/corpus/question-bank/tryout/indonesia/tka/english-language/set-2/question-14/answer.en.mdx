Classify each source by **the information it provides**.

| Source | Category | Evidence in the passage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The printed timetable | Planned | Gives the service that was scheduled. |
| The official service page | Live | Reports the current road closure. |
| The clinic receptionist | Direct | Confirms the specific situation of Niko's mother. |

A source's format does not determine the category. The timetable cannot locate the bus, and the service page cannot confirm his mother's situation at the clinic.
