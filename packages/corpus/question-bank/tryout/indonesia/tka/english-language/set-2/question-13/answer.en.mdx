# Given

The germination procedure (paragraphs two, three, and five) sequences **dampening the paper towels**, **spreading and checking seeds daily**, **stopping on the last test day**, **calculating the rate**, and **choosing how to sow**.

# Asked

Which numbered statements about the order of steps in the germination test are correct.

# Solution

Think of following a recipe in strict order: you cannot **grade the finished cake before baking ends**, and you cannot compute the germination rate before the final test day closes.

> Sequence markers like "dampen them, and spread" or "Then work out" lock down exactly which step must come first.

## Checking the step order

| Statement | Verdict | Direct sequence evidence from the text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Correct | Paragraph two states in exact order <Highlight>Lay two sheets of paper towel on a tray, dampen them, and spread the seeds</Highlight>. |
| II | Incorrect | Paragraph three says <Highlight>Stop counting on the last day of the test length</Highlight> and only <Highlight>Then work out the germination rate</Highlight>, placing the calculation after counting ends. |
| III | Correct | Paragraph two instructs <Highlight>Remove each germinated seed as you count it</Highlight>. |
| IV | Correct | Paragraph five's sowing bands (<InlineMath math="80\%" /> and <InlineMath math="50\%" />) can only be applied once the final germination rate is known. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Any option including II** calculates the germination rate before the test period ends.
- **Options dropping IV or I** overlook either that sowing choices depend on the finished rate or that towels are dampened before spreading seeds.

# Answer

The correct statements about the order of steps are **I, III, and IV only**.
