Bu Sari's **words and actions don't match at first, and then they do**. Each statement picks out one part of that.

- I is supported. She tells *her first customer* that she never keeps what isn't hers, *and she said it again at noon*.
- II is supported. She *held the umbrella over her pots until dusk, and that night she dried it beside her stove*. For a day and a night, she keeps it.
- III is not supported. She leaves it at the shelter, and Raka finds it there *already open*, with *nobody* near it. They never meet.
- IV is supported. On Tuesday she leaves it *where its owner might look*, which is what her words promised.

So the supported statements are I, II and IV. Leaving out II hides the gap between her words and actions, leaving out IV hides how she closes it, and adding III invents a meeting that never happens.
