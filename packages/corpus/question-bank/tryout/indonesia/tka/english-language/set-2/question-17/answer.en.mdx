# Given

Throughout the story, the yellow umbrella tagged <Highlight>For whoever needs it most</Highlight> passes from one rain-caught person to the next with no owner ever appearing, and on Friday Wulan hangs it back at Jalan Melati with a new line reading <Highlight>Returned dry. Pass it on</Highlight>.

# Asked

Based on everything the story shows, what is most likely to happen to the yellow umbrella next.

# Solution

Imagine watching a relay baton placed back on its hook with a fresh note saying "Pass it on." The strongest prediction **continues the story's established pattern** without contradicting any character's actions.

> A sound prediction extends both the recurring pattern across the whole story and the final cue left in the closing paragraph.

## Evidence pointing to the next step

- The original tag in paragraph one reads <Highlight>For whoever needs it most</Highlight>, and each holder passes it on: Bu Sari to the shelter, Raka to Pak Darmo, and Pak Darmo to Wulan.
- No owner ever shows up, even after Bu Sari leaves it where its owner might look.
- Paragraph five ends with Wulan hanging it on the Jalan Melati hook and adding <Highlight>Returned dry. Pass it on</Highlight>, setting up the next rain-caught stranger to take and share it.

## Why the other four predictions fail

- **Bu Sari taking it back to her cart** contradicts her own principle in paragraph one: <Highlight>I never keep what isn't mine</Highlight>.
- **Raka returning to pay for the bent rib** breaks down because the rib is already <Highlight>straight again</Highlight> and he knows no owner.
- **Staying on the hook until an owner collects it** ignores that no owner ever appears and Wulan's note explicitly invites passing it on.
- **Wulan taking it home again** reverses her deliberate choice to return it and write <Highlight>Pass it on</Highlight>.

# Answer

The most likely next event is that **the next person caught at the shelter without cover in the rain will take the umbrella and later pass it on**.
