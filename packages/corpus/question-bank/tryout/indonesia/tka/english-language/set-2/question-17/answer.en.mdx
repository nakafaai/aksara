A good prediction **fits every pattern and condition** the story sets. Gather them first.

- The tag says *For whoever needs it most*, and every holder passed it to someone caught in the rain: Bu Sari to the shelter, Raka to Pak Darmo, Pak Darmo to Wulan.
- No owner ever appears, though Bu Sari left it *where its owner might look*.
- Wulan hung it back on the shelter hook and added *Returned dry. Pass it on.*

So the umbrella is set up to go to the next person who needs it, who will pass it on in turn.

Why the other predictions break a condition:

- Bu Sari says *I never keep what isn't mine*, and she gave it up the next day.
- The bent rib is *straight again*, so there's nothing to pay for, and Raka never learns who the owner is.
- Nothing in the story suggests an owner will come. The umbrella only moves from one person in need to the next.
- Wulan chose to return it and wrote *Pass it on*. Taking it home again goes against her own action.

So the most likely next event is that the next person caught at the shelter in the rain will take the umbrella and later pass it on.
