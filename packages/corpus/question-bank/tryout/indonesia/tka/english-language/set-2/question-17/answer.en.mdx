The procedure moves from **identifying needs** to **testing the completed plan**. Follow the sequence in the passage.

| Stage | Action | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Identify household members and their needs. | Establish what the plan must accommodate. |
| 2 | Choose two meeting places and an out-of-area contact. | Agree where to gather and how to coordinate. |
| 3 | Record essential numbers, medication names, and practical instructions. | Make needed information available on paper. |
| 4 | Practise and revise confusing parts. | Check whether people can follow the plan. |

A powered device needs a provider-approved backup procedure. Inventing instructions or relying on one phone contradicts the passage.
