# Given

The bird-count account sets out **four timing and sequence clues**: start time relative to high water (paragraph one), the end-of-hour swap routine (paragraph two), the sandpiper flock at <InlineMath math="8\text{:}25" /> (paragraph three), and the squall from <InlineMath math="9\text{:}20" /> to <InlineMath math="9\text{:}45" /> (paragraph four).

# Asked

Which numbered statements about the timing of the bird count are correct.

# Solution

Picture checking a shift log with a watch in one hand and the rule sheet in the other. Verify every statement straight against **its exact clock time or order phrase** in the text.

> Clock-time and sequence questions settle cleanly once you match start times, pause durations, and order markers like "only then".

## Checking each timing statement

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence and location in the text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Correct | Paragraph one states counting waited until the tide covered the stone marker, <Highlight>about <InlineMath math="2" /> hours before high water</Highlight>. |
| II | Incorrect | Paragraph two orders it as reading figures aloud, Nadia copying them, <Highlight>and only then did the partners swap jobs</Highlight>, so swapping came after reading aloud. |
| III | Correct | Paragraph four puts the squall from <InlineMath math="9\text{:}20" /> to <InlineMath math="9\text{:}45" />, a <InlineMath math="25" />-minute stop exceeding the <InlineMath math="15" />-minute limit in paragraph two. |
| IV | Incorrect | Paragraph three places the sandpipers at <InlineMath math="8\text{:}25" /> (inside the complete <InlineMath math="8\text{:}00" /> to <InlineMath math="9\text{:}00" /> hour), not in the incomplete <InlineMath math="9\text{:}00" /> to <InlineMath math="10\text{:}00" /> hour. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including II** flip the end-of-hour routine stated in paragraph two.
- **Options including IV** place the <InlineMath math="8\text{:}25" /> sandpiper flock in the wrong hour.
- **I only** overlooks statement III, directly proven by the <InlineMath math="25" />-minute squall stop.

# Answer

The correct timing statements are **I and III only**.
