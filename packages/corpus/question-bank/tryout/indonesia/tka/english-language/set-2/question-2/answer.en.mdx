# Given

The bird-count account is organised across **six paragraphs and a table**: starting time and reason (paragraph one), counting rules (paragraph two), the sandpiper hour (paragraph three), the squall hour (paragraph four), the subtraction explanation followed by the table (paragraph five), and the closing reflection (paragraph six).

# Asked

Which numbered statements accurately describe how the account is organised.

# Solution

Think of checking the table of contents of a field notebook. Match **each statement to its exact paragraph**, and double-check **the table figures** before trusting a character's hunch inside the story.

> A narrator's in-the-moment hunch can turn out wrong later in the text, so always verify claims about the biggest hour against the table.

## Checking each organisational statement

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence from text and table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Accurate | Paragraph one explains counting begins once the tide covers <Highlight>the stone marker</Highlight> because <Highlight>Rising water pushes the birds off the outer mudflats</Highlight> into a narrow stream. |
| II | Accurate | Paragraph two lays out the rules and closes with <Highlight>The last rule was the one I hardly noticed</Highlight>, the <InlineMath math="15" />-minute interruption rule. |
| III | Inaccurate | Paragraph three covers <InlineMath math="8\text{:}00" /> to <InlineMath math="9\text{:}00" /> when the writer thought nothing would beat it, yet the table shows <InlineMath math="512" /> southbound birds versus <InlineMath math="690" /> in the next hour. |
| IV | Inaccurate | The paragraph explaining northbound subtraction appears right above the table, not after it. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III** mistake the writer's early guess for fact despite the table showing <InlineMath math="690" /> beating <InlineMath math="512" />.
- **Options including IV** reverse the order of the subtraction paragraph and the table.
- **I only** omits statement II, which directly describes paragraph two.

# Answer

The accurate statements about the organisation of the account are **I and II only**.
