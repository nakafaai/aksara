# Given

Paragraph six details the **observatory dome**: its daily use as the reading room, the stone telescope pier still standing at its center, the first-Friday sky-watching sessions for visitors over age <InlineMath math="12" />, and the rule against bringing books up on those evenings.

# Asked

Which numbered statements about the dome are explicitly stated in the text.

# Solution

Picture reading the rule sign beside the dome stairs. Three statements match paragraph six word for word, while **one distractor erases an explicit age limit** by swapping it for the blanket word "anyone".

> Blanket words like "anyone" are classic reading traps whenever the passage states an explicit age limit or entry restriction.

## Checking each statement about the dome

| Statement | Verdict | Direct evidence from paragraph six |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Stated | Opens paragraph six verbatim: <Highlight>The dome itself is the reading room</Highlight>. |
| II | Not stated | Paragraph six restricts entry to the sky-watching club <Highlight>which welcomes visitors over the age of <InlineMath math="12" /></Highlight>, so "anyone" is too broad. |
| III | Stated | The closing sentence says <Highlight>On those evenings, no books are carried up, because the night air off the hill is damp</Highlight>. |
| IV | Stated | Sentence two states <Highlight>The telescope was taken away, but the stone pier that held it still stands in the middle</Highlight>. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Any option including II** drops the explicit over-<InlineMath math="12" /> age requirement.
- **Options omitting IV or I** ignore the stated facts that the stone pier remained and that the dome serves as the reading room.

# Answer

The statements about the dome stated in the text are **I, III, and IV only**.
