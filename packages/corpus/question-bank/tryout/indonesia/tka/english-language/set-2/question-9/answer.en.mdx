A comparison is right only if it **describes each text correctly**. Two of these statements get one text slightly wrong.

- I is correct. Text 1 cites the Lindung bee visits, and Text 2 says *I agree that more native planting would help insects*.
- II is not correct, and it's the trap. Text 1 doesn't present the saving as immediate. It says native plants need less water *once they are established*. Text 2 agrees and adds the first dry season.
- III is not correct. Text 2 doesn't reject the idea. It agrees with the aim and argues for a slower, measured start on *the edges of two parks*.
- IV is correct. Text 1 never mentions how a meadow is cut, and Text 2 says it needs *heavy equipment that the town does not own*.

So the correct comparisons are I and IV. Adding II misreads *once they are established* in Text 1, and adding III mistakes a qualified answer for a rejection.
