Each statement joins **details from at least two days**, so track the umbrella day by day.

- Monday: Bu Sari, who *dried it beside her stove* that night.
- Tuesday: left at the shelter, then taken by Raka.
- Wednesday: lent to Pak Darmo, who *opened the umbrella that evening*.
- Thursday: given to Wulan at the market.
- Friday: hung on the hook at the shelter on Jalan Melati.

Now each statement:

- A different person each night from Monday to Thursday: correct. Bu Sari, Raka, Pak Darmo and Wulan, one night each.
- The rib bent on the day Pak Darmo borrowed it: not correct. It bent on Tuesday, on Raka's way to the exam. Pak Darmo borrowed it on Wednesday.
- Raka meaning to give it for good: not correct. He said *Borrow it* and *Just until he comes*.
- Left at the shelter on the heaviest day: not correct. Bu Sari left it on Tuesday, and the heaviest rain was on Thursday.
- Back where Bu Sari left it: correct. Bu Sari left it *at the bus shelter on Jalan Melati*, and Wulan *carried the umbrella to the shelter on Jalan Melati*.

So the correct statements are that the umbrella spent each night from Monday to Thursday with a different person, and that it came back to the shelter where Bu Sari had left it.
