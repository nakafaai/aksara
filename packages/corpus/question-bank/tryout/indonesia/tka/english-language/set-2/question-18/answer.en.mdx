# Given

The yellow umbrella travels across **five consecutive days** from Monday to Friday: Monday night with Bu Sari, Tuesday night with Raka, Wednesday night with Pak Darmo, Thursday night with Wulan, and Friday back at the Jalan Melati bus shelter.

# Asked

Which statements accurately combine information from different parts of the story.

# Solution

Picture sketching a five-box calendar from Monday to Friday to track where the umbrella spends each day and night. Once the **day-by-day route** is laid out, true cross-paragraph links stand out immediately while swapped days collapse.

> Tracking a moving object day by day stops you from accidentally merging Tuesday's events with Wednesday's or Thursday's.

## Statements combining parts accurately

- **From Monday night to Thursday night it spent each night with a different person** is correct. Paragraphs one through four track four consecutive nights: Bu Sari (Monday), Raka (Tuesday), Pak Darmo (Wednesday), and Wulan (Thursday).
- **By Friday it had returned to the place where Bu Sari left it** is correct. Paragraph two states Bu Sari left it <Highlight>at the bus shelter on Jalan Melati</Highlight>, and paragraph five notes Wulan carried it back <Highlight>to the shelter on Jalan Melati</Highlight>.

## Why the other three options fail

- **The rib bent on the same day Pak Darmo borrowed it** mixes days because the rib bent on Tuesday while Pak Darmo borrowed it on Wednesday.
- **Raka meant to give it to Pak Darmo for good** contradicts paragraph three, where Raka says <Highlight>Borrow it</Highlight> and <Highlight>Just until he comes</Highlight>.
- **Bu Sari left it at the shelter on the day of heaviest rain** swaps days because Bu Sari left it on Tuesday whereas the heaviest rain fell on Thursday (paragraph four).

# Answer

The two correct statements are that **from Monday night to Thursday night the umbrella spent each night with a different person, and that by Friday it had come back to the place where Bu Sari had left it**.
