A fact can be **checked by measuring or observing**. An opinion is a judgement, and a number inside a sentence doesn't change which one it is.

- The Lindung trial: fact. Bee visits on two kinds of ground can be counted and compared, and the trial did exactly that.
- Weeds taking over: fact. It sounds like a complaint, but it describes something a gardener can watch happen on a new meadow.
- The <InlineMath math="10\%" /> cut as *the smartest decision the council has made in years*: opinion. The number is only part of the sentence. "Smartest decision in years" is a judgement no measurement can settle.
- The <InlineMath math="3{,}000" /> litres of fuel: fact. Fuel used for mowing can be measured.

So three sentences state facts, and only the one calling a <InlineMath math="10\%" /> cut the smartest decision in years is an opinion.
