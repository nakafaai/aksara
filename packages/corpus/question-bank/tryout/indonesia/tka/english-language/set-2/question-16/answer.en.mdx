# Given

The story of the yellow umbrella mixes ordinary bad-weather mishaps with several mysterious moments across Monday through Friday, including **a bent umbrella rib turning straight again with nobody touching it**.

# Asked

Which event in the story cannot be explained by anything that happens in the ordinary world.

# Solution

Play detective and try to find a normal, everyday explanation for each strange event. Several moments look eerie yet **still leave room for an ordinary human cause**, whereas one event **explicitly shuts the door on any ordinary explanation**.

> An odd event stays realistic as long as an unseen person or slip of memory could explain it, but a broken metal rib repairing itself untouched breaks ordinary physics.

## Why the straightened rib is impossible

- In paragraph two, a gust of wind on the bridge bends one rib backward.
- In paragraph three, when Pak Darmo opens the umbrella that evening, <Highlight>the bent rib was straight again. Nobody had touched it</Highlight>. A bent rib cannot fix itself, and the final sentence explicitly rules out anyone repairing it.

## Why the other four events have ordinary explanations

- **The umbrella hooked on Bu Sari's cart** could have been placed there unseen or missed when she set out, since the text only says she <Highlight>was sure</Highlight>.
- **Raka finding the umbrella already open at the shelter** could simply mean a passerby left it open at an unwatched public bus stop.
- **A gust on the bridge bending one rib** happens routinely in strong wind.
- **Wulan's umbrella blowing inside out** is completely ordinary during the heaviest rain of the week.

# Answer

The only event that cannot be explained in the ordinary world is **the bent rib being straight again when Pak Darmo opens the umbrella, even though nobody has touched it**.
