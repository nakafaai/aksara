Several events are **strange but still possible**. Only one has no ordinary explanation at all, so try to explain each one.

- The rib straightening itself: no ordinary explanation. A bent rib stays bent unless someone fixes it, and the story closes that door: *Nobody had touched it.*
- The umbrella on Bu Sari's cart: odd, but explainable. Someone could have hooked it there after she set out, or she could simply be wrong about it. The story only says she *was sure*.
- The umbrella already open at the shelter: also explainable. Someone could have left it open there, and a shelter on a public street isn't watched all the time.
- The gust bending a rib: ordinary. Wind does this to umbrellas.
- Wulan's umbrella blowing inside out: ordinary, especially in *the heaviest* rain of the week.

So the only event that ordinary life can't explain is the bent rib becoming straight again with nobody touching it.
