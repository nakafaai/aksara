The first point is for gathering immediately after leaving the home. In this situation, responders instruct the household to leave the neighborhood by a designated route. The second point is outside that neighborhood and fits this wider evacuation. The planned fallback is the **second meeting place outside the neighborhood**. Its location must be agreed in advance. Distance alone does not guarantee that an arbitrary place is safe or usable.
