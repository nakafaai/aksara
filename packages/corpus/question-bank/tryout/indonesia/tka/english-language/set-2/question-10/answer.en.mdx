A summary of Text 2 needs **its agreement, its objections and its proposal**, and nothing it doesn't say.

- I belongs. It opens *I agree that more native planting would help insects*.
- II belongs. These are its three objections: *weeding by hand* for two years, *watering through its first dry season*, and cutting *with heavy equipment that the town does not own*.
- III doesn't belong, because it's false. He never asks to drop the idea. He asks to start smaller.
- IV belongs. He would *start with the edges of two parks*, record bee visits and hours of work, and *let the council decide from our own numbers*.

So the summary contains I, II and IV. Leaving out I or II loses the agreement or the objections, and adding III turns a cautious reply into a rejection.
