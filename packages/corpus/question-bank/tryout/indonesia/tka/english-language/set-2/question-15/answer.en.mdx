The passage limits each source to information it could reasonably provide. The timetable gives the planned service, the official page reports the road closure, and the clinic confirms where Niko's mother is. None of them solves the situation alone. The sources are credible because each has a **realistic limit** and the characters combine their information. Niko's reading of the timetable uses a comparison. The printed numbers do not literally speak or predict events.
