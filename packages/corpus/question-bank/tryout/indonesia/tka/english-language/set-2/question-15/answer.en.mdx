# Given

The procedure sets **two simultaneous criteria** for counting a mung bean seed as germinated: a root **at least <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" /> long** (paragraph two) appearing **no later than day <InlineMath math="7" />** (paragraphs three and four).

# Asked

Whether each of the four listed seeds counts as germinated or does not count as germinated.

# Solution

Picture a theme-park gate with two rules at once: **meet the <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" /> height bar** and **arrive before closing time on day <InlineMath math="7" />**. Failing either rule disqualifies the seed.

> Paragraph two requires <Highlight>its root is at least <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" /> long</Highlight> and paragraph three adds <Highlight>Seeds that sprout after that day are left out</Highlight>.

## Classifying every seed

Check each seed against **both the root-length minimum and the seven-day deadline**:

| Seed case | Status | Two-rule check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packet B seed whose root first appeared on day <InlineMath math="8" /> | Does not count | Fails the deadline because day <InlineMath math="8" /> is past the seven-day test length. |
| Packet A seed with a <InlineMath math="3\text{ mm}" /> root on day <InlineMath math="4" /> | Counts | Passes both rules: <InlineMath math="3\text{ mm}" /> meets the <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" /> minimum and day <InlineMath math="4" /> is inside the seven-day window. |
| Seed with a <InlineMath math="1\text{ mm}" /> root on day <InlineMath math="7" /> | Does not count | Fails the length rule because <InlineMath math="1\text{ mm}" /> is shorter than <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" /> when counting stops on day <InlineMath math="7" />. |
| Seed that swelled and split its coat with no root by day <InlineMath math="7" /> | Does not count | Fails the root requirement because swelling without a root does not count as germination. |

# Answer

Only the **packet A seed with a <InlineMath math="3\text{ mm}" /> root on day <InlineMath math="4" />** counts as germinated, while the other three seeds **do not count as germinated**.
