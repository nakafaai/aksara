A seed counts only if it passes **two tests at once**: a root of at least <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" />, and by the last day of the test length, which is day 7 for these packets.

- The packet B seed rooted on day 8: does not count. Its root came after day 7, and *Seeds that sprout after that day are left out*. The club *left them out*.
- The packet A seed with a <InlineMath math="3\text{ mm}" /> root on day 4: counts. <InlineMath math="3\text{ mm}" /> is at least <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" />, and day 4 is inside the test.
- The seed with a <InlineMath math="1\text{ mm}" /> root on day 7: does not count. It's inside the test, but <InlineMath math="1\text{ mm}" /> is shorter than <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" />, and the counting stops on day 7.
- The seed that split its coat but has no root: does not count. Swelling isn't germination, because the rule is about the root.

So only the packet A seed with a <InlineMath math="3\text{ mm}" /> root on day 4 counts as germinated, and the other three do not.
