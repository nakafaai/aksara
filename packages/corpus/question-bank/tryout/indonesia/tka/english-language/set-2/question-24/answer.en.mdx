The categories distinguish **three different checking actions**.

| Action | Category | What the action establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writing the exact claim in one sentence | Identifying | What statement needs checking. |
| Finding the original report behind a screenshot | Tracing | Where that statement came from. |
| Checking another source with independent evidence | Comparing | Whether independent evidence supports the statement. |

In this example, stopping at the screenshot would leave the original report unexamined. Finding that report and comparing independent evidence are separate steps.
