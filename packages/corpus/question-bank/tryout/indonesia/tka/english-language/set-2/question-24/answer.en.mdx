A purpose has to **cover the whole text without going beyond it**. Test each statement against every paragraph.

- I describes the purpose. The text goes floor by floor, from the workshop to the dome, and shows what each part does now.
- II goes beyond the text. It describes one building and never says what should happen to other observatories.
- III describes the purpose. Old features keep shaping new uses: the wide doors now let in wheelchairs, the windowless vault protects the archive, and the dome still opens for the sky-watching club.
- IV is too narrow. The astronomers appear only as background, in a phrase about the first floor, and their history is never told.

So the writer's purpose is described by I and III. Any option with II overreaches, and any option with IV turns a background detail into the purpose.
