# Given

Positions of the two writers:
- **Student council (Text 1)** wants a <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" /> charge on sweetened drinks to fund water fountains, citing SMP Mutiara's trial.
- **Bu Rosa (Text 2)** accepts the sales numbers, points out other changes during the trial (free fountain and rainy months), and proposes installing two fountains for one term without changing prices and comparing sales with the same term last year.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral statements belong in an accurate summary of both texts.

# Solution

Keep each proposal **matched to the right speaker** and ensure Bu Rosa's methodological doubt is never overstated as proof that the fountain caused the drop.

> An accurate summary preserves both attribution and nuance: Bu Rosa questions the council's single cause without proving her own, and the no-charge fountain trial is her proposal alone.

## Checking each summary statement

| Statement | Belongs? | Evidence from both texts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | The council proposes adding <Highlight><InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" /> to the price of every sweetened drink</Highlight> and cites SMP Mutiara. |
| II | **Yes** | Bu Rosa accepts the sales numbers yet warns <Highlight>SMP Mutiara did more than add a charge</Highlight>. |
| III | No | She lists the fountain as one possible confounding factor, never proving it caused the sales drop. |
| IV | **Yes** | Matches her exact proposal to <Highlight>install two water fountains for one term without changing any price</Highlight> and compare against last year. |
| V | No | Installing fountains before any charge is Bu Rosa's proposal, not the student council's. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with III** exaggerate Bu Rosa's doubt into causal proof she never gives.
- **Options with V** swap Bu Rosa's proposal onto the student council.
- **Options without II** leave out Bu Rosa's central objection to the SMP Mutiara evidence.

# Answer

The statements that belong in an accurate summary are **I, II, and IV only**.
