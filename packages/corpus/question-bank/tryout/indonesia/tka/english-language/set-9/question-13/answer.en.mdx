# Given

The narrator's actions in paragraphs three and four:
- Wakes at two o'clock and immediately recognises Nenek's sewing song.
- Notes that the cabinet is locked, Ibu's bag hangs beside her bed, and Bayu is asleep across the room.
- Does not argue in the morning when Ibu calls it a dream and Bayu agrees.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral statements about the narrator are supported by the story.

# Solution

Distinguish sharply between **staying quiet** and **agreeing aloud**, and verify every claim against what the narrator actually observes, says, or withholds.

> Choosing not to argue is not the same as agreeing, especially after the narrator has checked the locked cabinet, Ibu's bag, and sleeping Bayu.

## Checking each statement about the narrator

| Statement | Supported? | Evidence from passage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | The narrator <Highlight>woke at two o'clock to a tune I knew at once</Highlight>. |
| II | No | Only Bayu agreed with Ibu, whereas the narrator simply <Highlight>didn't argue</Highlight>. |
| III | **Yes** | While the tune played, the narrator noted <Highlight>Ibu's bag hung on its hook</Highlight> and <Highlight>Bayu was asleep</Highlight>. |
| IV | No | The narrator never accuses Bayu of playing the box. |
| V | **Yes** | The narrator stays quiet about Ibu's dream explanation despite seeing the open lid, locked cabinet, and hearing the tune finish. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with II** turn the narrator's silence into spoken agreement.
- **Options with IV** invent an accusation against Bayu that is absent from the text.
- **Options without I** leave out the narrator's immediate recognition of Nenek's tune.

# Answer

The supported statements about the narrator are **I, III, and V only**.
