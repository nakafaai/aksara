# Given

Kilat's passage sets four operating rules:
- Night data works only from **0:00 to 6:00**, and daytime data covers every other hour.
- Every package ends at **23:59** on its last calendar day.
- Early renewal carries over leftover daytime data only, never night data.
- With two different active packages, the phone uses the one **ending sooner** first.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral cause-and-effect statements are supported by the passage.

# Solution

Test both parts of every statement: verify that **the stated rule** appears in the passage and that the claimed effect truly follows from it.

> A cause-and-effect statement fails whenever its conclusion contradicts a rule, even if its opening clause sounds familiar.

## Checking each cause and effect link

| Statement | Supported? | Evidence from passage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Night data ends at 6:00 and <Highlight>at any other time the phone uses daytime data</Highlight>, so 23:30 draws from daytime data. |
| II | **Yes** | A Harian ends that same night, far earlier than a Mingguan, and the phone <Highlight>uses the one that ends sooner first</Highlight>. |
| III | **Yes** | Early renewal closes the old package immediately and <Highlight>Leftover night data is never carried over</Highlight>. |
| IV | No | A Harian bought at 23:00 ends at 23:59 the same day, lasting <InlineMath math="59" /> minutes instead of <InlineMath math="24" /> hours. |
| V | No | Carry-over applies only to daytime data, so Bulanan night data stays at <InlineMath math="20" /> GB. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with IV** mistake a single calendar day for a full <InlineMath math="24" />-hour window.
- **Options with V** extend the daytime carry-over rule to night data.
- **Options missing I or III** omit a direct consequence clearly established by the passage.

# Answer

The supported cause-and-effect statements are **I, II, and III only**.
