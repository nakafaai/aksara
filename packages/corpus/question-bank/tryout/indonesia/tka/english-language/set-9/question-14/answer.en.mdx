A reasonable prediction follows from a pattern or a fact the story sets up.

- **I is reasonable**. Bayu showed that the evening tinkling was "the wind chimes on the neighbour's porch".
- **II is reasonable**. The cabinet's "only key is on Ibu's key ring".
- III isn't reasonable. Nenek smiles at the box. Nothing suggests she will get rid of it.
- **IV is reasonable**. Ibu said "the room cools at night and the glass sweats".
- V isn't reasonable. The story never suggests Bayu will do it again.

The options with III or V predict a change the story gives no reason for.

So the reasonable predictions are I, II and IV only.
