# Given

The blank `[GAP]` sits at the end of paragraph two, right after Bu Wening's weighing rule and right before the table of four Friday weights (<InlineMath math="1.4" />, <InlineMath math="2.1" />, <InlineMath math="1.9" />, and <InlineMath math="2.6" /> kg).

Later facts in the account:
- Lina weighed the small basket empty on the first Friday.
- Dani and the narrator weighed chilies in the bigger basket from the second Friday without ever weighing it empty.
- The notebook was summed at month's end, and Bu Wening checked only after packing.

# Asked

All sentences that can fill `[GAP]` without contradicting anything in the account (select every correct option).

# Solution

Check each candidate sentence against every detail in the table and later paragraphs, eliminating any sentence that clashes with who weighed the chilies or how the baskets were used.

> A valid gap sentence need not already appear verbatim, yet it must stay completely consistent with every number and event later in the account.

## Testing each candidate sentence

| Candidate sentence | Fits? | Reason and textual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lina followed it on the first Friday, and nobody looked at the book again until month's end | **Yes** | Matches Lina's accurate first Friday and the fact that the error surfaced only after summing the book at month's end. |
| We weighed the big basket empty on the first day we used it | No | Directly contradicts <Highlight>we never weighed it empty</Highlight>. |
| The canteen scale shows weights to one decimal place | **Yes** | Matches every weight in the table (<InlineMath math="1.4" />, <InlineMath math="2.1" />, <InlineMath math="1.9" />, <InlineMath math="2.6" /> kg) and the packed total (<InlineMath math="6.8" /> kg). |
| We weighed the chilies straight on the scale without any basket | No | Directly contradicts <Highlight>weighed them in a plastic basket</Highlight>. |
| Bu Wening weighed every basket herself | No | Contradicts the account that students (Lina, then Dani and the narrator) did the weighing. |

## Why the other sentences fail

- Weighing the big basket empty, omitting baskets altogether, or having Bu Wening do every weighing would each contradict the central mistake of the story.

# Answer

The sentences that fit `[GAP]` are **Lina followed it on the first Friday, and nobody looked at the book again until the end of the month** and **The canteen scale shows weights to one decimal place**.
