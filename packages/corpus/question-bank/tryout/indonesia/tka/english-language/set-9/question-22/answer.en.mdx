# Given

Text 2 is Bu Rosa's reply as canteen manager to the student council:
- She agrees students consume too much sugar and calls SMP Mutiara's drop from <InlineMath math="900" /> to <InlineMath math="540" /> drinks a week striking.
- She identifies two rival factors (a free cold-water fountain and rainy months), notes stallholders' rent and reliance on drink sales, and proposes installing two fountains first without changing prices.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral argumentative moves Text 2 makes.

# Solution

Trace Bu Rosa's reply move by move, separating her agreement on the problem and sales figures from her critique of causality and her single-variable counter-proposal.

> Bu Rosa accepts SMP Mutiara's sales numbers while questioning what caused them, then proposes changing just one factor at a time to test water fountains cleanly.

## Checking each move in Text Two

| Statement | Made by Text 2? | Evidence from Text 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | She opens with agreement: <Highlight>I agree with the student council that our students drink too much sugar</Highlight>. |
| II | **Yes** | She names two rival explanations: <Highlight>a free cold-water fountain</Highlight> and <Highlight>ran through the rainy months</Highlight>. |
| III | No | She repeats the <InlineMath math="900" />-to-<InlineMath math="540" /> figures and calls the drop <Highlight>striking</Highlight>, never disputing the numbers. |
| IV | **Yes** | She highlights costs to canteen staff: <Highlight>Our stallholders also rent their stalls and depend on drink sales.</Highlight> |
| V | **Yes** | She proposes two fountains <Highlight>without changing any price</Highlight>, isolating one change at a time. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with III** confuse questioning the cause of the drop with doubting the sales figures themselves.
- **Options missing I or V** omit either Bu Rosa's opening agreement or her controlled-trial proposal at the end.

# Answer

Text 2 makes moves **I, II, IV, and V only**.
