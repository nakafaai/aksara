# Given

Pak Rudi makes a **double batch** in a kitchen at **<InlineMath math="28" /> degrees**, following every step.

Single-batch recipe (<InlineMath math="12" /> rolls):
- <InlineMath math="500" /> g flour, <InlineMath math="7" /> g dried yeast, <InlineMath math="300" /> ml warm water.
- First rise at about <InlineMath math="28" /> degrees takes **<InlineMath math="60" /> minutes**.
- Scaling rule: multiply every amount and the number of pieces by <InlineMath math="2" />, except yeast, which scales by **<InlineMath math="1.5" />**.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral statements about Pak Rudi's baking are supported by the instructions.

# Solution

Scale flour, water, and piece count by <InlineMath math="2" /> and yeast by <InlineMath math="1.5" />, while remembering that rising time depends on **room temperature**, never batch size.

> Doubling the batch scales ingredients and roll count, yet the first rise stays sixty minutes in a twenty-eight-degree kitchen.

## Scaling ingredients and checking rising time

The closing note states <Highlight>multiply every amount and the number of pieces by the same number, except the yeast: a double batch needs only <InlineMath math="1.5" /> times the yeast</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="\text{Flour: } 500 \times 2 = 1{,}000\text{ g}, \quad \text{Yeast: } 7 \times 1.5 = 10.5\text{ g}, \quad \text{Water: } 300 \times 2 = 600\text{ ml}, \quad \text{Rolls: } 12 \times 2 = 24" />

| Statement | Supported? | Recipe evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> g flour and <InlineMath math="10.5" /> g yeast follow the exact <InlineMath math="1.5" />-times yeast exception. |
| II | **Yes** | Water doubles from <InlineMath math="300" /> ml to <InlineMath math="600" /> ml. |
| III | No | At <InlineMath math="28" /> degrees the first rise takes <InlineMath math="60" /> minutes, not <InlineMath math="120" /> (doubling time applies only below <InlineMath math="24" /> degrees). |
| IV | **Yes** | Piece count doubles from <InlineMath math="12" /> to <InlineMath math="24" /> rolls. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with III** wrongly double the rising time along with the batch size.
- **Options missing I, II, or IV** omit a scaled quantity directly confirmed by the recipe.

# Answer

The supported statements about Pak Rudi's baking are **I, II, and IV only**.
