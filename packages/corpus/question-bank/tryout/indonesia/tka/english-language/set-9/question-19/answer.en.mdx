Scale the recipe by <InlineMath math="2" />, except the yeast, which goes up by <InlineMath math="1.5" />.

- **I is supported**. <InlineMath math="500 \times 2 = 1{,}000" /> g of flour, and <InlineMath math="7 \times 1.5 = 10.5" /> g of yeast.
- **II is supported**. <InlineMath math="300 \times 2 = 600" /> ml of water.
- III isn't supported. The rise depends on the room, not the batch. At <InlineMath math="28" /> degrees it takes <InlineMath math="60" /> minutes.
- **IV is supported**. The number of pieces doubles too: <InlineMath math="12 \times 2 = 24" />.

The options with III double the rising time along with the batch. The options without I or II leave out a scaled amount.

So the supported statements are I, II and IV only.
