# Given

Kilat's table lists three packages:
- **Harian**: <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}6{,}000" />, <InlineMath math="1" /> GB daytime, no night data, <InlineMath math="1" /> day.
- **Mingguan**: <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}25{,}000" />, <InlineMath math="5" /> GB daytime, <InlineMath math="5" /> GB night, <InlineMath math="7" /> days.
- **Bulanan**: <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}80{,}000" />, <InlineMath math="20" /> GB daytime, <InlineMath math="20" /> GB night, <InlineMath math="30" /> days.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral comparison statements are supported by the table.

# Solution

Compute the **unit price per daytime gigabyte**, the daily night-data rate, and the bundle totals first, then test each statement against the exact numbers.

> Rate comparisons require dividing by gigabytes or days first instead of comparing raw package totals.

## Testing each comparison with the table

The cost per daytime gigabyte and night data per day for the packages are:

<BlockMath math="\text{Harian: }\frac{6{,}000}{1}=\text{Rp}6{,}000\text{/GB}, \quad \text{Mingguan: }\frac{25{,}000}{5}=\text{Rp}5{,}000\text{/GB}, \quad \text{Bulanan: }\frac{80{,}000}{20}=\text{Rp}4{,}000\text{/GB}" />

<BlockMath math="\text{Night/day Bulanan: }\frac{20}{30}\approx 0.67\text{ GB}, \qquad \text{Mingguan: }\frac{5}{7}\approx 0.71\text{ GB}" />

| Statement | Supported? | Evidence from table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | Bulanan (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000" />/GB) costs less per daytime GB than Mingguan (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" />/GB). |
| II | No | Harian costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}6{,}000" />/GB, the highest unit price of all three. |
| III | No | Bulanan gives <InlineMath math="0.67" /> GB of night data per day, less than Mingguan's <InlineMath math="0.71" /> GB. |
| IV | No | Seven Harian cost <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}42{,}000" /> (more), yet give <InlineMath math="7" /> GB daytime data (more than <InlineMath math="5" /> GB, not less). |
| V | **Yes** | Four Mingguan cost <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}100{,}000" />, more than one Bulanan at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}80{,}000" />. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with II** mistake the lowest sticker price for the lowest price per gigabyte.
- **Options with III** compare raw totals (<InlineMath math="20" /> GB vs <InlineMath math="5" /> GB) instead of daily rates.
- **Options with IV** match the cost half of the claim while missing that <InlineMath math="7" /> GB exceeds <InlineMath math="5" /> GB.

# Answer

The supported statements are **I and V only**.
