A package counts the day of purchase as the first day and ends at 23:59 on its last day, whatever time it was bought.

**Statement 1**: already ended. A Harian has one day, so it ends at 23:59 on the Monday. At 0:30 on Tuesday it is over.

**Statement 2**: still valid. Wednesday is the first day, so the seventh is the Tuesday, which runs to 23:59.

**Statement 3**: already ended. It ended at 23:59 on the Tuesday, so at 7:00 on Wednesday it is over, even though that is less than <InlineMath math="7 \times 24" /> hours after 8:00.

**Statement 4**: still valid. 1 March is the first day, so 30 March is the thirtieth, which runs to 23:59.

**Statement 5**: already ended. It ended at 23:59 on 30 March.

Counting <InlineMath math="7 \times 24" /> or <InlineMath math="30 \times 24" /> hours from the purchase time gets statements 3 and 5 wrong.
