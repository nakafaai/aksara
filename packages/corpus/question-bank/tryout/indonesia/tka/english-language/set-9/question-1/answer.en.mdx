# Given

Sari buys a **Mingguan** package at **21:00 on Tuesday**, then buys another **Mingguan** package at **23:00 on the following Monday** while she still has **<InlineMath math="2" /> GB of daytime data** and **<InlineMath math="3" /> GB of night data** left.

Kilat's rules:
- **Mingguan** gives **<InlineMath math="5" /> GB of daytime data** and **<InlineMath math="5" /> GB of night data** for **<InlineMath math="7" /> days**.
- The day of purchase counts as the first day, and the package ends at **23:59** on its last day.
- Renewing the same package early adds leftover **daytime data**, while leftover **night data** is never carried over.

# Asked

How much daytime data and night data Sari has once her new Mingguan package starts.

# Solution

First check whether the old package is **still running at 23:00 on Monday**, then apply the carry-over rule separately to daytime data and night data.

> Counting the purchase day as day one means a seven-day package bought on Tuesday ends at 23:59 on the following Monday, so buying at 23:00 counts as an early renewal.

## Checking expiry and data carry over

The passage states that a package <Highlight>ends at 23:59 on its last day, counting the day of purchase as the first day</Highlight>. Counting Tuesday as day one makes Monday day seven, so the old package is still active at 23:00 on Monday.

Because Sari buys the same package before expiry, the rule applies: <Highlight>its leftover daytime data is added to the new one. Leftover night data is never carried over</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="\text{Daytime: } 5 + 2 = 7\text{ GB}, \qquad \text{Night: } 5\text{ GB}" />

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="7" /> GB daytime and <InlineMath math="8" /> GB night** carries over the leftover <InlineMath math="3" /> GB of night data against the rule.
- **<InlineMath math="5" /> GB daytime and <InlineMath math="5" /> GB night** treats the old package as already expired with <InlineMath math="59" /> minutes still left.
- **<InlineMath math="10" /> GB daytime and <InlineMath math="5" /> GB night** adds the leftover night data into daytime data.
- **<InlineMath math="10" /> GB daytime and <InlineMath math="10" /> GB night** doubles an entire package instead of adding only the leftover daytime quota.

# Answer

Sari has **<InlineMath math="7" /> GB of daytime data and <InlineMath math="5" /> GB of night data**.
