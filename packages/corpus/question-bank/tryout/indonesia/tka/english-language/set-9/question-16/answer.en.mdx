# Given

Bu Tini bakes one batch of bread rolls in a kitchen at **<InlineMath math="22" /> degrees**.

Timing rules after kneading finishes:
- The first rise takes **<InlineMath math="60" /> minutes** at about <InlineMath math="28" /> degrees, and **twice as long** below <InlineMath math="24" /> degrees.
- The second rise takes **half as long as the first rise**.
- Baking takes **<InlineMath math="15" /> minutes** at <InlineMath math="180" /> degrees.

# Asked

How long it takes from the end of kneading until the rolls come out of the oven.

# Solution

Double the first rise for the cool room, take half of that **actual first-rise time** for the second rise, add the <InlineMath math="15" />-minute bake, and convert total minutes into hours and minutes.

> Because the second rise is half of the actual first rise, a cool kitchen stretches both rising stages before baking begins.

## Computing rising and baking times

At <InlineMath math="22" /> degrees the kitchen falls under <Highlight>in a room below <InlineMath math="24" /> degrees, allow twice as long</Highlight>, and step four sets the second rise to <Highlight>half as long as the first rise took</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="\text{First rise: } 60 \times 2 = 120\text{ min}, \qquad \text{Second rise: } \frac{120}{2} = 60\text{ min}" />

<BlockMath math="\text{Total: } 120 + 60 + 15 = 195\text{ min} = 3\text{ hours } 15\text{ minutes}" />

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="1" /> hour <InlineMath math="45" /> minutes** uses warm-kitchen rising times (<InlineMath math="60 + 30 + 15" /> minutes).
- **<InlineMath math="2" /> hours <InlineMath math="45" /> minutes** halves the warm-kitchen <InlineMath math="60" /> minutes instead of the actual <InlineMath math="120" />-minute first rise.
- **<InlineMath math="4" /> hours <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes** sets the second rise equal to the first rise.
- **<InlineMath math="3" /> hours** omits the <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes of baking.

# Answer

It takes **<InlineMath math="3" /> hours <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes** from the end of kneading until the rolls leave the oven.
