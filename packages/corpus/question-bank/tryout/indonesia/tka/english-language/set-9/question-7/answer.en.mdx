# Given

The notebook records four Friday weights:
- First Friday: **<InlineMath math="1.4" /> kg** (already net chili weight).
- Second, third, and fourth Fridays: **<InlineMath math="2.1" /> kg**, **<InlineMath math="1.9" /> kg**, and **<InlineMath math="2.6" /> kg**, each including the **<InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg** big basket.

# Asked

How much more chili was picked on the third and fourth Fridays combined than on the first and second Fridays combined once the mistake is corrected.

# Solution

Subtract <InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg from **the second, third, and fourth Fridays only**, then subtract the first-half total from the second-half total.

> Because Lina already subtracted her empty basket on the first Friday, only the last three Fridays need the zero point four kilogram basket removed.

## Correcting the weights and finding the gap

The error started on the second Friday (<Highlight>From the second Friday on</Highlight>), and <Highlight>The big basket weighs <InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg</Highlight>.

| Friday | Book weight | Basket correction | True chili weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | <InlineMath math="1.4" /> kg | None needed | <InlineMath math="1.4" /> kg |
| Second | <InlineMath math="2.1" /> kg | Minus <InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg | <InlineMath math="1.7" /> kg |
| Third | <InlineMath math="1.9" /> kg | Minus <InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg | <InlineMath math="1.5" /> kg |
| Fourth | <InlineMath math="2.6" /> kg | Minus <InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg | <InlineMath math="2.2" /> kg |

Comparing the two halves gives:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Fridays 3 and 4: } 1.5 + 2.2 = 3.7\text{ kg}, \qquad \text{Fridays 1 and 2: } 1.4 + 1.7 = 3.1\text{ kg}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{Difference: } 3.7 - 3.1 = 0.6\text{ kg}" />
</MathContainer>

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="1" /> kg** uses the uncorrected notebook figures directly.
- **<InlineMath math="0.2" /> kg** corrects the third and fourth Fridays while forgetting to correct the second Friday.
- **<InlineMath math="3.7" /> kg** stops at the second-half total instead of taking the difference.
- **<InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg** is simply the weight of one empty basket.

# Answer

**<InlineMath math="0.6" /> kg** more chili was picked on the third and fourth Fridays together.
