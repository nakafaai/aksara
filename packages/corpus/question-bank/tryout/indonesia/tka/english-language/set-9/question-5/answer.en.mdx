Check each statement with the rules.

- **A Harian bought at 23:30 lasts at most <InlineMath math="29" /> minutes: supported**. It ends at 23:59 that day.
- Night data at 6:30: not supported. Night data works only until 6:00.
- **A Mingguan bought on a Friday is valid on the following Thursday: supported**. Friday is the first day, so Thursday is the seventh.
- Leftover night data is added to a renewed package: not supported. It "is never carried over".
- A Bulanan gives <InlineMath math="40" /> GB for any time of day: not supported. Half of it is night data, usable only from 0:00 to 6:00.

So the supported statements are the ones about the Harian bought at 23:30 and the Mingguan bought on a Friday.
