An opinion is a **personal judgment** people can disagree on. *None feels as calm as my grandmother's house* rates the house as **the most peaceful** of all. That's the writer's feeling, and others might feel differently. So it's an opinion.

- The house's age and timber can be checked.
- The ladder steps can be counted.
- The roof's shape and material can be seen.
- The number of rooms can be counted.
