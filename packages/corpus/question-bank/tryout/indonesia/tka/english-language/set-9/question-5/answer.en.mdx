# Given

Five statements make claims about Kilat's expiry cutoff, night-data hours, renewal carry-over, and the **Bulanan** package allowance.

Key rules from the passage:
- Every package ends at **23:59** on its final day, counting purchase day as day one.
- Night data works strictly from **0:00 to 6:00** and never carries over.
- **Bulanan** splits into **<InlineMath math="20" /> GB daytime** and **<InlineMath math="20" /> GB night** data.

# Asked

All statements supported by the passage (select every correct option).

# Solution

Evaluate each option individually against the 23:59 expiry rule, the 0:00 to 6:00 night window, and the split between daytime and night allowances.

> Checking each statement independently prevents missing a second valid option in a multi-select question.

## Verifying every statement against the rules

| Statement | Supported? | Evidence from passage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harian bought at 23:30 lasts at most <InlineMath math="29" /> minutes | **Yes** | A package <Highlight>ends at 23:59 on its last day</Highlight>, leaving <InlineMath math="29" /> minutes from 23:30 to 23:59. |
| Night data can be used at 6:30 | No | The text states <Highlight>Night data can be used only from 0:00 to 6:00</Highlight>. |
| Mingguan bought Friday is valid next Thursday | **Yes** | Counting Friday as day <InlineMath math="1" /> makes the following Thursday day <InlineMath math="7" />, valid until 23:59. |
| Leftover night data is added on renewal | No | The rule explicitly says <Highlight>Leftover night data is never carried over</Highlight>. |
| Bulanan gives <InlineMath math="40" /> GB usable any time | No | Half of its total is <InlineMath math="20" /> GB of night data restricted to 0:00 to 6:00. |

## Why the other statements fail

- **6:30** falls outside the 0:00 to 6:00 night-data window.
- **Night carry-over** and **<InlineMath math="40" /> GB at any time** both ignore the strict rules separating night data from daytime data.

# Answer

The supported statements are **A Harian bought at 23:30 can be used for at most <InlineMath math="29" /> minutes** and **A Mingguan bought on a Friday is still valid on the following Thursday**.
