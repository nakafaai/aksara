Judge each event on its own, without the story around it.

- **I could happen**. A new spring and key make a music box play again.
- **II could happen**. Wind chimes can be heard through a wall.
- III couldn't happen. A box with a snapped spring and no key has nothing to drive it.
- **IV could happen**. A dream can feel real on waking.
- **V could happen**. Glass mists over when a room cools.

The options without I treat a repaired box as impossible. The option with III accepts the one event with nothing to power it.

So the events that could happen are I, II, IV and V only.
