How to Make a Simple Paper Lantern

These lanterns are decorations, not lights. Never put a candle inside a paper lantern, because the paper can catch fire easily.

You will need a sheet of colored A4 paper, a ruler, a pencil, scissors, glue, and a strip of paper about 15 centimeters long for the handle.

1. Fold the sheet in half lengthwise, so that the long edges meet.
2. With the fold at the bottom, draw a line 2 centimeters below the open edges. This line shows where to stop cutting.
3. Starting at the fold, cut straight up to the line. Make a cut every 2 centimeters along the paper. Do not cut past the line, or the lantern will fall apart.
4. Unfold the sheet and roll it into a tube, with the cuts running from top to bottom.
5. Glue the short edges together and hold them for a few seconds until they stick.
6. Gently press the top and bottom of the tube toward each other so that the strips bend outward.
7. Glue the paper strip across the top as a handle.

Tip: Lanterns made from thicker paper keep their shape better.
