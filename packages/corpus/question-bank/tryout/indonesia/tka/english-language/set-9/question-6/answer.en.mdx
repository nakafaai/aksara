# Given

The class chili-harvest record spans four Fridays:
- Book weights: <InlineMath math="1.4" /> kg, <InlineMath math="2.1" /> kg, <InlineMath math="1.9" /> kg, and <InlineMath math="2.6" /> kg (total <InlineMath math="8" /> kg promised to the canteen).
- Packed chilies weighed only **<InlineMath math="6.8" /> kg** and lost no weight in the canteen fridge.
- Lina followed Bu Wening's rule on the first Friday, while the next three Fridays included a **<InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg** big basket.

# Asked

Which Roman-numeral statements belong in an accurate summary of the account.

# Solution

Match each statement against the <InlineMath math="1.2" /> kg gap between **promised and actual weight**, and verify who followed or broke Bu Wening's rule.

> Counting the big basket three times adds exactly one point two kilograms, explaining the entire gap between the notebook and the packed chilies.

## Checking each summary statement

The notebook excess equals three times the weight of the big basket:

<BlockMath math="8 - 6.8 = 1.2\text{ kg}, \qquad 3 \times 0.4 = 1.2\text{ kg}" />

| Statement | Belongs? | Evidence from passage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | **Yes** | From the second Friday on, Dani and the narrator used the <InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg basket and <Highlight>never weighed it empty</Highlight>. |
| II | **Yes** | The class promised <InlineMath math="8" /> kg despite picking <InlineMath math="6.8" /> kg, a gap of <InlineMath math="1.2" /> kg. |
| III | No | The text states <Highlight>The chilies themselves had lost no weight, because we had kept them in the canteen fridge</Highlight>. |
| IV | No | On the first Friday Lina <Highlight>weighed it empty, as the rule says</Highlight>. |
| V | **Yes** | Three <InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg baskets total <InlineMath math="1.2" /> kg, matching the exact notebook overcount. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options with III** blame weight loss in the fridge, which the text explicitly rules out.
- **Options with IV** blame Lina, the only student who weighed her basket empty.
- **Options missing I or V** leave out the core reason behind the <InlineMath math="1.2" /> kg error.

# Answer

The statements that belong in an accurate summary are **I, II, and V only**.
