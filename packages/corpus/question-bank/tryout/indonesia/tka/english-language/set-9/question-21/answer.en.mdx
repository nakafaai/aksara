# Given

Two texts debate adding a <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" /> charge to sweetened drinks at SMP Cendana:
- **Text 1 (student council)** judges a small charge a fair price and asserts that nothing changes habits like a price.
- **Text 2 (Bu Rosa)** notes SMP Mutiara's trial ran during rainy months, prior-year headache visits were never counted, and stallholders depend on drink sales.

# Asked

Whether each statement is **Stated as a fact**, **Stated as an opinion**, or **Not stated in either text**.

# Solution

Separate checkable factual claims written in the texts from value judgements made by a writer and extra conclusions that neither writer ever actually states.

> Pointing out a possible confounding factor like rain or mentioning stallholders' income is not the same as claiming rain caused the drop or that stallholders oppose the charge.

## Sorting facts opinions and unstated claims

| Statement | Classification | Evidence from the texts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nothing changes habits like a price | **Opinion** | Student council judgement: <Highlight>Nothing changes habits like a price.</Highlight> |
| SMP Mutiara's trial ran through the rainy months | **Fact** | Stated factually by Bu Rosa: <Highlight>the trial ran through the rainy months</Highlight>. |
| Rainy months caused the sales drop at SMP Mutiara | **Not stated** | Bu Rosa notes cold drinks sell less in rain, never claiming rain caused this specific drop. |
| Nobody counted nurse headache visits the year before | **Fact** | Stated factually by Bu Rosa: <Highlight>Nobody counted the nurse's headache visits the year before</Highlight>. |
| A small charge is a fair price for healthier students | **Opinion** | Value judgement by the council: <Highlight>A small charge is a fair price for healthier students.</Highlight> |
| Canteen stallholders oppose the charge | **Not stated** | Bu Rosa notes stallholders depend on drink sales, never stating their stance on the charge. |

## Common traps to watch for

- Marking statement 3 as stated turns a rival factor into a proven causal claim Bu Rosa never makes.
- Marking statement 6 as stated reads an unstated opinion into a factual comment about stallholders' livelihoods.

# Answer

Statements **2 and 4** are **Stated as a fact**, statements **1 and 5** are **Stated as an opinion**, and statements **3 and 6** are **Not stated in either text**.
