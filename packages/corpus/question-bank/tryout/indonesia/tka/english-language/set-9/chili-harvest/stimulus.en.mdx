Our class weighed its chili harvest wrongly for a month, and we found out only when we packed the chilies to sell.

Every Friday, two of us picked the ripe chilies in the school garden and weighed them in a plastic basket on the canteen scale. Bu Wening's rule was simple: weigh the basket empty first, then weigh it full, and write down the difference. [GAP]

We added up the book, wrote that the month's harvest was <InlineMath math="8" /> kg, and promised the canteen <InlineMath math="8" /> kg of chili for its sambal.

When we packed the chilies, they weighed only <InlineMath math="6.8" /> kg. Bu Wening checked the book and the baskets. On the first Friday, Lina had used the small basket and weighed it empty, as the rule says. From the second Friday on, the harvest was growing, so Dani and I used a bigger basket, and we never weighed it empty. We had written the full weight each time. The big basket weighs <InlineMath math="0.4" /> kg. The chilies themselves had lost no weight, because we had kept them in the canteen fridge.
