# Given

Six baking actions must be classified against the soft bread rolls recipe:
- Water must stay at or below <InlineMath math="45" /> degrees, salt and yeast start on opposite sides, dough splits into <InlineMath math="12" /> equal-weight pieces, the second rise is half the first, and a double batch uses only <InlineMath math="1.5" /> times the yeast (<InlineMath math="10.5" /> g).

# Asked

Whether each action **Follows the instructions**, **Breaks the instructions**, or is **Not covered**.

# Solution

Match each action to the exact step that governs it, and avoid mistaking silence on an extra ingredient for a prohibition.

> Saying nothing about an ingredient like raisins means not covered, whereas crossing the water limit, yeast scaling rule, or salt separation breaks the recipe.

## Classifying all six baking actions

| Statement | Category | Recipe evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adding water heated to <InlineMath math="50" /> degrees | **Breaks** | Step <InlineMath math="2" /> warns that water <Highlight>must not be above 45 degrees</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> g flour and <InlineMath math="14" /> g yeast for double batch | **Breaks** | A double batch needs <Highlight>only 1.5 times the yeast</Highlight>, which is <InlineMath math="10.5" /> g instead of <InlineMath math="14" /> g. |
| Second rise <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes after <InlineMath math="60" />-minute first rise | **Follows** | Step <InlineMath math="4" /> specifies <Highlight>half as long as the first rise took</Highlight>, and half of <InlineMath math="60" /> is <InlineMath math="30" />. |
| Adding raisins to the dough | **Not covered** | The recipe neither asks for nor forbids raisins. |
| Pouring salt straight onto the yeast | **Breaks** | Step <InlineMath math="1" /> separates them because <Highlight>salt touching yeast directly slows it down</Highlight>. |
| Weighing pieces so all <InlineMath math="12" /> match in weight | **Follows** | Step <InlineMath math="4" /> directs making <Highlight>12 pieces of the same weight</Highlight>. |

## Common pitfalls to avoid

- Doubling yeast to <InlineMath math="14" /> g overlooks the explicit <InlineMath math="1.5" />-times exception for double batches.
- Marking raisins as breaking the instructions reads a ban into the recipe's silence.

# Answer

Statements **3 and 6** **Follow the instructions**, statements **1, 2, and 5** **Break the instructions**, and statement **4** is **Not covered**.
