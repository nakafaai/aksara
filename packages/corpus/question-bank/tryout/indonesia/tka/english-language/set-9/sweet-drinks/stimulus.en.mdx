**Text 1: a letter from the student council of SMP Cendana**

Our students drink far too much sugar. Our canteen should add <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" /> to the price of every sweetened drink and spend the money on drinking-water fountains. A small charge is a fair price for healthier students. Last year, SMP Mutiara tried the same charge for one term. Its canteen's sales of sweetened drinks fell from <InlineMath math="900" /> to <InlineMath math="540" /> a week, and visits to the school nurse for headaches fell too. Nothing changes habits like a price.

**Text 2: a reply from Bu Rosa, who runs the canteen**

I agree with the student council that our students drink too much sugar, and the fall in sales at SMP Mutiara, from <InlineMath math="900" /> to <InlineMath math="540" /> drinks a week, is striking. But SMP Mutiara did more than add a charge. It put a free cold-water fountain in the yard in the same term, and the trial ran through the rainy months, when cold drinks sell less anyway. Nobody counted the nurse's headache visits the year before, so we cannot say they fell. Our stallholders also rent their stalls and depend on drink sales. I propose that we first install two water fountains for one term without changing any price, and compare our drink sales with the same term last year.
