# Given

The **indigo dyeing** guide states six steps: a fresh <InlineMath math="8" />-litre bath takes **<InlineMath math="200" /> grams** of paste, **<InlineMath math="25" /> grams** are added after every <InlineMath math="3" /> cloths before the next, **one dip (<InlineMath math="5" /> min) plus one airing (<InlineMath math="15" /> min) makes one round** (<InlineMath math="6" /> rounds for dark blue), and after the last round the cloth **dries in the shade for a day** before vinegar rinsing. Salt is never mentioned.

# Asked

Whether each of the six actions **Follows the instructions**, **Breaks the instructions**, or is **Not covered**.

# Solution

Match each action directly to its numbered step. Watch out for **bundling four dips before one long airing**: total minutes look identical (<InlineMath math="80" /> minutes), yet skipping airing between dips breaks the core rule of alternating rounds.

> Check not only quantities and durations, but also sequence rules like alternating dips and drying for a full day before rinsing.

## Checking all six actions against the steps

Key rules include <Highlight>One dip and one airing make one round</Highlight> and <Highlight>leave the cloth to dry in the shade for a day. Then rinse it</Highlight>. Evaluating **all six actions**:

| Action | Category | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mixing <InlineMath math="250" /> g into a fresh bath | **Breaks the instructions** | Fresh <InlineMath math="8" />-L bath needs <InlineMath math="200" /> g, not <InlineMath math="250" /> g |
| Adding <InlineMath math="25" /> g before the fourth cloth | **Follows the instructions** | Matches step <InlineMath math="5" /> after every <InlineMath math="3" /> cloths |
| <InlineMath math="6" /> rounds (<InlineMath math="5" />-min dip + <InlineMath math="15" />-min airing) | **Follows the instructions** | Matches step <InlineMath math="3" /> and step <InlineMath math="4" /> for dark blue |
| <InlineMath math="4" /> dips in a row then one <InlineMath math="60" />-min airing | **Breaks the instructions** | Each dip must have its own airing |
| Adding a spoonful of salt to the bath | **Not covered** | Salt is never mentioned in any step |
| Rinsing with vinegar on the same day | **Breaks the instructions** | Must dry in the shade for a day first |

# Answer

Actions **two and three** follow the instructions, actions **one, four, and six** break the instructions, and action **five** is **Not covered**.
