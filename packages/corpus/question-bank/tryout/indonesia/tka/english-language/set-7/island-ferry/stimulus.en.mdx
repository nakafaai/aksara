The ferry Kapal Bunga links Tanjung Rawa on the mainland with Pulau Lima. The crossing takes <InlineMath math="90" /> minutes, and the boat waits <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes at each end before sailing back. It makes two round trips a day, and on a normal day the first ferry leaves Tanjung Rawa at 6:00.

The harbour at Pulau Lima is shallow, and at low tide the ferry cannot reach the jetty. So when low tide at Pulau Lima falls within <InlineMath math="1" /> hour before or after a planned arrival there, the whole day's timetable moves <InlineMath math="1" /> hour later, and it moves only once in a day. The harbour office posts the low-tide times a week ahead.

A return ticket costs the same as two one-way tickets, but the trip back must be made within <InlineMath math="7" /> days, counting the day of the outward trip as the first day. Islanders with a resident card pay half the adult price; the card does not change any other fare. On public holidays every fare rises by <InlineMath math="25\%" />.
