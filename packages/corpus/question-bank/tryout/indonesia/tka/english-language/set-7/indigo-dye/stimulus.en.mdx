**Dyeing a cotton cloth with indigo**

These steps are for cloths of <InlineMath math="2" /> metres.

1. Wash the cloth in warm water with a spoonful of soap and rinse it twice. Starch left in new cloth stops the dye from taking.
2. Mix the dye bath with <InlineMath math="50" /> grams of indigo paste for every <InlineMath math="2" /> litres of water. A bath needs <InlineMath math="8" /> litres.
3. Dip the cloth in the bath for <InlineMath math="5" /> minutes, then lift it out and hang it in the air for <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes. It comes out of the bath green and turns blue as it meets the air. One dip and one airing make one round.
4. Each round darkens the cloth. Light blue needs <InlineMath math="2" /> rounds, mid blue <InlineMath math="4" /> rounds and dark blue <InlineMath math="6" /> rounds.
5. The bath weakens with use. After every <InlineMath math="3" /> cloths, stir in another <InlineMath math="25" /> grams of paste before the next cloth goes in.
6. After the last round, leave the cloth to dry in the shade for a day. Then rinse it in cold water with a cup of vinegar until the water runs clear.
