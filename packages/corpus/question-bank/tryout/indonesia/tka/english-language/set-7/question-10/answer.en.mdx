# Given

The gap **[GAP]** sits at the end of paragraph two in the **Kenari bridge** recount, right before the table. Later paragraphs confirm three firm constraints: the mistake went unnoticed for **two weeks**, the bridge camera was checked only **two weeks later**, and the group reported **7:00 to 8:00** as the busiest hour.

# Asked

All candidate sentences that can fill **[GAP]** without contradicting anything in the account.

# Solution

Test each sentence like a puzzle piece against **every fact revealed later in the story**. Keep any sentence that fits smoothly with the hidden two-week error and camera check, and reject any sentence that clashes with them.

> A sentence fits a gap in a recount only if it creates zero contradiction with the timeline and results stated afterward.

## Checking all five candidate sentences

Later in the text, <Highlight>Two weeks later, Bu Lestari compared our table with the count from the camera</Highlight> and the group reported **7:00 to 8:00** as busiest. Testing **every sentence**:

- **Nobody checked the table against the tally sheets, since the totals only ever went up**: **Fits**, explaining cleanly why the mistake stayed hidden for two weeks.
- **Yosef checked each figure against the camera as he wrote it**: **Does not fit**, because camera comparison happened only two weeks later.
- **I looked over Yosef's table at 9:00 and found nothing wrong**: **Fits**, because the smooth rising table looks normal on its own.
- **We all expected 6:00 to 7:00 to be busiest, and the table proved us right**: **Does not fit**, because the table showed 7:00 to 8:00 as busiest.
- **The camera on the bridge had been put up the month before**: **Fits**, agreeing naturally with Bu Lestari using the camera's count.

# Answer

The sentences that fit **[GAP]** without contradiction are:
- **Nobody checked the table against the tally sheets, since the totals only ever went up**.
- **I looked over Yosef's table at 9:00 and found nothing wrong**.
- **The camera on the bridge had been put up the month before**.
