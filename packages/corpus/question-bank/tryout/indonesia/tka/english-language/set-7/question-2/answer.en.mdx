# Given

*Kapal Bunga* leaves Tanjung Rawa at 6:00, takes **<InlineMath math="90" /> minutes**, and waits **<InlineMath math="30" /> minutes** at each pier. Planned **arrivals at Pulau Lima** are **7:30** and **11:30**, with normal departures at **8:00** and **12:00**.

If low tide falls **within <InlineMath math="1" /> hour before or after a planned arrival there**, the entire day shifts **<InlineMath math="1" /> hour later** (once per day).

# Asked

Whether the second ferry leaves Pulau Lima at **12:00** or **13:00** for each of the five low-tide times.

# Solution

Mark two alert windows around the **planned arrivals** (7:30 and 11:30): **6:30 to 8:30** and **10:30 to 12:30**. Low tide inside either window shifts the 12:00 sailing to **13:00**, while departure times do not count.

> Check low tide strictly against planned *arrivals* at Pulau Lima (7:30 and 11:30), never against departure times.

## Testing all five low tide times against both windows

The rule specifies <Highlight>within <InlineMath math="1" /> hour before or after a planned arrival there</Highlight>. Only **6:45** (<InlineMath math="45" /> minutes before 7:30) and **12:20** (<InlineMath math="50" /> minutes after 11:30) fall inside those two windows, shifting departure to **13:00**.

By contrast, **8:45** (<InlineMath math="75" /> minutes after 7:30), **10:15** (<InlineMath math="75" /> minutes before 11:30), and **13:00** (<InlineMath math="90" /> minutes after 11:30) all lie outside both arrival windows, keeping the **12:00** departure.

## Summary of all five statements

| Low tide time | Distance to nearest arrival | Second departure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6:45 | <InlineMath math="45" /> min before 7:30 (inside) | **At 13:00** |
| 8:45 | <InlineMath math="75" /> min after 7:30 (outside) | **At 12:00** |
| 12:20 | <InlineMath math="50" /> min after 11:30 (inside) | **At 13:00** |
| 10:15 | <InlineMath math="75" /> min before 11:30 (outside) | **At 12:00** |
| 13:00 | <InlineMath math="90" /> min after 11:30 (outside) | **At 12:00** |

# Answer

The second ferry leaves Pulau Lima **At 13:00** for **6:45** and **12:20**, and **At 12:00** for **8:45**, **10:15**, and **13:00**.
