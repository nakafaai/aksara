Paragraph two says it directly: *If they dry too quickly in direct sun, they crack.* So pots that dry too fast in direct sun **crack**. That's why they dry slowly in the shade for about a week.

- The reddish-brown color comes from firing in the kiln, not from drying.
- The text never says the pots become too hard to shape.
- Collapsing is what happens to visitors' first pots on the wheel.
- Soft unfired pots dent easily anyway. That isn't a result of drying too fast.
