# Given

In the **Jalan Pasar** exchange, the traders propose closing Jalan Pasar **every Sunday** citing Kota Lama's <InlineMath math="30\%" /> shopper jump (<InlineMath math="2{,}000" /> to <InlineMath math="2{,}600" />). Pak Hendra accepts the shopper count, notes the trial **did more than close the street** (holidays and free parking), and proposes closing the **northern half for four Sundays** compared with the same Sundays last year.

# Asked

Which of statements I through V belong in an accurate summary of both texts.

# Solution

Keep each side's **exact claim and exact proposal** strictly in its own lane. Never turn Pak Hendra's *doubt* about free parking into proof (**III**), and never hand Pak Hendra's four-Sunday trial over to the traders (**V**).

> A faithful two-text summary preserves both who proposed each measure and how strongly each writer actually states their claim.

## Checking statements I through V against both texts

Text 1 asks to close Jalan Pasar <Highlight>every Sunday</Highlight>, while Text 2 observes the Kota Lama trial <Highlight>did more than close the street</Highlight> and proposes closing the **northern half for four Sundays**. Checking **all five statements**:

- **I (belongs)**: Traders cite Kota Lama, where average shoppers rose by <InlineMath math="600 \div 2{,}000 = 30\%" />.
- **II (belongs)**: Pak Hendra calls the count impressive yet notes other changes happened at the same time.
- **III (false)**: He raises the parking lot as a confounding factor, never proving it caused the rise.
- **IV (belongs)**: Matches his exact counter-proposal (northern half, four Sundays, compared with last year).
- **V (false)**: Traders want closure **every Sunday**, whereas four Sundays is Pak Hendra's trial.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I, II, IV, and V only** assigns Pak Hendra's four-Sunday trial to the traders.
- **I, II, III, and IV only** inflates Pak Hendra's doubt into proof about parking.
- **II and IV only** and **I and IV only** drop one accurate core point.

# Answer

An accurate summary of the two texts should include **I, II, and IV only**.
