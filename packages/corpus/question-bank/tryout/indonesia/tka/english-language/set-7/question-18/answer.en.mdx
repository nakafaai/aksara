A detail can explain a lighter colour only if it describes something that makes the dye take less.

- **I can explain it**. If the cloth wasn't washed, "Starch left in new cloth stops the dye from taking."
- **II can explain it**. A bath that has dyed several cloths without the extra paste is weaker, so four rounds darken the cloth less.
- III can't. The airing time is the same in every round and doesn't make a cloth lighter.
- IV can't. It's about dark blue, and the cloth had the right number of rounds for mid blue.
- V can't. The size of the bath doesn't change how strong it is, since the paste is measured to the water.

The options with III or IV treat a rule that the cloth followed as a reason it went wrong.

So the details that could explain it are I and II only.
