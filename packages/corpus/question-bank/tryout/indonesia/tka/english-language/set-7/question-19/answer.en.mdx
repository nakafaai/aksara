# Given

Pak Rahmat prepares a fresh **<InlineMath math="8" />-litre** bath (<InlineMath math="50" /> g per <InlineMath math="2" /> L) and dyes **<InlineMath math="7" /> cloths** sequentially to **mid blue** (<InlineMath math="4" /> rounds each, <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes per round), stirring in **<InlineMath math="25" /> g** extra paste **after every <InlineMath math="3" /> cloths before the next cloth goes in**.

# Asked

Which of statements I through IV about Pak Rahmat's work are supported by the instructions.

# Solution

Track the top-ups on a quick cloth line: cloths <InlineMath math="1, 2, 3" />, then **top-up one**, then cloths <InlineMath math="4, 5, 6" />, then **top-up two**, then cloth <InlineMath math="7" />, then **stop!** Because no eighth cloth enters, there is no third top-up.

> Only add a top-up when another cloth actually follows the batch of <InlineMath math="3" />.

## Computing bath paste and total time

Following <Highlight>before the next cloth goes in</Highlight>, **paste and total duration** work out cleanly:

<BlockMath math="\text{Fresh bath} = \frac{8}{2} \times 50 = 200\text{ g}, \qquad \text{Total paste} = 200 + 25 + 25 = 250\text{ g}" />

<BlockMath math="\text{Total time} = 7 \times 4 \times 20 = 560\text{ min} = 9\text{ h } 20\text{ min}" />

- **I (supported)**: Fresh <InlineMath math="8" />-litre bath uses <InlineMath math="200" /> grams.
- **II (supported)**: Extra paste goes in after cloth <InlineMath math="3" /> and after cloth <InlineMath math="6" />, so **twice**.
- **III (false)**: Total paste is <InlineMath math="250" /> grams, not <InlineMath math="275" /> grams.
- **IV (supported)**: Total time is <InlineMath math="560" /> minutes, or **<InlineMath math="9" /> hours <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I, II, III, and IV** wrongly adds a pointless third top-up after the seventh cloth (<InlineMath math="275" /> g).
- **I and II only**, **II and IV only**, and **I and IV only** each drop one verified statement.

# Answer

The supported statements are **I, II, and IV only**.
