**Text 1: a letter from the traders of Jalan Pasar**

Jalan Pasar should be closed to motorbikes and cars from 6:00 to 11:00 every Sunday. On Sunday mornings the street is so crowded with parked motorbikes that shoppers walk in the gutter, and a street without engines is simply a pleasure to shop in. Last year, Kota Lama closed its market street on eight Sundays as a trial. The average number of shoppers rose from <InlineMath math="2{,}000" /> to <InlineMath math="2{,}600" /> a Sunday, and the stallholders say their takings rose by <InlineMath math="20\%" />. Nothing else could bring so many shoppers back so cheaply.

**Text 2: a reply from Pak Hendra, head of the town transport office**

I agree with the traders that Jalan Pasar is too crowded on Sunday mornings, and the shopper count from Kota Lama is impressive. But Kota Lama's trial did more than close the street. It ran during the school holidays, when markets are busy anyway, and the town opened a free parking lot at the edge of the market at the same time. The <InlineMath math="20\%" /> rise in takings is what the stallholders remember; nobody compared it with the same Sundays the year before. There is also the clinic at the southern end of Jalan Pasar, which ambulances must be able to reach. I propose a smaller trial: close only the northern half of the street, away from the clinic, for four Sundays, and count shoppers and takings on those Sundays and on the same Sundays of last year.
