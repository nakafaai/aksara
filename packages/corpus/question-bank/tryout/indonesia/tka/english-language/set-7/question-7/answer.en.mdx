# Given

In the **Kenari bridge** running-total table, Yosef recorded **<InlineMath math="1{,}700" />** at 7:30, **<InlineMath math="2{,}230" />** at 8:00, and **<InlineMath math="2{,}560" />** at 8:30. Every entry from 7:30 onward carries the same **extra <InlineMath math="640" />** from double-counting Nadia's sheet at 7:00.

# Asked

The corrected number of motorbikes that crossed the bridge between **7:30** and **8:30**.

# Solution

Imagine weighing a dog while standing on a scale holding a heavy backpack, and weighing yourself again later still wearing **the exact same backpack**. When you subtract the two readings, **the backpack's weight cancels completely**. Here the extra <InlineMath math="640" /> sits inside both 7:30 and 8:30, so subtracting cancels it cleanly.

> A constant offset added to two cumulative totals disappears automatically when you take their difference.

## Subtracting the cumulative totals

Because the account states <Highlight>every total after that carried the same mistake</Highlight>, correcting both numbers first gives <InlineMath math="1{,}700 - 640 = 1{,}060" /> at 7:30 and <InlineMath math="2{,}560 - 640 = 1{,}920" /> at 8:30. Either way, **the difference between 7:30 and 8:30** is:

<BlockMath math="2{,}560 - 1{,}700 = 1{,}920 - 1{,}060 = 860" />

## Why the other four numbers fail

- **<InlineMath math="220" />** **subtracts** <InlineMath math="640" /> a second time from an already error-free difference (<InlineMath math="860 - 640" />).
- **<InlineMath math="330" />** covers only 8:00 to 8:30 (<InlineMath math="2{,}560 - 2{,}230" />).
- **<InlineMath math="1{,}500" />** mistakenly adds <InlineMath math="640" /> to <InlineMath math="860" />.
- **<InlineMath math="530" />** covers only 7:30 to 8:00 (<InlineMath math="2{,}230 - 1{,}700" />).

# Answer

Exactly **<InlineMath math="860" />** motorbikes crossed the bridge between 7:30 and 8:30.
