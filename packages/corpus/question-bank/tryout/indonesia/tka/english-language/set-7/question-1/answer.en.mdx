# Given

On a **public holiday**, Bu Wati (no resident card) boards the ferry *Kapal Bunga* with her **motorbike** plus three children on foot aged **<InlineMath math="2" />**, **<InlineMath math="5" />**, and **<InlineMath math="12" />**. Standard one-way fares are **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}75{,}000" />** for a motorbike with its rider, **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}40{,}000" />** for adults aged <InlineMath math="12" /> or over, **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" />** for children aged <InlineMath math="3" /> to <InlineMath math="11" /> (free under <InlineMath math="3" />), and **all fares rise <InlineMath math="25\%" /> on public holidays**.

# Asked

The total amount Bu Wati's family pays for the one-way crossing on a public holiday.

# Solution

Think of buying tickets like filling one basket at normal prices first, then adding the **<InlineMath math="25\%" /> holiday surcharge** at the register. Checking the exact age cutoffs and rider rule keeps you from paying twice.

> Read boundary phrases like *with its rider* and *aged <InlineMath math="12" /> or over* closely before multiplying by the holiday increase.

## Adding normal fares and applying the holiday surcharge

The table states <Highlight>Motorbike, with its rider</Highlight> costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}75{,}000" />, so Bu Wati pays no separate adult ticket. The <InlineMath math="12" />-year-old pays the **adult fare** (<Highlight>Adult, aged <InlineMath math="12" /> or over</Highlight>: <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}40{,}000" />), the <InlineMath math="5" />-year-old pays **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" />**, and the <InlineMath math="2" />-year-old rides **free**.

Summing normal fares and applying <Highlight>every fare rises by <InlineMath math="25\%" /></Highlight> gives **the total holiday cost**:

<BlockMath math="\text{Normal total} = 75{,}000 + 40{,}000 + 20{,}000 = \text{Rp}135{,}000" />

<BlockMath math="\text{Holiday total} = 135{,}000 \times 1.25 = \text{Rp}168{,}750" />

## Why the other four totals fail

- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}135{,}000" />** omits the <InlineMath math="25\%" /> public holiday increase.
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}143{,}750" />** mistakenly charges the <InlineMath math="12" />-year-old the child fare (<InlineMath math="115{,}000 \times 1.25" />).
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}193{,}750" />** mistakenly charges the <InlineMath math="2" />-year-old a child ticket (<InlineMath math="155{,}000 \times 1.25" />).
- **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}218{,}750" />** double-charges Bu Wati an adult ticket even though the motorbike fare includes its rider.

# Answer

The family pays **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}168{,}750" />** in all.
