# Given

In **The Dark Lighthouse**, the old Tanjung Batu tower has been **dark for six years** with its power cut, its **only door locked**, and the **only key hanging in Nenek's kitchen**.

During the storm, the **new breakwater beacon went out**, the old tower flashed until the last boat entered, and **the key stayed on its hook all night**.

# Asked

Which of statements I through IV the reader can conclude by combining details across the story.

# Solution

Piece together the mystery clues from paragraphs one and three like a detective board. The **locked door + visible kitchen key**, the **failed breakwater beacon**, and **six dark years** all click together, while Arif's earlier swim happened on a completely different night.

> Synthesizing across paragraphs links early background facts with the climax without blaming a character for an unrelated night.

## Combining clues across paragraphs for I through IV

Paragraph one establishes the **only locked door**, **only key**, and **six dark years**, while paragraph three adds <Highlight>the beacon on the breakwater went out</Highlight> and <Highlight>All that night the key hung on its hook in the kitchen</Highlight>. Checking **all four conclusions**:

- **I (concluded)**: Since the single key stayed on the kitchen hook all night, nobody could unlock the door with it.
- **II (concluded)**: The breakwater beacon went out first, and then the old tower shone until every boat returned.
- **III (unsupported)**: Arif admitted only to swimming off the rocks on an earlier night, with zero link to the storm.
- **IV (concluded)**: Paragraph one states the tower had been dark for six years since the new beacon was built.

## Why the other four options fail

- Options containing **III** wrongly stretch Arif's earlier nighttime swim into a storm prank.
- **I and IV only** and **II and IV only** miss one key piece of the storm mystery.

# Answer

By combining details across the story, the reader can conclude **I, II, and IV only**.
