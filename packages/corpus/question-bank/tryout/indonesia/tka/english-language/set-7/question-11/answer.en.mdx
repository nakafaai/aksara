Each statement joins details from different paragraphs.

- **I can be concluded**. The only key hangs in Nenek's kitchen, and "All that night the key hung on its hook in the kitchen", in full view of the narrator.
- **II can be concluded**. "The beacon on the breakwater went out", and then the old lighthouse shone "until the last boat was in".
- III can't be concluded. The wet footprints on another morning came from Arif swimming at night, as he admitted. Nothing ties him to the storm.
- **IV can be concluded**. The lighthouse "has been dark for six years, since the new beacon was built".

The options with III carry Arif's earlier trick over to a different night. The options without I or II leave out what makes the night hard to explain.

So the reader can conclude I, II and IV only.
