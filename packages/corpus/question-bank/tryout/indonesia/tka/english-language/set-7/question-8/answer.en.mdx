# Given

In the **Kenari bridge** recount, Nadia counts 6:00 to 7:00, Farid counts 7:00 to 8:00, the narrator counts 8:00 to 9:00, Yosef records table totals every half hour (so the first inflated total <InlineMath math="1{,}700" /> appears at **7:30**), and the group writes its report after 9:00.

# Asked

The chronological order of events P, Q, R, S, and T.

# Solution

Pin a **clock time** onto every letter first. The key insight is that Yosef entered the first wrong total (**Q**) halfway through Farid's shift at **7:30**, thirty minutes **before** Farid finished his hour (**P**) at 8:00.

> Putting exact timestamps on every event immediately settles whether an error appeared midway through a shift or at its end.

## Placing each event on the timeline

The text states <Highlight>At 7:30, Yosef added Farid's whole sheet to Nadia's old one, so the <InlineMath math="640" /> went in twice</Highlight>. Listing **all five events by clock time** gives the exact sequence:

- **R (7:00)**: Nadia hands over to Farid (table total <InlineMath math="640" /> is still accurate).
- **Q (7:30)**: Table first shows an inflated total (<InlineMath math="1{,}700" />).
- **P (8:00)**: Farid's tally marks reach their final count of <InlineMath math="950" />.
- **T (9:00)**: The narrator's third-hour marks reach their final count.
- **S (after 9:00)**: The group writes its report of <InlineMath math="2{,}900" />.

## Why the other four orders fail

- **R, P, Q, T, S** delays the first table mistake until after 8:00 instead of 7:30.
- **Q, R, P, T, S** puts the mistake before the 7:00 handover even happened.
- **R, Q, T, P, S** swaps the second and third hours.
- **R, Q, P, S, T** has the group write its final report before the third hour finishes.

# Answer

The events happened in the order **R, Q, P, T, S**.
