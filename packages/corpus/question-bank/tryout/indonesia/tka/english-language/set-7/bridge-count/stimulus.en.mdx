Our traffic count on the Kenari bridge was wrong for two weeks, and the mistake was in our table, not on the bridge.

For a geography project, our group had to count the motorbikes crossing the bridge on a school day between 6:00 and 9:00. Bu Lestari's rule was that each counter would work for one hour and then hand over, and the next counter would carry on the running total. Nadia counted the first hour, Farid the second and I the third, while Yosef read the running total off the tally sheets every half hour and wrote it in our table. [GAP]
