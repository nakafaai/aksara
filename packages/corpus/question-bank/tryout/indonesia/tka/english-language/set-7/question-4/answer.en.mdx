# Given

For *Kapal Bunga*, the Pulau Lima harbour is **shallow**, low tide near a planned arrival moves the entire timetable **<InlineMath math="1" /> hour later**, normal second departure from Pulau Lima is **12:00** (taking <InlineMath math="90" /> minutes), low-tide times are posted **a week ahead**, and return tickets cost the same as two one-way tickets with a **<InlineMath math="7" />-day** return window.

# Asked

Which of the five cause-and-effect statements (I through V) are supported by the text.

# Solution

A cause-and-effect statement passes only when **both the stated result and the causal link appear in the text**. If a statement invents a discount or guesses why there are only two round trips, reject it.

> Never accept a plausible-sounding *because* link unless the passage actually establishes that cause and effect.

## Checking all five cause and effect claims

The text explains <Highlight>is shallow, and at low tide the ferry cannot reach the jetty. So</Highlight> the day shifts (**I**) and <Highlight>posts the low-tide times a week ahead</Highlight> (**III**). Checking **every claim**:

- **I (supported)**: Shallow water stops docking at low tide, triggering the full-day delay.
- **II (supported)**: Normally the second return leaves Pulau Lima at 12:00 and reaches Tanjung Rawa <InlineMath math="90" /> minutes later at 13:30, so a <InlineMath math="1" />-hour shift makes arrival **14:30**.
- **III (supported)**: Posting low-tide times a week ahead lets passengers check for shifts in advance.
- **IV (false)**: A return ticket costs the **same** as two one-way tickets, not less.
- **V (false)**: The text never claims the <InlineMath math="90" />-minute crossing limits the ferry to two round trips (one round trip takes <InlineMath math="240" /> minutes).

## Why the other four options fail

- **I and II only** and **I and III only** drop one supported statement (III or II).
- **II, III, and IV only** invents a return-ticket discount in IV.
- **I, II, III, and V only** adds an unstated cause in V.

# Answer

The supported cause-and-effect statements are **I, II, and III only**.
