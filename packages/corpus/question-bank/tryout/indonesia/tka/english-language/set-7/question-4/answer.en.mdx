A cause-and-effect statement is supported only if the text gives both the effect and the link.

- **I is supported**. The harbour "is shallow, and at low tide the ferry cannot reach the jetty. So" the day's timetable moves.

- **III is supported**. The harbour office "posts the low-tide times a week ahead", so a passenger can check them against the planned arrivals.
- IV isn't supported. A return ticket "costs the same as two one-way tickets". The <InlineMath math="7" /> days limit when you travel back, not the price.

The options with IV turn a time limit into a discount. The option with V adds a cause the text never gives.

So the supported statements are I, II and III only.
