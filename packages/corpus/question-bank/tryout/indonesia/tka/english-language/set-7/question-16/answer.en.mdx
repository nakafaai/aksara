# Given

In **Dyeing a cotton cloth with indigo**, an **<InlineMath math="8" />-litre** fresh bath uses **<InlineMath math="50" /> grams per <InlineMath math="2" /> litres**, plus **<InlineMath math="25" /> grams** stirred in **after every <InlineMath math="3" /> cloths before the next cloth goes in**. One round is a **<InlineMath math="5" />-minute dip plus a <InlineMath math="15" />-minute airing** (<InlineMath math="20" /> minutes). Mid blue takes **<InlineMath math="4" /> rounds** and dark blue **<InlineMath math="6" /> rounds**. Bu Sari dyes **two mid blue** then **two dark blue** cloths sequentially.

# Asked

The total dipping-and-airing time and total indigo paste used for the four cloths.

# Solution

Split the work into two clean totals: **count every round** (multiplying by <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes) and **count paste from the fresh bath plus top-ups**. With four cloths, only **one** top-up happens right before the fourth cloth.

> Keep dip-plus-airing together as one <InlineMath math="20" />-minute round, and add the <InlineMath math="25" />-gram boost only when another cloth actually follows cloth <InlineMath math="3" />.

## Calculating total time and total indigo paste

The instructions set <Highlight><InlineMath math="50" /> grams of indigo paste for every <InlineMath math="2" /> litres</Highlight>, <Highlight>A bath needs <InlineMath math="8" /> litres</Highlight>, and <Highlight>After every <InlineMath math="3" /> cloths, stir in another <InlineMath math="25" /> grams</Highlight>. Computing **both totals**:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Rounds} = 4 + 4 + 6 + 6 = 20 \implies \text{Time} = 20 \times (5 + 15) = 400\text{ min} = 6\text{ h } 40\text{ min}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{Paste} = \frac{8}{2} \times 50 + 25 = 200 + 25 = 225\text{ g}" />
</MathContainer>

## Why the other four options fail

- **<InlineMath math="6" /> hours <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes, <InlineMath math="200" /> grams** forgets the <InlineMath math="25" />-gram top-up before cloth four.
- **<InlineMath math="5" /> hours <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes, <InlineMath math="225" /> grams** drops the final <InlineMath math="15" />-minute airing of all four cloths (<InlineMath math="60" /> minutes).
- **<InlineMath math="6" /> hours <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes, <InlineMath math="250" /> grams** adds the top-up twice.
- **<InlineMath math="1" /> hour <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes, <InlineMath math="225" /> grams** counts only the <InlineMath math="5" />-minute dips (<InlineMath math="100" /> minutes).

# Answer

Bu Sari takes **<InlineMath math="6" /> hours <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes, with <InlineMath math="225" /> grams of paste**.
