# Given

In the **Kenari bridge traffic count** recount, Nadia, Farid, and the narrator each counted motorbikes for one hour (6:00 to 9:00). At 7:00, Farid wrote Nadia's **<InlineMath math="640" />** atop his sheet, and Yosef added both sheets so **<InlineMath math="640" /> went in twice**. Two weeks later, Bu Lestari checked the table against the bridge camera, showing hourly totals of **<InlineMath math="640" />**, **<InlineMath math="950" />**, and **<InlineMath math="670" />**.

# Asked

Which of statements I through IV belong in an accurate summary of the account.

# Solution

An accurate summary keeps **what truly happened** and trims out distortions. Even after removing the extra <InlineMath math="640" />, the second hour (<InlineMath math="950" />) still beats the first (<InlineMath math="640" />) and third (<InlineMath math="670" />), and nobody went back to recount traffic.

> Fixing an inflated count does not automatically change which hour ranked first unless another hour actually surpasses the corrected number.

## Checking all four candidate summary statements

The passage notes Nadia's <InlineMath math="640" /> <Highlight>went in twice, and every total after that carried the same mistake</Highlight> while Farid's true count was <InlineMath math="950" />. Checking **statements I through IV**:

- **I (belongs)**: Three students took one-hour turns counting motorbikes across three hours.
- **II (belongs)**: Double-counting Nadia's <InlineMath math="640" /> at handover inflated both the second hour (<InlineMath math="1{,}590" /> instead of <InlineMath math="950" />) and the three-hour total (<InlineMath math="2{,}900" /> instead of <InlineMath math="2{,}260" />).
- **III (wrong)**: Corrected hourly counts are <InlineMath math="640" />, <InlineMath math="950" />, and <InlineMath math="670" />, so 7:00 to 8:00 was **still the busiest hour**.
- **IV (wrong)**: Two weeks later Bu Lestari compared the table with camera records, and no second manual count happened.

## Why the other four options fail

- Options including **III** confuse a wrong count for 7:00 to 8:00 with naming the wrong busiest hour.
- Options including **IV** mistake comparing camera data for repeating the bridge count.

# Answer

An accurate summary should contain **I and II only**.
