# Given

In **The Dark Lighthouse**, the only lighthouse key **hangs on a hook in Nenek's kitchen** because Grandfather was the last keeper. The morning after the storm, Pak Darto blames a fisherman's generator, Arif nods, and **Nenek says nothing**, simply taking the key down, **wiping it on her sleeve, and hanging it back**.

# Asked

Which of statements I through V about Nenek are supported by the story.

# Solution

Judge Nenek strictly by **her own words and quiet actions**, never by Arif's nod or wild guesses about the storm. She keeps the key, cares for it tenderly, and stays silent despite knowing the key never left her kitchen.

> A quiet character's actions carry real meaning, yet you must never invent unseen actions the narrator never describes.

## Testing all five claims about Nenek

The final lines show her exact gesture: <Highlight>Nenek said nothing. She took the key down, wiped it on her sleeve and hung it back on its hook</Highlight>. Checking **statements I through V**:

- **I (supported)**: Paragraph one states the single key hangs on a hook in Grandmother's kitchen.
- **II (false)**: **Arif** nods at Pak Darto, whereas Nenek says nothing.
- **III (supported)**: Wiping the key gently on her sleeve and returning it to its hook shows she cares for it.
- **IV (false)**: The story never states where Nenek was or suggests she lit the tower herself.
- **V (supported)**: She remains silent while hearing a generator theory that clashes with the key never leaving her kitchen.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I and III only** misses the significance of her silence in V.
- **I, II, and III only** confuses Arif's nod with Nenek agreeing aloud.
- **III and V only** drops I, while **I, III, IV, and V only** invents IV without proof.

# Answer

The supported statements about Nenek are **I, III, and V only**.
