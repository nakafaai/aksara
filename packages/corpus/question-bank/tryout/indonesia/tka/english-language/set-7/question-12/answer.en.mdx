# Given

**The Dark Lighthouse** has four paragraphs. Paragraph one introduces the **cut power line, locked door, and kitchen key**, and paragraph two lists **three eerie happenings** explained by **Arif** and **Pak Darto**.

Paragraph three tells of the **storm night when the key stayed on its hook**, and paragraph four gives **Pak Darto's generator theory** while **Nenek stays silent** and wipes the key.

# Asked

Which of statements I through V accurately describe how the story is organized.

# Solution

Scan the story like a four-box storyboard. Notice who actually explains each strange clue (**Arif** and **Pak Darto**, never the narrator) and how **the kitchen key frames both the opening and the silent ending**.

> Structural questions reward checking exact paragraph order and verifying *who* actually speaks or explains each detail.

## Checking the four paragraph structure for I through V

The closing paragraph highlights Nenek's silence: <Highlight>Nenek said nothing. She took the key down, wiped it on her sleeve and hung it back on its hook</Highlight>. Testing **all five structural statements**:

- **I (correct)**: Paragraph one sets up the locked door and key before any strange summer event appears in paragraph two.
- **II (wrong)**: Arif explains the moving light and wet footprints, and Pak Darto explains the moaning, so the narrator explains none of them.
- **III (wrong)**: Nenek stays completely silent and never explains how the tower lit up.
- **IV (correct)**: The key appears on its hook in paragraph one and returns in the final sentence.
- **V (correct)**: Paragraph three reveals the key never left the kitchen **before** Pak Darto offers his generator theory in paragraph four.

## Why the other four options fail

- Options with **II** credit the narrator with explanations spoken by Arif and Pak Darto.
- **I and IV only** misses V, while **IV and V only** misses the setup order in I.

# Answer

The accurate descriptions of the story's organization are **I, IV, and V only**.
