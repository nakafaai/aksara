A narrative is built from **orientation, complication, and resolution**. The complication is the missing bike-lock key when Sari has to leave early. The resolution comes when that problem is solved: Sari **finds the key in the fridge** next to the eggs and **rides her bike to school** after all.

- Searching her bag and pockets is part of the complication, while the problem is still open.
- Dito's question comes during the complication, and Sari doesn't believe him yet.
- Asking for a ride is a stopgap before the key turns up.
- Going to bed angry shows the problem is still going on.
