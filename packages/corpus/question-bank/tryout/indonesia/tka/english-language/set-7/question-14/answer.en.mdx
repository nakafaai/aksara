# Given

In **The Dark Lighthouse**, Pak Darto already explained the windy moaning as **a broken vent pipe**, Arif admitted leaving wet footprints from **swimming off the rocks at night**, the tower's **only door is locked**, and both **Nenek and the narrator stayed quiet** the morning after the storm.

# Asked

Which of statements I through V are reasonable predictions grounded in the story.

# Solution

A reasonable prediction extends **patterns and physical facts the story already established**, rather than guessing sudden character reversals. Pak Darto's vent explanation, Arif's night swims, and the locked door all follow straight from the text.

> Strong predictions build on established character habits and physical rules instead of imagining unprompted changes of heart.

## Testing all five predictions against story patterns

The text gives three firm patterns: <Highlight>the wind was blowing through a broken vent pipe</Highlight>, Arif's nighttime swims, and <Highlight>its only door is locked</Highlight>. Checking **predictions I through V**:

- **I (reasonable)**: Pak Darto already attributes windy moaning to the broken vent pipe.
- **II (reasonable)**: Wet steps previously came from Arif's night swimming, making it a plausible repeat cause.
- **III (unreasonable)**: Nenek treasures and cares for Grandfather's key with no hint of giving it away.
- **IV (unreasonable)**: The narrator stayed silent all morning with zero sign of confronting Pak Darto.
- **V (reasonable)**: Because the single door is locked, carrying a generator inside requires getting through it first.

## Why the other four options fail

- **I and V only** and **II and V only** omit one established pattern (II or I).
- Options with **III** or **IV** predict abrupt actions contradicted by Nenek's attachment and the narrator's silence.

# Answer

The reasonable predictions are **I, II, and V only**.
