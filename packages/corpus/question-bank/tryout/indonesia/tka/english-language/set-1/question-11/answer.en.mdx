> Tracing where the fault sits along the signal chain separates effects caused by the wrong socket from things wired upstream of it.

- **No sound reached the classroom speakers** followed directly because paragraph three notes the cable sat <Highlight>into the socket labelled AUX <InlineMath math="2" /> instead of AUX <InlineMath math="1" /></Highlight> while <Highlight>AUX <InlineMath math="2" /> was turned down to zero</Highlight>.
