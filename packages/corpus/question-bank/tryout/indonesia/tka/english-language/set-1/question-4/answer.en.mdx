The story mixes real events with a few that **only magic could explain**, so check each event against how the real world works.

- The moving pencil line: could not happen. The story says *Nobody had erased it* and *The line itself had moved, smudge and all*. A pencil line can't move by itself.
- The talking heron: could not happen. A heron can call, but it can't say *"Sih, you're late for school"* or know a family nickname.
- Remembering a house only from its spot: could happen. This one sounds magical, but it isn't. Places really do bring back memories that a table at home doesn't, and the story describes it as a limit of Grandmother's memory, with nothing impossible in it.
- The knee-high shoot by morning: could not happen. A seed needs days to sprout, and no seedling grows that tall in one night.

So three events need magic, and only Grandmother's place-bound memory could happen in real life.
