Distinguish **measured outcomes** from **unequal burdens** using the roles assigned in the passage.

| Item | Classification | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Late arrivals | Outcome to measure | Named among the indicators used to evaluate the trial. |
| Transport delays | Outcome to measure | Also listed as an outcome to compare with the previous term. |
| Difficulty collecting a younger sibling | Potential unequal effect | Shows how the schedule can disadvantage a particular group. |
