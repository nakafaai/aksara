# Given

Four candidate summary statements cover the **cause, blind spot, aftermath, and lasting fix** of the Radio Sekar incident.

# Asked

Which numbered statements belong in an accurate summary of the Radio Sekar account.

# Solution

Imagine retelling the Radio Sekar story to a friend in three crisp sentences. A strong summary must stay **factually true** and capture the three pillars of the account: **what caused the silence**, **why studio checks missed it**, and **how the club fixed it**.

> Every summary statement must be both true to the text and central to explaining the whole story from problem to solution.

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence and location in the text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Belongs | Captures the root cause in paragraph three: the cable sat in AUX <InlineMath math="2" /> and <Highlight>AUX <InlineMath math="2" /> was turned down to zero</Highlight>. |
| II | Belongs | Captures the blind spot from paragraphs one and two: studio checks listened <Highlight>before the signal left the studio for the amplifier</Highlight>, so <Highlight>In the studio we noticed nothing</Highlight>. |
| III | Excluded | Factually false because paragraph five states <Highlight>On Wednesday we broadcast again</Highlight>, live as before. |
| IV | Belongs | Captures the lasting fix in paragraph five: Dimas confirms from Class 11B <Highlight>before Rina said her first word</Highlight> and <Highlight>We now begin every broadcast that way</Highlight>. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Options including III** introduce a false claim that the club quit live broadcasting.
- **Options dropping I, II, or IV** leave out one of the account's three core pillars: cause, hidden studio blind spot, or permanent fix.

# Answer

The statements belonging in an accurate summary are **I, II, and IV only**.
