A summary statement must be **both true and important to the whole account**. Check each one.

- I belongs. The third paragraph gives the cause: the cable went into AUX 2, and *AUX 2 was turned down to zero*.
- II belongs. The headphones and the recorder took their sound *before the signal left the studio for the amplifier*, which is why, in the writer's words, *In the studio we noticed nothing*. The text leaves you to join these, but it's the reason the failure lasted four minutes.
- III doesn't belong, because it's false. *On Wednesday we broadcast again*, live.
- IV belongs. Dimas confirms from Class 11B *before Rina said her first word*, and *We now begin every broadcast that way*.

So an accurate summary contains I, II and IV. Dropping II loses why the fault stayed hidden, dropping I or IV loses the cause or the fix, and adding III adds something false.
