- Ms Hartono: *"A skill you use once a week fades between lessons. Ten minutes a day keeps it."*
- Pak Darmawan: *"My son crams for three hours on Sunday night and has forgotten half of it by Wednesday."* and *"I would rather he did a little each evening."*

- I: both agree. She says daily minutes keep a skill, and he prefers *a little each evening* to a three-hour cram.
- II: neither says it. It's the writer's proposal, and Pak Darmawan's concern is *not whether their weekend practice should be graded*.
- III: both agree. Her skill *fades between lessons*, and his son *has forgotten half of it by Wednesday*.
- IV: only Ms Hartono says ten minutes. Pak Darmawan never gives a number.

So they agree on I and III. An option with I alone misses the shared point about forgetting, and options with II or IV give one person's view, or the writer's, to both.
