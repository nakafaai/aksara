# Given

The story's ages and dates sit in **two separate paragraphs**: paragraph one records when Grandmother's family moved out and when the dam gates closed, while paragraph five gives her age when she counted the steps.

# Asked

Which statement agrees with every date and age stated in the story.

# Solution

Picture lining up three milestone cards on a single straight timeline so no two events get swapped. First pull the exact two clues from the passage.

- Paragraph one states her family moved out <Highlight>in <InlineMath math="1987" />, when she was <InlineMath math="12" /></Highlight>, and the dam gates closed <InlineMath math="2" /> years later before the valley filled during a single rainy season.
- Paragraph five explains that the steps were counted <Highlight>by a girl of <InlineMath math="7" />, in the year she started school</Highlight>.

> Placing every age and year gap onto one timeline immediately exposes any swap between the move, the school year, and the flooding.

Now calculate her age when the gates closed and the gap between starting school and moving out:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
12 + 2 &= 14 \\
12 - 7 &= 5
\end{aligned}" />

Because she turned <InlineMath math="14" /> when the gates closed and the valley filled afterward, she was **at least <InlineMath math="14" />**. Counting the steps at age <InlineMath math="7" /> happened **about <InlineMath math="5" /> years before** her family moved at age <InlineMath math="12" />.

## Why the other options fail

- **Grandmother was <InlineMath math="12" /> when the valley filled** confuses her moving age with her age <InlineMath math="2" /> years later after the dam closed.
- **Steps were counted about two years before the move** swaps the <InlineMath math="2" />-year gap after the move for the <InlineMath math="5" />-year gap before it.
- **She kept the seed since starting school** fails because paragraph six states she carried it since <InlineMath math="1987" />, the relocation year.
- **She started school the same year the dam closed** ignores that starting school was <InlineMath math="5" /> years before the move while the dam closed <InlineMath math="2" /> years after it, a gap of <InlineMath math="7" /> years.

# Answer

The statement that fits every date and age is that Grandmother was **at least <InlineMath math="14" />** when the valley filled, and the steps had been counted **about five years before her family moved**.
