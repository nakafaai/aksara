A sentence fits only if it **follows from the third paragraph and clashes with nothing else in the text**. Test each against the facts about glasswort.

- I fits. Glasswort is flooded *only by the spring tides*, and its stems *swell after each flooding*. So they're plumpest just after a spring tide.
- II doesn't fit. *Glasswort does not push salt out*. The glittering crystals belong to the low-marsh grass, which gets rid of salt through its leaves.
- III fits. Glasswort *holds extra water* in its stems, and they *shrivel in long dry spells*. Between spring tides, the stored water is all it has.
- IV doesn't fit. The middle marsh floods *about twice a month*, not twice a day. Twice a day is the low marsh.

So the sentences that fit are I and III. Any option with II gives glasswort another plant's habit, and any option with IV gives it another zone's tides.
