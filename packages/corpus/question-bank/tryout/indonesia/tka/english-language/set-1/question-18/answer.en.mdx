The first movement has a known competing explanation: someone used water during the waiting period. A repeat under the original no-use conditions removes that explanation from the new observation. Its purpose is **To separate evidence of ongoing flow from the one known accidental use**. Even a second movement would show only that water is flowing somewhere; it would not identify a particular pipe.
