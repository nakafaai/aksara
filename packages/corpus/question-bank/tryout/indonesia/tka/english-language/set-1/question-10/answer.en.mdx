# Given

On Monday morning the gauge water stood **<InlineMath math="6\text{ mm}" /> above zero** and **was not emptied**. On Tuesday morning it stood **<InlineMath math="15\text{ mm}" /> above zero**, and the table shows <InlineMath math="1\text{ mm}" /> of rain produces <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" /> of water height.

# Asked

Which conclusion is supported by the text, the calibration table, and the Monday condition.

# Solution

Think of a piggy bank you forgot to empty on Monday. To find what arrived between Monday and Tuesday, **subtract Monday's leftover water first**, then convert the extra height into rainfall using the table's ratio.

> Paragraph five warns that <Highlight>Each reading is the rain that fell since the last emptying</Highlight> and <Highlight>It says nothing about how hard the rain fell at any moment</Highlight>.

## Computing the rainfall between Monday and Tuesday

Since the gauge stayed unemptied on Monday, subtract <InlineMath math="6\text{ mm}" /> from <InlineMath math="15\text{ mm}" /> to isolate the new rise, then halve it because <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" /> of height represents <InlineMath math="1\text{ mm}" /> of rain:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\Delta h &= 15\text{ mm} - 6\text{ mm} = 9\text{ mm} \\
\text{Rain} &= 9\text{ mm} \div 2 = 4.5\text{ mm}
\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, exactly **<InlineMath math="4.5\text{ mm}" /> of rain** fell between Monday's and Tuesday's readings.

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="7.5\text{ mm}" />** halves the full <InlineMath math="15\text{ mm}" /> and forgets Monday's <InlineMath math="6\text{ mm}" /> was never poured out.
- **<InlineMath math="9\text{ mm}" />** reports the added height inside the bottle without dividing by <InlineMath math="2" />.
- **Rain falling harder on Monday night** is unsupported because a gauge measures total depth, never rainfall intensity.
- **Tuesday's reading staying the same if emptied on Monday** fails because emptying to zero would have left Tuesday's water at <InlineMath math="9\text{ mm}" />, not <InlineMath math="15\text{ mm}" />.

# Answer

The supported conclusion is that **<InlineMath math="4.5\text{ mm}" /> of rain** fell between Monday's and Tuesday's readings.
