# Given

The Radio Sekar account jumps across **Friday afternoon, Saturday, Monday morning, and Wednesday**, so every claim must be anchored to its actual day and clock time.

# Asked

Which numbered statements about the timing of events in the account are correct.

# Solution

Think of sorting shuffled snapshots onto a single calendar line before judging them: **Friday final rehearsal**, **Saturday painting and cable swap**, **Monday <InlineMath math="6\text{:}40" /> mic test**, **<InlineMath math="7\text{:}02" /> silence**, and **<InlineMath math="7\text{:}06" /> Pak Hadi's knock**.

> When a narrative uses flashbacks, verify every timing claim against the real calendar and clock rather than paragraph order.

## Checking each timing statement

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence and location in the text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Correct | Paragraph three puts the cable move <Highlight>On Saturday</Highlight>, while paragraph four puts Rina's mic check <Highlight>On Monday at <InlineMath math="6\text{:}40" /></Highlight>, long before anyone fixed the cable. |
| II | Incorrect | Paragraph four states <Highlight>Our final rehearsal had taken place on Friday afternoon, the day before the painting</Highlight>, placing it before painting. |
| III | Correct | Paragraph two identifies the four recorded minutes as the silent broadcast ending at the knock, and paragraph five states the writer found the cable only <Highlight>Once Pak Hadi's word sank in</Highlight>. |
| IV | Incorrect | Paragraph one places the silence <Highlight>At two minutes past seven</Highlight> and the knock <Highlight>At <InlineMath math="7\text{:}06" /></Highlight>, four minutes later rather than under a minute. |

## Why the other options fail

- **Any option including II** puts Friday's final rehearsal after Saturday's painting.
- **Any option including IV** shrinks a four-minute gap (<InlineMath math="7\text{:}02" /> to <InlineMath math="7\text{:}06" />) into less than one minute.
- **I only** leaves out statement III, which clearly precedes the writer finding the cable.

# Answer

The correct timing statements are **I and III only**.
