Before the first meter reading, select the actions that establish a controlled test without interrupting essential use:

- **Choose a quiet period.**
- **Check whether essential equipment uses water.**

The irrigation timer should be checked so it will remain off during the test. Starting irrigation before the reading would introduce water use and undermine the quiet period. Breaking a wall is also outside the purpose and safety limits of this meter check.
