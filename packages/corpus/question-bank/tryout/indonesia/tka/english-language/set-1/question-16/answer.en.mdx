Each comparison needs **facts from two different paragraphs**, the second about the high marsh and the fourth about the low marsh.

- I is supported. The sea reaches the high marsh *only during the highest tides of the year or in storms*, while *the tide covers* the low marsh *twice every day*.
- II is supported. Water left in the high marsh's hollows *evaporates, leaving its salt behind*, so in the dry months the salt pans *can be saltier than the sea itself*.
- III is not supported. The low-marsh mud smells of rotten eggs, *a smell that forms only in mud with no air in it*. Soil with no air can't hold more air than anything.
- IV is supported, but you have to join two plants. Sea lavender in the high marsh *pushes it out again through tiny glands on its leaves*, and the low-marsh grass, *Like sea lavender, it gets rid of salt through glands on its leaves*.

So the supported statements are I, II and IV. An option without IV misses the plant that both marshes share, and any option with III gets the air backwards.
