Movement during the controlled repeat supports ongoing flow even though the visible fixtures look dry. The meter still cannot identify the location. The supported response is **Continuous flow is still suspected, so keep the records and contact the responsible manager or a qualified plumber.** Opening a wall would claim a location the test has not established, while deleting the records would remove evidence needed for the next inspection.
