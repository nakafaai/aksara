A detail like this usually **supports the sentence around it**. Read the fourth paragraph as a chain.

- First comes the claim: the soil *is so waterlogged that air cannot get in*.
- Then the smell: a stick *comes out smelling of rotten eggs*. That's something a visitor can notice for themselves, a sign of soil without air.
- Then the consequence: the grass *survives by carrying air down to its roots through hollow channels in its stems*.

So the smell backs up the claim that the soil has no air, and that claim is why the grass needs its air channels.
