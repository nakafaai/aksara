Each statement joins **two details**, and the wrong ones join them a little off. Check every link.

- The swelling plant keeps salt inside: stated. Glasswort *stores salt in its fleshy stems*, *which is why the stems swell after each flooding*. Both details belong to glasswort.
- The mudflat and the low marsh under water for the same time: not stated. The tide covers the mudflat *twice a day, as it does the low marsh, but for longer*. Same number of times, but not the same length of time.
- Glasswort staying swollen in dry spells: not stated. Its stems *shrivel in long dry spells*.
- Sea lavender and the grass removing salt the same way: stated. *Like sea lavender, it gets rid of salt through glands on its leaves*.
- Salt pans on ground flooded twice a day: not stated. Salt pans are in the high marsh, which the sea reaches *only during the highest tides of the year or in storms*. They form because the water *evaporates*, which needs rare flooding.

So the text states only that the swelling plant keeps salt inside itself and that sea lavender and the low-marsh grass get rid of salt in the same way.
