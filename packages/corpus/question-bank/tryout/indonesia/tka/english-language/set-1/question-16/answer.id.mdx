Teks menunjukkan bahwa **the first three steps prevent planned or essential water use from confusing the test**. Karena itu, jawaban yang tepat adalah:

- **Choose a quiet period.**
- **Check whether essential equipment uses water.**
- **Make sure irrigation will not start.**
