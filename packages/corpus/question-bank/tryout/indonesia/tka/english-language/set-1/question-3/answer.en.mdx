Each statement needs **its own evidence and its own place in time**, so check them one by one.

- I is supported. When Laras mentions the map, Grandmother *turned up the radio* and said *"There is nothing to map. It is all water."*
- II is supported. The morning after Laras's questions about the well and the path, Grandmother *asked for the pencil and drew the tree herself*. The drought that shows the minaret comes later, *In August*.
- III is not supported. It gets the order wrong. She drew the tamarind long before August, so her help did not wait for the heron.
- IV is not supported. The last paragraph says she *could name a house only from the spot where it had stood*. That's a limit on where she can remember, not a loss of memory, and on the boat she *named the houses that had stood below*.

So the true statements are I and II. Any option that adds III or IV is wrong, and an option with I alone leaves out the change itself.
