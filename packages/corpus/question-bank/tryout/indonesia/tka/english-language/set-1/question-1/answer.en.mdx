# Given

The story about Laras's map of Karangsari spans **six connected paragraphs** covering Grandmother Warsih's relocation, the tamarind tree, the mosque, and the boat trip across Sembada Reservoir.

# Asked

Which statements accurately combine facts from two different paragraphs of the story.

# Solution

Think of matching **two puzzle pieces** taken from different pages of a storybook. Two pieces fit together only when both pieces point to the exact same object, place, and order of events without clashing.

> A cross-paragraph link holds only when both quoted details match the same object and respect the story's timeline.

## Checking the correct statements

- **The tree bending the river road and the planted seed** is correct. Paragraph three says the river road curved around <Highlight>the tamarind</Highlight>, and paragraph six says Grandmother planted <Highlight>a tamarind seed</Highlight>, so both refer to the exact same kind of tree.
- **The August minaret and the mosque first drawn on the wrong side** is correct. Paragraph four names <Highlight>the mosque's minaret</Highlight>, while paragraph two reports that Laras first drew it <Highlight>west of the school</Highlight> before Grandmother pointed east, confirming west was the wrong side.

## Why the other options fail

- **Grandmother named every missing house from the boat** fails because paragraph six, set after the boat ride, still notes <Highlight>there were still <InlineMath math="11" /> houses without names</Highlight>.
- **Grandmother kept the seed since the valley filled** fails because paragraph six says she carried it since <InlineMath math="1987" /> (the relocation year), whereas paragraph one states the dam gates closed <InlineMath math="2" /> years later in <InlineMath math="1989" />.
- **Laras moved the mosque only after seeing the minaret from the boat** fails because paragraph two already states <Highlight>Laras rubbed it out and moved it</Highlight> right after Grandmother pointed east, long before August.

# Answer

The two correct statements are that **the tree bending the river road was the same kind as the seed Grandmother planted, and that the minaret rising in August belonged to the mosque Laras first drew on the wrong side of the school**.
