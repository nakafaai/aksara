Each place takes **two steps**: first find which zone it belongs to, then read how often that zone floods.

- Where glasswort grows: the middle marsh, *flooded only by the spring tides that come about twice a month*. Twice a month is far less than once a day, so it's flooded less than once a day.
- Where the tall, stiff grass grows: the low marsh, which *the tide covers twice every day*. That's at least once a day.
- The bare mud where the boardwalk ends: the mudflat, which the tide *covers twice a day*. That's at least once a day too.
- Around the salt pans: that's where sea lavender grows, in the high marsh, which the sea reaches *only during the highest tides of the year or in storms*. That's less than once a day.

So the low marsh and the mudflat are flooded at least once a day, while the glasswort ground and the edges of the salt pans are flooded less than once a day.
