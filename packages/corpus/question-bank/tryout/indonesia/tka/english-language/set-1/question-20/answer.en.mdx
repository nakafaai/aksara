The plumber needs evidence that can be checked during the follow-up inspection. The most useful record is the **readings, times, and visible water signs**.

Two readings establish whether the meter changed, and their times establish the observation interval. Visible water signs can guide a later inspection without pretending to locate a hidden pipe. Room colors, electricity records, guesses, and an unsupported assurance do not provide that evidence.
