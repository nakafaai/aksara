# Sheets for each module

The question asks for the total printing and binding cost of <InlineMath math="30" /> modules in one order. The price per sheet depends on the number of **sheets**, not pages. So first you turn pages into sheets.

One sheet holds <InlineMath math="2" /> pages, and every module uses its own sheets. A module with an odd number of pages still needs a whole sheet for its last page, so **you divide by <InlineMath math="2" /> and round up for each module**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
27\text{ pages}&:\ \frac{27}{2}=13.5\ \Rightarrow\ 14\text{ sheets} \\
41\text{ pages}&:\ \frac{41}{2}=20.5\ \Rightarrow\ 21\text{ sheets}
\end{aligned}" />

Now add up the sheets of every module in the order:

<BlockMath math="18\cdot14+12\cdot21=252+252=504\text{ sheets}" />

# The price per sheet and the total

The order has <InlineMath math="504" /> sheets, which is <InlineMath math="500" /> or more. By the table, **every** sheet in the order costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}300" />, not only the sheets after the <InlineMath math="499" />th one. Binding is charged per module, and there are <InlineMath math="18+12=30" /> modules:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{printing}&=504\cdot300=151{,}200 \\
\text{binding}&=30\cdot4{,}000=120{,}000 \\
\text{total}&=151{,}200+120{,}000=271{,}200
\end{aligned}" />

Compare this with counting the pages of all modules together: <InlineMath math="\frac{18\cdot27+12\cdot41}{2}=\frac{978}{2}=489" /> sheets. That number lands in the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}400" /> tier. The <InlineMath math="15" /> extra sheets come from the empty back side of each module's last sheet, and they're exactly what pushes the order into the cheaper tier.

So the total cost of the order is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}271{,}200" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}315{,}600" /> uses <InlineMath math="489" /> sheets at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}400" />. Pages from different modules can't share a sheet, so **the rounding has to happen per module**.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}321{,}600" /> prices the <InlineMath math="252" /> sheets of each module type like two separate orders, each at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}400" />. The tier depends on the number of sheets in one order.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}341{,}000" /> treats the tiers like steps: the first <InlineMath math="199" /> sheets at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500" />, the next <InlineMath math="300" /> at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}400" />, the rest at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}300" />. The rule is different: one price applies to all sheets.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}413{,}400" /> counts one page as one sheet and gets <InlineMath math="978" /> sheets. The modules are printed on both sides, so one sheet holds two pages.
