There are two transactions at different rates. When Sinta **buys** ringgit she pays the selling rate, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}500" />. When she **sells** what is left she gets the buying rate, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}400" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{ringgit bought}&=\frac{7{,}000{,}000}{3{,}500}=2{,}000 \\
\text{ringgit left}&=2{,}000-1{,}450=550 \\
\text{rupiah received}&=550\cdot3{,}400=1{,}870{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

So Sinta receives <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}870{,}000" />.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}897{,}500" /> sells the leftover ringgit at the average rate, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}450" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}925{,}000" /> sells the leftover ringgit at the selling rate, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}500" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}070{,}000" /> subtracts <InlineMath math="1{,}450\cdot3{,}400" /> from the starting money, which mixes up the two rates.
