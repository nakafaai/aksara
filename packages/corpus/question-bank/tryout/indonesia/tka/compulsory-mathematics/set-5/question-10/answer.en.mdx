# The distance from the corner

Work out the volume of the pyramid <InlineMath math="B.ACF" /> in two ways. With base <InlineMath math="ABC" /> and height <InlineMath math="BF" />, the volume is easy. With base <InlineMath math="ACF" />, the height is the distance <InlineMath math="d_B" /> you want. The triangle <InlineMath math="ACF" /> is equilateral, and its sides are face diagonals of length <InlineMath math="6\sqrt2" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
V&=\tfrac13\cdot\tfrac12\cdot6\cdot6\cdot6=36 \\
\text{area }ACF&=\tfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\left(6\sqrt2\right)^2=\tfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot72=18\sqrt3 \\
d_B&=\frac{3V}{\text{area }ACF}=\frac{108}{18\sqrt3}=\frac{6}{\sqrt3}=2\sqrt3
