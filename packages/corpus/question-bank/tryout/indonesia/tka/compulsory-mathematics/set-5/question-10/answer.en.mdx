**An exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two interior angles that are not next to it**. The exterior angle at <InlineMath math="C" /> is not next to <InlineMath math="\angle A" /> and <InlineMath math="\angle B" />.

The interior angle at <InlineMath math="C" /> sits next to the exterior angle, so <InlineMath math="\angle C=180^\circ-125^\circ=55^\circ" />. Check: <InlineMath math="58^\circ+67^\circ+55^\circ=180^\circ" />.

So the largest angle of triangle <InlineMath math="ABC" /> is <InlineMath math="67^\circ" />.

- <InlineMath math="24^\circ" /> is the value of <InlineMath math="x" />, not an angle.

- <InlineMath math="58^\circ" /> is <InlineMath math="\angle A" />.
