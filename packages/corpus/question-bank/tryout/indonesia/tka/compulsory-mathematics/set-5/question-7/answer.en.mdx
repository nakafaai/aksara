# Building the composition

The question asks for the sum of **all** possible values of <InlineMath math="f(1)" />. The word all is a hint that more than one function <InlineMath math="f" /> might fit.

Put <InlineMath math="f(x)" /> into <InlineMath math="f" /> itself. Every <InlineMath math="x" /> in the formula <InlineMath math="ax+b" /> is replaced by <InlineMath math="ax+b" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(f\circ f)(x)&=a(ax+b)+b \\
&=a^2x+ab+b
\end{aligned}" />

This has to equal <InlineMath math="4x+9" /> for every <InlineMath math="x" />, so the coefficients of <InlineMath math="x" /> and the constants must match:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
a^2&=4 \\
ab+b=b(a+1)&=9
\end{aligned}" />

# Two possible functions

The equation <InlineMath math="a^2=4" /> has **two solutions**, <InlineMath math="a=2" /> and <InlineMath math="a=-2" />. You have to find <InlineMath math="b" /> again for each one:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
a=2&:\ 3b=9\ \Rightarrow\ b=3,\quad f(x)=2x+3,\quad f(1)=5 \\
a=-2&:\ -b=9\ \Rightarrow\ b=-9,\quad f(x)=-2x-9,\quad f(1)=-11
\end{aligned}" />

Check both. <InlineMath math="2(2x+3)+3=4x+9" /> and <InlineMath math="-2(-2x-9)-9=4x+18-9=4x+9" />. Both functions satisfy the condition.

<BlockMath math="5+(-11)=-6" />

So the sum of all possible values of <InlineMath math="f(1)" /> is <InlineMath math="-6" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="5" /> only uses <InlineMath math="a=2" />. The root <InlineMath math="a=-2" /> **also satisfies** <InlineMath math="a^2=4" /> and gives a valid function.
- <InlineMath math="-11" /> only uses <InlineMath math="a=-2" /> and drops <InlineMath math="a=2" />.
- <InlineMath math="9" /> writes the composition as <InlineMath math="a^2x+2b" />, forgetting that the inner <InlineMath math="b" /> is also multiplied by <InlineMath math="a" />. That gives <InlineMath math="b=\frac92" /> for both functions.
- <InlineMath math="12" /> makes a sign slip in the case <InlineMath math="a=-2" />: from <InlineMath math="-2b+b=9" /> it gets <InlineMath math="b=9" />. The function <InlineMath math="-2x+9" /> gives <InlineMath math="f(f(x))=4x-9" />, not <InlineMath math="4x+9" />.
