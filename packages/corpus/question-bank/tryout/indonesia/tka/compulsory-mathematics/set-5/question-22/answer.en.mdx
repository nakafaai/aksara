# A table of member counts

All three statements talk about **a share of a club's members**, but the percentages in the table are shares of a grade's students. The bases are different, so first turn the percentage table into a table of numbers of students. Each cell is the percentage times the number of students in that grade, for example <InlineMath math="35\%\cdot160=56" />.

| XI | <InlineMath math="36" /> | <InlineMath math="36" /> | <InlineMath math="24" /> | <InlineMath math="24" /> |
| XII | <InlineMath math="28" /> | <InlineMath math="20" /> | <InlineMath math="12" /> | <InlineMath math="20" /> |

Check: the four clubs add up to <InlineMath math="104+112+84+60=360" />, the number of students in the school.

# Judging each statement

Statement 1. **True**. Futsal has <InlineMath math="112" /> members, and <InlineMath math="56" /> of them are in grade X. Since <InlineMath math="\frac{56}{112}=\frac12" />, exactly half of the Futsal members come from grade X. The <InlineMath math="35\%" /> in the table doesn't contradict this, because that <InlineMath math="35\%" /> is a share of the grade X students, not a share of the Futsal members.

Statement 2. **False**. Robotik has <InlineMath math="104" /> members, and <InlineMath math="28" /> of them are in grade XII:

<BlockMath math="\frac{28}{104}\cdot100\%\approx26.9\%<30\%" />

The trap is the <InlineMath math="35\%" /> in the table. It's a share of the <InlineMath math="80" /> grade XII students, not of the <InlineMath math="104" /> Robotik members.

Statement 3. **False**. Jurnalistik has <InlineMath math="20" /> members from grade XII and <InlineMath math="24" /> from grade XI. The difference is <InlineMath math="4" />. The phrase "fewer than the number of members from grade XI" makes grade XI the base of the comparison:

<BlockMath math="\frac{24-20}{24}\cdot100\%\approx16.7\%" />

You only get <InlineMath math="20\%" /> if you divide the difference by <InlineMath math="20" />, which is the wrong base. So statement 1 is true, and statements 2 and 3 are false.
