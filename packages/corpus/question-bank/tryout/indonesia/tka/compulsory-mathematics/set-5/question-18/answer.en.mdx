The glass area is **the rectangle plus the semicircle**. The semicircle's radius is half the window's width, <InlineMath math="0.7\text{ m}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L_{\text{rectangle}}&=1.4\cdot1=1.4\text{ m}^2 \\
L_{\text{semicircle}}&=\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac{22}{7}\cdot0.7^2=0.77\text{ m}^2 \\
L&=1.4+0.77=2.17\text{ m}^2 \\
\text{cost}&=2.17\cdot350{,}000=759{,}500
\end{aligned}" />

So the glass for the window costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}759{,}500" />.
