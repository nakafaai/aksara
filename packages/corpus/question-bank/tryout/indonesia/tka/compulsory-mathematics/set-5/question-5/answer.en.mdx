Let the charged time be <InlineMath math="n" /> hours, where <InlineMath math="n" /> is a positive whole number. Written per hour, tariff A charges <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> for **the first hour** and <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" /> for **each of the other <InlineMath math="n-1" /> hours**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A(n)&=5{,}000+3{,}000(n-1)=3{,}000n+2{,}000 \\
B(n)&=4{,}000n \\
A(n)<B(n)&\iff3{,}000n+2{,}000<4{,}000n\iff n>2
\end{aligned}" />

So tariff A is cheaper exactly when <InlineMath math="n\ge3" />. Since a started hour counts as a full hour, <InlineMath math="n\ge3" /> happens exactly when the parking time is **more than <InlineMath math="2" /> hours**. At exactly <InlineMath math="2" /> hours both cost <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}8{,}000" />, and for at most <InlineMath math="1" /> hour tariff B is cheaper.

So tariff A is cheaper exactly for parking times of more than <InlineMath math="2" /> hours.

- Tariff A isn't always cheaper: <InlineMath math="1" /> hour costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> at A and <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000" /> at B.
- For <InlineMath math="3" /> hours, A charges <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}11{,}000" /> and B charges <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}12{,}000" />, so they aren't equal.
- After <InlineMath math="2" /> hours it is A that is cheaper, not B.
- At exactly <InlineMath math="2" /> hours the tariffs are equal, so **at least <InlineMath math="2" /> hours** makes the statement false.
