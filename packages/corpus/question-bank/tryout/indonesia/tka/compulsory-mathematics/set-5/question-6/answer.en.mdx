Replace every <InlineMath math="x" /> in the rule for <InlineMath math="f" /> with <InlineMath math="a+1" />, then with <InlineMath math="a-1" />. **Put each input in brackets first** so the minus signs come along.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
f(a+1)&=(a+1)^2-2(a+1)=a^2+2a+1-2a-2=a^2-1 \\
f(a-1)&=(a-1)^2-2(a-1)=a^2-2a+1-2a+2=a^2-4a+3 \\
f(a+1)-f(a-1)&=(a^2-1)-(a^2-4a+3)=4a-4
\end{aligned}" />

Check with <InlineMath math="a=2" />: <InlineMath math="f(3)-f(1)=3-(-1)=4" />, and <InlineMath math="4\cdot2-4=4" />.

So <InlineMath math="f(a+1)-f(a-1)=4a-4" />.

- <InlineMath math="4a" /> only takes the difference of the two squares and forgets the <InlineMath math="-2x" /> term.
- <InlineMath math="4a+4" /> gets a sign wrong when subtracting <InlineMath math="-2(a+1)" /> and <InlineMath math="-2(a-1)" />.
- <InlineMath math="2a-4" /> takes <InlineMath math="(a+1)^2-(a-1)^2" /> as <InlineMath math="2a" />, but it is <InlineMath math="4a" />.
- <InlineMath math="-4" /> assumes the two squares cancel completely.
