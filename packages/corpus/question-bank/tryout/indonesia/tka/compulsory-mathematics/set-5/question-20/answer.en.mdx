Draw the height from the top of the frame to the middle of the base. It splits the frame into two right triangles with a **horizontal side of <InlineMath math="4\text{ m}" />**, half the base. In each triangle, <InlineMath math="\cos\alpha" /> is the horizontal side divided by the slanted side.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\cos\alpha=\frac{4}{s}=\frac45\ &\Rightarrow\ s=5\text{ m} \\
L&=2\cdot(5\cdot10)=100\text{ m}^2
\end{aligned}" />

Check: the frame's height is <InlineMath math="\sqrt{5^2-4^2}=3\text{ m}" />, so the right triangle has sides <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />.

So the whole roof covers <InlineMath math="100\text{ m}^2" />.
