Each turn of the cable uses **a length equal to the spool's circumference**. The number of turns is the cable's length divided by the circumference, in the same unit.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
C&=\pi d=\frac{22}{7}\cdot14=44\text{ cm} \\
n&=\frac{2{,}200}{44}=50
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="50\cdot44=2{,}200\text{ cm}=22\text{ m}" />.

So the cable makes <InlineMath math="50" /> turns.

- <InlineMath math="5" /> turns converts <InlineMath math="22\text{ m}" /> into <InlineMath math="220\text{ cm}" />, although <InlineMath math="1\text{ m}=100\text{ cm}" />.
- <InlineMath math="25" /> turns uses <InlineMath math="2\pi d=88\text{ cm}" /> as the circumference.
- <InlineMath math="100" /> turns puts the radius <InlineMath math="7\text{ cm}" /> into <InlineMath math="\pi d" />, so the circumference becomes <InlineMath math="22\text{ cm}" />.
- <InlineMath math="500" /> turns converts <InlineMath math="22\text{ m}" /> into <InlineMath math="22{,}000\text{ cm}" />.
