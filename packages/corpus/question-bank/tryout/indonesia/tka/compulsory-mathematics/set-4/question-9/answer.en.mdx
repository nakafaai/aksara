The rebound heights form a **geometric sequence**. The first rebound is <InlineMath math="\frac34" /> of the <InlineMath math="160\text{ cm}" /> fall, and each later rebound is <InlineMath math="\frac34" /> of the rebound before it. So the rebound after the k-th touch has height <InlineMath math="r_k=160\left(\frac34\right)^k" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
r_1&=120 \\
r_2&=90 \\
r_3&=\frac{135}{2}=67.5 \\
r_4&=\frac{405}{8}=50.625 \\
r_5&=\frac{1215}{32}=37.96875
\end{aligned}" />

So the distance up to touch <InlineMath math="k+1" /> equals the distance up to touch <InlineMath math="k" /> **plus <InlineMath math="2r_k" />**. The distance up to the first touch is the fall, <InlineMath math="160\text{ cm}" />.
