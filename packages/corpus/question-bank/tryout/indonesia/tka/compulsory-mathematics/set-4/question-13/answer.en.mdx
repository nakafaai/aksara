# Two points on the image

The question asks for the equation of the **image** line, not of <InlineMath math="g" /> itself. A line is fixed by two points, so you need two points on the image. One is given: <InlineMath math="(6,4)" />. You get the second by **carrying the point <InlineMath math="(2,1)" /> through both transformations**, because a point on <InlineMath math="g" /> always lands on the image of <InlineMath math="g" />.

Reflecting in the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis turns <InlineMath math="(x,y)" /> into <InlineMath math="(x,-y)" />. A dilation about <InlineMath math="O" /> with factor <InlineMath math="2" /> multiplies both coordinates by <InlineMath math="2" />.
