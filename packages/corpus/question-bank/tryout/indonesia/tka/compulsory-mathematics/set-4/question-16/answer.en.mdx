The can is a cylinder, so its volume is **base area times height**, <InlineMath math="V=\pi r^2h" />, with the radius half the diameter, <InlineMath math="r=3.5\text{ cm}" />.

<BlockMath math="V=\frac{22}{7}\cdot3.5^2\cdot10=38.5\cdot10=385\text{ cm}^3" />

As a check, the base area is <InlineMath math="\frac{22}{7}\cdot12.25=38.5\text{ cm}^2" />, and <InlineMath math="38.5\cdot10=385" />.

So one can holds <InlineMath math="385\text{ cm}^3" /> of milk.
