You need the value of <InlineMath math="t" /> that makes <InlineMath math="M(t)=6" />. Divide both sides by <InlineMath math="192" />, then write the right side as a **power of the same base**, <InlineMath math="\frac12" />. Two powers with the same base are equal only when their exponents are equal.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
192\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac t5}&=6 \\
\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac t5}&=\frac{6}{192}=\frac{1}{32}=\left(\frac12\right)^{5} \\
\frac t5&=5 \\
t&=25
\end{aligned}" />
