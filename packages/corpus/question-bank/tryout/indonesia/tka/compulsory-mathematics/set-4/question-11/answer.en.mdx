A mirror reflects light with **the angle of incidence equal to the angle of reflection**. So the right triangle made by Rina's eye and the mirror is similar to the right triangle made by the top of the tower and the mirror.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{h}{18}&=\frac{1.6}{2.4} \\
h&=18\cdot\frac{1.6}{2.4}=18\cdot\frac23=12\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, height over distance to the mirror is the same in both triangles: <InlineMath math="\frac{1.6}{2.4}=\frac{12}{18}=\frac23" />.

So the water tower is <InlineMath math="12\text{ m}" /> high.
