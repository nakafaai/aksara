Only grade X students who chose sports (*olahraga*) switch to arts (*seni*), so **the <InlineMath math="25\%" /> is taken of <InlineMath math="48" /> students**, not of <InlineMath math="84" /> or <InlineMath math="120" /> students. Grade X still has <InlineMath math="120" /> students, because the students who switch are still in grade X. Let <InlineMath math="k" /> be the number of students who switch and <InlineMath math="S" /> the number of grade X students who choose arts after the switch.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
k&=\frac{25}{100}\cdot48=12 \\
S&=30+12=42 \\
\frac{S}{120}\cdot100\%&=\frac{42}{120}\cdot100\%=35\%
\end{aligned}" />

| Grade X choice | Before | Change | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sports | <InlineMath math="48" /> | <InlineMath math="-12" /> | <InlineMath math="36" /> |
| Arts | <InlineMath math="30" /> | <InlineMath math="+12" /> | <InlineMath math="42" /> |
| Science | <InlineMath math="42" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> | <InlineMath math="42" /> |
| Total | <InlineMath math="120" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> | <InlineMath math="120" /> |

After the switch, <InlineMath math="35\%" /> of grade X students therefore choose arts.

- <InlineMath math="25\%" /> is the share of arts before the switch, <InlineMath math="\frac{30}{120}" />, so the switch has not been counted.
- <InlineMath math="31.25\%" /> appears when the number of arts students is increased by <InlineMath math="25\%" />, giving <InlineMath math="30\cdot1.25=37.5" /> students. The percentage belongs to the sports students, and a number of students cannot be a fraction anyway.
- <InlineMath math="42.5\%" /> appears when <InlineMath math="25\%" /> of all <InlineMath math="84" /> sports students in grades X and XI are assumed to switch, although grade XI students do not switch.
- <InlineMath math="50\%" /> appears when <InlineMath math="25\%" /> of all <InlineMath math="120" /> grade X students, which is <InlineMath math="30" /> students, are assumed to switch to arts.
