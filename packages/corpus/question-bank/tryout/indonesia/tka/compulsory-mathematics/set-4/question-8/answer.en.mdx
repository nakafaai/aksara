<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
r^4&=1.5^4=5.0625 \\
U_5&=1{,}600\cdot5.0625=8{,}100
\end{aligned}" />

As a check month by month, <InlineMath math="1{,}600\to2{,}400\to3{,}600\to5{,}400\to8{,}100" />.

So in the fifth month there are <InlineMath math="8{,}100" /> users.
