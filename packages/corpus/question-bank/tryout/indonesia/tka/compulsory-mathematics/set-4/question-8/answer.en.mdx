# The difference and the first term

Write the profit on day <InlineMath math="n" />, in thousands of rupiah, as <InlineMath math="U_n=a-(n-1)d" />, where <InlineMath math="a" /> is the first day's profit and <InlineMath math="d" /> is the drop per day. The question asks for the first day with <InlineMath math="U_n<0" />, **truly negative, not zero**.

Start with the gap between day <InlineMath math="4" /> and day <InlineMath math="9" />. Going from day <InlineMath math="4" /> to day <InlineMath math="9" /> takes <InlineMath math="9-4=5" /> drops, not <InlineMath math="4" /> or <InlineMath math="6" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
U_4-U_9&=5d=60 \\
d&=12
\end{aligned}" />

Day <InlineMath math="3" /> and day <InlineMath math="11" /> are equally far from day <InlineMath math="7" />, so their sum equals <InlineMath math="2U_7" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
U_3+U_{11}&=2U_7=432 \\
U_7&=216 \\
a=U_7+6d&=216+72=288
\end{aligned}" />

So <InlineMath math="U_n=288-12(n-1)=300-12n" />.

# The first day with a loss

Now find the smallest <InlineMath math="n" /> with a negative profit.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
300-12n&<0 \\
n&>25
\end{aligned}" />

On day <InlineMath math="25" /> the profit is <InlineMath math="300-300=0" />. That's **not a loss yet**, just breaking even. On day <InlineMath math="26" /> the profit is <InlineMath math="300-312=-12" />, a loss of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}12{,}000" />. So the stall first makes a loss on day <InlineMath math="26" />.

# The tempting options

Each of the other options comes from **one step that's off**.

- Day <InlineMath math="22" /> comes from counting <InlineMath math="4" /> drops from day <InlineMath math="4" /> to day <InlineMath math="9" />, which gives <InlineMath math="d=15" /> and <InlineMath math="U_n=321-15n" />.
- Day <InlineMath math="25" /> is the day the profit is exactly zero. A zero profit isn't a loss.
- Day <InlineMath math="29" /> comes from counting <InlineMath math="6" /> drops because there are six days from day <InlineMath math="4" /> to day <InlineMath math="9" />, which gives <InlineMath math="d=10" /> and <InlineMath math="U_n=286-10n" />.
- Day <InlineMath math="50" /> is the first day the **total** profit since day one turns negative. That total is <InlineMath math="S_n=n(294-6n)" />, which is exactly zero at <InlineMath math="n=49" />. The question is about the daily profit, not the total.

So the stall first makes a loss on day <InlineMath math="26" />.
