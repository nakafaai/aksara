The distance from a point to a plane is measured along the perpendicular to that plane. For the point <InlineMath math="O" /> and the lateral face <InlineMath math="TAB" />, that perpendicular can be found in triangle <InlineMath math="TOM" />, where <InlineMath math="O" /> is the center of the base and <InlineMath math="M" /> is the midpoint of <InlineMath math="AB" />.

Triangle <InlineMath math="TOM" /> has a right angle at <InlineMath math="O" />, with <InlineMath math="TO=3\text{ m}" /> and <InlineMath math="OM=4\text{ m}" />, half of a base side. The edge <InlineMath math="AB" /> is perpendicular to <InlineMath math="TO" /> and to <InlineMath math="OM" />, so <InlineMath math="AB" /> is perpendicular to the plane <InlineMath math="TOM" />. The plane <InlineMath math="TAB" /> contains <InlineMath math="AB" />, so the plane <InlineMath math="TAB" /> is perpendicular to the plane <InlineMath math="TOM" />. The two planes meet along the line <InlineMath math="TM" />, so **the required distance is the segment from <InlineMath math="O" /> perpendicular to <InlineMath math="TM" />** inside triangle <InlineMath math="TOM" />.

The area of triangle <InlineMath math="TOM" /> can be computed in two ways, <InlineMath math="\frac12\cdot TO\cdot OM" /> and <InlineMath math="\frac12\cdot TM\cdot d" />, where <InlineMath math="d" /> is the distance from <InlineMath math="O" /> to <InlineMath math="TM" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
TM&=\sqrt{TO^2+OM^2}=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5 \\
d&=\frac{TO\cdot OM}{TM}=\frac{3\cdot4}{5}=\frac{12}{5}
\end{aligned}" />

Check with coordinates. Take <InlineMath math="O=(0,0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="T=(0,3,0)" />, <InlineMath math="A=(-4,0,4)" />, and <InlineMath math="B=(4,0,4)" />. The plane <InlineMath math="TAB" /> has the equation <InlineMath math="4y+3z=12" />, so the distance from <InlineMath math="O" /> to that plane is <InlineMath math="\frac{|-12|}{\sqrt{4^2+3^2}}=\frac{12}{5}" />.

Therefore the distance from <InlineMath math="O" /> to the lateral face <InlineMath math="TAB" /> is <InlineMath math="\frac{12}{5}\text{ m}" />.

- <InlineMath math="3\text{ m}" /> is the height of the pyramid, the distance from <InlineMath math="T" /> to the base. It is not the distance from <InlineMath math="O" /> to a lateral face.
- <InlineMath math="4\text{ m}" /> is <InlineMath math="OM" />, the distance from <InlineMath math="O" /> to the edge <InlineMath math="AB" />. Segment <InlineMath math="OM" /> is not perpendicular to the plane <InlineMath math="TAB" />, so it is not that distance.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{24}{5}\text{ m}" /> appears when the area of triangle <InlineMath math="TOM" /> is taken as <InlineMath math="TO\cdot OM=12" /> instead of half of that, giving <InlineMath math="2\cdot\frac{12}{5}=\frac{24}{5}" />.
- <InlineMath math="5\text{ m}" /> is <InlineMath math="TM" />, the slant height of the lateral face. It is not the distance from <InlineMath math="O" />.
