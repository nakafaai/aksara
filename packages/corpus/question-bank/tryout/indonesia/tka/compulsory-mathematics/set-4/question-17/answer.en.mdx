Let <InlineMath math="O" /> be the center of the base and <InlineMath math="M" /> the midpoint of <InlineMath math="AB" />. Triangle <InlineMath math="TOM" /> has a right angle at <InlineMath math="O" />, with <InlineMath math="TO=3\text{ m}" /> and <InlineMath math="OM=4\text{ m}" />, half of a base side. Let <InlineMath math="L" /> be the area of the four lateral faces and <InlineMath math="K" /> the cost in rupiah.

Check by computing the cost per face. One lateral face of <InlineMath math="20\text{ m}^2" /> costs <InlineMath math="20\cdot\text{Rp}85{,}000=\text{Rp}1{,}700{,}000" />, so four faces cost <InlineMath math="4\cdot\text{Rp}1{,}700{,}000=\text{Rp}6{,}800{,}000" />.

The metal roofing for the four lateral faces therefore costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}6{,}800{,}000" />.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}700{,}000" /> is the cost of only one lateral face, although the roof has four.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}400{,}000" /> appears when the base of each triangle is taken as <InlineMath math="4\text{ m}" />, the half side used to find <InlineMath math="TM" />, so that each lateral face seems to have an area of <InlineMath math="10\text{ m}^2" />.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}440{,}000" /> is the cost for the base area <InlineMath math="8\cdot8=64\text{ m}^2" />, although the lateral faces are covered, not the base.
