The lateral faces of the roof are triangles <InlineMath math="TAB" />, <InlineMath math="TBC" />, <InlineMath math="TCD" />, and <InlineMath math="TDA" />. The base <InlineMath math="ABCD" /> does not need covering, so the cost depends only on the **area of the four lateral faces**.

The height of 3 m in the stimulus is the distance from the apex <InlineMath math="T" /> to the base plane. It is not the height of each lateral face. The height of a face is the segment <InlineMath math="TM" />, where <InlineMath math="M" /> is the midpoint of <InlineMath math="AB" />. Let <InlineMath math="O" /> be the centre of the base. Segment <InlineMath math="OM" /> is half of a base side, and <InlineMath math="TO" /> is perpendicular to the base. So <InlineMath math="TM" /> is also perpendicular to <InlineMath math="AB" />, and triangle <InlineMath math="TOM" /> has a right angle at <InlineMath math="O" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
TM&=\sqrt{TO^2+OM^2}=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{25}=5\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

One lateral face is a triangle with base 8 m and height 5 m, so its area is <InlineMath math="\frac12\cdot8\cdot5=20\text{ m}^2" />. The four lateral faces have an area of <InlineMath math="4\cdot20=80\text{ m}^2" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost}&=80\cdot\text{Rp }85{,}000=\text{Rp }6{,}800{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, half the base perimeter times the face height gives the same area: <InlineMath math="\frac12\cdot32\cdot5=80\text{ m}^2" />.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp }1{,}700{,}000" /> covers only one lateral face, which is <InlineMath math="20\cdot\text{Rp }85{,}000" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp }3{,}400{,}000" /> covers only two lateral faces, but the question asks for all four.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp }4{,}080{,}000" /> uses the 3 m height as the height of each face. That wrong area is <InlineMath math="4\cdot\frac12\cdot8\cdot3=48\text{ m}^2" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp }5{,}440{,}000" /> uses the base area <InlineMath math="8\cdot8=64\text{ m}^2" />. The base does not need covering.

So the cost of the metal roofing for the four lateral faces is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp }6{,}800{,}000" />.
