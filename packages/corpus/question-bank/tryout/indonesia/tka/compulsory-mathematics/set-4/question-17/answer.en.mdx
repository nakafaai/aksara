The angle between two planes is measured at their **line of intersection**. Plane <InlineMath math="TAB" /> and the base meet along the edge <InlineMath math="AB" />. Take the midpoint <InlineMath math="M" /> of <InlineMath math="AB" />, and find the line in each plane that passes through <InlineMath math="M" /> and is perpendicular to <InlineMath math="AB" />.

In the base, that line is <InlineMath math="OM" />, because <InlineMath math="O" /> is the centre of the square and <InlineMath math="M" /> is the midpoint of <InlineMath math="AB" />. In plane <InlineMath math="TAB" />, the line is <InlineMath math="TM" />, because <InlineMath math="TA=TB" /> makes triangle <InlineMath math="TAB" /> isosceles, so the median <InlineMath math="TM" /> is also an altitude. The angle between the two planes is therefore the angle <InlineMath math="TMO" />.

Triangle <InlineMath math="TOM" /> has **a right angle at <InlineMath math="O" />**, because <InlineMath math="TO" /> is perpendicular to the base. Its legs are <InlineMath math="TO=3\text{ m}" /> and <InlineMath math="OM=4\text{ m}" />, which is half the side of the base. The tangent of the angle <InlineMath math="TMO" /> is

<BlockMath math="\tan\angle TMO=\frac{TO}{OM}=\frac{3}{4}" />

Check the slant side. <InlineMath math="TM=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\text{ m}" />, so <InlineMath math="\sin\angle TMO=\frac35" /> and <InlineMath math="\cos\angle TMO=\frac45" />. These three values agree, because <InlineMath math="(3,4,5)" /> is a Pythagorean triple.

Check the result with coordinates as well. Take <InlineMath math="O=(0,0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="T=(0,3,0)" />, <InlineMath math="A=(-4,0,4)" />, and <InlineMath math="B=(4,0,4)" />. Plane <InlineMath math="TAB" /> has the equation <InlineMath math="4y+3z=12" />, so a normal vector is <InlineMath math="(0,4,3)" />. A normal vector of the base is <InlineMath math="(0,1,0)" />. Therefore <InlineMath math="\cos\theta=\frac45" />, and <InlineMath math="\tan\theta=\frac34" />.

So the tangent of the angle between plane <InlineMath math="TAB" /> and the base is <InlineMath math="\frac34" />.

- <InlineMath math="\frac43" /> is the reciprocal of the tangent. It appears when <InlineMath math="OM" /> is divided by <InlineMath math="TO" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac35" /> is the sine. Its denominator is the slant side <InlineMath math="TM" />, not <InlineMath math="OM" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac45" /> is the cosine. It uses <InlineMath math="OM" /> and <InlineMath math="TM" />, not <InlineMath math="TO" /> and <InlineMath math="OM" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{3\sqrt2}{8}" /> is the tangent of the angle between the edge <InlineMath math="TA" /> and the base, which is <InlineMath math="TO" /> divided by <InlineMath math="OA=4\sqrt2" />. That is an angle between a line and a plane, not between two planes.
