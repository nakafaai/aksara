Use **elimination by addition**. The coefficients of <InlineMath math="y" /> are one and negative one, so the terms containing <InlineMath math="y" /> cancel when the equations are added.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(x+y)+(2x-y)&=5+1 \\
3x&=6 \\
x&=\frac63=2
\end{aligned}" />

To check the result, substitute <InlineMath math="x=2" /> into the first equation and find <InlineMath math="y" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2+y&=5 \\
y&=5-2=3 \\
2x-y&=2(2)-3=1
\end{aligned}" />

Both equations hold. The requested value is <InlineMath math="x=2" />.
