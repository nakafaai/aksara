# The group in the denominator

The question asks for a fraction, so you need a numerator and a denominator. The words "di antara panggilan yang bukan panggilan singkat" (among the calls that are not short) set the denominator: **you count only calls of at least <InlineMath math="7.5" /> minutes**, not all <InlineMath math="80" /> calls.

The <InlineMath math="7.5" />-minute cut falls in the middle of class <InlineMath math="5\le t<10" />. Since calls in that class are spread evenly, half of its <InlineMath math="22" /> calls last at least <InlineMath math="7.5" /> minutes. The later classes count in full.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{not short}&=\tfrac12\cdot22+14+18+12+8 \\
&=11+52=63
\end{aligned}" />

# The calls in the numerator

From those <InlineMath math="63" /> calls, take the ones shorter than <InlineMath math="22.5" /> minutes. The <InlineMath math="22.5" />-minute cut falls in the middle of class <InlineMath math="20\le t<25" />, so **only half of that class counts**.

| <InlineMath math="7.5\le t<10" /> | <InlineMath math="11" /> | <InlineMath math="11" /> |

Check it from the other side. The calls that aren't short but last at least <InlineMath math="22.5" /> minutes number <InlineMath math="6+8=14" />, and <InlineMath math="49+14=63" />. So the complement is <InlineMath math="\frac{14}{63}=\frac29" />, and <InlineMath math="\frac79+\frac29=1" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

Each wrong option comes from the wrong denominator or from cutting a class the wrong way. **Both cuts that fall inside a class** have to be split in half.

- <InlineMath math="\frac{43}{63}" /> leaves out the <InlineMath math="6" /> calls with <InlineMath math="20\le t<22.5" />. The numerator stops at <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{8}{13}" /> uses whole classes only: <InlineMath math="10\le t<20" /> out of <InlineMath math="10\le t<30" />, which is <InlineMath math="\frac{32}{52}" />. Both cuts inside a class are ignored.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{49}{80}" /> divides by all <InlineMath math="80" /> calls. The question only asks among the calls that aren't short.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{2}{9}" /> is the share lasting at least <InlineMath math="22.5" /> minutes, the complement.

So the estimated fraction is <InlineMath math="\frac{7}{9}" />.
