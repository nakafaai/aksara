Because distances are nonnegative, **equal squared distances** mean equal lengths. Insert <InlineMath math="P=(t,0)" /> into the distance formula.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(t-(-2))^2+(0-3)^2&=(t-4)^2+(0-5)^2 \\
(t+2)^2+3^2&=(t-4)^2+5^2 \\
t^2+4t+13&=t^2-8t+41 \\
4t+8t&=41-13 \\
12t&=28 \\
t&=\frac{28}{12}=\frac73
\end{aligned}" />

Hence <InlineMath math="P=(\frac73,0)" />. The requested value is:

<BlockMath math="3t=3\left(\frac73\right)=7" />
