<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
B'&=M+k\,(B-M)=(1,2)+(-2)\,(0,3)=(1,-4)
\end{aligned}" />

Luas segitiga semula diperoleh dengan rumus determinan.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L_{ABC}&=\frac12\,\bigl|(1-3)(1-2)-(-1-3)(5-2)\bigr|=\frac12\,|2+12|=7 \\

\end{aligned}" />
