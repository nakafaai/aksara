Express the point relative to the **rotation center** <InlineMath math="C=(1,2)" />.

<BlockMath math="(4,6)-(1,2)=(3,4)" />

A <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> counterclockwise rotation maps <InlineMath math="(x,y)" /> to <InlineMath math="(-y,x)" />. Add the rotation center back, then apply the translation.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(3,4)&\longmapsto(-4,3) \\
R&=(1,2)+(-4,3) \\
&=(-3,5) \\
Q&=(-3,5)+(2,-1) \\
&=(-1,4)
\end{aligned}" />

The final point is <InlineMath math="(-1,4)" />.
