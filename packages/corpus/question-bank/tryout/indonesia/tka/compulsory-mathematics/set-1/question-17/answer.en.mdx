The path reduces **both ends** of each dimension. The inner rectangle measures <InlineMath math="26\,\mathrm{m}" /> by <InlineMath math="18\,\mathrm{m}" />. Subtract the pond area from this inner area.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&=(30-2\cdot2)(22-2\cdot2)-8\cdot5 \\
&=26\cdot18-40=428
\end{aligned}" />

The entire garden has area <InlineMath math="30\cdot22=660\,\mathrm{m}^2" />. The fraction that can be planted is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{428}{660}=\frac{428\div4}{660\div4}=\frac{107}{165}" />
