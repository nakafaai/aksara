With a tiered tariff, **each tier has its own price**: the first <InlineMath math="10\text{ m}^3" /> are charged at the first rate, and only the remaining <InlineMath math="5\text{ m}^3" /> at the second. The fixed charge comes on top.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{first tier}&=10\cdot4{,}000=40{,}000 \\
\text{second tier}&=5\cdot6{,}500=32{,}500 \\
\text{bill}&=40{,}000+32{,}500+18{,}000=90{,}500
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, the usage is below <InlineMath math="20\text{ m}^3" />, so the third tier does not apply.

So the bill is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}90{,}500" />.
