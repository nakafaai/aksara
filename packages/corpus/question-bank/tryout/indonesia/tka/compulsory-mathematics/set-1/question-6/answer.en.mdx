Let <InlineMath math="b" /> be the price of one notebook and <InlineMath math="p" /> the price of one pencil, in rupiah. The two bundles give the following system.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2b+3p&=15{,}750 \\
3b+p&=15{,}750
\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the first equation from the second, then substitute the resulting relationship.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
b-2p&=0 \\
b&=2p \\
3(2p)+p&=15{,}750 \\
7p&=15{,}750 \\
p&=2{,}250 \\
b&=4{,}500
\end{aligned}" />

Use these unit prices for five notebooks and two pencils.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
5b+2p&=5(4{,}500)+2(2{,}250) \\
&=22{,}500+4{,}500 \\
&=27{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

The total purchase price is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp }27{,}000" />.
