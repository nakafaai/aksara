Let <InlineMath math="b" /> be the price of one notebook and <InlineMath math="p" /> the price of one pencil, in rupiah. The two bundles give the following system.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2b+3p&=15{,}750 \\
3b+p&=15{,}750
\end{aligned}" />

The two bundles have **the same price**. Subtracting the first equation from the second makes the right-hand side zero. Substitute the resulting relationship between unit prices into the second equation.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(3b+p)-(2b+3p)&=0 \\
b-2p&=0 \\
b&=2p \\
3(2p)+p&=15{,}750 \\
7p&=15{,}750 \\
p&=\frac{15{,}750}{7}=2{,}250 \\
b&=2(2{,}250)=4{,}500
\end{aligned}" />

The price difference between one notebook and one pencil is:

<BlockMath math="b-p=4{,}500-2{,}250=2{,}250" />

The difference is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp }2{,}250" />.
