There are <InlineMath math="6\cdot6=36" /> **equally likely ordered outcomes**. Sums of <InlineMath math="9,10,11,12" /> occur in <InlineMath math="4,3,2,1" /> outcomes respectively, giving <InlineMath math="10" /> outcomes with a sum of at least <InlineMath math="9" />.

The first number in each pair is the result on the first die. Reversing two different numbers therefore gives a different outcome.

| Sum | Ordered dice outcomes | Number of outcomes |
| ---: | --- | ---: |
| 9 | (3, 6), (4, 5), (5, 4), (6, 3) | 4 |
| 10 | (4, 6), **(5, 5)**, (6, 4) | 3 |
| 11 | (5, 6), (6, 5) | 2 |
| 12 | **(6, 6)** | 1 |

Matching dice occur in <InlineMath math="6" /> outcomes. Two of them, <InlineMath math="(5,5)" /> and <InlineMath math="(6,6)" />, also have a sum of at least <InlineMath math="9" />. Subtract these two outcomes to avoid counting them twice.
