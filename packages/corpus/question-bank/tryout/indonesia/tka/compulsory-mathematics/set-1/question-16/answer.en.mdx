Because the **holes do not overlap**, subtract their total area from the rectangle. The two semicircles have the combined area of one full circle.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&=10\cdot8-2\left(\frac12\pi(2)^2\right)-3^2 \\
&=80-4\pi-9 \\
&=71-4\pi
\end{aligned}" />

The remaining plate area is <InlineMath math="(71-4\pi)\,\mathrm{cm}^2" />.
