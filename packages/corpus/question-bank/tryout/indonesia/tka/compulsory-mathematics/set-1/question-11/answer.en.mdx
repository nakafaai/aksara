The height-shadow triangles each have a right angle and the same sunlight angle. They are **similar triangles**, so their height-to-shadow ratios are equal. Let <InlineMath math="H" /> be the combined height of the tree and platform.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{H}{8}&=\frac{1.8}{1.2} \\
H&=8\left(\frac{18}{12}\right) \\
&=8\left(\frac32\right)=12
\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the platform height to find the tree height.

<BlockMath math="h=12-2=10\,\mathrm{m}" />
