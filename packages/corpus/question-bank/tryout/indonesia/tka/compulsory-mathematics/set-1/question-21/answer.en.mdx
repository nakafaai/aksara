The frequencies sum to <InlineMath math="2+3+5+4+2=16" />. The **lower half** contains the first eight observations, so its median uses the fourth and fifth values. Both are <InlineMath math="14" />. The upper-half median uses the twelfth and thirteenth observations, both <InlineMath math="18" />.

The positions below identify the values used for each half’s median.

| Value | Frequency | Positions in the ordered data |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| 12 | 2 | 1, 2 |
| 14 | 3 | 3, **4, 5** |
| 16 | 5 | 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 |
| 18 | 4 | 11, **12, 13**, 14 |
| 20 | 2 | 15, 16 |

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
Q_1&=\frac{14+14}{2}=14 \\
Q_3&=\frac{18+18}{2}=18
\end{aligned}" />

The quartile deviation is half the difference between the quartiles.

<BlockMath math="\frac{Q_3-Q_1}{2}=\frac{18-14}{2}=2" />
