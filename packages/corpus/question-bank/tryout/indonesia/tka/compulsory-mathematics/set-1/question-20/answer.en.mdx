After the move, the foot is <InlineMath math="5+3=8\,\mathrm{m}" /> from the wall. The ladder is the hypotenuse of a right triangle. Use Pythagoras to find the new height <InlineMath math="h" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
h^2+8^2&=13^2 \\
h&=\sqrt{169-64}=\sqrt{105}
\end{aligned}" />

The sine of the angle with the ground is the height divided by the ladder length.

<BlockMath math="\sin\theta=\frac{h}{13}=\frac{\sqrt{105}}{13}" />
