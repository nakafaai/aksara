The ladder, the ground and the slide form a right triangle. The height <InlineMath math="2.4\text{ m}" /> is opposite the angle <InlineMath math="\alpha" />, and the slide is the hypotenuse. So you need **the sine**, but the tangent is given.

From <InlineMath math="\tan\alpha=\frac34" /> you get a right triangle with legs <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> and hypotenuse <InlineMath math="5" />, so <InlineMath math="\sin\alpha=\frac35" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\sin\alpha&=\frac{2.4}{\ell} \\
\ell&=\frac{2.4}{\frac35}=4\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, the horizontal distance is <InlineMath math="\frac{2.4}{\tan\alpha}=3.2\text{ m}" />, and <InlineMath math="2.4^2+3.2^2=5.76+10.24=16=4^2" />.

So the slide is <InlineMath math="4\text{ m}" /> long.
