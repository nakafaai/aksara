The daily lengths form an **arithmetic sequence** with <InlineMath math="u_1=24" /> and <InlineMath math="d=4" />. The length of the whole road is the sum over all days. Use it to find the number of working days <InlineMath math="n" /> first.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
S_n=\frac n2\bigl(2\cdot24+(n-1)\cdot4\bigr)&=1{,}240 \\
n(2n+22)&=1{,}240 \\
n^2+11n-620&=0 \\
(n-20)(n+31)&=0
\end{aligned}" />

A number of days is positive, so <InlineMath math="n=20" />. The last day is the twentieth.

<BlockMath math="u_{20}=24+19\cdot4=100" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="S_{20}=\frac{20}{2}(24+100)=10\cdot124=1{,}240" />.

So <InlineMath math="100\text{ m}" /> were paved on the last day.

- <InlineMath math="20\text{ m}" /> confuses the number of days with a length.
- <InlineMath math="96\text{ m}" /> is <InlineMath math="u_{19}" />, the length on the second-to-last day.
- <InlineMath math="104\text{ m}" /> computes <InlineMath math="24+20\cdot4" />. Up to the twentieth day there are only <InlineMath math="19" /> increases.
- <InlineMath math="124\text{ m}" /> is <InlineMath math="u_1+u_{20}" /> from <InlineMath math="S_{20}=\frac{20}{2}(u_1+u_{20})" />. You still have to subtract <InlineMath math="u_1=24" />.
