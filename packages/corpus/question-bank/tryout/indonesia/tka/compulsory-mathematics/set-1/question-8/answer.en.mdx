# The middle term from the first condition

The terms <InlineMath math="u_3" /> and <InlineMath math="u_7" /> are equally far from <InlineMath math="u_5" />. In an arithmetic sequence, **two terms equally far from a term average to that term**:

<BlockMath math="u_3+u_7=2u_5=34\quad\Rightarrow\quad u_5=17" />

# Finding the common difference from the second condition

Write the other terms with the common difference <InlineMath math="b" />: <InlineMath math="u_2=17-3b" /> and <InlineMath math="u_4=17-b" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(17-3b)(17-b)&=112 \\

(b-3)(3b-59)&=0
\end{aligned}" />

So <InlineMath math="b=3" /> or <InlineMath math="b=\frac{59}{3}" />. The problem says **every term is a whole number**, so <InlineMath math="b=\frac{59}{3}" /> is out and <InlineMath math="b=3" />.
