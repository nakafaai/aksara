The bill consists of **a fixed charge and a tiered usage charge**. Subtract the fixed charge first, then spend the rest tier by tier.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{usage charge}&=195{,}000-18{,}000=177{,}000 \\
\text{first }20\text{ m}^3&=10\cdot4{,}000+10\cdot6{,}500=105{,}000 \\
\text{remainder}&=177{,}000-105{,}000=72{,}000 \\
\text{usage above }20\text{ m}^3&=\frac{72{,}000}{9{,}000}=8\text{ m}^3
\end{aligned}" />

The total usage is <InlineMath math="20+8=28\text{ m}^3" />.

As a check, <InlineMath math="40{,}000+65{,}000+8\cdot9{,}000+18{,}000=195{,}000" />.
