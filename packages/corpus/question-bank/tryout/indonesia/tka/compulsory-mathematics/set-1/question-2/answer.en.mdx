Tax applies **after both the discount and voucher**. A <InlineMath math="20\%" /> discount leaves <InlineMath math="100\%-20\%=80\%" /> of the original price. Let <InlineMath math="P" /> be the taxable price in thousands of rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P&=480(1-0.20)-30 \\
&=384-30=354
\end{aligned}" />

The tax is <InlineMath math="11\%" /> of <InlineMath math="P=354" />, rather than of the original price. Since <InlineMath math="11\%=\frac{11}{100}=0.11" />, we get:

<BlockMath math="0.11(354)=38.94" />

The tax portion of the final price is <InlineMath math="38.94" /> thousand rupiah.
