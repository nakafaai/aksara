Tax applies after both the discount and voucher. Calculate the taxable price in thousands of rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P&=480(1-0.20)-30 \\
&=384-30=354
\end{aligned}" />

The <InlineMath math="11\%" /> tax is calculated from this price.

<BlockMath math="0.11(354)=38.94" />

The tax portion of the final price is <InlineMath math="38.94" /> thousand rupiah.
