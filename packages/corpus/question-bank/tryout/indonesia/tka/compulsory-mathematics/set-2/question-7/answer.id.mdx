Hitung argumen fungsi invers terlebih dahulu.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
f(4)&=\frac{3(4)-2}{5}=2 \\
f(4)+2&=4
\end{aligned}" />

Untuk memperoleh invers, selesaikan <InlineMath math="y=f(x)" /> terhadap <InlineMath math="x" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
5y&=3x-2 \\
x&=\frac{5y+2}{3} \\
f^{-1}(y)&=\frac{5y+2}{3}
\end{aligned}" />
