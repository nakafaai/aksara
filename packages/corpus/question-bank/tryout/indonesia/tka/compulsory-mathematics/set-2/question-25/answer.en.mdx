The order of selection does not matter, so count with **binomial coefficients**. Exactly one goalkeeper means one of the <InlineMath math="2" /> goalkeepers and four of the <InlineMath math="6" /> outfield players.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{all selections}&=\binom85=56 \\
\text{exactly 1 goalkeeper}&=\binom21\binom64=2\cdot15=30 \\
P&=\frac{30}{56}=\frac{15}{28}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, no goalkeeper gives <InlineMath math="\binom65=6" /> selections, two goalkeepers give <InlineMath math="\binom22\binom63=20" />, and <InlineMath math="6+30+20=56" />.
