<BlockMath math="h=\sqrt{5^2-3^2}=\sqrt{25-9}=\sqrt{16}=4\,\mathrm{m}" />

The fabric covers two sloping rectangles and two triangular ends. The **floor is excluded**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&=2(5\cdot10)+2\left(\frac12\cdot6\cdot4\right) \\
&=100+24=124
\end{aligned}" />

The required fabric area is <InlineMath math="124\,\mathrm{m}^2" />.
