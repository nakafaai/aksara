Reflection in <InlineMath math="y=-x" /> maps <InlineMath math="(x,y)" /> to <InlineMath math="(-y,-x)" />. Thus <InlineMath math="(3,-1)" /> first becomes <InlineMath math="(1,-3)" /> and then, after dilation by factor <InlineMath math="2" />, becomes <InlineMath math="(2,-6)" />. The coordinate sum is <InlineMath math="-4" />.
