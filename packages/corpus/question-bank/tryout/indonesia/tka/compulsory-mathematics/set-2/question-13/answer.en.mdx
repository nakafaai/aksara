Apply the **reflection before the dilation**, as the question specifies. Reflection in <InlineMath math="y=-x" /> swaps the coordinates and changes both signs. Call the reflected point R.

<BlockMath math="R=(-(-1),-3)=(1,-3)" />

Dilation about the origin multiplies both coordinates of R by 2.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}Q&=(2\cdot1,2\cdot(-3))=(2,-6) \\ x_Q+y_Q&=2+(-6)=-4\end{aligned}" />

To check the reflection, the midpoint of P and R is (2,−2), on the mirror line. Segment PR has slope 1, perpendicular to the mirror line of slope −1. The final coordinate sum is −4.
