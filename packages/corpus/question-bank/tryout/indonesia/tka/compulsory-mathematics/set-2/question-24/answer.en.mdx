The two eggs are drawn **at the same time**, so without replacement and without order. Count the favourable pairs and all pairs with binomial coefficients.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{all pairs}&=\binom{12}{2}=66 \\
\text{pairs of sound eggs}&=\binom92=36 \\
P&=\frac{36}{66}=\frac{6}{11}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, drawing one after the other, <InlineMath math="\frac{9}{12}\cdot\frac{8}{11}=\frac{72}{132}=\frac{6}{11}" />.

So the probability is <InlineMath math="\frac{6}{11}" />.

- <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{22}=\frac{3}{66}" /> is the probability that both eggs are cracked.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{9}{22}=\frac{27}{66}" /> is the probability of exactly one cracked egg.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{9}{16}=\left(\frac34\right)^2" /> works with replacement, as if the first egg went back into the container.
- <InlineMath math="\frac34" /> is the probability for a single sound egg.
