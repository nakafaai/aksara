Multiply each **mean by its number of observations** to obtain the total. Subtract class A’s total from the combined total.

| Class | Number of values | Mean | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 20 | 72 | 1,440 |
| B | 30 | Unknown | 2,340 |
| Combined | 50 | 75.6 | 3,780 |

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
S&=50(75.6)=3{,}780 \\
S_A&=20(72)=1{,}440 \\
S_B&=3{,}780-1{,}440=2{,}340
\end{aligned}" />

Class B has <InlineMath math="30" /> observations, so its mean is:

<BlockMath math="\bar x_B=\frac{2{,}340}{30}=78" />
