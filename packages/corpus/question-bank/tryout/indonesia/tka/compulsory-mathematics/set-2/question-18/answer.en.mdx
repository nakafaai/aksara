Two parts are painted: the **lateral surface of the cylinder** and the **hemisphere**. The surface of a whole sphere is <InlineMath math="4\pi r^2" />, so the curved surface of a hemisphere is <InlineMath math="2\pi r^2" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
M&=2\pi rh=2\cdot\frac{22}{7}\cdot3.5\cdot10=220\text{ m}^2 \\
A_{\text{hemisphere}}&=2\pi r^2=2\cdot\frac{22}{7}\cdot12.25=77\text{ m}^2 \\
A&=220+77=297\text{ m}^2
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="\frac{22}{7}\cdot3.5=11" />, so <InlineMath math="M=2\cdot11\cdot10=220" /> and <InlineMath math="A_{\text{hemisphere}}=2\cdot11\cdot3.5=77" />.

So <InlineMath math="297\text{ m}^2" /> are painted.

- <InlineMath math="220\text{ m}^2" /> is only the lateral surface. The roof is missing.
- <InlineMath math="258.5\text{ m}^2" /> takes only a flat disc <InlineMath math="\pi r^2=38.5" /> for the roof.
- <InlineMath math="335.5\text{ m}^2" /> also paints the base of <InlineMath math="38.5\text{ m}^2" />.
- <InlineMath math="374\text{ m}^2" /> takes the surface of a whole sphere, <InlineMath math="4\pi r^2=154" />, for the roof.
