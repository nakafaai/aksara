Multiplying the conjugates cancels the radical cross terms. Also calculate the difference and sum of the numbers.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
ab&=(\sqrt5+2)(\sqrt5-2) \\
&=5-4=1 \\
a-b&=(2+\sqrt5)-(\sqrt5-2) \\
&=4 \\
a+b&=2\sqrt5
\end{aligned}" />

Use the square-of-a-sum identity to find the sum of the squares.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
a^2+b^2&=(a+b)^2-2ab \\
&=(2\sqrt5)^2-2=18
\end{aligned}" />

To check the final equation, use <InlineMath math="a-2=\sqrt5" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(a-2)^2&=5 \\
a^2-4a+4&=5 \\
a^2-4a-1&=0
\end{aligned}" />

Every statement is true except <InlineMath math="a+b=4" />, since the sum is <InlineMath math="2\sqrt5" />.
