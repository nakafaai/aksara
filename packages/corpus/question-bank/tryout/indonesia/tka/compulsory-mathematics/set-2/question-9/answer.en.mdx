The first term is <InlineMath math="u_1=S_1=5" />. For <InlineMath math="n\ge2" />, subtract consecutive partial sums to leave only the <InlineMath math="n" />th term.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
u_n&=S_n-S_{n-1} \\
&=3n^2+2n \\
&\quad-\bigl(3(n-1)^2+2(n-1)\bigr) \\
&=6n-1
\end{aligned}" />

This formula also gives <InlineMath math="u_1=5" />. The difference <InlineMath math="u_{n+1}-u_n=6" /> is constant, so the sequence is arithmetic. Evaluate the values in the statements.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
u_{10}&=6(10)-1=59 \\
S_{10}&=3(10)^2+2(10)=320 \\
u_5&=6(5)-1=29
\end{aligned}" />

The statements about the first term, common difference, <InlineMath math="u_{10}" />, and <InlineMath math="S_{10}" /> are true. The claim <InlineMath math="u_5=31" /> is false.
