# Smallest and largest possible means

A value between two known positions can be anything between those two known values, including equal to either one. So **the smallest total** comes from setting every unknown value equal to the known value just below it, and the largest total from setting it equal to the known value just above it.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{smallest A}&=7\cdot40+8\cdot56+8\cdot70+7\cdot82+98=1{,}960,\quad \bar a=\tfrac{1{,}960}{31}\approx63.23 \\
\text{largest B}&=50+6\cdot62+7\cdot68+7\cdot76+6\cdot92=1{,}982,\quad \bar b=\tfrac{1{,}982}{27}\approx73.41
\end{aligned}" />
