The question asks for the height <InlineMath math="h" /> of the tower. The trap is where the tower stands: its foot <InlineMath math="F" /> is beside the road, not on it. Since <InlineMath math="B" /> is the point on the road closest to <InlineMath math="F" />, **segment <InlineMath math="FB" /> is perpendicular to the road**. So on the ground there's a triangle <InlineMath math="FBA" /> with its right angle at <InlineMath math="B" />.

# Horizontal distances to the foot

The tower, the line of sight and the horizontal distance form an upright right triangle with the right angle at <InlineMath math="F" />. In such a triangle, <InlineMath math="\tan(\text{angle of elevation})=\frac{h}{\text{horizontal distance}}" />. Use this **to write both distances in terms of <InlineMath math="h" />**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\tan60^\circ&=\frac{h}{FB}&&\Rightarrow\ FB=\frac{h}{\sqrt3} \\
\tan30^\circ&=\frac{h}{FA}&&\Rightarrow\ FA=\frac{h}{1/\sqrt3}=h\sqrt3
\end{aligned}" />

Pythagoras' theorem in this triangle gives an equation for <InlineMath math="h" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
FA^2&=FB^2+AB^2 \\
3h^2&=\frac{h^2}{3}+\left(20\sqrt6\right)^2 \\
3h^2-\frac{h^2}{3}&=2{,}400 \\
\frac{8h^2}{3}&=2{,}400 \\
h^2&=900 \\
h&=30\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

Check: with <InlineMath math="h=30" />, <InlineMath math="FB=10\sqrt3" /> and <InlineMath math="FA=30\sqrt3" />. Then <InlineMath math="FB^2+AB^2=300+2{,}400=2{,}700=FA^2" />, which matches.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="12\sqrt5\text{ m}" /> puts the right angle at the tower's foot, <InlineMath math="FA^2+FB^2=AB^2" />. The right angle is at <InlineMath math="B" />, because <InlineMath math="B" /> is the closest point to the foot.
- <InlineMath math="20\sqrt2\text{ m}" /> uses <InlineMath math="AB" /> as the horizontal distance from <InlineMath math="A" /> to the foot, <InlineMath math="h=20\sqrt6\cdot\tan30^\circ" />. But <InlineMath math="B" /> isn't the foot of the tower.
- <InlineMath math="30\sqrt2\text{ m}" /> assumes the foot is on the road, so that <InlineMath math="FA-FB=AB" />. The question says the foot is **not** on the road.
- <InlineMath math="30\sqrt3\text{ m}" /> is <InlineMath math="FA" />, the horizontal distance from <InlineMath math="A" /> to the foot, not the height.

So the tower is <InlineMath math="30\text{ m}" /> tall.
