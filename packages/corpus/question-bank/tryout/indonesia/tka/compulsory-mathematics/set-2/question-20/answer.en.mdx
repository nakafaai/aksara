An angle of depression from the top equals the angle of elevation from the boat to the top, because they are alternate angles at parallel lines. So there are **two right triangles** sharing the leg <InlineMath math="30\text{ m}" />. The tangent gives each boat's horizontal distance from the foot of the lighthouse.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\tan45^\circ=\frac{30}{d_1}\ &\Rightarrow\ d_1=30\text{ m} \\
\tan30^\circ=\frac{30}{d_2}\ &\Rightarrow\ d_2=\frac{30}{\frac{1}{\sqrt3}}=30\sqrt3\text{ m} \\
d_2-d_1&=30\sqrt3-30=30(\sqrt3-1)\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="30(\sqrt3-1)\approx30\cdot0.732\approx22\text{ m}" />. The farther boat is about <InlineMath math="52\text{ m}" /> from the lighthouse, and the nearer one <InlineMath math="30\text{ m}" />.

So the boats are <InlineMath math="30(\sqrt3-1)\text{ m}" /> apart.
