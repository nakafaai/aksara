Let <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" /> be the solution masses in kilograms. Both total mass and sugar mass are conserved during mixing.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A+B&=12 \\
0.20A+0.50B&=0.325(12) \\
&=3.9
\end{aligned}" />

Substitute <InlineMath math="A=12-B" /> into the sugar-mass equation.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
0.20(12-B)+0.50B&=3.9 \\
2.4+0.30B&=3.9 \\
0.30B&=1.5 \\
B&=5
\end{aligned}" />

The technician uses <InlineMath math="5\,\mathrm{kg}" /> of solution B and <InlineMath math="7\,\mathrm{kg}" /> of solution A. Their sugar mass is <InlineMath math="0.20(7)+0.50(5)=3.9\,\mathrm{kg}" />, matching the mixture.
