Unroll the cylinder’s **lateral surface** into a rectangle. Diametrically opposite points are half a circumference apart horizontally, giving <InlineMath math="\pi r=3\pi\,\mathrm{cm}" />. Their vertical separation remains <InlineMath math="8\,\mathrm{cm}" />.

The shortest path becomes a straight segment on the unrolled surface. Pythagoras gives its squared length.

<BlockMath math="L^2=8^2+(3\pi)^2=64+9\pi^2" />

Either direction around the cylinder gives the same distance. Extra turns increase the horizontal travel. The minimum squared length is therefore <InlineMath math="(64+9\pi^2)\,\mathrm{cm}^2" />.
