Three unknown prices and three days give a **system of linear equations in three variables**. Let <InlineMath math="t" />, <InlineMath math="m" /> and <InlineMath math="i" /> be the prices in thousands of rupiah. Divide each equation by a common factor to keep the numbers small.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
20t+30m+10i=580\ &\Rightarrow\ 2t+3m+i=58 \\
15t+20m+25i=590\ &\Rightarrow\ 3t+4m+5i=118 \\
10t+40m+20i=640\ &\Rightarrow\ t+4m+2i=64
\end{aligned}" />

Solve the first equation for <InlineMath math="i" /> and substitute into the other two.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
i&=58-2t-3m \\
3t+4m+5(58-2t-3m)=118\ &\Rightarrow\ 7t+11m=172 \\
t+4m+2(58-2t-3m)=64\ &\Rightarrow\ 3t+2m=52
\end{aligned}" />

From <InlineMath math="3t+2m=52" />, <InlineMath math="m=26-1.5t" />. Substituting gives <InlineMath math="7t+286-16.5t=172" />, so <InlineMath math="t=12" />, <InlineMath math="m=8" /> and <InlineMath math="i=58-24-24=10" />.

As a check on Wednesday, <InlineMath math="10\cdot12+40\cdot8+20\cdot10=120+320+200=640" />.
