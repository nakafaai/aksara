An absolute value is nonnegative, so <InlineMath math="x+4\ge0" />. Split the calculation according to the sign of <InlineMath math="2x-3" />.

If <InlineMath math="x\ge\frac32" />, the absolute value equals <InlineMath math="2x-3" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x-3&=x+4 \\
x&=7
\end{aligned}" />

If <InlineMath math="x<\frac32" />, the absolute value equals <InlineMath math="3-2x" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3-2x&=x+4 \\
-3x&=1 \\
x&=-\frac13
\end{aligned}" />

Both solutions satisfy their case restrictions and <InlineMath math="x\ge-4" />. Their product is:

<BlockMath math="7\left(-\frac13\right)=-\frac73" />
