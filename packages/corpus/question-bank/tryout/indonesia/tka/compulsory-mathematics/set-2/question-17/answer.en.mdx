The base area is <InlineMath math="2(1.5)=3\,\mathrm{m}^2" />. Initial volume equals base area times depth. The **difference between inflow and outflow** determines the added volume.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
V_0&=3(0.8)=2.4\,\mathrm{m}^3 \\
\Delta V&=(18-6)40=480\,\mathrm{L} \\
&=\frac{480}{1000}\,\mathrm{m}^3=0.48\,\mathrm{m}^3 \\
V&=2.4+0.48=2.88\,\mathrm{m}^3
\end{aligned}" />

Divide the final volume by the base area to find the depth.

<BlockMath math="h=\frac{2.88}{3}=0.96\,\mathrm{m}" />
