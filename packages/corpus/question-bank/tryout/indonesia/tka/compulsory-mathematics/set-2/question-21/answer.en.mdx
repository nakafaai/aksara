For the mean from a frequency table, **each value counts as often as it occurs**. Multiply each temperature by its number of days, add, and divide by the <InlineMath math="12" /> days.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{sum}&=30\cdot1+31\cdot2+32\cdot4+33\cdot2+34\cdot2+36\cdot1 \\
&=30+62+128+66+68+36=390 \\
\bar x&=\frac{390}{12}=32.5
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, the frequencies add to <InlineMath math="1+2+4+2+2+1=12" />.

So the mean is <InlineMath math="32.5^\circ\text{C}" />.

- <InlineMath math="32^\circ\text{C}" /> is the median and also the mode, not the mean.
- About <InlineMath math="32.7^\circ\text{C}" /> is the mean of the six different temperatures, without weighting by the days.
- <InlineMath math="33^\circ\text{C}" /> is halfway between the smallest and largest values, <InlineMath math="\frac{30+36}{2}" />.
- <InlineMath math="65^\circ\text{C}" /> divides the sum <InlineMath math="390" /> by the <InlineMath math="6" /> rows instead of the <InlineMath math="12" /> days.
