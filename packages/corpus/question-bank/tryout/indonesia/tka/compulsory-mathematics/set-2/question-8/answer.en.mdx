# Splitting into three groups

The chosen numbers are divisible by <InlineMath math="4" /> only or by <InlineMath math="6" /> only. The numbers divisible by both are the multiples of <InlineMath math="12" />, because <InlineMath math="12" /> is the **least common multiple** of <InlineMath math="4" /> and <InlineMath math="6" />. So you need three sums: multiples of <InlineMath math="4" />, multiples of <InlineMath math="6" />, and multiples of <InlineMath math="12" />.

# Three arithmetic series

Each group is an arithmetic series. **Its sum is the number of terms times the average of the first and last terms**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
4+8+\dots+100&=\tfrac{25}{2}(4+100)=1{,}300 \\
6+12+\dots+96&=\tfrac{16}{2}(6+96)=816 \\
12+24+\dots+96&=\tfrac{8}{2}(12+96)=432
\end{aligned}" />

# Removing the multiples of twelve twice

The multiples of <InlineMath math="12" /> show up in the sum for <InlineMath math="4" /> and again in the sum for <InlineMath math="6" />. Since they mustn't be chosen at all, **subtract their sum twice**, not once.

<BlockMath math="1{,}300+816-2\cdot432=1{,}252" />

So the sum of all the chosen numbers is <InlineMath math="1{,}252" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

Most wrong options come from **how the numbers divisible by both are handled**.

- <InlineMath math="2{,}116" /> doesn't remove the multiples of <InlineMath math="12" /> at all, so they're counted twice.
- <InlineMath math="1{,}684" /> removes the multiples of <InlineMath math="12" /> only once. That's the sum of numbers divisible by <InlineMath math="4" /> or <InlineMath math="6" />, including those divisible by both.
- <InlineMath math="1{,}636" /> assumes the numbers divisible by both are the multiples of <InlineMath math="4\cdot6=24" />. But <InlineMath math="12" /> and <InlineMath math="36" /> are divisible by both <InlineMath math="4" /> and <InlineMath math="6" /> too.
- <InlineMath math="868" /> counts only the numbers divisible by <InlineMath math="4" /> but not by <InlineMath math="6" />, and forgets the opposite group.
