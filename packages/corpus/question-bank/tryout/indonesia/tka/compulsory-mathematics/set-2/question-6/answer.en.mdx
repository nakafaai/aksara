The inverse reverses the mapping: **<InlineMath math="f^{-1}(2)=a" /> means <InlineMath math="f(a)=2" />**. So you do not need the whole inverse function, only the solution of <InlineMath math="f(a)=2" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{3a-2}{a+4}&=2 \\
3a-2&=2a+8 \\
a&=10
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="f(10)=\frac{30-2}{14}=\frac{28}{14}=2" />.

So <InlineMath math="f^{-1}(2)=10" />.
