Adding pure water changes the total mass while the sugar mass remains <InlineMath math="0.325(12)=3.9\,\mathrm{kg}" />. Let <InlineMath math="w" /> be the added water mass in kilograms.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{3.9}{12+w}&=0.25 \\
12+w&=\frac{3.9}{0.25}=15.6 \\
w&=3.6
\end{aligned}" />

The required water mass is <InlineMath math="3.6\,\mathrm{kg}" />. The final mixture weighs <InlineMath math="15.6\,\mathrm{kg}" />, and <InlineMath math="25\%" /> of that is the unchanged <InlineMath math="3.9\,\mathrm{kg}" /> of sugar.
