The price per GB is **package price divided by data allowance**. The validity period plays no part in it. Compute the value for each package.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Hemat}&:\ \frac{75{,}000}{25}=3{,}000 \\
\text{Jumbo}&:\ \frac{110{,}000}{40}=2{,}750 \\
\text{Harian}&:\ \frac{8{,}000}{2}=4{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="40\cdot2{,}750=110{,}000" />.

So the cheapest is the Jumbo package at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}750" /> per GB.
