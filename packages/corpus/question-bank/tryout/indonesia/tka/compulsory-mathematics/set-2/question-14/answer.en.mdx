A rotation about a point <InlineMath math="P" /> other than the origin takes three steps: **form the vector from <InlineMath math="P" /> to <InlineMath math="A" />, rotate that vector, and attach the result to <InlineMath math="P" /> again**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\overrightarrow{PA}&=(1-(-1),\ 5-2)=(2,3) \\
\text{rotated}&:\ (2,3)\mapsto(-3,2) \\
A'&=P+(-3,2)=(-1-3,\ 2+2)=(-4,4)
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="PA=\sqrt{2^2+3^2}=\sqrt{13}" /> and <InlineMath math="PA'=\sqrt{(-3)^2+2^2}=\sqrt{13}" />. Also <InlineMath math="(2,3)\cdot(-3,2)=-6+6=0" />, so the two vectors are perpendicular.

So the image is <InlineMath math="(-4,4)" />.
