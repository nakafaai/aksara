A dilation centered at <InlineMath math="C" /> satisfies <InlineMath math="A'=C+3(A-C)" />. Rearrange to find the center.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A'&=3A-2C \\
2C&=3A-A' \\
&=(6,3)-(8,10) \\
&=(-2,-7) \\
C&=\left(-1,-\frac72\right)
\end{aligned}" />

Add the center’s coordinates and multiply by <InlineMath math="2" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2(x_C+y_C)&=2\left(-1-\frac72\right) \\
&=-9
\end{aligned}" />
