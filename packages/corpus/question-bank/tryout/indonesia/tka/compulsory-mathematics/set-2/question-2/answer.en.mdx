The **scale** means <InlineMath math="1\,\mathrm{cm}" /> on the map represents <InlineMath math="25{,}000\,\mathrm{cm}" /> on the ground. Since <InlineMath math="1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}" />, divide the length in centimetres by 100 before adding the detour.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
d&=3.6\cdot25{,}000=90{,}000\,\mathrm{cm} \\
&=\frac{90{,}000}{100}\,\mathrm{m}=900\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}" />

The distance increases by <InlineMath math="15\%" />, so multiply by <InlineMath math="1.15" />.

<BlockMath math="900\left(1+\frac{15}{100}\right)=900(1.15)=1{,}035\,\mathrm{m}" />
