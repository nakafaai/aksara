The cavity of the mould is **the large cylinder minus the cylinder of the hole**. Both have the same height, so the volume is <InlineMath math="\pi\,(R^2-r^2)\,h" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
R^2-r^2&=14^2-7^2=196-49=147 \\
V&=\frac{22}{7}\cdot147\cdot5=22\cdot21\cdot5=2{,}310\text{ cm}^3
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, the large cylinder holds <InlineMath math="\frac{22}{7}\cdot196\cdot5=3{,}080" /> and the hole <InlineMath math="\frac{22}{7}\cdot49\cdot5=770" />, and the difference is <InlineMath math="2{,}310" />.

So it takes <InlineMath math="2{,}310\text{ cm}^3" /> of batter.
