The mould holds **the large cylinder minus the cylinder of the hole**. Both have the same height, so the volume is <InlineMath math="\pi\,(R^2-r^2)\,h" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}" />

As a check, the large cylinder holds <InlineMath math="\frac{22}{7}\cdot110.25\cdot7=2{,}425.5" /> and the hole <InlineMath math="\frac{22}{7}\cdot12.25\cdot7=269.5" />, and the difference is <InlineMath math="2{,}156" />.
