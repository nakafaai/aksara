The region between the minor arc and chord is a **circular segment**. Its area is the sector area minus the triangle formed by the radii.

<BlockMath math="A_1=\frac{120}{360}\pi(12)^2=48\pi" />

For a triangle with two known sides and their included angle, use the sine of that angle to find its area.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A_2&=\frac12(12)(12)\sin120^\circ \\
&=72\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=36\sqrt3
\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the triangle area from the sector area.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&=A_1-A_2 \\
&=(48\pi-36\sqrt3)\,\mathrm{cm}^2
\end{aligned}" />
