The question asks for the smallest number of fans. Three conditions work at once: all the fans **together have to move the whole air volume within <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes**, the number of fans is a whole number, and both end walls get the same number of fans.

# The air volume

The air space is made of two stacked parts. The lower part is a cuboid as tall as the walls. The upper part is a **half cylinder** with radius half the diameter, <InlineMath math="r=\frac{7}{2}=3.5\text{ m}" />, and length <InlineMath math="12\text{ m}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
V_{\text{cuboid}}&=12\cdot7\cdot1.5=126\text{ m}^3 \\
V_{\text{roof}}&=\tfrac12\cdot\pi r^2\cdot12=\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac{22}{7}\cdot\tfrac{49}{4}\cdot12=231\text{ m}^3 \\
V&=126+231=357\text{ m}^3
\end{aligned}" />

# Air moved by one fan

The fan's airflow is given per hour, but the condition is per <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes. Match the units first. One hour is <InlineMath math="60" /> minutes, so **<InlineMath math="2" /> minutes is <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{30}" /> of an hour**.

<BlockMath math="\frac{1{,}200\text{ m}^3}{30}=40\text{ m}^3\text{ every }2\text{ minutes}" />

# The number of fans

With <InlineMath math="n" /> fans, the air moved in <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes is <InlineMath math="40n" />. That has to be at least <InlineMath math="357" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
40n&\ge357 \\
n&\ge\frac{357}{40}=8.925
\end{aligned}" />

The smallest whole number that works is <InlineMath math="9" />. But the fans are split equally between two end walls, so the total has to be an **even number**. The smallest even number that's at least <InlineMath math="9" /> is <InlineMath math="10" />, which means <InlineMath math="5" /> fans on each end.

Check: <InlineMath math="10" /> fans move <InlineMath math="400\text{ m}^3" /> in <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes, enough for <InlineMath math="357\text{ m}^3" />. The next even number down, <InlineMath math="8" /> fans, moves only <InlineMath math="320\text{ m}^3" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="9" /> fans are enough for the volume, but <InlineMath math="9" /> **can't be split equally** between two end walls.
- <InlineMath math="8" /> fans come from rounding <InlineMath math="8.925" /> down. Even a small shortfall means the air isn't replaced within <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes, so you have to round up.
- <InlineMath math="6" /> fans come from counting only the roof volume, <InlineMath math="231\text{ m}^3" />. The space as tall as the brick walls holds air too.
- <InlineMath math="5" /> is the number of fans on **one** end wall, not the total.

So the smallest number of fans to install is <InlineMath math="10" /> fans.
