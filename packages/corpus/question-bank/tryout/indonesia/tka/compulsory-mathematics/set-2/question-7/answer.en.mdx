# Area as a quadratic function

The question asks for the largest area of the pen. Two details are easy to miss: **the gate gap saves wire**, and the side along the wall **can't be longer than <InlineMath math="20\text{ m}" />**.

Let each side perpendicular to the wall be <InlineMath math="x" /> metres and the side parallel to the wall be <InlineMath math="y" /> metres. Wire goes on both <InlineMath math="x" /> sides and on the <InlineMath math="y" /> side except for the <InlineMath math="2\text{ m}" /> gap:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x+(y-2)&=46 \\
y&=48-2x \\
L(x)&=x(48-2x)=-2x^2+48x=-2(x-12)^2+288
\end{aligned}" />

# The vertex isn't allowed

The last form shows the vertex at <InlineMath math="x=12" /> with area <InlineMath math="288" />. But for <InlineMath math="x=12" /> the side along the wall is <InlineMath math="y=48-24=24\text{ m}" />, longer than the wall. So that vertex **doesn't meet the condition**.

The wall limits the values of <InlineMath math="x" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
48-2x&\le20 \\
x&\ge14
\end{aligned}" />

For <InlineMath math="x\ge14" /> you're already to the right of the vertex, where <InlineMath math="L(x)" /> keeps falling as <InlineMath math="x" /> grows. So the largest area is at the boundary, <InlineMath math="x=14" />:

<BlockMath math="L(14)=14\cdot(48-28)=14\cdot20=280" />

Check: the wire used is <InlineMath math="2\cdot14+(20-2)=46\text{ m}" />, and the <InlineMath math="20\text{ m}" /> side fits the wall exactly. For comparison, <InlineMath math="x=15" /> gives <InlineMath math="15\cdot18=270" />, which is smaller.

So the largest area of the pen is <InlineMath math="280\text{ m}^2" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="288\text{ m}^2" /> is the area at the vertex. Its size is <InlineMath math="12\times24" />, but **a <InlineMath math="24\text{ m}" /> side doesn't fit along a <InlineMath math="20\text{ m}" /> wall**.
- <InlineMath math="264.5\text{ m}^2" /> forgets the gate gap and the wall limit. From <InlineMath math="2x+y=46" /> the vertex is <InlineMath math="11.5\times23" />.
- <InlineMath math="260\text{ m}^2" /> uses the wall limit but forgets the gap. From <InlineMath math="2x+20=46" /> you get <InlineMath math="x=13" />, so the area is <InlineMath math="13\cdot20" />.
- <InlineMath math="252\text{ m}^2" /> takes the gap off the side length, so the side along the wall becomes <InlineMath math="18\text{ m}" />. The gap reduces the wire you need, not the length of the pen's side.
