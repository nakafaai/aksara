The discounts and voucher determine the taxable prices. All prices below are in thousands of rupiah. For Promotion A, apply the discount before adding tax.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P_A&=800(1-0.25)(1+0.11) \\
&=600(1.11)=666
\end{aligned}" />

For Promotion B, deduct the voucher before charging tax.

Let <InlineMath math="B" /> be the taxable price after the discount and voucher.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
B&=800(1-0.20)-50 \\
&=640-50=590 \\
P_B&=B(1+0.11) \\
&=590(1.11)=654.9
\end{aligned}" />

The difference between the payments is:

<BlockMath math="P_A-P_B=666-654.9=11.1" />

Promotion B is <InlineMath math="11.1" /> thousand rupiah cheaper.
