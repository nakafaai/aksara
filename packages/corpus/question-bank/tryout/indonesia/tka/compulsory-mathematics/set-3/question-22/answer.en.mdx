For grouped data, **the median and the quartiles are located with the cumulative frequencies**, and the mean uses the midpoint of each class.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{cumulative frequency}&:\ 4,\ 13,\ 25,\ 35,\ 40 \\
\bar x&=\frac{4\cdot7+9\cdot12+12\cdot17+10\cdot22+5\cdot27}{40}=\frac{695}{40}=17.375
\end{aligned}" />

- The median is the mean of the <InlineMath math="20" />th and <InlineMath math="21" />st values. The cumulative frequency passes <InlineMath math="20" /> in the class <InlineMath math="15" /> to <InlineMath math="19" /> (from <InlineMath math="13" /> to <InlineMath math="25" />), so this statement is true.
- The mean is <InlineMath math="17.375" /> minutes, more than <InlineMath math="17" />. This statement is true.
- The largest frequency, <InlineMath math="12" />, is in the class <InlineMath math="15" /> to <InlineMath math="19" />, so the mode is not in the class <InlineMath math="20" /> to <InlineMath math="24" />. This statement is false.
- <InlineMath math="4+9=13" /> students need less than <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes, fewer than half of <InlineMath math="40" />. This statement is false.
- The lower quartile is the <InlineMath math="10" />th value. The cumulative frequency passes <InlineMath math="10" /> in the class <InlineMath math="10" /> to <InlineMath math="14" /> (from <InlineMath math="4" /> to <InlineMath math="13" />), so this statement is true.

So the true statements are the ones about the median, the mean and the lower quartile.
