Water that is poured **keeps its volume**. Work out the volume of the pyramid, then divide by the base area of the box to get the water's height.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
V&=\frac13\cdot(12\cdot12)\cdot15=\frac13\cdot144\cdot15=720\text{ cm}^3 \\
h&=\frac{720}{10\cdot8}=\frac{720}{80}=9\text{ cm}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="10\cdot8\cdot9=720" />, the volume of the pyramid.

So the water in the box stands <InlineMath math="9\text{ cm}" /> high.
