A question that is neither geometry nor statistics is **an algebra or a probability question**. Count them and divide by <InlineMath math="40" />.

<BlockMath math="P=\frac{12+10}{40}=\frac{22}{40}=\frac{11}{20}" />

As a check with the complement, <InlineMath math="10+8=18" /> questions are geometry or statistics, and <InlineMath math="1-\frac{18}{40}=\frac{22}{40}" />.

So the probability is <InlineMath math="\frac{11}{20}" />.

- <InlineMath math="\frac15" /> is the probability of a statistics question, <InlineMath math="\frac{8}{40}" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac14" /> is the probability of a geometry question, <InlineMath math="\frac{10}{40}" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{9}{20}" /> is the probability of a geometry or statistics question, the opposite of what is asked.
- <InlineMath math="\frac34" /> leaves out only the geometry questions, <InlineMath math="\frac{30}{40}" />.
