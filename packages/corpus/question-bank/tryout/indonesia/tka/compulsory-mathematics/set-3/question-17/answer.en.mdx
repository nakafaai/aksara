The question asks for the fewest bags of cement for both stages. The key rule is that **each stage buys its own cement in whole bags**: you round up within each stage, not on the combined total.

# The concrete volume of each stage

From the garage floor to the terrace there are <InlineMath math="11" /> risers, including the last one up to the terrace. So one riser is <InlineMath math="\frac{220}{11}=20\text{ cm}" />. Every block stands on the garage floor, so its height is the height of its top surface. **The upper steps are still measured from the garage floor**, not from the landing.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Stage I, steps}&:\ 30\cdot100\cdot(20+40+60+80+100)=900{,}000\text{ cm}^3=0.9\text{ m}^3 \\
\text{Stage I, landing}&:\ 100\cdot100\cdot120=1{,}200{,}000\text{ cm}^3=1.2\text{ m}^3 \\
V_{\text{I}}&=0.9+1.2=2.1\text{ m}^3 \\
V_{\text{II}}&=30\cdot100\cdot(140+160+180+200)=2{,}040{,}000\text{ cm}^3=2.04\text{ m}^3
\end{aligned}" />

# The cement for each stage

One cubic metre of mix needs <InlineMath math="360\text{ kg}" /> of cement, which is <InlineMath math="\frac{360}{40}=9" /> bags. The mix prepared is <InlineMath math="10\%" /> more than the stage volume, so multiply the volume by <InlineMath math="1.1" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Stage I}&:\ 1.1\cdot2.1\cdot9=20.79\text{ bags}\ \Rightarrow\ 21\text{ bags} \\
\text{Stage II}&:\ 1.1\cdot2.04\cdot9=20.196\text{ bags}\ \Rightarrow\ 21\text{ bags} \\
\text{Total}&=21+21=42\text{ bags}
\end{aligned}" />

Each stage rounds up because bags are only sold whole. Twenty bags aren't enough for stage I, and the cement left over from stage I can't be used in stage II. **If you bought everything at once**, you'd need <InlineMath math="20.79+20.196=40.986" /> bags, so <InlineMath math="41" /> would do. But buying per stage makes one extra bag unavoidable.

So Pak Darmo has to buy at least <InlineMath math="42" /> bags of cement.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="41" /> bags rounds the two stages together. That breaks the rule that each stage buys its own cement and **the leftovers aren't reused**.
- <InlineMath math="38" /> bags forgets the extra <InlineMath math="10\%" />: <InlineMath math="2.1\cdot9=18.9" /> becomes <InlineMath math="19" />, and <InlineMath math="2.04\cdot9=18.36" /> becomes <InlineMath math="19" />.
- <InlineMath math="30" /> bags forgets the landing block in stage I. Stage I then has only <InlineMath math="0.9\text{ m}^3" />, and <InlineMath math="0.9\cdot9.9=8.91" /> becomes <InlineMath math="9" /> bags.
- <InlineMath math="27" /> bags measures the upper steps from the landing surface, so stage II is only <InlineMath math="30\cdot100\cdot(20+40+60+80)=0.6\text{ m}^3" /> and needs <InlineMath math="6" /> bags. The staircase is solid, so the concrete under the upper steps goes down to the garage floor.
