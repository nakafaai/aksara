The path reduces **both ends of each dimension**. The inner rectangle’s area equals the garden area plus the pool area.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="744+12\cdot6=816" />

  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(40-2x)(30-2x)&=816 \\
1{,}200-140x+4x^2&=816 \\
4x^2-140x+384&=0 \\
x^2-35x+96&=0 \\
(x-3)(x-32)&=0
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

The candidate widths are <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="32" />. The width must satisfy <InlineMath math="0<x<15" /> so the shorter inner side remains positive. Thus <InlineMath math="x=3\,\mathrm{m}" />.

The inner sides are <InlineMath math="40-2(3)=34" /> and <InlineMath math="30-2(3)=24" />. Check the garden area: <InlineMath math="34\cdot24-12\cdot6=816-72=744" />.
