# The total time of class XI A

**Mean times number of values gives the total**. Use this for all <InlineMath math="24" /> students and for XI B, then subtract:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}" />

Read each row of the diagram: the stem is the whole seconds, the leaf is the tenths. # Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="11.3" /> seconds treats <InlineMath math="14.2" /> as the XI A mean, so the XI A total becomes <InlineMath math="14\cdot14.2=198.8" />. But <InlineMath math="14.2" /> is the mean of both classes together.
- <InlineMath math="14.2" /> seconds fills the missing value with the combined mean. A missing value isn't automatically equal to the mean.
- <InlineMath math="14.8" /> seconds uses a plain average of the two class means: <InlineMath math="\frac{\bar x_A+13.95}{2}=14.2" /> gives <InlineMath math="\bar x_A=14.45" /> and a total of <InlineMath math="202.3" />. **The two classes aren't the same size**, so that formula is wrong.
- <InlineMath math="16.2" /> seconds swaps the weights: <InlineMath math="10\,\bar x_A+14\cdot13.95=340.8" /> gives <InlineMath math="\bar x_A=14.55" /> and a total of <InlineMath math="203.7" />. XI A carries weight <InlineMath math="14" /> and XI B carries weight <InlineMath math="10" />.
