The ratio of the fifth term to the second is the cube of the common ratio <InlineMath math="r" />. A negative ratio makes the term signs alternate.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
r^3&=\frac{162}{-6}=-27 \\
r&=-3 \\
u_1&=\frac{-6}{-3}=2
\end{aligned}" />

Use <InlineMath math="u_n=2(-3)^{n-1}" /> to obtain the terms and their sum.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
u_3&=2(-3)^2=18 \\
u_4&=2(-3)^3=-54 \\
S_5&=2-6+18-54+162 \\
&=122 \\
u_6&=2(-3)^5=-486
\end{aligned}" />

The statements about <InlineMath math="u_3" />, <InlineMath math="u_4" />, and <InlineMath math="S_5" /> are true. The claim <InlineMath math="u_6=486" /> is false.
