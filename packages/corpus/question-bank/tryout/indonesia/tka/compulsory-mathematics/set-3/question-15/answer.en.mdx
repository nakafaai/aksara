# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="(0,-3)" /> can come from two slips. You used <InlineMath math="C(6,5)" />, which is above <InlineMath math="AB" />, or you stopped after the first reflection in <InlineMath math="x=3" />. **You need both the below-<InlineMath math="AB" /> condition and the second reflection**.
- <InlineMath math="(-1,-2)" /> treats the two reflections as a <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> clockwise rotation about <InlineMath math="(3,1)" />. Reflections in two perpendicular lines give a rotation through twice the angle between them, <InlineMath math="2\cdot90^\circ=180^\circ" />.
- <InlineMath math="(4,5)" /> comes from a sign slip when finding <InlineMath math="a" />. The equation is <InlineMath math="2a-(-2)=8" />, so <InlineMath math="2a+2=8" /> and <InlineMath math="a=3" />, not <InlineMath math="2a-2=8" />, which gives <InlineMath math="a=5" />.
- <InlineMath math="(12,-1)" /> adds <InlineMath math="(2a,2b)=(6,2)" /> to the coordinates of <InlineMath math="C" />. The correct rule is <InlineMath math="(2a-x,\ 2b-y)" />: you subtract the coordinates of <InlineMath math="C" /> from <InlineMath math="2a" /> and <InlineMath math="2b" />, you don't add them.
