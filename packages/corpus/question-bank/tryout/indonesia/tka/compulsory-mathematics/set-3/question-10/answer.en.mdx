A point on <InlineMath math="y=x+1" /> can be written <InlineMath math="P=(t,t+1)" />. Compare **squared distances** from the two given points.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
PA^2&=(t+2)^2+(t-3)^2 \\
&=t^2+4t+4+t^2-6t+9 \\
&=2t^2-2t+13 \\
PB^2&=(t-6)^2+(t+1)^2 \\
&=t^2-12t+36+t^2+2t+1 \\
&=2t^2-10t+37
\end{aligned}" />

Equate the squared distances and simplify.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2t^2-2t+13&=2t^2-10t+37 \\
8t&=24 \\
t&=3
\end{aligned}" />

Thus <InlineMath math="P=(3,4)" />. Its squared distance from the origin is:

<BlockMath math="OP^2=3^2+4^2=25" />
