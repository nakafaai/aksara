The horizontal distance, the difference in height and the path form a right triangle. **The tangent of an angle is the opposite side over the adjacent side**: opposite <InlineMath math="\alpha" /> is the difference in height, and adjacent is the horizontal distance <InlineMath math="1{,}560\text{ m}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\tan\alpha&=\frac{h}{1{,}560} \\
h&=1{,}560\cdot\frac{5}{12}=650\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, the triangle is similar to the <InlineMath math="5" />-<InlineMath math="12" />-<InlineMath math="13" /> triangle with factor <InlineMath math="130" />: <InlineMath math="h=650" />, horizontal distance <InlineMath math="1{,}560" />, and path length <InlineMath math="1{,}690" />.

So the difference in height is <InlineMath math="650\text{ m}" />.

- <InlineMath math="1{,}690\text{ m}" /> is the length of the path, not the difference in height.
