The horizontal distance, the difference in height and the path form a right triangle. **The tangent of an angle is the opposite side over the adjacent side**: opposite <InlineMath math="\alpha" /> is the difference in height, and adjacent is the horizontal distance <InlineMath math="1{,}640\text{ m}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\tan\alpha&=\frac{h}{1{,}640} \\
h&=1{,}640\cdot\frac{9}{40}=369\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />
