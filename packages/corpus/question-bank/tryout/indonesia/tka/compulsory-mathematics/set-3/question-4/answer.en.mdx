A fraction of the tank becomes litres only once the **tank capacity** is known. The <InlineMath math="27" /> litres fill exactly the gap between the two levels.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{15}{16}-\frac38=\frac{15}{16}-\frac{6}{16}=\frac{9}{16}\ &\Rightarrow\ \text{tank}=27\div\frac{9}{16}=48\text{ litres} \\
\frac{15}{16}-\frac14=\frac{11}{16}\ &\Rightarrow\ \frac{11}{16}\cdot48=33\text{ litres} \\
33\cdot12&=396\text{ km}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="\frac38\cdot48=18" /> litres plus <InlineMath math="27" /> litres give <InlineMath math="45=\frac{15}{16}\cdot48" /> litres.

So the car travels <InlineMath math="396\text{ km}" />.
