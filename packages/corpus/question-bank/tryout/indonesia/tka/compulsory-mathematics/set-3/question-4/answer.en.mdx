The question asks for <InlineMath math="8^{x}-8^{-x}" />, not for <InlineMath math="x" /> itself. So you don't have to solve for <InlineMath math="x" /> directly. **Rewrite every base as <InlineMath math="2" />**, then work with algebraic identities. # Rewrite with base two

Let <InlineMath math="a=2^{x}" /> and <InlineMath math="b=2^{-x}" />. Both are positive, and **their product is always <InlineMath math="1" />**, because <InlineMath math="ab=2^{x}\cdot2^{-x}=2^{0}=1" />. Now rewrite both terms on the left-hand side.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
4^{x+1}&=4\cdot4^{x}=4\left(2^{x}\right)^{2}=4a^{2} \\
2^{2-2x}&=2^{2}\cdot2^{-2x}=4\left(2^{-x}\right)^{2}=4b^{2} \\
4a^{2}+4b^{2}&=72\ \Rightarrow\ a^{2}+b^{2}=18
\end{aligned}" />

# The sign from x being negative

You can get the square of the difference from <InlineMath math="a^{2}+b^{2}" /> and <InlineMath math="ab" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(a-b)^{2}&=a^{2}+b^{2}-2ab=18-2=16 \\
a-b&=4\ \text{ or }\ a-b=-4
\end{aligned}" />

Draw the left side as a function of <InlineMath math="x" />. **The graph is symmetric about the vertical axis, so the line <InlineMath math="y=72" /> meets it at two points of opposite sign**. # The difference of cubes

Since <InlineMath math="8^{x}=\left(2^{x}\right)^{3}=a^{3}" /> and <InlineMath math="8^{-x}=b^{3}" />, you're looking for <InlineMath math="a^{3}-b^{3}" />. Use the **difference of two cubes** formula.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
a^{3}-b^{3}&=(a-b)\left(a^{2}+ab+b^{2}\right) \\
&=(-4)(18+1)=-76
\end{aligned}" />

Check with the actual values: from <InlineMath math="a-b=-4" /> and <InlineMath math="ab=1" /> you get <InlineMath math="a=\sqrt5-2" /> and <InlineMath math="b=\sqrt5+2" />. Their product is <InlineMath math="5-4=1" />, and <InlineMath math="a^{2}+b^{2}=(9-4\sqrt5)+(9+4\sqrt5)=18" />. Everything fits.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="-72" /> forgets the <InlineMath math="ab" /> term in the second factor, so it works out <InlineMath math="(-4)\cdot18" />.
- <InlineMath math="-64" /> treats <InlineMath math="a^{3}-b^{3}" /> as <InlineMath math="(a-b)^{3}" />. **The cube of a difference isn't the difference of the cubes**.
- <InlineMath math="-4" /> stops at <InlineMath math="2^{x}-2^{-x}" /> and never moves on to the cubes.
- <InlineMath math="76" /> uses <InlineMath math="a-b=4" /> and ignores the condition <InlineMath math="x<0" />.

So <InlineMath math="8^{x}-8^{-x}=-76" />.
