# Where P is

\end{aligned}" />

# The case you drop

The point <InlineMath math="F(f,24)" /> is on the line <InlineMath math="DC" /> and satisfies <InlineMath math="FA=FP" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}" />

# The length of the fold

Now <InlineMath math="E(25,0)" /> and <InlineMath math="F(7,24)" />. Use Pythagoras a second time, with a horizontal gap of <InlineMath math="18" /> and a vertical gap of <InlineMath math="24" />.
