A rotation about a centre other than the origin takes three steps: **move the centre to the origin, rotate, then move back**. A rotation of <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> anticlockwise about the origin maps <InlineMath math="(a,b)" /> to <InlineMath math="(-b,a)" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
B-C&=(7-2,\ 3-(-1))=(5,4) \\
(5,4)&\to(-4,5) \\
B'&=(-4+2,\ 5+(-1))=(-2,4)
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="CB=\sqrt{5^2+4^2}=\sqrt{41}" /> and <InlineMath math="CB'=\sqrt{(-4)^2+5^2}=\sqrt{41}" />, while <InlineMath math="(5,4)\cdot(-4,5)=-20+20=0" />, so the two segments are perpendicular.

So the image of <InlineMath math="B" /> is <InlineMath math="(-2,4)" />.
