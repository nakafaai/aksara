Undo the transformations in reverse order. Let <InlineMath math="R" /> be the point after reflection and before dilation. With scale factor <InlineMath math="-2" />, the equation <InlineMath math="Q=C-2(R-C)" /> gives:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
R&=C-\frac12(Q-C) \\
&=(1,-2)-\frac12(6,2) \\
&=(-2,-3)
\end{aligned}" />

Reflection in <InlineMath math="y=x" /> swaps the coordinates, so the original point is <InlineMath math="P=(-3,-2)" />. Check by applying the dilation again to its reflected image.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
Q&=C-2(R-C) \\
&=(1,-2)-2(-3,-1) \\
&=(1,-2)+(6,2) \\
&=(7,0)
\end{aligned}" />
