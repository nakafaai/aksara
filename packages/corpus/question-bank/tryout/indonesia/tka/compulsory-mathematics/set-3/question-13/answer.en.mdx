# Writing the point with one letter

You don't know <InlineMath math="P" /> yet, but it lies on the line <InlineMath math="y=x+3" />. So **one letter is enough to write <InlineMath math="P" />**, for example <InlineMath math="P=(a,\,a+3)" />. That leaves only two unknowns, <InlineMath math="a" /> and <InlineMath math="k" />.

# Running both transformations

Reflecting in the line <InlineMath math="y=-x" /> **swaps the coordinates and flips both signs**: <InlineMath math="(x,y)" /> becomes <InlineMath math="(-y,-x)" />. After that, the translation adds <InlineMath math="k" /> to the first coordinate and <InlineMath math="2k" /> to the second.

<BlockMath math="(a,\,a+3)\ \longmapsto\ (-a-3,\,-a)\ \longmapsto\ (-a-3+k,\,-a+2k)" />

# Matching the final image

The final image is <InlineMath math="(1,5)" />, so **set each coordinate equal**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
-a-3+k&=1\ \Rightarrow\ -a+k=4 \\
-a+2k&=5
\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the first equation from the second and you get <InlineMath math="k=1" />. Then <InlineMath math="-a+1=4" />, so <InlineMath math="a=-3" /> and <InlineMath math="P=(-3,0)" />.

Check: <InlineMath math="(-3,0)" /> lies on <InlineMath math="y=x+3" />. Reflecting in <InlineMath math="y=-x" /> gives <InlineMath math="(0,3)" />, and shifting by <InlineMath math="(1,2)" /> gives <InlineMath math="(1,5)" />.

So the coordinates of <InlineMath math="P" /> are <InlineMath math="(-3,0)" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

Each wrong option comes from **one step that got swapped or skipped**.

- <InlineMath math="(-9,-6)" /> reflects in <InlineMath math="y=x" />, which only swaps the coordinates without flipping the signs.
- <InlineMath math="(-6,-3)" /> translates first and reflects second. You can't reverse the order of the transformations.
- <InlineMath math="(1,2)" /> is the translation vector for <InlineMath math="k=1" />, not the point <InlineMath math="P" />. It doesn't even lie on <InlineMath math="y=x+3" />.
- <InlineMath math="(6,9)" /> uses a <InlineMath math="180^\circ" /> rotation about <InlineMath math="O" />, which only flips the signs without swapping the coordinates.
