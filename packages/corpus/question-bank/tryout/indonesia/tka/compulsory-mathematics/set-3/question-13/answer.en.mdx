Add the two translation vectors, then **subtract their sum** from the final point to recover the starting point.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
v&=(6,-1)+(-2,4) \\
&=(6-2,-1+4)=(4,3) \\
P&=Q-v=(10,9)-(4,3) \\
&=(10-4,9-3)=(6,6)
\end{aligned}" />

The Manhattan distance adds the absolute values of the two coordinates.

<BlockMath math="|6|+|6|=12" />
