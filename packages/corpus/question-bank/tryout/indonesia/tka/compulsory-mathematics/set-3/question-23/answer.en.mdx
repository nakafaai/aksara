# Judging each statement

First statement: the XI A mean is <InlineMath math="\frac{201.3}{14}\approx14.38" />. That's **more than <InlineMath math="14.3" />**. This statement is **True**.

Second statement: faster than Raka means a time below <InlineMath math="13.8" />. Picture all ten XI B students running exactly <InlineMath math="13.95" />. The mean is still <InlineMath math="13.95" />, but nobody is faster than Raka. One **counterexample** is enough. This statement is **False**.

Third statement: if every XI B student had a time of <InlineMath math="14.25" /> or more, their mean would also be <InlineMath math="14.25" /> or more. But the mean is <InlineMath math="13.95" />. So **at least one value has to be below <InlineMath math="14.25" />**, because a mean can't be smaller than all of its values. This statement is **True**.

So the answers are True, False, True.
