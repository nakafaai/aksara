# Frequencies and the kth value

Each class's frequency is the difference between two neighbouring cumulative rows:

| Class | <InlineMath math="0\text{ to }10" /> | <InlineMath math="10\text{ to }20" /> | <InlineMath math="20\text{ to }30" /> | <InlineMath math="30\text{ to }40" /> | <InlineMath math="40\text{ to }50" /> | <InlineMath math="50\text{ to }60" /> | <InlineMath math="60\text{ to }70" /> |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Frequency | <InlineMath math="2" /> | <InlineMath math="6" /> | <InlineMath math="24" /> | <InlineMath math="20" /> | <InlineMath math="16" /> | <InlineMath math="6" /> | <InlineMath math="6" /> |

To find the <InlineMath math="k" />th value, find the class it's in, then **move forward proportionally inside that class**. If the class's lower boundary is <InlineMath math="L" />, the number of values before the class is <InlineMath math="F" />, and the class frequency is <InlineMath math="f" />, then

<BlockMath math="k\text{th value}=L+\frac{k-F}{f}\cdot10" />
