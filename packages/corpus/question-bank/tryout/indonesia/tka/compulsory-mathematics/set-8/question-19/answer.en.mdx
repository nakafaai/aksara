The <InlineMath math="1\text{ m}" /> path runs along all four sides, so the planting part loses <InlineMath math="1" /> metre at the left end and <InlineMath math="1" /> metre at the right end, and its width loses two metres the same way. <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A_\text{plant}&=(p-2)(l-2)=pl-2(p+l)+4 \\

\end{aligned}" />

So for this perimeter, **the planting area is always the garden's area minus** <InlineMath math="46" />. The largest planting area comes from the largest garden area that's still allowed.
