# The discount changes the order

Now check the discount rule for each total. The total <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}396{,}000" /> **doesn't reach <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}400{,}000" />**, so it gets no discount. The other two totals get <InlineMath math="5\%" /> off.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2\text{ cartons}+4\text{ loose}&:\quad 396{,}000 \\
1\text{ carton}+16\text{ loose}&:\quad 408{,}000-0.05(408{,}000)=387{,}600 \\
0\text{ cartons}+28\text{ loose}&:\quad 420{,}000-0.05(420{,}000)=399{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

Compare the total after the promo and the member's bill for the three ways. **Only the first way has a total bar below the line at <InlineMath math="400" /> thousand**, so only that way misses the discount, and its bill is not the smallest.
