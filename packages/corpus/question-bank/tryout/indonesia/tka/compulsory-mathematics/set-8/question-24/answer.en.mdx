**The empirical probability is how often the event happened divided by the number of trials**. Point down happened <InlineMath math="200-74=126" /> times.

<BlockMath math="P(\text{point down})=\frac{126}{200}=0.63" />

Check: point up has empirical probability <InlineMath math="\frac{74}{200}=0.37" />, and <InlineMath math="0.37+0.63=1" />.

So the empirical probability of the drawing pin landing point down is <InlineMath math="0.63" />.
