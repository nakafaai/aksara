Each translation adds its vector to the point's coordinates, so **two translations in a row equal one translation by the sum of their vectors**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{pmatrix}2\\-3\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-5\\4\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-3\\1\end{pmatrix}" />

Check with <InlineMath math="K(0,0)" />: the first translation gives <InlineMath math="(2,-3)" />, the second gives <InlineMath math="(-3,1)" />.

So both translations can be replaced by the translation by <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix}-3\\1\end{pmatrix}" />.

- <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix}7\\-7\end{pmatrix}" /> subtracts the second vector from the first.
- <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix}3\\-1\end{pmatrix}" /> is the opposite of the right answer.
- <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix}-10\\-12\end{pmatrix}" /> multiplies matching components.
- <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix}-3\\-1\end{pmatrix}" /> gets the sign of the second component wrong, since <InlineMath math="-3+4=1" />.
