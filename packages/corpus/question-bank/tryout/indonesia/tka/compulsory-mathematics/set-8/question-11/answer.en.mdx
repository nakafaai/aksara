Each straight part of the belt is **an external common tangent** of the two wheels. Both radii to the points of contact are perpendicular to the belt, so they are parallel. Slide the belt parallel by <InlineMath math="3\text{ cm}" /> to the centre of the small wheel. That makes a right triangle with hypotenuse <InlineMath math="25\text{ cm}" />, the distance between the centres, and one leg <InlineMath math="10-3=7\text{ cm}" />.
