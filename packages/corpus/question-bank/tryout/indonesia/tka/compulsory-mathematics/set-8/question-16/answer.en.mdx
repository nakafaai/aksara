The wrapping paper covers the whole surface without overlap, so its area equals **the cuboid's surface area**. A cuboid has three pairs of equal faces: <InlineMath math="30\times20" />, <InlineMath math="30\times10" />, and <InlineMath math="20\times10" />.

<BlockMath math="L=2(30\cdot20+30\cdot10+20\cdot10)=2(600+300+200)=2{,}200" />

So the box needs <InlineMath math="2{,}200\text{ cm}^2" /> of wrapping paper.

- <InlineMath math="1{,}100\text{ cm}^2" /> counts only one face of each pair.
- <InlineMath math="1{,}200\text{ cm}^2" /> counts only the top and the bottom.
- <InlineMath math="1{,}600\text{ cm}^2" /> leaves out the pair of <InlineMath math="30\times10" /> faces.
- <InlineMath math="1{,}800\text{ cm}^2" /> leaves out the pair of <InlineMath math="20\times10" /> faces.
