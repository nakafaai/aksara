The colour of the second marble depends on the first, so **split into two cases by the colour of the first marble**, then add the two probabilities.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(\text{R then R})&=\frac35\cdot\frac24=\frac{6}{20} \\
P(\text{B then R})&=\frac25\cdot\frac34=\frac{6}{20} \\
P(\text{second red})&=\frac{6}{20}+\frac{6}{20}=\frac{12}{20}=\frac35
\end{aligned}" />

The result equals the probability that the first marble is red. Before you see the first marble, every marble is equally likely to come out second.

So the probability that the second marble is red is <InlineMath math="\frac35" />.
