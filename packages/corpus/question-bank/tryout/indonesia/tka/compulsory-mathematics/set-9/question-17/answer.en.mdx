A stone that sinks completely **pushes aside exactly its own volume of water**. So the stone's volume equals the volume of the rise, a cuboid with the aquarium's base and the rise as its height.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{rise}&=37.5-35=2.5\text{ cm} \\
V_{\text{stone}}&=80\cdot40\cdot2.5=8{,}000\text{ cm}^3
\end{aligned}" />

So the stone's volume is <InlineMath math="8{,}000\text{ cm}^3" />.
