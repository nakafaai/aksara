Equilibrium happens at **the price where both functions have the same value**. Find that price first, then substitute it into either function to get the quantity.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
120-2P&=3P-30 \\
150&=5P\ \Rightarrow\ P=30 \\
Q&=120-2\cdot30=60
\end{aligned}" />

Check with the supply function: <InlineMath math="3\cdot30-30=60" />.

So the equilibrium quantity is <InlineMath math="60" />.
