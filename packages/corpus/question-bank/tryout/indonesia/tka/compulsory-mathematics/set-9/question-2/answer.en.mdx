# The blood right after the last tablet

Every <InlineMath math="6" /> hours, everything in the blood halves, and then the next tablet adds <InlineMath math="160\text{ mg}" />. **The old tablets don't vanish**: what's left of them carries over.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{06:00}&:\ 160 \\
\text{12:00}&:\ \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot160+160=240 \\
\text{18:00}&:\ \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot240+160=280
\end{aligned}" />

After 18:00 there are no more tablets. So starting from <InlineMath math="280\text{ mg}" />, the amount only goes down.
