The question asks for the first check whose result is **less than** <InlineMath math="35\text{ mg}" />. For that you need two things: the amount of active substance right after the last tablet, and its value at every check time.

# The blood right after the last tablet

Every <InlineMath math="6" /> hours, everything in the blood halves, and then the next tablet adds <InlineMath math="160\text{ mg}" />. **The old tablets don't vanish**: what's left of them carries over.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{06:00}&:\ 160 \\
\text{12:00}&:\ \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot160+160=240 \\
\text{18:00}&:\ \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot240+160=280
\end{aligned}" />

After 18:00 there are no more tablets. So starting from <InlineMath math="280\text{ mg}" />, the amount only goes down.

# The result of each check

The checks are <InlineMath math="3" /> hours apart, which is half of <InlineMath math="6" /> hours. So each check **multiplies the amount by** <InlineMath math="\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^{1/2}=\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}" />, and two checks in a row multiply it by <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1}{2}" />.

At 12:00 the amount is exactly <InlineMath math="35\text{ mg}" />. The number <InlineMath math="35" /> isn't less than <InlineMath math="35" />, so this check **doesn't meet the condition yet**. The next check, at 15:00, shows about <InlineMath math="24.7\text{ mg}" />, and that's below <InlineMath math="35\text{ mg}" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- 06:00 comes from forgetting to add the 18:00 tablet and using <InlineMath math="120\text{ mg}" />, the amount just before that tablet. From <InlineMath math="120" />, only <InlineMath math="30" /> is left at 06:00.
- 09:00 comes from **counting only the last tablet**. From <InlineMath math="160" />, only <InlineMath math="20\sqrt{2}\approx28.2" /> is left at 09:00. But at 18:00 the two earlier tablets still leave <InlineMath math="120\text{ mg}" />, so that estimate is too low.
- 12:00 is when the amount is exactly <InlineMath math="35\text{ mg}" />. The condition is less than <InlineMath math="35" />, not less than or equal to.
- 18:00 comes from assuming the amount only drops at multiples of <InlineMath math="6" /> hours, so that it's still <InlineMath math="35" /> at 15:00. You also land here if you add the three tablets with no decrease, giving <InlineMath math="480\text{ mg}" />. The substance decreases continuously, so by 15:00 it's already below <InlineMath math="35" />.

So the earliest time the patient may get the other medicine is Tuesday at 15:00.
