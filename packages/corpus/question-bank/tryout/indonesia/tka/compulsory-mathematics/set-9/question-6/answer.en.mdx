A linear function has the form <InlineMath math="f(x)=mx+n" />. **The slope <InlineMath math="m" /> is the change in the function's value divided by the change in <InlineMath math="x" />**, then <InlineMath math="n" /> comes from one of the given values.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{f(4)-f(1)}{4-1}=\frac{-1-5}{3}=-2 \\
f(1)&=-2\cdot1+n=5\ \Rightarrow\ n=7
\end{aligned}" />

Check: <InlineMath math="f(4)=-8+7=-1" />.

So <InlineMath math="f(x)=-2x+7" />.

- <InlineMath math="f(x)=-2x+3" /> subtracts <InlineMath math="2" /> from <InlineMath math="5" />, but <InlineMath math="-2\cdot1" /> has to move to the right side as <InlineMath math="+2" />.
