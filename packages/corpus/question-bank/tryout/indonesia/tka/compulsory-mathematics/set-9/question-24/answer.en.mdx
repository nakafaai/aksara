The sample space is **all ten letters of the word**, repeats included: M, A, T, E, M, A, T, I, K, A. The letter A appears <InlineMath math="3" /> times.

<BlockMath math="P(\text{A})=\frac{3}{10}" />

So the probability that the chosen letter is A is <InlineMath math="\frac{3}{10}" />.

- <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{10}" /> counts only one A.
- <InlineMath math="\frac16" /> uses the <InlineMath math="6" /> different letters, M, A, T, E, I, K, as the sample space, but repeated letters can be chosen too.
- <InlineMath math="\frac15" /> finds only <InlineMath math="2" /> letters A.
- <InlineMath math="\frac13" /> miscounts the letters as <InlineMath math="9" />.
