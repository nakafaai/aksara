The question asks for an expected frequency: the number of rounds times the probability of winning one round. Winning has two stages: **the coin is only tossed if the card total is even**, so a win only counts after the first stage succeeds.

# The card stage

Two cards are taken at once, so order doesn't matter and the two cards are different. There are <InlineMath math="\binom52=10" /> possible pairs. A total is even when both numbers are odd or both are even:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{both odd}&:\ \{1,3\},\{1,5\},\{3,5\}\ \to\ 3 \\
\text{both even}&:\ \{2,4\}\ \to\ 1 \\
P(\text{even total})&=\frac{3+1}{10}=\frac25
\end{aligned}" />

Notice the probability **isn't <InlineMath math="\frac12" />**. There are three odd cards but only two even ones, and the same card can't be taken twice.

# The coin stage

Three tosses have <InlineMath math="2^3=8" /> equally likely outcomes. At least two number sides (the Indonesian coin's "angka" side) means **exactly two or exactly three**:

<BlockMath math="P(\text{at least 2 number sides})=\frac{\binom32+\binom33}{8}=\frac{3+1}{8}=\frac12" />

# Expected frequency

A win needs both stages to succeed, so **you multiply the probabilities**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(\text{win})&=\frac25\cdot\frac12=\frac15 \\
F_e&=60\cdot\frac15=12\text{ rounds}
\end{aligned}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="15" /> rounds assumes an even total has probability <InlineMath math="\frac12" />: <InlineMath math="60\cdot\frac12\cdot\frac12" />.
- <InlineMath math="21" /> rounds reads **at least two** as at least one, with probability <InlineMath math="\frac78" />: <InlineMath math="60\cdot\frac25\cdot\frac78" />.
- <InlineMath math="24" /> rounds forgets the coin stage and counts every even total as a win: <InlineMath math="60\cdot\frac25" />.
- <InlineMath math="30" /> rounds forgets the card stage and uses only the coin's probability: <InlineMath math="60\cdot\frac12" />.

So the expected number of rounds won is <InlineMath math="12" />.
