The angle of elevation is measured from the observer's eye. So the right triangle has a horizontal side of <InlineMath math="30" /> m at eye level, and **its vertical side is the part of the building above the eye**. The vertical side is opposite the angle and the horizontal side is next to it, so use <InlineMath math="\tan60^\circ=\sqrt3" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
h_{\text{above eye}}&=30\tan60^\circ=30\sqrt3 \\
h&=30\sqrt3+1.6
\end{aligned}" />

That is about <InlineMath math="30\cdot1.732+1.6\approx53.6" /> m.

So the building is <InlineMath math="(30\sqrt3+1.6)" /> m tall.
