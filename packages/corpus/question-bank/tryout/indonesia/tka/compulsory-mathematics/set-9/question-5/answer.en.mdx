Average speed is **total distance divided by total time**, not the mean of the two speeds. Let the distance from home to school be <InlineMath math="d" /> km. The trips there and back take different times, because the speeds differ.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{d}{12}+\frac{d}{18}=\frac{3d+2d}{36}=\frac{5d}{36} \\
\bar v&=\frac{2d}{\frac{5d}{36}}=\frac{72}{5}=14.4
\end{aligned}" />

The <InlineMath math="d" /> cancels, so the answer is the same for any distance. Check with <InlineMath math="d=36" /> km: <InlineMath math="3" /> hours there, <InlineMath math="2" /> hours back, so <InlineMath math="\frac{72}{5}=14.4" /> km per hour.

So Rina's average speed is <InlineMath math="14.4" /> km per hour.
