A dilation with scale factor <InlineMath math="3" /> multiplies every length by <InlineMath math="3" />. Area is length times length, so **the area is multiplied by <InlineMath math="3^2=9" />**.

<BlockMath math="L'=3^2\cdot12=9\cdot12=108" />

So the enlarged logo has an area of <InlineMath math="108\text{ cm}^2" />.

- <InlineMath math="4\text{ cm}^2" /> divides the area by <InlineMath math="3" />, as if the logo were shrunk.
- <InlineMath math="12\text{ cm}^2" /> assumes the area doesn't change.
- <InlineMath math="36\text{ cm}^2" /> multiplies the area by <InlineMath math="3" />, the factor for lengths.
- <InlineMath math="72\text{ cm}^2" /> multiplies the area by <InlineMath math="6" />, that is <InlineMath math="2\cdot3" />, not <InlineMath math="3^2" />.
