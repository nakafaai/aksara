In one turn without slipping, the wheel travels **exactly its circumference**. Put the distance and the circumference in centimetres.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
K&=\pi d=\frac{22}{7}\cdot70=220\text{ cm} \\
1.1\text{ km}&=110{,}000\text{ cm} \\
n&=\frac{110{,}000}{220}=500
\end{aligned}" />

So the wheel turns <InlineMath math="500" /> times.
