The horizontal and vertical differences between the points are the legs of a right triangle, and **the distance <InlineMath math="AB" /> is its hypotenuse**. Use Pythagoras' theorem.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\Delta x&=4-(-2)=6,\qquad\Delta y=-5-3=-8 \\
AB&=\sqrt{6^2+(-8)^2}=\sqrt{100}=10
\end{aligned}" />

So the distance between <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" /> is <InlineMath math="10" /> units.

- <InlineMath math="6" /> is only the horizontal difference.
- <InlineMath math="8" /> is only the size of the vertical difference.
- <InlineMath math="14" /> adds the two differences, as if walking along grid lines instead of a straight line.
- <InlineMath math="100" /> is the square of the distance, before taking the root.
