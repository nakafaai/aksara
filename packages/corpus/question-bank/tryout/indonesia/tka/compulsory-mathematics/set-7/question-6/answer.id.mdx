<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
f(g(x))&=2g(x)-3=4x^2+6x-1 \\
2g(x)&=4x^2+6x+2 \\
g(x)&=2x^2+3x+1
\end{aligned}" />

Cek: <InlineMath math="f(2x^2+3x+1)=4x^2+6x+2-3=4x^2+6x-1" />.

Jadi, <InlineMath math="g(x)=2x^2+3x+1" />.
