Equal volumes **don't guarantee equal surface areas**, so work out each design's surface area with <InlineMath math="L=2(pl+pt+lt)" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L_P&=2(200+100+50)=700\text{ cm}^2 \\
L_Q&=2(200+125+40)=730\text{ cm}^2 \\
L_R&=2(100+100+100)=600\text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}" />

Design R, the cube, has the smallest surface area. The closer a box is to a cube, the less material it needs for the same volume.

So design R needs the least material.

- All three have the same volume, but their surface areas are <InlineMath math="700" />, <InlineMath math="730" />, and <InlineMath math="600\text{ cm}^2" />.
- Design Q actually needs the most material.
- Design P needs <InlineMath math="100\text{ cm}^2" /> **more** than R, not less.
- Design Q needs <InlineMath math="30\text{ cm}^2" /> **more** than P.
