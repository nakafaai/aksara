# The highest possible salary

The total <InlineMath math="7.8" /> can be split in many ways. To make <InlineMath math="s_1" /> as large as possible, <InlineMath math="s_2" /> has to be as small as possible, which is <InlineMath math="3.5" />. Then <InlineMath math="s_1=7.8-3.5=4.3" />. **The split <InlineMath math="4.3" /> and <InlineMath math="3.5" /> meets every condition**, so this value is really reached.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3.9\text{ million}" /> assumes both new employees earn the same. The question only gives a lower limit, so their salaries may differ.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4.9\text{ million}" /> assumes the person who left earned <InlineMath math="7.2" />, the design mean, so the new employees' total reads as <InlineMath math="8.4" />. **That salary has to come from the new design mean**.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}7.8\text{ million}" /> is the two new employees' combined salary, as if the other one earned nothing.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3.5\text{ million}" /> is the lowest salary allowed, not the highest.

So the highest possible salary for one of the new employees is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4.3\text{ million}" /> per month.
