Translations, rotations, and reflections are **isometries**: they only move, turn, or flip a figure without changing its side lengths. A dilation with scale factor <InlineMath math="k" /> multiplies every length by <InlineMath math="|k|" />, so the size changes exactly when <InlineMath math="|k|\ne1" />.

<BlockMath math="\left|\tfrac12\right|=\tfrac12\ne1,\qquad|-1|=1" />

So the dilation with centre <InlineMath math="(2,0)" /> and scale factor <InlineMath math="\frac12" /> gives an image of a different size, with every length halved.

- The translation by <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix}3\\-2\end{pmatrix}" /> only slides the figure.
- The <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> rotation only turns the figure, wherever its centre is.
- The reflection in <InlineMath math="y=x" /> only flips the figure.
- The dilation with factor <InlineMath math="-1" /> flips the figure through <InlineMath math="O" />, but <InlineMath math="|-1|=1" />, so its size stays the same.
