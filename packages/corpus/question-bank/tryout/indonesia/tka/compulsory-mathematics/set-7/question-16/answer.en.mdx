The ice cream inside the cone is a cone, so its volume is **one third of the base area times the height**. The radius is half the opening's diameter, <InlineMath math="3\text{ cm}" />.

<BlockMath math="V=\tfrac13\pi r^2t=\tfrac13\cdot3.14\cdot3^2\cdot12=3.14\cdot36=113.04" />

So the ice cream inside the cone has a volume of <InlineMath math="113.04\text{ cm}^3" />.
