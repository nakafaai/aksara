All three statements are about changes in a mean salary. The safest way is to **always work with salary totals, not with means**, and divide by the number of people only at the end. Every amount below is in millions of rupiah.

# The March situation

From the table, the sewing total is <InlineMath math="15\cdot4.8=72" /> and the design total is <InlineMath math="5\cdot7.2=36" />. So the overall total and the overall mean are:

<BlockMath math="\bar{x}=\frac{72+36}{20}=\frac{108}{20}=5.4" />

When the head count stays at <InlineMath math="20" />, **the rise in the overall mean equals the rise in the total divided by <InlineMath math="20" />**. You'll use this for the first and third statements.

# Judging each statement

1. **False**. The <InlineMath math="25\%" /> raise only applies to the design total, so it adds <InlineMath math="0.25\cdot36=9" />. The overall mean goes up by <InlineMath math="\frac{9}{20}=0.45" />, which isn't more than <InlineMath math="0.5" />. Each design employee does get <InlineMath math="0.25\cdot7.2=1.8" /> more, but only <InlineMath math="5" /> of the <InlineMath math="20" /> people get it. If you take the mean of the two means, the rise looks like <InlineMath math="\frac{1.8}{2}=0.9" />, but the two departments aren't the same size.
2. **True**. The total becomes <InlineMath math="108+10\cdot7.2=180" /> for <InlineMath math="20+10=30" /> people, so the mean is <InlineMath math="\frac{180}{30}=6" />. That makes sense: both departments now have <InlineMath math="15" /> people, so the overall mean sits exactly halfway between <InlineMath math="4.8" /> and <InlineMath math="7.2" />.
3. **True**. A <InlineMath math="10\%" /> raise for sewing adds <InlineMath math="0.1\cdot72=7.2" /> to the total. A raise of <InlineMath math="1.44" /> for each design employee adds <InlineMath math="5\cdot1.44=7.2" />. Both add the same amount to the total for the same <InlineMath math="20" /> people, so the overall mean goes up by <InlineMath math="\frac{7.2}{20}=0.36" /> either way.

So the first statement is false, and the second and third statements are true.
