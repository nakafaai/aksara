The students who do both are counted in basketball and again in futsal, so **the number doing at least one activity comes from inclusion-exclusion**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
n(B\cup F)&=20+15-8=27 \\
n(\text{neither})&=36-27=9 \\
P&=\frac{9}{36}=\frac14
\end{aligned}" />

Check with a Venn diagram: basketball only <InlineMath math="12" />, futsal only <InlineMath math="7" />, both <InlineMath math="8" />, neither <InlineMath math="9" />, and <InlineMath math="12+7+8+9=36" />.

So the probability is <InlineMath math="\frac14" />.
