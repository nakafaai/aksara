Sort the eight scores: <InlineMath math="56,\ 58,\ 66,\ 68,\ 70,\ 72,\ 82,\ 88" />. There's an even number of values, so the median is the mean of the <InlineMath math="4" />th and <InlineMath math="5" />th values:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{median}&=\frac{68+70}{2}=69 \\
\text{mean}&=\frac{560}{8}=70
\end{aligned}" />
