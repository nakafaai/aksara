Each tip moves along an arc. **Its length depends on two things at once**: how far the hand turns and how long it is. In <InlineMath math="1" /> hour the minute hand makes one full turn, while the hour hand makes only <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{12}" /> of a turn.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{minute hand}&=2\pi\cdot10=20\pi \\
\text{hour hand}&=\frac{1}{12}\cdot2\pi\cdot6=\pi \\
\frac{20\pi}{\pi}&=20
\end{aligned}" />

The turns compare as <InlineMath math="12" /> and the lengths as <InlineMath math="\frac{10}{6}" />, so the result is <InlineMath math="12\cdot\frac{10}{6}=20" />.

So the minute hand's tip travels <InlineMath math="20" /> times as far as the hour hand's tip.
