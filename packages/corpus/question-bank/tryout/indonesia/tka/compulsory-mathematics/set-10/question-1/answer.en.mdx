# Breaking down the total

The question asks for the monthly installment, but the down payment isn't given. All you know is the total credit payment, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}026{,}000" />, for a <InlineMath math="12" />-month term with a flat interest of <InlineMath math="1.5\%" /> per month.

Call the principal <InlineMath math="p" /> rupiah. Then the down payment is <InlineMath math="9{,}000{,}000-p" />, because the down payment and the principal always add up to the normal price. The admin fee is <InlineMath math="1\%" /> of the principal. Over <InlineMath math="12" /> months the interest is <InlineMath math="12\cdot1.5\%=18\%" /> of the principal, and the installments together pay the principal plus all of that interest.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{total}&=\text{down payment}+\text{admin fee}+\text{all installments} \\
&=(9{,}000{,}000-p)+0.01p+(p+0.18p) \\
&=9{,}000{,}000+0.19p
\end{aligned}" />

So **everything above the normal price comes from the admin fee and the interest**, and both are proportional to the principal.

# Principal and installment

Now set that expression equal to the given total.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
9{,}000{,}000+0.19p&=10{,}026{,}000 \\
0.19p&=1{,}026{,}000 \\
p&=5{,}400{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

The down payment is <InlineMath math="9{,}000{,}000-5{,}400{,}000=3{,}600{,}000" />, which is <InlineMath math="40\%" /> of the normal price, so **the rule of at least <InlineMath math="20\%" /> down is met**. The installment is the principal plus all the interest, divided by <InlineMath math="12" />.

<BlockMath math="\text{installment}=\frac{5{,}400{,}000+0.18\cdot5{,}400{,}000}{12}=\frac{6{,}372{,}000}{12}=531{,}000" />

Check it by adding every payment: <InlineMath math="3{,}600{,}000+54{,}000+12\cdot531{,}000=10{,}026{,}000" />. That matches the total in the question.

So the buyer's monthly installment is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}531{,}000" />.
