Write <InlineMath math="2^{a+1}" /> as <InlineMath math="2\cdot2^a" />, then **take out the common factor <InlineMath math="2^a" />**.

<BlockMath math="f(a+1)-f(a)=2^{a+1}-2^a=2\cdot2^a-2^a=2^a(2-1)=2^a" />

Check with <InlineMath math="a=3" />: <InlineMath math="f(4)-f(3)=16-8=8=2^3" />.

So <InlineMath math="f(a+1)-f(a)=2^a" />.

- <InlineMath math="1" /> subtracts the exponents, <InlineMath math="(a+1)-a" />, but exponents are subtracted only when dividing.
- <InlineMath math="2" /> is the quotient <InlineMath math="\frac{f(a+1)}{f(a)}" />, not the difference.
- <InlineMath math="2^{a+1}" /> forgets to subtract <InlineMath math="f(a)" />.
- <InlineMath math="2^{2a+1}" /> multiplies <InlineMath math="2^{a+1}" /> by <InlineMath math="2^a" /> instead of subtracting.
