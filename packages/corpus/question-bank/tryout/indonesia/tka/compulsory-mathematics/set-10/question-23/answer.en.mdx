# The range of Gita's score

Gita's score isn't known, so the word **"pasti"** (surely) in each statement means it has to hold for every score Gita could have. The first step is to find all those scores from the condition on the mean.

Call Gita's score <InlineMath math="x" />. The eight original scores add up to <InlineMath math="560" />, and the mean of the nine students must be more than <InlineMath math="71" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{560+x}{9}&>71 \\

Scores also go up to <InlineMath math="100" /> at most, so Gita's possible scores are <InlineMath math="79<x\le100" />.

**Statement 1**. There are <InlineMath math="9" /> values, an odd number, so the median is the <InlineMath math="5" />th value in order, with no averaging. The original scores in order are <InlineMath math="56,\ 58,\ 66,\ 68,\ 70,\ 72,\ 82,\ 88" />. Since <InlineMath math="x>79" />, Gita's score always lands above <InlineMath math="70" />.
