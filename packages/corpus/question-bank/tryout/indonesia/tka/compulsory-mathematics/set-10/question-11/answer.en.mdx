East and north are perpendicular, so the harbour and the two ships form **a right triangle with the right angle at the harbour**. The distance between the ships is its hypotenuse.

<BlockMath math="d=\sqrt{24^2+18^2}=\sqrt{576+324}=\sqrt{900}=30" />

So the ships are now <InlineMath math="30" /> km apart.
