# First statement

Half of the <InlineMath math="6" />-month installment is <InlineMath math="\frac{1.15p}{12}" />. Compare it with the <InlineMath math="12" />-month installment.

<BlockMath math="\frac{1.18p}{12}>\frac{1.15p}{12}" />

The term really is twice as long, but **all the interest grows from <InlineMath math="15\%" /> to <InlineMath math="18\%" /> of the principal**, so the installment doesn't drop all the way to half. For example, with the minimum down payment the installment is <InlineMath math="708{,}000" />, while half of <InlineMath math="1{,}380{,}000" /> is only <InlineMath math="690{,}000" />. This statement is **False**.

# Second statement

The admin fee is the same for the same down payment, so the difference in totals comes only from the interest.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{difference}&=(9{,}000{,}000+0.235p)-(9{,}000{,}000+0.19p)=0.045p \\
0.045p&\le0.045\cdot7{,}200{,}000=324{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

Since <InlineMath math="p>0" />, the difference is always positive. It's **largest when the principal is largest**, which happens with the minimum down payment, and then it's exactly <InlineMath math="324{,}000" />. So the difference is never more than <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}324{,}000" />. This statement is **True**.

# Third statement

The cash price is <InlineMath math="8{,}640{,}000" />, so <InlineMath math="10\%" /> above it is <InlineMath math="9{,}504{,}000" />. Find when the <InlineMath math="6" />-month total goes past that line.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
9{,}000{,}000+0.16p&>9{,}504{,}000 \\

\end{aligned}" />

That means the statement only holds when the down payment is less than <InlineMath math="5{,}850{,}000" />. With the minimum down payment the total really is <InlineMath math="17.5\%" /> above the cash price, and that's the trap. **A large down payment is allowed too**. For example, a down payment of <InlineMath math="70\%" />, or <InlineMath math="6{,}300{,}000" />, gives <InlineMath math="p=2{,}700{,}000" /> and a total of <InlineMath math="9{,}432{,}000" />, which is less than <InlineMath math="9{,}504{,}000" />. This statement is **False**.
