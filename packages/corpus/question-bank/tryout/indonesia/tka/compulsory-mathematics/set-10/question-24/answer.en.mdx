Every card is equally likely, so **the probability is the number of aces divided by the number of cards**.

<BlockMath math="P(\text{ace})=\frac{4}{52}=\frac{1}{13}" />

So the probability of drawing an ace is <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{13}" />.

- <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{52}" /> is the probability of one particular ace, such as the ace of hearts.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{26}" /> counts only <InlineMath math="2" /> aces.
- <InlineMath math="\frac14" /> is the probability of a card from one particular suit, such as hearts.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{4}{13}" /> divides the <InlineMath math="4" /> aces by the <InlineMath math="13" /> cards of one suit, but the sample space is all <InlineMath math="52" /> cards.
