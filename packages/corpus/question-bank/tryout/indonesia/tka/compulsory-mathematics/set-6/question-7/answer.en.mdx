# Both colonies as exponential functions

The question asks for the first on-the-hour count at which colony P is larger than colony Q. Let <InlineMath math="t" /> be the number of hours since 05:00. Colony P is multiplied by <InlineMath math="2" /> every <InlineMath math="2" /> hours and colony Q by <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1}{2}" /> every <InlineMath math="3" /> hours, so

<BlockMath math="P(t)=500\cdot2^{\frac{t}{2}}, \qquad Q(t)=32{,}000\cdot2^{-\frac{t}{3}}" />

Both colonies change at the same time, one up and one down. **Compare them together**, rather than following each one until it reaches the other's starting count.

# When P overtakes Q

Solve the inequality <InlineMath math="P(t)>Q(t)" /> by writing everything with base <InlineMath math="2" />. Notice that <InlineMath math="32{,}000\div500=64=2^{6}" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
500\cdot2^{\frac{t}{2}}&>32{,}000\cdot2^{-\frac{t}{3}} \\
2^{\frac{t}{2}}\cdot2^{\frac{t}{3}}&>64 \\
2^{\frac{5t}{6}}&>2^{6} \\
\frac{5t}{6}&>6 \\
t&>7.2
\end{aligned}" />

Going from the third line to the fourth is allowed because <InlineMath math="2^{x}" /> always increases, so a bigger exponent gives a bigger value. The colonies are equal at exactly <InlineMath math="t=7.2" /> hours, which is 12:12. **There's no count at 12:12**, because counts only happen on the hour. The smallest whole number above <InlineMath math="7.2" /> is <InlineMath math="8" />, so the answer is 13:00.

# Checking two counts

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
t=7&: \quad \tfrac{5\cdot7}{6}=\tfrac{35}{6}<6, \text{ so } P(7)\lt Q(7) \\
t=8&: \quad \tfrac{5\cdot8}{6}=\tfrac{40}{6}>6, \text{ so } P(8)>Q(8)
\end{aligned}" />

In numbers, <InlineMath math="P(8)=500\cdot2^{4}=8{,}000" /> cells while <InlineMath math="Q(8)\approx5{,}040" /> cells. **At 12:00 colony Q is still ahead**, with about <InlineMath math="6{,}350" /> cells against <InlineMath math="5{,}657" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

The wrong options come from **dropping one of the changes or the on-the-hour rule**.

- 12:00 comes from rounding <InlineMath math="7.2" /> down. At that count colony Q is still larger.
- 12:12 is when the colonies are equal, not a counting time. At that moment P isn't larger yet, only equal.
- 17:00 comes from ignoring Q's decline: <InlineMath math="500\cdot2^{\frac{t}{2}}=32{,}000" /> gives <InlineMath math="t=12" />.
- 23:00 comes from ignoring P's growth: <InlineMath math="32{,}000\cdot2^{-\frac{t}{3}}=500" /> gives <InlineMath math="t=18" />.

So colony P is first recorded as larger than colony Q at the 13:00 count.
