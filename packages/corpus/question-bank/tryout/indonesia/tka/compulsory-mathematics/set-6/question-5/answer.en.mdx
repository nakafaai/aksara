# Statement two

The condition <InlineMath math="b>p" /> gives <InlineMath math="p<5{,}200" />, not <InlineMath math="p<5{,}000" />. The pair <InlineMath math="p=5{,}000" /> and <InlineMath math="b=5{,}500" /> meets every condition: <InlineMath math="2\cdot5{,}500+3\cdot5{,}000=26{,}000" />, the notebook is dearer, and both are multiples of <InlineMath math="500" />. **A pen can cost exactly <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" />**, so this statement is **False**.
