# Basic measures of both cities

The range is the largest value minus the smallest. The mean is the sum of the values divided by how many there are.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{range of P}&=330-40=290\text{ mm} \\
\text{range of Q}&=320-180=140\text{ mm} \\
\bar x_P&=\frac{1{,}200}{6}=200\text{ mm} \\
\bar x_Q&=\frac{250+230+180+320+300+280}{6}=\frac{1{,}560}{6}=260\text{ mm}
\end{aligned}" />

City Q's range isn't <InlineMath math="320-250" />. Its smallest value is in March, <InlineMath math="180\text{ mm}" />, so **find the smallest value over all six months**, not in the first month.
