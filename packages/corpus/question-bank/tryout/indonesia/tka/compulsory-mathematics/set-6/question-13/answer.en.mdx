A translation moves **every point by the same vector**. You get that vector from the image minus the original point <InlineMath math="A" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
T&=\begin{pmatrix}-1-2\\4-(-3)\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-3\\7\end{pmatrix} \\
B'&=(5+(-3),\ 0+7)=(2,7)
\end{aligned}" />

So the image of <InlineMath math="B" /> is <InlineMath math="(2,7)" />.

- <InlineMath math="(-3,7)" /> is the translation vector, not the image of <InlineMath math="B" />.
- <InlineMath math="(2,-7)" /> gets the sign of the second component wrong, as if <InlineMath math="A" /> moved down.
- <InlineMath math="(4,4)" /> adds the coordinates of <InlineMath math="A'" /> to <InlineMath math="B" /> instead of the translation vector.
- <InlineMath math="(8,-7)" /> uses the opposite vector, <InlineMath math="A-A'" />.
