# Statement one

Once both taps are closed, water only leaves through the crack, <InlineMath math="8" /> liters per minute. This leak **stops when the water surface drops to the crack**, that is, when <InlineMath math="480" /> liters are left.

<BlockMath math="\text{time spent dropping}=\frac{1{,}200-480}{8}=90\text{ minutes}" />

Ninety minutes after 6:45 is 8:15. After that the volume stays at <InlineMath math="480" /> liters. This statement is **True**. If you assume the tank leaks until it's empty, you get <InlineMath math="1{,}200\div8=150" /> minutes and think the level only stops dropping at 9:15.

# Statement two

The word **pasti** (certainly) means the claim has to hold for every opening time of tap Q that's still before the tank is full. Look for the worst case, which is opening tap Q very late. Without tap Q at all, the tank is full at

<BlockMath math="\text{6:20}+\frac{1{,}200-480}{24-8}\text{ minutes}=\text{6:20}+45\text{ minutes}=\text{7:05}" />

So tap Q could still be opened at, say, 6:59, while the tank isn't full yet. Work out that case:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{volume at 6:59}&=480+16\cdot39=1{,}104\text{ liters} \\
\text{time until full}&=\frac{1{,}200-1{,}104}{32}=3\text{ minutes}
\end{aligned}" />

The tank is only full at 7:02, after 7:00. One example like this is enough to break the word certainly. This statement is **False**. The correct bound is before 7:05, because the later tap Q opens, the closer the full time gets to 7:05.

# Statement three

If tap P stops at 6:25, the tank still holds <InlineMath math="560" /> liters at that moment, just as in the reading. After that only tap Q fills it, while the leak keeps going because the water is above the crack. **The net rate is still positive**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{net rate}&=16-8=8\text{ liters per minute} \\
\text{time until full}&=\frac{1{,}200-560}{8}=80\text{ minutes}
\end{aligned}" />

Eighty minutes after 6:25 is 7:45, so the tank still fills up at that time. This statement is **True**. If you forget the leak, you get <InlineMath math="640\div16=40" /> minutes, which is 7:05.

So the answers are True, False, True.
