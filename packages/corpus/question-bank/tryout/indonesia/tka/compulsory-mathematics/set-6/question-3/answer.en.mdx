# Statement one

Once both taps are closed, water only leaves through the crack, <InlineMath math="8" /> liters per minute. This leak **stops when the water surface drops to the crack**, that is, when <InlineMath math="480" /> liters are left.

<BlockMath math="\text{time spent dropping}=\frac{1{,}200-480}{8}=90\text{ minutes}" />

Ninety minutes after 6:45 is 8:15. After that the volume stays at <InlineMath math="480" /> liters. This statement is **True**. If you assume the tank leaks until it's empty, you get <InlineMath math="1{,}200\div8=150" /> minutes and think the level only stops dropping at 9:15.
