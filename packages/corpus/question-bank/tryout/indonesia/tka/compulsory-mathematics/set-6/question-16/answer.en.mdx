The tent is an upright prism, so **its volume is the base area times the prism's length**. The base is the triangle at the front, and the prism's length runs from the front of the tent to the back.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L_{\text{triangle}}&=\tfrac12\cdot3\cdot2=3\text{ m}^2 \\
V&=3\cdot4=12\text{ m}^3
\end{aligned}" />

So the space inside the tent is <InlineMath math="12\text{ m}^3" />.
