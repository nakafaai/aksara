# The area on the map

The vertical line <InlineMath math="x=2" /> through <InlineMath math="E" /> splits the plot into **two trapezoids with vertical parallel sides**. The left one is bounded by <InlineMath math="AD" />, of length <InlineMath math="8" />, and the segment from <InlineMath math="(2,0)" /> to <InlineMath math="E" />, of length <InlineMath math="4" />. The right one is bounded by that same segment, of length <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="BC" />, of length <InlineMath math="6" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A_{\text{left}}&=\frac{8+4}{2}\cdot2=12 \\
A_{\text{right}}&=\frac{4+6}{2}\cdot8=40 \\
A_{\text{map}}&=12+40=52\text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}" />

Check with the polygon area formula from coordinates, using the fence order <InlineMath math="A,B,C,E,D" />: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1}{2}\left|0+60+28+16+0\right|=52" />. Same result.

# From the map to real size

A scale of <InlineMath math="1:500" /> means <InlineMath math="1\text{ cm}" /> on the map stands for <InlineMath math="500\text{ cm}=5\text{ m}" /> in real life. A <InlineMath math="1\text{ cm}\times1\text{ cm}" /> square on the map becomes <InlineMath math="5\text{ m}\times5\text{ m}" />, so **the area is multiplied by <InlineMath math="25" />, not by <InlineMath math="5" />**:

<BlockMath math="A_{\text{real}}=52\cdot25=1{,}300\text{ m}^2" />

# Why the other options are wrong

Each wrong option **misses one of the two deciding points or one calculation step**.

- <InlineMath math="260\text{ m}^2" /> multiplies the map area by <InlineMath math="5" /> only, as if the length scale applied to area.
- <InlineMath math="1{,}550\text{ m}^2" /> uses alphabetical order <InlineMath math="A,B,C,D,E" />. That order makes a different pentagon with a map area of <InlineMath math="62\text{ cm}^2" />, so <InlineMath math="62\cdot25=1{,}550" />.
- <InlineMath math="2{,}000\text{ m}^2" /> uses the <InlineMath math="10\times8" /> rectangle around the plot and forgets the dent at <InlineMath math="E" />.
- <InlineMath math="2{,}600\text{ m}^2" /> forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1}{2}" /> in the polygon area formula: <InlineMath math="104\cdot25=2{,}600" />.

So the real area of the plot is <InlineMath math="1{,}300\text{ m}^2" />.
