<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2b+3p&=26{.}000 \\
0{,}9\,(6b+9p)&=70{.}200 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 6b+9p=78{.}000 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 2b+3p=26{.}000
\end{aligned}" />
