The sample space is every seating order of the five students, <InlineMath math="5!=120" /> orders. To count the orders where Ani and Budi sit together, **treat Ani and Budi as one block**. That block and the other three students can be ordered in <InlineMath math="4!" /> ways, and inside the block Ani and Budi can swap in <InlineMath math="2!" /> ways.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
n(A)&=4!\cdot2!=24\cdot2=48 \\
P(A)&=\frac{48}{120}=\frac25
\end{aligned}" />

So the probability that Ani and Budi sit next to each other is <InlineMath math="\frac25" />.
