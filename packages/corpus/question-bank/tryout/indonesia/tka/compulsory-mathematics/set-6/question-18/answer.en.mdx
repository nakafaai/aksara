The edge of a sector is **one arc and two radii**. The arc is the share of the full circumference given by the central angle, <InlineMath math="\frac{120^\circ}{360^\circ}=\frac13" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{arc}&=\frac13\cdot2\cdot\frac{22}{7}\cdot21=44\text{ m} \\
\text{fence}&=44+21+21=86\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

So the fence needs to be <InlineMath math="86\text{ m}" /> long.

- <InlineMath math="44\text{ m}" /> is the arc alone, without the two radii.
- <InlineMath math="65\text{ m}" /> adds only one radius to the arc.
- <InlineMath math="132\text{ m}" /> is the circumference of the whole circle.
- <InlineMath math="462" /> is the sector's area in <InlineMath math="\text{m}^2" />, not the length of the fence.
