# Given

# Asked

# Solution

> Break-even hits when total sales revenue matches fixed setup cost plus total unit production cost.

First build **the profit function as the difference of two functions**.

2. **True.** Breaking even means <InlineMath math="P(x)=0" />, that is <InlineMath math="40{,}000x=2{,}000{,}000" />, so <InlineMath math="x=50" />.
3. **False.** <InlineMath math="140{,}000x-2{,}000{,}000" /> adds revenue and variable cost, but the cost must be subtracted.
4. **False.** Each bag adds <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}90{,}000.00" /> of revenue but also <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}50{,}000.00" /> of cost. The extra profit is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}40{,}000.00" />, the slope of <InlineMath math="P" />.
