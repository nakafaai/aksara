# Given

A **cubic polynomial equation** has three roots <InlineMath math="p" />, <InlineMath math="q" />, and <InlineMath math="r" />.

# Asked

The **symmetric expression value** of the three roots <InlineMath math="p" />, <InlineMath math="q" />, and <InlineMath math="r" />.

# Solution

> Rewrite the target using the sum of roots, pairwise sum of products, and triple product so Vieta formulas evaluate it directly.

If <InlineMath math="y=x+1" /> where <InlineMath math="x" /> is a root of the original equation, then <InlineMath math="x=y-1" />. So **substitute <InlineMath math="x=y-1" />** into the original equation to get an equation in <InlineMath math="y" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
&(y-1)^3-4(y-1)^2+(y-1)+6 \\
&=y^3-3y^2+3y-1-4y^2+8y-4+y-1+6 \\
&=y^3-7y^2+12y
\end{aligned}" />

Draw both curves. **The new curve is the original one shifted <InlineMath math="1" /> unit to the right**, so every root goes up by <InlineMath math="1" />.

Check with the roots: the equation below, so the roots are <InlineMath math="-1" />, <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="3" />. Adding <InlineMath math="1" /> gives <InlineMath math="0" />, <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />, and the equation below.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
x^3-4x^2+x+6=(x+1)(x-2)(x-3) \\
y(y-3)(y-4)=y^3-7y^2+12y
\end{gathered}" />

So the equation is the equation below.

<BlockMath math="x^3-7x^2+12x=0" />

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- The equation below substitutes <InlineMath math="x+1" />, so its roots are <InlineMath math="p-1" />, <InlineMath math="q-1" /> and <InlineMath math="r-1" />.

  <BlockMath math="x^3-x^2-4x+4=0" />

- The equation below only adds <InlineMath math="1" /> to the constant, which does not shift the roots.

  <BlockMath math="x^3-4x^2+x+7=0" />

- The equation below has the signs wrong, so its roots are <InlineMath math="0" />, <InlineMath math="-3" /> and <InlineMath math="-4" />.

  <BlockMath math="x^3+7x^2+12x=0" />

- The equation below forgets that the constant term changes too. The root <InlineMath math="0" /> forces the constant to be zero.

  <BlockMath math="x^3-7x^2+12x+6=0" />

# Answer

Therefore, the **correct answer** is given below.

<BlockMath math="x^3-7x^2+12x=0" />
