Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the number of per-kilo packages and <InlineMath math="y" /> the number of bed cover packages per day. Each machine gives **one limit on working hours**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
x+2y&\le16\quad(\text{washer}) \\
2x+y&\le20\quad(\text{dryer}) \\
x\ge0,\quad y&\ge0
\end{aligned}" />

The profit, in thousands of rupiah, is <InlineMath math="P=15x+20y" />. The corner where both machines are fully used comes from solving the two equations. Multiply the second by <InlineMath math="2" /> and subtract the first.

<BlockMath math="(4x+2y)-(x+2y)=40-16\ \Rightarrow\ 3x=24\ \Rightarrow\ x=8,\quad y=4" />

The other corners are <InlineMath math="(0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="(10,0)" /> on the dryer line and <InlineMath math="(0,8)" /> on the washer line.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(10,0)&=150 \\
P(8,4)&=120+80=200 \\
P(0,8)&=160
\end{aligned}" />

So the maximum profit is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}200{,}000.00" />, with <InlineMath math="8" /> per-kilo and <InlineMath math="4" /> bed cover packages.
