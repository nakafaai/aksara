# Given

We are given **two rectangular matrices**, a <InlineMath math="2\times3" /> matrix <InlineMath math="A" /> and a <InlineMath math="3\times2" /> matrix <InlineMath math="B" />.

# Asked

The **truth value of each statement** about dimensions and properties of rectangular matrix products.

# Solution

> Two matrices multiply only when inner dimensions match, and the outer dimensions give the size of the product.

Two rules about orders settle every statement. Addition needs **exactly the same order**. A product <InlineMath math="PQ" /> is defined when the number of columns of <InlineMath math="P" /> equals the number of rows of <InlineMath math="Q" />, and the result has order (rows of <InlineMath math="P" />) <InlineMath math="\times" /> (columns of <InlineMath math="Q" />). Transposing swaps the order, so <InlineMath math="A^T" /> is <InlineMath math="3\times2" /> and <InlineMath math="B^T" /> is <InlineMath math="2\times3" />.

Write the order of every matrix and every product. **The middle numbers in each pair must match**, and the two outer numbers give the order of the result.

  title={<>The order of each sum and product</>}
  description={<>The matrix <InlineMath math="A" /> is <InlineMath math="2\times3" /> and <InlineMath math="B" /> is <InlineMath math="3\times2" />. The product <InlineMath math="AB" /> is <InlineMath math="2\times2" />, while <InlineMath math="BA" /> and <InlineMath math="A^TB^T" /> are <InlineMath math="3\times3" />. The sum <InlineMath math="A+B" /> is undefined because the orders differ.</>}
  chart={`flowchart LR
  A["A: 2×3"] --> AB["AB: (2×3)(3×2) → 2×2"]
  B["B: 3×2"] --> AB
  B --> BA["BA: (3×2)(2×3) → 3×3"]
  A --> BA
  AT["Aᵀ: 3×2"] --> TT["AᵀBᵀ: (3×2)(2×3) → 3×3"]
  BT["Bᵀ: 2×3"] --> TT
  A -.-> S["A + B: undefined, orders differ"]
  B -.-> S`}
/>

1. **False.** The orders <InlineMath math="2\times3" /> and <InlineMath math="3\times2" /> differ, so <InlineMath math="A+B" /> is not defined.
2. **True.** <InlineMath math="(2\times3)(3\times2)" />: the columns of <InlineMath math="A" /> and the rows of <InlineMath math="B" /> are both <InlineMath math="3" />, and the result is <InlineMath math="2\times2" />.
3. **False.** <InlineMath math="(3\times2)(2\times3)" /> is defined, but the result is <InlineMath math="3\times3" />.
4. **True.** <InlineMath math="(3\times2)(2\times3)" /> gives order <InlineMath math="3\times3" />. You can also see this from <InlineMath math="A^TB^T=(BA)^T" />.

# Answer

Therefore, **statements <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> are True**, while **statements <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="3" /> are False**.
