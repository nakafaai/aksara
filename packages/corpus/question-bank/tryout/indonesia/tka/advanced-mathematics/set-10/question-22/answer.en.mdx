<BlockMath math="\frac{x\tan6x}{2\sin^2\frac{3x}{2}}=\frac12\cdot\frac{\tan6x}{x}\cdot\left(\frac{x}{\sin\frac{3x}{2}}\right)^2" />

Use <InlineMath math="\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\tan6x}{x}=6" /> and <InlineMath math="\lim_{x\to0}\frac{x}{\sin\frac{3x}{2}}=\frac23" />.

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to0}\frac{x\tan6x}{1-\cos3x}=\frac12\cdot6\cdot\left(\frac23\right)^2=\frac12\cdot6\cdot\frac49=\frac43" />

Numerical check: at <InlineMath math="x=0.01" /> the value is about <InlineMath math="1.3350" />, close to <InlineMath math="\frac43\approx1.3333" />.
