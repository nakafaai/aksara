# Given

**Three field stakes** sit at <InlineMath math="O(0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="P(6,0)" />, and <InlineMath math="Q(3,9)" /> on the plan.

# Asked

The **center, radius, or circle equation** passing through all three stakes.

# Solution

> Symmetry places the circle center right on the vertical midline of the base, reducing the whole search to one height.

The centre is equally far from all three points, so **it lies on the perpendicular bisector of every chord**. The perpendicular bisector of <InlineMath math="OP" /> is <InlineMath math="x=3" />, so the centre is <InlineMath math="(3,b)" />. Set its distances to <InlineMath math="O" /> and to <InlineMath math="Q" /> equal.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3^2+b^2&=(3-3)^2+(9-b)^2 \\
9+b^2&=81-18b+b^2 \\
18b&=72\ \Rightarrow\ b=4
\end{aligned}" />

The centre is <InlineMath math="(3,4)" /> and <InlineMath math="r^2=25" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
r^2=9+16=25 \\
(x-3)^2+(y-4)^2=25\ \Leftrightarrow\ x^2+y^2-6x-8y=0
\end{gathered}" />

Draw the three stakes, the perpendicular bisector of <InlineMath math="OP" /> and the track's circle. **The centre lies on <InlineMath math="x=3" /> at height <InlineMath math="4" />**, so it is <InlineMath math="5" /> away from each of <InlineMath math="O" />, <InlineMath math="P" /> and <InlineMath math="Q" />.

## Checking common traps

- The equation below has centre <InlineMath math="(-3,-4)" />, a sign slip when expanding.

  <BlockMath math="x^2+y^2+6x+8y=0" />

- The equation below takes the centroid of the triangle, <InlineMath math="(3,3)" />, as the centre. That circle misses <InlineMath math="Q" />.

  <BlockMath math="x^2+y^2-6x-6y=0" />

- The equation below puts the centre halfway up the triangle, at <InlineMath math="\left(3,\frac92\right)" />. That circle misses <InlineMath math="Q" /> too.

  <BlockMath math="x^2+y^2-6x-9y=0" />

- The equation below subtracts <InlineMath math="25" /> without accounting for the <InlineMath math="9+16" /> from the expansion.
