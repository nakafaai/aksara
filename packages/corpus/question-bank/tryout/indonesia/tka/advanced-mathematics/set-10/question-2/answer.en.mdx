# Given

A <InlineMath math="2\times2" /> matrix <InlineMath math="X" /> satisfies a **linear matrix equation** with known matrices <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" />.

# Asked

The **solution matrix** <InlineMath math="X" /> or its requested value.

# Solution

> Gather all terms with the unknown matrix onto one side using the identity matrix before multiplying by the inverse.

Collect the terms containing <InlineMath math="X" /> on one side. Since <InlineMath math="2X=2IX" />, **<InlineMath math="X" /> can be factored out on the right**.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
AX-2IX=-B\ \Leftrightarrow\ (A-2I)X=-B \\
A-2I=\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix},\qquad \det(A-2I)=2-3=-1,\qquad (A-2I)^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 3 & -2 \end{pmatrix}
\end{gathered}" />
</MathContainer>

Multiply both sides on the left by <InlineMath math="(A-2I)^{-1}" />, because <InlineMath math="X" /> sits to the right of <InlineMath math="A-2I" />.

<BlockMath math="X=-(A-2I)^{-1}B=-\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 3 & -2 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}=-\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & -4 \end{pmatrix}" />

Follow the order of the steps. **The second step multiplies from the left**, because <InlineMath math="X" /> sits to the right of <InlineMath math="A-2I" />.

/>

Check: the matrix below, so the matrix below, which equals <InlineMath math="2X" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
AX=\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ -3 & -9 \end{pmatrix} \\
AX+B=\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ -2 & -8 \end{pmatrix}
\end{gathered}" />

So the matrix below.

<BlockMath math="X=\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & -4 \end{pmatrix}" />
