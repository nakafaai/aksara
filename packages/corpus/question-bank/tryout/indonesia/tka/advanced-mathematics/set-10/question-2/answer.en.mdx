Collect the terms containing <InlineMath math="X" /> on one side. Since <InlineMath math="2X=2IX" />, **<InlineMath math="X" /> can be factored out on the right**.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="AX-2IX=-B\ \Leftrightarrow\ (A-2I)X=-B" />
<BlockMath math="A-2I=\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix},\qquad \det(A-2I)=2-3=-1,\qquad (A-2I)^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 3 & -2 \end{pmatrix}" />
</MathContainer>

Multiply both sides on the left by <InlineMath math="(A-2I)^{-1}" />, because <InlineMath math="X" /> sits to the right of <InlineMath math="A-2I" />.

<BlockMath math="X=-(A-2I)^{-1}B=-\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 3 & -2 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}=-\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & -4 \end{pmatrix}" />

Follow the order of the steps. **The second step multiplies from the left**, because <InlineMath math="X" /> sits to the right of <InlineMath math="A-2I" />.

Check: <InlineMath math="AX=\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ -3 & -9 \end{pmatrix}" />, so <InlineMath math="AX+B=\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ -2 & -8 \end{pmatrix}" />, which equals <InlineMath math="2X" />.

So <InlineMath math="X=\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & -4 \end{pmatrix}" />.
