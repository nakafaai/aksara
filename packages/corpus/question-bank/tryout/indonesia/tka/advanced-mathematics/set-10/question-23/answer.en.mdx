This makes sense: over time almost all of the tank's contents come from the incoming solution, so the concentration approaches that solution's concentration, <InlineMath math="2" /> grams per litre. For example, <InlineMath math="K(10)=\frac{230}{200}=1.15" /> and <InlineMath math="K(1000)=\frac{20{,}030}{10{,}100}\approx1.98" />.

So the salt concentration approaches <InlineMath math="2" /> grams per litre.

- <InlineMath math="20" /> is the amount of salt flowing in each minute, in grams per minute, not a concentration.
- <InlineMath math="0" /> assumes the solution keeps getting weaker, but the incoming liquid contains salt too.
