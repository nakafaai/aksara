# Given

Circle <InlineMath math="L" /> with **the equation below** is tested against four geometric statements.

<BlockMath math="x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 4y - 5 = 0" />

# Asked

Whether each statement about circle <InlineMath math="L" /> is **True** or **False**.

# Solution

> Find the center and radius of L right away so every point, line, and second-circle check reduces to a quick distance test.

Each statement compares a distance with the radius, so start from **the centre and radius** of <InlineMath math="L" />.

<BlockMath math="(x+1)^2+(y-2)^2=1+4+5=10" />

Draw <InlineMath math="L" /> with the line, the point, and the big circle from the four statements. **Each statement can be read off its position relative to <InlineMath math="L" />**: the line touches it at one point, and <InlineMath math="(2,\ 3)" /> sits exactly on its edge.

The centre is <InlineMath math="M(-1,2)" /> and <InlineMath math="r" />.

<BlockMath math="r=\sqrt{10}" />

1. The statement about the line given below is true. A line is tangent exactly when its distance from the centre equals the radius: <InlineMath math="d=r" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} x+3y-15=0 \\ d=\frac{|-1+6-15|}{\sqrt{1+9}}=\frac{10}{\sqrt{10}}=\sqrt{10}=r \end{gathered}" />

2. The statement that <InlineMath math="(2,3)" /> lies inside <InlineMath math="L" /> is false. Its squared distance from the centre is <InlineMath math="r^2" />, so the point lies on the circle, not inside it.

   <BlockMath math="(2+1)^2+(3-2)^2=10=r^2" />

3. The statement about the <InlineMath math="y" />-axis is false. Substituting <InlineMath math="x=0" /> gives the equation below, that is the equation below. The intersection points are <InlineMath math="(0,5)" /> and <InlineMath math="(0,-1)" />, which are <InlineMath math="6" /> apart.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} y^2-4y-5=0 \\ (y-5)(y+1)=0 \end{gathered}" />

4. The statement about the circle <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2=25" /> is true. That circle has centre <InlineMath math="O(0,0)" /> and radius <InlineMath math="5" />. The centres are <InlineMath math="|OM|=\sqrt5" /> apart. Two circles meet in two points exactly when <InlineMath math="R+r" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} |OM|=\sqrt{1+4}=\sqrt5 \\ |R-r|<|OM|<R+r \end{gathered}" />

<BlockMath math="5-\sqrt{10}\approx1.84<\sqrt5\approx2.24<5+\sqrt{10}\approx8.16" />

# Answer

So the statements about the tangent line and about meeting <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2=25" /> are true, and the statements about the point <InlineMath math="(2,3)" /> and about the <InlineMath math="y" />-axis are false.
