The orthogonal projection of <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" /> onto <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" /> is a vector that **points along** <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" />. Its signed length is <InlineMath math="\frac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{b}|}" />, and its direction is the unit vector <InlineMath math="\frac{\vec{b}}{|\vec{b}|}" />. Multiplying the two gives this formula.

<BlockMath math="\vec{c}=\frac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{b}|^2}\,\vec{b}" />

Compute the dot product and the squared length of <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}&=4\cdot2+(-2)\cdot1+4\cdot2=8-2+8=14 \\
|\vec{b}|^2&=2^2+1^2+2^2=9
\end{aligned}" />

Substitute into the projection formula.

<BlockMath math="\vec{c}=\frac{14}{9}\left(2\vec{i}+\vec{j}+2\vec{k}\right)=\frac{28}{9}\vec{i}+\frac{14}{9}\vec{j}+\frac{28}{9}\vec{k}" />

Check: the remainder <InlineMath math="\vec{a}-\vec{c}" /> must be perpendicular to <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" />. Indeed <InlineMath math="\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}-\vec{c}\cdot\vec{b}=14-\frac{14}{9}\cdot9=0" />.

So the orthogonal projection of <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" /> onto <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" /> is <InlineMath math="\frac{28}{9}\vec{i}+\frac{14}{9}\vec{j}+\frac{28}{9}\vec{k}" />.

- The vector with denominators <InlineMath math="3" /> divides by <InlineMath math="|\vec{b}|=3" /> instead of <InlineMath math="|\vec{b}|^2=9" />.
- The vector <InlineMath math="\frac{14}{9}\vec{i}-\frac{7}{9}\vec{j}+\frac{14}{9}\vec{k}" /> is the projection of <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" /> onto <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" />, namely <InlineMath math="\frac{14}{36}\vec{a}" />.
- The vector <InlineMath math="28\vec{i}+14\vec{j}+28\vec{k}" /> forgets to divide by <InlineMath math="|\vec{b}|^2" />.
- The vector with a negative <InlineMath math="\vec{j}" />-component copies the signs of <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" />, but a projection must be parallel to <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" />.
