# Given

**The radical limit** at infinity **the expression below** takes the infinity-minus-infinity form.

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to\infty} \left(\sqrt{4x^2 + 12x - 1} - (2x - 1)\right)" />

# Asked

The **value of the limit** as <InlineMath math="x" /> goes to infinity.

# Solution

> Multiply by the conjugate to turn the square-root difference into a fraction, then divide top and bottom by x.

Both terms grow without bound, so the form is <InlineMath math="\infty-\infty" />, which does not give the limit yet. Multiply by **the conjugate** to turn the difference into a fraction. For large <InlineMath math="x" />, <InlineMath math="2x-1>0" />, so <InlineMath math="\sqrt{4x^2-4x+1}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
2x-1=\sqrt{(2x-1)^2}=\sqrt{4x^2-4x+1} \\
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{4x^2+12x-1}-\sqrt{4x^2-4x+1}&=\frac{(4x^2+12x-1)-(4x^2-4x+1)}{\sqrt{4x^2+12x-1}+\sqrt{4x^2-4x+1}} \\
&=\frac{16x-2}{\sqrt{4x^2+12x-1}+\sqrt{4x^2-4x+1}}
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />

Divide numerator and denominator by <InlineMath math="x" />. Inside a square root, dividing by <InlineMath math="x" /> means dividing by <InlineMath math="x^2" />, because <InlineMath math="x>0" />.

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{16-\frac2x}{\sqrt{4+\frac{12}{x}-\frac{1}{x^2}}+\sqrt{4-\frac4x+\frac{1}{x^2}}}=\frac{16}{2+2}=4" />

Draw the difference for large <InlineMath math="x" />. **The curve rises and hugs the line <InlineMath math="y=4" />**, so the two terms that both grow without bound end up differing by an almost fixed amount.

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="3" /> treats <InlineMath math="2x-1" /> as <InlineMath math="\sqrt{4x^2-1}" />, which loses the <InlineMath math="-4x" /> term.
- <InlineMath math="2" /> gets the sign of <InlineMath math="q" /> wrong and computes the expression below.

  <BlockMath math="\frac{12-4}{4}" />

- <InlineMath math="\infty" /> assumes the bigger root wins. The <InlineMath math="2x" /> parts of both terms cancel.
- <InlineMath math="8" /> forgets the factor <InlineMath math="2" /> in the denominator <InlineMath math="2\sqrt a" />.

# Answer

So the limit is <InlineMath math="4" />.
