The quickest route is to **factor** <InlineMath math="P(x)" /> **completely**, because almost every statement can be judged from the roots. Possible integer roots divide <InlineMath math="6" />. Try <InlineMath math="x=3" />.

<BlockMath math="P(3)=2(27)-3(9)-11(3)+6=54-27-33+6=0" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(x)&=(x-3)(2x^2+3x-2) \\
&=(x-3)(2x-1)(x+2)
\end{aligned}" />
