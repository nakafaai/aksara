Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the sachets of milk A and <InlineMath math="y" /> the sachets of milk B. There are **two lower limits** from the nutrition needs and **one upper limit** on the number of sachets.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x+y&\ge10 \\
x+3y&\ge15 \\
x+y&\le9 \\
x\ge0,\quad y&\ge0
\end{aligned}" />

The cost, in thousands of rupiah, is <InlineMath math="C=4x+6y" />. The feasible region is a triangle. Find its three corners from pairs of boundary lines.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x+y=10,\ x+3y=15&\ \Rightarrow\ (3,4) \\
2x+y=10,\ x+y=9&\ \Rightarrow\ (1,8) \\
x+3y=15,\ x+y=9&\ \Rightarrow\ (6,3)
\end{aligned}" />

Each corner meets the third limit: <InlineMath math="(3,4)" /> uses <InlineMath math="7" /> sachets, <InlineMath math="(1,8)" /> gives <InlineMath math="25" /> units of protein, and <InlineMath math="(6,3)" /> gives <InlineMath math="15" /> units of calcium.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
C(3,4)&=12+24=36 \\
C(1,8)&=4+48=52 \\
C(6,3)&=24+18=42
\end{aligned}" />

So the minimum cost is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}36{,}000.00" />, with <InlineMath math="3" /> sachets of milk A and <InlineMath math="4" /> of milk B.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}42{,}000.00" /> is the cost at the corner <InlineMath math="(6,3)" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}52{,}000.00" /> is the cost at the corner <InlineMath math="(1,8)" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}30{,}000.00" /> is the cost of <InlineMath math="5" /> sachets of milk B alone. That covers protein but gives only <InlineMath math="5" /> units of calcium.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000.00" /> is the cost of <InlineMath math="5" /> sachets of milk A alone. That covers calcium but gives only <InlineMath math="5" /> units of protein.
