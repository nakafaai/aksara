# Given

A **circle** centered at <InlineMath math="(2,-1)" /> with radius <InlineMath math="2" /> is reflected across horizontal line <InlineMath math="y=1" />.

# Asked

The **equation of the reflected circle**.

# Solution

> Reflection preserves the radius, so just jump the center the same vertical distance across the mirror line.

A reflection does not change the radius, so you only need to **reflect the centre**. Reflection in the horizontal line <InlineMath math="y=k" /> sends <InlineMath math="(a,b)" /> to <InlineMath math="(a,2k-b)" />, because the line must sit exactly halfway between a point and its image.

The original circle has centre <InlineMath math="(2,-1)" /> and <InlineMath math="r=2" />.

<BlockMath math="(2,-1)\mapsto(2,\ 2\cdot1-(-1))=(2,3)" />

Draw the starting circle, the mirror line, and the image. **The centre <InlineMath math="(2,-1)" /> is <InlineMath math="2" /> below the line <InlineMath math="y=1" />, so its image is <InlineMath math="2" /> above that line**, and the radius stays the same.
