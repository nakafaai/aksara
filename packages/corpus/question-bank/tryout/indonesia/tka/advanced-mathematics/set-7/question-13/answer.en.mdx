# Given

We are given two **space vectors** <InlineMath math="\vec{u}" /> and <InlineMath math="\vec{v}" /> with integer components.

# Asked

All **true statements** about lengths, dot product, and angle between the vectors.

# Solution

> Find both vector lengths and their dot product first because those three numbers unlock every statement.

Start from two basic facts: <InlineMath math="|\vec{u}|=3" /> and <InlineMath math="0" />. So the vectors are **equally long and perpendicular**, like two sides of a square. The vectors <InlineMath math="\vec{u}+\vec{v}" /> and <InlineMath math="\vec{u}-\vec{v}" /> are the two diagonals of that square.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
|\vec{u}|=|\vec{v}|=3 \\
\vec{u}\cdot\vec{v}=2+2-4=0 \\
\vec{u}+\vec{v}=3\vec{i}+3\vec{j}+0\vec{k},\qquad \vec{u}-\vec{v}=\vec{i}-\vec{j}+4\vec{k}
\end{gathered}" />

Draw <InlineMath math="\vec u" /> and <InlineMath math="\vec v" /> in the plane that holds both. **Both have length <InlineMath math="3" /> and are perpendicular, so they form a square**, and <InlineMath math="\vec u\pm\vec v" /> are its two diagonals.

1. The statement that the diagonals are perpendicular is true: <InlineMath math="0" />. In components: <InlineMath math="0" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} (\vec{u}+\vec{v})\cdot(\vec{u}-\vec{v})=|\vec{u}|^2-|\vec{v}|^2=0 \\ 3-3+0=0 \end{gathered}" />

2. The statement that the diagonals have equal length is true. Because <InlineMath math="\vec{u}\cdot\vec{v}=0" />, <InlineMath math="18" /> for both signs.

   <BlockMath math="|\vec{u}\pm\vec{v}|^2=|\vec{u}|^2+|\vec{v}|^2=18" />

3. The statement <InlineMath math="|\vec{u}+\vec{v}|=3\sqrt2" /> is true, because the equation below.

   <BlockMath math="\sqrt{18}=3\sqrt2" />

4. The statement the equation below is false, because its <InlineMath math="\vec{k}" />-component is <InlineMath math="2-(-2)=4" />, not <InlineMath math="0" />.

   <BlockMath math="\vec{u}-\vec{v}=\vec{i}-\vec{j}" />

5. The statement about a <InlineMath math="60^\circ" /> angle is false. A square's diagonal makes <InlineMath math="45^\circ" /> with its sides: <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{\sqrt2}" />.

   <BlockMath math="\cos\theta=\frac{\vec{u}\cdot(\vec{u}+\vec{v})}{|\vec{u}||\vec{u}+\vec{v}|}=\frac{9}{3\cdot3\sqrt2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}" />

# Answer

Therefore, the correct choices are **statements <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, and <InlineMath math="3" />**.
