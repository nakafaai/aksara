An adult ticket costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}40{,}000.00" /> and a child ticket <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}35{,}000.00" />. Check: <InlineMath math="3(40)+2(35)=190" /> and <InlineMath math="2(40)+3(35)=185" />.

<BlockMath math="4d+5a=4(40)+5(35)=160+175=335" />
