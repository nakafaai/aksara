A quadratic has three coefficients, and the problem gives exactly three values. Write <InlineMath math="P(x)=ax^2+bx+c" />, then **set up three equations and solve by elimination**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
a+b+c&=0 \\
4a+2b+c&=3 \\
9a+3b+c&=10
\end{aligned}" />

Subtract consecutive equations to remove <InlineMath math="c" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3a+b&=3 \\
5a+b&=7 \\
2a&=4\ \Rightarrow\ a=2,\quad b=-3,\quad c=1
\end{aligned}" />

So <InlineMath math="P(x)=2x^2-3x+1" />, and <InlineMath math="P(4)=32-12+1=21" />.
