The formula has two parts that limit the domain, so **both conditions must hold at once**: the number under the root is not negative, and the denominator is not zero.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
4-x^2\ge0&\iff x^2\le4\iff -2\le x\le2 \\
x-1\ne0&\iff x\ne1
\end{aligned}" />

The endpoints <InlineMath math="-2" /> and <InlineMath math="2" /> are included, because there <InlineMath math="\sqrt0=0" /> is defined and the denominator is not zero. For example <InlineMath math="f(2)=\frac{0}{1}=0" />.

So the natural domain is <InlineMath math="\{x\mid -2\le x\le2,\ x\ne1\}" />.

- <InlineMath math="\{x\mid -2\le x\le2\}" /> forgets that <InlineMath math="x=1" /> makes the denominator zero.
- <InlineMath math="\{x\mid x\ne1\}" /> only checks the denominator and forgets the root.
- <InlineMath math="\{x\mid -2<x<2,\ x\ne1\}" /> drops the endpoints <InlineMath math="\pm2" />, but <InlineMath math="\sqrt0" /> is defined.
- <InlineMath math="\{x\mid x\le-2\text{ or }x\ge2\}" /> reverses the inequality <InlineMath math="x^2\le4" />.
