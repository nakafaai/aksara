Direct substitution gives <InlineMath math="\frac00" />. The numerator contains the factor <InlineMath math="x-1" />, while the denominator is <InlineMath math="\sqrt x-1" />. Connect them with **a difference of squares**: <InlineMath math="x-1=(\sqrt x-1)(\sqrt x+1)" /> for <InlineMath math="x\ge0" />.

<BlockMath math="x^3-1=(x-1)(x^2+x+1)=(\sqrt x-1)(\sqrt x+1)(x^2+x+1)" />

For <InlineMath math="x\ne1" />, the factor <InlineMath math="\sqrt x-1" /> is not zero and may be cancelled.

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to1}\frac{(\sqrt x-1)(\sqrt x+1)(x^2+x+1)}{\sqrt x-1}=\lim_{x\to1}(\sqrt x+1)(x^2+x+1)=2\cdot3=6" />

Numerical check: for <InlineMath math="x=1.01" />, <InlineMath math="r(x)=\frac{0.030301}{0.0049876}\approx6.075" />, close to <InlineMath math="6" />.

So the limit is <InlineMath math="6" />.
