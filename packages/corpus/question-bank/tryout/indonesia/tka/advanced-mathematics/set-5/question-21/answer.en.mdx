A limit at <InlineMath math="x=2" /> only looks at the values of <InlineMath math="f" /> **near** <InlineMath math="2" />, not at <InlineMath math="f(2)" /> itself. So compare the left and right limits.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\to2^-}f(x)&=\lim_{x\to2^-}(x+1)=3 \\
\lim_{x\to2^+}f(x)&=\lim_{x\to2^+}\left(x^2-1\right)=4-1=3
\end{aligned}" />

Both one-sided limits agree, so the limit exists and equals <InlineMath math="3" />. The value <InlineMath math="f(2)=5" /> does not affect the limit. It only makes <InlineMath math="f" /> discontinuous at <InlineMath math="x=2" />.

So <InlineMath math="\lim_{x\to2}f(x)=3" />.
