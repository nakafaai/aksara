A vector along <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" /> with a chosen length is built from **the unit vector** of <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" />, which is <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" /> divided by its length. That unit vector has length <InlineMath math="1" />, so you just multiply it by the length you want.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
|\vec{a}|&=\sqrt{2^2+(-1)^2+2^2}=\sqrt9=3 \\
\vec{c}&=12\cdot\frac{\vec{a}}{|\vec{a}|}=\frac{12}{3}\left(2\vec{i}-\vec{j}+2\vec{k}\right)=8\vec{i}-4\vec{j}+8\vec{k}
\end{aligned}" />

Compare the lengths of the three vectors that point the same way. **The unit vector has length <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="\vec a" /> has length <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="\vec c" /> must have length <InlineMath math="12" />**, so <InlineMath math="\vec c" /> is <InlineMath math="4" /> times <InlineMath math="\vec a" />.

Check: <InlineMath math="|\vec{c}|=\sqrt{64+16+64}=\sqrt{144}=12" />, and <InlineMath math="\vec{c}=4\vec{a}" /> with a positive factor, so the direction is the same.

So <InlineMath math="\vec{c}=8\vec{i}-4\vec{j}+8\vec{k}" />.

- <InlineMath math="24\vec{i}-12\vec{j}+24\vec{k}" /> multiplies <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" /> by <InlineMath math="12" /> without dividing by <InlineMath math="|\vec{a}|" />. Its length is <InlineMath math="36" />.
- <InlineMath math="4\vec{i}-2\vec{j}+4\vec{k}" /> is only <InlineMath math="2\vec{a}" />, with length <InlineMath math="6" />.
- <InlineMath math="-8\vec{i}+4\vec{j}-8\vec{k}" /> points opposite to <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" />, even though its length is <InlineMath math="12" />.
