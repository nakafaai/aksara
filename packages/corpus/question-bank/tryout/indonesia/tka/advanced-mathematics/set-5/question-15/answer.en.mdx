# Given

Two **tangent lines** <InlineMath math="g" /> and <InlineMath math="h" /> touch an origin-centered circle at two known points.

# Asked

The **intersection point** or geometric relation between the two tangents.

# Solution

> The tangent-at-a-point formula gives each line equation immediately, and solving them together finds where they meet.

The tangent to <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2=r^2" /> at a point <InlineMath math="(x_1,y_1)" /> on the circle is <InlineMath math="x_1x+y_1y=r^2" />. The formula comes from the tangent being **perpendicular to the radius** at the point of tangency, and that radius points along <InlineMath math="(x_1,y_1)" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
g&:\ 3x+4y=25 \\
h&:\ 3x-4y=25
\end{aligned}" />

Add the two equations to remove <InlineMath math="y" />.

<BlockMath math="6x=50\ \Rightarrow\ x=\frac{25}{3},\qquad 4y=25-3\cdot\frac{25}{3}=0\ \Rightarrow\ y=0" />

Draw both tangents. **The points of contact are symmetric about the <InlineMath math="X" />-axis, so the two lines meet on the <InlineMath math="X" />-axis**, outside the circle.

This makes sense: the two points of tangency are symmetric about the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis, so the tangents meet on the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis too.

So <InlineMath math="g" /> and <InlineMath math="h" /> meet at <InlineMath math="\left(\frac{25}{3},0\right)" />.

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="(3,0)" /> is the midpoint of the two points of tangency, not where the tangents meet.
- <InlineMath math="\left(\frac{25}{4},0\right)" /> swaps the coordinates in the formula and uses <InlineMath math="4x+3y=25" /> and <InlineMath math="4x-3y=25" />.
- <InlineMath math="(5,0)" /> lies on the circle. Tangents at two other points do not meet on the circle.
- <InlineMath math="\left(0,\frac{25}{4}\right)" /> is where <InlineMath math="g" /> crosses the <InlineMath math="y" />-axis.

# Answer

Therefore, the **correct answer** is **<InlineMath math="\left(\frac{25}{3},0\right)" />**.
