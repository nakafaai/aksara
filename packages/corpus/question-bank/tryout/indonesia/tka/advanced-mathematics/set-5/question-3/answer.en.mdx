# Given

We are given a **square matrix** <InlineMath math="A" /> of size <InlineMath math="2\times2" /> with determinant one.

# Asked

The **truth value of each statement** about the inverse, determinant, and powers of <InlineMath math="A" />.

# Solution

> Because the determinant equals one, the inverse matrix drops all fractions and stays completely clean.

Start with the determinant, because three statements depend on it: <InlineMath math="\det A=1" />. A matrix with **determinant <InlineMath math="1" />** has an inverse with integer entries.

<BlockMath math="\det A=4\cdot1-3\cdot1=1" />

See <InlineMath math="\det A=1" /> as an area. **The columns of <InlineMath math="A" /> span a long but very thin parallelogram**, and its area is still <InlineMath math="1" />, the same as the unit square.

1. The statement <InlineMath math="\det\left(A^T\right)=-1" /> is false. Transposing does not change the determinant, so <InlineMath math="1" />.

   <BlockMath math="\det\left(A^T\right)=\det A=1" />

2. The statement about <InlineMath math="A+A^T" /> is false. The transpose is the matrix below, so the matrix below. A matrix plus its transpose is always symmetric, and the matrix in the statement is not.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} A^T=\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \\ A+A^T=\begin{pmatrix} 8 & 4 \\ 4 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \end{gathered}" />

3. The statement about <InlineMath math="A^{-1}" /> is true. Swap the main diagonal entries, change the signs of the others, and divide by <InlineMath math="1" />. Check: the matrix below.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ -1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}" />

4. The statement <InlineMath math="\det\left(A^2\right)=1" /> is true, because <InlineMath math="1" />.

   <BlockMath math="\det\left(A^2\right)=(\det A)^2=1" />

# Answer

So the statements about <InlineMath math="A^{-1}" /> and <InlineMath math="\det\left(A^2\right)" /> are true, and the statements about <InlineMath math="\det\left(A^T\right)" /> and <InlineMath math="A+A^T" /> are false.
