# Given

We are given a **sine trigonometric function** <InlineMath math="f(x)" /> with angles in degrees.

# Asked

The **maximum, minimum, or period** requested for the sine function.

# Solution

> A sine wave always swings between minus one and plus one, so the midline and amplitude set its highest and lowest points.

For <InlineMath math="f(x)=k+a\sin bx" />, the values swing <InlineMath math="|a|" /> above and below <InlineMath math="k" />, and **the period is the expression below**.

<BlockMath math="\frac{360^\circ}{|b|}" />

The value of <InlineMath math="\sin3x" /> always lies between <InlineMath math="-1" /> and <InlineMath math="1" />. Because the coefficient is <InlineMath math="-2" />, the largest value of <InlineMath math="f" /> occurs when <InlineMath math="\sin3x=-1" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
f_{\text{max}}&=1-2(-1)=3 \\
f_{\text{min}}&=1-2(1)=-1 \\
\text{period}&=\frac{360^\circ}{3}=120^\circ
\end{aligned}" />

Draw <InlineMath math="f" /> over two periods. **The peaks are <InlineMath math="3" /> high, and two neighbouring peaks are <InlineMath math="120^\circ" /> apart**.

Example: <InlineMath math="f(90^\circ)=3" />, and <InlineMath math="f(210^\circ)=3" /> too, exactly one period later.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
f(90^\circ)=1-2\sin270^\circ=1+2=3 \\
f(210^\circ)=1-2\sin630^\circ=3
\end{gathered}" />

So the maximum of <InlineMath math="f" /> is <InlineMath math="3" /> and its period is <InlineMath math="120^\circ" />.

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- A maximum of <InlineMath math="1" /> is the midline of the swing, not its peak.
- A period of <InlineMath math="360^\circ" /> belongs to <InlineMath math="\sin x" /> and ignores the coefficient <InlineMath math="3" />.
- A maximum of <InlineMath math="2" /> is the amplitude without the midline <InlineMath math="1" /> added.
- <InlineMath math="-1" /> is the minimum, and <InlineMath math="1080^\circ" /> multiplies <InlineMath math="360^\circ" /> by <InlineMath math="3" /> instead of dividing.

# Answer

Therefore, the function has **maximum value <InlineMath math="3" /> and period <InlineMath math="120^\circ" />**.
