# Given

A **cubic polynomial equation** has three roots <InlineMath math="x_1" />, <InlineMath math="x_2" />, and <InlineMath math="x_3" />.

# Asked

The **symmetric value** built from the cubic roots.

# Solution

> Vieta formulas hand us the sum and product of the roots straight from the coefficients.

There are two routes, and they check each other. **Vieta's formulas** give the sums and products of the roots straight from the coefficients <InlineMath math="a=2" />, <InlineMath math="b=-5" />, <InlineMath math="c=-4" />, <InlineMath math="d=3" />.

<BlockMath math="x_1+x_2+x_3=-\frac ba=\frac52,\qquad x_1x_2+x_1x_3+x_2x_3=\frac ca=-2,\qquad x_1x_2x_3=-\frac da=-\frac32" />

You can also find the roots. Try <InlineMath math="x=3" />: <InlineMath math="0" />. Divide by <InlineMath math="x-3" /> and factor.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
54-45-12+3=0 \\
2x^3-5x^2-4x+3=(x-3)(2x^2+x-1)=(x-3)(2x-1)(x+1)
\end{gathered}" />

Draw the polynomial. **The graph crosses the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis at <InlineMath math="-1" />, <InlineMath math="\tfrac12" />, and <InlineMath math="3" />**, so the largest root is <InlineMath math="3" />, and the sum and product of the roots can be checked directly.

The roots are <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="\frac12" /> and <InlineMath math="-1" />, and they do add up to <InlineMath math="\frac52" />.

1. The statement about the sum of the roots is true.
2. The statement about the product of the roots is true: the equation below.

   <BlockMath math="3\cdot\frac12\cdot(-1)=-\frac32" />

3. The statement that the sum of pairwise products is <InlineMath math="2" /> is false. It is the equation below. Its sign is not flipped, unlike the sum of the roots.

   <BlockMath math="\frac ca=-2" />

4. The statement that the largest root is <InlineMath math="\frac12" /> is false, because the largest root is <InlineMath math="3" />.
5. The statement about the sum of the reciprocals is true. Use a common denominator.

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{x_1}+\frac{1}{x_2}+\frac{1}{x_3}=\frac{x_2x_3+x_1x_3+x_1x_2}{x_1x_2x_3}=\frac{-2}{-\frac32}=\frac43" />

# Answer

So the true statements are the sum <InlineMath math="\frac52" />, the product <InlineMath math="-\frac32" />, and the reciprocal sum <InlineMath math="\frac43" />.
