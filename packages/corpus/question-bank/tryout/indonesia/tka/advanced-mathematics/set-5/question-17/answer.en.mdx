# Given

We are given a **family of circles** <InlineMath math="L_a" /> depending on a nonzero real parameter <InlineMath math="a" />.

# Asked

The **truth value of each statement** about center, radius, and position of the circle family.

# Solution

> Complete the square so both the center and radius appear cleanly in terms of the parameter.

Rewrite the equation in standard form so the centre and radius show up **as functions of <InlineMath math="a" />**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(x^2-2ax+a^2)+(y^2-2ay+a^2)&=a^2 \\
(x-a)^2+(y-a)^2&=a^2
\end{aligned}" />

Draw a few members of the family <InlineMath math="L_a" />. **Every circle has its centre on the line <InlineMath math="y=x" /> and touches both axes**, while the origin always stays outside the circle.

Each circle <InlineMath math="L_a" /> has centre <InlineMath math="(a,a)" /> and radius <InlineMath math="|a|" />.

1. The statement that <InlineMath math="L_a" /> touches only the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis is false. The centre <InlineMath math="(a,a)" /> is <InlineMath math="|a|" /> from the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis and also <InlineMath math="|a|" /> from the <InlineMath math="y" />-axis. Both equal the radius, so the circle touches both axes.
2. The statement that the centre lies on <InlineMath math="y=x" /> is true, because both coordinates of the centre are equal.
3. The statement that <InlineMath math="(2,0)" /> lies on <InlineMath math="L_2" /> is true. <InlineMath math="L_2" /> has centre <InlineMath math="(2,2)" /> and radius <InlineMath math="2" />, and <InlineMath math="4" />. That point is where <InlineMath math="L_2" /> touches the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis.

   <BlockMath math="(2-2)^2+(0-2)^2=4" />

4. The statement that <InlineMath math="L_1" /> passes through the origin is false. Substituting <InlineMath math="(0,0)" /> gives <InlineMath math="1" />. The origin is <InlineMath math="\sqrt2" /> from the centre <InlineMath math="(1,1)" />, farther than the radius <InlineMath math="1" />.

   <BlockMath math="(0-1)^2+(0-1)^2=2\ne1" />

# Answer

Therefore, **statements <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="3" /> are True**, while **statements <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> are False**.
