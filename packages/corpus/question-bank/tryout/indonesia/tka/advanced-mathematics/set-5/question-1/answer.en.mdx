You do not need the entries of <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" />. **Three determinant rules** for <InlineMath math="2\times2" /> matrices are enough.

<BlockMath math="\det(kM)=k^2\det M,\qquad \det\left(M^{-1}\right)=\frac{1}{\det M},\qquad \det\left(M^T\right)=\det M" />

The determinant of a product is the product of the determinants, so each factor can be handled on its own.

<BlockMath math="\det\left(2A^{-1}B^T\right)=2^2\cdot\frac{1}{\det A}\cdot\det B=4\cdot\frac13\cdot(-2)=-\frac83" />

The factor <InlineMath math="2" /> is squared because it multiplies both rows of the matrix.

So <InlineMath math="\det\left(2A^{-1}B^T\right)=-\frac83" />.

- <InlineMath math="-\frac43" /> uses the factor <InlineMath math="2" /> without squaring it.
- <InlineMath math="\frac83" /> has the wrong sign, for example by assuming a transpose flips the sign of the determinant.
- <InlineMath math="-24" /> uses <InlineMath math="\det A=3" /> directly, but the expression contains <InlineMath math="A^{-1}" />.
- <InlineMath math="-\frac23" /> forgets the factor <InlineMath math="2" /> altogether.
