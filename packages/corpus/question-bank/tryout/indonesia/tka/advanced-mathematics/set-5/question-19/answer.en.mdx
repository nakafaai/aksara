The rule is <InlineMath math="x'=x+2y" /> and <InlineMath math="y'=y" />. To get the image equation, **write <InlineMath math="x" /> and <InlineMath math="y" /> in terms of <InlineMath math="x'" /> and <InlineMath math="y'" />** and substitute into the original line.

<BlockMath math="y=y',\qquad x=x'-2y'" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2(x'-2y')-y'+3&=0 \\
2x'-5y'+3&=0
\end{aligned}" />
