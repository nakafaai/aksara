The equation has the form <InlineMath math="AX=B" />, so <InlineMath math="X=A^{-1}B" />. **The inverse goes on the left**, because <InlineMath math="A" /> stands to the left of <InlineMath math="X" />.

Each row of the matrix equation is a line. **The solution of the system is where the two lines cross**, and the determinant <InlineMath math="1\ne0" /> guarantees they aren't parallel.
