You do not need formulas for <InlineMath math="f" /> and <InlineMath math="g" />. **The limit laws** let you take the limit of each part separately, as long as each part's limit exists and the limit of the denominator is not zero.

The denominator's limit is <InlineMath math="3+(-1)=2\ne0" />, so the quotient law applies.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\to2}\frac{2f(x)-\big(g(x)\big)^2}{f(x)+g(x)}&=\frac{2\lim f(x)-\big(\lim g(x)\big)^2}{\lim f(x)+\lim g(x)} \\
&=\frac{2(3)-(-1)^2}{3+(-1)}=\frac{6-1}{2}=\frac52
\end{aligned}" />

So the limit is <InlineMath math="\frac52" />.
