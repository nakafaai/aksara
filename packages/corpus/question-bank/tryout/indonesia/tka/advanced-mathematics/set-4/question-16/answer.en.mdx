Both radii to the points of tangency are **perpendicular to the rope**, so they are parallel. Slide the rope parallel to itself until it passes through the centre of the small pulley. This makes a right triangle with hypotenuse equal to the distance between the centres, <InlineMath math="d=15" /> cm, one leg <InlineMath math="R-r=11-2=9" /> cm, and the other leg equal to the rope length you want.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\ell^2&=d^2-(R-r)^2 \\
&=15^2-9^2=225-81=144 \\
\ell&=12
\end{aligned}" />

The pulleys are apart because <InlineMath math="d=15>R+r=13" />, so the external common tangent exists.

So the rope between the two points of tangency is <InlineMath math="12" /> cm long.

- <InlineMath math="9" /> cm is the difference of the radii, a leg of the triangle, not the rope.
- <InlineMath math="15" /> cm is the distance between the centres, the hypotenuse.
- <InlineMath math="3\sqrt{34}" /> cm adds <InlineMath math="15^2+9^2" />, but a leg comes from subtracting.
- <InlineMath math="2\sqrt{14}" /> cm uses <InlineMath math="R+r=13" />, the length of the internal common tangent, <InlineMath math="\sqrt{225-169}" />.
