Since the equation below, the remainder <InlineMath math="-4x-1" /> gives the values of <InlineMath math="P" /> at <InlineMath math="x=1" /> and <InlineMath math="x=-1" />. The factor <InlineMath math="x-2" /> gives <InlineMath math="P(2)=0" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}

\begin{aligned}
P(1)&=1+p+q+r=-4(1)-1=-5 &&\Rightarrow\ p+q+r=-6 \\

P(2)&=8+4p+2q+r=0 &&\Rightarrow\ 4p+2q+r=-8
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />

The difference of the first two equations gives <InlineMath math="2q=-10" />, so <InlineMath math="q=-5" /> and <InlineMath math="p+r=-1" />. The third equation becomes <InlineMath math="4p+r=2" />. Subtract <InlineMath math="p+r=-1" />: <InlineMath math="3p=3" />, so <InlineMath math="p=1" /> and <InlineMath math="r=-2" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}" />
