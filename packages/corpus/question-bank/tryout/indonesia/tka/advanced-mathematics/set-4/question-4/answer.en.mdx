# Given

Organizers rent **two vehicle types** to carry participants under seating, parking, and rental-cost constraints.

# Asked

The **minimum total rental cost** the organizers must pay.

# Solution

> To find the cheapest legal plan, just test the corner points of the region that meets every capacity rule.

Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the number of minibuses and <InlineMath math="y" /> the number of buses. There are **two limits**: seating capacity and parking space. The seating limit can be divided by <InlineMath math="8" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
16x+40y\ge320\iff 2x+5y&\ge40 \\
x+y&\le14 \\
x\ge0,\quad y&\ge0
\end{aligned}" />

The rental cost objective (each unit representing <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" />) is <InlineMath math="C=500x+1{,}400y" />. The feasible region is a triangle. Its corners are <InlineMath math="(0,8)" /> on the seating line, <InlineMath math="(0,14)" /> on the parking line, and the point where the two lines cross.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x+5(14-x)&=40 \\
70-3x&=40\ \Rightarrow\ x=10,\quad y=4
\end{aligned}" />

All three corners have whole-number coordinates, so each one is a possible plan.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
C(0,8)&=11{,}200 \\
C(10,4)&=5{,}000+5{,}600=10{,}600 \\
C(0,14)&=19{,}600
\end{aligned}" />

So the minimum rental cost is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}600{,}000.00" />, with <InlineMath math="10" /> minibuses and <InlineMath math="4" /> buses. They seat <InlineMath math="160+160=320" /> students and fill exactly <InlineMath math="14" /> parking places.

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}11{,}200{,}000.00" /> is the cost of <InlineMath math="8" /> buses only, the corner <InlineMath math="(0,8)" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000{,}000.00" /> is the cost of <InlineMath math="20" /> minibuses. They seat enough students, but <InlineMath math="20" /> vehicles exceed the parking space.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}19{,}600{,}000.00" /> is the cost at the corner <InlineMath math="(0,14)" />, the largest one.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}9{,}800{,}000.00" /> is the cost of <InlineMath math="7" /> buses, which seat only <InlineMath math="280" /> students.

# Answer

Therefore, the **correct answer** is **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}600.000.00" />**.
