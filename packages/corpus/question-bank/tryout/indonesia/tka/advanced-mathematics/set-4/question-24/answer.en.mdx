The denominator tends to <InlineMath math="0" />. If the numerator tended to anything other than zero, the fraction would grow without bound and have no real limit. So **the numerator must tend to zero too**.

<BlockMath math="\sqrt{2\cdot2+5}-a=0\ \Rightarrow\ a=\sqrt9=3" />

With <InlineMath math="a=3" /> the form is <InlineMath math="\frac00" />. Multiply by the conjugate <InlineMath math="\sqrt{2x+5}+3" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\to2}\frac{\sqrt{2x+5}-3}{x-2}&=\lim_{x\to2}\frac{(2x+5)-9}{(x-2)\left(\sqrt{2x+5}+3\right)} \\
&=\lim_{x\to2}\frac{2(x-2)}{(x-2)\left(\sqrt{2x+5}+3\right)}=\frac{2}{3+3}=\frac13
\end{aligned}" />
