The scalar projection of <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" /> onto <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" /> is the signed length of the shadow of <InlineMath math="\vec{a}" /> along <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" />. Its formula **divides the dot product by the length** of <InlineMath math="\vec{b}" />, not by its square.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}&=2\cdot1+(-1)\cdot2+m\cdot2=2m \\
|\vec{b}|&=\sqrt{1+4+4}=3 \\
\frac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{b}|}&=\frac{2m}{3}=2\ \Rightarrow\ m=3
\end{aligned}" />
