# Given

A degree-four polynomial <InlineMath math="P(x)" /> is divided by the **quadratic divisor** <InlineMath math="x^2+x-1" />.

# Asked

All **true statements** about the quotient and remainder of the polynomial division.

# Solution

> Long division cleanly splits the polynomial into quotient times divisor plus remainder so every claim can be verified.

The divisor has degree two, so use **long division** and stop once the remainder has degree less than two. At each step, divide the leading term of what is left by <InlineMath math="x^2" />, multiply the divisor by the result, and subtract.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x^4-x^3+3x^2-5x+1-2x^2(x^2+x-1)&=-3x^3+5x^2-5x+1 \\
-3x^3+5x^2-5x+1-(-3x)(x^2+x-1)&=8x^2-8x+1 \\
8x^2-8x+1-8(x^2+x-1)&=-16x+9
\end{aligned}" />

Draw <InlineMath math="P(x)" /> with the remainder line <InlineMath math="S(x)=-16x+9" />. **They meet exactly at the two roots of the divisor <InlineMath math="x^2+x-1" />**, because there the part <InlineMath math="(x^2+x-1)H(x)" /> is zero.

The quotient is <InlineMath math="2x^2-3x+8" /> and the remainder is <InlineMath math="S(x)=-16x+9" />. Check at <InlineMath math="x=0" />: <InlineMath math="P(0)=1" /> and <InlineMath math="1" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
H(x)=2x^2-3x+8 \\
H(0)\cdot(-1)+S(0)=-8+9=1
\end{gathered}" />

1. The statement about the quotient is true.
2. The statement that the remainder is <InlineMath math="-16x-7" /> is false. It is <InlineMath math="-16x+9" />. The <InlineMath math="-7" /> comes from subtracting <InlineMath math="8\cdot(-1)" /> with the wrong sign.
3. The statement <InlineMath math="S(0)=9" /> is true, because <InlineMath math="S(x)=-16x+9" />. Notice that this differs from <InlineMath math="P(0)=1" />.
4. The statement that <InlineMath math="x-1" /> is a factor of <InlineMath math="P(x)" /> is true, because <InlineMath math="P(1)=0" />.

   <BlockMath math="P(1)=2-1+3-5+1=0" />

5. The statement about the coefficient sum of the quotient is false: <InlineMath math="7" />, not <InlineMath math="8" />.

   <BlockMath math="2-3+8=7" />

# Answer

Therefore, the correct choices are **statements <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="4" />**.
