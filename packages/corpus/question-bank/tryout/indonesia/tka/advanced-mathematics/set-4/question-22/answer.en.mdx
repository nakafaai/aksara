Direct substitution gives <InlineMath math="\frac{1-1}{0}=\frac00" />. **Write <InlineMath math="\sec(2x)" /> as <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{\cos2x}" />** so the numerator takes the form <InlineMath math="1-\cos2x" />.

<BlockMath math="\sec(2x)-1=\frac{1-\cos2x}{\cos2x}=\frac{2\sin^2x}{\cos2x}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}" />
