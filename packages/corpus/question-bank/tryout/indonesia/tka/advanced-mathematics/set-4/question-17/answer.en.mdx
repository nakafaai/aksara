# Given

We are given an **origin-centered circle** <InlineMath math="L" /> and the family of lines with slope <InlineMath math="2" />.

# Asked

The **truth value of each statement** about tangent, secant, and exterior lines.

# Solution

> Compare center-to-line distance with the radius because equal means tangent, smaller cuts through, and larger misses.

The circle <InlineMath math="L" /> has centre <InlineMath math="O(0,0)" /> and <InlineMath math="r=2\sqrt5" />. How the line given below sits relative to it depends on **its distance from the centre**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
r=\sqrt{20}=2\sqrt5 \\
2x-y+c=0 \\
d=\frac{|2\cdot0-0+c|}{\sqrt{2^2+(-1)^2}}=\frac{|c|}{\sqrt5}
\end{gathered}" />

Draw the circle with several lines of gradient <InlineMath math="2" />. **Only two parallel lines touch the circle, one on each side**, while <InlineMath math="y=2x+5" /> is too close to the centre and cuts through it.

The line is tangent when <InlineMath math="d=r" />, that is <InlineMath math="|c|=10" />.

<BlockMath math="|c|=2\sqrt5\cdot\sqrt5=10" />

1. The statement that <InlineMath math="y=2x+10" /> is tangent to <InlineMath math="L" /> is true, because <InlineMath math="|c|=10" />.
2. The statement that <InlineMath math="y=2x+5" /> misses <InlineMath math="L" /> is false. Its distance is <InlineMath math="2\sqrt5" />, so it cuts the circle in two points.

   <BlockMath math="\frac{5}{\sqrt5}=\sqrt5<2\sqrt5" />

3. The statement about the point of tangency <InlineMath math="(-4,2)" /> is true. The point is on the line, because <InlineMath math="2(-4)+10=2" />, and on the circle, because <InlineMath math="16+4=20" />. A tangent touches the circle at only one point, so that is the point of tangency.
4. The statement that only one tangent is parallel to <InlineMath math="y=2x" /> is false. The condition <InlineMath math="|c|=10" /> gives two lines, <InlineMath math="y=2x+10" /> and <InlineMath math="y=2x-10" />, on opposite sides of the circle.

# Answer

So the statements about <InlineMath math="y=2x+10" /> and the point <InlineMath math="(-4,2)" /> are true, and the statements about <InlineMath math="y=2x+5" /> and the number of tangents are false.
