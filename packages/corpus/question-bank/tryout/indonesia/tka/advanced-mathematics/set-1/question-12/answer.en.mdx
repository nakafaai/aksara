# Given

We know the **lengths of** <InlineMath math="\vec{u}" />, <InlineMath math="|\vec{v}| = 6" />, and the sum length <InlineMath math="|\vec{u} + \vec{v}|" />.

<BlockMath math="|\vec{u} + \vec{v}| = 2\sqrt{7}" />

# Asked

The **dot product** <InlineMath math="\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}" /> and the **angle** between the two vectors.

# Solution

> Squaring the sum length reveals the dot product right away, and dividing by both lengths gives the cosine of the angle.

Only lengths are given. The bridge to the dot product is the **square of the length of a vector sum**, because <InlineMath math="|\vec u+\vec v|^2" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
|\vec u+\vec v|^2=(\vec u+\vec v)\cdot(\vec u+\vec v) \\
\begin{aligned}
|\vec u+\vec v|^2&=|\vec u|^2+2\,\vec u\cdot\vec v+|\vec v|^2 \\
(2\sqrt7)^2&=16+2\,\vec u\cdot\vec v+36 \\
28&=52+2\,\vec u\cdot\vec v \\
\vec u\cdot\vec v&=-12
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />

The angle follows from the equation below.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
\vec u\cdot\vec v=|\vec u||\vec v|\cos\theta \\
\cos\theta=\frac{-12}{4\cdot6}=-\frac12\quad\Rightarrow\quad\theta=120^\circ
\end{gathered}" />

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="12" /> and <InlineMath math="60^\circ" /> come from the wrong sign, that is, from the expression below. That form belongs to <InlineMath math="|\vec u-\vec v|^2" />.

  <BlockMath math="|\vec u|^2+|\vec v|^2-2\,\vec u\cdot\vec v" />

- <InlineMath math="-12" /> and <InlineMath math="150^\circ" /> have the right dot product, but <InlineMath math="\cos150^\circ" />, not <InlineMath math="-\frac12" />.

  <BlockMath math="\cos150^\circ=-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}" />

- <InlineMath math="-24" /> and <InlineMath math="180^\circ" /> forget the factor <InlineMath math="2" /> in front of <InlineMath math="\vec u\cdot\vec v" />.
- <InlineMath math="24" /> and <InlineMath math="0^\circ" /> combine both mistakes.

# Answer

So the dot product is <InlineMath math="-12" /> and the angle is <InlineMath math="120^\circ" />.
