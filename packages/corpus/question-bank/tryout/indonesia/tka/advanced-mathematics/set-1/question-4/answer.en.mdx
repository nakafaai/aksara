Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the number of lettuce racks and <InlineMath math="y" /> the number of pak choi racks. The nutrient solution and the care hours give **two limits**, plus the condition that the numbers of racks are not negative. The care-hours limit can be divided by <InlineMath math="2" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3x+2y&\le120 \\
2x+4y\le160\iff x+2y&\le80 \\
x\ge0,\quad y&\ge0
\end{aligned}" />

The corner where both limits are used up at once comes from subtracting the two equations.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(3x+2y)-(x+2y)&=120-80 \\
2x&=40\ \Rightarrow\ x=20,\quad y=\frac{80-20}{2}=30
\end{aligned}" />

Substitute the corners into the profit <InlineMath math="P=180{,}000x+200{,}000y" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(40,0)&=7{,}200{,}000 \\

\end{aligned}" />

As a check, at <InlineMath math="(20,30)" /> exactly <InlineMath math="60+60=120" /> litres of nutrient solution and <InlineMath math="40+120=160" /> care hours are used.
