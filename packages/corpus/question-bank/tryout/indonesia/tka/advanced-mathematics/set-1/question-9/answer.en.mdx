Cosine only takes values from <InlineMath math="-1" /> to <InlineMath math="1" />. The **smallest function value** therefore occurs when <InlineMath math="\cos(2x-40^\circ)=-1" />.

<BlockMath math="f_{\min}=1+4\cdot(-1)=-3" />

Cosine equals <InlineMath math="-1" /> when its argument is <InlineMath math="180^\circ" /> plus a multiple of <InlineMath math="360^\circ" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x-40^\circ&=180^\circ+k\cdot360^\circ \\
x&=110^\circ+k\cdot180^\circ
\end{aligned}" />

In the interval <InlineMath math="0^\circ\le x\le180^\circ" /> only <InlineMath math="x=110^\circ" /> qualifies.

As a check, <InlineMath math="f(110^\circ)=1+4\cos180^\circ=1-4=-3" />.

So the minimum value is <InlineMath math="-3" />, reached at <InlineMath math="x=110^\circ" />.
