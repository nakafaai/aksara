# Given

**The trigonometric limit** **the expression below** uses <InlineMath math="x" /> in radians.

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to 0} \frac{\tan 3x \cdot \sin 2x}{1 - \cos 2x}" />

# Asked

The **value of the trigonometric limit** as <InlineMath math="x" /> approaches zero.

# Solution

> Rewrite one minus cosine of two x as two sine squared x so every tangent and sine factor pairs neatly with x.

As <InlineMath math="x\to0" />, both the numerator and the denominator tend to <InlineMath math="0" />. Rewrite the denominator with the **identity <InlineMath math="1-\cos2x=2\sin^2x" />**. The fraction then splits into factors with known limits.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{\tan3x\cdot\sin2x}{1-\cos2x}&=\frac{\tan3x\cdot\sin2x}{2\sin^2x} \\
&=\frac12\cdot\frac{\tan3x}{\sin x}\cdot\frac{\sin2x}{\sin x}
\end{aligned}" />

With <InlineMath math="a" /> and <InlineMath math="b" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{\tan ax}{\sin x}=a \\
\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{\sin bx}{\sin x}=b \\
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\tan3x\cdot\sin2x}{1-\cos2x}=\frac12\cdot3\cdot2=3
\end{gathered}" />

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="0" /> sees only that the numerator tends to <InlineMath math="0" /> and overlooks that the denominator does too.
- <InlineMath math="\frac32" /> approximates the denominator by <InlineMath math="(2x)^2=4x^2" /> and forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\frac12" /> in the equation below.

  <BlockMath math="1-\cos u\approx\frac{u^2}{2}" />

- <InlineMath math="6" /> uses the wrong identity <InlineMath math="1-\cos2x=\sin^2x" />, without the factor <InlineMath math="2" />.
- <InlineMath math="12" /> puts <InlineMath math="u=x" /> instead of <InlineMath math="u=2x" /> into the equation below.

  <BlockMath math="1-\cos u\approx\frac{u^2}{2}" />
