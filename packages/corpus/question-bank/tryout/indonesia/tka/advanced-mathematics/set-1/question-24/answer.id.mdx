<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{1-\cos4x}{x\sin2x}&=\frac{2\sin^22x}{x\sin2x}=\frac{2\sin2x}{x} \\

\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to0}\frac{1-\cos4x}{x\sin2x}=4\cdot1=4" />

Jadi, pecahan itu mendekati <InlineMath math="\frac{\frac{(4x)^2}{2}}{x\cdot2x}=\frac{8x^2}{2x^2}=4" />.
