The center is already known, so only **the radius** is missing. A tangent line touches the circle at one point, and the radius to that point is perpendicular to the line. So the radius equals **the distance from the center to the tangent line**.

The distance from a point <InlineMath math="(x_0,y_0)" /> to the line <InlineMath math="ax+by+c=0" /> is <InlineMath math="\frac{|ax_0+by_0+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}" />. For the center <InlineMath math="(3,-2)" /> and the line <InlineMath math="4x-3y+2=0" />, watch the sign of the term <InlineMath math="-3y" /> when <InlineMath math="y=-2" />.

<BlockMath math="r=\frac{|4\cdot3-3\cdot(-2)+2|}{\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}}=\frac{|12+6+2|}{\sqrt{25}}=\frac{20}{5}=4" />

A circle with center <InlineMath math="(a,b)" /> and radius <InlineMath math="r" /> has the equation <InlineMath math="(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2" />. Substitute the center and radius, then expand into general form.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2&=16 \\
x^2-6x+9+y^2+4y+4&=16 \\
x^2+y^2-6x+4y-3&=0
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, the point of tangency is <InlineMath math="\left(-\frac15,\frac25\right)" />. It lies on the line, because <InlineMath math="4\left(-\frac15\right)-3\cdot\frac25+2=-\frac45-\frac65+2=0" />, and its distance from the center is <InlineMath math="\sqrt{\left(\frac{16}{5}\right)^2+\left(\frac{12}{5}\right)^2}=\sqrt{\frac{400}{25}}=4" />.

So the equation of the circle is <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2-6x+4y-3=0" />.

- <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2+6x-4y-3=0" /> is the circle with center <InlineMath math="(-3,2)" />. The signs of the center coordinates were reversed when writing <InlineMath math="(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2" />.
- <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2-6x+4y+9=0" /> comes from <InlineMath math="(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=4" />, with the radius <InlineMath math="4" /> written on the right side without squaring it.
- <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2-6x+4y-7=0" /> comes from <InlineMath math="r^2=20" />. The numerator of the distance formula, <InlineMath math="20" />, was used as <InlineMath math="r^2" /> without dividing by <InlineMath math="\sqrt{25}=5" />.
- <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2-6x+4y-12=0" /> comes from <InlineMath math="r=5" />, mistaking the length <InlineMath math="\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}" /> in the denominator of the distance formula for the radius.
