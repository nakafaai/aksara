Both parts grow without bound, and the form <InlineMath math="\infty-\infty" /> says nothing yet about the limit. So **combine everything into a single fraction first**. The squared terms then cancel.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{3x^2+7x}{x-1}-3x&=\frac{3x^2+7x-3x(x-1)}{x-1} \\
&=\frac{3x^2+7x-3x^2+3x}{x-1}=\frac{10x}{x-1}
\end{aligned}" />

Divide the numerator and the denominator by <InlineMath math="x" />.

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{10}{1-\frac1x}=\frac{10}{1}=10" />

As a check, at <InlineMath math="x=1{,}001" /> the value is <InlineMath math="\frac{10\cdot1{,}001}{1{,}000}=10.01" />, close to <InlineMath math="10" />.

So the limit is <InlineMath math="10" />.
