Both parts grow without bound, and the form <InlineMath math="\infty-\infty" /> says nothing yet about the limit. So **combine everything into a single fraction first**. The squared terms then cancel.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{x^2+4x}{x+1}-x&=\frac{x^2+4x-x(x+1)}{x+1} \\
&=\frac{x^2+4x-x^2-x}{x+1}=\frac{3x}{x+1}
\end{aligned}" />

Divide the numerator and the denominator by <InlineMath math="x" />.

<BlockMath math="\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{3}{1+\frac1x}=\frac{3}{1}=3" />

As a check, at <InlineMath math="x=999" /> the value is <InlineMath math="\frac{3\cdot999}{1{,}000}=2.997" />, close to <InlineMath math="3" />.

So the limit is <InlineMath math="3" />.
