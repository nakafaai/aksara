All four statements test properties of **matrix multiplication**: the order of the factors, the determinant of a product, and the transpose of a product. Compute <InlineMath math="AB" /> and <InlineMath math="BA" /> first, because both are used more than once. Each entry of a product is the sum of the products of a row of the left matrix with a column of the right matrix.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
AB&=\begin{pmatrix} 1\cdot1+2\cdot3 & 1\cdot0+2\cdot1 \\ 0\cdot1+1\cdot3 & 0\cdot0+1\cdot1 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 7 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \\
BA&=\begin{pmatrix} 1\cdot1+0\cdot0 & 1\cdot2+0\cdot1 \\ 3\cdot1+1\cdot0 & 3\cdot2+1\cdot1 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 7 \end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}" />

1. The statement <InlineMath math="AB=BA" /> is false. The entry in the first row and first column of <InlineMath math="AB" /> is <InlineMath math="7" />, while the same entry of <InlineMath math="BA" /> is <InlineMath math="1" />. Matrix multiplication is not commutative in general.
2. The statement <InlineMath math="\det(AB)=1" /> is true. From the matrix <InlineMath math="AB" /> above, <InlineMath math="\det(AB)=7\cdot1-2\cdot3=1" />. This agrees with the property <InlineMath math="\det(AB)=\det A\cdot\det B=1\cdot1=1" />.
3. The statement <InlineMath math="(AB)^T=A^TB^T" /> is false. The transpose of a product **reverses the order** of the factors: <InlineMath math="(AB)^T=B^TA^T" />. The calculation below shows that <InlineMath math="A^TB^T" /> really differs from <InlineMath math="(AB)^T" />.
4. The statement <InlineMath math="A^2=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}" /> is true, as the calculation of <InlineMath math="A^2=AA" /> below shows.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(AB)^T&=\begin{pmatrix} 7 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \\
A^TB^T&=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & 7 \end{pmatrix} \\
A^2&=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}" />

So the statements about <InlineMath math="\det(AB)" /> and <InlineMath math="A^2" /> are true, while the statements <InlineMath math="AB=BA" /> and <InlineMath math="(AB)^T=A^TB^T" /> are false.
