# Given

The circle **the equation below** needs a tangent line **perpendicular to** the equation below.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 8y = 0 \\
3x - 4y + 6 = 0
\end{gathered}" />

# Asked

**One equation of the tangent line** meeting that perpendicular condition.

# Solution

> Find the center and radius first, write the perpendicular line family, and pick the constant whose distance to the center equals the radius.

A line touches a circle exactly when **its distance from the centre equals the radius**. So first find the centre and the radius by completing the square.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(x^2+6x+9)+(y^2-8y+16)&=9+16 \\
(x+3)^2+(y-4)^2&=25
\end{aligned}" />

The centre is <InlineMath math="(-3,4)" /> and the radius is <InlineMath math="5" />. The given line has slope <InlineMath math="\frac34" />, so a perpendicular line has slope <InlineMath math="-\frac43" />. Such lines have the form the equation below.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
4x+3y+c=0 \\
\begin{aligned}
\frac{|4\cdot(-3)+3\cdot4+c|}{\sqrt{4^2+3^2}}&=5 \\
|c|&=25 \\
c=25\ \text{or}\ c&=-25
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />

As a check, the point of contact <InlineMath math="(1,7)" /> lies on the circle, because <InlineMath math="25" />, and on the tangent, because <InlineMath math="0" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}

4+21-25=0
\end{gathered}" />

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="3x-4y=0" /> does touch the circle at the origin, but it is parallel to the given line instead of perpendicular.
- <InlineMath math="4x+3y=0" /> passes through the centre, because <InlineMath math="-12+12=0" />, and therefore cuts the circle at two points.
- the equation below comes from <InlineMath math="|c|=5" />, when the radius is not multiplied by <InlineMath math="5" />. Its distance from the centre is only <InlineMath math="1" />.

  <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} 4x+3y-5=0 \\ \sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5 \end{gathered}" />

- the equation below has slope <InlineMath math="\frac43" /> instead of <InlineMath math="-\frac43" />. It is not perpendicular to the given line, and its distance from the centre is <InlineMath math="\frac{49}{5}" />.

  <BlockMath math="4x-3y-25=0" />

# Answer

So among the options, the equation below is one of the required tangents.

<BlockMath math="4x+3y-25=0" />
