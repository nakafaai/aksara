The tangent line we want is **parallel** to <InlineMath math="2x-y+3=0" />, so it has the same gradient. Writing that line as <InlineMath math="y=2x+3" /> shows its gradient <InlineMath math="m=2" />.

The circle <InlineMath math="(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=5" /> has center <InlineMath math="(1,-2)" /> and radius <InlineMath math="r=\sqrt5" />. A tangent line with gradient <InlineMath math="m" /> to a circle with center <InlineMath math="(a,b)" /> and radius <InlineMath math="r" /> satisfies the formula below. The factor <InlineMath math="\sqrt{1+m^2}" /> appears because the distance from the center to the line must equal <InlineMath math="r" />.

<BlockMath math="y-b=m(x-a)\pm r\sqrt{1+m^2}" />

Substitute <InlineMath math="a=1" />, <InlineMath math="b=-2" />, <InlineMath math="m=2" />, and <InlineMath math="r=\sqrt5" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
y+2&=2(x-1)\pm\sqrt5\cdot\sqrt{1+2^2} \\
y+2&=2x-2\pm5 \\
y&=2x-4\pm5
\end{aligned}" />

There are two tangent lines: <InlineMath math="y=2x+1" /> and <InlineMath math="y=2x-9" />. Only <InlineMath math="y=2x+1" /> appears among the options. Check with the distance formula: the line <InlineMath math="2x-y+1=0" /> lies at distance <InlineMath math="\frac{|2\cdot1-(-2)+1|}{\sqrt{2^2+1^2}}=\frac{5}{\sqrt5}=\sqrt5" /> from the center, exactly the radius.

So one tangent line parallel to <InlineMath math="2x-y+3=0" /> is <InlineMath math="y=2x+1" />.

- <InlineMath math="y=2x+3" /> is the given line. Its gradient is right, but its distance from the center, <InlineMath math="\frac{|2+2+3|}{\sqrt5}=\frac{7}{\sqrt5}" />, is more than <InlineMath math="\sqrt5" />, so it does not touch the circle.
- <InlineMath math="y=2x-4+\sqrt5" /> comes from forgetting the factor <InlineMath math="\sqrt{1+m^2}" />, so the added term is only <InlineMath math="r=\sqrt5" />.
- <InlineMath math="y=-\frac12x+1" /> uses the gradient of a **perpendicular** line, not a parallel one.
- <InlineMath math="y=2x+9" /> comes from taking the center as <InlineMath math="(-1,2)" /> with reversed signs, which gives <InlineMath math="y-2=2(x+1)+5" />.
