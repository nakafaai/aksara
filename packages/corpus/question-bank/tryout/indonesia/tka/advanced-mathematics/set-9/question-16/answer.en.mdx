# Given

A pond fence forms a **circle** on a map while a drone hovers above a given map coordinate.

# Asked

The **shortest or longest distance** from the drone position to the pond fence.

# Solution

> Compute the distance from the drone to the pond center first, then subtract or add the radius for closest and farthest.

Complete the squares: the equation below, so the centre is <InlineMath math="M(1,1)" /> and <InlineMath math="r=5" />. The point <InlineMath math="P" /> is outside the circle, since <InlineMath math="0" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
(x-1)^2+(y-1)^2=25 \\
49+81-14-18-23=75>0
\end{gathered}" />

The point of the circle nearest to <InlineMath math="P" /> lies **on the segment from the centre to <InlineMath math="P" />**. So the shortest distance is the distance from the centre to <InlineMath math="P" /> minus the radius.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
MP=\sqrt{(7-1)^2+(9-1)^2}=\sqrt{36+64}=10 \\
d_{\min}=MP-r=10-5=5
\end{gathered}" />
</MathContainer>

Draw the pond fence, its centre and the point <InlineMath math="P" />. **The segment from the centre to <InlineMath math="P" /> crosses the fence exactly at the nearest point**, so the part of that segment outside the fence is the shortest distance.

The nearest point is <InlineMath math="(4,5)" />. Check: <InlineMath math="(4,5)" /> satisfies <InlineMath math="9+16=25" />, and its distance to <InlineMath math="P" /> is <InlineMath math="5" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
M+\frac{5}{10}\overrightarrow{MP}=(1,1)+(3,4)=(4,5) \\
\sqrt{9+16}=5
\end{gathered}" />

So the shortest distance is <InlineMath math="5" /> metres.

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="10" /> is the distance from <InlineMath math="P" /> to the centre of the pond, not to the fence.
- <InlineMath math="15" /> is the distance from <InlineMath math="P" /> to the farthest point of the fence, <InlineMath math="MP+r" />.
- <InlineMath math="5\sqrt3" /> is the tangent length from <InlineMath math="P" />, <InlineMath math="\sqrt{MP^2-r^2}" />, not the shortest distance.
- <InlineMath math="75" /> is the square of that tangent length.

# Answer

Therefore, the **correct answer** is **<InlineMath math="5" />**.
