The centre <InlineMath math="\left(-\frac A2,-\frac B2\right)" /> and radius <InlineMath math="r=\sqrt{\frac{A^2}{4}+\frac{B^2}{4}-C}" /> apply to the form <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2+Ax+By+C=0" />, where the coefficients of <InlineMath math="x^2" /> and <InlineMath math="y^2" /> are <InlineMath math="1" />. So **divide the whole equation by <InlineMath math="2" /> first**.

<BlockMath math="x^2+y^2-4x+6y-3=0" />

Now <InlineMath math="A=-4" />, <InlineMath math="B=6" /> and <InlineMath math="C=-3" />, so the centre is <InlineMath math="(2,-3)" />.

<BlockMath math="r=\sqrt{4+9-(-3)}=\sqrt{16}=4" />
