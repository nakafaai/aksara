These statements test properties of transposes and determinants. **The properties that always hold** are <InlineMath math="(A+B)^T=A^T+B^T" />, <InlineMath math="(AB)^T=B^TA^T" /> and <InlineMath math="\det(AB)=\det A\cdot\det B" />. There is no general rule for <InlineMath math="\det(A+B)" />.

<BlockMath math="AB=\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 8 \\ 3 & 12 \end{pmatrix},\qquad A+B=\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 1 & 7 \end{pmatrix},\qquad \det A=3,\quad \det B=8" />

1. **True.** The transpose of a sum is the sum of the transposes. Here both are <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 7 \end{pmatrix}" />.
2. **False.** <InlineMath math="(AB)^T=\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 3 \\ 8 & 12 \end{pmatrix}" />, while <InlineMath math="A^TB^T=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 4 & 14 \end{pmatrix}" />. The correct order is <InlineMath math="B^TA^T" />.
3. **False.** <InlineMath math="\det(A+B)=21-2=19" />, while <InlineMath math="\det A+\det B=3+8=11" />.
4. **True.** <InlineMath math="\det(AB)=4\cdot12-8\cdot3=24" />, equal to <InlineMath math="\det A\cdot\det B=3\cdot8" />.

So the answers are True, False, False, True.
