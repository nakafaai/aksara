# Given

We are given two **square matrices** of size <InlineMath math="2\times2" /> to compare transpose and determinant rules.

# Asked

The **truth value of each statement** about product transposes and determinants.

# Solution

> Transposing leaves the determinant unchanged, while transposing a product always reverses the order of the factors.

These statements test properties of transposes and determinants. **The properties that always hold** are the equation below, <InlineMath math="(AB)^T=B^TA^T" /> and <InlineMath math="\det(AB)=\det A\cdot\det B" />. There is no general rule for <InlineMath math="\det(A+B)" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
(A+B)^T=A^T+B^T \\
AB=\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 8 \\ 3 & 12 \end{pmatrix},\qquad A+B=\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 1 & 7 \end{pmatrix},\qquad \det A=3,\quad \det B=8
\end{gathered}" />

Compare the determinant of a sum with the sum of determinants, then the determinant of a product with the product of determinants. **The first pair differs, the second pair matches**, because only multiplication has a general rule for determinants.

1. **True.** The transpose of a sum is the sum of the transposes. Here both are the matrix below.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 7 \end{pmatrix}" />

2. **False.** The matrix below, while the matrix below. The correct order is <InlineMath math="B^TA^T" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} (AB)^T=\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 3 \\ 8 & 12 \end{pmatrix} \\ A^TB^T=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 4 & 14 \end{pmatrix} \end{gathered}" />

3. **False.** <InlineMath math="19" />, while <InlineMath math="11" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} \det(A+B)=21-2=19 \\ \det A+\det B=3+8=11 \end{gathered}" />

4. **True.** <InlineMath math="\det(AB)=24" />, equal to <InlineMath math="\det A\cdot\det B=3\cdot8" />.

   <BlockMath math="\det(AB)=4\cdot12-8\cdot3=24" />

# Answer

So the answers are True, False, False, True.
