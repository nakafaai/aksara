Turn both facts into equations with **the remainder theorem**: divisible by <InlineMath math="x-2" /> means <InlineMath math="P(2)=0" />, and remainder <InlineMath math="-6" /> on division by <InlineMath math="x+1" /> means <InlineMath math="P(-1)=-6" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

P(-1)&=-2+a-b-4=-6 &&\Rightarrow\ a-b=0
\end{aligned}" />

The second equation gives <InlineMath math="a=b" />. Substituting into the first: <InlineMath math="3a=-6" />, so <InlineMath math="a=b=-2" />. Factor by dividing by <InlineMath math="x-2" />.

So the true statements are <InlineMath math="a+b=-4" />, that <InlineMath math="P(x)=0" /> has exactly one real root, and that the remainder on division by <InlineMath math="x-1" /> is <InlineMath math="-6" />.
