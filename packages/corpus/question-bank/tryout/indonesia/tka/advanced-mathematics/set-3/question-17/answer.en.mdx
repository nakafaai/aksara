# Given

We are given a **circle equation** <InlineMath math="L" /> and a point <InlineMath math="P(4,5)" /> in the coordinate plane.

# Asked

The **truth value of each statement** about the center, radius, and position of point <InlineMath math="P" />.

# Solution

> Once you read off the center and radius, comparing the point distance to the radius tells you immediately where it sits.

Every statement depends on **where <InlineMath math="P" /> sits relative to the centre**. Complete the square, then find the distance from <InlineMath math="P" /> to the centre.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(x-3)^2+(y-2)^2&=9+4+12=25 \\
|MP|^2&=(4-3)^2+(5-2)^2=1+9=10
\end{aligned}" />

The centre is <InlineMath math="M(3,2)" />, <InlineMath math="r=5" />, and <InlineMath math="|MP|" />.

<BlockMath math="|MP|=\sqrt{10}" />

1. The statement that <InlineMath math="P" /> lies outside the circle is false, because the equation below. The point <InlineMath math="P" /> is inside.

   <BlockMath math="\sqrt{10}<5" />

2. The statement about the longest chord is false. The longest chord through any point inside a circle is the diameter through that point, of length <InlineMath math="2r=10" />.
3. The statement about the shortest chord is true. The shortest chord is perpendicular to <InlineMath math="MP" />. Its distance from the centre is <InlineMath math="\sqrt{10}" />, so half its length is the equation below and its length is <InlineMath math="2\sqrt{15}" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} |MP|=\sqrt{10} \\ \sqrt{25-10}=\sqrt{15} \end{gathered}" />

4. The statement about the line given below is true. The line from the centre to the midpoint of a chord is always perpendicular to the chord. So the chord with midpoint <InlineMath math="P" /> is perpendicular to <InlineMath math="\overrightarrow{MP}=(1,3)" /> and passes through <InlineMath math="P" />.

   <BlockMath math="x+3y-19=0" />

<BlockMath math="1(x-4)+3(y-5)=0\iff x+3y-19=0" />

# Answer

So the statements about the shortest chord and the line given below are true, and the statements about the position of <InlineMath math="P" /> and the longest chord are false.
