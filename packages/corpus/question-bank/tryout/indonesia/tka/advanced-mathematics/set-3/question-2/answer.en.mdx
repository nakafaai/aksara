# Given

We are given the **weekend sales matrix** <InlineMath math="Q" /> and the **unit price matrix** <InlineMath math="H" />.

# Asked

The **total sales revenue** found from the matrix product <InlineMath math="QH" />.

# Solution

> Multiplying each day of item counts by the column of prices immediately gives that day total revenue.

The product <InlineMath math="QH" /> multiplies each row of <InlineMath math="Q" /> with the column <InlineMath math="H" />, entry by entry, and adds the results. So **each entry of <InlineMath math="QH" /> is a sum of quantity times price** for one day.

<BlockMath math="QH=\begin{pmatrix} 10\cdot15+5\cdot20+8\cdot25 \\ 6\cdot15+12\cdot20+4\cdot25 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 150+100+200 \\ 90+240+100 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 450 \\ 430 \end{pmatrix}" />

Break each entry of <InlineMath math="QH" /> into what each item contributes. **One entry is the total revenue of the three items on one day**, so the second row is the total of Sunday's bars.

The second row of <InlineMath math="Q" /> holds Sunday's sales. The result is <InlineMath math="430" /> thousand rupiah, Sunday's revenue. The unit of each product is items times thousand rupiah per item, so the result is money, not a number of items.

So the second entry of <InlineMath math="QH" /> is Sunday's revenue, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}430{,}000.00" />.

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- Saturday's revenue of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}430{,}000.00" /> swaps the rows. Saturday's revenue is the first entry, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}450{,}000.00" />.
- Sunday's revenue of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}450{,}000.00" /> takes the value of the first row.
- <InlineMath math="22" /> items is <InlineMath math="6+12+4" />, Sunday's item count without prices. Multiplying by <InlineMath math="H" /> turns counts into money.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}340{,}000.00" /> is <InlineMath math="20\cdot(5+12)" />, the revenue from notebooks. It comes from one column of <InlineMath math="Q" />, not from one row of <InlineMath math="QH" />.
