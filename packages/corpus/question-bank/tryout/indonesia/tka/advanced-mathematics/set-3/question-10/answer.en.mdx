# Given

We are given an **exponential function** <InlineMath math="f(x)" /> with base <InlineMath math="3" /> on all real numbers.

# Asked

The **truth value of each statement** about growth, function values, and its inverse.

# Solution

> Exponentials and logarithms undo each other, so basic exponent laws check every statement quickly.

Two tools cover all four statements: **the laws of exponents** for <InlineMath math="f" /> and **the definition of a logarithm** for its inverse. Here <InlineMath math="{}^{3}\log" /> is the base-3 logarithm.

Draw <InlineMath math="f" /> and its inverse. **The two graphs mirror each other in <InlineMath math="y=x" />**, so <InlineMath math="f^{-1}\left(\tfrac19\right)" /> is the value of <InlineMath math="x" /> that makes <InlineMath math="f(x)=\tfrac19" />, which is <InlineMath math="-1" />.

1. The statement <InlineMath math="\frac{10}{3}" /> is true: <InlineMath math="f(2)=3" /> and <InlineMath math="f(0)=\frac13" />, which add up to <InlineMath math="\frac{10}{3}" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} f(2)+f(0)=\frac{10}{3} \\ f(2)=3^1=3 \\ f(0)=3^{-1}=\frac13 \end{gathered}" />

2. The statement about <InlineMath math="f^{-1}" /> is true. From <InlineMath math="y=3^{x-1}" />, the definition of a logarithm gives <InlineMath math="x-1={}^{3}\log y" />, so <InlineMath math="x=1+{}^{3}\log y" />. The condition <InlineMath math="x>0" /> is needed because the values of <InlineMath math="f" /> are always positive.
3. The statement that <InlineMath math="f(x+1)=3f(x)" /> holds only for <InlineMath math="x>0" /> is false. For every real <InlineMath math="x" />, <InlineMath math="3f(x)" />.

   <BlockMath math="f(x+1)=3^{x}=3\cdot3^{x-1}=3f(x)" />

4. The statement <InlineMath math="f^{-1}\left(\frac19\right)=-2" /> is false. <InlineMath math="-1" />. Check: <InlineMath math="\frac19" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} f^{-1}\left(\frac19\right)=1+{}^{3}\log\frac19=1+(-2)=-1 \\ f(-1)=3^{-2}=\frac19 \end{gathered}" />

# Answer

So the statements about <InlineMath math="f(2)+f(0)" /> and the formula for <InlineMath math="f^{-1}" /> are true, and the statements about <InlineMath math="f(x+1)" /> and <InlineMath math="f^{-1}\left(\frac19\right)" /> are false.
