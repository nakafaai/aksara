# Given

We are given two **space vectors** <InlineMath math="\vec{u}" /> and <InlineMath math="\vec{v}" /> with their components.

# Asked

The **trigonometric value** or angle <InlineMath math="\theta" /> between the two vectors.

# Solution

> Dividing the dot product by both vector lengths gives the cosine of the angle, and a right triangle gives the sine.

The dot product gives <InlineMath math="\cos\theta" /> directly, not <InlineMath math="\sin\theta" />. So find **the cosine first**, then use <InlineMath math="1" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1 \\
\begin{aligned}
\vec{u}\cdot\vec{v}&=2\cdot3+1\cdot(-4)+(-2)\cdot0=2 \\
|\vec{u}|&=\sqrt{4+1+4}=3,\qquad |\vec{v}|=\sqrt{9+16+0}=5 \\
\cos\theta&=\frac{\vec{u}\cdot\vec{v}}{|\vec{u}||\vec{v}|}=\frac{2}{15}
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />

The angle between two vectors lies between <InlineMath math="0^\circ" /> and <InlineMath math="180^\circ" />, so <InlineMath math="\sin\theta\ge0" />. Take the positive root.

<BlockMath math="\sin\theta=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{2}{15}\right)^2}=\sqrt{\frac{225-4}{225}}=\frac{\sqrt{221}}{15}" />

Draw both vectors in the plane that holds them. **The angle is nearly a right angle because <InlineMath math="\cos\theta=\tfrac{2}{15}" /> is small**, so <InlineMath math="\sin\theta" /> is close to <InlineMath math="1" />.

<BlockMath math="\sin\theta=\frac{1}{15}\sqrt{221}" />

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="\frac{2}{15}" /> is <InlineMath math="\cos\theta" />, not yet turned into a sine.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{5}" /> comes from a dot product of <InlineMath math="10" />, a sign slip in <InlineMath math="1\cdot(-4)" />, which gives <InlineMath math="\cos\theta=\frac23" />.
- <InlineMath math="\frac23" /> is the cosine with that same sign slip.
- <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{15}\sqrt{229}" /> adds <InlineMath math="1+\cos^2\theta" /> instead of subtracting.

# Answer

Therefore, the **correct answer** is **<InlineMath math="\frac{1}{15}\sqrt{221}" />**.
