# Given

A circular gate arch spans <InlineMath math="12" /> m along the **flat floor** and rises <InlineMath math="4" /> m high at its **center peak**.

# Asked

The **radius of the circle** forming the arch.

# Solution

> Split the arch down the middle so half the floor span, the vertical offset, and the radius form a right triangle.

The floor segment between the two ends of the arch is **a chord** of length <InlineMath math="12" /> m. The line from the centre to the highest point is perpendicular to the chord and splits it into two parts of <InlineMath math="6" /> m.

Let the radius be <InlineMath math="r" />. The highest point is <InlineMath math="r" /> from the centre and <InlineMath math="4" /> m above the floor, so the centre is <InlineMath math="r-4" /> m below the floor. The centre, the midpoint of the chord and one end of the arch form a right triangle with hypotenuse <InlineMath math="r" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
r^2&=6^2+(r-4)^2 \\
r^2&=36+r^2-8r+16 \\
8r&=52 \\
r&=6.5
\end{aligned}" />
