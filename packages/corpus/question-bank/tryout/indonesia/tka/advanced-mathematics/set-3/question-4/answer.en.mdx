Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the number of lamps and <InlineMath math="y" /> the number of fans per week. There are **three limits**: assembly hours, testing hours, and the stock of fan blades.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x+y&\le40 \\
x+y&\le30 \\
y&\le25 \\
x\ge0,\quad y&\ge0
\end{aligned}" />

The objective, in thousands of rupiah, is <InlineMath math="P=60x+40y" />. Its maximum sits at a corner of the feasible region. Find the corners where neighbouring boundary lines cross.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x+y=40,\ x+y=30&\ \Rightarrow\ x=10,\ y=20 \\
x+y=30,\ y=25&\ \Rightarrow\ x=5,\ y=25
\end{aligned}" />

The other corners are <InlineMath math="(0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="(20,0)" /> on the line <InlineMath math="2x+y=40" />, and <InlineMath math="(0,25)" />. The point <InlineMath math="(10,20)" /> satisfies <InlineMath math="y\le25" />, and <InlineMath math="(5,25)" /> satisfies <InlineMath math="2\cdot5+25=35\le40" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(20,0)&=1{,}200 \\
P(10,20)&=600+800=1{,}400 \\
P(5,25)&=300+1{,}000=1{,}300 \\
P(0,25)&=1{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

So the maximum profit is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}400{,}000.00" />, from assembling <InlineMath math="10" /> lamps and <InlineMath math="20" /> fans.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}200{,}000.00" /> is the profit at <InlineMath math="(20,0)" />, lamps only.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}600{,}000.00" /> is the profit of <InlineMath math="(20,10)" />. That plan needs <InlineMath math="50" /> assembly hours, more than <InlineMath math="40" />.
