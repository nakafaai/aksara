# Solution

> A circle center sits right at the midpoint of its diameter, and its radius is half the distance between the two ends.

The centre of the circle is **the midpoint of the diameter**, and the radius is half the diameter's length.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
M&=\left(\frac{-1+5}{2},\ \frac{4+(-4)}{2}\right)=(2,0) \\
|AB|&=\sqrt{(5+1)^2+(-4-4)^2}=\sqrt{36+64}=10,\qquad r=5
\end{aligned}" />

Write the standard form and expand it.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

x^2+y^2-4x-21&=0
\end{aligned}" />
