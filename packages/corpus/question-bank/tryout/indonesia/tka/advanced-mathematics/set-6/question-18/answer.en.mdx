A dilation with factor <InlineMath math="k" /> multiplies every length by <InlineMath math="|k|" />. Area is a product of two lengths, so **area is multiplied by <InlineMath math="k^2" />**.

<BlockMath math="A'=k^2\cdot A=(-3)^2\cdot6=9\cdot6=54" />

The negative sign of the factor only puts the image on the other side of the centre, turned around. The area stays positive. In matrix form the dilation is <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix} -3 & 0 \\ 0 & -3 \end{pmatrix}" /> with determinant <InlineMath math="9" />, and areas are multiplied by the absolute value of the determinant.

So the image triangle has area <InlineMath math="54" /> square units.
