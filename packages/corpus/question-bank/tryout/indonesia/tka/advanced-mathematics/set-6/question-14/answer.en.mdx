For a circle <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2+Ax+By+C=0" />, **the squared radius** is <InlineMath math="r^2=\left(\frac A2\right)^2+\left(\frac B2\right)^2-C" />. This comes from completing the square.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(x+2)^2+(y-1)^2&=4+1-c \\
r^2&=5-c
\end{aligned}" />

The radius is <InlineMath math="3" />, so <InlineMath math="r^2=9" />.

<BlockMath math="5-c=9\ \Rightarrow\ c=-4" />

Check: <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2+4x-2y-4=0" /> becomes <InlineMath math="(x+2)^2+(y-1)^2=9" />, a circle with centre <InlineMath math="(-2,1)" /> and radius <InlineMath math="3" />.

So <InlineMath math="c=-4" />.

- <InlineMath math="14" /> adds <InlineMath math="4+1+9" />, but <InlineMath math="c" /> is subtracted.
- <InlineMath math="-14" /> negates the whole sum <InlineMath math="4+1+9" />.
- <InlineMath math="2" /> uses the radius <InlineMath math="3" /> without squaring it, <InlineMath math="5-c=3" />.
