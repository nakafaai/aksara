Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the number of bags and <InlineMath math="y" /> the number of wallets per week. There are **two resource limits**: cloth and sewing hours.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2x+y&\le40 \\
x+2y&\le50 \\
x\ge0,\quad y&\ge0
\end{aligned}" />

The objective, in thousands of rupiah, is <InlineMath math="P=30x+20y" />. The corner where both resources run out comes from substituting <InlineMath math="y=40-2x" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
x+2(40-2x)&=50 \\
80-3x&=50\ \Rightarrow\ x=10,\quad y=20
\end{aligned}" />

The other corners are <InlineMath math="(0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="(20,0)" /> on the cloth line, and <InlineMath math="(0,25)" /> on the sewing line.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(20,0)&=600 \\
P(10,20)&=300+400=700 \\
P(0,25)&=500
\end{aligned}" />

So the maximum profit is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}700{,}000.00" />, from <InlineMath math="10" /> bags and <InlineMath math="20" /> wallets.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}600{,}000.00" /> is the profit at <InlineMath math="(20,0)" />, bags only.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}500{,}000.00" /> is the profit at <InlineMath math="(0,25)" />, wallets only.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}750{,}000.00" /> is the profit of <InlineMath math="(15,15)" />. That plan needs <InlineMath math="45\text{ m}^2" /> of cloth, more than <InlineMath math="40\text{ m}^2" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}800{,}000.00" /> is the profit of <InlineMath math="(20,10)" />, which needs <InlineMath math="50\text{ m}^2" /> of cloth.
