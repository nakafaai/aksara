# Given

Polynomial <InlineMath math="P(x)" /> satisfies a **forward difference identity** for every real <InlineMath math="x" />.

# Asked

The **formula or requested value** of polynomial <InlineMath math="P(x)" />.

# Solution

> A linear difference means the original polynomial must be quadratic, so matching coefficients solves it cleanly.

Start with the degree of <InlineMath math="P" />. If <InlineMath math="P" /> has degree <InlineMath math="n" /> with leading term <InlineMath math="ax^n" />, that term cancels in <InlineMath math="P(x+1)-P(x)" />, so the difference has degree <InlineMath math="n-1" />. The difference <InlineMath math="6x+2" /> has degree one, so **<InlineMath math="P" /> has degree two**. Write <InlineMath math="P(x)" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
P(x)=ax^2+bx+c \\
\begin{aligned}
P(x+1)-P(x)&=a\left[(x+1)^2-x^2\right]+b\left[(x+1)-x\right] \\
&=2ax+(a+b)
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />

Matching coefficients with <InlineMath math="6x+2" /> gives <InlineMath math="2a=6" /> and <InlineMath math="a+b=2" />, so <InlineMath math="a=3" /> and <InlineMath math="b=-1" />. The identity says nothing about <InlineMath math="c" />, because the constant cancels too. That is where the remainder theorem comes in.

<BlockMath math="P(2)=12-2+c=13\ \Rightarrow\ c=3" />

So <InlineMath math="P(x)" />, and the remainder on division by <InlineMath math="x+1" /> is <InlineMath math="P(-1)" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
P(x)=3x^2-x+3 \\
P(-1)=3+1+3=7
\end{gathered}" />

Draw <InlineMath math="P" /> with its values at whole numbers. **The height difference between neighbouring points is <InlineMath math="6x+2" />**: from <InlineMath math="x=0" /> to <InlineMath math="1" /> it rises <InlineMath math="2" />, from <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="2" /> it rises <InlineMath math="8" />.

Check: <InlineMath math="P(0)=3" />, <InlineMath math="P(1)=5" />, and <InlineMath math="P(1)-P(0)=2" />, which equals <InlineMath math="6\cdot0+2" />. Then <InlineMath math="P(2)=13" /> and <InlineMath math="6\cdot1+2" />.

<BlockMath math="P(2)-P(1)=8=6\cdot1+2" />

So the remainder is <InlineMath math="7" />.
