1. The statement <InlineMath math="\det M=0" /> is true: <InlineMath math="1\cdot4-2\cdot2=0" />. Rows that are multiples of each other always give determinant zero.
2. The statement that <InlineMath math="M" /> is symmetric is true. Both off-diagonal entries are <InlineMath math="2" />, so transposing does not change <InlineMath math="M" />.
3. The statement that <InlineMath math="M" /> has an inverse is false. The inverse formula divides by <InlineMath math="\det M" />, and division by zero is undefined.
4. The statement <InlineMath math="M^2=4M" /> is false. Compute the product.

<BlockMath math="M^2=\begin{pmatrix} 1+4 & 2+8 \\ 2+8 & 4+16 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 10 \\ 10 & 20 \end{pmatrix}=5M" />

The multiple is <InlineMath math="5" />, the sum of the diagonal entries <InlineMath math="1+4" />, not <InlineMath math="4" />.

So the statements about the determinant and symmetry are true, and the statements about the inverse and <InlineMath math="M^2=4M" /> are false.
