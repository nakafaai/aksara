# Given

Points <InlineMath math="A(1,0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="B(0,2,0)" />, and <InlineMath math="C(0,0,3)" /> each sit on **one coordinate axis**.

# Asked

The **geometric property of triangle** <InlineMath math="ABC" /> or requested vector quantity.

# Solution

> Form two edge vectors from one vertex, then use dot or cross products to read off angles and area.

Compute the side vectors, then **judge each angle by the sign of the dot product** of the two vectors leaving that vertex. A positive dot product means the angle is acute.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
\overrightarrow{AB}=(-1,2,0),\qquad \overrightarrow{AC}=(-1,0,3),\qquad \overrightarrow{BC}=(0,-2,3) \\
\begin{aligned}
\text{at }A&:\ \overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\overrightarrow{AC}=1+0+0=1 \\
\text{at }B&:\ \overrightarrow{BA}\cdot\overrightarrow{BC}=(1,-2,0)\cdot(0,-2,3)=4 \\
\text{at }C&:\ \overrightarrow{CA}\cdot\overrightarrow{CB}=(1,0,-3)\cdot(0,2,-3)=9
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />
</MathContainer>

Draw the triangle in its own plane at true size. **All three angles are acute, and the height from <InlineMath math="C" /> to <InlineMath math="AB" /> is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{7}{\sqrt5}" />**, so the area is the equation below.

<BlockMath math="\tfrac12\cdot\sqrt5\cdot\tfrac{7}{\sqrt5}=\tfrac72" />

1. The statement that triangle <InlineMath math="ABC" /> has a right angle is false, because none of the dot products is zero.
2. The statement about the area is true. With <InlineMath math="|\overrightarrow{AB}|^2=5" /> and <InlineMath math="|\overrightarrow{AC}|^2=10" />, the triangle's area is half the area of the parallelogram on those two sides.

<BlockMath math="A=\frac12\sqrt{|\overrightarrow{AB}|^2|\overrightarrow{AC}|^2-\left(\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\overrightarrow{AC}\right)^2}=\frac12\sqrt{50-1}=\frac72" />

3. The statement that all three angles are acute is true, because all three dot products above are positive.
4. The statement <InlineMath math="|\overrightarrow{AB}|" /> is false, because the equation below.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} |\overrightarrow{AB}|=|\overrightarrow{AC}| \\ \sqrt5\ne\sqrt{10} \end{gathered}" />

5. The statement about the vector <InlineMath math="6\vec{i}+3\vec{j}+2\vec{k}" /> is true: <InlineMath math="0" /> and <InlineMath math="0" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} (6,3,2)\cdot(-1,2,0)=-6+6=0 \\ (6,3,2)\cdot(-1,0,3)=-6+6=0 \end{gathered}" />

# Answer

So the true statements are the area of <InlineMath math="\frac72" /> square units, the three acute angles, and the vector <InlineMath math="6\vec{i}+3\vec{j}+2\vec{k}" /> perpendicular to both sides.
