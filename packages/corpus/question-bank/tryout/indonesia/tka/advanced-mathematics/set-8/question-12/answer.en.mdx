Angle <InlineMath math="ABC" /> is the angle at <InlineMath math="B" /> between two vectors that **both start at <InlineMath math="B" />**, namely <InlineMath math="\overrightarrow{BA}" /> and <InlineMath math="\overrightarrow{BC}" />.

<BlockMath math="\overrightarrow{BA}=A-B=(1,1,0),\qquad \overrightarrow{BC}=C-B=(0,-1,1)" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\cos\angle ABC&=\frac{\overrightarrow{BA}\cdot\overrightarrow{BC}}{\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|} \\
&=\frac{0-1+0}{\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2}=-\frac12
\end{aligned}" />

The angle between <InlineMath math="0^\circ" /> and <InlineMath math="180^\circ" /> with cosine <InlineMath math="-\frac12" /> is <InlineMath math="120^\circ" />.

Check with the cosine rule: <InlineMath math="AC^2=1+4+1=6" />, and <InlineMath math="BA^2+BC^2-2\cdot BA\cdot BC\cos120^\circ=2+2+2=6" />.

So angle <InlineMath math="ABC" /> is <InlineMath math="120^\circ" />.
