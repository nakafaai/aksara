Substituting <InlineMath math="x=1" /> gives <InlineMath math="\frac{2-2}{0}=\frac00" />. A square-root conjugate does not clear a cube root, so **let <InlineMath math="u=\sqrt[3]{x+7}" />**. Then <InlineMath math="x=u^3-7" />, and <InlineMath math="u\to2" /> as <InlineMath math="x\to1" />.
