# Given

A counterclockwise <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> rotation around **center** <InlineMath math="P" /> maps a known point to its image.

# Asked

The **rotation center** <InlineMath math="P" /> or requested image point.

# Solution

> Shift relative to the rotation center first, apply the quarter turn, then shift back to solve for the unknown.

Let <InlineMath math="P(a,b)" />. A <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> anticlockwise rotation turns **the vector from the centre to the point** by the rule <InlineMath math="(u,v)\mapsto(-v,u)" />. The vector the equation below must become the equation below.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
\overrightarrow{PA}=(5-a,\,1-b) \\
\overrightarrow{PA'}=(3-a,\,5-b) \\
\begin{aligned}
3-a&=-(1-b) &&\Leftrightarrow\quad a+b=4 \\
5-b&=5-a &&\Leftrightarrow\quad a=b
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />

Draw the centre of rotation, <InlineMath math="A" />, and its image. **The arrow from <InlineMath math="P" /> to <InlineMath math="A" /> turns a quarter turn anticlockwise and becomes the arrow from <InlineMath math="P" /> to <InlineMath math="A'" />**, with the same length.

<BlockMath math="a=b=2" />

Check: <InlineMath math="\overrightarrow{PA}=(3,-1)" /> turns into <InlineMath math="(1,3)" />, and <InlineMath math="A'" />. Both vectors have length <InlineMath math="\sqrt{10}" /> and their dot product is <InlineMath math="3-3=0" />, so the angle is indeed a right angle.

<BlockMath math="P+(1,3)=(3,5)=A'" />

So <InlineMath math="P(2,2)" />.

## Checking common traps

Let us check why these **common trap choices** miss the mark.

- <InlineMath math="(4,3)" /> is the midpoint of <InlineMath math="AA'" />. The centre does lie on the perpendicular bisector of <InlineMath math="AA'" />, but not at the midpoint for a <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> rotation.
- <InlineMath math="(6,4)" /> is the centre of the <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> clockwise rotation that sends <InlineMath math="A" /> to <InlineMath math="A'" />.
- <InlineMath math="(0,0)" /> assumes the centre is the origin. A <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> rotation about <InlineMath math="O" /> sends <InlineMath math="A" /> to <InlineMath math="(-1,5)" />.
- <InlineMath math="(0,1)" /> also lies on the perpendicular bisector of <InlineMath math="AA'" />, but the angle <InlineMath math="APA'" /> there is not <InlineMath math="90^\circ" />.
