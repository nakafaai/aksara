# Given

# Asked

# Solution

> The mirror point sits right halfway between start and image, so repeat the same step once more past the midpoint.

Under reflection in a point <InlineMath math="M" />, the point <InlineMath math="M" /> becomes **the midpoint of the segment joining <InlineMath math="P" /> and its image <InlineMath math="P'" />**. So <InlineMath math="P'=2M-P" />.

<BlockMath math="P'=\bigl(2\cdot(-1)-3,\ 2\cdot5-(-2)\bigr)=(-5,12)" />

Draw <InlineMath math="P" />, the centre <InlineMath math="M" />, and the image. **The step from <InlineMath math="M" /> to <InlineMath math="P'" /> is the same as the step from <InlineMath math="P" /> to <InlineMath math="M" />**, so <InlineMath math="M" /> sits exactly in the middle.
