Rewrite <InlineMath math="1-\cos ax" /> in terms of sine with **the double-angle identity the equation below**, using the equation below.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}

\begin{aligned}
