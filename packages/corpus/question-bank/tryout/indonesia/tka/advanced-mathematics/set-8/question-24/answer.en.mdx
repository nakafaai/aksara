Rewrite <InlineMath math="1-\cos ax" /> in terms of sine with **the double-angle identity <InlineMath math="1-\cos2\theta=2\sin^2\theta" />**, using <InlineMath math="\theta=\frac{ax}{2}" />.

<BlockMath math="1-\cos ax=2\sin^2\frac{ax}{2}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{2\sin^2\frac{ax}{2}}{x^2}&=2\left(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin\frac{ax}{2}}{x}\right)^2 \\
&=2\left(\frac a2\right)^2=\frac{a^2}{2}
\end{aligned}" />

The limit equals <InlineMath math="8" />, so <InlineMath math="\frac{a^2}{2}=8" /> and <InlineMath math="a^2=16" />. Since <InlineMath math="a" /> is positive, <InlineMath math="a=4" />.

Numerical check: for <InlineMath math="a=4" /> and <InlineMath math="x=0.01" />, <InlineMath math="\frac{1-\cos0.04}{0.0001}\approx7.9989" />.
