# Given

We are given a **linear rational function** <InlineMath math="f(x)" /> for <InlineMath math="x\ne-4" />.

# Asked

The **truth value of each statement** about asymptotes, range, and the inverse of <InlineMath math="f" />.

# Solution

> The denominator root gives the vertical asymptote, while the leading coefficient ratio gives the horizontal asymptote.

Find the inverse first. Write <InlineMath math="y" /> and **solve for <InlineMath math="x" /> in terms of <InlineMath math="y" />**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
y=\frac{3x-2}{x+4} \\
\begin{aligned}
y(x+4)&=3x-2 \\
xy-3x&=-2-4y \\
x&=\frac{4y+2}{3-y}
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}" />

Draw <InlineMath math="f" /> and <InlineMath math="f^{-1}" /> with the line <InlineMath math="y=x" />. **The vertical line <InlineMath math="x=3" /> is the boundary of the graph of <InlineMath math="f^{-1}" />**, so <InlineMath math="3" />, not <InlineMath math="-4" />, is left out of the domain of <InlineMath math="f^{-1}" />. To the right of that line, the graph of <InlineMath math="f^{-1}" /> is below the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis.

So the equation below, defined for <InlineMath math="x\neq3" />. Indeed <InlineMath math="f" /> never outputs <InlineMath math="3" />, because the equation below would mean <InlineMath math="-2=12" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}
f^{-1}(x)=\frac{4x+2}{3-x} \\
\frac{3x-2}{x+4}=3
\end{gathered}" />

1. **False.** The domain of <InlineMath math="f^{-1}" /> is the range of <InlineMath math="f" />, which is <InlineMath math="x\neq3" />. The condition <InlineMath math="x\neq-4" /> is the domain of <InlineMath math="f" />.
2. **False.** <InlineMath math="3" />. The value <InlineMath math="\frac15" /> is <InlineMath math="f(1)" />. Check: <InlineMath math="f(3)=1" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} f^{-1}(1)=\frac{4+2}{3-1}=3 \\ f(3)=\frac77=1 \end{gathered}" />

3. **True.** <InlineMath math="f(0)=-\frac12" />, so <InlineMath math="f^{-1}\left(-\frac12\right)=0" />. The inverse rule agrees: the equation below.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} f(0)=-\frac24=-\frac12 \\ \frac{-2+2}{3+\frac12}=0 \end{gathered}" />

4. **True.** For <InlineMath math="x>3" /> the numerator <InlineMath math="4x+2" /> is positive and the denominator <InlineMath math="3-x" /> is negative, so the quotient is negative. Example: <InlineMath math="-18" />, and <InlineMath math="4" />.

   <BlockMath math="\begin{gathered} f^{-1}(4)=\frac{18}{-1}=-18 \\ f(-18)=\frac{-56}{-14}=4 \end{gathered}" />

# Answer

Therefore, **statements <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> are True**, while **statements <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="2" /> are False**.
