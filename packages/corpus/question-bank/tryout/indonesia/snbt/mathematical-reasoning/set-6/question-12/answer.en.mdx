Budi has two goals in order: first arrive as early as possible, then, among equally fast options, pay the least. So **work out the arrival time for every bus A Budi can take**, find the earliest arrival, and then compare the fares.

# Arrival time for each bus A

The buses A Budi can take are the ones after 21:45: 21:54, 22:12, 22:30, and so on. Bus A reaches Halte Pasar <InlineMath math="25\text{ minutes}" /> after it leaves. Bus B has to leave at least <InlineMath math="4\text{ minutes}" /> after bus A arrives, and then takes <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" /> to Halte Kampus.

| Bus A leaves | Reaches Halte Pasar | Earliest bus B | Wait | Reaches Halte Kampus |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 21:54 | 22:19 | 22:52 | <InlineMath math="33\text{ minutes}" /> | 23:07 |
| 22:12 | 22:37 | 22:52 | <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" /> | 23:07 |
| 22:30 | 22:55 | 23:22 | <InlineMath math="27\text{ minutes}" /> | 23:37 |

The 22:22 bus B can't be caught from the 21:54 bus A, because the gap is only <InlineMath math="3\text{ minutes}" />. Later buses A only arrive later. The earliest arrival is 23:07, and **two buses A give the same arrival time**.

# The fare for the two equally fast options

A rider doesn't pay for bus B if bus B leaves at most <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" /> after bus A arrives.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{bus A at 21:54: } & \text{wait } 33 > 15,\ \text{fare } 5{,}000+4{,}000=9{,}000 \\ \text{bus A at 22:12: } & \text{wait } 15 \le 15,\ \text{fare } 5{,}000 \end{aligned}" />

So **the later bus A is actually cheaper** and still arrives at 23:07. Budi takes the 22:12 bus A.

# Checking the choice

On the 22:12 bus A, Budi reaches Halte Pasar at 22:37, walks for at least <InlineMath math="4\text{ minutes}" />, boards the 22:52 bus B, and reaches Halte Kampus at 23:07. His wait is exactly <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" />, and the rule **at most <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" /> includes <InlineMath math="15" /> itself**, so the transfer is free.

# Why the other options fail

Each wrong option **ignores one rule or one of Budi's goals**.

- The 21:54 bus A arriving at 22:37 ignores the walk, so the 22:22 bus B seems catchable.
- The 21:54 bus A arriving at 23:07 is as fast as it gets, but it costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}9{,}000" />, while an equally fast option costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" />.
- The 22:12 bus A arriving at 22:52 forgets that bus B still needs <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" /> after leaving at 22:52.
- The 22:48 bus A does give a free transfer, but Budi would only arrive at 23:37. That option puts the fare ahead of speed.

So Budi takes the 22:12 bus A and reaches Halte Kampus at 23:07.
