# Three borderline cases

Three rows are easy to misread.

- The 22:12 bus A waits exactly <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" />. The rule says **at most <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" />**, so exactly <InlineMath math="15\text{ minutes}" /> is still free.
- The 21:54 bus A arrives at 22:19. The 22:22 bus B leaves only <InlineMath math="3\text{ minutes}" /> later, less than the walk, so its riders wait for the 22:52 bus.
- The 23:24 bus A arrives at 23:49, only <InlineMath math="3\text{ minutes}" /> before the last bus B. The 23:42 bus A arrives at 00:07, after the last bus B has left. Riders on these two buses get no bus B at all.
