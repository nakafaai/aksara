For each date, count **the number of days after 1 January 2027**, then shift Friday by the remainder on division by 7.

# The end of the year and the next new year

31 December is 364 days after 1 January, and <InlineMath math="364=7\cdot52" /> is divisible by 7. So 31 December is a Friday, and the second statement is true.

1 January 2028 is 365 days after 1 January 2027, and <InlineMath math="365=7\cdot52+1" />. The weekday **moves one on from Friday**, to Saturday. The first statement is true.

# 1 March and the number of Saturdays

1 March is <InlineMath math="31+28=59" /> days after 1 January, and <InlineMath math="59=7\cdot8+3" />. Three days after Friday is Monday, **not Sunday**. The third statement is false.

Since <InlineMath math="365=7\cdot52+1" />, only Friday appears 53 times. Saturday appears 52 times, so the fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **false**.
