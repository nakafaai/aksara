# Car S and the meaning of average speed

Car S takes 6 min 24 s, which is 384 seconds.

Its speed is 112.5 km/h, not 115 km/h, so the third statement is false.

An average of 100 km/h can come from a car that briefly drove at 120 km/h and then slowed down. So **a car that is not flagged was not necessarily always below 100 km/h**, and the first statement is false.
