#### Constructing the Mathematical Model

Let <InlineMath math="r" /> be the rate per minute in rupiah. The total call duration for the month is <InlineMath math="x" /> minutes, so the calls cost <InlineMath math="xr" /> rupiah. Adding the subscription fee <InlineMath math="z" /> gives:

<BlockMath math="y=xr+z" />

The pricing rule gives <InlineMath math="r=x+150" /> and <InlineMath math="z=60{,}000" />.

#### Substituting into the Formula

Substitute these two values:

<BlockMath math="y=x(x+150)+60{,}000" />

Expand the multiplication:

<BlockMath math="y=x^2+150x+60{,}000" />

Therefore, <InlineMath math="y=x^2+150x+60{,}000" /> models the monthly bill.
