#### Constructing the Mathematical Model

The basic formula for the phone tariff (<InlineMath math="y" />) is given as:

<BlockMath math="y = (\text{duration} \times \text{rate per minute}) + \text{subscription fee}" />

The pricing rule gives:
1. Call duration is <InlineMath math="x\text{ minutes}" />.
2. Subscription fee (<InlineMath math="z" />) is <InlineMath math="60{,}000" />.
3. The rate per minute is <InlineMath math="x + 150" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Rate per minute} = x + 150" />
</MathContainer>

#### Substitution into the Formula

Substitute these quantities into the billing formula:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="y = x(x + 150) + 60{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

Expand the multiplication:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="y = x^2 + 150x + 60{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, <InlineMath math="y = x^2 + 150x + 60{,}000" /> models the monthly bill.
