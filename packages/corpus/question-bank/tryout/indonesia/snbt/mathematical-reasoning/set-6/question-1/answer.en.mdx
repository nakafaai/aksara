From <InlineMath math="07:00" /> to <InlineMath math="09:24" />, the runners are on the track for:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&09{:}24-07{:}00 \\ &\quad=2\text{ h }24\text{ min}\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&(2\times60)+24=120+24 \\ &\quad=144\text{ min}\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

The fourth common crossing occurs after <InlineMath math="144\text{ minutes}" />, so consecutive common crossings are separated by:

<BlockMath math="\frac{144}{4}\text{ min}=36\text{ min}" />

This meeting interval corresponds to the **Least Common Multiple** (LCM) of the runners' lap times.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{Runner A} &= 12 \text{ minutes} \\ \text{Runner B} &= x \text{ minutes} \\ \text{Runner C} &= 9 \text{ minutes} \end{aligned}" />

We need to find the value of <InlineMath math="x" /> such that <InlineMath math="\text{LCM}(12, x, 9) = 36" />. First, find the LCM of <InlineMath math="12" /> and <InlineMath math="9" />:

<BlockMath math="\text{LCM}(12, 9) = 36" />

Since <InlineMath math="\text{LCM}(12, 9)" /> is already <InlineMath math="36" />, the lap time <InlineMath math="x" /> must keep <InlineMath math="\text{LCM}(12, 9, x)" /> equal to <InlineMath math="36" />. Check the choices:

| Lap time | LCM with 36 |
| --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="5" /> | <InlineMath math="180" /> |
| <InlineMath math="6" /> | <InlineMath math="36" /> |
| <InlineMath math="7" /> | <InlineMath math="252" /> |
| <InlineMath math="10" /> | <InlineMath math="180" /> |
| <InlineMath math="13" /> | <InlineMath math="468" /> |


Among the listed options, only <InlineMath math="x=6" /> gives the required interval between common crossings.
