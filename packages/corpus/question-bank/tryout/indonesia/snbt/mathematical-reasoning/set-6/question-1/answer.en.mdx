A team's points are **3 times its wins plus its draws**. Compute them for all five teams, then add.

# Points of each team

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&:\quad 3\cdot3+1=10\\
B&:\quad 3\cdot2+1=7\\
C&:\quad 3\cdot1+2=5\\
D&:\quad 3\cdot1+1=4\\
E&:\quad 3\cdot0+1=1
\end{aligned}" />

**The total of the five teams** is <InlineMath math="10+7+5+4+1=27" />.

# Check by matches

There are <InlineMath math="\binom52=10" /> matches. The draws add up to 6, and **each drawn match is recorded for two teams**, so there are 3 drawn matches and 7 matches with a winner. A match with a winner gives 3 points, and a draw gives <InlineMath math="1+1=2" /> points.

<BlockMath math="7\cdot3+3\cdot2=27" />

So the total of all teams' points is **27 points**.
