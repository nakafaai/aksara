A large but light box is often charged by **its volumetric weight**. Compute it and compare with the actual weight.

# Volumetric weight

<BlockMath math="\frac{50\cdot40\cdot30}{6{,}000}=\frac{60{,}000}{6{,}000}=10\text{ kg}" />

# Cost

The volumetric weight of 10 kg is greater than the actual 7.5 kg, so **10 kg is charged**.

<BlockMath math="10\cdot9{,}000=90{,}000" />

A cost of Rp67,500 uses the actual weight without rounding, and Rp72,000 uses the actual weight rounded up to 8 kg. Both ignore the rule that the larger weight is charged. A cost of Rp99,000 adds one more kilogram to a volumetric weight that is already whole.

So shipping the parcel costs **Rp90,000**.
