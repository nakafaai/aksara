For this box the volumetric weight is fixed, whatever is inside. The cost rises only when **the actual weight makes the charged weight exceed the volumetric weight**.

# Volumetric weight of the box

<BlockMath math="\frac{50\cdot40\cdot30}{6{,}000}=\frac{60{,}000}{6{,}000}=10\text{ kg}" />

An empty box **is charged 10 kg**, Rp90,000.

# Limit on the actual weight

As long as the actual weight is at most 10 kg, the charged weight stays 10 kg. If the actual weight is slightly above 10 kg, say 10.2 kg, it **becomes the larger weight and rounds up to 11 kg**, so the cost rises to Rp99,000.

The choice 10.9 kg is wrong because it rounds up to 11 kg. The choice 9.9 kg assumes the actual weight must be below 10 kg, although exactly 10 kg still gives the same cost. The choices 7.5 kg and 9 kg do give the same cost, but they are not the largest.

So the largest actual weight is **10 kg**.
