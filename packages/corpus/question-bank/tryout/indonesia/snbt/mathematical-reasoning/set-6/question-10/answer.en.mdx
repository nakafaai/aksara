For this box the volumetric weight is fixed, whatever is inside. The cost rises only when **the actual weight makes the charged weight exceed the volumetric weight**.

# Volumetric weight of the box

<BlockMath math="\frac{40\cdot40\cdot30}{6{,}000}=\frac{48{,}000}{6{,}000}=8\text{ kg}" />

An empty box **is charged 8 kg**, Rp72,000.

# Limit on the actual weight

As long as the actual weight is at most 8 kg, the charged weight stays 8 kg. If the actual weight is slightly above 8 kg, say 8.2 kg, it **becomes the larger weight and rounds up to 9 kg**, so the cost rises to Rp81,000.

The choice 8.9 kg is wrong because it rounds up to 9 kg. The choice 7.9 kg assumes the actual weight must be below 8 kg, although exactly 8 kg still gives the same cost. The choices 5.5 kg and 7 kg do give the same cost, but they are not the largest.

So the largest actual weight is **8 kg**.
