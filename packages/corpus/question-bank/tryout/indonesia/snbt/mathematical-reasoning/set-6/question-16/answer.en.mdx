Meetings fall on day <InlineMath math="1+10k" /> of the year, for <InlineMath math="k=0,1,2,\dots" />. A meeting is on a Friday again when **its distance from 1 January is divisible by 7**, that is, when <InlineMath math="10k" /> is a multiple of 7.

# Finding the smallest k

Since 10 and 7 share no factor other than 1, <InlineMath math="10k" /> is a multiple of 7 only when <InlineMath math="k" /> is a multiple of 7. The smallest positive value is <InlineMath math="k=7" />, which is **70 days after 1 January**.

# Turning the day number into a date

That meeting falls on day 71. January and February have <InlineMath math="31+28=59" /> days, so day 71 is <InlineMath math="71-59=12" /> March.

The date 11 March appears if February is taken as 29 days, although 2027 is not a leap year. The date 10 March appears if the 70 days are counted with 1 January as the first day.

So the next Friday meeting is on **12 March 2027**.
