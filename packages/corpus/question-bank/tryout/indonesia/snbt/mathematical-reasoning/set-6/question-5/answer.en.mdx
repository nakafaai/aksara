Each fold doubles the thickness, so after <InlineMath math="n" /> folds the thickness is **<InlineMath math="0.1\cdot2^n" /> mm**. This is exponential growth, not a fixed addition.

# Layers after 10 folds

<BlockMath math="2^{10}=1{,}024\text{ layers}" />

# Thickness of the stack

<BlockMath math="0.1\cdot1{,}024=102.4\text{ mm}" />

Treating each fold as **adding 0.1 mm** gives only 1.1 mm. That is wrong because a fold doubles the whole stack.

So the stack is **102.4 mm** thick after 10 folds.
