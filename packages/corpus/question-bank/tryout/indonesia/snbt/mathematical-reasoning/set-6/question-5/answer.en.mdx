You need the total cost of all eight trips under both payment options, and then the comparison. The regular tariff is a plain sum. The subscription is trickier because **it has two limits: free minutes on every trip and a monthly cap on minute charges**.

# Cost with the regular tariff

Every trip pays the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> unlock fee plus <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}250" /> for every minute. So **every minute counts and the unlock fee is paid eight times**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{total time} &= 25+6+38+44+15+31+9+27=195 \\ \text{regular cost} &= 8\cdot 2{,}000+250\cdot 195 \\ &= 16{,}000+48{,}750=64{,}750 \end{aligned}" />

# The monthly cap

Without the cap, the minute charges would be <InlineMath math="65\cdot 500=\text{Rp}32{,}500" />. That's over the monthly cap, so **Dita only pays <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}30{,}000" /> in minute charges**.

<BlockMath math="\text{subscription cost}=30{,}000+30{,}000=60{,}000" />

# The difference between the two

<BlockMath math="64{,}750-60{,}000=4{,}750" />

The subscription is **cheaper by <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}750" />**. Check it with the upper bound: a month on the subscription never costs more than <InlineMath math="30{,}000+30{,}000=\text{Rp}60{,}000" />. Dita's regular cost of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}64{,}750" /> is already above that, so the subscription has to be cheaper.

# Why the other options fail

Each wrong option **skips one rule** from the price list.

- Cheaper by <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}250" /> comes from ignoring the cap, so the subscription is taken as <InlineMath math="30{,}000+32{,}500=62{,}500" />.
- Cheaper by <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}17{,}250" /> comes from taking the free minutes off the total time, <InlineMath math="195-8\cdot 20=35" />. That lets the short trips add negative minutes.
- Cheaper by <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}34{,}750" /> comes from forgetting the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}30{,}000" /> subscription fee.
- More expensive by <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}11{,}250" /> comes from forgetting the unlock fees on the regular tariff, so the regular cost is only <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}48{,}750" />.

So on the subscription, Dita's November trips cost <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}750" /> less than on the regular tariff.
