# Determining Side Lengths

The cube has edge length <InlineMath math="s=4" />.

<InlineMath math="HF" /> is the **face diagonal** of the top face <InlineMath math="EFGH" />.

<BlockMath math="HF = \sqrt{EH^2 + EF^2}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}HF&=\sqrt{4^2+4^2} \\ &=\sqrt{16+16} \\ &=\sqrt{32}=4\sqrt2\end{aligned}" />

<InlineMath math="HD" /> is the vertical edge of the cube.

<BlockMath math="HD = 4" />

<InlineMath math="DF" /> is the space diagonal of the cube. Triangle <InlineMath math="HDF" /> is a right-angled triangle at <InlineMath math="H" /> (since <InlineMath math="HD \perp EFGH" />, then <InlineMath math="HD \perp HF" />).

<BlockMath math="DF = \sqrt{HD^2 + HF^2}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}DF&=\sqrt{4^2+(4\sqrt2)^2} \\ &=\sqrt{16+32} \\ &=\sqrt{48}=4\sqrt3\end{aligned}" />

# Calculating Sine Alpha

Angle <InlineMath math="\alpha" /> is at point <InlineMath math="D" />. Sine is the length of the **opposite side divided by the hypotenuse**. In this triangle:
- The side opposite to angle <InlineMath math="\alpha" /> is <InlineMath math="HF" />.
- The hypotenuse is <InlineMath math="DF" />.

<BlockMath math="\sin\alpha=\frac{HF}{DF}" />

Substitute the calculated values:

<BlockMath math="\sin \alpha = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{4\sqrt{3}}" />

<BlockMath math="\sin \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}}" />

Rationalize the denominator by multiplying by <InlineMath math="\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}}" />:

<BlockMath math="\sin \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}}" />

<BlockMath math="\sin \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} = \frac{1}{3}\sqrt{6}" />

Thus, the value of <InlineMath math="\sin \alpha" /> is <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{6}" />.
