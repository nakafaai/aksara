A car breaks the rule if it covers 12 km **in less than the minimum time** that matches 100 km/h. Compute that minimum, then compare.

# Minimum time without speeding

<BlockMath math="\frac{12}{100}\text{ h}=0.12\cdot60\text{ min}=7.2\text{ min}=7\text{ min }12\text{ s}" />

# Travel time of each car

| Car | Travel time | Speeding? |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| P | 7 min 12 s | no, exactly 100 km/h |
| Q | 6 min | yes, 120 km/h |
| R | 8 min | no, 90 km/h |
| S | 6 min 24 s | yes, 112.5 km/h |

Car P is **exactly 100 km/h**, and the rule only forbids speeds above 100 km/h, so P does not break it.

The choices that include P treat exactly 100 km/h as speeding. The choices Q only or S only miss one of the cars.

So the cars that break the limit are **cars Q and S**.
