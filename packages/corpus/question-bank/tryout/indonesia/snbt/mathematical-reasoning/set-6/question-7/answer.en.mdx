# The cost of one trip

On the regular tariff, one <InlineMath math="40\text{-minute}" /> trip pays the unlock fee and every minute. On the subscription, the first <InlineMath math="20\text{ minutes}" /> are free, so **only <InlineMath math="20\text{ minutes}" /> are paid**.

# The subscription stops growing

The minute charges grow by <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" /> per trip until they reach the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}30{,}000" /> cap. That happens exactly on the third trip, because <InlineMath math="3\cdot 10{,}000=30{,}000" />. **From the third trip on, the subscription total stays at <InlineMath math="30{,}000+30{,}000=\text{Rp}60{,}000" />**, however many more trips she takes.
