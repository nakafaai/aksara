First match the units: 1 metre is 1,000 mm. Then find **the smallest whole number <InlineMath math="n" />** with <InlineMath math="0.1\cdot2^n>1{,}000" />.

# Simplifying the condition

<BlockMath math="0.1\cdot2^n>1{,}000\;\Longleftrightarrow\;2^n>10{,}000" />

# Finding a large enough power of two

<BlockMath math="2^{13}=8{,}192,\qquad 2^{14}=16{,}384" />

The power 13 **does not yet pass 10,000**, while the power 14 does. After 13 folds the stack is only 819.2 mm thick, and after 14 folds it is 1,638.4 mm.

The value 13 stops one step too early. The value 100 treats each fold as adding 10 mm, and 10 uses <InlineMath math="2^{10}" />, which gives only 102.4 mm.

So at least **14 folds** are needed.
