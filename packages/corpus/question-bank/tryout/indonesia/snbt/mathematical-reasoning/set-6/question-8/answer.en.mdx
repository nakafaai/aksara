# The longest time on the regular tariff

Eight trips always pay the unlock fee eight times. **The budget left after the unlock fees** pays for the minutes.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{budget left} &= 45{,}000-8\cdot 2{,}000=29{,}000 \\ \text{minutes} &= 29{,}000\div 250=116 \end{aligned}" />

# The longest time on the subscription

After the subscription fee, the budget left pays for the paid minutes.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{budget left} &= 45{,}000-30{,}000=15{,}000 \\ \text{paid minutes} &= 15{,}000\div 500=30 \end{aligned}" />

Minute charges of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}15{,}000" /> are below the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}30{,}000" /> cap, so the monthly cap gives no extra free minutes here.
