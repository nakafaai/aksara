After <InlineMath math="n" /> folds the thickness is <InlineMath math="0.1\cdot2^n" /> mm. The ratio of two thicknesses **depends only on the difference in folds**, because the factor 0.1 cancels.

# Comparing two thicknesses

<BlockMath math="\frac{0.1\cdot2^{12}}{0.1\cdot2^{8}}=2^{12-8}=2^4=16" />

# What the result means

Four extra folds double the thickness four times: <InlineMath math="2\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2=16" />. **Each fold multiplies, it does not add**, so a difference of 4 folds means 16 times as thick.

The value 1.5 is the ratio of the numbers of folds, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{12}{8}" />. The value 4 is the difference in folds, and 32 uses <InlineMath math="2^5" />.

So the stack after 12 folds is **16 times** as thick as after 8 folds.
