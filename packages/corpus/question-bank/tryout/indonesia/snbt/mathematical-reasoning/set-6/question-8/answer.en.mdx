The top area shrinks by **a factor of <InlineMath math="\tfrac12" /> with every fold**, so after <InlineMath math="n" /> folds it is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{600}{2^n}" />.

# Setting up the condition

<BlockMath math="\frac{600}{2^n}<10\;\Longleftrightarrow\;2^n>60" />

# Finding the smallest n

<BlockMath math="2^5=32,\qquad 2^6=64" />

Five folds give an area of <InlineMath math="\tfrac{600}{32}=18.75" /> square centimetres, **still more than 10**. Six folds give <InlineMath math="\tfrac{600}{64}=9.375" /> square centimetres, already less than 10.

The value 60 divides 600 by 10, as if each fold removed a fixed amount of area. The value 5 stops one fold too early.

So at least **6 folds** are needed.
