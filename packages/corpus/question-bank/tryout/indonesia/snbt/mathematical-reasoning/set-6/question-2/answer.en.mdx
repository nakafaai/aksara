Runner <InlineMath math="A" /> completes one lap in <InlineMath math="12\text{ minutes}" />. Convert that time to seconds first:

<BlockMath math="12\text{ min}=12\times60\text{ s}=720\text{ s}" />

Speed <InlineMath math="v" /> equals distance <InlineMath math="s" /> divided by time <InlineMath math="t" />:

<BlockMath math="v = \frac{s}{t}" />

The question asks for the length of the track (<InlineMath math="s" />):

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="s = v \times t" />
  <BlockMath math="s = 2 \times 720" />
  <BlockMath math="s = 1440 \text{ meters}" />
</MathContainer>

Converting to kilometers:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}1440\text{ m}&=\frac{1440}{1000}\text{ km} \\ &=1.44\text{ km}\end{aligned}" />

One lap is therefore <InlineMath math="1440\text{ meters}" />, or <InlineMath math="1.44\text{ km}" />, long.
