The total of all teams' points can be counted **per match** instead of per team. All that is needed is the number of matches with a winner and the number of draws.

# Matches by result

The table has 7 wins, so 7 matches had a winner. The draw records add up to 6, so there were <InlineMath math="\tfrac62=3" /> drawn matches. **That makes 10 matches**, matching <InlineMath math="\binom52=10" />.

# Total points under the new rule

A match with a winner now gives 2 points, and a draw still gives 2 points.

<BlockMath math="7\cdot2+3\cdot2=20" />

Check by team: <InlineMath math="7+5+4+3+1=20" />.
