Given:
- Initial capital (<InlineMath math="M_0" />) is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}15{,}000{,}000.00" />
- Final capital (<InlineMath math="M_t" />) is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}16{,}500{,}000.00" />
- Interest rate (<InlineMath math="i" />) is <InlineMath math="12\%" /> per year

Let <InlineMath math="B" /> be the interest earned, in rupiah. Subtract the initial capital from the final balance:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="B = M_t - M_0" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}B&=16{,}500{,}000 \\ &\quad-15{,}000{,}000\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="B = 1{,}500{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

For simple interest, the time <InlineMath math="t" /> is the earned interest divided by the interest earned in one full year:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="t = \frac{B}{M_0 \times i}" />
  <BlockMath math="t = \frac{1{,}500{,}000}{15{,}000{,}000 \times \frac{12}{100}}" />
  <BlockMath math="t = \frac{1{,}500{,}000}{1{,}800{,}000}" />
  <BlockMath math="t = \frac{15}{18} = \frac{5}{6} \text{ year}" />
</MathContainer>

Converting to months:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="t = \frac{5}{6} \times 12 \text{ months}" />
  <BlockMath math="t = 5 \times 2 = 10 \text{ months}" />
</MathContainer>

Bima kept the money in the account for <InlineMath math="10\text{ months}" />.
