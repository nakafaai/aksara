Average speed is **distance divided by travel time**. The time must be converted to hours to get km/h.

# Travel time of car R

From 09:05:00 to 09:13:00, **8 minutes** pass.

<BlockMath math="8\text{ min}=\frac{8}{60}\text{ h}=\frac{2}{15}\text{ h}" />

# Average speed

<BlockMath math="v=\frac{12}{2/15}=12\cdot\frac{15}{2}=90\text{ km/h}" />

This speed is **below the 100 km/h limit**, so car R does not break the rule.

So car R's average speed is **90 km/h**.
