A year of 365 days consists of some full weeks and a few leftover days. Each full week contains **exactly one of every weekday**.

# Full weeks and the leftover day

<BlockMath math="365=7\cdot52+1" />

There are 52 full weeks and 1 leftover day. That leftover day is the last day of the year, 31 December, which **falls on the same weekday as 1 January**, a Friday.

# Number of Sundays

Every weekday appears 52 times, except Friday, which appears 53 times. Sunday is not the leftover day, so it appears 52 times.

Only if 2027 began on a Sunday would there be 53 Sundays.

So there are **52 Sundays** in 2027.
