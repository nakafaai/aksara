# Pasar X's overall percentage

The QRIS users in each group are the group's respondents times its percentage. Add them up, then **divide by all of Pasar X's respondents**.

# Why the other options fail

Each wrong option **uses the wrong weights**.

- "Equal" comes from thinking the same mix automatically gives the same percentage. The group percentages differ, so the results differ too.
- "Pasar Y higher by <InlineMath math="2.5" /> points" comes from averaging Pasar Y's percentages without weights, <InlineMath math="\frac{65\%+20\%}{2}=42.5\%" />.
- "Pasar Y higher by <InlineMath math="16" /> points" is the gap in the original table, <InlineMath math="56\%-40\%" />. Pasar Y's mix hasn't been changed.
- "Pasar Y higher by <InlineMath math="30" /> points" comes from using Pasar X's percentages with Pasar Y's mix instead, <InlineMath math="\frac{160\cdot 80\%+40\cdot 30\%}{200}=70\%" />.

So with the same age mix, Pasar X's overall share of QRIS users is <InlineMath math="11" /> percentage points higher than Pasar Y's.
