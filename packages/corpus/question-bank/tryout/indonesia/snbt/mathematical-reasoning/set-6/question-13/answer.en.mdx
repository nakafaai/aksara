Count how many days after 1 January the date 17 August lies, then take **the remainder on division by 7**, because the days of the week repeat every 7 days.

# Position in the year

Add the days from January to July, then add 17.

<BlockMath math="31+28+31+30+31+30+31+17=229" />

So 17 August is day 229, which is **228 days after 1 January**.

# Shifting the weekday

<BlockMath math="228=7\cdot32+4" />

After 32 full weeks the day is Friday again, then it moves 4 more days: Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday.

A common mistake is using 229 as the number of days after 1 January, which shifts the weekday by one, to Wednesday.

So 17 August 2027 falls on a **Tuesday**.
