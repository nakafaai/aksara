#### Calculating Cost Components

The total cost Bedu has to pay is the sum of the base fare (for the first kilometer) and the total fare for the subsequent kilometers.

Given:
- Total distance is <InlineMath math="18\text{ km}" />.
- Base fare (first <InlineMath math="1 \text{ km }" />) is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}500" />.
- Additional fare per km is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" />.

Remaining distance subject to additional fare:

<BlockMath math="18\text{ km}-1\text{ km}=17\text{ km}" />

#### Calculating Total Fare

Let <InlineMath math="T" /> be the total fare in rupiah. Add the base fare and the cost of the remaining distance:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="T=2{,}500+(17\times1{,}000)" />
  <BlockMath math="T=2{,}500+17{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="T=19{,}500" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the total fare Bedu has to pay is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}19{,}500.00" />.
