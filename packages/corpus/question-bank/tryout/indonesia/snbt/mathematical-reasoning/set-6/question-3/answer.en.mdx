Given:
- Initial capital (<InlineMath math="M_0" />) is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}12{,}500{,}000.00" />
- Final capital (<InlineMath math="M_t" />) is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}15{,}000{,}000.00" />
- Saving period (<InlineMath math="t" />) is <InlineMath math="1\text{ year}" />

Let <InlineMath math="B" /> be the interest earned, in rupiah. Subtract the initial capital from the final balance:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="B = M_t - M_0" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}B&=15{,}000{,}000 \\ &\quad-12{,}500{,}000\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="B = 2{,}500{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

The saving period is exactly one year. To find the annual interest rate <InlineMath math="i" />, divide the interest by the initial capital:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="i = \frac{B}{M_0 \times t} \times 100\%" />
  <BlockMath math="= \frac{2{,}500{,}000}{12{,}500{,}000 \times 1} \times 100\%" />
  <BlockMath math="= \frac{25}{125} \times 100\%" />
  <BlockMath math="= \frac{1}{5} \times 100\%" />
  <BlockMath math="= 20\%" />
</MathContainer>

The annual interest rate is <InlineMath math="20\%" />.
