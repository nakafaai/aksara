Given:
- Initial capital (<InlineMath math="M_0" />) is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}12{,}500{,}000.00" />
- Final capital (<InlineMath math="M_t" />) is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}15{,}000{,}000.00" />
- Saving period (<InlineMath math="t" />) is <InlineMath math="1\text{ year}" />

First, we calculate the interest amount earned:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Interest} = M_t - M_0" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Interest} = 15{,}000{,}000 - 12{,}500{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Interest} = 2{,}500{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

Because the saving period is exactly one year, divide the interest by the initial capital:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Percentage} = \frac{\text{Interest}}{M_0 \times t} \times 100\%" />
  <BlockMath math="= \frac{2{,}500{,}000}{12{,}500{,}000 \times 1} \times 100\%" />
  <BlockMath math="= \frac{25}{125} \times 100\%" />
  <BlockMath math="= \frac{1}{5} \times 100\%" />
  <BlockMath math="= 20\%" />
</MathContainer>

The annual interest rate is <InlineMath math="20\%" />.
