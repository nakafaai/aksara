You're looking for the smallest number of batches for all of the dry mix. The plan: find the dry mix including the reserve, then find **the largest content of one batch**, keeping in mind that its cement has to be whole bags.

# The dry mix and the content of one batch

The garage floor takes <InlineMath math="5\cdot4\cdot0.1=2\text{ m}^3" /> of concrete, its dry mix is <InlineMath math="2\cdot1.5=3\text{ m}^3" />, and with the reserve that becomes <InlineMath math="3.3\text{ m}^3" />.

One bag of cement carries <InlineMath math="6" /> parts of dry mix, which is <InlineMath math="6\cdot0.025=0.15\text{ m}^3" />. Two bags carry <InlineMath math="0.3\text{ m}^3" />, more than the mixer's capacity of <InlineMath math="0.2\text{ m}^3" />. So **every batch uses just one bag** and holds <InlineMath math="0.15\text{ m}^3" />, even though the mixer isn't full.
