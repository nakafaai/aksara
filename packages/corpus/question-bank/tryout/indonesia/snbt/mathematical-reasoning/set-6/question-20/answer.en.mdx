To avoid a violation, the 12 km must take **at least 7.2 minutes**. The average speed cannot be found by averaging the two speeds, because the two parts take different times.

# Time already used

<BlockMath math="\frac{6}{150}\text{ h}=0.04\text{ h}=2.4\text{ min}" />

# Time and speed for the rest

The remaining time must be at least <InlineMath math="7.2-2.4=4.8" /> minutes, or 0.08 hours, for 6 km.

<BlockMath math="v\le\frac{6}{0.08}=75\text{ km/h}" />

Check: at 75 km/h the total time is <InlineMath math="2.4+4.8=7.2" /> minutes, exactly the limit without a violation.

The value 50 km/h comes from assuming <InlineMath math="\tfrac{150+v}{2}=100" />, averaging the speeds, although the car spends less time on the fast part.

So the largest constant speed for the rest is **75 km/h**.
