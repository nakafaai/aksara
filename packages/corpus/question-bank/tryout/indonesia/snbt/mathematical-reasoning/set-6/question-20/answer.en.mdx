#### Calculating Track Length

It is known that runner A takes <InlineMath math="12\text{ minutes}" /> to complete <InlineMath math="1" /> full lap. Converting this to seconds:

<BlockMath math="12\text{ min}=12\times60\text{ s}=720\text{ s}" />

Runner A's speed is <InlineMath math="v_A = 2\text{ m/s}" />. The track length <InlineMath math="L" /> follows from the distance formula <InlineMath math="s = v \times t" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="L = v_A \times t_A" />
  <BlockMath math="L = 2 \times 720" />
  <BlockMath math="L = 1440 \text{ meters}" />
  <BlockMath math="L = 1.44 \text{ km}" />
</MathContainer>

#### Calculating Distance Covered by Runner A and C

The running time is <InlineMath math="72\text{ minutes}" />. Let <InlineMath math="n_A" /> and <InlineMath math="n_C" /> be the numbers of laps completed by the two runners.

For runner A (period <InlineMath math="12\text{ minutes}" />):

<BlockMath math="n_A=\frac{72}{12}=6" />

For runner C (period <InlineMath math="9\text{ minutes}" />):

<BlockMath math="n_C=\frac{72}{9}=8" />

#### Calculating Distance Difference

The difference in distance covered (<InlineMath math="\Delta s" />) is the difference in the number of laps multiplied by the track length:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\Delta s = (n_C - n_A) \times L" />
  <BlockMath math="\Delta s = (8 - 6) \times 1.44" />
  <BlockMath math="\Delta s = 2 \times 1.44" />
  <BlockMath math="\Delta s = 2.88 \text{ km}" />
</MathContainer>

Alternative calculation of individual distances:
- Distance A: <InlineMath math="6 \times 1.44 = 8.64\text{ km}" />
- Distance C: <InlineMath math="8 \times 1.44 = 11.52\text{ km}" />
- Difference: <InlineMath math="11.52 - 8.64 = 2.88\text{ km}" />

So, the difference in the length of the track covered by runner A and C is <InlineMath math="2.88\text{ km}" />.
