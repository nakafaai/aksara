Two tickets are drawn together and the prizes are the same, so **the order of drawing does not matter**. This is a combination of 2 from 20.

# Counting combinations

<BlockMath math="\binom{20}{2}=\frac{20\cdot19}{2}=190" />

Dividing by 2 is needed because the pair of tickets 3 and 7 is the same as the pair 7 and 3. Without it the result is 380, which **counts every pair twice**.

So there are **190 possible pairs** of tickets.
