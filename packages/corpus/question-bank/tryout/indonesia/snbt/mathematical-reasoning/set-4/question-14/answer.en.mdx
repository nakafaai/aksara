The volume of a cylinder is **base area times height**. Compute the volume of design A, then find the height of design C that gives the same volume.

# Volume of design A

<BlockMath math="V_A=\pi\cdot4^2\cdot10=160\pi\text{ cm}^3" />

# Height of design C

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\pi\cdot2^2\cdot t&=160\pi\\
4t&=160\\
t&=40
\end{aligned}" />

The radius is halved, so the base area becomes a quarter. To keep the volume, **the height must become four times as large**, from 10 cm to 40 cm.

So the height of a design C can is **40 cm**.
