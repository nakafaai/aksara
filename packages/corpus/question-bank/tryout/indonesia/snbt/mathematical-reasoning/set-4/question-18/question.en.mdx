A fishing boat is initially at point <InlineMath math="C" />. An observer sees the top of a hill at an elevation angle of <InlineMath math="37^\circ" />, and the line-of-sight distance to the hilltop is <InlineMath math="20\text{ km}" />. The boat then travels <InlineMath math="7\text{ km}" /> in a straight line toward the foot of the hill and reaches a pier at point <InlineMath math="D" />. From the pier, the elevation angle to the hilltop is <InlineMath math="53^\circ" />. What is the height of the hill?

Given:

  <BlockMath math="\sin 53^\circ = 0.8 \rightarrow \cos 53^\circ = 0.6" />
  <BlockMath math="\sin 37^\circ = 0.6 \rightarrow \cos 37^\circ = 0.8" />
