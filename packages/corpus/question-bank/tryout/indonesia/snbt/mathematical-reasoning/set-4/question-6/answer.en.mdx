Compute the cost of each ingredient for one recipe, add them, then **divide by 12 pieces**. The price units must match the amounts, for example grams with kilograms.

# Cost of each ingredient

Grams and millilitres are **converted to kilograms and litres** to match the price units.

| Ingredient | Amount | Cost |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Flour | 0.25 kg | <InlineMath math="0.25\cdot12{,}000=3{,}000" /> |
| Eggs | 3 | <InlineMath math="3\cdot2{,}000=6{,}000" /> |
| Sugar | 0.15 kg | <InlineMath math="0.15\cdot20{,}000=3{,}000" /> |
| Milk | 0.2 L | <InlineMath math="0.2\cdot15{,}000=3{,}000" /> |

# Cost per piece

One recipe costs 15,000 in total, and **one recipe makes 12 pieces**.

<BlockMath math="\frac{15{,}000}{12}=1{,}250" />

The value Rp15,000 is the cost of the whole recipe, not per piece. The value Rp1,000 leaves out an ingredient, for example adding only flour, sugar and eggs.

So the ingredient cost per piece is **Rp1,250**.
