The whole journey includes **two flights and the wait in Makassar**. The most direct way is to convert the departure time of P1 to WIT, the zone where P2 arrives.

# Matching the time zones

WIT is 2 hours ahead of WIB, so 06:40 WIB is **08:40 WIT**.

# Length of the whole journey

From 08:40 to 16:15 WIT, **7 hours 35 minutes** pass.

Check: the flying time of P1 and P2 is 6 hours 10 minutes, and the wait in Makassar is 1 hour 25 minutes. Together they give <InlineMath math="6\text{ h }10\text{ min}+1\text{ h }25\text{ min}=7\text{ h }35\text{ min}" />.

The time 6 hours 10 minutes counts only the time in the air. The time 9 hours 35 minutes subtracts 06:40 from 16:15 without regard to the time zones.

So the whole journey takes **7 hours 35 minutes**.
