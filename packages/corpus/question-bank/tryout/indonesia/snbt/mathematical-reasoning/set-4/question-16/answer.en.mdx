Use the relationship between an average and the corresponding total score. After the groups are combined, the new total must equal the sum of both original totals.

Let <InlineMath math="n" /> be the initial number of students. The total scores give the following equation:

<BlockMath math="59n + (70 \times 4) = 60(n + 4)" />

Group the terms containing <InlineMath math="n" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="59n + 280 = 60n + 240" />
  <BlockMath math="280 - 240 = 60n - 59n" />
  <BlockMath math="40 = n" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the number of students before being combined is <InlineMath math="40" />.
