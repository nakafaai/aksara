Flight P3 departs in WIT and arrives in WIB. WIT **is 2 hours ahead of WIB**, so first convert the departure time to WIB.

# Matching the time zones

08:00 WIT equals <InlineMath math="08{:}00-2\text{ hours}" />, which is **06:00 WIB**.

# Computing the flight time

From 06:00 to 10:50 WIB, **4 hours 50 minutes** pass.

Check: the plane leaves at 08:00 WIT and flies 4 hours 50 minutes, arriving at 12:50 WIT. That is 10:50 WIB, as scheduled.

The time 2 hours 50 minutes ignores the time zones. The time 6 hours 50 minutes adds 2 hours, whereas the departure time should be reduced by 2 hours.

So flight P3 takes **4 hours 50 minutes**.
