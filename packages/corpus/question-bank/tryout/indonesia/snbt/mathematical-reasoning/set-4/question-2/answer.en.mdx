First, identify the problem constraints:

1.  Total budget: **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}400{,}000.00" />**.
2.  Total items: **<InlineMath math="4" /> bouquets**.
3.  Mandatory purchase: At least **<InlineMath math="1" /> Snack Bouquet** (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}70{,}000.00" />).

The first step is to subtract the price of the mandatory item from the total budget to find the remaining funds.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&400{,}000-70{,}000 \\ &=330{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Now we have a remaining budget of **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}330{,}000.00" />** to buy **<InlineMath math="3" /> more bouquets** (since the desired total is <InlineMath math="4" /> items, and <InlineMath math="1" /> is already accounted for).

Check possible combinations to spend <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}330{,}000.00" /> on <InlineMath math="3" /> items. Looking at the price list, a **Money Bouquet** costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}130{,}000.00" />.

If we buy **<InlineMath math="2" /> Money Bouquets**:

<BlockMath math="2 \times 130{,}000 = 260{,}000" />

The remaining money after buying <InlineMath math="2" /> Money Bouquets is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&330{,}000-260{,}000 \\ &=70{,}000\end{aligned}" />

This remaining **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}70{,}000.00" />** is exactly enough to buy **<InlineMath math="1" /> more Snack Bouquet**.

So, the breakdown of our total purchase is:

| Item | Quantity | Unit Price | Total Price |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Snack Bouquet (Mandatory)** | <InlineMath math="1" /> | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}70{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}70{,}000" /> |
| **Money Bouquet** | <InlineMath math="2" /> | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}130{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}260{,}000" /> |
| **Snack Bouquet (Additional)** | <InlineMath math="1" /> | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}70{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}70{,}000" /> |
| **Total** | **<InlineMath math="4" />** | | **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}400{,}000" />** |

Two snack bouquets, one required and one additional, plus two money bouquets meet all three conditions: <InlineMath math="4" /> bouquets in total, a cost of exactly <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}400{,}000.00" />, and at least one snack bouquet.
