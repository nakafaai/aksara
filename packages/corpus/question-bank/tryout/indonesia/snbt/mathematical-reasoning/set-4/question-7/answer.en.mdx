# Cost and eggs

The 12-piece recipe costs 15,000. For 24 pieces every amount exactly doubles, including 6 eggs, so the cost **doubles too**, to 30,000, not 25,000. The first statement is false.

For 30 pieces, the eggs needed are <InlineMath math="3\cdot\tfrac{30}{12}=7.5" />. Eggs are used whole, so this rounds up to 8 eggs. The second statement is true.
