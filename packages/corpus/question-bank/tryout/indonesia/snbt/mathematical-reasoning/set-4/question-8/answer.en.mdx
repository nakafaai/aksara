Each ingredient is multiplied by **the factor <InlineMath math="\tfrac{30}{12}=2.5" />**, except the eggs, which must be rounded up to whole eggs.

# Amount and cost of each ingredient

The cost comes from **the amounts actually bought**, including the rounded number of eggs.

| Ingredient | Amount for 30 pieces | Cost |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Flour | 625 g | <InlineMath math="0.625\cdot12{,}000=7{,}500" /> |
| Eggs | 7.5, rounded up to 8 | <InlineMath math="8\cdot2{,}000=16{,}000" /> |
| Sugar | 375 g | <InlineMath math="0.375\cdot20{,}000=7{,}500" /> |
| Milk | 500 ml | <InlineMath math="0.5\cdot15{,}000=7{,}500" /> |

# Total cost

<BlockMath math="7{,}500+16{,}000+7{,}500+7{,}500=38{,}500" />

This is **more than <InlineMath math="2.5\cdot15{,}000=37{,}500" />**, because half an egg cannot be bought.

The value Rp37,500 multiplies the cost of one recipe by 2.5 without rounding the eggs. The value Rp36,500 rounds the eggs down to 7. The value Rp45,000 makes three full recipes, although ingredients other than eggs can be measured as needed.

So the ingredient cost for 30 pieces is **Rp38,500**.
