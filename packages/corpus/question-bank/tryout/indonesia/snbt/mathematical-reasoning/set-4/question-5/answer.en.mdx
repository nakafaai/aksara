Flour is used **in proportion to the number of pieces**, so find the scale factor from 12 pieces to 30 pieces.

# The scale factor

<BlockMath math="\frac{30}{12}=2.5" />

# Flour needed

<BlockMath math="250\cdot2.5=625\text{ g}" />

Unlike the eggs, **the flour does not need rounding**, because it can be measured exactly.

So the flour needed is **625 grams**.
