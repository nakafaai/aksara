Compute the volume, curved-side area and material area of both designs. **Being equal in one measure does not mean being equal in another**, so check each statement with its own formula.

# Volume and changing the radius

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
V_A&=\pi\cdot4^2\cdot10=160\pi\\
V_B&=\pi\cdot5^2\cdot6.4=160\pi
\end{aligned}" />

The volumes are equal, so the first statement is true. Volume is proportional to <InlineMath math="r^2" />, so **doubling the radius multiplies the volume by 4**. The third statement is true.

# Curved side and material area

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{side A}&=2\pi\cdot4\cdot10=80\pi,\quad \text{material A}=32\pi+80\pi=112\pi\\
\text{side B}&=2\pi\cdot5\cdot6.4=64\pi,\quad \text{material B}=50\pi+64\pi=114\pi
\end{aligned}" />

Design B needs slightly more material, <InlineMath math="114\pi" /> against <InlineMath math="112\pi" />, so the second statement is false. B's curved side is smaller than A's, so the fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **false**.
