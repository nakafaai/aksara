The first step is to determine the total daily allowance budget for all children. We need to calculate the total monthly allowance based on the salary percentage, then divide it by the number of effective **school days**.

# Total daily allowance

Let <InlineMath math="M" /> be the **monthly budget** in rupiah, <InlineMath math="d" /> the number of school days, and <InlineMath math="H" /> the daily budget in rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}M &=10\%\times4{,}800{,}000 \\ &=480{,}000\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="d = 30 - 6 = 24 \text{ days}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}H &=\frac{480{,}000}{24} \\ &=20{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Next, we use the allowance proportion ratio. It is known that child <InlineMath math="A" /> receives <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000" /> with a proportion of <InlineMath math="4" />, while the total allowance is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}20{,}000" /> with a total proportion of <InlineMath math="4 + 3 + x" />.

# Finding the missing ratio term

Set the **share for child A** in the daily budget equal to its share in the ratio.

<BlockMath math="\frac{4{,}000}{20{,}000} = \frac{4}{4 + 3 + x}" />

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{5} = \frac{4}{7 + x}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}7+x&=5\times4=20 \\ x&=20-7=13\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, the value of <InlineMath math="x" /> is <InlineMath math="13" />.
