For flight times, **match the zones of departure and arrival**. For a wait in one city, both times are already in the same zone.

# Flight times of P1 and P2

P1: 06:40 WIB is 07:40 WITA, and up to 10:05 WITA, 2 hours 25 minutes pass.

P2: 11:30 WITA is 12:30 WIT, and up to 16:15 WIT, **3 hours 45 minutes** pass. The first statement is true.

<BlockMath math="2\text{ h }25\text{ min}+3\text{ h }45\text{ min}=6\text{ h }10\text{ min}" />

The fourth statement is true.

# The time in Jakarta and the waiting time

P1 lands at 10:05 WITA. Jakarta is **one hour behind**, so it is 09:05 WIB, not 11:05 WIB. The second statement is false.

Passengers arrive at 10:05 WITA and leave again at 11:30 WITA, so they wait 1 hour 25 minutes, less than 1.5 hours. The third statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
