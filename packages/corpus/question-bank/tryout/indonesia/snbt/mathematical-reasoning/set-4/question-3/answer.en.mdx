We focus on the product **"Minyakmu"** which has an initial price of **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}80{,}000.00" />** per <InlineMath math="5\text{ liters}" />. We need to find the price difference after discount at two supermarkets: **Indoapril** and **Betamidi**.

Here is the comparison of the final price calculations:

| Calculation | Indoapril | Betamidi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Discount | <InlineMath math="25\%" /> | <InlineMath math="30\%" /> |
| Reduction (rupiah) | <InlineMath math="25\% \times 80{,}000" /><br /><InlineMath math="= 20{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="30\% \times 80{,}000" /><br /><InlineMath math="= 24{,}000" /> |
| Final price (rupiah) | <InlineMath math="80{,}000 - 20{,}000" /><br /><InlineMath math="= 60{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="80{,}000 - 24{,}000" /><br /><InlineMath math="= 56{,}000" /> |

From the table above, the price of "Minyakmu" at Indoapril is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}60{,}000.00" /> and at Betamidi is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}56{,}000.00" />.

The price difference between the two is found by subtracting the Betamidi price from the Indoapril price:

<BlockMath math="60{,}000 - 56{,}000 = 4{,}000" />

So, the price difference of "Minyakmu" at these two supermarkets is **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000.00" />**.
