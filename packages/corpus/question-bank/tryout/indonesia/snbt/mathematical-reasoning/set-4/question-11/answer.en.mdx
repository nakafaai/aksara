The probability of selecting a student who wears a watch is determined by the ratio of **students wearing watches** to the total number of students. Calculate these components first.

| Group | Total students | Wearing watches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Male students | <InlineMath math="10" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{2}\times10=5" /> |
| Female students | <InlineMath math="20" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{2}\times20=10" /> |

Thus, the total number of students wearing watches (<InlineMath math="n(A)" />) and the total number of students (<InlineMath math="n(S)" />) are:

<BlockMath math="n(A) = 5 + 10 = 15" />

<BlockMath math="n(S) = 10 + 20 = 30" />

The probability of selecting a student who wears a watch is:

<BlockMath math="P(A) = \frac{n(A)}{n(S)} = \frac{15}{30} = \frac{1}{2}" />

Therefore, the probability is <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{2}" />.
