The departure and arrival times are in different zones, so **convert both to the same zone** before subtracting.

# Matching the time zones

**WITA is 1 hour ahead of WIB**, so 06:40 WIB is 07:40 WITA.

# Computing the flight time

From 07:40 to 10:05 WITA, 2 hours 25 minutes pass.

<BlockMath math="2\cdot60+25=145\text{ minutes}" />

Ignoring the time zones, the gap between 06:40 and 10:05 is 3 hours 25 minutes or 205 minutes, which is **one hour too long**.

So flight P1 takes **145 minutes**.
