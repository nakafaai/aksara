We will compare the total volume of oil <InlineMath math="A" /> received in April (<InlineMath math="30 \text{ days }" />) and May (<InlineMath math="31 \text{ days }" />). Note that the delivery schedule for oil <InlineMath math="A" /> is every <InlineMath math="3\text{ days}" />.

Here is the detailed comparison:

| Detail | April | May |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Total Days | <InlineMath math="30" /> | <InlineMath math="31" /> |
| Volume per Shipment | <InlineMath math="10\text{ liters}" /> | <InlineMath math="15\text{ liters}" /> |
| Delivery Dates | <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="7" />, <InlineMath math="10" />, <InlineMath math="13" />, <InlineMath math="16" />, <InlineMath math="19" />, <InlineMath math="22" />, <InlineMath math="25" />, <InlineMath math="28" /> | <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="7" />, <InlineMath math="10" />, <InlineMath math="13" />, <InlineMath math="16" />, <InlineMath math="19" />, <InlineMath math="22" />, <InlineMath math="25" />, <InlineMath math="28" />, <InlineMath math="31" /> |
| Total Shipments | <InlineMath math="10\text{ times}" /> | <InlineMath math="11\text{ times}" /> |
| Total Volume | <InlineMath math="10 \times 10 = 100\text{ liters}" /> | <InlineMath math="11 \times 15 = 165\text{ liters}" /> |

Notice that in May, there are <InlineMath math="11" /> shipments because May <InlineMath math="31" /> is also a scheduled delivery date (<InlineMath math="3 \text{ days }" /> after the <InlineMath math="28" />th).

Thus, the difference in volume for oil <InlineMath math="A" /> is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\Delta V = V_{\text{May}} - V_{\text{April}}" />
  <BlockMath math="\Delta V = 165 - 100 = 65 \text{ liters}" />
</MathContainer>

So, the difference in the volume of oil brand <InlineMath math="A" /> received is <InlineMath math="65\text{ liters}" />.
