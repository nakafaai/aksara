The payback time is **the installation cost divided by the saving per unit of time**. Compute both for 10 panels.

# Cost and saving

One panel makes 0.9 kWh a day, so **10 panels are needed**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{cost}&=10\cdot2{,}430{,}000=24{,}300{,}000\\
\text{saving per month}&=9\cdot1{,}500\cdot30=405{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

# Payback time

<BlockMath math="\frac{24{,}300{,}000}{405{,}000}=60\text{ months}=5\text{ years}" />

Each month the saving covers **one sixtieth of the installation cost**, so 60 months are needed.

The value 6 years appears if 12 panels are installed without higher use. The value 10 years appears if only half the saving is counted, and 4 years if the monthly saving is inflated to more than Rp500,000.

So the installation cost is covered after **5 years**.
