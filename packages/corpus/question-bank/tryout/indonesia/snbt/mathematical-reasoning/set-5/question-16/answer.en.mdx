We need to find the number of days in April <InlineMath math="2024" /> on which Pak Saiful does **not** receive shipments from either Supplier B or C.

First, identify the time parameters. April has a total of <InlineMath math="30\text{ days}" />.

Next, we determine the delivery dates for each supplier, starting from April <InlineMath math="1" />:

| Set | Delivery dates |
| :--- | :--- |
| <InlineMath math="B" /> | <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="5" />, <InlineMath math="9" />, <InlineMath math="13" />, <InlineMath math="17" />, <InlineMath math="21" />, <InlineMath math="25" />, <InlineMath math="29" /> |
| <InlineMath math="C" /> | <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="8" />, <InlineMath math="15" />, <InlineMath math="22" />, <InlineMath math="29" /> |

The days on which Pak Saiful receives a shipment from one or both suppliers are the union of sets <InlineMath math="B" /> and <InlineMath math="C" />:

| Set | Delivery dates |
| :--- | :--- |
| <InlineMath math="B\cup C" /> | <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="5" />, <InlineMath math="8" />, <InlineMath math="9" />, <InlineMath math="13" />, <InlineMath math="15" />, <InlineMath math="17" />, <InlineMath math="21" />, <InlineMath math="22" />, <InlineMath math="25" />, <InlineMath math="29" /> |

The number of days with shipments is <InlineMath math="11\text{ days}" />.

To find the days **without** shipments, we subtract the number of shipment days from the total days in April:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="30-|B\cup C|" />
  <BlockMath math="= 30 - 11" />
  <BlockMath math="= 19 \text{ days}" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, there are <InlineMath math="19\text{ days}" /> where Pak Saiful receives no shipments from B or C.
