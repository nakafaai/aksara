The toll part must be driven, so **first set aside the fuel for the toll road**, then use the rest at the city rate.

# Fuel for the toll road

<BlockMath math="\frac{150}{15}=10\text{ litres}" />

# Remaining fuel for city roads

That leaves <InlineMath math="25-10=15" /> litres. On city roads each litre covers only 10 km.

<BlockMath math="15\cdot10=150\text{ km}" />

The distance 225 km uses 15 km per litre on city roads, although **that rate only applies on the toll road**. The distance 100 km appears if the toll fuel is computed at the city rate, as 15 litres.

So the farthest city distance is **150 km**.
