To find the combined average of all classes, we need to calculate the total score of all students and divide it by the total number of students.

**Step** 1: Calculating Total Scores for Each Class

Class IPA (<InlineMath math="n_1 = 30" />, <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_1 = 6.5" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\Sigma x_1&=n_1\cdot\bar{x}_1 \\ &=30\times 6.5=195\end{aligned}" />

Class IPS (<InlineMath math="n_2 = 25" />, <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_2 = 7" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\Sigma x_2&=n_2\cdot\bar{x}_2 \\ &=25\times 7=175\end{aligned}" />

Class Bahasa (<InlineMath math="n_3 = 20" />, <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_3 = 8" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\Sigma x_3&=n_3\cdot\bar{x}_3 \\ &=20\times 8=160\end{aligned}" />

**Step** 2: Calculating Combined Average

The total score of all classes is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\sum x_{\text{total}}&=195+175+160 \\ &=530\end{aligned}" />

The total number of students in all classes is:

<BlockMath math="n_{\text{total}} = 30 + 25 + 20 = 75" />

Thus, the combined average (<InlineMath math="\bar{x}_{\text{total}}" />) is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\bar{x}_{\text{total}} = \frac{\sum x_{\text{total}}}{n_{\text{total}}}" />
  <BlockMath math="\bar{x}_{\text{total}} = \frac{530}{75}" />
  <BlockMath math="\bar{x}_{\text{total}} =7.066\ldots" />
  <BlockMath math="\bar{x}_{\text{total}} \approx 7.07" />
</MathContainer>

So, the combined average for the three grade <InlineMath math="12" /> classes is <InlineMath math="7.07" />.
