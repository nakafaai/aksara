The mean is **the sum of all times divided by the number of students**, 8.

# Sum of the times

**Pair the values** to make the addition easier.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(14.2+13.8)+(15.1+14.0)+(14.6+14.4)+(13.5+16.4)&=28+29.1+29+29.9\\
&=116
\end{aligned}" />

# The mean

<BlockMath math="\frac{116}{8}=14.5" />

This lies between the fastest time of 13.5 seconds and the slowest of 16.4 seconds, so it **makes sense**.

So the mean time is **14.5 seconds**.
