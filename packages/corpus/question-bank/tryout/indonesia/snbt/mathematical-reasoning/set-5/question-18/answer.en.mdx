We will determine the ratio of the number of male to female employees using the concept of combined average (weighted average).

Let:
- <InlineMath math="n_L" /> = number of male employees
- <InlineMath math="n_P" /> = number of female employees
- <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_L = 320{,}000" /> (average income of males)
- <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_P = 285{,}000" /> (average income of females)
- <InlineMath math="\bar{x} = 300{,}000" /> (combined average income)

The formula for the combined average is:

<BlockMath math="\bar{x} = \frac{n_L \cdot \bar{x}_L + n_P \cdot \bar{x}_P}{n_L + n_P}" />

Let <InlineMath math="S" /> be the total monthly income of all employees in rupiah. Substituting the given values gives:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S&=320{,}000n_L \\ &\quad+285{,}000n_P \\ 300{,}000&=\frac{S}{n_L+n_P}\end{aligned}" />

To simplify the calculation, divide all numbers by <InlineMath math="1{,}000" />:

<BlockMath math="300 = \frac{320 n_L + 285 n_P}{n_L + n_P}" />

Cross-multiply the denominator to the left side:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&300(n_L+n_P) \\ &\quad=320n_L+285n_P\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&300n_L+300n_P \\ &\quad=320n_L+285n_P\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Group like terms:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&300n_P-285n_P \\ &\quad=320n_L-300n_L\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="15 n_P = 20 n_L" />
</MathContainer>

Find the ratio <InlineMath math="n_L : n_P" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\frac{n_L}{n_P} = \frac{15}{20}" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{n_L}{n_P} = \frac{3}{4}" />
</MathContainer>

There are therefore <InlineMath math="3" /> male employees for every <InlineMath math="4" /> female employees, so the required ratio is <InlineMath math="3:4" />.
