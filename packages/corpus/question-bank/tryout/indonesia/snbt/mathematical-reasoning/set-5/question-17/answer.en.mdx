The **four-day delivery interval continues** across the month boundary. The delivery after April 29 is therefore on May 3, not May 1. Only the weight per shipment changes to 20 kg.

| Month | Delivery dates | Count | Total weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| April | <InlineMath math="1,5,9,13,17,21,25,29" /> | <InlineMath math="8" /> | <InlineMath math="8\times12=96\text{ kg}" /> |
| May | <InlineMath math="3,7,11,15,19,23,27,31" /> | <InlineMath math="8" /> | <InlineMath math="8\times20=160\text{ kg}" /> |

Subtract the April total from the May total.

<BlockMath math="160-96=64\text{ kg}" />

The May deliveries therefore weigh 64 kg more than the April deliveries.
