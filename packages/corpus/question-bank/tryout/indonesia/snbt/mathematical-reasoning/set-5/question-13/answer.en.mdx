Peak power is not daily energy. A panel's daily energy is **peak power times 4.5 hours**, and that is what is compared with the 9 kWh used.

# Daily energy of one panel

<BlockMath math="0.2\text{ kW}\cdot4.5\text{ h}=0.9\text{ kWh}" />

# Number of panels

<BlockMath math="\frac{9}{0.9}=10" />

Ten panels make exactly 9 kWh a day, while nine panels make only 8.1 kWh, **not enough**.

The value 45 panels divides 9 by the peak power 0.2 without the 4.5 hours. The value 9 panels assumes each panel makes 1 kWh.

So at least **10 panels** are needed.
