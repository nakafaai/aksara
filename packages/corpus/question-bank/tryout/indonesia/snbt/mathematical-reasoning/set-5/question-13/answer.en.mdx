The stated <InlineMath math="100\text{ kg}" /> is the gross weight, which includes the sack. Because soybeans are priced by weight, we first remove the tare and calculate the net weight of the soybeans.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Netto} = \text{Bruto} \times (100\% - \% \text{Tare})" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Netto} = 100 \times (1 - 0.015)" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Netto} = 100 \times 0.985 = 98.5 \text{ kg}" />
</MathContainer>

Multiply the net weight by the price per kilogram to obtain the price before the discount.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Gross Price} = \text{Netto} \times \text{Price per kg}" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Gross Price} = 98.5 \times 4{,}000 = 394{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

A discount of <InlineMath math="5\%" /> means the buyer pays <InlineMath math="95\%" /> of that price:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Payment} = \text{Gross Price} \times (1 - \% \text{Discount})" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Payment} = 394{,}000 \times (1 - 0.05)" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Payment} = 394{,}000 \times 0.95" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Payment} = 374{,}300" />
</MathContainer>

The amount to be paid is therefore <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}374{,}300.00" />.
