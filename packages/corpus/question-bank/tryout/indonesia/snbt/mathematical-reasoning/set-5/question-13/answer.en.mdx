The stated <InlineMath math="100\text{ kg}" /> is the gross weight, which includes the sack. Because soybeans are priced by weight, we first remove the tare and calculate the net weight of the soybeans, denoted by <InlineMath math="N" /> in kilograms.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="N = 100 \times (1 - 0.015)" />
  <BlockMath math="N = 100 \times 0.985 = 98.5 \text{ kg}" />
</MathContainer>

Multiply the net weight by the price per kilogram to obtain the price before the discount, <InlineMath math="H" />, in rupiah.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="H = N \times 4{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}H&=98.5\times4{,}000 \\ &=394{,}000\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

A discount of <InlineMath math="5\%" /> means the buyer pays <InlineMath math="95\%" /> of that price. The payment <InlineMath math="P" /> is also in rupiah:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="P = H \times (1 - 5\%)" />
  <BlockMath math="P = 394{,}000 \times (1 - 0.05)" />
  <BlockMath math="P = 394{,}000 \times 0.95" />
  <BlockMath math="P = 374{,}300" />
</MathContainer>

The amount to be paid is therefore <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}374{,}300.00" />.
