The mean is the total score divided by the number of students. Conversely, the **total score** equals the mean multiplied by the number of students.

| Group | Students | Mean | Total score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Without Yanto | <InlineMath math="39" /> | <InlineMath math="45" /> | <InlineMath math="\Sigma x_1=45\times39=1755" /> |
| With Yanto | <InlineMath math="39+1=40" /> | <InlineMath math="46" /> | <InlineMath math="\Sigma x_2=46\times40=1840" /> |

Yanto contributes the only additional score, so his score is the **difference between the totals**.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="Y = \Sigma x_2 - \Sigma x_1" />
  <BlockMath math="Y = 1840 - 1755" />
  <BlockMath math="Y = 85" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, Yanto's exam score is <InlineMath math="85" />.
