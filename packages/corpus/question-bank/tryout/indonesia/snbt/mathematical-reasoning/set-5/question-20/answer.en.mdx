Because the question specifies flat simple interest, the interest is calculated from the original principal. We first find the interest for <InlineMath math="15\text{ months}" />, then divide the total repayment equally across the loan term.

All monetary values in the calculations below are in rupiah.

The annual rate covers <InlineMath math="12\text{ months}" />. For <InlineMath math="15\text{ months}" />, the applicable simple-interest rate is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&\frac{15}{12}\times16\% \\ &\quad=1.25\times16\% \\ &\quad=20\%\end{aligned}" />

Apply that rate to the original principal of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}500{,}000.00" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&20\%\times4{,}500{,}000 \\ &\quad=900{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Add the interest to the principal:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&4{,}500{,}000+900{,}000 \\ &\quad=5{,}400{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Finally, divide the total repayment into <InlineMath math="15\text{ months}" /> of equal installments:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&\frac{5{,}400{,}000}{15}=360{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Zul must therefore pay <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}360{,}000.00" /> each month.
