Count **every delivery in April**, including the first on April 1. The month has 30 days.

| Supplier | Delivery dates in April | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B | <InlineMath math="1,5,9,13,17,21,25,29" /> | <InlineMath math="8" /> |
| C | <InlineMath math="1,8,15,22,29" /> | <InlineMath math="5" /> |

Multiply each count by the weight per delivery, then add the two weights.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="8 \times 12 = 96 \text{ kg}" />
  <BlockMath math="5 \times 30 = 150 \text{ kg}" />
  <BlockMath math="96 + 150 = 246 \text{ kg}" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the total weight from suppliers B and C during April <InlineMath math="2024" /> is <InlineMath math="246 \text{ kg}" />.
