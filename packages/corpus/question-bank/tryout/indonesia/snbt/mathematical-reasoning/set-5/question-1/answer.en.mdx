Fuel use differs on the two kinds of road, so **compute each part of the trip separately**, then add.

# Fuel for each part

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{city roads}&:\quad \frac{60}{10}=6\text{ litres}\\
\text{toll road}&:\quad \frac{150}{15}=10\text{ litres}
\end{aligned}" />

# Total fuel

<BlockMath math="6+10=16\text{ litres}" />

The amount of fuel is **distance divided by distance per litre**, not distance times distance per litre.

So the car uses **16 litres** of fuel.
