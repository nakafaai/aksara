Let <InlineMath math="x" />, <InlineMath math="y" />, and <InlineMath math="z" /> be the prices in rupiah of one pack of soy sauce, sauce, and pepper, respectively. The three purchases form a system of linear equations. In the order Angga, Bimo, and Cinta, the equations are:

<BlockMath math="\begin{cases} 3x + y + 2z = 20{,}000 \\ x + 2y + z = 12{,}500 \\ 2x + y + 2z = 16{,}000 \end{cases}" />

Our target is to find out how much Rizal has to pay if he buys one of each item, which means we are looking for the value of <InlineMath math="x + y + z" />.

Angga's and Cinta's equations contain the same sauce term <InlineMath math="y" /> and pepper term <InlineMath math="2z" />. Subtracting Cinta's equation from Angga's therefore isolates <InlineMath math="x" /> immediately:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}3x-2x&=20{,}000-16{,}000 \\ x&=4{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Now we know the price of soy sauce is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000.00" />. Substitute this value into Bimo's and Cinta's equations to simplify the system:

Bimo’s equation becomes:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}4{,}000+2y+z&=12{,}500 \\ 2y+z&=8{,}500\end{aligned}" />

Cinta’s equation becomes:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}2(4{,}000)+y+2z&=16{,}000 \\ y+2z&=8{,}000\end{aligned}" />

We get a new system with two variables. From the equation <InlineMath math="y + 2z = 8{,}000" />, we can rewrite it as <InlineMath math="y = 8{,}000 - 2z" />. Substitute this into the equation <InlineMath math="2y + z = 8{,}500" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 2(8{,}000 - 2z) + z &= 8{,}500 \\ 16{,}000 - 4z + z &= 8{,}500 \\ 16{,}000 - 3z &= 8{,}500 \\ 3z &= 7{,}500 \\ z &= 2{,}500 \end{aligned}" />

After getting <InlineMath math="z = 2{,}500" />, we can find <InlineMath math="y" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}y&=8{,}000-2(2{,}500) \\ &=8{,}000-5{,}000 \\ &=3{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Now all unit prices are known: <InlineMath math="x = 4{,}000" /> (soy sauce), <InlineMath math="y = 3{,}000" /> (sauce), and <InlineMath math="z = 2{,}500" /> (pepper).

The total amount Rizal must pay, <InlineMath math="T" />, is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="T = x + y + z" />
  <BlockMath math="T = 4{,}000 + 3{,}000 + 2{,}500" />
  <BlockMath math="T = 9{,}500" />
</MathContainer>

So, Rizal must pay <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}9{,}500.00" />.
