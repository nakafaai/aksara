# Answers that often come up

Some other answers come from **adding things up the wrong way**:

- Date <InlineMath math="17" /> comes from treating the day the quota runs out as already slow, even though the quota runs out right at the end of that day's download.
- Date <InlineMath math="12" /> comes from adding in the night downloads: <InlineMath math="7\text{ GB}" /> per weekday and <InlineMath math="15\text{ GB}" /> per weekend day.
