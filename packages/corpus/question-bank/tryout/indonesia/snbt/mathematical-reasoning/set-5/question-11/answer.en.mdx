The ratio of tea bags to spoons of sugar stays at <InlineMath math="2:3" />. With <InlineMath math="5" /> tea bags and <InlineMath math="b" /> spoons of sugar, the proportion is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{2}{3}=\frac{5}{b}" />

Since <InlineMath math="P=2b" />, cross-multiplication gives the value we need directly:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="2\cdot b=5\cdot3" />
  <BlockMath math="2b=15" />
</MathContainer>

We have <InlineMath math="P=15" /> and <InlineMath math="Q=10" />. Comparing these values gives:

<BlockMath math="15>10\implies P>Q" />

The correct relationship is <InlineMath math="P>Q" />.
