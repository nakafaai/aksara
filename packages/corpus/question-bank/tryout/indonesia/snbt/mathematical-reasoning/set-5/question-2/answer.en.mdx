The average distance per litre is **total distance divided by total fuel**. It is not the mean of 10 km and 15 km, because the two kinds of road use different amounts of fuel.

# Total distance and total fuel

The total distance is <InlineMath math="60+150=210" /> km. The city part uses <InlineMath math="\tfrac{60}{10}=6" /> litres and the toll part <InlineMath math="\tfrac{150}{15}=10" /> litres, so **the total fuel is 16 litres**.

# Average per litre

<BlockMath math="\frac{210}{16}=13.125\text{ km per litre}" />

The value 12.5 km is the plain mean of 10 km and 15 km. It is smaller because the more efficient toll part uses more fuel than the city part and **carries more weight**. The value 14 km divides 210 by 15.

So the average distance per litre is **13.125 km**.
