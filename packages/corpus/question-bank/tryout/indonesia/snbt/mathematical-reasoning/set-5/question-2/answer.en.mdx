We can calculate the LCM of the three numbers by dividing the total number of days by the number of intervals:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\mathrm{LCM}&=\frac{1260}{16-1} \\ &=\frac{1260}{15}=84\text{ days}\end{aligned}" />

So, the LCM of <InlineMath math="x" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="7" /> is <InlineMath math="84" />.
