# Other measures and the comparison

The mean is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{116}{8}=14.5" />, so **the mean is greater than the median**. This happens because 16.4 seconds is far above the other times and pulls the mean up, while the median is not affected.

The median is not 14.4, because 14.4 is only the 5th value. No time appears more than once, so the data have no mode. The range is <InlineMath math="16.4-13.5=2.9" /> seconds, not 3.1 seconds.

So the true statement is that **the mean time is greater than the median time**.
