The two digit positions have **different conditions**. List the allowed digits for each position.

| Position | Allowed digits | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First position with an odd digit | <InlineMath math="\{1,3,5,7,9\}" /> | <InlineMath math="5" /> |
| Second position with an even digit | <InlineMath math="\{0,2,4,6,8\}" /> | <InlineMath math="5" /> |

**Zero is allowed in the second position**, as in house number 10. Each of the five first digits can be paired with any of the five second digits.

<BlockMath math="5\times5=25" />

There are therefore 25 possible house numbers.
