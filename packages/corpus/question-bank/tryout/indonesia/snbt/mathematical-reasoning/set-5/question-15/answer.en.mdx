One panel makes <InlineMath math="0.2\cdot4.5=0.9" /> kWh a day, so the second statement is true. **Ten panels cover the whole 9 kWh**, and everything else follows from this.

# Saving per month

<BlockMath math="9\cdot1{,}500\cdot30=405{,}000" />

The saving is Rp405,000 per month, **not Rp450,000**. The first statement is false.

# Higher use and the payback time

If use rises by 20%, the need is <InlineMath math="1.2\cdot9=10.8" /> kWh, so <InlineMath math="\tfrac{10.8}{0.9}=12" /> panels are needed. The third statement is true.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\frac{24{,}300{,}000}{405{,}000}&=60\text{ months}=5\text{ years}
\end{aligned}" />

The installation cost is covered after **5 years, not 4**. The fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **false**.
