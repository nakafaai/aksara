Let <InlineMath math="B" /> be Bimo's current age and <InlineMath math="A" /> his father's current age. We translate each time statement into an equation.

Six years ago, both were six years younger. Bimo's age then was <InlineMath math="4\text{ years}" /> less than one-sixth of his father's age then:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="(B - 6) = \frac{1}{6}(A - 6) - 4" />
</MathContainer>

Today, Bimo's age is <InlineMath math="3\text{ years}" /> more than one-eighth of his father's current age:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="B = \frac{1}{8}A + 3" />
</MathContainer>

We now simplify the first equation by clearing the fraction and collecting the variables:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
B - 6 &= \frac{1}{6}(A - 6) - 4 \\
B - 2 &= \frac{1}{6}(A - 6) \\
6(B - 2) &= A - 6 \\
6B - 12 &= A - 6 \\
6B - A &= 6 \quad \dots(1)
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Rewrite the second equation in the same form as equation <InlineMath math="(1)" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
B &= \frac{1}{8}A + 3 \\
8B &= A + 24 \\
8B - A &= 24 \quad \dots(2)
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Subtract equation <InlineMath math="(1)" /> from equation <InlineMath math="(2)" />. The variable <InlineMath math="A" /> cancels:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(8B - A) - (6B - A) &= 24 - 6 \\
2B &= 18 \\
\therefore B &= 9
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Bimo is <InlineMath math="9\text{ years}" /> old. Substitute <InlineMath math="B" /> into equation <InlineMath math="(1)" /> to find his father's age:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
6(9) - A &= 6 \\
54 - A &= 6 \\
A &= 54 - 6 \\
\therefore A &= 48
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Finally, add their current ages:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="A + B = 48 + 9 = 57" />
</MathContainer>

Their current ages therefore sum to <InlineMath math="57 \text{ years }" />.
