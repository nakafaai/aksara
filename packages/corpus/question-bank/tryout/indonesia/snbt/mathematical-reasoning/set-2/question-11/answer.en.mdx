#### Counting the good eggs

Of the <InlineMath math="40" /> eggs, <InlineMath math="3" /> are rotten. The number of non-rotten eggs is therefore:

<BlockMath math="40-3=37" />

#### Calculating the probability

Each egg is equally likely to be chosen. Divide the number of suitable eggs by the total number of eggs to find the probability of choosing a non-rotten egg.

Let <InlineMath math="A" /> be the event of choosing a non-rotten egg and <InlineMath math="S" /> the set of all eggs that could be selected. The notation <InlineMath math="n" /> counts the members of each set. Then:

<BlockMath math="P(A)=\frac{n(A)}{n(S)}=\frac{37}{40}" />

The probability of selecting a non-rotten egg is <InlineMath math="\frac{37}{40}" />.
