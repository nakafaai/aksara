Two steps are easy to miss: **the distance is rounded up first**, and the base fare already covers the first 2 km.

# Rounding the distance

The distance 7.3 km rounds up to 8 km. Of those 8 km, the first 2 km are covered by the base fare, so **the remaining 6 km** are charged per kilometre.

# Computing the fare

<BlockMath math="10{,}000+6\cdot2{,}500=10{,}000+15{,}000=25{,}000" />

The trip is outside peak hours, so the fare is not multiplied by 1.2.

A fare of Rp22,500 rounds 7.3 km down to 7 km. A fare of Rp23,250 does not round the distance at all. A fare of Rp30,000 appears if the fare is multiplied by 1.2 as in peak hours, or if the first 2 km are also charged Rp2,500 per kilometre.

So the fare Dewi pays is **Rp25,000**.
