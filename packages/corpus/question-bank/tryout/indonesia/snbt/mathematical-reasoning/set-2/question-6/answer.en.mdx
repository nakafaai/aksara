The tip of the shadow lies at <InlineMath math="x+s" /> from the pole, **the child's distance plus the shadow length**. From the similar triangles, the shadow is always <InlineMath math="s=\tfrac x3" />.

# Position of the shadow's tip

The ratio <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1.5}{s}=\tfrac{6}{x+s}" /> gives <InlineMath math="s=\tfrac x3" />.

<BlockMath math="x+s=x+\tfrac x3=\tfrac43x" />

So **the tip always lies at four thirds of the child's distance** from the pole.

# The speed

After <InlineMath math="t" /> seconds the child is <InlineMath math="x=t" /> metres from the pole, so the tip of the shadow is at <InlineMath math="\tfrac43t" /> metres. That distance **grows by <InlineMath math="\tfrac43" /> metres every second**.

Check: at <InlineMath math="t=3" /> the tip is at 4 m, and at <InlineMath math="t=6" /> at 8 m, so it moves 4 m in 3 seconds.

The speed <InlineMath math="\tfrac13" /> m/s is how fast the shadow lengthens, not how fast its tip moves. The speed 1 m/s is the child's speed, and 4 m/s uses the ratio of the pole's height to the child's height.

So the tip of the shadow moves at **<InlineMath math="\tfrac43" /> m/s**.
