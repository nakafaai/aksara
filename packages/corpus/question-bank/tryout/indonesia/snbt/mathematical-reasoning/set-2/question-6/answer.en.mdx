The tip of the shadow lies at <InlineMath math="x+s" /> from the pole, **the child's distance plus the shadow length**. From the similar triangles, the shadow is always <InlineMath math="s=\tfrac x2" />.

# Position of the shadow's tip

The ratio <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1.6}{s}=\tfrac{4.8}{x+s}" /> gives <InlineMath math="s=\tfrac x2" />.

<BlockMath math="x+s=x+\tfrac x2=\tfrac32x" />

So **the tip always lies at one and a half times the child's distance** from the pole.

# The speed

After <InlineMath math="t" /> seconds the child is <InlineMath math="x=1.2t" /> metres from the pole, so the tip is at <InlineMath math="\tfrac32\cdot1.2t=1.8t" /> metres. That distance **grows by 1.8 metres every second**.

Check: after 10 seconds the child is at 12 m and the tip at 18 m, which is 1.8 m per second.

The speed 0.6 m/s is how fast the shadow lengthens, not how fast its tip moves. The speed 1.2 m/s is the child's speed, and 3.6 m/s uses the ratio of the pole's height to the child's height.

So the tip of the shadow moves at **1.8 m/s**.
