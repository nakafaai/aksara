The ray from the lamp past the top of the child's head forms **two similar right triangles**: a large one with the pole and a small one with the child. They share the angle at the tip of the shadow.

# Setting up the ratio

Let the child's distance from the pole be <InlineMath math="x" /> and the shadow length <InlineMath math="s" />. **The base of the large triangle** is <InlineMath math="x+s" />.

<BlockMath math="\frac{1.6}{s}=\frac{4.8}{x+s}" />

# Solving for x equal to 9

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
4.8s&=1.6(x+s)\\
3.2s&=1.6x\\
s&=\tfrac{x}{2}=\tfrac92=4.5
\end{aligned}" />

The relation <InlineMath math="s=\tfrac x2" /> means **the shadow is always half the child's distance from the pole**, because the child is one third as tall as the pole.

So the shadow is **4.5 m** long.
