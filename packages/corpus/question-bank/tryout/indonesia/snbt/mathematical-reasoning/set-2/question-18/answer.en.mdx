#### Choosing the lowest scores

The smallest mean occurs when the total score is as small as possible. The participants form two groups with different score limits:

- <InlineMath math="21" /> participants scored at least <InlineMath math="80" />. The lowest possible score for each of them is <InlineMath math="80" />.
- The remaining <InlineMath math="60-21=39" /> participants scored below <InlineMath math="80" />. The score range starts at <InlineMath math="0" />, so each of them can score <InlineMath math="0" />.

Both choices satisfy the conditions. Lowering any score further would violate its lower limit.

#### Calculating the mean

Let <InlineMath math="S" /> be the total score. With these lowest possible scores:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} S&=21\times80+39\times0 \\ &=1{,}680+0 \\ &=1{,}680 \end{aligned}" />

There are still <InlineMath math="60" /> participants, so the smallest mean is:

<BlockMath math="\bar{x}_{\min}=\frac{1{,}680}{60}=28" />
