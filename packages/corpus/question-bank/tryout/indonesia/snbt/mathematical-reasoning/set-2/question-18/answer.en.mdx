Each dose **decays on its own from the moment it is taken**, and the remainders are added. At 22:00 the first dose has been in the body for 16 hours and the second for only 8 hours.

# What remains of each dose

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{first dose}&:\quad 360\cdot\left(\tfrac12\right)^{16/8}=90\\
\text{second dose}&:\quad 360\cdot\left(\tfrac12\right)^{8/8}=180
\end{aligned}" />

# Total amount

<BlockMath math="90+180=270" />

Another way: right after the second dose the body holds <InlineMath math="180+360=540" /> mg, and **8 hours later that amount halves** to 270 mg.

The value 540 mg is the amount right after the second dose. The value 450 mg does not decay the second dose, and 180 mg forgets what remains of the first dose.

So the amount at 22:00 is **270 mg**.
