The tip of the shadow is <InlineMath math="x+s" /> from the foot of the pole. Since **the shadow is always half the child's distance**, the tip is at <InlineMath math="x+\tfrac x2=\tfrac32x" />.

# Finding the child's distance

<BlockMath math="\tfrac32x=27\;\Longrightarrow\;x=18" />

# Turning distance into time

The child walks 1.2 m every second, so **the time is the distance divided by the speed**.

<BlockMath math="t=\frac{18}{1.2}=15" />

At that moment the shadow is 9 m, and <InlineMath math="18+9=27" />.

The value 9 is the shadow length at that moment, not the time. The value 13.5 divides 27 by 2, and 12 and 6 do not satisfy <InlineMath math="1.8t=27" />.

So the tip is 27 m from the foot of the pole after **15 seconds**.
