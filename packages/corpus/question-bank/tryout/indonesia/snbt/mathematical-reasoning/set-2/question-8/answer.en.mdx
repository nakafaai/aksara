The tip of the shadow is <InlineMath math="x+s" /> from the foot of the pole. Since **the shadow is always one third of the child's distance**, the tip is at <InlineMath math="x+\tfrac x3=\tfrac43x" />.

# Finding the child's distance

<BlockMath math="\tfrac43x=20\;\Longrightarrow\;x=15" />

# Turning distance into time

The child walks 1 m every second, so **the time equals the distance in metres**, 15 seconds. At that moment the shadow is 5 m, and <InlineMath math="15+5=20" />.

The value 5 is the shadow length at that moment, not the time. The other values fail <InlineMath math="\tfrac43x=20" />: at 12 seconds, for example, the tip is only at 16 m.

So the tip is 20 m from the foot of the pole after **15 seconds**.
