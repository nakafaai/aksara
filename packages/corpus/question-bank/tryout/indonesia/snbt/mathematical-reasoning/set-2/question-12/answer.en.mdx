#### Find the impact time

At impact, the rocket and the aircraft carrier are at the same position. Set their position equations equal:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="s_R(t) = s_K(t)" />
  <BlockMath math="4000 - t^2 = 60t" />
  <BlockMath math="t^2 + 60t - 4000 = 0" />
</MathContainer>

Factor the quadratic equation:

<BlockMath math="(t + 100)(t - 40) = 0" />

The factors give <InlineMath math="t = -100" /> and <InlineMath math="t = 40" />. Elapsed time cannot be negative, so <InlineMath math="t = 40" /> seconds.

<BlockMath math="t = 40 \text{ seconds}" />

#### Find the impact position

Substitute <InlineMath math="t = 40" /> into the aircraft carrier's equation:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="s_K(40) = 60(40)" />
  <BlockMath math="s_K(40) = 2400 \text{ m}" />
</MathContainer>

Convert meters to kilometers:

<BlockMath math="2400 \text{ m} = 2.4 \text{ km}" />

The rocket hits the aircraft carrier at <InlineMath math="2.4\text{ km}" />.
