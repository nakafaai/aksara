From 06:00 to 18:00, 12 hours pass. In this model, **the amount of medicine halves every 6 hours**, so 12 hours means halving twice.

# Substituting into the formula

<BlockMath math="M(12)=400\cdot\left(\frac12\right)^{12/6}=400\cdot\left(\frac12\right)^2=400\cdot\frac14=100" />

Step by step: 400 mg becomes 200 mg at 12:00, then 100 mg at 18:00.

So the medicine left at 18:00 is **100 mg**.
