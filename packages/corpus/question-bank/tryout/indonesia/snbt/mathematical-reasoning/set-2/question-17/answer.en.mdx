From 06:00 to 22:00, 16 hours pass. In this model, **the amount of medicine halves every 8 hours**, so 16 hours means halving twice.

# Substituting into the formula

<BlockMath math="M(16)=360\cdot\left(\frac12\right)^{16/8}=360\cdot\left(\frac12\right)^2=360\cdot\frac14=90" />

Step by step: 360 mg becomes 180 mg at 14:00, then 90 mg at 22:00.

So the medicine left at 22:00 is **90 mg**.
