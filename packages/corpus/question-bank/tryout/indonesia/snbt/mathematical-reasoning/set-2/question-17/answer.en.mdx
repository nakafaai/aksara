**Initial Total Weight Analysis**

First, calculate the total body weight of each group before the swap. Group <InlineMath math="A" /> has <InlineMath math="4" /> members with an average of <InlineMath math="30\text{ kg}" />. Group <InlineMath math="B" /> has <InlineMath math="4" /> members with an average of <InlineMath math="33\text{ kg}" />. Let their total weights be <InlineMath math="S_A" /> and <InlineMath math="S_B" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="S_A = 4 \times 30 = 120\text{ kg}" />
  <BlockMath math="S_B = 4 \times 33 = 132\text{ kg}" />
</MathContainer>

**The Swap Process**

Let <InlineMath math="x_A" /> and <InlineMath math="x_B" /> be the weights, in kilograms, of the children leaving groups <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" />. Group <InlineMath math="A" /> loses <InlineMath math="x_A" /> and gains <InlineMath math="x_B" />. Group <InlineMath math="B" /> has the opposite change.

For the averages of both groups to be equal after the swap (given the number of members remains the same), the total body weight of both groups must also be equal.

We can write this new balance equation as follows:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&120-x_A+x_B \\ &=132-x_B+x_A\end{aligned}" />

**Calculating the Difference**

Our goal is to find the difference in body weight between the two swapped children, which is <InlineMath math="|x_B - x_A|" />. We group the terms:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&x_B+x_B-x_A-x_A \\ &=132-120\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="2x_B - 2x_A = 12" />
  <BlockMath math="2(x_B - x_A) = 12" />
  <BlockMath math="x_B - x_A = 6" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the difference in body weight between the two swapped children is <InlineMath math="6\text{ kg}" />.
