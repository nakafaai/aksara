**Modeling the Problem into Equations**

Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the daily wage and <InlineMath math="y" /> be the additional wage per overtime day, both in rupiah. Based on the given information, we can form a system of linear equations with two variables:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="6x + 4y = 74{,}000 \quad (1)" />
  <BlockMath math="5x + 2y = 55{,}000 \quad (2)" />
</MathContainer>

Equation (1) comes from Mr. Budi working <InlineMath math="6\text{ days}" /> with <InlineMath math="4" /> overtime days, while equation (2) comes from Mr. Agus working <InlineMath math="5\text{ days}" /> with <InlineMath math="2" /> overtime days.

**Solving the System of Equations**

First, simplify equation (1) by dividing both sides by <InlineMath math="2" />:

<BlockMath math="3x + 2y = 37{,}000 \quad (3)" />

Now we have two simpler equations:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="5x + 2y = 55{,}000 \quad (2)" />
  <BlockMath math="3x + 2y = 37{,}000 \quad (3)" />
</MathContainer>

We can eliminate variable <InlineMath math="y" /> by subtracting equation (3) from equation (2):

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&(5x+2y)-(3x+2y) \\ &=55{,}000-37{,}000\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="2x = 18{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="x = 9{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

After finding <InlineMath math="x = 9{,}000" />, substitute it into equation (3) to find <InlineMath math="y" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="3(9{,}000) + 2y = 37{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="27{,}000 + 2y = 37{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="2y = 10{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="y = 5{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

So, the daily wage is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}9{,}000.00" /> and the overtime wage is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000.00" />.

**Calculating Mr. Dani's Wage**

Mr. Dani works for <InlineMath math="5\text{ days}" /> and does overtime continuously, which means he receives the basic wage for <InlineMath math="5" /> days and the overtime supplement for those same <InlineMath math="5" /> days. The wage he earns is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&5x+5y \\ &=5(9{,}000)+5(5{,}000)\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="= 45{,}000 + 25{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="= 70{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

Alternatively, we can factor the equation:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&5(x+y) \\ &=5(9{,}000+5{,}000)\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="= 5(14{,}000)" />
  <BlockMath math="= 70{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the wage Mr. Dani will earn is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}70{,}000.00" />.
