Both fares change formula at certain points: Jalan at 2 km, and Cepat at 4 km, where <InlineMath math="3{,}000k" /> reaches the minimum fare. So **compare interval by interval** in the distance <InlineMath math="k" /> km.

# Distances from 1 to 4 km

Cepat **is always Rp12,000** on this interval.

| <InlineMath math="k" /> | Jalan | Cepat | Cheaper |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1 or 2 | 10,000 | 12,000 | Jalan |
| 3 | 12,500 | 12,000 | Cepat |
| 4 | 15,000 | 12,000 | Cepat |

# Distances of 4 km or more

For <InlineMath math="k\ge2" />, the Jalan fare is <InlineMath math="10{,}000+2{,}500(k-2)=5{,}000+2{,}500k" />, and for <InlineMath math="k\ge4" /> the Cepat fare is <InlineMath math="3{,}000k" />.

<BlockMath math="5{,}000+2{,}500k<3{,}000k\;\Longleftrightarrow\;5{,}000<500k\;\Longleftrightarrow\;k>10" />

At <InlineMath math="k=10" />, both fares **are Rp30,000**, so Jalan is not yet cheaper. From 11 km on, Jalan is cheaper.

The choices at least 11 km and at most 2 km each contain only one part of the answer. The choice at most 2 km or at least 10 km includes 10 km, where the fares are equal.

So Jalan is cheaper exactly when the distance is **at most 2 km or at least 11 km**.
