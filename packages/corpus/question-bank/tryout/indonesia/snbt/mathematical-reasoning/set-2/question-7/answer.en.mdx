#### Annual salary increases

Mr. Brikson's monthly salary forms an arithmetic sequence because it increases by the same amount each year. We use his salary at age <InlineMath math="40" /> as the starting term, <InlineMath math="G_{40}=15{,}750{,}000" /> rupiah. The common difference is the annual increase <InlineMath math="d=750{,}000" /> rupiah.

The time between ages <InlineMath math="40" /> and <InlineMath math="54" /> is:

<BlockMath math="54-40=14\text{ years}" />

There are therefore <InlineMath math="14" /> salary increases.

#### Salary after all increases

The monthly salary at the required age is the starting salary plus <InlineMath math="14" /> increases:

<BlockMath math="G_{54}=G_{40}+14d" />

In rupiah, the accumulated increase is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 14d&=14\times750{,}000 \\ &=10{,}500{,}000 \end{aligned}" />

Add it to the salary at age <InlineMath math="40" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} G_{54}&=15{,}750{,}000 \\ &\quad+10{,}500{,}000 \\ &=26{,}250{,}000 \end{aligned}" />

Mr. Brikson's monthly salary at age <InlineMath math="54" /> is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}26{,}250{,}000.00" />.
