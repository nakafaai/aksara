Rina **buys** foreign currency, so the selling rate applies, the price the customer pays. The service fee is added to a purchase.

# Price of 300 SGD

<BlockMath math="300\cdot11{,}800=3{,}540{,}000" />

# Adding the service fee

<BlockMath math="3{,}540{,}000+25{,}000=3{,}565{,}000" />

The service fee is paid **once per transaction**, however many dollars are bought.

The values Rp3,480,000 and Rp3,505,000 use the buying rate, which applies when a customer sells. The value Rp3,455,000 uses the buying rate and subtracts the fee. The value Rp3,590,000 adds the fee twice.

So Rina must pay **Rp3,565,000**.
