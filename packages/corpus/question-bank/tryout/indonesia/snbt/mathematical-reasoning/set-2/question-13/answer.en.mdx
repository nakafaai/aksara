#### Counting track segments

The path from <InlineMath math="A" /> to <InlineMath math="C" /> covers <InlineMath math="2" /> segments, <InlineMath math="A \to B \to C" />. Each later arrival at <InlineMath math="C" /> requires another full lap of <InlineMath math="4" /> segments.

- First arrival: <InlineMath math="2" /> segments.
- From the first arrival to the second: another <InlineMath math="4" /> segments.
- From the second arrival to the third: another <InlineMath math="4" /> segments.

The number of segments covered by the third arrival is:

<BlockMath math="2+4+4=10" />

#### Calculating travel time

The runner covers <InlineMath math="10" /> equal segments in <InlineMath math="12\text{ minutes}" />. At a constant speed, the time per segment is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{12}{10}=1.2\text{ minutes}" />

The fourth arrival requires one more lap. The additional time for these <InlineMath math="4" /> segments is:

<BlockMath math="4\times1.2=4.8\text{ minutes}" />

The time from the start to the fourth arrival at <InlineMath math="C" /> is:

<BlockMath math="12+4.8=16.8\text{ minutes}" />
