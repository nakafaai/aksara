The motorcycle's original price is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}75{,}000{,}000.00" />, the discount is <InlineMath math="10\%" />, and the tax on the discount amount is <InlineMath math="2.5\%" />. All monetary values in the calculations below are in rupiah.

#### Calculating the discount

Let <InlineMath math="D" /> be the discount amount. It is calculated from the original price:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} D&=10\%\times75{,}000{,}000 \\ &=7{,}500{,}000 \end{aligned}" />

Subtracting the discount from the original price gives the discounted price, <InlineMath math="H" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} H&=75{,}000{,}000 \\ &\quad-7{,}500{,}000 \\ &=67{,}500{,}000 \end{aligned}" />

#### Calculating tax and total payment

The tax <InlineMath math="P" /> is charged on the discount amount <InlineMath math="D" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} P&=2.5\%\times D \\ &=\frac{2.5}{100}\times7{,}500{,}000 \\ &=187{,}500 \end{aligned}" />

Add this tax to the discounted price. The total payment <InlineMath math="T" /> is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} T&=H+P \\ &=67{,}500{,}000+187{,}500 \\ &=67{,}687{,}500 \end{aligned}" />

The father therefore pays <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}67{,}687{,}500.00" />.
