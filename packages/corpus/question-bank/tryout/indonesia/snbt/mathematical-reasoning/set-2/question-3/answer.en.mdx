For each statement, round the distance up and compute the fare. In the Cepat app, **the minimum fare of Rp12,000 applies when the per-kilometre amount is smaller**.

# Cepat fares for short trips

The distance 3.2 km rounds up to 4 km, and <InlineMath math="4\cdot3{,}000=12{,}000" />. The first statement is true.

A distance of 1 km gives <InlineMath math="3{,}000" />, but that is **below the minimum fare**, so the fare is still Rp12,000. The second statement is false.

# Comparing the two apps

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
6\text{ km}&:\quad \text{Jalan}=10{,}000+4\cdot2{,}500=20{,}000,\quad \text{Cepat}=6\cdot3{,}000=18{,}000\\
12\text{ km}&:\quad \text{Jalan}=10{,}000+10\cdot2{,}500=35{,}000,\quad \text{Cepat}=12\cdot3{,}000=36{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

At 6 km, Cepat is cheaper, so the third statement is false. At 12 km, **Jalan is cheaper by Rp1,000**, so the fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
