Points are handed out match by match. Each match **gives exactly 3 points**, 3 to the winner and 0 to the loser, whatever the result.

# Number of matches

**Every two teams play once**, so there are <InlineMath math="\binom62=15" /> matches.

# Total points

Since each match contributes 3 points, the total **does not depend on who wins**.

<BlockMath math="15\cdot3=45" />

The value 15 is the number of matches, not points. The value 30 assumes each match gives only 2 points. The values 36 and 40 do not fit the rule of 3 points per match.

So the total of all teams' points is **45**.
