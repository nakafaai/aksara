We will sort all apple masses from smallest to largest, then divide them into two groups: the <InlineMath math="6" /> heaviest apples for Ferdian and the <InlineMath math="6" /> lightest apples for Belle.

#### Sorting Apple Mass Data

The combined apple mass data (with <InlineMath math="x = 180" />) is:


<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}157, 163, 182, \\ 171, 167, 180, \\ 180, 175, 162, \\ 154, 161, 170\end{gathered}" />

Sorted from smallest to largest:

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}154, 157, 161, \\ 162, 163, 167, \\ 170, 171, 175, \\ 180, 180, 182\end{gathered}" />

#### Apple Distribution

**Belle (<InlineMath math="6" /> lightest apples):**

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}154, 157, 161, \\ 162, 163, 167\end{gathered}" />

**Ferdian (<InlineMath math="6" /> heaviest apples):**

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}170, 171, 175, \\ 180, 180, 182\end{gathered}" />

#### Calculating Total Mass Each

**Total Mass for Belle:**

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&154 + 157 + 161 \\ &\quad+162 + 163 + 167\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="964 \text{ grams}" />
</MathContainer>

**Total Mass for Ferdian:**

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&170 + 171 + 175 \\ &\quad+180 + 180 + 182\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="1{,}058 \text{ grams}" />
</MathContainer>

#### Calculating Mass Difference

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="1{,}058 - 964" />
  <BlockMath math="94 \text{ grams}" />
</MathContainer>

So, the difference in apple mass carried by Ferdian and Belle is **<InlineMath math="94 \text{ grams}" />**.
