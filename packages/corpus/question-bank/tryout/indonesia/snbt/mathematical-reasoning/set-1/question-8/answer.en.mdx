The condition is that **the volume entering is at least the tank capacity**. Let the roof area be <InlineMath math="A" /> square metres.

# The capacity to reach

**The tank capacity** is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{22}{7}\cdot0.7^2\cdot2=3.08" /> cubic metres.

# Setting up the inequality

A rainfall of 20 mm equals 0.02 m, and only 80% of the water enters.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A\cdot0.02\cdot0.8&\ge3.08\\
0.016A&\ge3.08\\
A&\ge192.5
\end{aligned}" />

The minimum roof area is 192.5 square metres, **a little more than two and a half times the current roof**.

The area 154 square metres forgets the 80% efficiency. The area 123.2 square metres multiplies by 0.8 when it should divide. The area 19.25 square metres uses 20 mm as 0.2 m.

So the minimum roof area needed is **<InlineMath math="192.5\text{ m}^2" />**.
