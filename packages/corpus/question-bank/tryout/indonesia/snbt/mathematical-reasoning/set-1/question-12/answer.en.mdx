The cement-to-sand mass ratio is <InlineMath math="1:5" />. Convert the sand volume to mass before using this ratio.

#### Sand mass

Density <InlineMath math="\rho" /> is mass per unit volume. With volume <InlineMath math="V=0.24\text{ m}^3" /> and density <InlineMath math="\rho=1{,}400\text{ kg/m}^3" />, the sand mass <InlineMath math="m_p" /> is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}m_p&=\rho V\\&=1{,}400\times0.24\\&=336\text{ kg}\end{aligned}" />

#### Cement mass

Every five parts of sand mass require one part of cement mass. The cement mass <InlineMath math="m_s" /> is therefore one fifth of the sand mass.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}m_s&=\frac15m_p\\&=\frac15\times336\\&=67.2\text{ kg}\end{aligned}" />

Kardi needs **<InlineMath math="67.2\text{ kg}" /> of cement**.
