# When the amount peaks

The amount only rises right after a dose, so you only need the amounts after each dose. Between two daytime doses the amount halves, and **the** <InlineMath math="12" /> **night hours divide it by** <InlineMath math="4" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{first day} &: D,\quad \tfrac{1}{2}D+D=\tfrac{3}{2}D,\quad \tfrac{3}{4}D+D=\tfrac{7}{4}D\\
\text{second morning} &: \tfrac{1}{4}\cdot\tfrac{7}{4}D+D=\tfrac{23}{16}D\\
\text{second midday} &: \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{23}{16}D+D=\tfrac{55}{32}D\\
\text{second evening} &: \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{55}{32}D+D=\tfrac{119}{64}D
\end{aligned}" />

The largest value is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{119}{64}D" />, after the evening dose on the second day, because what's left from the first day builds up. So **the peak comes on the second day, not the first**.

\tfrac{119}{64}D &\le 700\\
D &\le \frac{44{,}800}{119}=\frac{6{,}400}{17}\approx376.5\text{ mg}
\end{aligned}" />
