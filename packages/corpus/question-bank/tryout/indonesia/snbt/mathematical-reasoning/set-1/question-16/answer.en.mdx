We will first calculate the average mass of Belle's apples, then use the **relationship between Ferdian's and Belle's averages** to find the value of <InlineMath math="x" />.

All masses in the calculations below are in grams. Let <InlineMath math="S_B" /> and <InlineMath math="S_F" /> be the total apple masses for Belle and Ferdian. Their means are <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_B" /> and <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_F" />.

#### Average Apple Mass for Belle

The mean is **total mass divided by apple count**. Belle’s six apple masses are <InlineMath math="180" />, <InlineMath math="175" />, <InlineMath math="162" />, <InlineMath math="154" />, <InlineMath math="161" />, and <InlineMath math="170" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_B&=180+175+162 \\ &\quad+154+161+170 \\ &=1{,}002\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\bar{x}_B = \frac{1{,}002}{6} = 167" />
</MathContainer>

The average mass of Belle's apples is <InlineMath math="167 \text{ grams }" />.

#### Average Apple Mass for Ferdian

Ferdian’s mean apple mass is **three grams greater** than Belle’s mean.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\bar{x}_F = \bar{x}_B + 3" />
  <BlockMath math="\bar{x}_F = 167 + 3 = 170" />
</MathContainer>

#### Finding the Unknown Value

The masses of apples collected by Ferdian are <InlineMath math="157" />, <InlineMath math="163" />, <InlineMath math="182" />, <InlineMath math="171" />, <InlineMath math="167" />, and <InlineMath math="x" />. The **mean of Ferdian’s six apples** is <InlineMath math="170" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_F&=157+163+182 \\ &\quad+171+167+x \\ &=840+x\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="170 = \frac{840 + x}{6}" />
</MathContainer>

Multiply both sides by <InlineMath math="6" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="170 \times 6 = 840 + x" />
  <BlockMath math="1{,}020 = 840 + x" />
  <BlockMath math="x = 1{,}020 - 840" />
  <BlockMath math="x = 180" />
</MathContainer>

So, the value of <InlineMath math="x" /> is <InlineMath math="180" />.

Check using total mass: <InlineMath math="840+180=1{,}020" /> grams. The difference from Belle’s total is <InlineMath math="1{,}020-1{,}002=18" /> grams for six apples, giving a mean difference of <InlineMath math="18/6=3" /> grams, as required.
