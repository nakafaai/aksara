For usage up to 10 GB, Hemat costs only Rp50,000, far below Rp95,000. So the break-even point must be **above 10 GB**, where Hemat's cost starts to rise.

# Usage between 10 and 20 GB

For <InlineMath math="10<x\le20" />, the Hemat cost is <InlineMath math="50{,}000+9{,}000(x-10)" /> and **the Bebas cost stays 95,000**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
50{,}000+9{,}000(x-10)&=95{,}000\\
9{,}000(x-10)&=45{,}000\\
x&=15
\end{aligned}" />

# Making sure there is no other point

Above 20 GB, Hemat adds Rp9,000 per GB while Bebas adds only Rp5,000. The difference **keeps growing**, so the two costs never meet again.

Check: at 15 GB, Hemat costs <InlineMath math="50{,}000+5\cdot9{,}000=95{,}000" />, the same as Bebas.

So the two plans cost the same at **15 GB**.
