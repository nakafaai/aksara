# Seats per category

The seats per row form an arithmetic sequence, so a category's total is **the number of rows times the average of its first and last rows**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{A, rows }1\text{ to }6 &: \tfrac{6(19+29)}{2}=144\\
\text{B, rows }7\text{ to }16 &: \tfrac{10(31+49)}{2}=400\\
\text{C, rows }17\text{ to }30 &: \tfrac{14(51+77)}{2}=896
\end{aligned}" />
