We need **the number of cups sold**, rather than revenue in rupiah. Use the price-to-sales relationship specified in the question’s model.

#### Counting Price Reductions

The price falls from Rp12,000 to Rp9,000 per cup. Each reduction of Rp1,000 counts as **one price-reduction step**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\Delta p&=12{,}000-9{,}000=3{,}000\\k&=\frac{3{,}000}{1{,}000}=3\end{aligned}" />

#### Finding Sales at the Lower Price

Three steps add <InlineMath math="3\times20=60" /> cups per day. Add that increase to **the original 200 daily cups**.

<BlockMath math="J=200+20k=200+20(3)=200+60=260" />

| Step | Price per cup (Rp) | Sales (cups per day) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 12,000 | 200 |
| 1 | 11,000 | 220 |
| 2 | 10,000 | 240 |
| 3 | 9,000 | 260 |

The model therefore gives **260 cups per day**. The operating-cost threshold is not needed to find the sales quantity at this specified price.
