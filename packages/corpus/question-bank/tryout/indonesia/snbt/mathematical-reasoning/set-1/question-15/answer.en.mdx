The difference is 65,000, so the first statement is true. For 12 GB, Hemat costs <InlineMath math="50{,}000+2\cdot9{,}000=68{,}000" />, **cheaper than 95,000**. The second statement is false.

# Rate of the difference and the budget limit

Above 20 GB, each GB adds Rp9,000 to Hemat and Rp5,000 to Bebas, so the difference **grows by Rp4,000 per GB**. The third statement is true.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
50{,}000+9{,}000(x-10)&\le150{,}000\\

\end{aligned}" />

Usage is counted in whole GB, so at most 21 GB. A usage of 22 GB costs 158,000, above the budget. The fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **false**.
