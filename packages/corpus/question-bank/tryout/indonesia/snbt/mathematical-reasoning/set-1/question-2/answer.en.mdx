Work from the bill back to the usage. First subtract the fixed charge, then **use up the remaining money block by block** until nothing is left.

# Usage cost and the blocks passed

<BlockMath math="550{,}000-20{,}000=530{,}000" />

A full block 1 costs <InlineMath math="100\cdot1{,}000=100{,}000" />, and a full block 2, which is 200 kWh, costs <InlineMath math="200\cdot1{,}500=300{,}000" />. Together they cost 400,000 for 300 kWh. Since 530,000 is more than 400,000, **the usage reaches block 3**.

# Usage in block 3

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
530{,}000-400{,}000&=130{,}000\\
\frac{130{,}000}{2{,}000}&=65\text{ kWh}
\end{aligned}" />

The total usage is <InlineMath math="300+65=365" /> kWh. Check: <InlineMath math="20{,}000+100{,}000+300{,}000+65\cdot2{,}000=550{,}000" />.

So Sari's family used **365 kWh** of electricity.
