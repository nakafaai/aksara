We will calculate the total cost required by Rani, which consists of the purchase price of the seedling packages and the cost of fertilizer for the land used.

#### Determining the Number of Seedlings

Rani buys <InlineMath math="2" /> packages of fruit seedlings. Each package contains <InlineMath math="2" /> strawberry seedlings, <InlineMath math="1" /> orange seedling, and <InlineMath math="4" /> mango seedlings. Thus, the total seedlings obtained from <InlineMath math="2" /> packages are:

- Strawberry: <InlineMath math="2 \times 2 = 4" /> seedlings.
- Orange: <InlineMath math="2 \times 1 = 2" /> seedlings.
- Mango: <InlineMath math="2 \times 4 = 8" /> seedlings.

#### Calculating the Required Land Area

We calculate the land area for each type of seedling:

**Strawberry Seedlings**

The land size for one strawberry seedling is <InlineMath math="10 \text{ cm} \times 5 \text{ cm}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&10 \times 5 \\ &=50 \text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}" />

Total area for <InlineMath math="4" /> strawberry seedlings:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&4 \times 50 \\ &=200 \text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}" />

**Orange Seedlings**

The orange plot has twice the strawberry plot's length and twice its width, so its dimensions are <InlineMath math="20 \text{ cm} \times 10 \text{ cm}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&20 \times 10 \\ &=200 \text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}" />

Total area for <InlineMath math="2" /> orange seedlings:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&2 \times 200 \\ &=400 \text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}" />

**Mango Seedlings**

The land size for one mango seedling is <InlineMath math="5 \text{ cm} \times 2.5 \text{ cm}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&5 \times 2.5 \\ &=12.5 \text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}" />

Total area for <InlineMath math="8" /> mango seedlings:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&8 \times 12.5 \\ &=100 \text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}" />

**Total Land Area**

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="200 + 400 + 100" />
  <BlockMath math="700 \text{ cm}^2" />
</MathContainer>

The available plot has an area of <InlineMath math="0.35 \text{ m}^2" /> (<InlineMath math="3{,}500 \text{ cm}^2" />), so the seedlings fit. Fertilizer is needed only for the <InlineMath math="700 \text{ cm}^2" /> actually planted.

We convert the area unit to square meters (<InlineMath math="1 \text{ m}^2 = 10{,}000 \text{ cm}^2" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&\frac{700}{10{,}000} \\ &=0.07 \text{ m}^2\end{aligned}" />

#### Calculating Total Cost

**Seed Cost**

The price of <InlineMath math="1" /> package is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}45{,}000.00" />. For <InlineMath math="2" /> packages:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&2 \times 45{,}000 \\ &=90{,}000\end{aligned}" />

**Fertilizer Cost**

The price of fertilizer is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}100{,}000.00/\text{m}^2" />. For land with an area of <InlineMath math="0.07 \text{ m}^2" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&0.07 \times 100{,}000 \\ &=7{,}000\end{aligned}" />

**Total Expenditure**

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="90{,}000 + 7{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="97{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

So, the amount of money Rani has to spend is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}97{,}000.00" />.
