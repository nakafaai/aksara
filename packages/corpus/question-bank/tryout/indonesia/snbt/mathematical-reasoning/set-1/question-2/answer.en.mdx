Rani pays for **two seedling packages and fertilizer for the planted area**. The full plot area is not the basis for fertilizer cost because part of the plot remains unplanted.

#### Finding the Planted Area

Two packages contain 4 strawberry, 2 orange, and 8 mango seedlings. Each orange plot has twice a strawberry plot's length and twice its width. Its **area is therefore four times as large**, not twice as large.

| Seedling | Number | Area per seedling (cm²) | Total area (cm²) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strawberry | <InlineMath math="2\times2=4" /> | <InlineMath math="10\times5=50" /> | <InlineMath math="4\times50=200" /> |
| Orange | <InlineMath math="2\times1=2" /> | <InlineMath math="(2\times10)(2\times5)=200" /> | <InlineMath math="2\times200=400" /> |
| Mango | <InlineMath math="2\times4=8" /> | <InlineMath math="5\times2.5=12.5" /> | <InlineMath math="8\times12.5=100" /> |

Add the three group areas. Convert cm² to m² by dividing by <InlineMath math="100^2=10{,}000" />, because both length dimensions change units.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&=200+400+100=700\,\mathrm{cm}^2 \\
&=\frac{700}{10{,}000}\,\mathrm{m}^2 \\
&=0.07\,\mathrm{m}^2
\end{aligned}" />

The plots can also be arranged without overlap. Put the two orange plots in a 40 cm × 10 cm row, the four strawberry plots in a 40 cm × 5 cm row, and the eight mango plots in a 40 cm × 2.5 cm row. Together they form a 40 cm × 17.5 cm rectangle, which fits within the 100 cm × 35 cm plot.

#### Calculating Seedling and Fertilizer Costs

Seedlings are priced by package, while fertilizer is charged for **the 0.07 m² planted area**. All costs in the calculation below are in rupiah.

Here, <InlineMath math="C_b" /> is the seedling cost and <InlineMath math="C_p" /> is the fertilizer cost.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
C_b&=2\times45{,}000=90{,}000 \\
C_p&=0.07\times100{,}000=7{,}000 \\
C&=90{,}000+7{,}000=97{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

Rani therefore pays **Rp97,000.00**.
