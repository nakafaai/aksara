Jojo buys a series <InlineMath math="A" /> laptop, so the original price receives the <InlineMath math="20\%" /> discount. The anniversary offer **removes the down payment** and uses the lowest annual interest rate, <InlineMath math="4\%" />.

The lowest annual rate is 6% minus the maximum reduction of two percentage points, giving 4%.

#### Price and interest

The discounted price is <InlineMath math="100\%-20\%=80\%" /> of the original price. This entire amount becomes the loan principal <InlineMath math="P" />, measured in rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P&=80\%\times15{,}000{,}000\\&=12{,}000{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Simple interest is calculated from the **fixed principal**. Divide the annual interest by <InlineMath math="12" /> to find the monthly interest <InlineMath math="B" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}B&=\frac{4\%}{12}P\\&=\frac{1}{300}P\\&=40{,}000\end{aligned}" />

#### Number of installments

With <InlineMath math="n" /> installments, each monthly payment consists of <InlineMath math="P/n" /> in principal and <InlineMath math="B" /> in interest. Their sum must be at most <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}600{,}000.00" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\frac{P}{n}+B&\le600{,}000\\\frac{12{,}000{,}000}{n}&\le560{,}000\\n&\ge\frac{12{,}000{,}000}{560{,}000}\\n&\ge\frac{150}{7}\end{aligned}" />

This lower bound lies between <InlineMath math="21" /> and <InlineMath math="22" />. The number of installments must be an integer, so Jojo needs at least **<InlineMath math="22" /> installments**.

Compare the two installment counts around the boundary. **Rounding down** would exceed Jojo's payment limit.
