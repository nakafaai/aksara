Rain on the roof forms **a layer as high as the rainfall over the roof area**, so its volume is area times height. The units must first be made the same, in metres.

# Volume of water on the roof

A rainfall of 25 mm **equals 0.025 m**.

<BlockMath math="V_{\text{roof}}=11\cdot7\cdot0.025=77\cdot0.025=1.925\text{ m}^3" />

# The part that enters the tank

Only **80% reaches the tank**.

<BlockMath math="V_{\text{tank}}=0.8\cdot1.925=1.54\text{ m}^3" />

So the water that enters the tank is **1.54 cubic metres**.
