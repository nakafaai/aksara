Count the matches **stage by stage**: the group stage, then the two deciding matches.

# Group stage

Each group has 3 teams and every two teams play once, so **each group plays three matches**, <InlineMath math="\binom32=3" />. Two groups play <InlineMath math="2\cdot3=6" /> matches.

# Deciding matches

After the group stage there are **one final and one third-place match**.

<BlockMath math="6+1+1=8" />

This format needs 8 matches, fewer than the 15 matches when every team plays every other team.

The value 6 counts only the group stage, and 7 forgets the third-place match.

So the number of matches in this format is **8**.
