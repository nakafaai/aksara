Two quantities underlie every statement: **the tank capacity** and **the volume entering for a given rainfall**. Compute both first.

# Tank capacity and daily use

<BlockMath math="V_{\text{tank}}=\tfrac{22}{7}\cdot0.7^2\cdot2=1.54\cdot2=3.08\text{ m}^3" />

The first statement is true. With a use of 0.14 cubic metres per day, a full tank runs out after

<BlockMath math="\frac{3.08}{0.14}=22\text{ days}" />

The tank **lasts only 22 days**, fewer than 25, so the fourth statement is false.

# Volume of water for a given rainfall

The volume entering is <InlineMath math="77\cdot\text{rainfall}\cdot0.8" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
40\text{ mm}&:\quad 77\cdot0.04\cdot0.8=2.464\text{ m}^3\\
25\text{ mm with no loss}&:\quad 77\cdot0.025=1.925\text{ m}^3
\end{aligned}" />

The volume 2.464 cubic metres **does not reach the capacity of 3.08 cubic metres**, so the second statement is false. The third statement is true.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **false**.
