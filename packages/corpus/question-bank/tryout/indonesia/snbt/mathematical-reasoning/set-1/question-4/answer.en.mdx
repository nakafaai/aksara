# The rule that decides it

The machine adds a shortfall of <InlineMath math="3\text{ kWh}" /> to its hour. There are three possibilities. If the battery is at its reserve, the shortfall is bought straight away. If the battery can cover it, the battery level drops, and **that drop does no harm only if some energy is still wasted later** to refill the battery. If no more energy is wasted, the battery reaches the afternoon's end lower and the evening purchase grows.
