Compute the cost of each plan for 13 GB. What matters is **the part of the usage above each plan's quota**.

# Cost of the Hemat plan

Its quota is 10 GB, so **3 GB are charged as extra**, because <InlineMath math="13-10=3" />.

<BlockMath math="50{,}000+3\cdot9{,}000=77{,}000" />

# Cost of the Bebas plan and the difference

Its quota is 20 GB, so a usage of 13 GB **stays within the quota** and the cost is still 95,000.

<BlockMath math="95{,}000-77{,}000=18{,}000" />

A difference of Rp27,000 is only the extra charge of the Hemat plan. The choices saying Bebas is cheaper come from treating 13 GB as above the Bebas quota or from charging Hemat's extras from 0 GB.

So **the Hemat plan is cheaper by Rp18,000** for a usage of 13 GB.
