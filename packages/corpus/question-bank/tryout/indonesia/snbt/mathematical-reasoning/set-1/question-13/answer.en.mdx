Kardi and Anton work on separate walls, so we can add their **daily work rates**. All walls have the same area, and each worker maintains a constant rate.

#### Adding the Work Rates

A rate tells us **how many walls are completed in one day**, rather than how many days the work takes. Kardi completes ten walls per day and Anton completes six.

| Worker | Rate (walls per day) |
| --- | --- |
| Kardi | 10 |
| Anton | 6 |
| Both | <InlineMath math="10+6=16" /> |

#### Finding the Time to Finish All Walls

Divide **the number of walls by the combined rate**. Since the rate is measured in walls per day, the quotient is a duration in working days.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}t&=\frac{56\text{ walls}}{16\text{ walls/day}}\\&=\frac{56\div8}{16\div8}\text{ days}\\&=\frac72\text{ days}=3.5\text{ days}\end{aligned}" />

After three days, they finish <InlineMath math="3\times16=48" /> walls. The remaining eight walls take another <InlineMath math="8/16=1/2" /> day. They therefore need **three and a half working days**.
