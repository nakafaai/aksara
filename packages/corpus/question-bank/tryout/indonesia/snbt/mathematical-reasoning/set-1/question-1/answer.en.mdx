The panels cover **all four walls of the coop**. Each panel is as tall as a wall, so it contributes 0.8 m along the perimeter. We first need base dimensions that use whole panels, then we can calculate the cost.

#### Finding the Base Dimensions

Write the width as <InlineMath math="w=0.8m" /> and the length as <InlineMath math="l=0.8n" />. The positive integers <InlineMath math="m" /> and <InlineMath math="n" /> count the panels along one short wall and one long wall. The base area is 256 m², so:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
lw&=256 \\
(0.8n)(0.8m)&=256 \\
0.64mn&=256 \\
mn&=\frac{256}{0.64}=400
\end{aligned}" />

The width must be between 5 m and 7.5 m, which limits the short wall's panel count.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
5\le0.8m&\le7.5 \\
6.25\le m&\le9.375
\end{aligned}" />

Thus <InlineMath math="m" /> can only be 7, 8, or 9. The key condition is that **both panel counts must be integers**.

| Short-wall panels <InlineMath math="m" /> | Width (m) | Long-wall panels <InlineMath math="n=400/m" /> | Whole panels? |
| ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 7 | 5.6 | <InlineMath math="400/7=57+1/7" /> | No |
| 8 | 6.4 | <InlineMath math="400/8=50" /> | Yes |
| 9 | 7.2 | <InlineMath math="400/9=44+4/9" /> | No |

The width is therefore 6.4 m and the length is <InlineMath math="0.8(50)=40\,\mathrm{m}" />. Check the area: <InlineMath math="6.4\times40=256\,\mathrm{m}^2" />.

#### Counting Panels and Calculating Cost

There are **two pairs of equal walls**. Each short wall uses 8 panels, and each long wall uses 50.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
N&=2(8)+2(50) \\
&=16+100=116
\end{aligned}" />

Multiply 116 panels by Rp40,000.00 per panel.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
C&=116\times40{,}000 \\
&=(100+16)\times40{,}000 \\
&=4{,}000{,}000+640{,}000 \\
&=4{,}640{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

The panels cost **Rp4,640,000.00**.
