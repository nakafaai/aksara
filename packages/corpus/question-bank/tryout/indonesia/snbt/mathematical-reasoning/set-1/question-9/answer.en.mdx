We seek values of <InlineMath math="x" /> for which **length, width, and height are all positive**. A zero edge would flatten the box, and a negative length cannot measure an edge.

#### Determining Cuboid Dimensions

Use **the base perimeter** to derive the length without relying on another question. All dimensions below are in centimeters.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2(p+l)&=6x \\
p&=3x-l \\
&=3x-(4x-5)=5-x
\end{aligned}" />

- Length (<InlineMath math="p" />) is <InlineMath math="5 - x" />
- Width (<InlineMath math="l" />) is <InlineMath math="4x - 5" />
- Height (<InlineMath math="t" />) is <InlineMath math="5 - \frac{1}{3}x" />

#### Positive Dimension Conditions

For a nondegenerate box, **every edge dimension must be positive** (<InlineMath math=">0" />).

**Condition 1: <InlineMath math="p > 0" />**

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="p > 0" />
  <BlockMath math="5 - x > 0" />
  <BlockMath math="5 > x" />
  <BlockMath math="x < 5" />
</MathContainer>

**Condition 2: <InlineMath math="l > 0" />**

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="l > 0" />
  <BlockMath math="4x - 5 > 0" />
  <BlockMath math="4x > 5" />
  <BlockMath math="x > \frac{5}{4}" />
</MathContainer>

**Condition 3: <InlineMath math="t > 0" />**

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="t > 0" />
  <BlockMath math="5 - \frac{1}{3}x > 0" />
  <BlockMath math="5 > \frac{1}{3}x" />
  <BlockMath math="15 > x" />
  <BlockMath math="x < 15" />
</MathContainer>

#### Determining Set Intersection

Find **the intersection of all three conditions**, so every condition holds at once:
1. <InlineMath math="x < 5" />
2. <InlineMath math="x > \frac{5}{4}" /> (or <InlineMath math="1.25" />)
3. <InlineMath math="x < 15" />

From conditions 1 and 3, we take the stricter one, which is <InlineMath math="x < 5" />. Combined with condition 2, we get the range:

  />

So, the set <InlineMath math="A" /> is:

<BlockMath math="A=\left\{x\in\mathbb R\mid\frac54<x<5\right\}" />

Both endpoints are excluded. At <InlineMath math="x=5/4" />, the width is zero. At <InlineMath math="x=5" />, the length is zero. That is why the number line has open endpoints.
