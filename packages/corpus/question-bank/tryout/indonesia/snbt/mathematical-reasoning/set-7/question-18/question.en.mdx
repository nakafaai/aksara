In a simplified geometric model, an eagle sees a fish that is <InlineMath math="3\text{ m}" /> below the surface of a river. The eagle, the point where its line of sight crosses the water surface, and the fish lie on one straight line. This line makes an angle of <InlineMath math="30^\circ" /> with the horizontal water surface.

If the distance along the line of sight from the eagle to the fish is <InlineMath math="18\text{ m}" />, how high is the eagle above the river surface? Ignore the refraction of light at the water surface.
