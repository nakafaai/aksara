# Mathematical Modeling

Known:

- Income <InlineMath math="y" /> equals the **base salary plus the bonus** <InlineMath math="x \cdot z" /> rupiah
- The passenger count is <InlineMath math="x" />
- The satisfaction score <InlineMath math="z" /> is <InlineMath math="x - 100" />
- The base salary is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}680{,}000.00" />

So the mathematical model is:

<BlockMath math="y = 4{,}680{,}000 + xz" />

<BlockMath math="y = 4{,}680{,}000 + x(x - 100)" />

<BlockMath math="y = x^2 - 100x + 4{,}680{,}000" />
