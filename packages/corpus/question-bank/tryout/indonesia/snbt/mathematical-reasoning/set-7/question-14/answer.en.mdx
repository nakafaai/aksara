Equal numbers on the two scales means <InlineMath math="F=C" />. Put this condition into the conversion formula and **solve it as a linear equation**.

# Setting up and solving the equation

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
C&=\tfrac95C+32\\
C-\tfrac95C&=32\\
-\tfrac45C&=32\\
C&=-40
\end{aligned}" />

# Check

<BlockMath math="\tfrac95\cdot(-40)+32=-72+32=-40" />

So <InlineMath math="-40^\circ\text{C}" /> is exactly <InlineMath math="-40^\circ\text{F}" />. Above that temperature the Fahrenheit number is **always larger** than the Celsius number.

So the two scales show the same number at **<InlineMath math="-40^\circ\text{C}" />**.
