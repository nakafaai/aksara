Travel distance on the grid is **the number of blocks east or west plus the number of blocks north or south**, because the robot cannot cut diagonally.

# From A to C and the routes to B

From A(3,4) to C(1,6) the robot moves 2 blocks west and 2 blocks north, which is 4 blocks or 400 m, **not 500 m**. The first statement is false.

A shortest route from G to B consists of 5 steps east and 1 step north. The routes differ only in **when the step north is taken**, at step 1 up to step 6, so there are 6 routes. The second statement is true.

# Battery use

From G to B there are <InlineMath math="5+1=6" /> blocks, so a round trip is 12 blocks and uses <InlineMath math="12\cdot2\%=24\%" /> of the battery. The third statement is true.

A full battery lasts for <InlineMath math="\tfrac{100\%}{2\%}=50" /> blocks, exactly 5,000 m or 5 km. The robot **cannot travel more than 5 km**, so the fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **false**.
