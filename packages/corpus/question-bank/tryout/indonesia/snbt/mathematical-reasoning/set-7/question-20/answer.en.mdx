The load is the same both ways, so the whole 12 km trip uses the battery at one rate. The condition is that **the flight time with that load is at least the travel time**.

# Travel time

<BlockMath math="\frac{12}{0.6}=20\text{ minutes}" />

# Heaviest load

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
30-4m&\ge20\\
4m&\le10\\
m&\le2.5
\end{aligned}" />

With 2.5 kg a full battery lasts exactly 20 minutes, so the trip **can just be completed**. A heavier load empties the battery before the drone gets back.

The choices 1 kg, 1.5 kg, 2 kg and 2.25 kg also allow the trip, but they are not the heaviest.

So the heaviest possible load is **2.5 kg**.
