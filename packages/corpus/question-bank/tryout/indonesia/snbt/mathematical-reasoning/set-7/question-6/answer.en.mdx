The long-run average prize is **the expected value**: multiply each prize by its probability, then add.

# Probability of each prize

Of **the 36 equally likely pairs**, sum 12 occurs once, sum 11 twice and sum 7 six times.

# Expected prize

E&=36{,}000\cdot\tfrac{1}{36}+18{,}000\cdot\tfrac{2}{36}+9{,}000\cdot\tfrac{6}{36}\\
&=1{,}000+1{,}000+1{,}500=3{,}500
\end{aligned}" />

The average prize of Rp3,500 is less than the Rp5,000 fee, so **in the long run the player loses** Rp1,500 per game.

The value Rp1,500 is the player's average loss, not the average prize. The value Rp21,000 averages the three prizes without their probabilities.

So the average prize per game approaches **Rp3,500**.
