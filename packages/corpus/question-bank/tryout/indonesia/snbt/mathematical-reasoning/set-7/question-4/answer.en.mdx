# Geometric Analysis

The spotlights at the four top corners are points <InlineMath math="E" />, <InlineMath math="F" />, <InlineMath math="G" />, and <InlineMath math="H" /> in the figure. Each beam runs to the diagonally opposite bottom corner, so all four beams are **space diagonals**. Every space diagonal joins opposite vertices, so its midpoint lies halfway along each of the three dimensions. All four therefore share the same midpoint, the cube's center.

The distance from the center of the cube to the floor (plane <InlineMath math="ABCD" />) is **half of the cube's height** (edge length).

The edge length is <InlineMath math="s=5\text{ m}" />.

Then the distance from the intersection point to the floor is:

<BlockMath math="d=\frac{s}{2}=\frac{5}{2}=2\frac12\text{ m}" />

So, the distance from the intersection point to the floor is <InlineMath math="2\frac{1}{2}\text{ meters}" />.
