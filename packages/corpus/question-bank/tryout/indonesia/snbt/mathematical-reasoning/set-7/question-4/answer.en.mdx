The download happens in **two stages with different speeds**: 40 Mbps for the first 60 seconds, then 30 Mbps until it ends.

# Amount downloaded in the first stage

<BlockMath math="40\cdot60=2{,}400\text{ megabits}=\frac{2{,}400}{8}=300\text{ MB}" />

# Time of the second stage and total time

The remaining <InlineMath math="900-300=600" /> MB, or 4,800 megabits, download at <InlineMath math="40-10=30" /> Mbps.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{4{,}800}{30}&=160\text{ seconds}\\
60+160&=220\text{ seconds}
\end{aligned}" />

The value 160 seconds is only the second stage. The value 180 seconds ignores the television, and 240 seconds uses 30 Mbps from the start. The total time **must lie between those two values**, because the television starts only after 60 seconds.

So the total download time is **220 seconds**.
