#### Find the Undamaged Share

Let <InlineMath math="A" /> mean selecting an undamaged item. The **undamaged items are the complement** of the damaged items:

<BlockMath math="n(A)=80-20=60" />

All <InlineMath math="80" /> items are equally likely to be selected, so divide the favorable count by the total count:

<BlockMath math="P(A)=\frac{n(A)}{n(S)}=\frac{60}{80}=\frac34" />

Thus, the probability is <InlineMath math="\frac34" />. This also equals <InlineMath math="1-\frac{20}{80}" />, which checks the result using the damaged share.
