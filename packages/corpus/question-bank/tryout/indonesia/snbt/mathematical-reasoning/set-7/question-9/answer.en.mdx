You're looking for the salinity after the tank is refilled with artificial seawater. The plan: **track the amount of salt and the amount of water separately**, because salinity is salt divided by water, and evaporation only takes away water.

# Salt and water before the refill

When full, the tank holds <InlineMath math="120\cdot34=4{,}080" /> grams of salt. Over <InlineMath math="6" /> days, <InlineMath math="6\cdot4=24\text{ liters}" /> of water evaporate. **The salt doesn't evaporate**, so there are still <InlineMath math="4{,}080" /> grams of salt in <InlineMath math="96\text{ liters}" /> of water.

<BlockMath math="\frac{4{,}080}{96}=42.5\text{ g/L}" />

The salinity goes up because the water shrinks while the salt stays.

# Refilling with artificial seawater

To fill the tank again, <InlineMath math="24\text{ liters}" /> of artificial seawater go in. **This refill water brings its own salt**, namely <InlineMath math="24\cdot35=840" /> grams.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{final salt} &= 4{,}080+840=4{,}920\text{ grams} \\
\text{final salinity} &= \frac{4{,}920}{120}=41\text{ g/L}
\end{aligned}" />

As a check, <InlineMath math="41" /> lies between <InlineMath math="35" /> and <InlineMath math="42.5" />, as it should when you mix <InlineMath math="96\text{ liters}" /> at <InlineMath math="42.5\text{ g/L}" /> with <InlineMath math="24\text{ liters}" /> at <InlineMath math="35\text{ g/L}" />. It's above <InlineMath math="36\text{ g/L}" />, so it's no longer safe for the fish.

# The wrong options

- <InlineMath math="34\text{ g/L}" /> comes from assuming a refill always restores the old salinity. That's only true **when the refill water is fresh water**.
- <InlineMath math="34.2\text{ g/L}" /> comes from treating the <InlineMath math="96\text{ liters}" /> left as still being at <InlineMath math="34\text{ g/L}" />, when evaporation has already concentrated it.
- <InlineMath math="35\text{ g/L}" /> comes from assuming the tank takes on the salinity of the refill water.
- <InlineMath math="42.5\text{ g/L}" /> is the salinity before the refill, so it stops one step too early.

So the salinity after the refill is <InlineMath math="41\text{ g/L}" />.
