Rain upstream only arrives <InlineMath math="2" /> hours later, so **each gauge hour is paired with the rain from two hours earlier**. The change is <InlineMath math="(2p-20)\text{ cm}" /> per hour.

# Rising until the floodgate opens

The level becomes <InlineMath math="140+40=180\text{ cm}" /> at 07.00 and <InlineMath math="180+30=210\text{ cm}" /> at 08.00. From 08.00 it rises <InlineMath math="60\text{ cm}" /> per hour, so **the floodgate opens when the level reaches <InlineMath math="250\text{ cm}" />**.

<BlockMath math="\frac{250-210}{60}\text{ h}=\frac{40}{60}\text{ h}=40\text{ min}" />

The floodgate opens at 08.40.

# Rising more slowly once the gate is open

While the gate is open, every change drops by <InlineMath math="30\text{ cm}" /> per hour. **From 08.40 to 09.00 the rate is only <InlineMath math="60-30=30\text{ cm}" /> per hour**, and from 09.00 to 10.00 it's <InlineMath math="90-30=60\text{ cm}" /> per hour.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{level at 09.00} &= 250+30\cdot\frac{20}{60}=250+10=260\text{ cm} \\

\end{aligned}" />
