#### Convert the Map Scale

The scale applies to distances expressed in **the same unit**. Since <InlineMath math="1\text{ km}=100{,}000\text{ cm}" />:

<BlockMath math="2{,}000{,}000\text{ cm}=\frac{2{,}000{,}000}{100{,}000}\text{ km}=20\text{ km}" />

Thus, every <InlineMath math="1\text{ cm}" /> on the map represents <InlineMath math="20\text{ km}" />. Convert each segment:

| Segment | Map length in centimeters | Actual length in kilometers |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| <InlineMath math="A\to x" /> | <InlineMath math="2.5" /> | <InlineMath math="2.5\times20=50" /> |
| <InlineMath math="x\to B" /> | <InlineMath math="3" /> | <InlineMath math="3\times20=60" /> |
| <InlineMath math="A\to y" /> | <InlineMath math="4" /> | <InlineMath math="4\times20=80" /> |
| <InlineMath math="y\to B" /> | <InlineMath math="4.5" /> | <InlineMath math="4.5\times20=90" /> |

#### Compare the Complete Routes

Each traveler uses **two segments**, so add both before comparing. Let <InlineMath math="J_B" /> be Benu's distance and <InlineMath math="J_M" /> Mamang's distance, in kilometers.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}J_B&=50+60=110 \\ J_M&=80+90=170 \\ J_M-J_B&=170-110=60\end{aligned}" />

Mamang travels <InlineMath math="60\text{ km}" /> farther. As a check, the map routes differ by <InlineMath math="(4+4.5)-(2.5+3)=3\text{ cm}" />, corresponding to <InlineMath math="3\times20=60\text{ km}" />.
