#### Convert the Averages into Totals

Call the two groups <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" />, with member counts <InlineMath math="n_A=n_B=4" />. Their mean weights are <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_A=30" /> and <InlineMath math="\bar{x}_B=33" /> in kilograms. Find the total weights <InlineMath math="W_A" /> and <InlineMath math="W_B" /> by multiplying each mean by its member count:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}W_A&=n_A\cdot\bar{x}_A \\ &=4\cdot30=120\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}W_B&=n_B\cdot\bar{x}_B \\ &=4\cdot33=132\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

#### Represent the Exchange

Let the exchanged children have weights <InlineMath math="x_A" /> and <InlineMath math="x_B" />, where <InlineMath math="x_A" /> comes from group <InlineMath math="A" />. Both groups still have four members after the exchange. Therefore, equal averages also mean equal total weights.

New total weight of group <InlineMath math="A" /> (<InlineMath math="W_A'" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}W_A'&=W_A-x_A+x_B \\ &=120-x_A+x_B\end{aligned}" />

New total weight of group <InlineMath math="B" /> (<InlineMath math="W_B'" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}W_B'&=W_B-x_B+x_A \\ &=132-x_B+x_A\end{aligned}" />

#### Find the Weight Difference

Since the new averages are equal, the new total sums are also equal:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="W_A' = W_B'" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&120-x_A+x_B \\ &\quad=132-x_B+x_A\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Collect the exchanged weights on one side:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&x_B+x_B-x_A-x_A \\ &\quad=132-120\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="2x_B - 2x_A = 12" />
  <BlockMath math="2(x_B - x_A) = 12" />
  <BlockMath math="x_B - x_A = 6" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the difference in weight between the two exchanged children is <InlineMath math="6 \text{ kg}" />.
