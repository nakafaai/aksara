The flight time comes straight from the formula <InlineMath math="T=30-4m" />. **Each kilogram of load cuts the flight time by 4 minutes**.

# Substitution

<BlockMath math="T=30-4\cdot1.5=30-6=24\text{ minutes}" />

A load of 1.5 kg lies within <InlineMath math="0\le m\le5" />, so the formula applies.

So the drone can fly for **24 minutes**.
