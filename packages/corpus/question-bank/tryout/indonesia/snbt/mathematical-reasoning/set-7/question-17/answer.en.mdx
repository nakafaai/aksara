You're looking for the longest lesson period that still lets every day from Monday to Thursday finish by 15.00. The plan: work out the time available for lesson periods on each kind of day, divide by <InlineMath math="10" />, then take the tightest limit and round down.

# Time available for lesson periods

From 07.00 to 15.00 there are <InlineMath math="480" /> minutes. **The opening activity and the breaks don't count as lesson time**, so subtract them. Monday to Thursday still have two breaks after the same periods as now, and both still come before period <InlineMath math="10" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Monday} &: 480-40-15-30=395\text{ min} \\
\text{Tuesday to Thursday} &: 480-15-15-30=420\text{ min}
\end{aligned}" />

# The limit on one period

Ten periods of equal length have to fit into that time.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Monday} &: 10t\le395 \Rightarrow t\le39.5 \\
\text{Tuesday to Thursday} &: 10t\le420 \Rightarrow t\le42
\end{aligned}" />

**The period length is the same on all four days**, so it has to meet both limits at once. The tighter limit is Monday's, because its ceremony is longer. The length is a whole number of minutes, so <InlineMath math="t\le39.5" /> means **at most <InlineMath math="39" /> minutes**.

# Checking

With <InlineMath math="39" />-minute periods, Monday ends <InlineMath math="40+390+45=475" /> minutes after 07.00, which is 14.55. With <InlineMath math="40" />-minute periods, Monday ends <InlineMath math="40+400+45=485" /> minutes after 07.00, which is 15.05, <InlineMath math="5" /> minutes too late. Tuesday to Thursday with <InlineMath math="39" />-minute periods end at 14.30, which is fine.

# Common slips

If you **only use the Tuesday to Thursday row**, you get <InlineMath math="42" /> minutes, but Monday with <InlineMath math="42" />-minute periods wouldn't end until 15.25. If you round <InlineMath math="39.5" /> up, you get <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes, which makes Monday run late.

So one lesson period can last at most <InlineMath math="39" /> minutes.
