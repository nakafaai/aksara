# Why a run of three fits only one session

Two sessions can't share a period, so two sessions need <InlineMath math="4" /> different periods in one run. **A run of <InlineMath math="3" /> periods fits only one session**, and the leftover period is wasted. In general, a run of <InlineMath math="n" /> periods fits at most <InlineMath math="\left\lfloor \frac{n}{2}\right\rfloor" /> sessions.
