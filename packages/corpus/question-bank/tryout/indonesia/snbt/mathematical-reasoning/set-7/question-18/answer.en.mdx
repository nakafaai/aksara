#### Read the Geometry

The diagram shows one straight line of sight crossing the water surface. The fish is below the surface, while the eagle is above it.

Let <InlineMath math="h" /> be the eagle's height above the water. The total vertical separation between the eagle and the fish is then <InlineMath math="h+3" /> meters. In the right triangle, this separation is opposite the <InlineMath math="30^\circ" /> angle, while the <InlineMath math="18\text{ m}" /> line of sight is the hypotenuse.

#### Use the Sine Ratio

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\sin 30^\circ = \frac{h+3}{18}" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{1}{2} = \frac{h+3}{18}" />
  <BlockMath math="h+3=9" />
  <BlockMath math="h=6" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the eagle is <InlineMath math="6\text{ m}" /> above the river surface.
