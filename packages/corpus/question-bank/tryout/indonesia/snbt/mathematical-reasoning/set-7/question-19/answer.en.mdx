All four statements need the start and end time of every period, and **the timetable differs by day**. The plan: build a clock timetable for the three kinds of day, then judge each statement with it.

# A clock timetable for the three kinds of day

**Each start time is the previous time plus the length of a period or a break**, beginning after the opening activity.

| Day | First period starts | First break | Second break | Last period ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 07.40 | 10.20-10.35 | 12.35-13.05 | 14.25 |
| Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday | 07.15 | 09.30-09.45 | 12.00-12.30 | 14.45 |
| Friday | 07.30 | 09.50-10.05 | none | 11.15 |

For example on Monday: periods <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="4" /> take <InlineMath math="4\cdot40=160" /> minutes from 07.40, so the first break starts at 10.20.

# Period eight on Monday and Tuesday

On Monday, period <InlineMath math="8" /> starts after the second break, at 13.05. On Tuesday, period <InlineMath math="8" /> starts after period <InlineMath math="7" /> (12.30 to 13.15), at 13.15. **Monday's ceremony is <InlineMath math="25" /> minutes longer, but each of the seven Monday periods before period <InlineMath math="8" /> is <InlineMath math="5" /> minutes shorter**, so Monday comes out <InlineMath math="35-25=10" /> minutes earlier.

Monday's period <InlineMath math="8" /> starts earlier, so the first statement is **false**.

# Comparing total lesson time

Total lesson time is the number of periods times their length: Friday <InlineMath math="6\cdot35=210" /> minutes, Monday <InlineMath math="9\cdot40=360" /> minutes, and Tuesday <InlineMath math="9\cdot45=405" /> minutes. Half of Monday's lesson time is <InlineMath math="180" /> minutes, and half of Tuesday's is <InlineMath math="202.5" /> minutes.

The first part holds because <InlineMath math="210>180" />, but **the second part fails because <InlineMath math="210>202.5" />**. A statement joined with "but" is true only when both parts are true, so the second statement is **false**.

# Who is on a break

10.25 falls inside Monday's first break (10.20 to 10.35). From Tuesday to Thursday, 10.25 falls inside period <InlineMath math="4" /> (09.45 to 10.30). On Friday, 10.25 falls inside period <InlineMath math="5" /> (10.05 to 10.40). **Only Monday is on a break**, so the third statement is **true**.

# Finishing times from Monday to Thursday

Monday ends at 14.25, before 14.30. But **Tuesday to Thursday only end at 14.45**.

<BlockMath math="15+9\cdot45+15+30=465\text{ min after 07.00}" />

So the first statement is false, the second is false, the third is true, and the fourth is false.
