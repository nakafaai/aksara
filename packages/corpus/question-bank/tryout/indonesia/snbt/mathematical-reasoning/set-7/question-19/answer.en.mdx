# Type of relation

The coefficient <InlineMath math="-4" /> means each extra kilogram cuts the flight time by 4 minutes. The second statement is true.

Under inverse proportion the product <InlineMath math="T\cdot m" /> would stay fixed. But 1 kg gives <InlineMath math="26\cdot1=26" />, while 2 kg gives <InlineMath math="22\cdot2=44" />. With no load the flight time is 30 minutes, **not infinite**. The first statement is false.

# Doubling the load and half the flight time

A 1 kg load gives 26 minutes, while 2 kg gives 22 minutes, **not 13 minutes**. The third statement is false.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
