#### Recover the Two Totals

Use **sum equals count times mean** before comparing the data sets:

| Data set | Count | Mean | Sum |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original | <InlineMath math="22" /> | <InlineMath math="5" /> | <InlineMath math="22\times5=110" /> |
| After removing two values | <InlineMath math="22-2=20" /> | <InlineMath math="4.9" /> | <InlineMath math="20\times4.9=98" /> |

The two removed values therefore sum to <InlineMath math="110-98=12" />.

#### Combine the Sum and the Range

Let <InlineMath math="m" /> be the minimum and <InlineMath math="M" /> the maximum. The **range is maximum minus minimum**, so:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}m+M&=12 \\ M-m&=4\quad\Rightarrow\quad M=m+4\end{aligned}" />

Substitute the range relation into the sum:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}m+(m+4)&=12 \\ 2m&=12-4=8 \\ m&=4\end{aligned}" />

The lowest score is <InlineMath math="4" />. Check: the highest is <InlineMath math="8" />, giving range <InlineMath math="8-4=4" /> and original sum <InlineMath math="98+4+8=110" />.
