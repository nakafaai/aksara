Two fair dice give **36 equally likely ordered pairs**. The player wins a prize when the sum is 12, 11 or 7.

# Pairs for each sum

Count **the dice pairs for each prize-winning sum**.

| Sum | Pairs | Count |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 12 | <InlineMath math="(6,6)" /> | 1 |
| 11 | <InlineMath math="(5,6),(6,5)" /> | 2 |
| 7 | <InlineMath math="(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)" /> | 6 |

# Probability of a prize

The three events cannot happen together, so **their pairs are added**.

<BlockMath math="\frac{1+2+6}{36}=\frac{9}{36}=0.25" />

So the probability of winning a prize is **0.25**.
