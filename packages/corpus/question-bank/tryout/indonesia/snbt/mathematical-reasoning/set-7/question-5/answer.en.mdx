#### Volume Analysis

Because every ball is completely submerged, its full volume displaces the same volume of water. The remaining water is therefore the cylinder volume minus the combined volume of the balls.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="V_{\text{remaining}} = V_{\text{cylinder}} - n \cdot V_{\text{ball}}" />
  <BlockMath math="11{,}388\pi = \pi r_1^2 h - n \left(\frac{4}{3} \pi r_2^3\right)" />
  <BlockMath math="11{,}388\pi = \pi \left(r_1^2 h - n \cdot \frac{4}{3} r_2^3\right)" />
</MathContainer>

Substitute <InlineMath math="r_1 = 20 \text{ cm}" /> and <InlineMath math="h = 30 \text{ cm}" /> for the cylinder, and <InlineMath math="r_2 = 3 \text{ cm}" /> for each ball.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="11{,}388 = (20)^2(30) - n \left(\frac{4}{3}(3)^3\right)" />
  <BlockMath math="11{,}388 = 12{,}000 - n \left(\frac{4}{3}(27)\right)" />
  <BlockMath math="11{,}388 = 12{,}000 - 36n" />
</MathContainer>

Now isolate <InlineMath math="n" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="36n = 12{,}000 - 11{,}388" />
  <BlockMath math="36n = 612" />
  <BlockMath math="n = \frac{612}{36}" />
  <BlockMath math="n = 17" />
</MathContainer>

The result is a positive integer. As a check, the balls occupy <InlineMath math="17 \cdot 36\pi = 612\pi\text{ cm}^3" />, and <InlineMath math="12{,}000\pi - 612\pi = 11{,}388\pi\text{ cm}^3" /> of water remains.

Therefore, <InlineMath math="17" /> balls were inserted.
