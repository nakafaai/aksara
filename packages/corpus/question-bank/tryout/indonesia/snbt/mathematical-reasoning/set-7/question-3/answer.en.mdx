For each type of item, let <InlineMath math="R" /> be the sales revenue, <InlineMath math="C" /> the purchase cost, and <InlineMath math="U" /> the profit, all in rupiah. One dozen contains <InlineMath math="12" /> items.

#### Notebooks

The purchase cost for <InlineMath math="6" /> dozen notebooks is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}144{,}000.00" />.

Sales:

- <InlineMath math="3" /> dozen (<InlineMath math="36" /> pieces) sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}500.00" /> each
- <InlineMath math="2" /> dozen (<InlineMath math="24" /> pieces) sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}300.00" /> each
- Remaining <InlineMath math="1" /> dozen (<InlineMath math="12" /> pieces) sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}100.00" /> each

Total revenue from notebooks:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}R&=(36\times2{,}500) \\ &\quad+(24\times2{,}300) \\ &\quad+(12\times2{,}100)\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}R&=90{,}000+55{,}200 \\ &\quad+25{,}200\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="R = 170{,}400" />
</MathContainer>

Profit from notebooks:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="U = R - C" />
  <BlockMath math="U = 170{,}400 - 144{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="U = 26{,}400" />
</MathContainer>

#### Ballpoint Pens

The purchase cost for <InlineMath math="5" /> dozen ballpoint pens is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}90{,}000.00" />.

Sales:

- <InlineMath math="1" /> dozen (<InlineMath math="12" /> pieces) sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000.00" /> each
- <InlineMath math="3" /> dozen (<InlineMath math="36" /> pieces) sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}700.00" /> each
- Remaining <InlineMath math="1" /> dozen (<InlineMath math="12" /> pieces) sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}600.00" /> each

Total revenue from ballpoint pens:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}R&=(12\times2{,}000) \\ &\quad+(36\times1{,}700) \\ &\quad+(12\times1{,}600)\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}R&=24{,}000+61{,}200 \\ &\quad+19{,}200\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="R = 104{,}400" />
</MathContainer>

Profit from ballpoint pens:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="U = 104{,}400 - 90{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="U = 14{,}400" />
</MathContainer>

#### Pencils

The purchase cost for <InlineMath math="6" /> dozen pencils is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}72{,}000.00" />.

Sales:

- <InlineMath math="4" /> dozen (<InlineMath math="48" /> pieces) sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}500.00" /> each
- Remaining <InlineMath math="2" /> dozen (<InlineMath math="24" /> pieces) sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}300.00" /> each

Total revenue from pencils:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}R&=(48\times1{,}500) \\ &\quad+(24\times1{,}300)\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="R = 72{,}000 + 31{,}200" />
  <BlockMath math="R = 103{,}200" />
</MathContainer>

Profit from pencils:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="U = 103{,}200 - 72{,}000" />
  <BlockMath math="U = 31{,}200" />
</MathContainer>

#### Conclusion

Profit comparison:

- Notebooks: <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}26{,}400.00" />
- Ballpoint Pens: <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}14{,}400.00" />
- Pencils: <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}31{,}200.00" />

Pencils yield the greatest profit.
