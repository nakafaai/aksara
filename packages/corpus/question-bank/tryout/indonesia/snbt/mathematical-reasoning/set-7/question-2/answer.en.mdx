#### Calculate the Interest

Flat simple interest uses the **original principal every month**. Let <InlineMath math="I" /> be one month's interest and <InlineMath math="B" /> the total interest, in rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}I&=3{,}000{,}000\times\frac{2}{100} \\ &=60{,}000 \\ B&=60{,}000\times10=600{,}000\end{aligned}" />

#### Divide the Total Repayment

The installments must cover **principal plus total interest**. Let <InlineMath math="T" /> be the total repayment and <InlineMath math="A" /> the monthly installment, in rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}T&=3{,}000{,}000+600{,}000 \\ &=3{,}600{,}000 \\ A&=\frac{3{,}600{,}000}{10}=360{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Each monthly installment is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}360{,}000.00" />. As a check, it consists of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}300{,}000.00" /> toward the principal and <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}60{,}000.00" /> in interest.
