#### Calculating Loan Interest

Given:

- Principal loan (<InlineMath math="M" />) is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000{,}000.00" />
- Monthly interest (<InlineMath math="i" />) is <InlineMath math="2\%" />
- Loan duration (<InlineMath math="n" />) is <InlineMath math="10\text{ months}" />

Let <InlineMath math="B" /> be the total interest owed, <InlineMath math="T" /> the total repayment, and <InlineMath math="A" /> the monthly installment, all in rupiah. Interest is calculated from the original principal each month:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="B = M \times i \times n" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}B&=3{,}000{,}000\times2\% \\ &\quad\times10\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}B&=3{,}000{,}000\times\frac{2}{100} \\ &\quad\times10\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="B = 600{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

#### Calculating Monthly Installment

The total amount to be paid is the principal plus total interest:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}T&=3{,}000{,}000+600{,}000\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="T = 3{,}600{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

Since the repayment period is <InlineMath math="10\text{ months}" />, the monthly installment is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="A = \frac{T}{n}" />
  <BlockMath math="A = \frac{3{,}600{,}000}{10}" />
  <BlockMath math="A = 360{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

So, the money Panjul must deposit per month is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}360{,}000.00" />.
