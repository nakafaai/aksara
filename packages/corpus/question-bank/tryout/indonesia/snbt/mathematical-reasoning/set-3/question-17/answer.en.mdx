An order of 450 sheets falls in the group 101 to 500, and that group's price **applies to every sheet**, not only to the sheets after the 100th.

# Cost of the order

<BlockMath math="450\cdot400=180{,}000" />

In thousands of rupiah, the cost is 180.

Charging the first 100 sheets Rp500 and the rest Rp400 gives 190,000. That is wrong because this printer's rule **is not a tiered rate by parts**.

So printing 450 sheets costs **180 thousand rupiah**.
