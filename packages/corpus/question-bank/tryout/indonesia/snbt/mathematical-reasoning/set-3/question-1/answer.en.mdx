**Delivery Period Analysis**

April <InlineMath math="2023" /> has a total of <InlineMath math="30\text{ days}" />. The first delivery was made on April <InlineMath math="1" />, which corresponds to day <InlineMath math="1" />. We need to calculate how many times suppliers <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" /> delivered goods within that <InlineMath math="30" />-day period.

The delivery schedule follows an arithmetic progression pattern with the first term <InlineMath math="a = 1" /> (first delivery date) and common difference <InlineMath math="b" /> corresponding to each supplier's interval. The condition for the delivery date is <InlineMath math="U_n \le 30" />.

**Cooking Oil <InlineMath math="A" /> Volume**

Supplier <InlineMath math="A" /> delivers every <InlineMath math="3\text{ days}" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="1 + (n_A - 1)3 \le 30" />
  <BlockMath math="(n_A - 1)3 \le 29" />
  <BlockMath math="n_A - 1 \le \frac{29}{3}" />
  <BlockMath math="n_A \le \frac{32}{3}" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, supplier <InlineMath math="A" /> delivers <InlineMath math="10\text{ times}" />.

Total volume <InlineMath math="A" />:

<BlockMath math="10 \times 10 = 100 \text{ liters}" />

**Cooking Oil <InlineMath math="B" /> Volume**

Supplier <InlineMath math="B" /> delivers every <InlineMath math="4\text{ days}" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="1 + (n_B - 1)4 \le 30" />
  <BlockMath math="(n_B - 1)4 \le 29" />
  <BlockMath math="n_B - 1 \le 7.25" />
  <BlockMath math="n_B \le 8.25" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, supplier <InlineMath math="B" /> delivers <InlineMath math="8\text{ times}" />.

Total volume <InlineMath math="B" />:

<BlockMath math="8 \times 15 = 120 \text{ liters}" />

**Combined Total Volume**

Let <InlineMath math="V" /> be the combined volume and <InlineMath math="V_A" /> and <InlineMath math="V_B" /> the volumes of the two brands.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="V = V_A + V_B" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}V &= 100 + 120 \\ &=220\text{ liters}\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the total volume of cooking oil brands <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" /> received is <InlineMath math="220\text{ liters}" />.
