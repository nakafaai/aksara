**Formula Analysis**

The lateral surface of a cylinder is a rectangle with length <InlineMath math="2\pi r" /> and height <InlineMath math="t" />. Thus, the formula for the lateral surface area is <InlineMath math="L_S = 2\pi r t" />.

Let <InlineMath math="v" /> be the numerical value of <InlineMath math="V = \pi r^2 t" /> in cubic centimetres and <InlineMath math="\ell_S" /> the numerical value of the lateral surface area in square centimetres. With <InlineMath math="r" /> and <InlineMath math="t" /> measured in centimetres:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\frac{v}{\ell_S} = \frac{\pi r^2 t}{2\pi r t}" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{v}{\ell_S} = \frac{r}{2}" />
</MathContainer>

**Calculating the Ratio**

From <InlineMath math="r=\frac12t" /> and <InlineMath math="\pi r^2t=\frac{729\pi}{4}" />, we obtain <InlineMath math="t=9\text{ cm}" /> and <InlineMath math="r=\frac92\text{ cm}" />.

Substitute the value of <InlineMath math="r" /> into the ratio equation:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\frac{v}{\ell_S} = \frac{\frac{9}{2}}{2}" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{v}{\ell_S} = \frac{9}{4}" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the ratio of the numerical values is <InlineMath math="v:\ell_S=9:4" />.
