The intersection of the diagonals is the centre of the lawn, and the centre is **equally far from all four corners**. So the smallest range is the distance from <InlineMath math="A" /> to the centre.

# Distance to the centre

The centre is 6 m from <InlineMath math="A" /> along <InlineMath math="AB" /> and 4 m along <InlineMath math="AD" />, **half of 12 m and 8 m**.

<BlockMath math="\sqrt{6^2+4^2}=\sqrt{52}=\sqrt{4\cdot13}=2\sqrt{13}\approx7.21\text{ m}" />

# Checking the choices

The ranges 6 m, 6.5 m, <InlineMath math="4\sqrt3\approx6.93" /> m and 7 m are all less than 7.21 m, so **the centre is not reached**. The choice 7 m is tempting because it is closest, but it is still about 0.21 m short.

So the smallest range needed is **<InlineMath math="2\sqrt{13}\text{ m}" />**.
