The dry part is **the area of the lawn minus the area that gets water**.

# Lawn area and wetted area

<BlockMath math="L_{\text{lawn}}=12\cdot8=96\text{ m}^2" />

Each corner sprinkler wets a quarter circle of radius 6 m. The two quarter circles **only touch at the midpoint of side <InlineMath math="AB" />**, because <InlineMath math="AB=12=6+6" />, so nothing overlaps.

<BlockMath math="L_{\text{wet}}=2\cdot\tfrac14\cdot3.14\cdot6^2=56.52\text{ m}^2" />

# The dry area

<BlockMath math="96-56.52=39.48\text{ m}^2" />

So the area that gets no water is **39.48 square metres**.
