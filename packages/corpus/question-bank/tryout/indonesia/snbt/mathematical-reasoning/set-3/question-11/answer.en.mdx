#### Choosing the two ends

Armenia and Belgium must occupy the **two end positions**. Swapping these flags gives two possible choices.

| Left end | Five middle positions | Right end |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Armenia | China, Denmark, Ecuador, Finland, Ghana in any order | Belgium |
| Belgium | China, Denmark, Ecuador, Finland, Ghana in any order | Armenia |

#### Filling the middle positions

For each choice of ends, the other five flags occupy **five distinct positions**. There are 5 choices for the first middle position. After placing one flag, there are 4 choices for the next position, then 3, 2, and 1.

<BlockMath math="5!=5\times4\times3\times2\times1=120" />

Each of the two end choices allows 120 middle arrangements. By the multiplication rule, the total number of arrangements is:

<BlockMath math="N=2\times5!=2\times120=240" />
