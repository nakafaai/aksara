**Rice available for sale**

Let <InlineMath math="G" />, <InlineMath math="T" />, and <InlineMath math="N" /> be the total gross, tare, and net weights in kilograms. The net weight is the rice remaining after the packaging weight is subtracted.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="G = 5 \times 50 = 250" />
  <BlockMath math="T = 1\% \times 250 = 2.5" />
  <BlockMath math="N = 250 - 2.5 = 247.5" />
</MathContainer>

**Revenue and loss**

All <InlineMath math="247.5\text{ kg}" /> of rice is sold at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000.00" /> per kilogram. The sales revenue <InlineMath math="S" /> is calculated in rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&S = 247.5 \times 2{,}000 \\ &S = 495{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Revenue is less than the purchase price. The loss <InlineMath math="L" /> is the purchase price minus revenue, also in rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&L = 650{,}000 - 495{,}000 \\ &L = 155{,}000\end{aligned}" />

The merchant therefore loses <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}155{,}000.00" />.
