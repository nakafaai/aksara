One pedal turn turns the front gear once, so **the chain passes 48 teeth**. The same chain passes 48 teeth on the rear gear.

# Wheel turns per pedal turn

The 24-tooth rear gear turns <InlineMath math="\tfrac{48}{24}=2" /> times, and **the rear wheel turns 2 times with it**.

# Distance per pedal turn

The circumference of the wheel is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{22}{7}\cdot70=220" /> cm, which is 2.2 m. **Two wheel turns** move the bicycle

<BlockMath math="2\cdot2.2=4.4\text{ m}" />

The distance 2.2 m is only one circumference. The distance 6.6 m is the result for the 16-tooth rear gear, and 1.1 m turns the gear ratio upside down to <InlineMath math="\tfrac{24}{48}" />.

So each pedal turn moves the bicycle **4.4 m**.
