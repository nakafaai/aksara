# The fee for way one

The car is still in the building at 00.00, so the midnight rule **cuts the stay into two parts** and the overnight rule adds the overnight fee.

- The part from 21.20 to 24.00 is <InlineMath math="2\text{ h }40\text{ min}" />, which counts as <InlineMath math="3\text{ h}" />.
- The part from 00.00 to 06.10 is <InlineMath math="6\text{ h }10\text{ min}" />, which counts as <InlineMath math="7\text{ h}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{way I} &= (6{,}000+2\cdot4{,}000)+(6{,}000+6\cdot4{,}000)+10{,}000 \\
&= 14{,}000+30{,}000+10{,}000 \\
&= 54{,}000\text{ rupiah}
\end{aligned}" />

The second part is exactly <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}30{,}000" />, so it doesn't go over the cap for one part.

# The fee for way two

At 00.00 the car is outside the building, so **there's no overnight fee and no cut**. The re-entry rule turns way II into two separate stays.

- The stay from 21.20 to 23.50 is <InlineMath math="2\text{ h }30\text{ min}" />, which counts as <InlineMath math="3\text{ h}" />.
- The stay from 00.20 to 06.10 is <InlineMath math="5\text{ h }50\text{ min}" />, which counts as <InlineMath math="6\text{ h}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{way II} &= (6{,}000+2\cdot4{,}000)+(6{,}000+5\cdot4{,}000) \\
&= 14{,}000+26{,}000 \\
&= 40{,}000\text{ rupiah}
\end{aligned}" />

# The difference and a check

Subtract the total of way II from the total of way I.

<BlockMath math="54{,}000-40{,}000=14{,}000\text{ rupiah}" />

The difference comes from **two separate sources**. The first is the overnight fee of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" />. The second is that the morning part of way I counts as <InlineMath math="7\text{ h}" />, while the morning stay of way II is only <InlineMath math="6\text{ h}" />, a gap of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000" />. Together that's <InlineMath math="10{,}000+4{,}000=14{,}000" />, the same as above.

# Why the other options are wrong

Each other option **skips one rule**.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}000" /> comes from forgetting the overnight fee in way I.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}8{,}000" /> comes from treating way I as one <InlineMath math="9" />-hour stay with no cut and no cap, so <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}38{,}000" /> plus the overnight fee.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" /> comes from seeing only the overnight fee and assuming both mornings cost the same.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}18{,}000" /> comes from rounding <InlineMath math="2\text{ h }30\text{ min}" /> in way II down to <InlineMath math="2\text{ h}" />.

So way II is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}14{,}000" /> cheaper than way I.
