The school may print more than 480 sheets, so compare **the cost of every way that gives at least 480 sheets**, including ways that cross a group boundary.

# Cost of each way

Each way gives **at least 480 sheets**.

| Way | Cost |
| :---: | :---: |
| 480 sheets at once | <InlineMath math="480\cdot400=192{,}000" /> |
| 500 sheets at once | <InlineMath math="500\cdot400=200{,}000" /> |
| two orders of 240 | <InlineMath math="2\cdot240\cdot400=192{,}000" /> |
| five orders of 96 | <InlineMath math="5\cdot96\cdot500=240{,}000" /> |
| 501 sheets at once | <InlineMath math="501\cdot300=150{,}300" /> |

# Choosing the cheapest

Ordering 501 sheets brings the price down to Rp300 per sheet, so **the cost is the smallest** even with 21 sheets to spare. The saving is <InlineMath math="192{,}000-150{,}300=41{,}700" /> rupiah compared with ordering exactly 480.

Splitting the order does not help, because each small order stays in the same price group or a more expensive one.

So the cheapest way is **ordering 501 sheets in one order for Rp150,300**.
