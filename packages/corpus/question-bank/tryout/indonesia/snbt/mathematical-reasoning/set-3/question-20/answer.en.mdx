We need to find the number of days **without** shipments of oil <InlineMath math="A" /> or <InlineMath math="B" />. We count the days **with** shipments, then subtract that from the total days in April (<InlineMath math="30\text{ days}" />).

Here is the breakdown of the delivery schedule:

| Oil Type | Delivery Dates (Arithmetic Sequence) | Total Days |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| Oil <InlineMath math="A" /> (Every <InlineMath math="3\text{ days}" />) | <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="7" />, <InlineMath math="10" />, <InlineMath math="13" />, <InlineMath math="16" />, <InlineMath math="19" />, <InlineMath math="22" />, <InlineMath math="25" />, <InlineMath math="28" /> | <InlineMath math="10" /> |
| Oil <InlineMath math="B" /> (Every <InlineMath math="4\text{ days}" />) | <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="5" />, <InlineMath math="9" />, <InlineMath math="13" />, <InlineMath math="17" />, <InlineMath math="21" />, <InlineMath math="25" />, <InlineMath math="29" /> | <InlineMath math="8" /> |
| **Union** (<InlineMath math="A \cup B" />) | <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="5" />, <InlineMath math="7" />, <InlineMath math="9" />, <InlineMath math="10" />, <InlineMath math="13" />, <InlineMath math="16" />, <InlineMath math="17" />, <InlineMath math="19" />, <InlineMath math="21" />, <InlineMath math="22" />, <InlineMath math="25" />, <InlineMath math="28" />, <InlineMath math="29" /> | <InlineMath math="15" /> |

Both brands arrive on dates 1, 13, and 25. To count **days with at least one shipment**, subtract these three dates that were counted twice:

<BlockMath math="|A\cup B|=10+8-3=15" />

The number of **days with neither shipment** is the number of days in April minus the union count:

<BlockMath math="30-15=15" />
