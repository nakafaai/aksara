The price per sheet depends on the order group, so check **each group** to see how many sheets are affordable.

# Checking each group

- Group 1 to 100: at most 100 sheets for 50,000.
- Group 101 to 500: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{150{,}000}{400}=375" /> sheets, and 375 does lie in this group.
- Group above 500: the smallest order, 501 sheets, costs <InlineMath math="501\cdot300=150{,}300" />, **slightly more than the money available**.

# Finding the largest number

The largest affordable number is 375 sheets for exactly 150,000.

The value 501 is almost affordable but 300 rupiah short. The value 300 divides the money by the Rp500 price, which applies only up to 100 sheets.

So at most **375 sheets** can be printed.
