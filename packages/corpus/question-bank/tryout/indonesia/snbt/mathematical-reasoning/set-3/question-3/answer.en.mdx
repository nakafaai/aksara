Let <InlineMath math="T" /> and <InlineMath math="N" /> be the **tare and net weights** in kilograms. Write the revenue from each sales group as <InlineMath math="S_1" />, <InlineMath math="S_2" />, and <InlineMath math="S_3" />. Total revenue <InlineMath math="S" />, purchase price <InlineMath math="P" />, and profit <InlineMath math="U" /> are calculated in rupiah.

# Net Weight Analysis

First, we calculate how much watermelon is actually sold by subtracting the **tare** (packaging weight) from the gross weight.

<BlockMath math="T = 5\% \times 100 = 5" />

<BlockMath math="N = 100 - 5 = 95" />

# Total Revenue

Next, we calculate the total revenue from the watermelon sales. The **remaining weight of the watermelons** is <InlineMath math="95 - 60 - 20 = 15 \text{ kg}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_1 &= 60 \times 5{,}000 \\ &=300{,}000\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_2 &= 20 \times 4{,}000 \\ &=80{,}000\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_3 &= 15 \times 3{,}000 \\ &=45{,}000\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S &= 300{,}000 + 80{,}000 \\ &\quad +45{,}000 \\ &=425{,}000\end{aligned}" />

# Calculating Purchase Price

The merchant makes a <InlineMath math="70\%" /> profit, meaning the total revenue is <InlineMath math="170\%" /> of the **purchase price**.

<BlockMath math="S = P + U" />

<BlockMath math="425{,}000 = P + 0.7 P" />

<BlockMath math="425{,}000 = 1.7 P" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P &= \frac{425{,}000}{1.7} \\ &=250{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Thus, the purchase price for one crate of watermelons is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}250{,}000.00" />.
