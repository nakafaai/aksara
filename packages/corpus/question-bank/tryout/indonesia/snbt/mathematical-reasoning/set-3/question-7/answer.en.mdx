A point gets water when **its distance to one of the sprinklers is at most that sprinkler's range**. Place <InlineMath math="A" /> at <InlineMath math="(0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="B" /> at <InlineMath math="(12,0)" /> and <InlineMath math="D" /> at <InlineMath math="(0,8)" />.

# The midpoint of AB and the point D

The midpoint of <InlineMath math="AB" /> is exactly 6 m from <InlineMath math="A" /> and from <InlineMath math="B" />. A distance of 6 m is still within range, so the first statement is true.

The point <InlineMath math="D" /> is 8 m from <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="\sqrt{12^2+8^2}=\sqrt{208}" /> m from <InlineMath math="B" />. **Both are more than 6 m**, so the third statement is false.

# The intersection of the diagonals

The diagonals meet at <InlineMath math="(6,4)" />, which is equally far from <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" />.

<BlockMath math="\sqrt{6^2+4^2}=\sqrt{52}\approx7.21\text{ m}" />

This is more than 6 m, so the second statement is true. It is **still more than 7 m**, so a range of 7 m is not enough and the fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **false**.
