Each book can be renewed or not, and the choice for one book doesn't affect any other book. So the plan: **compare the two choices for each book type**, take the cheaper one, then add up over all the books.

# Two possible delays

Without a renewal, every book is <InlineMath math="23" /> days late. With a renewal, the deadline moves <InlineMath math="7" /> days later, so that book is <InlineMath math="23-7=16" /> days late and there's **a renewal fee of <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}000" />**. Both delays are more than <InlineMath math="3" /> days, so the grace period doesn't help, and every late day is fined.

# The choice for a regular book

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{no renewal} &= \min(23\cdot1{,}000,\,20{,}000) = 20{,}000 \\
\text{renewal} &= 3{,}000+16\cdot1{,}000 = 19{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

Without a renewal, the fine of <InlineMath math="23{,}000" /> is cut by the cap to <InlineMath math="20{,}000" />. With a renewal it costs <InlineMath math="19{,}000" />, so **a regular book should be renewed**, saving <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1{,}000" /> per book.

# The choice for a reference book

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{no renewal} &= \min(23\cdot2{,}500,\,40{,}000) = 40{,}000 \\
\text{renewal} &= 3{,}000+\min(16\cdot2{,}500,\,40{,}000) = 43{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

The fine for <InlineMath math="16" /> days is already exactly <InlineMath math="40{,}000" />, the same as the cap. So renewing doesn't lower the fine at all, but it still adds <InlineMath math="3{,}000" />. That means **a reference book shouldn't be renewed**.

# The smallest total

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{total} &= 4\cdot19{,}000+2\cdot40{,}000 \\
&= 76{,}000+80{,}000 \\
&= 156{,}000\text{ rupiah}
\end{aligned}" />

Check it against the two all-or-nothing plans. Renewing every book gives <InlineMath math="4\cdot19{,}000+2\cdot43{,}000=162{,}000" />, and renewing nothing gives <InlineMath math="4\cdot20{,}000+2\cdot40{,}000=160{,}000" />. Both cost more, so **the mixed plan really is the cheapest**.

# Why the other options are wrong

Each other option **makes the same renewal choice for every book** or misreads one rule.

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}135{,}000" /> comes from fining only the days after the grace period. That way both book types look worth renewing.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}160{,}000" /> is the cost if no book is renewed.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}162{,}000" /> is the cost if every book is renewed, including the reference books whose fine doesn't drop.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}207{,}000" /> comes from ignoring the cap per book and renewing nothing.

So the smallest total Lina can pay is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}156{,}000" />.
