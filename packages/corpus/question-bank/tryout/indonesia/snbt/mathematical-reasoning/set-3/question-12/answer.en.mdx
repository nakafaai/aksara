**Ratio Analysis**

The ratio of visitors on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday is given as <InlineMath math="3 : 4 : 5" />. The total number of visitors for these three days is <InlineMath math="144" />.

We can calculate the total number of parts (units) in the ratio:

<BlockMath math="3 + 4 + 5 = 12" />

This means <InlineMath math="12\text{ units}" /> correspond to <InlineMath math="144\text{ people}" />.

**Calculating Monday's Visitors**

Let <InlineMath math="M" /> be Monday's visitor count. Divide Monday's share by the total number of shares, then multiply by the total visitor count.

Monday has a share of <InlineMath math="3\text{ units}" />. We use the fraction method to find the number of visitors.

<BlockMath math="M = \frac{3}{12} \times 144" />

Simplify the fraction <InlineMath math="\frac{3}{12}" /> to <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{4}" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="M = \frac{1}{4} \times 144" />
  <BlockMath math="M = 36" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the number of visitors on Monday is <InlineMath math="36" />.
