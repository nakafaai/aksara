Split the votes of Citra and Dimas **according to each ratio**, then add them to the fixed votes of Andra and Bunga.

# Splitting the votes of Citra and Dimas

The ratio <InlineMath math="1:3" /> splits Citra's 48 votes into **4 equal parts** of 12. The ratio <InlineMath math="3:1" /> splits Dimas's 24 votes into parts of 6.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Citra}&:\quad \text{Andra }12,\;\text{Bunga }36\\
\text{Dimas}&:\quad \text{Andra }18,\;\text{Bunga }6
\end{aligned}" />

# Second-round totals

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Andra}&=96+12+18=126\\
\text{Bunga}&=72+36+6=114
\end{aligned}" />

The totals add up to <InlineMath math="126+114=240" />, the number of valid votes. **Andra leads by 12 votes**.

The choice that Bunga wins by 12 appears if the two ratios are swapped. The choice of a tie appears if Citra's and Dimas's voters are split evenly.

So **Andra wins by 12 votes**.
