The **total initial amount** is <InlineMath math="120+90+150=360" />. The next period's **total net shipment** <InlineMath math="N" /> is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}N&=(150-6)+(120-10)+(180-4)\\&=144+110+176=430\end{aligned}" />

Thus, the combined growth <InlineMath math="g" /> is

<BlockMath math="g=\frac{430-360}{360}\times100\%=\frac{70}{360}\times100\%=19.444\ldots\%" />
