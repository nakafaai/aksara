The distance from a point to a plane is the length of a segment **perpendicular to the plane**. First find the equation of plane <InlineMath math="ABC" />, then calculate its distance from <InlineMath math="O=(0,0,0)" />.

#### Finding the plane equation

The plane meets the axes at <InlineMath math="A=(4,0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="B=(0,6,0)" />, and <InlineMath math="C=(0,0,8)" />. Its intercept form is

<BlockMath math="\frac{x}{4}+\frac{y}{6}+\frac{z}{8}=1" />

Multiply both sides by <InlineMath math="24" /> to remove every denominator.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}6x+4y+3z&=24\\6x+4y+3z-24&=0\end{aligned}" />

The coefficients <InlineMath math="(6,4,3)" /> form a **normal vector**, which points perpendicular to the plane.

#### Calculating the perpendicular length

For plane <InlineMath math="ax+by+cz+d=0" /> and point <InlineMath math="(x_0,y_0,z_0)" />, the **distance formula** is

<BlockMath math="D=\frac{|ax_0+by_0+cz_0+d|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}" />

Substitute the plane coefficients and the origin coordinates.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}D&=\frac{|6(0)+4(0)+3(0)-24|}{\sqrt{6^2+4^2+3^2}}\\&=\frac{24}{\sqrt{36+16+9}}\\&=\frac{24}{\sqrt{61}}\end{aligned}" />

Segment <InlineMath math="OH" /> shows this distance in the model. To check the construction, its perpendicular foot is <InlineMath math="H=(144/61,96/61,72/61)" />. Segment <InlineMath math="OH" /> follows the normal direction because the coordinates of <InlineMath math="H" /> equal <InlineMath math="(24/61)(6,4,3)" />. Substituting into the plane equation gives

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}6x_H+4y_H+3z_H&=\frac{864+384+216}{61}\\&=\frac{1464}{61}=24\end{aligned}" />

Thus <InlineMath math="H" /> lies on plane <InlineMath math="ABC" />, and the required distance is <InlineMath math="24/\sqrt{61}" /> units.
