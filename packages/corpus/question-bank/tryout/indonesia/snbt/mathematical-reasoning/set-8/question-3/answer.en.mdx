Every garden size must meet **two conditions at once**: the wire is exactly 44 m and the side parallel to the wall is at most 20 m, that is, <InlineMath math="x\ge12" />.

# Sides of 15 m and 10 m

For <InlineMath math="x=15" /> the parallel side is <InlineMath math="44-30=14" /> m. The first statement is true.

For <InlineMath math="x=10" /> the parallel side is <InlineMath math="44-20=24" /> m, **longer than the wall**. That garden cannot be built, so the third statement is false.

# An area of 192 m²

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
x(44-2x)&=192\\
x^2-22x+96&=0\\
(x-6)(x-16)&=0
\end{aligned}" />

The value <InlineMath math="x=6" /> makes the parallel side 32 m, **which is impossible**. Only <InlineMath math="x=16" /> works, with a parallel side of 12 m. The second statement is true.

# How the area changes

For <InlineMath math="x\ge12" />, for example <InlineMath math="x=12" /> gives 240 m², <InlineMath math="x=14" /> gives 224 m² and <InlineMath math="x=16" /> gives 192 m². The area **gets smaller**, so the fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **false**.
