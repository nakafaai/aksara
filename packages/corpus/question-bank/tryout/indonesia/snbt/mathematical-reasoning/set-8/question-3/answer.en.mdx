Let the original price be <InlineMath math="P" /> thousand rupiah. The discount leaves <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of the original price. The **voucher is deducted next**, and the tax multiplies that amount by <InlineMath math="1.11" />. Thus

<BlockMath math="(0.8P-30)(1.11)=392.94" />

Undo the operations in reverse order: divide out the tax, add the voucher, then divide by the remaining price fraction.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}0.8P-30&=\frac{392.94}{1.11}=354 \\ 0.8P&=354+30=384 \\ P&=\frac{384}{0.8}=480\end{aligned}" />
