The number of floors walked is **the sum of the distances from the stop to each destination**. Distances down and up both count.

# Why the median

If the lift stops one floor above floor 6, the three employees going to floors 3, 6 and 6 each walk one floor more, while only two employees walk less. **The median, floor 6, makes the total distance smallest**.

Floor 8 is the mean of the destinations, and floor 9 is the midpoint between the lowest and highest floors. Both give a larger total.

So the lift should stop at **floor 6**.
