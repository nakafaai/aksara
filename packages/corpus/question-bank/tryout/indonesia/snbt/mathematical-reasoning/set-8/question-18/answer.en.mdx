The safest way is to **convert each side to kilometres first**, then multiply. At this scale 1 cm on the map stands for 2.5 km.

# Real sides of the lake

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2\cdot2.5&=5\text{ km}\\
1.2\cdot2.5&=3\text{ km}
\end{aligned}" />

# Area of the lake

<BlockMath math="5\cdot3=15\text{ km}^2" />

Note that the drawn area <InlineMath math="2.4\text{ cm}^2" /> times <InlineMath math="2.5" /> gives only 6, because **area changes by the square of the scale factor**, <InlineMath math="2.5^2=6.25" />. Indeed <InlineMath math="2.4\cdot6.25=15" />.

So the real area of the lake is **15 <InlineMath math="\text{km}^2" />**.
