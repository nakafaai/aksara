To determine the last two digits, track the powers of <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="2" /> modulo <InlineMath math="100" />. Since <InlineMath math="2026\equiv6\pmod{20}" /> and <InlineMath math="2025\equiv5\pmod{20}" />,

<BlockMath math="3^{2026}\equiv3^6\equiv29\pmod{100}" />

and <InlineMath math="2^{2025}\equiv2^5\equiv32\pmod{100}" />. Their sum has remainder <InlineMath math="29+32=61" />.
