# Real distance

Remember that **1 km is 100,000 cm**.

# Travel time

<BlockMath math="\frac{16}{40}=0.4\text{ h}=0.4\cdot60\text{ min}=24\text{ min}" />

The value 40 minutes reads 0.4 hours as 40 minutes, although **an hour has 60 minutes**, not 100. The value 2 hours 30 minutes divides 40 by 16. The values 2.4 minutes and 4 hours come from a wrong centimetre to kilometre conversion, giving 1.6 km or 160 km.

So the bus needs **24 minutes**.
