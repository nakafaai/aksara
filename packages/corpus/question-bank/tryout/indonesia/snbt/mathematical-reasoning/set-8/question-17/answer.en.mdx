# Donors who suit that patient

By the ABO rule, a group B patient can take blood from B donors and O donors. By the rhesus rule, a rhesus positive patient can take blood from rhesus positive donors and **from rhesus negative donors too**. So the suitable donors are <InlineMath math="\mathrm{B}^+" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{B}^-" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{O}^+" /> and <InlineMath math="\mathrm{O}^-" />.
