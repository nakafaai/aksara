The joint probability of a unit being from A, defective, and flagged is <InlineMath math="0.10\cdot0.80\cdot0.90=0.072" />. For B, the corresponding probability is <InlineMath math="0.90\cdot0.05\cdot0.40=0.018" />.

Bayes' theorem gives

<BlockMath math="P(A\mid\text{defective and flagged})=\frac{0.072}{0.072+0.018}=\frac45" />
