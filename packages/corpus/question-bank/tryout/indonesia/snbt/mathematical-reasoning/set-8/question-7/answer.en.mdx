Check each statement with **the two rates in the information**: 2 seconds for each floor passed and 12 seconds for each stop.

# Stopping time

One stop, 12 seconds, equals the time to move <InlineMath math="\tfrac{12}{2}=6" /> floors. The first statement is true.

Two stops add <InlineMath math="2\cdot12=24" /> seconds, **not 12 seconds**. The second statement is false.

# Going up then down

From floor 3 to 11 the lift rises 8 floors, then from 11 to 6 it drops 5 floors. **The distances up and down add up**:

<BlockMath math="(8+5)\cdot2=26\text{ seconds}" />

So the third statement is false.

# Load

Eleven people averaging 62 kg weigh <InlineMath math="11\cdot62=682" /> kg, **not more than 700 kg**. The fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
