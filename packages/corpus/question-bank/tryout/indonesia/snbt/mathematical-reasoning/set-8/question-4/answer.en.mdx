If all four sides are fenced, the perimeter is 44 m, so **length plus width is always 22 m**. You want two numbers adding up to 22 with the largest product.

# Comparing some sizes

| Length | Width | Area |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 14 m | 8 m | 112 m² |
| 13 m | 9 m | 117 m² |
| 12 m | 10 m | 120 m² |
| 11 m | 11 m | 121 m² |

The closer length and width are, the larger the area. With length <InlineMath math="11+d" /> and width <InlineMath math="11-d" /> the area is <InlineMath math="121-d^2" />, so **the largest area occurs at d = 0**, a square.

# Checking the choices

The values 112, 117 and 120 m² are possible but smaller than the square. The value 100 m² is a square with 10 m sides, whose perimeter is only 40 m.

So the largest area is **121 m²**, a square garden with 11 m sides.
