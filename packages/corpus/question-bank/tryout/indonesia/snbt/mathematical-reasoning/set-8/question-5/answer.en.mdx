The total time is made of **moving time** and **stopping time**. Work out each separately, then add them.

# Moving time

From floor 1 to floor 12 the lift passes <InlineMath math="12-1=11" /> floors, **however often it stops on the way**.

<BlockMath math="11\cdot2=22\text{ seconds}" />

# Stopping time

The lift stops at floors 4, 9 and 12. The stop at floor 12 counts too, because **the time ends only when the doors close again**.

<BlockMath math="3\cdot12=36\text{ seconds}" />

The total is <InlineMath math="22+36=58" /> seconds.

So the time needed is **58 seconds**.
