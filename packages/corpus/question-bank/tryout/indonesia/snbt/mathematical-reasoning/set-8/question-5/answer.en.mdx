Let <InlineMath math="L" /> and <InlineMath math="W" /> be the length and width, both in metres. The perimeter gives <InlineMath math="L+W=26" />. The difference condition gives

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="L-W\ge4" />
  <BlockMath math="L-(26-L)\ge4" />
  <BlockMath math="L\ge15" />
</MathContainer>

With a fixed sum of side lengths, the area is largest when the sides are as close as possible. Since <InlineMath math="L\ge15" />, choose the smallest permitted multiple of three, <InlineMath math="L=15" />. The width is <InlineMath math="W=26-15=11" />.

This pair satisfies every condition, so the maximum area is <InlineMath math="15\cdot11=165\text{ m}^2" />.
