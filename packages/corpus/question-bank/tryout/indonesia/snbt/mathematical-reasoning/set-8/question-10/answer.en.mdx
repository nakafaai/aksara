Let <InlineMath math="a" /> be the first term and let <InlineMath math="r>0" /> be the ratio. The two conditions give

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="a(1+r+r^2)=21" />
  <BlockMath math="ar^3=24" />
</MathContainer>

Substituting <InlineMath math="a=24/r^3" /> into the first equation gives

<BlockMath math="7r^3-8r^2-8r-8=0=(r-2)(7r^2+6r+4)" />

The second quadratic has no real root because its discriminant is <InlineMath math="6^2-4(7)(4)=-76" />. Therefore <InlineMath math="r=2" /> is the only real ratio, and <InlineMath math="a=24/2^3=3" />.

The first four terms are <InlineMath math="3,6,12,24" />. Their reciprocal sum is
