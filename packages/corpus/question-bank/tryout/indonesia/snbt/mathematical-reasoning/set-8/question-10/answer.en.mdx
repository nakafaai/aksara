The sum of the first eight terms of an arithmetic sequence is **the number of terms times the average of the first and last terms**.

# First and eighth terms

<BlockMath math="U_8=120+7\cdot15=225" />

# Sum over eight years

<BlockMath math="S_8=\frac{8}{2}(120+225)=4\cdot345=1{,}380" />

The value 960 assumes 120 seedlings every year. The value 1,065 adds the increase <InlineMath math="7\cdot15" /> only once. The value 1,155 is the sum for seven years. The value 1,320 multiplies 8 by the fourth year's number, although **eight terms have no single middle term**.

So **1,380 seedlings** are planted in eight years.
