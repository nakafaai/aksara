The **profit function**, in thousand rupiah, is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}L(p)&=(p-2)(230-10p)-300 \\ &=-10p^2+250p-760 \\ &=-10\bigl((p-12.5)^2-156.25\bigr)-760 \\ &=-10(p-12.5)^2+802.5\end{aligned}" />

The condition <InlineMath math="L(p)\ge790" /> is equivalent to <InlineMath math="(p-12.5)^2\le1.25" />. The **integer solutions** are <InlineMath math="p=12" /> and <InlineMath math="p=13" />. Therefore, there are <InlineMath math="2" /> price values.

Dividing the inequality by <InlineMath math="-10" /> reverses its direction. This gives <InlineMath math="|p-12.5|\le\sqrt{1.25}\approx1.118" />, or approximately <InlineMath math="11.382\le p\le13.618" />. The nearby integer values confirm the boundary:
