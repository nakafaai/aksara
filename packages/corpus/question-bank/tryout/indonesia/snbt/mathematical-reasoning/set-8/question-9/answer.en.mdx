First solve for the intersection:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}x+2&=-2x+8 \\ 3x&=6 \\ x&=2,\qquad y=2+2=4\end{aligned}" />

The lines intersect at <InlineMath math="(2,4)" />. The small triangle to the right of <InlineMath math="x=k" /> is **similar to the full triangle**, with linear scale factor <InlineMath math="\frac{2-k}{2}" />.

Its **area must be half** of the full area, so

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\left(\frac{2-k}{2}\right)^2&=\frac12 \\ (2-k)^2&=2 \\ 2-k&=\sqrt2 \\ k&=2-\sqrt2\end{aligned}" />

Since <InlineMath math="0<k<2" />, <InlineMath math="2-k=\sqrt2" />, and therefore <InlineMath math="k=2-\sqrt2" />.
