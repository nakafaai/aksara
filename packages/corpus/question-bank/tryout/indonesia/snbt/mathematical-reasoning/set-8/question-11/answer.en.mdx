Because the sphere remains fully submerged, it displaces <InlineMath math="\frac{4}{3}\pi(2)^3=\frac{32}{3}\pi\text{ cm}^3" />. Since the cylinder's base area is <InlineMath math="16\pi\text{ cm}^2" />, the sphere raises the level by <InlineMath math="\frac{2}{3}\text{ cm}" />.

Removing <InlineMath math="8\pi\text{ cm}^3" /> lowers it by <InlineMath math="\frac{8\pi}{16\pi}=\frac{1}{2}\text{ cm}" />. The final height, in centimetres, is

<BlockMath math="6+\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{37}{6}" />
