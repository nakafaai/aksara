Because the sphere remains **fully submerged**, it displaces <InlineMath math="\frac{4}{3}\pi(2)^3=\frac{32}{3}\pi\text{ cm}^3" />. Since the **cylinder's base area** is <InlineMath math="\pi(4)^2=16\pi\text{ cm}^2" />, the sphere raises the level by <InlineMath math="\frac{(32/3)\pi}{16\pi}=\frac23\text{ cm}" />.

Removing <InlineMath math="8\pi\text{ cm}^3" /> lowers it by <InlineMath math="\frac{8\pi}{16\pi}=\frac{1}{2}\text{ cm}" />. The final height, in centimetres, is

<BlockMath math="h=6+\frac23-\frac12=\frac{36+4-3}{6}=\frac{37}{6}" />
