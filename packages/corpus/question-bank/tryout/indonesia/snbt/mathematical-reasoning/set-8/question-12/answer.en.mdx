What is compared is **the total number of seedlings since the first year**, not the number in a single year.

# Total after n years

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Sukamaju}&:\quad \tfrac{n}{2}\bigl(240+15(n-1)\bigr)=120n+7.5n(n-1)\\
\text{neighbour}&:\quad 195n
\end{aligned}" />

# Setting them equal

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
120n+7.5n(n-1)&=195n\\
7.5(n-1)&=75\\
n&=11
\end{aligned}" />

Check: after 11 years Sukamaju has planted <InlineMath math="1{,}320+825=2{,}145" /> seedlings, and the neighbour <InlineMath math="11\cdot195=2{,}145" />.

Year 6 is when **the number planted in one year** is equal, 195, while the neighbour is still far ahead in total. At the end of years 8 and 10 the neighbour still has more, and at the end of year 12 Sukamaju already has more.

So the totals are first equal at the end of **year 11**.
