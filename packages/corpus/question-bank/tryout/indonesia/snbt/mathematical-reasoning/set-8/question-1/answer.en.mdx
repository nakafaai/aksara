Let <InlineMath math="t" /> be the number of minutes after 08:00. A simultaneous departure satisfies <InlineMath math="t\equiv0\pmod{12}" /> and <InlineMath math="t\equiv6\pmod{18}" />. The first solution is <InlineMath math="t=24" />, and the pattern repeats every <InlineMath math="\operatorname{lcm}(12,18)=36" /> minutes.

Within <InlineMath math="0\le t\le180" />, the times are <InlineMath math="24,60,96,132,168" />. Therefore, there are **5** simultaneous departures.
