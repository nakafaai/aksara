The wire covers **the two sides perpendicular to the wall and the one side parallel to it**. What is left after the two perpendicular sides is the length of the parallel side.

# Length of the side parallel to the wall

<BlockMath math="44-2\cdot14=44-28=16\text{ m}" />

16 m does not exceed the 20 m wall, so this garden **can be built**.

# Area of the garden

<BlockMath math="14\cdot16=224\text{ m}^2" />

The value 112 <InlineMath math="\text{m}^2" /> fences all four sides, leaving only 8 m for the other side. The value 280 <InlineMath math="\text{m}^2" /> uses the whole 20 m wall as a side, although there is not enough wire. The value 308 <InlineMath math="\text{m}^2" /> takes half the wire, 22 m, as the parallel side. The value 420 <InlineMath math="\text{m}^2" /> subtracts only one perpendicular side.

So the area of the garden is **224 <InlineMath math="\text{m}^2" />**.
