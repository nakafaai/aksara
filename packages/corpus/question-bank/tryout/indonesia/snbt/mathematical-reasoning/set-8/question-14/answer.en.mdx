# Students who qualify

Among the dengue recipients, those who also had the influenza vaccine are **the students with both**, <InlineMath math="300+200-420=80" /> students.

# Probability

<BlockMath math="\frac{80}{200}=\frac25" />

The value <InlineMath math="\tfrac16" /> divides 80 by all students. The value <InlineMath math="\tfrac{4}{15}" /> divides 80 by the influenza recipients, **conditioning the wrong way round**. The values <InlineMath math="\tfrac{5}{12}" /> and <InlineMath math="\tfrac58" /> are the chances that a student in the whole school had the dengue or the influenza vaccine.

So the probability is **<InlineMath math="\tfrac25" />**.
