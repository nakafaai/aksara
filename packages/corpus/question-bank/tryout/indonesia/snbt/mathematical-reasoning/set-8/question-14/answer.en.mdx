**Relative to the centre**, the point has vector <InlineMath math="(6-2,2-1)=(4,1)" />. A <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> rotation maps it to <InlineMath math="(u,v)\mapsto(-v,u):\ (4,1)\mapsto(-1,4)" />, giving <InlineMath math="(2-1,1+4)=(1,5)" />. **Reflection across <InlineMath math="y=x" />** then gives <InlineMath math="(5,1)" />.

The distance from <InlineMath math="(5,1)" /> to <InlineMath math="2x-y=0" /> is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}d&=\frac{|2(5)-1|}{\sqrt{2^2+(-1)^2}} \\ &=\frac{9}{\sqrt5} \\ &=\frac{9\sqrt5}{5}\end{aligned}" />
