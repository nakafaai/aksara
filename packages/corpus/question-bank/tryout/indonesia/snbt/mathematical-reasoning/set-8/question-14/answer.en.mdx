All of these statements are about the morning trip, so start from **the table of road sections on a normal morning**, then change only the sections where the riders change. Fuel costs <InlineMath math="\tfrac{13{,}000}{10}=1{,}300" /> rupiah per kilometre.

| Section | Cost | In the car | Share per person |

Twice Citra's share is <InlineMath math="44{,}600" />, and <InlineMath math="45{,}700" /> is more than that. The first statement is true. The gap is small, so **every section has to be counted carefully**, including the one Pak Andi drives alone.

# When Dewi stays home

Without Dewi, only the section from km <InlineMath math="48" /> to <InlineMath math="60" /> changes: it's split three ways, not four. Citra's share there becomes <InlineMath math="\tfrac{15{,}600}{3}=5{,}200" />, a rise of <InlineMath math="1{,}300" /> rupiah.

<BlockMath math="10\%\cdot22{,}300=2{,}230 > 1{,}300" />

The rise is **less than ten percent**, about <InlineMath math="5.8\%" />. The second statement is false.

# When Budi stays home

Without Budi, Pak Andi pays the section from km <InlineMath math="12" /> to <InlineMath math="24" /> alone, the toll road is split two ways, and the last section three ways.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Andi's rise}&=(15{,}600-7{,}800)+(15{,}600-10{,}400)+(12{,}000-8{,}000)+(5{,}200-3{,}900)\\

\text{Citra's rise}&=5{,}200+4{,}000+1{,}300=10{,}500

Pak Andi's rise is bigger, because **he also takes over the section he used to share with Budi**, and Citra never rides that section. The third statement is true.

# When Citra and Dewi both stay home

Without Citra and Dewi, Budi shares every section only with Pak Andi:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}43{,}200" /> is **not more than** <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}45{,}000" />, so the fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is true, the second is false, the third is true, and the fourth is false.
