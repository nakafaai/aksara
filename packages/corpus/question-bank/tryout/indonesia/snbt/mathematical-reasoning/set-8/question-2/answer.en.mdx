The area of the garden is <InlineMath math="L=x(44-2x)" />. The peak of this function may not be allowed, because **the side parallel to the wall is at most 20 m**.

# Limit on x

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
44-2x&\le20\\
2x&\ge24\\
x&\ge12
\end{aligned}" />

# A peak that is not allowed

The function <InlineMath math="L=44x-2x^2" /> peaks at <InlineMath math="x=\tfrac{44}{4}=11" />. Then the parallel side is <InlineMath math="44-22=22" /> m, **longer than the wall**, so that size is impossible.

To the right of the peak the area shrinks as <InlineMath math="x" /> grows. Since <InlineMath math="x" /> is at least 12, the largest area occurs exactly at <InlineMath math="x=12" />.

<BlockMath math="L=12\cdot(44-24)=12\cdot20=240\text{ m}^2" />

So the largest possible garden is **240 <InlineMath math="\text{m}^2" />**, with the parallel side using the whole wall.
