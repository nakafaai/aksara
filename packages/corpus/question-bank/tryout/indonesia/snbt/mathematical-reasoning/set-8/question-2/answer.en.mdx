Let <InlineMath math="C(n)" /> be the cost of <InlineMath math="n" /> tickets, in thousand rupiah. The number of complete groups of five is <InlineMath math="\lfloor n/5\rfloor" />, so

<BlockMath math="C(n)=12n+18-10\left\lfloor\frac n5\right\rfloor" />

For the two ticket counts near the budget limit:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="C(13)=156+18-20=154" />
  <BlockMath math="C(14)=168+18-20=166" />
</MathContainer>

Each additional ticket raises the cost by <InlineMath math="12" /> thousand rupiah, or by <InlineMath math="12-10=2" /> thousand rupiah when it completes another group of five. The cost always increases, so every count above <InlineMath math="14" /> also exceeds the budget. The maximum is therefore <InlineMath math="13" /> tickets.
