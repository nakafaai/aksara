You need to track the gas left in that cylinder day by day, because **the daily use changes partway through**. Here's the plan: work out the daily use before and after <InlineMath math="12" /> July, subtract it from the gas left each day, then find the first morning when the gas left isn't enough for that day.

# Daily use before and after the change

The daily use is the gas of the big stove plus the gas of the small stove. From <InlineMath math="12" /> July, **the big stove runs five hours in total**, not five hours more. The small stove stays at seven hours.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{up to 11 July: } d&=0.3\cdot3+0.1\cdot7=0.9+0.7=1.6\text{ kg}\\
\text{from 12 July: } d&=0.3\cdot5+0.1\cdot7=1.5+0.7=2.2\text{ kg}
\end{aligned}" />

# Tracking the gas left

Every morning, Bu Sari compares the gas left with that day's use. As long as there's enough, the cylinder stays, and the gas left drops by that day's use.

On the morning of <InlineMath math="15" /> July, the <InlineMath math="0.6\text{ kg}" /> left is less than that day's use of <InlineMath math="2.2\text{ kg}" />. By the rule, **the cylinder is swapped on the morning of <InlineMath math="15" /> July**, and the <InlineMath math="0.6\text{ kg}" /> left is lost.

Check it with the totals. The cylinder served <InlineMath math="3" /> days at the old use and <InlineMath math="3" /> days at the new use:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3\cdot1.6+3\cdot2.2&=4.8+6.6=11.4\text{ kg}\\
12-11.4&=0.6\text{ kg}
\end{aligned}" />

The <InlineMath math="0.6\text{ kg}" /> left really isn't enough for a fourth day at the new use.

# The wrong options

Each other option comes from one wrong step.

- <InlineMath math="14" /> July with <InlineMath math="0.6\text{ kg}" /> names the last day the cylinder is used. But **the last day of use isn't the day of the swap**: the cylinder is swapped the next morning.
- <InlineMath math="14" /> July with <InlineMath math="1\text{ kg}" /> uses <InlineMath math="2.2\text{ kg}" /> a day from <InlineMath math="9" /> July, so <InlineMath math="12-5\cdot2.2=1" />.
- <InlineMath math="15" /> July with <InlineMath math="1.2\text{ kg}" /> starts the change on <InlineMath math="13" /> July, so the rest is <InlineMath math="12-4\cdot1.6-2\cdot2.2=1.2" />.
- <InlineMath math="16" /> July with <InlineMath math="0.8\text{ kg}" /> ignores the change, so the cylinder serves <InlineMath math="7" /> days and <InlineMath math="12-7\cdot1.6=0.8" />.

So the cylinder installed on <InlineMath math="9" /> July is swapped on <InlineMath math="15" /> July, and the <InlineMath math="0.6\text{ kg}" /> of gas left in it is lost.
