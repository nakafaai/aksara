The cost of <InlineMath math="n" /> tickets is

<BlockMath math="C(n)=12n+18-10\left\lfloor\frac n5\right\rfloor" />

For <InlineMath math="n=13" />, <InlineMath math="C(13)=156+18-20=154" />. For <InlineMath math="n=14" />, the cost is <InlineMath math="168+18-20=166" />, which exceeds the budget. The maximum is **13 tickets**.
