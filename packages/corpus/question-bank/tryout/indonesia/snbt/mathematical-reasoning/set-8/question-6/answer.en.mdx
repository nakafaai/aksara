There are <InlineMath math="\binom92-\binom52=26" /> pairs containing at least one red ball. The number of all-red pairs is <InlineMath math="\binom42=6" />, so the conditional probability is <InlineMath math="\frac6{26}=\frac3{13}" />.

The expected payout is <InlineMath math="39\cdot\frac3{13}=9" /> thousand rupiah. Thus, the fair entry fee is **9 thousand rupiah**.
