A sack cannot be split, so what matters is **the number of whole sacks in one trip**, not the total weight of all sacks.

# Sacks per trip

Pak Wahyu always rides along, so the load left for sacks is <InlineMath math="700-70=630" /> kg.

<BlockMath math="\frac{630}{60}=10.5" />

One trip carries **at most 10 sacks**, because 11 sacks weigh 660 kg.

# Number of trips

Four trips carry 40 sacks, and the remaining 2 sacks need one more trip.

<BlockMath math="10+10+10+10+2=42" />

The value 4 trips divides the total weight <InlineMath math="42\cdot60=2{,}520" /> kg by 630 kg as if sacks could be split, or forgets Pak Wahyu's weight. The value 3 trips divides the total weight by 700 kg and rounds down. The values 6 and 7 trips are enough, but not the fewest.

So the lift must go up at least **5 times**.
