Each statement needs its own calculation. The first two are about averages, and for those **each price has to be weighted by its number of tickets**. The last two are about one purchase, so the order is always: price by ticket number, the discount if there are exactly four tickets, then the service fee.

# The average of all tickets

The total price of all tickets is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
150\cdot120{,}000+300\cdot150{,}000+550\cdot200{,}000&=18{,}000{,}000+45{,}000{,}000+110{,}000{,}000\\
&=173{,}000{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

The average is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{173{,}000{,}000}{1{,}000}=173{,}000" /> rupiah, which is more than <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}170{,}000" />. The first statement is true. Watch out for the plain average of the three prices, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{120{,}000+150{,}000+200{,}000}{3}\approx156{,}667" />. That value is wrong because **the normal tier holds the most tickets**, so its price must count for more.

# The average of the early tickets

For the first <InlineMath math="450" /> tickets, the total price is <InlineMath math="18{,}000{,}000+45{,}000{,}000=63{,}000{,}000" /> rupiah, so:

<BlockMath math="\frac{63{,}000{,}000}{450}=140{,}000" />

The average is **exactly equal to** <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}140{,}000" />, not less than it. The second statement is false.

# The cheapest four ticket purchase

The cheapest purchase of four tickets holds four early bird tickets, for example numbers <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="4" />. It has exactly four tickets, so **the ten percent discount applies**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
4\cdot120{,}000&=480{,}000\\
90\%\cdot480{,}000+5{,}000&=432{,}000+5{,}000=437{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}437{,}000" /> is less than <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}450{,}000" />, so such a purchase really exists. The third statement is true.

# A purchase across a tier limit

Tickets <InlineMath math="449" /> and <InlineMath math="450" /> are still presale, while <InlineMath math="451" /> and <InlineMath math="452" /> are already normal. **Each ticket is priced by its own number**, and the discount applies because the purchase holds exactly four tickets:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2\cdot150{,}000+2\cdot200{,}000&=700{,}000\\
90\%\cdot700{,}000+5{,}000&=630{,}000+5{,}000=635{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}635{,}000" /> is more than <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}630{,}000" />, so the fourth statement is true. If you forget the service fee, you get exactly <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}630{,}000" />, and the statement looks false.

So the first statement is true, the second is false, the third is true, and the fourth is true.
