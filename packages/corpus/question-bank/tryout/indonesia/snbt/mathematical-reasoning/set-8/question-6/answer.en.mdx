There are <InlineMath math="\binom92-\binom52=\frac{9\cdot8}{2}-\frac{5\cdot4}{2}=36-10=26" /> pairs containing **at least one red ball**. The number of all-red pairs is <InlineMath math="\binom42=\frac{4\cdot3}{2}=6" />, so the conditional probability is <InlineMath math="\frac6{26}=\frac3{13}" />.

The **expected payout** is <InlineMath math="39\cdot\frac3{13}=9" /> thousand rupiah. Thus, the fair entry fee given this information is <InlineMath math="9" /> thousand rupiah.
