Let the tower height be <InlineMath math="h" />. Since the angle at P is <InlineMath math="45^circ" />, P's horizontal distance to the tower is also <InlineMath math="h" />. From Q,

<BlockMath math="\tan30^circ=\frac{h}{h+20}" />

This gives <InlineMath math="h=10(\sqrt3+1)" />. The sight line from P is the hypotenuse of an isosceles right triangle, so its length is

<BlockMath math="h\sqrt2=10(\sqrt6+\sqrt2)" />
