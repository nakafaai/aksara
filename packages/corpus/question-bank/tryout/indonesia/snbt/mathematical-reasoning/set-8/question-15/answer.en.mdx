Let the tower height in metres be <InlineMath math="h" />. Since the angle at P is <InlineMath math="45^\circ" />, P's horizontal distance to the tower is also <InlineMath math="h" />. From Q,

<BlockMath math="\tan 30^\circ=\frac{h}{h+20}" />

Using <InlineMath math="\tan30^\circ=1/\sqrt3" /> gives

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}h+20&=h\sqrt3\\h(\sqrt3-1)&=20\\h&=\frac{20}{\sqrt3-1}\\&=10(\sqrt3+1)\end{aligned}" />

The sight line from P is the hypotenuse of an isosceles right triangle, so its length is

<BlockMath math="h\sqrt{2}=10(\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2})" />
