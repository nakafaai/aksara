**Rationalise each term**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt n}&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt n}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n}{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n}\\&=\frac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n}{(n+1)-n}\\&=\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n\end{aligned}" />

The **sum telescopes**. Write the requested sum as <InlineMath math="S" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S&=(\sqrt2-1)+(\sqrt3-\sqrt2) \\ &\quad+\cdots+(\sqrt{25}-\sqrt{24}) \\ &=5-1 \\ &=4\end{aligned}" />
