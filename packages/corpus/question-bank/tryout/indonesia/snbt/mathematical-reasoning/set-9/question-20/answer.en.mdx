One section is at most 6 m long horizontally. First find **the largest rise one section can still manage**, then compare it with 60 cm.

# Largest rise for one section

<BlockMath math="\frac{600}{12}=50\text{ cm}" />

# Raising the yard

If the yard is raised by <InlineMath math="h" /> cm, the ramp must rise <InlineMath math="60-h" /> cm.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
60-h&\le50\\
h&\ge10
\end{aligned}" />

So the yard **must be raised by at least 10 cm**.

The value 50 cm is the largest rise of one section, not the raise of the yard. The value 60 cm raises the yard to the door's level, so no ramp is needed at all. The values 20 cm and 30 cm also make one section enough, but they are not the smallest.

So the yard must be raised by at least **10 cm**.
