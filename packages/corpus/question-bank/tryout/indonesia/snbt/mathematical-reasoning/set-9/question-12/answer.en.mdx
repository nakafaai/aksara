In one 45-minute session at most 4 matches can be played. Since rounds cannot overlap, **count the sessions for each round separately**.

# Sessions per round

| Round | Matches | Sessions |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| First | 8 | 2 |
| Second | 16 | 4 |
| Third | 8 | 2 |
| Quarter-final | 4 | 1 |
| Semi-final | 2 | 1 |
| Final | 1 | 1 |

The semi-finals and the final use only some courts, but **each still needs a full session**.

# Total time

<BlockMath math="11\cdot45=495\text{ min}=8\text{ h }15\text{ min}" />

The value 7 hours 30 minutes spreads 39 matches evenly over 4 courts, as if rounds could overlap. The value 6 hours 45 minutes forgets the first round. The value 4 hours 30 minutes treats each round as one session, and 29 hours 15 minutes uses one court only.

So the shortest time is **8 hours 15 minutes**.
