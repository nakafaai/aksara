Let <InlineMath math="a=f(2)" /> and <InlineMath math="b=f(\frac12)" />. Substituting <InlineMath math="x=2" /> and <InlineMath math="x=\frac12" /> gives

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="a+2b=6" />
  <BlockMath math="2a+b=\frac32" />
</MathContainer>

Multiply the first equation by <InlineMath math="2" />, then subtract the second:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}3b&=12-\frac32 \\ &=\frac{21}{2} \\ b&=\frac72\end{aligned}" />

Substituting into the first equation gives <InlineMath math="a=6-2(7/2)=-1" />. Therefore <InlineMath math="f(2)=-1" />.
