The combined mean <InlineMath math="\mu" /> is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\mu&=\frac{30\cdot70+20\cdot80}{50} \\ &=74\end{aligned}" />

The combined variance includes both variation within each group and the difference between each group mean and <InlineMath math="\mu" />. Let <InlineMath math="S_A" /> and <InlineMath math="S_B" /> be each group's sum of squared deviations from the combined mean:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_A&=30\bigl(16+(70-74)^2\bigr) \\ &=960\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_B&=20\bigl(9+(80-74)^2\bigr) \\ &=900\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

The combined population variance is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\sigma^2&=\frac{S_A+S_B}{50} \\ &=\frac{1860}{50} \\ &=37.2\end{aligned}" />
