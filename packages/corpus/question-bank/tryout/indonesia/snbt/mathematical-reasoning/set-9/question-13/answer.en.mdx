Let <InlineMath math="c" /> be the current speed in kilometres per hour. The boat can return upstream, so <InlineMath math="0<c<12" />. Excluding the one-hour stop, the boat travels for <InlineMath math="8" /> hours. Therefore,

<BlockMath math="\frac{45}{12+c}+\frac{45}{12-c}=8" />

Combining the fractions gives <InlineMath math="1080=8(144-c^2)" />, so <InlineMath math="c^2=9" />. Since <InlineMath math="c>0" />, <InlineMath math="c=3\text{ km/h}" />.
