A percentage increase is always measured against **the starting value**, the September rainfall.

# Increase and its percentage

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
140-70&=70\text{ mm}\\
\tfrac{70}{70}\cdot100\%&=100\%
\end{aligned}" />

October's rainfall is exactly twice September's. **Doubling means a rise of 100%**, not 200%.

So the rainfall rose by **100%**.
