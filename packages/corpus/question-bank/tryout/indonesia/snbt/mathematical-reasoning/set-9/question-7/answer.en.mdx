The six values total <InlineMath math="6\cdot18=108" />. The five known values sum to <InlineMath math="10+14+17+23+26=90" />, so <InlineMath math="x=108-90=18" />. Removing <InlineMath math="10" /> and <InlineMath math="26" /> leaves <InlineMath math="14,17,18,23" />, whose mean is <InlineMath math="(14+17+18+23)/4=72/4=18" />.

The **population variance** is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\sigma^2&=\frac{(14-18)^2+(17-18)^2+(18-18)^2+(23-18)^2}{4}\\&=\frac{16+1+0+25}{4}=\frac{42}{4}=\frac{21}{2}\end{aligned}" />

Because this is population variance, divide by the full remaining count, four.
