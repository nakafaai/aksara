Compare the pumps and the drain through **their rates**: A <InlineMath math="\tfrac16" />, B <InlineMath math="\tfrac19" /> and the drain <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1}{18}" /> of the pool per hour.

# Comparing rates

<InlineMath math="\tfrac16:\tfrac19=\tfrac96=1.5" />, so pump A is 1.5 times as fast. The first statement is true.

<InlineMath math="\tfrac{1}{18}" /> is half of <InlineMath math="\tfrac19" />, so **the drain's rate is half of pump B's rate**. The second statement is true.

# Tempting but wrong ideas

A pump twice as fast as A fills <InlineMath math="\tfrac13" /> of the pool per hour. With B the rate is <InlineMath math="\tfrac13+\tfrac19=\tfrac49" />, so the time is 2.25 hours. Half of 3.6 hours is 1.8 hours, **so the time is not halved**, because pump B is not sped up. The third statement is false.

Half the mean of the two times is <InlineMath math="\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac{6+9}{2}=3.75" /> hours, while the real time is 3.6 hours. The fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **false**.
