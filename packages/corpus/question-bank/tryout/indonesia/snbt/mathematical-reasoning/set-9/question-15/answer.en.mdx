Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the distance from the pole's base to the anchor of the <InlineMath math="25" />-metre cable. The other distance is <InlineMath math="17-x" />. If the pole height is <InlineMath math="h" />, then

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="x^2+h^2=25^2" />
  <BlockMath math="(17-x)^2+h^2=26^2" />
</MathContainer>

Subtracting gives <InlineMath math="289-34x=51" />, so <InlineMath math="x=7" />. Thus <InlineMath math="h^2=625-49=576" />, and <InlineMath math="h=24" /> metres.
