Each statement needs **a different way of handling the data**: a sum, a ratio, consecutive differences, and a count of values meeting a condition.

# Sums and ratio

The first six months add up to <InlineMath math="310+280+250+190+120+80=1{,}230" /> mm, the last six to <InlineMath math="50+40+70+140+230+340=870" /> mm. **They are not equal**, so the first statement is false.

December against August is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{340}{40}=8.5" />, more than 8. The second statement is true.

# Consecutive drops

| From to | Drop |
| :--- | :---: |
| March to April | 60 mm |
| April to May | 70 mm |
| May to June | 40 mm |

The other drops from January to August are at most 30 mm. **The largest drop is from April to May**, so the third statement is false.

# Wet and dry months

The wet months are January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December, 8 months. The dry months are only July and August, 2 months. Since 8 is more than <InlineMath math="2\cdot2=4" />, the fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
