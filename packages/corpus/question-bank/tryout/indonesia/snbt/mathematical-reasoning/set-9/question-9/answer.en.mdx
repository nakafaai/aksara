# Money going in each year

Half of the <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000{,}000" /> deposit is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000{,}000" />. Each start of year, <InlineMath math="10+4=14" /> million rupiah goes in.

# How each deposit grows

The balance is multiplied by <InlineMath math="1.1" /> at the end of every year. The deposit made at the start of year <InlineMath math="1" /> grows at the end of year <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, and so on up to <InlineMath math="5" />, so it grows <InlineMath math="5" /> times and its factor is <InlineMath math="1.611" />. **Earlier deposits grow for longer**: the year <InlineMath math="2" /> deposit grows <InlineMath math="4" /> times, and the year <InlineMath math="5" /> deposit grows only <InlineMath math="1" /> time.
