The quickest way is to count **the players knocked out**. Every match knocks out exactly one player, and every player except the champion is knocked out exactly once.

# Counting the players knocked out

Of 40 players only the champion never loses, so <InlineMath math="40-1=39" /> players are knocked out. **The number of matches equals the number of players knocked out**.

# Check round by round

The first round cuts 40 players to 32, so it has 8 matches. After that come <InlineMath math="16+8+4+2+1=31" /> matches, and <InlineMath math="8+31=39" />. **Players with a bye add no matches**.

So there are **39 matches** in total.
