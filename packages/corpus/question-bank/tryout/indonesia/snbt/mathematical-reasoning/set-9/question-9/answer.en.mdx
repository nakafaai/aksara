In the shoelace formula, let <InlineMath math="S_1" /> be the sum of the forward coordinate products and <InlineMath math="S_2" /> the sum of the reverse products in vertex order.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_1&=1\cdot2+7\cdot7+6\cdot9 \\ &\quad+2\cdot5+(-1)\cdot1 \\ &=114\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_2&=1\cdot7+2\cdot6+7\cdot2 \\ &\quad+9\cdot(-1)+5\cdot1 \\ &=29\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Twice the oriented area is the difference of these sums:

<BlockMath math="S_1-S_2=114-29=85" />

Therefore, the area is <InlineMath math="85/2=42.5" /> square units.
