Set <InlineMath math="A=(0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="B=(12,0)" />, <InlineMath math="C=(12,12)" />, and <InlineMath math="D=(0,12)" />. Then <InlineMath math="E=(12,3)" /> and <InlineMath math="F=(8,12)" />. The slopes are:

<BlockMath math="m_{AE}=\frac{3-0}{12-0}=\frac14,\qquad m_{BF}=\frac{12-0}{8-12}=-3" />

The lines are <InlineMath math="AE:y=x/4" /> and <InlineMath math="BF:y=-3x+36" />. The **intersection satisfies** <InlineMath math="x/4=-3x+36" />, so <InlineMath math="13x=144" />. Hence

<BlockMath math="P=\left(\frac{144}{13},\frac{36}{13}\right)" />

Using **base <InlineMath math="AB=12" /> and height** <InlineMath math="36/13" />, the area is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}[ABP]&=\frac12\cdot12\cdot\frac{36}{13} \\ &=\frac{216}{13}\end{aligned}" />
