The total paid is **the cash price plus the total interest**. So a cap on the total paid gives a cap on the total interest.

# Cap on total interest

<BlockMath math="4{,}118{,}400-3{,}600{,}000=518{,}400" />

# Cap on the monthly rate

With a flat rate <InlineMath math="r" /> per month, total interest is <InlineMath math="12\cdot r\cdot2{,}880{,}000=34{,}560{,}000\,r" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
34{,}560{,}000\,r&\le518{,}400\\
r&\le0.015=1.5\%
\end{aligned}" />

At <InlineMath math="1.5\%" /> the total paid is **exactly Rp4,118,400**, so it still qualifies.

The value <InlineMath math="1.2\%" /> charges interest on the cash price of Rp3,600,000 instead of the principal. The values <InlineMath math="0.5\%" />, <InlineMath math="1\%" /> and <InlineMath math="1.25\%" /> qualify, but they are not the highest.

So the highest flat rate is **<InlineMath math="1.5\%" /> per month**.
