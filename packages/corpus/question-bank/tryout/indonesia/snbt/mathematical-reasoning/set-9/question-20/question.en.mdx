A directed transport network runs from S through exactly one node in each column to T. Travel times in minutes are:

| Segment | Time | Segment | Time |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| S-A | 4 | S-B | 7 |
| A-C | 4 | A-D | 7 |
| B-C | 2 | B-D | 5 |
| C-T | 4 | D-T | 6 |

An express pass may be used on exactly one travelled segment and halves that segment's travel time. What is the least possible total travel time?
