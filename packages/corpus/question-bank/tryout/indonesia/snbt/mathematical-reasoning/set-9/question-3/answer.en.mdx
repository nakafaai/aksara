The key to flat interest is that **interest is always charged on the original principal**, however much has been repaid.

# Down payment and the meaning of flat interest

The down payment is <InlineMath math="20\%\cdot3{,}600{,}000=720{,}000" />, so the first statement is true.

Each month <InlineMath math="2\%" /> of the original principal is charged, so over 12 months the total is <InlineMath math="12\cdot2\%=24\%" /> of the principal, **not 2%**. The second statement is false.

Since the base is always the original principal, **the interest in month 12 equals the interest in month 1**, Rp57,600. The third statement is true.

# Effect of the down payment

A bigger down payment makes the principal smaller. Total interest is <InlineMath math="24\%" /> of the principal, so **total interest goes down**, not up. For example, a <InlineMath math="30\%" /> down payment gives a principal of Rp2,520,000 and total interest of Rp604,800, less than Rp691,200. The fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **false**.
