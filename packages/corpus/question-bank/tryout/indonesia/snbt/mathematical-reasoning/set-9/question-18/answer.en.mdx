Write <InlineMath math="n=4k" />. From <InlineMath math="4k\equiv2\pmod5" />, we get <InlineMath math="k\equiv3\pmod5" />. From <InlineMath math="4k\equiv3\pmod7" />, we get <InlineMath math="k\equiv6\pmod7" />. The least positive value satisfying both is <InlineMath math="k=13" />, so <InlineMath math="n=4(13)=52" />.
