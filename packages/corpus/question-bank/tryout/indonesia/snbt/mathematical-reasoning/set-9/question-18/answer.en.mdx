The sloped part needs a horizontal length of at least <InlineMath math="60\cdot12=720" /> cm, or 7.2 m. Since one section is at most 6 m, first find **how many sections and landings are needed**.

# Sections and landings

7.2 m is more than 6 m, so one section is not enough. Two sections are enough, say 3.6 m and 3.6 m, and **between two sections there is one landing**.

# Total horizontal length

<BlockMath math="7.2+1.5=8.7\text{ m}" />

The value 7.2 m forgets the landing. The value 10.2 m uses two landings, although two sections need only one. The value 12 m uses two 6 m sections with no landing, and 13.5 m uses two 6 m sections with a landing, both longer than needed.

So the shortest total horizontal length of the ramp is **8.7 m**.
