Induction gives <InlineMath math="x_n=\frac{n+1}{n}" />. The formula is true for <InlineMath math="n=1" /> because <InlineMath math="x_1=2=\frac{1+1}{1}" />. If it holds for <InlineMath math="n" />, then

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}x_{n+1}&=2-\frac1{x_n} \\ &=2-\frac n{n+1} \\ &=\frac{n+2}{n+1}\end{aligned}" />

The product therefore telescopes:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&\prod_{n=1}^{2026}x_n \\ &\quad=\frac21\cdot\frac32\cdots\frac{2027}{2026} \\ &\quad=2027\end{aligned}" />
