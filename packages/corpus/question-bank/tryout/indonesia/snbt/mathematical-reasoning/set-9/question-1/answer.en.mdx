Induction gives <InlineMath math="x_n=\frac{n+1}{n}" />. The formula is true for <InlineMath math="n=1" />. If it holds for <InlineMath math="n" />, then

<BlockMath math="x_{n+1}=2-\frac{1}{x_n}=2-\frac{n}{n+1}=\frac{n+2}{n+1}" />

The product therefore telescopes:

<BlockMath math="\prod_{n=1}^{2026}x_n=\frac21\cdot\frac32\cdots\frac{2027}{2026}=2027" />
