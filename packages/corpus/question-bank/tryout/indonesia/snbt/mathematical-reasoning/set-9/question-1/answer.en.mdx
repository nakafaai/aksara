The instalment is based on **the principal**, the cash price minus the down payment, not on the cash price.

# Down payment and principal

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{down payment}&=20\%\cdot3{,}600{,}000=720{,}000\\
\text{principal}&=3{,}600{,}000-720{,}000=2{,}880{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

# Monthly instalment

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\tfrac{1}{12}\cdot2{,}880{,}000&=240{,}000\\
2\%\cdot2{,}880{,}000&=57{,}600\\
240{,}000+57{,}600&=297{,}600
\end{aligned}" />

In thousands of rupiah, **the instalment is 297.6**.

So the monthly instalment is **297.6 thousand rupiah**, which is Rp297,600.
