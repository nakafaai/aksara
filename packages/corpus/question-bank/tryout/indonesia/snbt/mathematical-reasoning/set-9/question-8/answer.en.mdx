The mixture is **homogeneous**, so the removed liquid has the same acid-to-water ratio as the tank. The acid fraction is <InlineMath math="3/(3+7)=3/10" />, and the water fraction is <InlineMath math="7/10" />.

| Stage | Acid in liters | Water in liters |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Initially | <InlineMath math="80\cdot3/10=24" /> | <InlineMath math="80\cdot7/10=56" /> |
| Removed | <InlineMath math="20\cdot3/10=6" /> | <InlineMath math="20\cdot7/10=14" /> |
| Remaining | <InlineMath math="24-6=18" /> | <InlineMath math="56-14=42" /> |
| After adding pure acid | <InlineMath math="18+20=38" /> | <InlineMath math="42" /> |

Only the acid amount increases in the final step. Thus:

<BlockMath math="\text{acid}:\text{water}=38:42=19:21" />

The final volumes total <InlineMath math="38+42=80" /> liters, matching the initial volume after equal removal and addition.
