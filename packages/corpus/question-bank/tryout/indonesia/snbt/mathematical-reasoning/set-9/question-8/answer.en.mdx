The bus leaves at 07:00 and 07:50 and arrives at 07:25 and 08:15. Its next departure, at 08:40, is too late. Each minibus leaves at 07:00 and 07:40, while the 08:20 departure only arrives at 08:40. So **there are at most two bus trips and four minibus trips**.
