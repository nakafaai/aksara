First work out **the part of the pool pump A can fill alone** by 11.00. Pump B must fill the rest.

# Part filled by pump A

From 07.00 to 11.00 pump A **runs for 4 hours** and fills <InlineMath math="4\cdot\tfrac16=\tfrac23" /> of the pool. The part still to fill is

<BlockMath math="1-\frac14-\frac23=\frac{12}{12}-\frac{3}{12}-\frac{8}{12}=\frac{1}{12}" />

# How long pump B runs

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{12}:\frac19=\frac{9}{12}=\frac34\text{ hour}=45\text{ minutes}" />

Pump B must run at least 45 minutes before 11.00, so **it must be switched on by 10.15 at the latest**.

The answer 08.00 forgets that the pool is already a quarter full, so pump B would fill <InlineMath math="\tfrac13" /> of the pool in 3 hours. The times 09.00, 09.45 and 10.00 also fill the pool in time, but they are not the latest.

So pump B must be switched on by **10.15** at the latest.
