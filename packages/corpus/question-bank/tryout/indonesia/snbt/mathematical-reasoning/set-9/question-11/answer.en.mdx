Count **the rounds a player goes through**. Every round played means one match.

# Matches played by a champion

From the second round to the final there are 5 rounds: 32 to 16, 16 to 8, 8 to 4, 4 to 2, and the final. A champion with a bye plays only **5 matches**, so the first statement is false.

A champion who plays from the first round plays <InlineMath math="1+5=6" /> matches, and no champion plays more. The second statement is true.

# Matches per round

The quarter-finals have 8 players, so there are <InlineMath math="\tfrac82=4" /> matches. The third statement is true.

The second round has 16 matches, while all later rounds have <InlineMath math="8+4+2+1=15" />. **They differ by one**, so the fourth statement is false.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **false**.
