Measure water depth <InlineMath math="h" /> and surface radius <InlineMath math="r" /> in centimetres. **Similarity** gives <InlineMath math="r/h=4/12=1/3" />, so <InlineMath math="r=h/3" />. The **water volume** in cubic centimetres is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}V(h)&=\frac13\pi\left(\frac h3\right)^2h \\ &=\frac{\pi h^3}{27}\end{aligned}" />

Initially, <InlineMath math="V(6)=\pi(6)^3/27=8\pi" />. Adding <InlineMath math="19\pi" /> raises the volume to <InlineMath math="27\pi" />. Thus <InlineMath math="\pi h^3/27=27\pi\Rightarrow h^3=729" />, so <InlineMath math="h=9\text{ cm}" />.
