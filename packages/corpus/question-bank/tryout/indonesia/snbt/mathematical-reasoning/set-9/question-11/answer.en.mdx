At water depth <InlineMath math="h" />, similarity gives a surface radius of <InlineMath math="h/3" />. Hence the water volume is

<BlockMath math="V(h)=\frac13\pi\left(\frac h3\right)^2h=\frac{\pi h^3}{27}" />

Initially, <InlineMath math="V(6)=8\pi" />. Adding <InlineMath math="19\pi" /> raises the volume to <InlineMath math="27\pi" />. Thus <InlineMath math="\pi h^3/27=27\pi" />, so <InlineMath math="h=9" /> cm.
