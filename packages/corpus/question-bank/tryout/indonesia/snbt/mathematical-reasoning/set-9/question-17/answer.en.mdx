Let <InlineMath math="F" /> be system failure and <InlineMath math="A_F" /> be failure of component A. The system fails when at most one component works. The probability that at least two work is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P(F^c)&=0.8(0.7)(0.6) \\ &\quad+0.8(0.7)(0.4) \\ &\quad+0.8(0.3)(0.6) \\ &\quad+0.2(0.7)(0.6) \\ &=0.788\end{aligned}" />

Thus <InlineMath math="P(F)=1-0.788=0.212" />. Given that A fails, the system fails precisely when B and C do not both work, so

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&P(A_F\cap F) \\ &=0.2(1-0.7\cdot0.6) \\ &=0.116\end{aligned}" />

Therefore,

<BlockMath math="P(A_F\mid F)=\frac{0.116}{0.212}=\frac{29}{53}" />
