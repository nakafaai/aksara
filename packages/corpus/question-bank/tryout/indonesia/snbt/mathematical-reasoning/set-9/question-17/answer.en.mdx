A slope of 1 : 12 means **the horizontal length is at least 12 times the rise**. The ramp has to rise 60 cm in total.

# Horizontal length of the sloped part

<BlockMath math="60\cdot12=720\text{ cm}=7.2\text{ m}" />

A flat landing adds no height, so **the whole 60 cm rise must come from the sloped sections**, however many there are.

So the shortest total horizontal length of the sloped sections is **7.2 m**.
