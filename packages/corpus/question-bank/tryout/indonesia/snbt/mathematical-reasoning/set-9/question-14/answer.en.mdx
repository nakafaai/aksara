For <InlineMath math="P=(x,3)" />, the squared distance to <InlineMath math="A" /> is <InlineMath math="(x-2)^2+(3+1)^2" />. The **squared distance to the line** <InlineMath math="x=-4" /> is <InlineMath math="(x+4)^2" />. Equating them gives

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}(x-2)^2+16&=(x+4)^2\\x^2-4x+20&=x^2+8x+16\\12x&=4\\x&=\frac13\end{aligned}" />

Simplification yields <InlineMath math="12x=4" />, so <InlineMath math="x=\frac13" />.
