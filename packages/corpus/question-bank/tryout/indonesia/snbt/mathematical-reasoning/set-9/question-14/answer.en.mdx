The median can only be read after **sorting the data**. There are 12 values, so the median is the mean of the 6th and 7th values.

# Sorted data

# Median

<BlockMath math="\frac{140+190}{2}=165\text{ mm}" />

The value 65 mm takes the 6th and 7th values in calendar order, June and July, **without sorting**. The values 140 mm and 190 mm are just one of the two middle values. The value 175 mm is the mean, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{2{,}100}{12}" />, not the median.

So the median monthly rainfall is **165 mm**.
