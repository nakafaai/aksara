There are <InlineMath math="4" /> seat pairs with exactly one seat between A and B, <InlineMath math="2" /> orders for A and B, and <InlineMath math="4!" /> arrangements of the others, giving <InlineMath math="4\cdot2\cdot4!=192" /> arrangements. Across the four possible A-B seat pairs, the remaining seats contain <InlineMath math="2+1+1+2=6" /> adjacent seat pairs for C and D. Each such pair permits <InlineMath math="2\cdot2\cdot2=8" /> orders of A-B, C-D, and E-F. Thus <InlineMath math="6\cdot8=48" /> arrangements are invalid. The required count is <InlineMath math="192-48=144" />.
