The drain **removes** water, so its rate is subtracted from the two pumps' combined rate.

# Net rate

<BlockMath math="\frac16+\frac19-\frac{1}{18}=\frac{3}{18}+\frac{2}{18}-\frac{1}{18}=\frac{4}{18}=\frac29" />

# Filling time

<BlockMath math="1:\frac29=\frac92=4.5\text{ hours}" />

The value 3.6 hours ignores the drain. The value 6 hours uses pump A only. The value 7.5 hours is the mean of the two pumps' times, and 11 hours is the mean of all three times, although **times cannot be averaged like rates**.

So the pool is full in **4.5 hours**.
