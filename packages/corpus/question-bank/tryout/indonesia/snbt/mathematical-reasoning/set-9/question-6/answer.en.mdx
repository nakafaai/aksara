# The first statement about cost per seat

A bus seat costs <InlineMath math="150{,}000\div30=5{,}000" /> and a minibus seat <InlineMath math="65{,}000\div14\approx4{,}643" />. The minibus is cheaper per seat, but **empty seats are still paid for**. Take <InlineMath math="29" /> participants.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\text{one bus} &: 1\times150{,}000=150{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

Two minibus trips hold only <InlineMath math="28" /> people, so minibuses alone need three trips. One bus is cheaper. The first statement is false.

# The third statement about doubling the participants

# The fourth statement about the late bus

If the bus is only ready at 07:15, its timetable shifts.
