A slope is **the rise divided by the horizontal length**. The smaller the quotient, the gentler the ramp.

# Reading slopes

<InlineMath math="\tfrac{1}{12}\approx0.083" />, which is about <InlineMath math="8.3\%" />. The first statement is true.

A 1 : 10 slope is <InlineMath math="10\%" />, more than <InlineMath math="8.3\%" />, so **1 : 10 is steeper**, not gentler. The second statement is false.

# Length of the sloped surface

A 6 m section at 1 : 12 rises <InlineMath math="\tfrac{600}{12}=50" /> cm. By Pythagoras,

<BlockMath math="\sqrt{600^2+50^2}=\sqrt{362{,}500}\approx602\text{ cm}" />

That is about 6.02 m, **less than 6.1 m**. The third statement is false.

# Checking the two-section design

Each 3.6 m section is at most 6 m, and there is one landing between them. At 1 : 12 each section rises <InlineMath math="\tfrac{360}{12}=30" /> cm, so together they rise exactly 60 cm. The fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
