Because P and Q lie halfway up diagonally opposite vertical edges, the possible unfolding directions must be compared. A route using only side faces has length <InlineMath math="4+8=12" />. The three essentially different unfoldings through the top or bottom give

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\sqrt{4^2+(8+2)^2}=\sqrt{116}" />
  <BlockMath math="\sqrt{8^2+(4+2)^2}=10" />
  <BlockMath math="\sqrt{(4+1)^2+(8+1)^2}=\sqrt{106}" />
</MathContainer>

The minimum of <InlineMath math="12,\sqrt{116},10,\sqrt{106}" /> is <InlineMath math="10" />. Comparing all unfoldings is essential; one sketch alone is not sufficient.
