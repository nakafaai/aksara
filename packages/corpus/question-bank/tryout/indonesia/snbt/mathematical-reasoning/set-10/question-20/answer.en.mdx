A width of 4 cm stays within budget, while **5 cm goes over it**.

The value 5 cm appears if the frame area is taken as just <InlineMath math="120x" />, without the four corners <InlineMath math="4x^2" />. The values 1, 2 and 3 cm are within budget, but they are not the largest.
