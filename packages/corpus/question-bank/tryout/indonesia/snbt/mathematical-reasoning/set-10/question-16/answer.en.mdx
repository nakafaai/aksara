Evaporation removes only water, so **the volume of juice stays the same**. Find the final volume that makes that juice 32%.

# Juice that stays

<BlockMath math="20\%\cdot800=160\text{ mL}" />

# Final volume and water evaporated

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
32\%\cdot V&=160\\
V&=500\text{ mL}\\
800-500&=300\text{ mL}
\end{aligned}" />

The value 500 mL is the final volume, not the water evaporated. The value 160 mL is the volume of juice. The value 256 mL is 32% of 800 mL, and 96 mL is the change in concentration, 12%, times 800 mL. **Both use the starting volume**, although 32% must be taken of the final volume.

So **300 mL** of water must evaporate.
