Let <InlineMath math="C" /> be the event that a person has the condition and <InlineMath math="T" /> the event that both tests are positive. For a person with the condition, both tests are positive with probability <InlineMath math="0.9^2=0.81" />. For a person without it, this occurs with probability <InlineMath math="0.05^2=0.0025" />. Including prevalence gives joint probabilities <InlineMath math="0.1(0.81)=0.081" /> and <InlineMath math="0.9(0.0025)=0.00225" />. Therefore,

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P(C\mid T)&=\frac{0.081}{0.081+0.00225} \\ &=\frac{36}{37}\end{aligned}" />
