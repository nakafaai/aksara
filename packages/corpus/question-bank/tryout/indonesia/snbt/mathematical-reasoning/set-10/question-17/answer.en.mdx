The total area is **the area of the outer rectangle**, and the photo's area is <InlineMath math="24\cdot36=864" /> cm². Set up the equation, then solve the quadratic.

# Setting up the equation

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(24+2x)(36+2x)&=2\cdot864\\
864+120x+4x^2&=1{,}728\\
x^2+30x-216&=0
\end{aligned}" />

# Solving the equation

<BlockMath math="(x+36)(x-6)=0" />

A frame cannot have negative width, so **only x = 6 works**. The outer size is <InlineMath math="36\times48" />, and <InlineMath math="36\cdot48=1{,}728=2\cdot864" />.

So the frame must be **6 cm** wide.
