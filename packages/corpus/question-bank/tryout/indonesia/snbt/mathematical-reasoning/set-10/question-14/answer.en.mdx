Intersection points satisfy <InlineMath math="x^2-6x+5=kx+1" />, or

<BlockMath math="x^2-(6+k)x+4=0" />

A tangent has one intersection with the parabola. The quadratic must therefore have a repeated real root, so the **discriminant must be zero**:

<BlockMath math="\Delta=(-(6+k))^2-4(1)(4)=(6+k)^2-16=0" />

Thus <InlineMath math="6+k=\pm4" />, giving <InlineMath math="k=-2" /> or <InlineMath math="k=-10" />. Their product is <InlineMath math="20" />.
