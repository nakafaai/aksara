# Check

375 mL of syrup A gives 225 mL of juice, and 625 mL of syrup B gives 125 mL, **350 mL together**.

The value 375 mL is the amount of syrup A, not B. The value 500 mL mixes equal amounts, giving 40%. With 600 mL of syrup B the mixture is 36%, and with 300 mL it is 48%, neither 35%.

So **625 mL** of syrup B is needed.
