A train leaves at a given time if **the time since 05.00 is divisible by its gap**.

# Line A

06.00 is 60 minutes after 05.00, and 60 is not divisible by 16. The multiples of 16 between 60 and 120 are 64, 80, 96 and 112, so line A leaves at 06.04, 06.20, 06.36 and 06.52, **exactly 4 times**. The first statement is true.

21.00 is 960 minutes after 05.00, and <InlineMath math="\tfrac{960}{16}=60" />, a whole number. The fourth statement is true.

# Lines B and C

07.15 is 135 minutes after 05.00, and <InlineMath math="\tfrac{135}{24}=5.625" />, **not a whole number**. The second statement is false.

12.10 is 430 minutes after 05.00, and 430 is not divisible by 40. The third statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
