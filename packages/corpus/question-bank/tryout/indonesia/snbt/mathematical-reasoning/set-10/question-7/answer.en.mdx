A train leaves at a given time if **the time since 05.00 is divisible by its gap**.

# Line A

06.00 is 60 minutes after 05.00, and 60 is divisible by 12. So line A leaves at 06.00, 06.12, 06.24, 06.36 and 06.48, 5 times before 07.00. The first statement is true.

22.00 is 1,020 minutes after 05.00, and <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1{,}020}{12}=85" />, **a whole number**. The fourth statement is true.

# Lines B and C

07.15 is 135 minutes after 05.00, and <InlineMath math="\tfrac{135}{18}=7.5" />, **not a whole number**. The second statement is false.

12.10 is 430 minutes after 05.00, and 430 is not divisible by 30. The third statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
