# Waiting time

Line A leaves in 2 minutes and line B in 18, so **line A comes first**.

The values 14 and 6 minutes are remainders, the times since the last line A and line B trains left, not waits. The value 18 minutes is the wait for line B, and 8 minutes is just half of line A's gap.

So Rina waits at least **2 minutes**.
