# Waiting time

Line A leaves in 4 minutes and line B in 10, so **line A comes first**.

The value 8 minutes is the remainder, the time since the last line A train left, not the wait. The value 10 minutes is the wait for line B. The values 6 and 12 minutes use half or all of line A's gap without looking at the timetable.
