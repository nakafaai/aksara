Let <InlineMath math="a=2^x" /> and <InlineMath math="b=2^y" />. Then <InlineMath math="a+b=20" /> and <InlineMath math="ab=2^{x+y}=64" />, so <InlineMath math="a" /> and <InlineMath math="b" /> are the roots of

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}t^2-20t+64&=0 \\ (t-4)(t-16)&=0\end{aligned}" />

Thus <InlineMath math="\{2^x,2^y\}=\{4,16\}" /> and <InlineMath math="\{x,y\}=\{2,4\}" />. Therefore, <InlineMath math="x^2+y^2=4+16=20" />.
