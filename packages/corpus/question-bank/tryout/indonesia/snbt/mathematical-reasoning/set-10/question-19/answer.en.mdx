<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

# Monday compared with Wednesday

The words "bigger than Wednesday's gross profit" mean that **Wednesday's profit is the base of the percentage**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{330{,}000-190{,}000}{190{,}000} &= \frac{140}{190}=73.6\ldots\%>70\%
\end{aligned}" />

The third conclusion is true. If you wrongly use Monday's profit as the base, you get <InlineMath math="\tfrac{140}{330}=42.4\ldots\%" /> and the conclusion looks false.
