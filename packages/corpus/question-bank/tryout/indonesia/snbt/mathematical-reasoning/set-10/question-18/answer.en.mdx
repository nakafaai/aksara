Factor <InlineMath math="840=2^3\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7" />. Euler's **totient function** counts the integers in the given range that are relatively prime to <InlineMath math="840" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\varphi(840)&=840\left(1-\frac12\right)\left(1-\frac13\right)\left(1-\frac15\right)\left(1-\frac17\right)\\&=840\cdot\frac12\cdot\frac23\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac67\\&=420\cdot\frac23\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac67\\&=280\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac67\\&=224\cdot\frac67=192\end{aligned}" />

Each qualifying integer <InlineMath math="a" /> **pairs with** <InlineMath math="840-a" />. Both are relatively prime to <InlineMath math="840" />, and they sum to <InlineMath math="840" />. No integer pairs with itself because <InlineMath math="420" /> is not relatively prime to <InlineMath math="840" />. There are <InlineMath math="192/2=96" /> pairs, so the required sum is
