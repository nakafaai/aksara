Factor <InlineMath math="840=2^3\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7" />. The number of relatively prime residues is

<BlockMath math="\varphi(840)=840\left(1-\frac12\right)\left(1-\frac13\right)\left(1-\frac15\right)\left(1-\frac17\right)=192" />

Each relatively prime value <InlineMath math="a" /> pairs with <InlineMath math="840-a" />, and every pair sums to <InlineMath math="840" />. There are <InlineMath math="192/2=96" /> pairs, so the required sum is

<BlockMath math="96\cdot840=80{,}640" />

The sum is **80,640**.
