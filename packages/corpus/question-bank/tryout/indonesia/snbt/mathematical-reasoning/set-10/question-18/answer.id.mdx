<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\varphi(840)&=840\left(1-\frac12\right)\left(1-\frac13\right)\left(1-\frac15\right)\left(1-\frac17\right)\\&=840\cdot\frac12\cdot\frac23\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac67\\&=420\cdot\frac23\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac67\\&=280\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac67\\&=224\cdot\frac67=192\end{aligned}" />
