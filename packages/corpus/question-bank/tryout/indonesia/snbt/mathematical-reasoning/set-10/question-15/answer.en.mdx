From B, the sight line makes an angle of <InlineMath math="30^\circ" /> west of north. Its westward component to A is <InlineMath math="30" /> km. If <InlineMath math="d" /> is the distance from B to the lighthouse, then <InlineMath math="d\sin30^\circ=30" />. Hence <InlineMath math="d=60" /> km.
