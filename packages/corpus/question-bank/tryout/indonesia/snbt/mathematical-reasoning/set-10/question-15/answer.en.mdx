The bearing angle is measured **from north toward west**. At B, the lighthouse direction is therefore <InlineMath math="30^\circ" /> from the northward reference. The horizontal distance <InlineMath math="AB=30\text{ km}" /> is opposite that angle, while the sight distance <InlineMath math="d" /> is the hypotenuse:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\sin30^\circ&=\frac{30}{d}\\\frac12&=\frac{30}{d}\\d&=\frac{30}{1/2}=60\text{ km}\end{aligned}" />

Check the westward component: <InlineMath math="60\sin30^\circ=30\text{ km}" />. In the diagram, <InlineMath math="L" /> marks the lighthouse and <InlineMath math="N" /> the northward reference. Coordinates are measured in kilometers.
