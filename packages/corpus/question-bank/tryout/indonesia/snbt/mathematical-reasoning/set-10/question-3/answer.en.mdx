The first plan exceeds the oven, while the second **needs more flour than there is**, even though the oven is fine.

# Limit on cheese buns

With cheese buns only, the flour is enough for <InlineMath math="\tfrac{7{,}000}{100}=70" /> buns, and 70 buns still fit in the oven. So the third statement is true.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **false**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **true**.
