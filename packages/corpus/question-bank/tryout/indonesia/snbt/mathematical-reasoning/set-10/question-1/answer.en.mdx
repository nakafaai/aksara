There are <InlineMath math="\lfloor1000/6\rfloor=166" /> multiples of <InlineMath math="6" />, <InlineMath math="100" /> multiples of <InlineMath math="10" />, and <InlineMath math="66" /> multiples of <InlineMath math="15" />. The least common multiple of any pair is <InlineMath math="30" />, so every number divisible by at least two of the three is divisible by all three. There are <InlineMath math="\lfloor1000/30\rfloor=33" /> such numbers. The count divisible by exactly one is

<BlockMath math="(166-33)+(100-33)+(66-33)=233" />
