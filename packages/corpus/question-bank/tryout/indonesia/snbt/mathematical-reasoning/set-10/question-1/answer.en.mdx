Say Bu Sari bakes <InlineMath math="x" /> chocolate buns and <InlineMath math="y" /> cheese buns. The largest profit always occurs at **a corner of the region that meets every constraint**.

# Constraints

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
x+y&\le100\\
50x+100y&\le7{,}000\iff x+2y\le140
\end{aligned}" />

**The two boundary lines meet** when <InlineMath math="y=140-100=40" /> and <InlineMath math="x=60" />.

# Profit at each corner

| Corner | Profit |
| :---: | :---: |
| <InlineMath math="(100,0)" /> | Rp150,000 |
| <InlineMath math="(60,40)" /> | Rp170,000 |
| <InlineMath math="(0,70)" /> | Rp140,000 |

**The corner (60, 40) gives the largest profit**, <InlineMath math="60\cdot1{,}500+40\cdot2{,}000=170{,}000" />.

So the largest profit is **170 thousand rupiah**, from 60 chocolate buns and 40 cheese buns.
