# Heavy vehicles that can't share a trip

Check the pairs of heavy vehicles.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

Suppose <InlineMath math="3" /> trips were enough. The two buses would have to be on two different trips, and **a trip with a bus can't carry a truck**. Then all three trucks would have to go on the one remaining trip, but three trucks weigh <InlineMath math="45\text{ tonnes}" />. That's impossible, so at least <InlineMath math="4" /> trips are needed.
