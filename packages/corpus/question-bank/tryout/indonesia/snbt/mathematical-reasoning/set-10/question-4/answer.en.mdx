The volume condition is <InlineMath math="x(30-2x)(22-2x)=936" />. Expanding and moving all terms to the left gives

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&4x^3-104x^2+660x \\ &\quad-936=0\end{aligned}" />

Divide both sides by <InlineMath math="4" />, then factor:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="x^3-26x^2+165x-234=0" />
  <BlockMath math="(x-2)(x^2-24x+117)=0" />
</MathContainer>

The two roots other than <InlineMath math="2" /> are <InlineMath math="12\pm3\sqrt3" />. Both lie outside <InlineMath math="0<x<3" />, so <InlineMath math="x=2" />.

The base area, in square centimetres, is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&(30-4)(22-4) \\ &=26\cdot18 \\ &=468\end{aligned}" />
