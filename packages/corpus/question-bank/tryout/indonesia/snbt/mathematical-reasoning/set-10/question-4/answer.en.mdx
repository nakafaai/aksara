# Checking the constraints

Eighty chocolate buns fit in the oven and use <InlineMath math="80\cdot50=4{,}000" /> g of flour, **within the 7 kg limit**. So the plan works.

The value 60 buns uses the cheese bun profit of Rp2,000, although only chocolate buns are made that day. The value 70 buns gives only Rp105,000, still less than the fixed cost. The values 90 and 100 buns avoid a loss, but they are not the fewest.

So Bu Sari must make and sell at least **80 chocolate buns**.
