The logarithm laws give <InlineMath math="(x-1)(9-x)=8" />, with domain <InlineMath math="1<x<9" />. Expanding gives

<BlockMath math="x^2-10x+17=0" />

The two solutions are <InlineMath math="5\pm2\sqrt2" />, both within the domain. Their sum is <InlineMath math="10" /> and their product is <InlineMath math="17" />, so the sum of their squares is <InlineMath math="10^2-2(17)=66" />.
