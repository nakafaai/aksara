The logarithm laws give <InlineMath math="(x-1)(9-x)=8" />, with domain <InlineMath math="1<x<9" />. Expanding gives

<BlockMath math="x^2-10x+17=0" />

The two solutions are <InlineMath math="5\pm2\sqrt2" />, both within the domain. Let the two roots be <InlineMath math="r_1" /> and <InlineMath math="r_2" />. Their sum is <InlineMath math="10" /> and their product is <InlineMath math="17" />, so

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}r_1^2+r_2^2&=(r_1+r_2)^2-2r_1r_2 \\ &=10^2-2(17) \\ &=66\end{aligned}" />
