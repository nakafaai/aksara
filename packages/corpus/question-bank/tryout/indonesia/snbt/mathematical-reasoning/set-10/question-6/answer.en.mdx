# Joint departures

At 05.00, 08.00, 11.00, 14.00, 17.00 and 20.00. The next joint departure would be at 23.00, **outside the timetable**. So there are 6.

The value 7 also counts 23.00. The value 12 uses only the LCM of 18 and 30, 90 minutes, and the values 17 and 18 use only the LCM of 12 and 30, 60 minutes.

So all three trains leave together **6 times**.
