What you compare is **price divided by the amount of juice**, not price divided by the amount of syrup.

# Juice in one litre of syrup B

<BlockMath math="20\%\cdot1{,}000\text{ mL}=200\text{ mL}" />

# Price per mL of juice

<BlockMath math="\frac{24{,}000}{200}=120\text{ rupiah}" />

For comparison, syrup A gives 600 mL of juice for Rp60,000, which is Rp100 per mL. So **syrup B is dearer** measured by its juice, even though it is cheaper per litre.

So the juice in syrup B costs **Rp120** per mL.
