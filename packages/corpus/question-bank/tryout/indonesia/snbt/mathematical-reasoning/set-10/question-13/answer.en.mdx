Water adds no juice, but it **adds volume to the mixture**. So find the juice first, then divide by the whole volume.

# Juice and mixture volume

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{juice}&=60\%\cdot300=180\text{ mL}\\
\text{mixture volume}&=300+900=1{,}200\text{ mL}
\end{aligned}" />

# Concentration

<BlockMath math="\frac{180}{1{,}200}\cdot100\%=15\%" />

The volume becomes four times as large, so **the concentration becomes a quarter**, from 60% to 15%.

So the mixture is **15%** juice.
