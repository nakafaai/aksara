Pump A fills <InlineMath math="2/10=1/5" /> of the tank in the first <InlineMath math="2" /> hours. With both pumps and the drain operating, the net rate in tanks per hour is

<BlockMath math="\frac1{10}+\frac1{15}-\frac1{30}=\frac2{15}" />

The next <InlineMath math="3" /> hours add <InlineMath math="3(2/15)=2/5" /> of the tank. It is now <InlineMath math="1/5+2/5=3/5" /> full. After B stops, the net rate in tanks per hour becomes

<BlockMath math="\frac1{10}-\frac1{30}=\frac1{15}" />

Filling the remaining <InlineMath math="2/5" /> of the tank takes

<BlockMath math="\frac{2/5}{1/15}=6" />

The total time since A started is <InlineMath math="2+3+6=11" /> hours.
