The distance from the centre <InlineMath math="(0,0)" /> to the line <InlineMath math="x+2y-5=0" /> is <InlineMath math="5/\sqrt5=\sqrt5" />. If <InlineMath math="a" /> is half the chord length, then the right triangle gives

<BlockMath math="a^2+(\sqrt5)^2=5^2" />

Thus <InlineMath math="a=2\sqrt5" />, and the full chord has length <InlineMath math="4\sqrt5" />.
