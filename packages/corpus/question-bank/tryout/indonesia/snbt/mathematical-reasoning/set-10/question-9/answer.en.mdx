The distance from the centre <InlineMath math="(0,0)" /> to the line <InlineMath math="x+2y-5=0" /> is <InlineMath math="\frac{|0+2(0)-5|}{\sqrt{1^2+2^2}}=\frac5{\sqrt5}=\sqrt5" />. The **perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord**. If <InlineMath math="a" /> is half the chord length, then the right triangle gives

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}a^2+(\sqrt5)^2&=5^2\\a^2&=25-5=20\\a&=\sqrt{20}=2\sqrt5\end{aligned}" />

Thus <InlineMath math="a=2\sqrt5" />, and the full chord has length <InlineMath math="4\sqrt5" />.
