**Data were collected at the city park information desk and compared afterward.**

**Were collected** and **were compared** are passive forms. The second participle shares the first auxiliary. **At the city park information desk** identifies the collection location.

Other versions leave a preposition incomplete, use incorrect verb forms, add an object to the passive coordination, or substitute *comparison* for a participle.
