**Grouping ingredients by recipe stage could shorten the search when groups moved to the next stage.**

The observed problem is time spent searching during a stage change. Arranging ingredients by recipe stage places them according to their order of use.

That relationship gives a reason to test the layout. Measurement is still needed, and the outcome cannot be certain before the trial.
