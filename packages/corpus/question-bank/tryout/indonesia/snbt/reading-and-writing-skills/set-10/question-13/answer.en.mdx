**the team's follow-up study of maps showing walking times**

The possessive **team's** refers to one team. **Follow-up** acts as a compound modifier before *study*.

The alternatives use the verb form *follow up* in that position, misspell it as *follow-uup*, double its hyphen, or omit the possessive while adding an unnecessary hyphen before *study*.
