**Nevertheless, the team limited its conclusion to the short trial with that type of recipe.**

The comma sets off **Nevertheless**. **The team** stays together and connects directly to **limited**. A full stop ends the statement.

The other options split the subject internally, use a colon after the connector, split subject and verb, or end the statement with a question mark.
