**Nevertheless, the team limited its conclusion for this setting (teen cooking class) to the short trial.**

“Nevertheless” acts as an opening linking adverb and is followed by a comma. The following clause is a complete statement, so it ends with a full stop. This punctuation makes the limit easy to identify.
