**an analysis of the effectiveness of small maps showing walking times**

The phrase correctly spells **analysis** and **effectiveness**. It identifies a study of how well maps with walking times help visitors.

The alternatives contain *analysiss*, *effectivness*, *analisis*, or *effectivenes*. Each is a spelling error.
