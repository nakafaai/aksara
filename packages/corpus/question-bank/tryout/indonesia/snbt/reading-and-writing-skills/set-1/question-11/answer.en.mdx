Word choice must fit the relationship between ideas. Sentences <InlineMath math="(1)" /> through <InlineMath math="(4)" /> explain that healthy ecosystems reduce risk, while ecosystem degradation is a driver of risk.

The verb _reduce_ in sentence <InlineMath math="(7)" /> therefore contradicts the context. Ecosystem damage can **increase** people's vulnerability and exposure to hazards. Replacing that one word restores the intended cause-and-effect relationship without changing the sentence's focus.
