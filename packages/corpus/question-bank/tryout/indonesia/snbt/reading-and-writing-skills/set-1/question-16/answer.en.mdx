The demonstrative phrase _that decline_ points back to a specific change already introduced in the preceding sentence.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" /> states that GAIKINDO's wholesale sales fell from <InlineMath math="1{,}030{,}126\ \text{units}" /> in 2019 to <InlineMath math="532{,}027\ \text{units}" /> in 2020. Sentence <InlineMath math="(3)" /> then quantifies the same fall as <InlineMath math="48.35\%" />.

Therefore, _that decline_ refers to **the fall in wholesale sales from 2019 to 2020**. The April, May, and December figures appear later, so none of them can be the antecedent.
