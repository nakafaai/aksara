Sentence <InlineMath math="(4)" /> uses two subjects for one predicate: the noun phrase _poor fecal-waste management_ and the pronoun _it_. This unnecessary double subject breaks the sentence structure.

Keep the specific subject and remove the pronoun:

_**Poor fecal-waste management** allows contaminants to seep into the surrounding environment and nearby water sources._

The revised sentence now has one subject, the predicate _allows_, the object _contaminants_, and the complement _to seep_ with its modifiers.
