Sentence <InlineMath math="(8)" /> opens a parenthesis before the aside *interpreted as people close to the deceased* but never closes it. The reader therefore cannot see where the aside ends and the main predicate resumes.

A closing parenthesis belongs after *deceased*: **Other figures on the lid (interpreted as people close to the deceased) are carved in various poses.** Thus, the punctuation error is in **sentence <InlineMath math="(8)" />**.
