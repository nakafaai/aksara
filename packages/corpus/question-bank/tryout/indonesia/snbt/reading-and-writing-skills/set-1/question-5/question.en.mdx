<InlineMath math="(1)" /> A sarcophagus is a stone burial container that generally has a receptacle and a lid. <InlineMath math="(2)" /> On Samosir Island, sarcophagi are among the megalithic remains associated with Toba Batak death traditions. <InlineMath math="(3)" /> The Tomok sarcophagus is rectangular, widens at the top, and resembles a traditional *solu bolon* boat. <InlineMath math="(4)" /> In Bali, more than one hundred sarcophagi have been recorded at various locations. <InlineMath math="(5)" /> The boat form of the Tomok sarcophagus is interpreted as a vehicle carrying the deceased to the spirit world.

<InlineMath math="(6)" /> Its receptacle has an opening used to store bones. <InlineMath math="(7)" /> Figures carved on the front often depict a guardian against harmful influences. <InlineMath math="(8)" /> Other figures on the lid (interpreted as people close to the deceased are carved in various poses. <InlineMath math="(9)" /> [...], the sarcophagus faces Mount Pusuk Buhit or Lake Toba, possibly reflecting a belief that mountains are sacred places for spirits.
