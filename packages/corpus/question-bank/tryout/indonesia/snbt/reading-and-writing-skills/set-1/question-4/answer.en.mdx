Sentence <InlineMath math="(1)" /> does have a subject, *Indonesia*, and a predicate, *faces*. The problem is therefore not a missing subject.

The weakness is the redundant pattern *As an archipelagic country, therefore ...*. The opening phrase already explains Indonesia's relevant circumstance, so *therefore* does not belong there. A clear revision is: **As an archipelagic country, Indonesia faces risks from sea-level rise and coastal flooding caused by climate change.**
