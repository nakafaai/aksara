Sentence <InlineMath math="(1)" /> already has a subject, *Indonesia*, and a predicate, *faces*. The error lies elsewhere.

The pattern *As an archipelagic country, therefore ...* repeats the same connection twice. The opening phrase already explains Indonesia's relevant circumstance, so *therefore* can be removed. A clear revision is: **As an archipelagic country, Indonesia faces risks from sea-level rise and coastal flooding caused by climate change.**
