Sentence (1) already has a subject, *Indonesia*, and a predicate, *faces*. The error lies elsewhere.

The pattern *As an archipelagic country, therefore ...* repeats the same connection twice. The opening phrase already explains Indonesia's relevant circumstance, so **removing *therefore*** makes the connection direct. A clear revision is: As an archipelagic country, Indonesia faces risks from sea-level rise and coastal flooding caused by climate change.
