In sentence <InlineMath math="(3)" />, *resembles* describes a visual similarity between the sarcophagus and a *solu bolon* boat. It does not imply competition, deliberate imitation, replacement, or following.

The phrase that preserves both the meaning and grammar is **looks like**: the sarcophagus *looks like* a traditional *solu bolon* boat.
