A sentence core keeps what the predicate needs and removes modifiers. In sentence <InlineMath math="(7)" />:

- the core subject is _water-source contamination_;
- the core predicate is _is linked_;
- the required complement is _to sanitation and wastewater_.

The phrases _in many Indonesian communities_ and _closely_ add detail. The clauses modifying _sanitation_ and _wastewater_ can also be removed. The core is therefore **Water-source contamination is linked to sanitation and wastewater.**
