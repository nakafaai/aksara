The word *These* points back to information that has already been stated, so its referent should be sought in the sentence immediately before sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" />.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(5)" /> names droughts and floods as two disruptions that can damage crops. The phrase *These disruptions* in the next sentence summarizes both. Therefore, the answer is **droughts and floods that can damage crops**.
