The sentence needs the noun meaning the rate or ability to produce. The standard spelling listed by [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/productivity) is *productivity*.

The form *productivty* in sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" /> is missing the second *i*. It should therefore be corrected to **productivity**. The other options have different grammatical roles or meanings.
