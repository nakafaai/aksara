Sentence <InlineMath math="(8)" /> describes contaminated water as the result of household activities. The water receives the action; it does not perform the producing.

After the auxiliary _is_, the passive construction needs the past participle _produced_, not the base form _produce_:

_Household wastewater includes contaminated water that is **produced** by washing, bathing, and toilet use._

Changing the verb form repairs the agent-result relationship without changing the information.
