Sentence (5) gives a definition, and sentence (6) shows how that decline becomes visible. The second sentence explains the first, so the connector must express an explanation.

The relative pronoun _that_ turns sentence (6) into a clause modifying _a decline in environmental quality_. The combined sentence preserves every idea:

_Environmental degradation is a decline in environmental quality **that is evident**, for example, in damaged soil, polluted water and air, and biodiversity loss._
