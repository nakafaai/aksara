Sentence <InlineMath math="(5)" /> gives a definition, and sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" /> describes how the decline in that definition becomes visible. The relationship is explanation, not cause, result, contrast, or a separate addition.

The relative pronoun _that_ turns sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" /> into a clause modifying _a decline in environmental quality_. The combined sentence preserves every idea:

_Environmental degradation is a decline in environmental quality **that is evident** in damaged soil, polluted water and air, and biodiversity loss._
