(1) Healthy, resilient ecosystems help reduce disaster risk. (2) According to the United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR), environmental degradation can be both a driver and a consequence of disasters. (3) Such degradation reduces the environment's capacity to meet social and ecological needs. (4) The Sendai Framework recognizes poor environmental management, ecosystem degradation, and unsustainable use of natural resources as drivers of risk.

(5) Environmental degradation is a decline in environmental quality. (6) This decline is evident, for example, in damaged soil, polluted water and air, and biodiversity loss. (7) Ecosystem damage can also reduce people's vulnerability and exposure to hazards. (8) Protecting and restoring ecosystems therefore requires planning, governance, and risk-informed investment.

Sources: adapted from UNDRR, ["Nature for Resilience"](https://www.undrr.org/implementing-sendai-framework/sendai-framework-action/nature-resilience) and ["Underlying Disaster Risk Drivers"](https://www.undrr.org/terminology/underlying-disaster-risk-drivers).
