**Therefore**

The photographs show only a small number of tree species. **Therefore**, the team cannot yet extend its conclusion across the whole district. The connector presents the restricted conclusion as a consequence of the limited examples.

*Meanwhile* indicates time or a shift of focus, *nevertheless* marks a contrast, *for example* introduces an illustration, and *previously* refers to an earlier time.
