**Mean agreement in trial sessions exceeded both the baseline and comparison means.**

Sentence (7) compares a mean of 26 agreed records in trial sessions with 19 at baseline and 21 in comparison sessions. The summary must preserve the relation **26 exceeds both 19 and 21**.

An overall mean does not establish equal improvement for every pair. The sentence also does not identify a sole cause or a long-term result. The baseline and comparison values differ.
