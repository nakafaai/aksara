**Based on the data, the team reached a limited conclusion about the school-studio trial.**

The sentence contains the subject **the team**, the finite verb **reached**, and the object **a limited conclusion**. It also states the evidence and the trial being discussed.

The first alternative has no finite verb. The version beginning *the team that* is a noun phrase with a relative clause; the *because* version is only a dependent clause. The last alternative lacks a subject and the object of *reached*.
