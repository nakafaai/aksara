**the team's follow-up study of the recording checklist**

**Follow-up** modifies *study*, and **team's** marks possession by a single team. The selected phrase writes both relationships correctly.

The verb form *follow up* does not fit as written before *study*. *Follow-uup* is misspelled, *follow--up* doubles the hyphen, and the final option drops the possessive ending while attaching an unnecessary hyphen to *study*.
