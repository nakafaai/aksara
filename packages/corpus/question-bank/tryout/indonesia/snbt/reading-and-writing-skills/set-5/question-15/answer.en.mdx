**The mean number of recordings without technical retakes was higher. Nevertheless, the conclusion about the school-studio trial remained limited.**

The trial mean of 28 recordings exceeds 20 at baseline and 22 in comparison sessions. **Nevertheless** signals that the result remains limited by the short trial and by a measure that does not assess artistic quality.

The higher mean does not cause those limitations. Causal connectors therefore misstate the relationship. *Moreover* adds information, and *previously* points to an earlier time.
