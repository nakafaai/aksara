**Nevertheless, the team limited its conclusion to the tree species represented in the sample photographs.**

A comma after **Nevertheless** sets off the opening connector. The subject **the team** stays together and connects directly to **limited**. The statement ends with a full stop.

The other versions insert a comma inside the subject, use a colon after the connector, split the subject from its verb, or end this declarative statement with a question mark.
