**an analysis of the effectiveness of the recording checklist**

The words **analysis**, **effectiveness**, **recording**, and **checklist** are all spelled correctly. Together, they describe an examination of how well the checklist works.

The alternatives contain *analysiss*, *effectivness*, *analisis*, or *effectivenes*. These are spelling errors, not different findings about the checklist.
