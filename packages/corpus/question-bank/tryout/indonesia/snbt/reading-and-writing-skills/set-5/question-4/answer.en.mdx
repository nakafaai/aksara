**Sample photographs were chosen to clarify the written categories that had led to differing assessments.**

The opening problem is that two observers assign different categories to the same damage. Descriptions such as “moderate” and “severe” are insufficiently specific. Sample photographs offer a visual reference for those categories.

That connection explains why the team selects photographs before running the test. It does not guarantee success, make all comparison values equal, or remove the need to measure agreement.
