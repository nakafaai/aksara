**Data were collected in the school recording studio and compared afterward.**

**Were collected** is passive. The participle **compared** shares *were*, so both actions have the data as their subject. **In the school recording studio** gives the location.

The other options use incomplete prepositional phrases, incorrect verb forms, an extra object after a passive construction, or the noun *comparison* in place of the required participle.
