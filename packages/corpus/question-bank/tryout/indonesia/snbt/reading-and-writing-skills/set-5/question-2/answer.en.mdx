**kept the same so that the tested change could be interpreted more clearly**

**Were kept the same** refers to route length, observer pairs, and recording forms. Holding those conditions constant helps keep the comparison from being mixed with changes to the route, the people observing, or the form they use.

Photographs are the addition in trial sessions. The other conditions are not gradually increased, recorded only after the result, changed only for comparison sessions, or ignored.
