**In addition**

Sentence (7) presents numerical evidence, and sentence (8) adds user comments that point in the same direction. The relationship is additive, so “In addition” fits. “As a result” would claim cause and effect, “By contrast” and “Nevertheless” signal opposition, and “Before that” merely places events in time.
