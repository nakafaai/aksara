**Based on the data, the team reached a limited conclusion for this setting (composting workshop).**

After the opening phrase “Based on the data”, the main clause is complete: “the team” is the subject and “reached” is the finite verb. “A limited conclusion” supplies the object. The other versions omit the verb, use a pronoun without a clear referent, or begin a reason with “because” and never complete it.
