**(4)-(3)-(1)-(2)**

Summary sentence (4) introduces the baseline and hypothesis. Sentence (3) can then refer to *that hypothesis* and name the tested change. Sentence (1) reports the result of that test, and sentence (2) closes with the limitation and planned repetition.

The other sequences put references before their antecedents or separate the result from the closing qualification.
