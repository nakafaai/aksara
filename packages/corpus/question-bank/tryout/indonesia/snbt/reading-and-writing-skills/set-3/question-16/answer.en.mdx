**Users in this setting (composting workshop) provided brief comments.**

Every part of this sentence contributes a distinct fact: who commented, where, what they did, and the length of the comments. Phrases such as “brief comments in short form” repeat the same idea. Adding “their own personally” is also unnecessary because the subject already identifies the people giving the comments.
