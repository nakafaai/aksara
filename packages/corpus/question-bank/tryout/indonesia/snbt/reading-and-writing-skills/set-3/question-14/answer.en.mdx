**Data were collected in this setting (composting workshop) and compared afterward.**

The passive verb phrase needs “were collected”, and “in” must remain separate before the phrase naming the setting. “Afterward” is the adverb required to describe when the comparison happened. The alternatives confuse the preposition with a verb form or use a word that does not fit the sentence's grammatical role.
