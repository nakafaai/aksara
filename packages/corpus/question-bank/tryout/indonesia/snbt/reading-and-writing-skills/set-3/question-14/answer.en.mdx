**Data were collected in the composting workshop and compared afterward.**

**Were collected** and **were compared** are coordinated passive forms. The second *were* can be omitted because the two participles share it. **In the composting workshop** locates the data collection, and **afterward** places the comparison later.

The other options leave a preposition without a complement, use malformed verb forms, or combine an unsuitable preposition with an incomplete construction.
