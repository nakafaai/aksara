**On Monday, the team tested sequencing cards in the composting workshop.**

**On** begins the sentence with a capital, and **Monday** is the name of a day. The common noun phrase *the team* stays lowercase inside the sentence. A full stop closes the statement.

The comma after the short opening time phrase is acceptable. The alternative without that comma also lacks the required closing full stop, so it is not a complete correctly punctuated statement.
