**On Monday, the team tested cards showing the order of brown and green materials in this setting (composting workshop).**

“Monday” is a proper name and therefore begins with a capital letter. The opening time phrase is followed by a comma before the main clause, and the sentence ends with a full stop. Common nouns such as “team” and “composting workshop” do not need capitals in the middle of the sentence.
