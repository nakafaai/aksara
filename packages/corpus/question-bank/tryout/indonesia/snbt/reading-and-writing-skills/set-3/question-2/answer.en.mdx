**developing a provisional claim that can be tested**

The team has observed only an initial pattern, so it cannot yet name the symbols as the cause of users' hesitation. Instead, the idea tells the team what to test next. That is the role of a hypothesis: it offers a provisional answer that evidence can challenge, rather than declaring a conclusion before the test begins.
