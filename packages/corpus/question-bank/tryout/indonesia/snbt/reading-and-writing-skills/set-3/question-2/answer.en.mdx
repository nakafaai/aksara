**developing a provisional claim that can be tested**

The baseline records have not separated the effect of the symbols from participants' familiarity with the building. The team uses that pattern to decide what proposition to test next.

A hypothesis is a provisional answer that evidence can challenge. Developing one does not establish a cause, discard inconvenient data, wait until every trial is complete, or replace measurements with opinions.
