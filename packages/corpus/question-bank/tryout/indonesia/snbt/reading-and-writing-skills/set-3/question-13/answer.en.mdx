**the team's follow-up study of the sequencing cards**

**Follow-up** is the hyphenated adjective modifying *study*. **Team's** is the singular possessive, indicating whose study it is.

The other forms separate the modifier into *follow up*, add an extra *u*, double the hyphen, or join *study* into the compound while losing the possessive form. The selected phrase avoids those errors.
