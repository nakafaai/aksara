**the team's follow-up study of cards showing the order of brown and green materials in this setting (composting workshop)**

“Follow-up” acts as a compound modifier before “study”, so it takes a hyphen. “Team's” needs an apostrophe because the study belongs to the team. The remaining words stay separate. The alternatives either drop the possessive mark, fuse independent words, or remove the hyphen that keeps the modifier clear.
