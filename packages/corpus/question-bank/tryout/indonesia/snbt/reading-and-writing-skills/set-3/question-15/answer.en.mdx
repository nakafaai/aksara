**The trial mean was higher. Nevertheless, the conclusion remained limited.**

The higher result supports the possibility that the cards help. **Nevertheless** signals that the conclusion remains limited despite that favourable result.

The limitation comes from the short test's scope, not from the higher value itself. Causal connectors therefore misstate the relationship. *Moreover* only adds information, and *Previously* changes the relationship to one of time.
