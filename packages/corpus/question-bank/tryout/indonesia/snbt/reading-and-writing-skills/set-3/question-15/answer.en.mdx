**The figures increased. Nevertheless, the conclusion for this setting (composting workshop) remained limited.**

The first sentence reports evidence in favour of the change. The second holds back the strength of the claim because the trial was short. “Nevertheless” connects those two directions without denying the increase. A causal connector would be misleading, since the higher figure is not a reason to remove the study's limitation.
