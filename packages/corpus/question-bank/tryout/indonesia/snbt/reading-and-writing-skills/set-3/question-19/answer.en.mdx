**The team changed only one factor: the use of illustrated cards.**

**One factor** takes a singular noun. **Only** supplies the restriction without repeating it through *merely*. The complete clause before the colon introduces an explanation of that factor.

A semicolon cannot join this clause to the following noun phrase. The version with *namely:* also combines an explanatory connector with the wrong punctuation at that position.
