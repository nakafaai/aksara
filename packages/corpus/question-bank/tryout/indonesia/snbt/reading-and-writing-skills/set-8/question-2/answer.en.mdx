**retaining group-size requirements so that participant conditions remain comparable**

The phrase concerns the number of people in each group, not every measurement procedure. The team will retain those requirements while adding question classification and an assessment of understanding.

Keeping group-size rules does not mean forcing identical results or questions, changing group size to suit the results, or reusing old data without collecting new evidence.
