**Nevertheless, the team had not measured visitor understanding.**

The comma sets off **Nevertheless**. **The team** stays together and connects directly to **had not measured**. A full stop ends the statement.

The other choices split the subject internally, use a colon after the connector, split subject and verb, or end the declarative sentence with a question mark.
