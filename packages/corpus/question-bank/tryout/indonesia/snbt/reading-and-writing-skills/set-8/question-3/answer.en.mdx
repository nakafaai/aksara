**The possible benefit of question cards therefore needed to be tested at each demonstration table.**

Sentence (3) names several influences on the number of visitors asking questions. The baseline does not establish the general invitation as the sole cause.

The added sentence should lead from the possible benefit of cards into the test in sentence (4). A definite causal claim or permanent adoption is premature, and changing many features at once would make the cards harder to assess.
