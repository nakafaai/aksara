**(4)-(3)-(1)-(2)**

Sentence (4) supplies the baseline and the referent for “that hypothesis”. Sentence (3) then introduces the question-card trial, and sentence (1) reports its result. Sentence (2) belongs last because “However” limits the result and points to the next trial.
