**In trial sessions, each table displayed a card with two questions specific to its demonstration, while comparison sessions used the previous general invitation.**

**While** preserves the contrast between two specific questions on each card and the general invitation. It keeps the number of questions and the separation between trial and comparison sessions.

The alternatives add universal failure, permanent adoption, no separate comparison condition, or a comparison based only on comments.
