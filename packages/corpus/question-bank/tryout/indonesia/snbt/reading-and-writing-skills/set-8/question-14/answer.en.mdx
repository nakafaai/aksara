**Data were collected at the lost-property office and compared afterward.**

**Were collected** and **were compared** are passive forms. The second participle can share the first auxiliary. **At the lost-property office** identifies where the data were collected.

Other versions leave a preposition incomplete, use incorrect verb forms, add an object to the passive coordination, or substitute the noun *comparison* for a participle.
