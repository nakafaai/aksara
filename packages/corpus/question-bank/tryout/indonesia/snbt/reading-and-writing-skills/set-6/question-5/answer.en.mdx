**In contrast**

**In contrast** marks the difference between trial sessions with added arrows and comparison sessions without them.

*For that reason* signals a consequence, *indeed* reinforces a point, and *previously* or *afterwards* assigns a time order. The passage does not establish the ordering or simultaneous timing of the two session types.
