**(4)-(3)-(1)-(2)**

Sentence (4) introduces the baseline and hypothesis. **That hypothesis** in sentence (3) needs this reference before introducing the arrow test. **That trial** in sentence (1) then links the result to the test.

Sentence (2) belongs last because it identifies the missing information and the plan to collect it. The sequence is observation, testing, result, and follow-up.
