**Data were collected for the neighbourhood noise log and compared afterward.**

**Were collected** and **were compared** are passive forms. The second participle can share the first auxiliary. **For the neighbourhood noise log** states the purpose of collecting the data.

Other versions leave a preposition incomplete, use incorrect verb forms, add an object to the passive coordination, or substitute the noun *comparison* for a participle.
