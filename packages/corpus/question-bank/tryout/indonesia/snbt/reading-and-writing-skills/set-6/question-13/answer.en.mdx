**the team's follow-up study of an example showing how to record sound duration in this setting (neighbourhood noise log)**

The apostrophe in “team's” marks possession, and the hyphen in “follow-up” joins the words used together before “study”. The rest of the phrase completes what was studied and in which setting. The alternatives misplace or omit one of these forms.
