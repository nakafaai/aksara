**Nevertheless, the team limited its conclusion to the exhibition tested.**

The comma sets off **Nevertheless**. The subject **the team** remains intact and is followed directly by the verb **limited**. A full stop ends the statement.

Other versions split the subject, place a colon after the connector, separate the subject from its verb, or turn the statement into a question through its final punctuation.
