**(4)-(3)-(1)-(2)**

Sentence (4) introduces the baseline and hypothesis. “That hypothesis” in sentence (3) then links the tentative explanation to a test of photo labels. “That trial” in sentence (1) refers back to the test and reports its result.

Sentence (2) belongs last because it limits the result's reach and proposes a longer test. This order preserves both the references and the sequence of observation, testing, result, and follow-up.
