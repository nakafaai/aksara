**the team's follow-up study of genre signs**

In **follow-up study**, *follow-up* is the compound modifier before *study*. The apostrophe in **team's** marks a study belonging to one team.

*Follow up* is the verb form, rather than the modifier needed here. *Follow-uup* contains a typo, and *follow--up* has a doubled hyphen. The last option omits the possessive ending and unnecessarily joins *study* to the modifier.
