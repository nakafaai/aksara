**the team's follow-up study of genre signs on each table in this setting (book exchange market)**

The apostrophe in “team's” marks possession, while “follow-up” takes a hyphen because it modifies “study”. Those two details keep the relationships between the words clear. The alternatives either lose the possessive form or break the compound modifier.
