**an analysis of the effectiveness of genre signs at the book exchange**

The standard spellings are **analysis** and **effectiveness**. The phrase describes an examination of how well the genre signs work at the book exchange.

The other options introduce spelling errors: *analysiss*, *effectivness*, *analisis*, or *effectivenes*. Each differs from the correctly written word in the selected phrase.
