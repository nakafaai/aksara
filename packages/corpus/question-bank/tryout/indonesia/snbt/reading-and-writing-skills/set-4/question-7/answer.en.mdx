**The team tested photo labels on the return shelves on selected days, while the earlier process remained on comparison days.**

Sentences (4) and (5) describe two conditions that must remain distinct. “While” places trial days beside comparison days clearly and concisely. A causal link would be wrong because the earlier process is a reference condition, not a result of the photo labels.
