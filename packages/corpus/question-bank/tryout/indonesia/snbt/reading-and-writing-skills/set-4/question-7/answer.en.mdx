**Shelves displayed photographs alongside letter codes in trial sessions, while comparison sessions used only letter codes.**

“While” links the two conditions without merging them. Photographs supplement the letter codes in trial sessions; comparison sessions use only the codes.

The combined sentence also retains sessions as the unit rather than changing them to days. Other options add unsupported claims about useless codes, permanent adoption, the absence of comparison sessions, or comments being the only reference.
