**The trial mean was higher. Nevertheless, the conclusion about the book exchange remained limited.**

The trial mean of 28 books exceeds the baseline mean of 18 and the comparison mean of 20. **Nevertheless** signals that the promising result does not remove the limits on the conclusion.

The conclusion is restricted because the data come from only a few short sessions. A higher mean is not the cause of that restriction, so causal connectors do not fit. *Moreover* adds information, while *previously* introduces an earlier time.
