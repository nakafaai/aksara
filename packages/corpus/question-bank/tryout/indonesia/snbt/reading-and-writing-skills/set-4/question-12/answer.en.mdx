**On Monday, the team tested genre signs on every table at the book exchange.**

The sentence begins with a capital letter, and the weekday **Monday** is capitalised. The common noun phrase *the team* stays lowercase within the sentence. A full stop closes the statement.

A comma after a short introductory time phrase can be optional. The final option still fails because it lacks a full stop. The other options incorrectly capitalise the sentence opening, the weekday, or *the*.
