**Nevertheless, the team limited its conclusion to the short trial.**

A comma after the opening transition “Nevertheless” clearly separates it from the main clause. The subject “the team” remains intact and is not separated from “limited” by a comma. A full stop closes the declarative statement.

The other versions insert a comma within the subject or between subject and verb, put a colon after the transition, or use a question mark for the requested statement. The answer does not depend on treating every omission of a comma after a short introductory adverb as an error.
