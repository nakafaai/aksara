**Data were collected at the book exchange and compared afterward.**

**Were collected** is a passive verb phrase, and **compared** shares its auxiliary *were*. The prepositional phrase **at the book exchange** identifies where the data were collected.

The other options leave *in* without an object, use incorrect verb forms, add an object to the passive coordination, or use the noun *comparison* where a participle is required.
