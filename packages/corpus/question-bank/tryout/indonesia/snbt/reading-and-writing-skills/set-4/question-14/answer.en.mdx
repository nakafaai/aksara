**Data were collected in this setting (book exchange market) and compared afterward.**

The passive construction needs “were collected”, followed by the separate preposition “in” before the setting. “Compared” shares the same passive auxiliary, and “afterward” functions as an adverb. The other forms confuse these grammatical roles.
