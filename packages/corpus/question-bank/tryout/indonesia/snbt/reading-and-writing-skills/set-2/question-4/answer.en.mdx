A main idea must cover the entire text. One detail is too narrow.

The first paragraph defines hypothermia, explains how the body loses heat, and identifies its warning signs. The second paragraph then explains the urgent and safe steps to take while waiting for medical help. Both paragraphs therefore support one central message: hypothermia is dangerous and must be handled promptly and correctly.

The best answer is **hypothermia is a medical emergency that requires prompt and safe action**. The other options also misstate the facts: hypothermia is not limited to mountains, shivering is not its only symptom, direct heat such as a hot bath should be avoided, and a conscious person still needs medical help.
