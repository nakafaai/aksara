An expansion adds information to a basic sentence while preserving its subject and predicate.

The basic sentence has **PSBB** as its subject and **began to be implemented** as its predicate. The sentence **"PSBB began to be implemented in DKI Jakarta on 10 April 2020"** keeps both elements unchanged and only adds place and time information.

The other options change either the subject or the predicate. They may discuss the same event, but they are not direct expansions of the given sentence. Therefore, the sentence that retains **PSBB + began to be implemented** is the correct answer.
