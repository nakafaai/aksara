The verb *explains* introduces the information stated in the clause that follows it. That clause has its own subject, *their dense roots*, and its own predicates, *slow* and *cause*.

The conjunction *that* correctly introduces this content clause:

> The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration explains **that** their dense roots slow the movement of tidal water and cause fine sediment to settle.

The alternatives express addition, time, or condition and do not establish the required relationship between *explains* and the information being explained.

Therefore, the correct answer is **that**.
