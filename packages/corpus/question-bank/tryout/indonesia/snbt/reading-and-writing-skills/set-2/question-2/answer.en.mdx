The *incomplete* sentence in paragraph <InlineMath math="(2)" /> is sentence <InlineMath math="(11)" />:

_In addition, also protects privacy and safety when children go online._

The predicate _also protects_ has no subject. The reader can infer _the plan_ from sentence <InlineMath math="(9)" />, but a complete standalone sentence must state it: _In addition, the plan protects privacy and safety when children go online._
