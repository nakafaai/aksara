The word *commonly* in sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" /> can be replaced with **often**.

Both adverbs express that something happens frequently. The replacement also preserves the sentence's grammar: "It often develops after prolonged exposure to cold weather or cold water."

The other options either express a different meaning or do not fit the adverbial position in the sentence. Therefore, **often** is the only choice that preserves both meaning and structure.
