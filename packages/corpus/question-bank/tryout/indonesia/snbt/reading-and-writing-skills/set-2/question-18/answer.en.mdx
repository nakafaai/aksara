A coherent paragraph moves from a general idea to a concrete example. The reference words and transitions reveal the order:

- Sentence (4) introduces the topic: effective family engagement requires planning.
- Sentence (5) presents one evidence-informed approach to that planning.
- Sentence (1) restates the approach more plainly through *In other words*.
- Sentence (3) asks how *that* practical advice could look.
- Sentence (2) answers the question with concrete examples.

Sentence (5) refers to *that engagement*, introduced in sentence (4). Placing sentence (1) or (3) first would leave its reference without an earlier idea. Placing the examples before the question would also weaken the progression.
