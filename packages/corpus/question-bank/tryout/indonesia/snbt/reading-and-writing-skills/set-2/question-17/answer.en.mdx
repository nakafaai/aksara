Sentence <InlineMath math="(7)" /> states a benefit, while sentence <InlineMath math="(8)" /> gives the reason for that benefit:

- **Result:** coastal communities benefit from healthy mangrove forests.
- **Reason:** the forests reduce wave energy and provide nursery habitat.

The conjunction *because* makes this causal relationship explicit. It also preserves both original subjects, so the combined sentence remains clear:

> Coastal communities benefit from healthy mangrove forests **because** the forests reduce wave energy and provide nursery habitat for fish and crustaceans.

The alternatives either create an ungrammatical construction or express addition, condition, or contrast instead of cause.

Therefore, the option using **because** is correct.
