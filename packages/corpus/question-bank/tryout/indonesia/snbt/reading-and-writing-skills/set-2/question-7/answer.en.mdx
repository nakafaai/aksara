The appropriate phrase is **occurs when**.

Inserted into the blank, it produces a complete sentence: "Hypothermia occurs when the body loses heat faster than it can produce it." The verb *occurs* supplies the predicate, while *when* introduces the condition under which hypothermia develops.

The other options either leave the sentence without a complete predicate or express a contrast, purpose, exception, or time sequence that does not match the intended relationship. Therefore, **occurs when** is the only precise and grammatical answer.
