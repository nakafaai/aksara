The colon after *namely* should be replaced with a comma.

MLA punctuation guidance uses a comma after *namely*, which introduces an explanation of the preceding expression. The corrected segment is:

> three key rates, namely, year-on-year growth ..., growth ..., and a quarter-on-quarter contraction ....

A semicolon would wrongly separate the introduction from its explanation. Removing the comma before *namely* or inserting a colon after *The agency* would not fix the colon after *namely*. Full stops between the list items would turn them into fragments.

Reference: [MLA punctuation guidance](https://style.mla.org/quiz-colons/).
