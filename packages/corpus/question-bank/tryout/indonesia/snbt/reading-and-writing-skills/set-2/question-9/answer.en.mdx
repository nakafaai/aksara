The colon after *namely* should be removed.

The word *namely* already introduces the items as part of the same sentence. A colon is used before a list only when the words before it form a complete independent clause. Here, the list completes the expression introduced by *namely*, so adding a colon is redundant.

The corrected segment is: **"three key rates, namely year-on-year growth ..., growth ..., and a quarter-on-quarter contraction ...."** A comma is required after *namely*. A colon would be incorrect here.

Reference: [MLA punctuation guidance](https://style.mla.org/quiz-colons/).
