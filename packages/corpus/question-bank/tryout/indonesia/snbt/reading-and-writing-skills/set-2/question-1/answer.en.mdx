The punctuation error is in sentence <InlineMath math="(12)" />.

The sentence contains a direct quotation followed by the reporting clause _a family might decide_. In American English, the comma that separates the quotation from that clause belongs inside the closing quotation mark.

The text incorrectly writes:

_"At the start of every school year, we will review this plan", a family might decide._

It should read:

_"At the start of every school year, we will review this plan," a family might decide._
