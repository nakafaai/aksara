An effective sentence needs a clear grammatical core. In sentence <InlineMath math="(10)" />, the intended core is:

- **Subject:** *the census results*
- **Predicate:** *provide a benchmark for current agricultural statistics*

The opening word *Regarding* turns *the census results* into part of a prepositional phrase. The remaining words then have no subject, so the sentence is incomplete.

Removing *Regarding* restores the complete sentence: *The census results also provide a benchmark for current agricultural statistics.* The other edits do not repair the missing subject.

Therefore, the correct revision is **removing the word *Regarding* at the beginning of the sentence**.
