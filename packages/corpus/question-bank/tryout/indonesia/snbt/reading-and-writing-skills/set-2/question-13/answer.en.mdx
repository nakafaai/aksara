Sentence <InlineMath math="(7)" /> contains a cause-and-reason relationship:

- **Claim:** the two trends must be read together.
- **Reason:** individual agricultural holdings and agricultural households are different statistical units.

The second clause explains why a decline in one count and a rise in the other do not form a direct contradiction. The causal conjunction *because* expresses that relationship precisely.

The other choices express contrast, purpose, condition, or time, none of which matches the logic of the sentence.

Therefore, the correct conjunction is **because**.
