**were unsure which action should follow the current stage**

The volunteers have reached a junction but are unsure which plot to take the tray to. The **next step** is the action following that stage.

The hesitation does not mean rejecting the programme, waiting for a new goal, lacking knowledge of its final outcome, or deliberately choosing a different route to test a hypothesis.
