**Previous-day ordering was chosen so that students' choices would be known before they reached the serving table.**

The opening problem is that choices are made at the serving table, leaving some options exhausted and others unused. Previous-day ordering moves that decision earlier.

This purpose connects the problem to the tested change. The passage does not say that students were confused about what to do or that advance ordering is guaranteed to eliminate all leftovers.
