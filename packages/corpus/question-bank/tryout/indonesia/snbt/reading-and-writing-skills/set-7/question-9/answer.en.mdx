**(4)-(3)-(1)-(2)**

Sentence (4) first introduces the baseline and the hypothesis. Sentence (3) can then refer to “that hypothesis” and name the trial, followed by its result in sentence (1). “However” makes sentence (2) the closing limitation and follow-up plan.
