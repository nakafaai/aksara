**Nevertheless, the team did not claim that advance orders always reduced leftovers.**

The comma sets off **Nevertheless**. The subject **the team** remains together and connects directly to **did not claim**. A full stop closes the statement.

Other choices insert a comma inside the subject, use a colon after the connector, split subject and verb, or end the declarative sentence with a question mark.
