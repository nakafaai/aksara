A class designed a simulated investigation of leaf growth under different light colours. The question was whether using a blue light filter was associated with a change in new leaf area in square centimetres. Before collecting data, the students agreed to keep plant variety, water, light duration, and distance from the lamp the same in every condition.

| Condition | Mean result |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | 12 |
| With the change | 19 |
| Comparison | 13 |

The condition with the change differed from both other conditions. The class did not treat this pattern as proof that the same result would always occur. Its report identified an important limitation: one filter also reduced total light intensity slightly.

The report used the term *confounding variable* to mean a factor that changes together with another factor and can confuse interpretation. The students proposed a longer repetition with more units and the same measurement rules. This next step would test the pattern without hiding the uncertainty in the first investigation.


Which title best captures both the central issue and the passage's cautious scope?
