The organisers of a neighbourhood flood-warning exercise received suggestions from residents, emergency volunteers, schools, and local authorities. They did not turn the first suggestion into a permanent rule. For one week, the team recorded households identifying the correct safe route and established a baseline of 41.

The team then tested an alert that named the street, expected depth, and safe route. The trial condition reached 62, while the comparison condition reached 43. Schedules and staffing remained stable so that the two conditions would be easier to interpret.

| Stage | Mean value |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | 41 |
| Trial | 62 |
| Comparison | 43 |

The figures were discussed with the people affected by the service. The change appeared worth extending on a limited basis, but the exercise could not reproduce stress during an actual flood. In the report, *actionable information* meant a message that states what action to take and why it matters.
