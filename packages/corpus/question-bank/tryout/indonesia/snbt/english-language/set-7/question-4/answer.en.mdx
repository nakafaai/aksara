A **confounding variable** changes alongside the factor being tested and can affect the result. Separate the three roles in this experiment:

| Variable | Role in the passage |
| --- | --- |
| Light colour | The factor the class intended to investigate. |
| Total light intensity | An additional factor that fell when the blue filter was used. |
| New leaf area | The measured outcome. |

The definition explains why **colour and intensity are not isolated**. It names the reason a colour-only conclusion is too strong, rather than proving which factor caused the larger leaves.
