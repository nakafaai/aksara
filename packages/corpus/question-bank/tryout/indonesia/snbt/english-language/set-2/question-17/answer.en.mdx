This statement is not supported: **removing walls always increases face-to-face collaboration**.

The research tracking two workplaces through a move to open layouts found the opposite. After the move to more open layouts, face-to-face interaction fell by about <InlineMath math="70\%" />, while electronic communication increased.

The word **always** also exceeds the evidence. The passage explicitly says that no layout has been proved best for every organization and that decisions should depend on context.
