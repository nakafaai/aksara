The fifth choice preserves the meaning: **when nutrient intake is already adequate, taking more supplements usually does not prevent infection or speed recovery**.

The original sentence applies specifically to people without a deficiency. It distinguishes meeting the body's nutrient needs from taking additional amounts after those needs are met.

The other choices add absolute claims, reverse the role of deficiency, or state that supplements always cure or prevent infection. None of those ideas appears in the sentence.
