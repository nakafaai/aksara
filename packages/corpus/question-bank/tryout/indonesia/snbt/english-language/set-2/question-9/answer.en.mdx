The fourth statement is not stated and is explicitly rejected: **an individual bird immediately loses immune genes when it migrates**.

The study compares evolutionary patterns among lineages over many generations. Lower MHC-I diversity was associated with historical colonization and the evolution of long-distance migration. That is different from a change occurring immediately within one migrating bird.

The other four statements are direct details from the passage: the sample size, the direction of colonization, the role of MHC-I genes, and the proposed benefit-cost trade-off.
