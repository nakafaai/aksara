The third statement is false: **Swimming has health benefits and therefore carries no risks.**

The final paragraph says the opposite. Contaminated water can spread illness, and injuries can occur in or around the water. The passage therefore recommends weighing personal circumstances and safety measures alongside possible benefits.

Each of the other statements is directly supported by the passage. The word **therefore** in the third choice creates an invalid conclusion: an activity can offer benefits and still involve risks.
