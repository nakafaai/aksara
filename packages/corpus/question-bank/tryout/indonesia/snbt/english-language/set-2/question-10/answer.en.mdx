The fourth choice is the reasonable inference: **different pathogen environments can alter selection on immune-gene diversity over evolutionary time**.

The observed lineages moved between regions with different pathogen diversity and later showed different MHC-I diversity. The authors interpret this pattern as a change in the balance between recognizing more pathogens and avoiding harmful immune reactions.

The inference remains evolutionary and comparative. It does not imply an immediate change in an individual bird, a cost-free advantage from higher diversity, or medical guidance for humans.
