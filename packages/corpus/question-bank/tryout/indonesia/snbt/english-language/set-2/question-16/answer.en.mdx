In this context, **trade-off** means **a compromise between competing benefits and costs**.

An open layout may offer flexibility and fewer physical boundaries, but it may also reduce privacy and increase distraction. An enclosed layout may protect privacy while creating stronger separation. Gaining one benefit can therefore involve giving up part of another.

The word does not mean a guaranteed advantage, legal requirement, historical sequence, or complete failure.
