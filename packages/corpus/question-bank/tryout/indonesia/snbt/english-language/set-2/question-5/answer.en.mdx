The fifth choice accurately restates the result: **participants lost <InlineMath math="1.24\text{ kg}" /> on average during Ramadan, but most of it was regained within weeks**.

The phrase **on average** matters. It describes the pooled result across <InlineMath math="35" /> studies, so individual participants may have changed by different amounts. The follow-up result is equally important because it shows that most of the reduction did not persist.

The other choices reverse the direction of change, invent a prescribed calorie target, or turn an average into a universal result.
