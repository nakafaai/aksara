The fifth choice is best supported: **the studies show small and varied average changes over a short period, and the results do not apply to everyone over the long term**.

The passage repeatedly limits the claim. It reports pooled averages, notes that individual responses differ, and explains that weight moved back toward its starting level after Ramadan. The <InlineMath math="2025" /> review also found only small changes in fat mass and no significant change in fat-free mass or total body water.

None of this supports a guarantee, a large permanent change, or the claim that religious and clinical purposes are identical.
