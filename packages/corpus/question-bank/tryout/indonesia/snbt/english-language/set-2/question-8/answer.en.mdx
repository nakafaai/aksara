The pronoun **They** refers to **the researchers**.

The previous sentence introduces the researchers as the people who examined the evolutionary history of the birds. The next sentence says that **they asked** a research question, an action performed by those same people.

The other choices are objects or subjects of the investigation, not the group conducting it.
