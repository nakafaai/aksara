During Ramadan, many adult Muslims fast from dawn until sunset as a religious observance, refraining from food and drink during daylight hours. Meals are eaten at night. The practice does not prescribe a fixed calorie target, so food intake, activity, sleep, and body-weight responses can differ among individuals and settings.
