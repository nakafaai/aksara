The sentence says that marine and coastal ecosystems give communities several kinds of support.

**"Coastal ecosystems provide communities with several forms of value"** preserves both the subject and the breadth of the benefits. The passage later names nutritional, economic, social, and cultural value.

The other choices narrow the claim to business or rainfall, turn it into a recommendation, or claim identical dependence. The third choice is correct.
