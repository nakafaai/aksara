The fourth choice is the best summary: **UNESCO builds peace through international cooperation, shared standards, knowledge, and programs across its fields of expertise**.

The first two paragraphs connect UNESCO's identity and founding rationale to lasting peace. The next two show how that purpose becomes present-day work in education, science, culture, communication, and information.

The correct choice preserves both the purpose and the means. Each distractor either reduces UNESCO to one activity, gives it authority the passage never claims, or reverses its cooperative role.
