The passage repeatedly says technology should help children learn and develop.

Paragraph 2 says digital tools should add to experiences that suit a child's stage of development. Paragraph 3 then asks educators to compare a digital option with simpler traditional materials before choosing it.

The author would therefore agree that **educators should choose technology intentionally so that it supports existing developmental and learning goals**.
