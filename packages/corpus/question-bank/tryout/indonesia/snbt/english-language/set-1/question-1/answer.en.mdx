The passage does not argue that technology is always beneficial or that it should replace teachers. It repeatedly asks what makes its use educationally sound.

The first paragraph introduces mixed evidence, the second discusses learning objectives and teachers, the third explains equity, and the conclusion names four conditions for responsible use. Therefore, the best summary is **the conditions under which technology can support education**.
