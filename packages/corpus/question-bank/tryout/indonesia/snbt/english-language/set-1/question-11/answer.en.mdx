The next paragraph would most likely present **a current UNESCO initiative that puts its mission into practice**.

The final paragraph narrows the discussion from UNESCO's broad mission to emerging issues such as artificial intelligence and digital platforms. Its last sentence explicitly prepares the reader for a detailed example.

An example of a current initiative is therefore the strongest continuation. The other choices introduce subjects that the passage neither develops nor signals.
