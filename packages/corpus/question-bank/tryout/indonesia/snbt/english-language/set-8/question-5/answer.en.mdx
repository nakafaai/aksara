The passage builds paper bridge models, changes the deck to a triangular truss, and measures how many washers each can hold. **Testing a triangular truss in a paper bridge model** names that investigation without turning a preliminary result into an engineering rule. The other titles misuse *truss* as a measurement or detach the limitation from the need for another test.
