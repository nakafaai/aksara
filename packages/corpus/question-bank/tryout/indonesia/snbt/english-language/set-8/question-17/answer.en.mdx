**Putting both greetings on the recurring card preserves their different contexts instead of declaring one wrong, which gives the ending its reconciliatory force.**

The correct option connects the action, recurring object, and response at the ending. That sequence supports the interpretation. The distractors detach the object from the action, give the setting all explanatory weight, or claim an explicitly stated and fixed meaning.
