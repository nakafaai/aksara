A **metaphor** treats one thing as another to create meaning. Here, the card becomes a **doorway between relationships**, rather than a literal entrance.

| Story detail | Meaning in the comparison |
| --- | --- |
| Two greetings on opposite sides | Two ways of addressing people in different social settings. |
| Mei turns the card for the visitor | She chooses the greeting that fits that relationship. |
| Neither side cancels the other | Moving between settings preserves both forms of welcome. |

The definition explains this connection between language and social movement. The card's shape alone would not establish the metaphor without Mei's choices.
