The organisers of a multilingual youth translation club received suggestions from students, families, teachers, and community interpreters. They did not turn the first suggestion into a permanent rule. For one week, the team recorded translations accepted after the first peer review and established a baseline of 19.

The team then tested a shared glossary with examples from each participant. The trial condition reached 31, while the comparison condition reached 20. Schedules and staffing remained stable so that the two conditions would be easier to interpret.

| Stage | Mean value |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | 19 |
| Trial | 31 |
| Comparison | 20 |

The figures were discussed with the people affected by the service. The change appeared worth extending on a limited basis, but one glossary could not capture every regional or family usage. In the report, *register* meant the situation and purpose that shape the most suitable wording.
