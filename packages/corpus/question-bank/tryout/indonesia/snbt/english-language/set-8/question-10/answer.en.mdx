Higher first-review acceptance is observed with the shared glossary, but regional and family usage cannot be compressed into one universal list. **Testing a shared glossary without flattening language** captures both the promising result and the reason for a cautious extension. The other titles focus on a definition, claim permanent adoption, omit comparable outcomes, or deny the follow-up.
