*Self-efficacy* is confidence in one's ability to manage a specific task. Leah develops that confidence when one completed interview gives direction to the next step. The definition therefore helps interpret her action, so **The definition of *self-efficacy* provides a lens for reading the character's specific choice as a response to uncertainty, not as an isolated moral label.**
