A class designed a simulated investigation of root growth in germinating beans. The question was whether placing the seed opening toward the side of a clear container was associated with a change in roots reaching the lower half of the container. Before collecting data, the students agreed to keep seed type, moisture, container size, and observation time the same in every condition.

| Condition | Mean result |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | 9 |
| With the change | 16 |
| Comparison | 10 |

The condition with the change differed from both other conditions. The class did not treat this pattern as proof that the same result would always occur. Its report identified an important limitation: the containers were small and the trial lasted only six days.

The report used the term *gravitropism* to mean a directional growth response to gravity. The students proposed a longer repetition with more units and the same measurement rules. This next step would test the pattern without hiding the uncertainty in the first investigation.
