The organisers of a neighbourhood bus information board received suggestions from passengers, drivers, local shops, and transport planners. They did not turn the first suggestion into a permanent rule. For one week, the team recorded passengers choosing the intended stop without asking and established a baseline of 28.

The team then tested a map showing walking time from each bus stop. The trial condition reached 44, while the comparison condition reached 30. Schedules and staffing remained stable so that the two conditions would be easier to interpret.

| Stage | Mean value |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | 28 |
| Trial | 44 |
| Comparison | 30 |

The figures were discussed with the people affected by the service. The change appeared worth extending on a limited basis, but walking times differed for people with different mobility needs. In the report, *wayfinding* meant information that helps a person understand where they are and how to continue.
