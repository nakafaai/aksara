Irony is a contrast between expectation and reality. An accurate board should help passengers, yet its orientation sends them the wrong way. Rotating a copy makes the same information useful. Thus, **The definition of *irony* clarifies the contrast between an accurate board that misdirects passengers and the same information becoming useful after a simple rotation.**
