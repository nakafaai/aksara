Irony is a contrast between expectation and reality. An accurate board should help passengers, yet its orientation sends them the wrong way. After the rotation, passengers can use the same information correctly. Thus, **The definition of *irony* clarifies the contrast between an accurate board that misdirects passengers and the same information becoming useful after a simple rotation.**
