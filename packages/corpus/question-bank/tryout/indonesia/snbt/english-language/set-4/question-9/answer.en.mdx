*Plain language* is language that can be understood without specialist knowledge. This defines the aim of the revised captions, but it does not prove that every visitor can access them. The measured comparison and testing with screen-reader users are still needed. Thus, **The definition explains the accessibility goal of the revised captions, while the comparison and later screen-reader testing still determine whether that goal is met.**
