A class designed a simulated investigation of the freezing point of salt solutions. The question was whether adding a measured mass of table salt was associated with a change in the temperature at which visible ice first formed. Before collecting data, the students agreed to keep water volume, container material, and thermometer position the same in every condition.

| Condition | Mean result |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | -1 |
| With the change | -4 |
| Comparison | 0 |

The condition with the change differed from both other conditions. The class did not treat this pattern as proof that the same result would always occur. Its report identified an important limitation: the classroom freezer did not cool every shelf at exactly the same rate.

The report used the term *solute* to mean a substance dissolved uniformly in another substance. The students proposed a longer repetition with more units and the same measurement rules. This next step would test the pattern without hiding the uncertainty in the first investigation.


Which title best captures both the central issue and the passage's cautious scope?
