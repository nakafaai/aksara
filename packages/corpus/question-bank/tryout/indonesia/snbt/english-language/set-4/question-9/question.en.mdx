The organisers of a local history display received suggestions from visitors, curators, teachers, and accessibility advisers. They did not turn the first suggestion into a permanent rule. For one week, the team recorded visitors accurately describing one displayed object and established a baseline of 17.

The team then tested short captions that separated observation from interpretation. The trial condition reached 29, while the comparison condition reached 18. Schedules and staffing remained stable so that the two conditions would be easier to interpret.

| Stage | Mean value |

The figures were discussed with the people affected by the service. The change appeared worth extending on a limited basis, but the trial did not include visitors using screen readers. In the report, *plain language* meant language that can be understood without specialist knowledge.
