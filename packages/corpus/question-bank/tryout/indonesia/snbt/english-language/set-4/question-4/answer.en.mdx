A *solute* is the substance dissolved in a solvent. Here, salt is the solute and water is the solvent. The definition clarifies what was changed, but it does not prove causation or remove the shelf-cooling problem. Thus, **By defining *solute*, the report identifies the role of the dissolved salt before interpreting the temperature comparison and the study's limitation.**
