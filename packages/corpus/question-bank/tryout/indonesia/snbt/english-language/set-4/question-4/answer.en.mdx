A *solute* is the substance dissolved in a solvent. Here, salt is the solute and water is the solvent. The definition clarifies what was changed, but it does not prove causation or remove the shelf-cooling problem. Thus, **The definition of *solute* identifies salt's role in the solution, while the temperature comparison and its limitations determine what the results support.**
