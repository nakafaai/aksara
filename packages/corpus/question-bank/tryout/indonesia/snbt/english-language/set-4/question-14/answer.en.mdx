*Co-design* means designing with the people who will use the result. Amina is not merely asked for an opinion after the map is finished; she helps test the route, mark failures, and shape the sign changes. Thus, **The definition of *co-design* explains why Amina's participation in testing and revising the map is part of the design process rather than an afterthought.**
