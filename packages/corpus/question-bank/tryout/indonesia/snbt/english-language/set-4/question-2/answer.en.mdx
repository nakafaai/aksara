The salted samples do produce a mean of -4°C. That value comes from one classroom freezer whose shelves cool unevenly, so it cannot stand for every freezer. The interpretation that preserves both result and limit is **The salted samples produced a mean of -4°C under the stated test conditions; this is the trial result, not a claim about every freezer.**
