The conflict is whether the repair should be concealed. Miles chooses the **contrasting gold seam**, then the ending shows it producing a **line of light** when the lamp is switched on. That final image connects his choice with the new meaning of repair.

The repair becomes **evidence of care** instead of damage to hide. This interpretation follows the choice and ending together.
