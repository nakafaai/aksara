The organisers of a community repair café received suggestions from visitors, volunteer repairers, tool lenders, and venue staff. They did not turn the first suggestion into a permanent rule. For one week, the team recorded repairs completed without returning for a missing tool and established a baseline of 14.

The team then tested a card listing the tools needed for each repair. The trial condition reached 25, while the comparison condition reached 15. Schedules and staffing remained stable so that the two conditions would be easier to interpret.

| Stage | Mean value |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | 14 |
| Trial | 25 |
| Comparison | 15 |

The figures were discussed with the people affected by the service. The change appeared worth extending on a limited basis, but repair difficulty differed greatly between objects. In the report, *product-life extension* meant keeping a product useful for longer through maintenance or repair.
