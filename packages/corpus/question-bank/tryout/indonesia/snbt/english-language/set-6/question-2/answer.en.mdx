The reported mean at 37°C is 31, while the sample volume, pH, and reaction time are controlled. That gives the result a clear experimental context. However, a colour scale estimated by eye cannot provide a highly precise concentration. The interpretation that preserves both facts is **The mixture held at 37°C produced a mean of 31 under the stated sample controls; an eye-estimated colour scale keeps the result provisional.**
