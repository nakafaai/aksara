The class measures colour change as an indicator of enzyme activity. Defining an enzyme as a catalyst explains the biological process the indicator is meant to represent. The definition does not make the eye-based scale more precise or prove a concentration. Its role is **The definition identifies the biological process represented by the colour-change measure without turning that indirect measure into proof of concentration.**
