Theo refuses to hide an uncertain date behind a confident guess. By documenting the uncertainty, he leaves room for a visitor's verifiable clue. **Documenting uncertainty so evidence can follow** captures the choice and its later value. The other titles describe waiting, hiding evidence, or a definition with no connection to the action.
