The organisers of a community food pantry received suggestions from volunteers, households, donors, and food-safety advisers. They did not turn the first suggestion into a permanent rule. For one week, the team recorded packages used in the intended order and established a baseline of 32.

The team then tested shelf labels showing the date each package entered. The trial condition reached 47, while the comparison condition reached 33. Schedules and staffing remained stable so that the two conditions would be easier to interpret.

| Stage | Mean value |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | 32 |
| Trial | 47 |
| Comparison | 33 |

The figures were discussed with the people affected by the service. The change appeared worth extending on a limited basis, but package demand changed with school holidays and local events. In the report, *stock rotation* meant using older stock before newer stock when both remain suitable.


Which interpretation combines an explicitly reported result with the passage's appropriate evidential limit?
