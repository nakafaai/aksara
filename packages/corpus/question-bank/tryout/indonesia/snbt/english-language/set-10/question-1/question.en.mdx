A class designed a simulated investigation of voltage in simple cell arrangements. The question was whether connecting two cells in series was associated with a change in the measured voltage. Before collecting data, the students agreed to keep cell type, wire length, meter, and connection time the same in every condition.

| Condition | Mean result |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline | 1.4 |
| With the change | 2.9 |
| Comparison | 1.5 |

The condition with the change differed from both other conditions. The class did not treat this pattern as proof that the same result would always occur. Its report identified an important limitation: internal resistance and cell age were not measured separately.

The report used the term *potential difference* to mean electric potential difference between two points. The students proposed a longer repetition with more units and the same measurement rules. This next step would test the pattern without hiding the uncertainty in the first investigation.
