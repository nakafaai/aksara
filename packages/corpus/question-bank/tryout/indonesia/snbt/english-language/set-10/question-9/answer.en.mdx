The phrase “**within a stated range of activity**” is the key. For example, a venue fee may stay fixed for up to <InlineMath math="200" /> attendees and then change when a **larger room** is needed. Fixed therefore describes a cost inside a defined range. The term fixed does not promise that the price can never move.

The definition helps classify a cost. It does not by itself establish affordability or the final quoted price.
