# Given

**The Northmoor table lists** installed capacity (MW), potential output, curtailed energy (GWh), and line connection for four wind farms.

# Asked

Which numerical and logical statements about the four wind farms can be concluded.

# Solution

**Three statements need a** calculation and two need a careful reading. Check each one on its own.

> Before comparing two wind farms, always check whether the claim asks about total energy, percentage lost, or loss per unit of size.

## Verifying each calculation and scope boundary

Statement <InlineMath math="1" /> is about energy **delivered**, which is the possible energy minus the curtailed energy. Brackenmoor delivered <InlineMath math="800 - 160 = 640" /> GWh. Fellside delivered <InlineMath math="200 - 50 = 150" /> GWh and Gullrock also <InlineMath math="150" /> GWh, so together <InlineMath math="300" /> GWh. Twice that is <InlineMath math="600" />, and <InlineMath math="640" /> is more. Yes.

Statement <InlineMath math="2" /> goes too far. Saltmarsh lost only <InlineMath math="40" /> GWh, but losing a little isn't the same as the line never being full. Some of that loss could come from a full line on a few nights, and the table can't tell you which nights. No.

Statement <InlineMath math="3" /> divides by capacity. Fellside: <InlineMath math="50 \div 80 = 0.625" /> GWh per MW. Brackenmoor: <InlineMath math="160 \div 320 = 0.5" /> GWh per MW. Fellside lost more for each megawatt. Yes.

Statement <InlineMath math="4" /> is the trap. Gullrock lost <InlineMath math="50 \div 200 = 25\%" />, but Fellside on the North line also lost <InlineMath math="25\%" />. **The same share isn't a larger one**. No.

Statement <InlineMath math="5" /> needs Fellside's share, <InlineMath math="25\%" />. Brackenmoor at that share would lose <InlineMath math="25\% \times 800 = 200" /> GWh. The other three farms together lost 

<BlockMath math="50 + 40 + 50 = 140" />

 GWh, and <InlineMath math="200" /> is more. Yes.

# Answer

Statements <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="5" /> follow from exact arithmetic, while <InlineMath math="2" /> overreaches and <InlineMath math="4" /> ignores a tie.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Brackenmoor delivered more than twice Fellside and Gullrock together | Yes |
| The South line was never full | No |
| Fellside lost more per megawatt than Brackenmoor | Yes |
| Gullrock lost a larger share than any North farm | No |
| Brackenmoor at Fellside's share would exceed the other three together | Yes |
