# Given

**The campaign group claims** that bigger wind farms waste more energy, measured by total curtailed energy in GWh alongside installed capacity in MW.

# Asked

Which pairs of wind farms contradict the campaign group's claim even using its own GWh measure.

# Solution

The claim is "the bigger the farm, the more it wastes", and the question fixes the measure: **the amount of energy curtailed**, in GWh. A pair goes against the claim only when the smaller farm had more curtailed than the bigger one. Size means installed capacity.

> A rule saying bigger buckets always spill more water breaks the moment a tiny cup spills more water than a giant bucket.

## Checking every pair by size and energy curtailed

| Pair | Bigger farm | Smaller farm | Against the claim? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gullrock and Saltmarsh | Saltmarsh, <InlineMath math="300" /> MW, <InlineMath math="40" /> GWh | Gullrock, <InlineMath math="70" /> MW, <InlineMath math="50" /> GWh | Yes |
| Brackenmoor and Saltmarsh | Brackenmoor, <InlineMath math="320" /> MW, <InlineMath math="160" /> GWh | Saltmarsh, <InlineMath math="300" /> MW, <InlineMath math="40" /> GWh | No |
| Fellside and Saltmarsh | Saltmarsh, <InlineMath math="300" /> MW, <InlineMath math="40" /> GWh | Fellside, <InlineMath math="80" /> MW, <InlineMath math="50" /> GWh | Yes |
| Fellside and Brackenmoor | Brackenmoor, <InlineMath math="320" /> MW, <InlineMath math="160" /> GWh | Fellside, <InlineMath math="80" /> MW, <InlineMath math="50" /> GWh | No |
| Gullrock and Brackenmoor | Brackenmoor, <InlineMath math="320" /> MW, <InlineMath math="160" /> GWh | Gullrock, <InlineMath math="70" /> MW, <InlineMath math="50" /> GWh | No |

- Gullrock and Saltmarsh: the much smaller farm lost more energy. Against the claim.
- Brackenmoor and Saltmarsh: the bigger farm lost more. That fits the claim.
- Fellside and Saltmarsh: again the much smaller farm lost more. Against the claim.
- Fellside and Brackenmoor: Fellside lost more **for its size**, but less in amount. On the campaign's measure, it fits.
- Gullrock and Brackenmoor: the smaller farm lost less. That fits.

# Answer

Only **Gullrock with Saltmarsh** and **Fellside with Saltmarsh** show a much smaller farm curtailing more energy than a larger one.

Two pairs go against the claim on its own measure: Gullrock with Saltmarsh, and Fellside with Saltmarsh.
