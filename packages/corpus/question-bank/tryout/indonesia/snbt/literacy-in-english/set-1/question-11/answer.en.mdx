# Given

Northmoor wind-farm figures for <InlineMath math="2025" />:
- **North line**: Fellside (<InlineMath math="200" /> GWh potential, <InlineMath math="50" /> GWh curtailed) and Brackenmoor (<InlineMath math="800" /> GWh potential, <InlineMath math="160" /> GWh curtailed).
- **South line**: Saltmarsh (<InlineMath math="800" /> GWh potential, <InlineMath math="40" /> GWh curtailed) and Gullrock (<InlineMath math="200" /> GWh potential, <InlineMath math="50" /> GWh curtailed).

# Asked

How much more energy the four farms would have delivered in <InlineMath math="2025" /> if a doubled North line cut each North farm's loss to the same **share** of potential energy that Saltmarsh lost.

# Solution

Compute **Saltmarsh's curtailed share** first, apply that percentage to each North farm's own potential energy, and add the energy saved across both North farms while keeping South farms unchanged.

> Matching Saltmarsh's five percent share scales each North farm's new loss to five percent of its own potential rather than a flat forty gigawatt-hours.

## Computing Saltmarsh share and saved energy

From the table, <Highlight>Saltmarsh</Highlight> curtailed <InlineMath math="40" /> GWh out of <InlineMath math="800" /> GWh potential:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Saltmarsh curtailed share: } \frac{40}{800} = 5\% \\
\text{Fellside new loss: } 5\% \times 200 = 10\text{ GWh}, \qquad \text{Saved: } 50 - 10 = 40\text{ GWh} \\
  \text{Brackenmoor new loss: } 5\% \times 800 = 40\text{ GWh}, \qquad \text{Saved: } 160 - 40 = 120\text{ GWh} \\
\text{Extra energy delivered: } 40 + 120 = 160\text{ GWh}
\end{aligned}" />

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="120" /> GWh** uses the entire South line's share (<InlineMath math="90 \div 1{,}000 = 9\%" />) instead of Saltmarsh's share.
- **<InlineMath math="130" /> GWh** assigns Saltmarsh's flat <InlineMath math="40" /> GWh loss to both North farms instead of its percentage share.
- **<InlineMath math="210" /> GWh** assumes zero curtailment for the North farms.
- **<InlineMath math="60" /> GWh** applies the region-wide <InlineMath math="15\%" /> average, which still includes the North farms' own heavy losses.

# Answer

The four wind farms would have delivered **<InlineMath math="160" /> GWh** more energy.
