The third paragraph explains that digital education must account for different learners' access, costs, disabilities, and support needs.

The word **equitable** means fair while recognizing that people face different circumstances. *Appropriate* concerns suitability, *sustainable* concerns long-term viability, and *digital* only names the medium. Therefore, **equitable** is the best answer.
