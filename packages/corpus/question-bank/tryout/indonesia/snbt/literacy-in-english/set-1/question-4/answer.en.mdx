Check each statement on its own. The question is whether the passage gives you enough to conclude it.

Statement 1 joins two paragraphs. Paragraph 2 says that under opponent coding "every extra channel should make the comparisons finer still". Paragraph 1 gives the shrimp <InlineMath math="12" /> kinds of colour receptor against <InlineMath math="3" /> in people. Put together, that model predicts finer separation than a person manages. Yes.

Statement 2 is the trap. Failing at gaps of <InlineMath math="12" /> to <InlineMath math="25" /> nanometres shows poor discrimination. Statement 4 uses paragraph 3. Honeybees, "which have far fewer receptor types than the shrimp", did better. Fewer types beat more types, so the count alone doesn't decide how finely an animal separates colours. Yes.
