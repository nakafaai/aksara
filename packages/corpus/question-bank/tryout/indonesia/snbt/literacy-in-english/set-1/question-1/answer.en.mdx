To find what a passage is primarily concerned with, look for the idea that every paragraph serves. Paragraph one says most octopus neurons sit in the arms. Paragraph two shows arms acting locally. Paragraph three explains why a boneless body benefits from **delegating control**. Paragraph four adds the suckers, and paragraph five concludes that the brain sets goals while the arms handle execution. Together they explain **how control is shared** between brain and arms.

- The neuron count is compared with "most other invertebrates", not with all invertebrates, and it is only the opening fact.
- Paragraph five rejects the idea that the arms behave like independent animals.
- Separated arms appear as evidence in one paragraph, and the passage does not describe how scientists separate them.
- Soft robots appear only in the final sentence, as a side note about engineers.

So the passage is mainly about how an octopus divides the control of movement between its brain and its arms.
