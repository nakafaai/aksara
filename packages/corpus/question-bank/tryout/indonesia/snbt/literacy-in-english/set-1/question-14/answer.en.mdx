# Given

**The operator plans to** double North-line capacity by <InlineMath math="2028" />, while noting a three-week February repair shutdown and low region-wide demand on windy nights.

# Asked

Which conclusions about the <InlineMath math="2028" /> North-line upgrade are supported by the passage and table.

# Solution

**The operator's two cautions** in the last paragraph decide most of these, so hold them in mind. First, the North line "was shut for repairs for <InlineMath math="3" /> weeks in February", so the North figures for <InlineMath math="2025" /> "may be higher than in a typical year". Second, a bigger line "will not help on nights when demand across the whole region is too low", and such nights are "typically" windy ones.

> Widening a water pipe stops jams inside the pipe, yet it still cannot push water into houses whose taps are all turned off.

## Checking each conclusion against the operator two cautions

Statement <InlineMath math="1" />. Saltmarsh's share would follow only if the line were the one difference between farms. But Gullrock, on the South line, lost <InlineMath math="25\%" />, so the line isn't the whole story. No.

Statement <InlineMath math="2" />. The <InlineMath math="210" /> GWh the North farms lost in <InlineMath math="2025" /> includes losses from the repair and maybe from low demand. The plan fixes only the full-line part. No.

Statement <InlineMath math="3" />. It sounds right, since the North line is "often full on windy evenings". But low demand comes "typically on windy nights", and a bigger line doesn't help then. So **some windy-night losses would stay**. No.

Statement <InlineMath math="4" />. The <InlineMath math="2025" /> North losses mix a full line, a repair shutdown and possibly low demand, and only the first is what the plan fixes. Yes.

Statement <InlineMath math="5" />. That's the operator's own second caution. Yes.

# Answer

Only the last two cautious conclusions hold because repair outages skewed <InlineMath math="2025" /> and bigger wires cannot fix low demand.

| All windy-evening losses removed | No |
| Gain can't be read off the 2025 figures | Yes |
| No help on low-demand nights | Yes |
