Read the word in its sentence: the detailed work is "computed within the arm itself rather than dictated move by move from the brain". The phrase **rather than** sets up a contrast between two sources of control: the arm working things out, or the brain giving orders for each move. In this sense, "dictated" means **commanded** or imposed.

- "Spoken aloud" is the meaning of dictating a letter to a typist, which does not fit a brain controlling an arm.
- "Recorded" describes keeping information, not directing movement.
- "Predicted" describes forecasting a movement, not ordering it.
- "Interrupted" would mean the brain stops the arm, which is not what the contrast describes.

So "dictated" here means commanded.
