Paragraph 2 makes two connected statements. Developers pass the cost of parking on to tenants and buyers, "including those who do not own a car at all", and minimums therefore require households without cars to **subsidise** those with them. A household that subsidises parking it does not use is **paying for spaces it does not need**.

- The passage says the opposite of an exemption: car-free households are included in the cost.
- No discount for giving up a space is mentioned.
- Paragraph 3 says spread-out land use increases the need for cars, but paragraph 2 does not say car-free households are pushed to buy one.
- The passage implies car-free households pay more than they should, not less than car owners.

So, under parking minimums, a household without a car helps pay for parking spaces it does not use.
