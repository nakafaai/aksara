# Given

Grandma describes her rice scale as <Highlight>honest for some, and generous for others</Highlight> using a **small brass screw** at the back to shift the empty-tray needle between zero and just below zero.

# Asked

Which pair of moments in the story illustrates both halves of Grandma's description?

# Solution

Match **one concrete scene to "honest"** (exact zero setting) and **one concrete scene to "generous"** (below-zero setting giving extra rice).

> Turning the screw so the empty needle sits exactly on zero gives the well-off customer an honest weight, while turning it back below zero gives the nurse's daughter generous extra rice.

## Evidence for both halves

- **Honest half**: When the man in a good coat arrives, <Highlight>Grandma gave the screw a quarter turn, and the needle sat exactly on zero</Highlight>, ensuring an exact weight.
- **Generous half**: When the nurse's daughter comes with coins she counted twice, <Highlight>Grandma turned it back</Highlight> to just below zero, so the scale under-reads and gives more rice than paid for.

## Why the other options fail

- **Empty tray below zero plus turning it back** shows the generous setting twice and omits the honest setting.
- **Inspector's clean record** occurs on closed Mondays, not as an act of generosity toward customers.
- **Calling out weights early and narrator's wrong turns** show neither half, while the narrator's mistakes actually short-changed customers until Grandma fixed them.
- **Fish seller's wink and quarter turn** pair market gossip with only the honest half.

# Answer

**The needle sits exactly on zero for the man in the good coat, and Grandma turns the screw back for the nurse's daughter**.
