The phrase "do not so much X as Y" means **Y describes the situation better than X**. Here X is "resist" (stand up to the conditions) and Y is "wait them out" (stay passive until the conditions end). The sentences around it support this reading: the tardigrade dries into a tun, its metabolism nearly stops, and it becomes active again only when water returns. The statement means tardigrades survive by **becoming inactive until conditions improve**.

- Moving away would be escaping, and the passage never says tardigrades travel to avoid danger.
- Nothing says tardigrades grow stronger with longer exposure.
- Tardigrades live in films of water, but when these dry out they survive in the tun state, so they do not avoid harsh conditions.
- Paragraph three says many tardigrades produce little trehalose.

So the statement suggests that tardigrades survive by becoming inactive until conditions improve, not by withstanding harsh conditions while active.
