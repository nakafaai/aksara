Paragraph 4 opens by stating the manufacturers' case, then the author answers it: "These concerns are real, but they do not justify blanket restrictions." The first half **accepts** the concerns, and the second half **limits** what they justify. The author then proposes other ways to protect safety and security. That is a partly accepting attitude.

- The author does not conclude that only manufacturers should repair devices, which is the practice the essay opposes.
- Calling the concerns "real" rules out a dismissive attitude.
- The author takes a clear position, so the attitude is not uncertain.
- A whole paragraph addresses the concerns, so the author is not indifferent.

So the author's attitude is partly accepting: the concerns are real but do not justify blanket restrictions.
