Riverton Public Library has always closed at 6 p.m. on weekdays. In a council survey, many residents who work until five or six said they could visit only on Saturdays. In response, the library ran a three-month pilot from June to August in which it stayed open until 9 p.m. from Monday to Friday. Staff counted visitors as they entered and recorded the time of each visit.

The librarians also handed a short questionnaire to evening visitors. Of those who answered, 45 percent said they had not visited the library at all in the previous twelve months. Keeping the library open longer cost about 9,000 dollars over the three months, mostly for extra staff.

At a council meeting, the head librarian recommended making the evening hours permanent. One council member was less convinced. She pointed out that daytime visits had fallen during the pilot and suggested that many evening visitors were simply regular users who had changed the time of their visits. The head librarian replied that the pilot took place during the school holidays, when many families travel, and that daytime visits usually dip in the summer.
