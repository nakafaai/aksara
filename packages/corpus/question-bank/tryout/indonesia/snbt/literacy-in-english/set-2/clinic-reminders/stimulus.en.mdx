In 2024 the Westbank health clinic began offering text-message reminders the day before each appointment. Patients could sign up at the front desk, and about a third did. At the end of the year the clinic manager shared the figures below and proposed sending reminders automatically to every patient, saying this would "cut missed appointments by more than half".

A nurse pointed out that the patients who signed up in 2024 had already been more reliable before reminders existed. In 2023 they missed <InlineMath math="5\%" /> of their appointments, while the patients who later did not sign up missed <InlineMath math="11\%" />.
