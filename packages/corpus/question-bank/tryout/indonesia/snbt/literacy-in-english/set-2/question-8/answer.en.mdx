Look for the author's own statement, and reject any point that **turns a qualified claim into an absolute one** or assigns a duty to the wrong party.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent shops are often small local businesses | Yes | Paragraph 3: independent repair shops, "which are often small local businesses". |
| Safety concerns have no basis | No | Paragraph 4: "These concerns are real", even though they do not justify blanket restrictions. |
| Instructions reduce poor repairs | Yes | Paragraph 4: proper instructions "reduce botched repairs rather than encourage them". |
| The EU requires owners to repair | No | Paragraph 5: the EU rule applies to **makers**, who must keep parts available, and such rules "do not force anyone to repair a product". |

The fourth statement moves the obligation from manufacturers to owners, which reverses the point of the last paragraph.
