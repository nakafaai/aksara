# Given

Five proposed readings interpret **three key remarks** in the story: "Wages go up when they remember", "Watch her hands, not the needle", and "Honest for some, and generous for others".

# Asked

Is each reading supported by the story? Choose Yes or No for each reading.

# Solution

Test every reading against **its surrounding context** so literal misreadings (late paydays, free rice, cheating hands) are separated from the story's actual figurative meaning.

> Figurative remarks in the story contrast monthly price rises with rare wage rises and define a scale rule that allows giving extra but forbids giving less.

## Reading by reading check

| Reading | Verdict | Contextual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" />. Employers often forget to pay wages on time | **No** | The pairing <Highlight>Prices go up every month</Highlight> and <Highlight>Wages go up when they remember</Highlight> contrasts rate of increases, not late paydays. |
| <InlineMath math="2" />. Warns that Grandma cheats customers by sleight of hand | **No** | Grandma never cheats anyone, while her two settings give either exact weight or extra rice, never less. |
| <InlineMath math="3" />. Pay rises come rarely and late, falling behind prices | **Yes** | Personifying wages as rising only "when they remember" directly contrasts slow, irregular pay rises against monthly price hikes. |
| <InlineMath math="4" />. Grandma gives some customers rice without taking money | **No** | The nurse's daughter still pays with <Highlight>a handful of coins</Highlight>, while generous means extra rice, not free rice. |
| <InlineMath math="5" />. Rule under which no customer gets less than the needle shows | **Yes** | Both settings (zero and below zero) never under-deliver, and Grandma immediately sends a second cone when the narrator's error gives less. |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, and <InlineMath math="4" />** are **No**.
