# Given

Five statements about the speed-camera programme must be judged on whether **both** Text A and Text B would accept them.

# Asked

Which statements would both writers accept? Choose Yes or No for each statement.

# Solution

Mark **Yes** only where **shared agreement** exists across both texts, while statements backed by only one writer or by neither writer must be marked **No**.

> Both writers agree that crashes dropped at the sites and that fixed cameras genuinely prevent some crashes, while disagreeing sharply on how large that causal share really is.

## Statement by statement verification

| Statement | Verdict | Text A and Text B check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" />. Comparing sites with the city measures what cameras achieved | **No** | Claimed by Text A, but Text B says the counts cannot show <Highlight>how much of the fall the cameras caused</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="2" />. Injury crashes at camera sites fell after installation | **Yes** | Text A cites the drop from <InlineMath math="12" /> to <InlineMath math="7" />, and Text B opens <Highlight>Nobody should doubt that the crash counts at the camera sites went down</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="3" />. The <InlineMath math="60" /> new sites should wait until the first <InlineMath math="40" /> are measured | **No** | Text A plans the <InlineMath math="60" /> sites next year, and Text B asks for proper measurement without demanding a freeze. |
| <InlineMath math="4" />. Cameras probably prevented at least some injury crashes | **Yes** | Text A credits safer roads to cameras, and Text B affirms the effect <Highlight>remained real</Highlight> and <Highlight>None of this is an argument against cameras</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="5" />. The drop is larger than cameras alone explain | **No** | Argued by Text B, but Text A attributes the entire gap above the city trend to the cameras. |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **No**.
