A percentage increase compares the change with **the starting value**, here the total before the pilot. The total rose from 510 to 590 visitors per weekday.

<BlockMath math="\frac{590 - 510}{510} \times 100\% = \frac{80}{510} \times 100\% \approx 15.7\%" />

That is about 16 percent.

- About 14 percent divides the change by 590, the total during the pilot, so the base is wrong.
- About 22 percent divides the evening count, 130, by 590. It ignores the drop in daytime visits.
- About 25 percent divides 130 by 510, again treating every evening visit as an addition.
- 80 is the number of extra visitors, not a percentage.

So the average total rose by about 16 percent.
