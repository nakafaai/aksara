# Given

Between <InlineMath math="2023" /> and <InlineMath math="2024" />, the signed-up group's missed rate changed from **<InlineMath math="5%" /> to <InlineMath math="4%" />**, while the no-reminder group changed from **<InlineMath math="11%" /> to <InlineMath math="10%" />**.

# Asked

Using both the table and the nurse's figures, what is the best estimate of how many percentage points the reminders lowered the signed-up group's missed rate?

# Solution

Compare the **year-on-year change** in the reminded group against the **year-on-year change** in the unreminded group (difference-in-differences).

> Subtracting the one-point drop seen without reminders from the one-point drop seen with reminders leaves an estimated reminder effect of zero percentage points.

## Difference in differences calculation

| Group | <InlineMath math="2023" /> missed rate | <InlineMath math="2024" /> missed rate | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Signed up (reminders) | <InlineMath math="5\%" /> | <InlineMath math="4\%" /> | <InlineMath math="-1" /> point |
| Did not sign up (no reminders) | <InlineMath math="11\%" /> | <InlineMath math="10\%" /> | <InlineMath math="-1" /> point |

Tracking **each group's change** from <InlineMath math="2023" /> to <InlineMath math="2024" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{Signed-up change} &= 4\% - 5\% = -1 \text{ percentage point} \\ \text{No-reminder change} &= 10\% - 11\% = -1 \text{ percentage point} \\ \text{Estimated reminder effect} &= (-1) - (-1) = 0 \text{ percentage points} \end{aligned}" />

Because the unreminded group fell by the exact same <InlineMath math="1" /> percentage point, **none of the signed-up drop** can be attributed to reminders.

## Why the other options fail

- **<InlineMath math="6" />** is the **cross-group gap** in <InlineMath math="2024" />, which already stood at <InlineMath math="6" /> points in <InlineMath math="2023" /> before reminders existed.
- **<InlineMath math="1" />** counts the signed-up group's raw fall without subtracting the <InlineMath math="1" />-point drop that happened without reminders.
- **<InlineMath math="5" />** compares the entire clinic in <InlineMath math="2023" /> (<InlineMath math="9%" />) with only the reliable subgroup in <InlineMath math="2024" /> (<InlineMath math="4%" />).
- **<InlineMath math="2" />** adds both groups' falls instead of subtracting the control baseline.

# Answer

**<InlineMath math="0" />, because the group without reminders fell by the same amount**.
