# Given

The clinic manager compares <InlineMath math="4%" /> (signed up) with <InlineMath math="10%" /> (not signed up) in <InlineMath math="2024" /> and claims automatic reminders would <Highlight>cut missed appointments by more than half</Highlight>. The nurse adds <InlineMath math="2023" /> baselines: **<InlineMath math="5%" />** for future sign-ups and **<InlineMath math="11%" />** for the rest.

# Asked

Which pair of facts from the passage, taken together, best undermines the manager's claim?

# Solution

To break the manager's causal claim, pair **pre-existing self-selection** in <InlineMath math="2023" /> with **identical improvement without reminders** in <InlineMath math="2024" />.

> The six-point gap between the two groups already existed before reminders began, and the unreminded group improved by the exact same single point, leaving zero evidence of a reminder effect.

## Why both facts work together

- **Already more reliable in <InlineMath math="2023" />**: The nurse notes <Highlight>the patients who signed up in 2024 had already been more reliable before reminders existed</Highlight> (<InlineMath math="5%" /> vs. <InlineMath math="11%" />), explaining the entire <InlineMath math="6" />-point gap via self-selection.
- **Equal improvement without reminders**: Both groups fell by <InlineMath math="1" /> percentage point (<InlineMath math="5%" /> to <InlineMath math="4%" /> and <InlineMath math="11%" /> to <InlineMath math="10%" />), showing reminders added no extra drop.

## Why the other options fail

- **Same total appointments in both years** says nothing about whether reminders worked.
- **Signed-up group being one third of appointments** does not alter what the missed rates show.
- **Overall rate falling one point** paired with signed-up patients missing less does not refute causality, because the manager relies on that very difference.
- **Less reliable in <InlineMath math="2023" />** directly reverses the nurse's report, and day-before timing is never critiqued.

# Answer

**The signed-up patients were already more reliable in 2023, and the group without reminders improved by as much as the group with them**.
