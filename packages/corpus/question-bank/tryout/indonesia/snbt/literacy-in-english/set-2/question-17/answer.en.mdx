The key word is "**until**": the book "sat in the collection without attracting attention, until a Korean librarian working there identified its printing date and method". Attention began once its date and method were **recognised**, which implies that the lack of attention came from no one having recognised them before.

- Nothing says the library hid the book. A Korean librarian worked there and found it.
- Language is not mentioned as a barrier. What changed was the identification of date and method.
- The book was acquired in the late nineteenth century and sat in the collection "for decades", so it did not arrive shortly before the 1970s.
- The passage says nothing about the diplomat refusing to show it.

So *Jikji* went unnoticed mainly because no one had yet recognised when and how it was printed.
