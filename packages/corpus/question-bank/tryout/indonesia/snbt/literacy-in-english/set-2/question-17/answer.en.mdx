# Given

Five factual and interpretive statements about Mrs Adeyemi's rice stall must be checked as **Yes** or **No** directly against the narrative.

# Asked

Which of the five statements are supported by the story? Choose Yes or No for each.

# Solution

Check each claim against **its exact scene** and connect separated details where needed (the noon empty-tray test and the afternoon screw turn).

> Linking the noon needle check with the quarter turn for the man in the good coat confirms Grandma shifts the scale from just below zero to zero, while the Monday inspections explain its spotless record.

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence from the story |
| --- | --- | --- |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="3" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **No**.
