Each statement points to a different moment, and two need you to connect moments that are far apart.

Statement 1. For the man, "the needle sat exactly on zero", so the scale weighed correctly. He got what the needle showed, not less. No.

Statement 2. The market is on Thursdays, and the inspector "comes on Mondays, when the market is shut". His record shows the scale at a time when nobody is turning the screw. Yes.

Statement 3. At noon the empty tray showed "a hair below zero". Before the man's rice, Grandma gives the screw a quarter turn, "and the needle sat exactly on zero". Put together, she moves it **from just below zero to zero**. Yes.

Statement 4. The narrator says it "took me the rest of the afternoon to work out why". Her understanding comes slowly. No.

Statement 5. When the narrator's wrong turns gave customers less rice, Grandma "made me run after them both with a second cone". She won't let any customer lose. No.

So statements 2 and 3 are Yes, and statements 1, 4 and 5 are No.
