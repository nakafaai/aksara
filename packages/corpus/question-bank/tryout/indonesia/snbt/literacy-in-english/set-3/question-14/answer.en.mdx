# Given

Using the route rates (**Northline**: <InlineMath math="70\%" /> urban, <InlineMath math="90\%" /> rural and **Southway**: <InlineMath math="65\%" /> urban, <InlineMath math="85\%" /> rural) and trip counts (<InlineMath math="800" /> and <InlineMath math="200" />), five conclusions must be tested.

# Asked

Which conclusions can be drawn from the passage and the table? Select all that apply.

# Solution

Check each claim with **direct table arithmetic**, including re-weighting **Southway's route rates** onto Northline's <InlineMath math="800" />-urban / <InlineMath math="200" />-rural mix.

> Applying Southway's own punctuality rates to Northline's heavily urban route mix drops Southway's overall score to sixty-nine percent, five points below Northline's seventy-four percent.

## Checking all five conclusions

| Conclusion | Verdict | Table verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rural trips were on time more often than urban trips for both operators | **True** | Northline: <InlineMath math="90\% > 70\%" />, Southway: <InlineMath math="85\% > 65\%" />. |
| On Northline's <InlineMath math="800" /> urban / <InlineMath math="200" /> rural mix, Southway would score about <InlineMath math="69\%" /> | **True** | <InlineMath math="800 \times 65\% + 200 \times 85\% = 520 + 170 = 690" />, giving <InlineMath math="\frac{690}{1{,}000} = 69\%" />. |
| Southway's drivers take more care than Northline's | **False** | Driver care is never measured, and Southway actually trails on every route type. |
| Northline ran more trips on time in total than Southway | **False** | Northline had <InlineMath math="560 + 180 = 740" /> on-time trips, fewer than Southway's <InlineMath math="130 + 680 = 810" />. |
| Each operator ran <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> trips last year | **True** | Northline ran <InlineMath math="800 + 200 = 1{,}000" /> and Southway ran <InlineMath math="200 + 800 = 1{,}000" />. |

# Answer

- **For both operators, rural trips were on time more often than urban trips**.
- **Run at its own rates on Northline's mix of <InlineMath math="800" /> urban and <InlineMath math="200" /> rural trips, Southway would have an overall rate of about <InlineMath math="69\%" />**.
- **Each operator ran <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> trips last year**.
