Paragraph 4 lists what the author wants: public bodies could "fund trials in sectors that have so far been left out", require employers to share data, and "publish the results whether they are good or bad". Care homes are one of the sectors paragraph 3 says were missing. A **publicly funded trial in care homes** with **results published either way** matches every part of that proposal.

- Closing bus companies every Friday ignores the author's point that some services must run every hour of every day.
- Four ten-hour days is a compressed week, which the author doubts, and it is also a rule for everyone.
- Banning trials without positive results contradicts the call to publish results whether good or bad.
- Stopping data collection contradicts the call for participating employers to share their data.

So the author would most likely support a publicly funded trial in care homes with results published whatever they show.
