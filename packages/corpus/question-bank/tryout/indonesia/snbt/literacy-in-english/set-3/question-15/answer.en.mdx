# Given

The regional transport authority promised to renew the contract of **the more punctual operator**. Northline was assigned most urban routes when <Highlight>the network was divided ten years ago</Highlight>, yet beats Southway on both urban (<InlineMath math="70\%" /> vs. <InlineMath math="65\%" />) and rural (<InlineMath math="90\%" /> vs. <InlineMath math="85\%" />) routes.

# Asked

If the authority keeps its promise, which operator and justification are best supported?

# Solution

Compare operators **like with like** on each route category rather than letting an inherited route allocation distort the decision.

> Because Northline outperforms Southway on both urban and rural routes and trails overall solely due to its heavier urban assignment, Northline is genuinely the more punctual operator.

## Comparing punctuality fairly

On city routes, Northline beats Southway (<InlineMath math="70\%" /> vs. <InlineMath math="65\%" />). On rural routes, Northline again beats Southway (<InlineMath math="90\%" /> vs. <InlineMath math="85\%" />). Northline's lower <InlineMath math="74\%" /> overall rate happens only because <Highlight>Northline was given most of the urban routes</Highlight>, where congestion slows buses down.

Therefore, keeping the promise requires choosing **Northline** for leading on both kinds of route.

## Why the other options fail

- **Southway by seven points overall** mistakes an inherited rural route advantage for greater punctuality.
- **Southway by total on-time trips** (<InlineMath math="810" /> vs. <InlineMath math="740" />) is the exact same distorted overall metric.
- **Northline merely for running harder urban trips** picks the right winner for an incomplete reason: running harder trips alone does not prove punctuality without beating Southway on each route type.
- **Neither can be compared** is false: operators are straightforwardly comparable within each matching route type.

# Answer

**Northline, because it was more punctual on both kinds of route, and its lower overall rate comes from being given most of the urban routes**.
