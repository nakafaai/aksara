# Given

Five statements about the city's <InlineMath math="10" />-cent carrier-bag charge must be judged on whether **both** Text A and Text B accept them.

# Asked

Which of the five statements do both writers accept? Choose Yes or No for each.

# Solution

Mark **Yes** only when **both passages agree** on the statement, keeping shared facts separate from contested totals and unanswered side issues.

> Both writers agree that supermarkets hand out far fewer carrier bags and that total plastic use fell, while disagreeing on the size of the saving and declining to take a shared stance on paper bags.

## Verifying each statement across both texts

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence from Text A and Text B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" />. Supermarkets hand out far fewer carrier bags | **Yes** | Text A reports <Highlight>about <InlineMath math="85\%" /> fewer carrier bags</Highlight>, and Text B agrees <Highlight>The charge has worked</Highlight> without disputing the bag drop. |
| <InlineMath math="2" />. The city uses about <InlineMath math="320" /> tonnes less plastic per year | **No** | Claimed by Text A, but Text B counters it measures avoided carrier bags rather than net plastic saved. |
| <InlineMath math="3" />. Results should be reported by weight including bin liners | **No** | Proposed only by Text B, never endorsed in Text A. |
| <InlineMath math="4" />. The charge reduced plastic used in the city | **Yes** | Text A reports a large reduction, and Text B confirms <Highlight>The plastic saved is real, but it is smaller than the bag count suggests</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="5" />. Paper bags are worse for the environment than plastic bags | **No** | Text A never makes that claim, and Text B states <Highlight>Whether paper bags are better for the environment than plastic ones is a separate question</Highlight>. |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **No**.
