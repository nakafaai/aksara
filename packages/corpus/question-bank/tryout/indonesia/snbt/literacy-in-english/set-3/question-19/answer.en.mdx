# Given

When asked if he took his tablets daily, the father jumped in directly with **"Every day"** in the doctor's language, later explaining <Highlight>Because that one was about me, not about my heart</Highlight>, before retrieving the untouched box from his drawer at home.

# Asked

Which of the five statements about the father's answer to the tablet question are supported? Choose Yes or No for each.

# Solution

Connect **all three clues** (his swift direct reply, his bus explanation, and the drawer) to verify his motive without inventing future plans.

> Answering the tablet question himself in the doctor's language defended his personal conduct and reputation, whereas he fully trusted his daughter's translation and never planned a future confession.

## Checking the five statements

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence from the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" />. Answered himself because it concerned his own conduct, not illness | **Yes** | He states directly <Highlight>Because that one was about me, not about my heart</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="2" />. Distrusted the narrator to translate accurately | **No** | He says <Highlight>When you tell me, I hear the news</Highlight>, showing complete trust in her words. |
| <InlineMath math="3" />. Answer was probably meant to protect how the doctor saw him | **Yes** | Fearing <Highlight>I watch her decide what kind of man I am</Highlight> while leaving the box untouched in a drawer shows his confident "Every day" protected his image. |
| <InlineMath math="4" />. Hearing news through the narrator made it harder to bear | **No** | The opposite is true: through his daughter he hears only the news without the doctor's judgement. |
| <InlineMath math="5" />. Plans to tell the doctor the truth at his next visit | **No** | The story never mentions any next visit or plan to confess, only moving the box to the windowsill. |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="3" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **No**.
