Irony is a contrast between **expectation and reality**. Compare the two guides in the story:

| Guide | What the details show |
| --- | --- |
| Permanent, accurate board | You would expect it to help, but its orientation sends arriving passengers the wrong way. |
| Rotated paper copy | It contains the same route information, yet the child can use it to find the platform. |

The definition explains this reversal. The contrast depends on **Nora's rotation and the child's response**, so calling the board “ironic” without those details would miss the reasoning.
