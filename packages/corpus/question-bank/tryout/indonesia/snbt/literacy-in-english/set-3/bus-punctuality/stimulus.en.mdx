The regional transport authority will renew the contract of only one of its two bus operators this year, and it has promised to choose the more punctual one. Last month a local newspaper ran the headline "Southway beats Northline by seven points" above the figures below, which count a trip as on time if it reached its last stop no more than five minutes late.

Urban routes run through the city centre, where traffic lights, roadworks and school traffic often hold buses up. Rural routes run between villages on open roads. Northline was given most of the urban routes when the network was divided ten years ago, and Southway most of the rural ones.
