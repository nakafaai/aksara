# Given

# Asked

Which of the five statements are supported by the passage and the table? Choose Yes or No for each.

# Solution

Test each claim against **the four route rates** and **the <InlineMath math="7" />-point overall difference**, separating true arithmetic from misleading causal claims.

> The newspaper's seven-point arithmetic is accurate, yet because Northline outperforms Southway on both urban and rural routes, the overall gap reflects route mix rather than superior punctuality.

| Statement | Verdict | Supporting numbers and text |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />** are **No**.
