A city collects about 6,000 tonnes of household waste each month. Residents put part of it in recycling bins, which go to a sorting plant. There, workers and machines remove items that cannot be sold to recycling companies, such as greasy food containers or plastics that no buyer wants. Those items are sent to landfill with the rest of the waste.

The city's annual report states that "45 percent of household waste goes into recycling bins". A local newspaper checked the sorting plant's records for one typical month.
