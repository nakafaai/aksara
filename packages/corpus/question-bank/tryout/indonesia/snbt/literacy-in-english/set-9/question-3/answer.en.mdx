Paragraph 2 explains the chain. The eruption threw **sulphur dioxide into the stratosphere**, where it formed **tiny droplets** that spread around the globe and **reflected part of the sunlight back into space**. Less sunlight reaching the ground meant cooler weather far away.

- The passage credits sulphur droplets, not ash, for the cooling.
- Ocean currents are not mentioned.
- No rain falls in the stratosphere, so rain did not carry the gas.
- The loud blast is mentioned only to show its size, not as a cause of cooling.

So the eruption cooled distant places because sulphur droplets high in the atmosphere reflected part of the sunlight.
