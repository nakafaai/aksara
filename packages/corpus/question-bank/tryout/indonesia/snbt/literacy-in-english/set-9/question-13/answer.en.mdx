The report says 45 percent of household waste "goes into recycling bins". That is true: 2,700 of 6,000 tonnes. But much of that material is **removed at the sorting plant** and sent to landfill. Only 1,860 tonnes, about **31 percent**, is actually recycled. A reader who takes "goes into recycling bins" to mean "is recycled" would overestimate the city's recycling.

- The passage gives about 6,000 tonnes as the city's own figure and does not dispute it.
- Factory waste is not mentioned.
- The newspaper checked a typical month.
- Paper and cardboard are included in the bin totals.

So the figure could mislead because it counts what goes into bins, not what is actually recycled.
