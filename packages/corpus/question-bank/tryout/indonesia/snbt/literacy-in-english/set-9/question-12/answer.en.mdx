Compare tonnes and shares carefully, and check what happens at the sorting plant.

| Statement | Answer | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More mixed plastics recycled than bottles | False | 150 tonnes against 300 tonnes. |
| Glass had the highest recycled share | True | Glass: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{450}{500} = 90\%" />. Paper 80%, bottles 75%, mixed plastics 25%. |
| Paper was the largest amount in bins | True | 1,200 tonnes is more than any other material. |
| Everything in the bins was recycled | False | The sorting plant removes unsellable items and sends them to landfill. |

The second statement needs a **share**, not a raw amount. Paper had the most tonnes recycled, but glass had the highest share.
