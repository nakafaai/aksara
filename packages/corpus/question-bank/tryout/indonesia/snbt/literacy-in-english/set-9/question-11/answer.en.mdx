# Given

The table records Westford school roof panels in **March** (<InlineMath math="2{,}000" /> kWh arriving sunlight, <InlineMath math="380" /> kWh electricity produced) and in **July** (<InlineMath math="3{,}800" /> kWh arriving sunlight, <InlineMath math="627" /> kWh electricity produced).

# Asked

If the panels had kept their **March efficiency** in July, how many extra kWh of electricity would they have generated in July?

# Solution

> The share of the arriving energy that comes out as electricity is the panels' efficiency.

Find March's **conversion efficiency**, apply that rate to July's arriving sunlight, and subtract July's actual output.

## Computing the lost electricity in July

Divide March output by March sunlight, multiply that efficiency by July sunlight, and subtract July's <InlineMath math="627" /> kWh to find the **extra July output**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{March efficiency} &= \frac{380}{2{,}000} = 0.19 = 19\% \\ \text{Expected July output at } 19\% &= 0.19 \times 3{,}800 = 722 \text{ kWh} \\ \text{Extra electricity} &= 722 - 627 = 95 \text{ kWh} \end{aligned}" />

| Quantity | Value | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| March efficiency | <InlineMath math="19\%" /> | Baseline cool-weather conversion rate |
| July output at March efficiency | <InlineMath math="722" /> kWh | What <InlineMath math="3{,}800" /> kWh of sunlight would yield |
| Actual July output | <InlineMath math="627" /> kWh | Measured output at <InlineMath math="16.5\%" /> efficiency |
| Extra electricity | <InlineMath math="95" /> kWh | Difference between expected and actual |

Watch the distractors: <InlineMath math="722" /> kWh stops one step early before subtracting <InlineMath math="627" /> kWh, <InlineMath math="247" /> kWh wrongly subtracts March output from July output, and <InlineMath math="57" /> kWh or <InlineMath math="38" /> kWh use May (<InlineMath math="18\%" />) or August (<InlineMath math="17.5\%" />) efficiency instead of March.

# Answer

The panels would have produced **<InlineMath math="95" /> kWh** more electricity in July.
