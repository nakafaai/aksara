Each statement has to be checked against what the badge studies **actually measured**. Text 2 tells you three things: the badges recorded "when they were talking face to face, though not what they said", face-to-face interaction fell by about <InlineMath math="70\%" />, and "email and instant messaging rose".

**Statement 1** is a "Yes". The badges caught talking but not its content, and Text 2 itself says "some of the lost talk may have been chatter". So the badges alone can't tell you whether the lost conversations were about work.

**Statement 2** is a "No". One channel went down and two went up. Text 2 gives no totals, so you **can't add them up** to say that communication fell overall.

**Statement 3** is a "No". Text 2 says the studies "did not measure whether the work itself got better or worse". Less talk is not the same as worse work.

**Statement 4** is a "No". "The studies covered two companies." Stretching two cases to most companies is an overreach in scope.
