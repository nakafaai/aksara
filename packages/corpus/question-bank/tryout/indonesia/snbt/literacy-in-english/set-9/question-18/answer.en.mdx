Some statements need **two paragraphs together**: paragraph 4 says what the Peters map keeps, and paragraph 2 gives the real sizes.

Statement 1. His map "kept the areas of countries in proportion, but it squeezed their shapes". Yes.

Statement 2. James Gall "had described almost the same projection in 1855". No.

Statement 3. An equal-area map keeps areas in proportion, and "Africa has roughly fourteen times" Greenland's area. Greenland looks as large as Africa only on Mercator's map. No.

Statement 4. Same two facts: areas in proportion, and Africa about fourteen times Greenland. Yes.

Statement 5. The cartographers objected that "the shapes were badly distorted" and that equal-area maps "were nothing new". Areas were what the map got right. No.

So statements 1 and 4 are Yes, and statements 2, 3 and 5 are No.
