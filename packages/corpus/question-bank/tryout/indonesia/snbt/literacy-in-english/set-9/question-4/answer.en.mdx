Paragraph 5 gives two reasons. **News travelled slowly**, so many people far away may not have known about the eruption, and **the idea that a volcano could cool the whole planet had not yet been developed**. Without that idea, there was no reason to connect a distant eruption with local weather.

- The novel was written during the summer and did not explain the weather.
- The idea of volcanic cooling had not yet been developed, so nothing had been proved either way.
- Tambora was the largest eruption in recorded history.
- Ice cores later helped link the sulphate to that time, not to another volcano.

So people failed to see the link because news travelled slowly and the science to make it did not yet exist.
