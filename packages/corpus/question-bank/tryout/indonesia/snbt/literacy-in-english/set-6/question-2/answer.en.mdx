# Given

Five statements about the <InlineMath math="1854" /> Broad Street cholera outbreak and John Snow's water-company investigation must be verified as **Yes** or **No**.

# Asked

Which of the five statements can be concluded from the passage? Choose Yes or No for each.

# Solution

Watch closely for **myths corrected by the passage** (the pump handle stopping the outbreak and officials being persuaded) as well as **which company** moved its intake upstream.

> Deaths were already dropping before the pump handle came off, few officials were convinced at the time, and Lambeth drew cleaner water upstream while Southwark and Vauxhall drew below the sewage outlets.

## Checking the five statements

| Statement | Verdict | Passage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" />. Removing the handle ended the Broad Street outbreak | **No** | Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> corrects this myth: <Highlight>Deaths had already fallen sharply before the handle came off</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="2" />. A cluster around the pump could also fit shared local air | **Yes** | Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> states <Highlight>people who live close to a pump also share the same air, so a cluster around the pump fitted the bad-air theory almost as well as Snow's</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="3" />. Snow's evidence persuaded most officials of his day | **No** | The passage closes bluntly: <Highlight>Few officials were persuaded at the time</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="4" />. Brewery workers' escape fits the water theory because they avoided pump water | **Yes** | They <Highlight>drank beer and water from the brewery's own supply, escaped almost entirely</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="5" />. Lambeth drew water below the city's sewage outlets | **No** | Southwark and Vauxhall drew below the outlets, whereas <Highlight>The Lambeth company had moved its intake upstream, beyond them</Highlight>. |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **No**.
