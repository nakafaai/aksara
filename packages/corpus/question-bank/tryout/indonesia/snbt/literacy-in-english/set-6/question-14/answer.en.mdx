# Given

Five conclusions test **shares, percentage equivalence, raw loan counts, and the director's summary** across the <InlineMath math="2023" /> and <InlineMath math="2024" /> library-loan table.

# Asked

Which conclusions can be drawn from the passage and the table? Select all that apply.

# Solution

Check each claim numerically, watching out for the **one false option** that confuses audiobooks' <InlineMath math="20\%" /> rate with raw loan volume (<InlineMath math="2{,}200" /> vs. <InlineMath math="18{,}000" />).

> Four of the five statements hold directly from the table, while only the claim that audiobooks added more loans than e-books fails because two thousand two hundred is far below eighteen thousand.

## Checking all five conclusions

| Conclusion | Verdict | Table verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Print books held a smaller share of all loans in <InlineMath math="2024" /> | **True** | Print dropped from <InlineMath math="90\%" /> (<InlineMath math="180{,}000" /> of <InlineMath math="200{,}000" />) to <InlineMath math="\approx 80\%" /> (<InlineMath math="162{,}000" /> of <InlineMath math="202{,}200" />). |
| E-book loans rose by <InlineMath math="200\%" />, the same as tripling | **True** | Adding <InlineMath math="200\%" /> of <InlineMath math="9{,}000" /> (<InlineMath math="18{,}000" />) yields <InlineMath math="27{,}000" />, three times the start. |
| Audiobook loans rose by more loans than e-book loans | **False** | Audiobooks rose by only <InlineMath math="2{,}200" /> loans, far fewer than e-books' <InlineMath math="18{,}000" /> gain. |
| The director's phrase fits total loans | **True** | Totals shifted only <InlineMath math="1.1\%" /> (<InlineMath math="200{,}000" /> to <InlineMath math="202{,}200" />) while formats shifted. |
| Print loans fell by more loans than audiobook loans rose | **True** | Print fell by <InlineMath math="18{,}000" />, exceeding audiobooks' rise of <InlineMath math="2{,}200" />. |

# Answer

- **Print books made up a smaller share of all loans in <InlineMath math="2024" /> than in <InlineMath math="2023" />**.
- **E-book loans rose by <InlineMath math="200\%" />, which is the same as saying they tripled**.
- **The director's phrase "about the same number of books, in different shapes" fits the change in total loans**.
- **Print loans fell by more loans than audiobook loans rose**.
