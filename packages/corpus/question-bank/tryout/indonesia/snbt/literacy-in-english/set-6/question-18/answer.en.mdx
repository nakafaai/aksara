Two statements tempt you to take Mrs Varga's actions **at face value**. Check each against what the story actually says.

Statement 1. She said "Good. Much better." after a performance so bad that the narrator stopped. The praise reflects her hearing, not the playing. No.

Statement 2. She moved her chair "to the end of the keyboard, where she could see my hands". Yes.

Statement 3. The narrator was annoyed "the way you are annoyed when someone lets you win", that is, by praise she knew she hadn't earned. Yes.

Statement 4. The story gives the reason for the move: "where she could see my hands". Seeing, not hearing. No.

Statement 5. Mr Hollis "stopped me at bar twelve", the bar with the old wrong note. It was still there after the spring. Yes.

So statements 2, 3 and 5 are Yes, and statements 1 and 4 are No.
