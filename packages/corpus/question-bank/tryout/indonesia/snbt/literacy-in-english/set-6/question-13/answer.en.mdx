Theo turns a conflict between sources into **a question someone else can check**. Follow the evidence in order:

| Evidence | Consequence for the record |
| --- | --- |
| Envelope: <InlineMath math="1984" />, later note: <InlineMath math="1987" /> | Neither date can be entered as certain from these conflicting sources alone. |
| Theo records the uncertainty and both sources | A later researcher can see what needs checking. |
| A visitor identifies a shop sign and a dated newspaper | The new evidence narrows the date to <InlineMath math="1987" />. |

The progress begins with **reviewable documentation**, not with a guess or a completed catalogue. The visitor adds evidence to the question Theo preserved.
