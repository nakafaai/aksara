A percentage fall is measured against **the starting value**, here the year before the trial.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{fall} &= 620 - 527 = 93 \\ \text{percentage} &= \frac{93}{620} \times 100\% = 15.0\% \end{aligned}" />

- 2.0% is Area C's fall, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{12}{600}" />.
- 9.3% divides the fall by 1,000 instead of by 620.
- 12.1% is Area B's fall, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{70}{580}" />.
- 17.6% divides the fall by the new value, 527, instead of the starting value.

So average daily use in Area A fell by about 15.0%.
