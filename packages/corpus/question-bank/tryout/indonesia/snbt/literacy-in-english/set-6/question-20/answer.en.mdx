Each reading needs a line from the story, and two ask you to see how the narrator tells it **looking back**.

Statement 1. For three years Mrs Varga "told me exactly what was wrong", and the narrator wanted to stay "until I played the Schubert well enough for Mrs Varga to say something was wrong with it". Being told what's wrong meant **being heard**, and that's what she wanted back. Yes.

Statement 2. She "took it as a kindness and was annoyed by it, the way you are annoyed when someone lets you win". She knew it wasn't earned. No.

Statement 3. She lists the changes and adds, "though I did not put them together". Telling them as clues she missed shows she understands them now. Yes.

Statement 4. Mr Hollis "was a good teacher", and nothing describes him as harsh. Her reason for staying concerns Mrs Varga, not him. No.

Statement 5. Mrs Varga only says she "had taught me everything she could". The real reason comes through the clues, never from her. No.

So statements 1 and 3 are Yes, and statements 2, 4 and 5 are No.
