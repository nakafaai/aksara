# Given

Five interpretive statements examine **the narrator's feelings, retrospective insight, and knowledge** across her lessons with Mrs Varga and Mr Hollis.

# Asked

Which of these five readings of the narrator are supported by the story? Choose Yes or No for each.

# Solution

Distinguish **what the narrator felt at fifteen** (annoyed at unearned praise, longing for Mrs Varga's real attention) from **her adult hindsight** (<Highlight>though I did not put them together</Highlight>).

> Wanting to stay until Mrs Varga found something wrong shows she prized honest criticism as proof of being heard, and framing the spring changes as unconnected clues confirms she understands them looking back.

## Checking the five readings

| Reading of the narrator | Verdict | Story evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" />. Wishing to stay until Mrs Varga found a flaw shows she valued being heard | **Yes** | She wanted to stay <Highlight>until I played the Schubert well enough for Mrs Varga to say something was wrong with it</Highlight>, longing for genuine listening again. |
| <InlineMath math="2" />. She believed the spring praise was deserved | **No** | She felt annoyed <Highlight>the way you are annoyed when someone lets you win</Highlight>, knowing she had not earned it. |
| <InlineMath math="3" />. Looking back, she presents spring changes as clues she missed then | **Yes** | She introduces the clues with <Highlight>Other things changed too, though I did not put them together</Highlight>, signalling hindsight. |
| <InlineMath math="4" />. She wanted to stay because Mr Hollis was known to be harsh | **No** | She simply calls Mr Hollis <Highlight>a good teacher</Highlight> and gives no hint of a harsh reputation. |
| <InlineMath math="5" />. She learned from Mrs Varga herself why lessons changed | **No** | Mrs Varga never mentioned hearing loss, saying only she had taught her everything she could. |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="3" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **No**.
