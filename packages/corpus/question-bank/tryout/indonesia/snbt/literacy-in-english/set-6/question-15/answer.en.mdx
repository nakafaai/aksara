The table shows a clear pattern: use fell by 93 and 70 litres in the **smart-meter areas** and by only 12 and 5 litres in the others. The report, however, names two complications: leak repairs in Area A and residents in A and B who already wanted to save water. The data therefore support a larger fall where meters were installed, but **not how much of it the meters themselves caused**.

- The falls differ by area (15.0% and about 12.1%), so "exactly 15 percent everywhere" is wrong.
- Use in Areas C and D fell slightly, so ordinary meters did not increase it.
- Area B fell by 70 litres without a repair programme, so repairs cannot explain everything.
- Areas C and D started at similar levels to A and B, and the passage says nothing about waste.

So the best-supported conclusion is that use fell more where meters were installed, but how much the meters caused is unclear.
