A water company tested whether smart meters reduce household water use. Smart meters record use every hour and send it to an app, so households can see how much water they use each day. The company installed them in all homes in two areas, A and B, which had applied to take part. Two similar areas, C and D, kept their ordinary meters, which are read once every three months. The table compares average daily use per household over the same three summer months in two years: the year before the trial and the first year of the trial. Water prices did not change.

The company's report notes two complications. In Area A, the local council ran a programme to repair leaking pipes in homes during the same year. In addition, the residents of Areas A and B had shown an interest in saving water before the trial began, which was one reason their councils applied.
