# Given

# Asked

# Solution

Check **each numerical comparison directly** (shares, raw differences, totals, and percentage growth rates) and separate format switching from reading growth.

> E-books gained the exact eighteen thousand loans that print lost and audiobooks grew twenty percent against total growth of one point one percent, while print still accounted for eighty percent of all loans.
