Watch the dates and numbers. Two statements attach a correct fact to the **wrong year or number**.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bristol's noon was about ten minutes after London's | True | Paragraph 1 states this directly. |
| Greenwich time became legal in 1840 | False | 1840 is when the Great Western Railway adopted London time. Parliament acted "only in 1880". |
| The railroads agreed on six zones | False | Paragraph 4: they agreed "to use four time zones". |
| The government made the zones law in 1918 | True | Paragraph 4: "The government did not make the zones law until 1918." |

The second statement mixes up **a company's decision** in 1840 with **the law** of 1880.
