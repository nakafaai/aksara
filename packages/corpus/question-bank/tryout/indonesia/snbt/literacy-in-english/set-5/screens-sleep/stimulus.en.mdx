A secondary school surveyed all 1,200 of its students about their evenings on school days. Students estimated how long they usually spent on phones, computers, or other screens after 8 p.m., how long they usually slept, and whether they often felt sleepy during morning lessons.

The school counsellor presented the results to parents with two warnings. First, the figures come from students' own estimates, which may be inaccurate. Second, some students stay up late for other reasons, such as homework or caring for younger siblings, and they may also use screens more simply because they are awake longer.

After the survey, 60 students volunteered to switch off all screens at 9 p.m. for two weeks. Their average sleep rose by about 25 minutes over that period.
