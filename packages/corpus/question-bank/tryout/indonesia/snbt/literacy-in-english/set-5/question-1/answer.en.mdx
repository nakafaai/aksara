Paragraphs 3 to 5 then show that **some effects are still real**: placebo pain relief can be blocked by naloxone, expectation can cause side effects, and even an open placebo may help. The passage moves from "smaller than claimed" to "but real".

- Beecher said a third of patients felt better, not that a third of illnesses are imaginary.

- Naloxone blocked the relief. It is not presented as a treatment.

So the main idea is that placebo effects are smaller than once claimed, yet some of them are real and measurable.
