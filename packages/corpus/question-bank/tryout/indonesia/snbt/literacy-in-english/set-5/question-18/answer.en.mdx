# Given

Across the night frost watch and the following spring, the grandfather speaks very little, yet **two of his deliberate actions** quietly reveal that he is preparing the narrator to run the orchard without him.

# Asked

Which details reveal something about the grandfather that he never says aloud? Choose all that apply.

# Solution

Select only **deliberate personal actions of the grandfather** that expose an unspoken motive, filtering out neutral weather, sprinkler, and neighbour details.

> Requiring the narrator to repeat the valve rule back to him and tucking a handwritten step-by-step list into the green coat pocket silently show he is passing the orchard's care on to her.

## Checking all five details

| Story detail | Verdict | Why it reveals an unspoken motive or does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thermometer reads minus one at two in the morning | **False** | Pure weather setting, revealing nothing about the grandfather's inner thoughts. |
| Water rises in thin arcs when the valve is turned | **False** | Describes the sprinkler hardware rather than the grandfather's character. |
| Makes her say back why the valve stays open until the ice runs off | **True** | Paragraph <InlineMath math="8" /> notes <Highlight>he made me say back to him why</Highlight>, showing he is quietly training her to handle frost nights herself. |
| Hallers' trees across the road stand bare in May | **False** | Shows the outcome for an unwatered orchard, not an unspoken feeling of the grandfather. |
| Leaves a handwritten list of steps in the green coat pocket | **True** | Paragraph <InlineMath math="9" /> stresses <Highlight>I had never seen him write down anything he expected to remember</Highlight>, revealing he wrote it for her future springs without him. |

# Answer

- **He makes her say back why the valve stays open until the ice has run off**.
- **He leaves a list of the steps, in his own handwriting, in the pocket of the green coat**.
