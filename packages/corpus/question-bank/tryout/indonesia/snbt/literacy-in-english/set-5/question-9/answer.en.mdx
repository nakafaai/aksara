Text 2 grants that the reserve plants "stay cheap and clean enough only while they run rarely", so it **accepts** that they would emit little. What it rejects is relying on them in the long run: "A country that means to stop burning gas will need something else for the windless week."

- This swaps the two parts. Text 2 grants the low emissions and doubts that burning gas will stay acceptable.
- The acceptance is right, but Text 2 never disputes how fast wind and solar can be built. It wants this decade's money to go mainly to them.
- Text 2 says storage may not improve "faster than anyone can promise", so it doesn't accept that storage will close the gap.
- The acceptance is right, but Text 2 gives no building cost for gas plants against reactors.

So Text 2 accepts that rarely used gas plants would emit little and rejects relying on them once the country stops burning gas.
