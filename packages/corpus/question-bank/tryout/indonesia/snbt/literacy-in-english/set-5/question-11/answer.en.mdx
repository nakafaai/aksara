Each percentage applies to a group of a different size, so the overall figure must be built from **numbers of students**, not by averaging the percentages.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 240 \times 0.15 &= 36 \\ 420 \times 0.25 &= 105 \\ 330 \times 0.40 &= 132 \\ 210 \times 0.50 &= 105 \\ 36 + 105 + 132 + 105 &= 378 \\ \frac{378}{1{,}200} \times 100\% &= 31.5\% \end{aligned}" />

- 25.0% is only the figure for the largest group.
- 32.5% is the plain average of the four percentages, which ignores the different group sizes.
- 37.8% divides 378 by 1,000 instead of 1,200.
- 50.0% is only the figure for the heaviest screen users.

So about 31.5% of all the surveyed students often felt sleepy in morning lessons.
