A **motif** is an element that recurs and develops a broader meaning. The stamp connects these parts of the story:

| Appearance | What the action contributes |
| --- | --- |
| A package has no entry date on it | The record in the ledger has not yet been transferred to the package. |
| Samira checks the ledger and stamps the date | She closes that gap and leaves the stamp where it can be used. |
| The next volunteer dates more packages | Another person continues the care shown through record keeping. |

The recurring object links uncertainty, action, and continuation into **care passed forward**. Its blue colour alone would not justify that interpretation.
