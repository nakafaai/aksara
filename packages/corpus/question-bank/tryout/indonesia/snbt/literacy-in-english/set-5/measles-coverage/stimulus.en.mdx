After a measles outbreak last year, the provincial health office reported that <InlineMath math="94\%" /> of children in the province had received both doses of the measles vaccine, close to the level of about <InlineMath math="95\%" /> that public health agencies aim for to stop the disease from spreading. A local newspaper concluded that the outbreak "cannot be blamed on low vaccination".

The health office added that most of Eastport's cases were among pupils of a single private school, where many families had declined the vaccine, even though the district as a whole had reached <InlineMath math="95\%" />. Measles spreads so easily that a pocket of unvaccinated children can sustain an outbreak inside a well-vaccinated area.
