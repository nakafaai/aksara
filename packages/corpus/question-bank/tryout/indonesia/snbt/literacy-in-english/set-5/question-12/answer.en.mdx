# Given

A local newspaper concluded the measles outbreak <Highlight>cannot be blamed on low vaccination</Highlight>. Across all four districts, there were **<InlineMath math="120" /> total cases**, with **<InlineMath math="108" /> cases** (<InlineMath math="90\%" />) concentrated in **Northgate (<InlineMath math="70" />)** and **Eastport (<InlineMath math="38" />)**.

# Asked

Which finding, if true, would most strongly support the newspaper's conclusion?

# Solution

Strengthen the newspaper's claim by showing that **the vast majority of actual outbreak cases occurred among fully two-dose vaccinated children**, focusing on the two districts that account for <InlineMath math="90\%" /> of all cases.

> Because Northgate and Eastport together account for one hundred and eight of the one hundred and twenty cases, discovering that most sick children there had already received both vaccine doses would directly exonerate low vaccination.

## Weighting the districts by outbreak share

| District | Measles cases | Share of outbreak |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Northgate + Eastport | <InlineMath math="70 + 38 = 108" /> | <InlineMath math="90\%" /> |
| Riverside + Hillcrest | <InlineMath math="8 + 4 = 12" /> | <InlineMath math="10\%" /> |
| Province total | <InlineMath math="120" /> | <InlineMath math="100\%" /> |

If **most cases in Northgate and Eastport** had already received **both doses**, then low vaccination could not explain the main body of the outbreak.

## Why the other options fail

- **Northgate had the lowest coverage (<InlineMath math="80\%" />)** weakens the newspaper by linking low coverage directly to the biggest hotspot.
- **Vaccinated cases in Riverside and Hillcrest** covers only <InlineMath math="12" /> of <InlineMath math="120" /> cases (<InlineMath math="10\%" />), leaving <InlineMath math="90\%" /> of the outbreak unexplained.
- **At least one dose in Eastport** covers only <InlineMath math="38" /> cases (leaving Northgate's <InlineMath math="70" /> untouched) and falls short of the two-dose standard.
- **Unvaccinated pocket at Eastport's private school** supports the health office and weakens the newspaper.

# Answer

**Most of the children who caught measles in Northgate and Eastport had received both doses of the vaccine**.
