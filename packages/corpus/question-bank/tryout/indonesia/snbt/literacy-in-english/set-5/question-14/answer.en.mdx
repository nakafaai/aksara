The team should go where unvaccinated children are gathered, so you need information that shows **pockets, not averages**.

- **Unvaccinated children in each school**. Eastport's pocket sat inside one school, so counts by school find such pockets. Helps.
- **District coverage, skipping districts at <InlineMath math="95\%" />**. Eastport was at <InlineMath math="95\%" /> and still had <InlineMath math="38" /> cases from one school. Doesn't help.
- **Where the cases came from**. The cases show where the virus found unvaccinated children. Helps.
- **Children in each district**. Riverside has the most children and the fewest cases for each child, so size alone doesn't show where the gaps are. Doesn't help.
- **Past provincial coverage**. A figure for the whole province over time shows no community at all. Doesn't help.

So the useful information is the number of unvaccinated children in each school and the communities and schools the outbreak's cases came from.
