The 60 students **volunteered**. Students who choose to switch off screens may already be more motivated to sleep better, or may change other habits at the same time. There was also **no comparison group** of similar students who kept their screens on. Without one, the 25-minute rise cannot be separated from these other differences.

- Sixty is a small number, not too many to follow.
- Remembering bedtimes is not mentioned as a problem.
- The passage says their sleep rose, not fell.
- Nothing says who the volunteers were, so calling them the heaviest users is unsupported.

So the result is weak evidence because students who chose to take part may differ from those who did not.
