Each statement needs its own sentence of the passage, and two ask you to apply **the mood explanation** to a new case.

Statement 1. The improvement "had faded within about fifteen minutes". No.

Statement 2. The study used "a lively Mozart sonata", and later work found that lively music helped "whether or not the lively piece was by Mozart". A lively piece by someone else should help about as much. Yes.

Statement 3. The review "found the average effect to be small". Small isn't zero. No.

Statement 4. The mood explanation says "differences in mood accounted for the differences in scores". Same music, different mood: the student who didn't get the lift should gain less. Yes.

Statement 5. "None of the original researchers had tested babies." The babies claim came later, from others. No.

So statements 2 and 4 are Yes, and statements 1, 3 and 5 are No.
