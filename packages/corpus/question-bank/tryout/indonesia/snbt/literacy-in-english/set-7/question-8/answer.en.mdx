The experiment comes right after the question "what happens when the points go away". Children who had expected a certificate later **spent less time drawing** during free play than children who had not. The author draws the lesson: an activity once enjoyed "for its own sake had become something they did for a prize". Paragraph 3 applies it to classroom points.

- The quality of the drawings is not discussed.
- The study suggests certificates may reduce interest, the opposite of being most effective.
- Free play is where the effect was measured, not something the author wants to remove.
- Drawing and reading are not compared.

So the author describes the experiment to show how a reward can reduce interest once it is taken away.
