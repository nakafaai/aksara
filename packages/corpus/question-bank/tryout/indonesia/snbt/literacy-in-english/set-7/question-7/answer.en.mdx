A statement counts only if **both** texts support it. Check each one in both places.

Statement 1. Text A reports that "lessons are calmer". Text B says "I don't doubt that the school feels calmer." Yes.

Statement 2. Text A credits the ban with the rise. Text B says the rise "could come from any of the three changes". Only Text A. No.

Statement 3. Text A's ban covers lessons and the whole day, and it urges other schools to follow. Text B says keeping phones out of lessons "seems sensible" and "I would support a ban." Yes.

Statement 4. Text B only says it "matters" whether bullying moved to the evening, and that "nobody counted that". Neither text claims it happened. No.

Statement 5. Text A urges other schools to follow but never predicts a rise for them, and Text B says "I would not promise parents a nine-point rise." Neither text accepts it. No.

So statements 1 and 3 are Yes, and statements 2, 4 and 5 are No.
