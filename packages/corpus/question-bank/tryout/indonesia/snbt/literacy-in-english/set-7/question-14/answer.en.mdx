The clearest change is among **families**: monthly visits rose from 3,000 to 4,500, and the average visit grew from 70 to 105 minutes. The staff report, however, notes that the second period **included the long school holiday**, when families have more free time. The data support a rise among families, but the holiday may explain part of it.

- Families rose from 3,000 to 4,500, which is one and a half times, not double.
- The table shows fewer adults, but nothing shows that the gallery drove them away.
- Shorter school visits do not show that pupils disliked the gallery.
- Two periods cannot show a steady rate of growth for the future.

So the best-supported conclusion is that families came more and stayed longer, but the holiday may explain part of it.
