# Given

The passage describes the **original <InlineMath math="1993" /> study** on thirty-six college students who heard ten minutes of a lively Mozart sonata, sat in silence, or heard relaxation instructions before taking a single spatial reasoning task. The improvement faded within about fifteen minutes.

# Asked

Which claims from the popular retelling went beyond what the <InlineMath math="1993" /> study actually showed?

# Solution

> None of the original researchers had tested babies, and none had claimed a lasting effect.

To separate what the study proved from what people added later, compare each option with the **exact scope of the <InlineMath math="1993" /> experiment**.

## Comparing each claim to the study

The <InlineMath math="1993" /> experiment tested Mozart against silence and relaxation instructions on <Highlight>a spatial reasoning task</Highlight>, and the gain <Highlight>had faded within about fifteen minutes</Highlight>.

| Claim | Tested in <InlineMath math="1993" /> | Goes beyond study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Better after Mozart than after relaxation instructions | Yes | No |
| Benefit lasts long after the music stops | No | Yes |
| Better after Mozart than after silence | Yes | No |
| Raises intelligence in general | No | Yes |
| Comes from Mozart in particular rather than music in general | No | Yes |

Because the study only compared Mozart against silence and relaxation instructions, it never tested **other composers or other lively music**. Later work showed lively music helped <Highlight>whether or not the lively piece was by Mozart</Highlight>. Meanwhile, the IQ-style figure only described the size of the short gain on one spatial task, not general intelligence.

# Answer

The claims that went beyond what the <InlineMath math="1993" /> study showed are:

- That the benefit lasts long after the music stops.
- That the music raises intelligence in general rather than performance on one kind of task.
- That the benefit comes from Mozart's music in particular rather than from music in general.
