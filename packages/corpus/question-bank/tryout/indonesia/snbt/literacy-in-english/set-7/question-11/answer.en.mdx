Add the three groups in each period, then measure the rise against **the first period**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{before} &= 2{,}400 + 3{,}000 + 4{,}600 = 10{,}000 \\ \text{after} &= 2{,}600 + 4{,}500 + 4{,}100 = 11{,}200 \\ \text{rise} &= \frac{11{,}200 - 10{,}000}{10{,}000} \times 100\% = 12.0\% \end{aligned}" />

- 8.3% is the rise in school groups alone, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{200}{2{,}400}" />.
- 10.7% divides the rise by the second period's total, 11,200, instead of the first.
- 15.8% averages the three groups' own changes (+8.3%, +50.0%, and about −10.9%), ignoring that the groups differ in size.
- 50.0% is the rise in families alone, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1{,}500}{3{,}000}" />.

So total monthly visitors rose by 12.0%.
