A city museum opened a new hands-on gallery where visitors can build simple machines, test materials, and run small experiments. To judge the gallery's effect, the museum compared the six months before it opened (January to June) with the six months after (July to December). The museum's director called the gallery "a clear success". A staff report added two notes. First, the second period included the long school holiday, when families have more free time. Second, the city's other large museum, an art museum, closed for repairs in August and stayed closed until the end of the year.
