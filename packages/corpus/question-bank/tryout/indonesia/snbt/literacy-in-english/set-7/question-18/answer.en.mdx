# Given

Mrs Okonjo's stand normally held **about a dozen umbrellas** (<InlineMath math="12" />), yet when she returned from three weeks in hospital there were **nineteen umbrellas** (<InlineMath math="19" />) in it, accompanied by notes including "Sorry it took so long. Hope you're mending."

# Asked

Taken together with the notes, what does the number nineteen suggest?

# Solution

> When she came home, there were nineteen umbrellas in the stand. People had brought them back while she was away, some with notes tied to the handles. One note said only, "Sorry it took so long. Hope you're mending."

Compare the returned count with the **normal capacity of the stand** and match it to the exact phrasing in the note.

## Connecting the count to the note

Nineteen umbrellas exceed the dozen her stand usually held, and the narrator specifies that people <Highlight>had brought them back while she was away</Highlight>.

Because she had bought replacements every few months and spent those three weeks in hospital, the returns must include **long-kept older loans**, not just recent ones. The note <Highlight>Sorry it took so long</Highlight> directly confirms that delay.

The other choices misread either the plot or the note. She could not lend umbrellas during the three weeks she spent in hospital. People brought umbrellas back rather than buying new get-well gifts. The sign <Highlight>never went up</Highlight>, and <Highlight>Hope you're mending</Highlight> expresses concern for her health rather than proving a long delay.

# Answer

The number suggests **that people returned umbrellas they had kept for a long time, not only recent loans, as the note "Sorry it took so long" also shows**.
