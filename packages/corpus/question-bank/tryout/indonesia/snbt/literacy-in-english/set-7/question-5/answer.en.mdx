Paragraph 3 gives the rule: tubes that carry more nutrients **thicken**, while those that carry little **shrink and disappear**. A tube that carries almost nothing for a long time fits the second case.

- Tubes thicken because they carry more, not to attract food.
- The slime trail is left where the organism has been, a different thing from a tube.
- The rule says tubes change with use, so it would not stay the same.
- Splitting into new tubes is not described.

So the tube would most likely shrink and eventually disappear from the network.
