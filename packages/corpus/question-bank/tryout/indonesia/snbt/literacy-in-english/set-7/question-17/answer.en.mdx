Follow the sequence: the card first **describes danger without directing action**. Asha removes irrelevant detail and adds the safe street and meeting point. The same card becomes usable guidance and reaches the final house before the siren stops.

The ending links **her revision to the card's changed function**. It does not make her action irrelevant or leave the card with a fixed meaning.
