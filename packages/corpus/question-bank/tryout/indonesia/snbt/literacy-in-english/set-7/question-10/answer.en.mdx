Paragraph 4 lists what makes games worth borrowing: **immediate feedback**, **freedom to fail and try again without penalty**, and **challenge that grows with skill**. A puzzle that gives instant hints and lets pupils retry until they solve it uses the first two without any points or badges.

- A weekly ranking is a leaderboard, which the author criticises.
- Badges for streaks are the kind of reward the author warns may stop working.
- A prize for the most books is a reward of the same kind as the 1973 certificate.
- One attempt with delayed feedback lacks both features the author praises.

So the author would most likely approve of a puzzle that gives instant hints and lets pupils retry until they solve it.
