Match **two features** of her lending: she lent freely and never put up the sign asking for returns, and what the lending brought her was people, the notes and the neighbours who came "to ask how she was".

- The first half fits, but a notice asking for returns is the sign she let go, and it judges the lending by what comes back.
- The first half fits, but she gave the umbrellas out again one by one to her visitors, so the returns were not a repayment she had been waiting for.
- The first half fits, but she never hid her losses. Her father named them out loud, and she kept lending anyway.
- The first half fits, but she kept buying more every few months although "hardly any came back".
