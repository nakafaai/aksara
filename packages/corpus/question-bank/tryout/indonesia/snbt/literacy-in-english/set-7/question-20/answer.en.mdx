The title Cutting a warning **down to action** captures the move from a detailed but directionless warning to usable guidance. Asha removes distraction and adds the safe street and meeting point, then the message arrives before the drill ends.

The title describes a **change in function**. Titles treating the card as static background, or the conflict as resolved before Asha acts, miss that change.
