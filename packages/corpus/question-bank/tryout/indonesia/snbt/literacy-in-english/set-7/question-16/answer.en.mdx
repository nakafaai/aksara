Paragraph 3 states the condition: a town "had to provide the land and promise to pay for running the library every year", usually about **a tenth of the grant**, through **local taxes**.

- Naming the library and displaying a portrait are not mentioned.
- Buying books from Carnegie's company is not mentioned.
- Towns did not have to prove they had many readers. Many had never had a library.
- Saturday opening for working boys describes Colonel Anderson's private library, not the grant condition.

So a town had to provide the land and promise to pay yearly for running the library.
