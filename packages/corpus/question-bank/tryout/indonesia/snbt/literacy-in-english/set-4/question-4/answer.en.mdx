Paragraph 4 gives the reason: "Most vent bacteria need oxygen to extract energy from hydrogen sulphide, and the oxygen dissolved in seawater was made by photosynthesis in sunlit waters and on land." The link to the sun is **indirect**: no light reaches the vents, but the **oxygen** the bacteria depend on comes from photosynthesis in sunlit places far away.

- Paragraph 2 says there is "no sunlight at all" at the depth of the vents.
- Adult tube worms stay at the vents, and nothing suggests they travel to the surface.
- The passage does not say the vent heat comes from the sun.
- The bacteria make their own organic matter from chemical energy. Sinking sugars are not mentioned.

So the author qualifies the claim because most vent bacteria need oxygen that photosynthesis in sunlight produced.
