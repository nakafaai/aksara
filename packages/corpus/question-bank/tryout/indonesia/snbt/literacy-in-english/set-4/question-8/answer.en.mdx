Look for the author's own statement, and watch for points that **turn a limited claim into a sweeping one**.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time slots spread visits across the day | Yes | Paragraph 3: booking slots "spreads visits across the day instead of letting everyone arrive at once". |
| Tourist cities should close their gates | No | Paragraph 5: such cities "cannot simply close their gates, and they should not try". |
| Revenue should be reported and visibly spent | Yes | Paragraph 4: revenue "must be reported openly and spent where residents can see the results". |
| Venice charges on every day | No | Paragraph 1: the fee applies "on its busiest days". |

The fourth statement widens "busiest days" to "every day of the year", which the passage never says.
