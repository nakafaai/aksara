Paragraph 3 says the plume "absorbs hydrogen sulphide, oxygen, and carbon dioxide from the surrounding water and **delivers them to the bacteria**", which then supply the worm with food. The plume is a **collection and delivery** organ: it gathers what the bacteria need and passes it on.

- The worm has no mouth or gut, and the plume is not described as catching prey.
- The bacteria make the food, not the plume, and the passage never says the bacteria digest anything the plume makes.
- The red colour comes from haemoglobin, and nothing suggests it is a warning.
- Larvae drift to new vents. Adult worms are not said to swim between vents.

So the plume's main job is to take in the chemicals the bacteria need and pass them on.
