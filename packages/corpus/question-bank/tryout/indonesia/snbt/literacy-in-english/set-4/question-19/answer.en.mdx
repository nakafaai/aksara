# Given

On the motorway, the narrator reconstructs **specific clues from before and during the phone call** showing that Gran had failed to locate the spare key on her own.

# Asked

Which details suggest that Gran had trouble finding the key? Select all that apply.

# Solution

Select only clues pointing to **an unsuccessful search**, while rejecting the option that reverses the order of the phone call or merely repeats Gran's cover story.

> Having walked past all five flowerpots before calling and still needing to ask which flowerpot once told it was in one prove Gran had searched and failed to spot the key.

## Evaluating all five details

| Detail | Verdict | Story check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asked "Which flowerpot?" instead of where the key was | **True** | Paragraph <InlineMath math="8" /> flags this exact clue: <Highlight>She had not asked me where the key was. She had asked me which flowerpot</Highlight>, showing she needed directions to pinpoint it. |
| Already knew it was in a flowerpot before the narrator said so | **False** | Reverses the dialogue order: the narrator first said <Highlight>the spare was in the flowerpot at the back</Highlight>, and only then did Gran ask which one. |
| Walked past all five flowerpots on her way round before calling | **True** | Paragraph <InlineMath math="8" /> stresses <Highlight>she had walked past all five on her way round the house before she rang</Highlight> without finding it. |
| Sat on the bench with the key in her lap when the narrator arrived | **False** | That happened forty minutes later after the call gave her the location, showing she eventually retrieved it. |
| Told the narrator she had found the key at once | **False** | That is Gran's cover claim denying any trouble, not evidence of trouble. |

# Answer

- **She asked "Which flowerpot?" instead of asking where the key was**.
- **She had walked past all five flowerpots on her way round the house before she rang**.
