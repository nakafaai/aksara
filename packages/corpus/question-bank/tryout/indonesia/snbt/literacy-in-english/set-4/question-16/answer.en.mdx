# Given

After driving off angry because Gran claimed she had found the key at once and merely tested whether the narrator would come, the narrator suddenly **replays the phone call on the motorway**.

# Asked

What does the narrator realise on the motorway?

# Solution

Join the **two concrete clues** the narrator recalls in paragraph <InlineMath math="8" /> to see straight through Gran's face-saving story.

> Recalling that Gran had already walked past all five back-wall flowerpots yet still had to ask which flowerpot proves Gran could not find the key and invented the loyalty test to hide her struggle.

## Piecing together the motorway realisation

Paragraph <InlineMath math="8" /> lays out both pieces of evidence: <Highlight>She had not asked me where the key was. She had asked me which flowerpot. There were five of them along the back wall, and she had walked past all five on her way round the house before she rang</Highlight>.

Gran had already searched outside without success and genuinely needed the narrator's hint ("The geranium. The dead one."). Her claim of testing the narrator was **a proud cover story**.

## Why the other options fail

- **Found the key at once to test the narrator** is Gran's cover story, which the narrator rejects on the motorway.
- **Pretended to be locked out to get a visit** has no support: Gran truly locked herself out and stayed on the bench with the door shut.
- **Narrator hid it in the wrong flowerpot** is false: the narrator chose the cracked flowerpot under the geranium herself as promised.
- **Forgot which door the key opened** invents a problem with doors rather than finding the key.

# Answer

**That Gran had not been able to find the key and needed the narrator's directions, and that her story of a test covered this**.
