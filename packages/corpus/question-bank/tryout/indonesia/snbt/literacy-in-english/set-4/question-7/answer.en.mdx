Paragraph 2 builds a **dilemma**. A fee that is politically acceptable is small, "smaller than the price of a coffee", so it "may barely change behaviour". A fee high enough to deter visitors "would turn the city into a place only the wealthy can see". Either way, a fee is a poor tool for keeping numbers down.

- The passage does not discuss whether visitors know about the fee.
- The fee is charged to day visitors, not residents.
- Avoiding the fee by arriving late is not mentioned.
- Souvenirs appear only as part of the cost of a day trip, not as a use of fee money.

So a fee is unlikely to keep numbers down because an acceptable fee is too small to deter, while a deterring fee would shut out the less wealthy.
