# Given

Five statements on teenage sleep and later high-school starts must be checked on whether **both** Text A and Text B accept them.

# Asked

Which of the five statements would both writers accept? Choose Yes or No for each.

# Solution

Mark **Yes** only where **genuine agreement** appears in both texts, keeping shared biological premises separate from Text B's local cost trade-offs.

> Both writers agree that teenagers' shifted body clocks make early starts hard and that later starts benefit students, while disagreeing on ignoring costs and projecting thirty-four minutes locally.

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **No**.
