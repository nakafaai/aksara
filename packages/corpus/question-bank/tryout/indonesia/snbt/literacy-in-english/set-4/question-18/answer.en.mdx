# Given

Five statements trace **the sequence of events** across hiding the spare key, the phone call, the drive home, and the Sunday rearrangement.

# Asked

Which of the five statements are supported by the story? Choose Yes or No for each.

# Solution

Test every statement against **narrative evidence rather than Gran's cover story**, checking exact order and timing for each event.

> The narrator kept the original hiding spot secret until telling Gran on the phone, Gran's claim of finding it immediately fails against the five-flowerpot clue, anger gave way on the motorway, and neither ever discussed it later.

## Verification of the five statements

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence from the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" />. Gran found the key as soon as she started looking | **No** | Although Gran claimed <Highlight>She had found it at once</Highlight>, she had walked past all five pots and still asked <Highlight>Which flowerpot?</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="2" />. Narrator hid the key without telling Gran where | **Yes** | Gran <Highlight>made me promise not to tell her where</Highlight> and the narrator <Highlight>told no one</Highlight>. |
| <InlineMath math="3" />. Gran learned on the phone it was in a flowerpot at the back | **Yes** | During the call, the narrator <Highlight>said the spare was in the flowerpot at the back</Highlight>, prompting Gran's follow-up question. |
| <InlineMath math="4" />. Narrator was still angry when the drive home ended | **No** | She was angry <Highlight>for most of the drive home</Highlight>, before understanding hit on the motorway. |
| <InlineMath math="5" />. They talk about Gran's memory after moving the key | **No** | Paragraph <InlineMath math="9" /> explicitly says <Highlight>neither of us mentioned it again</Highlight>. |

# Answer

Statements **<InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="3" />** are **Yes**, while statements **<InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />** are **No**.
