Each statement points to one moment, and the first one tests whether you believe **Gran's version** or the narrator's evidence.

Statement 1. Gran says "She had found it at once", but she "had walked past all five" flowerpots before she rang and then asked "Which flowerpot?" The evidence goes against her claim. No.

Statement 2. Gran "made me promise not to tell her where", and the narrator "told no one". Yes.

Statement 3. On the phone the narrator "said the spare was in the flowerpot at the back". Only then did Gran ask "Which flowerpot?" Yes.

Statement 4. The narrator "was angry for most of the drive home, and then, on the motorway, I worked out what she had not said". The anger gave way before the drive ended. No.

Statement 5. "Neither of us mentioned it again." No.

So statements 2 and 3 are Yes, and statements 1, 4 and 5 are No.
