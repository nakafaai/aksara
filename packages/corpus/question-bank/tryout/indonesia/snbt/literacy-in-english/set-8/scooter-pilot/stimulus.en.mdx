A city ran a four-month pilot of shared electric scooters. Riders unlocked a scooter with a phone app, rode it, and left it at any marked parking spot. The operator reported the figures below. At the start of month 3, the city did two things at once: it lowered the scooters' top speed from 25 to 15 kilometres per hour, and it allowed the operator to add 300 more scooters.
