When the traffic disappeared, the sparrows **sang more softly**, yet their songs still carried about twice as far. If the birds lowered their volume as soon as the noise went away, their earlier loud singing was most likely **a response to the traffic**. The lockdown acted like a natural experiment that removed the noise and showed what changed.

- The doubled distance describes how far songs carried, not a limit on hearing.
