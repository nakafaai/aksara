# Given

Out of **<InlineMath math="50{,}000" /> total guests**, <InlineMath math="7{,}000" /> answered the survey (<InlineMath math="6{,}440" /> positive ratings) and <InlineMath math="43{,}000" /> did not answer. Suppose **half (<InlineMath math="50\%" />)** of the non-answerers would have rated their stay good or excellent.

# Asked

About what percentage of **all** the chain's guests would then have rated their stay good or excellent?

# Solution

> A hotel chain advertises that <InlineMath math="92\%" /> of guests who answered its online survey last year rated their stay "good" or "excellent".

Weight the two groups by their **actual guest counts** rather than taking an unweighted average of <InlineMath math="92\%" /> and <InlineMath math="50\%" />.

## Adding satisfied guests across both groups

Count the positive ratings among respondents, add half of the <InlineMath math="43{,}000" /> non-respondents, and divide by the **full guest total** (<InlineMath math="50{,}000" />):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{Non-respondents} &= 50{,}000 - 7{,}000 = 43{,}000 \\ \text{Satisfied non-respondents} &= \frac{1}{2} \times 43{,}000 = 21{,}500 \\ \text{Total satisfied share} &= \frac{6{,}440 + 21{,}500}{50{,}000} = \frac{27{,}940}{50{,}000} = 55.88\% \approx 56\% \end{aligned}" />

| Guest group | Group size | Satisfied guests | Share satisfied |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Answered survey | <InlineMath math="7{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="6{,}440" /> | <InlineMath math="92\%" /> |
| Did not answer | <InlineMath math="43{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="21{,}500" /> | <InlineMath math="50\%" /> |
| All guests combined | <InlineMath math="50{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="27{,}940" /> | <InlineMath math="55.88\%" /> |

Because the <InlineMath math="43{,}000" /> non-respondents vastly outnumber the <InlineMath math="7{,}000" /> respondents, the true combined share (<InlineMath math="56\%" />) sits much closer to <InlineMath math="50\%" /> than to the plain unweighted average of <InlineMath math="71\%" />.

# Answer

About **<InlineMath math="56\%" />** of all the chain's guests would have rated their stay good or excellent.
