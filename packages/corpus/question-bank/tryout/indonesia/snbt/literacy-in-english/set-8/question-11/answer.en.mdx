A rate per 10,000 trips **divides injuries by trips** and scales the result to 10,000.

<BlockMath math="\frac{16}{80{,}000} \times 10{,}000 = 2.0" />

- 2.7 is the rate for all four months together, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{60}{224{,}000} \times 10{,}000" />, not for month 4.
- 2.5 is the rate for month 3, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{18}{72{,}000} \times 10{,}000" />.
- 4.0 is the rate for month 1, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{12}{30{,}000} \times 10{,}000" />.
- 16.0 is the raw number of injuries in month 4, not a rate.

So month 4 had 2.0 injuries per 10,000 trips.
