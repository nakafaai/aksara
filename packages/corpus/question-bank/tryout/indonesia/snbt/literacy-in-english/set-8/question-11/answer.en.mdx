# Given

The hotel chain advertises that **<InlineMath math="92\%" /> of guests who answered** its optional survey rated their stay good or excellent. Harbour had <InlineMath math="20{,}000" /> guests (<InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> answers, <InlineMath math="950" /> positive) and Station had <InlineMath math="30{,}000" /> guests (<InlineMath math="6{,}000" /> answers, <InlineMath math="5{,}490" /> positive).

# Asked

Which statement accurately describes what the advertised <InlineMath math="92\%" /> represents?

# Solution

> A hotel chain advertises that <InlineMath math="92\%" /> of guests who answered its online survey last year rated their stay "good" or "excellent".

Check both the **denominator of the <InlineMath math="92\%" />** (survey answers, not all guests) and **which hotel supplied most of those answers**.

## Checking the combined percentage and weights

Add the positive ratings and divide by total survey answers, then compare Station's share of answers:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{Combined positive rate} &= \frac{950 + 5{,}490}{1{,}000 + 6{,}000} = \frac{6{,}440}{7{,}000} = 92\% \\ \text{Station share of answers} &= \frac{6{,}000}{7{,}000} = \frac{6}{7} \end{aligned}" />

| Hotel | Guests | Survey answers | Positive rate | Share of answers |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Harbour | <InlineMath math="20{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="95\%" /> | <InlineMath math="1/7" /> |
| Station | <InlineMath math="30{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="6{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="91.5\%" /> | <InlineMath math="6/7" /> |

Because **six in seven answers** came from Station, the combined <InlineMath math="92\%" /> sits close to Station's <InlineMath math="91.5\%" /> rather than Harbour's <InlineMath math="95\%" />. It is not a share of all <InlineMath math="50{,}000" /> guests (which is about <InlineMath math="13\%" />), not an equal average (<InlineMath math="93.25\%" />), and three in five is Station's share of guests rather than answers.

# Answer

The correct statement is that **it is the share of answers that rated the stay good or excellent, and about six in seven of those answers came from Station, so it mostly reflects Station's guests**.
