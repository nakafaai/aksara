# Given

The narrator's mother explains that Mr Lin **lived two streets past theirs** and took the same bus anyway. Yet after the narrator opened her front door, she saw him **walk back down the lane towards the main road**.

# Asked

What does the narrator's final thought about "which way he had walked when he left" suggest?

# Solution

> He waited until I had the door open, lifted one hand, and walked back down the lane towards the main road.

Compare the mother's explanation of where Mr Lin lived with the **actual direction he walked** once the narrator was safely inside.

## Spotting the mismatch in direction

Her mother believed Mr Lin <Highlight>lived two streets past ours</Highlight>, meaning his home lay further up the lane past her gate. Instead, he <Highlight>walked back down the lane towards the main road</Highlight>, returning the way they had come.

That mismatch suggests **he may not live past her house at all** and quietly went out of his way to see her home safely.

The other interpretations conflict with the text. Her mother worked at the same hospital, so his job is never in doubt. Nothing hints at confusion, he walked away rather than continuing to follow her, and standing openly under the streetlight while lifting a hand shows he was not trying to hide.

# Answer

The last thought suggests **that Mr Lin may not live past her house at all, and went out of his way to see her home**.
