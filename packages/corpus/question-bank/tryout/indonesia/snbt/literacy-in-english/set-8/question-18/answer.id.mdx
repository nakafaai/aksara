# Diketahui

Cerita memberi rincian jelas soal **waktu, pergerakan, dan pengetahuan tokoh**: bus terakhir berangkat pukul sebelas, giliran kerja Mr Lin selesai pukul setengah sebelas, ia berhenti di bawah lampu tengah gang lalu kembali ke jalan utama, dan narator tidak mengenal namanya.

# Ditanyakan

Berdasarkan cerita, apakah masing-masing dari lima pernyataan didukung teks (**Yes** atau **No**)?

# Penyelesaian

> She said he was the night porter at the hospital where she worked, that his shift ended at half past ten, that he lived two streets past ours, and that she had asked him, since he took the same bus anyway, to keep an eye on me as far as the gate.

Cocokkan setiap pernyataan dengan **urutan kejadian dan detail eksplisit** dalam cerita.
