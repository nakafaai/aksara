# Given

The story gives concrete details about **timing, movement, and knowledge**: the bus left at eleven, Mr Lin's shift ended at half past ten, he waited under the halfway streetlight before walking back down the lane, and the narrator had never heard his name.

# Asked

Based on the story, is each of the five statements supported (**Yes** or **No**)?

# Solution

> She said he was the night porter at the hospital where she worked, that his shift ended at half past ten, that he lived two streets past ours, and that she had asked him, since he took the same bus anyway, to keep an eye on me as far as the gate.

Check every statement directly against the **exact sequence of events** in the story.
