Lumen, a streaming service, reports its subscribers every quarter. Anyone with an active account counts as a subscriber, including people in a free trial. Lumen had never offered a trial before July, when it began giving new customers their first month free. On the same day, it raised its monthly price.

In his letter to investors, Lumen's chief executive called July to September "our best quarter ever", pointing to the <InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> subscribers it had added. An analyst replied that the quarter's churn rate, the share of the subscribers at the start of a quarter who cancel during it, had more than doubled. She added that two changes made on the same day could not be told apart.

The company answered that most of the extra cancellations came from people ending their trial without ever paying, which it called "healthy sorting" rather than lost customers. Neither side published how many of the cancellations came from trial accounts, or how many came from people who had signed up during the same quarter in which they cancelled.
