A language-learning app followed 10,000 people who signed up in the same month. At random, half of them received a daily reminder notification and the other half received none. The table shows the share of each group still using the app after 7, 30, and 90 days.

**Share of new users still active**

| Group | Day 7 | Day 30 | Day 90 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No reminder (5,000 people) | <InlineMath math="40\%" /> | <InlineMath math="18\%" /> | <InlineMath math="8\%" /> |
| Daily reminder (5,000 people) | <InlineMath math="52\%" /> | <InlineMath math="24\%" /> | <InlineMath math="9\%" /> |

A week after the study ended, the company's blog announced: "Daily reminders keep learners coming back for good."
