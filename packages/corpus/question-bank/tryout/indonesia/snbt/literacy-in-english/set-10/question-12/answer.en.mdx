# Given

Lumen counts **anyone with an active account** as a subscriber, including free-trial users (first month free starting July). Between July and September, subscribers rose from <InlineMath math="480" /> thousand to <InlineMath math="580" /> thousand (<InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> added), with **<InlineMath math="90" /> thousand on a free trial** at quarter end.

# Asked

Which statements can be validly concluded from the passage and the table?

# Solution

> Anyone with an active account counts as a subscriber, including people in a free trial.

Separate facts that follow directly from the **subscriber definition and table counts** from claims requiring unpublished breakdowns.

## Testing all five candidate conclusions

| Statement | Verification from text and table | Concluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price rise caused most extra July to September cancellations | Two changes happened same day and <Highlight>could not be told apart</Highlight> | **No** |
| About <InlineMath math="90" /> thousand end-of-September subscribers had not yet paid | <InlineMath math="90" /> thousand were <Highlight>on a free trial at end</Highlight> (first month free) | **Yes** |
| Most July to September triallists cancelled by end of December | Table never splits cancellations vs conversions to paid | **No** |
| CEO's <InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> includes free-trial users and payers | Net gain <InlineMath math="580 - 480 = 100" /> thousand uses the all-active definition | **Yes** |
| Without the trial, Lumen would have lost subscribers in July to September | No counterfactual data (prior quarters grew by <InlineMath math="40" /> thousand) | **No** |

Notice how the three rejected statements all depend on **unpublished splits** (price-rise vs trial cancellations, conversion vs cancellation rates, or a counterfactual without trials).

# Answer

**The statements that can be concluded are**:

- Some <InlineMath math="90" /> thousand of the subscribers counted at the end of September had not yet paid anything.
- The chief executive's figure of <InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> counts people on a free trial as well as paying customers.
