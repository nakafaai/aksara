The comparable workshop rounds support a **limited real-event trial** of the separated sheet. Their measured outcome is better cost classification, not proof of an accurate final total.

At the real event, **final supplier quotes** replace the workshop estimates. The team can then check whether costs are still classified correctly and whether the resulting total is reliable. That follow-up addresses the stated limitation without making every change permanent.
