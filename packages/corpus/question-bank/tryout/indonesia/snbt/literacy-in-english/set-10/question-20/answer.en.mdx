# Given

The whole story relies on **understated showing rather than telling**: an extra twelfth crossing justified by the timetable, a silent drift, reading thirty-one years of ledger entries, skipping the opening, and counting the first cars crossing the bridge from the kitchen window.

# Asked

Based on the story as a whole, is each statement about its meaning and telling supported (**Yes** or **No**)?

# Solution

> My father had made the crossing eleven times a day for thirty-one years, and on the last day he made it twelve times, because, he said, the timetable had never said anything about a last day.

Test each statement against the **narrator's restrained technique** and the **father's actual behaviour** through the final scene.
