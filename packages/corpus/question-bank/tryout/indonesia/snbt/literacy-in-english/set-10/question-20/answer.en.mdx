The designers drew the cover's instructions **as diagrams** because they **could not assume the finders would know our language**. The town faces a smaller version of the same problem: in 500 years, today's language **may be hard to read**. Explaining the contents through diagrams follows the designers' reasoning.

- The record carried music **from many cultures**, the opposite of one region only.
- A pulsar map locates the Sun among the stars. People on Earth do not need it to find the town.
- Instructions in today's language are exactly what may become hard to read.
- Speed of discovery was not part of the designers' plan.

So the most useful idea is to explain how to open and use the contents through diagrams.
