Statement 1. The father never says how he feels. He speaks of the sale "in the voice he used for the weather", says nothing during the drift, and gives "a bad knee" as his reason. The reader has to infer the feeling. Yes.

Statement 2. The narrator calls the bridge "a long pale curve" and reports the band and the cars without judgement. Nowhere does she call it a mistake. No.

Statement 4. The opposite is suggested. He reads the ledger "for a long time" back to the first year and keeps counting the next day. Those aren't the actions of someone who thinks the ferry never mattered. No.
