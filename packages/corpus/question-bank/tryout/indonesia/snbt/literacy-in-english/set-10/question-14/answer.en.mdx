The two explanations blame **different people**. "Healthy sorting" says the extra cancellations came from triallists who never paid. A price-rise story says they came from existing customers who now paid more. So the information you need splits the cancellations by who cancelled.

That's exactly the missing figure the passage points to: "Neither side published how many of the cancellations came from trial accounts". Count the July to September cancellations by trial accounts and by people who had been paying before July, and you can see which group grew.

- Revenue mixes the higher price with the number of payers, so it can't say who cancelled.
- Knowing where sign-ups came from tells you about joiners, not about who left.
- Rival services differ in shows, prices and customers, so their churn is a weak comparison. Even a perfect one wouldn't split Lumen's own cancellations.
- Triallists left at the end of December tell you about October to December, not about who cancelled in July to September.

The best information is how many July to September cancellations came from trial accounts and how many from subscribers who had paid before July.
