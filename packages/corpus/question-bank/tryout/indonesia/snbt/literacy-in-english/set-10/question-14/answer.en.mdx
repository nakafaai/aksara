# Given

Before July, Lumen averaged about **<InlineMath math="22{,}000" /> cancellations** per quarter (<InlineMath math="20{,}000" /> then <InlineMath math="22{,}000" />). In July to September, cancellations jumped to **<InlineMath math="60{,}000" />** (about <InlineMath math="38{,}000" /> extra). Lumen claims most of those extra cancellations were free-trial users leaving **without ever paying**.

# Asked

Would each of the five findings, if true, **weaken** Lumen's "healthy sorting" claim (**Yes** or **No**)?

# Solution

> The company answered that most of the extra cancellations came from people ending their trial without ever paying, which it called "healthy sorting" rather than lost customers.

Compare each finding against the **<InlineMath math="38{,}000" /> extra cancellations** and the requirement that those users left **without ever paying**.

## Checking each finding against the extra cancellations

First note the size of the jump above the pre-July baseline of <InlineMath math="22{,}000" /> cancellations:

<BlockMath math="\text{Extra cancellations in July to September} = 60{,}000 - 22{,}000 = 38{,}000" />

| Statement | Impact on Lumen's claim | Weakens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" /> | Long-paying cancellations jump by <InlineMath math="28{,}000" /> (<InlineMath math="50{,}000 - 22{,}000" />), most of the <InlineMath math="38{,}000" /> extra | **Yes** |
| <InlineMath math="2" /> | <InlineMath math="30{,}000" /> never-paid trial exits explain most of the <InlineMath math="38{,}000" /> extra | **No** |
| <InlineMath math="3" /> | Trial users paying for at least one month contradicts <Highlight>without ever paying</Highlight> | **Yes** |
| <InlineMath math="4" /> | Churn dropping when trials shrink supports Lumen's trial explanation | **No** |
| <InlineMath math="5" /> | Only <InlineMath math="3{,}000" /> extra (<InlineMath math="25{,}000 - 22{,}000" />) from long-paying customers leaves trials as main source | **No** |

Watch Statement <InlineMath math="2" /> vs Statement <InlineMath math="1" />: putting <InlineMath math="30{,}000" /> of the <InlineMath math="38{,}000" /> extra exits on unpaid trials **supports** Lumen, whereas putting <InlineMath math="28{,}000" /> extra exits on long-paying customers directly **contradicts** it.

# Answer

- Statement <InlineMath math="1" />: **Yes**
- Statement <InlineMath math="2" />: **No**
- Statement <InlineMath math="3" />: **Yes**
- Statement <InlineMath math="4" />: **No**
- Statement <InlineMath math="5" />: **No**
