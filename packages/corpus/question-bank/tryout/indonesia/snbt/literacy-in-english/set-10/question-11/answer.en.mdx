# Given

**Lumen's subscriber table tracks** starting subscribers, sign-ups, cancellations, ending subscribers, and free-trial accounts across all four quarters of the year.

# Asked

Which statements about Lumen's paying subscribers and churn rates can be concluded.

# Solution

Two ideas do most of the work. **Paying** subscribers are the subscribers at the end minus those still on a free trial, and before July there were no trials at all. The **churn rate** is cancellations divided by the subscribers at the start of the quarter.

> Guests tasting free samples at the shop door inflate the crowd inside, so you have to subtract free trials to see how many people actually bought a ticket.

## Computing paying counts and churn rates per quarter

| Quarter | Paying at end | Churn rate |

| January to March | <InlineMath math="440 - 0 = 440" /> | <InlineMath math="20 \div 400 = 5\%" /> |
| April to June | <InlineMath math="480 - 0 = 480" /> | <InlineMath math="22 \div 440 = 5\%" /> |
| July to September | <InlineMath math="580 - 90 = 490" /> | <InlineMath math="60 \div 480 = 12.5\%" /> |
| October to December | <InlineMath math="592 - 10 = 582" /> | <InlineMath math="58 \div 580 = 10\%" /> |

Statement <InlineMath math="3" />. <InlineMath math="582" /> paying at the end of December against <InlineMath math="490" /> at the end of September. Yes.

Statement <InlineMath math="4" />. <InlineMath math="10\%" /> is lower than <InlineMath math="12.5\%" />. No.

Statement <InlineMath math="5" />. The number of paying subscribers rose by <InlineMath math="582 - 490 = 92" /> thousand in October to December, and by <InlineMath math="40 + 40 = 80" /> thousand in January to June. This is the net change in the count, not the number of sign-ups. **<InlineMath math="92" /> is more than <InlineMath math="80" />**. Yes.

# Answer

Subtracting trial accounts and dividing cancellations by starting subscribers confirms statements <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="5" />.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Only about <InlineMath math="10" /> thousand more paying than in June | Yes |
| Faster growth in October to December | No |
| More paying in December than in September | Yes |
| Higher churn in October to December | No |
| Paying count rose more in October to December than in all of January to June | Yes |
