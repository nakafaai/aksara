Paragraph 2 explains that **meltwater can collect between the ice and the rock**, and the glacier **slides on it**. It then says that **because sliding depends on this water**, many glaciers move faster in summer, when there is more melting.

- The crystals **deform slowly** under pressure. They do not harden and push.
- The passage says snow builds up where more falls in winter, not that more falls in summer.
- Friction **slows** the ice near the walls, and warm weather is not said to increase it.
- The snout melts back, but it is not said to pull the glacier.

So many glaciers move faster in summer because more meltwater collects at the base and the glacier slides on it.
