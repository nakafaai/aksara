Opening the app is what the table already measures. To learn whether reminders improve **learning**, the company needs a direct measure of **language skill** that can be **compared between the two groups**. A single vocabulary test taken by both groups at the same point does this, and because the groups were formed at random, a difference in scores can be linked to the reminders.

- Counting reminders describes the treatment, not what people learned.
- Openings on the first day measure use, and only before reminders could have much effect.
- Phone sound settings might affect whether reminders are noticed, not what was learned.
- The number of languages offered is the same for everyone and says nothing about learning.

So the most useful measure is scores on one vocabulary test taken by both groups on day 90.
