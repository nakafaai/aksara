# Given

**Lumen's chief executive called** July to September its best quarter ever, while the table distinguishes total counted subscribers from paying subscribers after free trials.

# Asked

Which statements about the July-to-September quarter and trial conversions follow from the data.

# Solution

"Best" depends on what you count. The table lets you count **all subscribers** or only **paying subscribers**, who are the subscribers at the end minus those on a trial.

> Calling a quarter the best depends completely on whether you count everyone holding a free sample or only the people who actually paid.

## Comparing total sign ups against real paying growth

Statement <InlineMath math="1" />. Subscribers counted grew by <InlineMath math="40" />, <InlineMath math="40" />, <InlineMath math="100" /> and <InlineMath math="12" /> thousand. July to September is the largest. Yes.

Statement <InlineMath math="2" />. Paying subscribers grew by <InlineMath math="40" />, <InlineMath math="40" />, <InlineMath math="490 - 480 = 10" /> and <InlineMath math="582 - 490 = 92" /> thousand. July to September is the **smallest**. No.

Statement <InlineMath math="3" />. This one takes a careful step. Paying subscribers rose by <InlineMath math="92" /> thousand in October to December, yet only <InlineMath math="70" /> thousand people signed up in that quarter, and they started on a free month. Even if every one of them had paid, that covers at most <InlineMath math="70" /> of the <InlineMath math="92" />. The rest must have come from the <InlineMath math="90" /> thousand who were still on a trial at the end of September, people who signed up in July to September. Yes.

Statement <InlineMath math="4" />. Nobody published which cancellations came from trial accounts, so the cancellation rate of triallists can't be worked out. No.

# Answer

**July to September led in total counted subscribers and supplied paying conversions later, yet ranked lowest in immediate paying growth**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Best quarter by subscribers counted | Yes |
| Best quarter by paying subscribers | No |
| Some July to September sign-ups went on to pay | Yes |
| Triallists cancelled at a higher rate | No |
