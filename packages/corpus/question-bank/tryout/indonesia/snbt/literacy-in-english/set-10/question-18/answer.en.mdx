Check each statement against **what the story shows about the ledger**.

Statement 1. "I had seen that ledger all my life and had never once seen him read it." No.

Statement 2. The last scene puts "the ledger closed on the sill" beside him as he watches the bridge, tying the end to thirty-one years of records. Yes.

Statement 3. The story never says why he kept it, and nothing mentions the company requiring it. No.

Statement 4. Every entry held "the time, the number of passengers, the state of the river". "River quiet" is that third item, the same kind of note as always. Yes.

Statement 5. The ledger recorded how many passengers crossed. At the window he is "counting them under his breath", the cars that now cross without him. Yes.

So statements 2, 4 and 5 are Yes, and statements 1 and 3 are No.
