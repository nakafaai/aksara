The text recommends one method: ask about the missing data, **"siapa atau apa yang tidak sampai ke dalam catatan, dan mengapa"** (who or what didn't make it into the record, and why). Pick the questions that look for houses no longer visible.

- **Houses of the same era that collapsed**. This looks for houses that dropped out before being checked. The text says **"bangunan tua yang rapuh sudah lama runtuh"** (fragile old buildings collapsed long ago). This question fits.
- **Fragile houses already demolished**. This asks why some houses aren't in the record. This question fits.

The other three don't touch the missing data.

- The number of new houses doesn't explain which old houses disappeared.
- Building-material prices don't touch the filter that left only sturdy houses.
- Repainting only concerns how surviving houses look.

So the fitting questions ask about same-era houses that collapsed or were demolished and about fragile houses demolished before being checked.
