The definition distinguishes **sound level from sound-decay duration**.

| Measure | What is observed | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sound level | The measured level while a tone plays | Decibels |
| Reverberation time | Time for a 60 dB level drop after the source stops | Seconds |

The change from 0.86 to 0.71 seconds indicates faster decay in that measurement. It complements the sound-level table, but one microphone position does not represent the whole room. Defining the measure does not remove that limitation.
