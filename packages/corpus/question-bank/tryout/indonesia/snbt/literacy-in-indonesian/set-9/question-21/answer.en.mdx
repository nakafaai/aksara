# Given

Paragraph one of the pantun passage reports **official UNESCO recognition** following a joint submission by Indonesia and Malaysia. The final paragraph warns that this recognition **"should not be read as the end of the journey"** because a certificate merely records a tradition, whereas **"pantun survives as long as people keep answering one another with it."**

# Asked

The statement that best describes how the opening paragraph relates to the closing paragraph.

# Solution

See how the closing paragraph **qualifies the significance** of the official milestone announced in paragraph one without rejecting it.

> Paragraph one reports official recognition, while the final paragraph qualifies its meaning: a certificate only records a tradition, whereas pantun stays alive only through active use.

## Checking the five candidate relationships

Testing **each option against the opening and closing paragraphs** rules out all four misses.

| Relationship | Fit with opening and closing paragraphs | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening reports official recognition, closing qualifies its meaning because pantun lives only when actively used | Accurately frames both paragraphs | **Correct** |
| Closing argues UNESCO status alone guarantees survival | Reversed, explicitly says a certificate is not enough | **Incorrect** |
| Opening rejects the joint submission | False, calls it acknowledging a long-standing reality | **Incorrect** |
| Both explain pantun rhyme structure | False, form is discussed in paragraph two | **Incorrect** |
| Closing warns UNESCO will revoke recognition | Never stated in the passage | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The first paragraph **reports official recognition**, while the last paragraph **qualifies its meaning: a certificate merely records a tradition, whereas pantun lives on only if people keep using it**.
