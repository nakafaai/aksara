# Given

**The passage includes** a sample pantun alongside the eight-to-twelve syllable guideline, a-b-a-b rhyme, and wedding exchange customs.

# Asked

Which statements follow from counting syllables in the sample pantun and checking the passage.

# Solution

> Count the syllables one beat at a time and match the endings of lines one and three against lines two and four.

Two statements need syllable counting, the rest need matching to the text.

The statement about palang pintu can be concluded. The two sides exchange pantun **"sebelum pengantin pria dipersilakan masuk"** (before the groom is invited in).

The statement that pantun is only for advice can't be concluded. It's used **"untuk menasihati, merayu, berkelakar, atau menyindir dengan halus"** (to advise, woo, joke or tease gently).

The statement about the first line can be concluded. *Bu-rung ca-mar ter-bang ke mu-a-ra* has <InlineMath math="10" /> syllables.

The statement about an a-a-b-b rhyme can't be concluded. The end sounds are *muara, perahu, saudara, tertipu*: lines one and three end in *-ra*, lines two and four in *-u*. The pattern is a-b-a-b.

The statement about the fourth line can't be concluded. *Su-pa-ya ke-lak ti-dak ter-ti-pu* has <InlineMath math="10" /> syllables, within the eight-to-twelve range.
