There are two clues. **Wax crack lines** show the cloth was really waxed, so it is not printed. **A neatly repeating pattern of the same size** is a feature of **stamped** batik, while hand-drawn motifs are rarely exactly alike. Together they point to stamped batik.

- The passage says cracks can appear on both kinds, not only hand-drawn batik.
- Printed cloth uses no wax, so it would have no wax cracks.
- Hand-drawn motifs are rarely exactly the same.
- Cloth made with a copper stamp and wax is stamped batik, not printed textile.

So the cloth is most likely stamped batik, because its pattern repeats neatly and it shows wax cracks.
