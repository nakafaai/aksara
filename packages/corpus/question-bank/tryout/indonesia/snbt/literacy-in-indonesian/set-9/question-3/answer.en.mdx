Paragraph three says water dropped from the air **often only puts out the fire on the surface**, while **embers underground keep spreading** and flare up elsewhere. Because peat fires smoulder inside the soil, water falling on the surface does not reach every source of fire.

- Water evaporating before it lands is not mentioned.
- Planes being grounded by smoke is not mentioned.

So water from the air is often not enough because embers spreading underground keep burning even when the surface is out.
