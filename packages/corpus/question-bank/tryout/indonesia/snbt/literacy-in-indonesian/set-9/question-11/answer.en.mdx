# Given

At Pasar Baru (**Text B**), average daily revenue fell from Rp<InlineMath math="600{,}000" /> to Rp<InlineMath math="250{,}000" /> (leaving <InlineMath math="250 : 600 = \tfrac{5}{12}" />). A Jalan Merdeka vendor currently earning **Rp<InlineMath math="500{,}000" /> per day** suffers the same percentage drop, while monthly rent after the grace period is **Rp<InlineMath math="150{,}000" />**.

# Asked

The vendor's estimated daily revenue after moving to Pasar Sentra and how it compares with one month's rent.

# Solution

Apply the **remaining-share ratio (<InlineMath math="\tfrac{5}{12}" />)** to Rp<InlineMath math="500{,}000" />, then compare that daily figure against monthly rent of Rp<InlineMath math="150{,}000" />.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{remaining share} = \frac{250{,}000}{600{,}000} = \frac{5}{12} \approx 41.7\%" />
<BlockMath math="\text{new daily revenue} = \frac{5}{12} \times 500{,}000 \approx 208{,}000\text{ rupiah/day}" />
</MathContainer>

> Daily revenue drops to roughly Rp<InlineMath math="208{,}000" />, so one month's rent (Rp<InlineMath math="150{,}000" />) is covered by less than a single day's revenue.

## Tracing the distractor options

Checking **percentage versus rupiah drop and rent comparison** eliminates all four distractors.

| Option | Source of figures | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About Rp<InlineMath math="208{,}000" />, monthly rent covered by under one day's revenue | Exact <InlineMath math="\tfrac{5}{12}" /> ratio and <InlineMath math="150{,}000 < 208{,}000" /> | **Correct** |
| Rp<InlineMath math="150{,}000" /> | Subtracts raw Rp<InlineMath math="350{,}000" /> drop instead of percentage | **Incorrect** |
| Rp<InlineMath math="290{,}000" /> | Multiplies by the <InlineMath math="58\%" /> lost share instead of remaining share | **Incorrect** |
| Rp<InlineMath math="208{,}000" />, rent takes three days | Wrong comparison, Rp<InlineMath math="150{,}000" /> is under one day | **Incorrect** |
| Rp<InlineMath math="250{,}000" /> | Copies Pasar Baru's post-move figure directly | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Daily revenue is **about Rp<InlineMath math="208{,}000" />**, and **a month's rent is covered by less than one day's revenue**.
