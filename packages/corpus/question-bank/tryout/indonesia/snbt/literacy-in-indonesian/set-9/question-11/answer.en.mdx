While other pickers exchange looks and complain, Mak Inah **only nods and goes back to her row**, which shows calm. When Laras asks, she explains that machines cut everything evenly so the tea tastes different, and that "orang pabrik tahu itu" (the factory people know it). At the end she says the best shoots "tidak bisa dipetik mesin" (cannot be picked by a machine). She is **sure her skill still has value**.

- She does not panic. She calmly returns to work.
- She does not protest. Some other pickers complain.
- She does not say her work will become lighter.
- She has no plan to move to the city. It is her granddaughter who studies there.

So Mak Inah's attitude is best described as calm and confident that her picking skill still has value.
