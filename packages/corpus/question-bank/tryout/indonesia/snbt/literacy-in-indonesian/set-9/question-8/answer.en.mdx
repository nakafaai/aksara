Leaked papers and booming tutoring centres are examples of **what happens when one exam decides graduation**. The author concludes that students learn to guess question formats and that "taruhannya terlalu tinggi sehingga ujian itu tidak lagi mengukur kemampuan dengan jujur" (the stakes are so high that the exam no longer measures ability honestly). The examples support that conclusion.

- Tutoring centres are presented as a bad sign, not the best preparation.
- The author says report-card marks are hard to compare.
- The author says the government needs a measure to find lagging regions.
- The author does not compare students' effort past and present.

So the examples support the view that high-stakes exams no longer measure ability honestly.
