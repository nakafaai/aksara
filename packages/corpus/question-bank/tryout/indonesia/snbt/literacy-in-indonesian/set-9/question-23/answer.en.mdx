Check each statement against its paragraph, especially the **tools** used for hand-drawn and stamped batik.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wax is removed by boiling the cloth | Benar (true) | Paragraph one: the wax is removed by boiling the cloth, a step called *nglorod*. |
| Hand-drawn batik uses a copper stamp | Salah (false) | Hand-drawn batik uses a **canting**. The copper stamp belongs to **stamped** batik. |
| Cracks appear on both kinds | Benar (true) | Paragraph three: "Pada keduanya, malam dapat retak ketika kain dicelup" (in both, the wax can crack when the cloth is dyed). |
| Stamping developed in the 15th century | Salah (false) | Paragraph two: stamping developed in the 19th century. |

The second statement swaps the tools of the two kinds. What separates hand-drawn from stamped batik is **the tool used to apply the wax**.
