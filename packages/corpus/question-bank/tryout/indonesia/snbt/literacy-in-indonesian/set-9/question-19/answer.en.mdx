# Given

In table one, all **<InlineMath math="120" /> tutored students** averaged <InlineMath math="62" /> (August) and <InlineMath math="74" /> (February). In table two, **<InlineMath math="50" /> tutored students** (starting between <InlineMath math="60" /> and <InlineMath math="65" />) averaged <InlineMath math="62" /> and <InlineMath math="73" />, leaving **<InlineMath math="70" /> tutored students** outside table two.

# Asked

The approximate average gain from August to February for the <InlineMath math="70" /> tutored students omitted from table two.

# Solution

Subtract **table two's total points** from **all tutored students' total points** in August and February, then divide each remainder by <InlineMath math="70" />.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{August mean (70 students)} = \frac{120 \times 62 - 50 \times 62}{70} = \frac{4{,}340}{70} = 62" />
<BlockMath math="\text{February mean (70 students)} = \frac{120 \times 74 - 50 \times 73}{70} = \frac{5{,}230}{70} \approx 74.7" />
<BlockMath math="\text{gain} = 74.7 - 62 \approx 12.7\text{ points}" />
</MathContainer>

> Because the <InlineMath math="50" />-student subset gained less (<InlineMath math="11" />) than the overall <InlineMath math="120" />-student mean (<InlineMath math="12" />), the remaining <InlineMath math="70" /> students must have gained more: about <InlineMath math="12.7" /> points.

## Checking the distractor figures

Identifying **which subgroup each number belongs to** isolates the exact gain.

| Option | What the number represents | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About <InlineMath math="12.7" /> points | Exact gain of the <InlineMath math="70" /> tutored students outside table two | **Correct** |
| <InlineMath math="12" /> points | Gain of all <InlineMath math="120" /> tutored students combined | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="11" /> points | Gain of the <InlineMath math="50" /> students inside table two | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="1" /> point | February difference <InlineMath math="74 - 73" />, not a gain | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="9" /> points | Gain of non-tutored students | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The tutored students omitted from table two gained **about <InlineMath math="12.7" /> points**.
