The author grants that **the comparison problem is real** and that a common measure is needed. However, the author rejects an exam that **decides graduation**, because the stakes are too high. The proposal is **a middle way**: a common exam that maps quality and helps students, but is not the only basis for graduation.

- The author says report-card marks are hard to compare between schools.
- Making the exam decide graduation is the proposal the author rejects.
- The author wants a common exam for all, so that lagging regions can be identified.
- Tutoring centres are named as a bad side effect of high-stakes exams, not a replacement.

So the author's view is that a common exam is still needed but should not be the only basis for graduation.
