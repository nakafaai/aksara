# Given

**The story follows** Pak Sastro and Dita translating old letters, recalling a necklace thought lost in a move, and choosing wording.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded from the sequence of events and who spoke in the story.

# Solution

> Track the exact order in which the letters were translated and who suggested each word so Dita and Pak Sastro never get swapped.

Some statements need the order of events and who said what.

The statement about the fifth letter can be concluded. Dita asks about the **"kalung dalam surat kelima itu"** (necklace in the fifth letter) after reading the translations, so the fifth letter was translated before she asked.

The statement that the letters were written to her husband can't be concluded. They were written **"kepada ibunya di Wonogiri"** (to her mother in Wonogiri).

The statement about the necklace can be concluded. **"Selama ini ia mengira kalung itu hilang ketika mereka pindah rumah"** (all this time he had thought the necklace was lost when they moved).

The statement that Dita suggested "pendiam" can't be concluded. Pak Sastro weighed that word himself. Dita suggested **"sukses"** (successful).

The statement that he has decided can't be concluded. He **"belum memutuskan apakah akan menerjemahkannya sama sekali"** (hasn't decided whether to translate it at all).

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The fifth letter was translated before Dita asked | Ya |
| The letters were written to her husband | Tidak |
| He thought the necklace was lost in the move | Ya |
| Dita suggested "pendiam" | Tidak |
| He has already decided | Tidak |
