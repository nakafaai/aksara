Follow the chain in paragraph two, then check each link.

- **Stomata close**. When soil water runs low, leaves close their stomata **"agar tidak kehilangan terlalu banyak uap air"** (so as not to lose too much water vapour).
- **The gas shifts**. So <InlineMath math="\mathrm{CO_2}" /> **"sulit masuk, sementara oksigen hasil fotosintesis menumpuk"** (struggles to get in while oxygen from photosynthesis builds up), and the balance **"bergeser ke arah oksigen, bahkan ketika suhu tidak berubah"** (shifts towards oxygen, even when the temperature doesn't change).
- **Photorespiration**. Rubisco binds oxygen more often, and recycling the product **"melepaskan kembali sebagian karbon"** (releases some carbon again).

Each other option has one wrong link.

- The text stresses the shift happens **"bahkan ketika suhu tidak berubah"** (even when the temperature doesn't change), so temperature isn't the link.
- Stomata close, not open, and oxygen builds up.
- <InlineMath math="\mathrm{CO_2}" /> doesn't build up. It struggles to get in.
- PEP carboxylase works in C4 plants and **"tidak bereaksi dengan oksigen"** (doesn't react with oxygen).

So the right chain is: stomata close, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{CO_2}" /> struggles to get in while oxygen builds up, then Rubisco binds oxygen more often and some carbon is released again.
