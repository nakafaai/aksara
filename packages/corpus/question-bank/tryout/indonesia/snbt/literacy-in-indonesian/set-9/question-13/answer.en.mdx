A theme is the basic idea that holds a story together. The key event here is **the announcement of machine harvesting**, and Mak Inah's response centres on **the skill of choosing shoots** that machines lack. Laras's recording and Mak Inah's closing line reinforce that idea. So the theme is the value of human handwork as machines arrive.

- There is no competition to become foreman.
- Laras's assignment is only why she came, not the heart of the story.
- The morning mist is only setting, not the main idea.
- The grandmother-granddaughter bond is present but centres on Mak Inah's skill, and Mak Inah does not live in the city.

So the most fitting theme is the value of skilled human hands as machines arrive.
