The claim assumes genes and their location are enough. A weakening finding must show that even correctly switched-on genes don't make rice work like a C4 plant.

- **Another condition in the text**. The C4 pump needs a bundle sheath **"tersusun rapat dan kaya kloroplas"** (tightly packed and rich in chloroplasts) so <InlineMath math="\mathrm{CO_2}" /> **"tidak lolos lagi"** (doesn't escape), while rice's sheath **"hanya memuat sedikit kloroplas"** (holds few chloroplasts).
- **The weakening finding**. If rice with every C4 enzyme still loses <InlineMath math="\mathrm{CO_2}" /> from its sheath, the problem is leaf structure, not genes.

The other findings don't weaken the claim.

- Enzymes in more C3 plants fit the optimists' view.
- C4 appearing many times is also why they're optimistic.
- Enzymes made in the right cells support the claim.
- High cost has nothing to do with whether genes alone are enough.

So the most weakening finding is rice with every C4 enzyme still losing <InlineMath math="\mathrm{CO_2}" /> from its bundle sheath.
