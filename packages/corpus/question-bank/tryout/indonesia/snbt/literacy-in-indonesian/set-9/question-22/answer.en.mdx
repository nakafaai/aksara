# Given

According to the passage, a valid pantun stanza must meet **three criteria together**: four lines, an **a-b-a-b rhyme scheme**, and **two opening sampiran lines** (imagery of nature or daily life) before **two message lines**.

# Asked

All stanzas that fulfill every pantun criterion including nature or daily-life sampiran lines.

# Solution

Since all five options have four lines, check **rhyme scheme and opening sampiran** on each stanza.

> Only the coconut-tree stanza and the sepat-fish stanza satisfy both the a-b-a-b rhyme scheme and two opening imagery lines ahead of the two-line message.

## Checking rhyme and sampiran across all five stanzas

Testing **both rhyme pattern and structure** selects the two true pantun stanzas.

| Stanza | End-rhyme pattern | Two opening sampiran lines | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coconut tree (*pantai, angin, santai, rajin*) | a-b-a-b | Yes (nature imagery) | **Pantun** |
| Sepat fish (*kolam, makan, semalam, dimaafkan*) | a-b-a-b | Yes (fish imagery) | **Pantun** |
| Listen child (*pesan, perlahan, bohongan, ketenangan*) | a-a-a-a (syair) | No (all four lines give advice) | **Not pantun** |
| Study every day | a-b-a-b | No (all four lines give advice) | **Not pantun** |
| Picking guava (*jambu, tebu, pandai, lalai*) | a-a-b-b | Yes | **Not pantun** |

# Answer

The stanzas that fulfill all pantun criteria are **the coconut-tree stanza** and **the sepat-fish stanza**.
