By the text, a stanza is a pantun if it meets three conditions at once: **four lines**, **abab rhyme**, and **two lines of sampiran** before **two lines of isi** (message). All the stanzas have four lines, so check the rhyme and the sampiran.

**The coconut tree: a pantun**. The line endings are *pantai, angin, santai, rajin*, abab. The first two lines are a nature image, the last two a message.

**The sepat fish: a pantun**. The endings are *kolam, makan, semalam, dimaafkan*, abab. The first two lines picture a fish, the last two give a message.

**"Dengarlah anak": not a pantun**. The endings *pesan, perlahan, bohongan, ketenangan* all end in *-an*, aaaa. All four lines are message. That's a syair.

**"Belajarlah setiap hari": not a pantun**. The rhyme is abab, but all four lines are already message. There's no sampiran.

**Picking guavas: not a pantun**. There's a sampiran, but the endings *jambu, tebu, pandai, lalai* are aabb.

So the stanzas that are pantun are the one about the coconut tree and the one about the sepat fish.
