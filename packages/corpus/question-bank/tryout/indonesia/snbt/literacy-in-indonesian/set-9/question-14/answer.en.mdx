# Given

**Text B proposes** either time-limited pavement vending leaving at least <InlineMath math="1.5" /> metres clear for pedestrians, or moving commuter stops closer to the market.

# Asked

Whether each proposed arrangement fits the conditions set out in Text B.

# Solution

> Check every option against Text B's concrete rules, namely limited hours, at least <InlineMath math="1.5" /> metres of clear walkway, or shifting transit stops to the market.

Text B's proposal has two forms. Check each arrangement against its conditions.

- **Selling on the pavement** is allowed if three conditions hold together: at **set hours**, on pavement **at least <InlineMath math="3" /> metres** wide, with **<InlineMath math="1.5" /> metres kept clear**.
- **If vendors are moved**, the bus stop can be moved to the front of the new market.

Lunchtime on a <InlineMath math="4" />-metre pavement with <InlineMath math="1.5" /> metres clear fits. All three conditions hold.

All day on a <InlineMath math="3" />-metre pavement leaving <InlineMath math="1" /> metre doesn't fit. The time isn't limited, and the clear space is less than <InlineMath math="1.5" /> metres.

Moving the bus stop to the front of Pasar Sentra fits. This is the second form of Text B's proposal, so that **"arus orang ikut bergeser"** (the flow of people shifts too).

A ban with no replacement site doesn't fit. Text B doesn't propose a ban, and both forms of its proposal still give vendors a place.

Evenings on a <InlineMath math="3" />-metre pavement with <InlineMath math="1.5" /> metres clear fits. <InlineMath math="3" /> metres meets the **at least** <InlineMath math="3" /> metres condition, and evenings count as set hours.
