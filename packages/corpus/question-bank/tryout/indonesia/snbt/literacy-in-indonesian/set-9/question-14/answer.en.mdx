Each village's percentage applies to a different number of children. First convert them to **numbers of stunted children**, then compare with **all the children**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 150 \times 14\% &= 21 \\ 200 \times 21\% &= 42 \\ 100 \times 27\% &= 27 \\ 100 \times 33\% &= 33 \\ \frac{21 + 42 + 27 + 33}{150 + 200 + 100 + 100} &= \frac{123}{550} \approx 22.4\% \end{aligned}" />

- 21.0 percent is Karangsari's figure alone.
- 23.8 percent is the plain average of the four percentages, which ignores the different numbers of children.
- 27.0 percent is Tanjungrejo's figure alone.
- 33.0 percent is Wonosari's figure alone.

So about 22.4 percent of the children in the four villages are stunted.
