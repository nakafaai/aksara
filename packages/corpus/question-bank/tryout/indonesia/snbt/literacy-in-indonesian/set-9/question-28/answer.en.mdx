A **fakta** (fact) can be checked by anyone, for example by timing the film. An **opini** (opinion) is the writer's judgement, which others may not share. A running time of 98 minutes can be checked, so it is a fact.

- "Kekuatan terbesar" (greatest strength) is the reviewer's judgement.
- "Terasa berlarut-larut" (feels drawn out) is the reviewer's impression. Others may feel differently.
- "Paling menyentuh" (most moving) is the reviewer's feeling.
- "Layak ditonton" (worth watching) is a recommendation, an opinion.

So the factual sentence is that the film runs for 98 minutes.
