The problem is that villages with safe water **also have higher incomes**, so the effects of water and income are mixed. To separate them, researchers need to compare families with **similar incomes** but **different water access**. If stunting is still lower in families with safe water, the link cannot be explained by income alone.

- The number of health centres in the district does not separate water from income.
- Village income averages only confirm a difference already known.
- Birth numbers concern neither water nor income.
- How water tastes does not show whether it is safe, and does not separate the effect of income.

So the most helpful data are stunting rates among families with similar incomes but different water access.
