Check each statement against its paragraph, especially **how peat forms** and **what canal blocking does**.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Peat forms from fully decayed plants | Salah (false) | Paragraph one: peat forms from plant remains piled up over thousands of years "tanpa membusuk sempurna" (without fully decaying). |
| Peat lies mainly in Sumatra, Kalimantan, and Papua | Benar (true) | Paragraph one says so directly. |
| Peat fires spread underground | Benar (true) | Paragraph three: the fire creeps underground, "membara tanpa nyala yang besar" (smouldering without large flames). |
| Blocking canals puts out burning fires | Salah (false) | Paragraph four: it "tidak memadamkan api yang sudah menyala" (does not put out fires already burning) but keeps peat from becoming fuel. |

The fourth statement traps readers who remember only that canal blocking is linked to fire. Blocking is a way to **prevent** fires, not to **put them out**.
