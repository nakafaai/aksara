# Given

**The passage compares** C3 and C4 photosynthesis, photorespiration under drought or cool shade, and the challenges of engineering C4 rice.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded from the text's explanation of C3 and C4 plants.

# Solution

> C4 plants do not win in every climate, and simply inserting C4 enzymes into rice is not enough without matching leaf structure.

Each statement has to be matched with the part of the text that addresses it.

The statement that drought can raise photorespiration without a rise in temperature can be concluded. Paragraph 2 says closed stomata shift the gas balance towards oxygen **"bahkan ketika suhu tidak berubah"** (even when the temperature doesn't change).

The statement that in cool, shady places C3 plants can do as well or better can be concluded. Paragraph 4 says that there C4 plants are **"tidak lebih unggul dan kadang malah kalah"** (no better and sometimes worse).

The statement that C4 enzymes are entirely absent from C3 plants can't be concluded. The last paragraph says those enzymes are **"sebenarnya juga ada pada tumbuhan C3"** (actually also present in C3 plants), only working elsewhere and in small amounts.

The statement that rice making C4 enzymes doesn't necessarily work like a C4 plant can be concluded. Some teams have made rice produce several C4 enzymes, **"Akan tetapi"** (However) its leaf veins are far apart and its bundle sheath has few chloroplasts. Paragraph 3 shows that such structure is needed to keep <InlineMath math="\mathrm{CO_2}" /> from escaping.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| --- | --- |

| In cool, shady places C3 plants can do as well or better | Ya |
