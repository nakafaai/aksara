A moral comes from **the main events** and **a character's key words**. The wayang broadcast the grandfather listened to is gone and cannot be brought back. The radio, however, can be repaired and speak again. Pak Darsa sums it up: "Siarannya sudah tidak ada. Tapi radionya masih bisa bersuara." (The broadcast is gone. But the radio can still make sound.) The ending reinforces this when he starts repairing the radio abandoned longest. The moral: the past cannot be replayed, but what remains of it can still be cared for.

- The story repairs an old radio rather than replacing it.
- The girl does not follow her mother's advice to throw the radio away, and the story does not blame her.
- She pays with money folded into small squares, not a high price.
- Pak Darsa's honesty costs him nothing, since the girl still pays.

So the moral is that the past cannot be replayed, but what remains of it can still be cared for.
