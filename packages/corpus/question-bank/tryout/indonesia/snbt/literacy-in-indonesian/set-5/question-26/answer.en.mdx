The passage names two elements of wayang's flexibility. First, **the main story is already familiar** to the audience. Second, there are **comic characters**, the punakawan, who are free to talk about the audience's own times in everyday language that makes people laugh. The step that combines both is to bring pupils' everyday problems in through comic characters within a familiar story.

- Memorising the plot concerns only the main story and leaves no room for present-day issues.
- Changing the story every week throws away the familiar story.
- The passage says all-night performances are less and less often watched to the end.
- The passage says Semar's advice is often wiser than the kings', and reading advice out directly uses no flexibility at all.

So the most fitting step is to bring pupils' everyday problems in through comic characters within a familiar story.
