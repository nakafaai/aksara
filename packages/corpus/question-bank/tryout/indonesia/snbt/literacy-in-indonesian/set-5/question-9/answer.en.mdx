# Given

**The ending brings** together the old engine, the retirement letter Pak Wiryo folds, and Darto's request to learn listening by ear.

# Asked

Whether each symbolic interpretation of the story's ending is true or false.

# Solution

> A sound literary reading fits every surrounding clue together rather than stretching one isolated gesture.

The end of the story is full of objects and sentences that carry two meanings. A reading is right when it fits **all** the clues around it, not just one sentence.

The reading about the old engine is right. The full sentence says: "Mesin tua harus dipanaskan pelan-pelan, pikirku, dan orang yang tahu caranya tidak bisa pergi begitu saja." (An old engine has to be warmed up slowly, I thought, and the person who knows how can't just leave.) The second half moves straight from engine to person. Pak Wiryo has spent thirty-one years on that ship, has a knee that cracks, and is being asked to go. The old engine that mustn't be rushed is **the narrator**.

The reading about folding the letter is wrong. Folding it "mengikuti bekas lipatannya" (along its old creases) means the letter has been opened and folded the same way many times. That's repeating a habit, not making a decision. It's also put away "untuk besok" (for tomorrow), so the matter is postponed again.

The reading about Darto's request is right. Before Darto asked, nobody had asked for that knowledge in thirty-one years. Afterwards the narrator "ingin berbicara lebih lama lagi" (wanted to talk even longer), answers "Besok aku masih di sini" (tomorrow I'm still here) faster than intended, and at the end uses Darto as the reason: Darto "belum mengenal bunyi pompa bahan bakar" (doesn't know the sound of the fuel pump yet).

The reading that Darto will never be able to take over is wrong. Pak Wiryo teaches Darto, and reckons that for the pump sound Darto "butuh seminggu" (needs a week). A week is a short time. Pak Wiryo believes Darto can do it, just not yet, and uses that estimate to stretch out the remaining time.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The old engine stands for Pak Wiryo | Benar |
| Folding the letter means accepting its date | Salah |
| Darto's request gives a reason to feel needed | Benar |
| Darto will never be able to take over | Salah |
