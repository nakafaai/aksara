The proposal in paragraph four regulates **how platforms work**: private accounts from the start, notifications off at night, and feeds not built to hold attention. Such a proposal only makes sense if the author assumes that **platform design helps decide the harm** teenagers face. If harm depended on users' age alone, changing the design would make no difference.

- The author designs protection that **does not depend on children being honest** about their age.
- The proposal lets users choose other settings, so adults can still change them.
- Private accounts reduce some risk, but the author does not assume bullying happens only on public accounts.
- Closing small platforms is neither proposed nor needed for the proposal.

So the proposal rests on the assumption that platform design, not only users' age, shapes the harm to teenagers.
