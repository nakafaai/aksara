Text 2's main objection rests on one condition: its country's electricity **comes mostly from coal**, so emissions merely move to the smokestack and the advantage of electric cars is small. Text 1 itself says the advantage **grows as electricity gets cleaner**. If most of that country's electricity came from clean plants, Text 2's main reason would weaken, and its writer would have stronger grounds to accept Text 1's conclusion.

- Charging stations make electric cars easier to use but do not change where the power comes from.
- Bus prices do not touch Text 2's objection about coal emissions.
- A higher petrol tax does not change the emissions of electric cars in that country.
- Falling petrol car sales do not answer the question of the power source.

So Text 2's writer would most likely support Text 1's conclusion more if most of the country's electricity came from clean plants.
