# Given

**The text and** table compare 2021 and 2024 river DO and BOD readings alongside rice fields, season timing, tofu workshops, and housing estates.

# Asked

Which statements are guaranteed by the text and table rather than left as speculation.

# Solution

> Ask which exact figure or sentence locks in the claim, whenever another factor could still act, words like certainly fail immediately.

Here you separate what **the data already guarantees** from what's only a guess. For each statement, ask which figure or sentence guarantees it.

The statement about the rice fields can be concluded. The last paragraph says the fields "sudah ada sebelum 2021" (were already there before 2021). So the fields are present at both measurements, and their presence isn't something that changed between 2021 and 2024.

The statement about the season can be concluded. The text says both measurements were taken "pada puncak kemarau" (at the peak of the dry season), and the river's flow was "hampir sama" (nearly the same). Season and flow are held steady, so neither can be the main explanation.

The statement about BOD without the housing estate can't be concluded. The text says the agency only "menduga" (suspects), and measurements upstream and downstream of the drain have "belum pernah" (never yet) been taken. There's no comparison stretch without the estate in 2024, so the word _pasti_ ("certainly") goes beyond the data.

The statement about incoming organic matter can be concluded. BOD shows how much organic matter is in the water. In 2024, BOD **rises** from <InlineMath math="4.0" /> mg/L at T4 to <InlineMath math="6.5" /> mg/L at T5.

A river always breaks down some organic matter along its course, as 2021 shows, when BOD on the same stretch fell from <InlineMath math="7.0" /> to <InlineMath math="5.0" /> mg/L. If the figure still rises, more must be coming in than is being broken down. The statement doesn't name the source, so it doesn't carry the guess about the estate.

The statement about cleaner water can't be concluded. Water arriving at the tofu workshops is measured at T2. There BOD rises from <InlineMath math="2.5" /> to <InlineMath math="3.0" /> mg/L and DO falls from <InlineMath math="6.6" /> to <InlineMath math="6.4" /> mg/L. The water is slightly dirtier.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The rice fields don't distinguish 2021 from 2024 | Ya |
| The season isn't the main explanation | Ya |
| Without the estate, BOD at T5 would certainly be lower | Tidak |
| More organic matter comes in than is broken down | Ya |
| Water reaching the workshops is cleaner in 2024 | Tidak |
