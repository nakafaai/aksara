The order book records an increase in **size reductions and repurposing old garments** over the last ten years. The interviews add the tailors' memories of customers wanting to extend garment life or adapt inherited clothes.

Together these details support an interpretation of changing repair needs. The order book does not record customer motives, however, and only three tailors were interviewed. The pattern can be linked to possible reasons without assigning those reasons to every customer.
