# Given

Between stations **T4** (km <InlineMath math="23" />) and **T5** (km <InlineMath math="31" />), 2024 BOD climbed from <InlineMath math="4.0" /> to <InlineMath math="6.5\text{ mg/L}" />. That <InlineMath math="8\text{ km}" /> stretch contains **both a housing estate and rice fields**, and the agency suspects the housing estate caused the jump.

# Asked

The additional data most needed to test whether the housing estate degrades water quality at T5.

# Solution

To isolate the **housing estate's outfall** from rice fields and other sources along T4-T5, sample river BOD **immediately upstream and immediately downstream of the housing outfall on the same day**.

> Bracketing the housing outfall with upstream and downstream measurements on the same day isolates its contribution from farmland and seasonal variation.

## Evaluating the five candidate datasets

Checking **whether each option isolates the housing outfall** identifies the only decisive test.

| Proposed data | Isolates housing-estate outfall | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| River BOD right upstream and right downstream of the housing outfall on the same day | Yes, any jump across the pipe points directly to the estate | **Correct** |
| Future housing construction plans | Does not test what already happened in 2024 | **Incorrect** |
| Wet-season readings at T5 | Still mixes all T4-T5 sources plus seasonal change | **Incorrect** |
| Treatment-plant outflow BOD | Covers T2-T3, far upstream of the housing estate | **Incorrect** |
| Rice-field area in 2024 | Does not measure what leaves the housing estate | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The most needed data are **river BOD measured right upstream and right downstream of the housing estate's discharge pipe on the same day**.
