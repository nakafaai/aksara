# Given

**The engine-room story** is told in the first person through Pak Wiryo's own voice.

# Asked

What main effect this first-person point of view creates for the reader.

# Solution

> Seeing everything through one character's filter lets readers spot the gap between how he explains himself and what others reveal.

A first-person point of view means everything you read has already been **filtered by one person**. The question is what that filter produces in this story.

Watch the pattern that keeps coming back. Pak Wiryo gives an explanation, then the story shows something that doesn't fit it.

| Pak Wiryo's explanation | What the story shows |
| --- | --- |
| "belum sempat membacanya sampai habis" (no time to read it to the end) | the first paragraph is "sudah kuhafal" (known by heart) |
| "Jarumnya yang manja" (the needle is fussy) | "Injektornya memang mulai menetes" (the injector really was dripping) |
| "Aku tidak tersinggung" (I'm not offended) | a dig, then a secret check |
| the knee cracks because "lantai pelat di situ licin" (the plate floor is slippery) | the passageway "makin tahun terasa makin sempit" (feels narrower every year) |
| "aku cuma ingin tahu" (I just wanted to know) | the chief engineer "memandangku agak lama" (looked at me for quite a while) |

Pak Wiryo never says anything about ageing, being pushed aside or fearing retirement. You learn it from the **gap** between the narrator's words and what happens around them. A narrator like this lets readers see more than the narrator is willing to admit. That's the main effect of this point of view.

The other options don't fit.

- Darto's and the chief engineer's thoughts are never opened up. You only see their words and looks from outside.
- Pak Wiryo's view of himself is contradicted by events again and again, so readers aren't invited to simply trust it.
- The story doesn't turn readers against Pak Wiryo. The teaching is patient, and the self-deception invites sympathy.
- Darto's needle turns out right, so the story doesn't claim hearing beats instruments.

# Answer

So **this point of view mainly lets readers see the gap between Pak Wiryo's explanations about himself and what the actions and the words of the people around him show**.
