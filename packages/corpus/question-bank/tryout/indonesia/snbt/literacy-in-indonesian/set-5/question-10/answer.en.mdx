Check each statement against the story, especially **where the wayang sound at the end comes from**.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The mother suggested throwing the radio away | Benar (true) | The girl says, "Ibu bilang dibuang saja" (Mum said to just throw it away). |
| The old broadcast was picked up again | Salah (false) | On that frequency "yang terdengar hanya desis" (only hissing could be heard) because the station had stopped broadcasting. The wayang came from a recording on his phone. |
| The girl came late | Salah (false) | The story says she came "tepat waktu" (on time) on Thursday afternoon. |
| Pak Darsa replaced two capacitors | Benar (true) | Over three nights he replaced two swollen capacitors and joined a broken wire. |

The second statement traps readers who only remember that wayang was heard in the end. Pak Darsa himself says, "Siarannya sudah tidak ada" (the broadcast no longer exists).
