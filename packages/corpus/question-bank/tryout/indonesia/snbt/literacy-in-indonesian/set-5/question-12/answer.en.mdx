The ending should be read together with **the opening**. Paragraph one mentions the radios on the shelf whose tags have yellowed because "pemiliknya tidak pernah kembali" (their owners never came back). After the girl leaves with her grandfather's radio working again, Pak Darsa looks at the shelf for a long time, then **takes down the radio with the yellowest tag** and opens its back, starting to repair it. The radio abandoned longest is now the one he works on. The meeting seems to have revived his care for the abandoned radios.

- Nothing suggests he wants to sell the radios.
- He opens the back of a radio, not looking for a phone cable.
- He shows no regret. He spoke honestly and the girl paid.
- Opening a radio to work on it runs against the idea that he will close the kiosk.

So his action most likely shows that meeting the girl revived his care for the radios that had been left behind.
