The phrase "menurut cerita yang diwariskan turun-temurun" (according to stories handed down through the generations) shows **the source** of the information: a tradition told from one generation to the next. The next sentence also uses **"disebut"** (is said to). With these two markers the author sets the Sunan Kalijaga story apart from other facts in the passage, such as UNESCO's 2003 recognition, and tells readers it **does not come from a firm record**.

- The passage does not say Sunan Kalijaga created the punakawan.
- UNESCO is not linked to the Sunan Kalijaga story.
- "Always" goes beyond the passage, which gives only one example.
- Paragraph four does not discuss young dalang, and its tone is not mocking.

So the phrase marks the story as coming from oral tradition rather than a firm record.
