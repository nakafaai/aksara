The writer's stance is signalled in the first two sentences: the label is "tidak salah, tetapi mudah menyesatkan" (not wrong, but easily misleading). So the writer neither rejects it nor accepts it whole. Your job is to find **which part is accepted** and **which part is called misleading**.

The accepted part is in the third sentence of the first paragraph. What the Portuguese brought was "alat musik petik kecil yang sekerabat dengan _cavaquinho_, beserta lagu-lagu para pelaut" (a small plucked instrument related to the cavaquinho, along with sailors' songs). The instrument's origin really is Portuguese.

The misleading part is in the next sentence. Keroncong "sebagai jenis musik, dengan susunan alat dan pola iringannya yang khas, tidak dikenal di Portugal. Ia tumbuh di Nusantara." (as a kind of music, with its own line-up and accompaniment pattern, isn't known in Portugal. It grew in the archipelago.) If "Portuguese heritage" is taken to mean the music arrived ready-made, that meaning is wrong.

The writer separates two things: where the **instrument** came from and where the **kind of music** came from. The rest of the passage then shows the music being shaped here, through Tugu, the travelling stambul theatre, radio and standardisation.

The other options take only one side, or add something that isn't there.

- The writer doesn't say keroncong comes entirely from Java. The small plucked instrument came from outside, and Kampung Tugu wasn't a Javanese community.
- The Tugu people did keep their music, but the passage is full of change, and the fifth paragraph says Tugu music differs from _keroncong asli_.
- The writer traces the origin in detail, so clearly doesn't think that's impossible.
- The writer doesn't use the late 19th century as a line between right and wrong. For the writer, the kind of music wasn't known in Portugal from the start.

So for the writer, the label is right about where the plucked instrument came from, but wrong if it's taken to mean the kind of music itself was brought from Portugal.
