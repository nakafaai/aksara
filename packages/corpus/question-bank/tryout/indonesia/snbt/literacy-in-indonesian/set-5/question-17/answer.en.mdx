# Given

Between stations **T2** and **T3**, the tofu hub is the sole waste source. In August 2021, BOD jumped from <InlineMath math="2.5" /> (T2) to <InlineMath math="10.0\text{ mg/L}" /> (T3). In August 2024, BOD rose from <InlineMath math="3.0" /> (T2) to <InlineMath math="4.5\text{ mg/L}" /> (T3).

# Asked

The percentage decrease in added BOD between T2 and T3 from August 2021 to August 2024.

# Solution

Compute the **added BOD across segment T2-T3** for each year (T3 minus T2), then divide the drop by the 2021 baseline addition.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{2021 addition} &= 10.0 - 2.5 = 7.5\text{ mg/L} \\
\text{2024 addition} &= 4.5 - 3.0 = 1.5\text{ mg/L}
\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{reduction} = \frac{7.5 - 1.5}{7.5} = \frac{6.0}{7.5} = 80\%" />
</MathContainer>

> The tofu hub's added BOD fell from <InlineMath math="7.5" /> to <InlineMath math="1.5\text{ mg/L}" />, an exact reduction of <InlineMath math="80\%" />.

## Checking the distractor percentages

Comparing **numerator and base** exposes why the four other percentages fail.

| Option | Ratio | What it computes | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="80\%" /> | <InlineMath math="(7.5 - 1.5) : 7.5" /> | Reduction in added BOD between T2 and T3 | **Correct** |
| <InlineMath math="55\%" /> | <InlineMath math="(10.0 - 4.5) : 10.0" /> | Raw BOD drop at T3 without subtracting T2 | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="45\%" /> | <InlineMath math="4.5 : 10.0" /> | Remaining share of raw T3 BOD | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="20\%" /> | <InlineMath math="1.5 : 7.5" /> | Remaining share of added BOD instead of reduction | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="60\%" /> | <InlineMath math="(7.5 - 3.0) : 7.5" /> | Subtracts 2024 T2 BOD instead of 2024 addition | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The added BOD between T2 and T3 decreased by **<InlineMath math="80\%" />**.
