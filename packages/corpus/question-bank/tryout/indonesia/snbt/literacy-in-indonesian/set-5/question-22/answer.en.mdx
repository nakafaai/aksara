A main idea must cover **the whole passage**. Paragraph one calls wayang a **school**. Paragraph two introduces the punakawan, paragraph three explains that their freedom in the goro-goro **makes wayang flexible** as a means of teaching, paragraph four gives an example of that flexibility being used, and the last paragraph shows that room for the punakawan is kept. Every paragraph meets in one idea: wayang is a flexible means of teaching, above all through the punakawan.

- The passage does not give the Indian origin of the stories as UNESCO's reason.
- Semar's wisdom is only a detail in paragraph two.
- The author reports shorter performances but does not recommend replacing the long ones.
- Sunan Kalijaga is one example, and the passage also mentions other dalang.

So the main idea is that wayang is a flexible means of teaching, especially through the part filled by the punakawan.
