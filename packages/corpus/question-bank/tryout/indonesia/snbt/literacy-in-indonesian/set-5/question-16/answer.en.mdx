# Given

Class-two water standards require **DO of at least <InlineMath math="4\text{ mg/L}" />** and **BOD of at most <InlineMath math="3\text{ mg/L}" />**. At **T3** (first point downstream of the tofu hub), BOD dropped from <InlineMath math="10.0" /> (2021) to <InlineMath math="4.5\text{ mg/L}" /> (2024) while DO rose to <InlineMath math="5.2\text{ mg/L}" />.

# Asked

The conclusion best supported by the water-quality passage and table.

# Solution

Test **T3** against both its **percentage BOD drop** and **both class-two water thresholds together**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{T3 BOD drop} &= \frac{10.0 - 4.5}{10.0} = 55\% \\
\text{T3 2024 DO} &= 5.2 \ge 4\text{ (passes)} \\
\text{T3 2024 BOD} &= 4.5 > 3\text{ (still fails)}
\end{aligned}" />

> BOD at T3 fell by more than half (<InlineMath math="55\%" />), yet T3 still satisfies only one of the two class-two thresholds (DO passes, BOD remains above <InlineMath math="3\text{ mg/L}" />).

## Checking all five conclusions against the table

Verifying **every station from T1 to T5** eliminates the four flawed statements.

| Conclusion | Check against table data | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T3 BOD fell by over half, yet T3 meets only one of two class-two standards | Drop is <InlineMath math="55\%" />, DO <InlineMath math="5.2 \ge 4" />, BOD <InlineMath math="4.5 > 3" /> | **Correct** |
| T3 now passes all class-two standards | False, BOD <InlineMath math="4.5" /> exceeds the <InlineMath math="3" /> limit | **Incorrect** |
| More stations now meet both standards | False, only T1 and T2 pass in both 2021 and 2024 | **Incorrect** |
| Rising BOD at T2 reflects the treatment plant | False, T2 lies upstream of the plant (km <InlineMath math="11" />) | **Incorrect** |
| Every point downstream of the tofu hub improved | False, T5 worsened (DO <InlineMath math="4.6 \to 4.2" />, BOD <InlineMath math="5.0 \to 6.5" />) | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The best-supported conclusion is that **BOD at T3 fell by more than half, yet that station still meets only one of the two class-two water-quality standards**.
