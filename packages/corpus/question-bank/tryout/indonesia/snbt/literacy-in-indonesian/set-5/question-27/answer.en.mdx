# Given

A high school runs the same growth-mindset intervention with **two features**: most of its students are **high-achieving** (a group whose grades barely change per paragraph two), and its school culture **values tackling hard problems** (amplifying gains for students who do benefit per paragraph three).

# Asked

The most likely outcome at this high school based on the passage.

# Solution

Combine **both features accurately**: most students (already high achievers) show almost no grade change, while the small group of lower-achieving students there stands to gain more than peers at unsupportive schools.

> A supportive peer culture enlarges gains for the few lower-achieving students without creating new gains among the high-achieving majority.

## Checking the five predicted outcomes

Verifying **both paragraph-two and paragraph-three patterns** eliminates every distorted prediction.

| Predicted outcome | Fit with both school traits | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most students' grades barely change, while the few lower achievers have a better chance of larger gains than at unsupportive schools | Combines both findings accurately | **Correct** |
| Every student rises by about <InlineMath math="0.1" /> points | False, that gain applied to lower-achieving students | **Incorrect** |
| Supportive culture lifts high achievers sharply too | False, culture amplifies gains for those who benefit | **Incorrect** |
| Nobody benefits at all | False, small averages hide subgroup variation | **Incorrect** |
| Some students' grades decline | False, no grade decline is mentioned | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The most likely outcome is that **most students' grades barely change, while the few lower-achieving students at that school stand to benefit more than similar students at schools with unsupportive environments**.
