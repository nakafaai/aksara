Notice **how far** each text grants the advantage of electric cars and **which way** each wants money to flow.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text 2: electric cars have no advantage at all | Tidak (no) | Text 2: "Keunggulan mobil listrik atas mobil bensin memang ada, tetapi kecil" (the advantage does exist, but it is small). |
| Text 1 admits a larger starting footprint | Ya (yes) | Text 1: a new electric car "sudah membawa jejak karbon lebih besar daripada mobil bensin" (already carries a larger carbon footprint than a petrol car). |
| Text 1: the advantage grows as electricity gets cleaner | Ya (yes) | Text 1 says so directly. |
| Text 2 proposes moving bus money to private cars | Tidak (no) | Text 2 proposes the opposite: public transport before private cars. |

The first statement turns "small" into "none at all". Text 2's criticism does not go that far.
