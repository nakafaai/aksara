A character's nature shows in their **actions** and **words** at the key moment. When Pak Darsa finds the station has stopped broadcasting, he does not give up. He finds a recording of a wayang performance and solders a cable so the sound comes out of the grandfather's radio. When the girl asks, "Ini siarannya, Pak?" (Is this the broadcast?), he **answers honestly** that it no longer exists. He is honest about his limits but still meets what the girl really wanted, to hear wayang from her grandfather's radio.

- He hides nothing, since he says himself that the broadcast is gone.
- He works carefully for three nights, so he is not indifferent.
- Once he knows the station has stopped, he looks for another way.
- The story shows no wish to buy the radio. He recognises the brand because of his skill.

So the attitude that shows most is honesty about what he cannot restore, while still meeting the girl's purpose.
