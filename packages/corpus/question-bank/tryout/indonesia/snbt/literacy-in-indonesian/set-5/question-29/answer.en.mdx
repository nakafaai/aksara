# Given

**The passage reports** findings from a growth-mindset programme comparing lower and higher achievers across different school climates.

# Asked

Which statements can validly be concluded from the passage's figures and wording.

# Solution

> A group average never means every single student gets the exact same gain, and the school climate helps decide how much it works.

A statement can be concluded when the passage guarantees it, either directly or through **one step of working out or two sentences combined**. A statement that's merely plausible but not guaranteed can't be concluded.

The statement about the grade gap can be concluded. The second paragraph says lower-achieving students' grades rise by about <InlineMath math="0.1" /> point, while higher-achieving students' grades "hampir tidak berubah" (hardly change). If the lower group rises and the upper group stays put, the gap between them narrows. The passage never writes that sentence, but the two figures guarantee it.

The statement about an equally small benefit can't be concluded. The third paragraph argues against it: "rata-rata menyembunyikan sebaran" (an average hides the spread). Some students are helped more, and others hardly at all. A small average can come from a mix of both.

The statement about more sessions can't be concluded. The passage only mentions an intervention with <InlineMath math="2" /> sessions. There's no comparison with a longer one, so you don't know whether extra sessions add benefit.

The statement about the school's climate can be concluded. The third paragraph says the benefit is "lebih besar di sekolah yang siswa-siswanya terbiasa menghargai keberanian mencoba soal sulit" (larger in schools whose students are used to valuing the courage to try hard problems), and in a school without that support the message is like "benih yang jatuh di tanah keras" (seed falling on hard ground).

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The grade gap between lower and higher achievers narrows | Ya |
| Every student gets the same small benefit | Tidak |
| More sessions give a larger benefit | Tidak |
| The school's climate helps decide the size of the benefit | Ya |
