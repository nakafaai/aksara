The researcher only **observed** a few orchards where fruit increased after leaf litter was left. In such observations, other things, such as weather or tree care, may change too. A controlled experiment would compare orchards where litter is left with similar orchards where it is cleared. Since that has not been done, **the increase cannot yet be attributed to the litter**.

- The researcher says farmers did leave fruit husks and leaves to rot.
- The observations were made in orchards, and nothing says midges can only be studied in a laboratory.
- The text says fruit increased, not decreased.
- The cost of fruit husks is not discussed, and the husks come from the orchard itself.

So she calls it not yet a controlled experiment because the rise in fruit cannot yet be attributed to the leaf litter.
