# Given

In paragraph five, **Pak Wiryo** jabs that young crew "trust paper more than the man standing beside them," then says "I am not offended." Afterward he waits until Darto climbs on deck to check cylinder four secretly (claiming it is so Darto "won't feel lectured"), finds the injector indeed dripping, and cleans it alone.

# Asked

The interpretation of "I am not offended" that best fits paragraph five and the following paragraph.

# Solution

Test a first-person narrator's denial against **the sarcastic remark right before it** and **the secretive inspection right after it**.

> His jab about trusting paper and his secret check once Darto leaves show that Pak Wiryo is indeed offended while refusing to admit it to protect his pride.

## Checking the five interpretations

Comparing **his words with his actions** rules out taking his denial at face value.

| Interpretation | Fit with paragraphs five and six | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He is offended yet denies it, shown by his sarcasm and secret inspection | Links the jab, waiting for Darto to leave, and finding the drip | **Correct** |
| He is genuinely not offended | Fails to explain his sarcasm and secrecy | **Incorrect** |
| He openly refuses to check the cylinder | False, he checks and cleans it secretly | **Incorrect** |
| Darto's reading proved wrong | False, the injector really was dripping | **Incorrect** |
| Offended because Darto went up on deck | False, Darto leaving was simply his cue to check alone | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The best-fitting interpretation is that **Pak Wiryo is offended yet refuses to admit it, as shown by his sarcastic remark and his secretive check of the cylinder**.
