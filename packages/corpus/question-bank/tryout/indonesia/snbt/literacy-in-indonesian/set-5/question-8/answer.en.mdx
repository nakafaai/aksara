Paragraph three argues from a predicted cause and effect: a ban **pushes teenagers** to places that are harder to monitor. That reason weakens if teenagers who lose their accounts **do not move**. A finding that most teenagers in countries with a ban did not switch to other apps attacks the prediction directly.

- How popular messaging apps are among adults says nothing about banned teenagers.
- More harmful content on small sites strengthens the author's concern.
- Teenagers moving to unmoderated apps is evidence for the author.
- How parents feel has nothing to do with whether teenagers move.

So the finding that weakens the reason most is that most teenagers who lost their accounts did not move to other apps.
