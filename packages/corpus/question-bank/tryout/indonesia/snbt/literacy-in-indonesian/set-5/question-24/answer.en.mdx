# Given

**The final paragraph** answers critics who call langgam Jawa and campursari a decline by applying their own purity test back to 1930s radio keroncong.

# Asked

Which statements align with the writer's reasoning in the final paragraph.

# Solution

> The writer turns the critics' own purity yardstick back on earlier eras to show that adapting is what kept keroncong alive.

The last paragraph holds a short rebuttal. Some fans call langgam Jawa and campursari a decline. The writer answers by **using those fans' own yardstick** and then showing where it leads.

The key sentence is: "kalau ukurannya adalah kesetiaan pada bentuk awal, keroncong sudah mundur sejak meninggalkan Tugu" (if the yardstick is loyalty to the earliest form, keroncong has been in decline ever since it left Tugu). In other words, if that yardstick is applied honestly, nearly all of keroncong's history has to be called a decline. The writer finds that result absurd, so it's the yardstick that's wrong.

The statement about radio keroncong fits. Radio keroncong in the 1930s was already far from the earliest form: it had violin, cello and bass, and its song form had been standardised. Anyone who uses loyalty to the earliest form as the yardstick has to apply it here too. That's exactly the writer's rebuttal.

The statement about extinction doesn't fit. The writer presents campursari as a continuation of the "rangkaian penyesuaian" (series of adjustments). The writer doesn't say keroncong is dying out. On the contrary, it "bertahan" (lasts).

The statement about the best yardstick doesn't fit. The writer mentions that yardstick only to show its weakness. The word "kalau" (if) marks that the yardstick is being tested, not adopted.

The statement about staying power fits. The closing sentence says "kemampuan menyesuaikan diri itulah yang membuatnya bertahan" (that ability to adapt is what makes it last).

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Campursari shows keroncong heading for extinction | Tidak |

| Keroncong's staying power is tied to its willingness to change | Ya |
