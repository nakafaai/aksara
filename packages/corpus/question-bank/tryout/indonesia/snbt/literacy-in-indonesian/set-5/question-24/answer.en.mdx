Paragraph three gives the cause and effect. The main plot **cannot be changed at will**, but the goro-goro **is not bound to the main plot**, "sehingga dalang bebas mengisinya" (so the dalang is free to fill it). That is where the dalang mocks officials, reminds people to keep the village clean, or advises the young.

- The passage says the audience already knows the main plot.
- Semar is shown as wise, but the passage does not say his advice must be heeded.
- Shorter performances are a recent development, not the reason advice can be inserted.
- The passage attaches no obligation to UNESCO's recognition.

So the dalang can insert advice because the goro-goro is not bound to the main plot and the dalang is free to fill it.
