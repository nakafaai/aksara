The text gives three yardsticks. Paragraph 2 says the width of a strait doesn't decide things, its depth does. Paragraph 3 says birds and plant seeds do cross often. Paragraph 5 says animals found on both sides were "kemungkinan dibawa manusia" (probably brought by people), and "beberapa jenis yang hidup di kedua sisi belum cukup untuk membantah garis itu" (a few species living on both sides aren't enough to refute the line). What's compared is **the make-up of the fauna as a whole, together with the history of its arrival**.

Fossils showing that most of Bali's land mammal species already lived on Lombok long before people arrived meet all three yardsticks.
