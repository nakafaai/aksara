Paragraph four sets out a cause and effect that loops back: the hotter the city, **the more air conditioners are switched on**, and they **dump heat into the street**, which makes the city hotter still. The effect becomes a cause again. That is what "Lingkaran ini memperburuk dirinya sendiri" (this cycle worsens itself) means.

- Breakdowns are not discussed.
- Air conditioners cool the inside of buildings and send the heat outdoors.
- Road building is not linked to air conditioning.
- Electricity prices and people giving up air conditioning are not mentioned.

So the phrase means that a hotter city drives more use of machines that dump heat outdoors, making it hotter still.
