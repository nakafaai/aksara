# Given

**Pak Wiryo narrates** events in the engine room, Darto's instrument reading, and how the chief engineer treats him.

# Asked

Which statements are supported by actual events and dialogue in the story.

# Solution

> A first-person narrator can make excuses for himself, so always weigh what he claims against what actually happens around him.

Pak Wiryo tells this story, and Pak Wiryo isn't always honest with himself. So for each statement, compare **what the narrator says** with **what happens**.

The statement about admitting it to Darto isn't supported. To Darto, Pak Wiryo only says "Jarumnya yang manja" (the needle is just being fussy). The cylinder gets checked "sesudah ia naik ke geladak" (after Darto has gone up on deck), so Darto never hears any admission.

The statement that Darto's reading was right is supported. After checking, Pak Wiryo reports that the injector "memang mulai menetes" (really had started to drip), and that the drip is "yang membuat gas buangnya lebih panas" (what made the exhaust hotter). The word "memang" (really, indeed) confirms the needle that was called fussy a moment ago.

The statement about the chief engineer is supported. The chief talks about Darto learning fast and about the office event, then says the spare parts are "urusan orang lain sekarang" (someone else's business now). The word "sekarang" (now) shows the matter used to be Pak Wiryo's and has been handed on.

The statement that Darto looks down on the method isn't supported. Darto does keep writing, but that afternoon asks "yang Bapak dengar itu apa?" (what is it that you hear?) and asks to try alone. Someone who looks down on a skill doesn't ask to be taught it.

The statement about the knee isn't supported. Pak Wiryo blames the slippery floor and adds "Siapa pun akan begitu" (anyone would be the same). Just before that comes the passageway that "makin tahun terasa makin sempit" (feels narrower every year). A steel passageway doesn't shrink. The body is ageing, and the slippery floor covers for it.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Pak Wiryo admits the reading to Darto | Tidak |
| The check proves Darto's reading right | Ya |
| The chief engineer treats Pak Wiryo as someone about to leave | Ya |
| Darto looks down on working by ear | Tidak |
| The reason given for the knee can be taken at face value | Tidak |
