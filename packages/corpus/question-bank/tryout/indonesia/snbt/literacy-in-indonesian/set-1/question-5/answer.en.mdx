# Given

**The author interprets** the temple surface-loss table and states the study's limits in the last two paragraphs.

# Asked

Whether each statement matches the author's view in the passage.

# Solution

> An author's view includes both the conclusions drawn from the numbers and the limits the author warns about.

The author's view shows in how the table is read and in the limits named in the last two paragraphs.

The statement that the data show moss doesn't widen cracks doesn't fit. Deep cracks were found under thick moss on two group III panels, and the team **"tidak dapat memastikan apakah lumut melebarkan retakan atau hanya tumbuh di retakan yang sudah ada"** (couldn't be sure whether the moss widened the cracks or only grew in cracks that were already there). The data don't settle it either way.

The statement about the gap between groups I and II fits. The moss died in both groups. The difference is that group I was also scrubbed. Group I lost <InlineMath math="0.8" /> mm and group II <InlineMath math="0.5" /> mm, so scrubbing alone goes with about <InlineMath math="0.3" /> mm more loss. That matches the closing sentence about stone lost through the way moss is removed.

The statement that moss should be left on the reliefs doesn't fit. The team **"tidak menyarankan agar lumut dibiarkan tumbuh di seluruh candi"** (doesn't recommend letting moss grow over the whole temple). Carved reliefs are still monitored, because a crack of any width can wipe out a carving.

The statement about the order of surface loss fits. The more wet-dry cycles per year, the more surface was lost.

| Group | Cycles per year | Loss (mm) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| I | <InlineMath math="110" /> | <InlineMath math="0.8" /> |
| II | <InlineMath math="85" /> | <InlineMath math="0.5" /> |
| III | <InlineMath math="60" /> | <InlineMath math="0.3" /> |

The statement that the results apply directly to limestone temples in dry climates doesn't fit. The team says the trial was **"hanya dilakukan di satu candi dengan satu iklim"** (done at only one temple in one climate), an andesite temple in Central Java.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The data show moss doesn't widen cracks | Tidak sesuai |
| The gap between groups I and II shows stone lost through how moss is removed | Sesuai |
| Moss should be left on carved reliefs | Tidak sesuai |
| The order of surface loss fits the wet-dry cycle idea | Sesuai |
| The results apply directly to limestone temples in dry climates | Tidak sesuai |
