# Given

**SMA Harapan Bangsa** rose from <InlineMath math="68" /> to <InlineMath math="74" /> with both a phone ban and an exam-format change, while SMA Pelita rose from <InlineMath math="70" /> to <InlineMath math="75" /> with only the format change.

# Asked

What share of SMA Harapan Bangsa's score rise most likely comes from the phone ban under Text B's reasoning.

# Solution

> Think of a comparison school as a clean baseline that removes the shared change so only the extra policy's own slice remains.

Text B's reasoning uses SMA Pelita as a comparison. Pelita had the same change of exam format but didn't ban phones. So Pelita's rise shows the effect of the exam format alone.

**Step 1. The rise at each school**.

| School | 2023 | 2024 | Rise |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SMA Harapan Bangsa | <InlineMath math="68" /> | <InlineMath math="74" /> | <InlineMath math="6" /> |
| SMA Pelita | <InlineMath math="70" /> | <InlineMath math="75" /> | <InlineMath math="5" /> |

**Step 2. Take out the exam format's effect**. If the format raised scores equally at both schools, <InlineMath math="5" /> points of Harapan Bangsa's rise come from the format. The rest, <InlineMath math="6-5=1" /> point, can be linked to the phone ban.

**Step 3. Express it as a share of Harapan Bangsa's rise**. <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{6}" />, or about a sixth. This matches Text B's point that **"sebagian besar kenaikan"** (most of the rise) may have nothing to do with phones.

The other options are wrong.

- <InlineMath math="6" /> points rejects the comparison. It's precisely because Pelita didn't ban phones that it shows the format's effect alone.
- <InlineMath math="5" /> points is the part that comes from the format, not from the ban.
- The <InlineMath math="1" />-point difference is right, but a fifth means <InlineMath math="1" /> divided by Pelita's rise, <InlineMath math="5" />. The question asks for a share of Harapan Bangsa's rise, <InlineMath math="6" /> points.
- <InlineMath math="2" /> points compares the two schools' starting scores, <InlineMath math="70-68" />, not their rises.

# Answer

So by Text B's reasoning, about **<InlineMath math="1" />** point, or roughly a sixth of SMA Harapan Bangsa's rise, most likely comes from the phone ban.
