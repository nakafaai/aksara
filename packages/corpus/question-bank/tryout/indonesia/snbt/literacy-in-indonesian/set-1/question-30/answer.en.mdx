A message must cover **the whole poem**, not a single line. Stanzas one and two show work that depends on the sun. Stanza three shows a harvest paid "lebih murah dari keringat" (cheaper than sweat). Stanza four shows nature wiping out the work, together with the father's call to learn to wait. The message that ties these together is that work dependent on nature demands **patience**, even when the reward may fall short.

- Changing jobs contradicts the father's call to learn patience from the land.
- In this poem the rain does harm because it comes too early.
- The children do help sweep the salt, but the poem does not present that as a duty or a lesson.
- Government oversight is never mentioned. That option adds an idea from outside the poem.

So the poem's message is that work dependent on nature demands patience, even when the reward may not match the effort.
