The line compares two things of different kinds: **money and sweat**. Sweat stands for the hard work described in the earlier stanzas, from leading seawater into the pans and waiting day after day to sweeping the salt into baskets. By saying the salt is paid "lebih murah dari keringat" (cheaper than sweat), the poet implies that **the price does not match** the labour spent.

- "Dengan cepat" (quickly) shows the buyers do not value the salt, but the poem never mentions cheating on the scales.
- The poem says nothing about the quality of the salt.
- Nothing suggests the father lowered the price himself. The word "orang" (people) points to the buyers as the ones who pay little.
- Reading sweat literally as something that makes salt saltier ignores the comparison with payment.

So the line implies that the price of the salt does not match the family's hard work.
