# Given

Average exam scores rose from <InlineMath math="68" /> to <InlineMath math="74" /> at **SMA Harapan Bangsa** and from <InlineMath math="70" /> to <InlineMath math="75" /> at **SMA Pelita**. Assume SMA Pelita's entire gain came from the exam format change and lifted both schools by the same amount.

# Asked

The share of Text A's claimed gain that remains left over to attribute to the phone ban.

# Solution

Compute **each school's score gain**, subtract the exam-format effect from SMA Harapan Bangsa's gain, and divide the remainder by Text A's full claimed gain.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{SMA Harapan Bangsa gain} &= 74 - 68 = 6\text{ points} \\
\text{SMA Pelita gain} &= 75 - 70 = 5\text{ points}
\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="6 - 5 = 1\text{ point}" />
<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{6} \approx 16.7\%" />
</MathContainer>

> Divide by Text A's full claimed <InlineMath math="6" />-point rise, not by SMA Pelita's <InlineMath math="5" />-point rise or the final score of <InlineMath math="74" />.

## Tracing the calculation of each option

Comparing **numerator and denominator** across options isolates the exact remaining share.

| Option | Ratio | What it computes | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| About <InlineMath math="16.7\%" /> | <InlineMath math="1 : 6" /> | Remaining phone-ban point divided by Text A's claimed gain | **Correct** |
| About <InlineMath math="8.1\%" /> | <InlineMath math="6 : 74" /> | Total gain divided by final 2024 score | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="20\%" /> | <InlineMath math="1 : 5" /> | Divides by SMA Pelita's gain instead of Text A's claim | **Incorrect** |
| About <InlineMath math="83.3\%" /> | <InlineMath math="5 : 6" /> | Exam-format share instead of phone-ban share | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="100\%" /> | <InlineMath math="6 : 6" /> | Text A's unadjusted claim ignoring SMA Pelita | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Only **about <InlineMath math="16.7\%" />** of Text A's claimed gain remains attributable to the phone ban.
