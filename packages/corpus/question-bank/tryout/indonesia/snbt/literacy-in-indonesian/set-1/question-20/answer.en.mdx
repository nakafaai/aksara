For a statement about **both texts**, the evidence must appear in each text.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both texts acknowledge traders who benefit | Yes | Text 1 says street vendors have many customers. Text 2 opens by admitting that traders with stalls feel the benefit. |
| Text 1 measures air on nearby roads | No | The monitor in Text 1 stands in the middle of Jalan Merdeka. Text 2 even stresses that it is the only one. |
| Text 2 rejects the whole idea | No | Text 2 writes "Kami tidak meminta program ini dihentikan" (we are not asking for the programme to stop). |
| Text 2 proposes loading times | Yes | Text 2 proposes earlier loading, for example at five in the morning. |

The third statement is often marked Yes because Text 2 raises objections. Objecting to **how and when the programme expands** is not the same as rejecting the whole programme.
