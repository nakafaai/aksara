The last stanza shows a loss: the rain comes too early and the salt pans **turn back into sea** before the harvest. The father's reaction is to sit and look, then say "tanah ini tahu caranya menunggu, / kita pun harus belajar dari tanah" (this land knows how to wait, / we too must learn from the land). He neither complains nor blames anything. He asks his family to **learn to wait** like the land. That is patient acceptance and a choice to wait.

- Asking the family to learn patience contradicts any plan to abandon the salt field.
- No word or action shows anger. The father "hanya duduk memandang" (only sits and looks).
- Sitting and looking at the ruined pans shows that he feels the loss, not that he is indifferent.
- Rain that comes too early ruins the salt harvest, so there is no reason for joy.

So the father is shown accepting the situation patiently and choosing to wait.
