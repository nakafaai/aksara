# Given

**The story follows** lighthouse keeper Pak Darsa, Wulan, his rightmost logbook column, the new lamp sensor, and the brass key.

# Asked

Which statements can genuinely be concluded from the story's events.

# Solution

> Wrong options often borrow a real story detail and stretch it too far, so stop exactly where the written evidence stops.

Some statements use details that are in the story but stretch them further than what's written.

The statement that the keeper had to climb up more than once a night can be concluded. The crank of the lens clock had to be turned **"setiap empat jam"** (every four hours), and the lighthouse **"tidak boleh tidur"** (mustn't sleep). A night is longer than four hours, so the keeper had to climb more than once.

The statement that Wulan doubts the sensor can't be concluded. Asked about fog, Wulan answers with confidence that **"sensornya membaca kabut sebagai gelap"** (the sensor reads fog as darkness). Her polite smile doesn't show doubt.

The statement about the rightmost column can be concluded. That column was **"tidak pernah diminta oleh kantor di kota"** (never asked for by the office in town), and it holds sky observations that, as Pak Darsa says, have worked that way **"sudah tiga puluh tahun"** (for thirty years).

The statement that Pak Darsa became keeper after his father died can't be concluded. His father handed over the key when he was **"sudah batuk-batuk dan tidak sanggup lagi memutar engkol"** (coughing and no longer able to turn the crank). The story says the father was ill, not that he died.

The statement that the key was no longer of any use can't be concluded. Pak Darsa hands it over **"untuk pintu"** (for the door). It's the lamp that no longer needs a key.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The keeper had to climb up more than once a night | Ya |
| Wulan doubts the sensor can read fog | Tidak |
| The rightmost column comes from Pak Darsa's own observation | Ya |
| Pak Darsa became keeper after his father died | Tidak |
| The brass key was no longer of any use in the tower | Tidak |
