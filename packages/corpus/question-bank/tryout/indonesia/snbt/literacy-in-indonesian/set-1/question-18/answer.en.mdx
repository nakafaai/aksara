Compare the **final proposals** of the two texts, because that is where each writer's position is clearest.

Text 1 ends by proposing that the city add Jalan Pahlawan and hold the car-free day twice a week. Text 2 writes "Kami tidak meminta program ini dihentikan" (we are not asking for the programme to stop), then asks for air-quality measurements on the diversion roads and for shop owners to be heard **before** any expansion. The difference is expanding now versus measuring the effects first.

- Text 2 explicitly does not ask for the programme to stop.
- Text 2 discusses shop takings and pollution on diversion roads. Children's education is not discussed.
- Text 1 actually says street vendors have many customers, and Text 2 speaks for shop owners on nearby streets, not street vendors.
- Jalan Pahlawan is an additional road proposed by Text 1, not a replacement, and Text 2 does not ask for it.

So Text 1 proposes expanding the programme, while Text 2 asks for its effects on the diversion roads to be measured first.
