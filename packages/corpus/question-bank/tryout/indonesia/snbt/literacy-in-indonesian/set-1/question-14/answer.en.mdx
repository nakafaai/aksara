The key word is **sekaligus** (both at once). A statement goes in the Ya (Yes) column only if both writers agree with it. A statement held by just one writer goes in Tidak (No).

The statement that phones during lessons hurt concentration has both writers' support. A writes that "setiap notifikasi menarik perhatian dari pelajaran" (every notification pulls attention from the lesson). B writes, **"Saya setuju bahwa ponsel yang bergetar di saku merusak konsentrasi"** (I agree that a phone buzzing in a pocket ruins concentration).

The statement that an all-day ban is best is held only by A. B calls a full all-day ban "obat yang diresepkan sebelum penyakitnya diperiksa" (medicine prescribed before the illness is examined) and prefers lockers during lessons.

The statement that the rise in scores proves restrictions work is held only by A. B argues that the rise may come from the changed exam format. Careful: B does support restrictions, but not because of that figure.

The statement about exceptions is held only by B. A says a full ban "justru adil karena berlaku sama bagi semua" (is fair precisely because it applies to everyone equally), so A doesn't want exceptions.
