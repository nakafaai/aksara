The phrase *cara pandang ini* points to an **idea already explained**. Identify its referent through the connection between ideas, rather than simply choosing the nearest sentence.

| Part of the passage | Role in the reasoning |
| --- | --- |
| Sentence (10) | Main idea: war is a last resort because its high cost gives both sides a reason to avoid it |
| Sentences (11) and (12) | Development of the consequences of that idea |
| Sentence (13) | Reference back to the idea as *cara pandang ini* |

The primary referent is therefore **sentence (10)**. Sentences (11) and (12) remain relevant, but they develop the perspective already introduced in sentence (10).
