# Given

Two official documents follow the official spelling of their era except that **personal names** may keep older spellings. **Document P** reads *Soekarno membatja koran pagi.* while **Document Q** reads *Soekarno membaca koran pagi.*

# Asked

The most accurate conclusion about when Document P and Document Q were written.

# Solution

Set aside *Soekarno* because personal names may retain older spelling, and compare the **ordinary words** *membatja* versus *membaca*.

> The letter *c* places Document Q in 1972 or later, whereas *tj* only places Document P before 1972 without distinguishing before or after 1947.

## Dating both documents from ordinary words

Checking **which spelling rules distinguish each era** pins down the exact valid date ranges.

| Document | Key word | Official spelling clue | Certain time range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Document P | *membatja* (*tj*) | Used in 1901 and kept in 1947, with no /u/ or glottal stop appears to separate 1901 from 1947 | Before 1972 (either before or after 1947) |
| Document Q | *membaca* (*c*) | *tj* became *c* under EYD in 1972, while *oe* in *Soekarno* is allowed for personal names | In 1972 or later |

# Answer

The best conclusion is that **Document P was written before 1972 (either before or after 1947), whereas Document Q was written in 1972 or later**.
