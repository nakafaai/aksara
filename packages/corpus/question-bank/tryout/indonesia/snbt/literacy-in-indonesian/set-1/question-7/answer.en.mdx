# Given

In the lighthouse story, **Pak Darsa's attitude** toward Wulan and the new solar lamp shows through concrete actions: walking ahead in silence, polishing the lens, logging sky signs, and letting Wulan record his explanations.

# Asked

All statements about Pak Darsa's attitude toward Wulan and the new lamp that are supported by the story.

# Solution

Test each statement individually against **specific actions and dialogue** in the text rather than guessing motives outside the passage.

> Pak Darsa shifts from quiet distance in the morning to sharing sea knowledge that a light sensor cannot read.

## Checking textual support for each statement

Matching **each claim against story evidence** separates supported statements from unsupported ones.

| Statement | Evidence in the story | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passes on knowledge the sensor cannot read | Explains copper-yellow western skies, west winds, and low gulls, while the sensor only detects dark and light | **Supported** |
| Lets Wulan take notes despite initial distance | Walks ahead quietly in the morning, then does not stop her typing at night and explains even more | **Supported** |
| Asks to delay the installation | Only asks about fog while letting Wulan keep installing | **Unsupported** |
| Refuses to help out of anger | Shows no anger and calmly shares his logbook knowledge | **Unsupported** |
| Says the key can no longer open anything | Hands her the key "for the door", only the lamp needs no key | **Unsupported** |

# Answer

The supported statements are that **Pak Darsa passes on knowledge the light sensor cannot read** and that **he lets Wulan record his knowledge even though he kept his distance at first**.
