The passage does not infer the source from a product label, population size, or distance alone. Sentences (4) to (8) describe a transport model and its result.

For many Seychelles sites, particularly for debris that remains afloat longer, the model estimates Indonesia as the largest single terrestrial source country. That is the precise reason for the wording in the passage.

The answer therefore rests on a **model estimate**, especially for long-floating terrestrial debris.
