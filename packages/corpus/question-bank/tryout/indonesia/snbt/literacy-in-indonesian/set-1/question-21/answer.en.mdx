There are two steps: first work out **which spelling was in force**, then test every letter.

**Step 1. The spelling in force**. EYD was made official in 1972, so the school opened in 1971. The official spelling then was the **Ejaan Republik**, in force from 1947 until EYD replaced it.

**Step 2. Features of the Ejaan Republik**. From the text, the sign needs three features.

- The sound /u/ is written *u*, because *oe* was replaced in 1947. So *Umum*, not *Oemoem*.
- The sound /y/ is still written *j*, because *j* was only replaced by *y* in 1972. So *Rakjat*.
- The sounds /j/ and /c/ are still written *dj* and *tj*, because they were also only replaced in 1972. So *Djalan Tjempaka*.

The founder's name is the exception the question states. It's written the way the founder writes it, **Soedjono**, even though *oe* is no longer used for other words.

The other options fail.

- *Oemoem* uses the *oe* of the 1901 spelling, but *oe* was replaced in 1947.
- *Rakyat* uses *y*, but EYD only replaced *j* with *y* in 1972, a year after the school opened.
- *Sudjono* changes the founder's name, but the question says it's written the way the founder writes it.
- *Jalan Cempaka* uses EYD letters, but *dj* and *tj* were only replaced in 1972.

So the right sign is *Sekolah Rakjat Umum Soedjono, Djalan Tjempaka*.
