# Given

The school opened **one year before EYD (1972)**, namely in 1971 while the **Republican Spelling (1947)** was still in force. The passage states that the 1947 system already replaced *oe* with *u* while keeping *j* for /y/, *dj* for /j/, and *tj* for /c/, and the founder's own name remains *Soedjono*.

# Asked

The correct school signboard under 1971 official spelling rules plus the founder-name exception.

# Solution

Apply the **1947 Republican Spelling rules** to every ordinary word on the signboard while preserving the founder's personal spelling for his name.

> In 1971 ordinary words use *u* instead of *oe*, still keep *j*, *dj*, and *tj*, and leave the personal name *Soedjono* untouched.

## Checking each part of the signboard

Testing **all four signboard elements** against the 1947 rules eliminates every distractor.

| Signboard element | Correct 1971 form | Rule from passage | Distractor error |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Word *Umum* | *Umum* | *oe* became *u* in 1947 | *Oemoem* uses 1901 spelling |
| Word *Rakjat* | *Rakjat* | *j* stayed until EYD in 1972 | *Rakyat* jumps ahead to 1972 |
| Founder's name | *Soedjono* | Personal name exception stated in prompt | *Sudjono* alters the founder's spelling |
| Street name | *Djalan Tjempaka* | *dj* and *tj* stayed until 1972 | *Jalan Cempaka* uses post-1972 EYD |

# Answer

The correct signboard is ***Sekolah Rakjat Umum Soedjono, Djalan Tjempaka***.
