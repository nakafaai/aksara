# Given

Across the four trial schools with <InlineMath math="100" /> school days per semester, **SD Kalirejo** (<InlineMath math="400" /> students) rose by <InlineMath math="8" /> percentage points, **SD Tegalsari** (<InlineMath math="100" /> students) by <InlineMath math="2" /> points, **SD Sumberan** (<InlineMath math="250" /> students) by <InlineMath math="8" /> points, and **SD Watugede** (<InlineMath math="250" /> students) by <InlineMath math="2" /> points.

# Asked

The total reduction in student absence days across all four trial schools from odd semester 2023 to odd semester 2024.

# Solution

Because **school enrollments differ**, compute the absence-day drop for each school using <InlineMath math="\text{students} \times 100 \times \Delta\text{attendance}" /> and add all four totals.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Kalirejo} &= 400 \times 100 \times 8\% = 3{,}200 \\
\text{Tegalsari} &= 100 \times 100 \times 2\% = 200 \\
\text{Sumberan} &= 250 \times 100 \times 8\% = 2{,}000 \\
\text{Watugede} &= 250 \times 100 \times 2\% = 500 \\
\text{Total} &= 3{,}200 + 200 + 2{,}000 + 500 = 5{,}900\text{ days}
\end{aligned}" />

> Each school's attendance gain must be weighted by its student count rather than averaged equally across schools.
