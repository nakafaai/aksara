# Given

**The text and** table record the three temple stone panel groups, wet-dry cycles, and surface loss measurements.

# Asked

Whether each statement can be concluded directly from the temple trial text and table.

# Solution

> A statement counts as supported only when the text and table lock it in directly, not merely when it sounds plausible.

Judge every statement by one question: are the text and the table enough to conclude it? A statement that goes beyond the data or names a cause that can't be confirmed goes in the Tidak (No) column.

The statement about cycles and surface loss can be concluded. Order the three groups: their cycles are <InlineMath math="110" />, <InlineMath math="85" /> and <InlineMath math="60" /> per year, and their surface losses are <InlineMath math="0.8" />, <InlineMath math="0.5" /> and <InlineMath math="0.3" /> mm. Both lists fall in the same order. The statement only talks about the groups in that trial, so it doesn't go beyond the table.

The statement that moss caused the cracks can't be concluded. The text says the team **"tidak dapat memastikan apakah lumut melebarkan retakan atau hanya tumbuh di retakan yang sudah ada"** (couldn't tell whether the moss widened the cracks or just grew in cracks that were already there). The cause is left open on purpose.

The statement about dead moss can be concluded. Group II, whose moss died and fell off on its own, lost <InlineMath math="0.5" /> mm, less than the scrubbed group I at <InlineMath math="0.8" /> mm. Both groups were sprayed, so the difference really is the scrubbing.

The statement about any climate can't be concluded. The text says the trial was **"hanya dilakukan di satu candi dengan satu iklim"** (done at only one temple, in one climate).

The statement about the drawing of lots can be concluded. The panels were split into the three groups by lot **on each side of the wall**, so every group has panels from every side. Chance can still play a small part, but the lottery on each side keeps any difference in sun from being systematic, so sun exposure can hardly explain the gaps in the results.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| More cycles, more surface loss | Ya |
| Moss caused the deep cracks | Tidak |
| Dead moss falling off on its own does less damage than scrubbing | Ya |
| The results hold in any climate | Tidak |
| Drawing lots per side rules out sun exposure as the explanation | Ya |
