Look at how paragraph four is built. The author states as fact that the Balinese, Javanese, and Old Sundanese scripts developed from Kawi. For the Batak, Rejang, Lampung, and Lontara scripts, the author uses the word **menghubungkan** (link, associate) and adds that the line of descent is still debated. That shift in wording shows **caution**: the author separates established relationships from uncertain ones.

- The author actually gives evidence of spread beyond Java, namely the inscription from the Philippines.
- The author does not reject the experts. The text reports their view together with the debate.
- The words **seluruh** (all) and **langsung** (directly) contradict the phrase saying the line of descent is debated.
- The author still mentions the scripts of Sumatra and Sulawesi and simply avoids overstating how certain their origin is.

So the phrase shows an author who carefully separates established relationships from those that cannot yet be confirmed.
