# Given

The four-year **andesite temple stone trial** recorded average surface loss of <InlineMath math="0.8\text{ mm}" /> in group I (scrubbed and sprayed), <InlineMath math="0.5\text{ mm}" /> in group II (sprayed only), and <InlineMath math="0.3\text{ mm}" /> in group III (untouched).

# Asked

The percentage of group I's excess surface loss over group III that comes from scrubbing.

# Solution

Find group I's **excess surface loss** above group III first, because group III also loses stone without any treatment.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="0.8 - 0.3 = 0.5\text{ mm}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{scrubbing share} &= 0.8 - 0.5 = 0.3\text{ mm} \\
\text{lost-moss share} &= 0.5 - 0.3 = 0.2\text{ mm}
\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\frac{0.3}{0.5} = 60\%" />
</MathContainer>

> The divisor must be the <InlineMath math="0.5\text{ mm}" /> excess loss, not group I's entire <InlineMath math="0.8\text{ mm}" /> surface loss.

## Comparing the ratio of each option

Checking the **numerator and divisor** behind each choice separates the true scrubbing share from the distractors.

| Option | Ratio | What it measures | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="60\%" /> | <InlineMath math="0.3 : 0.5" /> | Scrubbing part divided by excess loss | **Correct** |
| <InlineMath math="37.5\%" /> | <InlineMath math="0.3 : 0.8" /> | Divides by all of group I's surface loss | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="40\%" /> | <InlineMath math="0.2 : 0.5" /> | Lost-moss share instead of scrubbing share | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="62.5\%" /> | <InlineMath math="0.5 : 0.8" /> | Excess loss divided by total group I loss | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="75\%" /> | <InlineMath math="0.3 : 0.4" /> | Uses <InlineMath math="0.4" />, absent from the data | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Exactly **<InlineMath math="60\%" />** of group I's excess surface loss comes from scrubbing.
