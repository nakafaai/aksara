# Given

In the lighthouse story, **Pak Darsa** skips climbing up to wind the lens clockwork for the first time in thirty-one years, even though he still climbed up that afternoon to polish the lens and the clockwork will not be removed until the next day.

# Asked

The pair of details that together best show he stayed downstairs by choice rather than being forced by the crank or by his own body.

# Solution

To show a **free personal choice**, the pair of details must rule out both kinds of compulsion at once: a crank that no longer works and a body too weak to climb the stairs.

> Knowing the clockwork will be removed tomorrow rules out mechanical necessity, while climbing up to polish the lens that afternoon rules out physical inability.

## Checking each pair of details

Each **pair of textual clues** is tested against both mechanical and physical compulsion.

| Pair of details | Rules out crank failure | Rules out physical inability | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clockwork removed tomorrow and climbing up that afternoon to polish the lens | Yes, the crank still works tonight | Yes, he still climbs all <InlineMath math="112" /> steps | **Correct** |
| Wulan typing on her phone and sitting downstairs | Says nothing about the crank | Does not test his stamina | **Incorrect** |
| Why his father stopped winding the crank | Concerns his father years ago | Does not show Pak Darsa's strength | **Incorrect** |
| Wulan's work schedule and sitting downstairs | Says nothing about the crank tonight | Only restates the action itself | **Incorrect** |
| Handing over the brass key the next morning | Does not prove the crank worked tonight | Does not prove physical ability | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The strongest pair of details is **that the lens clockwork is only being removed the next day and that Pak Darsa still climbed to the lantern room that afternoon to polish the lens**.
