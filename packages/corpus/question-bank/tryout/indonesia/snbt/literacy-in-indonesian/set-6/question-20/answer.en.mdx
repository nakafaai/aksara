Text 1 promises efficient harvests and lower cost per tonne from **large areas of land**. That promise only holds if the land opened **is actually suitable for rice**. Text 2 attacks exactly that point: opening forest or peatland for new rice fields "berisiko gagal jika tanahnya tidak cocok" (risks failure if the soil is unsuitable). The assumption Text 2 questions is that new land suits rice.

- Text 1 does not discuss smallholders, and help for them is Text 2's proposal, not Text 1's assumption.
- Text 1 says world rice prices can soar.
- Neither text compares the nutrition of local foods.
- If imports were always cheaper, Text 1's case for growing at home would weaken. That is not its assumption.

So the assumption is that land opened for food estates is suitable for growing rice.
