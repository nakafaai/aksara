A shared view must appear in **both texts**. Text 1 fears the country being **shaken** when world rice prices soar. Text 2 ends by warning that depending on one staple makes us **vulnerable**. Both want the country's food supply to be strong and hard to shake, though by different routes: Text 1 through large food estates, Text 2 through existing rice fields and local food.

- Text 2 warns against opening peatland.
- Text 1 rejects dependence on imports but does not demand an end to all imports.
- Text 2 wants local food developed, not to replace rice entirely.
- Neither text judges whether machines suit smallholders.

So both texts hold that the country must strengthen its food supply so that it is not easily shaken.
