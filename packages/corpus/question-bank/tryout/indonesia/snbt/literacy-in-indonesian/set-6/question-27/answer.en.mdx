**Personifikasi** (personification) gives human traits or actions to something that is not human. A market is a place, yet in this line it **opens its eyes** like someone waking up. With this figure, the poet shows the market coming to life before dawn.

- The line does not exaggerate any number or size.
- The line does not use "seperti" (like). A simile appears in another line, "seperti buah yang belum matang" (like unripe fruit).
- The market really is busy, so there is no gap between what is said and what is meant.
- "Pasar" does not stand for its owner's name.

So the line uses personification.
