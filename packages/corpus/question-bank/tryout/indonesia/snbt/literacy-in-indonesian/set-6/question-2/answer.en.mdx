# Given

District records show severe drought occurred in **<InlineMath math="6" /> of <InlineMath math="10" /> El Nino years** (<InlineMath math="0.6" />) and in **<InlineMath math="2" /> of <InlineMath math="20" /> non-El-Nino years** (<InlineMath math="0.1" />). Next year's El Nino probability is **<InlineMath math="70\%" />** (<InlineMath math="0.7" />), leaving <InlineMath math="0.3" /> for no El Nino.

# Asked

The probability that the district experiences severe drought next year.

# Solution

Add the probabilities of **both paths to severe drought** (with El Nino and without El Nino) via the law of total probability.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P(\text{severe}) &= 0.7 \times \frac{6}{10} + 0.3 \times \frac{2}{20} \\
&= 0.7 \times 0.6 + 0.3 \times 0.1 \\
&= 0.42 + 0.03 = 0.45 = 45\%
\end{aligned}" />

> Combining the El Nino branch (<InlineMath math="42\%" />) and the non-El-Nino branch (<InlineMath math="3\%" />) yields an overall probability of <InlineMath math="45\%" />.

# Answer

The probability that the district experiences a severe drought next year is **<InlineMath math="45\%" />**.
