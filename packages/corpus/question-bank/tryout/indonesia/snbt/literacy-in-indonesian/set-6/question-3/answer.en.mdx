Paragraph three names three conditions for the greatest danger: **loose sand**, **saturated with water**, and **close to the surface**, with former swamps and reclaimed land as examples. Reclaimed land of loose sand on a former swamp with shallow groundwater meets all three.

- Hard rock is not loose sand, and the soil layer is thin.
- Compact clay is described as far more resistant.
- Dense sand is safer, and lying far above the groundwater it is not saturated.
- Dry sand is not saturated, yet liquefaction needs water-filled gaps.

So the most vulnerable land is reclaimed loose sand on a former swamp with shallow groundwater.
