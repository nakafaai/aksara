The last stanza shows **the sun arriving late**: when it comes, all that is left is torn banana leaves, prices already lowered, and people going home. In other words, **the market's work is done before the day begins**. The phrase "membawa lelah sebagai upah pertama" (carrying tiredness as their first wage) stresses that what they earn first is **the fatigue of hard work**.

- The poem does not invite people to come after sunrise. By then only leftovers remain.
- Prices have "sudah turun" (already fallen) when the sun arrives.
- The sun finds only leftovers. It brings no profit.
- People go home because their work is finished, not out of laziness.

So the last stanza is best read as a picture of hard work finished before most people start their day.
