Stanzas two and three are full of **activity**: a mother arranges spinach, the scale creaks, fish arrive from the sea, a parking attendant yawns, and a child sleeps on a sack. The activity is drawn with **warm, intimate details**, such as spinach arranged "seperti menyisir rambut anak yang hendak sekolah" (like combing the hair of a child about to go to school). The mood is busy but not harsh.

- There is no quarrelling. Bargaining appears only through the scale's sound.
- The market is full of activity, not quiet.
- Small lamps like unripe fruit do not suggest the glitter of a night fair.
- Nothing in these stanzas suggests anger or exploitation.

So the most prominent mood is busy yet intimate, drawn with warm details of work.
