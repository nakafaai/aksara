The line must be read with **the action that goes with it**. As he speaks, Ilham's father is **replacing the old patches with new cloth**, the same patches that "berderik seperti pintu tua" (creaked like an old door) in strong wind. Mentioning "next year" while strengthening the sail shows that he **imagines Ilham racing again** and is preparing the boat to withstand stronger wind.

- Preparing a new sail contradicts forbidding another race.
- Reading the line only as a weather forecast ignores the sail he is sewing.
- He never scolds Ilham, so there is no mocking tone.
- Repairing the sail contradicts any plan to sell the boat.

So the line most likely means that his father supports Ilham racing again and has prepared a stronger sail.
