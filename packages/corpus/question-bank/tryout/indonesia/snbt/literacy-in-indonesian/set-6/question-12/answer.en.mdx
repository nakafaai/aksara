None of these assumptions is quoted from the texts. Each one is **the base under one step of reasoning**. For each, find the step that collapses if the assumption is taken away.

The assumption about control and obligations belongs to Text A. Its closing sentence says a company "yang mengendalikan cara orang bekerja sudah semestinya menanggung kewajiban seorang pemberi kerja" (that controls how people work ought to carry an employer's obligations). The whole first paragraph of Text A gathers evidence of that control: fares, commission, ratings and cutting off accounts.

The assumption that a protection can harm belongs to Text B. Its writer fears that a minimum wage would make the company split schedules and cap numbers, so part-time drivers "kemungkinan besar tidak mendapat tempat" (most likely won't get a place). A protection meant for drivers could push some drivers out.

The assumption about rights without a change of status belongs to Text B. Its last paragraph proposes advance notice of commission changes, an appeal route and accident cover, all "tanpa mengubah status kami" (without changing our status).

The assumption about restricted freedom belongs to Text A. Its writer grants that drivers "memang bebas memilih jam kerja" (are indeed free to choose their hours), then says that freedom "menyempit ketika penghasilan bergantung pada bonus" (narrows when income depends on bonuses). Without this assumption, free hours would be evidence that drivers really are partners.
