The line sets **the scale** beside **the people bargaining**. The scale "bargains" with its creak, and the figure it shows is the weight **as it is**. People who bargain, by contrast, often **play tactics**, such as pretending not to be interested or naming a price that is too low. That is why the scale's voice is called "lebih jujur daripada kami" (more honest than us).

- The scale is old and creaky but still in use.
- The line does not say traders refuse offers.
- "More honest" is about honesty, not loudness.
- No buyer bargains with the scale. The phrase is figurative.

So the line means the scale shows the weight as it is, while people often use tactics when they bargain.
