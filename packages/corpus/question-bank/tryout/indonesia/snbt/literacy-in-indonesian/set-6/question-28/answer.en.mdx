# Given

**The passage compares** the original marshmallow test with the 2018 replication that accounted for children's family and economic backgrounds.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded accurately without turning measured wording into absolutes.

# Solution

> Saying an effect shrinks by half never means it drops to zero, always match the exact strength of the passage's claim.

This passage is full of words that measure strength, like _rata-rata_ (on average), _sekitar separuh_ (about half), _hampir tidak berarti_ (hardly mattered) and _sebagian_ (partly). Many statements here swap a word like that for an absolute one. So check **how strong** each statement is compared with the passage.

The statement about the 2018 study can't be concluded. The third paragraph says the link was "masih ditemukan" (still found), at about half the original strength. Even after family background was counted, the link "hampir tidak berarti" (hardly mattered), which isn't the same as vanishing.

The statement about the children in the original test can be concluded. They were "sebagian besar adalah anak dosen, karyawan, dan mahasiswa Stanford" (mostly children of Stanford lecturers, staff and students). The 2018 study deliberately used children "dari latar ekonomi dan pendidikan orang tua yang jauh lebih beragam" (from far more varied economic and parental education backgrounds), so the original group really wasn't varied.

The statement about part of the link's strength can be concluded. The link shrank when family background was counted. So part of the link seen in the original finding **was carried by family background**, and the original finding didn't account for it.

The statement about children who ate straight away can't be concluded. The second paragraph only says children who waited longer scored higher "rata-rata" (on average). A group average doesn't settle each child's fate.

The statement about the writer's position can't be concluded. The fifth paragraph says "Semua ini tidak berarti bahwa pengendalian diri tidak penting." (None of this means self-control is unimportant.)

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The 2018 study found no link at all | Tidak |
| The original children didn't represent varied backgrounds | Ya |
| Part of the link came from family background | Ya |
| Children who ate straight away certainly scored low | Tidak |
| The writer thinks self-control has no effect | Tidak |
