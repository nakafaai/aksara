# Given

At the end of paragraph four of the El Nino passage, the author notes **"several severe droughts that occurred without El Nino."** Paragraph five immediately opens with "Part of those severe droughts without El Nino can be explained by the neighbor to the west," introducing the **Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD)**.

# Asked

The structural role of mentioning severe droughts without El Nino in the passage.

# Solution

Track the sentence's **two-way structural job**: looking backward, it shows El Nino is not the only path to severe drought, and looking forward, it bridges directly into the Indian Ocean Dipole section.

> Noting severe droughts without El Nino shows El Nino is not the sole cause of severe drought while bridging smoothly into the discussion of the Indian Ocean Dipole.

## Checking the five candidate functions

Checking **the paragraph-four to paragraph-five bridge** isolates the exact textual function.

| Candidate function | Fit with passage structure | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shows severe drought can occur without El Nino while introducing the Indian Ocean Dipole | Matches both backward nuance and forward transition | **Correct** |
| Shows El Nino's effect is too weak to matter | Contradicts paragraphs one and six | **Incorrect** |
| Explains El Nino's weaker role at peak rainy season | That is paragraph three, not non-El-Nino droughts | **Incorrect** |
| Shows El Nino forecasts usually fail | Paragraph four separates probability from impact | **Incorrect** |
| Claims non-El-Nino severe droughts are more frequent | Overstates text ("many" with El Nino vs "several" without) | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The detail **shows that severe droughts can happen without El Nino while leading the reader into the discussion of the Indian Ocean Dipole**.
