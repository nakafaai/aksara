An inner conflict is a struggle within a character. While Ilham is **in third place**, he sees the child whose boat has capsized. The short lines "Perahu-perahu lain melaju. Ilham memutar kemudi." (The other boats sped on. Ilham turned the tiller.) mark **the moment he chooses**: giving up his chance to win in order to help. That choice decides how the race ends and how his father responds.

- School and practice are not set against each other. He practises after school.
- He borrows the boat with his father's permission, not secretly.
- Ilham does not scold the organisers.
- The story does not set fishing against learning to sew sails.

So the inner conflict that shapes the story most is Ilham's choice between chasing victory and helping the child whose boat had capsized.
