A **fakta** (fact) is information that can be checked, for example by reading a research report. An **opini** (opinion) is the writer's judgement or proposal, often marked by words such as **sebaiknya** (should) or **lebih berguna** (more useful).

| Statement | Answer | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The link is stronger in secondary school | Fakta (fact) | It is a finding of a research review, which can be checked. |
| The review covered dozens of US studies | Fakta (fact) | The scope of the review can be checked in its report. |
| Homework should be abolished in primary school | Opini (opinion) | "Sebaiknya" (should) marks the writer's proposal. |
| Afternoons are more useful for reading, play, and sleep | Opini (opinion) | "Lebih berguna" (more useful) is the writer's judgement, and others may judge differently. |

The first statement contains "lebih kuat" (stronger), but that comparison is **a research result**, not the writer's personal judgement.
