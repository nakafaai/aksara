# Given

**The text explains** how El Nino and positive IOD raise dry-season drought odds across different regions of Indonesia through the year.

# Asked

Which statements can validly be concluded from the passage on El Nino and positive IOD.

# Solution

> A claim about higher probability leaves room for exceptions, so one ordinary dry year never disproves a rise in risk.

Judge each statement on its own evidence. A statement goes in the Ya column only if the text really supports it, not merely if the text doesn't contradict it.

**Statement 1**, about El Nino years with an ordinary dry season, goes in the Tidak column. The first paragraph says El Nino "menaikkan peluang" (raises the chance) of a dry season and "tidak memastikannya" (doesn't guarantee it). A claim about chance leaves room for exceptions, so an El Nino year with an ordinary dry season doesn't weaken it.

**Statement 2**, about northern Sumatra, goes in the Tidak column. The third paragraph says the opposite: Java and Nusa Tenggara "umumnya merasakan penurunan hujan yang lebih jelas" (generally feel a clearer drop in rain) than some areas near the equator in northern Sumatra.

**Statement 3**, about the second half of the year, goes in the Ya column. The fifth paragraph says El Nino together with a positive IOD "dapat memperparah kekeringan" (can make drought worse), and a strong IOD peaks in the second half of the year. The third paragraph adds that El Nino's influence weakens at the peak of the rainy season. Together those two parts point to **the second half of the year** as the riskiest time.

**Statement 4**, about the IOD being the more frequent cause, goes in the Tidak column. The fifth paragraph only says "sebagian" (some) of the severe dry seasons without El Nino can be explained by the IOD. The text never counts how often each one is the cause.

# Answer

The first statement is the trickiest. The writer never claimed certainty in the first place, so **an exception doesn't refute a chance**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| An El Nino year with an ordinary dry season weakens the claim about chance | Tidak |
| Northern Sumatra loses more rain than Nusa Tenggara | Tidak |
| El Nino with a positive IOD is riskiest in the second half of the year | Ya |
| A positive IOD is the cause more often than El Nino | Tidak |
