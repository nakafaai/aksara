The definition of *periode* changes the table reading from the time for ten oscillations to **the time for one oscillation**. Apply the same division to every row.

| Condition | Time for ten oscillations | Estimated period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 cm string, initial | 20.1 seconds | <InlineMath math="20.1/10=2.01\,\mathrm{s}" /> |
| 60 cm string | 15.6 seconds | <InlineMath math="15.6/10=1.56\,\mathrm{s}" /> |
| 100 cm string, final | 20.0 seconds | <InlineMath math="20.0/10=2.00\,\mathrm{s}" /> |

The term therefore determines how the measurements should be processed. Its definition alone does not establish a general law connecting length and period.
