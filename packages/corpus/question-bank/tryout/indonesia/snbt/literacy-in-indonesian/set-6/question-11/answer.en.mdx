Pak Rasyid is described as a man who **rarely speaks**, so his attitude shows in **his actions**. When Ilham comes in last, his father does not scold him. He **nods once** and helps carry the boat. That night he **replaces the sail's patches with new cloth** and speaks of "tahun depan" (next year). All of this shows that he approves of Ilham's decision and supports him, with few words.

- Nothing shows disappointment or anger. He does not scold at all.
- Sewing the sail for Ilham shows care, not indifference.
- He only nods. He does not praise loudly.
- Preparing a new sail for next year contradicts any regret about lending the boat.

So Pak Rasyid's attitude is best described as approving Ilham's decision with few words and showing it through his actions.
