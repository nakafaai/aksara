# Given

In a Central Java village, **Lintang Waluku** has reached its usual position for preparing rice fields, yet **the soil is still dry and frogs are not calling**. Paragraphs four and five note that stars stay fixed so sowing by stars risks dried-out seedlings, whereas living creatures track actual weather and farmer groups cross-check them against official forecasts.

# Asked

The action most consistent with the author's reasoning when these two cues conflict.

# Solution

Follow **living-nature and soil cues** over fixed star positions, and cross-check them with **official weather forecasts** as recommended in paragraph five.

> Delaying sowing until frogs and soil confirm rain has truly arrived while checking official forecasts puts both final paragraphs into practice.

## Checking the five proposed actions

Matching **each option against paragraphs four and five** isolates the single sound action.

| Proposed action | Alignment with author's reasoning | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delay sowing until living signs and soil show rain has arrived, while cross-checking official forecasts | Applies both living cues and forecast cross-checking | **Correct** |
| Sow right away by Lintang Waluku's position | Exact mistake warned against in paragraph four | **Incorrect** |
| Abandon pranata mangsa altogether | Dismissed by the author as "hasty" | **Incorrect** |
| Sow on the exact same calendar date next year | Still relies on fixed dates that no longer hold | **Incorrect** |
| Copy the schedule of a region with a different rain pattern | Violates paragraph three's regional caution | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The most consistent action is to **delay sowing until signs from living creatures and soil conditions show rain has truly arrived, while cross-checking with official forecasts**.
