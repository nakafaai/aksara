# Given

**The passage explains** Java's pranata mangsa calendar, the star constellation Lintang Waluku, its local scope, and nature signs.

# Asked

Whether each statement about pranata mangsa's features and usefulness is true or false.

# Solution

> Tricky statements reuse familiar words from the text while flipping a key fact like sun versus moon or local versus universal use.

Each statement here touches one feature of the pranata mangsa. Some use words from the passage but **flip or widen** what it says. Match each statement to the right sentence.

The statement about the moon is false. The second paragraph says the pranata mangsa "tidak mengikuti peredaran bulan seperti kalender Jawa yang lain, tetapi mengikuti peredaran matahari" (doesn't follow the moon like other Javanese calendars, but follows the sun). The statement flips that sentence.

The statement about Lintang Waluku is true. The fourth paragraph says Lintang Waluku "tetap muncul di tempat yang sama pada waktu yang sama setiap tahun, sedangkan hujan tidak lagi selalu datang sesudahnya" (still appears in the same place at the same time every year, while rain no longer always follows). The stars don't depend on rain, so even in a year with late rain they appear as usual.

The statement about other regions is true. The third paragraph says the same schedule "tidak dapat dipindahkan begitu saja ke daerah yang musim hujannya datang pada bulan lain" (can't simply be moved to a region whose rainy season comes in a different month).

The statement about animals and plants is false. The fourth paragraph says signs from living things "menanggapi cuaca yang benar-benar terjadi" (respond to the weather that actually happens) and "cenderung ikut bergeser bersama hujan" (tend to shift along with the rain). Those are the signs that **stay useful**. The misleading ones are tied to the sun and stars.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Built on the cycle of the moon | Salah |
| Lintang Waluku still appears as usual | Benar |
| Can't be used directly in other regions | Benar |
| Signs from animals and plants are no longer useful | Salah |
