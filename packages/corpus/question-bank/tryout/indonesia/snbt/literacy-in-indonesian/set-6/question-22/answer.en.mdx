A main idea must cover the whole passage. Paragraph one says Sriwijaya's history was **rebuilt from two kinds of sources**: travellers' records and inscriptions. Paragraphs two to four describe those sources, and the last paragraph shows **their limits**: the site of the capital is still debated, and inscriptions record only what kings wanted to announce.

- The passage does not call Sriwijaya the largest kingdom. The thousand monks are only I-Tsing's record.
- I-Tsing was a traveller who wrote records, not the finder of the inscriptions.
- The passage says only some words are still recognisable, so the language has changed.
- The passage says most experts point to Palembang and does not side with the Jambi view.

So the main idea is that Sriwijaya's history is pieced together from inscriptions and travellers' records, which have limits.
