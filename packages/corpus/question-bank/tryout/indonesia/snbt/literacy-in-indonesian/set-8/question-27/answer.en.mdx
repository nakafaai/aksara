The phrase is followed by "padahal yang ingin kukirim adalah tanganku / untuk memijat punggungmu" (when what I wanted to send was my hands / to massage your back). Money sent through a screen appears only as **a number**: exact in amount ("rapi", neat) but **without warmth** ("dingin", cold). What the speaker really wants to give is **presence and touch**. So the transfer feels unable to carry that love.

- The size of the salary is not discussed.
- "Cold" is figurative, meaning lacking warmth, not the air temperature.
- The poem does not mention the mother's reaction to the money.
- "Neat" points to an exact amount, not a mistake.

So the phrase shows that sending money feels unable to express the love the speaker wants to give.
