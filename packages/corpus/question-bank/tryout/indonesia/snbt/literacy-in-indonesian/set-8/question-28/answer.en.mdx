The line uses the image of **a plant**, something watered so it does not wilt. What is "watered" is **the village's name**, and it lives **inside the chest**, that is, in the heart. Every night the speaker **tends the memory of and bond with** the home village so that it does not fade amid city life.

- Watering here is figurative, not real gardening.
- The poem mentions no plan to go home and farm.
- "Agar tidak layu" (so it does not wilt) shows the speaker is trying not to forget.
- "Di dalam dadaku" (inside my chest) points to feeling, not illness.

So the line most likely means the speaker is trying to keep alive the memory of and bond with the home village.
