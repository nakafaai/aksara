The question "sudah makan?" (have you eaten?) is a form of **everyday care**, usually asked by someone close, such as a mother. The line follows "nasi dibeli dalam bungkus kertas" (rice is bought in paper wrapping), a picture of quick, solitary meals. In the city the speaker can still buy food, but **no one looks after them** as before.

- The speaker has a salary and rice can be bought, so money is not the problem.
- The poem does not judge the rice sellers.
- The line does not say the speaker forgets to eat.
- The speaker's friends are not discussed.

So the line implies that in the city the speaker has lost the simple care they once received.
