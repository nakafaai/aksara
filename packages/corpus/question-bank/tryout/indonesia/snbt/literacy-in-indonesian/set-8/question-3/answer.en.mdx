Paragraph four explains that B12 is **not made by the mould** but by **certain bacteria that grow along** during fermentation. How many such bacteria there are depends on how clean the workplace is and which starter is used. That is why B12 levels vary so much, and tempe from a very clean factory using pure mould starter may contain almost none.

- *Rhizopus* does not make B12, and cold temperatures are not mentioned.
- Where the soybeans come from is not linked to B12.
- Teak leaves are only a wrapping, not a vitamin source.
- Tempe that is too wet is said to spoil, not to gain B12.

So B12 levels can differ widely because the vitamin comes from bacteria that grow alongside the mould, and their numbers vary.
