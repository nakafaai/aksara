# Given

**An operator spends** <InlineMath math="2" /> hours in an <InlineMath math="88" /> dBA room and <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes in a <InlineMath math="94" /> dBA room under the daily noise-dose rule in paragraph <InlineMath math="3" />.

# Asked

The maximum time the operator may still spend in a <InlineMath math="97" /> dBA room on the same day.

# Solution

> Picture the daily noise limit as one whole pie, subtract the slices used in the first two rooms, then convert the leftover slice into minutes.

You need to work out what's left of the day's noise allowance, then turn it into minutes in the <InlineMath math="97" /> dBA room.

Paragraph 3 gives two rules. First, the allowances **"<InlineMath math="88" /> dBA untuk <InlineMath math="4" /> jam"** (<InlineMath math="88" /> dBA for <InlineMath math="4" /> hours) and **"<InlineMath math="94" /> dBA untuk <InlineMath math="1" /> jam"** (<InlineMath math="94" /> dBA for <InlineMath math="1" /> hour), and **each <InlineMath math="3" /> dB rise halves the allowed time**. Second, the daily dose is found by **adding up the share of the allowance used** at each noise level.

Work out the dose already used:

| Level | Share used |
| --- | ---: |
| <InlineMath math="88" /> dBA | <InlineMath math="2" /> hours <InlineMath math=":4" /> hours <InlineMath math="=50\%" /> |
| <InlineMath math="94" /> dBA | <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes <InlineMath math=":60" /> minutes <InlineMath math="=25\%" /> |
| Left | <InlineMath math="100\%-50\%-25\%=25\%" /> |

The allowance for <InlineMath math="97" /> dBA isn't written, so you derive it. <InlineMath math="97" /> dBA is <InlineMath math="3" /> dB above <InlineMath math="94" /> dBA, so its allowance is half of <InlineMath math="1" /> hour, that is <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes.

<InlineMath math="25\%\times30" /> minutes <InlineMath math="=7.5" /> minutes.

Why the other options are wrong:

- <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes: you forgot the <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes at <InlineMath math="94" /> dBA, or used the <InlineMath math="94" /> dBA allowance for the <InlineMath math="97" /> dBA room.
- <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes: that's the whole <InlineMath math="97" /> dBA allowance, without taking off the dose already used.
- <InlineMath math="3.75" /> minutes: you used the <InlineMath math="15" />-minute allowance for <InlineMath math="100" /> dBA, one step too far.
- <InlineMath math="2" /> hours: you took <InlineMath math="25\%" /> of <InlineMath math="8" /> hours, but the remaining dose has to be multiplied by the allowance at <InlineMath math="97" /> dBA.

# Answer

So the operator may spend at most **<InlineMath math="7.5" />** minutes in the **<InlineMath math="97" />** dBA room.
