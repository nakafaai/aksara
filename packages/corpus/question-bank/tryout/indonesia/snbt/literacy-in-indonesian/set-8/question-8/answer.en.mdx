Test each conclusion against the story's lines about the two characters.

- **Trust with money**. Bu Sri has **"sudah dua puluh tahun menitipkan uang receh untuk kembalian kepada Pak Harun setiap pagi"** (for twenty years left coins for change with Pak Harun every morning). This statement is true.
- **A gift, not a sale**. Pak Harun holds out money, and Bu Sri **"pura-pura tidak melihatnya"** (pretends not to see it). She won't take payment, so the nasi uduk is a gift. This statement is true.
- **It happens again and again**. Bu Sri puts down the nasi uduk **"seperti setiap pagi"** (as every morning), and **"Seperti setiap pagi pula"** (as every morning too) Pak Harun holds out money. This statement is true.

The other two aren't supported.

- Pak Harun holds out money, so he isn't shown as unable to pay.
- The story doesn't link the coins to the nasi uduk. Leaving coins is a separate habit, never described as payment in kind.

So the story supports that Bu Sri trusts Pak Harun with money, that the nasi uduk is a gift, and that the habit happens again and again.
