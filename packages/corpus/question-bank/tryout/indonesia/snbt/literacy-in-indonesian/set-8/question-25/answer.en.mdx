The author's attitude shows in **"Cerita itu menyentuh, tetapi menyembunyikan sesuatu"** (That story is touching, but hides something). The sentence has two parts.

- **Touching**. The author grants the story's power, and in the last paragraph still counts spirit as part of the victory.
- **Hides something**. The story skips years of development in badminton clubs.

So the author grants the story is touching but judges it incomplete, because it skips club-level development.

The other options are wrong.

- The author doesn't reject it entirely, writing that the spirit **"bertemu dengan"** (met) development, so spirit still mattered.
- The author doesn't accept it entirely, because the story is said to hide something.
- The author does judge the story, by naming what it leaves out.
- The text doesn't say the story was made up on purpose. Business support is only named as one way clubs were kept going.
