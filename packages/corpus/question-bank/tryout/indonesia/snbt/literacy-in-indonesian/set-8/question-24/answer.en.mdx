# Given

The author's lesson from 1958 is **"not merely that spirit can overcome shortages,"** but that **spirit met long-standing club development** powered by regular competitions, member dues, and local support.

# Asked

The district plan most deserving of criticism under the author's lesson, and the right reason why.

# Solution

Identify the plan that **skips long-term club training in favor of short-term motivation**, paired with a critique that faults relying on spirit alone rather than claiming spirit is useless.

> Picking top athletes for a one-month motivational camp most merits criticism because it relies on spirit alone while skipping long-term club development.

## Checking the five plan and reason pairs

Verifying **both targeted plan and accurate rationale** leaves one faithful answer.

| Plan and criticism pair | Plan clashes with lesson | Reason matches author's stance | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Squad of top athletes with a one-month motivational camp, because it relies only on spirit and skips long-term club training | Yes | Yes (keeps spirit's role intact) | **Correct** |
| Long-term club training drains fighting spirit | Wrong (praised by author) | Wrong | **Incorrect** |
| Funding clubs through member dues and local business | Wrong (exact 1958 model) | Wrong | **Incorrect** |
| One-month motivation camp because spirit is useless | Yes | Wrong (author still credits spirit) | **Incorrect** |
| Regular inter-club competitions | Wrong (core praised element) | Wrong | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The plan most deserving criticism is **forming a squad with a one-month motivational camp, because it relies solely on spirit and skips long-term club development**.
