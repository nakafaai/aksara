# Given

**The text and** table give rice spending across five household groups under a <InlineMath math="20" /> percent price rise with unchanged rice quantities.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded about how a rice price jump and price subsidies affect each group.

# Solution

> Richer households pay more extra rupiah in total, yet the same price hike bites a far larger slice out of a poor household's budget.

Work out what each group spends on rice, then the extra cost if the price rises <InlineMath math="20\%" />.

| Group | Rupiah on rice | Extra cost | Share of spending |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Bottom | <InlineMath math="18\%\times600{,}000=108{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="21{,}600" /> | <InlineMath math="3.6\%" /> |
| Top | <InlineMath math="4\%\times3{,}600{,}000=144{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="28{,}800" /> | <InlineMath math="0.8\%" /> |

The statement that the bottom group's extra cost is about <InlineMath math="3.6\%" /> can be concluded. <InlineMath math="18\%\times20\%=3.6\%" />.

The statement that the top group's extra cost is bigger in rupiah can be concluded. Rp<InlineMath math="28{,}800" /> is more than Rp<InlineMath math="21{,}600" />.

The statement that a price subsidy gives the most rupiah to the bottom group can't be concluded. A price subsidy covers each buyer's extra cost. The top group spends more rupiah on rice, so it's the group that gets the bigger subsidy.

The statement that the rise weighs most on the bottom group as a share of spending can be concluded. <InlineMath math="3.6\%" /> is far more than <InlineMath math="0.8\%" />. This is what the economist means by **"siapa yang paling terbebani"** (who is most burdened).

The statement that the top group's rice share is bigger can't be concluded. Its share is <InlineMath math="4\%" />, far below <InlineMath math="18\%" />.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The bottom group's extra cost is about <InlineMath math="3.6\%" /> of spending | Ya |
| In rupiah, the top group's extra cost is bigger | Ya |
| A price subsidy gives the most rupiah to the bottom group | Tidak |
| As a share of spending, the rise weighs most on the bottom group | Ya |
| The top group's rice share is bigger | Tidak |
