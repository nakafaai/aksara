# Given

**The Indonesian badminton** history passage notes 1949 sovereignty recognition, PBSI's founding in 1951, and Thomas Cup wins in 1958, 1961, and 1964.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded from the timeline and Thomas Cup history in the passage.

# Solution

> Subtract the exact years given in the text and reject claims that invent extra counts or training dates the passage never states.

Some statements need year arithmetic, and one traps you with a number the text never gives.

The statement that sovereignty was recognised more than ten years earlier can't be concluded. The text says **"kurang dari sembilan tahun sebelumnya"** (less than nine years before).

The statement that Malaya won more than three times can't be concluded. The text only says Malaya **"selalu menang sejak kejuaraan itu pertama kali digelar pada 1949"** (always won since the event was first held in 1949). How many times it was held before 1958 isn't given.

The statement about PBSI's age can be concluded. PBSI **"baru berdiri pada 1951"** (was only founded in 1951), and the win came in 1958, so it was about seven years old.

The statement that the players only began training after PBSI was founded can't be concluded. The text says they were **"sudah bertahun-tahun ditempa"** (forged for years) in clubs, but not when they started.

The statement that Indonesia held the cup without a break until 1964 can be concluded. Indonesia **"mempertahankan Piala Thomas pada 1961 dan 1964"** (defended the Thomas Cup in 1961 and 1964), so the cup never left between those championships.
