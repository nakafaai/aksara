A main idea must cover the whole passage. Paragraphs one and two say who met and why: new nations wanted **to cooperate without depending on either bloc**. Paragraph three gives the result, the Dasasila Bandung. Paragraphs four and five describe **its influence and legacy**, the idea that even small nations can speak together.

- The five sponsoring countries are only a detail in paragraph one.
- Disagreement did not make the conference fail. It still produced the Dasasila Bandung.
- The last paragraph says the most important legacy is **not** the building.
- The passage does not say the aim was for Indonesia to lead the Non-Aligned Movement.

So the main idea is that the Asian-African Conference united new nations outside the blocs and left a legacy of ideas.
