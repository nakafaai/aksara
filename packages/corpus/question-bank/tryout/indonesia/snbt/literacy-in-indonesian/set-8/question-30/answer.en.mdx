Longing and loneliness appear in many lines: the speaker **lies awake alone**, wants to send **their own hands** to massage their mother, and feels that **no one** asks whether they have eaten. Yet the last stanza opens with "Ibu, jangan khawatir" (Mother, don't worry), which shows the speaker **still tries to reassure their mother**.

- No reply letter or anger is mentioned.
- Rice in paper wrapping and lying awake alone do not suggest luxury.
- Lamps that never sleep show a busy city, not a source of fear.
- No regret about the job is mentioned.

So the most prominent feeling is longing and loneliness, while still trying to reassure the mother.
