# Given

**Paragraph three's rule** states every **<InlineMath math="3\text{ dB}" />** rise cuts safe listening time in half, so every **<InlineMath math="3\text{ dB}" />** drop doubles safe listening time. The table lists <InlineMath math="94\text{ dBA}" /> for <InlineMath math="1" /> hour and <InlineMath math="91\text{ dBA}" /> for <InlineMath math="2" /> hours.

# Asked

By what factor safe exposure time increases when a concertgoer moves from <InlineMath math="97\text{ dBA}" /> to <InlineMath math="91\text{ dBA}" />.

# Solution

Compute **safe exposure at <InlineMath math="97\text{ dBA}" /> and <InlineMath math="91\text{ dBA}" />**, or count the number of <InlineMath math="3\text{ dB}" /> doubling steps directly.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{drop} = 97 - 91 = 6\text{ dB} = 2 \text{ steps of } 3\text{ dB}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{time multiplier} = 2^2 = 4\text{ times} \quad (30\text{ min} \to 120\text{ min})" />
</MathContainer>

> Dropping <InlineMath math="6\text{ dB}" /> applies two doublings, expanding safe exposure <InlineMath math="4" />-fold from <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes to <InlineMath math="2" /> hours.

## Checking the distractor multipliers

Tracing **each wrong factor** exposes linear decibel errors or wrong table rows.

| Option | Source of calculation | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="4" /> times | Two doublings (<InlineMath math="2 \times 2" />), from <InlineMath math="30" /> min to <InlineMath math="120" /> min | **Correct** |
| <InlineMath math="2" /> times | Counts the number of <InlineMath math="3\text{ dB}" /> steps rather than doubling twice | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="6" /> times | Treats the <InlineMath math="6\text{ dB}" /> difference directly as a multiplier | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="1.07" /> times | Divides <InlineMath math="97 : 91" /> as if decibels were linear | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="8" /> times | Uses <InlineMath math="15" /> min for <InlineMath math="100\text{ dBA}" /> instead of <InlineMath math="97\text{ dBA}" /> | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Safe exposure time becomes **<InlineMath math="4" /> times as long**, rising from <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes to <InlineMath math="2" /> hours.
