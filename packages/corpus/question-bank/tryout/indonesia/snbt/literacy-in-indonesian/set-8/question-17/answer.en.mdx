# Given

**The table lists** total spending and food share for five consecutive household groups illustrating Engel's law.

# Asked

Which consecutive pair of groups has the smallest rupiah increase in food spending and how big it is.

# Solution

> Convert each group's food share into rupiah first, then subtract neighboring groups step by step to spot the smallest jump.

Work out each group's money on food, then compare each pair in order.

**Step 1. Money on food**.

| Group | Calculation | Money on food |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Bottom | <InlineMath math="62\%\times600{,}000" /> | Rp<InlineMath math="372{,}000" /> |
| Second | <InlineMath math="58\%\times900{,}000" /> | Rp<InlineMath math="522{,}000" /> |
| Middle | <InlineMath math="54\%\times1{,}200{,}000" /> | Rp<InlineMath math="648{,}000" /> |
| Fourth | <InlineMath math="47\%\times1{,}800{,}000" /> | Rp<InlineMath math="846{,}000" /> |
| Top | <InlineMath math="35\%\times3{,}600{,}000" /> | Rp<InlineMath math="1{,}260{,}000" /> |

**Step 2. Increase between groups**. The increases are Rp<InlineMath math="150{,}000" />, Rp<InlineMath math="126{,}000" />, Rp<InlineMath math="198{,}000" /> and Rp<InlineMath math="414{,}000" />. The smallest is from the second group to the middle group.

The other options are wrong.

- Rp<InlineMath math="150{,}000" /> is the increase from the bottom group to the second, the second smallest.
- Rp<InlineMath math="198{,}000" /> is the increase from the middle group to the fourth.
- Rp<InlineMath math="300{,}000" /> is the gap in total spending between the second and middle groups, not in food money.
- Rp<InlineMath math="414{,}000" /> is actually the largest increase.

# Answer

So the smallest increase in food money is from the second group to the middle group, about Rp<InlineMath math="126{,}000" />.
