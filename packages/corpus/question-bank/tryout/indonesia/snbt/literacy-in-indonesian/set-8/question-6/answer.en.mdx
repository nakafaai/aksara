The right reading has to fit **two things**: why Pak Darto comes and what the news about his son means.

- **Why he comes**. After buying, **"Ia tidak membaca"** (he doesn't read). He just sits watching the buses. So he isn't after the news.
- **The news about his son**. The son won't come home for Lebaran, **"video call saja"** (just a video call). Then they are **"diam cukup lama"** (silent for quite a while), and Pak Harun says he'll bring papers again tomorrow. That's a promise to still be there, and Pak Darto answers that this is why he comes.

The other options are wrong.

- Pak Darto doesn't come for the news, since he doesn't read the paper. The story also never says he wants to save money.
- The story never shows Pak Darto angry with his son. He only passes on the news.
- Pak Harun doesn't promise Pagi Raya will stay. He says himself it has **"Tinggal hari ini dan tiga minggu lagi"** (just today and three more weeks).
- If Pak Darto comes for the company, the end of Pagi Raya is no reason to stop coming. Pak Harun also promises **"koran"** (papers), not Pagi Raya.

So Pak Darto comes for the company at the stall, and his son not coming home makes Pak Harun's promise to stay all the more meaningful.
