# Given

**The table shows** total spending and food shares per group, making non-food spending the remaining percentage of each budget.

# Asked

Which statements about food shares and non-food spending can be concluded from the table.

# Solution

> Non-food share is simply one hundred percent minus the food share, multiply by total spending whenever a claim compares rupiah amounts.

Non-food spending is **what's left after food**, that is <InlineMath math="100\%" /> minus the food share.

The statement that the middle group spends more than half on food can be concluded. Its share is <InlineMath math="54\%" />.

The statement that the bottom group's non-food spending exceeds its food spending can't be concluded. Its food share is <InlineMath math="62\%" />, so non-food is only <InlineMath math="38\%" />.

The statement about ten times can be concluded. Work it out in rupiah.

| Group | Non-food share | Rupiah on non-food |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Bottom | <InlineMath math="38\%" /> | <InlineMath math="38\%\times600{,}000=228{,}000" /> |
| Top | <InlineMath math="65\%" /> | <InlineMath math="65\%\times3{,}600{,}000=2{,}340{,}000" /> |

<InlineMath math="2{,}340{,}000:228{,}000\approx10.3" />, more than ten times.

The statement that the food share falls by the same amount each step can't be concluded. The falls are <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="7" /> and <InlineMath math="12" /> percentage points, bigger towards the top.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The middle group spends more than half on food | Ya |
| The bottom group's non-food exceeds its food spending | Tidak |
| The top group's non-food is more than ten times the bottom's | Ya |
| The food share falls by the same amount each step | Tidak |
