A shared view must appear in **both texts**. Text 1 defends domestic processing for its export value and jobs. Text 2, though critical, says **domestic processing should continue**, only with conditions. So both agree that processing should go on, though they differ on conditions and on extending the policy.

- Neither text asks to reopen raw ore exports.
- Text 2 criticises coal plants but does not demand their closure, and Text 1 does not discuss them.
- Extending the ban to other minerals is proposed only by Text 1.
- Text 2 demands a fair share of benefits for producing regions.

So both texts hold that domestic nickel processing should continue.
