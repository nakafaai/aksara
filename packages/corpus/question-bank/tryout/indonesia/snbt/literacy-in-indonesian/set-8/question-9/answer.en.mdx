Almost every statement depends on small clues that aren't spelled out.

The statement that sales are falling can be concluded. The stand used to sell twelve newspapers and six magazines, and now has only three thin piles. The figures in the notebook are also **"makin kecil dari halaman ke halaman"** (smaller from page to page).

The statement that Pak Darto doesn't buy the paper mainly to read it can be concluded. After buying it, **"Ia tidak membaca"** (He doesn't read). He only sits and watches the buses.

The statement that the bus driver buys a paper can't be concluded. The driver **"membeli dua permen, lalu pergi tanpa melirik koran"** (buys two sweets, then leaves without a glance at the papers).

The statement that Pagi Raya will stop within a few weeks can be concluded. Bu Sri says it will **"berhenti cetak bulan depan"** (stop printing next month), and Pak Harun says, **"Tinggal hari ini dan tiga minggu lagi"** (Just today and three more weeks).
