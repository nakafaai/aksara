# Given

**Text A gives** average citations for English and Indonesian articles, while Text B cites legislation and non-researcher readers.

# Asked

Which statements about calculations and examples from both texts can be concluded.

# Solution

> Averages alone never tell you total citations without article counts, and a ratio between languages does not prove translating multiplies citations.

Four statements test what can and can't be worked out from Text A's data.

The statement about an average of <InlineMath math="7.5" /> can be concluded. If the two languages have equal numbers of articles, the combined average is <InlineMath math="(12+3)\div2=7.5" />.

The statement about translating an article can't be concluded. The data compares two different groups of articles, and Text B shows they also differ in funding and team. It doesn't show what happens to one article when its language changes.

The statement that total citations can be worked out can't be concluded. Text A gives only <InlineMath math="400" /> articles, without saying how many are in English and how many in Indonesian. That's why the first statement needs an assumption.

The statement about four articles can be concluded. At an average of <InlineMath math="3" /> per article, four Indonesian articles together are cited <InlineMath math="4\times3=12" /> times, the same as one English article on average.

The statement about the law can't be concluded. Text B uses the law to show that Indonesian must be used, since it **"mewajibkan bahasa Indonesia dipakai dalam penulisan dan publikasi karya ilmiah"** (requires Indonesian in writing and publishing scientific work). The law says nothing about reader numbers.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Combined average of <InlineMath math="7.5" /> if half are in English | Ya |
| Translating makes citations four times higher | Tidak |
| Total citations can be worked out | Tidak |
| One English article equals four Indonesian ones | Ya |
| The law shows reader numbers | Tidak |
