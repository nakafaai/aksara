A moral comes from **how the main character changes**. Sari was once ashamed of her mother's work. After doing it herself, she finds that this seemingly simple job demands **knowledge** of measures and restrictions and **care** for each customer's condition. The moral: work thought of as simple can hold great knowledge and care.

- Sari keeps studying. The story does not ask her to stop.
- Resting a sore back is only the event that starts the plot, not the main message.
- The story shows how her studies and her mother's experience resemble each other.
- Sari's shame before her friends is an attitude that changes, not the story's message.

So the moral is that work thought of as simple can hold great knowledge and care.
