Two statements use the same citation figures. Tell **the size of the comparison** apart from **its cause**.

The statement about the law can be concluded. Text B says Law No. 24 of 2009 **"mewajibkan bahasa Indonesia dipakai dalam penulisan dan publikasi karya ilmiah di Indonesia"** (requires Indonesian to be used in writing and publishing scientific work in Indonesia).

The statement that English-language articles average four times the citations of Indonesian-language ones can be concluded. <InlineMath math="12:3=4" />.

The statement that the data prove English multiplies citations fourfold can't be concluded. The figure is indeed four times, but the data show only a comparison, not a cause. Text B shows the two groups differ in funding and team experience.

The statement about daring and luring can be concluded. Text B gives these terms as evidence that Indonesian **"terus membentuk kata baru untuk hal baru"** (keeps forming new words for new things).
