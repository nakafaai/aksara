The table's percentages apply to groups of different sizes. First turn each percentage into **a number of students**, then add them.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 60 \times 55\% &= 33 \\ 100 \times 40\% &= 40 \\ 240 \times 25\% &= 60 \\ 33 + 40 + 60 &= 133 \end{aligned}" />

- 40 students come only from the 2 or 3 days group.
- 60 students come only from the 4 or 5 days group.
- 160 students comes from averaging the three percentages (40%) and multiplying by 400, though the groups differ in size.
- 220 students applies the 0 or 1 day group's rate (55%) to all 400 students.

So 133 students said they often had trouble concentrating.
