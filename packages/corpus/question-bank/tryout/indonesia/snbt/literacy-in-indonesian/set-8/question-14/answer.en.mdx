*Comparability* requires **sufficiently aligned measurement meanings**, not merely tidy numbers.

| Record element | What can be checked |
| --- | --- |
| Original value and unit plus conversion | Lets reviewers check conversion to metres |
| Fixed reference point | Identifies where the distance to water starts |
| Date and time | Reveals daily or seasonal context differences |

The form records time but does not automatically synchronise measurements. Because water levels can change, a common schedule remains necessary for the comparison the team plans.
