# Given

Universitas Tirta Raya wants a publication policy meeting **three goals at once**: **Text A's** goal of joining global debates (publishing major findings in English), **Text B's** language rule (full Indonesian versions for domestic practitioners and law), and **Text B's** evaluation critique (not relying on citations alone).

# Asked

The policy that best satisfies Text A's goal alongside every objection raised by Text B.

# Solution

Test all five policies against **all three requirements together**: global English reach for key findings, complete Indonesian versions of all work, and impact evaluation beyond citations.

> Publishing all full results in Indonesian with English abstracts, also publishing major findings in English, and assessing real-world usefulness alongside citations passes all three tests.

## Testing all three criteria across the five policies

Checking **every criterion side by side** isolates the sole policy that fails none.

| Policy | Global English reach (A) | Full Indonesian version for all (B) | Evaluates impact beyond citations (B) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All results full in Indonesian with English abstracts, major findings also in English, plus usefulness alongside citations | Yes | Yes | Yes | **Correct** |
| English journals with Indonesian summaries only | Yes | Fails (not full text) | - | **Incorrect** |
| Indonesian only | Fails (misses global debate) | Yes | - | **Incorrect** |
| Evaluate faculty by citations alone | - | - | Fails | **Incorrect** |
| Major findings in English only | Yes | Fails (excludes Indonesian practitioners) | - | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The best-fitting policy **publishes all full results in Indonesian with English abstracts, also publishes major findings in English, and evaluates practical usefulness alongside citations**.
