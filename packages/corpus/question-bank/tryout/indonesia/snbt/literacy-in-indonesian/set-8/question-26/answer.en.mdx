# Given

In the **framing-effect experiment**, the passage notes that **programs A and C are identical** (certain outcome) and **programs B and D are identical** (risky outcome). Both student groups are comparable and differ only in survival versus mortality wording.

# Asked

The response pattern showing consistent choices with no framing effect.

# Solution

Consistent choices mean roughly the same percentage of both groups chooses **content-identical programs (A and C)** despite the shift in wording.

> Because programs A and C carry identical outcomes, consistent behavior appears when <InlineMath math="72\%" /> of group one picks A and roughly <InlineMath math="72\%" /> of group two picks C.
