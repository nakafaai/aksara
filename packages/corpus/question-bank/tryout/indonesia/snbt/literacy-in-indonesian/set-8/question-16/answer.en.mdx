# Given

**The table gives** monthly total spending and food percentage shares for five household spending groups from lowest to highest.

# Asked

All statements that fit both the food shares and rupiah food amounts in the table.

# Solution

> The food percentage shrinks as households grow richer, yet actual rupiah spent on food climbs sharply because total budgets are much larger.

Tell the **share** spent on food apart from the **amount in rupiah**. Work out the amounts first.

**The food share gets smaller: fits**. It falls from <InlineMath math="62\%" /> to <InlineMath math="35\%" /> as spending rises.

**More than three times: fits**. <InlineMath math="1{,}260{,}000:372{,}000\approx3.4" />.

**Groups below the fourth spend more than half: fits**. The bottom, second and middle groups spend <InlineMath math="62\%" />, <InlineMath math="58\%" /> and <InlineMath math="54\%" />, all above <InlineMath math="50\%" />.

**The bottom group spends the most rupiah: doesn't fit**. Its share is the biggest, but its amount is the smallest, Rp<InlineMath math="372{,}000" />.

**Less than half the bottom group's share: doesn't fit**. Half of <InlineMath math="62\%" /> is <InlineMath math="31\%" />, and the top group's share is <InlineMath math="35\%" />, more than half.

# Answer

So what fits the table is that the food share falls as spending rises, that the top group's food spending is more than three times the bottom group's, and that the three groups below the fourth spend more than half their budget on food.
