# Given

**The passage covers** noise damage to cochlear synapses and hair cells, mouse studies, the <InlineMath math="3" /> dB dose rule, and limits of a normal audiogram.

# Asked

Which statements the text genuinely supports at the same level of certainty.

# Solution

> A normal audiogram does not rule out hidden damage, yet tinnitus alone does not prove synapse loss either, so keep the exact strength of each claim.

For each statement, ask one thing: does the text really give grounds for it, with the same strength?

**Statement 1**, outer hair cells damaged. Paragraph 4 says the mice's threshold rose sharply, but **"sel rambut tikus-tikus itu tetap utuh"** (the mice's hair cells stayed intact). What was lost were synapses. So the cause the statement names doesn't fit the text. The answer is Tidak.

**Statement 2**, a normal audiogram isn't necessarily safe. Paragraph 4 ends with **"audiogram normal belum cukup untuk menyingkirkan kemungkinan itu"** (a normal audiogram isn't enough to rule out that possibility). "Belum tentu" (not necessarily) in the statement is as strong as "belum cukup" (not enough) in the text, because both only say damage is still possible. The answer is Ya.

**Statement 3**, the <InlineMath math="100" /> dBA dose is smaller. Compare the share of the allowance used, from paragraph 3:

| Exposure | Share used |
| --- | ---: |
| <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes at <InlineMath math="100" /> dBA | <InlineMath math="15:15=100\%" /> |
| <InlineMath math="4" /> hours at <InlineMath math="88" /> dBA | <InlineMath math="4:4=100\%" /> |

The doses are **equal**, not smaller. The much shorter time is offset by noise <InlineMath math="12" /> dB louder, four halvings. The answer is Tidak.

**Statement 4**, tinnitus proves synapse damage. Paragraph 5 only says the brain-gain explanation **"cocok"** (fits) tinnitus with normal audiograms, then adds that **this fit doesn't prove** their synapses are damaged. The statement turns "fits" into "proves". The answer is Tidak.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Noise damaged the mice's outer hair cells | Tidak |
| A normal audiogram doesn't necessarily mean no damage | Ya |
| <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes at <InlineMath math="100" /> dBA is a smaller dose | Tidak |
| Tinnitus with a normal audiogram proves synapse damage | Tidak |
