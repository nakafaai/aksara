Text 1 judges the policy a success by **rising export value** and **jobs**, as if the added value automatically benefits the country and its people. The question "siapa yang menikmatinya?" (who enjoys it?) challenges that assumption. Text 2 then names those who bear the costs, the environment, nearby residents, and workers, and demands a fair share of benefits for producing regions.

- The year of the ban is a fact Text 2 does not dispute.
- Smelters' power needs are mentioned by Text 2, not assumed by Text 1.
- Text 1 does not assume other countries stop needing nickel. It treats them as buyers.
- Text 1 does not discuss forests at all.

So the question challenges the assumption that the value added by nickel processing automatically reaches the wider public.
