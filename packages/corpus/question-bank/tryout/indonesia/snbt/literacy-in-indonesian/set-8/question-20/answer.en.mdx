# Given

The **second <InlineMath math="20\%" /> expenditure group** spends **Rp<InlineMath math="900{,}000" />** per capita per month, with **<InlineMath math="15\%" />** going to rice. Rice prices rise **<InlineMath math="20\%" />**, and the cash grant is sized to cover only the extra rice expense.

# Asked

The monthly per-capita cash grant for the second <InlineMath math="20\%" /> group.

# Solution

Compute the group's **baseline rice spending** first, then multiply by the **<InlineMath math="20\%" /> rice price rise**.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{baseline rice spending} = 15\% \times 900{,}000 = 135{,}000" />
<BlockMath math="\text{added rice cost} = 20\% \times 135{,}000 = 27{,}000" />
</MathContainer>

> Covering only the <InlineMath math="20\%" /> jump in rice expenditure requires exactly Rp<InlineMath math="27{,}000" /> per capita per month.

## Checking the distractor amounts

Tracing **each amount back to its base** exposes the four calculation mistakes.

| Option | Formula | What it computes | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rp<InlineMath math="27{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="20\% \times 135{,}000" /> | Extra rice cost for the second group | **Correct** |
| Rp<InlineMath math="180{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="20\% \times 900{,}000" /> | Applies <InlineMath math="20\%" /> to total spending | **Incorrect** |
| Rp<InlineMath math="104{,}400" /> | <InlineMath math="20\% \times 58\% \times 900{,}000" /> | Applies <InlineMath math="20\%" /> to all food spending | **Incorrect** |
| Rp<InlineMath math="135{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="15\% \times 900{,}000" /> | Full rice spending instead of extra cost | **Incorrect** |
| Rp<InlineMath math="21{,}600" /> | Extra rice cost of lowest group | Wrong income quintile | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The exact cash grant for the second group is **Rp<InlineMath math="27{,}000" />** per capita per month.
