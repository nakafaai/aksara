# Given

In the **1958 Thomas Cup** passage, paragraph one states the victory, paragraph two corrects the "spirit alone" tale with club training, paragraph three discusses national pride, and the closing paragraph concludes that **"spirit met long-standing club development"** that keeps producing players.

# Asked

The statement that best serves as the main idea of the whole passage.

# Solution

Because all five statements are true to the text, choose the one that **unites the correction of the spirit-only myth with the enduring lesson of club development**, rather than one paragraph's detail.

> The main idea is that Indonesia's 1958 victory came not from spirit alone, but from long-running club development whose tradition keeps producing players.

## Comparing the scope of all five statements

Evaluating **how broadly each statement spans the passage** separates the umbrella thesis from narrow facts.

| Statement | Passage coverage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The 1958 victory was not spirit alone, but also long-standing club development that continues producing players | Captures paragraph two's correction and the closing lesson | **Correct** |
| Facts of the 1958 Thomas Cup win | Opening background from paragraph one only | **Too narrow** |
| Source of national pride | Paragraph three detail only | **Too narrow** |
| Clubs funded by dues and local competitions | Supporting detail from paragraph two only | **Too narrow** |
| Popular story hides something | Opens the question without naming club development | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The main idea is that **the 1958 victory was not merely the fruit of fighting spirit, but also the result of long-standing club development whose tradition keeps producing players**.
