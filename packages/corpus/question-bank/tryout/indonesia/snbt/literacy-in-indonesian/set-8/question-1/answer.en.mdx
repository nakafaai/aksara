Paragraph two explains that the mould spores grow into **mycelium**, fine white threads that **spread between the soybeans and bind them** into one solid cake.

- Cooking water is not said to bind the beans. The beans are drained.
- Banana leaves are only a wrapping that lets air in, not a press.
- The B12-producing bacteria grow along, but are not said to bind the beans.
- The enzymes mentioned come from the mould and break down protein and fat. They do not bind.

So the soybeans join together because the mould's mycelium threads spread between them.
