The mood is built by **images and the characters' attitudes** from start to finish. The house leaks and the family sleeps crowded together, signs of a simple life. Yet the mother sets out basins "seperti menaruh doa" (like placing prayers), the father jokes that the dripping is more honest than the radio, and morning comes with a sky "seperti baru dicuci" (as if freshly washed). The mother even uses the leak water for her plants. Together these create a mood that is **warm and steadfast in simplicity**.

- Hardship is present, but there is no despair. The characters are calm.
- Heavy rain is described, but the family sleeps and the morning comes clean, so the mood is not frightening.
- There is no anger at the leaking house.
- The children sleep in the dry corner. The poem does not show them playing happily in the rain.

So the dominant mood is warm and steadfast in simplicity.
