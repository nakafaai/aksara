When an antibiotic is given, susceptible bacteria die, while resistant ones survive and **multiply with little competition**. The antibiotic does not create resistance. It makes already resistant bacteria the majority.

- Antibiotics do not turn susceptible bacteria into resistant ones. The genetic difference already exists by chance.
