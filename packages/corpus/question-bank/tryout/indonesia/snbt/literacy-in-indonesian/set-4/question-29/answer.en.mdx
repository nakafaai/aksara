In the morning, the mother pours the leak water from the basins into the water spinach pots and says, "tak ada yang benar-benar terbuang di rumah ini" (nothing is truly wasted in this house). Water that was a nuisance at night is **used to water the plants**. Her action and words show someone **who makes good use of hard circumstances** and wastes nothing. This matches stanza one, where she calmly places the basins one by one.

- There is no sign of anger. She acts calmly from the night onward.
- The poem never mentions wanting to move house.
- She is not delighted by the flooded yard. She uses the water from the basins.
- She clearly cares, as shown by how she catches the leaks and uses the water.

So the mother's action shows that she makes good use of hard circumstances and wastes nothing.
