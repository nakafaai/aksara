# Given

**The passage records** the timeline of the Reading Commission and Balai Pustaka, publishing languages, edits to Salah Asuhan, and the Nota Rinkes.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded directly from the historical facts in the passage.

# Solution

> Watch absolute words like all or banned in writing because they push a claim beyond what the historical text actually says.

Some statements set a trap with one word that's too strong. Match each one with the exact sentence.

- **Statement 1: Ya (Yes)**. The commission was set up on **14 September 1908**, and **"sembilan tahun kemudian, pada 1917"** (nine years later, in 1917) it became a publishing office.
- **Statement 2: Tidak (No)**. Balai Pustaka published books **"dalam bahasa Melayu, Jawa, Sunda, dan bahasa daerah lain"** (in Malay, Javanese, Sundanese and other regional languages).
- **Statement 3: Ya**. The manuscript was **"disunting dan diubah di Balai Pustaka sebelum diterbitkan"** (edited and changed at Balai Pustaka before publication).
- **Statement 4: Tidak**. The label "wild reading" was used **"oleh pihak kolonial"** (by the colonial side), not by the writers themselves.
- **Statement 5: Tidak**. The guidelines required that reading **"tidak memuat politik, tidak menyinggung agama, dan tidak melanggar kesusilaan"** (contain no politics, not offend religion and not breach decency). The text says criticism became **"sulit terbit"** (hard to publish) **"dalam praktik"** (in practice), not that all of it was banned in writing.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The commission ran for about nine years | Ya |
| All books were in Malay | Tidak |
| Salah Asuhan was changed before publication | Ya |
| Movement writers called their work "wild reading" | Tidak |
| The Nota Rinkes banned all criticism in writing | Tidak |
