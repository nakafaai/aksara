Paragraph two gives the background. The VOC forced Banda's leaders to sign agreements to **sell nutmeg only to the VOC**, but the Bandanese, used to free trade, **kept selling to other traders**, including the English on Run Island. Paragraph three follows directly with the 1621 attack. That sequence shows that breaking the monopoly was what drove the attack.

- The passage does not say the Bandanese refused to grow nutmeg or that European prices fell then.
- The English were the VOC's rivals who bought from the Bandanese, not the ones who asked for the attack.
- Chinese traders appear as carriers of nutmeg, not as buyers who stopped.
- Damage to nutmeg trees is not mentioned.

So the VOC attacked because the Bandanese kept selling nutmeg to other traders despite the agreement.
