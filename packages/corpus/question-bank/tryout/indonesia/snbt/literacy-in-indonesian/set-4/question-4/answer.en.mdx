Paragraph one says Fleming warned about resistance **when he received the Nobel Prize in 1945**, then states that "delapan dekade kemudian, peringatan itu terbukti" (eight decades later, the warning proved true). The story places the problem of resistance **at the very start of the antibiotic era**: its discoverer recognised the danger, and it happened anyway. The reader sees that the problem is not a new surprise.

- The passage says some infections in some places no longer respond to the usual antibiotics, not that penicillin cannot treat any infection.
- How Fleming discovered penicillin is not described.
- The passage does not discuss who deserves the Nobel Prize.
- No comparison with newer antibiotics is made. Paragraph five says almost no new classes have appeared.

So the story shows that the danger of bacterial resistance was recognised from the start of the antibiotic era.
