# Given

Paragraphs <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="5" /> **explain how groundwater** pumping compresses clay layers around sand and why subsidence slows gradually without rebounding.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded about what happens if groundwater pumping stops.

# Solution

> Picture squeezed clay like a flattened sponge that cannot spring back, stopping the pump slows the sinking gradually, not instantly.

The four statements test the mechanism in paragraph 2 from three sides: which layer compacts, whether compaction can recover, and how fast sinking stops.

Statement 1 can be concluded. Paragraph 2 says the ground keeps sinking after pumping is reduced, **"meski lajunya makin kecil"** (though more and more slowly), and clay **"nyaris tidak mengembang lagi walaupun muka air sumur kembali naik"** (hardly expands again even when the well water level rises). Paragraph 5 adds that **"ketinggian yang sudah hilang tidak akan kembali"** (height already lost won't come back). The rate shrinks, but lost height stays lost.

Statement 2 can't be concluded. The text says the opposite: clay hardly expands again. Only a small part of the sand's compaction can recover.

Statement 3 can't be concluded. The direction is right, because without sinking ground, relative sea level would in the end rise only with the sea, <InlineMath math="0.4" /> cm a year. The problem is the word "langsung" (straight away). Clay releases water **"dengan sangat lambat"** (very slowly), so the ground keeps sinking for years after pumping stops. All that time relative sea level at the port still rises faster than <InlineMath math="0.4" /> cm a year. The statement is **right in direction, wrong in timing**.

Statement 4 can't be concluded. It swaps the roles of the two layers. Water is pumped from the sand, but what compacts a lot is **the clay above and below it**. Sand **"hanya sedikit memampat"** (compacts only a little), so refilling the sand doesn't recover most of the sinking.

# Answer

**If pumping stops, sinking slows down step by step, and height already lost doesn't come back**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The rate shrinks, lost height doesn't come back | Ya |
| Clay expands again close to its original thickness | Tidak |
| Relative sea level drops straight away to the sea's rate alone | Tidak |
| Sinking comes mainly from sand and can be recovered | Tidak |
