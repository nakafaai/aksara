# Given

**Relative sea level** equals sea rise of <InlineMath math="0.4" /> cm a year plus ground sinking of <InlineMath math="6" /> cm a year at the port and <InlineMath math="1" /> cm a year on the east coast.

# Asked

Which statements are supported by the measurements and explanations in the passage.

# Solution

> Because the pier sinks with the ground, a tide gauge records rising sea and sinking land added together, not the ocean alone.

Every statement uses the rule from paragraph 1: relative sea level is **the sum of sea rise and ground sinking**. The figures are in paragraph 3: the sea rises <InlineMath math="0.4" /> cm a year, the port sinks <InlineMath math="6" /> cm a year, and the east coast sinks about <InlineMath math="1" /> cm a year.

Statement 1 is supported. Paragraph 3 says the tide station **"mencatat tinggi air terhadap dermaga"** (records the water height against the pier), and the pier **"ikut turun bersama tanah di bawahnya"** (sinks with the ground beneath it). So the station records both movements at once: <InlineMath math="6+0.4=6.4" /> cm a year.

Statement 2 isn't supported. The port and the east coast differ in two ways: the east coast stands on thinner clay, and most of its houses already have piped water. Paragraph 5 adds that the rate of sinking depends on **"tebal lempung di bawahnya"** (the thickness of the clay beneath) and on how much water is pumped nearby, including deep wells of factories and hotels. The text has no data on pumping per household, so the gap in rates **can't be pinned on every household**.

Statement 3 is supported. If the ground stopped sinking, only the sea rise would be left, <InlineMath math="0.4" /> cm a year. The water would still rise against the pier, but far more slowly than <InlineMath math="6.4" /> cm a year.

Statement 4 isn't supported. Work out the sea's share of the total on the east coast.

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Relative sea level | <InlineMath math="1+0.4=1.4" /> cm a year |
| The sea's share | <InlineMath math="0.4:1.4\approx29\%" /> |

The sea's share is only about <InlineMath math="29\%" />, less than half. The trap is comparing <InlineMath math="0.4" /> with <InlineMath math="1" /> and forgetting that the total is <InlineMath math="1.4" />.

# Answer

**Even on the east coast, most of the rise in relative sea level still comes from sinking ground**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The tide station records about <InlineMath math="6.4" /> cm a year | Ya |
| The gap in rates proves every household pumps more | Tidak |
| Without sinking ground, the tide still rises, but more slowly | Ya |
| On the east coast, the sea contributes more than half | Tidak |
