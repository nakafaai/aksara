The message comes from **the main character's change of heart**. Bu Asih arrives intending to resign because her pay was not raised. After a hard crossing, she finds pupils who swept the classroom and wrote that they had memorised the seven times table. Their welcome makes her teach with energy and put off resigning. The message: **the commitment of the people we serve** can renew our spirit even when the difficulties remain.

- The story does not warn against crossing. The brave crossing brings Bu Asih to her turning point.
- Pay is a real problem, but what keeps her in the story is not a pay rise.
- The captain and Pak Lamin are not compared for weather knowledge.
- The memorised table is part of the pupils' commitment, not a lesson that teachers love particular pupils.

So the story's message is that the commitment of the people we serve can renew our spirit in hard times.
