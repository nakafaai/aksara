# Given

**The play script** combines dialogue and stage directions involving Bu Warsih, Nia, Mak Rosnah, and Rahmat during an urgent patient referral.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded from both the dialogue and the stage directions.

# Solution

> In a play script, italic stage directions quietly reveal timing, gestures, and hidden tension that the spoken dialogue never states.

In a play script, many important details sit in the stage directions, the italic lines between the dialogue. Check each statement against the dialogue and the stage directions together.

The statement that Bu Warsih had decided to refer before Nia asked can be concluded. The scene opens at a quarter to eight, while the referral letter in the drawer is marked **"pukul tujuh kurang dua puluh"** (twenty to seven) and signed by Bu Warsih.

The statement that Bu Warsih doubts Nia's measurement can't be concluded. After checking herself, she says, **"Angkanya sama dengan angkamu"** (The numbers are the same as yours), then adds, **"Aku tidak pernah tidak percaya"** (I never didn't believe it). She checks again, but not out of doubt.

The statement about Mak Rosnah can be concluded. When Bu Warsih tells the story of Darmi, Mak Rosnah answers, **"Aku ada di mobil itu"** (I was in that car).

The statement that the ambulance was only called after Rahmat arrived can't be concluded. Udin's ambulance has been **"sudah menunggu di seberang jembatan sejak magrib"** (waiting across the bridge since sunset), long before Rahmat comes.

The statement that Bu Warsih is tense too can be concluded. At the start she tells Nia, **"Tanganmu gemetar"** (Your hands are shaking). At the end, sitting alone, her glass of tea **"bergetar sedikit di tangannya"** (shakes a little in her hand). The stage direction deliberately repeats the image of a shaking hand.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Bu Warsih had decided to refer before Nia asked | Ya |
| Bu Warsih doubts Nia's measurement | Tidak |
| Mak Rosnah knows the event from her own experience | Ya |
| The ambulance was only called after Rahmat arrived | Tidak |
| Bu Warsih is tense too, though she doesn't show it | Ya |
