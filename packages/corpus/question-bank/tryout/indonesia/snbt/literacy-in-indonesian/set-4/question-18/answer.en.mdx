Both texts share the same goal, saving regional languages, but differ in **how**. Text 1 proposes making regional-language lessons **compulsory** from primary to junior secondary school, plus one day a week in the local language. Text 2 rejects "satu aturan yang sama untuk semua sekolah" (one identical rule for every school) and proposes lessons **tailored to pupils' home languages**, such as interviewing grandparents in each child's own language.

- Text 2 opens by agreeing that regional languages should be saved.
- Text 1 does not reject Indonesian, and Text 2 does not treat Indonesian as the only language.
- Text 1 does not mention where schools are, while Text 2 speaks about a port city.
- Text 1 names parents' habits as the cause, while Text 2 does not blame parents.

So Text 1 proposes one firm requirement, while Text 2 wants lessons fitted to pupils' home languages.
