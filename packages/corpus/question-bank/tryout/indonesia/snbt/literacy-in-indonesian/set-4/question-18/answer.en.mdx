# Given

**The table lists** population and 2024 dengue cases for every subdistrict so raw case totals and per-capita rates can be compared.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded from comparing 2024 case counts and per-resident rates.

# Solution

> A crowded district can lead in total cases even while a smaller district carries a higher risk for each individual resident.

First work out each district's rate in 2024. Every statement turns on the difference between **the number of cases** and **the rate**.

| District | Cases 2024 | Rate per <InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> residents |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Kota Baru | <InlineMath math="200" /> | <InlineMath math="80" /> |
| Sukamulya | <InlineMath math="60" /> | <InlineMath math="120" /> |
| Karangtengah | <InlineMath math="90" /> | <InlineMath math="75" /> |
| Cibeureum | <InlineMath math="104" /> | <InlineMath math="130" /> |
| Pasirjaya | <InlineMath math="120" /> | <InlineMath math="60" /> |

The statement about Cibeureum can be concluded. Its rate, <InlineMath math="130" />, is the highest, though its <InlineMath math="104" /> cases are fewer than Kota Baru's and Pasirjaya's.

The statement that Pasirjaya's residents face more risk than Karangtengah's can't be concluded. Pasirjaya has more cases, but its rate is <InlineMath math="60" />, lower than Karangtengah's <InlineMath math="75" />. A resident's risk shows in the rate, not the number of cases.

The statement about the two districts with the most cases can be concluded. Kota Baru and Pasirjaya together recorded <InlineMath math="200+120=320" /> cases. Half of <InlineMath math="574" /> is <InlineMath math="287" />, and <InlineMath math="320" /> is more than that.

The statement that Kota Baru would still get the biggest share if the budget followed the rate can't be concluded. The highest rate is Cibeureum's, <InlineMath math="130" />. Kota Baru's <InlineMath math="80" /> is only third.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Cibeureum has the highest rate though not the most cases | Ya |
| Pasirjaya's residents face more risk than Karangtengah's | Tidak |
| The two districts with the most cases have more than half the cases | Ya |
| By rate, Kota Baru would still get the biggest share | Tidak |
