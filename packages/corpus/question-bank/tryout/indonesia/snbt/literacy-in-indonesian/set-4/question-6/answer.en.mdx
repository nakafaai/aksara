The remark is figurative. The key is **the referral letter**, and the door is **Sumi's trip to the city**. The question is who opens that door "dari dalam" (from inside).

Look at what Bu Warsih does before and after the remark.

- She wrote the referral letter at twenty to seven, and the ambulance has been waiting since sunset. So the means of referral are ready.
- She still waits for Rahmat, Sumi's husband, and deliberately tells a story first to Mak Rosnah, Sumi's mother-in-law.
- The referral only goes ahead after Mak Rosnah herself says, **"Bawa istrimu. Aku ikut"** (Take your wife. I'm coming along).

So "from inside" means from Sumi's family. The referral letter is only a means. The decision to go has to come from the family itself.

The other options are wrong.

- A hospital in the city isn't mentioned in the scene. This reading adds a party that isn't there.
- The reading about Nia learning to open the health post's door takes the word *door* literally.
- The drawer isn't locked. Nia opens it herself and finds the letter inside.
- Bu Warsih isn't in doubt. She says, **"Aku tidak pernah tidak percaya"** (I never didn't believe it), and the letter is complete and signed, not written just in case.

So the remark means the referral letter is only a means, while the decision to go has to come from Sumi's family itself.
