First unpack the figure, then look for the event that matches **the door**, not **the key**.

- **The key**. The referral letter. It's been ready since **"pukul tujuh kurang dua puluh"** (twenty to seven), but the letter alone doesn't get Sumi on her way.
- **The door opened from inside**. The decision to go has to come from **Sumi's own family**. That's why Bu Warsih tells Mak Rosnah about Darmi, who was **"diturunkan di tengah jalan"** (put out halfway) twelve years ago.

The door opens when Mak Rosnah, Sumi's mother-in-law, stands and says, **"Bawa istrimu. Aku ikut."** (Take your wife. I'm coming.) She decides before Rahmat can say a word. The family itself opens the way.

The other options point to other events.

- The waiting ambulance is Bu Warsih's preparation, part of the key and the means, not the family's decision.
- The letter in the drawer is **the key** itself.
- Handing the letter to Nia comes **after** the family decides. It carries out the decision rather than opening the door.
- Rahmat's arrival holds no decision yet. He hasn't even had a chance to speak.

So the best match is Mak Rosnah standing before Rahmat can speak and saying, "Bawa istrimu. Aku ikut."
