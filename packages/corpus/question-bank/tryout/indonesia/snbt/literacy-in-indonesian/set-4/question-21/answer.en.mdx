# Given

**The final two** paragraphs argue that Balai Pustaka's book distribution and its editorial filtering were two faces of one colonial policy.

# Asked

Whether each statement fits the author's main idea about Balai Pustaka.

# Solution

> The author treats spreading books and filtering manuscripts as two sides of one coin that must be weighed together, never in isolation.

The author's main idea is in the last two paragraphs: Balai Pustaka's two faces are **"bagian dari satu kebijakan"** (part of one policy), and it should be judged by **"bagaimana penyebaran dan penyaringan itu bekerja bersama"** (how spreading and filtering worked together). Test each statement against that idea.

- **Statement 1: matches**. The author writes that the government spread reading **"karena rakyat yang mulai melek huruf pasti akan membaca sesuatu, dan pemerintah ingin ikut menentukan apa yang mereka baca"** (because newly literate people would surely read something, and the government wanted a say in what they read).
- **Statement 2: doesn't match**. The author refuses to judge Balai Pustaka by its services alone. Judging it by its published novels is **"sama kelirunya"** (just as wrong) as judging it by rejected manuscripts.
- **Statement 3: matches**. The author describes both faces as real: cheap books and Taman Pustaka libraries in many schools, and the Nota Rinkes and the editing of *Salah Asuhan*. Both belong to one policy, neither disguises the other.
- **Statement 4: doesn't match**. Judging only by rejected manuscripts is also called wrong.
- **Statement 5: doesn't match**. The author never compares the quality of "wild reading" with Balai Pustaka's books.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The government wanted a say in what people read | Sesuai |
| Its services make the filtering beyond question | Tidak sesuai |
| Both faces are real | Sesuai |
| Best judged by rejected manuscripts | Tidak sesuai |
| "Wild reading" was better | Tidak sesuai |
