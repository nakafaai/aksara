The text introduces **two faces** of Balai Pustaka in the second and third paragraphs, and the fourth paragraph joins them: **"Padahal keduanya bagian dari satu kebijakan"** (Yet both are part of one policy). The last paragraph stresses that the question is how **"penyebaran dan penyaringan itu bekerja bersama"** (spreading and filtering worked together).

So the main idea is that spreading reading matter and filtering its content were two sides of one colonial policy.

The other options are wrong.

- Praising Balai Pustaka for its novels takes only the first face. The text calls judging it only by its list of novels mistaken.
- Calling Balai Pustaka only a censorship tool takes only the second face. The text says judging it only by rejected manuscripts is **"sama kelirunya"** (just as mistaken).
- The text doesn't compare the quality of "bacaan liar" with Balai Pustaka's books.
- In 1917 it was the reading commission that was changed, not native schools, and the text doesn't call those schools a failure.

So the main idea of the text is that spreading reading matter and filtering its content at Balai Pustaka were two sides of one colonial policy.
