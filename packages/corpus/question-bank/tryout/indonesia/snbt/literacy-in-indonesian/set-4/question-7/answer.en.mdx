# Given

When Nia urges a referral near eight, **Bu Warsih** has already written the referral letter timestamped twenty minutes to seven and stationed Udin's ambulance beyond the bridge since dusk. She waits for Rahmat and reminds Mak Rosnah of Darmi, who was "put off midway" twelve years earlier.

# Asked

Why Bu Warsih did not send Sumi to the city the instant Nia asked.

# Solution

Combine the **three closing clues** (the early letter, the waiting ambulance, and Darmi's story told to Mak Rosnah) to pinpoint the sole element still missing.

> Bu Warsih had already decided and prepared the referral, but waited for Sumi's own family to agree so the trip would not be aborted midway like Darmi's twelve years ago.

## Checking the five explanations against the script

Testing **each reason against dialogue clues** eliminates every misreading of Bu Warsih's actions.

| Explanation | Evidence in the script | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Already decided to refer Sumi, but waited for family consent so the trip would not stop midway | Fits the early letter, dusk ambulance, and Darmi's story | **Correct** |
| Doubted Nia's medical judgment | False, she says "I have never doubted you" | **Incorrect** |
| Waited for the rain to stop | False, the ambulance was placed beyond the bridge to beat the weather | **Incorrect** |
| Wanted to keep Sumi at the clinic | False, the referral letter was already ready | **Incorrect** |
| Left the decision to Nia | False, Bu Warsih quietly arranged every logistical step herself | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Bu Warsih did not send Sumi right away because **she had already decided to refer her, but was waiting for Sumi's own family to agree so the journey would not end midway like Darmi's once did**.
