The answer is spread over several clues that only come out at the end of the scene. Gather them first, then draw the conclusion.

- **The time on the referral letter**. The letter is marked **"pukul tujuh kurang dua puluh"** (twenty to seven), while Nia only asks for a referral close to eight. So Bu Warsih had decided to refer before Nia asked.
- **The ambulance**. Udin's ambulance has been **"sudah menunggu di seberang jembatan sejak magrib"** (waiting across the bridge since sunset). She has already dealt with the bridge.
- **Who she's waiting for**. She asks about Rahmat, Sumi's husband, then tells Mak Rosnah about Darmi, who was **"diturunkan di tengah jalan"** (set down halfway) twelve years ago. Mak Rosnah was in that car.

So what Bu Warsih is waiting for is the agreement of Sumi's own family. Darmi's journey ended halfway, and Bu Warsih doesn't want Sumi's to end the same way.

The other options are wrong.

- Bu Warsih doesn't doubt Nia. She says, **"Aku tidak pernah tidak percaya"** (I never didn't believe it), and the letter was written before Nia asked.
- Bu Warsih isn't waiting for the rain to stop. The ambulance was placed across the bridge on purpose so it wouldn't be cut off.
- Nothing suggests Bu Warsih wants Sumi to stay at the health post. The referral letter was ready from the start.
- Bu Warsih doesn't leave the decision to Nia. She made every preparation herself without telling Nia.

So Bu Warsih had already decided to refer Sumi but was waiting for Sumi's own family to agree, because a referral once ended halfway.
