Tin is a dull grey metal. A sky "berwarna timah" (the colour of tin) is a **grey, overcast** sky. The phrase is followed immediately by news of two-metre waves, a cancelled boat, and later rain at sea. It sets up **a hard day** from the very start of the story.

- No tin mine is mentioned. The phrase describes the colour of the sky, not the island's wealth.
- What the boat is made of is not discussed.
- It is a Monday and Bu Asih still teaches, so it is not a holiday.
- A clear sky contradicts the high waves and the rain that follow.

So the phrase describes overcast weather that signals a hard day.
