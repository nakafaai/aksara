The term **condensation** names one particular step, not the whole sequence. Match the processes observed in the box:

| Process in the box | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Heated liquid water becomes water vapour | Evaporation, liquid to gas |
| Water vapour forms droplets on the cold lid | Condensation, gas to liquid |
| Already formed droplets fall down | Change of location, the water remains liquid |

The definition connects an observable event with the natural process it represents. It does not add atmospheric conditions omitted by the lamp and ice.
