# Given

Throughout the scene, **Bu Warsih** appears calm, offers Nia tea, and speaks softly even though she prepared the referral and ambulance early. Once everyone leaves and she is alone, the stage direction notes **the tea glass trembles slightly in her hand**, echoing Nia's shaking hands earlier.

# Asked

The dramatic function of the closing stage direction about the trembling tea glass.

# Solution

Contrast **Bu Warsih's outward composure while others needed her** with **the slight tremor once she is alone** to see what the ending reveals about her inner state.

> The trembling tea glass reveals that Bu Warsih's steady demeanor throughout the scene was disciplined restraint rather than a lack of fear for Sumi.

## Checking the five interpretations of the stage direction

Evaluating **what causes the tremor** separates the true dramatic reveal from unsupported guesses.

| Interpretation | Fit with the full script | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reveals her calmness was deliberate self-control rather than lack of worry for Sumi | Fits early emergency preparations and trembling only when alone | **Correct** |
| Shows regret for keeping the letter in the drawer | False, she deliberately waited for family consent | **Incorrect** |
| Merely shows chill from the rain | False, it appears specifically once everyone exits | **Incorrect** |
| Shows anxiety because she distrusts Nia | False, she entrusts the escort directly to Nia | **Incorrect** |
| Shows she lost an argument | False, the referral unfolded exactly as she planned | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The stage direction **reveals that Bu Warsih's calm throughout the scene was self-restraint rather than a sign that she felt no anxiety about Sumi's condition**.
