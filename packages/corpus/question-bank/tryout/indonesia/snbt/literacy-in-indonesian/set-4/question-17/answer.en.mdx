# Given

**A news headline** highlights Sukamulya's <InlineMath math="100" /> percent jump in dengue cases from 2023 to 2024, while the table shows case counts and per-capita rates.

# Asked

Which response to the headline is most accurate and balanced according to the table and text.

# Solution

> A big percentage jump from a small base adds few raw cases, yet you still must check the rate per resident before dismissing the risk.

There are **two things** to check: how big the rise is, and Sukamulya's rate compared with the other subdistricts.

**Step 1. The size of the rise**. Sukamulya's cases went from <InlineMath math="30" /> to <InlineMath math="60" />. That's **<InlineMath math="100\%" />**, but only <InlineMath math="30" /> extra cases. A small starting number makes the percentage look big.

**Step 2. The 2024 rate per <InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> people**.

| Subdistrict | Working | Rate |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Cibeureum | <InlineMath math="104 : 80{,}000 \times 100{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="130" /> |
| Sukamulya | <InlineMath math="60 : 50{,}000 \times 100{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="120" /> |
| Kota Baru | <InlineMath math="200 : 250{,}000 \times 100{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="80" /> |
| Karangtengah | <InlineMath math="90 : 120{,}000 \times 100{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="75" /> |
| Pasirjaya | <InlineMath math="120 : 200{,}000 \times 100{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="60" /> |

Sukamulya comes **second**. So the percentage rise is misleading, but the risk to residents really is high.

The other options are wrong.

- Sukamulya's rate isn't the lowest. Pasirjaya's is.
- Kota Baru has more cases because it has far more people. The text says case counts show **workload**, not risk.
- The highest rate is Cibeureum's, <InlineMath math="130" />, not Sukamulya's.
- Unrecorded cases mean the true figure could be **higher**, not lower.

# Answer

So the best response is that the rise was only **<InlineMath math="30" />** extra cases, but Sukamulya's 2024 rate is among the highest, so the rise still deserves attention.
