A shared point must appear in **both texts**. Text 1 calls schools the "benteng terakhir" (last stronghold) for regional languages. Text 2 opens with "Saya setuju bahwa bahasa daerah perlu diselamatkan" (I agree that regional languages must be saved) and proposes classroom activities designed differently. Both agree that **regional languages must be saved** and that **schools can help**, even though they differ on how.

- Neither text proposes replacing Indonesian with regional languages.
- Text 1 proposes one day a week, and Text 2 rejects a uniform rule. No one proposes daily use.
- Text 1 names parents' habits as a cause but proposes no ban.
- Text 2 mentions nine home languages, but proposes that pupils share greetings from their own language, not learn nine languages at once.

So both texts agree that regional languages must be saved and that schools can play a part.
