Pay attention to **how far Text 2's objection goes** and to **the school levels** in Text 1.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text 1 names parents' habits as the cause | Ya (yes) | Parents choose to speak Indonesian to their children: "penyebabnya bukan larangan, melainkan kebiasaan" (the cause is not a ban but habit). |
| Text 2 rejects all regional-language lessons | Tidak (no) | Text 2: "Yang saya tolak bukan pelajaran bahasa daerah, melainkan satu aturan yang sama untuk semua sekolah" (what I reject is not regional-language lessons but one rule for every school). |
| Pupils speak nine languages at home | Ya (yes) | Text 2 says so directly. |
| Text 1 proposes lessons only in senior high school | Tidak (no) | Text 1 proposes a requirement from primary through junior secondary school. |

The second statement is often marked Ya because Text 2 objects. The writer rejects **a uniform rule**, not the lessons.
