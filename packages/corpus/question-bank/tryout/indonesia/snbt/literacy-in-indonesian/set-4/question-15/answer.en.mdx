The California example sits between two parts of Text B: the point about the district's few certified applicants, and the conclusion that **"Kelas kecil bermanfaat kalau diajar oleh guru yang layak"** (Small classes help if taught by qualified teachers).

The example shows what can happen when a teacher shortage is ignored.

- California cut class sizes **"hampir serentak"** (almost all at once).
- Schools competed for teachers, and many new teachers **"belum memiliki sertifikat mengajar penuh"** (didn't yet have full teaching certificates).
- The result was **"jauh lebih kecil daripada hasil Project STAR"** (far smaller than Project STAR's result).

So the example shows that the benefit of small classes can shrink when a policy is scaled up faster than qualified teachers become available.
