# Given

The **Balai Pustaka** passage closes by stating that judging Balai Pustaka solely by the novels it published is "just as flawed" as judging it solely by the manuscripts it rejected, because **distribution and filtering were two halves of a single policy working together**.

# Asked

The statement that uses the most similar reasoning pattern.

# Solution

Look for the statement that rejects **both one-sided yardsticks equally** because both are products of **one underlying selection process**.

> Judging a club solely by its trophies is just as flawed as judging it solely by the players it cut, because both result from how the club selects its squad.

## Comparing reasoning patterns across the options

Checking **symmetry of rejection and shared single process** identifies the exact parallel.

| Statement | Rejects both one-sided views equally | Credits one shared process | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Judging a club only by trophies is just as flawed as only by cut players, since both stem from how it selects players | Yes | Yes | **Correct** |
| Switches the yardstick to spectator counts | No | Introduces an unrelated metric | **Incorrect** |
| Treats one side as more accurate | No (favors one side) | No | **Incorrect** |
| Rejects making any judgment at all | Overreaches | Author proposes a fuller way to judge | **Incorrect** |
| Pairs trophies and stadium as unrelated to players | Not parallel | Not two halves of one process | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The closest parallel is that **judging a club solely by its trophies is just as flawed as judging it solely by the players it cut, because both come from how the club selects its players**.
