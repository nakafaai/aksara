The detail comes after the sentence **"Padahal keduanya bagian dari satu kebijakan"** (Yet both are part of one policy). So it's an example of **how** Balai Pustaka's two faces worked together.

Follow the line of thought.

- Low prices belong to the first face, spreading reading matter.
- What was made cheap was reading matter that had passed the filter, reading **"yang dianggap aman"** (considered safe).
- As a result, it was easier to get than reading **"yang dianggap berbahaya"** (considered dangerous). Spreading helped do the filtering.

So the detail shows that spreading cheap reading matter helped carry out the filtering.

- The text doesn't judge the quality of Balai Pustaka's books or say people preferred them for their quality.
- The text doesn't mention any loss to the colonial government.
- The text doesn't call low prices the only reason people became literate. *Only* goes too far.
- The text doesn't say "bacaan liar" was banned because of its price. The detail compares how easy the two kinds of reading were to get.

So the detail serves to show that spreading cheap reading matter helped carry out the filtering, because safe reading became easier to get.
