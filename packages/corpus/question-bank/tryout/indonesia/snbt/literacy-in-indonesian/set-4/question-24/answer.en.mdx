Balai Pustaka's pattern has **two elements working together**: reading matter is spread widely and made easy to get, and only content that passes a filter is spread. A matching case has to contain both.

**Free textbooks that print only manuscripts without criticism: fits**. The books reach villages, like the Taman Pustaka libraries, and the manuscripts are filtered so they don't criticise the government, like the Rinkes guidelines.

**An app that spreads writing for free but hides criticism of its owner: fits**. Free spreading and content filtering are run by the same party.

**A private publisher printing whatever sells: doesn't fit**. There's spreading, but no content filtering.

**A library buying every book: doesn't fit**. It spreads reading matter without choosing the content.

**A censorship office that only bans: doesn't fit**. There's filtering, but no spreading. The text stresses that Balai Pustaka wasn't just a censorship tool.

So the cases with the same pattern are the agency handing out free textbooks but printing only manuscripts without criticism, and the app spreading writing for free but hiding criticism of its owner.
