# Given

According to the passage, **Balai Pustaka** combined **two elements at once**: distributing reading material widely and affordably while filtering its content to keep out criticism of those in power.

# Asked

All cases that share this dual pattern of distribution plus content filtering.

# Solution

Test all five cases against **both simultaneous elements** (active distribution and content filtering by the same actor), rejecting cases that only distribute or only censor.

> Both the agency handing out free uncritical textbooks and the app spreading free articles while hiding criticism of its owners combine distribution and filtering in one hand.

## Checking both elements across all five cases

Testing **distribution and filtering side by side** isolates the two matching cases.

| Case | Actively distributes reading | Filters out criticism | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agency gives out free textbooks printing only manuscripts without criticism | Yes (free wide reach) | Yes (filters criticism) | **Matches** |
| App distributes free articles while hiding criticism of its owners | Yes (free reach) | Yes (hides criticism) | **Matches** |
| Private publisher prints whatever sells | Yes | No content filter | **Does not match** |
| Library buys every published book | Yes | No content filter | **Does not match** |
| Censor office that only bans books | No distribution | Yes | **Does not match** |

# Answer

The matching cases are **the agency distributing free textbooks that prints only manuscripts without criticism** and **the app distributing free articles while hiding criticism of its owners**.
