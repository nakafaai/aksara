Notice two actions that depart from her plan. That morning Bu Asih meant to hand in her resignation after returning from the island. In the afternoon she **does not tear up** the envelope, but she **does not hand it in** either. She folds it smaller and tucks it into the back page of the attendance book, a book she will keep using to teach. The act shows that her pupils' welcome made her **put off resigning**, while her disappointment about pay has not vanished, so she keeps the letter.

- The letter has not been handed in. She is still on the way home and holding the envelope.
- Tucking it into the back page keeps it away from her pupils' eyes.
- She takes the envelope out deliberately and folds it, so she has not forgotten it.
- The story mentions no other school on the island.

So the act shows that Bu Asih postpones resigning without fully giving up the possibility.
