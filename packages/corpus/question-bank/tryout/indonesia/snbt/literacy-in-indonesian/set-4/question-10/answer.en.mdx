# Given

At the doorway, **Nia** says, "Next time, tell me first, Ma'am," and **Bu Warsih** answers, "Next time, you tell the story." Earlier Nia was stunned to find the letter already written in the drawer, while Bu Warsih's story to Mak Rosnah was what won the family's consent.

# Asked

All interpretations of this doorway exchange supported by the script.

# Solution

Read **each speaker's line against prior events** to isolate the two supported interpretations without reading in rejections or apologies that never occur.

> Nia voices her objection to being kept out of the plan, while Bu Warsih invites Nia to take on the work of persuading families alongside her clinical measurements.

## Checking the five interpretations against the script

Matching **each option to dialogue clues** separates the two supported readings from three unsupported claims.

| Interpretation | Script evidence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nia objects to being left out of Bu Warsih's plan | Echoes her earlier "Why didn't you say so?" upon seeing the letter | **Supported** |
| Bu Warsih wants Nia to persuade families too in the future | "You tell the story" passes on the exact role that moved Mak Rosnah | **Supported** |
| Bu Warsih refuses to share her plans | False, she gives Nia a larger role rather than refusing | **Unsupported** |
| Nia regrets pressing Bu Warsih | False, Nia asks to be informed rather than apologizing | **Unsupported** |
| Clinical instruments matter less than storytelling | False, Bu Warsih still tells her "Bring your measuring gear" | **Unsupported** |

# Answer

The script supports that **Nia objects to being left out of Bu Warsih's plan** and that **Bu Warsih wants Nia in the future to persuade patients' families as well, not only take clinical measurements**.
