The line continues "hujan memukul atap seng" (rain beats on the tin roof). The word **seperti** (like) marks a comparison: the sound of rain is likened to **thousands of impatient fingers knocking**. "Thousands" conveys how many drops there are, and "impatient" conveys knocking that is fast and constant. The line pictures loud, relentless rain.

- There is no visitor in the poem. The knocking is only the image used for comparison.
- The children are asleep in the dry corner, and the line is about the rain, not the children.
- Drops falling into the basins are described in stanza two as "ting, tung, ting", not as thousands of fingers.
- Wind and a door blowing open are not mentioned.

So the line pictures loud, relentless rain on the tin roof.
