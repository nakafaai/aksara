# Given

In **Ciranda**, rice output fell from <InlineMath math="220" /> thousand tons in 2022 to <InlineMath math="150" /> thousand tons in 2023 (a drop of <InlineMath math="70" /> thousand tons). Harvested area shrank from <InlineMath math="40" /> to <InlineMath math="30" /> thousand ha, at a 2022 yield of <InlineMath math="220 : 40 = 5.5" /> tons/ha.

# Asked

The percentage of Ciranda's total output drop coming from smaller harvested area, valued at the 2022 yield per hectare.

# Solution

Find **total output loss**, calculate the **output lost from <InlineMath math="10" /> thousand fewer hectares** at 2022 yield, and divide by total loss.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{total output drop} &= 220 - 150 = 70\text{ thousand tons} \\
\text{area-loss component} &= (40 - 30) \times 5.5 = 55\text{ thousand tons}
\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\frac{55}{70} \approx 79\%" />
</MathContainer>

> Out of <InlineMath math="70" /> thousand tons lost, <InlineMath math="55" /> thousand tons (about <InlineMath math="79\%" />) come from shrinking harvested area, leaving only <InlineMath math="15" /> thousand tons from lower yield per hectare.

## Tracing the distractor percentages

Checking **what each ratio measures** separates the exact area share from the distractors.

| Option | Ratio | What it measures | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| About <InlineMath math="79\%" /> | <InlineMath math="55 : 70" /> | Share of output drop due to smaller area | **Correct** |
| About <InlineMath math="21\%" /> | <InlineMath math="15 : 70" /> | Share due to lower yield per hectare | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="25\%" /> | <InlineMath math="10 : 40" /> | Percentage drop in harvested area itself | **Incorrect** |
| About <InlineMath math="31.8\%" /> | <InlineMath math="70 : 220" /> | Percentage drop in total output | **Incorrect** |
| About <InlineMath math="9\%" /> | <InlineMath math="0.5 : 5.5" /> | Percentage drop in yield per hectare | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

About **<InlineMath math="79\%" />** of Ciranda's production decline came from shrinking harvested area.
