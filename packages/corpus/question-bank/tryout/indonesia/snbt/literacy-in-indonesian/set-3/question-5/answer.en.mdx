Find one rebuttal from the text for **each** assumption.

- **"Protection starts as soon as release begins"**. The text says Wolbachia mosquitoes first have to become the majority: **"Perlindungan bagi warga baru dapat diharapkan setelah proporsi itu tinggi."** (Protection for residents can only be expected once that share is high.)
- **"Wolbachia can replace clearing breeding sites"**. The text ends by saying that question **"tidak diuji oleh rancangan itu"** (wasn't tested by that design).

Every other option is wrong in one of its parts.

- Wolbachia doesn't wipe out local mosquitoes. Mosquitoes are **"sengaja dilepas"** (deliberately released) and take over through mating.
- The text doesn't **prove** both methods must be used together. It only says replacement wasn't tested.
- The text says efficacy is more likely **too low**, not too high.
- Once they're the majority, Wolbachia mosquitoes can **"bertahan tanpa pelepasan ulang"** (persist without re-release).

So the right pair of rebuttals is that protection can only be expected once the share of Wolbachia mosquitoes is high, and replacing breeding-site clearance wasn't tested by the AWED design.
