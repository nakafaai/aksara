Two things need linking: Warsito's **excuse** and the **last-minute routine** described earlier.

- **The excuse**. "Jam terminal itu kecepatan" (the terminal clock is fast) isn't a serious reason. Warsito still waits until **"pukul delapan lewat dua belas"** (twelve past eight).
- **The last-minute routine**. **"Biasanya, pada menit terakhir, seorang nenek berkerudung biru naik"** (Usually, at the last minute, a grandmother in a blue headscarf boards) and sits in seat three. That night, **"pukul delapan tepat, kursi nomor tiga kosong"** (at eight sharp, seat three is empty), and Warsito goes to the stall and comes back with peanuts left unopened.

So the delay makes room for the old routine, waiting for the grandmother who always boarded last, even though her seat stays empty.

Four options share the same excuse, so the reason decides.

- The story mentions no passengers still buying tickets. Only eleven seats were sold that night.
- If Warsito truly believed the terminal clock was fast, the bus would leave by the trusted clock. The story never mentions a watch of Warsito's.
- Ardi's request about Pemalang isn't tied to the delay, and Warsito stops there anyway.
- The story says nothing about wanting to arrive when the roads are quiet.

So the best-supported reading is that Warsito is making an excuse to delay, because part of Warsito is still waiting for the grandmother in the blue headscarf who usually boarded at the last minute.
