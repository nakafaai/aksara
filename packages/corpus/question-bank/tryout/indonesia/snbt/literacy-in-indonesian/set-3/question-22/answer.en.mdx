# Given

**The Java railway** history text lists construction dates for the Semarang line, the first train run, and the <InlineMath math="1{,}435" /> mm and <InlineMath math="1{,}067" /> mm gauges.

# Asked

Which statements follow from the dates and track-gauge numbers in the text.

# Solution

> Check start years, completion years, and gauge differences one by one so earlier and later railway lines never blur together.

Each statement needs one step: **a calculation** or **matching it to the right sentence in the text**.

- **Statement 1: Ya (Yes)**. The first stone was laid in June 1864 and trains started running in August 1867. That's **three years and two months**.
- **Statement 2: Tidak (No)**. The first train ran in 1867, but the line only reached Surakarta and Yogyakarta **"pada 1870-an"** (in the 1870s).
- **Statement 3: Ya**. <InlineMath math="1{,}435-1{,}067=368" /> mm, which is <InlineMath math="36.8" /> cm, **more than** <InlineMath math="35" /> cm.
- **Statement 4: Tidak**. The Semarang line used <InlineMath math="1{,}435" /> mm. The <InlineMath math="1{,}067" /> mm gauge was used on lines built **later** by the government-owned company.
- **Statement 5: Ya**. Trains on lines of different gauges **"tidak dapat saling menyambung, dan barang harus dipindahkan di stasiun pertemuan"** (couldn't connect, and goods had to be moved at junction stations).

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Construction took more than three years | Ya |
| The line had reached Surakarta when the first train ran | Tidak |
| The gauge gap is more than <InlineMath math="35" /> cm | Ya |
| The <InlineMath math="1{,}067" /> mm gauge was first used on the Semarang line | Tidak |
| Goods had to be moved to another train | Ya |
