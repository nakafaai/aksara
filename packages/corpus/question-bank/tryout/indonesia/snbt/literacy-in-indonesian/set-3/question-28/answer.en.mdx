A judgement sentence carries **the reviewer's view** of how good or bad the work is, while a summary sentence only reports content or technique. The words **patut dipuji** (deserves praise) express a judgement, so "Dialog dalam novel ini juga patut dipuji" (the dialogue in this novel also deserves praise) is the judgement sentence.

- "Separuh mata air itu kini kering" (half the springs are now dry) reports a situation in the story.
- The sentence about Sinta returning and inheriting the map summarises the plot.
- The sentence about alternating present-day chapters with the diary describes technique without judging it. The judgement comes in another sentence, "Kekuatan terbesar novel ini terletak pada strukturnya" (the novel's greatest strength is its structure).
- The sentence about Sinta investigating the dry springs also summarises the plot.

So the sentence containing the reviewer's judgement is "Dialog dalam novel ini juga patut dipuji."
