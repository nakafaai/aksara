Read this figure of speech in two steps: first understand the picture, then match it to Text B.

**The picture**. A pedestrian falls into a hole that was **"sengaja ditutup daun"** (deliberately covered with leaves). The hole isn't missing, it's hidden. Blaming the pedestrian for "not seeing it" forgets whoever covered it.

**Text B**. The loan costs are listed, but **"tersebar di beberapa halaman dan baru terlihat lengkap setelah pinjaman disetujui"** (spread over several pages and only complete after the loan is approved). That's the hole covered with leaves. The key words in the figure are **"seluruh masalah"** (the whole problem). B doesn't clear residents completely, since B still calls education useful. B only refuses to put **all** the blame on residents.

The other options are wrong.

- The first option flips the figure. The pedestrian in it is the victim, not someone careless.
- B doesn't tell residents to stop borrowing online. B proposes rules and oversight.
- B doesn't say the costs are **hidden entirely**. The figures are listed, just spread out. B also doesn't clear borrowers of all responsibility.
- B says education is still useful. The figure doesn't reject education.

So the figure means the blame can't be put entirely on residents, because the loan costs are presented in a way that makes them hard to see.
