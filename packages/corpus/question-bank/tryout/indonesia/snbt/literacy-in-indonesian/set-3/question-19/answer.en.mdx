# Given

**The text and** table record irrigation coverage, soil and canal differences, rice varieties, and 2022-2023 output across the three districts.

# Asked

Which statements follow from the text and table without jumping to unrecorded causes or motives.

# Solution

> Numbers in a table tell you what happened, not automatically which single cause or human emotion drove it.

Two statements name a cause or an intention, and two need a calculation. The table only shows **what happened**, not **why**.

The statement that technical irrigation made Sukamaju more resistant can't be concluded. Sukamaju was the least affected, but the researcher points out that the three districts also differ in **"jenis tanah, umur saluran irigasi, dan kebiasaan petani memilih varietas padi"** (soil type, the age of the irrigation channels, and farmers' habits in choosing rice varieties). Those other differences haven't been ruled out, so the cause isn't settled.

The statement about the biggest percentage fall can be concluded. Banyulor, with the narrowest technical irrigation at <InlineMath math="20\%" />, fell <InlineMath math="58:150\approx38.7\%" />, more than Ciranda at about <InlineMath math="31.8\%" /> and Sukamaju at <InlineMath math="7\%" />. This statement only describes the data and names no cause.

The statement about Banyulor farmers' reason can't be concluded. The text says some fields weren't planted **"karena air tidak cukup"** (because there wasn't enough water), but it never mentions farmers being afraid. Farmers' intentions or feelings aren't recorded in the table.

The statement about Sukamaju's 2022 production can't be concluded. Combined production in 2022 was <InlineMath math="670" /> thousand tonnes across all three districts.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="300 + 220 + 150 = 670" />
<BlockMath math="\frac{300}{670} \approx 44.8\% < 50\%" />
</MathContainer>

Sukamaju is less than half. Careful: in **2023** the share is <InlineMath math="279:521\approx53.6\%" />, more than half, but this statement is about 2022.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Irrigation made Sukamaju more resistant | Tidak |
| The biggest percentage fall was in the least irrigated district | Ya |
| Banyulor farmers planted less out of fear | Tidak |
| Sukamaju's 2022 production was more than half the total | Tidak |
