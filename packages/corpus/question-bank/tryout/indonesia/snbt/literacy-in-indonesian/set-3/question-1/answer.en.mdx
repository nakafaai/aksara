Each option has **three parts**. The right one has to be correct in all three, and every other option is wrong in just one.

- **Strength of the design**. The author rejects the quiet-year objection because **"yang dibandingkan adalah kedua kelompok pada periode yang sama"** (what's compared is the two groups over the same period). What's compared is also the **share of fever patients who tested positive**, not a case count.
- **Direction of the error**. Mosquitoes crossing into comparison clusters shrink the gap, so efficacy is **"lebih mungkin terlalu rendah daripada terlalu tinggi"** (more likely too low than too high).
- **Limits of the conclusion**. One city's result **"belum tentu terulang di kota lain"** (won't necessarily repeat elsewhere), and replacing breeding-site clearance **"tidak diuji oleh rancangan itu"** (wasn't tested by that design).

Check the wrong part in each other option.

- Efficacy is more likely **too low**, not too high.
- The trial didn't just count cases. The author calls comparing shares more careful than counting cases.
- The trial didn't prove Wolbachia can replace breeding-site clearance.
- A before-and-after comparison is exactly the kind of study that's **vulnerable** to a quiet year. AWED compared two groups over the same period.

So the right summary is that the design is strong because it compares two groups over the same period, the efficacy figure is more likely too low, and the result won't necessarily hold in other cities.
