Check each statement against the story and pay attention to **the reasons the characters give**.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passengers fell after the bus corridor opened | Benar (true) | Since the bus corridor opened, passengers shrank from twenty to one. |
| Mak Inah avoids the bus because the fare is higher | Salah (false) | Her reasons are the distant stop and drivers who will not wait for old people. Fares are never mentioned. |
| Pak Damar asks for the fare on the last day | Salah (false) | Pak Damar says "Hari ini gratis, Mak" (today it's free). |
| The nearest stop is about two hundred metres from the market | Benar (true) | Pak Damar says the nearest stop is at the end of the road, two hundred metres away. |

The second statement tempts readers who assume people avoid buses because of cost. The story names **distance and baskets** instead.
