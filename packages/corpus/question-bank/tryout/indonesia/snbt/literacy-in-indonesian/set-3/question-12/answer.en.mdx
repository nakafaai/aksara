# Solution

Compute **total borrowing cost** (repayment minus cash received), then divide by the **Rp<InlineMath math="900{,}000" /> actually received**.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{total cost} = 1{,}120{,}000 - 900{,}000 = 220{,}000" />
<BlockMath math="\text{cost percentage} = \frac{220{,}000}{900{,}000} \approx 24.4\%" />
</MathContainer>

> Use cash actually received (Rp<InlineMath math="900{,}000" />) as the base and full cost (Rp<InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> fee plus Rp<InlineMath math="120{,}000" /> interest) as the numerator.
