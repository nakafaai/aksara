The detail sits in the second paragraph, which opens with **"Arah jalurnya memberi petunjuk"** (The direction of the line gives a clue). So the detail about Tanggung is used as a clue to answer the question in the first paragraph: **modernisation for whom?**

Follow the line of thought.

- Tanggung isn't a big town, so it isn't the real destination.
- The line was heading for Surakarta and Yogyakarta, areas producing **"gula, kopi, dan tembakau dalam jumlah besar"** (sugar, coffee and tobacco in large amounts).
- The other end is Semarang, the port where produce was loaded onto ships.

The author's conclusion comes at the end of the paragraph: the line **"mengikuti arah barang yang diekspor, bukan arah perjalanan penduduk"** (followed the direction of exported goods, not the direction people travelled). The text's last sentence repeats it: who benefited most **"sudah tampak dari arah relnya"** (is already visible in the direction of the rails).

The other options are wrong.

- The port in the text is Semarang, not Tanggung. Tanggung is described as not a big town.
- The text doesn't say the line failed. The rails were extended to Surakarta and Yogyakarta.
- Tanggung's residents aren't named as the main winners. The text says the biggest gains went to plantation owners and trading companies.
- The line was built by a private company, and the text mentions no palace.

So the author gives that detail to show the line was designed to reach areas producing export goods, so the direction of the rails reveals whom it served.
