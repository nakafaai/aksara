A main idea must cover **the whole passage**. Paragraph one invites a careful reading of the pledge. Paragraph two examines the verb *menjunjung*. Paragraph three explains why a Malay-based language was chosen. Paragraphs four and five discuss the name *bahasa Indonesia* and its meaning as something shared. Together they show how **word choice and naming** made Indonesian a shared language.

- The place and participants of the congress are only background in paragraph one.
- Javanese was not chosen for other reasons. Its large number of speakers is not named as the reason it was passed over.
- The Yamin and Tabrani debate concerned the language's name, not the congress venue.
- The 1945 Constitution appears only at the end as a later step, not as a result of the 1926 debate.

So the main idea is that word choice and naming in the 1928 pledge made Indonesian a language belonging to everyone.
