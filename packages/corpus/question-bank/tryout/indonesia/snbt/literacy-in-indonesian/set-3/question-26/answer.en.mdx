The detail comes right before the sentence **"Angka itu tetap menarik tebakan mereka"** (The number still pulled their guesses). The word *tetap* (still) marks something that happens even though there's a reason it shouldn't.

Follow the line of thought.

- If participants thought the number on the wheel hinted at the answer, it would be natural for their guesses to move towards it. The effect wouldn't be surprising.
- Participants watched the wheel spin, so they took the number to be random and to carry no information.
- Their guesses were still pulled towards it. So the anchor's effect can't be explained by people treating the number as a hint.

The other options are wrong.

- The text doesn't say participants guessed at random. Their guesses shifted in an orderly way with the number on the wheel.
- The text says the wheel experiment **"tidak dapat membedakan keduanya"** (can't tell the two apart), so it doesn't prove either explanation.
- The wheel was rigged, but to split participants between two numbers, not to steer the result. Taking the number to be random doesn't confirm the rigging.
- The gap of about twenty points is the experiment's result. Randomness of the number doesn't explain its size.

So the author gives that detail to rule out the idea that the anchor works only because people treat it as a hint about the answer.
