Each option has **two parts**: the purpose of the remark and what remains unanswered.

- **The purpose**. If participants knew the researchers picked the number, they might treat it as a **hint** to the answer. Because they **thought the number was random**, its pull can't be explained that way. The text says, **"Angka itu tetap menarik tebakan mereka."** (The number still pulled their guesses.)
- **What's unanswered**. Both explanations **"meramalkan arah pengaruh yang sama, sehingga percobaan roda angka tidak dapat membedakan keduanya"** (predict the same direction of effect, so the wheel experiment can't tell them apart).

Every other option is wrong in one part.

- Participants aren't described as guessing at random. Their guesses were pulled steadily toward the anchor, from a median of <InlineMath math="25\%" /> to <InlineMath math="45\%" />.
- The experiment doesn't prove which explanation is right.
- The wheel was indeed rigged, but "mengira angkanya acak" (thought the number was random) stresses what **participants believed**, not how the wheel was set.
- The direction of the effect is clear: a higher number gave higher guesses. What's unanswered is **which explanation** is right.

So the remark shows that the anchor still had an effect even though it wasn't taken as a hint, and the experiment still can't tell the two explanations of anchoring apart.
