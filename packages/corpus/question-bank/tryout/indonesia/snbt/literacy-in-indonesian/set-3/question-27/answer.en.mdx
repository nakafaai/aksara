# Given

**The price-anchoring table** gives mean and median willingness to pay for three student groups, with the third group hearing no anchor.

# Asked

Which statements match the group comparison data in the table.

# Solution

> Use the no-anchor group as your baseline, and remember that a median describes the middle split rather than every single student.

The third group heard no number, so it's the comparison group. Work out each group's difference from the third group.

| Measure | First group | Second group |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Difference in mean | <InlineMath math="62{,}000-78{,}000=-16{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="118{,}000-78{,}000=40{,}000" /> |
| Difference in median | <InlineMath math="60{,}000-75{,}000=-15{,}000" /> | <InlineMath math="100{,}000-75{,}000=25{,}000" /> |

The statement that the second group's rise is bigger than the first group's fall fits. Its mean rose by Rp<InlineMath math="40{,}000" />, while the first group's fell by Rp<InlineMath math="16{,}000" />.

The statement that most students in the second group would pay more than Rp<InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> doesn't fit. The second group's median is exactly Rp<InlineMath math="100{,}000" />. The median is the middle value, so at least half the students wrote Rp<InlineMath math="100{,}000" /> or less. The higher mean, Rp<InlineMath math="118{,}000" />, shows some students wrote very high prices, not that most wrote above Rp<InlineMath math="100{,}000" />.

The statement that the gap between the second and third groups is bigger by the mean fits. The gap in means is Rp<InlineMath math="40{,}000" />, the gap in medians Rp<InlineMath math="25{,}000" />.

The statement that Rp<InlineMath math="160{,}000" /> raised every student's price by Rp<InlineMath math="40{,}000" /> doesn't fit. Random assignment does let the gap between groups be put down to the anchor, but that gap is a gap in **group averages**. The data don't show every student rising by the same amount. The median gap of only Rp<InlineMath math="25{,}000" /> shows the rise was uneven.

The statement about the first group's median fits. The difference is <InlineMath math="75{,}000-60{,}000=15{,}000" />.
