Paragraph four states Tabrani's reason directly: if the nation and the homeland are called Indonesia, the language **should also be named Indonesian**. His reasoning is about matching names: the language of unity takes the same name as the nation and homeland it is meant to unite.

- Having the most speakers would be a reason to choose Javanese, and Tabrani does not discuss numbers of speakers.
- Complex speech levels belong to Javanese according to paragraph three, not to Malay.
- Tabrani proposed the name in 1926 and it was accepted in 1928, long before independence.
- There is no proposal to abolish regional languages, and paragraph two shows the opposite.

So Tabrani's reasoning is that the language's name should match the name of the nation and its homeland.
