# Given

**The passage explains** the gauge mismatch between the Semarang and government lines and how seasons affected produce transport.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded from the details on track gauges and produce transport.

# Solution

> Watch timelines and absolute words like only or still today so an overly strong statement never slips past.

Each statement needs matching with the exact sentence. Some set a trap with the order of events or a word that's too strong.

- **Statement 1: Ya (Yes)**. Trains on lines with different gauges **"tidak dapat saling menyambung"** (couldn't connect), and goods had to be moved at junction stations.
- **Statement 2: Tidak (No)**. The wide gauge on the first line was **"kemudian diganti agar seragam dengan jaringan lainnya"** (later replaced to match the rest of the network).
- **Statement 3: Ya**. Carrying goods by cart and boat **"bergantung pada musim, karena jalan tanah berubah menjadi lumpur pada musim hujan"** (depended on the season, because dirt roads turned to mud in the rainy season).
- **Statement 4: Tidak**. The first line's gauge was **different** from the government network. Matching came later, by replacing that wide gauge.
- **Statement 5: Tidak**. The trains **"segera mengangkut penumpang"** (soon carried passengers), including small traders and workers. "Only" doesn't fit the text.
