# Given

For years **Warsito** dismissed the old driver's advice ("Passengers don't buy speed, To. They buy arriving") as a slow man's excuse. On this final night his needle never passes eighty, past Pekalongan **Ardi** stops glancing at his watch, yet upon reaching Pulogebang Ardi still remarks that being forty minutes late at the shuttle company could dock his pay.

# Asked

Warsito's attitude toward the old driver's advice that night and its effect on Ardi during and at the end of the trip.

# Solution

Verify that the chosen option gets **all three beats right**: Warsito's adoption of the advice, Ardi's calm on the highway, and Ardi's lingering shuttle-company mindset at arrival.

> Warsito now lives by the old driver's advice and calms Ardi's watch-checking on the road, even though at the terminal Ardi still measures lateness by his new shuttle job's pay rules.

## Checking all three beats across the options

Comparing **Warsito's change and Ardi's mid-trip versus terminal reactions** leaves one complete match.

| Option | Warsito's stance | Ardi on the road | Ardi at the end | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warsito accepts the advice, Ardi stops checking his watch, yet still uses shuttle-job standards at arrival | Exact | Exact | Exact | **Correct** |
| Ardi fully converts to the old advice | Exact | Exact | Wrong (still cites pay cuts) | **Incorrect** |
| Warsito still rejects the advice due to an aging engine | Wrong (deliberate pace) | - | - | **Incorrect** |
| Ardi decides not to take the shuttle job | Exact | - | Wrong (still going) | **Incorrect** |
| Follows company speed rules while Ardi keeps checking his watch | Wrong (unmentioned) | Wrong (he stops) | - | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

**Warsito now embraces the old driver's advice, which leads Ardi to stop checking his watch along the way, even though at the end Ardi still judges lateness by his shuttle company's standards**.
