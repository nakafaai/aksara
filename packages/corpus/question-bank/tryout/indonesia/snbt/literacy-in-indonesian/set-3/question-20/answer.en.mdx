# Given

The **agriculture official** sees combined rice output across the three districts drop by over one-fifth (from <InlineMath math="670" /> to <InlineMath math="521" /> thousand tons, down <InlineMath math="22.2\%" />) and concludes that El Nino cut **yield per hectare** by about one-fifth. Yet combined harvested area also fell from <InlineMath math="120" /> to <InlineMath math="95" /> thousand ha.

# Asked

The main flaw in the agriculture official's reasoning.

# Solution

Compare the **<InlineMath math="22.2\%" /> total output drop** against the **combined yield per hectare** across both years to see which two measures were conflated.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{2022 yield} &= \frac{670}{120} \approx 5.58\text{ tons/ha} \\
\text{2023 yield} &= \frac{521}{95} \approx 5.48\text{ tons/ha}
\end{aligned}" />

> Combined yield per hectare fell by only about <InlineMath math="2\%" />, so the official's core flaw is equating total production decline with yield-per-hectare decline when shrinking harvested area drove most of the loss.

## Checking the five proposed flaws

Verifying **each critique against the three-district table** rules out all four distractors.

| Proposed flaw | Match with data | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equates falling total output with falling yield per hectare even though shrinking harvested area drove most of the drop | Pinpoints the exact conflated measure | **Correct** |
| Miscalculated that total output fell over one-fifth | False, <InlineMath math="(670 - 521)/670 \approx 22.2\%" /> exceeds <InlineMath math="20\%" /> | **Incorrect** |
| Questions whether 2023 was an El Nino year | False, the passage defines 2023 as an El Nino year | **Incorrect** |
| Yield per hectare rose in every district | False, it rose only in Sukamaju and fell in Ciranda and Banyulor | **Incorrect** |
| Needs five years of data | False, the flaw is mixing up two metrics | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The main flaw is **equating the drop in total production with a drop in yield per hectare, when most of the decline actually came from shrinking harvested area**.
