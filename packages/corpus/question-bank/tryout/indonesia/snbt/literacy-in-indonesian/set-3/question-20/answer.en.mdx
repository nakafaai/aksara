<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

Combined yield per hectare fell only about <InlineMath math="2\%" />. Production fell much more because harvested area shrank from <InlineMath math="120" /> to <InlineMath math="95" /> thousand ha. In Ciranda alone, about <InlineMath math="79\%" /> of the fall in production came from area.

The other options are wrong.

- Combined production did fall by more than a fifth, about <InlineMath math="22.2\%" />. The official didn't miscalculate this part.
- The official is wrong precisely about yield per hectare. And the text doesn't doubt that 2023 was an El Nino year.
- The fourth option's first part is right, but its second part goes too far. Yield per hectare rose only in Sukamaju. In Ciranda and Banyulor it fell.
- The text sets no five-year condition. The problem is a swapped measure, not the length of the data.

So the official's main weakness is treating the fall in total production as a fall in yield per hectare, when most of the fall came from shrinking harvested area.
