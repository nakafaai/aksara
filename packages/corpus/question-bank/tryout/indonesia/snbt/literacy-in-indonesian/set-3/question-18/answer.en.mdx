# Given

**The table gives** 2022 and 2023 harvested area and rice production figures for Sukamaju, Ciranda, and Banyulor districts.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded through calculation and direct comparison of the table figures.

# Solution

> Never mix up percentage drop with absolute hectares lost, compute each exact figure before judging a statement.

Each statement needs one calculation or one comparison, and three of them set traps with limiting words.

The statement about Sukamaju's yield can be concluded. In 2022 the yield was <InlineMath math="300:50=6" /> tonnes per ha, and in 2023 it was <InlineMath math="279:45=6.2" /> tonnes per ha. Yield rose even though production fell.

The statement about combined production can't be concluded.

<BlockMath math="\frac{670-521}{670}=\frac{149}{670}\approx22.2\%" />

The fall is more than a fifth but **less than a quarter**.

The statement that the most irrigated district lost no harvested area can't be concluded. Sukamaju, with <InlineMath math="80\%" /> technical irrigation, still lost harvested area, from <InlineMath math="50" /> to <InlineMath math="45" /> thousand ha.

The statement about Banyulor and Ciranda's area can't be concluded because of the words **in thousand hectares**. Banyulor fell <InlineMath math="30-20=10" /> thousand ha, and Ciranda fell <InlineMath math="40-30=10" /> thousand ha. They're equal. Banyulor is only bigger in percent, <InlineMath math="33\%" /> against <InlineMath math="25\%" />.

| --- | --- |
