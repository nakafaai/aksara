A shared point must appear in **both texts**. Text 1 mentions guesthouses, tripled weaving sales, and young people returning as guides or guesthouse managers. Text 2 also admits that guesthouses and weaving "memberi penghasilan yang dulu tidak ada" (bring income that did not exist before) and that the writer is glad to see young people come home. Both acknowledge **new income and returning young people**.

- Holding the ceremony more often is Text 1's plan, which Text 2 rejects.
- Limiting visitors is proposed only by Text 2.
- Parking near the traditional house is Text 1's plan, while Text 2 wants parking moved outside the village.
- Text 2 still proposes performances for tourists, so it does not ban every cultural activity.

So both texts acknowledge that tourism brings new income and has brought some young people home.
