**Three things** have to be right at once.

- **The advice being echoed**. The old driver once said, **"Penumpang tidak beli cepat, To. Mereka beli sampai."** (Passengers don't buy speed, To. They buy arriving.) Warsito's line also talks about the road and passengers.
- **What's changed**. The old driver was talking on a night bus on the **north coast road**. Warsito talks about **the toll road**, the road Ardi will likely drive in the new travel job.
- **What's kept**. The core of the old advice is that passengers buy **reaching their destination**, not speed. The words **"tetap orang"** (still people) keep that core: passengers must be looked after.

Every other option is wrong in one part.

- What's kept isn't speed. The old advice rejects the idea that passengers buy speed.
- Bu Tarmi and Warsito **"tidak membicarakan apa pun selain harga cabai"** (talk about nothing but chilli prices). The line doesn't echo anything Bu Tarmi said.
- Care for passengers isn't what changes. That's exactly what's kept, while the road changes.
- Punctuality isn't the core of the old advice. That night the bus even arrives **"telat empat puluh menit"** (forty minutes late).

So the line echoes the old driver's advice. What changes is the road, from the north coast road to the toll road, and what's kept is care for passengers.
