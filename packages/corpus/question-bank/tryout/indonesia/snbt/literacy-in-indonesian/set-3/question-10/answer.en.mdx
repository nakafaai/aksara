# Given

At the bus door, **Warsito's** parting line to Ardi is, "The toll road is straight, Di. But your passengers are still people." It echoes the old driver's Pantura-road wisdom ("Passengers don't buy speed, To. They buy arriving") for Ardi's new shuttle job on the toll road.

# Asked

The advice being echoed, what changes, and what stays the same in Warsito's closing line.

# Solution

Match **all three elements of the echo**: its source (the old driver), the changed setting (Pantura highway to toll road), and the constant principle (care for passengers).

> Warsito echoes the old driver's wisdom, adapting the road from Pantura to the toll road while preserving the core principle of caring for passengers as people.

## Checking all three parts across the options

Testing **source, changed element, and preserved element** isolates the exact answer.

| Option | Advice echoed | What changes | What stays | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old driver's advice, road shifts from Pantura to toll road, care for passengers stays | Exact | Exact | Exact | **Correct** |
| Speed is what stays | Exact | Exact | Wrong (advice rejects speed) | **Incorrect** |
| Echoes Bu Tarmi's words | Wrong (they only discussed chili prices) | - | - | **Incorrect** |
| Care for passengers is what changes | Exact | Reversed | Reversed | **Incorrect** |
| Punctuality is what stays | Exact | Exact | Wrong (the bus arrived <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes late) | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The line **echoes the old driver's advice: the road changes from the Pantura highway to the toll road, while care for the passengers is what stays the same**.
