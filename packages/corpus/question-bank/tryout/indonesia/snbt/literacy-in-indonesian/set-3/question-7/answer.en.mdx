Paragraph two says sugar in drinks "tidak membuat kenyang seperti makanan sehingga orang jarang mengurangi porsi makan setelah minum minuman manis" (does not fill you up like food, so people rarely eat less after a sweet drink). The word **sehingga** (so) shows the consequence: sugar from drinks is **added** to what people eat rather than replacing some of it. That is why it is seen as more of a problem.

- Tooth decay is not discussed in the passage.
- The passage does not mention artificial sweeteners. It is about sugar.
- Drink prices compared with food are not discussed.
- Packaging labels are not mentioned. The passage only says sugar enters the body "tanpa disadari" (unnoticed).

So sugar in drinks is seen as more of a problem because it does not fill people up, and they rarely eat less as a result.
