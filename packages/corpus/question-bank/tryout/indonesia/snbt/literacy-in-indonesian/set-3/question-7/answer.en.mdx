The statement about the old driver's advice can be concluded. The story says, **"Bertahun-tahun Warsito menganggap kalimat itu dalih orang yang sudah lamban"** (For years Warsito took that line as the excuse of a man who had grown slow).

The statement that the bus arrived early can't be concluded. Warsito **turns the bus into Bu Tarmi's restaurant** in Pemalang, and Ardi says the bus is **"telat empat puluh menit"** (forty minutes late).
