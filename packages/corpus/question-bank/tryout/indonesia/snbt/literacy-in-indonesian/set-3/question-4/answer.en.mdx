The author shows two sides. Awareness of the carbon store is treated as a good motive, but the author writes "**Sayangnya, tidak semua berhasil**" (unfortunately, not all succeed), then gives examples: seedlings planted on mudflats that are too low, and dense planting of a single species. The last paragraph still calls planting useful, provided **the site and species suit** the local tides. That is support for restoration combined with criticism of poor planting.

- The author does not reject all planting and says it remains useful where no parent trees are left.
- The support is not unconditional, because the author criticises programmes that ignore site conditions.
- The long explanation of why programmes fail shows that the author cares about their success.
- The author does not rank the two roles. Paragraph one explains how mangrove roots protect against erosion.

So the author supports mangrove restoration but is critical of planting that ignores whether site and species are suitable.
