Match each statement to the time details in the poet's note.

The statement that the poem was finished after the conversation can be concluded. The conversation happened **"beberapa bulan sebelum puisi ini selesai"** (a few months before the poem was finished).

The statement that the tide time shifts by several hours in a week can be concluded. It **"bergeser hampir satu jam setiap hari"** (shifts almost an hour every day), so over seven days the shift adds up to several hours.

The statement about a tide table can't be concluded. The note says **"Ayah tidak pernah memakai tabel pasang"** (my father never used a tide table), and the first stanza has him reading the sky and the smell of mud.

The statement that the poet understood at once can't be concluded. The poet **"butuh waktu lama untuk memahami jawaban ayah"** (took a long time to understand the father's answer).

The statement that the poem was written before the father stopped fishing can't be concluded. It was written **"setahun setelah ayah saya berhenti melaut"** (a year after my father stopped fishing).
