The clues are spread through the story. When asked, Dimas smiles but **does not look at Wulan**, a sign he is hiding something. When asked to read, **his finger moves under the words but his mouth stays silent**, and what he says is not written on the page. He describes **the picture** instead of reading the text. So he cannot yet read well and hides it with a book whose pictures he understands.

- No school contest is mentioned.
- If he had read every other book, one page would not defeat him.
- Wulan forbids nothing. She adds more books.
- No wish to become a fisherman is mentioned.

So Dimas keeps borrowing the book because he cannot yet read well and hides it behind a book whose pictures he understands.
