Find the text's explanation for two things: why the rumah gadang flexes and why stiff buildings break.

- **Posts on flat stones**. **"Tiang dapat bergeser sedikit di atas batu sandi"** (the posts can shift a little on the flat stones). This statement is true.
- **Pegged joints**. **"Sambungan pasak dapat bergerak sedikit tanpa patah"** (pegged joints can move a little without breaking). This statement is true.
- **Stiff buildings**. **"Bangunan yang kaku justru menahan seluruh guncangan sampai bagian terlemahnya patah"** (a stiff building takes the whole shaking until its weakest part breaks). This statement is true.

The other two are wrong.

- The steep roof helps rain run off. The text stresses flexibility is **"bukan"** (not) from the roof.
- The pegs do need to be **"rapat"** (tight), but tight here means not loose, not locking the house still. Pegged joints **"dapat bergerak sedikit"** (can move a little).

So the true statements are posts that can shift on flat stones, pegged joints that move without breaking, and stiff buildings that take the whole shaking until their weakest part breaks.
