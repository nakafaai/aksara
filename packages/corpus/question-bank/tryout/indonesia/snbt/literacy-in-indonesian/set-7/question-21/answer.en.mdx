# Given

In the **rumah gadang** passage, paragraphs one and two introduce the roof and matrilineal tradition, paragraph three explains **how flexibility works** (posts on foundation stones and pegged joints), paragraph four explains **its limits** (rot, loose pegs, concrete footings), and the closing paragraph unites both: flexibility comes from "how posts and joints are assembled, and that design demands upkeep."

# Asked

The statement that best serves as the main idea of the entire passage.

# Solution

Because all five statements are factually true, choose the one whose **scope unites both the structural source of flexibility and its maintenance/construction limits**, rather than a single paragraph's detail.

> The main idea is that rumah gadang flexes during earthquakes thanks to how its posts and joints are assembled, yet that flexibility requires upkeep and the original construction method.

## Comparing the scope of all five true statements

Checking **overall paragraph coverage** separates the umbrella thesis from narrow details.

| Statement | Coverage of the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rumah gadang flexes in earthquakes from how its posts and joints are built, yet requires upkeep and authentic construction | Unites flexibility source, structural limits, and conclusion | **Correct** |
| Curved roof and gonjong spires | Opening hook from paragraph one only | **Too narrow** |
| Minangkabau matrilineal heritage | Paragraph two background only | **Too narrow** |
| Foundation stones and wooden pegs | Paragraph three mechanism without its limits | **Too narrow** |
| The earthquake-resistant label must be read carefully | Opens paragraph four without naming cause or conditions | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The main idea is that **rumah gadang flexes during earthquakes because of how its posts and joints are arranged, yet that flexibility demands ongoing maintenance and authentic construction**.
