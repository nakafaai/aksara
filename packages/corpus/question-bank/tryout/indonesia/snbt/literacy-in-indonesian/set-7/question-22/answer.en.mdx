The statement that a rumah gadang is never damaged by earthquakes can't be concluded. The text says rotten pillars or loose pegs make the house **"sama rentannya dengan bangunan lain"** (as vulnerable as other buildings).

The statement that the gonjong curve is meant for flexibility in earthquakes can't be concluded. The steep roof is said to shed rain, and the last paragraph stresses that what makes the house flexible is **"bukan atapnya yang indah"** (not its beautiful roof).

The statement that houses on concrete foundations shift more easily can't be concluded. Pillars cast into concrete can't shift the way pillars on base stones can. The text says such houses **"sudah berbeda"** (already differ) in how they stand.
