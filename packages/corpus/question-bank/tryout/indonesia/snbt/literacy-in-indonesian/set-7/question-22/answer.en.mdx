# Given

**The passage describes** the flexible post-on-stone frame of rumah gadang, the gonjong roof, modern cast-concrete posts, and family room customs.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded about rumah gadang's earthquake resistance and traditions.

# Solution

> Two houses that look identical outside can behave very differently in a quake because flexibility lives in the joints below, not the roof shape.

Some statements trap you by treating a house's shape as the same as how it stands.

The statement about new rumah gadang can be concluded. Flexibility comes from posts on flat stones and pegged joints. New houses with posts **"dicor ke fondasi beton dan disambung dengan paku"** (cast into concrete foundations and joined with nails) stand in a way that is **"sudah berbeda"** (already different).

The statement that stiffer buildings are less likely to break can't be concluded. The text says the opposite: **"Bangunan yang kaku justru menahan seluruh guncangan sampai bagian terlemahnya patah"** (a stiff building takes the whole shaking until its weakest part breaks).

The statement about the gonjong can't be concluded. The steep roof helps **"air hujan ... cepat mengalir turun"** (rainwater run off quickly), and the text stresses flexibility is **"bukan"** (not) from the roof.

The statement about two houses of the same shape can be concluded. A house with rotten posts or loose pegs is **"sama rentannya dengan bangunan lain"** (as vulnerable as other buildings), and a new house can share the shape but stand another way.

The statement about sons' rooms can't be concluded. Rooms are **"disediakan untuk anak perempuan yang sudah menikah"** (kept for married daughters), while sons growing up **"biasanya tidur di surau"** (usually sleep at the prayer house).

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| New houses with cast posts lose the flexible design | Ya |
| Stiffer buildings are less likely to break | Tidak |
| The gonjong is mainly for flexibility | Tidak |
| Same-shaped houses can differ in quake resistance | Ya |
| Married sons get a room | Tidak |
