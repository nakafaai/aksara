Check **the creator, timing, and coverage of each source** before using it to answer a historical question.

| Source | Origin and timing | Question it can answer | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Five posters | Organisers, before the exhibition | What image was intended across five cities? | Do not record every visitor's experience |
| 240 guest-book entries | Voluntary writers, after visiting, two cities | What experiences did these writers report? | Not shown to represent all visitors |

Posters help establish the official message. Guest books preserve particular experiences. Their different functions do not disappear simply because both concern the same exhibition.
