# Given

**The passage traces** bat echolocation from Spallanzani onward, sound echo timing, the barbastelle bat, and moth defenses.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded by connecting the relevant parts of the passage.

# Solution

> Link matching details across paragraphs carefully and never stretch a trait of one rare species to most animals in the wild.

Some statements need two parts of the text linked.

The statement about Spallanzani can be concluded. His experiment was **"Pada akhir abad ke-18"** (at the end of the 18th century), while bat sounds were first recorded **"pada akhir 1930-an"** (in the late 1930s). That's more than a hundred years apart.

The statement that bat calls don't bounce off something as small as a mosquito can't be concluded. Insect hunters pick high pitch, and high pitch **"memantul jelas dari benda sekecil nyamuk"** (bounces clearly off something as small as a mosquito).

The statement about a shorter gap can be concluded. The text says **"selang antara panggilan dan gema menunjukkan jarak"** (the gap between call and echo shows distance). An echo that comes back sooner comes from something closer.

The statement that the barbastelle detects from farther away can't be concluded. By **"berbisik"** (whispering), it **"hanya dapat mendeteksi mangsa dari jarak dekat"** (can only detect prey at close range).

The statement about moths in the wild can't be concluded. The text ends by warning that **"satu spesies di satu ruang terbang belum memberi tahu seberapa sering ngengat lain memakai taktik serupa di alam"** (one species in one flight room doesn't tell how often other moths use the tactic in the wild).
