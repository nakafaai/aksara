Paragraph two sets out the sequence. Fibre **passes through the stomach and small intestine undigested**. In the large intestine, bacteria **ferment** it and produce **short-chain fatty acids**. One of them, butyrate, is the main energy source for the cells lining the large intestine.

- Sugar is not mentioned, and the stomach does not process this fibre.
- The fibre passes through the small intestine without breaking down.
- Bacteria turn the fibre into fatty acids, so it does not leave unchanged.
- The passage does not say fibre is stored as a reserve.

So gut bacteria ferment the fibre into fatty acids that give energy to the cells of the large intestine.
