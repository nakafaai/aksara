Notice **how far** each text accepts or rejects recordings.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recordings help working students | Ya (yes) | Text 1 says students can study at a convenient time, "termasuk mahasiswa yang bekerja paruh waktu" (including students who work part-time). |
| Text 2 rejects recordings entirely | Tidak (no) | Text 2 wants recordings as a "pelengkap" (supplement), so it does not reject them. |
| Students put off watching recordings | Ya (yes) | Text 2 says so directly. |
| Text 1 abolishes class activity | Tidak (no) | Text 1 proposes using class time "untuk diskusi dan latihan soal" (for discussion and practice problems). |

The second and fourth statements both overstate. The texts disagree about the **role** of recordings, not about abolishing either form entirely.
