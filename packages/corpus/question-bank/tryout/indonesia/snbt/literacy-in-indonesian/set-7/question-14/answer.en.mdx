# Given

**Text B cites** a 2011 study showing one cotton bag's warming impact equals roughly <InlineMath math="131" /> single-use thin plastic bags.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded from Text B's discussion of the cotton-bag study.

# Solution

> Treat <InlineMath math="131" /> uses as the break-even line, below <InlineMath math="131" /> uses the cotton bag has a larger footprint, and above <InlineMath math="131" /> uses it comes out ahead.

The key is the figure <InlineMath math="131" /> times. By the study, one cotton bag's global warming impact **equals about <InlineMath math="131" /> thin bags** each used once. A cotton bag is only better if used more than <InlineMath math="131" /> times.

The statement about a cotton bag used <InlineMath math="50" /> times can be concluded. It replaces <InlineMath math="50" /> thin bags, but its impact equals about <InlineMath math="131" />. So its impact is still bigger.

The statement that cotton bags are always worse can't be concluded. *Always* contradicts the study itself. After more than <InlineMath math="131" /> uses, its global warming impact is lower.

The statement that the study can't be used to judge ocean pollution can be concluded. Text B says the study **"hanya menghitung dampak pemanasan global"** (counts only global warming impact), and bags escaping into rivers and the sea are **"tidak ikut dihitung"** (not counted).

The statement that Text B wants the levy scrapped can't be concluded. Text B says, **"Saya tidak menolak cukai"** (I'm not against the levy). What it asks for is measuring the weight of plastic.

The statement about a cotton bag used <InlineMath math="200" /> times can't be concluded. <InlineMath math="200" /> is more than <InlineMath math="131" />, so the bag has a **smaller** impact than <InlineMath math="200" /> thin bags.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| A cotton bag used <InlineMath math="50" /> times still has a bigger impact | Ya |
| Cotton bags are always worse | Tidak |
| The 2011 study doesn't judge ocean pollution | Ya |
| Text B wants the levy scrapped | Tidak |
| A cotton bag used <InlineMath math="200" /> times has a bigger impact | Tidak |
