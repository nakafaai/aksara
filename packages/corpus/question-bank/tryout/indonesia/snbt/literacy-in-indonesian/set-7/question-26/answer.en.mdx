The question asks for the **share of the five-minute memory** that was lost, not the difference in percentage points. So the difference has to be divided by the five-minute figure.

| Group | Difference | Share lost |

| Read four times | <InlineMath math="83-40=43" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{43}{83}\approx52\%" /> |

So the group that read four times lost about half its memory, while the group that recalled three times lost only about a seventh.
