# Given

**Text B** states it does not doubt the <InlineMath math="80\%" /> drop in thin bags and does not oppose the levy, yet asks the city to **measure total plastic weight** before calling the policy a success. Suppose measurement finds **total plastic weight** from thin bags and trash bags **did not change** from 2022 to 2023.

# Asked

The conclusion that best aligns with both texts given unchanged total plastic weight.

# Solution

Keep apart **the undisputed <InlineMath math="80\%" /> bag-count drop** and **Text A's success claim judged by Text B's plastic-weight standard (unproven)**.

> Thin bag counts still dropped <InlineMath math="80\%" />, yet Text A's claim of policy success is unproven under the total-weight yardstick demanded by Text B.
