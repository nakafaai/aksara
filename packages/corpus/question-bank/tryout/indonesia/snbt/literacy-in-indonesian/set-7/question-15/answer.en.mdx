Compare the table's columns carefully, and remember that panels work only by day.

| Statement | Answer | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q and R have the same capacity and output | Salah (false) | Their capacity is the same (3 kWp), but Q produced 345 kWh and R 330 kWh. |
| S bought no electricity at all | Salah (false) | House S still bought 150 kWh from the grid. |
| Largest capacity, most output | Benar (true) | House S (4 kWp) produced 470 kWh, the most. |
| S produced more than it used | Benar (true) | 470 kWh is more than 380 kWh. |

The second statement traps readers who only compare monthly totals. Although S's panels produced more than it used, that power came **by day**, while the house also used electricity **at night**.
