# Given

Stanzas one through three recall childhood by the coast ("When I was small"). Stanza four opens with **"Now I live in a city without a sea"** and stresses that the phone clock is "accurate to the second, yet never tells when the boat should be pushed into the water," directly preceding Father's closing reply.

# Asked

The structural role of stanza four in the poem as a whole.

# Solution

Notice **stanza four's two structural jobs**: pivoting from childhood memory to present city life, and exposing the phone clock's blind spot that Father's final words answer.

> Stanza four pivots from memory to present city life while setting up Father's closing line by showing that an exact clock cannot teach when to act.

## Checking the five candidate roles

Verifying **how stanza four bridges early stanzas and the ending** rules out all four distractors.

| Candidate role | Fit with poem structure | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transitions from memory to city life and sets up Father's ending by showing an exact clock cannot tell when to act | Accurately explains both pivot and setup | **Correct** |
| Closes the poem by praising phone accuracy | False, neither the ending nor praise of clocks | **Incorrect** |
| Shows the speaker already reads life's tides well | False, Father says the speaker has not learned yet | **Incorrect** |
| Repeats stanza three with no shift in setting | False, shifts time and place to the present city | **Incorrect** |
| Explains Father quit sailing because tides stopped | False, he quit because "his knees rusted" | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Stanza four **bridges childhood memory and present city life while setting up Father's closing words by showing that an accurate clock does not tell you when to act**.
