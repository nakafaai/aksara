Look at where the stanza sits in the poem, then at what it sets up.

- **Turning point**. Stanzas one to three are memories, **"Waktu aku kecil"** (when I was small). Stanza four opens with **"Kini aku tinggal di kota"** (now I live in a city). This is where the poem moves to the present.
- **What it sets up**. The phone clock is **"tepat sampai detik"** (accurate to the second) yet doesn't say when to act. That's exactly the gap the father answers at the end: the speaker **"belum belajar membacanya"** (hasn't learned to read it yet).

The other options are wrong.

- Stanza four doesn't close the poem, and it shows what the phone clock lacks.
- The turning point is right, but the speaker can't yet read life's tide. The father says so.
- Stanza four doesn't repeat stanza three. It moves the poem to the city and the present.
- The father stopped fishing **"sejak lututnya berkarat"** (since his knees rusted), not because the tides stopped.

So stanza four is the turn from memory to city life, and it sets up the father's last words by showing that an exact clock doesn't teach when to act.
