The fourth stanza has to be read with the first. In the first stanza the father reads the sea **"seperti membaca jam dinding / yang jarumnya tidak pernah kami lihat"** (like reading a wall clock / whose hands we never saw). He has no visible clock but knows when the water will come.

The fourth stanza turns this around. The speaker has a clock that's **"tepat sampai detik"** (accurate to the second), but it doesn't say **"kapan perahu harus didorong ke air"** (when the boat should be pushed into the water), that is, when to act. The two stanzas are a pair: one knows the time without a clock, the other has a clock without knowing the time. The father answers that pair in the last stanza.

So the stanza shows that measuring time precisely isn't the same as knowing when to act.
