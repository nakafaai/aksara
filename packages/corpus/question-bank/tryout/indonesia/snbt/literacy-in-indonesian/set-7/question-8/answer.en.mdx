Paragraph two says a wider road makes private driving **feel easier**, "sehingga makin banyak orang memakainya" (so more people use it), and the road jams again. That chain only makes sense if the author assumes **easier driving draws more people into cars**. Without that assumption, a wider road would stay clear.

- The author says sidewalks are "kerap" (often) sacrificed, not always.
- A growing population could also refill the road, so the argument does not need to assume no growth.
- Motorcycle taxis are not discussed in paragraph two.
- The author does not suggest leaving roads unchanged.

So the reason rests on the assumption that easier driving leads more people to use private vehicles.
