Useful extra data has to answer one of Text B's objections. There are three: number is measured instead of weight, the data come only from supermarkets, and they cover just one year.

**Total weight of plastic before and after the levy: helps**. This is exactly what Text B asks for: **"mengukur berat plastik, bukan sekadar jumlah kantong"** (measure the weight of plastic, not just the number of bags).

**Number of bags at traditional markets: helps**. Text B says Text A's data **"hanya mencakup supermarket"** (cover only supermarkets). If bags at markets rose, the fall at supermarkets wouldn't describe the whole city.

**Data for several years after 2023: helps**. Text B says the data cover **"baru satu tahun"** (only one year). Long-term data show whether the new habit lasts.

**Number of supermarket managers who agree: doesn't help**. Managers' opinions don't show how much plastic is actually used.

**Price of cloth bags: doesn't help**. Price doesn't show how many times a bag is used, which is what decides its impact by the 2011 study.

So the helpful data are total plastic weight before and after the levy, the number of bags at traditional markets, and data for several years after 2023.
