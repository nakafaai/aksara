Useful extra data has to answer one of Text B's objections. Text B raises several, and three matter here: number is measured instead of weight, the data come only from supermarkets, and they cover just one year.

**Total weight of plastic before and after the levy: helps**. This is exactly what Text B asks for: **"mengukur berat plastik, bukan sekadar jumlah kantong"** (measure the weight of plastic, not just the number of bags).

**Plastic weight at traditional markets: helps**. Text B says Text A's data **"hanya mencakup supermarket"** (cover only supermarkets). This data is also measured by weight, as Text B asks. If plastic at markets rose, the fall at supermarkets wouldn't describe the whole city.

**Plastic weight for several years after 2023: helps**. Text B says the data cover **"baru satu tahun"** (only one year). Long-term data, also measured by weight, show whether the new habit lasts.

**Number of supermarket managers who agree: doesn't help**. Managers' opinions don't show how much plastic is actually used.

**Price of cloth bags: doesn't help**. Price doesn't show how many times a bag is used, which is what decides its impact by the 2011 study.

So the helpful data are total plastic weight before and after the levy, plastic weight at traditional markets, and plastic weight for several years after 2023.
