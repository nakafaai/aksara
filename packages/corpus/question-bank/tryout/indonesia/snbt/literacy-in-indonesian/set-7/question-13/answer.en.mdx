The ending must be read against what came before. For months Dimas **clung to one book** because he could not read and understood only its pictures. After **three months of learning** with Wulan, he chooses a different book for the first time. That change shows he is now able enough, and brave enough, to read a book he does not yet know.

- An octopus is also a sea animal, so Dimas did not switch to land animals.
- Dimas returned the whale book himself. Wulan did not withdraw it.
- The story does not compare children's liking for octopuses.
- After three months of learning, choosing a new book marks progress, not pretending.

So the ending most likely shows that Dimas can now read, and dares to read, a book he does not yet know.
