# Given

In the plastic-bag levy debate, **Text B** raises three concrete objections against Text A's data: it measures **bag count** instead of **total plastic weight**, covers **supermarkets only** (excluding traditional markets), and spans **just one year**.

# Asked

All additional data that would help address at least one of Text B's objections.

# Solution

Match each dataset directly against **Text B's three specific gaps** (plastic weight, traditional-market coverage, and multi-year persistence).

> Total plastic weight before and after the levy, plastic weight in traditional markets, and plastic weight across several years after 2023 each directly answer one of Text B's objections.

## Checking all five datasets against the objections of Text B

Mapping **each dataset to Text B's stated criteria** isolates the three useful datasets.

| Additional dataset | Text B objection addressed | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total plastic weight before and after the levy | Directly meets demand to measure weight rather than bag count | **Helps** |
| Plastic weight in traditional markets | Closes the supermarkets-only coverage gap | **Helps** |
| Plastic weight over several years after 2023 | Closes the one-year-only time gap | **Helps** |
| Number of supermarket managers backing the levy | Opinion count does not measure actual plastic use | **Does not help** |
| Price of cloth bags | Price does not show how many times cloth bags are reused | **Does not help** |

# Answer

The helpful additional datasets are **total plastic weight before and after the levy**, **plastic weight in traditional markets**, and **plastic weight over several years after 2023**.
