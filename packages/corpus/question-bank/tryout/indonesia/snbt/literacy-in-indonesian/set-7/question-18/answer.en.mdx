Text 1 proposes that lectures be **watched at home as recordings**, with class time used for discussion and practice. In other words, recordings **replace** the lecture in class. Text 2 ends by saying recordings should be **a supplement to in-person lectures, not a replacement**. That is where they disagree.

- Exams appear in Text 2 only as the time students watch recordings, not as a point of debate.
- Neither text asks lecturers to talk longer.
- Scholarships are not discussed. Working students are mentioned as people recordings help.
- Text 1 proposes discussion in class, and Text 2 does not dispute it.

So the texts differ on whether recordings should replace the lecture in class or only supplement it.
