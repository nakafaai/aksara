# Given

A developer markets a new rumah-gadang-shaped house whose **posts are cast in concrete footings and joined with nails** as "earthquake-resistant like an old rumah gadang." The passage notes of such houses: **"The shape stays rumah gadang, yet the way it stands is already different."**

# Asked

The most accurate evaluation of the developer's advertisement based on the passage.

# Solution

Contrast **the copied outer appearance (roof/shape)** with **the missing structural mechanism (foundation stones and wooden pegs)** emphasized in paragraph four.

> The ad misleads because the new house imitates the outer look of a rumah gadang while abandoning the construction method that makes traditional ones flexible.

## Checking the five evaluations of the ad

Verifying **each option against paragraph four's exact claim** eliminates every exaggerated or false assessment.

| Evaluation | Fit with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Misleading because it copies the outer shape without using the construction method that gives old rumah gadang flexibility | Directly matches paragraph four | **Correct** |
| Accurate because the roof shape is identical | False, flexibility does not come from the roof | **Incorrect** |
| Concrete-foundation homes are guaranteed to collapse | Overstates text, which only says they stand differently | **Incorrect** |
| Wood is stronger than concrete | Text discusses flexibility, not material strength | **Incorrect** |
| Original rumah gadang are always just as vulnerable | False, only when posts rot or pegs loosen | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The advertisement is **misleading because the house imitates the shape of a rumah gadang without using the construction method that makes old rumah gadang flexible**.
