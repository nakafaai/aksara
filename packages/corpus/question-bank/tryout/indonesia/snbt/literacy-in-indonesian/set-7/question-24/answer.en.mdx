Compare the ad's claim with the text's account of where the flexibility comes from.

- **Source of flexibility**. The old rumah gadang flexes because its posts are **"diletakkan di atas batu datar yang disebut batu sandi"** (set on flat stones) and its joints are **"dikunci dengan pasak kayu, bukan paku"** (locked with wooden pegs, not nails).
- **The new house**. The text mentions new houses whose posts are **"dicor ke fondasi beton dan disambung dengan paku"** (cast into concrete foundations and joined with nails). **"Bentuknya tetap rumah gadang, tetapi cara berdirinya sudah berbeda"** (the shape is still a rumah gadang, but the way it stands is different). So the ad borrows the shape without what made the old house flex.

The other options are wrong.

- The text stresses flexibility is **"bukan"** (not) from the roof, so the same shape isn't enough.
- The text never says houses on concrete foundations will surely collapse. It only says they stand differently.
- The text doesn't compare the strength of concrete and wood. It discusses flexibility, not material strength.
- An original rumah gadang is **"sama rentannya"** (just as vulnerable) only if its posts are rotten or its pegs loose, not always.

So the ad is misleading because the house copies the rumah gadang's shape without the way of standing that made the old ones flex.
