The flexibility principle in the text has two elements.

- **A base that allows shifting**. Pillars rest on base stones, so they **"dapat bergeser sedikit"** (can shift a little).
- **Joints that can move without breaking**. Pegs make joints that **"dapat bergerak sedikit tanpa patah"** (can move a little without breaking).

A light timber house with joints that can move a little, standing on supports that allow a little shifting, meets both elements.

The other options are wrong.

- A concrete house built not to move at all is a rigid building, which by the text **"menahan seluruh guncangan sampai bagian terlemahnya patah"** (takes the whole shaking until its weakest part breaks).
- A gonjong roof and wood-coloured paint only copy the look. The text stresses that what makes the house flexible is **"bukan atapnya"** (not its roof).
- Pillars set into the ground and nailed joints remove both elements of flexibility at once.
- Higher ground addresses standing water, not earthquake shaking.

So the most suitable design is a light timber house whose joints can move a little, standing on supports that allow a little shifting.
