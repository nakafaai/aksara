Pak Hasan's objection to composting is **about time and effort**: "Siapa yang mau repot memilah sampah? Saya kerja dari pagi sampai magrib." (Who wants the trouble of sorting rubbish? I work from morning until sunset.) Once Dina offers that she and her friends will **collect the food-waste buckets every afternoon**, the objection disappears. He says, "Kalau anak-anak yang mengambil... ya sudah, ember saya taruh di depan pagar." (If the kids collect it... fine, I'll put my bucket by the fence.)

- Pak Karto does not scold him. He only says "Sebentar" (wait a moment) and gives his count.
- Bu Ning rejects his proposal but does not blame him for her child's cough.
- The truck schedule does not change at the meeting.
- Payment is not discussed.

So Pak Hasan changes his mind because of Dina's offer to collect food waste with her friends every afternoon.
