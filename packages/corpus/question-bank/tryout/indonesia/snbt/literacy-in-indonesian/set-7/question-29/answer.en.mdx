A fitting method has to meet two conditions: **an effort to recall without looking at the material**, and **feedback** to check the answers.

**Closing the notes, writing what you remember, then checking: fits**. Writing without the notes is recall practice, and checking against the notes is feedback.

**Doing practice questions, then checking the wrong ones: fits**. Practice questions force recall, and checking wrong answers stops mistaken answers being **"ikut teringat"** (remembered along with the rest).

**Explaining to a friend without notes, then checking what was missed: fits**. Explaining without notes also pulls the memory back, and checking the notes afterwards gives feedback.

**Highlighting while rereading: doesn't fit**. It's still rereading. There's no effort to recall without looking.

**Rereading four times the night before the exam: doesn't fit**. This is the read-four-times group, which forgot the most after a week.

So the methods that apply the finding are writing what you remember then checking, doing practice questions then checking the wrong ones, and explaining to a friend without notes then checking what was missed.
