# Given

The study's findings and advice establish **two conditions for effective study**: practicing **retrieval without looking at the material** and pairing it with **feedback** (checking answers so errors are not reinforced).

# Asked

All study habits that apply the passage's findings including its advice on feedback.

# Solution

Test all five habits against **both criteria together** (unaided recall plus answer-checking feedback) to separate three effective habits from two passive rereading habits.

> Writing from memory then checking notes, doing practice problems then reviewing mistakes, and explaining without notes then checking missed points meet both requirements.

## Checking both criteria across all five study habits

Scoring **active retrieval and feedback side by side** selects the three matching methods.

| Study habit | Unaided retrieval | Includes feedback | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Close notes, write what is remembered, then check against notes | Yes (writes from memory) | Yes (verifies against notes) | **Matches** |
| Solve practice questions, then review wrong answers | Yes (questions force recall) | Yes (corrects errors) | **Matches** |
| Explain to a friend without notes, then check what was missed | Yes (pulls from memory) | Yes (fills missed gaps) | **Matches** |

# Answer

The matching study methods are **closing notes to write what you remember and then checking**, **doing practice questions and checking mistakes**, and **explaining to a friend without notes and then checking what was missed**.
