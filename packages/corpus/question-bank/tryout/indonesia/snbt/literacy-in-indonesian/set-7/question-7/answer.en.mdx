# Given

**The poem depicts** a fishing family whose daily routine followed the tides and a father who still keeps parts of his old habit.

# Asked

Which statements can genuinely be concluded from specific lines of the poem.

# Solution

> Do not rely on a vague mood, check the exact line so rising tide versus ebbing tide and past versus present never get swapped.

Match each statement to the right line, not to a general impression of the poem.

The statement about meal and sleep times can be concluded. The third stanza says **"Maka kami makan ketika air surut, tidur ketika air naik"** (so we ate when the water went out, slept when it came in).

The statement that the father stopped because he couldn't read the tide can't be concluded. He stopped fishing **"sejak lututnya berkarat"** (since his knees rusted), and he still **"memandang air datang dan pergi"** (watches the water come and go).

The statement about old habits can be concluded. The father **"masih bangun sebelum subuh"** (still gets up before dawn) and **"duduk di ambang pintu yang sama"** (sits on the same doorstep), as he did when waiting for the tide.

The statement that he set out as the water went out can't be concluded. The tide is a guest that **"datang"** (comes), and the father waits, then **"baru berangkat setelah air setinggi lutut"** (only sets out once the water is knee-deep), that is, once the water has come in.

The statement that the speaker can already read life's tide can't be concluded. The father says, **"Kau saja yang belum belajar membacanya"** (it's just you who hasn't learned to read it yet).

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Meals and sleep followed the water | Ya |
| The father stopped because he couldn't read the tide | Tidak |
| The father keeps some old habits | Ya |
| The father set out as the water went out | Tidak |
| The speaker can already read life's tide | Tidak |
