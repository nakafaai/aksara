Each statement has to be matched with the right lines. Be careful with words that look similar but mean something else.

The statement that the family used to arrange daily life by the tides can be concluded. The lines **"kami makan ketika air surut, / tidur ketika air naik"** (we ate when the water was low, / slept when it rose) show this, in line with the father's words, **"kita yang menyesuaikan jam"** (it's us who adjust the clock).

The statement that the father stopped fishing because the sea no longer had tides can't be concluded. The poem says he stopped **"sejak lututnya berkarat"** (since his knees rusted), that is, because his body aged. He still **"memandang air datang dan pergi"** (watches the water come and go).

The statement that the speaker uses the phone clock to know when the tide comes can't be concluded. The city **"tidak punya laut"** (has no sea), and the phone clock **"tidak satu pun memberitahu / kapan perahu harus didorong ke air"** (none of it tells / when the boat should be pushed into the water).

The statement that the father set out when the water began to fall can't be concluded. He **"baru berangkat setelah air setinggi lutut"** (only set out once the water was knee-high), after the water had risen, not as it fell.
