Match each statement with the text. Two of them **reverse** the author's view.

| Statement | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sidewalks wide enough for two people to pass | Sesuai (matches) | Paragraph four lists this as a condition of a good sidewalk. |
| A walkable city bans private vehicles | Tidak sesuai (does not match) | Last paragraph: such a city "tidak menolak kendaraan" (does not reject vehicles). |
| Widening roads solves congestion for a long time | Tidak sesuai (does not match) | Paragraph two: the results "jarang bertahan lama" (rarely last), and the road jams again within a few years. |
| The hardest part is keeping sidewalks clear | Sesuai (matches) | Paragraph four: the hardest part is "bukan membangunnya, melainkan menjaganya" (not building them but keeping them). |

The second statement is often marked Sesuai because the author defends pedestrians. The author does not reject vehicles, only the idea of walking as **a last resort**.
