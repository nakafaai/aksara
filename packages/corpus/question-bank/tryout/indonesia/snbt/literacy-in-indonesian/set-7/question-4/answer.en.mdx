The answer has to be right in **three parts**: direction, distance and how the calls change.

- **Direction**. The text says **"selisih gema yang tiba di telinga kiri dan kanan menunjukkan arah"** (the difference between echoes reaching the left and right ears shows direction). An echo arriving first and louder in the right ear comes from the right.
- **Distance**. **"Selang antara panggilan dan gema menunjukkan jarak"** (the gap between call and echo shows distance). A shrinking gap means the moth is getting closer.
- **Call change**. **"Ketika mangsa sudah dekat, panggilannya makin rapat hingga terdengar seperti dengung, sebab setiap gema cepat usang selagi mangsa terus berbelok"** (when prey is close, calls come faster until they sound like a buzz, because each echo goes stale while the prey keeps turning).

The other options go wrong in one part.

- A shrinking gap means closer, not farther. The calls also speed up, not slow down.
- The echo reaches the right ear first, so the moth is on the right, not the left.
- The text never says bats call louder so moths can't hear. Moths actually read loud calls as **"tanda kelelawar sudah dekat"** (a sign the bat is close).
- The gap between call and echo shows distance, not direction. Direction comes from the difference between the two ears.

So the moth is on the right and getting closer, and the bat's calls speed up because each echo goes stale while the prey keeps turning.
