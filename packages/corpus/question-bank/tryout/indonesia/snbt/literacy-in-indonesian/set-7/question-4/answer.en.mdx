Paragraph three sets out the cause and effect. Antibiotics **also kill many useful gut bacteria**. The bacterial mix may not recover quickly, leaving **empty space**. That space **can be taken over by harmful bacteria** such as *C. difficile*.

- The passage does not say antibiotics contain bacteria.
- Useful bacteria are killed, not turned into another kind.
- Fibre is fermented into fatty acids, not toxins.
- Butyrate is made by bacteria, not the body, and is not linked to the infection.

So the infection can appear because antibiotics also kill useful bacteria and harmful bacteria then fill the space.
