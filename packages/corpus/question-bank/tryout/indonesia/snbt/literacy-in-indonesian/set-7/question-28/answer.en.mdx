# Given

The read-four-times group gave the **highest predicted one-week recall**, yet actually earned the lowest one-week score (<InlineMath math="40\%" />). Researchers explain that **"rereading produces a sense of fluency, but does not train retrieving memories without cues,"** and fluency is easily mistaken for mastery.

# Asked

The best conclusion from their high prediction and the part of the researchers' explanation that accounts for it.

# Solution

Pair the **gap between highest confidence and lowest one-week score** with the **illusion of fluency from rereading**, rather than the separate feedback caveat.

> Fluency from rereading made that group feel best prepared despite remembering least after a week, because rereading builds familiarity without training unaided recall.
