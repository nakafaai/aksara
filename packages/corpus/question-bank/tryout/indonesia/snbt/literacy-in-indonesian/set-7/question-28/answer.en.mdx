The line answers Pak Hasan's proposal to **burn the rubbish**. Bu Ning has said her child already coughs because of the smell. With **"bukan cuma anak saya"** (not only my child), she says the smoke will enter people's homes and **make more people cough**. The line rejects the burning proposal.

- She does not say her child has recovered. The child still coughs every morning.
- She is rejecting Pak Hasan's proposal, not blaming him for the existing cough.
- "Nanti" (later) points to a future consequence, not the present.
- She expresses no wish to move.

So the line means that burning rubbish would harm the health of more residents.
