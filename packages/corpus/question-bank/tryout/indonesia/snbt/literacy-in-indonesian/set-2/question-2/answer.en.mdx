# Given

From the methylmercury table, **upstream** averages are <InlineMath math="0.42" /> for predatory fish and <InlineMath math="0.06" /> for plant-eaters, while **downstream** averages are <InlineMath math="0.90" /> and <InlineMath math="0.10" />. The magnification factor at a site equals predatory level divided by plant-eater level at that same site.

# Asked

The accurate statement about both sites' magnification factors and why downstream predatory fish carry higher levels.

# Solution

Compute **each site's magnification factor** using that same site's denominator, then write each predatory level as baseline times magnification factor.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{upstream factor} &= \frac{0.42}{0.06} = 7 \\
\text{downstream factor} &= \frac{0.90}{0.10} = 9
\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{upstream} &: 0.06 \times 7 = 0.42 \\
\text{downstream} &: 0.10 \times 9 = 0.90
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

> Downstream predatory fish are more contaminated for two reasons together: their food-chain baseline is higher (<InlineMath math="0.10 > 0.06" />) and their magnification factor is larger (<InlineMath math="9 > 7" />).

## Checking the math and explanation of each option

Comparing **ratios and causal explanations** filters out all four distractors.

| Option | Check of numbers and reasoning | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upstream <InlineMath math="7" />, downstream <InlineMath math="9" />, driven by both higher baseline and larger magnification | Exact ratios and complete two-factor explanation | **Correct** |
| Uses <InlineMath math="0.36" /> and <InlineMath math="0.80" /> | Uses differences instead of ratios | **Incorrect** |
| Swaps <InlineMath math="9" /> upstream and <InlineMath math="7" /> downstream | Reverses the two sites | **Incorrect** |
| Uses <InlineMath math="15" /> (<InlineMath math="0.90 : 0.06" />) | Divides downstream predators by upstream plant-eaters | **Incorrect** |
| Claims the gap is solely due to magnification | Ignores that downstream plant-eaters start higher | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The magnification factor is **<InlineMath math="7" /> upstream and <InlineMath math="9" /> downstream**, and downstream predatory fish carry more methylmercury because **both the starting level and the magnification factor are larger**.
