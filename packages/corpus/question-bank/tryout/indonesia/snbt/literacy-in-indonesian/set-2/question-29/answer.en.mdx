Ningsih's proposal answers both of Bayu's reasons at once. Bayu says no one is left to farm the field, and Ningsih offers a mentoring programme that turns the field into a trial plot. Bayu wants his own workshop, and Ningsih points out that in the village **no one can repair farm machinery**, so Bayu could be a farmer who also runs a workshop. The proposal keeps the field and uses **the skills of both siblings**.

- Ningsih does not ask Bayu to study. She asks him to maintain the water pump and the threshing machine.
- The proposal is a way to avoid selling the field.
- Ningsih does not blame her father. Bayu is the one who mentions Pak Karta's back pain.
- The lecturer is mentioned only as running the mentoring programme, not as a buyer.

So Ningsih's proposal is an attempt to solve the problem without selling the field by combining Bayu's skills with her knowledge of farming.
