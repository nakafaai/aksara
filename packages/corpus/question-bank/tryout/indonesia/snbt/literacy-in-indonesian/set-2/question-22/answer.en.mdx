The text defines acclimatisation by three features: the plant **comes from another region or country**, it's planted **to test** whether it can live and produce in the local climate, and the test happens **before it's planted more widely**. The right activity has to meet all three.

Planting a rice variety from another province in a trial field meets them all. The rice comes from elsewhere, it's planted to test whether it grows and yields well, and the result decides whether the variety goes out to farmers.
