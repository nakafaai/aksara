# Given

Total auction revenue at Karangsari rose from **Rp<InlineMath math="1.2" /> billion** (Rp<InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> million) in January to **Rp<InlineMath math="1.4" /> billion** (Rp<InlineMath math="1{,}400" /> million) in February, while participating boats grew from <InlineMath math="50" /> to <InlineMath math="80" />.

# Asked

The percentage changes in total auction value and average auction value per boat from January to February.

# Solution

Compute the **percentage rise in total revenue** first, then divide each month's revenue by its boat count to find the **per-boat change**.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{total value change} = \frac{1{,}400 - 1{,}200}{1{,}200} = \frac{200}{1{,}200} \approx +16.7\%" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{January per boat} &= \frac{1{,}200}{50} = 24\text{ million rupiah} \\
\text{February per boat} &= \frac{1{,}400}{80} = 17.5\text{ million rupiah} \\
\text{per-boat change} &= \frac{17.5 - 24}{24} \approx -27\%
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

> Total revenue rose about <InlineMath math="16.7\%" />, yet because boat numbers jumped <InlineMath math="60\%" />, average revenue per boat dropped about <InlineMath math="27\%" />.

## Tracing the distractor figures

Comparing **total revenue, catch weight, and per-kg price** isolates the exact pair.

| Figure pair | Source of calculation | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total up about <InlineMath math="16.7\%" />, per boat down about <InlineMath math="27\%" /> | Exact total and boat-adjusted values | **Correct** |
| Both total and per-boat values rose | Ignores boat count jumping from <InlineMath math="50" /> to <InlineMath math="80" /> | **Incorrect** |
| Total up <InlineMath math="25\%" /> | Measures weight growth (<InlineMath math="40 \to 50" /> tons), not revenue | **Incorrect** |
| Total down <InlineMath math="6.7\%" /> | Measures average per-kg price dip (<InlineMath math="30 \to 28" />) | **Incorrect** |
| Per-boat value stayed flat | False, revenue rose <InlineMath math="16.7\%" /> while boats rose <InlineMath math="60\%" /> | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Total auction value **rose by about <InlineMath math="16.7\%" />**, while average auction value per boat **fell by about <InlineMath math="27\%" />**.
