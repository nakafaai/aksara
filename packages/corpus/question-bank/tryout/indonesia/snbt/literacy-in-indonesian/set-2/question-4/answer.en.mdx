The word *serbaguna* means useful for many purposes. Sentence (2) demonstrates that quality by naming rice dishes from Indonesia, Greece, Italy, Spain, and England.

Being a staple for many people shows the importance of rice. Versatility comes from the many different foods that can be made from it. Supply conditions and the question of washing do not explain why the word is used either.

Therefore, rice is versatile because it can become **many different dishes** from around the world.
