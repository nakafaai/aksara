Paragraph three opens with "Kepekaan ini terlihat jelas dalam sejarah kota-kota industri di Inggris" (This sensitivity shows clearly in the history of England's industrial cities). The English story is used as **supporting evidence** for the sensitivity explained in paragraph two. The evidence runs both ways: when sulphur dioxide was high, lichens almost vanished from cities, and when levels were brought down, lichens **slowly returned**. That rise-and-fall pattern shows that lichen presence follows sulphur dioxide levels.

- The passage says clean-air rules brought sulphur dioxide down, but it does not discuss why coal burning stopped.
- Indonesian lichens are not compared.
- Rural lichens thrived because the air there was cleaner, not because they were a hardier kind.
- The passage says "berbagai jenis" (various species) returned, not all of them. The word **semua** (all) goes beyond the evidence.

So the description serves as evidence that lichen presence follows the rise and fall of sulphur dioxide levels.
