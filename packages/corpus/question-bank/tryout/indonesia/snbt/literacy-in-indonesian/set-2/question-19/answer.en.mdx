Text 1 gives two nested figures: scores matched exactly on 78 percent of essays and differed by **at most one level** on 97 percent. The 97 percent already includes the exact matches. Essays that differed by **more than one level** are the rest:

<BlockMath math="100\% - 97\% = 3\%" />

<BlockMath math="3\% \times 3{,}000 = 0.03 \times 3{,}000 = 90" />

So 90 essays differed by more than one level.

- 570 essays is 19 percent, the essays that differed by exactly one level (97 percent minus 78 percent).

- 2,340 essays is 78 percent, the exact matches.
- 2,910 essays is 97 percent, the essays within one level.

The number of essays more than one level apart is 90.
