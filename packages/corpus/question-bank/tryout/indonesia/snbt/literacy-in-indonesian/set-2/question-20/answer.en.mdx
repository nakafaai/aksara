# Given

**The observer fixes** each fish species at its January weight and applies February prices to isolate the price effect within the Rp<InlineMath math="200" /> million total rise.

# Asked

How much of the auction value increase comes from higher prices under the observer's method.

# Solution

> Holding every fish weight steady at January's level and changing only the prices ensures any gap from January comes purely from price.

The observer's method **holds the weights fixed** at January's and uses February's prices. Any difference from January's value can then only come from price.

**Step 1. Value with January weights and February prices**. Remember that <InlineMath math="1" /> tonne is <InlineMath math="1,000" /> kilograms, and the table's prices are in thousand rupiah per kilogram.

| Fish | January weight | February price | Value |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Tongkol | <InlineMath math="20" /> t | <InlineMath math="33" /> thousand | Rp<InlineMath math="660" /> million |
| Kembung | <InlineMath math="10" /> t | <InlineMath math="41" /> thousand | Rp<InlineMath math="410" /> million |
| Teri | <InlineMath math="10" /> t | <InlineMath math="22" /> thousand | Rp<InlineMath math="220" /> million |

The total is Rp<InlineMath math="1,290" /> million.

**Step 2. Compare with January's value**. January's value is Rp<InlineMath math="1,200" /> million, so the price effect is **Rp<InlineMath math="90" /> million**. The other Rp<InlineMath math="110" /> million comes from the change in how much fish and which kinds.

The other options come from wrong working.

- Rp<InlineMath math="80" /> million comes from a plain average of February prices, <InlineMath math="32" /> thousand, times <InlineMath math="40" /> tonnes. That ignores that tongkol weighs twice as much as the others.
- Rp<InlineMath math="100" /> million comes from holding February's weights at January's prices, which gives Rp<InlineMath math="1,300" /> million. That isn't the observer's method.
- Rp<InlineMath math="110" /> million is the part that's **not** due to price.
- Rp<InlineMath math="200" /> million is the whole rise, including the effect of more fish.

# Answer

So by the observer's method, higher prices account for **Rp<InlineMath math="90" />** million.
