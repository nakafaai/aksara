This takes two steps: first name the two shortcomings, then check which effort answers **both**.

**Step 1. The two shortcomings**. The fifth paragraph says:

- each species is **"biasanya diwakili oleh beberapa individu saja"** (usually represented by only a few individuals), so its genetic diversity is narrow, and
- many plants depend on **"penyerbuk, jamur tanah, atau hewan penyebar biji yang tidak ikut pindah ke kebun"** (pollinators, soil fungi or seed-spreading animals that don't move to the garden).

**Step 2. Test each effort**.

| Effort | Genetic diversity | Links with other organisms |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| More individuals in the garden | partly | no |
| Protecting the original forest | yes, the natural population stays whole | yes, the pollinators and fungi are there |
| Bringing pollinators into the garden | no | partly |
| A seed bank | partly, if the seeds are varied | no |
| Enlarging the garden | no, the problem is individuals per species, not number of species | no |

Only protecting the forest where the plant lives answers both, because the natural population and the organisms it needs stay together there. That fits the warning that a botanic garden **"tidak dapat menggantikan hutan"** (can't replace a forest).

The other options answer only one shortcoming, or neither.

So the effort most likely to answer both shortcomings is protecting the forest where the plant grows together with its pollinators, soil fungi and seed spreaders.
