# Given

**Text A argues** that raising the minimum wage will act as an economic boost for small businesses in Sukaraja district.

# Asked

Which statements are necessary assumptions for Text A's argument to hold.

# Solution

> Test each assumption by imagining it is false, if Text A's argument falls apart right away, that assumption is required.

A **necessary** assumption is something that has to be true for A's argument to work. Test it like this: imagine the assumption is false. If A's argument collapses, the assumption is necessary.

The statement that a meaningful share of the extra pay is spent inside the district is necessary. A's whole argument rests on money that **"berputar di warung, pasar, dan bengkel di kabupaten ini sendiri"** (circulates in the shops, markets and workshops of this district itself). If the extra pay were saved or spent outside the district, the boost to Sukaraja's small businesses would vanish.

The statement that employers don't cut many workers is necessary. If many workers lost their jobs, the total pay being spent could actually fall, and the boost wouldn't happen. A even feels the need to cite research showing jobs fall **"tidak banyak"** (not by much).

The statement that all workers are in large firms isn't necessary. A's argument only needs workers whose pay rises and who spend it. Wherever they work, the money can still circulate in the district.

The statement that prices don't rise at all isn't necessary, because of the words **sama sekali** (at all). A's argument still works if prices rise a little, as long as the price rise doesn't eat up the whole wage rise. The assumption A needs is weaker than this statement.

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| A meaningful share of the extra pay is spent inside the district | Ya |
| Employers don't cut many workers | Ya |
| All minimum-wage workers are in large firms | Tidak |
| Prices don't rise at all | Tidak |
