The remark is a figure of speech, and it has **two parts**. The right option has to read both correctly.

Start with the situation. Laras has just promised to come home every holiday, and her mother answers with two sentences about thread. So the thread stands for the bond between mother and daughter, not real thread.

- **"Tidak putus kalau ditarik"** (doesn't break when it's pulled). Laras going far away is like thread being pulled. Their bond doesn't break because of distance.
- **"Baru rusak kalau terlalu lama digulung dan tidak pernah dipakai"** (only spoils if it stays wound up too long and is never used). Thread that's kept stored away spoils. Laras held back at home, never used for what she chose, would be wasted.

So letting Laras go won't break their bond, while holding her back could waste her.

The other options are wrong.

- Reading it as an instruction to take real thread along takes the figure of speech literally.
- The second option's first part is right, but its second part contradicts the mother's other remark, "Jangan berjanji soal benang yang belum ditenun" (Don't make promises about thread that hasn't been woven). The mother doesn't demand that Laras come back soon.
- The remark shows no disappointment. The part about thread spoiling actually defends Laras leaving.
- The last option flips the figure. Thread that's kept stored away **spoils**, it isn't protected.

So the remark means that letting Laras go won't break their bond, while holding her back could waste her.
