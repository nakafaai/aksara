# Given

**The table lists** catch weights in tonnes and prices per kilogram for tuna, mackerel, and anchovy in January and February.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded from the fish auction text and table.

# Solution

> An overall average price can drop even when every individual price rises if the cheapest fish suddenly makes up most of the weight.

Each species' auction value is weight times price. With weight in tonnes and price in thousand rupiah per kg, the result comes out straight in million rupiah.

| Species | January value (million rupiah) | February value (million rupiah) |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| Tuna | <InlineMath math="20\times30=600" /> | <InlineMath math="10\times33=330" /> |
| Mackerel | <InlineMath math="10\times40=400" /> | <InlineMath math="10\times41=410" /> |
| Anchovy | <InlineMath math="10\times20=200" /> | <InlineMath math="30\times22=660" /> |
| Total | <InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> | <InlineMath math="1{,}400" /> |

The statement about anchovy can't be concluded. Anchovy does make up <InlineMath math="30" /> of the <InlineMath math="50" /> tonnes, **more than half the weight**. But its value is <InlineMath math="660" /> out of <InlineMath math="1{,}400" /> million rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\frac{660}{1{,}400}\approx47\%<50\%" />

Half the weight isn't necessarily half the value, because anchovy is the cheapest fish.

The statement about the average price can be concluded. The average falls from <InlineMath math="30" /> to <InlineMath math="28" /> thousand rupiah per kg, even though all three species' prices rose.

The statement about tuna can be concluded. Its value falls from <InlineMath math="600" /> to <InlineMath math="330" /> million rupiah, because its weight halved.

The statement about mackerel can be concluded. Its weight stays at <InlineMath math="10" /> tonnes, and its value rises from <InlineMath math="400" /> to <InlineMath math="410" /> million rupiah because its price rose.

# Answer

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Anchovy brings in more than half of February's auction value | Tidak |
| The average price falls although every price rises | Ya |
| Tuna's February value is below January's | Ya |
| Mackerel's weight stays the same, but its value rises | Ya |
