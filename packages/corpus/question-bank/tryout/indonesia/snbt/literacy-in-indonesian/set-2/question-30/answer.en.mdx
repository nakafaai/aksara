The line comes after Pak Karta **looks at Ningsih for a long time, then at Bayu**, right after Ningsih explains her proposal. That look shows him weighing it. "Bertiga" (the three of us) means himself, Bayu, and Ningsih, the two children who have just been given roles in the proposal. Going to the field together is best read as a first step toward **considering Ningsih's proposal** with both of them involved.

- Throughout the scene Pak Karta refuses to sell, and nothing suggests he has changed his mind in that direction.
- There is no sign of punishment or anger.
- The buyer is not present and is not part of "bertiga".
- His long look at Ningsih and Bayu shows he has been listening closely.

So the line shows that Pak Karta is beginning to consider Ningsih's proposal and wants both children involved.
