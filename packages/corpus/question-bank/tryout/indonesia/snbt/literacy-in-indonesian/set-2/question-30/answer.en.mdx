# Given

**The text examines** default options in opt-out organ donor registries and automatic employee pension contribution schemes.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded accurately without swapping measures or overstating certainty.

# Solution

> Having more registered donors does not automatically guarantee more transplants, so never swap registration rates for final outcomes.

Three statements use words that are too strong or swap measures. Check each statement carefully.

The statement about the 2003 study can be concluded. The text says that in opt-out countries, the share of people registered as willing donors was **"jauh lebih tinggi"** (far higher) than in opt-in countries.

The statement that opt-out certainly increases transplants can't be concluded. The text says the change **"tidak otomatis menambah jumlah organ yang benar-benar didonorkan"** (doesn't automatically increase the number of organs actually donated), because families are still asked and hospitals have to be ready.

The statement that total contributions fell can't be concluded, because the opposite is true. Work out the total in "percent of one person's pay".

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{before}&=400\times6=2{,}400\\
\text{after}&=750\times3+150\times6=3{,}150
\end{aligned}" />

The total rises. What falls is the average **per participant**, from <InlineMath math="6\%" /> to <InlineMath math="3.5\%" />.

The statement that people who kept the default certainly didn't read the form can't be concluded. The text gives another explanation: people tend to read a default **"sebagai anjuran"** (as a recommendation). Someone who reads the form can still choose to stay with the default.

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The registered donor share was higher in opt-out countries | Ya |
| Opt-out certainly increases transplants | Tidak |
| Total contributions of all employees fell | Tidak |
| People who kept the default certainly didn't read the form | Tidak |
