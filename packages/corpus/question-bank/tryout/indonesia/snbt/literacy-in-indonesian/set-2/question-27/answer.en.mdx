# Given

At **PT Sinar Mandiri** (<InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> equal-salary employees), before auto-enrollment <InlineMath math="400" /> participants each saved <InlineMath math="6\%" />. After auto-enrollment at a <InlineMath math="3\%" /> default, <InlineMath math="750" /> employees saved <InlineMath math="3\%" /> and <InlineMath math="150" /> saved <InlineMath math="6\%" /> (<InlineMath math="900" /> participants total).

# Asked

The change in total savings as a percentage of total payroll, and the change in average savings per participant.

# Solution

Apply **two distinct denominators**: divide total savings by all <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> employees for payroll share, and divide by active participants (<InlineMath math="400" /> then <InlineMath math="900" />) for the per-participant mean.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{total before} &= \frac{400 \times 6\%}{1{,}000} = 2.4\% \\
\text{total after} &= \frac{750 \times 3\% + 150 \times 6\%}{1{,}000} = \frac{3{,}150\%}{1{,}000} = 3.15\%
\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{participant mean before} &= 6\% \\
\text{participant mean after} &= \frac{3{,}150\%}{900} = 3.5\%
\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

> Higher participation lifts total payroll savings from <InlineMath math="2.4\%" /> to <InlineMath math="3.15\%" />, while average savings per participant drops from <InlineMath math="6\%" /> to <InlineMath math="3.5\%" />.

## Checking the distractor options

Reviewing **both metrics and denominators** exposes every trap.

| Option | Source of figures | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total rises <InlineMath math="2.4\% \to 3.15\%" />, participant mean falls <InlineMath math="6\% \to 3.5\%" /> | Both metrics and denominators exact | **Correct** |
| Both total and participant mean rose | Wrong direction for participant mean | **Incorrect** |
| Total fell from <InlineMath math="6\%" /> to <InlineMath math="3.5\%" /> | Confuses participant mean with total payroll share | **Incorrect** |
| Total after is <InlineMath math="4.05\%" /> | Uses unweighted midpoint <InlineMath math="4.5\%" /> instead of <InlineMath math="750" /> vs <InlineMath math="150" /> | **Incorrect** |
| Total savings stayed unchanged | False, growth in participants outweighs lower per-person rate | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Total savings **rose from <InlineMath math="2.4\%" /> to <InlineMath math="3.15\%" /> of total payroll**, while the average contribution per participant **fell from <InlineMath math="6\%" /> to <InlineMath math="3.5\%" />**.
