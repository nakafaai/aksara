The main conflict is **the disagreement that drives the whole conversation**. From the start Bayu presses to sell the field because the price has risen and the money could fund his own workshop. Pak Karta refuses, saying the field was cleared by Bayu's grandfather with his own hands. Ningsih then offers a middle way. Every line circles one question: **sell the inherited field for Bayu's capital, or keep it**.

- Bu Karta only asks to postpone the talk because it is late. She does not forbid her children to work in the city.
- Ningsih actually defends her choice to study agriculture.
- The buyer from the city is only mentioned by Bayu and never appears or argues with Pak Karta.
- Bayu wants his own workshop, not a move to someone else's.

So the main conflict is the disagreement over selling the inherited field to fund Bayu's business.
