# Given

In paragraph three of the Bogor Botanic Garden passage, the author tells how **four West African oil-palm seedlings** planted in 1848 reportedly supplied Sumatra's plantations, prompting some historians to call the garden a "plantation laboratory." Paragraph four immediately qualifies that label as "accurate for part of its work, but not all of it."

# Asked

The function of the four oil-palm seedlings story within the passage as a whole.

# Solution

Track the story's **two-part structural role**: inside paragraph three it illustrates plantation acclimatization, and across paragraph four it sets up the historian label that the author proceeds to qualify.

> The oil-palm story serves both as a concrete example of plantation-serving acclimatization and as the basis for a historian label the author then narrows.

## Checking the five candidate functions

Checking **the second clause of each option** separates the true structural function from misreadings.

| Candidate function | Fit with passage structure | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Illustrates plantation acclimatization and sets up the label the author then qualifies | Links paragraphs three and four accurately | **Correct** |
| Validates "plantation laboratory" for the garden's entire history | False, paragraph four explicitly narrows that label | **Incorrect** |
| Illustrates saving an endangered plant from extinction | False, oil palm was commercial acclimatization, not conservation | **Incorrect** |
| Suggests oil-palm acclimatization failed | False, "often cited" marks attribution, not failure | **Incorrect** |
| Illustrates non-commercial laboratory research in the late 19th century | False, oil palm was commercial and planted in 1848 | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The story **provides an example of acclimatization serving plantations while setting up the historian label that the author subsequently qualifies**.
