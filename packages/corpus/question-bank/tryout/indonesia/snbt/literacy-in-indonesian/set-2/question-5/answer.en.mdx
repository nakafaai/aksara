Sentence (2) shows that rice appears in many dishes around the world. Sentence (3) then moves from that broad appeal to one practical issue, whether rice should be washed in sentence (4).

The relationship is concessive: although rice is widely valued and used, its preparation still raises a question. The other phrases have no clear reference or introduce opinions and government action that the passage never mentions.

Therefore, **Meskipun memiliki daya tarik universal**, despite its universal appeal, completes the sentence.
