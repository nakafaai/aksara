# Given

**Text A and** Text B debate how big a minimum wage rise should be and how it should apply to micro businesses in Sukaraja.

# Asked

Which statements the writer of Text B would agree with.

# Solution

> Criticizing how a policy is rolled out does not mean opposing the goal itself, so separate Text B's common ground from its objection.

The writer of Text B **doesn't oppose a wage rise**. The objection is to how it's done and how big it is. Several statements come from Text A, so don't answer Tidak (No) just because B seems to be on the other side.

- **Statement 1: Ya (Yes)**. B writes, **"saya setuju bahwa upah minimum saat ini belum cukup untuk hidup layak"** (I agree that the current minimum wage isn't enough to live decently), and then proposes a rise.
- **Statement 2: Tidak**. B asks for the 2022 figure to be **"dibaca hati-hati"** (read carefully), because that year the economy was only recovering after pandemic restrictions were lifted.
- **Statement 3: Tidak**. B proposes a **differentiated rise**: large businesses follow the council's proposal, micro businesses phase it in over three years.
- **Statement 4: Ya**. Micro businesses still raise wages, just gradually, and B stresses **"pekerja tetap mendapat kenaikan"** (workers still get a rise).
- **Statement 5: Tidak**. Text A leans on research from many countries, but B warns that this research mostly looks at large firms and **"hasilnya belum tentu berlaku bagi usaha yang hanya punya beberapa pekerja"** (its results don't necessarily apply to businesses with only a few workers).

# Answer

The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The minimum wage should rise | Ya |
| 2022 sales prove the rise helps | Tidak |
| Same size and timing for every business | Tidak |
| Micro-business workers still get a rise | Ya |
| Large-firm research applies directly to micro businesses | Tidak |
