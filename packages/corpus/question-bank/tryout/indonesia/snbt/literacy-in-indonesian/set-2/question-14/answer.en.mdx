# Given

At the bakery in **Text B**, wages take <InlineMath math="40\%" /> of revenue and net profit is <InlineMath math="5\%" /> of revenue. Assume every worker earns minimum wage (rising <InlineMath math="10\%" />), other costs stay fixed, and loaves sold stay unchanged.

# Asked

The percentage drop in net profit if prices stay fixed, and the percentage price increase needed to restore original rupiah profit.

# Solution

Set initial revenue to <InlineMath math="100" /> units so **initial wages (<InlineMath math="40" />)** and **initial profit (<InlineMath math="5" />)** compute cleanly.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{extra wage cost} &= 10\% \times 40 = 4 \\
\text{new profit} &= 5 - 4 = 1 \\
\text{profit drop} &= \frac{5 - 1}{5} = 80\%
\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{price increase to recover } 4 = \frac{4}{100} = 4\%" />
</MathContainer>

> An extra wage cost equal to <InlineMath math="4\%" /> of revenue wipes out <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of a thin <InlineMath math="5\%" /> profit margin, yet requires only a <InlineMath math="4\%" /> price hike to offset.

## Checking the distractor pairs

Matching **each wrong percentage to its base error** confirms the exact pair.

| Option figure | Base-rate mistake | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profit drops <InlineMath math="80\%" />, price rises <InlineMath math="4\%" /> | Exact profit base (<InlineMath math="4/5" />) and revenue base (<InlineMath math="4/100" />) | **Correct** |
| Profit drops <InlineMath math="10\%" /> | Confuses profit drop with wage-hike percentage | **Incorrect** |
| Price rises <InlineMath math="10\%" /> | Forgets wages are only <InlineMath math="40\%" /> of revenue | **Incorrect** |
| Profit drops <InlineMath math="20\%" /> | Computes remaining profit (<InlineMath math="1/5" />) instead of profit lost | **Incorrect** |
| Price rises <InlineMath math="40\%" /> | Uses total wage share instead of the wage increase | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

Net profit falls by **<InlineMath math="80\%" />**, and bread prices must rise by **<InlineMath math="4\%" />** to restore original rupiah profit.
