The phrase *alat ini* contains the demonstrative *ini*, meaning "this." To identify its referent, we look back for the device introduced before sentence (25).

Sentence (13) introduces the GPS Collar as a collar fitted with a positioning device. Sentence (14) explains that it records location. When sentence (25) says that *this device* records positions and transmits location data, it continues the description of the same object.

The phrase does not refer to a camera trap, compass, handheld radio, or satellite receiver. The correct answer is **GPS Collar**.
