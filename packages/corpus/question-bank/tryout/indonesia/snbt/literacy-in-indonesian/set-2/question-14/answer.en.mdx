Savings are measured against **the water used by the old method**, because the old method is the baseline. The difference in water use is:

<BlockMath math="12{,}000 - 7{,}800 = 4{,}200 \text{ m}^3" />

Divide that difference by the water used for continuous flooding:

<BlockMath math="\frac{4{,}200}{12{,}000} \times 100\% = 35\%" />

So alternate wetting and drying saves 35 percent of the irrigation water.

- About 5 percent is the rise in grain yield, <InlineMath math="0.3" /> tonnes on <InlineMath math="5.6" /> tonnes, not a water saving.
