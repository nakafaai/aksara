# Given

Assume **none of the <InlineMath math="400" /> original <InlineMath math="6\%" /> savers** at PT Sinar Mandiri left the program. One year after auto-enrollment, <InlineMath math="150" /> people in total save <InlineMath math="6\%" /> and <InlineMath math="750" /> save <InlineMath math="3\%" />.

# Asked

The minimum number of those <InlineMath math="400" /> original savers who now save <InlineMath math="3\%" />.

# Solution

To find the **minimum number of original savers now at <InlineMath math="3\%" />**, place as many of them as possible in the <InlineMath math="6\%" /> group. Because that group holds only <InlineMath math="150" /> people in total, at most <InlineMath math="150" /> original savers remain at <InlineMath math="6\%" />.

<BlockMath math="400 - 150 = 250\text{ employees}" />

> At least <InlineMath math="250" /> original savers must now be in the <InlineMath math="3\%" /> group, joining <InlineMath math="500" /> new enrollees to make exactly <InlineMath math="750" />.

| Option | What the number represents | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="250" /> employees | Minimum original savers now at <InlineMath math="3\%" /> (<InlineMath math="400 - 150" />) | **Correct** |
| <InlineMath math="150" /> employees | Entire <InlineMath math="6\%" /> group size | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="100" /> employees | Non-participants (<InlineMath math="1{,}000 - 900" />) | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="750" /> employees | Entire <InlineMath math="3\%" /> group including new enrollees | **Incorrect** |
| <InlineMath math="400" /> employees | Maximum possible if all <InlineMath math="150" /> at <InlineMath math="6\%" /> were new | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

At least **<InlineMath math="250" /> employees** who previously saved <InlineMath math="6\%" /> now save <InlineMath math="3\%" />.
