This question asks you to combine **reasoning from Text A** with **a fact from Text B**.

- **Text A's reasoning**. A wage rise is **"suntikan bagi usaha kecil"** (a boost for small businesses), because minimum-wage workers spend their extra pay right away in the district's shops, markets and workshops. The more workers whose pay rises now, the bigger the boost.
- **The fact from Text B**. B itself writes that **"sebagian besar pekerja di Sukaraja bekerja di usaha mikro dan kecil seperti ini, bukan di pabrik"** (most workers in Sukaraja work in micro and small businesses like this, not in factories).

If micro businesses raise wages gradually, their workers only get the full rise after three years. Since they're most of the workforce, most of the boost to spending is delayed too. This objection is the strongest because it uses the other side's own data against its proposal.

The other options are wrong.

- The first option accepts the proposal, but writer A rejects delays. A gradual rise also doesn't give the full rise next year.
- Text A says a moderate rise **"umumnya tidak banyak"** (generally doesn't much) reduce jobs, not that micro businesses aren't affected. "Proves" is too strong.
- The fourth option misreads Text B. Workers in micro and small businesses are **most** of the workforce, not a small part.
- The "complaints always come without evidence" line is in Text A, but Text B gives figures from its own shop. That line doesn't answer B's proposal.

So writer A's strongest objection is that the proposal delays most of the boost to spending, because by Text B's own account most Sukaraja workers are in micro and small businesses.
