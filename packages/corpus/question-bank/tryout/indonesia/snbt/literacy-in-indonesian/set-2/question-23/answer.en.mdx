Two statements set traps with details that are easy to miss. Check each statement against the exact sentence.

The statement that the garden was founded before Indonesian independence can be concluded. It was founded in **1817**, under the Dutch East Indies government.

The statement that its founder was Dutch can't be concluded. The text calls Reinwardt **"kelahiran Jerman yang bekerja untuk pemerintah Hindia Belanda"** (German-born, working for the Dutch East Indies government). Working for the Dutch government isn't the same as being Dutch, and the text says he came from Germany.

The statement that oil palm is native to the archipelago can't be concluded. The text says oil palm **"berasal dari Afrika Barat"** (comes from West Africa). The palms in Sumatra come from imported seedlings.

The statement about the garden's job today can be concluded. The text says, **"Kini tujuan utamanya adalah konservasi"** (Today its main aim is conservation), looking after plants threatened in the forest.

The statement that not every plant is kept alive can be concluded. Besides the living collection, the garden runs a **herbarium**, **"kumpulan spesimen tumbuhan yang dikeringkan"** (a collection of dried plant specimens).
