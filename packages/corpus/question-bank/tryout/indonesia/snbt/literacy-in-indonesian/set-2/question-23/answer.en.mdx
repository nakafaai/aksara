Separate the two layers of scheduling in paragraph two: **simultaneous for neighbouring subak** and **staggered between upstream and downstream**.

| Statement | Answer | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neighbouring subak start planting together | Benar (true) | Paragraph two: neighbouring subak plant at the same time. |
| Staggering makes every subak need the most water at once | Salah (false) | The aim is the reverse: so that not every subak needs the most water at the same time. |
| Fallowing at the same time reduces pests | Benar (true) | Paragraph three: pests lose their food over a wide area, so their numbers fall. |
| The 1970s programme made farmers follow the temple schedule more | Salah (false) | The programme pushed farmers to plant without following the temple schedule. |

The second statement uses the passage's own words but **reverses their purpose**. Staggering exists to spread water demand, not to bunch it together.
