# Given

**The story follows** Rani visiting the railway crossing where her father once worked, noticing the modern barrier, young guard, and old clipping.

# Asked

Which statements can genuinely be concluded from the written details in the story.

# Solution

> Never promote a reader's guess into a story fact, check whether a delay or a name is actually stated in the text.

Some statements trap you by treating a guess as something written down.

The statement about how the barrier is lowered can be concluded. Rani's father **"memutar engkol besi"** (turned an iron crank), while now **"engkolnya sudah diganti tombol"** (the crank has been replaced by a button) and the barrier lowers itself.

The statement that the four o'clock train was late can't be concluded. The story says, **"Kereta jam empat lewat"** (the four o'clock train passed). It was her father who was late.

The statement about the five o'clock train can be concluded. **"Kereta jam lima lewat"** (the five o'clock train passed). After that her father **"baru datang ketika langit sudah gelap"** (only came when it was already dark).

The statement that the young guard recognises Rani can't be concluded. He **"tidak mengenal nama ayahnya"** (doesn't know her father's name) and asks whether she used to live there.

The statement that the clipping names her father can't be concluded. **"Nama penjaga itu tidak disebut"** (the guard's name isn't given).

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How the barrier is lowered has changed | Ya |
| The four o'clock train was late | Tidak |
| The five o'clock train passed before her father came home | Ya |
| The young guard recognises Rani | Tidak |
| The clipping names her father | Tidak |
