# Given

On the night of Rani's birthday, her father comes home late and **"explains nothing"**. The question asks for the **best-supported interpretation of her father** and the **detail that backs it up most strongly**.

# Asked

The best-supported interpretation of Rani's father and the story detail that backs it up most strongly.

# Solution

Connect his silent behavior that night with what he quietly kept for years afterward.

- **Interpretation**. That afternoon a rider was struck by a train, and a guard **"helped lift the victim off the tracks in the rain"**. Rani's father came home **"in a wet uniform with a face Rani could not read"** and **"sat a long time on the porch, staring at the tracks"**. Together these details show he was **shaken and unable to talk about it**.
- **Strongest supporting detail**. He never threw away the news report. Rani found the clipping **"years later"** tucked inside his drawer, proving the event stayed with him even though he never spoke of it.

Check why the other options fail:

| Distractor | Why it fails |
| --- | --- |
| He was angry at Rani | Nothing in the story suggests anger. |
| He forgot Rani's birthday | He was delayed by the crossing accident, not forgetfulness. |
| He felt embarrassed by praise | The clipping simply mentions a guard helping, with no hint of embarrassment. |
| He was merely tired from the rain | His silence and long stare at the tracks point directly to the accident, not mere rain fatigue. |

> Silence does not imply anger or forgetfulness when keeping a newspaper clipping for years reveals lasting emotional shock.

# Answer

The story best supports that **Rani's father was deeply shaken and unable to speak of the accident**, backed most strongly by **the newspaper clipping he kept for years in his drawer**.
