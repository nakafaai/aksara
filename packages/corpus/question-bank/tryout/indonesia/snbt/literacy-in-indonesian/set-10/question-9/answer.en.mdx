# Given

The question asks for all **statements that can be inferred** from clues spread across the story about Rani and her father.

# Asked

All statements that can be validly inferred from direct facts and combined clues across the story.

# Solution

Test each candidate statement against direct details and combined clues from the text:

| Statement | Verdict | Evidence from the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rani's father was most likely the guard who helped | **Supported** | The crash happened **"on the afternoon of her birthday"**, the rescuer came **"from the nearest post"** in the rain, and her father returned late **"in a wet uniform"**. The qualifier *most likely* fits because the clipping omits the guard's name. |
| How the crossing gate operates has changed | **Supported** | It used to be lowered by **"turning an iron crank"**, whereas now **"the crank has been replaced by a button"** and the gate lowers automatically. |
| Rani brings the cake to remember that day | **Supported** | She carries **"the same small cake"** and sits on the exact bench where she once waited. |
| The young guard knew Rani's father | **Not supported** | The story explicitly says the young guard **"did not know her father's name"**. |
| Rani's father forgot her birthday | **Not supported** | He had promised to come home early and was delayed by the accident, not forgetfulness. |

> Sound inferences connect clues across paragraphs while matching the exact degree of certainty supported by the narrative.

# Answer

The valid inferences are that **Rani's father was most likely the guard who helped**, **crossing-gate operation has changed**, and **Rani brings the cake to remember that birthday**.
