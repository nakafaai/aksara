# Given

**The passage tracks** shorebird declines along the East Asian-Australasian Flyway, two figures from different timespans, and reliance on the Yellow Sea.

# Asked

Which statements can validly be concluded from the passage's figures and explanations.

# Solution

> Two decline percentages that look similar cannot be treated as identical when they measure different spans of years.

Each statement has to be checked against the right part of the text. Watch figures that look alike but cover different periods.

**Statement 1**. Paragraph 3 says <InlineMath math="28\%" /> of the tidal flats **"yang ada di pesisir itu pada 1980-an telah lenyap pada akhir 2000-an"** (present on that coast in the 1980s had vanished by the late 2000s). The <InlineMath math="65\%" /> figure covers about <InlineMath math="50" /> years and comes from old maps. For the 1980s to the late 2000s, the loss is <InlineMath math="28\%" />, less than half. The answer is **Tidak**.

**Statement 2**. The two figures measure different periods: <InlineMath math="28\%" /> for the 1980s to the late 2000s, <InlineMath math="65\%" /> for about <InlineMath math="50" /> years. A longer period would naturally record a bigger loss, so they don't conflict. The answer is **Ya**.

**Statement 3**. Paragraph 3 names the Yellow Sea as a stopover for many birds wintering in Australia and New Zealand **"ketika mereka kembali ke utara"** (on their way back north). If its mudflats disappear, those birds lose a stopover, and paragraph 4 shows that moving to other flats doesn't always help. The answer is **Ya**.

**Statement 4**. Paragraph 4 gives two other reasons: at many sites the number of birds is already limited by **the amount of prey**, and stopovers have to be within reach. The text never says the places are too small to land on. The answer is **Tidak**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| More than half lost by the late 2000s | Tidak |
| <InlineMath math="28\%" /> and <InlineMath math="65\%" /> can both be true | Ya |
| Losses at the Yellow Sea harm birds wintering in New Zealand | Ya |
| The remaining flats are too small to land on | Tidak |

# Answer

So **only the statements about the two figures covering different periods and about birds still depending on the Yellow Sea can be concluded**.
