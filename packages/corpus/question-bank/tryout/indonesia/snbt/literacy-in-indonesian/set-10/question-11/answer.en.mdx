# Given

**Text A and** Text B debate AI in homework, AI-detection tools, oral checks, and task designs that still require students' own thinking.

# Asked

Which statements can be concluded about what both writers agree on and where they part ways.

# Solution

> Both writers want homework to build real student thinking, yet Text B doubts AI detectors and prefers assignments that test understanding directly.

Separate what both writers agree on from what they dispute.

The statement about the purpose of homework can be concluded. Text A says homework is **"ada untuk melatih siswa berpikir"** (there to train students to think), and Text B agrees in the same words.

The statement that Text B rejects oral checks can't be concluded. Text B actually proposes that every student explain their writing aloud, **"seperti yang dilakukan penulis Teks A"** (as Text A's writer did).

The statement about the study can be concluded. It found detectors wrongly flagged over half of non-native students' essays, so they are **"tidak dapat diandalkan"** (not reliable) for enforcing a ban.

The statement that Text B disputes Text A's data can't be concluded. Text B doesn't question the students who couldn't explain their essays.

The statement about students' own thinking can be concluded. Students must **"menjelaskan mengapa ia menerima atau menolak saran mesin itu"** (explain why they accepted or rejected the machine's suggestions), so **"yang dinilai adalah pemikiran siswa"** (what's assessed is the student's thinking).

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Both writers agree on the purpose of homework | Ya |
| Text B rejects oral checks | Tidak |
| The study weakens enforcing a ban with detectors | Ya |
| Text B disputes Text A's data | Tidak |
| Text B's idea still requires students' own thinking | Ya |
