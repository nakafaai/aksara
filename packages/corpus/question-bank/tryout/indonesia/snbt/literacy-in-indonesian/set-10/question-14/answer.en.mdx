Text A's guess rests on one assumption: students can't explain their essays because they didn't write them. A weakening finding must show the same failure with essays they surely wrote themselves.

- **The weakening finding**. If the same students also fail to explain essays written in front of the teacher, failing to explain doesn't point to a machine. The cause could be something else, such as nerves or trouble speaking.

The other findings don't weaken the guess.

- Detector results support the guess, even though Text B says detectors are **"tidak dapat diandalkan"** (not reliable).
- Other students' writing style says nothing about why these <InlineMath math="18" /> failed to explain.
- Machines being easy to use at home makes the guess more plausible.
- Unlearned terms also support it, matching students who **"tidak tahu arti istilah yang mereka tulis"** (didn't know the meaning of terms they wrote).

So the most weakening finding is that the same students also fail to explain essays they wrote themselves in class.
