# Given

**The text and** table compare each income group's share of total fuel subsidies against how large the subsidy is in each household's own budget.

# Asked

Which statements follow when separating share of total subsidy from share of household spending.

# Solution

> Two facts hold side by side because richer groups absorb most of the subsidy rupiah while poorer families rely on it far more inside their own budgets.

Two statements require telling the **share of total subsidy** apart from the **share of household spending**.

The statement that the top two groups get more than half the subsidy can be concluded. <InlineMath math="23\%+40\%=63\%" />.

The statement that bottom households receive more can't be concluded. Per household, the bottom group gets Rp<InlineMath math="800{,}000" /> and the top group Rp<InlineMath math="4" /> million.

The statement that the subsidy matters more to bottom households as a share of their spending can be concluded.

| Group | Subsidy per household | Spending | Share of spending |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Bottom | Rp<InlineMath math="800{,}000" /> | Rp<InlineMath math="20" /> million | <InlineMath math="4\%" /> |
| Top | Rp<InlineMath math="4" /> million | Rp<InlineMath math="160" /> million | <InlineMath math="2.5\%" /> |

The statement that the middle group gets about a third can't be concluded. Its share is <InlineMath math="17\%" />, about a sixth.

The statement that removing the subsidy certainly won't hurt the bottom group can't be concluded. The economist warns that other prices usually rise with transport costs, so the table's arithmetic **"belum menangkap seluruh dampaknya"** (doesn't yet capture the full impact).

# Answer

**The table below summarizes the verdict for each statement**.

| Statement | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The top two groups get more than half the subsidy | Ya |
| Bottom households receive more | Tidak |
| The subsidy matters more to bottom households' budgets | Ya |
| The middle group gets about a third | Tidak |
| Removing the subsidy certainly won't hurt the bottom group | Tidak |
