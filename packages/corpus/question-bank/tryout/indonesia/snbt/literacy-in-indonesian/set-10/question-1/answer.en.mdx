Paragraph one says the algae inside coral tissue give the coral **its colour**. Paragraph two explains that when the water is too warm, the coral **expels the algae**. Without them, **the white limestone skeleton shows** through the clear tissue.

- The passage does not say heat dissolves the skeleton's colour.
- Other algae grow on reefs that have already died. They do not cause the whiteness.
- White mucus is not mentioned.
- The tissue is described as clear, not thickened.

So bleached coral looks white because it expels its algae and the white limestone skeleton shows through.
