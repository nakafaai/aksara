Text 1 stresses the **reasons** for return. Text 2 stresses the **preparation**.

| Idea | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taken by force | Teks 1 (Text 1) | Text 1: the objects were **taken by force in colonial times**. |
| Need stable temperature and humidity | Teks 2 (Text 2) | Text 2 names the need for **storage with stable temperature and humidity**. |
| Most meaningful to their makers | Teks 1 (Text 1) | Text 1: their meaning is **deepest for the communities that made them**. |
| Indonesian museums are ready | Teks 1 (Text 1) | Text 1 says so. Text 2 says **not every museum** has the facilities needed. |

The fourth idea is the easiest to mix up, because both texts discuss museums but judge them in opposite ways.
