# Given

The story provides Rani's two ages and an intervening discovery:

- When she waited with the small cake on her birthday, Rani **"was nine years old"**.
- On the afternoon she returns to the bench, Rani **"is already twenty-six years old"**.
- **"Years later"** after that birthday, she found a newspaper clipping in her father's drawer.

# Asked

The approximate number of years between Rani's birthday wait and her return, plus the event that happened in between.

# Solution

Subtract her earlier age from her current age and verify the **sequence of events**.

## Elapsed years and intervening event

The time span between the birthday and her return is:

<BlockMath math="26 - 9 = 17" />

Because she returns carrying **"the same small cake"**, **finding the newspaper clipping in her father's drawer** clearly happened after that birthday and before her return.

Check why the other options fail:

| Distractor | Why it fails |
| --- | --- |
| Her father coming home in a wet uniform | That occurred on the night of the birthday itself, not afterward. |
| <InlineMath math="26" /> years elapsed | <InlineMath math="26" /> is Rani's current age, not the difference. |
| <InlineMath math="9" /> years elapsed | <InlineMath math="9" /> was her age on the birthday, not the elapsed time. |
| The rider breaking through the gate | That accident occurred on the birthday afternoon itself, not later. |

> Separate current age, past age, and elapsed difference carefully, and distinguish events on the birthday itself from discoveries years later.

# Answer

Roughly **<InlineMath math="17" /> years** passed between those two afternoons, and in between **Rani found the newspaper clipping in her father's drawer**.
