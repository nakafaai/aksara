The teacher **does not lead from the front** and does not join in. The pupil tries alone, and the teacher **helps only when the pupil is about to give up**. Following from behind and giving encouragement matches *tut wuri handayani*, **from behind, giving encouragement**.

- Setting an example in front means showing first, but this teacher lets the pupil try.
- Building spirit in the middle means working alongside the pupil, but this teacher does not join in the experiment.
- Giving orders contradicts the among system, which rejects the teacher as a ruler.
- Punishment is not part of the motto.

So the teacher's approach best matches the part of the motto meaning from behind, giving encouragement.
