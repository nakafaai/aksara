The last stanza repeats Grandfather's habit in the same words: **every Monday morning, seven turns, no more**. Now the speaker does it. Having done it, the speaker **begins to understand** why Grandfather counted softly, like someone praying. A habit once only watched now **feels meaningful**.

- Grandfather's hearing is never mentioned.
- The speaker continues the habit.
- No word suggests the speaker feels forced.
- The number of turns is given in stanza one, and Mother does not speak about it.

So the last lines mean that the speaker begins to feel the meaning of the habit after carrying it out.
