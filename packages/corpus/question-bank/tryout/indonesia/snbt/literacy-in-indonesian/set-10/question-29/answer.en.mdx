Mother **refuses to fix** the late clock. It is the clock Grandfather used to wind, and it began running late after he died. By letting it **keep its own time**, she **keeps something tied to Grandfather** unchanged.

- She refuses to fix the clock. It is not that she cannot.
- Wanting a new clock contradicts her choice to leave the old one.
- Nothing suggests she is annoyed.
- She does not say time is unimportant, only leaves one clock as it is.

So her words show that she wants to keep the memory of Grandfather as it is.
