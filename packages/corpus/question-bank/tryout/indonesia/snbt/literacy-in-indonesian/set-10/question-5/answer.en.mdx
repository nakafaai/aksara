The author gives **two measures**: position in the chain of stopovers and roosting sites.

- **Position**. A mudflat that **"memiliki banyak pengganti dalam jangkauan terbang mungkin dapat hilang dengan kerugian kecil"** (has many substitutes within flying range might be lost at small cost), while **"satu-satunya persinggahan di suatu ruas dapat memutus perjalanan ribuan burung"** (the only stopover on a stretch can cut the journey of thousands of birds). P is the only stopover, so its loss costs most.
- **Roosts**. **"Hamparan lumpur yang luas belum tentu berguna bila tidak ada tempat bertengger yang aman di dekatnya"** (a wide mudflat isn't necessarily useful without a safe roost nearby). Reclaiming R cripples its substitute too.

Q has two substitutes, so it costs least, as long as the roosts near those substitutes stay safe.

The other options are wrong.

- Size doesn't decide. Losing P breaks the chain.
- R's substitute loses its roost, so its wide mudflat may not be useful.
- Birds can only use stopovers **"yang terjangkau dengan bahan bakar yang tersisa"** (reachable with the fuel left), so distance still matters.
- The choice is right, but the reason is wrong. The author calls the cost **"kecil"** (small), not zero, and what decides is the substitutes, not size.

So the mudflat that costs least is Q, since two substitutes remain within flying range, as long as the roosts near them stay safe.
