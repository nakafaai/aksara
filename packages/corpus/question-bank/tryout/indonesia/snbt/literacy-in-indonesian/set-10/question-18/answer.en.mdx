# Given

**An official proposes** replacing broad subsidies with direct cash aid for the bottom two household groups using the table's figures.

# Asked

Whether each statement about total aid cost and per-household gains versus lost subsidies is true or false.

# Solution

> Calculate the total cost for the bottom two groups first, then compare each household's direct aid straight against the subsidy it loses.

First work out the cost of the proposal and the subsidy per household in the two bottom groups.

The statement that the aid costs less than a quarter of the subsidy budget is true. Rp<InlineMath math="24" /> trillion is less than a quarter of Rp<InlineMath math="100" /> trillion, which is Rp<InlineMath math="25" /> trillion.

The statement that the second group receives less is false. Its aid equals the subsidy lost, Rp<InlineMath math="1.2" /> million.

The statement that the bottom group receives more is true. Rp<InlineMath math="1.2" /> million is more than Rp<InlineMath math="800{,}000" />.

The statement that the middle group also receives aid is false. The aid is only for **"dua kelompok terbawah"** (the two bottom groups).
