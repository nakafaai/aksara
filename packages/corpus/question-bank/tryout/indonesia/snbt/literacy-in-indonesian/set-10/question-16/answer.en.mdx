# Given

A total subsidy budget of Rp<InlineMath math="100" /> trillion spans five equal quintiles out of <InlineMath math="50" /> million households, placing <InlineMath math="10" /> million households in each group.

- **Middle quintile**: receives <InlineMath math="17\%" /> of total subsidies with average annual spending of Rp<InlineMath math="45" /> million.
- **Fourth quintile**: receives <InlineMath math="23\%" /> of total subsidies with average annual spending of Rp<InlineMath math="70" /> million.

# Asked

The per-household subsidy share of annual spending for the middle and fourth quintiles and which group faces the heavier burden if subsidies end.

# Solution

Compute the **rupiah subsidy per household** first, then express it as a share of annual spending.

## Rupiah subsidy per household

Divide each **quintile share of total subsidies** by <InlineMath math="10" /> million households:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Middle} = \frac{17\% \times 100\text{ trillion}}{10\text{ million}} = 1{,}7\text{ million}" />
<BlockMath math="\text{Fourth} = \frac{23\% \times 100\text{ trillion}}{10\text{ million}} = 2{,}3\text{ million}" />
</MathContainer>

## Share of annual spending

Compare each **per-household subsidy** to its annual spending:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\text{Middle} = \frac{1{,}7}{45} \times 100\% \approx 3{,}8\%" />
<BlockMath math="\text{Fourth} = \frac{2{,}3}{70} \times 100\% \approx 3{,}3\%" />
</MathContainer>

Removing the subsidy without replacement hits the group losing the larger share of its budget harder, namely the **middle quintile**.

Check why the other options fail:

| Distractor | Why it fails |
| --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="17\%" /> and <InlineMath math="23\%" /> | Those are shares of total national subsidy, not household spending. |
| Fourth quintile hit harder due to larger rupiah amount | Its annual spending is much larger too, lowering its relative burden. |
| Right percentages, reversed group | <InlineMath math="3{,}8\%" /> is greater than <InlineMath math="3{,}3\%" />. |
| <InlineMath math="37{,}8\%" /> and <InlineMath math="32{,}9\%" /> | Decimal point shifted by a factor of ten. |

> Measure policy burden by the percentage of household spending lost rather than raw rupiah amounts alone.

# Answer

The subsidy equals about **<InlineMath math="3{,}8\%" />** of middle-quintile spending and **<InlineMath math="3{,}3\%" />** of fourth-quintile spending, so the **middle quintile bears the heavier burden** if subsidies end.
