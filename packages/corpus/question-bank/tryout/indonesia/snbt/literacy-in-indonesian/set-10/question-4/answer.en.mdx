Irradiance is **radiant power per unit area**, measured in watts per square metre. It changes from 580 to 720 W/m², so comparisons across runs also involve different solar conditions.

| Comparison | Temperature difference | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 45 degrees in run three and 60 degrees in run two | <InlineMath math="66-60=6\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}" /> | Both angle and irradiance differ, mixing angle with run conditions |
| 45 and 60 degrees within run three | <InlineMath math="66-64=2\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}" /> | Shared irradiance makes this a better angle comparison |

The definition explains the table's context. It neither converts temperature into energy nor proves that irradiance alone determines temperature.
