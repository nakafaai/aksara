Paragraph three says **bleached coral is not yet dead**. If the water **cools soon**, the algae can return to its tissue and the coral **slowly recovers**. Here the temperature falls a week later, so recovery is still possible.

- White does not mean certain death. The passage says bleached coral is not yet dead.
- Other algae cover reefs that are **already dead**, not coral that can still recover.
- The passage does not say the skeleton dissolves.
- Coral is not said to move.

So the most likely result is that the algae return to the coral's tissue and the coral slowly recovers.
