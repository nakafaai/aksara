# Given

**The passage gives** starting and ending scores for the extra-class group, the comparison group, and the top-scoring group.

# Asked

Whether each statement about point changes and percentage gains from each group's starting score is true or false.

# Solution

> Always divide each group's point change by its own starting score so your percentage comparison stays accurate.

Work out each group's difference and percentage from its **starting** score.

| Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Difference from the comparison group's rise | <InlineMath math="10-6=4" /> points |
| Rise of the extra-class group | <InlineMath math="\frac{10}{45}\approx22.2\%" /> |
| Change of the top group | <InlineMath math="88-92=-4" /> points |
| Rise of the comparison group | <InlineMath math="\frac{6}{46}\approx13\%" /> |

The statement about roughly <InlineMath math="4" /> points is true. The teacher says what can be put down to the classes is **"selisihnya dengan kelompok pembanding"** (the difference from the comparison group), <InlineMath math="10-6=4" />.

The statement that the extra-class group rose by more than <InlineMath math="25\%" /> is false. Its rise is about <InlineMath math="22.2\%" />.

The statement that the top group fell <InlineMath math="4" /> points is true. It went from <InlineMath math="92" /> to <InlineMath math="88" />.

The statement that the comparison group rose by more than <InlineMath math="15\%" /> is false. Its rise is about <InlineMath math="13\%" />.
