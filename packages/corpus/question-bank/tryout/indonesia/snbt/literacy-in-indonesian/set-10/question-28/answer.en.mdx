The lines follow **"Kini Kakek sudah tiada. / Jarumnya masih berjalan, sedikit terlambat"** (Grandfather is gone now. The hands still move, a little late). The clock is compared to **a guest who will not leave until the host sees them to the door**. The host is Grandfather, who always wound the clock. The clock that keeps going is pictured as **still waiting for Grandfather** to see it off.

- There is no real guest in the poem. The guest is only an image.
- The clock is still running, so it is not broken and stopped.
- Mother says nothing about guests.
- The poem does not describe Grandfather's attitude to guests.

So the lines picture the clock running on as if it were still waiting for Grandfather.
