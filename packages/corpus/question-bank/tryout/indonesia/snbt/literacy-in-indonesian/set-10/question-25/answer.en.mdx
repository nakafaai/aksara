Paragraph two says that in the Netherlands Soewardi **studied European ideas about education**. Yet he did **not want simply to copy** European schools, because education **had to be rooted in the nation's own culture**. He took what was useful without giving up those roots.

- He did not reject the ideas outright, since he studied them.
- He did not rank them higher. His own culture stayed the foundation.
- The passage does not limit the ideas to children of European descent.
- He studied them in exile, **before** founding Taman Siswa.

So Ki Hadjar studied European ideas but kept education grounded in his own culture.
