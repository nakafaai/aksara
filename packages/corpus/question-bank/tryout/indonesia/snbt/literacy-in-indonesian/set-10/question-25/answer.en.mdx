Apply the text's three reasons to the organisation.

- **Already known across regions**. Malay was chosen because **"banyak orang dari berbagai daerah sudah mengenalnya"** (many people from various regions already knew it). This consideration fits.
- **Doesn't mark status**. A language without strict speech levels is **"lebih cocok"** (better suited) to a movement that wants all members equal. This consideration fits.
- **Doesn't favour one group**. Choosing one group's language **"dapat terkesan mengutamakan satu suku"** (could seem to favour one group). This consideration fits.

The other two go against the text's reasons.

- Number of native speakers wasn't the basis. The language with the most native speakers, Javanese, wasn't chosen.
- The leader's language favours one person and their home group.

So the fitting considerations are a language already known across regions, that doesn't mark the listener's status, and that doesn't favour any member's home group.
