# Given

Sentence (8) of the *food-bank* passage reports **Rina Ayu's opinion** that <Highlight>bank makanan dapat mengurangi makanan yang terbuang di kota itu</Highlight> (the food bank can reduce wasted food in the city).

# Asked

The statement that most strongly supports Rina Ayu's opinion.

# Solution

Look for **direct empirical evidence that food waste actually dropped** after the food bank opened. Showing that wasted food across the nine participating caterers fell by nearly half directly confirms her claim.

> A claim about measurable impact is strengthened most by direct evidence showing the target metric genuinely fell.

## Evaluating all five statements

| Candidate statement | Effect on Rina Ayu's claim | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Since the food bank started, food discarded by those nine caterers fell by nearly half | Direct proof that discarded food decreased | **Strengthens most** |
| Some recipient families also grow their own vegetables | Does not measure catering food waste | **Irrelevant** |
| The founding students come from various majors | Unrelated to food waste reduction | **Irrelevant** |
| Catering prices rose this year | Concerns pricing, not discarded volume | **Irrelevant** |
| Caterers in other cities do not yet know about food banks | Concerns other cities outside the claim | **Irrelevant** |

# Answer

The strongest supporting statement is ***Sejak bank makanan berdiri, makanan yang dibuang oleh sembilan usaha katering itu berkurang hampir separuhnya*** (Since the food bank opened, food thrown away by those nine caterers dropped by nearly half).
