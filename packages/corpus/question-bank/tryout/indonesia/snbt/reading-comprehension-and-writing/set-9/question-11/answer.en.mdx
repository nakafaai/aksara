# Given

The *silted-pond* passage explains **siltation of Desa Watugede's reservoir** in paragraph one and the villagers' dredging and tree-planting restoration in paragraph two.

# Asked

The author's main purpose in writing the passage.

# Solution

Determine the purpose from **what both paragraphs accomplish together**: explaining why the pond grew shallow and detailing how villagers restored its water capacity.

> An author's purpose unites the core thrust of both paragraphs and excludes topics absent from the text.

## Comparing all five purpose options

| Proposed purpose | Fit with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain why the pond grew shallow and how villagers restored it | Covers siltation, dredging, tree planting, and recovery | **Correct** |
| Urge the government to build new ponds in every village | Neutrally reports one village with no call to action | **Wrong** |
| Tell the history of building Desa Watugede's pond | Construction history is never told | **Wrong** |
| Compare ponds with wells as water sources | Wells are never mentioned | **Wrong** |
| Criticize villagers for cutting down hill trees | No logging by villagers is ever mentioned | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The author's purpose is **to explain why the pond became shallow and how villagers restored it** (*menjelaskan penyebab dangkalnya embung dan upaya warga memulihkannya*).
