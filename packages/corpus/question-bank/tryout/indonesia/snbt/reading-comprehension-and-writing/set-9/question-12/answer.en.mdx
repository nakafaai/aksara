Sentence (5) uses the pair _baik ... atau_. When _baik_ joins two things, both ... and, **it always pairs with _maupun_**, not _atau_, or. The right form is _Baik orang tua maupun anak muda ikut turun membawa cangkul dan ember dari rumah._

So the sentence with a wrongly used word is sentence (5).

- Sentence (4) uses _bersama-sama_ and _mengeruk_ correctly.
