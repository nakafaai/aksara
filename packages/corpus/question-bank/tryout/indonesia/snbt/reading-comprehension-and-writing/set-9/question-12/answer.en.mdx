# Given

Sentence (5) of the *silted-pond* passage mismatches **an Indonesian correlative conjunction pair**: <Highlight>Baik orang tua atau anak muda ikut turun membawa cangkul</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing an improperly used word.

# Solution

Apply **the fixed Indonesian correlative pairing rule**. When *baik* includes both groups together, it must pair with **maupun** (*both ... and ...*), never with *atau*, so standard Indonesian writes *Baik orang tua maupun anak muda ...*.

> Standard Indonesian correlative conjunctions follow fixed pairs: *baik ... maupun ...*, *bukan ... melainkan ...*, and *tidak hanya ... tetapi juga ...*.

## Reviewing word choice across all five sentences

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (1) | *menampung*, *sepanjang* | Standard and accurate | **Correct** |
| Sentence (2) | *Namun*, *makin dangkal* | Standard and accurate | **Correct** |
| Sentence (3) | *Akibatnya*, *sebelum* | Standard and accurate | **Correct** |
| Sentence (4) | *bersama-sama*, *mengeruk* | Standard and accurate | **Correct** |
| Sentence (5) | *Baik ... atau ...* | Must pair *baik* with **maupun**, not *atau* | **Error** |

# Answer

The improperly used word appears in **sentence (5)** (*kalimat (5)*: use *maupun* instead of *atau*).
