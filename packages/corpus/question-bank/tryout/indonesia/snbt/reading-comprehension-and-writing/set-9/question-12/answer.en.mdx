Sentence (5) uses _Masing-masing warga_. The word _masing-masing_, each, **does not go before the noun** it describes. It stands alone or follows the noun, as in _warga masing-masing_. To mean every resident one by one, the right word is _setiap_: _Setiap warga membawa cangkul dan ember dari rumah._

So the sentence with a wrongly used word is sentence (5).
