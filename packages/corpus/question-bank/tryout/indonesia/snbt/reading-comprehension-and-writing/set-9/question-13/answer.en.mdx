# Given

The *silted-pond* passage unfolds across **seven sequential sentences** moving from the pond's former capacity to siltation, community restoration, and renewed dry-season irrigation.

# Asked

The organizational pattern of the passage.

# Solution

Map **each section's role in strict order from sentence (1) to sentence (7)**. The passage opens with the past state, moves to the problem with its cause and effect, explains the villagers' actions, and closes with the result.

> Text organization is identified by tracing the sequence of rhetorical stages from opening to close.

## Mapping the passage structure

| Passage segment | Core content | Structural stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (1) | Pond formerly irrigated gardens all dry season | **Past situation** |
| Sentences (2) and (3) | Hillside mud made the pond shallow so water ran out early | **Problem, cause, and effect** |
| Sentences (4) to (6) | Villagers dredged mud and planted hillside trees | **Community efforts** |
| Sentence (7) | Pond holds more water and keeps gardens watered | **Results** |

The remaining options fail because the passage never starts with results, actions, or an opinion, and discusses only one pond.

# Answer

The passage follows the pattern **past situation, problem with its cause and effect, villagers' efforts, then results** (*keadaan dulu, masalah beserta penyebab dan akibatnya, upaya warga, lalu hasilnya*).
