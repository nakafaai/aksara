# Given

Sentence (3) of the *fire-volunteers* passage stacks **two synonymous approximation markers**: <Highlight>beranggotakan kurang lebih sekitar <InlineMath math="30" /> orang</Highlight> (approximately around thirty people).

# Asked

The revision that makes sentence (3) an effective standard Indonesian sentence.

# Solution

Remove **the redundant pairing of *kurang lebih* and *sekitar***. Keeping a single approximation word, **sekitar** (around), avoids wordiness and keeps the clause cleanly structured without an unwanted comma before *yang*.

> Effective prose avoids stacking synonymous approximators such as *kurang lebih sekitar* or *kira-kira sekitar*.

## Checking conciseness across all five revisions

| Candidate revision | Conciseness and clause structure | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *... beranggotakan sekitar <InlineMath math="30" /> orang.* | Uses one clean approximator with no redundancy | **Effective** |
| *... kurang lebih sekitar tiga puluhan orang.* | Stacks three approximation markers at once | **Ineffective** |
| *... kira-kira sekitar <InlineMath math="30" /> orang.* | Still doubles two synonymous approximators | **Ineffective** |
| *... pemadam, yang beranggotakan kurang lebih sekitar <InlineMath math="30" /> orang.* | Keeps redundancy and adds a faulty comma before *yang* | **Ineffective** |
| *... sekitar kurang lebih <InlineMath math="30" /> orang.* | Merely swaps the order of redundant words | **Ineffective** |

# Answer

The effective revision is ***Sejak itu, warga membentuk regu relawan pemadam yang beranggotakan sekitar <InlineMath math="30" /> orang***.
