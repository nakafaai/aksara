Sentence (3) uses both _kurang lebih_, more or less, and _sekitar_, about. Both mark an inexact number, so **using both is redundant**. An effective sentence needs only one.

So the right revision is _Sejak itu, warga membentuk regu relawan pemadam yang beranggotakan sekitar 30 orang._

- _kurang lebih sekitar tiga puluhan_ adds a third marker of vagueness, the suffix _-an_.
- _kira-kira sekitar_ also stacks two expressions with the same meaning.
- A comma before _yang_ does not fix _kurang lebih sekitar_.
- Reversing it to _sekitar kurang lebih_ still stacks the two expressions.
