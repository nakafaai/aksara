# Given

Row I of the *tooth-brushing* table records **<InlineMath math="30" /> examined students** and **<InlineMath math="12" /> students with cavities** after six months.

# Asked

The percentage of Grade I students with cavities after six months.

# Solution

Divide **the number of Grade I students with cavities after six months by the total Grade I students examined**, then multiply by <InlineMath math="100\%" />.

<BlockMath math="\frac{12}{30} \times 100\% = 40\%" />

> A percentage equals the target part divided by its matching class total times <InlineMath math="100\%" />.

## Checking all five percentage choices

| Option | Calculation behind it | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="40\%" /> | Grade I after six months (<InlineMath math="\frac{12}{30} \times 100\%" />) | **Correct** |
| <InlineMath math="50\%" /> | Grade II at start of year (<InlineMath math="\frac{16}{32} \times 100\%" />) | **Wrong** |
| <InlineMath math="60\%" /> | Grade I at start of year (<InlineMath math="\frac{18}{30} \times 100\%" />) | **Wrong** |
| <InlineMath math="70\%" /> | Matches no Grade I ratio in the table | **Wrong** |
| <InlineMath math="80\%" /> | Matches no Grade I ratio in the table | **Wrong** |

# Answer

After six months, **<InlineMath math="40\%" />** of Grade I students had cavities.
