In class I, $$30$$ students were checked and $$12$$ still had cavities after six months. **A percentage is the part divided by the whole, times one hundred percent**.

$$\frac{12}{30} \times 100\% = 40\%$$

So the percentage is $$40\%$$.

- $$50\%$$ is class II at the start of the year, $$\frac{16}{32}$$.
- $$60\%$$ is class I at the start of the year, $$\frac{18}{30}$$.
- $$70\%$$ and $$80\%$$ match no class I figure.
