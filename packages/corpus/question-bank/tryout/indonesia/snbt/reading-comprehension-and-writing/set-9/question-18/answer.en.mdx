# Given

Sentence (3) of the *tooth-brushing* passage contains **a nonstandard loanword spelling**: <Highlight>Kegiatan itu tidak hanya bersifat teoritis</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing a nonstandard Indonesian spelling.

# Solution

Check loanword spellings against **official KBBI entries**. The word *teoritis* in sentence (3) is nonstandard, because the Dutch-derived standard Indonesian spelling uses *e* before *-tis* as **teoretis**.

> Standard Indonesian spells *-etis* loanwords like *teoretis* with *e* rather than *i*.

# Answer

The nonstandard spelling appears in **sentence (3)** (*kalimat (3)*: *teoretis*, not *teoritis*).
