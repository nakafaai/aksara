A good title **covers the whole reading, no broader and no narrower**. The first paragraph gives the problem of narrow alleys and the forming of a volunteer fire crew. The second gives how the crew works and what happened at a fire.

So the best title is _Regu Relawan Pemadam Menjaga Kampung dengan Gang Sempit_, a volunteer fire crew guards a village of narrow alleys.

- _Kebakaran di Indonesia_, fires in Indonesia, is too broad, since the reading covers one village.
- The burning stove is only one event, in sentence (6).
- The fire engine cannot even get in and is not the topic.
- The notice board is only a detail of sentence (5).
