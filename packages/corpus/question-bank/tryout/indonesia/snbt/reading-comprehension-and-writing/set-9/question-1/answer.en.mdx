# Given

The *fire-volunteers* passage describes **Kampung Pasirluhur's narrow alleys** that block fire trucks in paragraph one and the volunteer firefighting squad's rapid five-minute response in paragraph two.

# Asked

The most fitting title for the entire passage.

# Solution

Pick the title that **combines the volunteer firefighting squad with the narrow-alley challenge across both paragraphs**, neither broader than one neighborhood nor narrowed to a single object.

> A strong title captures the exact scope of both paragraphs without broadening beyond the text or shrinking to a minor detail.

## Comparing all five title candidates

| Proposed title | Fit with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Regu Relawan Pemadam Menjaga Kampung dengan Gang Sempit* | Unites the volunteer squad and the narrow-alley problem | **Best fit** |
| *Kebakaran di Indonesia* | Far too broad for one neighborhood | **Too broad** |
| *Kompor yang Terbakar* | Covers only one incident from sentence (6) | **Too narrow** |
| *Mobil Pemadam Kebakaran* | Fire trucks cannot even enter and are not the main focus | **Off-topic** |
| *Papan Pengumuman Balai RW* | Covers only one detail from sentence (5) | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The best title is ***Regu Relawan Pemadam Menjaga Kampung dengan Gang Sempit*** (Volunteer Fire Squad Guards a Village with Narrow Alleys).
