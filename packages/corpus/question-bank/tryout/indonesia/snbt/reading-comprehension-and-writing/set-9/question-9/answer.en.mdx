# Given

Sentence (5) of the *food-bank* passage leaves **a causal-conjunction blank**: <Highlight>Bank makanan itu hanya menerima makanan yang belum disajikan kepada tamu ... makanan yang sudah disajikan mudah tercemar</Highlight> (The food bank only accepts food not yet served to guests ... served food spoils easily).

# Asked

The conjunction that properly fills the blank in sentence (5).

# Solution

Recognize **the rule-and-reason relationship** in sentence (5). Easy contamination of already-served food is the exact reason behind the policy, so the causal conjunction **karena** (because) is required.

> When the second clause gives the reason behind the rule in the first clause, join them with a causal conjunction like *karena*.

## Testing all five conjunction choices

| Conjunction | Logical relation | Fit in sentence (5) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *karena* (because) | Gives the reason | Directly explains why served food is not accepted | **Correct** |
| *sehingga* (so that / thus) | Gives a result | Illogically suggests the rule causes contamination | **Wrong** |
| *meskipun* (although) | Marks contrast | The reason supports the rule rather than opposing it | **Wrong** |
| *agar* (in order to) | Marks purpose | Nobody aims for food to become contaminated | **Wrong** |
| *lalu* (then) | Marks time sequence | Fails to express a reason | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The right conjunction for sentence (5) is ***karena*** (because).
