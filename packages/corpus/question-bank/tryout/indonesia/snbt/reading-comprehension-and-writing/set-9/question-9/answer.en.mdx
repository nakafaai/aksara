Sentence (5) gives a rule: the food bank takes only food not yet served. The part after the blank, _makanan yang sudah disajikan mudah tercemar_, food already served is easily contaminated, **is the reason for the rule**. A cause is marked by the conjunction _karena_, because.

So the right word is _karena_.

- _sehingga_, so that, reverses the link, as if the rule made food easily contaminated.
- _meskipun_, although, marks a contrast, but the two parts agree.
- _agar_, in order to, marks a purpose, and no one wants contaminated food.
- _lalu_, then, marks a sequence, not a cause.
