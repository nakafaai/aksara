# Given

Sentence (5) of the *fire-volunteers* passage contains **a transposed loanword spelling**: <Highlight>Sekertaris RW mencatat letak setiap alat pemadam</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing a nonstandard Indonesian spelling.

# Solution

Check each candidate against **standard KBBI dictionary spelling**. The word *Sekertaris* in sentence (5) reverses the syllable letters under spoken influence, whereas the official KBBI spelling is **sekretaris** (*-kre-*, not *-ker-*).

> Standard Indonesian preserves the *-kre-* letter order in *sekretaris* rather than the colloquial transposition *sekertaris*.
