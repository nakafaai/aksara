# Given

Sentence (5) reports **the smallest drop in Grade III**, and sentence (6) reveals <Highlight>Sebagian siswa kelas itu sering terlambat sehingga melewatkan kegiatan sikat gigi bersama</Highlight> (some students in that class often arrived late and missed group tooth-brushing).

# Asked

The relationship between sentence (6) and sentence (5).

# Solution

Connect **the outcome in sentence (5) with the reason in sentence (6)**. Missing morning brushing explains why Grade III improved the least, so sentence (6) **explains the cause of the event reported in sentence (5)**.

> When a following sentence supplies why a reported result happened, it functions as a causal explanation.

## Comparing all five proposed relationships

| Proposed relationship | Match with sentences (5) and (6) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explains the cause of the event in sentence (5) | Missing brushing explains Grade III's smaller drop | **Correct** |
| Contradicts sentence (5) | Supports and explains sentence (5) rather than opposing it | **Wrong** |
| Concludes that brushing is useless | All four grades saw declines in cavities | **Wrong** |
| Gives an example of Grade IV students | Discusses Grade III, not Grade IV | **Wrong** |
| Restates sentence (5) in other words | Adds a cause instead of merely paraphrasing | **Wrong** |

# Answer

Sentence (6) **explains the cause of the event in sentence (5)** (*Kalimat (6) menjelaskan penyebab peristiwa dalam kalimat (5)*).
