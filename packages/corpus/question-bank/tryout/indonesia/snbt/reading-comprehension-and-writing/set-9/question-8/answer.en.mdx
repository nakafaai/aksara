# Given

Sentence (3) identifies **the food collected by the food bank** as <Highlight>sisa makanan layak santap dari usaha katering</Highlight> (edible leftover food from catering businesses), and sentence (4) says *Makanan itu* (That food) is inspected, repacked, and distributed.

# Asked

The referent of *Makanan itu* (That food) in sentence (4).

# Solution

Follow **the demonstrative *itu* straight back to sentence (3)**. The exact food being inspected, repacked, and distributed is **the edible catering leftovers collected from catering businesses**.

> An opening demonstrative noun phrase refers directly to the specific object introduced in the preceding sentence.

## Checking all five candidate referents

| Candidate referent | Alignment with sentences (3) through (6) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edible leftover food picked up from catering businesses | Exact antecedent from sentence (3) | **Correct** |
| Food purchased by families in need | Families receive the food rather than buy it | **Wrong** |
| Food already served to guests | Explicitly refused under sentence (5) | **Wrong** |
| Smelly or slimy food | Immediately rejected under sentence (6) | **Wrong** |
| Food sold by students | Students distribute it rather than sell it | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The phrase *Makanan itu* refers to **the edible leftover food collected from catering businesses** (*sisa makanan layak santap yang dijemput dari usaha katering*).
