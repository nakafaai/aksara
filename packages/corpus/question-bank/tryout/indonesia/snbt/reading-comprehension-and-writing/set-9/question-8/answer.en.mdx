The demonstrative _itu_ **points to what was just mentioned**. Sentence (3) says the food bank _menjemput sisa makanan layak santap dari usaha katering_, picks up leftover food still fit to eat from caterers. Sentence (4) then says _Makanan itu_, that food, is checked, repacked, and handed out.

So _Makanan itu_ refers to the leftover food still fit to eat picked up from caterers.

- The families do not buy the food. They receive it.
- Food already served is not accepted, according to sentence (5).
- Food that smells or is slimy is rejected, according to sentence (6).
- The students do not sell the food. They give it away.
