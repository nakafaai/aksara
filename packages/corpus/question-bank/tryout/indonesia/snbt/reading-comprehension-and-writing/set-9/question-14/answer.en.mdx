# Given

Paragraph two of the *silted-pond* passage pairs **both restoration actions with their outcome**: dredging mud and planting hillside trees so <Highlight>embung dapat menampung air lebih banyak dan kebun warga tetap teraliri sampai akhir musim kemarau</Highlight> (the pond holds more water and gardens stay irrigated through the dry season).

# Asked

The best conclusion supported by the passage.

# Solution

Synthesize **both community measures and their stated payoff** accurately, rejecting choices that exaggerate maintenance-free permanence or contradict facts in the passage.

> A sound conclusion brings together the text's key actions and verified outcome without stretching beyond the evidence.

## Checking all five candidate conclusions

| Candidate conclusion | Alignment with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dredging mud and planting trees restored the pond so water again lasts all dry season | Accurately unites both efforts and sentence (7)'s outcome | **Correct** |
| The pond no longer needs any maintenance | Overstated because rain can still wash soil down over time | **Wrong** |
| Mud in the pond came from villagers' gardens | Contradicts sentence (2): mud came from surrounding hills | **Wrong** |
| Rain no longer falls on the surrounding hills | Unsupported and contradicts why trees were planted | **Wrong** |
| Gardens no longer need pond water | Contradicts sentence (7), which says gardens stay watered by the pond | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The best conclusion is that **dredging mud and planting trees restored the pond so its water again suffices throughout the dry season** (*Pengerukan lumpur dan penanaman pohon memulihkan embung sehingga airnya kembali cukup sepanjang musim kemarau*).
