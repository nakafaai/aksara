# Given

Sentence (2) of the *fire-volunteers* passage uses **a figurative fire verb** in <Highlight>api sudah melahap lima rumah sebelum petugas sampai</Highlight> (the fire had already devoured five houses before crews arrived).

# Asked

The meaning of *melahap* in sentence (2).

# Solution

Interpret *melahap* through **its subject *api* (fire) and object *lima rumah* (five houses)**. Used figuratively for fire, *melahap* means **burning down completely** (*membakar habis*), rather than literally eating food.

> Always choose the figurative sense that fits the sentence's actual subject and object rather than an out-of-context literal gloss.

## Comparing all five meaning options

| Proposed meaning | Fit with fire and five houses | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burn down completely (*membakar habis*) | Exact figurative meaning of fire *devouring* houses | **Correct** |
| Extinguish (*memadamkan*) | Opposite of what fire does | **Wrong** |
| Eat food greedily (*memakan makanan dengan rakus*) | Literal meaning for eating, not figurative fire context | **Wrong** |
| Approach (*mendekati*) | Far too mild for destroying five homes | **Wrong** |
| Illuminate (*menerangi*) | Ignores the destructive fire context | **Wrong** |

# Answer

In sentence (2), *melahap* means **to burn down completely** (*membakar habis*).
