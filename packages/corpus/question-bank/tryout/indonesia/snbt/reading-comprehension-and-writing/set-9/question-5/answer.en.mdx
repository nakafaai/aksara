A word's meaning is set **by the context of its sentence**. In sentence (2), what does the _melahap_ is fire, and what gets devoured is five houses. Fire does not literally eat, so the word is figurative for fire burning those houses down.

So _melahap_ in sentence (2) means to burn down completely.

- _Memadamkan_, to put out, is the opposite of what fire does.
- _Memakan makanan dengan rakus_, to eat greedily, is the literal sense, which does not fit fire and houses.
- _Mendekati_, to approach, is too weak, since the houses were already burned.
- _Menerangi_, to light up, does not fit the damage of a fire.
