# Given

Sentence (8) of the *food-bank* passage omits **commas around an appositive phrase**: <Highlight>Rina Ayu ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu berpendapat bahwa bank makanan dapat mengurangi makanan yang terbuang</Highlight>.

# Asked

The properly punctuated revision of sentence (8) under Indonesian EYD rules.

# Solution

Enclose **the appositive *ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu* (leader of the student group) in a matching pair of commas**, and omit any comma before *bahwa* (that) introducing the reported opinion.

> Under Indonesian EYD, an appositive identifying a subject takes both an opening and a closing comma, while *bahwa* takes no preceding comma.

## Auditing punctuation across all five options

| Candidate revision | EYD punctuation check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Rina Ayu, ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu, berpendapat bahwa ...* | Paired commas frame the appositive and no comma precedes *bahwa* | **Correct** |
| *Rina Ayu, ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu berpendapat ...* | Missing closing comma after the appositive | **Wrong** |
| *Rina Ayu ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu, berpendapat ...* | Missing opening comma before the appositive | **Wrong** |
| *Rina Ayu, ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu, berpendapat, bahwa ...* | Faulty comma separates verb *berpendapat* from *bahwa* | **Wrong** |
| *Rina Ayu: ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu berpendapat ...* | Colon cannot introduce an appositive | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The correct punctuation revision is ***Rina Ayu, ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu, berpendapat bahwa bank makanan dapat mengurangi makanan yang terbuang di kota itu***.
