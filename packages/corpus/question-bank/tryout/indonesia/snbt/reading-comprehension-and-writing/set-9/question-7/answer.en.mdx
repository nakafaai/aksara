The phrase _ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu_, the head of that student group, is **extra information that says who Rina Ayu is**. EYD says such an appositive is set off by two commas, one before and one after. The _bahwa_, that, introducing her view takes no comma.

So the right revision is _Rina Ayu, ketua kelompok mahasiswa itu, berpendapat bahwa bank makanan dapat mengurangi makanan yang terbuang di kota itu._

- Only the opening comma is there, so the appositive is never closed.
- Only the closing comma is there, so the appositive is never opened.
- A comma before _bahwa_ splits the predicate from what she thinks.
- A colon is not used to introduce an appositive like this.
