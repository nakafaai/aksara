# Given

The *fire-volunteers* passage links **narrow alleys** to blocked fire trucks in sentence (1) and the volunteer squad's five-minute extinguishment in sentence (7), after which the subdistrict head urged other narrow-alley neighborhoods to copy the squad.

# Asked

The most likely reason the subdistrict head asked other narrow-alley neighborhoods to copy the volunteer squad.

# Solution

Connect **the shared access bottleneck with the squad's proven rapid response**. Because fire trucks cannot enter narrow alleys, other neighborhoods with narrow alleys face the same delay risk and need **an on-site firefighting squad inside the neighborhood**.

> A valid inference links a recurring constraint in the text to the proven remedy that solved it.

## Evaluating all five candidate reasons

| Candidate reason | Alignment with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrow-alley neighborhoods are hard for fire trucks to reach and need an internal squad | Directly connects sentences (1), (2), (7), and (8) | **Correct** |
| Other neighborhoods already have too many volunteers | Never mentioned in the passage | **Wrong** |
| Fire trucks are no longer used in the subdistrict | Fire trucks still operate but cannot fit into narrow alleys | **Wrong** |
| The volunteer squad is slower than regular firefighters | Contradicts sentence (7): they put out the fire in five minutes | **Wrong** |
| Stoves in every resident's home frequently catch fire | Only a single stove fire is described | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The most likely reason is that **neighborhoods with narrow alleys are hard for fire trucks to reach and therefore need a fire squad inside the neighborhood** (*Kampung yang gangnya sempit sulit dijangkau mobil pemadam sehingga memerlukan regu pemadam di dalam kampung*).
