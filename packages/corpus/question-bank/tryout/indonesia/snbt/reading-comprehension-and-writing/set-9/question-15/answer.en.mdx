# Given

Sentence (2) of the *silted-pond* passage states **the exact cause of siltation**: <Highlight>embung itu makin dangkal karena lumpur dari bukit di sekitarnya terbawa air hujan</Highlight> (the pond grew shallower because mud from surrounding hills was washed in by rain).

# Asked

Why the reservoir in Desa Watugede became shallower.

# Solution

Read **the explicit causal clause following *karena* (because) in sentence (2)**. Rainwater washed mud down from nearby hillsides into the pond, steadily filling its bed.

> Explicit cause questions map directly to the text's stated *karena* clause.

## Matching all five options against sentence

| Proposed cause | Match with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mud from surrounding hills was carried in by rainwater | Stated verbatim in sentence (2) | **Correct** |
| Villagers drew too much water | Never mentioned as the cause | **Wrong** |
| Dry seasons grew longer | Not stated anywhere in the text | **Wrong** |
| Too many trees grew on the hillside | Trees were planted to hold soil back, not cause siltation | **Wrong** |
| Villagers' gardens expanded | Garden area is never discussed | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The pond grew shallow because **mud from surrounding hills was washed in by rainwater** (*lumpur dari bukit di sekitarnya terbawa air hujan*).
