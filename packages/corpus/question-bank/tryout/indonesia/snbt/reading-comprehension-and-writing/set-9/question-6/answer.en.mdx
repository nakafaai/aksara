# Given

The *food-bank* passage pairs **discarded catering leftovers in Kota Purbaya** with struggling families in paragraph one, then details the student food bank's safety rules and weekly reach in paragraph two.

# Asked

The main idea of the entire passage.

# Solution

Choose the statement that **captures the entire student food-bank model across both paragraphs**: collecting safe leftover catering food and distributing it to families in need.

> A passage's main idea unifies both paragraphs rather than repeating a single background fact or rule.

## Comparing all five options

| Proposed main idea | Coverage of the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A student-founded food bank distributes safe catering leftovers to families in need | Unites founders, food source, quality check, and beneficiaries | **Correct** |
| Much catering food is thrown away each night | Background problem from sentence (1) only | **Too narrow** |
| Families in Kota Purbaya still struggle to buy food | Background detail from sentence (2) only | **Too narrow** |
| Spoiled or slimy food is immediately rejected | Single screening rule from sentence (6) only | **Too narrow** |
| Nine catering businesses donate regularly | Single figure from sentence (7) only | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The main idea is that **the student-founded food bank channels safe leftover catering food to families in need** (*Bank makanan yang didirikan mahasiswa menyalurkan sisa makanan katering yang layak santap kepada keluarga yang membutuhkan*).
