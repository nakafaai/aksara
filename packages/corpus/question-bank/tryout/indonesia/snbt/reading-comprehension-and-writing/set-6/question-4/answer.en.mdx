# Given

Sentence (3) of the *senior-exercise* passage states <Highlight>Sebelum senam dimulai, kader posyandu memeriksa tekanan darah setiap peserta dan mencatatnya di sebuah buku kecil</Highlight> (Before exercise starts, health-post volunteers check each participant's blood pressure and record it in a small notebook).

# Asked

The referent of the suffix pronoun *-nya* in *mencatatnya* (record it) in sentence (3).

# Solution

Match *-nya* to **the logical object of the verb *mencatat* (to record)**. After volunteers measure each participant's blood pressure, what gets written into the notebook is **each participant's blood-pressure reading**, not a person or location.

> An object pronoun suffix like *-nya* points back to the exact thing acted upon by its verb.

## Checking all five candidate referents

| Candidate referent | Role in the sentence | Fits as object of *record* | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Each participant's blood-pressure check result | Reading obtained during the check | Exact information written in the notebook | **Correct** |
| Each exercise participant | Person being checked | Volunteers record the reading, not the person | **Wrong** |
| Health-post volunteers | Subject doing the recording | Agent of the verb, not its object | **Wrong** |
| Health-post courtyard | Exercise venue from sentence (1) | Location, not recorded data | **Wrong** |
| Small notebook | Container where notes go | Appears after *mencatatnya* as the writing surface | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The pronoun *-nya* refers to **each participant's blood-pressure check result** (*hasil pemeriksaan tekanan darah setiap peserta*).
