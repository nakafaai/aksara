The pronoun _-nya_ on the verb _mencatat_, to record, **stands for what is recorded**. In sentence (3), the cadre checks each participant's blood pressure and records the result in a small book. What is recorded is that result.

So _-nya_ refers to the result of each participant's blood pressure check.

- The participants are checked, but what goes into the book is the result, not the person.
- The cadre is the one recording, not what is recorded.
- The posyandu yard is only the exercise place in sentence (1).
- The small book is where the result is written, named after _mencatatnya_.
