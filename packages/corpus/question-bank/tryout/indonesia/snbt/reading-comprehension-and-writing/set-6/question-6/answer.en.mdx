# Given

The *larva-compost* passage opens with Pasar Sentul's daily organic waste being fed to black soldier fly larvae and closes paragraph two with <Highlight>sampah pasar yang diangkut ke tempat pembuangan akhir berkurang hampir separuhnya</Highlight> (market waste hauled to the landfill dropped by nearly half).

# Asked

The main idea of the whole passage.

# Solution

Select the sentence that **unifies the problem, method, and outcome across both paragraphs**. Paragraph one introduces black soldier fly larvae as a waste-processing method for Pasar Sentul, and paragraph two reports the resulting products and near-halving of landfill waste.

> A main idea encompasses the whole passage rather than repeating a single supporting sentence.

## Comparing the scope of all five choices

| Proposed main idea | Coverage of the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pasar Sentul cuts landfill waste by using black soldier fly larvae | Covers the problem, method, and outcome of both paragraphs | **Correct** |
| Hauling waste to the landfill costs a lot | Supporting background from sentence (2) only | **Too narrow** |
| Grown larvae are sold to farmers as animal feed | Single detail from sentence (5) only | **Too narrow** |
| Fertilizer from leftovers is bought by vegetable farmers | Single detail from sentence (6) only | **Too narrow** |
| Pasar Sentul produces nearly one ton of waste daily | Opening problem detail from sentence (1) only | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The main idea is that **Pasar Sentul reduces the waste it sends to landfills by using black soldier fly larvae** (*Pasar Sentul mengurangi sampah yang dibuang dengan memanfaatkan larva lalat tentara hitam*).
