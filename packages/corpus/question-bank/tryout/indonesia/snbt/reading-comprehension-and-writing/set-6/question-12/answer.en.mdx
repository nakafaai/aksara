# Given

Sentence (1) of the *water-turn* passage writes <Highlight>sebagian besar daripada sawah di bagian hilir Desa Sukadamai sering kekurangan air</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing an improper word choice.

# Solution

Check **the grammatical function of the Indonesian preposition *daripada***. In standard Indonesian, *daripada* introduces a comparison, never a part-of-a-whole quantifier, so the phrase should read **sebagian besar sawah** without *daripada*.

> Standard Indonesian reserves *daripada* strictly for comparisons, never for expressing part of a group.

| Sentence (8) | *pun*, *karena* | Particle and causal conjunction are used accurately | **Correct** |

# Answer

The improper word choice occurs in **sentence (1)** (*kalimat (1)*: drop *daripada*).
