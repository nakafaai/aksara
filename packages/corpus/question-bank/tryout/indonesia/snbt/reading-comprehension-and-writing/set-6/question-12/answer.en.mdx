Sentence (1) uses _sebagian besar daripada sawah_. The word _daripada_ **is used for comparisons**, as in _lebih luas daripada_, wider than. It does not mark a part of something. The right phrase is _sebagian besar sawah_, most of the fields, without _daripada_.

So the sentence that uses a word wrongly is sentence (1).
