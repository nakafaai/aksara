# Given

Sentence (6) of the *bike-parking* passage writes <Highlight>contoh bagi sekolah lain di propinsi tersebut</Highlight>, while the other choices point to sentences (1), (2), (3), and (7).

# Asked

The sentence containing a nonstandard Indonesian word spelling.

# Solution

Check loanword spellings against **official KBBI standards**. The word *propinsi* in sentence (6) comes from Dutch *provincie*, and standard Indonesian preserves the letter *v* as **provinsi**.

> Standard Indonesian retains the letter *v* in loanwords such as *provinsi* rather than replacing it with *p*.

## Auditing spelling across all five sentences

| Sentence | Words checked | KBBI standard check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (1) | *membangun*, *beratap* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (2) | *meminjamkan*, *dua puluh* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (3) | *mencatat*, *diantar* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (6) | *propinsi* | Standard spelling is **provinsi** with *v* | **Nonstandard** |
| Sentence (7) | *melindungi*, *pengendaranya* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |

# Answer

The nonstandard spelling appears in **sentence (6)** (*kalimat (6)*: *provinsi*, not *propinsi*).
