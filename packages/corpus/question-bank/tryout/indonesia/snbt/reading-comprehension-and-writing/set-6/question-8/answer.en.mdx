# Given

Sentence (5) of the *larva-compost* passage lists three livestock types without separators: <Highlight>Larva yang sudah besar dijual kepada peternak ikan ayam dan bebek sebagai pakan</Highlight>.

# Asked

The punctuation revision of sentence (5) that follows standard Indonesian spelling rules (EYD).

# Solution

Apply **the EYD serial-comma rule for lists of three or more items**. Separate every listed item with a comma, including directly before *dan* (and), and never insert a comma between the subject and its verb *dijual*.

> Under Indonesian EYD rules, a list of three or more items takes commas between each element and immediately before *dan*.

## Checking punctuation across all five options

| Candidate revision | EYD punctuation check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *... peternak ikan, ayam, dan bebek sebagai pakan.* | Commas separate every item and appear right before *dan* | **Correct** |
| *... peternak ikan ayam, dan bebek sebagai pakan.* | Missing comma between *ikan* and *ayam* | **Wrong** |
| *Larva yang sudah besar, dijual kepada ...* | Faulty comma splits the subject from the predicate *dijual* | **Wrong** |
| *... peternak ikan-ayam-dan bebek sebagai pakan.* | Hyphens are never used to separate list items | **Wrong** |
| *... peternak ikan, ayam dan, bebek sebagai pakan.* | Misplaces the comma after *dan* instead of before it | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The correct revision is ***Larva yang sudah besar dijual kepada peternak ikan, ayam, dan bebek sebagai pakan***.
