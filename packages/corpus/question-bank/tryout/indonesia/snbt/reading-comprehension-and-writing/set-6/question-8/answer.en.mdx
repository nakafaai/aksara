Sentence (5) lists three kinds of livestock: _ikan_, fish, _ayam_, chicken, and _bebek_, duck. EYD says **the items of a list are separated by commas**, including before the _dan_, and, that comes before the last item.

So the right revision is _Larva yang sudah besar dijual kepada peternak ikan, ayam, dan bebek sebagai pakan._

- A comma only before _dan_ still leaves _ikan ayam_ without a comma.
- A comma after _besar_ splits the subject from the predicate _dijual_.
- Hyphens do not separate the items of a list.
- The comma sits after _dan_ instead of before it, and _ayam dan_ is not separated.
