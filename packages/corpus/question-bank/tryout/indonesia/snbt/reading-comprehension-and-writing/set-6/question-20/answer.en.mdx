# Given

Starting in July the school built **a covered bike rack** and lent bicycles, while the table records cyclists climbing from <InlineMath math="40" /> to <InlineMath math="110" /> and motorbike drop-offs falling from <InlineMath math="260" /> to <InlineMath math="200" />.

# Asked

The best conclusion combining both the passage and the table.

# Solution

Synthesize **the July facilities with both numerical trends in the table**. Cycling rises every month while motorbike drop-offs decline every month, whereas the combined count actually edges up from <InlineMath math="300" /> to <InlineMath math="310" />.

> A sound conclusion joins text facts and table trends accurately without overstating them with absolutes like *all* or *never*.

## Testing all five candidate conclusions

| Candidate conclusion | Evidence check against text and table | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Since the bike rack and loaner bikes arrived, more students cycle and fewer arrive by motorbike | Matches sentences (1), (2), and both table columns exactly | **Correct** |
| All former motorbike riders now cycle | False because <InlineMath math="200" /> students still arrive by motorbike in September | **Wrong** |
| Students are never dropped off during the rainy season | Contradicts sentence (7), which warns some may return to drop-offs | **Wrong** |
| Total student count falls each month | Combined counts rise: <InlineMath math="300" />, <InlineMath math="305" />, <InlineMath math="305" />, then <InlineMath math="310" /> | **Wrong** |
| Vehicle queues at the gate grew longer | Contradicts sentence (5), which says queues grew shorter | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The best conclusion is that **since the bike rack was built and bicycles were lent out, more students cycle and fewer students are dropped off by motorbike** (*Sejak tempat parkir dibangun dan sepeda dipinjamkan, makin banyak siswa bersepeda dan makin sedikit siswa yang diantar sepeda motor*).
