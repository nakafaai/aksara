# Given

Sentence (4) of the *larva-compost* passage reads <Highlight>Walaupun ukurannya kecil, tetapi larva itu dapat menghabiskan tumpukan sampah yang besar dalam dua minggu</Highlight> (Although their size is small, but those larvae can finish a huge pile of waste in two weeks).

# Asked

The revision that makes sentence (4) an effective standard Indonesian sentence.

# Solution

Remove **the double contrast conjunction**. Because *walaupun* (although) at the start of the subordinate clause already expresses contrast, adding *tetapi* (but) before the main clause is redundant and must be dropped while keeping the main subject *larva itu*.

> In standard Indonesian complex sentences, never pair a subordinate contrast marker (*walaupun*, *meskipun*) with a second contrast marker (*tetapi*, *namun*).

## Checking all five revised sentences

| Candidate revision | Conjunction and clause structure | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Walaupun ukurannya kecil, larva itu dapat menghabiskan ...* | Single contrast marker *walaupun* plus complete main clause | **Effective** |
| *Walaupun ukurannya kecil, namun larva itu ...* | Still doubles contrast markers (*walaupun* + *namun*) | **Ineffective** |
| *Walaupun ukurannya kecil tetapi larva itu ...* | Drops the comma but keeps redundant *tetapi* | **Ineffective** |
| *Walaupun ukurannya kecil, sehingga larva itu ...* | Mismatches contrast (*walaupun*) with result (*sehingga*) | **Ineffective** |
| *Walaupun ukuran larva itu kecil, tetapi dapat menghabiskan ...* | Keeps redundant *tetapi* and drops the main-clause subject | **Ineffective** |

# Answer

The effective revision is ***Walaupun ukurannya kecil, larva itu dapat menghabiskan tumpukan sampah yang besar dalam dua minggu***.
