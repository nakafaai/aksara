Sentence (4) uses both _walaupun_, although, and _tetapi_, but. Both mark a contrast, so **only one is needed**. If _walaupun_ stays at the start of the clause, _tetapi_ before the main clause must go.

So the right revision is _Walaupun ukurannya kecil, larva itu dapat menghabiskan tumpukan sampah yang besar dalam dua minggu._

- _Walaupun ..., namun_ still uses two contrast words.
- Dropping the comma does not remove the extra _tetapi_.
- _sehingga_, so, marks an effect and does not fit _walaupun_.
- That sentence still uses _walaupun_ and _tetapi_, and its main clause loses its subject.
