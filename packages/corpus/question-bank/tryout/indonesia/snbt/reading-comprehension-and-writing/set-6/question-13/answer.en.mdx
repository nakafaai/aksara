The answer **is written in sentence (6)**: _pintu air hanya boleh dibuka oleh petugas yang ditunjuk kelompok_, the sluice may be opened only by the keeper the group appoints. _Hanya_, only, rules out anyone else.

So only the keeper appointed by the farmers' group may open the sluice.

- Upstream farmers opened it themselves before the schedule, and that habit is what changed.
- Downstream farmers may not open it themselves either.
- The village head is not mentioned.
- A dry field gives no right to open the sluice, since the schedule sets the turns.
