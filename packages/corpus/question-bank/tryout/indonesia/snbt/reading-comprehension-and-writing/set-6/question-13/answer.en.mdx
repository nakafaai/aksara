# Given

Sentence (6) of the *water-turn* passage states the gate rule explicitly: <Highlight>pintu air hanya boleh dibuka oleh petugas yang ditunjuk kelompok</Highlight> (the sluice gate may only be opened by an officer appointed by the farmers' group).

# Asked

Who is allowed to open the sluice gate once the rotation schedule takes effect.

# Solution

Focus on **the exclusive qualifier *hanya* (only) in sentence (6)**. Once the rotation schedule begins, upstream farmers no longer open the gate themselves, and sole authority rests with **the officer appointed by the farmers' group**.

> An explicit restrictive word like *hanya* (only) rules out every party outside the exact person or group named.

## Matching all five options against the rule

| Named party | Alignment with the text | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The officer appointed by the farmers' group | Explicitly granted sole authority in sentence (6) | **Correct** |
| Every upstream farmer | Old practice before the schedule replaced it | **Wrong** |
| Every downstream farmer | Not authorized to open the gate individually | **Wrong** |
| The head of Desa Sukadamai | Never mentioned anywhere in the passage | **Wrong** |
| Any farmer whose field is dry | Access follows the fixed schedule via the appointed officer | **Wrong** |

# Answer

Only **the officer appointed by the farmers' group** (*petugas yang ditunjuk kelompok tani*) may open the sluice gate.
