# Given

The *bike-parking* table shows cycling students rising from <InlineMath math="40" /> to <InlineMath math="110" />, and sentence (5) states <Highlight>Bertambahnya siswa yang bersepeda membuat antrean kendaraan di depan gerbang sekolah pada pagi hari menjadi lebih pendek</Highlight> (The rise in cycling students shortened morning vehicle queues at the school gate).

# Asked

The relationship between sentence (5) and the data in the table.

# Solution

Track **the causal verb *membuat* (made / caused) in sentence (5)**. The opening phrase *Bertambahnya siswa yang bersepeda* picks up the upward trend shown in the table, and the predicate reports its downstream effect: shorter morning vehicle queues at the gate.

> To link prose to a data table, check the causal direction: whether the sentence gives the cause behind the numbers or the consequence of the trend.

## Comparing all five proposed relationships

| Proposed relationship | Match with sentence (5) and the table | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| States an effect of the rise in cycling students shown in the table | Directly matches *made morning queues shorter* | **Correct** |
| Explains the cause of the rise in cycling students | Causes appear in sentences (1) and (2), not sentence (5) | **Wrong** |
| Contradicts the data in the table | Sentence (5) builds on the table's upward trend | **Wrong** |
| Gives an example of motorbike drop-off students | Mentions gate queues, not individual students | **Wrong** |
| States the education office's plan | That plan is reported in sentence (6) | **Wrong** |

# Answer

Sentence (5) **states an effect of the increase in cycling students shown in the table** (*Kalimat (5) menyebut akibat dari bertambahnya siswa bersepeda yang terlihat pada tabel*).
