# Given

Sentence (4) of the *senior-exercise* passage writes <Highlight>disimpan dengan rapih di dalam satu kotak</Highlight>, while the choices test spelling accuracy across sentences (1), (2), (4), (7), and (8).

# Asked

The sentence that contains a nonstandard Indonesian spelling.

# Solution

Check each candidate word against **standard Indonesian dictionary (KBBI) spelling**. The word *rapih* in sentence (4) is nonstandard and reflects spoken habit, whereas the standard spelling is **rapi** without a final *h*.

> Standard Indonesian spelling follows official KBBI headwords rather than informal pronunciation.

# Answer

The nonstandard spelling appears in **sentence (4)** (*kalimat (4)*: *rapi*, not *rapih*).
