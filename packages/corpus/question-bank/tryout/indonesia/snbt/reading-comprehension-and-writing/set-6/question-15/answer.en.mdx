# Given

The *water-turn* passage spans eight sentences across two paragraphs moving from downstream water shortages to the <Highlight>jadwal giliran air</Highlight> (water-rotation schedule) and recovered harvests.

# Asked

The organizational pattern of the passage.

# Solution

Map **the role of each sentence pair in exact order from start to finish**. Paragraph one presents the problem and its consequences, and paragraph two presents the solution and its outcomes.

> Text structure is revealed by tracing the sequence of rhetorical roles from the opening sentences to the closing sentences.

## Mapping the passage structure

| Passage segment | Core content | Structural stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentences (1) and (2) | Downstream fields lack water because upstream farmers open gates first | **Problem** |
| Sentences (3) and (4) | Crops dry out, farmers lose money, and disputes break out | **Consequences** |
| Sentences (5) and (6) | Farmers' group sets a two-day rotation and appoints a gate officer | **Solution** |
| Sentences (7) and (8) | Downstream fields recover while upstream fields still get enough water | **Outcome** |

The other choices fail because the text never opens with an opinion, an outcome, or a solution, and it covers one village rather than comparing two villages.

# Answer

The passage follows the pattern **problem and its consequences, followed by the solution and its outcomes** (*masalah dan akibatnya, lalu cara mengatasinya dan hasilnya*).
