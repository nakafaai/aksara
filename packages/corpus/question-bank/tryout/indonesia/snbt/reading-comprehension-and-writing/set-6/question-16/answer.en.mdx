# Given

The table in the *bike-parking* passage records **cycling students** at <InlineMath math="40" /> cycling students in June and <InlineMath math="110" /> cycling students in September.

# Asked

The increase in the number of cycling students from June to September.

# Solution

Subtract **the June cycling count from the September cycling count** directly from the second column of the table.

<BlockMath math="110 - 40 = 70" />

> An increase over a period equals the ending value minus the starting value.
