Take **the number of cycling students in September and in June** and subtract.

$$110 - 40 = 70$$

So the increase is $$70$$ students.

- $$20$$ students is only the rise from August to September.
- $$25$$ students is the rise over one month, for example June to July.
- $$110$$ students is the September total, not the increase.
- $$150$$ students is the sum of the two numbers, not their difference.
