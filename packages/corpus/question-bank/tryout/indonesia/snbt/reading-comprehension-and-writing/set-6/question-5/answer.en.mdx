# Given

Sentence (8) of the *senior-exercise* passage states the notebook's role: <Highlight>Catatan itu membantu petugas puskesmas memantau kesehatan warga lanjut usia tanpa harus mendatangi rumah mereka satu per satu</Highlight> (Those records help clinic staff monitor older residents' health without visiting every home one by one).

# Asked

The most likely outcome if volunteers stop recording participants' blood pressure.

# Solution

Base the inference directly on **the function the passage assigns to those records**. Because the log is what lets clinic staff track blood pressure without door-to-door visits, stopping the log directly makes it **harder for clinic staff to monitor participants' blood pressure without visiting their homes**.

> A valid inference reverses or extends a cause-and-effect link stated in the text without inventing unrelated claims.

## Evaluating all five predicted outcomes

| Proposed outcome | Support in the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clinic staff find it harder to monitor participants' blood pressure without home visits | Direct consequence of losing the function in sentence (8) | **Correct** |
| Participants can no longer attend exercise | Running the exercise session does not depend on keeping the notebook | **Wrong** |
| Exercise movements become faster | Slow movements accommodate weak knees, unrelated to record keeping | **Wrong** |
| Participants sleep more soundly | Better sleep comes from regular exercise, not stopping records | **Wrong** |
| Attendance doubles | Nothing in the passage suggests attendance would surge | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The most likely result is that **clinic staff will find it harder to monitor participants' blood pressure without visiting their homes** (*Petugas puskesmas lebih sulit memantau tekanan darah peserta tanpa mendatangi rumah mereka*).
