A good title **covers the whole reading, no broader and no narrower**. The first paragraph gives the problem: downstream fields lack water. The second gives the solution, a water rotation schedule, and its result: downstream fields no longer dry out.

So the best title is _Jadwal Giliran Air Mengakhiri Kekeringan Sawah Hilir_, a water rotation schedule ends the drought in downstream fields.

- _Musim Kemarau di Indonesia_, the dry season in Indonesia, is too broad, since the reading covers one village.
- The sluice keeper is only mentioned in sentence (6).
- The quarrels between farmers are only mentioned in sentence (4).
- How to plant rice is never discussed.
