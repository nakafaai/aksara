# Given

The *water-turn* passage explains dry downstream rice fields in Desa Sukadamai in paragraph one and their resolution through a water-rotation schedule in paragraph two, where <Highlight>sawah di hilir tidak lagi kekeringan dan hasil panennya hampir menyamai hasil panen sawah di hulu</Highlight> (downstream fields no longer dry out and harvests nearly match upstream yields).

# Asked

The most fitting title for the entire passage.

# Solution

Pick the title that **captures both the core problem and its solution across the two paragraphs**, neither broader than one village nor restricted to a single sentence detail.

> An effective title fits the exact scope of the whole text without broadening beyond it or shrinking to one minor detail.

## Comparing the scope of all five titles

| Proposed title | Fit with the whole passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Jadwal Giliran Air Mengakhiri Kekeringan Sawah Hilir* | Links the water schedule directly to ending downstream drought | **Best fit** |
| *Musim Kemarau di Indonesia* | Far too broad for a single village case | **Too broad** |
| *Petugas Pintu Air Desa Sukadamai* | Focuses only on one detail from sentence (6) | **Too narrow** |
| *Perselisihan antara Petani Hulu dan Petani Hilir* | Covers only the dispute noted in sentence (4) | **Too narrow** |
| *Cara Menanam Padi di Sawah Hilir* | Rice-planting techniques are never discussed | **Off-topic** |

# Answer

The best title is ***Jadwal Giliran Air Mengakhiri Kekeringan Sawah Hilir*** (Water Rotation Schedule Ends Downstream Rice-Field Drought).
