# Given

Paragraph two of the *larva-compost* passage highlights two joint results: larvae are sold as feed, residue becomes fertilizer sold to farmers, and <Highlight>sampah pasar yang diangkut ke tempat pembuangan akhir berkurang hampir separuhnya</Highlight> (landfill waste drops by nearly half).

# Asked

The best concluding sentence for paragraph two.

# Solution

Choose the sentence that **ties together the paragraph's dual outcomes without introducing an unrelated topic**. Because paragraph two combines waste reduction with commercial sales of larvae and fertilizer, stating that the effort both reduces waste and generates income for the youth group closes the paragraph cohesively.

> A strong closing sentence synthesizes the paragraph's existing evidence without drifting off-topic or contradicting earlier facts.

## Checking coherence across all five candidate closers

| Candidate closing sentence | Fit with paragraph two | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Selain mengurangi sampah, usaha itu juga memberi penghasilan bagi kelompok pemuda tersebut.* | Summarizes both waste reduction and income from feed and fertilizer | **Coherent** |
| *Lalat tentara hitam pertama kali ditemukan di benua Amerika.* | Introduces unrelated insect geography | **Off-topic** |
| *Sampah plastik di pasar juga harus dipilah sebelum dibuang.* | Shifts away from vegetable and fruit waste to plastic | **Off-topic** |
| *Oleh karena itu, Pasar Sentul akan ditutup untuk sementara.* | Illogical conclusion unrelated to solving the waste problem | **Contradictory** |
| *Namun, pengelola pasar tidak pernah memikirkan masalah sampah.* | Contradicts sentence (3), where managers actively donate waste | **Contradictory** |

# Answer

The best closing sentence is ***Selain mengurangi sampah, usaha itu juga memberi penghasilan bagi kelompok pemuda tersebut***. (Besides reducing waste, the venture also brings income to the youth group).
