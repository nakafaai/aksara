A good closing sentence **continues the paragraph's topic without adding a new one**. The second paragraph covers the results of using larvae: larvae and fertiliser are sold, and less waste is thrown away. A sentence that sums up both results, less waste and an income, closes the paragraph neatly.

So the right closing sentence is _Selain mengurangi sampah, usaha itu juga memberi penghasilan bagi kelompok pemuda tersebut._

- Where the black soldier fly comes from is a new topic.
- Plastic waste is also a new topic, since the paragraph is about vegetable and fruit waste.
- Closing the market makes no sense as an effect of less waste.
- The managers clearly do think about waste, so that sentence contradicts the reading.
