The claim names **the schedule as the cause** of the drought ending. It gets stronger when other causes are ruled out. The likeliest other cause is rain. If rainfall was as low as before the schedule, the only change left is the schedule itself.

So the strongest support is _Curah hujan pada dua musim kemarau terakhir sama rendahnya dengan musim kemarau sebelum jadwal dibuat._

- What upstream farmers want says nothing about why downstream fields stopped drying out.
- The price of unhusked rice has nothing to do with water supply.
- Planting maize does not explain why the rice fields no longer dry out.
- More frequent rain would be another cause, which weakens the claim.
