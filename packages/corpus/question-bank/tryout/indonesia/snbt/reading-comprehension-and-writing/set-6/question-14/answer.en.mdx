# Given

Paragraph two of the *water-turn* passage notes <Highlight>Sejak jadwal itu berlaku, sawah di hilir tidak lagi kekeringan</Highlight> (Since that schedule took effect, downstream fields no longer suffered drought), supporting the claim that the rotation schedule caused the recovery.

# Asked

The statement that most strongly reinforces that causal claim.

# Solution

Strengthen a causal explanation by **ruling out the most plausible alternative cause**, namely extra rainfall. If rainfall during the last two dry seasons stayed just as low as before the schedule existed, weather cannot explain the turnaround, leaving **the water-rotation schedule** as the clear driver.

> The strongest support for a cause-and-effect claim eliminates the main competing explanation.

## Evaluating how each statement affects the causal claim

| Candidate statement | Effect on the causal claim | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rainfall over the last two dry seasons was just as low as before the schedule | Rules out extra rain as a competing cause | **Strengthens most** |
| Upstream farmers want the gate open longer | States a preference, not why drought ended | **Irrelevant** |
| Grain prices rose over the last two years | Market prices do not explain field water supply | **Irrelevant** |
| Some downstream farmers began planting corn | Does not explain why rice fields stopped drying out | **Irrelevant** |
| Rain fell far more often last dry season | Supplies an alternative cause that weakens the claim | **Weakens** |

# Answer

The strongest reinforcement is ***Curah hujan pada dua musim kemarau terakhir sama rendahnya dengan musim kemarau sebelum jadwal dibuat***. (Rainfall in the past two dry seasons was just as low as before the schedule was introduced).
