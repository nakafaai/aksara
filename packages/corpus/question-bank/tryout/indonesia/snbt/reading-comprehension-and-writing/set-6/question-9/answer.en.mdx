# Given

Sentence (1) of the *larva-compost* passage states <Highlight>Pengelola Pasar Sentul kewalahan karena pasar itu menghasilkan hampir satu ton sampah sayur dan buah setiap hari</Highlight> (Pasar Sentul's managers were overwhelmed because the market produces nearly a ton of vegetable and fruit waste every day).

# Asked

The meaning of *kewalahan* in sentence (1).

# Solution

Read the word through **its direct cause in sentence (1)** and its KBBI definition. Nearly a ton of daily waste exceeds the managers' capacity to cope, so *kewalahan* means **unable to handle the workload anymore** (*tidak sanggup lagi menangani*).

> Contextual word meaning must fit both the dictionary sense and the stated cause in the sentence.

## Comparing all five meaning options

| Proposed meaning | Fit with sentence (1) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unable to handle anymore (*tidak sanggup lagi menangani*) | Directly matches being overwhelmed by nearly one ton of daily waste | **Correct** |
| Angry (*marah*) | Names an emotion rather than inability to cope | **Wrong** |
| Losing money (*kehilangan uang*) | Relates to hauling cost in sentence (2), not the word itself | **Wrong** |
| Confused about choosing (*bingung memilih*) | No dilemma between options is mentioned | **Wrong** |
| Disappointed (*kecewa*) | Names a feeling rather than operational overload | **Wrong** |

# Answer

In sentence (1), *kewalahan* means **unable to handle anymore** (*tidak sanggup lagi menangani*).
