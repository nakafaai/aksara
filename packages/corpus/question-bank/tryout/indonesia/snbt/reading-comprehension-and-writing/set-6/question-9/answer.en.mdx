A word's meaning is set **by the context of its sentence**. Sentence (1) says the market managers are _kewalahan_ because the market produces almost a tonne of waste a day. That huge amount is more than they can handle, which matches the KBBI sense of _kewalahan_.

So _kewalahan_ in sentence (1) means no longer able to cope.

- _Marah_, angry, is a feeling, but the reading is about a workload.
- _Kehilangan uang_, losing money, relates to the cost in sentence (2), not to the meaning of _kewalahan_.
- _Bingung memilih_, unsure which to choose, does not fit, since no choice is mentioned.
- _Kecewa_, disappointed, is also a feeling and does not fit the cause, the amount of waste.
