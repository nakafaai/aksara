The blank opens the phrase _rutin bersenam selama setahun_, exercising regularly for a year. Better sleep and less stiffness **come after a year of exercise**, so the phrase marks an earlier time. The word for that is _Setelah_, after.

So the right word is _Setelah_.

- _Sebelum_, before, reverses the order, as if the benefits came before exercising.
- _Meskipun_, although, marks a contrast, but exercise and good sleep do not contrast.
- _Agar_, so that, marks a purpose and does not fit _rutin bersenam selama setahun_.
- _Sehingga_, so, marks an effect and does not open a sentence.
