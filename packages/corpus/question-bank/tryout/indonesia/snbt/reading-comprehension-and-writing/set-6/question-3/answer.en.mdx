# Given

Sentence (6) of the *senior-exercise* passage reads <Highlight>... rutin bersenam selama setahun, banyak peserta mengaku tidurnya lebih nyenyak dan tubuhnya tidak mudah pegal</Highlight> (... exercising regularly for a year, many participants reported better sleep and less soreness).

# Asked

The conjunction that best fills the blank in sentence (6).

# Solution

Follow **the chronological order between the two events**. Participants first exercised regularly for a full year, and afterward they experienced deeper sleep and less body soreness, so the opening time marker must be **Setelah** (After).

> An opening conjunction must match the exact temporal or logical sequence between the introductory phrase and the main clause.

## Testing all five conjunction choices

| Conjunction | Meaning | Fit in sentence (6) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Setelah* (After) | Marks an earlier completed period | Matches benefits gained after a year of exercise | **Correct** |
| *Sebelum* (Before) | Reverses time order | Claims benefits appeared before exercising | **Wrong** |
| *Meskipun* (Although) | Marks contrast | Exercise and better sleep align rather than contrast | **Wrong** |
| *Agar* (So that) | Marks purpose | Does not pair grammatically with *selama setahun* | **Wrong** |
| *Sehingga* (So that / thus) | Marks result inside a sentence | Cannot open a standard Indonesian sentence | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The right word to complete sentence (6) is ***Setelah*** (After).
