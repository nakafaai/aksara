# Given

The *senior-exercise* passage describes the weekly elderly exercise session at the Mekarsari health post in paragraph one and details its ongoing benefits in paragraph two, including <Highlight>tidurnya lebih nyenyak dan tubuhnya tidak mudah pegal</Highlight> (better sleep and less body soreness).

# Asked

The author's main purpose in writing the passage.

# Solution

Identify the purpose from **what both paragraphs work together to do**, rather than from a minor detail. Paragraph one depicts how the exercise and blood-pressure checks run, while paragraph two reports the health gains for participants and how the log helps clinic staff.

> An author's purpose matches the overall arc connecting the opening paragraph and the closing paragraph.

## Comparing all five purpose options

| Proposed purpose | Fit with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe the health-post elderly exercise program and its benefits for participants | Captures both paragraph one and paragraph two completely | **Correct** |
| Urge all older adults across Indonesia to join exercise | The text reports one neighborhood neutrally without persuasion | **Wrong** |
| Compare elderly exercise with other sports | No other sport is mentioned or compared | **Wrong** |
| Explain how to measure blood pressure | Measurement is mentioned, not taught step by step | **Wrong** |
| Criticize clinic staff for rarely visiting homes | Sentence (8) says clinic staff are assisted, not criticized | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The author's purpose is **to describe the elderly exercise activity at the health post and its benefits for participants** (*menggambarkan kegiatan senam lansia di posyandu dan manfaatnya bagi peserta*).
