Sentence (2) has a comma after _halaman_. The part _Air hujan yang jatuh di halaman_, rain that falls in the yard, is the subject, and _langsung mengalir_, runs straight, is the predicate. EYD says **no comma separates subject and predicate**, even a long subject. The clause _karena ..._ after the main clause takes no comma either.

So the right revision is _Air hujan yang jatuh di halaman langsung mengalir ke selokan karena sebagian besar halaman rumah sudah ditutup semen._

- A comma after _halaman_ still splits subject and predicate, and the comma before _karena_ is not needed either.
- A comma after _Air hujan_ cuts the subject off from _yang jatuh di halaman_.
- No comma goes before _karena_, because that clause follows the main clause.
- A comma after _langsung_ splits it from the verb it describes.
