# Given

The *jamu-label* passage pairs **new buyers' earlier hesitation** in paragraph one with labeled bottles in paragraph two so <Highlight>pembelinya kini banyak dari kalangan pelajar dan pegawai</Highlight> (students and office workers now buy her jamu in large numbers).

# Asked

The most appropriate title for the passage.

# Solution

Pick the title that **captures both the labeled bottles and the new customers they won for Bu Sumiati**, avoiding options that are overly broad or limited to one minor detail.

> A well-crafted title matches the exact scope of the entire text without widening beyond it or narrowing to one sentence.

## Comparing all five title candidates

| Proposed title | Coverage of the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Botol Berlabel Mendekatkan Jamu Bu Sumiati kepada Pembeli Baru* | Connects labeled bottles directly to attracting new buyers | **Best fit** |
| *Jamu Gendong di Indonesia* | Far too broad for one seller's story | **Too broad** |
| *Resep Beras Kencur Bu Sumiati* | No recipe is ever discussed | **Off-topic** |
| *Pelatihan Dinas Koperasi* | Covers only sentence (4) | **Too narrow** |
| *Warung di Dekat Sekolah* | Covers only one detail from sentence (6) | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The best title is ***Botol Berlabel Mendekatkan Jamu Bu Sumiati kepada Pembeli Baru*** (Labeled Bottles Bring Bu Sumiati's Jamu Closer to New Buyers).
