# Given

Sentence (8) of the *infiltration-well* passage presents **the neighborhood chair's causal conclusion**: <Highlight>sumur resapan membuat sumur warga tidak lagi kering</Highlight> (infiltration wells stopped household wells from running dry this dry season).

# Asked

The statement that most strongly weakens that conclusion.

# Solution

Weaken a causal claim by **introducing a plausible competing explanation**. If this year's dry season was much shorter than in previous years, wells naturally had less time to dry up, casting doubt on whether the infiltration wells caused the improvement.

> The strongest attack on a cause-and-effect claim supplies an independent alternative cause for the observed outcome.

## Evaluating all five statements

| Candidate statement | Effect on the causal claim | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This year's dry season was much shorter than in previous years | Supplies a weather-based alternative explanation | **Weakens most** |
| The infiltration wells are filled with stones and palm fiber | Simply repeats a construction detail from sentence (5) | **Irrelevant** |
| Residents built the wells cooperatively | Merely repeats sentence (4) | **Irrelevant** |
| Homes without infiltration wells still ran dry | Acts as a control group that actually strengthens the claim | **Strengthens** |
| Street drains are cleaned monthly | Does not explain groundwater levels in wells | **Irrelevant** |

# Answer

The statement that weakens the conclusion most is ***Musim kemarau tahun ini jauh lebih pendek daripada musim kemarau tahun-tahun sebelumnya*** (This year's dry season was much shorter than in previous years).
