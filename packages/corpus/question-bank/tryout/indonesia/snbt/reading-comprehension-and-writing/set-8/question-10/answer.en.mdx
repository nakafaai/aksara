The RT head names **the infiltration wells as the cause** of the wells no longer drying out. The conclusion weakens if another cause could also explain why the wells still hold water. A much shorter dry season is such a cause, since the wells would have no time to run dry.

So the strongest weakener is _Musim kemarau tahun ini jauh lebih pendek daripada musim kemarau tahun-tahun sebelumnya._

- What the wells are filled with only repeats sentence (5).
- How residents built the wells says nothing about why the wells hold water.
- Houses without an infiltration well still running dry would strengthen the conclusion.
- Cleaned gutters have nothing to do with water in the wells.
