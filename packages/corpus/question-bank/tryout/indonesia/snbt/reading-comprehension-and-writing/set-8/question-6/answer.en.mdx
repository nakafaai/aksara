# Given

Sentence (2) of the *infiltration-well* passage states **the exact cause of rainwater runoff**: <Highlight>langsung mengalir ke selokan karena sebagian besar halaman rumah sudah ditutup semen</Highlight> (flows straight into drains because most yards are covered in cement).

# Asked

Why rainwater in Kampung Cibeureum flows directly into drains.

# Solution

Read **the causal clause right after *karena* (because) in sentence (2)**. Cement seals yard soil so rainwater cannot soak in and runs straight off into the street drains.

> Direct-detail questions are answered verbatim from the explicit causal clause in the passage.

## Matching all five choices against sentence

| Candidate cause | Match with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most house yards have been covered with cement | Stated directly after *karena* in sentence (2) | **Correct** |
| Residents' wells are too deep | Never stated as the cause of surface runoff | **Wrong** |
| The neighborhood rarely gets rain | Contradicts sentence (1), which reports heavy rain | **Wrong** |
| Drains are clogged with trash | Never mentioned in the passage | **Wrong** |
| House roofs are leaking | Unmentioned, and roof runoff is directed into infiltration wells | **Wrong** |

# Answer

Rainwater runs straight into drains because **most house yards have been paved with cement** (*Sebagian besar halaman rumah sudah ditutup semen*).
