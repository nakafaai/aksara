# Given

Once **the fixed departure schedule began in April**, the table shows average wait time plunging from around <InlineMath math="26" /> minutes to <InlineMath math="12" /> minutes while daily riders jumped from <InlineMath math="1{,}180" /> to <InlineMath math="1{,}650" />.

# Asked

The best conclusion supported by both the passage and the table.

# Solution

Join **the April launch of the fixed schedule with the sharp simultaneous shift in both table columns**: waiting time became much shorter while daily ridership grew substantially.

> A sound conclusion pairs the timing of the text's policy change with the exact data shift while respecting earlier monthly dips.

## Checking all five candidate conclusions

| Candidate conclusion | Evidence check against text and table | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Since minibuses ran on a fixed schedule, wait times became much shorter and riders increased | Matches sentence (1) and April's sharp data shift | **Correct** |
| Ridership rose every single month | False because ridership dipped in February (<InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> to <InlineMath math="1{,}150" />) | **Wrong** |
| Fixed schedules make minibuses always depart full | Contradicts sentence (5), which notes departures with few riders | **Wrong** |
| Stop schedule boards boosted April ridership | Sentence (6) says boards will only be installed next month | **Wrong** |
| Long wait times increased ridership | Reverses the table: ridership peaked when wait time was shortest | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The best conclusion is that **since minibuses started running on a fixed schedule, waiting times became much shorter and ridership increased** (*Sejak angkutan berangkat menurut jadwal tetap, waktu tunggu menjadi jauh lebih singkat dan penumpang bertambah*).
