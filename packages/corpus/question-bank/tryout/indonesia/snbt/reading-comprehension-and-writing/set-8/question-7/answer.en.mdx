# Given

Sentence (6) of the *infiltration-well* passage leaves **a purpose-conjunction blank**: <Highlight>Air hujan dari atap dialirkan ke lubang itu ... dapat meresap perlahan ke dalam tanah</Highlight> (Roof rainwater is channeled into that pit ... it can seep slowly into the soil).

# Asked

The conjunction that properly fills the blank in sentence (6).

# Solution

Identify **the action-and-goal link** in sentence (6). Channeling roof water into the pit is done for the purpose of letting it soak slowly into the ground, so the required purpose conjunction is **agar** (so that).

> Use a purpose conjunction like *agar* whenever the second clause states the goal of the action in the first clause.

## Testing all five conjunction choices

| Conjunction | Logical relation | Fit in sentence (6) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *agar* (so that) | Marks purpose | Directly links channeling water to its goal of slow seepage | **Correct** |
| *karena* (because) | Marks cause | Wrongly makes seepage the cause before channeling | **Wrong** |
| *meskipun* (although) | Marks contrast | The two clauses align rather than contrast | **Wrong** |
| *bahkan* (even) | Marks emphasis | Does not express a goal | **Wrong** |
| *setelah* (after) | Marks time order | Reverses sequence as if seepage happened before channeling | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The right conjunction for sentence (6) is ***agar*** (so that).
