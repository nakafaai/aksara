Sentence (6) names an action: rainwater is led into the infiltration well. The part after the blank, _dapat meresap perlahan ke dalam tanah_, can slowly soak into the ground, **is the purpose of that action**. A purpose is marked by the conjunction _agar_, so that.

So the right word is _agar_.

- _karena_, because, makes soaking a cause, but soaking happens only after the water is led in.
- _meskipun_, although, marks a contrast, but the two parts agree.
- _bahkan_, even, adds emphasis, not a purpose.
- _setelah_, after, reverses the order, as if the water soaked in before being led there.
