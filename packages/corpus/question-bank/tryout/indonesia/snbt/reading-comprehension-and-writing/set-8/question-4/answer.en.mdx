A word's meaning is set **by the context of its sentence**. Sentence (1) says the craft is _hampir hilang_, almost gone, because the weavers are old. Sentence (3) says the class aims to stop the craft from becoming _punah_, with nothing left at all, which matches the KBBI sense of _punah_.

So _punah_ in sentence (3) means to disappear completely.

- _Berkurang sedikit_, to decline a little, is too weak for a craft already almost gone.
- _Berpindah tempat_, to move elsewhere, is never mentioned.
- _Menjadi mahal_, to become expensive, is about price, not survival.
- _Berubah bentuk_, to change form, does not fit, since the fear is that the craft will no longer exist.
