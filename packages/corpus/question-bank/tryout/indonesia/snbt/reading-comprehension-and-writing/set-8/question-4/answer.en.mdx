# Given

Sentence (1) warns that the rattan craft was *almost gone*, and sentence (3) adds that classes began <Highlight>Untuk mencegah kerajinan itu punah</Highlight>, giving **direct lexical context** for *punah*.

# Asked

The meaning of *punah* in sentence (3).

# Solution

Link *punah* to **the phrase *hampir hilang* (almost lost) in sentence (1)** and its KBBI definition. Together they confirm that *punah* means **completely gone / extinct** (*hilang sama sekali*).

> Contextual clues in neighboring sentences reinforce the exact dictionary sense of a target word.

## Comparing all five meaning options

| Proposed meaning | Fit with sentence (3) and KBBI | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completely gone (*hilang sama sekali*) | Matches both *hampir hilang* and the KBBI definition of *punah* | **Correct** |
| Slightly reduced (*berkurang sedikit*) | Far too mild for total disappearance | **Wrong** |
| Moved elsewhere (*berpindah tempat*) | Unrelated to vanishing completely | **Wrong** |
| Became expensive (*menjadi mahal*) | Refers to price rather than survival of the craft | **Wrong** |
| Changed shape (*berubah bentuk*) | The fear is disappearance, not alteration | **Wrong** |

# Answer

In sentence (3), *punah* means **completely gone** (*hilang sama sekali*).
