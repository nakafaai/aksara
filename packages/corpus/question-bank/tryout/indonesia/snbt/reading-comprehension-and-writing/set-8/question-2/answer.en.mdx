# Given

Sentence (5) of the *rattan-weaving* passage contains **a nonstandard Indonesian spelling** in <Highlight>sepuluh pemuda sudah trampil membuat keranjang dan tas rotan</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing a nonstandard spelling.

# Solution

Check candidate words against **standard KBBI dictionary spelling**. The word *trampil* in sentence (5) drops the vowel *e* due to fast speech, whereas the standard Indonesian spelling is **terampil**.

> Standard Indonesian spelling follows official KBBI headwords such as *terampil* rather than clipped colloquial pronunciation.
