# Given

The *minibus-schedule* table records **average passenger waiting time** at <InlineMath math="26" /> minutes in March and <InlineMath math="12" /> minutes in April.

# Asked

The difference in average waiting time between March and April.

# Solution

Subtract **April's waiting time from March's waiting time** straight from the third column of the table.

<BlockMath math="26 - 12 = 14" />

> The difference between two months equals the larger value minus the smaller value on the exact target rows.

## Checking all five options against the table

| Option | Calculation behind it | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="14" /> minutes | March minus April (<InlineMath math="26 - 12" />) | **Correct** |
| <InlineMath math="1" /> minute | February minus March (<InlineMath math="27 - 26" />) | **Wrong** |
| <InlineMath math="2" /> minutes | February minus January (<InlineMath math="27 - 25" />) | **Wrong** |
| <InlineMath math="12" /> minutes | April's waiting time alone, not the difference | **Wrong** |
| <InlineMath math="13" /> minutes | January minus April (<InlineMath math="25 - 12" />) | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The difference in average waiting time between March and April is **<InlineMath math="14" /> minutes** (*<InlineMath math="14" /> menit*).
