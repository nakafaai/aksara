Take **the waiting times for March and April** and subtract.

$$26 - 12 = 14$$

So the difference is $$14$$ minutes.

- $$1$$ minute is the February to March difference, $$27 - 26$$.
- $$2$$ minutes is the January to February difference, $$27 - 25$$.
- $$12$$ minutes is only the April waiting time.
- $$13$$ minutes is the January to April difference, $$25 - 12$$.
