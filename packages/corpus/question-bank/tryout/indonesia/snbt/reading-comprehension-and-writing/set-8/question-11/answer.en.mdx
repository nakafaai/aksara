# Given

The *jamu-label* passage traces **Bu Sumiati's herbal-drink business** from limited neighborhood sales to packaging training in paragraph one, then labeled bottles sold at stalls with nearly tripled sales in paragraph two.

# Asked

The author's main purpose in writing the passage.

# Solution

Read the purpose from **what both paragraphs build together**. Paragraph one explains Bu Sumiati's old sales barrier and her training, while paragraph two shows how labeled bottles expanded her buyers and nearly tripled revenue.

> An author's purpose captures the main thread linking both paragraphs rather than a passing detail.

## Comparing all five purpose options

| Proposed purpose | Fit with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tell how a jamu seller grew her business through packaging and labeling | Unifies the old hurdle, training, labeled bottles, and sales jump | **Correct** |
| Explain recipes for *kunyit asam* and *beras kencur* | Names the drinks without giving any recipe | **Wrong** |
| Urge students to drink jamu daily | Reports neutrally with no persuasion | **Wrong** |
| Compare jamu with factory-bottled drinks | Factory drinks are never mentioned | **Wrong** |
| Criticize the cooperatives office for holding too few trainings | The office helped her via training in sentence (4) | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The author's purpose is **to tell how a jamu seller expanded her business through packaging and labeling** (*menceritakan usaha seorang penjual jamu mengembangkan dagangannya melalui pengemasan dan pelabelan*).
