# Given

Sentence (4) of the *rattan-weaving* passage contains **a redundant blended phrase**: <Highlight>menganyamnya berulang-ulang kali sampai para pemuda hafal</Highlight>.

# Asked

The revision that makes sentence (4) an effective standard Indonesian sentence.

# Solution

Eliminate **the contamination between *berulang-ulang* and *berulang kali***. Keep just one concise standard expression, **berulang kali** (repeatedly), without inserting an unnecessary comma.

> Effective Indonesian avoids blending two synonymous frequency expressions into redundant hybrids like *berulang-ulang kali*.

## Evaluating all five candidate revisions

| Candidate revision | Conciseness and standard form check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *... menganyamnya berulang kali sampai para pemuda hafal.* | Concise, standard, and free of redundancy | **Effective** |
| *... berulang-ulang kali hingga para pemuda hafal.* | Keeps the redundant hybrid *berulang-ulang kali* | **Ineffective** |
| Form repeating *kali* after *berkali-kali* | Repeats *kali* redundantly | **Ineffective** |
| *... ulang-ulang kali sampai para pemuda hafal.* | Nonstandard form | **Ineffective** |
| *... berulang-ulang kali, sampai para pemuda hafal.* | Keeps the hybrid and adds an unnecessary comma | **Ineffective** |

# Answer

The effective revision is ***Para perajin tua mengajarkan cara memilih rotan, merendamnya, dan menganyamnya berulang kali sampai para pemuda hafal***.
