Sentence (6) uses _jam 06.00_. The word _jam_ **expresses a length of time**, as in _dua jam_, two hours. For a point in time, the right word is _pukul_, so the correct form is _pukul 06.00_, at six o'clock.

So the sentence with a wrongly used word is sentence (6).
