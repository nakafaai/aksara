# Given

Sentence (6) of the *jamu-label* passage confuses **duration *jam* with clock-time *pukul*** in <Highlight>Setiap hari jam 06.00, ia menitipkan jamu itu di tiga warung</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing an improperly used word.

# Solution

Distinguish ***jam* (length of time) from *pukul* (specific point on the clock)** in standard Indonesian. Because the sentence names a specific morning time rather than a duration, it must use **pukul** instead of *jam*.

> Standard Indonesian uses *jam* for duration (such as *dua jam*) and *pukul* for a specific clock time.

The improperly used word appears in **sentence (6)** (*kalimat (6)*: use *pukul*, not *jam*).
