# Given

Sentences (1) and (2) of the *rattan-weaving* passage state **the baseline before classes started**: weavers were aging and <Highlight>Anak muda di desa itu lebih suka bekerja di pabrik di kota daripada menganyam rotan</Highlight> (village youth preferred working in city factories over weaving rattan).

# Asked

The most likely outcome if the weaving class had never been held.

# Solution

Project **the original trend in sentences (1) and (2)** without the youth group's training program. Without young successors learning the craft, **rattan weaving in the village becomes increasingly likely to vanish once elderly artisans can no longer work**.

> A counterfactual inference removes the text's intervention and follows the baseline trend to its natural outcome.

## Evaluating all five predicted outcomes

| Proposed outcome | Fit with the baseline in sentences (1) and (2) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rattan weaving is increasingly threatened with extinction once elderly artisans stop working | Direct continuation of having no young successors | **Correct** |
| Young people stay in the village to weave rattan | Contradicts sentence (2) and only happened because of the class | **Wrong** |
| Village rattan bags sell for more | High online prices resulted from the trained youth | **Wrong** |
| City buyers visit the village directly | Never mentioned in the passage | **Wrong** |
| Elderly artisans teach weaving at city factories | Completely unsupported by the text | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The most likely outcome is that **rattan weaving in the village becomes increasingly threatened with disappearing once elderly artisans can no longer work** (*Kerajinan rotan di desa itu makin terancam hilang ketika perajin tua tidak lagi mampu bekerja*).
