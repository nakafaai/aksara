The sentence containing a nonstandard Indonesian spelling.

# Solution

Check loanword spellings against **official KBBI headwords**. The word *frekwensi* in sentence (1) is nonstandard, because standard Indonesian spells it with *u* as **frekuensi**.

> Standard Indonesian writes loanwords such as *frekuensi*, *kualitas*, and *kuantitas* with *u* rather than *w*.
