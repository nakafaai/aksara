# Given

Sentence (1) introduces **the fixed departure schedule starting in April**, and sentence (2) opens with <Highlight>Sebelumnya, angkutan itu baru berangkat setelah penumpangnya penuh</Highlight> (Previously, minibuses only departed once full).

# Asked

The relationship between sentence (2) and sentence (1).

# Solution

Focus on **the time marker *Sebelumnya* (Previously) opening sentence (2)**. That signal explicitly frames sentence (2) as **describing the earlier situation before the change announced in sentence (1)**.

> Temporal openers such as *Sebelumnya* explicitly introduce background from before the new state described in the preceding sentence.

## Comparing all five proposed relationships

| Proposed relationship | Match with sentences (1) and (2) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explains the situation before the change in sentence (1) | Directly signaled by *Sebelumnya* describing the old practice | **Correct** |
| States an effect of the fixed schedule | Long waits happened before the fixed schedule, not because of it | **Wrong** |
| Contradicts sentence (1) | Adds prior context rather than contradicting sentence (1) | **Wrong** |
| Gives an example of waiting passengers | Mentions no individual passenger examples | **Wrong** |
| Summarizes the table | The table does not appear until after sentence (4) | **Wrong** |

# Answer

Sentence (2) **explains the situation before the change stated in sentence (1)** (*Kalimat (2) menjelaskan keadaan sebelum perubahan yang disebut dalam kalimat (1)*).
