# Given

Sentence (5) defines **an infiltration well** as <Highlight>lubang sedalam sekitar <InlineMath math="2" /> meter yang diisi batu dan ijuk</Highlight> (a pit about two meters deep filled with stones and palm fiber), and sentence (6) channels roof water into *lubang itu* (that pit).

# Asked

The referent of *lubang itu* (that pit) in sentence (6).

# Solution

Connect **the demonstrative *itu* straight back to the noun defined in sentence (5)**. Because sentence (5) defines the infiltration well as a stone-and-fiber-filled pit, *lubang itu* refers directly to **the infiltration well filled with stones and palm fiber**.

> A demonstrative phrase like *lubang itu* points back to the exact noun phrase defined immediately before it.

## Checking all five candidate referents

| Candidate referent | Role in the passage | Match for *lubang itu* | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infiltration well filled with stones and palm fiber | Explicitly called *lubang* (pit) in sentence (5) | Exact antecedent | **Correct** |
| Dry household water wells | Wells being replenished underground | Not the pit receiving roof pipes | **Wrong** |
| Street drains in front of houses | Old runoff channel in sentence (2) | Never called a pit | **Wrong** |
| Cement-paved yards | Impermeable surface from sentence (2) | Not a pit | **Wrong** |
| House roofs | Origin of the rainwater in sentence (6) | Source, not destination | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The phrase *lubang itu* refers to **the infiltration well filled with stones and palm fiber** (*sumur resapan yang diisi batu dan ijuk*).
