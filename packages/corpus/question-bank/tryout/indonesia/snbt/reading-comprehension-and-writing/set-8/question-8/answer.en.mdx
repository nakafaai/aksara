The demonstrative _itu_ **points to what was just mentioned**. Sentence (5) describes the infiltration well as _lubang sedalam sekitar 2 meter yang diisi batu dan ijuk_, a hole about 2 metres deep filled with stones and palm fibre. Sentence (6) then says rainwater is led into _lubang itu_, that hole.

So _lubang itu_ refers to the infiltration well filled with stones and palm fibre.

- The residents' wells are what should be refilled, not the hole the rainwater goes into.
- The gutter is where water ran before the infiltration wells.
- The cemented yards are never called a hole.
- The roof is where the rainwater comes from, not where it goes.
