# Given

The *rattan-weaving* passage describes **Desa Tegalsari's nearly lost rattan craft** in paragraph one and the youth group's weaving classes in paragraph two, which train ten young artisans who now earn a living in the village.

# Asked

The main idea of the entire passage.

# Solution

Select the sentence that **unifies the threat of decline, the youth group's classes, and the craft's revival across both paragraphs**, rather than repeating one supporting detail.

> A passage's main idea ties both paragraphs together into one complete summary instead of zooming in on a single sentence.

## Comparing all five options

| Proposed main idea | Coverage of the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weaving classes run by the youth group revive rattan weaving in Desa Tegalsari | Unites the problem, intervention, and revival across both paragraphs | **Correct** |
| Most rattan artisans in Desa Tegalsari are elderly | Covers only the opening background in sentence (1) | **Too narrow** |
| Young people prefer working in city factories | Covers only sentence (2) | **Too narrow** |
| Rattan bags are sold through online shops | Covers only sentence (6) | **Too narrow** |
| City buyers pay high prices for handmade rattan bags | Covers only sentence (7) | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The main idea is that **weaving classes held by the youth group are reviving the rattan craft in Desa Tegalsari** (*Kelas menganyam yang diadakan karang taruna menghidupkan kembali kerajinan rotan di Desa Tegalsari*).
