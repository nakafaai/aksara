The demonstrative _ini_ **points to what was just mentioned**, the content of the sentence right before it. Sentence (8) says bakers mix fermented cassava flour with a little wheat flour. Sentence (9) then says this way still saves money.

So _Cara ini_ refers to mixing fermented cassava flour with a little wheat flour.

- Soaking is in sentence (3), far back, and is how the flour is made, not how bakers save wheat flour.
- Cakes made only of cassava flour are called crumbly, so that is what bakers avoid.
- Farmers growing the cassava is the reason the cost is low, not the way referred to.
- Drying and grinding are in sentence (4), part of making the flour.
