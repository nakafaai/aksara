Sentence (7) has two parts. The first, _tepung singkong tidak mengandung gluten seperti terigu_, is **a cause**. The second, _kue ... biasanya lebih rapuh_, is **its effect**. A cause clause placed at the start of a sentence opens with the conjunction _karena_.

So the right word is _Karena_, because.

- _Meskipun_, although, marks a contrast, but the crumbly cake is the result of the missing gluten.
- _Sehingga_, so that, marks an effect and does not open a sentence.
- _Bahkan_, even, adds emphasis, not a cause.
- _Agar_, in order to, marks a purpose, and no one wants a crumbly cake.
