# Given

The peat-fire passage contains **three sentences tested for grammatical accuracy**: sentence (8) (*baru mereka akan tinggalkan*), sentence (9) (*para relawan saling bahu-membahu*), and sentence (11) (*lebih sulit terbakar daripada gambut yang kering*).

# Asked

Whether each of the four proposed revisions is **Tepat** ("Correct") or **Tidak tepat** ("Incorrect").

# Solution

Test passive word order, reciprocal redundancy, plural nouns, and comparatives. In Indonesian pronoun-agent passives, ***akan*** ("will") must precede *mereka tinggalkan*, and ***saling*** ("mutually") duplicates *bahu-membahu*. Meanwhile, *para relawan* and comparative *daripada* are already standard.

> In pronoun-agent passives, place auxiliary words before the pronoun (*akan mereka tinggalkan*), and keep *daripada* for comparisons.

## Checking all four proposed edits

The originals read <Highlight>Lahan itu baru mereka akan tinggalkan</Highlight>, <Highlight>para relawan saling bahu-membahu</Highlight>, and <Highlight>jauh lebih sulit terbakar daripada gambut yang kering</Highlight>.

| Proposed edit | Verdict | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *baru mereka akan tinggalkan*: *baru akan mereka tinggalkan* (8) | **Tepat** | Auxiliary *akan* must precede agent *mereka* before verb |
| Delete *saling* in sentence (9) | **Tepat** | *Bahu-membahu* already conveys mutual cooperation |
| Delete *para* in (9) claiming *relawan* is plural | **Tidak tepat** | *Relawan* is not inherently plural (*seorang relawan* is valid) |
| Replace *daripada* with *dari* in (11) | **Tidak tepat** | Comparison *lebih sulit ... daripada* requires *daripada* |

# Answer

Proposals **one and two** are **Tepat**, while proposals **three and four** are **Tidak tepat**.
