The words _pengolahan_ and _pengemasan_ are built with the affix pair _peN-an_, which means **a process or an act**. _Pengolahan_ is the act of processing, and _pengemasan_ is the act of packing. The ending _-an_ alone has another meaning: **a result or a thing**. _Olahan_ is what comes out of processing, and _kemasan_ is the container or wrapping that comes out of packing.

Now check what sentence (4) needs in both places.

First place: _cascara, yaitu ... kulit buah kopi yang dikeringkan_ ("cascara, that is, ... of dried coffee cherry husks"). The word _yaitu_ ("that is") equates _cascara_ with the word after it. _Cascara_ is the product being sold, so its equivalent has to be a thing. That thing is the **result** of processing coffee husks, so the right form is _olahan_ ("a processed product"). You can't sell a process in a pack.

Second place: _dalam ... kedap udara_ ("in airtight ..."). What's airtight is the container. A product sits inside a **thing**, so the right form is _kemasan_ ("packaging"). _Pengemasan kedap udara_ would mean the activity of packing, and goods aren't sold inside an activity.

The result: _Kelompok itu menjual cascara, yaitu olahan kulit buah kopi yang dikeringkan, dalam kemasan kedap udara._

The other options are only half right, or wrong in both places.

- _Pengolahan_ and _pengemasan_ leave both faults in.
- _Olahan_ and _pengemasan_ fix only the first place.
- _Pengolahan_ and _kemasan_ fix only the second place.
- _Pengolah_ and _pengemas_ mean the person or tool that processes and packs. _Cascara_ isn't a doer, and goods aren't sold inside a doer.

So the right forms, in order, are _olahan_ and _kemasan_.
