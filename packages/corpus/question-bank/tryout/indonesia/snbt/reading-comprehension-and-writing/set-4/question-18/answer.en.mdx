# Given

Sentence (4) reads: *"Kelompok itu menjual cascara, yaitu pengolahan kulit buah kopi yang dikeringkan, dalam pengemasan kedap udara."*

Indonesian *peN-an* nouns (*pengolahan*, *pengemasan*) denote **processes**, whereas *-an* nouns (*olahan*, *kemasan*) denote **results or physical objects**.

# Asked

The right word pair to replace *pengolahan* and *pengemasan* in sentence (4).

# Solution

Both slots in sentence (4) describe **physical goods**, not activities. *Cascara* is the **processed product** (***olahan***) sold inside an airtight **package** (***kemasan***), never a process sold inside a packaging act.

> Keep Indonesian *peN-an* (process) distinct from *-an* (product/object): you sell an *olahan* ("processed product") inside a *kemasan* ("package").

## Replacing process nouns with product nouns in sentence four

Sentence (4) misuses two process nouns: <Highlight>menjual _cascara_, yaitu pengolahan kulit buah kopi yang dikeringkan, dalam pengemasan kedap udara</Highlight>.

1. *cascara* is the resulting product: ***olahan***.
2. *dalam ... kedap udara* names the airtight container: ***kemasan***.

## Why the other four pairs fail

- ***Pengolahan* and *pengemasan*** leaves both process errors intact.
- ***Olahan* and *pengemasan*** fixes only the first slot.
- ***Pengolahan* and *kemasan*** fixes only the second slot.
- ***Pengolah* and *pengemas*** mean agents or machines ("processor" and "packer"), not product or package.

# Answer

The correct forms in order are ***olahan* dan *kemasan***.
