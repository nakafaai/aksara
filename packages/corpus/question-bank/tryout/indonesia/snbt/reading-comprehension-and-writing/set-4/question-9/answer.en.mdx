# Given

Paragraph two of the peat-fire passage builds four core steps: (4) introduces the crew's habit, (5) details soaking subsurface peat and monitoring temperature for three days, (7) describes probing at <InlineMath math="30" /> cm depth, and (8) states **they leave only once every point matches unburned peat temperature**.

# Asked

The main idea of the second paragraph.

# Solution

Combine every sentence into one complete summary rather than lifting a single detail or repeating paragraph one. The whole paragraph explains one point: **volunteers only leave burned peatland after wetting and temperature checks confirm subsurface fire is completely out**.

> A paragraph's main idea unites the action, method, and goal across all its sentences instead of copying one detail or an earlier paragraph.

## Synthesizing paragraph two into one main idea

Paragraph two peaks across sentences (5) and (8): <Highlight>menusukkan pipa berujung runcing ke dalam gambut, menyemprotkan air sampai lapisan bawahnya basah</Highlight> until <Highlight>setelah suhu gambut di setiap titik ukur sama dengan suhu gambut di sekitarnya yang tidak terbakar</Highlight>.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Measuring with a soil thermometer** repeats sentence (7) alone.
- **Peat fire can reignite days later** summarizes paragraph one, not paragraph two.
- **The crew's habit is excessive** ignores *mula-mula tampak* ("at first seems") in sentence (4).
- **Spraying water deep below** picks only one of three steps in sentence (5).

# Answer

The main idea is that **volunteers leave burned land only after soaking and measuring peat temperatures to ensure the underground fire is truly out** (*Regu relawan baru meninggalkan lahan yang terbakar setelah memastikan, dengan membasahi dan mengukur suhu gambut, bahwa api di dalam gambut benar-benar padam.*).
