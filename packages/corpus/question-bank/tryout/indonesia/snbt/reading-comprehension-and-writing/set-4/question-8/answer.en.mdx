The order of steps is **written out in sentences (3) and (4)**. Sentence (3) says the cassava is peeled, sliced, and then soaked. Sentence (4) opens with _Setelah itu_, after that, which points back to the soaking, and says the slices are dried, ground, and sifted until fine.

So after soaking, the slices are dried, ground, and then sifted.

- Peeling and slicing come before the soaking.
- The good bacteria go into the soaking water, so they are part of the soaking itself.
- Mixing with wheat flour is what bakers do when baking, not when making the flour.
- The reading says the fermented flour does not smell of cassava.
