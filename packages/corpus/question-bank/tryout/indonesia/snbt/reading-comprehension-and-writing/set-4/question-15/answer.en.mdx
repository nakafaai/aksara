# Given

Sentence (2) states the goal of placing platform bookshelves: helping riders **spend waiting and travel time reading**.

Sentence (13) states organizers' conclusion that almost all borrowers return books, and sentence (14) adds: ***"Meskipun begitu (Even so), that count cannot show whether the books were actually read."***

# Asked

The author's main purpose in adding sentence (14) right after the organizers' conclusion.

# Solution

Look at ***Meskipun begitu*** ("Even so"): the author accepts that books come back, yet **limits how far the data reaches**. Returned books show low loss, not whether the project's actual goal of reading was achieved.

> A closing sentence opened with *Even so* sets the boundary of the evidence so readers do not claim more than the data measures.

## Seeing the evidence limit in sentence fourteen

Sentence (14) states the limit clearly: <Highlight>Meskipun begitu, hitungan itu tidak dapat menunjukkan apakah buku-buku itu benar-benar dibaca</Highlight>.

Paired with the reading goal in sentence (2), it guards against treating book returns as proof that commuters actually read them.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Refuting the organizers' conclusion** misreads *Meskipun begitu*, which concedes sentence (13).
- **Showing the movement failed** confuses *unknown* with *unread*.
- **Explaining why counts stayed above <InlineMath math="95\%" />** was already handled in sentence (13).
- **Proposing a stricter card system** adds a suggestion absent from the passage.

# Answer

The author adds sentence (14) mainly **to limit what can be concluded from the count, since returned books are not necessarily read even though reading is the movement's goal** (*membatasi apa yang dapat disimpulkan dari hitungan itu, sebab buku yang kembali belum tentu dibaca, padahal membaca adalah tujuan gerakan itu*).
