A good title **covers the whole reading, no broader and no narrower**. The reading tells how students used a rain gauge and checked the drain to find out why the field floods, and found that the drain was blocked.

So the best title is _Penakar Hujan Siswa Mengungkap Penyebab Genangan di Lapangan Sekolah_, a students' rain gauge reveals why the school field floods.

- _Musim Hujan di Lembang_, the rainy season in Lembang, is too broad, since the reading covers one school field.
- How to make the rain gauge is only mentioned in passing in sentence (3).
- The cost of enlarging the drain is only in sentence (8).
- Cancelled ceremonies are only an effect of the problem in sentence (1).
