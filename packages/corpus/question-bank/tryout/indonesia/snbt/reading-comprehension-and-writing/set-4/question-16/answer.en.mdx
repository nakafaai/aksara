Each of the sentences named has one fault. A proposal is right when it **hits that fault and removes it**. So find each sentence's fault first, then judge the proposals.

**First proposal**: Right. Sentence (13) uses _baik pada pagi hari dan sore hari_. The word _baik_ is half of the fixed pair _baik ... maupun ..._ ("both ... and ..."). Its partner can't be swapped for _dan_ ("and"). Once repaired, it reads _baik pada pagi hari maupun sore hari_ ("both in the morning and in the afternoon").

**Second proposal**: Not right. The fault in sentence (11) isn't _agar_ ("so that"). Read the sentence: _Kulit yang dijemur di atas para-para bambu agar tidak bercampur dengan debu dan tanah._ The word _yang_ ("which") turns _dijemur di atas para-para bambu_ ("dried on bamboo racks") into a mere description of _kulit_ ("the husks"), so the sentence has no predicate. The repair is to delete _yang_. _Agar_ and _supaya_ mean the same, so swapping them fixes nothing.

**Third proposal**: Not right. Sentence (14) opens with _Menurut ketua kelompok mengatakan bahwa_. Two patterns are mixed here: _menurut ketua kelompok, ..._ ("according to the group leader, ...") and _ketua kelompok mengatakan bahwa ..._ ("the group leader says that ..."). Because of _menurut_, _ketua kelompok_ becomes part of an adverbial, and the verb _mengatakan_ has no subject. Deleting _bahwa_ ("that") gives _Menurut ketua kelompok mengatakan penjemuran ..._, and _mengatakan_ still has no subject.

**Fourth proposal**: Right. Without _menurut_, the sentence becomes _Ketua kelompok mengatakan bahwa penjemuran yang ketat itulah yang membuat cascara dari desa itu diminati kedai kopi di kota._ Now _ketua kelompok_ is the subject and _mengatakan_ is the predicate. Only one pattern is in use.

The third and fourth proposals both delete one word from sentence (14), but only one of them gives the subject back.

So the first and fourth proposals are right, while the second and third aren't.
