The structure shows in **the order of what the sentences say, from first to last**.

- Sentence (1) states the problem, the field floods often.
- Sentence (2) states the suspected cause, a drain that is too small.
- Sentences (3) and (4) describe how the suspicion is tested.
- Sentences (5) to (7) give the findings.
- Sentence (8) gives the students' proposal.

So the structure is problem, suspected cause, how it is tested, findings, then proposal.

The other options put these parts in an order the reading does not follow, or leave out key parts such as the test and the findings.
