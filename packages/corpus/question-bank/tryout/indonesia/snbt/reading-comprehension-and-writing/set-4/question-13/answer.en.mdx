# Given

The sentence to insert reads: *"Dari kedua nama stasiun itu, pengelola menghitung berapa stasiun yang dilalui setiap buku."* ("From those two station names, organizers count how many stations each book travelled through.")

Sentence (6) names the **pickup station**, sentence (7) adds the **return station**, and sentence (8) opens with ***"Hasilnya (As a result), short-story collections travelled the farthest..."***.

# Asked

Where the sentence should be inserted.

# Solution

Match both links at once: backward, ***kedua nama stasiun itu*** ("those two station names") is only complete after **sentence (7)** (pickup in sentence (6) plus return in sentence (7)), and forward, ***Hasilnya*** in (8) needs the counting step right before it.

> An insertion point must sit after a plural pointer (*both X*) is complete and immediately before a result opener (*As a result*).

## Bridging sentences seven and eight

Sentences (6) to (8) show the exact missing step: <Highlight>menuliskan tanggal dan nama stasiun tempat ia mengambil buku. (7) Saat mengembalikan buku, ia menambahkan nama stasiun tempat buku itu ditaruh kembali. (8) Hasilnya, kumpulan cerita pendek ternyata paling jauh berpindah</Highlight>.

Only **after sentence (7)** joins both station names to the calculation behind *Hasilnya*.

## Why the other four spots fail

- **After sentence (6)** has only one station name so far, not two.
- **After sentence (5)** introduces the card with zero station names written yet.
- **After sentence (4)** or **(3)** has not even mentioned the loan card.

# Answer

The sentence fits best **after sentence (7)** (*setelah kalimat (7)*).
