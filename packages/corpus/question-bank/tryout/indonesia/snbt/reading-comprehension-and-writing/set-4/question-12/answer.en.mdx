Sentence (9) lists who looks after the shelves. In a list, each comma separates one item from the next, so where the commas go decides **how many groups of caretakers there are**. That means you need to check two things: the number of groups the text gives, and the punctuation rules of EYD V (the official Indonesian spelling guide, fifth edition).

Start with the number of groups. With its current commas, sentence (9) has four items: _mahasiswa_ ("university students"), _pensiunan_ ("retirees"), _guru_ ("teachers"), and _pedagang kios di peron_ ("kiosk sellers on the platform"). Now read sentence (10): _Mahasiswa mendapat giliran sore, sedangkan kedua kelompok lainnya berbagi giliran pagi dan siang_, "the students take the afternoon shift, while the other two groups share the morning and midday shifts." That's the students plus two other groups.

<BlockMath math="1 + 2 = 3 \text{ groups}" />

So the list has to contain exactly three items, and the students are one of them. Two neighbouring words must merge into one item. _Guru_ can't merge with _pedagang kios_, but _pensiunan guru_ ("retired teachers") is a sensible single group. So the comma between _pensiunan_ and _guru_ has to go.

The other marks still follow EYD V.

- The sentence opens with the subordinate clause _Agar buku-buku tetap tersusun rapi_ ("so that the books stay neatly arranged"). EYD V separates a subordinate clause that comes before the main clause with a comma, so the comma after _rapi_ must stay.
- A list that completes the statement as its complement doesn't get a colon in front of it. Here the list is the agent after _oleh_ ("by"), so there's no colon.
- EYD V puts commas between list items, including before _dan_ ("and") in a list of three, as in its example _Buku, majalah, dan jurnal termasuk sumber kepustakaan_.

The result: _Agar buku-buku tetap tersusun rapi, rak-rak itu dirawat bergiliran oleh mahasiswa, pensiunan guru, dan pedagang kios di peron._ Check it against sentence (10): students plus retired teachers and kiosk sellers, exactly three groups.

Check the other versions.

- The colon after _oleh_ does give three groups, but it cuts _oleh_ off from its agent. EYD V doesn't use a colon before a list that serves as a complement.
- The unchanged sentence still has four groups, so it contradicts _kedua kelompok lainnya_ ("the other two groups") in sentence (10).
- The version without a comma after _rapi_ gives three groups, but it drops the comma that's required after an opening subordinate clause.
- Removing the comma before _dan_ doesn't merge any items. The list still has four groups: students, retirees, teachers, and kiosk sellers.

So the correct version is _Agar buku-buku tetap tersusun rapi, rak-rak itu dirawat bergiliran oleh mahasiswa, pensiunan guru, dan pedagang kios di peron._
