Sentence (6) uses _di mana_ to join two parts of the sentence. In standard Indonesian, _di mana_ is **a question word for place**, not a connector. The overflowing water and the drain full of leaves and sand stand in a cause relation, so the right word is _karena_, because: _air di saluran meluap karena saluran penuh oleh daun kering dan pasir_.

So the sentence that uses a word wrongly is sentence (6).
