# Given

Sentence (9) reads: *"Agar buku-buku tetap tersusun rapi, rak-rak itu dirawat bergiliran oleh mahasiswa, pensiunan, guru, dan pedagang kios di peron."*

Sentence (10) specifies: **"Students take the afternoon shift, whereas the other two groups share morning and midday shifts."**

# Asked

The punctuation of sentence (9) that satisfies both EYD V and the passage facts.

# Solution

Count the **volunteer groups** in sentence (10): students plus two other groups equals **three groups** total. The comma between *pensiunan* and *guru* splits them into four, so remove that comma to form ***pensiunan guru*** ("retired teachers") while keeping the commas after *rapi* and before *dan*.

> Commas in a list determine how many groups exist, so always cross-check list punctuation against the next sentence's count.

## Matching sentence nine punctuation to sentence ten

Sentence (10) locks the total at three groups: <Highlight>Mahasiswa mendapat giliran sore, sedangkan kedua kelompok lainnya berbagi giliran pagi dan siang</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="1 + 2 = 3\text{ groups}" />

Under EYD V, keep the comma after the front clause (*rapi,*), avoid a colon after *oleh*, and keep the serial comma before *dan*.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Colon after *oleh*** breaks EYD V because the list completes the clause directly.
- **Keeping *pensiunan, guru*** leaves four groups, contradicting sentence (10).
- **Dropping the comma after *rapi*** violates the rule for leading subordinate clauses.
- **Dropping the comma before *dan*** still leaves *pensiunan* and *guru* split into four groups.

# Answer

The correct sentence is **"Agar buku-buku tetap tersusun rapi, rak-rak itu dirawat bergiliran oleh mahasiswa, pensiunan guru, dan pedagang kios di peron."**
