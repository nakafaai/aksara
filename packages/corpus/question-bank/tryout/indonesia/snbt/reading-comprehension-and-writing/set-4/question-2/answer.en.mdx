# Given

**Sentence (6) in** the grafted-seedlings passage must be summarized while keeping both the information source and the detail that not all grafted seedlings flower in year three.

# Asked

The tightest summary that preserves every required detail of sentence (6).

# Solution

> Summarizing is like packing a suitcase: **drop wordy filler, yet never leave behind core qualifiers like *most* or *according to the farmers' records***.

Shortening a sentence means dropping words that add no information while **keeping all the information**. The question already names two things that must stay: the source, and the note that not all grafted seedlings flower in the third year. One more thing is easy to miss: the word _biasanya_ ("usually") for trees grown from seed.

First check which words in sentence (6) can go.

- _Pada umumnya_ ("in general") and _sebagian besar di antaranya_ ("most of them") say the same thing. _Sebagian besar_ ("most") is enough.
- _Bibit-bibit hasil sambungan itu_ can simply be _bibit sambungan itu_ ("those grafted seedlings"). The word _sebagian besar_ already shows there are many.
- _Sudah mulai dapat berbunga_ ("already able to start flowering") can simply be _mulai berbunga_ ("start flowering").
- _Tahun yang ketiga sesudah ditanam_ can simply be _tahun ketiga setelah ditanam_ ("the third year after planting").

Now test each version against the three required pieces.

| Version | Source | _sebagian besar_ | _biasanya_ | Still wordy? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Without _sebagian besar_ | Kept | Lost | Kept | No |
| Without _menurut catatan_ | Lost | Kept | Kept | No |
| Without _biasanya_ | Kept | Kept | Lost | No |
| With _dari bibit-bibit_, _sudah_ and _yang ketiga_ | Kept | Kept | Kept | Yes |
| Complete and lean | Kept | Kept | Kept | No |

The version without _sebagian besar_ makes it sound as if every grafted seedling flowers in the third year. The version without _biasanya_ makes it sound as if every tree from seed only flowers after the seventh year. Both are short, but they change the information. The version that still has _sebagian besar dari bibit-bibit_ and _tahun yang ketiga_ is complete but not the shortest.

So the right version is: _Menurut catatan kelompok tani itu, sebagian besar bibit sambungan itu mulai berbunga pada tahun ketiga setelah ditanam, sedangkan pohon dari biji biasanya baru berbunga setelah tahun ketujuh._

# Answer

The right summary is ***"Menurut catatan kelompok tani itu, sebagian besar bibit sambungan itu mulai berbunga pada tahun ketiga setelah ditanam, sedangkan pohon dari biji biasanya baru berbunga setelah tahun ketujuh."***
