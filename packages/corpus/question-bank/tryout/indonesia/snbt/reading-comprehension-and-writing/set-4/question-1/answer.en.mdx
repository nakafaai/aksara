The main idea of a reading is **what both of its paragraphs hold together**. The first paragraph names the problem, abrasion, and then the fishermen's effort of building a mangrove nursery. The second paragraph continues that effort: the seedlings are planted, protected, and will later hold back the waves.

So the main idea is that the fishermen's group of Desa Pasir Putih holds back abrasion by raising and planting mangroves.
