Sentence (8) **has no subject**. The phrase _di sela-sela akar bakau_ begins with the preposition _di_, so it is an adverbial of place, not a subject. The predicate _menjadi tempat_ is left without anything doing it. Dropping _di_ turns _sela-sela akar bakau_ into a clear subject.

So the right revision is _Selain itu, sela-sela akar bakau menjadi tempat kepiting dan ikan kecil berkembang biak._

- Adding _yang_ leaves the sentence with no predicate at all.
- Changing _menjadi_ to _dijadikan_ still leaves only an adverbial of place and no subject.
