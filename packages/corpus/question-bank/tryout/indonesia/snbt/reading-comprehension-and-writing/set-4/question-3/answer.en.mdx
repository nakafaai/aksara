A connector between sentences has to fit how the two sentences relate. So first put the relation between sentences (7) and (8) into words without looking at the connector.

- Sentence (7): the menteng tree in Dusun Babakan gives the sweetest fruit.
- Sentence (8): shoots from that tree are used for at most a fifth of the menteng seedlings.

If its fruit is the sweetest, you'd expect its shoots to be used as much as possible. In fact they're limited. Sentence (8) **goes against the expectation sentence (7) creates**. That relation is called concessive, and the sentence connector for it is _meskipun demikian_ ("even so"). The reason for the limit only comes in sentences (9) and (10): seedlings from one parent tree inherit the same weakness to disease.

_Oleh karena itu_ ("therefore") states a result. With it, the limit seems to happen because the fruit is sweet, and that isn't logical.

Check the other repairs.

- Swapping _paling banyak_ ("at most") for _paling sedikit_ ("at least") does make _oleh karena itu_ sound logical, but the information changes. The question asks to keep the information, and sentence (10) supports an upper limit.
- _Meskipun_ ("although") without _demikian_ is a connector inside a sentence. A sentence starting with bare _meskipun_ becomes a subordinate clause with no main clause, so it's incomplete.
- _Bahkan_ ("in fact", "even") signals reinforcement, as if the limit continued and went beyond sentence (7).
- _Sebaliknya_ ("on the contrary", "conversely") sets two different things against each other, for example two trees. Here the topic is still the same tree.

So the right repair is: _Meskipun demikian, pucuk dari pohon itu dipakai untuk paling banyak seperlima bibit menteng._
