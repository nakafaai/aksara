# Given

Sentence (7) states that **the menteng tree in Dusun Babakan yields the sweetest fruit** among all parent trees.

Sentence (8) writes *"Oleh karena itu (Therefore), shoots from that tree are used for at most one fifth of menteng seedlings"*, before sentences (9) and (10) explain this cap limits shared disease risk.

# Asked

The accurate revision of sentence (8) without changing its factual content.

# Solution

Sweetest fruit would normally lead readers to expect heavy propagation, yet sentence (8) **caps** its share at one fifth. That clash against expectation is concessive, so *Oleh karena itu,* must become ***Meskipun demikian***, ("Even so,").

> Use *Meskipun demikian,* (never standalone *Meskipun*) as an inter-sentence connector when the next sentence runs against the expected outcome.

## Fixing the connector between sentences seven and eight

Sentences (7) and (8) pull in opposite directions: <Highlight>menghasilkan buah paling manis</Highlight> ("produces the sweetest fruit") followed by <Highlight>Oleh karena itu, pucuk dari pohon itu dipakai untuk paling banyak seperlima bibit menteng</Highlight>.

Replacing *Oleh karena itu,* with ***Meskipun demikian***, restores logical concession while preserving the *at most one fifth* limit.

## Why the other four options fail

- Switching to **paling sedikit** ("at least") changes the passage's stated cap.
- Starting with **Meskipun** ("Although") alone turns sentence (8) into an incomplete fragment.
- ***Bahkan***, ("In fact/Even,") intensifies in the same direction rather than contrasting.
- ***Sebaliknya***, ("On the contrary,") contrasts two different subjects rather than one tree.

# Answer

The right revision is **"Meskipun demikian, pucuk dari pohon itu dipakai untuk paling banyak seperlima bibit menteng."**
