# Given

The *cascara* passage sets **two strict criteria** (sentences 5 and 10) plus a drying check (sentence 12): cherries must be **fully red**, husks must reach drying racks **within six hours** of pulping, and husks must stay **mold-free**. Sentence (3) states husks **failing the criteria become compost**.

# Asked

Which statement is **definitely** true based on the passage.

# Solution

Becoming *cascara* requires passing **every single filter**, since failing just one filter sends the husk straight to compost. Arriving **ten hours** after pulping breaks the six-hour limit, so that husk **definitely becomes compost** even if picked fully red.

> When a product requires condition A **and** condition B, passing one guarantees nothing, whereas failing either one guarantees rejection.

## Applying the three filters to every option

The passage sets the rule explicitly: <Highlight>kulit yang tidak memenuhi syarat dijadikan kompos</Highlight> ("husks not meeting the criteria become compost") and <Highlight>kulit harus sudah tiba di para-para paling lambat enam jam setelah buahnya dikupas</Highlight> ("must reach racks at most six hours after pulping").

Because ten hours exceeds six hours, that husk fails criterion two and must go to compost.

## Why the other four options are not guaranteed

- **All fully red husks become *cascara*** ignores the six-hour limit and mold filter.
- **Composted husks must be under-ripe** ignores late arrival and mold.
- **Never tastes astringent** is not guaranteed, as sorting errors or other causes are not ruled out.
- **Under six hours definitely becomes *cascara*** ignores ripeness and mold checks.

# Answer

The statement that is **definitely** true is that **husks from fully red cherries reaching the racks ten hours after pulping become compost** (*Kulit dari buah merah penuh yang baru tiba di para-para sepuluh jam setelah buahnya dikupas dijadikan kompos.*).
