# Given

Sentence (12) states **<InlineMath math="12" /> of <InlineMath math="20" /> plantation owners** rejected ditch blocking (so <InlineMath math="8" /> agreed initially).

Sentence (13) says **half of them changed their minds**, and sentence (14) says the team leader hopes ***jumlah itu*** ("that number") will eventually match the number who agreed from the start.

# Asked

What the phrase *jumlah itu* in sentence (14) refers to.

# Solution

Follow the chain back to sentence (13) and compute: *mereka* ("them") refers to the **<InlineMath math="12" /> holdouts**, so half of them is **<InlineMath math="6" /> owners** who changed their minds. Hoping <InlineMath math="6" /> eventually equals <InlineMath math="8" /> (initial supporters) fits cleanly.

> Cross-check numerical referents against the target clause: a number hoped to *eventually match* <InlineMath math="8" /> must currently be below <InlineMath math="8" />.

## Computing half of the holdouts in sentence thirteen

Sentences (12) and (13) give the exact inputs: <Highlight><InlineMath math="12" /> dari <InlineMath math="20" /> pemilik kebun di tepi hutan menolak paritnya disekat</Highlight> and <Highlight>separuh dari mereka berubah pikiran</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="20 - 12 = 8\text{ initial yes}, \qquad 12 \div 2 = 6\text{ changed minds}" />

## Why the other four numbers fail

- **<InlineMath math="12" /> initial holdouts** already exceeds <InlineMath math="8" />, so it cannot be hoped to match <InlineMath math="8" />.
- **<InlineMath math="8" /> initial supporters** is the benchmark itself.
- **<InlineMath math="10" /> owners** misreads *mereka* as all <InlineMath math="20" /> owners instead of the <InlineMath math="12" /> holdouts.
- **<InlineMath math="14" /> total supporters now** is unmentioned in sentence (13) and already exceeds <InlineMath math="8" />.

# Answer

*Jumlah itu* refers to the **<InlineMath math="6" /> plantation owners who changed their minds after seeing land near the dams stay unburned** (*<InlineMath math="6" /> pemilik kebun yang berubah pikiran setelah melihat lahan di sekitar sekat tidak terbakar*).
