# Given

Sentence (7) says the group leader asked **the pickers** (*para pemetik*) to sort out unripe cherries before **the coordinators** (*para pengurus*) weighed them.

Sentence (8) explains they refused because **they are paid by weight picked, so sorting slows work and cuts their pay**, and sentence (9) starts with ***"Keberatan itu (That objection) disappeared only after..."***.

# Asked

What the phrase *keberatan itu* in sentence (9) refers to.

# Solution

Verify both parts in sentence (8): **who** objected (**the pickers**, not the coordinators who merely weigh) and **why** (**sorting slows down work and reduces their pay**, not a complaint about the base rate per kilo).

> Resolve a pronoun like *mereka* ("they") by role rather than proximity: those paid by weight picked are clearly the pickers.

## Identifying who objected and why in sentence eight

Sentence (8) states the exact objection: <Highlight>mereka dibayar menurut berat buah yang dipetik sehingga memilah buah berarti memperlambat kerja dan mengurangi upah mereka</Highlight>.

Sentence (9) confirms this: paying more per kilogram for fully red cherries offsets the lost earnings from slower picking.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Group leader's objection** picks the person making the request.
- **Coordinators' objection** grabs the nearest noun in sentence (7) despite their weighing role.
- **Fully red cherries hard to find** never appears in the passage.
- **Base wage per kilogram too low** shifts the issue because pickers objected to lower output from sorting, not the original rate.

# Answer

*Keberatan itu* refers to **the pickers' objection that sorting fruit slows down their work and reduces their wages** (*keberatan para pemetik karena memilah buah memperlambat kerja sehingga mengurangi upah mereka*).
