The meaning of a word in a reading is set **by the context of its sentence**. Sentence (7) says mangrove roots _meredam kekuatan ombak sebelum mencapai daratan_. The waves still reach the land, but with less force. That fits the KBBI sense of _meredam_, to hold back or reduce the force of something.

So _meredam_ in sentence (7) means to reduce.

- _Membalikkan_ means turning the waves back to sea, which the reading never says.
- _Menenggelamkan_, to sink, does not fit waves.
- _Menghentikan sama sekali_, to stop completely, contradicts _sebelum mencapai daratan_, which shows the waves still arrive.
- _Membelokkan_ means changing direction, not force.
