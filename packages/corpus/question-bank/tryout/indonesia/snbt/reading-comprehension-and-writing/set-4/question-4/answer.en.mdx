# Given

Sentence (5) establishes that every grafted seedling's rootstock is **grown from seed so it has a taproot**.

Sentence (13) reports that last windy season on the eastern slope, **<InlineMath math="40" /> of <InlineMath math="60" /> air-layered trees** and **<InlineMath math="2" /> of <InlineMath math="30" /> grafted trees** fell.

# Asked

Which statement is **definitely** true based on the passage.

# Solution

Combine sentences (5) and (13): because **<InlineMath math="2" /> grafted trees** fell on the eastern slope and every grafted tree has a **taproot**, at least **<InlineMath math="2" /> taproot trees** definitely fell there (*at least* allows for any other seed-grown trees).

> For *definitely true* questions, join category definitions from early paragraphs with exact counts later, and check proportions as fractions rather than raw counts.

## Combining the taproot rule with eastern slope counts

Sentences (5) and (13) guarantee this directly: <Highlight>Batang bawah setiap bibit ditumbuhkan dari biji sehingga berakar tunggang</Highlight> and <Highlight>dari <InlineMath math="30" /> pohon sambungan asal kebun itu di lereng yang sama hanya <InlineMath math="2" /> yang tumbang</Highlight>.

Checking the fall-rate ratio confirms it is <InlineMath math="10" /> times, not <InlineMath math="20" /> times:

<BlockMath math="\frac{40}{60} = \frac{2}{3}, \qquad \frac{2}{30} = \frac{1}{15}, \qquad \frac{2/3}{1/15} = 10" />

## Why the other four options are not guaranteed

- **Equally wind-resistant on western slope**: Unproven, since hills shielded that slope from wind.
- **Over half of all fallen village trees**: Unproven, since non-sample trees in the village are uncounted.
- **Proportion <InlineMath math="20" /> times higher**: Compares raw counts (<InlineMath math="40" /> vs <InlineMath math="2" />) instead of rates (<InlineMath math="10" /> times).
- **At most <InlineMath math="4" /> would fall if replaced**: Speculative projection not guaranteed by past data.

# Answer

The statement that is **definitely** true is that **last windy season, at least <InlineMath math="2" /> taproot trees on the eastern slope fell** (*Pada musim angin tahun lalu, sedikitnya <InlineMath math="2" /> pohon berakar tunggang di lereng timur ikut tumbang.*).
