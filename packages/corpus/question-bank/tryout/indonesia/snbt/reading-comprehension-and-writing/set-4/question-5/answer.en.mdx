Sentence (10) reads: _Jika seluruh bibit berasal dari satu pohon induk, satu jenis penyakit dapat merusak semuanya sekaligus._ ("If all the seedlings come from one parent tree, a single kind of disease can ruin all of them at once.") Each word carries a piece of the meaning: whether this is a condition or a fact, how many seedlings, how certain the result is, what the result is, and when it happens. Test each replacement by asking: **which piece of meaning shifts with it**?

Replacing _seluruh_ with _semua_ shifts nothing. _Seluruh bibit_ and _semua bibit_ both mean that no seedling is left out. The sentence becomes _Jika semua bibit berasal dari satu pohon induk_, and the condition stays the same.

The other four replacements each shift one piece.

- _Dapat_ ("can") states a possibility, while _akan_ ("will") states something that's sure to happen. The original only says the disease may ruin all the seedlings. With _akan_, the damage becomes a certainty.
- _Sekaligus_ ("at once") means at the same time, while _berturut-turut_ ("one after another") means in sequence. The danger the writer means is all the seedlings being ruined together, with none left.
- _Merusak_ ("to ruin") already includes the result: the seedlings end up damaged. _Menyerang_ ("to attack") only says the disease arrives, and a seedling that's attacked isn't necessarily ruined.
- _Jika_ ("if") states a condition. The original doesn't say all the seedlings really do come from one parent tree. With _karena_ ("because"), that becomes a fact that has already happened, though sentence (8) shows the nursery actually limits how much one parent tree is used.

So the replacement that doesn't change the meaning of sentence (10) is swapping _seluruh_ for _semua_.
