**However**

The comments about the photographs are positive, but findings from a short trial cannot yet be generalised. “However” directly marks that limit on the positive feedback.

“Therefore” signals a consequence, “likewise” a similarity, “after that” a sequence, and “indeed” reinforcement. The original passage can use “therefore” because the end of sentence (8) identifies missing records. This question specifically asks for the contrast with the positive comments.
