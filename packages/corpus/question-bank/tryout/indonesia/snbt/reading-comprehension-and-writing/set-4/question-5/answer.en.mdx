An inference has to **rest on a reason the reading states**. Sentence (6) says the bamboo fence is set up _agar bibit tidak tersapu ombak_, so the seedlings are not swept away. Without the fence, what it was meant to prevent becomes the result: seedlings whose roots are not yet strong can be carried off by the waves.

So the most likely result is that some seedlings could be washed away before their roots are strong.

- The reading never says the fence slows growth, so faster growth is not supported.
- Abrasion would not stop at once even with a fence, because the roots hold the waves only _kelak_, later.
- Crabs and small fish have nothing to do with the fence.
- Washed-away seedlings would make the fishermen sow more, not stop.
