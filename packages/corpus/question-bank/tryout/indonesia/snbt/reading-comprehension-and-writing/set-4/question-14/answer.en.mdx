# Given

Sentence (3) lets passengers return a book to **any station shelf** on the line.

Sentence (11) reads: *"Setiap Jumat pukul sembilan malam, para perawat rak menghitung buku di kelima rak secara bergiliran agar tidak ada buku yang terhitung dua kali."* ("count the five shelves in turn so no book is counted twice").

# Asked

The single-word fix in sentence (11) that aligns the method with its stated goal.

# Solution

Counting shelves ***bergiliran*** ("one after another") lets a rider take an already-counted book from shelf one and drop it on shelf five before shelf five is counted, causing double-counting. All five shelves must be counted at the exact same moment, meaning ***serentak*** ("simultaneously").

> To prevent moving items from being counted twice across five stations, counts must happen simultaneously rather than sequentially.

## Aligning method and goal in sentence eleven

Sentence (11) contradicts itself directly: <Highlight>menghitung buku di kelima rak secara bergiliran agar tidak ada buku yang terhitung dua kali</Highlight>.

Replacing *bergiliran* ("in turn") with ***serentak*** ("simultaneously") eliminates double-counting between stations.

## Why the other four options fail

- ***Bergantian*** ("taking turns") means the exact same thing as *bergiliran*.
- ***Mencatat*** ("recording") instead of *menghitung* keeps the sequential timing flaw.
- ***Setiap*** ("each") instead of *kelima* ("all five") changes nothing about timing.
- ***Terbaca*** ("read") instead of *terhitung* ("counted") alters the goal entirely.

# Answer

The right fix is **replacing *bergiliran* with *serentak*** (*kata bergiliran diganti dengan serentak*).
