Sentence (11) holds a method and an aim. The method: counting the books on the five shelves _secara bergiliran_ ("taking turns", one after another). The aim: _agar tidak ada buku yang terhitung dua kali_ ("so that no book is counted twice"). Your job is to check whether that method really reaches the aim.

For that you need sentence (3). It says passengers may return a book to the shelf at **any** station. So a book can move from one shelf to another at any time.

Now picture counting in turns. The first shelf is counted, then the second, and so on. A book already counted on the first shelf could be taken by a passenger, carried onto a train and left on the fifth shelf before that shelf is counted. That book gets counted twice. So the word _bergiliran_ opens up exactly what the sentence wants to prevent.

For no book to be counted twice, the five shelves have to be counted **at the same moment**. The word that means this is _serentak_ ("simultaneously").

The other repairs don't solve that problem.

- _Bergantian_ ("alternately") means the same as _bergiliran_. The wrong word has been found, but its replacement repeats the mistake.
- Swapping _menghitung_ ("to count") for _mencatat_ ("to record") doesn't change the timing. The shelves are still handled one by one.
- _Kelima rak_ ("the five shelves") and _setiap rak_ ("every shelf") point to the same shelves, so nothing changes.
- _Terbaca_ ("read") shifts the aim of the sentence. The worry is a book entering the count twice, not a book being read.

So the right repair is to replace _bergiliran_ with _serentak_.
