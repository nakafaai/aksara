# Given

Lentera Peron started with **<InlineMath math="400" /> books** (sentence 2), and passengers **may place their own books onto the shelves** (sentence 4).

Over six months, cards logged over <InlineMath math="2{,}000" /> loans while Friday counts never dipped below **<InlineMath math="95\%" />** of starting stock (sentence 12), leading organizers to conclude **almost all borrowers returned their books** (sentence 13).

# Asked

The unstated assumption supporting the organizers' conclusion in sentence (13).

# Solution

Keeping <InlineMath math="95\%" /> of <InlineMath math="400" /> (<InlineMath math="380" /> books) proves high returns only if new donations are not masking missing loans. Because sentence (4) lets passengers add **their own books**, organizers must assume **passenger-donated books are not hiding shortfalls from unreturned borrowed books**.

> Any inference drawn from stable total inventory assumes outside inflows are not disguising unreturned items.

## Checking the stock loophole in sentences four and twelve

Sentence (4) allows extra books onto the shelves: <Highlight>Penumpang juga boleh menaruh buku miliknya di rak agar dapat dibaca orang lain</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="95\% \times 400 = 380, \qquad 400 - 380 = 20" />

If <InlineMath math="100" /> borrowed books vanished while riders added <InlineMath math="100" /> personal books, Friday counts would still show <InlineMath math="400" />, collapsing sentence (13).

## Why the other four options are not required

- **Returned to the same station**: Unneeded, since Friday counts cover all five stations together.
- **Read to the end**: Reading differs from returning, as sentence (14) notes.
- **Returned before Friday night**: Books still out lower the count rather than inflating it.
- **Donors never reclaiming their books**: Reclaiming lowers shelf counts, moving the opposite way.

# Answer

The required assumption is that **passengers' own books placed on the shelves do not cover shortfalls from unreturned borrowed books** (*Buku milik penumpang yang ditaruh di rak tidak menutupi kekurangan akibat buku pinjaman yang tidak kembali.*).
