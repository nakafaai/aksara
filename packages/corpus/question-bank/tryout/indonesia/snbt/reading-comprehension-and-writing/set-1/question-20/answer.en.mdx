A good title captures the text's full scope. A single detail is too narrow to represent the whole text.

The first paragraph gives age-specific guidance for children under <InlineMath math="5\ \text{years}" />. The second broadens the discussion to older children, adolescents, and adults through a family plan that considers content, sleep, activity, connection, privacy, safety, and communication.

**Age-Appropriate Family Media Use** covers both parts. The other titles are too narrow or contradict the passage: it rejects one universal safe age, does not call all media harmful, does not discuss buying a phone, and limits <InlineMath math="1\ \text{hour}" /> only for certain young children.
