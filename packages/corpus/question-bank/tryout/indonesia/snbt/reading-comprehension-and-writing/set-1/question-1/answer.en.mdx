The sentence to insert has two references that must both fit at the same spot. The first, *Selisih sebesar itu* ("a difference of that size"), points to a difference in amounts that was just mentioned. The second, *bunga yang dulu mereka bayar kepada tengkulak* ("the interest they used to pay the middleman"), points to a group of people who once owed a middleman and now use something else. The word *dulu* ("formerly") matters too: the debt to the middleman has to be in the past.

So test every position with three questions. What is the nearest difference? Who are *mereka* ("they")? Is the debt to the middleman already over?
