A main idea has to cover the whole paragraph, not just its most eye-catching sentence. The third paragraph runs from sentence (10) to (14), so map its content first.

| Sentence | Content |
| --- | --- |
| (10) | The app keeps the waiting room from getting crowded, even on Mondays |
| (11) | Nearly a third of app registrants are elderly patients using their own smartphones |
| (12) | Elderly patients without a smartphone can still get a number at the counter |
| (13) | However, some young patients ignore the notification, so their numbers often get moved to the end of the queue |
| (14) | Service stops at twelve, and numbers not yet called have to be registered again |

The word *Namun* ("however") at the start of sentence (13) splits the paragraph into **two parts**. The first part, (10) to (12), gives the good results: a roomier waiting room, and elderly patients are reached too. The second part, (13) and (14), gives the risk that remains. Put those two sentences together, and a patient who ignores the call time can be pushed to the end of the queue and then not be seen that day.

The right main idea has to include both parts without twisting either one. The version "the queue app eases the waiting room and is used by elderly patients too, but patients who ignore their call time may not be seen that day" does both.

Why the other versions fall short:

- The waiting room no longer being crowded is only sentence (10). The paragraph's first sentence isn't a summary here, because a turn with *Namun* comes after it.
- Elderly patients still being served covers only (11) and (12), without the risk part.
- The version saying elderly patients struggle and are often late does cover two parts, but it gets them backwards. Sentence (11) shows that many elderly patients use the app themselves, and according to (13) the ones often late are some young patients.
- Numbers that have to be registered again are only a detail of the rule in sentence (14).

So the main idea of the third paragraph is that the queue app eases the waiting room and is used by elderly patients too, but patients who ignore their call time may not be seen that day.
