In an argumentative text the main opinion is **the writer's stance that the reasons in the text support**. Sentences (3) and (4) give the benefits of a shared barn, and sentence (5) draws the opinion from them with _Oleh karena itu_ (therefore): the village government should revive the shared barn. Sentences (6) to (8) then explain how to carry that opinion out.

So the writer's main opinion is that the village government should revive the shared rice barn.

- Farmers preferring to sell the whole harvest is a fact that sets up the problem in sentence (2), not the writer's opinion.
- Rising rice prices are only mentioned as one situation in which the barn helps.
- Mouldy grain is mentioned as a risk that training should prevent, not as the main point.
- The passage only says the results must be reported, without saying when the report is read out.
