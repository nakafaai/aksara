In Indonesian the passive prefix _di-_ **is written joined to its verb**, while the preposition _di_ (at, in) is written as a separate word. In sentence (2), _di udarakan_ is the passive of _udara_ (air) with _di-...-kan_, so the correct spelling is _diudarakan_ (broadcast).

The _di_ in the other sentences, such as _di balai desa_ (at the village hall) in sentence (1), is the preposition of place, so it is rightly written apart.

So the spelling mistake is in sentence (2).

- Sentence (1) rightly writes _di balai desa_ apart, because _di_ there is a preposition.
- Sentence (4) rightly writes _didengar_ (listened to) as one word, because _di-_ there is the prefix.
- Sentence (7) has no misspelt word. The words _pengungsian_ and _mengabarkan_ are standard.
- Sentence (8) rightly writes _diperluas_ (extended) as one word.
