A pointing phrase such as _cara itu_ (that way) **refers to what has just been described**. Sentences (4) and (5) describe how the nutrient solution flows through the pipe without stopping: a pump lifts it to the top end of the pipe, and it flows back because the pipe slopes. Sentence (6) then states the result, that the roots always get water and nutrients.

So _cara itu_ refers to the continuous flow of nutrient solution through the pipe by the pump and the slope of the pipe.

- Planting seedlings in cups filled with coconut fibre is described in sentence (3), but it does not keep the roots supplied with water.
- Watering every day is exactly what sentence (6) says is unnecessary.
- Checking the acidity comes after sentence (6), in sentence (8).
- Selling the harvest is in sentence (9) and has nothing to do with watering the roots.
