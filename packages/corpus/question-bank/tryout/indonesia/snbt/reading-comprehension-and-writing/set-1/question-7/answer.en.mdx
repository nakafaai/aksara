Sentences (6) and (8) stand in **contrast**. Sentence (6) says the plants need no daily watering, while sentence (8) says the students must still check the solution. The word _tetap_ (still) in sentence (8) marks that contrast, so the fitting connector is _Meskipun demikian_ (even so).

The complete sentence (8) is _Meskipun demikian, siswa tetap harus memeriksa keasaman larutan dua kali seminggu karena larutan yang terlalu asam dapat merusak akar._

So the right connector is _Meskipun demikian_.

- _Oleh karena itu_ (therefore) states a result, but checking the solution is not a result of needing no watering.
- _Dengan demikian_ (thus) also states a conclusion or result of the sentence before.
- _Selain itu_ (besides) adds something in the same direction and clashes with _tetap_.
- _Akibatnya_ (as a result) states a result, like _Oleh karena itu_.
