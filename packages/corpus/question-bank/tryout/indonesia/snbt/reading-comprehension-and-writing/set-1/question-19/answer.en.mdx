# Given

Sentence (12) rules that every kite **wider than two meters** must fly in the **north zone**.

Sentence (13) states **this year the north zone opens only in the morning**, while sentence (14) gives dragon kites' **length** (up to six meters) and places their contest at 10:00 so it avoids the afternoon fighting contest.

# Asked

The most valid conclusion based on paragraph three.

# Solution

Chain **sentences (12) and (13)** forward without reversing the rule or confusing *length* with *width*. Kites wider than two meters must use the north zone, and the north zone opens only in the morning this year.

> A rule saying "every A must go to B" never implies "everything in B is A", and never swap *length* for *width*.

## Chaining the conditions in sentences twelve and thirteen

Paragraph three provides the two premises directly: <Highlight>semuanya wajib diterbangkan di zona utara</Highlight> ("all must fly in the north zone") and <Highlight>Tahun ini, zona utara hanya dibuka pada pagi hari</Highlight> ("this year, the north zone opens only in the morning").

Joining both premises yields one valid conclusion: **this year, kites wider than two meters can only be flown at the festival in the morning**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Every kite in the north zone is wider than two meters** reverses the rule, as smaller kites are not banned there.
- **Dragon kites must fly in the north zone** confuses six-meter *length* (*panjang*) with two-meter *width* (*lebar*).
- **Kites at most two meters wide may only fly in the afternoon** invents a ban absent from the text.
- **Dragon contest is in the morning due to north-zone hours** contradicts sentence (14)'s stated reason (avoiding the afternoon kite-fighting contest).

# Answer

The valid conclusion is **"Tahun ini, layang-layang yang lebarnya lebih dari dua meter hanya dapat diterbangkan di festival itu pada pagi hari."**
