Sentence (7) **has no subject**. A phrase that begins with the preposition _bagi_ (for) is an adverbial, so it cannot be the subject of the predicate _mendapat_ (receive). The sentence becomes complete once the preposition is dropped, so that _warga yang rajin menabung sampah_ (residents who save rubbish diligently) is the subject.

The correct sentence is _Warga yang rajin menabung sampah mendapat bingkisan dari pengurus pada akhir tahun._

So the best correction is the sentence without _bagi_.

- The first option still begins with _bagi_, so it still has no subject.
- The third option still begins with _bagi_ and adds the reduplication _warga-warga_.
- The fourth option only swaps _bagi_ for another preposition, _kepada_ (to), so there is still no subject.
- The fifth option has a subject but puts a comma between subject and predicate.
