**Linking expressions between sentences**, such as _selain itu_ (besides), _oleh karena itu_ (therefore) and _jadi_ (so), are followed by a comma at the start of a sentence. Sentence (6) begins with _Selain itu_ without a comma, so it should read _Selain itu, radio tersebut juga menyiarkan pengumuman kegiatan posyandu dan kerja bakti._

The other sentences are already right. Sentence (7) has the comma after the subordinate clause that comes before the main clause, and sentence (8) has the comma after the adverb _Kini_ (now).

So the sentence that needs an extra comma is sentence (6).

- Sentence (1) is a simple sentence that needs no comma.
- Sentence (5) has the _karena_ (because) clause after the main clause, so no comma goes before _karena_.
- Sentence (7) already has the comma after _Ketika banjir melanda desa tetangga_ (when a flood hit the neighbouring village).
- Sentence (8) already has the comma after _Kini_.
