The blank in sentence (7) needs a verb that describes how two participants treated the ban. The key clue is inside the sentence: both participants were **found hiding rolls of glass-coated string in their bags**. People hide something because they know it's banned and still choose to bring it. So you need a word that means knowing about the ban but deliberately not following it.

The start of the sentence adds another clue. *Meski sudah diumumkan sejak pendaftaran dibuka* ("even though it had been announced since registration opened") is a concessive clause, a part of a sentence that grants one fact before saying something that runs against it. The ban had been known for a long time, yet the two participants acted against it.

*Diabaikan* ("ignored") fits. KBBI, the official Indonesian dictionary, defines *abai* as not caring about something. A ban that's *diabaikan* is one people know about but don't care about, which is exactly what hiding the string shows.

- *Diacuhkan* is the trap. KBBI defines *acuh* as caring about or heeding something, so *diacuhkan* means "heeded". The sentence would say the opposite of what happened.
- *Dilupakan* ("forgotten") means no longer remembered. Someone who forgot has no reason to hide anything, and the ban had been announced since registration.
- *Disalahpahami* ("misunderstood") means understood wrongly. Hiding the string shows the two participants understood perfectly well that it was banned.
- *Dipertanyakan* ("questioned") means doubted or challenged openly. The text mentions no protest at all, only something done in secret.

So the most fitting word for sentence (7) is *diabaikan*.
