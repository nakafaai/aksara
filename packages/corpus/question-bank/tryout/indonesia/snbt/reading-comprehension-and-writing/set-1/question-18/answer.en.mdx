Sentence (3) presents a precise statistical value. In statistical writing, the [MLA Style Center](https://style.mla.org/styling-percentages/) recommends pairing numerals with the percent symbol, as in <InlineMath math="5\%" />. The hyphens in _forty-eight-point-three-five-percent_ do not represent that value clearly.

Use **2019 sales as the comparison base**. Subtract the 2020 total from the 2019 total to find the fall in units, then divide by the original total and multiply by one hundred percent:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
1{,}030{,}126-532{,}027 &= 498{,}099 \\
\frac{498{,}099}{1{,}030{,}126}\times100\% &\approx 48.35\%
\end{aligned}" />

The clearest revision is therefore <InlineMath math="48.35\%" />. A decimal point is used in English, and no hyphen separates the number from the percent sign.
