Under Indonesian spelling rules **the currency symbol _Rp_ takes no full stop and no space before the number**, and the symbol for kilogram is lower case, _kg_, without a full stop. The correct form is _Rp1.800,00 per kg_.

So the form that follows the rules is _Rp1.800,00 per kg_.

- _Rp. 1.800,00 per Kg_ keeps the full stop after _Rp_ and the capital in _Kg_, as in the passage.
- _Rp 1.800,00,- per kg_ puts a space after _Rp_ and adds _,-_ after an amount that already shows the cents.
- _Rp1.800,00 per Kg._ capitalises _Kg_ and adds a full stop to the unit symbol.
- _RP1.800,00 per KG_ writes both symbols entirely in capitals.
