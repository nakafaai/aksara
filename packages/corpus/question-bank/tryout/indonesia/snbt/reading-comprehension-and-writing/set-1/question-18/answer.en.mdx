# Given

Sentence (7) reads: *"Meski sudah diumumkan sejak pendaftaran dibuka, larangan itu ... oleh dua peserta yang didapati menyembunyikan gulungan benang gelasan di dalam tas."*

Both participants **knew about the ban** yet deliberately hid banned string in their bags.

# Asked

The verb that best fills the blank in sentence (7).

# Solution

Combine the concessive opener ***Meski sudah diumumkan*** ("even though announced") with **hiding rolls in their bags**. We need a verb meaning *known yet deliberately disregarded*, which is ***diabaikan*** ("ignored").

> Watch out for the classic Indonesian dictionary trap: *acuh* means "to care or heed", whereas *abai* means "to ignore or disregard".

## Matching context clues with KBBI meaning

Sentence (7) gives unmistakable proof of intent: <Highlight>dua peserta yang didapati menyembunyikan gulungan benang gelasan di dalam tas</Highlight> ("two participants found hiding rolls of glass-coated string in their bags").

In KBBI, *abai* means failing to care about or heed something, so a known rule broken on purpose is ***diabaikan***.

## Why the other four options fail

- ***Diacuhkan*** ("heeded") reverses the meaning because KBBI defines *acuh* as caring or paying attention.
- ***Dilupakan*** ("forgotten") clashes with hiding the string, which requires remembering the ban.
- ***Disalahpahami*** ("misunderstood") contradicts hiding the string, proving they knew it was forbidden.
- ***Dipertanyakan*** ("questioned openly") fits a protest, not sneaking rolls in a bag.

# Answer

The best word for the blank in sentence (7) is ***diabaikan***.
