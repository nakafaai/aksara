In standard Indonesian a selling price is expressed with **_menjual ... dengan harga_** (sell at a price), not _pada harga_. The preposition _pada_ is used for time or a recipient, as in _pada malam Jumat_ (on Thursday night) or _kepada petani_ (to the farmers), not for manner or price.

The correct sentence (5) is _Informasi itu penting karena petani dapat menentukan waktu panen dan menjual gabahnya dengan harga yang lebih baik._

So the correction is to replace _pada_ with _dengan_.

- _karena_ (because) is right: what follows gives the reason the information matters. _Sehingga_ (so that) would turn the reason into a result.
- _dapat_ (can) is right: the sentence states a possibility, not an obligation.
- _menentukan_ (determine) is complete with its object _waktu panen_. The suffix _-nya_ would give it a second object.
- _lebih_ (better) is right: it compares prices before and after the farmers get the information, not the highest price.
