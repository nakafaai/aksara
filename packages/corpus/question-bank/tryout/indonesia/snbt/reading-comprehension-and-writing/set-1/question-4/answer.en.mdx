To name **a point in time**, standard Indonesian uses _pukul_ (at, o'clock), while _jam_ means a length of time or a clock. The broadcast happens at a set moment, so the right phrase is _setiap pukul 06.00_ (every day at 6 a.m.).

The correct sentence (4) is _Acara yang paling banyak didengar ialah kabar harga gabah dan jadwal pembagian air irigasi yang disiarkan setiap pukul 06.00._

So the correction is to replace _jam_ with _pukul_.

- _ialah_ is right: _ialah_ and _adalah_ are both standard links between subject and predicate.
- _gabah_ (threshed rice grain) is right, because what farmers sell after the harvest is the threshed grain, not the rice plant (_padi_).
- _khabar_ is not standard. The standard form is _kabar_ (news).
- _setiap_ (every) is right, and _tiap-tiap_ corrects nothing.
