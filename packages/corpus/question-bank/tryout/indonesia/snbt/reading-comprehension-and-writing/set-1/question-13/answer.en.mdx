The word _secara_ **is followed by an adjective** to say how something is done, as in _secara terbuka_ (openly) or _secara jujur_ (honestly). In sentence (6), _secara_ is followed by _transparansi_ (transparency), a noun. The right form is the adjective _transparan_ (transparent): _Hasil pengelolaan lumbung harus dilaporkan kepada warga secara transparan._

So the word in the wrong form is in sentence (6).

- Sentence (2) uses _langsung menjual_ (sell directly) and _hasil panennya_ (their harvest) in the right forms.
- Sentence (3) uses _cadangan pangan_ (food reserve) and _melonjak_ (soar) in the right forms.
- Sentence (4) uses _meminjam_ (borrow) and _mengembalikannya_ (return it) in the right forms.
- Sentence (5) uses _menghidupkan kembali_ (revive) in the right form.
