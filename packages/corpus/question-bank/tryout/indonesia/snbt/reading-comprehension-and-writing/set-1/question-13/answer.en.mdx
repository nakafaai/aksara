This question tests **a pair of commas that changes the scope of a clause**. There are two steps: find the information the sentence contradicts, then pick the punctuation that fixes it without creating a new error.

Step one: work out what sentence (8) means right now. The clause *yang dikelilingi kenikir* ("that are surrounded by kenikir") sits between two commas. Under EYD V, Indonesian's official spelling and punctuation rules, a pair of commas encloses extra information. Extra information only adds a detail about things that are already identified. It doesn't pick out some of them. So sentence (8) currently says that all the chili beds are surrounded by kenikir and all of them got crowded with bugs. But sentence (3) says the kenikir was planted around only three of the six chili beds, and sentence (10) compares those three beds with the other three.

Step two: turn the clause into a restricting one. Without commas, *yang dikelilingi kenikir* picks out which beds are meant: only the chili beds surrounded by kenikir. Both commas have to go together. The result is "Bedeng cabai yang dikelilingi kenikir mulai ramai didatangi kepik pada minggu keenam." The subject is *bedeng cabai yang dikelilingi kenikir*, the predicate is *mulai ramai didatangi*, and no comma splits them.

- Dropping only the second comma leaves the first comma without its partner. Extra information that opens with a comma has to close with one, so the sentence breaks and still means all the beds.
- Dropping only the first comma fixes the meaning but leaves one comma between the subject and the predicate. A single comma there cuts the core of the sentence, and no EYD V rule asks for a comma in that spot.
- Moving the time phrase to the front correctly puts a comma after it, but the pair of commas around the *yang* clause stays. The meaning is still all the chili beds.
- Moving the clause behind *kepik* changes what it refers to. Now it's the bugs that are surrounded by kenikir, not the beds.

So the right fix is to drop both commas, giving "Bedeng cabai yang dikelilingi kenikir mulai ramai didatangi kepik pada minggu keenam."
