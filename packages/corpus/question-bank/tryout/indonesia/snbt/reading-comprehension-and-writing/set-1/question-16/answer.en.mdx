A fitting title **covers the whole passage** without being too broad or too narrow. The whole passage describes how the Sejahtera waste bank works: residents bring rubbish, it is weighed and recorded as savings, prices differ by type, and diligent members receive a gift.

So the most fitting title is _Menabung Sampah di Bank Sampah Sejahtera_ (Saving Rubbish at the Sejahtera Waste Bank).

- _Harga Kardus yang Terus Naik_ (The Ever-Rising Price of Cardboard) is too narrow: the price rise is only in sentence (6), and the passage does not say it keeps rising.
- _Jenis-Jenis Sampah Anorganik_ (Types of Inorganic Waste) is too broad and leaves out the waste bank's activity.
- _Pembagian Tempat Sampah Terpilah_ (Handing Out Sorting Bins) covers only sentence (5).
- _Kegiatan Warga Kelurahan Mekar pada Hari Sabtu_ (Saturday Activities in Mekar) is too broad, since the passage is only about the waste bank.
