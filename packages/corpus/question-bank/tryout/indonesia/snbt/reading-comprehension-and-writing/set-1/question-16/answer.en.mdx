Each proposal is judged by the two conditions in the question: **the error disappears, and no new error or change of meaning appears**. For every proposal, find the original error first, then read the whole corrected sentence.

The proposal for sentence (2) that deletes *yang* is correct. The original, "Festival tahun lalu yang menampilkan lebih dari dua ratus layang-layang dari berbagai daerah.", has no predicate. The word *yang* ("that/which") turns *menampilkan ...* ("featured ...") into a description of *festival tahun lalu* ("last year's festival"), so the whole string is just one long noun phrase. Once *yang* is gone, *festival tahun lalu* becomes the subject and *menampilkan* becomes the predicate: "Festival tahun lalu menampilkan lebih dari dua ratus layang-layang dari berbagai daerah."

The proposal for sentence (8) is not correct. The original error is the preposition. *Dua puluh meter* ("twenty meters") states a distance, and a distance is measured from a point, so the right preposition is *dari* ("from"): "dua puluh meter dari garis pantai". *Pada*, like *di*, marks a location ("at"), not the point a distance starts from. So the error is still there.

The proposal for sentence (6) that uses *sehingga* is not correct. *Agar* and *supaya* both mean "in order that", so using both at once is redundant. Replacing them with *sehingga* does remove the redundancy, but *sehingga* ("with the result that") marks a consequence, not a purpose. The sentence would then say participants were no longer tempted as a result of getting the string. What's meant is the committee's goal, and sentence (7) even shows that some participants still brought glass-coated string.

The proposal for sentence (6) that deletes *agar* is correct. One purpose word is enough, and *supaya* is just as standard as *agar*. The result, "Panitia juga membagikan benang katun kepada peserta lomba aduan supaya mereka tidak tergoda membawa benang gelasan.", still states a purpose.

So deleting *yang* in sentence (2) and deleting *agar* in sentence (6) are correct, while the *pada garis pantai* proposal and the *sehingga* proposal are not.
