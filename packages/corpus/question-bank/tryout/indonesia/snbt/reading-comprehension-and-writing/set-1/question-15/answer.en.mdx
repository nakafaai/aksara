# Given

Sentence (10) contrasts week-twelve average aphids per plant in the three kenikir beds (**<InlineMath math="6" />**) against the other three beds (**<InlineMath math="35" />**).

Sentence (11) describes identical watering and fertilizing across all six beds, and sentence (12) opens with ***perbedaan itu*** ("that difference").

# Asked

What the phrase *perbedaan itu* in sentence (12) refers to.

# Solution

Step past sentence (11) because it states a **sameness**, not a difference. One sentence earlier, sentence (10) presents the exact contrast marked by *sedangkan* (<InlineMath math="6" /> versus <InlineMath math="35" />), and sentence (3) identifies *tiga bedeng itu* as the kenikir beds.

> A pointer like *perbedaan itu* skips over an intervening sentence of sameness to reach the nearest actual comparison.

## Tracing the referent past sentence eleven

Sentence (10) supplies the comparison directly: <Highlight>Pada minggu kedua belas, rata-rata kutu daun di tiga bedeng itu tinggal <InlineMath math="6" /> ekor per tanaman, sedangkan di tiga bedeng lainnya masih <InlineMath math="35" /> ekor.</Highlight>

Linking *tiga bedeng itu* back to sentence (3) yields the exact referent: **the gap in average aphids per plant between the kenikir-surrounded beds and the other beds in week twelve**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Drop since month one** tracks one group over time, and no month-one count appears in the text.
- **Gap in bug counts** is never stated as a comparison.
- **Time gap between week five and week six** is about timing, not the aphid counts cited in sentence (12).
- **Garden before and after pesticides** is the whole passage's backdrop, not the specific comparison just stated.

# Answer

*Perbedaan itu* refers to **the gap in average aphids per plant between the beds surrounded by kenikir and the other beds in week twelve** (*selisih rata-rata kutu daun per tanaman antara bedeng yang dikelilingi kenikir dan bedeng lainnya pada minggu kedua belas*).
