_Oleh karena itu_ (therefore) is a connector between sentences that **states a result or conclusion of what came before**. Sentences (3) and (4) give the benefits of a shared barn, and sentence (5) concludes from them that the village government should revive the barn.

So _Oleh karena itu_ states a conclusion drawn from the reasons in the sentences before.

- A contrast is marked by expressions such as _namun_ (however) or _meskipun demikian_ (even so).
- An example is added with expressions such as _misalnya_ (for example) or _contohnya_ (for instance).
- An order in time is marked by expressions such as _kemudian_ (then) or _setelah itu_ (after that).
- A condition is marked by words such as _jika_ (if) or _asalkan_ (provided that), usually within one sentence.
