# Given

Sentence (11) reads: *"Pada bulan ketiga, hampir sepertiga dari para pendaftar-pendaftar aplikasi justru adalah merupakan pasien lansia yang memakai ponsel pintar milik mereka sendiri."*

The revision must be concise without changing any fact, keeping both the **approximate figure** and the **contrast with staff expectations** from sentence (3).

# Asked

The best concise revision of sentence (11).

# Solution

Cut **three redundant wordings** while keeping three meaning markers intact: ***hampir*** ("almost"), ***justru*** ("in fact, contrary to expectation"), and ***sendiri*** ("their own").

> Tightening a sentence removes doubled words, never modifiers that carry estimation, contrast, or ownership.

## Trimming three redundancies while keeping nuances

The original clause reads <Highlight>hampir sepertiga dari para pendaftar-pendaftar aplikasi justru adalah merupakan pasien lansia yang memakai ponsel pintar milik mereka sendiri</Highlight>.

1. *dari para pendaftar-pendaftar* repeats the plural marker, so **pendaftar** suffices.
2. *adalah merupakan* doubles the linking verb, so **merupakan** suffices.
3. *ponsel pintar milik mereka sendiri* condenses cleanly to **ponsel pintarnya sendiri**.

## Why the other four revisions fail

- **Tepat sepertiga** ("exactly a third") erases the estimate marked by *hampir*.
- **Hampir sepertiga pasien lansia** switches the base from app registrants to elderly patients.
- Dropping **justru** loses the contrast with what staff expected in sentence (3).
- Ending with plain **ponsel pintar** removes the detail that the phones were their own.

# Answer

The best revision is **"Pada bulan ketiga, hampir sepertiga pendaftar aplikasi justru merupakan pasien lansia yang memakai ponsel pintarnya sendiri."**
