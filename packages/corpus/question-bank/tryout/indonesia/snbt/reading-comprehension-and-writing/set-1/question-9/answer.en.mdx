The core of a sentence **keeps only the required parts**: subject and predicate, plus an object or complement if the predicate needs one. In sentence (4), the subject is _gelas-gelas itu_ (those cups) and the predicate is the passive verb _diletakkan_ (are placed). The part _pada pipa paralon yang dialiri larutan nutrisi secara terus-menerus_ (on PVC pipes through which nutrient solution flows continuously) is an adverbial of place and can be dropped without breaking the sentence.

So the core of sentence (4) is _Gelas-gelas itu diletakkan._

- _Gelas-gelas itu diletakkan pada pipa paralon._ still contains the adverbial of place.
- _Pipa paralon dialiri larutan nutrisi._ comes from the relative clause describing the pipe, not from the main clause.
- _Gelas-gelas dialiri larutan._ mixes the subject of the main clause with the predicate of the relative clause.
- _Larutan nutrisi diletakkan terus-menerus._ pairs the wrong subject with the predicate.
