Sentence (11) reads: "Pada bulan ketiga, hampir sepertiga dari para pendaftar-pendaftar aplikasi justru adalah merupakan pasien lansia yang memakai ponsel pintar milik mereka sendiri." You have two jobs: cut the wordy parts, and make sure **two nuances survive**. *Hampir* ("almost") shows the number is an estimate below one third, and *justru* ("in fact, contrary to expectation") shows the finding goes against what staff expected in sentence (3).

There are three wordy parts.

1. *Para* and the doubled *pendaftar-pendaftar* both mark the plural, so *pendaftar* alone is enough. The word *dari* can go too.
2. *Adalah* and *merupakan* both link the subject to the predicate, so one of them is enough.
3. *Ponsel pintar milik mereka sendiri* ("a smartphone belonging to themselves") can shrink to *ponsel pintarnya sendiri* ("their own smartphone") without changing the meaning.

The version that cuts all three and still keeps *hampir*, *justru* and *sendiri* is: "Pada bulan ketiga, hampir sepertiga pendaftar aplikasi justru merupakan pasien lansia yang memakai ponsel pintarnya sendiri."

The other versions change the information.

- *Sekitar sepertiga* ("about a third") isn't the same as *hampir sepertiga*. *Hampir* means not yet a third, while *sekitar* can be slightly above it.
- *Hampir sepertiga pasien lansia justru mendaftar lewat aplikasi* changes the base. The original talks about a third of the app's registrants, not a third of elderly patients.
- The version without *justru* is short, but it loses the nuance that the finding went against what staff expected.
- The version ending in plain *ponsel pintar* drops the fact that the phones were the patients' own.

So the best version cuts the wordy parts while keeping *hampir*, *justru* and *sendiri*.
