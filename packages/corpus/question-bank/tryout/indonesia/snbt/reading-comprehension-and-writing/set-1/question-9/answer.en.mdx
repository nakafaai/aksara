Sentence (11) reads: "Pada bulan ketiga, hampir sepertiga dari para pendaftar-pendaftar aplikasi adalah merupakan pasien lansia yang memakai ponsel pintar milik mereka sendiri." You have two jobs at once: cut the wasted words, then make sure no information changes.

Three parts are wasteful.

1. *Para* and the doubled form *pendaftar-pendaftar* both mark the plural of *pendaftar* ("registrant"). Just write *pendaftar*.
2. *Adalah* and *merupakan* both link the subject to the predicate, like "is". One is enough.
3. *Ponsel pintar milik mereka sendiri* ("smartphones belonging to themselves") can be tightened to *ponsel pintarnya sendiri* ("their own smartphones") without losing meaning.

You can also list the information that has to stay: the time is the third month, the share is *hampir* (**nearly**) a third, the base of the count is app registrants, the group is elderly patients, and the phones are their own. Now test every version against that list.

| Version | Still wasteful? | Information that changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "sekitar sepertiga pendaftar aplikasi ..." | No | *Hampir* ("nearly") means not yet a third, while *sekitar* ("about") can be a little over a third |
| "hampir sepertiga pasien lansia mendaftar lewat aplikasi ..." | No | The base switches from app registrants to all elderly patients |
| "... adalah merupakan pasien lansia ..." | Yes | None |
| "... memakai ponsel pintar." | No | The detail that the phones are their own is gone |
| "hampir sepertiga pendaftar aplikasi adalah pasien lansia yang memakai ponsel pintarnya sendiri" | No | None |

The trickiest trap is the version that swaps the base. Say there are <InlineMath math="300" /> app registrants and <InlineMath math="95" /> of them are elderly patients. The original sentence is true, because <InlineMath math="95" /> is nearly a third of <InlineMath math="300" />. If there are only <InlineMath math="150" /> elderly patients in total, then <InlineMath math="95" /> out of <InlineMath math="150" /> is more than half of the elderly patients, not nearly a third. Moving one word makes the number describe a different group.

The version with *sekitar* is also a trap, because it still sounds like an estimate, yet the upper limit is gone. The version without *sendiri* ("own") is the shortest, but it throws away information that sentence (12) needs: the difference between elderly patients who have a smartphone and those who don't yet.

So the best version is "Pada bulan ketiga, hampir sepertiga pendaftar aplikasi adalah pasien lansia yang memakai ponsel pintarnya sendiri."
