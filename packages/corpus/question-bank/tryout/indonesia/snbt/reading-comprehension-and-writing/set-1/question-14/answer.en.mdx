An argumentative text usually has a background, reasons, an opinion or recommendation, and a closing statement. A reason **answers why the opinion is needed**. Here, sentences (3) and (4) explain why a shared barn is useful: it is a food reserve, and villagers can borrow grain from it in the lean season. The writer's opinion only comes after them, in sentence (5), with _Oleh karena itu_ (therefore).

So the supporting reasons are in sentences (3) and (4).

- Sentences (1) and (2) give the background, an old custom that is fading.
- Sentence (5) is the writer's opinion, and sentence (6) is a recommendation for carrying it out.
- Sentences (6) and (7) recommend how to run the barn.
- Sentence (8) is the closing statement or hope, and sentence (7) is a recommendation.
