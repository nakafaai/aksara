# Given

Sentence (10) reports that the three kenikir beds still had an average of **<InlineMath math="6" /> aphids per plant** (versus <InlineMath math="35" /> in the other three beds).

Sentence (12) claims predatory bugs have ***memusnahkan*** ("wiped out") the aphid population, while sentence (4) calls the bugs **predators** of aphids.

# Asked

The single-word edit in sentence (12) that makes it consistent with all data in the passage.

# Solution

*Memusnahkan* means destroying completely until none remain, clashing with the **<InlineMath math="6" /> aphids per plant** still present in sentence (10). The replacement must allow survivors and match predation, which fits ***mengurangi*** ("reduce").

> Test word-choice edits against both the exact numbers in the passage and the mechanism described earlier.

## Matching the verb to sentence ten data

Sentence (12) currently claims <Highlight>Perbedaan itu menunjukkan bahwa kepik telah memusnahkan populasi kutu daun di bedeng yang dikelilingi kenikir</Highlight>.

Because predators lowered the aphid count to **<InlineMath math="6" /> per plant** rather than zero, replacing *memusnahkan* with ***mengurangi*** aligns with both sentence (4) and sentence (10).

## Why the other four options fail

- ***Membasmi*** ("eradicate") still means leaving nothing behind, contradicting the remaining <InlineMath math="6" /> aphids.
- ***Mengusir*** ("drive away") contradicts sentence (4), which defines the bugs as predators that eat aphids.
- **Changing *menunjukkan* to *membuktikan*** leaves *memusnahkan* unfixed and clashes with sentence (13)'s caution.
- **Changing to *seluruh bedeng cabai*** keeps *memusnahkan* and contradicts the <InlineMath math="35" /> aphids in non-kenikir beds.

# Answer

The correct fix is **replacing *memusnahkan* with *mengurangi*** (*mengganti kata memusnahkan dengan mengurangi*).
