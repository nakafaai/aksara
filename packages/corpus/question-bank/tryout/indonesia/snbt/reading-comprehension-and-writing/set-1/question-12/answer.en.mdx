# Given

Sentence (5) reports aphids attacked almost every chili plant so parents asked to spray again, and sentence (6) opens with a blank before **Bu Laras banned spraying of any kind**.

Sentence (8) notes predatory bugs became numerous in **week six**, and sentence (9) opens with a blank before **kenikir only flowered heavily in week five**.

# Asked

The pair of connectors that correctly fills the blanks in sentences (6) and (9).

# Solution

Identify each **logical relation separately**. Sentence (6) runs directly against the expectation built in (5) (concession), whereas sentence (9) gives the earlier trigger for (8) (week five precedes week six).

> Always check both expectation direction and timeline order before choosing between concessive, causal, and result connectors.

## Matching both blanks to their logical relations

Sentence (6) counters the parents' request with <Highlight>... Bu Laras melarang siswa menyemprot apa pun</Highlight>, while sentence (9) gives the cause behind week six with <Highlight>... kenikir yang ditanam pada awal semester baru berbunga lebat pada minggu kelima</Highlight>.

| Blank | Meaning relation | Best connector |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (6) | Concession (opposes parents' plea to spray in sentence (5)) | ***Meskipun demikian***, |
| Sentence (9) | Cause (week-five flowers drew week-six bugs) | ***Hal itu terjadi karena*** |

## Why the other four pairs fail

- ***Oleh karena itu***, ("therefore") makes the ban a consequence of the parents' request to spray.
- ***Akibatnya***, ("as a result") flips time order because week-five flowering came before week-six bugs.
- ***Selain itu***, ("besides") adds a parallel point instead of contrasting with sentence (5).
- ***Dengan kata lain***, ("in other words") restates prior content, whereas sentence (9) introduces new timing.

# Answer

The correct pair of connectors is ***Meskipun demikian,* dan *Hal itu terjadi karena***.
