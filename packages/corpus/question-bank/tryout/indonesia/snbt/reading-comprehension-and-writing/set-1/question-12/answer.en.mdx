A statement that strengthens an opinion **gives evidence that the writer's reason really holds**. The writer's main reason is in sentence (3): a shared barn is a food reserve when the harvest fails. The statement that villages still running a shared barn run short of rice less often when the harvest fails shows that reason holds in practice, so the case for reviving the barn gets stronger.

So the statement that strengthens it most is the one about villages that still run a shared barn.

- Farmers storing grain at their own homes do not show any benefit of a shared barn.
- Low grain prices after harvest only describe the market and do not directly support a shared barn.
- A high building cost is an objection to the writer's opinion.
- Villagers working outside farming weaken the reason to revive a rice barn.
