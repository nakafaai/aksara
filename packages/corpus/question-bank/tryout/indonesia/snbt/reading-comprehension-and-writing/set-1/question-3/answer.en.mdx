Sentence (7) reads: "Meskipun lumbung tidak memungut bunga uang, tetapi jumlah daripada gabah yang dikembalikan peminjam harus sebelas kilogram untuk setiap sepuluh kilogram pinjaman." The right repair has to fix every error and keep the relation between the two halves of the sentence.

The first error is in the conjunctions. *Meskipun* ("although") is a subordinating conjunction that opens a dependent clause, while *tetapi* ("but") is a coordinating conjunction that joins two equal clauses. Indonesian doesn't use both in one sentence, just as English doesn't say "although ..., but ...". The same goes for *meskipun ... namun*, because *namun* ("however") also marks contrast. *Meskipun* alone is enough.

The second error is *daripada*. This word is used for comparisons, as in *lebih ringan daripada bunga* ("lighter than the interest"). In *jumlah daripada gabah* nothing is being compared, so *daripada* has to go: *jumlah gabah* ("the amount of grain").

The third condition is meaning. The original states **a contrast**: the barn charges no money interest, yet borrowers still return more than they took. A repair must not change that relation.

Two traps are the trickiest. The repair with *namun* looks tidy because *tetapi* is gone, yet *meskipun ... namun* is still a doubled conjunction. The repair with *karena* ("because") has no form errors, but now it claims the eleven-kilogram return is caused by the lack of money interest, which the original never says.

So the best repair is "Meskipun lumbung tidak memungut bunga uang, jumlah gabah yang dikembalikan peminjam harus sebelas kilogram untuk setiap sepuluh kilogram pinjaman."
