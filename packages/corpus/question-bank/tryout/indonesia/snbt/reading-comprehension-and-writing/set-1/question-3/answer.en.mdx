Sentence (3) **marks the plural twice**: _para_ already marks a group of people, and the reduplicated _pemuda-pemuda_ marks the plural again. An effective sentence uses one of them, for example _para pemuda_ (the young people).

The right correction is _Penyiarnya adalah para pemuda desa yang belajar mengoperasikan pemancar secara otodidak._ The rest of the sentence needs no change.

So the correction is the option with _para pemuda desa_.

- The first option keeps _para pemuda-pemuda_, and _dengan cara otodidak_ is only longer without adding meaning.
- The second option turns _penyiarnya_ into _penyiar-penyiarnya_ while keeping _para_, so the plural is doubled elsewhere.
- The fourth option drops _para_ but adds a new reduplication, _desa-desa_.
- The fifth option removes the doubling but adds _yang mana_, a non-standard connector in a sentence like this.
