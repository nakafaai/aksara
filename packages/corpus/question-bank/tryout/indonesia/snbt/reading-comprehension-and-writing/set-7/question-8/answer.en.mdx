The demonstrative _itu_ **points back to something already mentioned**. Sentence (6) says participants used to be confused when they met _pengunjung tuli di tempat kerja mereka_, deaf visitors at their workplaces. Sentence (7) then says they can now greet _pengunjung itu_, those visitors.

So _pengunjung itu_ refers to the deaf visitors participants meet at their workplaces.
