# Given

The *coffee-picking* passage runs across eight sentences moving from Desa Lerengsari's old harvesting habit to the decision to <Highlight>hanya memetik buah yang sudah merah</Highlight> (pick only ripe red cherries) and the outcomes in paragraph two.

# Asked

The organizational pattern of the passage.

# Solution

Trace **each section's role in strict chronological order**. Sentences (1) through (3) describe the past situation and its problem, sentence (4) introduces the change in method, and sentences (5) through (8) present the results of that change.

> Identify text organization by mapping the exact sequence of functional stages from start to finish.

## Mapping the passage structure

| Passage segment | Core content | Structural stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentences (1) to (3) | Mixed harvesting, uneven taste, and low prices | **Past situation and problem** |
| Sentence (4) | Farmers' group agrees to pick only ripe red cherries | **Change in method** |
| Sentences (5) to (8) | Slower harvest yet nearly double price, monthly orders, neighbors visiting to learn | **Results of the change** |

The remaining choices fail because the passage never opens with results or an opinion, does not compare two villages side by side, and never details step-by-step coffee processing.

# Answer

The passage follows the pattern **past situation and its problem, change in method, then results of that change** (*keadaan dulu dan masalahnya, perubahan cara, lalu hasil perubahan itu*).
