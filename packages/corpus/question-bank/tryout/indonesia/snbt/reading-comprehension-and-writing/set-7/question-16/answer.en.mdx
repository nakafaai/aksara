# Given

# Asked

The increase in knowledge-comic loans from the odd semester to the even semester.

# Solution

Subtract **the odd-semester figure from the even-semester figure** on the knowledge-comics row.

<BlockMath math="260 - 80 = 180" />

> An increase between two periods equals the later count minus the earlier count on the exact category row.
