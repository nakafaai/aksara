# Given

Sentences (7) and (8) of the *medicine-return* passage state the final fate of collected leftovers: staff hand them to the health office, and <Highlight>dinas kesehatan memusnahkannya dengan alat pembakar khusus</Highlight> (the health office destroys them with a special incinerator).

# Asked

What ultimately happens to the leftover medicines collected in the return boxes.

# Solution

Combine **the two consecutive steps in sentences (7) and (8)**. Each month, staff sort the collected medicines and hand them over to the health office, which then incinerates them in a special burner so they cannot pollute the environment.

> Explicit procedures spanning two adjacent sentences should be read together as one continuous chain.

# Answer

Collected leftover medicines are **handed to the health office and then destroyed in a special incinerator** (*diserahkan kepada dinas kesehatan, lalu dimusnahkan dengan alat pembakar khusus*).
