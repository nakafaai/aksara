# Given

Sentences (2) and (3) of the *coffee-picking* passage explain why prices used to be low: ripe and unripe cherries were mixed so <Highlight>rasa kopinya tidak merata dan pembeli hanya mau membayar dengan harga rendah</Highlight> (the flavor was uneven and buyers only paid a low price).

# Asked

The most likely reason the coffee company now pays nearly double after red-only picking began.

# Solution

Trace **the exact quality-to-price causal link from paragraph one**. Because mixing unripe green cherries caused uneven flavor and low prices, harvesting only red cherries removes that flaw so **the beans now ripen evenly and taste much better**.

> When a text names the specific cause of a poor outcome, removing that cause provides the strongest explanation for the improved result.

## Evaluating all five candidate reasons

| Candidate reason | Support in the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The group's beans are now evenly ripe so the coffee tastes better | Directly resolves the cause of low prices in sentences (2) and (3) | **Correct** |
| Harvesting is now faster | Contradicts sentence (5), which says harvesting takes longer | **Wrong** |
| Their plantation is the largest in the city | No comparison of farm sizes appears in the text | **Wrong** |
| Neighboring villagers help pick coffee there | Sentence (8) says neighbors visit to learn, not to harvest | **Wrong** |
| The group now picks more green cherries | Directly contradicts red-only picking in sentence (4) | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The most likely reason is that **the farmers' group's coffee beans are now evenly ripe, giving them better flavor** (*Biji kopi dari kelompok tani itu kini matang merata sehingga rasanya lebih baik*).
