# Given

Sentence (3) of the *sign-language* passage reads <Highlight>Pesertanya beragam, mulai dari pelajar, pegawai bank, sampai perawat rumah sakit</Highlight> (Its participants are diverse, ranging from students and bank clerks to hospital nurses).

# Asked

The meaning of *beragam* in sentence (3).

# Solution

Read *beragam* alongside **the list of participant backgrounds right after it**. Naming students, bank clerks, and hospital nurses illustrates variety of types, matching the KBBI meaning **varied / of different kinds** (*bermacam-macam*).

> Examples immediately following an adjective directly confirm its contextual meaning.

## Comparing all five meaning choices

| Proposed meaning | Fit with sentence (3) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Varied / of many kinds (*bermacam-macam*) | Directly matches students, bank clerks, and nurses | **Correct** |
| Large in number (*berjumlah banyak*) | Refers to headcount rather than variety | **Wrong** |
| Wearing uniforms (*berpakaian seragam*) | Opposite of diversity | **Wrong** |
| Of the same age (*berusia sama*) | Contradicts mixing students with working professionals | **Wrong** |
| From one office (*berasal dari satu kantor*) | Contradicts multiple workplaces like banks and hospitals | **Wrong** |

# Answer

In sentence (3), *beragam* means **varied / of many kinds** (*bermacam-macam*).
