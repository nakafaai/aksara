A word's meaning is set **by the context of its sentence**. Sentence (3) calls the participants _beragam_ and then lists where they come from: students, bank staff, and hospital nurses. That list shows a mix of backgrounds, which matches the KBBI sense of _beragam_.

So _beragam_ in sentence (3) means of many kinds.

- _Berjumlah banyak_, numerous, is about number, but the list shows kinds of participants.
- _Berpakaian seragam_, in uniform, is the opposite of variety.
- _Berusia sama_, the same age, clashes with a mix of students and workers.
- _Berasal dari satu kantor_, from one office, clashes with their different jobs.
