Sentence (4) names _tiga hal dasar_, three basic things, and then lists them. EYD says **a colon goes at the end of a complete statement that a list follows**, and the list items are separated by commas, including before _dan_.

So the right revision is _Mereka belajar tiga hal dasar: mengeja abjad dengan jari, memperkenalkan diri, dan menanyakan arah._

- A comma after _dasar_ does not mark the start of the list, so _tiga hal dasar_ reads as the first item.
- The colon is right, but the list items are not separated by commas.
- A colon after _belajar_ splits the predicate from its object.
- The comma sits after _dan_ instead of before it.
