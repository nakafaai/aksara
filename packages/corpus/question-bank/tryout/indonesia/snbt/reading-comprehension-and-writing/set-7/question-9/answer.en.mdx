# Given

Sentence (4) of the *sign-language* passage omits the colon before its three-item list: <Highlight>Mereka belajar tiga hal dasar mengeja abjad dengan jari, memperkenalkan diri, dan menanyakan arah</Highlight>.

# Asked

The properly punctuated revision of sentence (4).

# Solution

Apply **two Indonesian EYD punctuation rules together**. Place a colon immediately after the complete introductory statement *Mereka belajar tiga hal dasar*, and separate all three listed items with commas, including before *dan*.

> Use a colon after a complete statement that introduces a list, and separate list items with commas including right before *dan*.

## Auditing punctuation across all five options

| Candidate revision | EYD punctuation check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Mereka belajar tiga hal dasar: mengeja abjad dengan jari, memperkenalkan diri, dan menanyakan arah.* | Proper colon after complete clause plus commas before *dan* | **Correct** |
| *Mereka belajar tiga hal dasar, mengeja abjad ...* | Comma merges the heading phrase into the list itself | **Wrong** |
| *Mereka belajar tiga hal dasar: mengeja abjad dengan jari memperkenalkan diri dan menanyakan arah.* | Omits commas between the listed elements | **Wrong** |
| *Mereka belajar: tiga hal dasar, ...* | Colon wrongly splits verb *belajar* from its direct object | **Wrong** |
| *... memperkenalkan diri dan, menanyakan arah.* | Misplaces the comma after *dan* instead of before it | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The correct punctuation revision is ***Mereka belajar tiga hal dasar: mengeja abjad dengan jari, memperkenalkan diri, dan menanyakan arah***.
