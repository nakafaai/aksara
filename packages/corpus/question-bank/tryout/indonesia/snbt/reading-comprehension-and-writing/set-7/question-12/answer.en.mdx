# Given

Sentence (6) of the *coffee-picking* passage writes a compound noun solid without a space: <Highlight>sebuah perusahaan kopi di ibukota provinsi</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing a nonstandard Indonesian spelling.

# Solution

Apply **the Indonesian EYD rule for compound nouns**. Unaffixed compound words stay separated by a space, so the standard spelling is **ibu kota** (two words), never *ibukota*.

> Standard Indonesian writes open compounds like *ibu kota*, *rumah sakit*, and *kereta api* as two separate words unless enclosed by both a prefix and suffix.
