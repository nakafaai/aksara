# Given

Sentence (4) presents the librarian's positive assessment that new books attract infrequent visitors, and sentence (5) opens with <Highlight>Namun, sebagian guru khawatir</Highlight> (However, some teachers worry) about students neglecting textbooks.

# Asked

The relationship between sentence (5) and sentence (4).

# Solution

Notice **the contrastive transition *Namun* (However) opening sentence (5)**. That signal sets the teachers' worry directly against the librarian's positive view, so sentence (5) **presents a different perspective from the evaluation in sentence (4)**.

> Contrastive sentence openers like *Namun* explicitly mark a shift to an opposing or contrasting viewpoint.

## Comparing all five proposed relationships

| Proposed relationship | Match with sentences (4) and (5) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presents a different view from the assessment in sentence (4) | Directly signaled by *Namun* shifting from benefit to concern | **Correct** |
| Gives an example of infrequent library visitors | Mentions no individual student examples | **Wrong** |
| Explains why new books attract students | Does not explain the appeal of the new collection | **Wrong** |
| Summarizes the entire table | Discusses only textbooks, not all four book types | **Wrong** |
| Restates sentence (4) in other words | Contrasts with sentence (4) rather than paraphrasing it | **Wrong** |

# Answer

Sentence (5) **presents a view different from the assessment in sentence (4)** (*Kalimat (5) menyampaikan pandangan yang berbeda dari penilaian dalam kalimat (4)*).
