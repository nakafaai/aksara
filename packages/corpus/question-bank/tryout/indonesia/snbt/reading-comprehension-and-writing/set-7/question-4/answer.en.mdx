# Given

Sentence (8) of the *medicine-return* passage reads <Highlight>... obat itu tidak mencemari lingkungan, dinas kesehatan memusnahkannya dengan alat pembakar khusus</Highlight> (... the medicine does not pollute the environment, the health office destroys it with a special incinerator).

# Asked

The conjunction that correctly completes the blank at the start of sentence (8).

# Solution

Recognize **the purpose relationship between the two clauses**. Preventing environmental pollution is the intended goal of burning the medicines in a special incinerator, so the opening clause requires the purpose conjunction **Agar** (So that).

> A clause expressing the goal of an action takes a purpose conjunction such as *agar* (so that).

## Testing all five conjunction options

| Conjunction | Logical relation | Fit in sentence (8) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Agar* (So that) | Expresses purpose | Directly marks preventing pollution as the goal | **Correct** |
| *Karena* (Because) | Expresses cause | Wrongly makes non-pollution the prior cause | **Wrong** |
| *Meskipun* (Although) | Expresses contrast | The two clauses support each other rather than contrast | **Wrong** |
| *Setelah* (After) | Expresses time order | Wrongly implies the drugs were already non-polluting beforehand | **Wrong** |
| *Sehingga* (Thus / so that) | Marks result inside a sentence | Cannot open a standard Indonesian sentence | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The right word to fill the blank in sentence (8) is ***Agar*** (So that).
