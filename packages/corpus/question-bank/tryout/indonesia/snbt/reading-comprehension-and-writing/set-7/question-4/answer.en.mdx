Sentence (8) has two parts. The second names an action: the health office destroys the medicine in a special incinerator. The first, _obat itu tidak mencemari lingkungan_, the medicine does not pollute the environment, **is the purpose of that action**. A purpose clause opens with _agar_, so that.

So the right word is _Agar_.

- _Karena_, because, makes the first part a cause, as if the medicine were destroyed because it no longer pollutes.
- _Meskipun_, although, marks a contrast, but the two parts agree.
- _Setelah_, after, marks time, as if the medicine stopped polluting before being destroyed.
- _Sehingga_, so, marks an effect and does not open a sentence.
