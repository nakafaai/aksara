# Given

# Asked

The sentence containing an improperly used word.

# Solution

Check **the exact KBBI meaning of the Indonesian word *acuh***. In standard Indonesian, *acuh* means **caring or attentive**, the exact opposite of indifferent, so expressing teachers' worry about students neglecting textbooks requires *tidak acuh* or *acuh tak acuh*.

> In KBBI, *acuh* means caring or paying attention, so indifference must be written *tidak acuh* or *acuh tak acuh*.
