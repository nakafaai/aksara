Sentence (5) uses the word _acuh_. In KBBI, **_acuh_ means to care or to heed**. Teachers would hardly worry that students care more about textbooks. They mean students care less, so the right words are _tidak acuh_ or _acuh tak acuh_, indifferent.

So the sentence with a wrongly used word is sentence (5).
