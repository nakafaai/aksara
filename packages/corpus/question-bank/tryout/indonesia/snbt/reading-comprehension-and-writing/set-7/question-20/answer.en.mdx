# Given

After the library added **knowledge comics and teen novels** at the start of the even semester, comics jumped from <InlineMath math="80" /> to <InlineMath math="260" />, teen novels rose from <InlineMath math="150" /> to <InlineMath math="310" />, and textbooks dipped only slightly from <InlineMath math="420" /> to <InlineMath math="400" />.

# Asked

The best conclusion supported by both the passage and the table.

# Solution

Combine **the collection update with the true magnitude of every key row**. Adding knowledge comics and teen novels coincided with sharp gains in both categories, while textbook loans edged down by just <InlineMath math="20" /> and stayed the top category overall.

> A sound data conclusion captures both the direction and scale of changes accurately without exaggerating minor shifts.

## Checking all five candidate conclusions

| Candidate conclusion | Evidence check against passage and table | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adding knowledge comics and teen novels was followed by rising loans for both, while textbooks dipped slightly | Matches sentence (1) and every row of the table accurately | **Correct** |
| Students stopped borrowing textbooks in the even semester | False because textbooks were still borrowed <InlineMath math="400" /> times | **Wrong** |
| Biographies were borrowed the most in the even semester | False because biographies ranked lowest at <InlineMath math="70" /> | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The best conclusion is that **adding knowledge comics and teen novels was followed by higher loans for both categories, while textbook loans fell only slightly** (*Penambahan koleksi komik pengetahuan dan novel remaja diikuti naiknya peminjaman kedua jenis buku itu, sedangkan peminjaman buku pelajaran hanya turun sedikit*).
