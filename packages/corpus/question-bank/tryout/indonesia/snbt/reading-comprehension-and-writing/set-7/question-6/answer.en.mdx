A good title **covers the whole reading, no broader and no narrower**. The first paragraph describes the sign language class at the community hall. The second gives its purpose and result: participants can now greet and serve deaf visitors.

So the best title is _Kelas Bahasa Isyarat Membantu Warga Melayani Orang Tuli_, a sign language class helps residents serve deaf people.

- _Balai Warga Kelurahan Bandarjaya_, the community hall, is too broad, since the hall hosts many activities.
- Fingerspelling is only one lesson, in sentence (4).
- The volunteer teachers are only in sentence (2).
- Bank staff are only one group of participants, in sentence (3).
