# Given

The *sign-language* passage introduces the sign-language class at the Bandarjaya community hall in paragraph one and its goal and payoff in paragraph two: <Highlight>agar warga tuli lebih mudah dilayani di tempat umum</Highlight> (so deaf residents are easier to serve in public places).

# Asked

The best title for the entire passage.

# Solution

Select the title that **unites the sign-language class with its public-service impact for deaf residents** across both paragraphs, avoiding titles that are either too vague or confined to one detail.

> A well-scoped title reflects both the central program and its outcome across the whole passage.

## Comparing all five title candidates

| Proposed title | Coverage of the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Kelas Bahasa Isyarat Membantu Warga Melayani Orang Tuli* | Covers both the sign-language class and serving deaf residents | **Best fit** |
| *Balai Warga Kelurahan Bandarjaya* | Too broad because the hall hosts many kinds of events | **Too broad** |
| *Mengeja Abjad dengan Jari* | Only one of three topics in sentence (4) | **Too narrow** |
| *Relawan dari Komunitas Tuli* | Mentions only the teachers in sentence (2) | **Too narrow** |
| *Pegawai Bank Belajar pada Akhir Pekan* | Mentions only one participant group from sentence (3) | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The best title is ***Kelas Bahasa Isyarat Membantu Warga Melayani Orang Tuli*** (Sign Language Class Helps Residents Serve Deaf People).
