# Given

Sentence (5) of the *medicine-return* passage writes <Highlight>Kotak serupa juga dipasang di setiap apotik</Highlight>, while the other choices test sentences (1) through (4).

# Asked

The sentence containing a nonstandard Indonesian spelling.

# Solution

Verify each candidate word against **official KBBI spelling**. The word *apotik* in sentence (5) is nonstandard, because the standard Indonesian spelling is **apotek** with an *e* in the final syllable.

> Standard Indonesian spelling follows official KBBI entries such as *apotek* and *kedaluwarsa* rather than spoken variants.

## Auditing spelling across all five sentences

| Sentence | Words checked | KBBI standard check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (1) | *kedaluwarsanya* | Matches standard headword *kedaluwarsa* | **Standard** |
| Sentence (2) | *begitu saja*, *saluran* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (3) | *mencemari*, *dipungut* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (4) | *pengembalian* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (5) | *apotik* | Standard spelling is **apotek** with *e* | **Nonstandard** |

# Answer

The nonstandard spelling appears in **sentence (5)** (*kalimat (5)*: *apotek*, not *apotik*).
