# Given

Sentence (3) of the *medicine-return* passage states the hazard of careless disposal: <Highlight>zat dalam obat dapat mencemari tanah dan air, dan obat yang dipungut orang lain dapat membahayakan kesehatan</Highlight> (substances in medicines can pollute soil and water, and medicines picked up by others can endanger health).

# Asked

The most likely outcome if residents keep dumping leftover medicines into drains.

# Solution

Derive the consequence straight from **the contamination risk stated in sentence (3)**. Continuing to pour leftover medicines into drains sends their chemical substances directly into local waterways, so **water around the neighborhood can become contaminated by substances from the medicines**.

> A grounded inference follows the exact causal chain stated in the passage without introducing unsupported events.

## Checking all five predicted outcomes

| Proposed outcome | Alignment with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water around residential areas can be contaminated by medicine substances | Direct result of the risk stated in sentence (3) | **Correct** |
| The medicines can safely be reused by other residents | Contradicts sentence (3), which says discarded drugs endanger health | **Wrong** |
| Return boxes fill up faster | Medicines dumped in drains never enter the boxes | **Wrong** |
| The health office incinerates those medicines | Incineration applies only to medicines collected from return boxes | **Wrong** |
| The clinic shuts down the return boxes | Nothing in the text mentions closing the boxes | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The most likely outcome is that **water around the neighborhood can become contaminated by substances from the medicines** (*Air di sekitar permukiman dapat tercemar zat dari obat*).
