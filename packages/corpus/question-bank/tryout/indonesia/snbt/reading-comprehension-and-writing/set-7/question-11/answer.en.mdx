# Given

The *coffee-picking* passage recounts Desa Lerengsari farmers shifting from mixed harvesting to red-only picking in paragraph one, then details its payoff in paragraph two where their coffee is <Highlight>dibeli dengan harga hampir dua kali lipat</Highlight> (bought for nearly double the price).

# Asked

The author's main purpose in writing the passage.

# Solution

Determine the purpose from **what both paragraphs accomplish together**. Paragraph one sets out the old harvesting problem and the switch to picking only ripe red cherries, while paragraph two reports the resulting tradeoffs and gains for the village farmers.

> An author's purpose captures the combined work of both paragraphs rather than an isolated detail.

## Comparing all five purpose options

| Proposed purpose | Fit with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain a village's change in coffee-picking method and its results | Summarizes both paragraph one and paragraph two accurately | **Correct** |
| Urge coffee farmers across Indonesia to sell to companies | The text reports one village neutrally with no call to action | **Wrong** |
| Compare coffee flavor across different regions | No other regions' coffee flavors are compared | **Wrong** |
| Detail how to process coffee beans into powder | Roasting and grinding steps are never explained | **Wrong** |
| Criticize a coffee company for paying low prices | The company actually pays nearly double the earlier price | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The author's purpose is **to explain the change in how coffee is picked in a village and its results** (*menjelaskan perubahan cara memetik kopi di sebuah desa dan hasilnya*).
