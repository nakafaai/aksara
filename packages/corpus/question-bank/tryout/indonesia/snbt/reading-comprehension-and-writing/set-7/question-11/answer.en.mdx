The writer's purpose shows in **what each paragraph does**. The first paragraph tells of the old way of picking coffee, its problem, and the decision to switch to picking only red cherries. The second tells of the result: a higher price, a company asking for regular supply, and other villages coming to learn.

So the purpose is to explain a change in how a village picks coffee and what it brought.

- There is no call to action, and only one village is discussed.
- Coffee from other regions is not compared.
- How beans are ground into powder is never explained.
- The low price came from the old buyers, while the city company pays more.
