The main idea of a paragraph is **what all of its sentences hold together**. Sentences (1) and (2) give the old way, picking every cherry at once. Sentence (3) gives the effect: uneven taste and a low price. Sentence (4) gives the decision to switch to picking only red cherries.

So the main idea is that picking every cherry at once kept the price low, so the farmers switched to picking only red ones.

- Picking red and green cherries together is only sentence (1).
- Uneven taste is only part of sentence (3).
- The low price is also only part of sentence (3).
- The longer harvest belongs to the second paragraph.
