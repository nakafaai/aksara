# Given

Paragraph one of the *coffee-picking* passage links four sentences: picking red and green cherries together yielded uneven flavor and low prices, so <Highlight>kelompok tani desa itu sepakat hanya memetik buah yang sudah merah</Highlight> (the village farmers' group agreed to pick only ripe red cherries).

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph one.

# Solution

Synthesize **the full cause-and-effect chain connecting sentences (1) through (4)**. The paragraph's main idea unites the old mixed-picking method, the resulting low price, and the farmers' decision to switch to red-only picking, without spilling into paragraph two.

> A paragraph's main idea binds all sentences inside that paragraph together without omitting its turning point or pulling in the next paragraph.

## Comparing all five options against paragraph one

| Proposed main idea | Coverage of sentences (1) to (4) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Picking all cherries at once kept prices low, so farmers switched to red-only picking | Unifies cause, effect, and decision across sentences (1) to (4) | **Correct** |
| Farmers picked red and green cherries together | Covers sentence (1) only | **Too narrow** |
| Coffee from Desa Lerengsari had uneven taste | Covers only half of sentence (3) | **Too narrow** |
| Buyers paid only a low price | Covers only the end of sentence (3) | **Too narrow** |
| Red-only harvesting takes longer | Comes from sentence (5) in paragraph two | **Wrong paragraph** |

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph one is that **picking all coffee cherries at once led to low prices, prompting farmers to switch to red-only picking** (*Cara memetik kopi sekaligus membuat harga kopi rendah sehingga petani beralih ke petik merah*).
