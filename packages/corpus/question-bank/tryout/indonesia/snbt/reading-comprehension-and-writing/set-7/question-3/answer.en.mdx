# Given

Sentence (6) of the *medicine-return* passage reads <Highlight>Warga cukup membawa obat sisa dalam kemasannya, lalu dimasukkan ke kotak itu</Highlight>, pairing one subject (*warga*) with an active verb (*membawa*) and then an unparallel passive verb (*dimasukkan*).

# Asked

The revision that makes sentence (6) an effective standard Indonesian sentence.

# Solution

Restore **parallel verb voice under the shared subject *warga***. Because residents perform both sequential actions, the second verb should stay active as **memasukkannya** (place them into it), keeping the sentence crisp and unambiguous.

> Two predicates sharing one agent subject in a single clause should use the same grammatical voice to stay clear and parallel.

## Checking parallelism across all five revisions

| Candidate revision | Voice and clause clarity | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Warga cukup membawa obat sisa dalam kemasannya, lalu memasukkannya ke kotak itu.* | Both predicates are parallel active (*membawa* and *memasukkannya*) | **Effective** |
| *... lalu kotak itu dimasukkan.* | Distorts meaning so the box itself appears to be inserted | **Ineffective** |
| *Warga cukup dibawa obat sisa ...* | Makes residents the item carried and stays unparallel | **Ineffective** |
| *... lalu dimasukkan ke dalam kotak itu oleh warga.* | Wordy repetition of *warga* and still mixes active with passive | **Ineffective** |
| *Warga cukup membawa obat sisa, dalam kemasannya lalu dimasukkan ...* | Faulty comma breaks the noun phrase and keeps passive *dimasukkan* | **Ineffective** |

# Answer

The effective revision is ***Warga cukup membawa obat sisa dalam kemasannya, lalu memasukkannya ke kotak itu***.
