Sentence (6) has one subject, _warga_, residents, with two predicates. The first is active, _membawa_, bring, but the second is passive, _dimasukkan_, be put in. Since the same people do both actions, **the two predicates must match in form**, both active: _membawa_ and _memasukkannya_, put it in.

So the right revision is _Warga cukup membawa obat sisa dalam kemasannya, lalu memasukkannya ke kotak itu._

- _lalu kotak itu dimasukkan_ changes the meaning, as if the box were put in.
- _Warga cukup dibawa_ makes the residents the ones carried, and the predicates still do not match.
- Adding _oleh warga_ makes the sentence wordy and still mixes active and passive.
- The comma after _sisa_ breaks up _obat sisa dalam kemasannya_, and _dimasukkan_ stays passive.
