The two parts of sentence (6) **contradict each other**: at first the learners think weaving is easy, then they realise it takes weeks. A contrast within one sentence is marked with the conjunction _tetapi_ (but).

The complete sentence (6) is _Awalnya banyak peserta mengira menenun itu mudah, tetapi mereka segera sadar bahwa satu helai kain memerlukan waktu berminggu-minggu._

So the right conjunction is _tetapi_.

- _sehingga_ (so that) states a result, but the learners' realisation is not a result of thinking weaving is easy.
- _karena_ (because) states a cause, and there is no causal link between the two parts.
- _bahkan_ (even) strengthens something in the same direction, but the two parts contrast.
- _lalu_ (then) only states order and hides the contrast.
