# Given

Paragraph three of the Kampung Lebak Tirta passage has four sentences: sentences (9) and (10) report **weekend visitors jumping to about three hundred** for photos, then sentence (11) pivots with ***Meskipun kampung itu kini ramai*** ("Although the village is now busy") and sentence (12) confirms **weekly river trash stayed unchanged**.

# Asked

The main idea of the third paragraph.

# Solution

See how sentence (11) binds the paragraph through **contrast**: the village drew crowds, yet some residents' habit of dumping trash in the river never changed. The main idea must capture both halves of that contrast.

> In a contrast paragraph built around *meskipun* ("although"), the main idea unites both opposing sides instead of lifting just one supporting line.

## Mapping the contrast structure in paragraph three

Sentence (11) anchors the paragraph directly: <Highlight>Meskipun kampung itu kini ramai, tetapi kebiasaan sebagian warga membuang sampah ke sungai belum berubah.</Highlight> ("Although the village is now busy, some residents' habit of dumping trash into the river has not changed.")

Sentences (9) and (10) set up the crowd growth, while sentence (12) supplies proof that weekly river trash never dropped.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Visitors grew rapidly** picks only the setup in sentence (9).
- **Trash has not decreased** picks only the supporting proof in sentence (12).
- **Visitors added more river trash** invents both a new culprit and an increase absent from the text.
- **Attracted visitors and reduced river trash** reverses the proof in sentence (12).

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph three is that **the village's new crowds have not been followed by a change in some residents' habit of throwing trash into the river** (*Ramainya kampung itu belum diikuti oleh berubahnya kebiasaan sebagian warga membuang sampah ke sungai.*).
