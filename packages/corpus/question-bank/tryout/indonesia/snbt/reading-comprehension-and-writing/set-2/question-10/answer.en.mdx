# Given

Sentence (7) reads: *"Lapisan minyak di bagian atas kemudian dijual ke sebuah pabrik yang mengelolanya menjadi biodiesel."*

In KBBI, ***mengelola*** means managing or administering (an organization or project), whereas ***mengolah*** means processing a raw material into another product.

# Asked

The word whose meaning clashes with its sentence context, together with its right replacement.

# Solution

Check both halves: the original word must be genuinely misused, and the substitute must fit. A factory does not *manage* oil into biodiesel, it *processes* oil into biodiesel, so *mengelolanya* must become ***mengolahnya***.

> Keep this Indonesian near-pair distinct: *mengelola* is administrative management, while *mengolah* is transforming raw material into a product.

## Fixing mengelola to mengolah in sentence seven

Sentence (7) describes physical transformation: <Highlight>dijual ke sebuah pabrik yang mengelolanya menjadi biodiesel</Highlight> ("sold to a factory that manages it into biodiesel").

Because the factory converts used oil into biodiesel, replacing *mengelolanya* with ***mengolahnya*** ("processes it") restores exact meaning.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Replacing *mengelolanya* with *mengurusnya*** ("handles/administers") repeats the exact same management error.
- **Replacing *menyetor* with *menitipkan*** ("entrusts temporarily") in (5) clashes with residents being paid per liter.
- **Replacing *berulang kali* with *terlalu lama*** ("too long") in (3) drops the link to reusing oil in sentence (2).
- **Replacing *dijernihkan* ("clarified visually") with *dimurnikan* ("purified")** in (13) implies safety, clashing with the health warning in (14).

# Answer

The correct fix is **replacing *mengelolanya* in sentence (7) with *mengolahnya*** (*kata mengelolanya pada kalimat (7) diganti mengolahnya*).
