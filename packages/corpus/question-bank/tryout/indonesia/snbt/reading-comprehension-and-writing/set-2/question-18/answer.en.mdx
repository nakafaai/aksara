Under Indonesian spelling rules **a number that opens a sentence is written in words**, not digits. Sentence (4) begins with the digits _18_, so the correction is _Delapan belas siswa tidur kurang dari enam jam setiap malam._ Rewording is also standard, for example _Sebanyak 18 siswa tidur..._, as long as no redundant word is added.

So the correction that follows the rules is _Delapan belas siswa tidur kurang dari enam jam setiap malam._

- The third option adds the digits in brackets and the reduplication _siswa-siswa_, redundant after a number.
- The fourth option uses _orang_ and _siswa-siswa_ together after a number, a redundant plural.
- The fifth option still opens with digits and puts a comma between subject and predicate.
