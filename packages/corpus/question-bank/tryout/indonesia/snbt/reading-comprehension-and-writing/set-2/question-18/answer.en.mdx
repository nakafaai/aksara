# Given

Paragraph three (sentence 10) reports that **most visitors come to take photos in front of the murals**.

Paragraph four (sentences 13 and 14) adds that **wall paint fades after two years**, district paint aid was **given only once**, and **repainting costs now depend entirely on visitor parking fees**.

# Asked

The statement best supported by combining paragraphs three and four.

# Solution

Link both paragraphs into one loop: **murals draw most visitors**, **visitors pay parking fees**, and **parking fees fund repainting**. Therefore, the murals **help determine the funds available to maintain the murals themselves**.

> Combining two paragraphs often reveals a feedback loop where an attraction generates the exact revenue needed for its own upkeep.

## Connecting the chain across paragraphs three and four

The text pairs attraction and funding directly: <Highlight>pengunjung itu datang untuk berfoto di depan mural</Highlight> ("visitors come to take photos in front of the murals") and <Highlight>biaya pengecatan ulang kini sepenuhnya bergantung pada uang parkir pengunjung</Highlight> ("repainting costs now depend entirely on visitor parking fees").

Using *ikut* ("helps determine") is also exact since *most* visitors, not all, come for the murals.

## Why the other four options fail

- **District donating paint again** contradicts sentence (14)'s one-time aid.
- **Parking fees are already sufficient** confuses *depending on* with *being enough*.
- **Visitors come because walls are repainted every two years** turns fading time into a schedule and motive.
- **River trash fades the paint** invents a link, as sentence (13) cites sun and rain instead.

# Answer

The best-supported statement is that **the village's murals help determine the size of the funds used to maintain the murals themselves** (*Mural di kampung itu ikut menentukan besarnya dana untuk merawat mural itu sendiri.*).
