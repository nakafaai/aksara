A proposed edit is right when **the new sentence is correct and still means what the text means**. So for each proposal, rewrite the sentence in your head, then check two things: the sentence is complete, and the relation in meaning fits.

Statement 1: _Tepat_ (right). Sentence (2) originally reads _Sebagian jelantah itu dipakai lagi untuk menggoreng sehingga sisanya dibuang ke selokan_. The word _sehingga_ ("so that, as a result") expresses a consequence, as if the rest of the oil were thrown away because some of it was reused. But the sentence only sets two different habits side by side. That kind of contrast is marked with _sedangkan_ ("whereas"). Under EYD V, a comma goes before _sedangkan_ in a compound sentence. The result, _Sebagian jelantah itu dipakai lagi untuk menggoreng, sedangkan sisanya dibuang ke selokan_, is correct.

Statement 2: _Tidak tepat_ (wrong). Sentence (3) is about oil heated again and again, and sentence (4) is about used oil poured into drains. They're two different habits, and _adapun_ ("as for") is exactly the word for moving on to the next point. Replacing it with _akibatnya_ ("as a result") would make sentence (4) a consequence of reheating oil. The text has no such link.

Statement 3: _Tidak tepat_ (wrong). The rule about a comma after an opening adverbial is real, but the problem in sentence (5) lies elsewhere. The phrase _bagi warga yang menyetor jelantah ke bank sampah kelurahan_ ("for residents who hand in used oil at the neighbourhood waste bank") starts with the preposition _bagi_ ("for"), so the whole phrase is just an adverbial. That leaves the predicate _mendapat_ ("receive") without a subject. A comma only marks where the adverbial ends. **There's still no subject**, so the sentence is still ineffective.

Statement 4: _Tepat_ (right). Without _bagi_, the sentence becomes _Warga yang menyetor jelantah ke bank sampah kelurahan mendapat imbalan Rp4.000,00 per liter dalam bentuk saldo tabungan_. Now _warga yang menyetor jelantah ke bank sampah kelurahan_ is the subject, _mendapat_ is the predicate, and _imbalan_ ("payment") is the object. The sentence is complete and its meaning hasn't changed.

So the right edits are replacing _sehingga_ with _sedangkan_ after a comma in sentence (2) and deleting _bagi_ in sentence (5). Replacing _adapun_ with _akibatnya_ and adding a comma after _kelurahan_ are wrong.
