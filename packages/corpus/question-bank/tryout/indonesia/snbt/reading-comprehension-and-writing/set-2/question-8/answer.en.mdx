In a compound sentence, **a comma goes before a contrasting conjunction** such as _tetapi_ (but), _melainkan_ (but rather) and _sedangkan_ (whereas). Sentence (7) joins two clauses with _tetapi_ but has no comma. It should read _Pengelola pasar lain sebaiknya meniru kebijakan itu, tetapi mereka perlu menyediakan tas pengganti yang murah._

So the sentence that needs an extra comma is sentence (7).

- Sentence (2) uses _atau_ (or) between two choices, which needs no comma.
- Sentence (3) has the _karena_ (because) clause after the main clause, so no comma goes before _karena_.
- Sentence (5) also has its _karena_ clause after the main clause.
- Sentence (8) already has the comma after the adverbial _Tanpa tas pengganti yang terjangkau_ (without affordable replacement bags).
