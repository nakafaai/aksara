# Given

Sentences (8) and (9) report split reading interests: adults in **downstream villages** borrow recipe and carpentry books, while children in **upstream villages** love science comics.

Sentence (10) describes **packing two crates with different books** for the two zones, sentence (11) adds coloured ribbons so crates do not mix up, and sentence (12) credits **cara ini** ("this approach") for shrinking unborrowed cargo from one third to under one tenth.

# Asked

What the phrase *cara ini* in sentence (12) refers to.

# Solution

Pick the step that **actually causes fewer unborrowed books**, not merely the nearest sentence. Coloured ribbons (11) only prevent mix-ups, whereas tailoring crate contents to each zone's readers (10) directly increases borrowing.

> A method pointer like *cara ini* tracks the action that produces the stated outcome, stepping past an intermediate helper detail.

## Matching sentence twelve to its true cause

Sentence (10) states the core method: <Highlight>Berbekal temuan itu, relawan menyusun dua peti buku dengan isi yang berbeda untuk kedua wilayah tersebut.</Highlight> ("Armed with that finding, volunteers packed two book crates with different contents for the two areas.")

| Sentence | Action | Cuts unborrowed share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (7) | Logging titles for three months | Not alone, only gathers data |
| Sentence (10) | Packing different crates for downstream and upstream | **Yes**, matches books to local readers |
| Sentence (11) | Tying coloured ribbons on crates | No, only avoids swapping crates |

## Why the other four options fail

- **Marking crates with ribbons** sits nearest but does not change what readers borrow.
- **Logging borrowed titles** stops at data collection before packing changes.
- **Sending more science comics everywhere** ignores downstream adults seeking recipes and carpentry.
- **Cutting total books loaded** misreads sentence (12), where only the *unborrowed* share fell.

# Answer

*Cara ini* refers to **packing different crate contents for downstream and upstream villages based on what each area borrows most** (*penyusunan isi peti yang berbeda untuk kampung hilir dan kampung hulu sesuai dengan buku yang paling banyak dipinjam di masing-masing wilayah*).
