Under Indonesian spelling rules **the word _desa_ (village) takes a capital letter when a proper name follows it**, because together they form one place name. In sentence (1), _desa Lewo_ is written in lower case, so the correct form is _Desa Lewo_. The day name _Minggu_ (Sunday) in that sentence is already right.

So the capitalisation mistake is in sentence (1).

- Sentence (2) rightly writes _Ibu Martha_, because _Ibu_ is a form of address followed by a name.
- Sentence (3) has no proper names, so all its words rightly take lower case.
- Sentence (7) writes _balai desa_ (village hall) in lower case, which is right because no village name follows.
- Sentence (8) again writes _Ibu Martha_ correctly.
