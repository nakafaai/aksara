A sound conclusion combines the text's central ideas without introducing a claim that the evidence does not support. This text establishes three points:

- The <InlineMath math="2023" /> Agricultural Census had broad national and subsector coverage.
- Its standardized method produced several kinds of structural evidence.
- The resulting evidence can inform agricultural policy.

The statement "Therefore, the 2023 Agricultural Census provides a **broad, standardized evidence base** for designing Indonesia's agricultural policies" brings those points together. Farmers aged 19 to 39 account for about 21.93 percent, not a majority. A decline in individual holdings does not by itself prove that the entire sector is shrinking. Nor do the data show that urban farming dominates or that census standards alone improve welfare.
