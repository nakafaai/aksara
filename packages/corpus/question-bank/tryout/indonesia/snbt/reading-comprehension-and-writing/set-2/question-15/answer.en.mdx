# Given

Sentence (10) records that last year only **<InlineMath math="40\%" /> of speeches directly answered an opponent's argument**.

Sentence (11) adds **"This year that figure rose to <InlineMath math="65\%" />."**, and sentence (12) refers to it as ***kenaikan tersebut*** ("that rise").

# Asked

What the phrase *kenaikan tersebut* in sentence (12) refers to.

# Solution

Trace the reference **two sentences back**: *kenaikan tersebut* (12) links to sentence (11) (*rose to* <InlineMath math="65\%" />), and *angka itu* ("that figure") in (11) links to the <InlineMath math="40\%" /> rebuttal share in sentence (10).

> Distinguish *rising to* (final value) from *rising by* (difference), and keep percentage points distinct from raw speech counts.

## Tracing the two step reference back to sentence ten

Sentences (10) and (11) state both endpoints directly: <Highlight>pada lomba tahun lalu hanya <InlineMath math="40\%" /> pidato yang langsung menanggapi argumen lawan. (11) Tahun ini angka itu naik menjadi <InlineMath math="65\%" /></Highlight>.

The size of the jump is a percentage-point gap, neither <InlineMath math="25" /> speeches nor a <InlineMath math="65\%" /> increase:

<BlockMath math="65\% - 40\% = 25\text{ percentage points}" />

## Why the other four options fail

- **Rising by <InlineMath math="25" /> speeches** turns percentage points into an unstated speech count.
- **Rising *by* <InlineMath math="65\%" />** confuses the ending figure with the size of the increase.
- **Speeches read from coach scripts** attaches the percentage to the wrong group of speeches.
- **Speakers' average score** turns a share of speeches into judge scores.

# Answer

*Kenaikan tersebut* refers to **the rise in the percentage of speeches directly addressing opponents' arguments from <InlineMath math="40\%" /> last year to <InlineMath math="65\%" /> this year** (*naiknya persentase pidato yang langsung menanggapi argumen lawan dari <InlineMath math="40\%" /> pada lomba tahun lalu menjadi <InlineMath math="65\%" /> pada lomba tahun ini*).
