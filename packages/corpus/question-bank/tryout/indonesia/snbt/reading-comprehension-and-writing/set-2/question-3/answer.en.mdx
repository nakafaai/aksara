In sentence (5), the phrase _Dalam kelas itu_ (in that class) begins with the preposition _dalam_, so **the phrase works as an adverbial, not as the subject**. The predicate is there, _membahas_ (discusses), but nobody is doing the discussing. The sentence can be fixed by dropping the preposition, _Kelas itu juga membahas makna motif-motif..._, or by naming who discusses, _Dalam kelas itu, peserta juga membahas makna motif-motif..._

So sentence (5) is not effective because it has no subject.

- The sentence does have a predicate, _membahas_.
- It has no two words with the same meaning.
- It uses no comma at all.
- The reduplicated forms _motif-motif_ and _turun-temurun_ are standard.
