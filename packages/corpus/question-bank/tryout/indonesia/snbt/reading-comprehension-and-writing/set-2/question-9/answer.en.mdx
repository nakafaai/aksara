# Given

The sentence to insert reads: *"Pabrik itu hanya mau membeli minyak yang kadar airnya rendah."* ("That factory will only buy oil with low water content.")

Sentence (7) introduces **a factory** making biodiesel, and sentence (8) abruptly starts with ***"Syarat itu masuk akal..."*** ("That requirement makes sense...") even though no requirement appeared yet.

# Asked

The best spot to insert the sentence into the passage.

# Solution

Match both ends at once: looking back, ***Pabrik itu*** ("that factory") hooks onto *sebuah pabrik* ("a factory") in (7), and looking ahead, ***Syarat itu*** ("that requirement") in (8) needs the low-water requirement right before it.

> The right insertion point fills a dangling forward pointer while anchoring its own backward reference to the preceding sentence.

## Checking the bridge between sentences seven and eight

Sentences (7) and (8) expose the exact gap: <Highlight>dijual ke sebuah pabrik yang mengelolanya menjadi biodiesel. (8) Syarat itu masuk akal karena air dalam minyak dapat membuat sebagian minyak berubah menjadi sabun</Highlight>.

Inserting the sentence **between sentences (7) and (8)** completes the chain seamlessly: *a factory*, then *that factory requires low water*, then *that requirement makes sense*.

## Why the other four spots fail

- **Between (8) and (9)** leaves *Syarat itu* in (8) pointing to nothing and puts reason before rule.
- **Between (9) and (10)** still leaves sentence (8) without any requirement to refer to.
- **Between (10) and (11)** has no factory in sentence (10) and leaves sentence (8) dangling.
- **Between (11) and (12)** forces *pabrik itu* to jump across paragraphs and makes *mereka* ("they") in (12) ambiguous.

# Answer

The sentence fits best **between sentences (7) and (8)** (*di antara kalimat (7) dan kalimat (8)*).
