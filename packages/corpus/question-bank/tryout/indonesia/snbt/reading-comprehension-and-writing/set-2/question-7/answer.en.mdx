# Given

Sentence (10) opens with **"Program ini memang belum menjangkau semua warung"** ("This program indeed has not reached every stall") because some owners sell to higher-paying travelling collectors.

Sentences (11) to (14) then expose the hidden cost: no quality checks, unknown destination, suspected resale as loose cooking oil, and harm to customers' health.

# Asked

The author's purpose in writing sentence (10) in paragraph three.

# Solution

Spot the concession marker ***memang*** ("indeed/admittedly") in sentence (10) and follow how sentences (11) to (14) pivot. The author concedes the program's limited reach first so the ensuing warning about the collectors' higher price hits harder.

> Opening a paragraph with *memang* typically concedes a shortcoming up front before exposing a bigger danger on the opposing side.

## Tracking paragraph three from sentence ten to fourteen

Sentence (10) concedes the gap directly: <Highlight>Program ini memang belum menjangkau semua warung karena sebagian pemilik warung masih menjual jelantah kepada pengumpul keliling yang berani membayar lebih mahal.</Highlight>

Sentences (11) through (14) immediately unpack the health danger hidden behind that slightly higher price.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Urging the program to stop** clashes with sentence (14), which criticizes selling to collectors instead.
- **Explaining high prices via resale** is absent from sentence (10), and sentence (13) is only a suspicion.
- **Advising the waste bank to raise payouts** never appears anywhere in the passage.
- **Confirming most stalls already joined** overreads *belum menjangkau semua* ("not reached every stall"), which gives no share.

# Answer

The author writes sentence (10) **to acknowledge the program's shortcoming before showing the risk behind the travelling collectors' higher price** (*mengakui kekurangan program sebelum menunjukkan risiko di balik harga pengumpul keliling yang lebih tinggi*).
