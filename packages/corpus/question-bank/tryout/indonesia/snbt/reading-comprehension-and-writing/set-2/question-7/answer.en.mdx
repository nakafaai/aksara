A cause-and-effect conclusion **weakens when another cause could explain the same effect**. The writer concludes that buyers increased because the market became known as clean. If the two nearest markets closed that year, their buyers may have moved to Sukamaju for lack of choice, not because it is clean.

So the statement that weakens it most is the one about the two nearest markets closing for renovation.

- Cheap cloth bags make shopping easier but give no other cause for the extra buyers.
- The fish sellers' support has nothing to do with why buyers come.
- Buyers liking a clean market strengthens the writer's conclusion.
- Streets free of puddles also fit a clean market and do not weaken the conclusion.
