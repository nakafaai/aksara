Sentence (3) says the residents _memelopori_ the colour-kampung idea from Kampung Sindang, even though Kampung Sindang had _sudah dua tahun lebih dulu dicat warna-warni_, been painted in bright colours two years earlier. In KBBI, _memelopori_ means "to pioneer", to open the way or do something before anyone else. The people of Lebak Tirta can't be the pioneers of an idea they took from a kampung that did it first. So the faulty word is _memelopori_, and its replacement has to mean **taking someone else's idea and applying it yourself**.

The replacement has to pass three filters. First, the direction and timing must fit: the idea comes from Kampung Sindang, which was earlier. Second, the source is a neighbouring kampung, not parents or predecessors. Third, the tone must fit sentences (4) and (5): the residents never hid where the idea came from and even put Kampung Sindang's name on the gate.

_Mengadopsi_ ("to adopt") passes all three. KBBI defines it as "to apply, use or follow a suggestion, way of thinking and so on". The sentence becomes _Pada tahun itu, warga mengadopsi gagasan kampung warna dari Kampung Sindang ..._

The other words each fail a filter.

- _Merintis_ ("to blaze a trail") means "to pioneer, to do for the first time" in KBBI. It's just a synonym of the faulty word, so the contradiction stays.
- _Menjiplak_ is close to "to copy", but KBBI also lists "to cheat" and "to steal someone's work and pass it off as your own". That tone clashes with sentences (4) and (5), which show the residents were open about their source.
- _Mewarisi_ ("to inherit") means getting something left behind by parents or predecessors. Kampung Sindang is a neighbour, not someone who left an inheritance.
- _Menularkan_ ("to pass on, to infect") means making something spread to others. The direction is backwards: it would make the people of Lebak Tirta the ones spreading the idea, when they were the ones receiving it.

So the right word to replace _memelopori_ is _mengadopsi_.
