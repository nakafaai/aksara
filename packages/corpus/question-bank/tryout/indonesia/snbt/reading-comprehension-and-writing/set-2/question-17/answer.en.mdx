# Given

Sentence (3) reads: *"Pada tahun itu, warga memelopori gagasan kampung warna dari Kampung Sindang, sebuah kampung di kota tetangga yang sudah dua tahun lebih dulu dicat warna-warni."*

Sentences (4) and (5) emphasize that residents **openly credit Kampung Sindang** on their welcome gate sign.

# Asked

The word that properly replaces the self-contradictory word in sentence (3).

# Solution

***Memelopori*** ("to pioneer") means doing something first, clashing with Kampung Sindang having painted its village two years earlier. Because Lebak Tirta openly took up another village's idea for themselves, ***mengadopsi*** ("adopt") fits cleanly.

> A word-choice replacement must pass three checks together: timeline direction, source relationship, and neutral or matching tone.

## Replacing memelopori in sentence three

Sentence (3) contradicts itself directly: <Highlight>warga memelopori gagasan kampung warna dari Kampung Sindang, sebuah kampung di kota tetangga yang sudah dua tahun lebih dulu dicat warna-warni</Highlight>.

KBBI defines ***mengadopsi*** as taking up and applying an idea from elsewhere without negative overtones, matching the open credit in sentences (4) and (5).

## Why the other four words fail

- ***Merintis*** ("to pioneer/initiate") repeats the exact same contradiction as *memelopori*.
- ***Menjiplak*** ("to plagiarize") carries a dishonest tone clashing with the gate sign crediting Kampung Sindang.
- ***Mewarisi*** ("to inherit") implies an ancestor or predecessor rather than a neighbouring city's village.
- ***Menularkan*** ("to spread/pass on") flips the direction from receiver to spreader.

# Answer

The right replacement for *memelopori* is ***mengadopsi***.
