This question asks for two connectors at once. You need to settle three things: how sentence (9) relates to sentence (8), how sentence (14) relates to sentence (13), and whether the chosen word is even allowed to open a new sentence.

Start with the first gap. Sentence (8) names the borrowers in the downstream villages: adults looking for recipe books and carpentry books. Sentence (9) names the borrowers in the upstream villages: children who love science comics. Two groups are compared, and what they borrow is opposite. That's a **contrast between two sides**, so the fitting word is _sebaliknya_ ("on the other hand, conversely"). Sentence (8) doesn't make you expect anything about the upstream villages, so no expectation gets broken. That's why _meskipun demikian_ ("even so") doesn't fit here. Sentence (9) isn't a result of sentence (8) either, so _oleh karena itu_ ("therefore") is out too.

Now the second gap. Sentence (13) says the fuel price is now almost double. From that fact, you'd naturally expect the visits to be cut. Sentence (14) says the opposite of that expectation: the schedule was never cut and the boat still stops at every village. The expectation raised by sentence (13) is broken by sentence (14). This is a **concessive relation**, meaning "even so". The connector between sentences that expresses it is _meskipun demikian_. The word _tetap_ ("still") in sentence (14) is an extra clue.

The last step is to check the form of the word. A connector between sentences links two sentences that are each complete, and it's followed by a comma. _Sebaliknya_, _meskipun demikian_ and _oleh karena itu_ all belong to this group. The bare word _meskipun_ ("although") is a connector inside one sentence. If you put it at the start of sentence (14), that sentence turns into a dependent clause with no main clause.

| Gap | Relation | Right word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| start of sentence (9) | contrast: downstream borrowers and upstream borrowers | _sebaliknya_ |
| start of sentence (14) | concessive: the price rose, but the schedule stayed | _meskipun demikian_ |

Why the other pairs are wrong:

- _Meskipun demikian_ then _sebaliknya_ swaps the two relations. Sentence (14) isn't the reverse of sentence (13), because it talks about the schedule, not the price.
- _Sebaliknya_ then _oleh karena itu_ makes the unchanged schedule a result of the higher price. The sensible result of a higher price would be a reduced schedule.
- _Sebaliknya_ then _meskipun_ gets both relations right, but _meskipun_ can't stand alone as a connector between sentences.
- _Oleh karena itu_ then _meskipun demikian_ treats the upstream borrowers as a result of sentence (8), even though sentence (8) only describes the downstream borrowers.

So the right pair is _sebaliknya_ for sentence (9) and _meskipun demikian_ for sentence (14).
