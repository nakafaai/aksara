Sentence (8) is long because many of its parts say the same thing twice.

- _Menurut keterangan dari para warga_ ("according to statements from the residents") can simply be _Menurut warga_.
- _oleh para pengunjung_ ("by the visitors") can be just _pengunjung_, because the agent may follow the passive verb _dipotret_ directly.
- _mural yang ... adalah mural yang bergambar ikan-ikan sungai_ repeats the word _mural_. _adalah gambar ikan_ ("is a picture of fish") is enough.
- _ikan-ikan sungai ... di Sungai Ciwangi_ mentions the river twice.
- _dulu pada masa lalu pernah_ marks the past three times. _dulu_ ("once, formerly") is enough.

When you shorten, you have to **cut the repetition without cutting information**. Three pieces of information must stay. First, the source: the residents, as the question requires. Second, the superlative _paling sering_ ("most often"). Third, _dulu_, which tells you the fish lived in the river in the past.

The version that keeps all three and drops the repetition is _Menurut warga, mural yang paling sering dipotret pengunjung adalah gambar ikan yang dulu hidup di Sungai Ciwangi_, according to the residents, the mural photographed most often is the picture of the fish that once lived in the Ciwangi River.

Check the other options.

- The version without _Menurut warga_ turns the residents' claim into a fact. That's exactly the information the question asks you to keep.
- The version with _yang sering dipotret_ drops _paling_. The mural is no longer the most photographed one, just one that's photographed often.
- The version with _ikan yang hidup di Sungai Ciwangi_ drops _dulu_, so the fish seem to live in the river even now.
- The version that starts with _Menurut keterangan para warga_ keeps all the information but is still wordy: _dulu pernah_, the word _mural_ used twice, and the river mentioned twice.

So the best shortened sentence is _Menurut warga, mural yang paling sering dipotret pengunjung adalah gambar ikan yang dulu hidup di Sungai Ciwangi._
