Sentence (8) reads _Setelah mosi dibacakan ketua juri tim menyusun argumen tanpa telepon genggam dan tanpa bantuan pelatih._ It opens with a subordinate clause, _Setelah ..._ ("after ..."), followed by the main clause. Under EYD V, **a subordinate clause that comes before the main clause is separated by a comma**. So a comma has to be there. What you need to decide is where it goes, because its position decides who reads the motion and who builds the arguments.

Find the boundary by asking the text two questions.

- Who reads the motion out? Sentence (3) says a team has only three speakers and an accompanying teacher, while sentence (4) says the judges are brought in by the organisers. A team has no judges, so _ketua juri tim_ can't be one phrase. The motion is read by _ketua juri_, the head judge, and _tim_ is the subject that builds the arguments. _Ketua_ ("head") on its own doesn't say head of what.
- Who builds the arguments? Sentence (9) says the arguments must come from the speakers' own thinking, and the speakers belong to a _tim_ ("team"). Judges score speeches, they don't build arguments.

So the subordinate clause is _Setelah mosi dibacakan ketua juri_, "after the motion is read out by the head judge", and the subject of the main clause is _tim_. The comma goes after _juri_.

_Setelah mosi dibacakan ketua juri, tim menyusun argumen tanpa telepon genggam dan tanpa bantuan pelatih._

Check the other fixes.

- A comma after _dibacakan_ makes the subject _ketua juri tim_, as if some team's head judge were building the arguments. It also leaves out who reads the motion.
- Saying sentence (8) needs no comma flips the rule. EYD V asks for a comma when the subordinate clause comes first, and drops it when the main clause comes first.
- A comma after _ketua_ leaves the reader as just _ketua_, head of nobody in particular, and turns _juri tim_ ("team judges") into the ones building arguments. Judges score speeches, they don't write them.
- A comma after _tim_ pulls _tim_ into the subordinate clause. The main clause _menyusun argumen ..._ is then left with no subject.

So the right fix puts the comma after _juri_: _Setelah mosi dibacakan ketua juri, tim menyusun argumen tanpa telepon genggam dan tanpa bantuan pelatih._
