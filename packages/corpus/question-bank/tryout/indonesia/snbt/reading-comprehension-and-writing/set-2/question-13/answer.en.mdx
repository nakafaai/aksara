The phrase _Keberhasilan itu_ (that success) uses the pointer _itu_, so **it refers to a result just mentioned**. The sentence right before, sentence (7), gives the result of the first three months: fewer elderly residents forgot their medicine. That result is what is called a success.

So _Keberhasilan itu_ refers to fewer elderly residents forgetting their medicine.

- Training the volunteers is preparation in sentence (2), not a result.
- The extra visit is a programme activity in sentence (6), not a result.
- Checking the records every Monday is a working step in sentence (3).
- Same-day delivery is a working step in sentence (4).
