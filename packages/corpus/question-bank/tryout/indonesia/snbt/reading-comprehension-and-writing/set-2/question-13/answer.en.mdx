# Given

Sentence (8) reads: *"Setelah mosi dibacakan ketua juri tim menyusun argumen tanpa telepon genggam dan tanpa bantuan pelatih."*

Sentences (3) and (4) establish that each **team** (*tim*) consists of speakers and a teacher, while **judges** (*juri*) are brought in by organizers.

# Asked

The punctuation fix for sentence (8) that fits both EYD V and the passage.

# Solution

Under EYD V, a **front subordinate clause must be followed by a comma**. Passage facts fix the exact boundary: the **chief judge** (*ketua juri*) reads the motion and the **team** (*tim*) prepares arguments, placing the comma right after *juri*.

> Placing the comma right where an opening clause ends keeps adjacent nouns from colliding into the wrong subject.

## Locating the exact clause boundary in sentence eight

Without a comma, sentence (8) jams three nouns together: <Highlight>Setelah mosi dibacakan ketua juri tim menyusun argumen</Highlight>.

Putting the comma after *juri* cleanly separates the opening clause **Setelah mosi dibacakan ketua juri** from the main subject **tim**.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Comma after *dibacakan*** creates a nonexistent *ketua juri tim* and leaves the motion reader unnamed.
- **No comma needed** reverses EYD V, which requires a comma after a leading subordinate clause.
- **Comma after *ketua*** leaves *ketua* vague and makes judges write the arguments.
- **Comma after *tim*** pulls *tim* into the opening clause, leaving the main verb without a subject.

# Answer

The correct punctuation is **"Setelah mosi dibacakan ketua juri, tim menyusun argumen tanpa telepon genggam dan tanpa bantuan pelatih."**
