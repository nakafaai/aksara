Sentence (2) reads _Dalam lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh para siswa-siswa dari enam belas SMA di kabupaten itu._ It has **two faults**, and the right revision has to remove both without changing what the sentence says.

The first fault is the subject. The passive verb _diikuti oleh ..._ ("is joined by ...") needs a subject that answers "what is joined?". The answer is _lomba tahun ini_, this year's competition. But that phrase comes after the preposition _Dalam_ ("in"), and a phrase that starts with a preposition is an adverbial, not a subject. So the sentence has no subject. Drop _Dalam_ and _Lomba tahun ini_ becomes the subject.

The second fault is _para siswa-siswa_. The word _para_ already marks a group of people, and the reduplicated _siswa-siswa_ also marks plural. Using both at once is redundant, so _para siswa_ is enough.

The result is _Lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh para siswa dari enam belas SMA di kabupaten itu_, this year's competition has students from sixteen high schools. The content stays the same.

Check the other revisions.

- _Dalam lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh para siswa ..._ drops the double plural, but _Dalam_ is still there, so the sentence still has no subject.
- _Lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh para siswa-siswa ..._ has a subject now, but the double plural is still there.
- _Dalam lomba tahun ini, para siswa ... ikut serta di dalamnya_ has the subject _para siswa_, but _di dalamnya_ ("in it") repeats _Dalam lomba tahun ini_. It's still redundant, just in a different way.
- _Lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh enam belas siswa SMA ..._ is free of both faults, but the content changes. The text names sixteen schools, and sentence (3) says each school sends three speakers, so there are <InlineMath math="16 \times 3 = 48" /> speakers, not sixteen students.

So the best revision is _Lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh para siswa dari enam belas SMA di kabupaten itu._
