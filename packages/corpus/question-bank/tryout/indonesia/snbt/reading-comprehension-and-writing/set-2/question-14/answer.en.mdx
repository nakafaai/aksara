According to the KBBI dictionary, the standard word for a pharmacy is **_apotek_**, not _apotik_. Sentence (4) uses _apotik_, so it should read _Relawan lalu mengambil obat di apotek puskesmas dan mengantarkannya ke rumah warga pada hari yang sama._

So the non-standard word in sentence (4) is _apotik_.

- _mengambil_ (take) is the standard form of the root _ambil_.
- _mengantarkannya_ (deliver it) is the standard form of _antar_ with _meng-...-kan_ and _-nya_.
- _puskesmas_ (community health centre) is standard and written in lower case when no name follows.
- _sama_ (same) is standard.
