# Given

Sentence (2) reads: *"Dalam lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh para siswa-siswa dari enam belas SMA di kabupaten itu."*

Sentence (3) adds that each school sends **three speakers**, so sixteen high schools send far more than sixteen students.

# Asked

The best effective-sentence revision of sentence (2).

# Solution

Fix **both syntax faults together** without altering the facts: delete the leading preposition ***Dalam*** so *Lomba tahun ini* becomes the subject of passive *diikuti*, and trim doubled plural ***para siswa-siswa*** to *para siswa*.

> Check passive sentences for two frequent defects: a preposition trapping the subject and a doubled plural marker.

## Fixing both flaws in sentence two

Sentence (2) packs both errors into one line: <Highlight>Dalam lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh para siswa-siswa dari enam belas SMA di kabupaten itu</Highlight>.

1. Drop **Dalam** so **Lomba tahun ini** serves as subject of *diikuti*.
2. Replace **para siswa-siswa** with **para siswa** to remove plural duplication.

With <InlineMath math="16" /> schools sending <InlineMath math="3" /> speakers each, total speakers are not sixteen:

<BlockMath math="16 \times 3 = 48\text{ speakers}" />

## Why the other four options fail

- Keeping **Dalam** leaves the passive verb with no subject.
- Keeping **para siswa-siswa** preserves the doubled plural.
- Using **Dalam lomba ... di dalamnya** introduces a new redundancy (*di dalamnya*).
- Writing **enam belas siswa SMA** changes the meaning from sixteen schools (<InlineMath math="48" /> speakers) to sixteen students.

# Answer

The best revision is **"Lomba tahun ini diikuti oleh para siswa dari enam belas SMA di kabupaten itu."**
