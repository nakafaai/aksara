Judge each proposal with two questions: is the part being changed really wrong, and is the result **correct and still saying the same thing**?

- _Delete sentence (5) because it only repeats sentence (4)_: Tidak tepat (not appropriate). Sentence (4) says the residents didn't hide where the idea came from. Sentence (5) adds new evidence: the sign at the gate naming Kampung Sindang. On top of that, sentence (6) opens with _Di samping papan itu_, "next to that sign". Delete sentence (5) and _papan itu_ has nothing to point back to.
- _Delete daripada in sentence (10)_: Tepat (appropriate). KBBI lists _daripada_ as a preposition that marks a comparison, as in _buku ini lebih bagus daripada buku itu_, this book is better than that one. Sentence (10) doesn't compare anything, so _daripada_ is out of place. The result, _Sebagian besar pengunjung itu datang untuk berfoto di depan mural_, most of the visitors come to take photos, is correct and keeps the meaning.
- _Delete tetapi in sentence (11)_: Tepat (appropriate). The word _Meskipun_ ("although") already marks the contrast between the two halves of the sentence. Adding _tetapi_ ("but") marks the same relation twice, and a sentence like this only needs one connector. Without _tetapi_ it reads _Meskipun kampung itu kini ramai, kebiasaan sebagian warga membuang sampah ke sungai belum berubah._
- _Replace Sebagian besar daripada with Semua_: Tidak tepat (not appropriate). This does get rid of the faulty _daripada_, but _sebagian besar_ ("most") also turns into _semua_ ("all"). The text only says most visitors come to take photos, not all of them, so the meaning shifts.

So the appropriate proposals are deleting _daripada_ in sentence (10) and deleting _tetapi_ in sentence (11). Deleting sentence (5) and replacing _Sebagian besar daripada_ with _Semua_ are not appropriate.
