# Given

The Kampung Lebak Tirta passage has sentence (5) introducing the gate sign referenced in (6) (*Di samping papan itu*), sentence (10) misusing comparative ***daripada*** without any comparison, and sentence (11) doubling contrast markers with ***Meskipun ... tetapi***.

# Asked

Whether each of the four proposed text edits is **Tepat** ("Correct") or **Tidak tepat** ("Incorrect").

# Solution

Check whether each target is truly flawed and whether meaning and references stay intact. Deleting ***daripada*** in (10) and ***tetapi*** in (11) fixes real errors cleanly, whereas cutting sentence (5) or switching to *Semua* ("All") breaks coherence or meaning.

> Reject any edit that orphans a reference in the next sentence or inflates *most* into *all*.

## Checking all four proposed edits

The passage reads <Highlight>Sebagian besar daripada pengunjung itu datang untuk berfoto</Highlight> and <Highlight>Meskipun kampung itu kini ramai, tetapi kebiasaan sebagian warga membuang sampah ke sungai belum berubah.</Highlight>

| Proposed edit | Verdict | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delete sentence (5) | **Tidak tepat** | Adds the gate-sign proof needed by *papan itu* in (6) |
| Delete *daripada* in (10) | **Tepat** | *Daripada* marks comparisons only, not part-of-group phrases |
| Delete *tetapi* in (11) | **Tepat** | Removes redundant double contrast *Meskipun ... tetapi* |
| Replace *Sebagian besar daripada* with *Semua* | **Tidak tepat** | Shifts meaning from *most* visitors to *all* visitors |

# Answer

Proposals **two and three** are **Tepat**, while proposals **one and four** are **Tidak tepat**.
