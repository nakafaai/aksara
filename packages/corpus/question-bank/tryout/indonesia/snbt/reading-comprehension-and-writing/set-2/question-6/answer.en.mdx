The word *commonly* in sentence (2) can be replaced with **often**.

Both adverbs express that something happens frequently. The replacement also preserves the sentence's grammar: "It often develops after prolonged exposure to cold weather or cold water."

The choice *rarely* indicates low frequency, the opposite of *commonly*. *Suddenly* describes an abrupt event, *separately* describes things occurring apart, and *perhaps* expresses uncertainty. None of those three words states how frequently hypothermia develops.
