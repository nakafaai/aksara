The writer's purpose **shows in the part that recommends or urges**, usually near the end. Sentences (1) to (6) tell of the plastic-bag ban at Sukamaju morning market and its good results. Sentences (7) and (8) then recommend that other markets copy the policy, provided cheap replacement bags are available.

So the purpose is to urge other market managers to copy the ban while providing cheap replacement bags.

- The passage does not explain how to make cloth bags. It only says they are sold at the entrance.
- The vegetable sellers' objection is reported, not criticised, and their worry is answered with facts.
- The passage only says the number of buyers grew, without monthly figures.
- Drains no longer blocked are only one good effect, not the point of the passage.
