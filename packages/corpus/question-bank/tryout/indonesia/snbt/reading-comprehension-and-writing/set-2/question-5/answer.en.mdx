When the prefix _me-_ meets a root that begins with _p_, **the _p_ merges into _m_**, as _pakai_ (use) becomes _memakai_. The root _pesona_ (charm) follows the same rule, so the standard form is _memesona_ (charm, enchant), not _mempesona_. The word _mempesona_ is in sentence (7).

So the non-standard prefixed word is in sentence (7).

- Sentence (2) uses _digagas_ (initiated) and _menekuni_ (devote oneself to) in standard form.
- Sentence (3) uses _memintal_ (spin), _mengikat_ (tie) and _mencelup_ (dip) in standard form.
- Sentence (4) uses _ikatan_ (tie) and _keliru_ (wrong) in standard form.
- Sentence (6) uses _mengira_ (assume) and _memerlukan_ (require) in standard form.
