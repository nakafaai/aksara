Sentence (5) has **two words with the same meaning**, _kembali_ and _lagi_ (again). Both say that something is done once more, so one is enough. Of the options, the word to drop is _lagi_: _Di rumah warga, relawan menjelaskan kembali aturan minum obat secara singkat dan jelas._

So the word to remove is _lagi_.

- _relawan_ (volunteers) is the subject, so dropping it leaves the sentence without an agent.
- _secara_ is needed to say how they explain, _secara singkat dan jelas_ (briefly and clearly).
- _singkat_ (brief) is part of how they explain and does not mean the same as _jelas_ (clear).
- _aturan_ (instructions) is the head of the object, so dropping it breaks the meaning.
