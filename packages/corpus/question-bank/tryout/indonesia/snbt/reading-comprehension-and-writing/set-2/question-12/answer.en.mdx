# Given

Judges' records show speeches directly answering opponents rose from **<InlineMath math="40\%" />** last year to **<InlineMath math="65\%" />** this year.

The committee concludes (sentence 12) that the new surprise-motion rule caused the rise, noting (sentence 13) that participating schools, rounds, and judges stayed identical.

# Asked

The statement that most strongly weakens the committee's conclusion in sentence (12).

# Solution

Since schools, rounds, and judges were held constant (sentence 13), the strongest weakener is a **change in how the percentage was measured**. Moving from free-form notes last year to a **mandatory score-sheet box** this year could raise recorded responses without any real change in debating.

> A year-over-year jump loses its force as proof whenever the measurement or recording method changed between the two years.

## Spotting the measurement shift behind the jump

The committee rests on <Highlight>pada lomba tahun lalu hanya <InlineMath math="40\%" /> pidato yang langsung menanggapi argumen lawan. (11) Tahun ini angka itu naik menjadi <InlineMath math="65\%" />.</Highlight>

Free-form notes easily miss rebuttals (undercounting <InlineMath math="40\%" />), whereas a required check-box records every single one, making the two percentages incomparable.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Judges' long-standing preference** was unchanged across both years, so it cannot explain a jump.
- **Script-carrying teams rarely rebutting** supports the committee's case once scripts were stopped.
- **Speakers finding thirty minutes short** critiques comfort, not whether the rule raised rebuttals.
- **Counting all speeches across all rounds** confirms fair sampling and strengthens the committee's data.

# Answer

The strongest weakener is that **last year the percentage came from judges' free notes, whereas this year every score sheet included a mandatory box for direct rebuttals** (*Tahun lalu persentase itu dihitung dari catatan bebas juri, sedangkan tahun ini setiap lembar nilai pidato memuat kolom wajib tentang tanggapan langsung terhadap lawan.*).
