# Given

Sentence (2) of the *running-club* passage contains **a nonstandard loanword spelling**: <Highlight>Pelatih klub itu adalah seorang mantan atlit lari jarak jauh</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing a nonstandard Indonesian spelling.

# Solution

Check loanword spellings against **official KBBI entries**. The word *atlit* in sentence (2) is nonstandard, because standard Indonesian spells it with *e* in the second syllable as **atlet**.

> Standard Indonesian retains *e* in loanwords such as *atlet* and *apotek* rather than replacing it with *i*.

## Auditing spelling across all five sentences

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (1) | *mengadakan*, *seminggu* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (2) | *atlit* | Standard spelling is **atlet** with *e* | **Nonstandard** |
| Sentence (3) | *mencatat*, *menempuh* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (5) | *sebagian*, *menghadapi* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |
| Sentence (6) | *menilai*, *membaik* | Standard spelling | **Standard** |

# Answer

The nonstandard spelling appears in **sentence (2)** (*kalimat (2)*: *atlet*, not *atlit*).
