# Given

The *running-club* table shows **attending members dipping in April** from <InlineMath math="30" /> to <InlineMath math="28" />, and sentence (5) states <Highlight>Pada bulan April, sebagian anggota tidak ikut latihan karena sedang menghadapi ujian sekolah</Highlight> (In April, some members skipped training because of school exams).

# Asked

The relationship between sentence (5) and April's data in the table.

# Solution

Match **April's attendance drop with the *karena* (because) reason in sentence (5)**. Sentence (5) discusses neither run times nor future races, instead directly **explaining why fewer members attended training in April**.

> A prose sentence citing the same month with *karena* directly explains the cause behind that month's shift in the table.

## Comparing all five proposed relationships

| Proposed relationship | Match with sentence (5) and April's row | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explains why fewer members attended training in April | Directly explains the drop from <InlineMath math="30" /> to <InlineMath math="28" /> via school exams | **Correct** |
| Explains why April run times improved | Sentence (5) addresses absences, not pace | **Wrong** |
| Contradicts April's table data | Supports and explains the table rather than contradicting it | **Wrong** |
| Mentions the club's race plan | The June race plan appears in sentence (7) | **Wrong** |
| Gives an example of the fastest member | Mentions no specific individual | **Wrong** |

# Answer

Sentence (5) **explains the cause of the drop in members attending training in April** (*Kalimat (5) menjelaskan penyebab berkurangnya anggota yang ikut latihan pada bulan April*).
