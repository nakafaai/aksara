The pronoun _-nya_ on the verb _mengolah_, to process, **stands for what is processed**. Sentence (4) says the nets are washed, cut up, and sent to a factory _yang mengolahnya menjadi benang_, which processes them into yarn. What the factory processes is those nets.

So _-nya_ refers to the nets that have been washed and cut up.

- The factory does the processing and is not processed.
- The cooperative only sends the nets and is not turned into yarn.
- Yarn is the product, named after _mengolahnya_.
- Carpet is what the yarn is made into.
