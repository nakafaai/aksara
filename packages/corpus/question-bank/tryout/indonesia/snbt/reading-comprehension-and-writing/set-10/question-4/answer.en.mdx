# Given

Sentence (4) of the *net-recycling* passage describes **the recycling workflow**: <Highlight>Jaring itu lalu dicuci, dipotong-potong, dan dikirim ke sebuah pabrik yang mengolahnya menjadi benang untuk karpet</Highlight> (The nets are washed, cut up, and sent to a factory that processes them into carpet yarn).

# Asked

The referent of the pronoun suffix *-nya* in *mengolahnya* (processes them) in sentence (4).

# Solution

Match *-nya* to **the material being acted upon by *mengolah* (to process)**. What reaches the factory to be turned into yarn is **the nets that have been washed and cut up**.

> An object suffix pronoun attached to an active verb points directly to the material or entity acted on by that verb.

## Checking all five candidate referents

| Candidate referent | Role in sentence (4) | Fits as object of *process* | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nets that have been washed and cut into pieces | Input sent to the factory | Exact object being processed | **Correct** |
| Carpet factory | Subject doing the processing | Agent, not the input material | **Wrong** |
| Fishing cooperative | Organization collecting and shipping nets | Sender, not processed material | **Wrong** |
| Yarn | Output mentioned after *mengolahnya* | Result, not the input | **Wrong** |
| Carpet | Final product made from yarn | Downstream product, not the input | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The pronoun *-nya* refers to **the nets that have been washed and cut into pieces** (*jaring yang sudah dicuci dan dipotong-potong*).
