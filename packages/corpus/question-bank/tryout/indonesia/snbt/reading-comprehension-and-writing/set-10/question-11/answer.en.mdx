A good title **covers the whole reading, no broader and no narrower**. The first paragraph describes the larva monitors, mostly children and teenagers. The second gives the result: fewer dengue cases and children used to keeping homes clean.

So the best title is _Anak-Anak Pemantau Jentik Menekan Demam Berdarah di Kelurahan Cempaka_, child larva monitors push down dengue in Kelurahan Cempaka.

- _Musim Hujan di Indonesia_, the rainy season in Indonesia, is too broad, since the reading covers one neighbourhood.
- Bathtubs and vases are only details of sentence (3).
- The monitoring card is only the record in sentences (4) and (5).
- The bird's water dish is only one find, in sentence (6).
