# Given

The *larva-monitors* passage describes **child and teen mosquito-larva monitors in Kelurahan Cempaka** in paragraph one and their two-year impact in paragraph two as dengue cases dropped from forty-two to nine.

# Asked

The most appropriate title for the entire passage.

# Solution

Select the title that **combines the youth larva monitors, the sharp drop in dengue cases, and Kelurahan Cempaka** across both paragraphs, avoiding titles that are too broad or confined to a single prop.

> A well-scoped title spans both paragraphs accurately without reaching beyond the text or shrinking to a single detail.

## Comparing all five title candidates

| Proposed title | Coverage of the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Anak-Anak Pemantau Jentik Menekan Demam Berdarah di Kelurahan Cempaka* | Unites the youth monitors and dengue reduction in Kelurahan Cempaka | **Best fit** |
| *Musim Hujan di Indonesia* | Far too broad for one neighborhood | **Too broad** |
| *Bak Mandi dan Vas Bunga* | Mentions only container examples from sentence (3) | **Too narrow** |
| *Kartu Pemantauan Jentik* | Covers only the tracking card from sentences (4) and (5) | **Too narrow** |
| *Burung dan Tempat Minumnya* | Covers only one example from sentence (6) | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The best title is ***Anak-Anak Pemantau Jentik Menekan Demam Berdarah di Kelurahan Cempaka*** (Child Larva Monitors Curb Dengue Fever in Kelurahan Cempaka).
