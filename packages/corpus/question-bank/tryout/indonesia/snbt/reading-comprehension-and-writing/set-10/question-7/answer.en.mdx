# Given

Sentence (6) of the *screen-printing* passage is **a contrastive compound sentence missing its comma**: <Highlight>Sablon dengan cat berbahan air lebih mahal tetapi hasilnya tidak berbau dan aman bagi kulit</Highlight>.

# Asked

The properly punctuated revision of sentence (6) under Indonesian EYD rules.

# Solution

Place **a single comma directly before the contrast conjunction *tetapi* (but)**, with no comma after *tetapi*, no comma splitting subject and predicate, and no comma before *dan* in a two-item pair.

> Indonesian EYD always requires a comma immediately before contrastive conjunctions like *tetapi*, *melainkan*, and *sedangkan*.

## Auditing comma placement across all five options

| Candidate revision | EYD punctuation check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Sablon dengan cat berbahan air lebih mahal, tetapi hasilnya tidak berbau dan aman bagi kulit.* | Exact comma before *tetapi* and clean flow elsewhere | **Correct** |
| *Sablon dengan cat berbahan air, lebih mahal tetapi ...* | Splits subject from predicate and omits comma before *tetapi* | **Wrong** |
| *... lebih mahal tetapi, hasilnya ...* | Misplaces the comma after *tetapi* instead of before it | **Wrong** |
| *... lebih mahal, tetapi, hasilnya ...* | Adds an invalid comma after *tetapi* | **Wrong** |
| *... lebih mahal tetapi hasilnya tidak berbau, dan aman ...* | Omits comma before *tetapi* and adds an unneeded comma before *dan* | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The correct punctuation revision is ***Sablon dengan cat berbahan air lebih mahal, tetapi hasilnya tidak berbau dan aman bagi kulit***.
