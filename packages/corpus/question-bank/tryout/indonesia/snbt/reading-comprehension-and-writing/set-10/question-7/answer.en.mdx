Sentence (6) is a compound sentence with a contrast: it costs more, but the result is better. EYD says **a comma goes before the conjunction _tetapi_**, but, in such a sentence.

So the right revision is _Sablon dengan cat berbahan air lebih mahal, tetapi hasilnya tidak berbau dan aman bagi kulit._

- A comma after _air_ splits the subject from the predicate _lebih mahal_.
- The comma sits after _tetapi_ instead of before it.
- _Tetapi_ joins clauses inside a sentence and does not open a new one.
- A comma before _dan_ is not needed, and the comma before _tetapi_ is still missing.
