# Given

Sentence (7) records **a sharp drop in Kelurahan Cempaka's dengue cases** from forty-two to nine over two years, supporting the claim that the larva-monitoring program caused the decline.

# Asked

The statement that most strongly weakens that causal claim.

# Solution

Challenge a causal link with **an untreated comparison group showing the same result**. If other neighborhoods without larva monitors saw nearly the same drop over those exact two years, a broader external factor may explain the decline rather than the program.

> A causal claim about a local program is weakened most strongly when comparable areas without the program experience the same drop.

## Evaluating all five statements

| Candidate statement | Effect on the causal claim | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Other neighborhoods without larva monitors saw almost the same drop in those two years | Shows the decline occurred even without the program | **Weakens most** |
| Monitors check tubs and vases weekly | Merely repeats sentence (3) | **Irrelevant** |
| Health volunteers collect cards monthly | Merely repeats sentence (5) | **Irrelevant** |
| Children enjoy being larva monitors | Does not test what lowered dengue cases | **Irrelevant** |
| Homes with monitors rarely have larvae | Supports effectiveness and strengthens the claim | **Strengthens** |

# Answer

The statement that weakens the claim most is ***Pada dua tahun itu, kasus demam berdarah di kelurahan lain yang tidak memiliki juru pemantau jentik juga turun hampir sebanyak itu*** (During those two years, dengue cases in other neighborhoods without larva monitors fell nearly as much).
