# Solution

Check candidate spellings against **official KBBI entries**. The word *hutang* in sentence (7) is nonstandard, because the standard Indonesian headword is **utang** without an initial *h*.

> Official KBBI spelling uses *utang* without a leading *h* rather than the spoken variant *hutang*.
