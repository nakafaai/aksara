The main idea of a paragraph is **what all of its sentences hold together**. Sentences (1) and (2) give the problem: the business took only T-shirt orders, so the machine often sat idle. Sentences (3) and (4) give Pak Darto's effort to find new buyers through cloth bags and local designs.

So the main idea is that Pak Darto looked for a new market for his printing business because T-shirt orders were not steady.

- T-shirt orders are only sentence (1), the starting point.
- Shops no longer giving plastic bags is only a reason inside sentence (3).
- The local designs are only a detail of sentence (4).
- The idle machine is only sentence (2), an effect of the starting point.
