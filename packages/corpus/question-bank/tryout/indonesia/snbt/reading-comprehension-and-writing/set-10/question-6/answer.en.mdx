# Given

Paragraph one of the *screen-printing* passage links **four connected sentences**: Pak Darto once printed only school-event shirts so his press sat idle, then he branched into cloth shopping bags featuring local city landmarks.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph one.

# Solution

Synthesize **the unsteady shirt orders with Pak Darto's search for a new market** across sentences (1) through (4), rather than selecting a single isolated detail.

> A paragraph's main idea unites the problem and response running across all of its sentences.

## Comparing all five options against paragraph one

| Proposed main idea | Coverage of sentences (1) to (4) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pak Darto sought a new market for his printing business because shirt orders were unsteady | Captures both the idle-machine problem and the pivot to cloth bags | **Correct** |
| Pak Darto's shop only printed school-event shirts | Covers sentence (1) only | **Too narrow** |
| Shops in Kota Sukasari stopped giving out plastic bags | Supporting reason in sentence (3) only | **Too narrow** |
| Local artwork shows the old bridge and floating market | Design examples in sentence (4) only | **Too narrow** |
| His printing press often sat idle for weeks | Consequence in sentence (2) only | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph one is that **Pak Darto looked for a new market for his screen-printing business because T-shirt orders were not always steady** (*Pak Darto mencari pasar baru bagi usaha sablonnya karena pesanan kaus tidak selalu ada*).
