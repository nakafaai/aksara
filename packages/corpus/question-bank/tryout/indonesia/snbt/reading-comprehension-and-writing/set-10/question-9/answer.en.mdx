Sentence (7) has two parts. The first says parents _tetap memilih_, still choose, bags printed with that paint. The second, _harganya lebih mahal_, they cost more, **runs against that choice**, since people usually pick the cheaper item. _Tetap_ signals a contrast, and a contrasting clause opens with _meskipun_, although.

So the right word is _meskipun_.

- _karena_, because, makes the higher price the reason for choosing, but the price is not why parents choose the bag.
- _sehingga_, so, makes the higher price an effect of the parents' choice, but sentence (6) says it costs more from the start.
- _agar_, so that, marks a purpose, and no parent wants to pay more.
- _lalu_, then, marks a sequence, not a contrast.
