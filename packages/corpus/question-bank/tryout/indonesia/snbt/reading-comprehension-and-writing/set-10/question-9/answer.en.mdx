# Given

Sentence (7) of the *screen-printing* passage leaves **a concessive-conjunction blank**: <Highlight>Banyak orang tua tetap memilih tas bersablon cat itu untuk anak-anak mereka ... harganya lebih mahal</Highlight> (Many parents still choose bags printed with that ink ... the price is higher).

# Asked

The conjunction that properly fills the blank in sentence (7).

# Solution

Notice **the contrast between *tetap memilih* (still choose) and *harganya lebih mahal* (it costs more)**. Choosing a product in spite of higher cost requires the concessive conjunction **meskipun** (although / even though).

> Pairing *tetap* (still) with an unfavorable factor signals concession, which takes *meskipun* or *walaupun*.

## Testing all five conjunction choices

| Conjunction | Logical relation | Fit in sentence (7) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *meskipun* (although) | Marks concession | Matches still choosing the ink despite higher price | **Correct** |
| *karena* (because) | Marks cause | Wrongly makes high price the reason parents buy it | **Wrong** |
| *sehingga* (so that / thus) | Marks result | Price was already higher in sentence (6), not caused by choosing it | **Wrong** |
| *agar* (in order to) | Marks purpose | Parents do not choose it in order to pay more | **Wrong** |
| *lalu* (then) | Marks time order | Fails to express contrast | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The right conjunction for sentence (7) is ***meskipun*** (although).
