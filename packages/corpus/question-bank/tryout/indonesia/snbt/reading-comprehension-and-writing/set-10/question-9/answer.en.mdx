Sentence (7) has two parts. The first, _banyak pembeli memesan tas itu untuk anak-anak_, many buyers order the bags for children, **is the cause**. The second, _Pak Darto memilih cat berbahan air_, Pak Darto chooses water-based paint, is the effect, since that paint is safe for skin. An effect placed after its cause opens with _sehingga_, so.

So the right word is _sehingga_.

- _karena_, because, reverses the link, as if buyers ordered for children because of his paint choice.
- _meskipun_, although, marks a contrast, but the two parts agree.
- _agar_, so that, marks a purpose, but choosing paint is not what the buyers' orders aim at.
- _bahkan_, even, adds emphasis, not an effect.
