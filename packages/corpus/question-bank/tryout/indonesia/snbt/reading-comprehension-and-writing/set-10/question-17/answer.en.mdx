The *running-club* table records **two monthly series** from January to April: attending members <InlineMath math="20" />, <InlineMath math="24" />, <InlineMath math="30" />, and <InlineMath math="28" />, and average times <InlineMath math="34" />, <InlineMath math="32" />, <InlineMath math="31" />, and <InlineMath math="29" /> minutes.

| Statement | Exact table check | Verdict |

| Attending members increased every month | Fell in April from <InlineMath math="30" /> to <InlineMath math="28" /> | **Not consistent** |

The four verdicts in order are **Not consistent, Not consistent, Consistent, Consistent** (*Tidak sesuai, Tidak sesuai, Sesuai, Sesuai*).
