# Given

The *net-recycling* passage describes **discarded plastic fishing nets along Desa Tanjungrejo's coast** in paragraph one and the cooperative's buyback, factory recycling, extra income, and cleaner beach in paragraph two.

# Asked

The main idea of the entire passage.

# Solution

Select the sentence that **unifies the waste problem, the fishing cooperative's program, and its economic and environmental payoff across both paragraphs**, rather than focusing on a single fact.

> A passage's main idea encompasses both paragraphs together instead of restating one narrow supporting detail.

## Comparing all five options

| Proposed main idea | Coverage of the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Desa Tanjungrejo fishing cooperative turns discarded nets into valuable material while making the beach cleaner | Captures problem, solution, and dual benefits across both paragraphs | **Correct** |
| Plastic nets do not break down easily and trap turtles | Background problem from sentence (2) only | **Too narrow** |
| The cooperative buys nets for <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{.}000{,}00" /> per kilogram | Price detail from sentence (3) only | **Too narrow** |
| Fishers discuss collection at monthly meetings | Single process detail from sentence (5) only | **Too narrow** |
| The cooperative collected over <InlineMath math="3" /> tons of nets in a year | Single figure from sentence (7) only | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The main idea is that **Desa Tanjungrejo's fishing cooperative turns once-discarded torn nets into valuable goods while making the beach cleaner** (*Koperasi nelayan Desa Tanjungrejo mengubah jaring rusak yang dulu dibuang menjadi barang bernilai sekaligus membuat pantai lebih bersih*).
