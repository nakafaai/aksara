**Nevertheless**

Sentence (8) adds participants' reports that the sequence was easier to follow. **Nevertheless** signals that this support does not remove the limits of the short trial and recipe type in sentence (9).

Cause, addition, restatement, and earlier time do not express this shift from support to limitation.
