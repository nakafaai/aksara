# Given

Sentence (1) records **the old habit before the buyback**: <Highlight>Jaring ikan yang sudah rusak sering dibuang nelayan Desa Tanjungrejo ke pantai atau ke laut</Highlight> (fishers often dumped torn nets onto the beach or into the sea), which changed only when the cooperative began buying them in sentence (3).

# Asked

The most likely outcome if the cooperative stops buying damaged nets.

# Solution

Compare **behavior before and after the cooperative's buyback incentive**. Once the cooperative stops paying for torn nets, fishers lose the incentive to collect and store them, so **fishers are most likely to return to dumping damaged nets on the beach or in the sea**.

> When a text's sole incentive for a new behavior ends, the strongest inference is a return to the earlier baseline habit.

## Evaluating all five predicted outcomes

| Proposed outcome | Support in the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fishers resume dumping torn nets on the beach or into the sea | Direct return to the baseline habit in sentence (1) | **Correct** |
| Sea turtles become safer from nets | Contradicts sentence (2) if nets return to the ocean | **Wrong** |
| The carpet factory harvests nets straight from the sea | Never mentioned in the passage | **Wrong** |
| The village beach gets even cleaner | Beach cleanliness depended on collecting nets for the buyback | **Wrong** |
| Fishers earn even larger extra income | Extra income stops when purchases end | **Wrong** |

# Answer

The most likely outcome is that **fishers return to dumping torn nets on the beach or into the sea** (*Nelayan kembali membuang jaring rusak ke pantai atau ke laut*).
