# Given

Sentence (6) of the *larva-monitors* passage misuses ***sesuatu* directly before a noun**: <Highlight>Pada sesuatu hari, seorang juru pemantau menemukan jentik</Highlight>.

# Asked

The sentence containing an improperly used word.

# Solution

Distinguish ***sesuatu* (a standalone pronoun meaning *something*) from *suatu* (an indefinite determiner placed before a noun)**. Because *hari* (day) follows immediately, standard Indonesian requires **Pada suatu hari** (One day).

> In standard Indonesian, *sesuatu* stands alone without a following noun, whereas *suatu* modifies a following noun like *suatu hari*.
