Sentence (6) uses _Pada sesuatu hari_. The word _sesuatu_, something, **stands alone as a noun**, as in _ia menemukan sesuatu_. The word that goes before a noun for one unspecified thing is _suatu_, so the right form is _Pada suatu hari_, one day.

So the sentence with a wrongly used word is sentence (6).
