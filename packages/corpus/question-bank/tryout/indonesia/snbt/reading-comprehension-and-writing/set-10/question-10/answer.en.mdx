# Given

Paragraph two of the *screen-printing* passage reports **weekly cloth-bag orders** from souvenir shops, government offices, and parents, directly resolving the idle-machine problem introduced in paragraph one.

# Asked

The best concluding sentence for paragraph two.

# Solution

Pick the sentence that **wraps up the steady cloth-bag demand and ties back cleanly to paragraph one's idle press**, avoiding off-topic tangents or contradictions.

> An effective closing sentence synthesizes the paragraph's outcome and brings the opening problem full circle.

## Checking coherence across all five candidate closers

| Candidate closing sentence | Fit with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Berkat tas kain, mesin sablon Pak Darto kini jarang menganggur.* | Sums up weekly orders and closes the loop on sentence (2) | **Coherent** |
| *Jembatan tua di kota itu dibangun lebih dari seratus tahun lalu.* | Veers off into bridge history | **Off-topic** |
| *Kantong plastik sebaiknya dibakar setelah dipakai.* | Drifts to plastic-waste disposal | **Off-topic** |
| *Oleh karena itu, Pak Darto menutup usaha sablonnya.* | Contradicts booming weekly orders | **Contradictory** |
| *Namun, tidak ada toko yang mau memesan tas kain.* | Directly contradicts sentence (5) on souvenir-shop orders | **Contradictory** |

# Answer

The best closing sentence is ***Berkat tas kain, mesin sablon Pak Darto kini jarang menganggur*** (Thanks to cloth bags, Pak Darto's printing press now rarely sits idle).
