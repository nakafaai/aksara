_Mendiskusikan_, to discuss, is **a transitive verb that takes its object directly**. The object here is _cara mengumpulkan jaring_, how to collect nets. The preposition _tentang_, about, between the verb and its object is redundant and must go.

So the right revision is _Para nelayan mendiskusikan cara mengumpulkan jaring dalam pertemuan bulanan koperasi._

- Swapping _tentang_ for _mengenai_ still wedges a preposition between verb and object.
- _berdiskusi_ is intransitive, so it needs a preposition, as in _berdiskusi tentang cara_.
- _Para nelayan-nelayan_ marks the plural twice.
- That sentence keeps _tentang_ and adds a needless _jaring-jaring_.
