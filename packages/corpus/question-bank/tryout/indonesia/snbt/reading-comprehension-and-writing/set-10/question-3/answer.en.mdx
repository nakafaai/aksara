# Given

Sentence (5) of the *net-recycling* passage inserts **an extra preposition *tentang* after a transitive verb**: <Highlight>Para nelayan mendiskusikan tentang cara mengumpulkan jaring dalam pertemuan bulanan koperasi</Highlight>.

# Asked

The revision that makes sentence (5) an effective standard Indonesian sentence.

# Solution

Delete **the redundant preposition *tentang* between the transitive verb *mendiskusikan* and its direct object *cara mengumpulkan jaring***. Transitive *meN-...-kan* verbs take their object directly without a preposition.

> Standard Indonesian transitive verbs like *mendiskusikan* and *membahas* take a direct object immediately, unlike intransitive *berdiskusi tentang*.

## Checking all five candidate revisions

| Candidate revision | Verb-object structure and conciseness | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Para nelayan mendiskusikan cara mengumpulkan jaring dalam pertemuan bulanan koperasi.* | Transitive verb followed directly by its object | **Effective** |
| *... mendiskusikan mengenai cara ...* | Still inserts preposition *mengenai* before the object | **Ineffective** |
| *... berdiskusi cara mengumpulkan jaring ...* | Intransitive *berdiskusi* needs a preposition like *tentang* | **Ineffective** |
| *Para nelayan-nelayan mendiskusikan ...* | Double plural redundancy in *Para nelayan-nelayan* | **Ineffective** |
| *... mendiskusikan tentang cara mengumpulkan jaring-jaring ...* | Keeps *tentang* and adds redundant *jaring-jaring* | **Ineffective** |

# Answer

The effective revision is ***Para nelayan mendiskusikan cara mengumpulkan jaring dalam pertemuan bulanan koperasi***.
