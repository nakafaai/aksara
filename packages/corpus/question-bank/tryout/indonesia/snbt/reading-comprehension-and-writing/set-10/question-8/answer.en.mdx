A word's meaning is set **by the context of its sentence**. Sentence (2) says the printing machine _menganggur_ when orders are slow. With no orders, the machine is not used for anything, which matches the KBBI sense of _menganggur_, idle or not working.

So _menganggur_ in sentence (2) means not in use.

- _Rusak_, broken, does not fit, since the machine works again when orders come.
- _Dijual_, sold, is never mentioned.
- _Dipinjamkan_, lent out, is never mentioned either.
- _Dibersihkan_, cleaned, has nothing to do with slow orders.
