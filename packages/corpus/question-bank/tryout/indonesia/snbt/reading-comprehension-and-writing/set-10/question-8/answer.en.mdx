# Given

Sentence (2) of the *screen-printing* passage describes slow periods: <Highlight>mesin sablonnya sering menganggur selama berminggu-minggu</Highlight> (his screen-printing press often sat idle for weeks), giving **clear context for *menganggur***.

# Asked

The meaning of *menganggur* in sentence (2).

# Solution

Read *menganggur* with **its subject *mesin sablon* (printing press) during slow weeks**. With no orders coming in, the machine still works fine but stands unused, so *menganggur* means **unused / idle** (*tidak dipakai*).

> Contextual word meaning matches both the dictionary sense and how the word applies to an inanimate machine.

## Comparing all five meaning options

| Proposed meaning | Fit with an idle printing press | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unused (*tidak dipakai*) | Directly matches sitting idle without orders | **Correct** |
| Broken (*rusak*) | Wrong because the machine works again once orders arrive | **Wrong** |
| Sold (*dijual*) | Never mentioned in the passage | **Wrong** |
| Lent out (*dipinjamkan*) | Never mentioned in the passage | **Wrong** |
| Cleaned (*dibersihkan*) | Unrelated to lacking orders | **Wrong** |

# Answer

In sentence (2), *menganggur* means **unused / idle** (*tidak dipakai*).
