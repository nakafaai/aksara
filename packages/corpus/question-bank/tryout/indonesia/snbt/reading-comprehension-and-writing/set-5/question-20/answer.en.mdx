# Given

The *seed-soak* table shows **a rise-then-fall pattern**: sprouting rises from <InlineMath math="30" /> (unsoaked) to <InlineMath math="42" /> (<InlineMath math="6" /> hours) and peaks at <InlineMath math="45" /> (<InlineMath math="12" /> hours), then drops back to <InlineMath math="35" /> (<InlineMath math="24" /> hours) as sentence (5) notes rotting.

# Asked

The conclusion that best fits both the passage and every row of the table.

# Solution

Capture **the rise-then-fall curve** across all four rows together with sentence (5)'s rotting note, rejecting options that look at only one end of the table.

> A sound experimental conclusion fits every single row of data, capturing both the initial improvement and the downturn past the optimum.

## Checking all five conclusions against the full dataset

Sentence (5) explains the downturn after <InlineMath math="12" /> hours: <Highlight>Biji yang direndam paling lama justru banyak yang membusuk</Highlight> (many of the longest-soaked seeds rotted).

| Candidate conclusion | Fit across all four table rows | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soaking helps, yet soaking too long reduces sprouting | Matches rise from <InlineMath math="30" /> to <InlineMath math="45" /> and drop to <InlineMath math="35" /> | **Best fit** |
| Seeds cannot sprout without soaking | False, <InlineMath math="30" /> seeds sprouted without soaking | **Contradicts data** |
| Longer soaking is always better | False, <InlineMath math="24" /> hours (<InlineMath math="35" />) falls below <InlineMath math="12" /> hours (<InlineMath math="45" />) | **Contradicts data** |
| <InlineMath math="24" />-hour soak gives the best result | False, the peak is <InlineMath math="12" /> hours (<InlineMath math="45" />) | **Contradicts data** |
| Soaking duration has no effect | False, counts shift markedly between <InlineMath math="30" /> and <InlineMath math="45" /> | **Contradicts data** |

# Answer

The best conclusion is that **soaking helps mung bean germination, yet soaking for too long actually reduces the number of seeds that sprout** (*Perendaman membantu perkecambahan biji kacang hijau, tetapi perendaman yang terlalu lama justru mengurangi biji yang berkecambah*).
