# Given

The *seed-soak* table tracks four groups of **<InlineMath math="50" /> seeds** each (half equals **<InlineMath math="25" /> seeds**) with sprouted counts: unsoaked **<InlineMath math="30" />**, <InlineMath math="6" /> hours **<InlineMath math="42" />**, <InlineMath math="12" /> hours **<InlineMath math="45" />**, and <InlineMath math="24" /> hours **<InlineMath math="35" />**.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements is **Sesuai** (Consistent) or **Tidak sesuai** (Inconsistent) with the table.

# Solution

Check **every statement directly against the table numbers**, keeping in mind that half of a <InlineMath math="50" />-seed group is <InlineMath math="25" /> seeds.

> A table that rises to a peak and then drops back down immediately disproves any monotonic "the longer, the more" claim.

## Verifying all four statements

Sentence (2) fixes group size at <Highlight>masing-masing berisi lima puluh biji</Highlight> (fifty seeds each), anchoring every comparison below.

| Statement | Table check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="6" />-hour soak sprouts more than unsoaked | <InlineMath math="42 > 30" /> | **Sesuai** |
| Longer soaking always yields more sprouts | Peaks at <InlineMath math="12" /> hours (<InlineMath math="45" />), then drops at <InlineMath math="24" /> hours (<InlineMath math="35" />) | **Tidak sesuai** |
| <InlineMath math="24" />-hour group yields the fewest sprouts | Unsoaked is lowest (<InlineMath math="30" />), not <InlineMath math="24" /> hours (<InlineMath math="35" />) | **Tidak sesuai** |
| Over half the seeds sprout in every group | Minimum count <InlineMath math="30" /> still exceeds half (<InlineMath math="25" />) | **Sesuai** |

# Answer

Statements **one and four** are **Sesuai** (Consistent), while statements **two and three** are **Tidak sesuai** (Inconsistent).
