# Given

Sentence (6) reports <Highlight>Banyak siswa berlari dan berdesakan di tangga sehingga jalur keluar macet</Highlight> (students ran and jammed the stairs during the twelve-minute first drill), while sentence (7) reports <Highlight>waktu itu turun menjadi lima menit karena siswa sudah hafal jalurnya dan berjalan dengan tertib</Highlight> (time dropped to five minutes once they walked in order).

# Asked

The conclusion best supported by comparing sentences (6) and (7).

# Solution

Compare **movement style and evacuation time** directly: walking in an orderly line cut evacuation time from twelve minutes to five, outperforming running and crowding.

> A comparative conclusion must link the changed behaviour directly to the measured difference in outcome without adding unstated claims.

## Checking all five candidate conclusions

| Candidate conclusion | Check against sentences (6) and (7) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walking orderly reaches the field faster than running and crowding | Five minutes walking versus twelve minutes jammed | **Correct** |
| First drill had no siren | No evidence the siren was omitted | **Unsupported** |
| School field is too small | All students assembled on the field cleanly | **Contradicts text** |
| Students no longer duck under desks | Sentence (2)'s shelter step was never dropped | **Unsupported** |
| A real earthquake struck during drill four | Sentence (7) still describes a practice drill | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The best conclusion is that **walking in an orderly manner gets students to the field faster than running and crowding** (*Berjalan dengan tertib membuat siswa lebih cepat sampai di lapangan daripada berlari dan berdesakan*).
