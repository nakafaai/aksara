# Given

Sentence (4) of the *quake-drill* passage writes <Highlight>siswa wajib mentaati aba-aba guru</Highlight>, even though the root *taat* opens with **a single *t* followed by a vowel that must drop** after prefix *meN-*.

# Asked

The sentence containing an improperly formed affixed word.

# Solution

Apply **the Indonesian K-P-T-S assimilation rule**: a root starting with *k*, *p*, *t*, or *s* followed by a vowel drops its initial consonant under *meN-*, so *meN-* + *taat* + *-i* yields **menaati**, never *mentaati*.

> When *meN-* attaches to a root starting with *k*, *p*, *t*, or *s* plus a vowel, the initial consonant drops, just as *tulis* becomes *menulis* and *taat* becomes *menaati*.

## Checking affixed verbs across all five sentences

| Sentence (2) | *berbunyi*, *berlindung*, *menutupi* | *ber-* prefixes and *tutup* assimilation to *menutupi* are right | **Correct** |
| Sentence (4) | *mentaati* | Initial *t* in *taat* must drop to form **menaati** | **Error** |
| Sentence (5) | *berkumpul* | *ber-* + *kumpul* is standard | **Correct** |
| Sentence (6) | *berlari*, *berdesakan* | Both *ber-* formations are standard | **Correct** |
| Sentence (7) | *berjalan* | *ber-* + *jalan* is standard | **Correct** |

# Answer

The incorrect affixed word appears in **sentence (4)** (*kalimat (4)*: *menaati*, not *mentaati*).
