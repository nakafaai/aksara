Sentence (4) uses _mentaati_. Its root _taat_ begins with _t_. With the prefix _meN-_, **an initial _t_ of the root merges** into _n_, so the right form is _menaati_, just as _tulis_ becomes _menulis_.

So the wrongly formed affixed word is in sentence (4).
