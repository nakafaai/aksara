# Given

Paragraph two of the *goat-milk* passage focuses on **how the cooperative enforces milk quality and lifts farmers' earnings**, yet sentence (7) inserts <Highlight>Kambing perah umumnya berasal dari daerah yang berhawa sejuk</Highlight> (dairy goats generally come from cool regions).

# Asked

The sentence that breaks paragraph coherence in paragraph two.

# Solution

Test every sentence in paragraph two against **the cooperative's quality-control chain**, isolating the one statement that drifts off into unrelated animal trivia.

> A coherent paragraph binds every sentence to one controlling topic, so a general fact disconnected from that chain breaks paragraph unity.

## Checking how each sentence connects to paragraph two

| Sentence | Core content | Fit with cooperative quality theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (5) | Equal price conditioned on cleanliness test | **Coherent**, sets the quality standard |
| Sentence (6) | Clean-milking training for those who fail | **Coherent**, direct follow-up to sentence (5) |
| Sentence (7) | Dairy goats usually come from cool regions | **Off-topic**, unrelated habitat trivia |
| Sentence (8) | Rejections drop and farmer income rises | **Coherent**, measurable outcome of the standard |
| Sentence (9) | Young farmers expand their herds | **Coherent**, downstream effect of higher income |

# Answer

The sentence that breaks coherence in paragraph two is **sentence (7)** (*kalimat (7)*).
