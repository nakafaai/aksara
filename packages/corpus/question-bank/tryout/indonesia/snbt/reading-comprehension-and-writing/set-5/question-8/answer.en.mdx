The second paragraph is about how the cooperative keeps the milk clean and what that brings: the cleanliness test, milking training, less rejected milk, then farmers adding goats. Sentence (7) is about where dairy goats come from, which has nothing to do with milk quality or the cooperative's work.

So the sentence that breaks the unity is sentence (7).

- Sentence (6) gives the training for farmers whose milk failed.
- Sentence (8) gives the result of the test and the training.
- Sentence (9) gives a further effect of that result.
