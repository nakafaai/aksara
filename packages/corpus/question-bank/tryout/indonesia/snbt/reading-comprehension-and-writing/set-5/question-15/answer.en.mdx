# Given

The *quake-drill* passage is **an informative case report built around one school**: paragraph one explains the drill steps at SMA Negeri 2 Sukaraja, and paragraph two documents the faster evacuation time and calmer response.

# Asked

The author's main purpose in writing the passage.

# Solution

Inspect **the tone and job of both paragraphs** together: the writer informs readers through one concrete school example without lobbying the government, comparing multiple schools, or scolding students.

> An author's purpose matches the dominant communicative function across the entire text rather than an unstated call to action or criticism.

## Evaluating all five purpose options

Sentence (8) crystallizes the informative focus: <Highlight>latihan yang diulang membuat siswa tidak panik dan tahu apa yang harus dilakukan ketika gempa sungguhan terjadi</Highlight> (repeated drills keep students calm and prepared).

| Candidate purpose | Alignment with the passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain the benefits of repeated drills via one school's experience | Matches both paragraphs about SMA Negeri 2 Sukaraja | **Correct** |
| Urge government to build quake-proof schools | No persuasion and no building-design topic | **Incorrect** |
| Recount a major earthquake hitting the school | No real earthquake ever occurs in the text | **Incorrect** |
| Compare drills across several schools | Only one school is discussed | **Incorrect** |
| Criticize students who ran on the stairs | Mentioned only as baseline contrast, not a scolding goal | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The author's purpose is **to explain the benefits of repeated earthquake drills based on one school's experience** (*menjelaskan manfaat latihan gempa yang diulang berdasarkan pengalaman sebuah sekolah*).
