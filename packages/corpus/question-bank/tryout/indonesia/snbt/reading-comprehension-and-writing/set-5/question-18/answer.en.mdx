# Given

Sentence (7) of the *seed-soak* passage reads <Highlight>Hasil percobaan ini akan dipresentasikan kepada <InlineMath math="3" /> kelas lain pada pekan depan</Highlight>, even though EYD requires **a standalone one-word number in narrative prose** to be spelled out in words.

# Asked

The standard EYD revision of sentence (7).

# Solution

Spell out the single numeral as *tiga* while avoiding **secondary EYD errors** such as redundant plural reduplication (*tiga kelas-kelas*), a split passive prefix (*di presentasikan*), or unnecessary capitals (*Pekan Depan*).

> Under Indonesian EYD, numbers expressible in one or two words are spelled out in letters unless used in a series or alongside measurement units.

## Checking all five candidate revisions

| Candidate revision | EYD rule check | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *...kepada tiga kelas lain pada pekan depan* | Spells out *tiga* cleanly with no new spelling errors | **Correct** |
| *...tiga kelas-kelas lain...* | Redundant plural since *tiga* already marks plurality | **Incorrect** |
| *...akan di presentasikan...* | Wrongly detaches passive prefix *di-* from *presentasikan* | **Incorrect** |
| *...pada Pekan Depan* | Common time phrase is not a proper noun | **Incorrect** |
| *...kepada <InlineMath math="3" /> kelas-kelas lain...* | Keeps the numeral and adds redundant plural reduplication | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The correct revision is ***Hasil percobaan ini akan dipresentasikan kepada tiga kelas lain pada pekan depan***.
