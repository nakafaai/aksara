EYD says **the first letter of day and month names is capitalised**, while the common words _hari_, day, and _tanggal_, date, stay lowercase. Sentence (7) already writes _Sabtu_ correctly but writes the month _november_ in lowercase.

So the right revision is _Hasil percobaan ini akan dipresentasikan pada hari Sabtu tanggal 14 November di aula sekolah._

- _sabtu_ is a day name, so it needs a capital.
- _Hari_ and _Tanggal_ are common words, so they take no capital.
- _november_ is still wrong, and _Aula Sekolah_ is not a proper name, so it needs no capitals.
- _di presentasikan_ is wrong because _di-_ here is a prefix and must be joined.
