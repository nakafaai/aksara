# Given

Sentence (2) of the *seed-soak* passage states <Highlight>empat kelompok biji yang masing-masing berisi lima puluh biji</Highlight> (four groups of fifty seeds each), and the table records **<InlineMath math="45" /> sprouted seeds** in the **<InlineMath math="12" />-hour** group.

# Asked

The percentage of seeds that sprouted in the <InlineMath math="12" />-hour soaking group.

# Solution

Compute **the part-to-whole ratio** by dividing sprouted seeds by the full group size (<InlineMath math="50" /> seeds) and multiplying by <InlineMath math="100\%" />.

> A percentage always divides by the true group total, so out of fifty seeds every single seed represents two percent.

<BlockMath math="\frac{45}{50} \times 100\% = 90\%" />

| <InlineMath math="95\%" /> | Matches no row in the table | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The percentage of seeds that sprouted in the <InlineMath math="12" />-hour group is **<InlineMath math="90\%" />**.
