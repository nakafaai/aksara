# Given

Paragraph two of the *indigo-batik* passage pairs **two contrasting sides**: sentences (5) and (6) describe how slow natural indigo dyeing is, while sentences (7) and (8) describe its higher price and clean runoff.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph two.

# Solution

Combine **both halves of the paragraph** so the main idea covers sentences (5) through (8) together instead of quoting a single supporting detail.

> A paragraph's main idea is the umbrella statement that unites every supporting sentence rather than a single isolated figure or example.

## Mapping how the paragraph balances both sides

Sentence (5) states the drawback: <Highlight>Pewarnaan dengan pasta daun tom memerlukan waktu yang sangat lama sekali</Highlight> (dyeing with indigo paste takes a very long time).

Sentences (7) and (8) balance it with two gains: <Highlight>laku dengan harga dua kali harga batik berwarna sintetis</Highlight> (sells at double the synthetic price) and <Highlight>tidak mencemari sungai sebab dapat dipakai sebagai pupuk</Highlight> (clean runoff usable as fertilizer).

| Paragraph part | Core point raised | Role in main idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentences (5) and (6) | Slow process requiring up to twenty dips | **Time cost** of indigo dyeing |
| Sentence (7) | Double selling price and tourist demand | **Economic benefit** for artisans |
| Sentence (8) | Safe wastewater reused as garden fertilizer | **Environmental benefit** for river and gardens |

## Eliminating the four narrow options

- **Dark, smelly river water** belongs to paragraph one, not paragraph two.
- **Dipping up to twenty times** is only one supporting detail from sentence (6).
- **Tourists seeking natural batik** explains the higher price in sentence (7) alone.
- **Soaking water used as fertilizer** covers only sentence (8).

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph two is that **dyeing with *daun tom* takes longer, yet benefits both artisans and the environment** (*Pewarnaan dengan daun tom lebih lama, tetapi menguntungkan perajin dan lingkungan*).
