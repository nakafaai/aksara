# Given

Paragraph two of the *quake-drill* passage contrasts **the congested twelve-minute first drill** with **the orderly five-minute fourth drill**, then ends with the supervising teacher's takeaway.

# Asked

The main idea of paragraph two.

# Solution

Combine **the improvement from drill one to drill four** with sentence (8)'s closing summary so the main idea spans the whole paragraph.

> A main idea for a progression paragraph captures the overall change and its final lesson rather than stopping at the initial problem.

## Connecting every sentence of paragraph two

Sentence (7) shows the gain: <Highlight>waktu itu turun menjadi lima menit karena siswa sudah hafal jalurnya dan berjalan dengan tertib</Highlight> (time fell to five minutes as students walked in order). Sentence (8) generalizes why: <Highlight>latihan yang diulang membuat siswa tidak panik dan tahu apa yang harus dilakukan</Highlight> (repeated drills prevent panic).

| Option tested | Fit with all of paragraph two | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated drills make students faster, more orderly, and calm | Unites sentences (5) through (8) | **Main idea** |
| School holds drills twice a semester | Appears in paragraph one (sentence 1) | **Wrong paragraph** |
| Students ran and crowded stairs in drill one | Covers only the opening problem (sentence 6) | **Too narrow** |
| Teacher will grade student readiness | Never stated in the text | **Unsupported** |
| Exit route is marked with green arrows | Appears in paragraph one (sentence 3) | **Wrong paragraph** |

# Answer

The main idea of paragraph two is that **repeated drills make students faster and more orderly in reaching the field without panicking** (*Latihan yang diulang membuat siswa makin cepat dan tertib menuju lapangan serta tidak panik*).
