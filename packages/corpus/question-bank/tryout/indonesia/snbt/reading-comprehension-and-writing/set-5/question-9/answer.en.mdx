The condition **is written in sentence (5)**: the cooperative buys milk at the same price from every farmer, _asalkan susu itu lolos uji kebersihan_, provided the milk passes the cleanliness test. The word _asalkan_ marks a condition.

So the condition is that the milk passes the cleanliness test.

- Chilling happens in the cooperative's tank, not at the farmer's.
- Yogurt is made from milk the cooperative has already collected, so it is no condition.
- Selling at the market is the old way that let milk spoil.
- Young farmers appear in sentence (9) as an effect, not a condition.
