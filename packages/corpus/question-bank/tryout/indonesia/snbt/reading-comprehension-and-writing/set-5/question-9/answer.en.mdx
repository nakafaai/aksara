# Given

Sentence (5) of the *goat-milk* passage states explicitly <Highlight>Koperasi membeli susu dengan harga yang sama dari semua peternak, asalkan susu itu lolos uji kebersihan</Highlight>, marked by **the conditional conjunction *asalkan*** ("provided that").

# Asked

The condition required for the cooperative to buy a farmer's milk at the uniform price.

# Solution

Locate **the explicit condition marker** (*asalkan*) in sentence (5) and separate that prerequisite from steps that happen before or after purchase.

> The conjunction *asalkan* ("provided that") signals a strict condition: the clause right after it states the exact requirement.

## Checking all five options against the text

| Option tested | What the passage actually states | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Milk passes the cleanliness test | Written directly after *asalkan* in sentence (5) | **Explicit condition** |
| Milk is chilled by the farmer | Chilling happens in the cooperative's tank (sentence 3) | **Incorrect** |
| Milk is already turned into yogurt | Processing follows collection at the cooperative (sentence 4) | **Incorrect** |
| Milk is sold at market before spoiling | Describes the old pre-cooperative practice (sentence 2) | **Incorrect** |
| Milk comes from young farmers | Young farmers appear only as a later outcome (sentence 9) | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The required condition is that **the milk passes the cleanliness test** (*Susu lolos uji kebersihan*).
