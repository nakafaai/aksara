A word's meaning is set **by the context of its sentence**. Sentence (1) says the cooperative _menampung susu kambing dari tiga puluh peternak setiap pagi_. The milk comes from many farmers to one place, the cooperative. The cooperative receives the milk and gathers it, which matches the KBBI sense of _menampung_.

So _menampung_ in sentence (1) means to receive and collect.

- _Memeras_, to squeeze, is the farmer's milking work, not the cooperative's.
- _Menyaring_, to filter, is not mentioned in the reading.
- _Menjual kembali_, to resell, does not fit, since the milk is chilled and processed.
- _Membagikan_, to hand out, goes the wrong way, since the milk comes in to the cooperative.
