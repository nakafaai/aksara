# Given

Sentence (1) of the *goat-milk* passage states <Highlight>Koperasi peternak di Desa Girimulyo menampung susu kambing dari tiga puluh peternak setiap pagi</Highlight>, and sentence (3) adds that the milk is chilled inside **the cooperative's cooling tank**.

# Asked

The meaning of the word *menampung* in sentence (1).

# Solution

Track **the direction of the action** in sentences (1) through (3): milk flows from thirty separate farmers into one shared cooperative tank, where it is received and collected for chilling.

> Word meaning is anchored by actor and direction: one cooperative taking in milk from thirty farmers means receiving and collecting it.

## Evaluating all five lexical options

| Candidate meaning | Fit with the cooperative's role | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *menerima dan mengumpulkan* (receive and collect) | Matches milk arriving from thirty farmers into one tank | **Correct** |
| *memeras* (milk / squeeze) | Done by farmers when milking (sentence 6), not the cooperative | **Incorrect** |
| *menyaring* (filter) | Never mentioned in the passage | **Incorrect** |
| *menjual kembali* (resell) | Does not match sentences (3) and (4), where milk is chilled and processed | **Incorrect** |
| *membagikan* (distribute) | Reverses the flow since milk moves into the cooperative | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The meaning of *menampung* in sentence (1) is **to receive and collect** (*menerima dan mengumpulkan*).
