# Given

Five sentences from the *indigo-batik* passage are checked for spelling, and sentence (4) writes <Highlight>lalu airnya di aduk dengan kapur</Highlight>, detaching **the passive prefix *di-* from its verb stem**.

# Asked

The sentence containing a word-spelling error.

# Solution

Distinguish **the passive verb prefix *di-*** (always attached with no space) from **the locative preposition *di*** (always spaced before a place noun).

> Whenever a *di* form pairs with an active *me-* verb, *di-* is a passive prefix and must be joined directly to the root without a space.

## Auditing spelling across all five candidate sentences

| Sentence | Target forms | EYD spelling check | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence (1) | *pewarna*, *sintetis* | Standard forms spelled properly | **Correct** |
| Sentence (3) | *beralih*, *penghasil* | Prefixes joined properly | **Correct** |
| Sentence (4) | *di aduk* | Passive of *mengaduk*, must be written **diaduk** | **Error** |
| Sentence (6) | *dicelup*, *dijemur*, *berulang kali* | Joined passives and spaced phrase all standard | **Correct** |
| Sentence (8) | *mencemari*, *dipakai* | Active and passive affixes accurate | **Correct** |


A location phrase like *di sungai* takes a space because *di* marks a place, whereas *diaduk* names the passive action of stirring.

# Answer

The spelling error occurs in **sentence (4)** (*kalimat (4)*, where *di aduk* must be *diaduk*).
