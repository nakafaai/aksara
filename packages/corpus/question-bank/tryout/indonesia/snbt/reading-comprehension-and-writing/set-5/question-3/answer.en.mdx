Sentence (4) writes _di aduk_ as two words. The word is a passive verb, not a place, so **_di-_ here is a prefix and must be joined: _diaduk_**. The preposition _di_ is written apart only before a word naming a place, as in _di sungai_.

So the spelling mistake is in sentence (4).
