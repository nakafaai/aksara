The claim is that **training made the milk cleaner, so less was rejected**. A weakening statement has to show another cause that could also lower the rejected amount. If the test was relaxed in the same year, milk that used to fail could pass without being any cleaner.

So the strongest weakener is _Pada tahun yang sama, koperasi melonggarkan syarat uji kebersihan susu._

- Farmers milking more cleanly after training would strengthen the claim.
- The tank's capacity has nothing to do with clean milk.
- The price of yogurt does not explain how much milk was rejected.
- Young farmers adding goats is an effect, not another cause.
