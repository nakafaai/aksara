# Given

The *goat-milk* passage pairs clean-milking training in sentence (6) with <Highlight>jumlah susu yang ditolak koperasi turun dari seperlima menjadi kurang dari sepersepuluh</Highlight> (rejected milk dropped from one-fifth to under one-tenth) in sentence (8), prompting the claim that **training caused the drop**.

# Asked

The statement that most strongly weakens the causal link between milking training and lower milk rejection.

# Solution

Look for **an alternative cause** that explains why fewer batches were rejected without milk cleanliness actually improving.

> A causal claim weakens most sharply when the measurement bar itself shifts, allowing the reported number to fall even if real quality stayed unchanged.

## Weighing how each option affects the causal claim

| Option | Effect on the training claim | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The cooperative relaxed the cleanliness test | Lowers rejections even if milk never got cleaner | **Weakens most** |
| Farmers milked more cleanly after training | Directly supports the training mechanism | **Strengthens** |
| Cooling tank holds milk from forty farmers | Concerns storage capacity, not cleanliness rejections | **Irrelevant** |
| Market price of yogurt rose | Concerns finished goods price, not rejection rate | **Irrelevant** |
| Young farmers added more goats | Downstream consequence after rejections fell (sentence 9) | **Does not weaken** |

# Answer

The statement that most strongly weakens the claim is that **in the same year, the cooperative relaxed its milk cleanliness test standards** (*Pada tahun yang sama, koperasi melonggarkan syarat uji kebersihan susu*).
