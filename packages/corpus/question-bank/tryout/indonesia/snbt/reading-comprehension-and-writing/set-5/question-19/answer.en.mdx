# Given

Sentence (5) of the *seed-soak* passage reports the event <Highlight>Biji yang direndam paling lama justru banyak yang membusuk</Highlight> (the longest-soaked seeds largely rotted), and sentence (6) follows with <Highlight>Siswa menduga hal itu terjadi karena biji kekurangan udara selama terendam</Highlight>, combining **hypothesis and cause markers**.

# Asked

The relationship between sentence (6) and sentence (5).

# Solution

Read **three exact linguistic cues** in sentence (6): *hal itu* ("that") refers back to the rotting in sentence (5), *karena* ("because") introduces a cause, and *menduga* ("suspect") marks it as a tentative explanation.

> A follow-up sentence combining a back-reference (*hal itu*), a tentative verb (*menduga*), and a causal marker (*karena*) proposes a suspected cause for the preceding event.

## Testing all five relationship options

| Candidate relationship | Fit with cues in sentence (6) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| States a suspected cause of sentence (5)'s event | Matches *menduga*, *hal itu*, and *karena* exactly | **Correct** |
| Gives an example of rotting seeds | Lists no specific example | **Incorrect** |
| Contradicts sentence (5) | Accepts the rotting and explains why it happened | **Incorrect** |
| Summarizes the whole experiment | Addresses only the rotted group, not all four | **Incorrect** |
| Restates sentence (5) in other words | Adds the new idea of air deprivation | **Incorrect** |

# Answer

The relationship is that **sentence (6) proposes a suspected cause for the event in sentence (5)** (*Kalimat (6) menyampaikan dugaan penyebab peristiwa dalam kalimat (5)*).
