Sentence (8) says the cooperative rejects less and less milk. Sentence (9) says young farmers add more goats. Adding goats **is an effect** of the business paying off better, so the two sentences are joined by a connector of result.

So the right connector is _Oleh karena itu_, therefore.

- _Namun_, however, marks a contrast, but the two sentences do not contrast.
- _Sebaliknya_, on the contrary, marks an opposite situation.
- _Misalnya_, for example, introduces an example, but sentence (9) is not an example of sentence (8).
- _Meskipun demikian_, even so, marks a contrast, like _Namun_.
