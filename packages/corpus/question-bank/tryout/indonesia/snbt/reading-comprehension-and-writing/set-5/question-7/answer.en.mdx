# Given

Sentence (8) states <Highlight>jumlah susu yang ditolak koperasi turun dari seperlima menjadi kurang dari sepersepuluh sehingga pendapatan peternak naik</Highlight> (rejected milk fell and farmers' income rose), and sentence (9) opens with a blank before **young farmers begin expanding their herds**.

# Asked

The inter-sentence connector that properly fills the blank in sentence (9).

# Solution

Identify **the cause-and-effect relationship** linking both sentences: higher income in sentence (8) gives young farmers the confidence and means to add more goats in sentence (9).

> When the second sentence states an action triggered directly by the positive result in the first sentence, use a result connector rather than a contrast or example marker.

## Testing all five connectors

| Candidate connector | Logical relation expressed | Fit between sentences (8) and (9) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Oleh karena itu* (Therefore) | Signals a **direct result** of the prior cause | **Correct** |
| *Namun* (However) | Signals contrast | **Incorrect**, both ideas move in the same direction |
| *Sebaliknya* (Conversely) | Signals an opposite situation | **Incorrect**, no reversal occurs |
| *Misalnya* (For example) | Introduces an illustration | **Incorrect**, buying goats is a consequence, not an instance of income |
| *Meskipun demikian* (Nevertheless) | Signals concession or contrast | **Incorrect**, no conflict exists |

# Answer

The right connector for sentence (9) is ***Oleh karena itu***.
