Sentence (5) uses both _sangat_ and _sekali_ to intensify _lama_, long. The two words mean the same, so **using both is redundant**. An effective sentence needs only one of them.

So the right revision is _Pewarnaan dengan pasta daun tom memerlukan waktu yang sangat lama._

- Moving _sangat_ before _memerlukan_ still leaves _sekali_ and changes the meaning.
- _amat sangat_ also stacks two intensifiers with the same meaning.
- A comma after _tom_ splits the subject from the predicate _memerlukan_.
- The word _yang_ after _Pewarnaan_ blurs the subject and is not needed.
