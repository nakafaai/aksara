# Given

Sentence (5) reads <Highlight>Pewarnaan dengan pasta daun tom memerlukan waktu yang sangat lama sekali</Highlight>, stacking **two synonymous intensifiers** (*sangat* and *sekali*) around the adjective *lama*.

# Asked

The effective revision of sentence (5).

# Solution

Remove **the redundant intensifier (pleonasm)** by keeping just one degree marker while preserving an unbroken subject-predicate structure.

> One adjective needs only a single intensifier (*sangat lama* or *lama sekali*), never two stacked degree words meaning the same thing.

## Comparing all five candidate revisions

| Candidate wording | Structural evaluation | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *memerlukan waktu yang sangat lama* | Keeps one intensifier with clean subject and verb | **Effective** |
| *sangat memerlukan waktu yang lama sekali* | Shifts *sangat* onto the verb and changes the meaning | **Ineffective** |
| *waktu yang amat sangat lama* | Replaces one double intensifier with another (*amat sangat*) | **Ineffective** |
| *daun tom, memerlukan waktu yang lama sekali* | Inserts a comma splitting subject from predicate | **Ineffective** |
| *Pewarnaan yang dengan pasta daun tom...* | Adds stray *yang*, breaking the subject phrase | **Ineffective** |


Dropping *sekali* removes the redundancy cleanly while keeping the exact meaning that natural dyeing takes a long time.

# Answer

The correct revision is ***Pewarnaan dengan pasta daun tom memerlukan waktu yang sangat lama***.
