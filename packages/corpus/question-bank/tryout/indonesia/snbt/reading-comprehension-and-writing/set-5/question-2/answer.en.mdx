# Given

Sentence (7) reads <Highlight>Meskipun begitu batik berwarna alami laku dengan harga dua kali harga batik berwarna sintetis karena banyak dicari wisatawan</Highlight>, omitting the comma after **the sentence-initial inter-sentence transition**.

# Asked

The properly punctuated revision of sentence (7).

# Solution

Apply **two Indonesian EYD comma rules** together: place a comma immediately after an inter-sentence connector opening a sentence, and avoid commas splitting subject from predicate or preceding a trailing *karena* clause.

> An Indonesian inter-sentence transition at the start of a sentence always takes a following comma, whereas a subordinate clause introduced by *karena* after the main clause takes no comma before it.

## Checking comma placement across all five options

| Comma placement tested | Structural effect | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After *Meskipun begitu* | Marks the required boundary after an inter-sentence connector | **Correct** |
| After *alami* | Splits subject *batik berwarna alami* from verb *laku* | **Incorrect** |
| After *Meskipun* | Breaks the fixed two-word connector *Meskipun begitu* in half | **Incorrect** |
| After *laku* | Separates predicate from modifier while missing the opening comma | **Incorrect** |
| Before *karena* | Adds an unwanted comma before a trailing reason clause | **Incorrect** |


Only a single comma right after *Meskipun begitu* preserves both the opening transition rule and an unbroken main-plus-subordinate clause flow.

# Answer

The correct revision is ***Meskipun begitu, batik berwarna alami laku dengan harga dua kali harga batik berwarna sintetis karena banyak dicari wisatawan***.
