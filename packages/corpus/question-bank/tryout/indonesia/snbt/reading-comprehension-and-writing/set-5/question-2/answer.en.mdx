_Meskipun begitu_, even so, is **a connector between sentences**. EYD says such a connector at the start of a sentence is followed by a comma. Also, the clause _karena banyak dicari wisatawan_ comes after the main clause, so no comma goes before it.

So the right revision is _Meskipun begitu, batik berwarna alami laku dengan harga dua kali harga batik berwarna sintetis karena banyak dicari wisatawan._

- A comma after _alami_ splits the subject from the predicate _laku_.
- A comma after _Meskipun_ breaks the phrase _Meskipun begitu_ apart.
- A comma after _laku_ splits the predicate from its adverbial, and there is still no comma after _Meskipun begitu_.
- No comma goes before _karena_, because that clause follows the main clause.
