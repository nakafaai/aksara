A good title **covers the whole reading, no broader and no narrower**. The first paragraph describes the school's earthquake drill. The second shows the result: students grow faster and more orderly, and stay calm, because the drill is repeated.

So the best title is _Latihan Gempa yang Berulang Membuat Siswa Lebih Siap_, repeated earthquake drills make students better prepared.

- _Gempa Bumi di Indonesia_, earthquakes in Indonesia, is too broad, since the reading covers one school.
- The green-arrow route is only a detail of sentence (3).
- Staff counting students is only a detail of sentence (4).
- The jammed stairs happened only in the first drill, sentence (6).
