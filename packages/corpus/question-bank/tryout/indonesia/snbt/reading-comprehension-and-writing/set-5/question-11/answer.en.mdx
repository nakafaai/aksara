# Given

The *quake-drill* passage outlines **regular earthquake drills at SMA Negeri 2 Sukaraja** in paragraph one, then shows in paragraph two how repeating the drill cuts evacuation time and keeps students calm.

# Asked

The most fitting title for the entire passage.

# Solution

Pick the title that **covers both paragraphs cleanly**: neither stretching beyond one school nor zooming in on a single minor detail.

> A strong title matches the exact scope of the whole text, avoiding both national overreach and isolated single-sentence details.

## Comparing the scope of all five titles

Paragraph two captures the unifying message: <Highlight>latihan yang diulang membuat siswa tidak panik dan tahu apa yang harus dilakukan ketika gempa sungguhan terjadi</Highlight> (repeated drills stop panic and teach students what to do).

| Candidate title | Scope compared with the full passage | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Latihan Gempa yang Berulang Membuat Siswa Lebih Siap* | Unites the drill routine and its readiness payoff | **Best fit** |
| *Gempa Bumi di Indonesia* | Far too broad since only one school is discussed | **Too broad** |
| *Jalur Keluar Bertanda Panah Hijau* | Captures only one detail from sentence (3) | **Too narrow** |
| *Petugas Menghitung Siswa di Lapangan* | Captures only one procedure from sentence (4) | **Too narrow** |
| *Tangga Sekolah yang Macet* | Mentions only the first drill's bottleneck in sentence (6) | **Too narrow** |

# Answer

The best title is ***Latihan Gempa yang Berulang Membuat Siswa Lebih Siap*** (Repeated Earthquake Drills Make Students Better Prepared).
