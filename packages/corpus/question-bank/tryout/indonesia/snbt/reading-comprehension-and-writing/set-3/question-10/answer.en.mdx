A pointer phrase like _hal itu_ (that thing) **usually points to the sentence right before it**. Sentence (8) says coffee in gardens near the bee boxes bears more fruit. Sentence (9) then gives the reason: well-pollinated flowers turn into fruit more often. What is being explained is that heavier crop.

So _Hal itu_ refers to the coffee near the bee boxes bearing more fruit.

- The moving of pollen is the cause that sentence (9) gives, not the thing being explained.
- The honey's price is in sentence (5), far from sentence (9), and unrelated to pollination.
