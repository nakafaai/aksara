Each option names a pointing word or phrase and what it refers to. So you have to trace **five reference chains**, each one backwards until you reach the sentence that actually names the thing. A pointing word doesn't always point to the nearest noun.

1. *Program yang sama* ("the same program") in sentence (12) points to the program already running in Blok C. It starts in sentence (4): every house in Blok C must build an infiltration well. Sentence (6) gives the rule: houses with fully cemented yards build two wells, houses that still have a garden build one. So it refers to every house's duty to build one or two wells depending on its yard. This statement is correct.
2. *Kedua masalah itu* ("both problems") in sentence (3) points to two different problems: flooding in the rainy season (sentence (1)) and wells drying up in the dry season (sentence (2), which opens with *Sebaliknya*, "on the other hand"). Flooding in Blok B and flooding in Blok C is one problem in two places. Sentence (3) names the two blocks with a different phrase, *kedua blok itu* ("both blocks").
3. *Musim yang sama* ("the same season") in sentence (10) does come right after *musim hujan tahun sebelumnya* ("the previous year's rainy season") in sentence (9), but that isn't what it refers to. Sentence (10) compares Blok B with Blok C in **the rainy season after the wells were built**, the season of sentences (7) and (8). The previous year's rainy season only serves as the rainfall comparison.
4. *Mereka* ("they") in sentence (12) points to *pengurus paguyuban* ("the association's board") in sentence (11). According to sentence (4), that association belongs to Blok C's residents only. Blok B has no role in the program and doesn't even have wells yet.
5. *Usulan itu* ("that proposal") in sentence (13) points to the plan in sentence (12). The ones planning the proposal are Blok C's association board. The neighbourhood board (*pengurus RW*) only receives it.

So the correct statement is that *program yang sama* in sentence (12) refers to every house's duty to build one or two infiltration wells depending on its yard.
