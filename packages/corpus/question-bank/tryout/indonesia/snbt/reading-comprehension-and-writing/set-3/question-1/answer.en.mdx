# Given

In the infiltration-well passage, sentences (1) and (2) contrast **rainy-season flooding** with **dry-season empty wells**, sentences (4) and (6) require **one or two wells per house** in **Block C**, sentences (8) and (10) compare Blocks C and B in the **same rainy season**, and sentences (11) to (13) describe **Block C leaders** proposing expansion to the RW committee.

# Asked

Which statement accurately identifies a reference phrase in the passage.

# Solution

Trace every pointer **back to its true anchor** rather than grabbing the nearest noun. Only ***program yang sama*** ("the same program") in sentence (12) matches its definition accurately across sentences (4) and (6).

> A reference phrase points to the matching concept and actor in context, not always the closest noun phrase in the sentence before.

## Checking all five reference statements

Sentences (4) and (12) link directly through <Highlight>mewajibkan setiap rumah di blok itu membuat sumur resapan</Highlight> ("required every house in that block to build infiltration wells") and <Highlight>mengusulkan agar program yang sama diterapkan di tiga blok lain</Highlight> ("propose applying the same program to three other blocks").

- **Program yang sama (12)**: **Correct**, referring to the rule in (4) and (6) requiring one or two wells based on yard paving.
- **Kedua masalah itu (3)**: Wrong, referring to flooding (1) and dry wells (2), not flooding in two blocks.
- **Musim yang sama (10)**: Wrong, referring to the post-well rainy season in (7) and (8), not last year's season in (9).
- **Mereka (12)**: Wrong, referring only to Block C's association, not Block B.
- **Usulan itu (13)**: Wrong, the proposal is Block C's plan submitted to the RW, not the RW's own plan.

# Answer

The accurate statement is that ***program yang sama* in sentence (12) refers to requiring each house to build one or two infiltration wells depending on its yard** (*Program yang sama pada kalimat (12) merujuk pada kewajiban setiap rumah membuat satu atau dua sumur resapan sesuai keadaan halamannya.*).
