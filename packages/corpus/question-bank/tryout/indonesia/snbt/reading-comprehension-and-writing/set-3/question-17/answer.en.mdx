# Given

Sentence (12) states the office's premise: **free buses now serve every junior high school in the hill villages**.

Sentence (13) concludes: **no transport-stipend student needs an ojek anymore to get to school**.

# Asked

The unstated assumption required to link sentence (12) to sentence (13).

# Solution

Spot the gap between home and school: buses reach every **school**, yet only pick up at **bus stops** (sentence 4). For zero students to need an ojek on the way to school, the office must assume **every stipend student can reach the bus stop for their school without an ojek**.

> Test an assumption by negating it: if its opposite immediately collapses the conclusion, it is the required assumption.

## Bridging the gap from home to the bus stop

The premise leaps straight from school coverage to morning travel: <Highlight>bus gratis kini sudah melayani semua SMP di desa-desa perbukitan itu</Highlight> to <Highlight>tidak seorang pun siswa penerima uang transpor perlu lagi naik ojek untuk berangkat ke sekolah</Highlight>.

If even one stipend student needs an ojek just to reach the stop, sentence (13) collapses immediately.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Living within <InlineMath math="1" /> km of a stop** is unnecessarily strict, as <InlineMath math="2" /> km can still be walked.
- **Every student rides the bus daily** confuses *not needing an ojek* with *always taking the bus*.
- **No stipend student joins extracurriculars** concerns the *return* trip, while sentence (13) covers going *to* school.
- **Bus costs being lower than stipends** concerns budget savings, not the logical gap from (12) to (13).

# Answer

The required assumption is that **every transport-stipend student can reach the bus stop serving their school without taking an ojek** (*Setiap siswa penerima uang transpor dapat mencapai halte bus yang menuju sekolahnya tanpa naik ojek.*).
