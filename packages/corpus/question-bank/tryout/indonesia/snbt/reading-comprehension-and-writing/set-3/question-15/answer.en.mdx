A good title **covers the whole passage, not just one part**. The passage tells how villagers built a suspension bridge together and how it helps pupils going to school and traders going to market. The title that covers all of that is _Jembatan Gotong Royong untuk Sekolah dan Pasar_ (A Bridge Built Together for School and Market).

So the best title is _Jembatan Gotong Royong untuk Sekolah dan Pasar_.
