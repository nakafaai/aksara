A mean is found by **adding all the values and dividing by how many there are**. The watch turnout over the five months adds up to

$$12 + 18 + 30 + 32 + 33 = 125.$$

There are five months, so the mean is

$$\frac{125}{5} = 25.$$

So on average $$25$$ residents joined the night watch each month.

- $$20$$ is the mean of the first three months only.
- $$24$$ comes from miscounting the total as $$120$$.
- $$30$$ is the median of the data, not the mean.
- $$32$$ is the April figure alone.
