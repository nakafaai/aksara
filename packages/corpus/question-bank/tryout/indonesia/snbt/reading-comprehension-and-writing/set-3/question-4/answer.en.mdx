Under Indonesian spelling rules **a comma follows a subordinate clause that comes before the main clause**. Sentence (5) opens with the subordinate clause _Jika hujan turun pada musim kemarau_ (if rain falls in the dry season), followed by the main clause _garam di dalam rumah prisma tidak larut kembali_ (the salt in the prism house does not dissolve again). A comma belongs between them.

The correct sentence (5): _Jika hujan turun pada musim kemarau, garam di dalam rumah prisma tidak larut kembali karena terlindung atap._

So the sentence that needs a comma is sentence (5).

- Sentence (1) does not open with a subordinate clause, so it needs no comma.
- Sentence (4) puts the clause _sehingga ..._ (so that) after the main clause, and in that order no comma is used.
- Sentence (6) also puts the clause _karena ..._ (because) after the main clause, so it needs no comma.
- Sentence (8) already has a comma after _Namun_ (however), and _sehingga ..._ in the middle needs none.
