Making a sentence concise means dropping words whose meaning other words already carry, without dropping information. So work in two steps: first find the redundant parts, then mark the information that **must not be lost**.

Step 1: the redundant parts of sentence (9).

- *Jumlah curah hujan yang turun* ("the amount of rainfall that falls"): according to KBBI (the official Indonesian dictionary), *curah hujan* already means the amount of rain that falls in an area over a given period. *Jumlah* ("amount") and *yang turun* ("that falls") repeat that meaning, so *curah hujan* alone is enough.
- *Hampir sama dan tidak jauh berbeda* ("almost the same and not very different"): both phrases mean the same thing. One is enough, *hampir sama*.

Step 2: the information that has to stay.

- The data source: the records of **the nearest weather station**, not just any station.
- The strength of the comparison: *hampir sama* ("almost the same"), not exactly the same.
- The comparison base: the previous year's rainy season. Careful here: sentences (4) and (7) show that the season right before that rainy season was the dry season when the wells were built. So *musim sebelumnya* ("the previous season") isn't the same as *musim hujan tahun sebelumnya* ("the previous year's rainy season").

Only one version passes all of these: *Menurut catatan stasiun cuaca terdekat, curah hujan pada musim hujan itu hampir sama dengan curah hujan pada musim hujan tahun sebelumnya.*

Now check the other options.

- *Musim sebelumnya*: it's short, but the comparison base shifts to the dry season. Comparing rainy-season rainfall with the dry season changes what the sentence says.
- *Sama dengan* ("the same as"): *hampir* ("almost") is gone, so the comparison becomes stronger than the data.
- *Jumlah curah hujan*: the comparison is right, but the redundant *jumlah* is still there, so the sentence isn't concise yet.
- *Stasiun cuaca* without *terdekat* ("nearest"): it's short, but the detail of which station kept the records is lost.

So the right version drops *jumlah*, *yang turun*, and *tidak jauh berbeda*, but keeps *stasiun cuaca terdekat*, *hampir*, and *musim hujan tahun sebelumnya*.
