# Given

Sentence (9) already reports that **twelve of the eighteen second-cohort participants stayed until year-end** and seven finished a scarf.

Sentence (10) then adds: *"Pada tahun itu, dua pertiga remaja yang mendaftar tidak berhenti di tengah jalan."* ("That year, two thirds of registered teens did not quit midway.")

# Asked

Which sentence can be deleted without losing a single piece of information.

# Solution

Simplify sentence (9)'s numbers into a fraction: **<InlineMath math="12" /> out of <InlineMath math="18" />** equals **two thirds** exactly. Since sentence (10) merely restates part of sentence (9) as a fraction and anchors no later pointer, **sentence (10)** can be cut cleanly.

> A sentence that only rewrites an already-stated ratio as a fraction without adding new facts or references can be removed safely.

## Checking the overlap between sentences nine and ten

Sentences (9) and (10) express the identical ratio: <Highlight>dua belas dari delapan belas peserta angkatan kedua bertahan hingga akhir tahun</Highlight> and <Highlight>dua pertiga remaja yang mendaftar tidak berhenti di tengah jalan</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="\frac{12}{18} = \frac{2}{3}" />

## Why the other four sentences must stay

- **Sentence (9)** alone reports that **seven participants** completed a scarf.
- **Sentence (2)** alone states year one's initial assignment (floral scarf on a handloom).
- **Sentence (7)** alone states bookmarks finish in one afternoon, anchoring *dalam waktu singkat* ("in a short time") in (12).
- **Sentence (8)** alone holds meetings and teachers constant to strengthen the causal comparison.

# Answer

The sentence that can be removed without losing information is **sentence (10)** (*kalimat (10)*).
