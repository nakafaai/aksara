A good closing sentence **stays on the paragraph's idea and does not go beyond the passage**. The second paragraph opens with the idea that stingless bees help coffee farmers, then explains pollination and the heavier coffee crop. A coherent close sums this up while linking it to the honey from the first paragraph.

So the best sentence is _Jadi, lebah klanceng tidak hanya menghasilkan madu, tetapi juga membantu panen kopi warga._ (So stingless bees not only make honey but also help the villagers' coffee harvest.)

- How to drink the honey has nothing to do with the second paragraph's idea.
- _wajib_ (must) and _semua petani kopi di Indonesia_ (all coffee farmers in Indonesia) go beyond a passage about one hamlet.
- Where the bees nest belongs to the first paragraph, not the close of the second.
- The material of protective clothing is not discussed in the second paragraph.
