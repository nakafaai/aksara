A sentence can be deleted without losing information only if **another sentence already says everything in it**. So for each option, look for a part that appears only in that sentence. If there's even one, the sentence can't go.

1. Sentence (10): *Pada tahun itu, dua pertiga remaja yang mendaftar tidak berhenti di tengah jalan* ("That year, two thirds of the teenagers who signed up didn't drop out halfway"). *Tahun itu* ("that year") is the second year, and the teenagers who signed up that year are the <InlineMath math="18" /> learners of the second group. Sentence (9) already says <InlineMath math="12" /> of those <InlineMath math="18" /> stayed to the end of the year, and <InlineMath math="\tfrac{12}{18} = \tfrac{2}{3}" />. So sentence (10) says exactly what part of sentence (9) says, just as a fraction. Nothing new, and no other sentence refers back to it.

4. Sentence (7) seems to repeat the bookmark from sentence (6), but only this sentence says the bookmark is finished in one afternoon. Sentence (12) relies on that through the phrase *dalam waktu singkat* ("in a short time").
