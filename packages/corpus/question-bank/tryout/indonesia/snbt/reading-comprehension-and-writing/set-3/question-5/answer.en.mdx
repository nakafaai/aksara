# Given

Flooding in **Block C** (with infiltration wells) drained in <InlineMath math="2" /> hours, while **Block B** (without wells) still took half a day under nearly equal rainfall. Leaders conclude in sentence (11) that **infiltration wells** caused Block C's faster drainage.

# Asked

The statement that, if true, most strongly weakens the leaders' conclusion in sentence (11).

# Solution

Because **Block B** acts as a control group, any estate-wide change (dredging every block or steadier rain everywhere) hits Block B too and cannot explain why only Block C improved. The strongest weakener must occur **specifically in Block C**: doubling the width of Block C's road culvert right before the rainy season.

> Against a controlled comparison, a strong rival cause must affect the treatment group alone rather than all groups equally.

## Identifying a Block C specific alternative cause

The conclusion rests on <Highlight>genangan di jalan Blok C rata-rata surut dalam <InlineMath math="2" /> jam</Highlight> against <Highlight>Di Blok B, yang belum memiliki sumur resapan, genangan pada musim yang sama tetap baru surut setelah setengah hari</Highlight>.

Replacing **Block C's narrow entrance culvert** with one twice as wide right before the rains explains Block C's fast runoff even without infiltration wells.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Dredging drains across all blocks** also helped Block B, yet Block B stayed slow to drain.
- **More even rainfall across the whole estate** also hit Block B without speeding its drainage.
- **Denser soil in other blocks** challenges expansion in sentence (12), not Block C's cause in sentence (11).
- **Puddles still forming during heavy downpours** does not contradict *draining faster*.

# Answer

The strongest weakener is that **a few weeks before that rainy season, the narrow culvert at Block C's entrance was replaced with one twice as wide** (*Beberapa pekan sebelum musim hujan itu, gorong-gorong di mulut jalan Blok C yang selama ini menyempit diganti dengan gorong-gorong yang dua kali lebih lebar.*).
