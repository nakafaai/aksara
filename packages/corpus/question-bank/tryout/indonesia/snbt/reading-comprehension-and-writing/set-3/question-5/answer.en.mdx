**In addition**

Sentence (7) presents numerical evidence. Sentence (8) adds participant comments pointing in the same direction. The relationship is additive, so **In addition** fits.

*As a result* expresses cause and effect. *By contrast* and *Nevertheless* mark opposition, while *Before that* places events in time.
