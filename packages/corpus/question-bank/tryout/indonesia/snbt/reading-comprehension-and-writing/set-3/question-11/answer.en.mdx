This question asks you to fix two verbs in sentence (6): *menaburkan* and *dialiri*. Both carry the wrong suffix, but the two mistakes **point in opposite directions**. So you can't fix them both the same way.

In Indonesian, the suffixes *-kan* and *-i* decide what role the object plays.

- With *-kan*, the object is the thing that gets moved or spread. KBBI's example is *mengalirkan air sungai ke sawah* ("to channel river water into the rice field"). The water is what flows.
- With *-i*, the object is the place that receives the thing. KBBI's example is *menaburi peti mati dengan bunga mawar dan melati* ("to strew the coffin with roses and jasmine"). The coffin is what receives the flowers.

Step 1: check the first verb. In sentence (6), the shaved ice (*es serut*) is what gets spread, and the fish is what receives it. *Menaburkan ikan dagangannya dengan es serut* says the traders scatter their fish around, but the writer means the fish get covered with ice. Since the object *ikan dagangannya* ("their fish for sale") is the receiving place, the right form is *menaburi ikan dagangannya dengan es serut*.

Step 2: check the second verb. The subject of the second part is *air bekas pencucian ikan* ("the water left from washing fish"), and *ke saluran tertutup* ("into the closed drain") is where it goes. The water is the thing made to flow, so the form is *dialirkan*. *Dialiri* only fits when the subject is the place receiving the flow, as in *saluran tertutup dialiri air* ("the closed drain has water flowing into it").

Step 3: pick the sentence that passes both steps: *Setiap pagi, petugas pasar memeriksa apakah pedagang sudah menaburi ikan dagangannya dengan es serut dan apakah air bekas pencucian ikan sudah dialirkan ke saluran tertutup.*

Now check the other options.

- The version with *menaburi* and *dialiri* gets the first verb right, but *dialiri ke saluran tertutup* is still wrong. It uses *-i* for both verbs, when the second one needs *-kan*.
- The version with *menaburkan es serut ke atas ikan dagangannya* ("spread shaved ice over their fish") also gets the first verb right, because now the object is the ice, the thing being spread. But *dialiri ke saluran tertutup* is still wrong.
- The version with *menaburkan ikan dagangannya dengan es serut* and *dialirkan* gets the second verb right, but the fish are still the thing being scattered.
- The version with *saluran tertutup sudah dialirkan air bekas pencucian ikan* reorders the clause so the subject is now the place receiving the flow. That subject needs *dialiri*, so the mistake has only moved.

So the right fix uses *menaburi*, because the fish receive the ice, and *dialirkan*, because the wash water is made to flow into the drain.
