The word _seluas_ (as wide as) is for **the area of a surface**, as in a field of two hectares. A detour is a distance travelled, so its measure takes _sejauh_ (as far as).

The correct sentence (9): _Pedagang dari kampung itu juga dapat membawa hasil kebun ke pasar tanpa harus memutar sejauh sembilan kilometer._

So the fix is to replace _seluas_ with _sejauh_.

- _membawakan_ means bringing something for someone else, but the traders carry their own crops.
- _berputar_ means spinning around an axis, not travelling a longer way.
- _dengan_ (with) would flip the meaning, as if the bridge forced a detour.
- _di pasar_ (at the market) marks a place, but the sentence states a direction, so _ke pasar_ (to the market) is right.
