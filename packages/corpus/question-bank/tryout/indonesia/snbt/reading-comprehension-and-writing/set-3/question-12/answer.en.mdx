# Given

Sentence (11) states daily market shoppers rose from **about <InlineMath math="400" />** to **about <InlineMath math="600" />** in July.

Sentence (12) adds that **two thirds of those additional shoppers** previously bought fish from travelling vendors.

# Asked

The most valid conclusion based on sentences (11) and (12).

# Solution

Compute the **additional shoppers** first (<InlineMath math="200" />), then apply **two thirds** to <InlineMath math="200" /> (never to all <InlineMath math="600" />). That yields roughly **<InlineMath math="133" /> shoppers**, safely over one hundred daily shoppers.

> Always verify which base group a fraction applies to (*additional shoppers* rather than *total shoppers*).

## Computing the gain and its two thirds share

Sentences (11) and (12) supply both inputs: <Highlight>jumlah pembeli harian pasar sudah naik dari sekitar empat ratus menjadi sekitar enam ratus orang</Highlight> and <Highlight>dua pertiga dari pembeli tambahan itu adalah warga yang sebelumnya membeli ikan dari pedagang keliling</Highlight>.

<BlockMath math="600 - 400 = 200, \qquad \frac{2}{3} \times 200 \approx 133 > 100" />

## Why the other four options fail

- **Two thirds of all daily shoppers** applies the fraction to <InlineMath math="600" /> instead of the <InlineMath math="200" /> gain.
- **Travelling vendors lost about two hundred shoppers** counts 100% of the gain instead of two thirds.
- **One third never bought fish before** overreaches, since they could have shopped at supermarkets.
- **Rise caused by travelling vendors moving in** reverses cause and effect from sentence (13).

# Answer

The best conclusion is that **in July, over a hundred daily market shoppers were residents who previously bought fish from travelling vendors** (*Pada bulan Juli, lebih dari seratus pembeli harian pasar adalah warga yang sebelumnya membeli ikan dari pedagang keliling.*).
