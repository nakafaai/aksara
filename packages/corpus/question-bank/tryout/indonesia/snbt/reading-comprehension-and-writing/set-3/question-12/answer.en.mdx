The second part of sentence (6) is passive: _kabel baja disediakan ..._ (the steel cable was supplied ...). In an Indonesian passive, **the doer is introduced with _oleh_ (by)**, not _dari_ (from). _dari_ marks a place or material of origin, so it does not fit a doer.

The correct sentence (6): _Warga menyumbang bambu, kayu, dan tenaga, sedangkan kabel baja disediakan oleh pemerintah kabupaten._

So the fix is to replace _dari_ with _oleh_.

- _sedangkan_ (whereas) is right for setting the villagers' gift against the government's. _sehingga_ (so that) would add a cause and effect that is not there.
- _tenaga_ (labour) is right for help with work. _kekuatan_ (strength) is not used for a donation.
- _menyediakan_ (supplies) would break the passive, since the subject _kabel baja_ (steel cable) cannot supply anything.
- _menyumbang_ (donate) is right because the subject _Warga_ (villagers) is the one giving.
