The sentence to insert has two parts that point backwards, and **both have to fit at the same spot**.

- *Perbedaan itu* ("that difference") needs a difference stated right before it.
- The reason *karena kedua blok hanya dipisahkan oleh satu jalan* ("because the two blocks are separated by only one road") needs two blocks being compared. This reason only makes sense if the difference is a difference between those two blocks, because two neighbouring blocks get the same rain.

So you're looking for a sentence that states a difference between two blocks. Sentence (10) does: in the same season, floodwater in Blok B still took half a day to drain, while sentence (8) says it drained in <InlineMath math="2" /> hours in Blok C. After sentence (10), *perbedaan itu* means the gap between Blok B and Blok C, and *kedua blok* means Blok B and Blok C. Sentence (11) also follows well: since rain can't explain the difference, the board concludes the infiltration wells are the cause.

Now check the other spots. Each one meets only one condition.

- After sentence (1): *kedua blok* fits because Blok B and Blok C have been named, but that sentence says both blocks flood alike. There's no difference yet.
- After sentence (3): *kedua blok itu* ("both blocks") has just been mentioned too, but sentence (3) gives the same cause for both. The contrast between sentences (1) and (2) is a difference between the rainy and the dry season, and a seasonal difference is exactly what rain does explain.
- After sentence (8): *perbedaan itu* could refer to the gap before and after the wells were built in Blok C. But that's a difference within one block, so the reason about two neighbouring blocks doesn't connect.
- After sentence (9): this spot is the most tempting because sentence (9) is about rain. But *perbedaan itu* can still only mean the before-and-after gap in Blok C, and Blok B hasn't been compared yet.

So the sentence fits best after sentence (10), the only spot that gives a difference between two blocks and also names both of them.
