# Given

The sentence to insert reads: *"Perbedaan itu sulit dijelaskan oleh hujan karena kedua blok hanya dipisahkan oleh satu jalan."* ("That difference is hard to explain by rainfall because the two blocks are separated only by a single street.")

Sentence (8) reports **Block C** drained in <InlineMath math="2" /> hours, sentence (10) reports **Block B** still took half a day, and sentence (11) states the committee's conclusion.

# Asked

Which sentence the inserted sentence should immediately follow.

# Solution

Find the spot that supplies **both a difference between two blocks and the two blocks themselves**. Only after **sentence (10)** has the contrast between Block C (<InlineMath math="2" /> hours) and Block B (half a day) been stated, right before sentence (11) draws the conclusion.

> An inserted sentence comparing two places only fits once the second place has been brought into the comparison.

## Matching both pointers after sentence ten

Sentence (10) completes the side-by-side comparison: <Highlight>Di Blok B, yang belum memiliki sumur resapan, genangan pada musim yang sama tetap baru surut setelah setengah hari</Highlight>.

Right after sentence (10), *perbedaan itu* ("that difference") points to Block C versus Block B and *kedua blok* ("the two blocks") points to Blocks B and C, feeding straight into sentence (11).

## Why the other four spots fail

- **After sentence (1)**: Both blocks flood alike, so no difference exists yet.
- **After sentence (3)**: Sentences (1) and (2) contrast rainy and dry seasons, which rain *does* explain.
- **After sentence (8)**: Compares Block C before and after (only one block), not two neighbouring blocks.
- **After sentence (9)**: Mentions rain, yet still compares only Block C across years without Block B.

# Answer

The sentence fits best right after **sentence (10)** (*kalimat (10)*).
