# Given

Sentence (7) states **return buses depart at 14:00, whereas extracurriculars only finish at 16:00**.

Sentence (8) begins with *Namun* ("However") and adds **students joining those activities must take an ojek home again**.

# Asked

The inter-sentence connector that best replaces *Namun* at the start of sentence (8).

# Solution

Check the direct link **from sentence (7) to sentence (8)**: because buses left two hours before activities end, needing an ojek is a **direct consequence**, never a contrast. Replace *Namun* with ***Akibatnya***, ("As a result,").

> Choose sentence connectors from the direct logical tie between two adjacent sentences, not from the paragraph's overall negative mood.

## Checking the cause and effect link in sentences seven and eight

Sentences (7) and (8) flow straight from cause to effect: <Highlight>Bus pulang berangkat dari sekolah pukul 14.00, sedangkan kegiatan ekstrakurikuler di sekolah-sekolah itu baru selesai pukul 16.00. (8) Namun, siswa yang mengikuti kegiatan itu harus kembali naik ojek untuk pulang</Highlight>.

Taking an ojek follows directly from missing the 14:00 bus, requiring ***Akibatnya***, ("As a result,").

## Why the other four connectors fail

- ***Sebaliknya***, ("On the contrary,") signals reversal rather than consequence.
- ***Meskipun demikian***, ("Even so,") marks concession, repeating the exact flaw of *Namun*.
- ***Selain itu***, ("Besides,") adds an unrelated point instead of an effect.
- ***Dengan kata lain***, ("In other words,") restates prior wording, yet sentence (8) adds a new consequence.

# Answer

The best replacement connector is ***Akibatnya***.
