First name the fault in each of the two sentences being fixed, then judge every proposal with two questions: does the fault go away without creating a new one, and does the meaning still match the passage?

- Sentence (3) reads *Setelah ditenun selama berminggu-minggu, barulah peserta dapat melihat selendang buatannya utuh* ("After being woven for weeks, only then can the learners see their scarf whole"). The clause *setelah ditenun* ("after being woven") leaves its subject out. In a well-formed Indonesian sentence, the subject left out of a clause has to be **the same as the subject of the main clause**. The main subject is *peserta* ("the learners"), so the sentence says the learners were woven.
- Sentence (6) reads *Peserta baru kini memulai dengan menenun pembatas buku, lalu pembuatan taplak kecil, dan baru menenun selendang pada bulan keenam* ("New learners now start with weaving a bookmark, then the making of a small tablecloth, and only weave a scarf in the sixth month"). The three learning steps are equal parts of one series, but they aren't parallel: two are verbs (*menenun*, "weave") and one is a noun (*pembuatan*, "making"). Equal parts of a series need the same form.

Now judge the proposals one by one.

1. The first proposal, *Setelah ditenun ..., barulah selendang buatan peserta terlihat utuh* ("After being woven ..., only then does the learners' scarf look whole"): Tepat (correct). *Ditenun* ("being woven") stays, but the main subject is now *selendang buatan peserta* ("the learners' scarf"). The scarf is what gets woven, so the missing subject matches. The meaning stays: the scarf only looks whole after weeks.
2. The second proposal, *Setelah menenun ..., barulah selendang buatan peserta terlihat utuh* ("After weaving ..., only then does the learners' scarf look whole"): Tidak tepat (not correct). It looks almost the same as the first, but *ditenun* became *menenun* ("weaving"). The main subject is the scarf, so the sentence says the scarf did the weaving. The fault has only moved.
3. The third proposal, *..., lalu menenun taplak kecil, sehingga dapat menenun selendang pada bulan keenam* ("..., then weaving a small tablecloth, so that they can weave a scarf in the sixth month"): Tidak tepat (not correct), even though the forms are now parallel. *Sehingga* ("so that, as a result") adds a cause-and-effect claim: the small pieces make learners able to weave a scarf. The passage never says that, and it clashes with sentence (11), which says small pieces **don't yet train learners to read complex patterns**. The word *baru* ("only then"), which shows the scarf is held back until the sixth month, is lost too.
4. The fourth proposal, *..., lalu menenun taplak kecil, dan baru menenun selendang pada bulan keenam* ("..., then weaving a small tablecloth, and only weaving a scarf in the sixth month"): Tepat (correct). All three steps now share the form *menenun ...*, and the original meaning is intact.

So the first and fourth proposals are correct, while the second and third are not.
