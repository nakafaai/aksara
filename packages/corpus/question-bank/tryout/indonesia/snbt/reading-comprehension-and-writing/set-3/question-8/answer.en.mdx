Look at how the two parts of sentence (4) relate. The first part says keepers need not give extra feed. The second says the bees find nectar themselves. The second part is **the reason for the first**, so the right connector expresses cause.

The full sentence (4): _Peternak juga tidak perlu memberi pakan tambahan karena lebah mencari nektar sendiri dari bunga di sekitar dusun._

So the right connector is _karena_ (because).

- _sehingga_ (so that) reverses the link, as if the bees found nectar because they were not fed.
- _agar_ (in order that) expresses a goal, but the keepers have no goal of making the bees search.
- _meskipun_ (although) expresses contrast, but the two parts support each other.
- _bahkan_ (even) adds emphasis, but the second part gives a reason.
