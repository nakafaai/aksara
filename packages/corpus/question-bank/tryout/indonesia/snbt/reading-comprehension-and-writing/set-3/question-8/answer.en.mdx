# Given

The weaving passage contains two syntax flaws: sentence (3) (*Setelah ditenun ..., barulah peserta...*) makes the main subject **peserta** ("participant") seem like what was woven, and sentence (6) mixes the verb *menenun* ("weaving") with the noun *pembuatan* ("the making of").

# Asked

Whether each of the four proposed revisions for sentences (3) and (6) is **Tepat** ("Correct") or **Tidak tepat** ("Incorrect").

# Solution

Match the implied subject in (3) (**the scarf is woven**, neither the participant woven nor the scarf weaving) and make all three steps parallel verbs in (6) without inserting a false causal *sehingga* ("so that") that contradicts sentence (11).

> An omitted subject in a front clause must match the main clause's subject, and parallel steps must share the same grammatical form.

## Evaluating all four edits for sentences three and six

The originals read <Highlight>Setelah ditenun selama berminggu-minggu, barulah peserta dapat melihat selendang buatannya utuh.</Highlight> and <Highlight>menenun pembatas buku, lalu pembuatan taplak kecil, dan baru menenun selendang</Highlight>.

| Proposed edit | Verdict | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Setelah ditenun ..., barulah selendang buatan peserta terlihat utuh.* | **Tepat** | Passive *ditenun* ("woven") now matches subject *selendang* |
| *Setelah menenun ..., barulah selendang buatan peserta terlihat utuh.* | **Tidak tepat** | Active *menenun* makes the scarf do the weaving |
| *..., lalu menenun taplak kecil, sehingga dapat menenun selendang...* | **Tidak tepat** | *Sehingga* adds a causal claim contradicted by sentence (11) |
| *..., lalu menenun taplak kecil, dan baru menenun selendang...* | **Tepat** | All three actions are parallel verbs and preserve *baru* ("only then") |

# Answer

Proposals **one and four** are **Tepat**, while proposals **two and three** are **Tidak tepat**.
