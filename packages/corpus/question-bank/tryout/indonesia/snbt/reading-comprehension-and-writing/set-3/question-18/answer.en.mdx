# Given

Paragraph two holds two errors: in sentence (4), ***yang berangkat dari terminal kecamatan pukul 06.00*** ("leaving the subdistrict terminal at 06:00") sits behind *tiga puluh halte* ("thirty stops"), and in sentence (5), ***mengindahkan*** (meaning "to heed/obey" in KBBI) reprimands drivers who *obey* the schedule.

# Asked

Whether each of the four proposed edits for sentences (4) and (5) is **Tepat** ("Correct") or **Tidak tepat** ("Incorrect").

# Solution

Place *yang berangkat...* directly after **delapan bus** ("eight buses", not *siswa*), and replace *mengindahkan* with ***mengabaikan*** ("ignore"), since *menghiraukan* also means "to heed" in KBBI.

> Remember KBBI's trap pair: both *mengindahkan* and *menghiraukan* mean "pay attention to", while *mengabaikan* means "ignore".

## Evaluating all four edits in paragraph two

The originals read <Highlight>delapan bus menjemput siswa di tiga puluh halte yang berangkat dari terminal kecamatan pukul 06.00</Highlight> and <Highlight>Sopir yang mengindahkan jadwal itu akan ditegur</Highlight>.

| Proposed edit | Verdict | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replace *mengindahkan* with *menghiraukan* in (5) | **Tidak tepat** | KBBI defines *menghiraukan* as heeding, keeping the error |
| Place *yang berangkat...* right after *delapan bus* in (4) | **Tepat** | Correctly states the eight buses leave the terminal at 06:00 |
| Replace *mengindahkan* with *mengabaikan* in (5) | **Tepat** | Reprimands drivers who ignore the schedule |
| Place *yang berangkat...* right after *siswa* in (4) | **Tidak tepat** | Falsely says students depart from the terminal instead of bus stops |

# Answer

Proposals **two and three** are **Tepat**, while proposals **one and four** are **Tidak tepat**.
