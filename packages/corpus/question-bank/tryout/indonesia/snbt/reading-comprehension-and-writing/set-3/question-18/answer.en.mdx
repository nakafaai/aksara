Sentence (7) uses _ijin_. According to KBBI, **the standard form is _izin_** (permission), with a _z_. The form _ijin_ comes from everyday pronunciation.

The correct sentence (7): _Pengurus akan meminta izin kepada kelurahan untuk menambah satu pos ronda di ujung gang._

So the nonstandard word is in sentence (7).

- Sentence (1) uses _mencatat_ (record), _ronda_ (night watch) and _laporan_ (report), all standard.
- Sentence (2) uses _grup_ (group), which is the standard form in KBBI.
- Sentence (3) uses _diganti_ (replaced) and _terang_ (bright), both standard.
- Sentence (6) uses _meragukan_ (doubt) and _bersamaan_ (at the same time), both standard.
