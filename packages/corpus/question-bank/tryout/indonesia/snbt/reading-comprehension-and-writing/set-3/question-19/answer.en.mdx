# Given

Paragraph three chains four sentences: (7) **return buses leave at 14:00** while **extracurriculars end at 16:00**, (8) participants must **pay for an ojek home**, (9) **parents who cannot afford it forbid their children from joining**, and (10) extracurriculars are now **filled mostly by wealthier students**.

# Asked

The main idea of the third paragraph.

# Solution

Join the opening cause and closing outcome across sentences (7) through (10). The trigger is **buses departing before extracurriculars finish**, and the outcome is **students from lower-income families struggling to join those activities**.

> In a cause-and-effect chain paragraph, the main idea links the root cause to the final consequence instead of stopping halfway.

## Linking the opening cause and final result

Paragraph three begins with <Highlight>Bus pulang berangkat dari sekolah pukul 14.00, sedangkan kegiatan ekstrakurikuler di sekolah-sekolah itu baru selesai pukul 16.00.</Highlight> and culminates in <Highlight>Sebagian orang tua yang tidak sanggup membayar ongkosnya akhirnya melarang anak mereka ikut kegiatan ekstrakurikuler.</Highlight>

Combining both ends yields the exact main idea.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Forcing participants back onto ojeks** stops midway at sentence (8) and omits the exclusion of poorer students.
- **Losing interest** invents a motive, as sentence (9) blames unaffordable ojek fares, not low interest.
- **Failing to ease family costs** overgeneralizes, since non-extracurricular riders still save on morning and afternoon fares.
- **Mostly attended by wealthier students** copies sentence (10) alone without its cause.

# Answer

The main idea is that **buses departing earlier than extracurriculars end makes it hard for students from lower-income families to join those activities** (*Jadwal pulang bus yang lebih awal daripada akhir kegiatan ekstrakurikuler membuat siswa dari keluarga kurang mampu sulit mengikuti kegiatan itu.*).
