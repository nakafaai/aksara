A main idea is what **every sentence in the paragraph** holds together, not the content of just one sentence. The third paragraph runs from sentence (7) to sentence (10). Its first sentence is only a schedule detail, so you need to connect all four.

Step 1: follow the chain of cause and effect.

- Sentence (7): the homebound bus leaves at 14.00, but after-school activities (*kegiatan ekstrakurikuler*) don't finish until 16.00.
- Sentence (8): students in those activities have to take a motorbike taxi (*ojek*) home again.
- Sentence (9): some parents who can't afford the ojek forbid their children to take part.
- Sentence (10): the activities are now mostly attended by students from better-off families.

Step 2: join the start and the end of the chain. It starts with a homebound bus that leaves too early. It ends with students from low-income families being pushed out of after-school activities. So the main idea is that the bus's early return time, before after-school activities end, makes it hard for students from low-income families to take part.

Now check the other options.

- The bus schedule forcing participants back onto ojeks only sums up sentences (7) and (8). It leaves out where the paragraph is heading: who ends up being pushed out.

- Activities being mostly attended by better-off students is only sentence (10). It names the effect without its cause.
