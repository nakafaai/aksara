The word _menekan_ has several meanings, so **the word next to it decides the sense**. In sentence (3), what is pressed is _biaya pakaian pelindung_ (the cost of protective clothing). Since the bees do not sting, keepers need to buy little protective gear, so that cost gets smaller. So _menekan biaya_ means to reduce the cost.

So _menekan_ in sentence (3) means to reduce.

- _menindih_ (press down on) is the physical sense, as in pressing a button, and does not fit a cost.
- _memaksa_ (force) is used for people, as in pressing someone to confess.
- _menaikkan_ (raise) is the opposite of what the sentence says.
- _mendesak_ (urge) is also used for people or situations, not costs.
