Each option gives a word and a reason. The right one has to pass **three checks at once**: its meaning fits sentence (1), its meaning fits sentence (14), and its affix fits the way sentence (13) is built.

1. Sentence (1) says the studio *tidak memungut biaya apa pun dari pesertanya* ("charges its learners no fee of any kind"). According to KBBI (the official Indonesian dictionary), *menyewakan* ("to rent out") means handing something over for use in exchange for rent. So the word in the passage contradicts sentence (1).
2. Sentence (14) says the looms are *dikembalikan setiap akhir bulan* ("returned at the end of every month"). So they're only used for a while and still belong to the studio.
3. The meaning you need is: hand something over for temporary use, free of charge. That's the meaning of the root *pinjam* ("borrow, lend").
4. Sentence (13) is built as *sanggar ... alat tenun kecil kepada peserta* ("the studio ... small looms to the learners"). The object is the thing, and the receiver is marked with *kepada* ("to"). According to KBBI, *meminjamkan* means giving something as a loan, with the thing as its object. That form fits.

Now check the other options.

- *Menyewakan* needs no change: the reason is wrong. Sentence (1) doesn't only waive the class fee. It says the studio charges **no fee of any kind to its learners**, and rent for a loom is a fee paid by a learner.
- *Meminjam* ("to borrow"): sentence (14) does show the looms come back, but the direction is reversed. *Meminjam alat tenun kecil kepada peserta* means the studio borrows looms from the learners.
- *Menghibahkan* ("to give away for good"): sentence (1) does say there's no fee, but *menghibahkan* means handing something over to keep, while sentence (14) says the looms are returned.
- *Meminjami*: its reason is exactly the same as the right option's, and its meaning is right too. The problem is the affix. KBBI defines *meminjami* as lending to someone, so its object is the person, as in *meminjami peserta alat tenun* ("lend the learners looms"). In sentence (13) the object is the thing and the receiver comes after *kepada*, so the *-kan* form is the one that fits.

So the correct statement is that *meminjamkan* ("to lend") should replace *menyewakan*, because sentences (1) and (14) show the looms are used for a while without payment.
