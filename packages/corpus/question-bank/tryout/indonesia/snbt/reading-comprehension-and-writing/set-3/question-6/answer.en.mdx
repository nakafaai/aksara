# Given

Sentence (1) states the weaving workshop **charges no fee of any kind to its participants**.

Sentence (13) writes *"sanggar juga menyewakan alat tenun kecil kepada peserta"* ("also rents out small looms to participants"), while sentence (14) says **the looms are returned at the end of every month**.

# Asked

Which replacement word and reason correctly fix *menyewakan* in sentence (13).

# Solution

Three clues lock the answer: **zero cost** (1), **temporary use with monthly return** (14), and a **thing as direct object** (*alat tenun kecil*) followed by *kepada peserta*. Only ***meminjamkan*** ("lend [an object] to [someone]") passes all three checks.

> Keep Indonesian *pinjam* suffixes straight: *meminjamkan [object] kepada [person]* takes the item as object, whereas *meminjami [person] [object]* takes the recipient as object.

## Checking meaning and suffix fit in sentence thirteen

Sentences (1) and (14) set both conditions: <Highlight>tidak memungut biaya apa pun dari pesertanya</Highlight> ("charges no fee of any kind") and <Highlight>Alat itu dikembalikan setiap akhir bulan</Highlight> ("returned every month-end").

Free plus temporary rules out *menyewakan* ("rent out"), and object + *kepada* requires ***meminjamkan***.

## Why the other four options fail

- **Keeping *menyewakan*** ignores *biaya apa pun* ("no fee of any kind") in sentence (1).
- ***Meminjam*** ("borrow") flips direction, making the workshop borrow looms from students.
- ***Menghibahkan*** ("donate permanently") clashes with returning the loom each month in (14).
- ***Meminjami*** has the right meaning and reason, yet grammatically requires a person as direct object rather than *alat tenun kecil kepada peserta*.

# Answer

The accurate statement is that ***meminjamkan* is right because sentences (1) and (14) show the tool is used temporarily without payment** (*Meminjamkan tepat karena kalimat (1) dan (14) menunjukkan alat itu dipakai sementara tanpa bayaran.*).
