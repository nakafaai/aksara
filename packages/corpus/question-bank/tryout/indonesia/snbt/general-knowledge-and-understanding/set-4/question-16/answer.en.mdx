*Toponymy* concerns **place names, their origins, and their contexts of use**. In the passage, names reveal who used the place, approaches to it, and remembered events.

Drawing boundaries and measuring distances have different purposes. A history-free list or a single enforced name also omits the usage and historical information the team investigates.
