*Representative* means **adequately reflecting the groups the claim is intended to describe**. An average dominated by young shoppers with few items need not describe larger purchases or people without suitable devices.

A large sample or easy recruitment does not guarantee coverage. Equal allocation must also be considered against the population's composition rather than assumed representative by itself.
