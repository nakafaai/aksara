# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **Minangkabau** passage contains four exact elements:

> Harta pusaka tinggi, seperti sawah, ladang, dan rumah gadang, memang diturunkan dari ibu kepada anak perempuannya, tetapi <Highlight>harta itu adalah milik bersama kaum dan tidak boleh dijual sesuka hati</Highlight>.

The four elements are the scope (*harta pusaka tinggi*), inheritance path (mother to daughter), owner (communal clan), and restriction (cannot be sold arbitrarily).

# Asked

The sentence that best paraphrases sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) without altering its meaning.

# Solution

The concessive pair *memang ... tetapi* (indeed ... yet) maps cleanly onto **although ... still** (*walaupun ... tetap*). Only the fifth option keeps all four elements intact and replaces *sesuka hati* with its exact synonym *semaunya* (at will).

## How Each Distractor Distorts One Element

Every other choice breaks **one core detail** of sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

- Calling it *the daughter's own property* shifts ownership away from the clan.
- Saying it *cannot be sold at all* turns *not arbitrarily* into an absolute ban.
- Saying the daughter *may sell it* reverses the restriction.
- Expanding the subject to *all Minangkabau family property* ignores *harta pencaharian* in sentence (<InlineMath math="15" />).

# Answer

**Although passed down from mother to daughter, ancestral property such as rice fields, dry fields, and the *rumah gadang* remains communal clan property that cannot be sold at will**.

(*Walaupun diturunkan dari ibu kepada anak perempuannya, harta pusaka tinggi seperti sawah, ladang, dan rumah gadang tetap merupakan milik bersama kaum yang tidak dapat dijual semaunya.*)
