# Given

**From sentences (4)** to (7), *sisa tumbuhan* (plant remains) accumulate over time to form *gambut* (peat).

# Asked

The word pair with the closest relationship to *sisa tumbuhan* and *gambut*.

# Solution

> Check the direction of the natural process: **fine material piling up over time to build a larger deposit**, never a large rock breaking down into tiny pieces.

In an analogy question, first put the relation of the original pair into one full sentence. The more detailed that sentence, the fewer pairs get through.

By sentence (4), plant remains pile up layer on layer. By sentence (7), a peat layer several metres thick takes thousands of years to form. So the relation between _sisa tumbuhan_ ("plant remains") and _gambut_ ("peat") has two features.

1. The first is a **material**, and the second is a **formation built from that material**.
2. The formation arises because the material **piles up little by little, naturally, over a very long time**.

Now test each pair against both features.

| Pair | Material becomes formation? | Piles up slowly and naturally? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| _kayu : arang_ (wood : charcoal) | Yes | No, the wood is burned and changes fast |
| _benang : kain_ (thread : cloth) | Yes | No, people weave the thread |
| _batu : kerikil_ (rock : gravel) | No, the rock breaks into small pieces | No, this is erosion |
| _lumpur : delta_ (mud : delta) | Yes | Yes, river mud settles at the mouth for years |
| _air : es_ (water : ice) | No, only a change of state | No |

Only _lumpur : delta_ has both features at once. A delta is land at a river mouth formed from mud that keeps settling, just as peat forms from plant remains that keep piling up.

The two most tempting pairs are _kayu : arang_ and _batu : kerikil_. Wood and charcoal both come from plants, but similar objects don't matter. What's compared is the relation, and charcoal is made by burning. Rock turning into gravel is indeed a long natural process, but it runs the other way: a big thing breaks down into small ones, not small material piling up into a big formation.

So the most similar relation is _lumpur : delta_.

# Answer

**The most similar relationship is** *lumpur : delta*.
