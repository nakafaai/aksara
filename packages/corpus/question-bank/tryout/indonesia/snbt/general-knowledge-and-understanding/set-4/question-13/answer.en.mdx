The word *andal* means **reliable**, something you can trust. Sentence (5) compares two ways of checking news: judging a site's appearance, which sentences (3) and (4) show is easily fooled, and lateral reading, called **lebih andal** (more reliable). So lateral reading gives more trustworthy results.

The other options do not fit:

- *Mudah* (easy) describes difficulty, not trust in the result.
- *Cepat* (fast) is mentioned in sentence (6) as a feature of lateral reading, but that is separate information, not the meaning of *andal*.
- *Mahal* (expensive) has nothing to do with checking news.
- *Sulit dimengerti* (hard to understand) contradicts sentence (9), which calls the habit simple.

So the synonym of *andal* in sentence (5) is *dapat dipercaya* (trustworthy).
