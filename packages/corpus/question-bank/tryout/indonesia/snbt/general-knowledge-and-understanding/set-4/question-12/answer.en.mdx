# Given

Paragraph <InlineMath math="3" /> of the **Minangkabau** passage opens with sentence (<InlineMath math="14" />): *Sistem ini juga cukup luwes* (This system is also quite flexible).

Sentences (<InlineMath math="16" />) through (<InlineMath math="18" />) explain that self-earned property now follows Islamic inheritance law, yet this change does not shift the foundation of the system.

# Asked

The meaning of the word *luwes* in sentence (<InlineMath math="14" />).

# Solution

Applied to a kinship system, sentences (<InlineMath math="16" />) to (<InlineMath math="18" />) define *luwes* through **two traits at once**: accommodating change while preserving core rules. Thus it means **able to adapt without abandoning its foundation**.


> Meski begitu, <Highlight>perubahan ini tidak menggeser dasar sistemnya</Highlight>: anak tetap masuk ke suku ibunya, dan harta pusaka tinggi kaum seorang ayah tetap tidak beralih kepada anak-anaknya.
## Checking the Other Four Options

The remaining options either describe **personal mannerisms** or contradict sentence (<InlineMath math="18" />).

- Graceful movement and being sociable describe people rather than a social system.
- Being loose enough to break rules fails because the change follows Islamic inheritance law rather than rule-breaking.
- Changing down to its foundation directly contradicts sentence (<InlineMath math="18" />).

# Answer

The word *luwes* in sentence (<InlineMath math="14" />) means **able to adapt without abandoning its foundation**.

(*dapat menyesuaikan diri tanpa meninggalkan dasarnya*)
