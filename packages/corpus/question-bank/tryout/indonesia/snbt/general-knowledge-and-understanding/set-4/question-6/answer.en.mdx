The expression *berpindah tangan* (literally "to move hands") is not literal. It means **to change owners**, from one person to another, usually through trade.

Sentence (3) says traders from many nations carried the spices through a chain of ports, and the price rose every time they *berpindah tangan*. Each trader bought the spices and sold them on at a higher price. So the price kept rising every time the spices **changed owners**.

The other options do not fit:

- *Moved by hand* is the literal meaning. Physically moving goods does not explain why the price rises.
- *Stored for a long time in a warehouse* is not mentioned.
- *Stolen by pirates* is not mentioned and does not fit a price rise at every port.
- *Weighed again* does not raise the price and is not mentioned.

So *berpindah tangan* means passing from one owner or trader to another.
