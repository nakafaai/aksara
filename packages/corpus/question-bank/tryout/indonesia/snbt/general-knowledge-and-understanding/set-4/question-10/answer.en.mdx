# Given

**The word *membaca*** in sentence (10) means interpreting signals or cues rather than reading printed text or meter digits.

# Asked

The sentence that uses *membaca* with a different meaning from sentence (10).

# Solution

> Separate **interpreting cues or patterns** from **reading printed digits straight off a dial**: an officer reading numbers off an electricity meter uses the literal reading sense.

First settle what _membaca_ ("to read") means in sentence (10). There the brain _membaca jarak suatu benda dari petunjuk di sekitarnya_ ("reads an object's distance from the clues around it"). The brain isn't looking at any writing. It's **working out something it can't see directly from signs it can see**. The pattern is always the same: there's a sign, and something else is inferred from that sign.

Now test each sentence against that pattern. Ask: what's the sign, and what's inferred?

| Sentence about | Sign | What's inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The fisher | movement of the clouds | wind direction |
| The doctor | the way the patient walks | the patient's tiredness |
| The farmer | a constellation appearing | the coming of the rainy season |
| The chess player | three opening moves | the opponent's plan |

Those four sentences follow the pattern of sentence (10). The word _dari_ ("from") in each of them points to the sign.

The sentence about the meter reader is different. The number on an electricity meter is writing that's really printed there, and the officer just looks at it and notes it down. Nothing else is inferred from a sign. Here _membaca_ has its literal meaning: looking at writing and understanding it.

This sentence is tempting because a meter is a measuring device, so it feels close to a brain measuring distance. But what's being tested is the meaning of _membaca_, not how similar the objects are.

So the different meaning is in the sentence about the officer reading the number on the electricity meter.

# Answer

The different meaning appears in the sentence about the officer reading the number on the electricity meter.
