# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="8" />) and (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **fish salting** passage state:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): in this way, salt *meredam* (dampens) the activity of spoilage bacteria.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): salt kills some bacteria, yet many remain alive and some still grow, though much more slowly.

# Asked

The word that can replace *meredam* in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) without changing the passage's meaning.

# Solution

*Meredam* means **to weaken or curb activity without eliminating it completely**. Because sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) confirms that bacteria survive and continue growing slowly, **suppress** (*menekan*) preserves the exact meaning.


> Choose a replacement word matching what salt actually does to bacteria by curbing growth without wiping them all out.
## Checking the Other Four Words

Each distractor alters **how completely salt affects bacteria**.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *menghentikan* (stop) | Wrong, implies activity ends completely |
| *memusnahkan* (eradicate) | Wrong, many bacteria survive |
| *menekan* (suppress) | Correct, curbs growth without total removal |
| *menunda* (delay) | Wrong, merely postpones activity rather than weakening it |
| *mengalihkan* (divert) | Wrong, implies redirecting activity elsewhere |

# Answer

The word *meredam* in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) can be replaced with **suppress** (*menekan*).
