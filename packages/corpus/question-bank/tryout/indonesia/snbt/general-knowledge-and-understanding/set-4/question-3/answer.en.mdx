The replacement has to fit two things: the meaning of _meredam_ ("to dampen") and **what really happens to the bacteria in the passage**. Sentence (8) alone can't settle it, so read the sentence after it too.

_Meredam_ means making something weaker or quieter, the way you muffle a sound. Something that's been dampened is still there, it's just less strong.

Sentence (9) says the same about the bacteria: salt does kill some of them, but many others stay alive, and some still grow, though much more slowly. So the bacteria's activity **drops, it doesn't disappear**. The word that fits is _menekan_ ("to suppress"), which means holding something back so it can't develop freely.

The other four words change what the passage means.

- _Menghentikan_ ("to stop") means the activity ends completely. Sentence (9) says some bacteria still grow.
- _Memusnahkan_ ("to wipe out") means the bacteria are gone. Sentences (9) and (10) say many survive and can multiply fast again.
- _Menunda_ ("to postpone") means the activity is only moved to a later time at the same strength. The passage doesn't say that. The activity goes on, but weaker.
- _Mengalihkan_ ("to divert") means moving the activity to another place or target. No sentence supports that.

So _meredam_ in sentence (8) can be replaced with _menekan_.
