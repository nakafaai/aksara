Sentence (8) says the value of spices began to *merosot* when seedlings were planted outside Maluku. The spices were no longer scarce, so their value **fell**. The antonym of a word meaning to fall sharply is a word meaning **to rise sharply**, which is *melonjak* (to soar).

The other options are wrong because they point the same way as *merosot*:

- *Menurun* (to decline) and *berkurang* (to decrease) both mean becoming lower or less.
- *Jatuh* (to fall) and *anjlok* (to plunge) also mean going down, even sharply.

So the antonym of *merosot* in sentence (8) is *melonjak*.
