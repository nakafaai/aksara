# Given

Paragraphs <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="3" /> of the **fish salting** passage have a clear structural shift:

- Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="4" /> to <InlineMath math="8" />): explains how salt cuts free water and suppresses spoilage bacteria.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="3" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="9" /> to <InlineMath math="12" />): opens with *memang ... tetapi* (indeed ... yet), showing that many bacteria survive, revive in moisture, and some microbes tolerate high salt.

# Asked

The relationship of paragraph <InlineMath math="3" /> to paragraph <InlineMath math="2" />.

# Solution

Paragraph <InlineMath math="3" /> does not refute paragraph <InlineMath math="2" />. Instead, it **qualifies or limits its conclusion**: salt works, yet it does not eliminate all microbes.


> Garam memang dapat membunuh sebagian bakteri, tetapi <Highlight>banyak bakteri lain tetap hidup</Highlight>, dan sebagian di antaranya masih tumbuh walaupun jauh lebih lambat.
## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor mislabels the **qualifying role** of paragraph <InlineMath math="3" />.

- It presents limits of salting rather than examples of how salt works.
- It never disputes lower free water, and sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />) relies on that principle when moisture returns.
- Subsequent processing steps such as drying appear in paragraph <InlineMath math="4" />, not paragraph <InlineMath math="3" />.
- It does not list prerequisites needed for salting to happen.

# Answer

**Paragraph <InlineMath math="3" /> qualifies the conclusion of paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> by showing that salt does not eliminate all microbes**.

(*Paragraf ketiga membatasi simpulan paragraf kedua dengan menunjukkan bahwa garam tidak menyingkirkan semua mikroba.*)
