The word *semai* means **seed or seedling**, as in *menyemai padi*, sowing rice seed so it grows. In sentence (3), the seeding material (*bahan semai*) is named that way because it acts like a seed for raindrops. Sentence (4) explains that water drops start forming and growing around those salt grains.

The other options do not fit:

- *Garam* (salt) is an **example** of a seeding material, not the meaning of *semai*. Sentence (3) calls salt the material "commonly used" in Indonesia, so other materials can be used.
- *Pupuk* (fertilizer) feeds plants, which does not match the material's role in a cloud.
- *Debu* (dust) is not mentioned and is not the meaning of *semai*.
- *Obat* (medicine) has nothing to do with *semai*.

So *semai* is closest in meaning to *benih* (seed).
