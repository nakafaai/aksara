The phrase is defined directly in sentence (5) after the word *yaitu* (namely): opening a new tab and finding out **what other sources say** about the claim or the site. *Lateral* means sideways. The reader does not only move down one page but also **sideways** to other sources.

The other options do not fit:

- Skimming from top to bottom still stays on one page without checking other sources.
- Reading only the headline is the habit sentence (2) blames for fake news spreading.
- Reading the same article repeatedly on one site adds no outside source.
- Reader comments sit on the same page and are not named as a way of checking.

So *membaca secara lateral* means checking what other sources say about the claim or site being read.
