# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) to (<InlineMath math="4" />) and sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **wetlands** passage trace stored carbon:

- Sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) to (<InlineMath math="4" />): waterlogging blocks oxygen, slowing decay so carbon becomes *terkunci* (locked) inside stacked plant remains.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): as soon as water is drained via canals, oxygen enters, microbes grow active, and carbon escapes into the air as carbon dioxide.

# Asked

The figurative meaning of *terkunci* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) that fits the passage.

# Solution

Carbon is figuratively *locked* because it is **held inside the pile of plant remains as long as the deposit stays waterlogged**. Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) proves this lock is conditional rather than permanent: draining the water releases the carbon.


> Begitu air disurutkan melalui kanal, oksigen masuk ke pori-pori gambut, mikroba pengurai menjadi giat, dan <Highlight>karbon yang tertimbun selama ribuan tahun terlepas ke udara</Highlight> sebagai karbon dioksida, bahkan tanpa kebakaran.
## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor misstates the **role of waterlogging and microbes**.

- *Stored forever* is refuted by sentences (<InlineMath math="8" />) through (<InlineMath math="10" />).
- *Sealed so water cannot enter* reverses the fact that the deposit is saturated with water.
- *Bound to water and carried away* misrepresents water's role, which is blocking oxygen.
- *Decay stops because microbes die* contradicts sentences (<InlineMath math="3" />) and (<InlineMath math="9" />), where microbes slow down and revive once oxygen returns.

# Answer

The figurative word *terkunci* means **held within the deposit of plant remains as long as that deposit remains waterlogged**.

(*tertahan di dalam timbunan sisa tumbuhan selama timbunan itu tetap tergenang*)
