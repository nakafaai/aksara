# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="9" />) and (<InlineMath math="10" />) of the **Minangkabau** passage describe stewardship of ancestral property:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): ancestral property belongs jointly to the clan and may not be sold at will.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />): that property is *diawasi* (overseen) by the *mamak*, who also leads clan affairs.

# Asked

The word closest in meaning to *diawasi* in sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />).

# Solution

Because the property belongs to the clan and cannot be sold freely, the *mamak*'s oversight means **safeguarding it so it stays intact under clan rules**. The closest synonym is **guarded** (*dijaga*).


> Pick the synonym from the context of the *mamak*'s duty to protect communal clan property under customary rules.
## Evaluating the Other Four Words

Two distractors reduce the role to **passive watching**, while two grant **excessive ownership rights**.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *dimiliki* (owned) | Wrong, the clan owns the property jointly |
| *diamati* (observed) | Wrong, passive watching ignores leadership responsibility |
| *dijaga* (guarded) | Correct, matches protecting communal property |
| *dikuasai* (controlled freely) | Wrong, contradicts the ban on arbitrary sale |
| *diselidiki* (investigated) | Wrong, no investigation is taking place |

# Answer

The word *diawasi* in sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />) is closest in meaning to **guarded** (*dijaga*).
