The word _mengawasi_ points in two directions. One is watching something closely. The other is **keeping something running by the rules**. The right match depends on which direction sentence (10) takes, and sentence (9) decides that.

Sentence (9) says high ancestral property belongs to the whole lineage and can't be sold at will. Sentence (10) then says that property is _diawasi_ by the _mamak_ (the mother's brother), who also leads the lineage's affairs. So the _mamak_'s job is to make sure the shared property stays whole and is used by the lineage's rules. The _mamak_ isn't just looking at it. The word that fits is _dijaga_ ("guarded", "looked after").

The other four words fail for two different reasons.

Two of them take the first direction.

- _Diamati_ ("observed") means looked at carefully. The _mamak_ doesn't only look, because the _mamak_ leads the lineage's affairs.
- _Diselidiki_ ("investigated") means examined to find something out. Nobody is trying to find anything out about the property.

The other two give the _mamak_ too much right.

- _Dimiliki_ ("owned") clashes with sentence (9). The property belongs to the whole lineage, not to the _mamak_.
- _Dikuasai_ ("controlled", "held in one's power") means the _mamak_ could do anything with the property. Sentence (9) actually forbids selling it at will.

Check by putting the word into the sentence: _Harta itu dijaga oleh mamak, yaitu saudara laki-laki ibu, yang juga memimpin urusan kaum._ The sentence still says the same thing.

So _diawasi_ in sentence (10) is closest in meaning to _dijaga_.
