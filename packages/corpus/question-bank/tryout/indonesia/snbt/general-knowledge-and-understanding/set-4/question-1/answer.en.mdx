An *ambiguous* expression allows more than one interpretation. **“Soon” without a time limit** lets two operators choose different times after reading the same instruction.

The other choices concern implicit meaning, complex interactions, a provisional conclusion, or a flexible method. They do not directly show differing interpretations of an expression.
