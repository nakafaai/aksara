# Given

The **fish salting** passage states several key facts:

- Sentences (<InlineMath math="8" />) and (<InlineMath math="9" />): salt suppresses spoilage bacteria, yet many remain alive and some still grow slowly.
- Sentences (<InlineMath math="11" />) and (<InlineMath math="12" />): reddish spots come from salt-tolerant microbes stored too long in warm conditions.
- Sentences (<InlineMath math="14" />) and (<InlineMath math="15" />): salt quickly makes the outer flesh salty, yet moves slowly inward so the center of thick fish may begin spoiling before salt arrives.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="17" />): salt high in calcium and magnesium slows salt penetration.

# Asked

The statement that is **CERTAINLY** true based on the passage.

# Solution

Combining **sentences (<InlineMath math="14" />) and (<InlineMath math="15" />)** guarantees one careful conclusion: a salty outer layer only proves salt reached the surface, so a thick fish can still spoil at its core before salt penetrates.


> Meskipun begitu, garam bergerak ke bagian tengah daging dengan lambat, sehingga <Highlight>bagian tengah ikan yang tebal dapat mulai membusuk sebelum garam sampai ke sana</Highlight>.
## Why the Other Four Statements Fail

Each distractor stretches beyond or **contradicts specific sentences** in the text.

- Dried salted fish can still harbor surviving bacteria that grow rapidly again when damp (sentences <InlineMath math="9" />, <InlineMath math="10" />, and <InlineMath math="18" />).
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="17" />) never claims calcium-free salt *always* keeps fish longer.
- Reddish spots come from microbes thriving in *high* salt, not too little salt (sentences <InlineMath math="11" /> and <InlineMath math="12" />).
- Lower free water slows bacterial activity rather than killing all bacteria (sentences <InlineMath math="8" /> and <InlineMath math="9" />).

# Answer

**A thick fish whose outer layer already tastes salty is not necessarily safe from spoilage in its center**.

(*Ikan tebal yang lapisan luarnya sudah terasa asin belum tentu aman dari pembusukan di bagian tengahnya.*)
