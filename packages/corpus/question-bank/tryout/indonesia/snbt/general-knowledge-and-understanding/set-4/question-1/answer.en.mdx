The word *menaburkan* is used for fine grains that are **scattered in many directions**, such as sprinkling sugar or sowing seeds. In sentence (3), salt powder is released from a plane into the cloud, so the grains spread through the cloud.

This fits sentence (4): each grain of salt draws in the water vapor around it. For many drops to form, the grains must be **spread out**, not gathered in one place.

The other options do not fit:

- *Mengumpulkan* (to gather) is the opposite of scattering.
- *Melarutkan* (to dissolve) means mixing into a liquid until the form disappears, while the salt is released into the cloud as a powder.
- *Membakar* (to burn) has nothing to do with seeding.
- *Menimbun* (to pile up) means heaping in one place.

So *menaburkan* in sentence (3) means to scatter (*menyebarkan*).
