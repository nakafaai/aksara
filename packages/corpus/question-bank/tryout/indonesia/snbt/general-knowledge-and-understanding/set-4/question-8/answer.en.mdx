Sum up **what each paragraph does**, then look at the link between them.

- The first paragraph explains that cloves and nutmeg grew only in Maluku and became many times more expensive after passing through many traders.
- The second paragraph opens with "That scarcity drove ...". It explains what happened because of the scarcity: the search for a sea route, the struggle over the spice sources, and the suffering of the people of Banda.

*Kelangkaan itu* (that scarcity) points back to the first paragraph, and *mendorong* (drove) marks cause and effect. So the first paragraph gives the cause and the second gives **its consequences**.

The other options do not fit:

- The passage does not compare cloves with nutmeg. Both are treated together as scarce spices.
- The second paragraph does not refute the high prices. It explains what those prices and that scarcity led to.
- The first paragraph is not an example, and the second is not a definition.
- The passage does not tell the life story of a trader.

So the first paragraph explains the scarcity and high price of the spices, and the second explains the consequences.
