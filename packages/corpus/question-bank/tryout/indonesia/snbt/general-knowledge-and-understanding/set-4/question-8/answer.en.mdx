# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) of the **full moon** passage contains the affixed word *kemerahan*:

> Udara juga menghamburkan lebih banyak cahaya biru sehingga bulan yang rendah tampak kemerahan, tetapi perubahan warna itu tidak menambah ukuran.

# Asked

The sentence whose italicized word uses the circumfix *ke-an* with the same meaning as *kemerahan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).

# Solution

In *tampak kemerahan*, *ke-an* attaches to the color adjective *merah* to form an adjective meaning **somewhat reddish** (*agak merah*). Only *kekuningan* (*somewhat yellowish*) in *Daun yang mulai layu itu tampak kekuningan* shares this exact **color-degree adjective meaning**.

## Testing All Five Sentences

Substituting *agak* (somewhat) plus the root cleanly separates **somewhat colored** from other *ke-an* meanings.

| Italicized Word | Meaning of *ke-an* | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *kebesaran* (shirt) | Excessively large (*terlalu*) | Wrong, expresses excess |
| *kekuningan* (leaf) | Somewhat yellow (*agak kuning*) | Correct, matches *kemerahan* |
| *kemalaman* (hikers) | Caught out by nightfall | Wrong, expresses unintended experience |
| *kemerahan* (skin) | Noun referring to a red patch | Wrong, noun subject rather than adjective |
| *kehilangan* (wallet) | Suffered a loss | Wrong, expresses adverse experience |

# Answer

The matching *ke-an* meaning appears in **The wilting leaf looks *yellowish***.

(*Daun yang mulai layu itu tampak kekuningan.*)
