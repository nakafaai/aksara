# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="7" />) and (<InlineMath math="8" />) of the **urban heat island** passage describe a feedback loop:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />): higher temperatures lead residents to run air conditioners longer.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): that habit uses more electricity and vents extra heat onto the street, so the problem *berputar seperti lingkaran* (spins like a circle).

# Asked

The meaning of *berputar seperti lingkaran* in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).

# Solution

Heat drives longer air-conditioner use, which expels even more heat outdoors and raises temperatures further. The figure means **the consequence of the problem amplifies its own cause**.

> Kebiasaan itu menambah pemakaian listrik sekaligus membuang lebih banyak panas ke jalan, sehingga <Highlight>masalahnya berputar seperti lingkaran</Highlight>.

## Checking the Other Options

Each distractor misses the **self-reinforcing cycle** in sentences (<InlineMath math="7" />) and (<InlineMath math="8" />).

- It does not refer to a fixed month every year.
- Residents are not described walking in circles.
- Electricity distribution across neighborhoods is not discussed.
- A self-reinforcing loop makes a problem harder, not quicker, to solve.

# Answer

The expression means **the effect of the problem actually enlarges its own cause**.

(*akibat dari masalah itu justru memperbesar penyebabnya sendiri*)
