The final sentence connects **traceability, diversity, and coherence**. Match each condition to the correct choice.

| Condition in the passage | Meaning in the paraphrase |
| --- | --- |
| Changes are traceable | Their origins and reasons are open |
| Source diversity is not concealed | Differences between sources remain visible |
| The theatrical experience remains coherent | The performance still forms a whole |

The correct choice includes all three. Matching one script’s wording alone omits diversity and traceability. Rejecting every change or ignoring sources reverses the meaning, while the publication decade is only a source detail.
