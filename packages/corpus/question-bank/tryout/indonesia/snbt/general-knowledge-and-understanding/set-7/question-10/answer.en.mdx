# Given

Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> of the **eel** (*sidat*) passage traces how eels live in rivers, migrate to the deep sea to spawn, and return as young eels up rivers.

Sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) continue:

> <Highlight>Pola hidup ini membuat sidat sangat rentan terhadap bendungan</Highlight>.

# Asked

The referent of *Pola hidup ini* (this life pattern) in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

The demonstrative *ini* refers back to the **two-way migration between river and ocean** described across paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />. Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) confirms this by noting that dams block young eels swimming upstream and adults returning to sea.

## Checking the Other Options

Each distractor cites details **absent from the text** or unrelated to dams.

- Hiding in mud and eating small fish are never mentioned.
- Pond farming appears later in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).
- Having a long body (sentence <InlineMath math="1" />) does not explain why dams interrupt migration.

# Answer

The phrase *Pola hidup ini* refers to **the eel's travel between rivers and the sea throughout its life**.

(*perjalanan sidat antara sungai dan laut sepanjang hidupnya*)
