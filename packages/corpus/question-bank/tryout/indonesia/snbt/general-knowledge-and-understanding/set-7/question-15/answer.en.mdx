# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="5" />) and (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **urban heat island** passage connect directly:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />): in areas with many trees, part of the solar heat evaporates water from leaves, making surrounding air cooler.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): therefore, many cities are adding parks and shade trees along streets.

# Asked

What adding parks and shade trees in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) is primarily expected to achieve.

# Solution

*Therefore* links tree planting directly back to the **cooling mechanism in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />)**: cooling city air by using solar heat to **evaporate water from leaves**.

> Di daerah yang banyak pepohonan, <Highlight>sebagian panas matahari terpakai untuk menguapkan air dari daun</Highlight>, sehingga udara di sekitarnya terasa lebih sejuk.

## Checking the Other Options

Each distractor **clashes with the passage** or stretches beyond its evidence.

- Cooler streets reduce rather than increase air-conditioner demand.
- Saying trees absorb *all* engine heat exaggerates and alters sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />).
- Adding street trees does not mean replacing every paved road.
- Raising nighttime temperatures in the outskirts is never a goal.

# Answer

Adding parks and shade trees is mainly expected to **cool city air through water evaporation from leaves**.

(*mendinginkan udara kota melalui penguapan air dari daun*)
