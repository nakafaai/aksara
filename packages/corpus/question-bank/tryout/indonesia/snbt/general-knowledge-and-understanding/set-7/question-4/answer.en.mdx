# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) of the **angklung** passage appear in sequence:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />): in <InlineMath math="1938" />, Daeng Soetigna built a diatonic angklung able to play modern songs.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />): *Sejak itu* (since then), angklung has been played in schools and performances, not only in traditional ceremonies.

# Asked

# Solution

The transition *Sejak itu* links the **diatonic innovation** in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) to its **consequence**: broader use of angklung across schools and public performances.

> <Highlight>Sejak itu, angklung tidak hanya mengiringi upacara adat, tetapi juga dimainkan di sekolah dan dalam pertunjukan</Highlight>.

## Checking the Other Options

Each distractor misreads the **change-and-result link** between the two sentences.

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) builds on rather than contradicts sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).
- No specific modern songs are named.
- The construction steps for diatonic angklung are not explained.
- Adding new venues introduces fresh information instead of restating sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Answer

**Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) states the result of the change explained in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />)**.

(*Kalimat (7) menyebutkan akibat dari perubahan yang dijelaskan pada kalimat (6).*)
