# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **wind power** passage reads:

> Saat angin terlalu lemah, baling-baling berhenti, sedangkan saat angin terlalu kencang, <Highlight>turbin justru dihentikan agar tidak rusak</Highlight>.

# Asked

What the word *justru* (contrary to expectation / instead) indicates in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

*Justru* highlights a **counterintuitive outcome**. One might expect stronger wind to yield even more power, yet overly strong wind instead forces operators to halt the turbine to prevent damage.

## Checking the Other Four Options

Every distractor misses the **contrast with expectation** signaled by *justru*.

- The turbine is shut down rather than spinning faster for higher output.
- Site selection is explained in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />), not marked by *justru*.
- *Justru* does not indicate chronological sequence.
- The clause after *justru* covers *overly strong* wind, not weak wind.

# Answer

The word *justru* indicates **a situation contrary to expectation, where strong wind instead causes the turbine to stop**.

(*keadaan yang berlawanan dengan dugaan, yaitu angin kencang malah membuat turbin berhenti*)
