# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) of the **eel** (*sidat*) passage contrasts two directions along a river:

> Bendungan yang tidak dilengkapi jalur ikan menghalangi <Highlight>sidat muda naik ke hulu</Highlight> dan menghalangi sidat dewasa turun ke laut.

# Asked

The sentence whose italicized word is opposite in meaning to *hulu* in sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />).

# Solution

In a river context, *hulu* means **upstream** (near the river's source). Its direct antonym is **hilir** (**downstream**, near the river mouth and sea), as used in *Banjir kiriman merendam permukiman di hilir sungai*.

## Comparing All Five Italicized Words

Keeping the **river-direction sense** rules out the homonym trap *hulu keris* (dagger hilt).

| Italicized Word | Meaning and Relation to River *hulu* | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *puncak* (hilltop) | Highest point of land, opposite of foot of hill | Wrong, not river flow |
| *mata air* (spring) | River source, part of the upstream area | Wrong, same end as *hulu* |
| *tepian* (riverbank) | Side edge of a river | Wrong, not an upstream/downstream pair |
| *hilir* (downstream) | Lower river near the estuary | Correct, direct antonym of *hulu* |
| *hulu* (dagger hilt) | Handle of a traditional blade | Wrong, unrelated homonym |

# Answer

The opposite meaning appears in **Floodwaters from upstream inundated settlements in the *downstream* part of the river**.

(*Banjir kiriman merendam permukiman di hilir sungai.*)
