*Hulu* is **the part of a river near its source**, where the current comes from (upstream). Its opposite is *hilir*, the part toward the mouth and the sea (downstream). Sentence (7) shows this contrast: young eels swim up to the *hulu*, while adult eels go down to the sea, toward the *hilir*.

The other options do not fit:

- *Puncak* (peak) is the top of a mountain or object, not a part of a river.
- *Mata air* (spring) lies near the upstream end, so it points the same way, not the opposite.
- *Tepian* (bank) is the side of a river, not a direction of flow.
- *Dasar* (bed, bottom) is the bottom of a river, not the opposite of upstream.

So the opposite of *hulu* is *hilir*.
