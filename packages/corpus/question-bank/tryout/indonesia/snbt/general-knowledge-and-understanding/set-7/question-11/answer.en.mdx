# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="1" />) and (<InlineMath math="2" />) of the **urban heat island** passage state:

> Pada malam hari, <Highlight>suhu udara di pusat kota besar sering beberapa derajat lebih tinggi daripada suhu di daerah pinggiran</Highlight> yang masih banyak pepohonan.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />) names this phenomenon *pulau panas perkotaan* (urban heat island).

# Asked

The meaning of *pulau panas perkotaan* based on the passage.

# Solution

The term names the **condition in which a city center is hotter than its surrounding outskirts**. *Island* is figurative for a pocket of warm air surrounded by cooler, tree-filled outskirts.

## Checking the Other Options

Each distractor takes *island* literally, confuses a cause with the phenomenon, or **reverses the temperature comparison**.

- It is not a physical island near the equator.
- Areas with many shade trees are cooler rather than hotter.
- Buildings venting air-conditioner heat are one cause (sentence <InlineMath math="6" />), not the phenomenon itself.
- The city center is warmer than the outskirts, not the reverse.

# Answer

*Pulau panas perkotaan* means **a condition in which the city center is hotter than the surrounding outskirts**.

(*keadaan ketika pusat kota lebih panas daripada daerah pinggiran di sekitarnya*)
