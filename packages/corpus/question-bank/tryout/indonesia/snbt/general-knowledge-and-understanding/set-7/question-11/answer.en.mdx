Sentence (2) names *gejala ini* (this phenomenon) the urban heat island, and *gejala ini* refers to sentence (1): the temperature in **the city center is higher** than in the tree-filled outskirts. So the term names a condition, not an island or a building.

The other options do not fit:

- *Pulau* (island) is a figure of speech for a hot area surrounded by cooler land, not a real island.
- Areas with many trees are in fact cooler.
- Buildings releasing hot air are only one cause, not the phenomenon itself.
- The direction is reversed. The center is hotter, not the outskirts.

So the urban heat island is the condition in which the city center is hotter than the outskirts around it.
