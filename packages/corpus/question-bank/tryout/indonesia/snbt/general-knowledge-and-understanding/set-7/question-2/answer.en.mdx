# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="3" />) and (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **angklung** passage describe its pitch design:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />): the tubes are cut to specific lengths so they sound the same note one octave apart.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />): one angklung produces only one note, so a song requires many players.

# Asked

The statement best supported by sentences (<InlineMath math="3" />) and (<InlineMath math="5" />).

# Solution

Together, both sentences show that **each angklung is crafted to sound one specific pitch**, which is why an ensemble of players is needed for a full melody.

> Karena <Highlight>satu angklung hanya menghasilkan satu nada</Highlight>, sebuah lagu dimainkan oleh banyak pemain yang masing-masing memegang satu atau dua angklung.

## Why the Other Four Options Fail

Every other choice **directly contradicts** sentences (<InlineMath math="3" />) and (<InlineMath math="5" />).

- One angklung produces a single note, not every note of a song.
- Its tubes play the same note an octave apart rather than different notes.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) states that a song is played by many performers, not one solo player.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) shows tube length directly sets the pitch.

# Answer

**Each angklung is made to produce one specific note**.

(*Setiap angklung dibuat untuk menghasilkan satu nada tertentu.*)
