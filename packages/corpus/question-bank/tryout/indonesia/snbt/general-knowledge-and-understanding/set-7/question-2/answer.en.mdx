Sentence (3) says the tubes of one angklung **sound the same note**, only an octave apart. Sentence (5) adds that one angklung **produces only one note**. So each angklung is made for one particular note.

The other options contradict the reading:

- One angklung produces only one note, so it cannot play every note of a song.
- Its tubes sound the same note, not different notes.
- Sentence (5) says a song is played by many players.
- Sentence (3) says the tubes are cut to a certain length to sound their note, so tube length does decide the sound.

So the most accurate statement is that each angklung is made to produce one particular note.
