The word *stabil* means **steady, constant, not changing much**. In sentence (4), wind that is *stabil* all year blows with roughly the same strength. That matters because sentences (5) and (6) explain that wind power varies when the wind varies.

The other options do not fit:

- Wind strength is already given by *kencang* (strong), while *stabil* describes a different quality.
- *Berubah-ubah arah setiap saat* (changing direction all the time) is the opposite of steady.
- Wind from the sea is not mentioned and is not the meaning of *stabil*.
- Feeling cool and damp has nothing to do with *stabil*.

So *stabil* means steady and not changing much.
