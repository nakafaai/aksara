# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **wind power** passage states:

> Lokasinya dipilih di daerah terbuka yang anginnya bertiup cukup kencang dan <Highlight>stabil</Highlight> sepanjang tahun.

# Asked

The meaning of the word *stabil* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

*Stabil* means **steady and not changing much**. Consistent wind matters because sentences (<InlineMath math="5" />) and (<InlineMath math="6" />) note that wind electricity fluctuates whenever wind strength swings.

## Evaluating the Options

Since strength is already covered by *kencang* (strong), only **steady and consistent** captures *stabil*.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *tetap dan tidak banyak berubah* (steady) | Correct, standard meaning of *stabil* |
| *bertiup sangat kencang sekali* (very strong) | Wrong, wind strength is already stated by *kencang* |
| *berubah-ubah arah setiap saat* (shifting constantly) | Wrong, opposite of steady |
| *datang dari arah laut* (from the sea) | Wrong, refers to origin |
| *terasa sejuk dan lembap* (cool and humid) | Wrong, refers to temperature and humidity |

# Answer

The word *stabil* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) means **steady and not changing much** (*tetap dan tidak banyak berubah*).
