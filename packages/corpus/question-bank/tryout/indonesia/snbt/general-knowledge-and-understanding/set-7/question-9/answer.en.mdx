Follow the eel's life cycle in the reading. Young eels enter the river and **live there until adulthood** (sentence 5). If young eels are caught in large numbers as seed for farming (sentence 8), fewer eels get to grow up in the river. That is why the number of adult eels falls in later years.

The other options are not supported:

- The reading does not say young eels eat adults.
- The reading does not say adult eels move to farm ponds.
- Dams block eels heading upstream, but they do not keep young eels in the deep sea, and this is not an effect of catching.
- The reading does not say when young eels are caught.

So overfishing reduces adult eels because the young eels caught never get to grow up.
