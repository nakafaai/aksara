# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="5" />), (<InlineMath math="8" />), and (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **eel** (*sidat*) passage explain eel growth and harvesting:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />): young eels swim up rivers to live there until adulthood.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): eel farming still depends on wild-caught juveniles.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): excessive capture of young eels can reduce the adult eel population in later years.

# Asked

Why overharvesting young eels reduces the number of adult eels in subsequent years.

# Solution

Adult eels develop from young eels that grow in rivers. Removing too many juveniles means **captured young eels never get the chance to mature into adults**, shrinking future adult numbers.

> Akibatnya, <Highlight>penangkapan sidat muda yang berlebihan dapat mengurangi jumlah sidat dewasa pada tahun-tahun berikutnya</Highlight>.

## Checking the Other Options

Every distractor invents **unsupported claims** or confuses dams with harvesting.

- The passage never says young eels eat adults.
- Adult eels are not reported moving into farm ponds with juveniles.
- Dams obstruct rivers rather than trapping young eels in the deep sea, and they are unrelated to capture.
- The timing of capture is never tied to adult spawning.

# Answer

**Young eels that are caught do not get the chance to grow into adult eels**.

(*Sidat muda yang ditangkap tidak sempat tumbuh menjadi sidat dewasa.*)
