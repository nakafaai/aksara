# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) through (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **eel** (*sidat*) passage explain its life cycle:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />): eels do not reproduce in fresh water.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />): as adults, eels migrate to the deep sea to *memijah*.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />): hatched larvae drift on ocean currents toward the coast.

# Asked

The meaning of *memijah* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />).

# Solution

*Memijah* is the standard term for fish **spawning, laying eggs, and reproducing**. Sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />) on reproduction and sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) on hatching larvae confirm this directly.

> Setelah dewasa, sidat bermigrasi ke laut dalam yang jauhnya dapat mencapai ribuan kilometer untuk <Highlight>memijah</Highlight>.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **laying eggs and reproducing** accounts for the hatching larvae in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *mencari makan* (foraging) | Wrong, does not produce hatched larvae |
| *berganti kulit* (molting) | Wrong, unrelated to *memijah* |
| *bertelur dan berkembang biak* (spawning and reproducing) | Correct, matches sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) and (<InlineMath math="4" />) |
| *beristirahat panjang* (resting long) | Wrong, does not explain reproduction |
| *bersembunyi dari ikan pemangsa* (hiding from predators) | Wrong, not mentioned |

# Answer

The word *memijah* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) means **to lay eggs and reproduce** (*bertelur dan berkembang biak*).
