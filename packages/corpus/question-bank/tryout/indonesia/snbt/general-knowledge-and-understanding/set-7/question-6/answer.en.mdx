The word *memijah* means **to release eggs and breed**, a term used for fish (to spawn). It fits sentences (2) and (4): eels do not breed in fresh water, and after the migration to sea there are larvae that hatch. So eels go to sea to lay eggs and breed.

The other options do not fit:

- *Mencari makan* (to look for food) has nothing to do with hatching larvae.
- *Berganti kulit* (to shed skin) is not mentioned and is not the meaning of *memijah*.
- *Beristirahat panjang* (to rest for a long time) does not explain the larvae.
- *Bersembunyi dari ikan pemangsa* (to hide from predators) is not mentioned either.

So *memijah* means to lay eggs and breed (*bertelur dan berkembang biak*).
