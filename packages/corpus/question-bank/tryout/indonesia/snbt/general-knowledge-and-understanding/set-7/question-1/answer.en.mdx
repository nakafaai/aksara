# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **angklung** passage describes how the instrument sounds:

> Ketika bingkai <Highlight>digoyangkan</Highlight>, tabung bergerak dan membentur dasar bingkai sehingga terdengar bunyi.

# Asked

The meaning of *digoyangkan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

*Digoyangkan* comes from *goyang* (to shake or sway), meaning **moved back and forth** repeatedly so the bamboo tubes strike the base of the frame and produce sound.

## Comparing the Options

Matching how the **tubes hit the frame** separates shaking from methods used on other instruments.

| Option | Playing Method | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *dipukul dengan tongkat* | Struck with a stick | Wrong, angklung is not struck |
| *ditiup dengan kuat* | Blown strongly | Wrong, no air is blown |
| *dipetik dengan jari* | Plucked with fingers | Wrong, no strings are plucked |
| *digerakkan bolak-balik* | Moved back and forth | Correct, matches *digoyangkan* |
| *diputar perlahan-lahan* | Turned slowly | Wrong, would not strike repeatedly |

# Answer

The word *digoyangkan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) means **moved back and forth** (*digerakkan bolak-balik*).
