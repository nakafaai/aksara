The word *digoyangkan* comes from *goyang*, **to move side to side or back and forth** repeatedly. Sentence (4) says the frame is shaken so the tubes move and knock against its base. So the frame is moved back and forth.

The other options do not fit:

- *Dipukul dengan tongkat* (struck with a stick) is how other instruments are played, such as drums.
- *Ditiup* (blown) is how a flute is played, not an angklung.
- *Dipetik* (plucked) is how a zither or guitar is played.
- *Diputar perlahan-lahan* (turned slowly) would not make the tubes knock against the frame again and again.

So *digoyangkan* means moved back and forth (*digerakkan bolak-balik*).
