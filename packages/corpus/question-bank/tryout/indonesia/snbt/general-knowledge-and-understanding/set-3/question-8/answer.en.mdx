This question asks what the affix *per-an* means in *perpindahan* ("movement, transfer") in sentence (12), then asks for another word whose affix has the same meaning. Careful: three options use *per-an* built on a *ber-* verb too, so finding the root isn't enough. You also have to check what the word names.

Step one: turn *perpindahan* back into a clause. Sentence (12) says "terjadi perpindahan nitrogen dari daun dan batang kedelai ke dalam biji" (a transfer of nitrogen from the leaves and stems into the seeds takes place). That's the same as "nitrogen berpindah dari daun dan batang ke biji" (nitrogen moves from the leaves and stems into the seeds). The verb is *berpindah* ("to move"), the nitrogen itself does the moving, and *terjadi* ("takes place") marks the word as naming an event. So *per-an* here means **the event of a ber- verb**, which matches KBBI's entry for *perpindahan*, "perihal berpindah" (the matter of moving).

Step two: test each word with two questions. Which verb is it built from? And what does it name: an event, a place, an object, or someone's action?

| Word | Source verb | What it names | Meaning of the affix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *perputaran* kincir (the turning of the waterwheel) | *berputar* (to turn) | the event of the wheel turning | event of a ber- verb |
| *perhentian* near the market | *berhenti* (to stop) | the place where the bus stops | place of a ber- verb |
| *perhiasan* emas (gold jewelry) | *berhias* (to dress up) | an object used for dressing up | object for a ber- verb |
| *pemindahan* bibit (moving the seedlings) | *memindahkan* (to move something) | someone's act of moving the seedlings | process of a meN- verb |
| *perbaikan* pematang (repairing the dikes) | *memperbaiki* (to repair) | the villagers' act of repairing the dikes | act of a memper- verb |

Only *perputaran* comes from a *ber-* verb and names its event. KBBI lists its first sense as "hal berputar" (the matter of turning).

The other options are traps in these ways.

- *Perhentian* is the strongest trap. It's also built on a *ber-* verb, *berhenti*. But KBBI defines it as "tempat berhenti" (a stopping place), and in its sentence the bus waits *at* the stop, so it names a place, not an event.
- *Perhiasan* also comes from *berhias*, but it names an object kept in a cupboard, not the event of dressing up.
- *Pemindahan* shares the root *pindah*, so it looks closest. But its affix is *pe-an* from *memindahkan*. Someone moves the seedlings, while the nitrogen in sentence (12) moves as an event.
- *Perbaikan* uses the same *per-an*, but it comes from *memperbaiki*, a verb that needs a doer and a target, the villagers and the dikes.

So the affix in *perpindahan* in sentence (12) has the same meaning as the affix in *perputaran* in the sentence about the waterwheel: the event of a ber- verb.
