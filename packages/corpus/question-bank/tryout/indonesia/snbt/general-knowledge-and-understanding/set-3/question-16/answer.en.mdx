# Given

Paragraph one introduces cooperative surplus sharing (SHU) and deposit-interest costs, paragraph two explains **compound interest** on an individual account, and paragraph three scales that compounding logic to the whole cooperative with two required conditions.

# Asked

What is the role of paragraph three in the passage as a whole?

# Solution

> Logika yang sama juga dapat bekerja pada koperasi sebagai satu kesatuan. ... Namun, putaran ini hanya berjalan jika dana tambahan itu benar-benar tersalur sebagai pinjaman yang lancar dibayar dan bunga pinjaman lebih tinggi daripada bunga simpanan.

Track where **each ingredient of paragraph three** originates and what operational conditions sentence (<InlineMath math="15" />) adds.

## Combining earlier paragraphs and setting conditions

Paragraph three merges SHU distribution from paragraph one with <Highlight>logika yang sama</Highlight> ("the same logic", compounding) from paragraph two at the **cooperative-wide level**, then specifies the **conditions needed** for that cycle to keep running (repaid loans and loan interest exceeding deposit interest).

The distractors miss this synthesis: paragraph three moves beyond a single member's account, never contradicts paragraph two, mentions defaulted loans only as a failed condition at the end, and explicitly denies that reinvested SHU compounds automatically.

# Answer

Paragraph three **combines SHU distribution from paragraph one with the compound-interest logic of paragraph two at the cooperative level, then sets the conditions for that cycle to work** (*Menggabungkan pembagian SHU dari paragraf pertama dengan logika bunga majemuk dari paragraf kedua pada tingkat koperasi, lalu menetapkan syarat agar putaran itu berjalan.*).
