Every statement here can only be decided by **combining two sentences**. One sentence alone often misleads, because two statements sound false but are true, and two sound true but aren't supported. A statement the text doesn't support counts as *Tidak sesuai* ("not consistent").

Statement 1 is *Sesuai* ("consistent"), even though it sounds contradictory. Sentence (6) says the job of bringing the story to life falls to the voice of the *dalang*, the storyteller. If you stop there, you might think the pictures show nothing without the *dalang*. Sentence (10) sets this straight: viewers who only look at the pictures still see a row of events, just disconnected ones. So the pictures still show events. What goes missing without the *dalang* is the link between them.

Statement 2 is *Tidak sesuai*, even though it sounds reasonable. Sentence (3) does say a scroll holds several scenes, so it's natural to think more scenes means a longer show. But sentence (8) says the *dalang* can narrate a scene at length or just briefly, and sentence (9) says the length of a show depends more on the narration than on the number of scenes. A scroll with more scenes could be performed in less time if each scene is narrated briefly. The word *pasti* ("certainly") isn't supported.

Statement 3 is *Tidak sesuai*. Sentence (4) only says that old scrolls are generally made of *daluang*, bark paper. Sentence (15) says artists today make new scrolls, and their material isn't mentioned at all. So a claim about every scroll performed today goes beyond those two sentences, both on "old" and on "generally".

Statement 4 is *Sesuai*, even though it sounds impossible if you only remember sentence (5). Sentence (5) says inherited scrolls are kept as heirlooms (*pusaka*) and only unrolled on certain occasions. Sentence (15) says artists today make new scrolls and perform them with a similar way of narrating. A newly made scroll isn't inherited, so it isn't an heirloom. That means you can see this way of narrating through a new scroll without unrolling a single heirloom.

So statements 1 and 4 are consistent with the text, while statements 2 and 3 aren't.
