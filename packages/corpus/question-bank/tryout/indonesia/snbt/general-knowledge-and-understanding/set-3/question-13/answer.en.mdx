The first pair states **what interest is calculated on**. By sentence (2), simple interest (*bunga tunggal*) is always calculated on the initial deposit (*setoran awal*). So the second word must name what compound interest (*bunga majemuk*) is calculated on.

Sentence (3) explains that with compound interest, interest received is added to the balance, and the next interest is calculated **on that new balance**. The base is the latest balance (*saldo terbaru*).

The other options do not fit:

- *Setoran awal* is the base of simple interest, not compound interest.
- *Kartu kredit* (credit card) and *tagihan bulanan* (monthly bill) belong to debt in the second paragraph, not to the base of savings interest.
- *Bank* is who pays the interest, not what it is calculated on.

So *bunga tunggal : setoran awal* matches *bunga majemuk : saldo terbaru*.
