# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="12" />) calls "greenhouse effect" for Earth's atmosphere only a ***perumpamaan*** ("analogy/metaphor"), before explaining how greenhouse gases really absorb infrared while air moves freely. The question replaces *perumpamaan* with ***pengandaian*** ("supposition/hypothetical").

# Asked

What happens to the meaning of sentence (<InlineMath math="12" />) if *perumpamaan* is replaced with *pengandaian*?

# Solution

> Karena itu, istilah "efek rumah kaca" untuk pemanasan atmosfer bumi sebenarnya hanya sebuah perumpamaan: di atmosfer, gas seperti karbon dioksida menyerap lalu memancarkan kembali inframerah, sementara udaranya tetap bebas bergerak.

Contrast KBBI's ***perumpamaan*** ("comparison/analogy between real things") with ***pengandaian*** ("something merely supposed or imagined").

## Contrasting analogy with mere supposition

Originally, <Highlight>hanya sebuah perumpamaan</Highlight> compares Earth's atmosphere to a greenhouse while keeping atmospheric warming real. Substituting *pengandaian* shifts the clause into claiming the atmospheric greenhouse effect is **merely hypothetical rather than real**, clashing with the physical mechanism described right after the colon.

The distractors miss this shift: fixed traditional sayings belong to *peribahasa* ("proverb"), an analogy describes real phenomena unlike a hypothetical, shared root meanings (*umpama*/*andai*) do not make derived nouns synonymous, and *pengandaian* does not mean "example".

# Answer

The accurate statement is that **the sentence no longer compares the atmosphere to a greenhouse, but claims that the atmospheric greenhouse effect is merely supposed rather than real** (*kalimat itu tidak lagi membandingkan atmosfer dengan rumah kaca, tetapi menyatakan bahwa efek rumah kaca di atmosfer hanya diandaikan dan bukan kenyataan*).
