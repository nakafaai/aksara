Look for the crop that **does not depend on bees for pollination**. Sentence (4) says rice and maize (*jagung*) do not depend on insects, and maize pollen is carried by the wind. So fewer bees affect maize yields the least.

The other options do not fit:

- Melon, coffee, and apple are named in sentence (1) as crops that bear more fruit when insects visit their flowers. Fewer bees can lower their yields.
- Watermelon (*semangka*) is also named in sentence (1) with melon, coffee, and apple, so its yield can fall too when bees decline.

So the crop least affected is maize.
