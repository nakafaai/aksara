This analogy asks for the pair whose relationship matches the one between *lempeng garam batu* ("rock-salt plate") and *inframerah* ("infrared") in the experiment in sentence (9). That relationship has two features at once, and the right pair has to match both.

The first feature comes from the word *bertutup* ("covered"). In sentence (9), the rock-salt plate serves as **the box's cover**, so it closes off a space. That feature matters, because by sentences (10) and (11), many physicists judge that most of the box's warming came from the air trapped under the cover.

The second feature comes from the description "a material that infrared passes through." Infrared can travel through the plate.

So the relationship is a cover that closes off a space but still lets something pass through it. Now test each pair against those two features.

| Pair | Closes off a space? | Lets it through? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kasa nyamuk (mosquito screen) : udara (air) | yes, the screen covers a window or vent | yes, air passes through the screen |
| kaca pembesar (magnifying glass) : cahaya (light) | no, a magnifying glass doesn't close off anything | yes |
| jendela terbuka (open window) : angin (wind) | no, the window is open | yes |
| tutup panci (pot lid) : uap (steam) | yes | no, the steam stays inside the pot |
| atap (roof) : air hujan (rainwater) | yes | no, the rain stays on top of the roof |

Only the mosquito screen and air match both features. Every other pair matches just one, and that's the trap.

- Magnifying glass and light are tempting because light and infrared are both radiation, and light really does pass through a magnifying glass. But a magnifying glass isn't a cover.
- Open window and wind look closest to the right answer, because wind gets through too. The difference is that an open window closes off nothing, while a mosquito screen still covers the window.
- Pot lid and steam, and roof and rainwater, match the cover feature, but both hold the steam or water back. That relationship resembles glass and infrared in sentence (5), not rock salt and infrared.

So the relationship between *lempeng garam batu* and *inframerah* matches the one between *kasa nyamuk* and *udara*: a cover that closes off a space but still lets something pass through it.
