# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) says cooperative revenue is used first to ***menutup*** ("cover") expenses, leaving SHU. Sentence (<InlineMath math="15" />) refers to the repeating loop of reinvested SHU, loans, interest, and higher SHU as ***putaran*** ("cycle").

# Asked

What are the accurate synonyms for *menutup* (sentence <InlineMath math="4" />) and *putaran* (sentence <InlineMath math="15" />) respectively?

# Solution

> Bunga yang dibayar peminjam menjadi sumber utama pendapatan koperasi, dan pendapatan itu dipakai lebih dulu untuk menutup biaya, termasuk bunga simpanan.

Match each word to its **financial-context KBBI synonym** rather than physical or unrelated senses.

## Matching both synonyms in context

| Word in text | Context clue | Accurate synonym |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *menutup* (<InlineMath math="4" />) | Revenue pays expenses before surplus remains (<Highlight>menutup biaya</Highlight>) | ***mencukupi*** ("to cover / suffice") |
| *putaran* (<InlineMath math="15" />) | Repeating annual loop from SHU to loans to larger SHU | ***siklus*** ("cycle") |

Every other pair misses at least one sense: *mengakhiri* ("end a meeting"), *menyembunyikan* ("hide"), *pusingan* ("physical spin"), *kisaran* ("range"), and *babak* ("single stage").

# Answer

The accurate synonyms in order are ***mencukupi* and *siklus***.
