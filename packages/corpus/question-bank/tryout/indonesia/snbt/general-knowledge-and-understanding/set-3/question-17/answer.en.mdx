This question asks for two synonyms at once, and both must fit the sense in the text, not the sense you hear most often. Every wrong pair contains at least one word from another sense. So decide each word on its own, then find the pair that's right on both.

The first word: *menutup* in sentence (4), *pendapatan itu dipakai lebih dulu untuk menutup biaya, termasuk bunga simpanan* ("that income is used first to *menutup* the costs, including interest on savings"). KBBI lists many senses of *menutup*, among them "to make not open", "to end (a meeting, a talk)", the figurative "to hide, to keep secret", and "to cover (costs, a shortfall, and so on)". Sentence (5) settles which one is meant: after the costs are covered, there is **income left over**. So the costs are paid out of income until they're covered, and the rest becomes the SHU, the residual operating surplus. The sense is *mencukupi* ("to cover, to meet"). The costs aren't being ended or hidden.

The second word: *putaran* in sentence (15), *putaran ini hanya berjalan jika ...* ("this *putaran* only runs if ..."). *Ini* ("this") points to the chain in sentences (13) and (14): SHU is saved again, the money for loans grows, that money earns interest, and the interest enlarges next year's SHU. Next year's SHU can be saved again, so the chain comes back to its start and can repeat every year. KBBI defines *siklus* as "a round of time in which a series of events repeats in a fixed and regular way, a cycle". That's the sense of *putaran* here.

Now check the other pairs:

- *Pusingan* is listed in KBBI as a synonym of *putaran*, but for the sense "a turning movement", like a wheel. Sentence (15) isn't about something spinning.
- *Kisaran* is also listed for the turning sense, and in money texts it's often read as "range", as in an interest range. Neither fits. *Menyembunyikan* ("to hide") is wrong too, since the costs aren't kept secret.
- *Babak* means a part of a process or a match, a stage or round. Sentence (15) is about the whole repeating chain, not one part of it.
- *Mengakhiri* ("to end") is the sense of *menutup* for meetings or talks. Sentence (5) shows the costs are paid, not ended.

The pair with *siklus* but *mengakhiri* is only half right, and so is the pair with *mencukupi* but *pusingan*.

So the synonyms of *menutup* in sentence (4) and *putaran* in sentence (15), in that order, are *mencukupi* and *siklus*.
