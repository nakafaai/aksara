Sentence (8) says the causes of the bee decline are *beragam*, referring back to sentence (6), which lists several causes: loss of flowering plants, pesticides, and parasitic mites. *Beragam* means **made up of various kinds**, so its synonym is *bermacam-macam* (of many kinds).

Sentence (8) also draws the consequence: because there are many kinds of causes, one step is not enough to protect bees.

The other options do not fit:

- *Seragam* (uniform) is the antonym of *beragam*.
- *Sedikit* (few) states a small amount, not a variety of kinds.
- *Tersembunyi dari pandangan* (hidden from view) means not visible, yet the causes are named clearly in sentence (6).
- *Sederhana* (simple) means not complicated, while sentence (8) stresses that many steps are needed.
