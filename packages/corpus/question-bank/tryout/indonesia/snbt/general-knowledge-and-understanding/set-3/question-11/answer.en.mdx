This question asks for a paraphrase of sentence (9): another sentence that says exactly the same thing. Sentence (9) reads *Akibatnya, panjang sebuah pertunjukan lebih banyak ditentukan oleh tuturan dalang daripada oleh jumlah adegan yang dibentangkan, sehingga gulungan yang sama dapat dibawakan dalam waktu yang berbeda* ("As a result, the length of a show is decided more by the narration of the *dalang*, the storyteller, than by the number of scenes unrolled, so the same scroll can be performed in different lengths of time").

All five options use almost the same words. So don't look for the one that feels closest. Split sentence (9) into four parts of meaning and check each part in every option.

1. The cause. *Akibatnya* ("as a result") points back to sentence (8). Sentence (8) has two parts: a granted point ("each section does capture only one moment") and the main point ("the *dalang* can narrate it at length or just briefly, depending on the scene's weight"). The part that produces the result in sentence (9) is the main point, **the *dalang*'s freedom to set the length of the narration**. One moment per section alone doesn't make the length of the show depend on the *dalang*.
2. The comparison. *Lebih banyak ditentukan oleh tuturan ... daripada oleh jumlah adegan* ("decided more by the narration than by the number of scenes") means both matter, and the narration matters more.
3. The modality. *Dapat* ("can") means the same scroll may be performed in different lengths of time, not that it must be.
4. The direction. The freedom to narrate is the cause, and the different lengths are the effect.

The option starting *Karena dalang dapat memanjangkan atau memendekkan tuturan setiap adegan sesuai bobotnya* ("Because the *dalang* can lengthen or shorten the narration of each scene according to its weight") passes all four checks: the right cause, the comparison kept as "more ... than", the modality kept as "can", and the direction from freedom to different lengths.

Each other option changes exactly one part:

- The option starting *Karena setiap adegan hanya menangkap satu saat* ("Because each scene captures only one moment") picks the wrong cause. That's the granted point of sentence (8), not the part that produces the result.
- The option with *pasti dipentaskan dengan durasi berlainan* ("is certainly performed in different lengths") changes "can" into "must". The text only states a possibility.
- The option with *hanya bergantung pada dalang, bukan pada banyaknya adegan* ("depends only on the *dalang*, not on the number of scenes") turns the comparison into a denial. Sentence (9) never says the number of scenes has no effect.
- The option starting *Karena satu gulungan bisa dipentaskan dengan durasi berlainan* ("Because one scroll can be performed in different lengths") reverses cause and effect. Different lengths are the result of the freedom to narrate, not its cause.

So the right paraphrase is that because the *dalang* can lengthen or shorten the narration of each scene according to its weight, the length of the show depends more on the *dalang* than on the number of scenes, and one scroll can be performed in different lengths of time.
