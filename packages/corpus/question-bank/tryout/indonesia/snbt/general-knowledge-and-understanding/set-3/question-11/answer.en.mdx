Literally, *membengkak* is used for a body part that swells from injury or inflammation. In sentence (7), what *membengkak* is **the debt**, so the word is figurative.

Sentence (7) explains that unpaid interest is added to the bill, so the debt *membengkak* "faster than expected". The debt grows, and it grows quickly. So *membengkak* here means **to grow large quickly**, to balloon.

The other options do not fit:

- *Inflamed from a wound* is the literal meaning, which applies to the body, not a debt.
- *Paid off* contradicts the sentence, since the bill is not paid.
- *Decreasing* runs opposite to a growing debt.
- *Staying the same* contradicts interest being added again and again.

So *membengkak* in sentence (7) means to grow large quickly.
