# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) says the narrator (*dalang*) can lengthen or shorten narration for any scene based on its weight. Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) concludes that show length depends **more on narration than on scene count**, so the same scroll **can** be performed in different durations.

# Asked

Which paraphrase most accurately restates sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) together with the preceding sentence?

# Solution

> Akibatnya, panjang sebuah pertunjukan lebih banyak ditentukan oleh tuturan dalang daripada oleh jumlah adegan yang dibentangkan, sehingga gulungan yang sama dapat dibawakan dalam waktu yang berbeda.

Check **four exact semantic elements** (cause, comparison, modality, and causal direction) because all five choices share almost identical wording.

## Checking all four paraphrase elements

| Element | Original meaning | Trap to reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cause | Dalang's freedom to lengthen or shorten narration | Not merely capturing one moment |
| Comparison | <Highlight>lebih banyak ditentukan ... daripada</Highlight> scene count | Not depending *only* on the dalang |
| Modality | <Highlight>dapat</Highlight> ("can") vary in duration | Not *pasti* ("must") vary |
| Direction | Narration freedom causes different durations | Not reversing cause and effect |

Only the first option preserves **all four elements** faithfully without altering the cause, softening or hardening modality, or flipping causal direction.

# Answer

The exact paraphrase is: **Because the dalang can lengthen or shorten narration for each scene according to its weight, performance length depends more on the dalang than on scene count, and one scroll can have different durations**.

(*Karena dalang dapat memanjangkan atau memendekkan tuturan setiap adegan sesuai bobotnya, lama pertunjukan lebih bergantung pada dalang daripada pada banyaknya adegan, dan satu gulungan bisa dipentaskan dengan durasi berlainan.*)
