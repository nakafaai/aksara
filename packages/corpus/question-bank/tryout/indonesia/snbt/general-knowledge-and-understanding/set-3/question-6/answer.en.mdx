# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) figuratively calls buried soybean roots, stems, and leaves a nitrogen ***tabungan*** ("savings account") for the field. Sentences (<InlineMath math="12" />) and (<InlineMath math="13" />) add that most of the plant's nitrogen moves into **seeds harvested and removed from the field**.

# Asked

Which statement is **NOT** consistent with the figurative meaning of *tabungan* in the passage?

# Solution

> Sisa akar, batang, dan daun kedelai yang dibenamkan ke dalam tanah menjadi semacam tabungan nitrogen bagi lahan.

Separate the plant parts **ploughed back into the soil** (the savings) from the part **carried off at harvest** (the seeds).

## Separating soil savings from harvested seeds

Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) defines the savings strictly as <Highlight>sisa akar, batang, dan daun kedelai yang dibenamkan</Highlight> ("buried roots, stems, and leaves"). Although most of the plant's nitrogen shifts into seeds, sentence (<InlineMath math="13" />) notes those seeds are **harvested and hauled off the field**, so seed nitrogen is not stored in the soil's savings.

The other four statements match the passage: the next crop uses the savings, it comes from unharvested plant parts, it usually does not eliminate fertiliser needs, and soil microbes release it gradually.

# Answer

The statement **NOT** consistent with *tabungan* is that **most of the savings consist of nitrogen stored inside soybean seeds** (*Sebagian besar isi tabungan itu berupa nitrogen yang tersimpan di dalam biji kedelai.*).
