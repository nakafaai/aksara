The word *kolom* can mean a pillar, a column in a table, or a tall vertical space. In sentence (2), the *kolom air* lies **above the seabed** and is pushed when the seabed rises or drops. So it means **the seawater stretching vertically from the bottom to the surface** above that part of the seabed, the water column.

This meaning explains why seabed movement causes a tsunami. When the seabed lifts, all the water above it is lifted too and then travels as a wave.

The other options do not fit:

- A fountain has nothing to do with the seabed.
- A table column is the meaning of *kolom* in writing or data, not in the sea.
- A river does not lie above a moving seabed.
- Rain clouds are in the air, not the sea.

So *kolom air* means all the seawater from the bottom to the surface above a given spot.
