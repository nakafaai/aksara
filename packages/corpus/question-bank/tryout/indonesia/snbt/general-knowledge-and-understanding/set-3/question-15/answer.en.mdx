# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) states a scroll arranges scenes in fixed sequence, and sentence (<InlineMath math="12" />) confirms **scene order is locked by the order of paintings on the scroll**.

# Asked

Which conditional ("if ..., ...") statement is **DEFINITELY** true based on the passage?

# Solution

> Urutan adegan sudah terkunci oleh urutan lukisan pada gulungan.

Turn the passage's strict rule into its logically equivalent **contrapositive**: staging all scenes of the same scroll guarantees the same scene order.

## Applying the contrapositive rule

Because <Highlight>urutan adegan sudah terkunci</Highlight> ("scene order is locked") on any scroll, two performances unrolling all scenes of one scroll must share the exact same order. Its contrapositive is therefore **guaranteed true**: if **two performances have different scene orders**, they cannot both be unrolling all scenes from the same scroll.

The other conditionals fail necessity: equal durations need not use the same scroll, narration lengths vary freely on the same scroll, spoken dialogue is not painted, and *daluang* paper does not guarantee heirloom status.

# Answer

The statement that is **definitely** true is: **if the order of scenes in two performances differs, the two performances are not unrolling all scenes from the same scroll** (*Jika urutan adegan dalam dua pertunjukan berbeda, kedua pertunjukan itu tidak membentangkan seluruh adegan dari satu gulungan yang sama.*).
