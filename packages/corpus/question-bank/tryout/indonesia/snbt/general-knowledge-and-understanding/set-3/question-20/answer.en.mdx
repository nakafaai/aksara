# Given

The cooperative passage distinguishes **principal and mandatory savings** (locked while a member) from **voluntary savings** (usually interest-bearing and withdrawable). Paragraph two discusses compound interest solely for voluntary savings.

# Asked

Which statement is **NOT** supported by the passage?

# Solution

> Anggota juga boleh menaruh simpanan sukarela yang biasanya diberi bunga dan dapat ditarik sesuai ketentuan koperasi.

Trace each statement's chain of evidence to spot the **missing link between mandatory savings and interest**.

## Spotting the missing link on mandatory savings

Claiming mandatory savings compound because they are never withdrawn merges two separate facts (sentences <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="8" />). However, the text states only that <Highlight>simpanan sukarela yang biasanya diberi bunga</Highlight> ("voluntary savings usually earn interest") and **never says mandatory savings earn interest at all**. Without interest, nothing compounds.

The other four statements follow directly across paragraphs: defaults shrink total SHU for timely borrowers too, voluntary-deposit interest is a cooperative expense, reinvested SHU expands loanable funds, and revenue can drop without fewer borrowers when loans default.

# Answer

The statement **NOT** supported by the passage is that **a member's mandatory savings grow through compound interest because they are never withdrawn during membership** (*Simpanan wajib seorang anggota ikut tumbuh dengan bunga majemuk karena simpanan itu tidak pernah diambil selama ia menjadi anggota.*).
