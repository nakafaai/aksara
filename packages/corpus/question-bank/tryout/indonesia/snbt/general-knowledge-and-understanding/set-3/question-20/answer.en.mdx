This **KECUALI** ("except") question asks for the one statement the text doesn't support. None of the statements is written out in a single sentence. Each needs two or three sentences from different paragraphs, so check them one by one.

The statement about the SHU share of members who always pay on time is supported. Chain three sentences.

1. Sentence (16): with a bad loan, its interest doesn't become income, while interest on savings still has to be paid.
2. Sentences (4) and (5): costs, including interest on savings, are covered first from income, and only what's left becomes the SHU, the residual operating surplus. Lower income with the same costs means a smaller SHU.
3. Sentence (5): part of the SHU is shared among members. If the SHU shrinks, every member's share shrinks too, including members who always pay on time.

The statement that interest on voluntary savings is a cost is supported. Sentence (3) says voluntary savings usually earn interest, and sentence (4) says interest on savings is one of the costs covered from income.

The statement about SHU saved again is supported. Sentence (13) says SHU saved again as voluntary savings adds to the money that can be lent, and sentence (1) says the cooperative lends its money to members who need it.

The statement that income can fall while the number of borrowers stays the same is supported, even though it sounds odd. Sentence (4) says the cooperative's main income comes from interest paid by borrowers. Sentence (16) says interest on bad loans doesn't become income. So income can fall because some borrowers stop paying, without any borrower leaving.

The statement *Simpanan wajib seorang anggota ikut tumbuh dengan bunga majemuk karena simpanan itu tidak pernah diambil selama ia menjadi anggota* ("A member's mandatory savings also grow with compound interest because they're never withdrawn while that person is a member") isn't supported. It sounds reasonable because it stitches together two true pieces: sentence (2) says mandatory savings can't be withdrawn while the owner is a member, and sentence (8) says interest that isn't withdrawn is added to the balance. But a link is missing. Sentence (3) only mentions **voluntary savings that usually earn interest**, and the whole second paragraph, starting at sentence (6), is about interest on voluntary savings. The text never says mandatory savings earn interest. Without interest, there's no interest to add to the balance.

So the statement the text doesn't support is that a member's mandatory savings grow with compound interest because they're never withdrawn during membership.
