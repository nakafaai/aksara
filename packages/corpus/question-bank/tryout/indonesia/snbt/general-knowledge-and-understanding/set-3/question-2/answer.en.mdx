In sentence (7), the motifs of stamped batik repeat in a *seragam* way because they come from the same stamp. *Seragam* here means **identical in form to one another**, uniform. Its antonym is a word meaning varied or different, which is *beragam*.

Sentence (8) confirms the opposite direction: hand drawing in *batik tulis* gives lines that are **slightly different** in every part. That is the opposite of uniform.

The other options do not fit:

- *Sama* (same) points the same way as *seragam*, so it is not an antonym.
- *Teratur* (orderly) and *rapi* (neat) describe tidy arrangement, not difference in form. Both are close to *seragam*.
- *Berulang* (repeating) is used in the same sentence to describe stamped motifs, so it is not the opposite.

So the antonym of *seragam* in sentence (7) is *beragam*.
