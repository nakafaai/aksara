# Given

The passage contrasts the **popular explanation** (glass absorbing and re-emitting infrared) with **physicists' view** (blocked air exchange contributes more heating than glass properties). Sentence (<InlineMath math="13" />) adds that cabin temperature rises fastest in the opening minutes after parking.

# Asked

Which conclusion can **NOT** be drawn from the passage?

# Solution

> Hal itu membuat banyak fisikawan menilai bahwa terhalangnya pertukaran udara menyumbang lebih banyak pemanasan daripada sifat kaca, baik pada rumah kaca maupun pada mobil.

Test whether each option follows from linking sentences or **invents an unsupported comparison**.

## Finding the unsupported conclusion

Sentence (<InlineMath math="13" />) only notes that warming is fastest during the <Highlight>menit-menit awal</Highlight> ("opening minutes"), saying nothing about which mechanism dominates then. The passage's only comparison (sentence <InlineMath math="11" />) puts **blocked air exchange** ahead of glass properties with no time exception.

All four other options follow from combining sentences: atmospheric warming works via infrared radiation like the popular explanation, the popular theory would predict a cooler rock-salt box, glass still makes a smaller contribution, and a hot dashboard warms the cabin via both air contact and infrared.

# Answer

The conclusion that can **NOT** be drawn is that **in the first few minutes after parking, glass properties contribute more heating than blocked air exchange** (*Pada menit-menit awal setelah mobil diparkir, sifat kaca menyumbang lebih banyak pemanasan daripada terhalangnya pertukaran udara.*).
