Sentence (8) says the sea **recedes far** before the wave arrives, and sentence (9) says the seabed is exposed. So *surut* here means the water level drops and pulls back from the shore. The fitting synonym is *berkurang* (to decrease, go down).

The other options do not fit:

- *Pasang* is the opposite of *surut*: the sea level rising, high tide.
- *Meluap* means to overflow, the opposite direction.
- *Membeku* means to freeze, which does not fit seawater pulling back from a tropical shore.
- *Menguap* means to evaporate. Receding seawater does not vanish by evaporating. It pulls back toward the sea.

So the synonym of *surut* in sentence (8) is *berkurang*.
