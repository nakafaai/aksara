This question asks what *bersih* means in the phrase *sumbangan bersih* ("net contribution") in sentence (11). Sentence (11) on its own isn't enough, because several senses of *bersih* still sound possible there. The sentences after it decide the meaning.

Step one: see how the passage counts the soybeans' contribution.

- Sentences (12) and (13) say most of the soybean's nitrogen moves into the seeds, and those seeds are harvested and carried off the field.
- Sentence (14) compares two amounts: the nitrogen carried off with the seeds and the nitrogen the bacteria fixed from the air. If more is carried off, growing soybeans actually **reduces** the soil's nitrogen supply.

Step two: draw out the meaning. The soybeans' contribution to the soil can fall below zero. That's only possible if the contribution is counted as the nitrogen fixed minus the nitrogen carried off. So *bersih* here means **counted after the part carried off at harvest is subtracted**. KBBI, the official Indonesian dictionary, records this sense as *neto* ("net"), the amount left after costs, packaging, and so on are taken out, as in "net income."

The other options use other KBBI senses of *bersih*, and each one is ruled out by a particular sentence.

- "Purely from the air" uses the sense "not mixed with other elements or substances." It's the most tempting, because sentence (5) does stress nitrogen from the air. But an amount of pure air nitrogen can't be less than zero, while sentence (14) shows the contribution can make the soil lose nitrogen.
- "Not mixed with nitrogen from urea fertilizer" uses the same sense and falls for the same reason. Urea only comes up in sentence (15), as an extra reason, not as a way of counting the contribution.
- "Free of dirty substances" is the most common sense, but the passage never discusses pollution, and this sense doesn't explain why the harvest shrinks the contribution.
- "Given sincerely" uses the sense "sincere, without strings." Sentence (5) says the plant pays the bacteria back with food, so this isn't a gift with nothing in return.

So *bersih* in *sumbangan bersih* says the contribution is counted after the nitrogen carried off at harvest is subtracted.
