The term is not defined directly in sentence (9), so take its meaning from **the batik process described at the start**. The word *rintang* means to block or hinder.

Sentences (1) and (2) explain that parts of the cloth are covered with wax (*malam*) before dyeing, and the covered parts do not absorb color. The wax **blocks the dye** from reaching those parts. That is the dye-resist technique. Sentence (9) uses the term to say that machine-printed cloth does not use this method.

The other options do not fit:

- Machine printing is exactly what sentence (9) says does **not** use the dye-resist technique.
- Mixing dyes is not mentioned.
- Washing in boiling water removes the wax after dyeing, so it is not the blocking technique.
- Drawing with a pencil is not discussed.

So *teknik rintang warna* means covering parts of the cloth so they do not absorb dye.
