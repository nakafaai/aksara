Sentence (1) describes interest as extra money a saver receives **because the funds are kept at the bank**. The saver gives the bank something, the use of the money, and receives something back in return. So *imbalan* here means **compensation** (*balas jasa*).

The other options do not fit:

- *Hukuman* (punishment) is given for a wrongdoing, and saving is not one.
- *Pinjaman* (loan) is money lent that must be repaid. A saver does not repay interest.
- *Potongan* (deduction) means a reduction, while interest is an addition.
- *Sumbangan sukarela* (voluntary donation) is given expecting nothing back, while interest is given precisely in return for the deposit.

So *imbalan* is best replaced by *balas jasa*.
