A complete declarative sentence needs an independent clause with a subject and a finite verb.

Expression (6), *the weeks that followed*, is only a noun phrase. *The weeks* is its head, while *that followed* merely modifies that noun. Because the expression has **no independent predicate**, it cannot stand as a complete sentence. Therefore, expression (6) is the answer.

A complete version is *The weeks that followed passed quickly*. The finite verb **passed** supplies the main predicate for the subject *the weeks that followed*.
