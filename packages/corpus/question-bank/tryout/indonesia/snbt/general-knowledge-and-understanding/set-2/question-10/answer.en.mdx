Here you check five pairs of a reference word and what it points to. A reference word like *itu* ("that") or *keduanya* ("both") points back to something already mentioned. To test it, **swap the reference word for the proposed antecedent** and see whether the sentence still says what the passage means.

Test the pair about *sel-sel itu* ("those cells"). Sentence (7) says a genetic change that lets bacteria survive can appear in *a small share of cells*. Sentence (8) then says "sel-sel itu luput dari kematian dan menjadi cikal bakal generasi berikutnya, sedangkan sel-sel lain mati", meaning "those cells escape death and seed the next generation, while the other cells die". The phrase *sel-sel lain* ("the other cells") proves that *sel-sel itu* isn't all the cells, but the small share carrying the genetic change. This pair is correct.

The other four pairs fail the test.

- *keduanya* in sentence (2) isn't bacteria and viruses. The sentence says "antibiotik tidak menyembuhkan keduanya" ("antibiotics don't cure either of them"), and what gets cured is an illness: the colds and common flu named in that same sentence. Bacteria and viruses sit in sentence (1), so they look close, but they don't fit.
- *aturan itu* ("that rule") in sentence (4) isn't the lax oversight. What's "easily slipped past" is the rule from sentence (3), the requirement to hand out antibiotics only with a prescription. Oversight is just the nearest noun in the same sentence.
- *Sifat itu* ("that trait") in sentence (6) isn't the drug's ability. Sentence (6) says the trait "belongs to the bacteria", so it points to the state of bacteria that can no longer be inhibited (sentence 5), which is resistance.
- *Hal itu* ("that") in sentence (16) isn't the search for new drugs plus the effort to keep drugs effective. All three steps after the colon, choosing antibiotics that hit only a few kinds of bacteria, taking them as prescribed, and preventing infection, protect the drugs we already have. None of them finds a new drug, so it points only to keeping drugs effective.

So the correct pair is *sel-sel itu* in sentence (8), which points to the small share of cells carrying the genetic change.
