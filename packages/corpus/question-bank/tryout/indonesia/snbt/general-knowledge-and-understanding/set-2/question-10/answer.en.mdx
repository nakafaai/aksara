# Given

Five options pair a **reference phrase** from the *antibiotik* passage (*keduanya*, *aturan itu*, *Sifat itu*, *Hal itu*, *sel-sel itu*) with a proposed referent.

# Asked

Which reference-and-referent pair is accurate?

# Solution

> Perubahan genetik yang membuat bakteri mampu bertahan dari obat dapat muncul secara acak pada sebagian kecil sel. Ketika obat diberikan, sel-sel itu luput dari kematian dan menjadi cikal bakal generasi berikutnya, sedangkan sel-sel lain mati.

Substitute each candidate referent directly into the sentence to test **meaning and grammatical fit**.

## Testing all five reference pairs

| Reference | Proposed referent | Actual referent | Correct |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *keduanya* (<InlineMath math="2" />) | bacteria and viruses | common cold and flu (illnesses to cure) | **No** |
| *aturan itu* (<InlineMath math="4" />) | loose oversight | prescription requirement in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) | **No** |
| *Sifat itu* (<InlineMath math="6" />) | drug's ability | bacterial resistance in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) | **No** |
| *Hal itu* (<InlineMath math="16" />) | new drugs plus preserving old ones | only keeping existing drugs effective | **No** |
| *sel-sel itu* (<InlineMath math="8" />) | <Highlight>sebagian kecil sel</Highlight> with genetic change | small fraction of cells carrying the genetic change | **Yes** |

Only ***sel-sel itu*** fits cleanly: the contrast with *sel-sel lain mati* ("other cells die") confirms it refers specifically to the small group of cells with the genetic change in sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />).

# Answer

The accurate pair is ***sel-sel itu* in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) referring to the small fraction of cells carrying the genetic change** (*sel-sel itu pada kalimat (8) merujuk pada sebagian kecil sel yang membawa perubahan genetik*).
