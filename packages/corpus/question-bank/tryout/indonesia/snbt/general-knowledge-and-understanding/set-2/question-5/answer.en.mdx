# Given

The passage covers **separate settling of sand** and silt, branching river channels, reworking of deposits by waves and tides, and independent natural compaction of old sediment.

# Asked

Which statement is **NOT** consistent with the passage?

# Solution

> Meskipun begitu, pengendapan saja tidak menentukan rupa sebuah delta. Rupa akhirnya bergantung pada tarik-menarik antara pasokan sedimen dari sungai dan kekuatan laut yang mengolah kembali sedimen itu.

Verify each choice against the passage to isolate the **one claim explicitly contradicted** by paragraph two.

## Identifying the contradicted claim

Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) explicitly states <Highlight>pengendapan saja tidak menentukan rupa sebuah delta</Highlight> ("deposition alone does not determine a delta's shape"), and sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) attributes shape to the tug-of-war between river supply and marine forces. Claiming shape is **mainly determined by river sediment volume** directly contradicts this.

The other four statements match the passage: sand and silt settle in different places, removing dams does not halt natural compaction, waves and tides move already-deposited sediment, and accumulating deposits split river channels.

# Answer

The statement **NOT** consistent with the passage is that **a delta's shape is mainly determined by the amount of sediment the river carries to the mouth** (*Rupa sebuah delta terutama ditentukan oleh banyaknya endapan yang dibawa sungai ke muara.*).
