An antonym expresses the opposite meaning of another word.

In sentence (1), *increase* means to make the residents' vulnerability greater. The choice *reduce* expresses the opposite action: making that vulnerability smaller. Therefore, **reduce** is the correct answer.

*Eliminate* is stronger than *reduce*: it means removing the vulnerability entirely. The passage contrasts making vulnerability greater with making it smaller, so *increase* and *reduce* are the direct pair on the same scale.
