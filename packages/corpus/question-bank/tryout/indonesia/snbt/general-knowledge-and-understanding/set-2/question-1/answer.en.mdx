# Given

Paragraph one explains how deltas grow seaward, paragraph two establishes the **balance between river sediment and ocean forces**, and paragraph three shows how human activity can tip that balance so the shoreline retreats landward.

# Asked

What is the function of paragraph three in relation to the first two paragraphs?

# Solution

> Keseimbangan itu kini mudah terganggu oleh kegiatan manusia.

Track what <Highlight>Keseimbangan itu</Highlight> ("that balance") refers back to and how sentence (<InlineMath math="16" />) reverses the seaward growth of paragraph one.

## Connecting all three paragraphs

Paragraph three picks up the **river-versus-sea balance** from paragraph two. When dams trap sediment while compaction and groundwater pumping lower the land, sentence (<InlineMath math="16" />) shows the seaward growth described in paragraph one can reverse into **landward retreat** (shrinkage).

The distractors misstate that role. Paragraph three adds no fourth delta shape, relies on rather than rejects paragraph two's balance, never claims dams automatically stop growth, and treats compaction as subsidence rather than a final growth stage.

# Answer

Paragraph three **uses the balance condition from paragraph two to show that the land growth in paragraph one can reverse into shrinkage** (*Paragraf ketiga memakai syarat keseimbangan dari paragraf kedua untuk menunjukkan bahwa pertumbuhan daratan pada paragraf pertama dapat berbalik menjadi penyusutan.*).
