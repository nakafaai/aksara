The phrase *mata telanjang* (literally "naked eye") is an idiom, so its words cannot be taken literally. In sky watching it means **looking directly without any aid**, such as binoculars, a telescope, or special glasses.

Sentence (8) compares a lunar eclipse with a solar eclipse. A solar eclipse is dangerous to look at directly and needs eye protection, while a lunar eclipse is safe to watch **without any equipment**. That comparison confirms the meaning.

The other options do not fit:

- *Without clothing* is the literal meaning of *telanjang*, which does not apply to eyes.
- *Eyes wide open* has nothing to do with using an aid or not.
- *Without blinking* is not the meaning of this idiom.
- *Tired eyes* are also unrelated to how the eclipse is observed.

So *mata telanjang* means looking without aids such as binoculars or special glasses.
