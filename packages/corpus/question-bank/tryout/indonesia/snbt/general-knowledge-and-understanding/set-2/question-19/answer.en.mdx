# Given

Paragraph two details the **social and economic functions of urban *kampungs*** (lodging, job networks, home businesses, low rent). Paragraph three questions evictions to peripheral flats and presents on-site upgrading.

# Asked

What is the role of paragraph three in relation to paragraph two?

# Solution

> Cap itu kerap dijadikan alasan untuk menggusur, padahal warga yang dipindahkan ke rumah susun di pinggir kota dapat kehilangan pelanggan dan jaringan kerja yang selama ini menopang penghasilan mereka.

Trace how paragraph three **applies paragraph two's kampung functions** as the standard for judging urban policy.

## Using kampung functions to evaluate policy

In sentence (<InlineMath math="12" />), the author challenges evictions because displaced residents lose <Highlight>pelanggan dan jaringan kerja</Highlight> ("customers and job networks"), directly recalling the home businesses (sentence <InlineMath math="8" />) and kin networks (sentence <InlineMath math="6" />) from paragraph two. Sentences (<InlineMath math="13" />) to (<InlineMath math="15" />) then put forward **in-place upgrading** instead.

The distractors misread that link: customers and networks are reused rather than new examples, the author doubts the slum label rather than accepting it, evictions stem from the slum label rather than density, and *kampungs* remain the sole focus.

# Answer

Paragraph three **uses the kampung functions described in paragraph two as the basis for judging policy toward kampungs, questioning eviction and proposing on-site upgrading** (*Paragraf ketiga memakai fungsi kampung kota yang diuraikan di paragraf kedua sebagai dasar untuk menilai kebijakan terhadap kampung: penggusuran dipersoalkan, perbaikan di tempat diajukan.*).
