You judge four statements. Each one needs **two sentences together** to decide, so don't stop at the first sentence that seems to fit. A statement is Sesuai ("consistent") when the passage guarantees it, and Tidak sesuai ("not consistent") when the passage contradicts it or doesn't support it.

**Statement 1**: Tidak sesuai. Sentence (14) does say new classes of antibiotics have rarely been found for a long time. But sentence (15) says "selain mencari obat baru, dunia kesehatan berupaya agar antibiotik yang kini tersedia tetap ampuh", meaning "besides searching for new drugs, health care works to keep today's antibiotics effective". *Selain* ("besides") means the search for new drugs goes on alongside the effort to protect existing ones. The statement sounds reasonable, but "stopped searching" contradicts sentence (15).

**Statement 2**: Sesuai. Sentence (6) says resistance belongs to the bacteria, not to the body of the person taking the drug. Sentence (13) says resistant bacteria can pass from one person to another. So someone who has never taken antibiotics can still catch already resistant bacteria. It sounds wrong, but the passage guarantees it.

**Statement 3**: Sesuai. Sentence (13) says resistant bacteria can pass to other people, and sentence (11) says the gene for resistance can be moved to other bacteria, including other species. Chain them: bacteria carrying the gene move into another person, and the gene then moves into another species there.

**Statement 4**: Tidak sesuai. Sentence (3) does say antibiotics may only be handed out with a prescription. But sentence (4) says that where oversight is lax, the rule is easily slipped past, so the drug can still be bought without one. A rule that requires a prescription doesn't guarantee the drug can *only* be obtained with one.

So statements 2 and 3 are consistent with the passage, while statements 1 and 4 are not.
