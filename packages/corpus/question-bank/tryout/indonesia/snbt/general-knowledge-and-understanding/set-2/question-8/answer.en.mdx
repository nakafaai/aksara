# Given

The passage covers **loosely enforced prescription rules** (sentences <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />), resistance residing in bacteria and jumping across species (sentences <InlineMath math="6" /> and <InlineMath math="11" />), person-to-person spread (sentence <InlineMath math="13" />), and both seeking new drugs and preserving existing ones (sentence <InlineMath math="15" />).

# Asked

Is each of the four statements **Sesuai** (supported) or **Tidak sesuai** (contradicted or unsupported)?

# Solution

> Oleh karena itu, selain mencari obat baru, dunia kesehatan berupaya agar antibiotik yang kini tersedia tetap ampuh selama mungkin.

Evaluate each statement by **combining two linked sentences** rather than stopping at the first matching sentence.

## Checking all four statements on resistance

| Statement | Linked evidence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" /> | Sentence (<InlineMath math="15" />) says <Highlight>selain mencari obat baru</Highlight> ("besides seeking new drugs"), so searching has not stopped | **Tidak sesuai** |
| <InlineMath math="2" /> | Resistance is in bacteria (sentence <InlineMath math="6" />) and spreads <Highlight>dari satu orang ke orang lain</Highlight> (sentence <InlineMath math="13" />) | **Sesuai** |
| <InlineMath math="3" /> | Resistant bacteria transfer between people (sentence <InlineMath math="13" />) and genes jump across species (sentence <InlineMath math="11" />) | **Sesuai** |
| <InlineMath math="4" /> | Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) notes weak enforcement allows buying antibiotics without a prescription | **Tidak sesuai** |

Watch how **Statements <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />** fail once you read the immediately following sentence: drug discovery continues alongside stewardship, and legal rules do not prevent over-the-counter sales.

# Answer

- Statement <InlineMath math="1" />: **Tidak sesuai**
- Statement <InlineMath math="2" />: **Sesuai**
- Statement <InlineMath math="3" />: **Sesuai**
- Statement <InlineMath math="4" />: **Tidak sesuai**
