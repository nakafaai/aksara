# Given

The passage covers **urban population growth sources** (births and reclassification of fringe villages), home-based businesses, eviction impacts of moving to flats, and Jakarta's in-place kampung improvement programme.

# Asked

Is each of the four statements **Sesuai** (supported) or **Tidak sesuai** (unsupported or contradicted)?

# Solution

> Perbaikan seperti itu juga lebih mungkin bertahan bila warga ikut menentukan bagian mana yang dibenahi lebih dulu.

Check **modal strength** (*lebih mungkin*, "more likely", versus *akan*, "will") and separate eviction to flats from on-site upgrading.
