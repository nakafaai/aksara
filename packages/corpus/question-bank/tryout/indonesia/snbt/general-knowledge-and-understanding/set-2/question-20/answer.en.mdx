A statement matches the reading (*Sesuai*) only when the reading backs it **with the same strength**. Every statement here needs two or more sentences. There are two traps: a word stronger than the reading, and two things from different sentences merged as if they were one. First impressions mislead too: statement 1 sounds right but isn't, and statement 2 sounds impossible but is right.

**Statement 1**: *Tidak sesuai*. Two sentences are involved. Sentence (14) says the Jakarta program's experience shows that kampungs can be improved without evicting their residents. Sentence (15) adds that improvements are *lebih mungkin* ("more likely") to last when residents help decide what gets fixed first. "More likely" means the odds go up, not that success is certain. The words *akan bertahan* ("will last") turn a likelihood into a certainty, so the statement already fails.

**Statement 2**: *Sesuai*, even though it sounds impossible. Sentence (2) names two sources of urban growth besides villagers moving in: births in the city itself, and villages on the outskirts being reclassified as urban. Sentence (3) adds that reclassification doesn't depend on newcomers arriving. So a city's population can grow even if nobody moves in from a village.

**Statement 3**: *Sesuai*. Sentence (8) says homes in the urban kampung often double as businesses, like small shops, tailoring and cooking to order. Businesses like that live off nearby customers. Sentence (12) says residents moved to public housing blocks can lose the customers and work networks that support their income. They do get a new place to live, yet they can still be harmed.

**Statement 4**: *Tidak sesuai*. Sentence (13) says Jakarta's kampung improvement program fixed alleys, drains and clean-water facilities *di dalam kampung itu sendiri* ("within the kampung itself"). Sentence (14) confirms kampungs can be improved without eviction. The housing blocks appear in sentence (12) as where evicted residents go, not as part of the improvement program.

So statements 2 and 3 match the reading, while statements 1 and 4 don't.
