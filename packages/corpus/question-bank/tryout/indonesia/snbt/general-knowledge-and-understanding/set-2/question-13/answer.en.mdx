This question has two steps. First, pin down the sense of *menampung* in sentence (10). Second, find the one sentence that uses a different sense, even though the thing involved looks similar.

Sentence (10) says the port of Barus *menampung* forest produce from the interior before the goods are loaded onto ships. The goods come from many collectors in a region and are gathered in one place before being passed on. KBBI records this sense as **"to receive and collect goods produced in a region"**. KBBI also has a nearby sense: "to catch something that drips or pours from above", with the examples *menampung air hujan* ("to catch rainwater") and *menampung getah* ("to catch sap").

Now test each sentence with two questions: is the thing a regional product received from many sources, and is it gathered rather than caught as it drips?

- The tappers who *menampung* rubber latex in cups on the tree trunk use the sense "to catch". Latex is a farm product, and that's the trap. But the cup catches the latex dripping from the tapping cut, exactly like KBBI's example *menampung getah*. Nothing is received from many people and gathered. So this is the sentence with a different sense.
- The middleman who takes in rattan from the forest gatherers uses the sense from sentence (10). Rattan is forest produce, it comes from many gatherers, and it's collected and then sold to a factory.
- The fish auction that takes in catches from fishermen on several islands is the same. The fish are the area's sea produce, received from many fishermen and collected for auction.
- The cooperative warehouse that takes in unhusked rice from farmers in three districts is the same. The rice is the area's harvest, received from many farmers and collected.
- The port that *tidak sanggup lagi* ("can no longer") take in copra may feel different because it's about capacity. But KBBI uses an example with the same pattern for this sense: a port that can no longer take in goods from many islands. Copra from the nearby islands is still a regional product being received and collected.

So the sentence that uses *menampung* in a different sense from sentence (10) is the one about tappers catching rubber latex in cups.
