# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />) states that the port of Barus ***menampung*** ("receives and gathers") forest products from the interior before shipping. One choice uses *menampung* in a **different dictionary sense**.

# Asked

Which sentence uses *menampung* with a different meaning from sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />)?

# Solution

> Pelabuhan Barus menampung hasil hutan dari pedalaman sebelum barang itu dimuat ke kapal dan akhirnya sampai di pasar India, Teluk Persia, dan Tiongkok.

Distinguish two KBBI senses of *menampung*: **receiving and gathering regional produce from suppliers** versus **catching dripping liquid from above**.

## Comparing the sense of menampung across options

| Sentence context | Sense of *menampung* | Same as sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rubber tappers catching latex in a bowl | Catching dripping liquid | **Different** |
| Village collector gathering rattan from forest gatherers | Receiving and pooling regional goods | **Same** |
| Fish auction hall taking catches from island fishers | Receiving and pooling regional goods | **Same** |
| Cooperative warehouse taking grain from farmers | Receiving and pooling regional goods | **Same** |
| Small port handling copra from nearby islands | Receiving and pooling regional goods | **Same** |

Even though latex is a forest product, a bowl strapped to a tree trunk **catches falling drops**, unlike <Highlight>menampung hasil hutan</Highlight> in sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />), which gathers goods from many suppliers.

# Answer

The sentence with a different meaning is **rubber tappers catching latex in small bowls attached to tree trunks** (*Para penyadap menampung getah karet dalam mangkuk kecil yang dipasang di batang pohon.*).
