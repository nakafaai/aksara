The word *menyiasati* comes from *siasat*, a plan or tactic for achieving something. There is also the word *menyiasat*, with a different meaning: to investigate. So decide the meaning from **what the waste banks do in sentence (9)**.

The *-nya* in *menyiasatinya* refers to the problem in sentence (8): residents stop sorting waste when prices crash. The waste banks then turn part of the waste into crafts or compost with steadier prices. That action is **a way to deal with** the price problem, not an investigation.

The other options do not fit:

- *Investigating carefully* is the meaning of *menyiasat*, and the waste banks in sentence (9) are not investigating anything.
- *Leaving it as it is* contradicts the act of processing waste.
- *Reporting to the government* is not mentioned.
- *Firmly refusing* does not fit, because the waste banks look for a way out of the problem rather than refusing it.

So *menyiasati* in sentence (9) means to find a way to deal with a problem.
