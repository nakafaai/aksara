Here you judge four statements one at a time. A statement is Sesuai ("consistent") when the passage supports it, even if the support comes from two sentences together. It's Tidak sesuai ("not consistent") when it contradicts the passage or **claims more than the passage says**. Watch limiting words such as *sebagian* ("part of"), *hanya* ("only"), and *lebih dekat daripada* ("closer than").

**Statement 1**: Sesuai. Sentence (13) says dams hold back *part* of the sediment, so the rest still reaches the mouth. Sentence (16) says the delta retreats when new sediment *can no longer keep up with* the sinking ground. Put them together: sediment still arrives, but not enough to match the sinking, so the delta can still retreat.

**Statement 2**: Tidak sesuai. Sentence (4) says the bigger, heavier sand settles first near the river mouth, while fine mud is carried farther. Sentence (5) only says clay in salt water sinks *faster* because it clumps, meaning faster than it would without clumping. The passage never says that clay overtakes sand and settles closer to the mouth.

**Statement 3**: Tidak sesuai. Sentence (14) says old deposits keep compacting, so the delta surface sinks slowly, with nobody doing anything. Sentence (15) only says pumping groundwater *speeds up* that sinking. So groundwater isn't the only condition for the surface to sink.

**Statement 4**: Sesuai. Sentence (8) says the sea *reworks that sediment*, meaning the sediment from the river. Sentence (10) says waves *sweep those deposits* along the coast. Waves change where the deposits sit and how they look, but the material is still sediment brought by the river.

So statements 1 and 4 are consistent with the passage, while statements 2 and 3 are not.
