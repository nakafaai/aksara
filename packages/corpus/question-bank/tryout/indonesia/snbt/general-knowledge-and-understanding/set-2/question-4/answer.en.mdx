# Given

The passage on *delta* describes **deposition of sand**, silt, and clay, reworking of river sediment by waves, partial sediment trapping by dams, and land subsidence from natural compaction plus groundwater extraction.

# Asked

Is each of the four statements **Sesuai** (supported) or **Tidak sesuai** (unsupported or contradicted)?

# Solution

> Bila sedimen baru tidak lagi mampu mengimbangi turunnya tanah, laut akan menggerus tepi delta dan garis pantainya bergeser ke arah daratan.

Test each statement by tracking qualifiers such as ***sebagian*** ("part of"), ***lebih cepat*** ("faster"), and ***hanya*** ("only").
