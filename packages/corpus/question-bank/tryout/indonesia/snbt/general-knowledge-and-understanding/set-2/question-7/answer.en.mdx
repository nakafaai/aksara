# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) states that oral antibiotics also reach **gut bacteria**, not only disease-causing bacteria. Sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />) states that **every antibiotic use, even when prescribed**, participates in selecting bacteria.

# Asked

Which statement is **DEFINITELY** true based on the passage?

# Solution

> Obat yang diminum juga mengenai bakteri yang hidup di usus, bukan hanya bakteri penyebab penyakit. Karena itu, setiap pemakaian antibiotik, bahkan yang sesuai resep, ikut menyeleksi bakteri, sedangkan pemakaian yang tidak perlu menambah seleksi tanpa memberi kesembuhan apa pun.

Find the statement **strictly guaranteed** by combining sentences in the text without any unstated assumption.

## Combining sentences nine and ten

When a patient takes a prescribed oral antibiotic for a urinary tract infection, sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) guarantees it <Highlight>juga mengenai bakteri yang hidup di usus</Highlight> ("also affects gut bacteria") and sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />) guarantees even prescribed use <Highlight>ikut menyeleksi bakteri</Highlight>. Therefore selecting gut bacteria is **certain**.

The distractors fail certainty: antibiotics filter rather than mutate bacteria (and mutations only *may* appear), narrow-spectrum drugs are not guaranteed to spare gut bacteria completely, sentence (<InlineMath math="11" />) contradicts restricting gene transfer to one species, and the text does not compare per-dose selection strength.

# Answer

The statement that is **definitely** true is that **a patient taking prescribed antibiotics for a urinary tract infection also selects bacteria in their gut** (*Pasien yang meminum antibiotik sesuai resep untuk infeksi saluran kemih ikut menyeleksi bakteri di ususnya.*).
