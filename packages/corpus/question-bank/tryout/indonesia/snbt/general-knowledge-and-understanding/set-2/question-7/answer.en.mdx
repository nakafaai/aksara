The prefix *pe-* can mark a person who does something, a person with a certain trait, or **a tool for doing something**. First decide what *pemecah* means in sentence (10).

Sentence (10) mentions "a simple bamboo *pemecah ombak*". This bamboo object is installed to break the waves, so *pemecah* means **a tool for breaking** (a breakwater).

| Word | Meaning of *pe-* |
| --- | --- |
| *pelari* | A person who runs |
| *pengering* | A tool for drying |
| *pemalu* | A person who is shy |
| *penduduk* | A person who lives in a place |
| *petinju* | A person whose job is boxing |

Only *pengering* marks a tool. The word *rambut* (hair) and the phrase *tidak menyala* (does not switch on) show that it is an object, a hair dryer, not a person.

So *pe-* in *pemecah* has the same meaning as *pe-* in *pengering*: a tool for doing something.
