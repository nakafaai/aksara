# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) says newcomers first receive ***tumpangan*** from relatives. Sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) explain they **sleep for months in the front room of a relative's house** before renting their own room.

# Asked

What does the word *tumpangan* mean in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />)?

# Solution

> Selama berbulan-bulan, mereka tidur di ruang depan rumah kerabat itu sambil mencari kerja lewat jaringan yang dimiliki kerabatnya.

Use sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) to choose KBBI's **temporary lodging** sense rather than the common "ride/vehicle" sense.

## Pinpointing the lodging sense from sentence six

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) specifies that newcomers <Highlight>tidur di ruang depan rumah kerabat</Highlight> ("sleep in the front room of the relative's house") for months, and sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) notes they rent a room only after earning steady income. Thus *tumpangan* means **temporary lodging at a relative's house**.

The distractors miss these details: it is not transport from the village, not a rented room (which comes later), not a job in a family business, and no cash allowance is mentioned.

# Answer

In sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />), *tumpangan* means **temporary lodging at a relative's house** (*tempat tinggal sementara di rumah kerabat*).
