This question has two stages. First, find what the affix *peN-an* means in *pengendapan* ("deposition") in sentence (7). Second, find what each italic word means **in its own sentence**, because a *peN-an* word can mean a process in one sentence and a place or a result in another.

Stage one. Sentence (7) reads "pengendapan saja tidak menentukan rupa sebuah delta", meaning "deposition alone doesn't decide a delta's shape". It talks about the river's load settling, as described in the first paragraph. KBBI defines *pengendapan* as the laying down of solid material carried by rivers, wind, glaciers, or seawater, which is a process. Careful: *pengendapan* isn't *endapan*. *Endapan* is the material, *pengendapan* is the process.

Stage two. Check each sentence.

| Word | Clue in the sentence | Meaning per KBBI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *pengungsian* | residents "stay at" it | a place of refuge |
| *pendapatan* | "enough to buy seeds" | earnings, a result of work |
| *pendaratan* | boats "dock at" it | a landing place |
| *pemberian* | the cloth was grandma's *pemberian* | something given, a gift |
| *penyeberangan* | "halted during high waves" | the process or act of crossing |

Only *penyeberangan* ("crossing") means a process. What gets halted during high waves is the activity of crossing, not a place.

The traps run in both directions.

- You often hear *pendaratan* and *pengungsian* as processes, as in an aircraft landing. In these sentences the word *di* ("at") marks a place, so both mean a place.
- *Pemberian* can mean a process too, but "kain tenun itu pemberian neneknya" ("that woven cloth was a gift from her grandma") points to the cloth as the thing given.
- *Pendapatan* here means income, which is a result.
- The other way round, *penyeberangan* often shows up as a place, as in *jembatan penyeberangan* ("footbridge"), yet in this sentence it means a process.

So the affix in *pengendapan* in sentence (7) means the same as the affix in *penyeberangan* in the sentence about crossings halted during high waves: both name a process.
