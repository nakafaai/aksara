Sentence (3) uses the paired words *not only ... but also*. This pair connects the humanitarian concern and the development-investment concern as equally relevant aspects of disasters.

Because *also* already follows the blank, **but** is the conjunction that completes the pair.

The completed sentence is: *Disaster risk is not only a humanitarian concern, but also an issue for development investment*.
