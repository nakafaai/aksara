Sentence (5) describes sunlight that **passes through Earth's atmosphere** and changes direction toward the Moon. In science, light that bends when it passes into a medium of different density is said to be **refracted**, *dibiaskan* in Indonesian.

The other options do not fit:

- *Dipantulkan* (reflected) means light bounces off a surface and comes back, not that it passes through the atmosphere and bends.
- *Diserap* (absorbed) means light is taken in and not passed on, while sentence (5) says the light reaches the Moon.
- *Dipadamkan* (extinguished) means switched off, which does not fit sunlight.
- *Dipancarkan* (emitted) means sent out from a source. The Sun does emit sunlight, but sentence (5) is about the light changing direction in the atmosphere.

So *dibelokkan* is best replaced by *dibiaskan*.
