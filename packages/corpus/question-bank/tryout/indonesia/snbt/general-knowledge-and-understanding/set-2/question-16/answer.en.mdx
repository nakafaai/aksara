# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="9" />) and (<InlineMath math="10" />) concede the flaws of urban *kampungs* (density, narrow lanes, informal permits) while highlighting affordable rent and walkability. Paragraph three praises **in-place kampung upgrading** without evictions.

# Asked

Which statement does **NOT** match the author's attitude toward urban *kampungs*?

# Solution

> Pengalaman itu menunjukkan bahwa kampung dapat dibenahi tanpa menggusur penghuninya. Perbaikan seperti itu juga lebih mungkin bertahan bila warga ikut menentukan bagian mana yang dibenahi lebih dulu.

Separate the author's **opposition to eviction** from any claim that flaws should be left unfixed.

## Distinguishing opposition to eviction from upgrading support

The author actively favours upgrading lanes, drains, and clean water inside the neighbourhood (<Highlight>dibenahi tanpa menggusur penghuninya</Highlight>, "upgraded without evicting residents"). Claiming the author thinks flaws **do not need fixing** directly contradicts paragraph three.

All four other options match the author's stance: acknowledging flaws without endorsing eviction, seeing density as both a drawback and an advantage, implying rent might not stay as cheap without density, and viewing flaws like narrow lanes as fixable on site.

# Answer

The statement that does **NOT** match the author's attitude is that **the author thinks the flaws of urban kampungs do not need fixing because they benefit newcomers** (*Penulis menganggap kekurangan kampung kota tidak perlu dibenahi karena kampung itu bermanfaat bagi para pendatang.*).
