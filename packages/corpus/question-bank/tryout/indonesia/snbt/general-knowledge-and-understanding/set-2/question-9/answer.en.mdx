This question has two stages. First, find what *longgar* means in sentence (4). Second, find the sentence whose italic word is the opposite of **that sense**, not of another sense of *longgar*. Three options use the same word *ketat*, so the word's form won't help you. Its meaning inside each sentence decides.

Stage one. Sentence (3) says the rule is already firm: antibiotics may only be handed out with a prescription. Sentence (4) then says "di tempat yang pengawasannya longgar, aturan itu mudah diterobos", meaning "where oversight is *longgar*, the rule is easily slipped past". So what's *longgar* isn't the rule, it's the guarding of it. KBBI lists this sense of *longgar* as not strict, easy to get through, said of guarding.

Stage two. The opposite of that sense is *ketat* in KBBI's sense "thorough, careful, and orderly (of guarding)". Now check each sentence.

| Sentence | Meaning of the italic word | Opposite of *longgar* in the sense of |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a school uniform rule that's *ketat*, with no exceptions | fixed, without exception (of rules) | not very binding (of rules) |
| the stadium gate is guarded *ketat* | thorough and careful (of guarding) | easy to get through (of guarding) |
| sports trousers that are too *ketat* | tight (of clothing) | loose, oversized (of clothing) |
| he's very *sibuk* ("busy") | not free | free time |
| a bolt that's *kencang* ("tight") again | not slack | not tight (of nails and screws) |

Only the guarded stadium gate is the opposite of *longgar* as sentence (4) uses it.

The strongest trap is the strict uniform rule. That sentence is about a rule, and so is the passage. But sentences (3) and (4) show that the prescription rule is already firm, while it's the oversight that's lax. In this passage a strict rule and lax oversight even exist side by side, so they aren't opposites here. Tight trousers, a busy person, and a tight bolt are opposites of *longgar* for clothing, time, and screws.

So *longgar* in sentence (4) is the opposite of *ketat* in the sentence about the stadium gate being closely guarded.
