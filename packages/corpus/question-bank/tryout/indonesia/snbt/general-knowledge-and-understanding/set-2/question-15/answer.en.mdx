Choose Ya (yes) or Tidak (no) for each word. *Enggan* means **unwilling or reluctant to do something**. In sentence (8), residents are *enggan* to sort waste because they feel the effort is not worth the return.

| Word | Can replace it? | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *tidak bersedia* | Yes | It means unwilling, the same as *enggan*. |
| *tidak mau* | Yes | It means "do not want to", the same as *enggan*. |
| *bersedia* | No | It means willing or ready, the opposite. |
| *berani* | No | It means brave, which changes the residents' reason. |

Note that *tidak bersedia* and *bersedia* differ only by the word *tidak* (not), yet their meanings are opposite. Only the form with *tidak* means the same as *enggan*.

So *tidak bersedia* and *tidak mau* can replace *enggan*, while *bersedia* and *berani* cannot.
