In this sentence, *sting* names the physical result or injury produced by the act of stinging. The question asks for another noun that names the product of an action. A place, activity, event, or response does not fit that meaning.

**Drawing** has that meaning because it can name the product created by drawing. *Storage room* names a place, *dance* an activity, *call* an event, and *scales* an instrument. Therefore, **drawing** names a product of an action, as *sting* does here.
