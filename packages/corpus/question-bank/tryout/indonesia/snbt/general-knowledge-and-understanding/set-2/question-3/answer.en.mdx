The conclusion needs to combine **planning, building safety, and preparedness**. The first paragraph connects investment to risk reduction. The second applies this idea to school sites, structures, and evacuation. The final paragraph explains that planning, maintenance, and drills must work together.

Therefore, disaster-risk reduction should guide development investment so buildings are safer and residents are better prepared. The school-only choice is too narrow. Early warnings or reinforcement alone cannot replace the other measures, and risks need assessment before investment, not after a project is finished.
