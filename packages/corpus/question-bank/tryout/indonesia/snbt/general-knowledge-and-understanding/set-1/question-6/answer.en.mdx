The word *pengepul* is built from *kepul* with the prefix *peng-*, which marks **a person who does a job**. In everyday Indonesian, a *pengepul* collects goods, such as scrap or harvest, from many sources and sells them on.

Sentences (5) to (8) support this meaning. Small *pengepul* process e-waste to recover copper and other metals, and sentence (8) says the method "quickly makes money". So a *pengepul* gathers used devices from many places and extracts their value to sell again.
