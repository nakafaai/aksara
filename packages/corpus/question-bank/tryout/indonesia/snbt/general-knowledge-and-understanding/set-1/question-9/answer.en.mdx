# Given

The passage explains that chilli price spikes are supply-driven, fresh chillies spoil rapidly, demand stays steady, interest rates work weakly, and local governments process fresh chillies into **long-lasting dried chilli or paste**.

# Asked

Which statement can **NOT** be concluded from the passage?

# Solution

> Langkah yang lebih sering ditempuh pemerintah daerah adalah memperlancar distribusi dari daerah surplus ke daerah defisit, mengatur pola tanam agar panen tidak menumpuk pada bulan yang sama, dan mengolah cabai segar menjadi cabai kering atau pasta yang tahan lama.

Check both the **claim and the causal reason** inside every option.

## Testing the reason behind each option

Processing chillies into <Highlight>cabai kering atau pasta yang tahan lama</Highlight> ("long-lasting dried chilli or paste") helps cushion price spikes on the **supply side** by overcoming rapid spoilage (sentences <InlineMath math="6" /> and <InlineMath math="10" />). Nothing in the passage claims it reduces how much chilli households buy.

All four remaining choices follow directly from linking sentences across paragraphs: excessive rain cuts harvests and fuels monthly inflation, interest rates cannot increase harvests, a single good's jump is not general inflation, and inelastic demand blunts rate hikes.

# Answer

The statement that can **NOT** be concluded is that **processing chillies into paste curbs price spikes because it reduces the amount of chilli households buy** (*Pengolahan cabai menjadi pasta membantu meredam lonjakan harga karena mengurangi jumlah cabai yang dibeli rumah tangga*).
