Sentence (7) and the temperature option share the order **Adverbial, Subject, Verb, Object**. Compare the parts directly.

| Function | Sentence (7) | Matching option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adverbial | *On Saturday, July 25* | *After sunrise* |
| Subject | *the number of visitors* | *the air temperature* |
| Verb | *reached* | *reached* |
| Object | *<InlineMath math="1{,}200" />* | *five degrees* |

The verb *reached* takes an object naming the amount or value attained. The other choices begin with a subject or use *formed* followed by a phrase of place rather than an object. Therefore, the matching sentence is After sunrise, the air temperature reached five degrees.
