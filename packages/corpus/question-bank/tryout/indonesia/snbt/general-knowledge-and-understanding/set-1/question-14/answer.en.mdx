## Identifying the speech level meaning of halus

Sentence (<InlineMath math="13" />) links speech levels to **marking respect**. Thus *halus* matches the KBBI sense "polite and respectful toward one's interlocutor".

# Answer

In sentence (<InlineMath math="11" />), *halus* means **polite and used to show respect to the person addressed** (*sopan dan dipakai untuk menunjukkan hormat kepada lawan bicara*).
