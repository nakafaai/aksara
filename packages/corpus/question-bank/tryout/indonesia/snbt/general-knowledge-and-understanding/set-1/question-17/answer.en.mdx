The word *berangsur* describes **how a change happens**. In sentence (7), the coral's color *berangsur* recovers after the algae grow back.

The recovery depends on the algae growing in the coral tissue. The algae do not appear all at once but increase over time, so the color returns stage by stage. The fitting meaning is **little by little** (*sedikit demi sedikit*).

The other options do not fit:

- *Quickly and all at once* and *suddenly* are the opposite of *berangsur*.
- *Only once in a while* means rarely, not a gradual process.
- *Repeatedly* means happening several times, while the color recovery is one gradual process.

So *berangsur* means little by little.
