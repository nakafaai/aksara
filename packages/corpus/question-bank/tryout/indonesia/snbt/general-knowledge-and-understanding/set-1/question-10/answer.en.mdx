# Given

Paragraph two consists of sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) to (<InlineMath math="9" />): **rapid spoilage and rain-vulnerable harvests** (supply), sticky household demand (demand), and the resulting price jump in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

# Asked

What is the main idea of paragraph two?

# Solution

> Akibatnya, penurunan pasokan yang kecil pun dapat mendorong kenaikan harga yang persentasenya jauh lebih besar daripada persentase penurunan pasokan itu.

A valid main idea must unite **both sides of the market** (vulnerable supply and rigid demand) together with the **disproportionate price effect**.

## Mapping all four sentences of paragraph two

| Sentences | Content | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) | Rapid spoilage and rain-damaged harvests | Vulnerable supply |
| (<InlineMath math="8" />) | Demand barely drops when prices rise | Inelastic demand |
| (<InlineMath math="9" />) | Percentage price rise is <Highlight>jauh lebih besar</Highlight> than supply drop | Concluding main point |

Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) ties the paragraph together: **fragile supply meets rigid demand**, so percentage price increases far exceed percentage drops in supply. The distractors either cover only one side, wrongly call the rise "proportional", or overstate bad weather as the sole main cause.

# Answer

The main idea is that **easily falling chilli supply meets hard-to-reduce demand, so percentage price changes are far larger than percentage supply changes** (*Pasokan cabai yang mudah merosot bertemu dengan permintaan yang sulit turun sehingga perubahan harga cabai, dalam persentase, jauh lebih besar daripada perubahan pasokannya*).
