Choose Ya (yes) or Tidak (no) for each word. The word *menuntut* has several senses: to demand forcefully, to sue in court, or to require. A good replacement must match **the sense in sentence (9)**.

Sentence (9) says that safe processing *menuntut* sorting components from the start and facilities able to capture hazardous materials. The subject is *pengolahan* (processing), which cannot demand or sue anyone. So *menuntut* here means **to require as a condition**.

| Word | Can replace it? | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *memerlukan* | Yes | Safe processing requires sorting, and the meaning stays the same. |
| *menggugat* | No | It means to take a case to court. |
| *membutuhkan* | Yes | It means the same as *memerlukan* (need) in this sentence. |
| *mendakwa* | No | It means to accuse someone of an offense. |

So *memerlukan* and *membutuhkan* can replace *menuntut*, while *menggugat* and *mendakwa* cannot.
