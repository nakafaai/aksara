# Given

Paragraph two concludes that in phosphorus-poor soil, mycorrhizal plants generally grow better because fungal hyphae reach distant phosphorus. Paragraph three examines what happens when **plants do not lack phosphorus**.

# Asked

What is the role of paragraph three in relation to the conclusion at the end of paragraph two?

# Solution

> Namun, persekutuan ini tidak selalu menguntungkan tanaman.

Compare the **condition behind paragraph two's benefit** with the boundary condition introduced in paragraph three.

## Linking the benefit condition to the boundary

Paragraph two relies on phosphorus being scarce near roots. Paragraph three opens with <Highlight>tidak selalu menguntungkan</Highlight> ("not always beneficial"): once phosphorus is plentiful, the fungus adds little while <Highlight>gula tetap mengalir</Highlight> ("sugar still flows") to it, turning the partnership into a burden. Thus paragraph three **qualifies and limits** the earlier conclusion rather than rejecting it.

The distractors misstate that role. The text never overturns the benefit in phosphorus-poor soil. Plants actually restrict fungal growth rather than paying more sugar, biofertiliser appears only in sentence (<InlineMath math="14" />), and heavy phosphate fertilisation is only an example, not an absolute condition.

# Answer

Paragraph three **limits the conclusion by showing that the benefit of hyphae disappears once phosphorus is no longer scarce, while sugar is still paid**.
