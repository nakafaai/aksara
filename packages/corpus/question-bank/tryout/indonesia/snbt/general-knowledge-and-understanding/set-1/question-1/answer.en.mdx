The word *penyangga* comes from *sangga*, meaning to hold something up so it does not fall. It has a literal sense and a figurative sense, so **the context of sentence (7)** decides its meaning.

Sentence (7) says that the supply inside the barn becomes a *penyangga* when harvests fail or the lean season (*paceklik*) arrives. What acts as the *penyangga* is **the stored rice**, and what it holds back is crop failure and the lean season. Stored rice does not hold up a building. It cushions the effect of a food shortage so that families still have food. So *penyangga* here means a reserve that softens the effects of a food shortage.
