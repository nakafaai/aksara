The word *tahan* has several senses, such as resistant to damage, able to bear pain, or patient. The antonym must come from **the sense used in sentence (5)**.

Sentence (5) says that rice grains still wrapped in husk are "more *tahan* against attacks by insects and fungi". Here *tahan* means not easily affected or damaged by an attack. Its opposite is **easily affected by an attack**, which is *rentan* (vulnerable). The opposite sentence would say that grains without husk are more *rentan* to insect attacks.

The other options are not antonyms:

- *Kebal* means immune, unable to be affected by an attack or disease. It points the same way as *tahan*, so it is a synonym.
- *Awet* means long-lasting and slow to spoil, which also points the same way as *tahan*.
- *Kuat* (strong) points the same way as *tahan*, because both describe the ability to hold out.
- *Tabah* is a synonym of *tahan* in a different sense, being steadfast in hardship. That sense is not used in sentence (5).

So the antonym of *tahan* in sentence (5) is *rentan*.
