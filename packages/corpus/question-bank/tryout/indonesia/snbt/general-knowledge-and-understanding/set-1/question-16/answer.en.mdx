The term for a relationship between living things depends on **who benefits and who is harmed**. Read sentence (2) to see what each side gets.

- The coral gets most of the sugar the algae produce by photosynthesis.
- The algae get shelter and the substances they need from the coral.

Both sides benefit, and sentence (3) calls it a mutually beneficial relationship. Such a relationship is called **mutualism** (*mutualisme*).

The other terms do not fit:

- Parasitism benefits one side and harms the other.
- Commensalism benefits one side while the other neither gains nor loses.
- Predation is a predator eating its prey.
- Competition is a contest for the same resource.

So the relationship between coral and algae in the passage is mutualism.
