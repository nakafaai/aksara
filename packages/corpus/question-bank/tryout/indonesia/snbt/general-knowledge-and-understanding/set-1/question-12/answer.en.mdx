# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />) states that the <InlineMath math="1988" /> General Guidelines for Term Formation ***menggariskan*** the search order for finding equivalents of new terms.

# Asked

Which word can replace *menggariskan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />) without changing the sentence's meaning?

# Solution

> Pedoman Umum Pembentukan Istilah edisi <InlineMath math="1988" /> menggariskan urutan pencarian padanan untuk istilah baru: bahasa serumpun, termasuk bahasa daerah, baru dirujuk jika bahasa Indonesia tidak menyediakan kata yang tepat, sedangkan bahasa asing baru dijadikan sumber jika kedua sumber itu sama-sama tidak menyediakannya.

Check the object of *menggariskan* to separate its **literal sense** ("to draw a line") from its **figurative sense** in KBBI.

## Matching the figurative rule setting sense

Because the object is <Highlight>urutan pencarian padanan</Highlight> ("an order of searching for equivalents"), the guidelines are formally laying down a rule. KBBI defines this figurative sense of *menggariskan* as **establishing rules or guidelines (*menetapkan*)**, which fits the sentence cleanly.

The other choices miss this meaning: *menggoreskan* is literal scratching, *menggarisbawahi* merely underscores something already present, *mencoret* crosses out or cancels, and *merintis* means pioneering.

# Answer

The word that can replace *menggariskan* without changing the meaning is ***menetapkan*** ("to establish / set down").
