# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) notes that fresh chillies spoil quickly and cannot be stored long **to prepare for *musim paceklik***, followed by sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) explaining sudden harvest drops during heavy rain.

# Asked

What does the phrase *musim paceklik* mean in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) in the context of the passage?

# Solution

> Cabai segar cepat busuk sehingga tidak dapat disimpan lama untuk berjaga-jaga menghadapi musim paceklik.

Read the **purpose of stockpiling** in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) alongside sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) to pick the right dictionary sense.

## Matching the dictionary sense to stockpiling

People store food <Highlight>untuk berjaga-jaga menghadapi musim paceklik</Highlight> ("as a precaution for lean times") so they have supplies when the item becomes scarce. Combined with sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) on collapsing chilli harvests, *musim paceklik* refers to a **period when chilli supply in the market is scarce**.

The distractors pick wrong senses or contradict the text. Stockpiling does not help a buyer slump, the text blames excessive rain rather than drought, the context concerns chillies rather than all foods, and crashing prices reflect oversupply rather than scarcity.

# Answer

In sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />), *musim paceklik* means **a period when the market supply of chillies is scarce** (*masa ketika pasokan cabai di pasar sedang sedikit*).
