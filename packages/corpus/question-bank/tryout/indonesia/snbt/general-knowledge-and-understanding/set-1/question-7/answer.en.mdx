The Indonesian circumfix *pe-an* can mark a process, a result, a place, or a thing. First decide **what *pengolahan* means in sentence (5)**, then find the word with the same meaning.

Sentence (5) says that "*pengolahan* of e-waste is still carried out by small collectors". Something that is *carried out* is an activity, so *pengolahan* means **the process of processing**.

Now test each word:

| Word | Meaning of *pe-an* | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Penginapan* | Place | A place to stay overnight. |
| *Pendapatan* | Result | The money earned. |
| *Pembangunan* | Process | The activity of building the bridge, which "finished earlier". |
| *Pemandian* | Place | A place to bathe in hot water. |
| *Pemandangan* | Thing seen | What is seen from the hilltop. |

Only *pembangunan* marks a process. The word *selesai* (finished) confirms an activity that runs and then ends.

So *pe-an* in *pengolahan* has the same meaning as *pe-an* in *pembangunan*: a process.
