The question asks what happens if *menjulur* in sentence (7) is replaced by *menjulurkan* without changing any other word: "Hifa jamur ... dapat menjulur melewati lapisan tanah yang sudah miskin fosfor itu ..." ("The fungal hyphae ... can *menjulur* past that phosphorus-poor layer of soil ..."). The key is the difference between the forms *meN-* and *meN-kan*.

Step one is comparing the two words. The KBBI (the official Indonesian dictionary) defines *menjulur* as "to come out and stretch", like a gecko's tongue shooting out to catch a mosquito. It needs no object, because what stretches out is the subject itself, the hyphae. The KBBI defines *menjulurkan* as "to put out", as in "putting one's head out of the window". The suffix *-kan* here makes the verb **demand an object**, the thing that gets put out.

Step two is looking for that object in sentence (7). Right after the verb comes *melewati lapisan tanah ...* ("passing the layer of soil ..."). *Melewati* is another verb, not a thing being stuck out. Since no other word may change, the sentence has no object for *menjulurkan*. So the sentence becomes incomplete, and a reader would ask, "The hyphae put out what?"

Here's why the other options are wrong.

- "The meaning stays the same" forgets that *-kan* turns a verb without an object into a verb with one. The root is the same, but the sentence structure changes.
- "Stretching out for the plant's benefit" uses the "for someone else" meaning of *-kan*, as in *membelikan* ("to buy for someone"). That meaning shows up on verbs that already take an object, and it still needs someone to benefit, who also isn't in this sentence.
- "The sentence becomes passive" is wrong because *menjulurkan* is still an active verb. Its passive form is *dijulurkan*.
- "The layer of soil becomes the thing put out" takes the nearest noun as the object. The soil layer is the place being passed, and the word *melewati* sits between the verb and the soil layer, so the layer doesn't fill the object slot.

So *menjulurkan* demands an object, something the hyphae put out, but there isn't one, so sentence (7) becomes incomplete.
