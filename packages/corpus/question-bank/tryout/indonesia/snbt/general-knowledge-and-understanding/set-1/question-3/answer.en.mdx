Sentence (8) joins two independent clauses with only a comma:

> Brain activity continues during sleep, however it changes across stages and differs from wakefulness.

In formal English, *however* is a conjunctive adverb. It cannot join those clauses after a comma alone. We could write a semicolon before *however*, or we can keep the existing comma and replace *however* with the coordinating conjunction *but*:

> Brain activity continues during sleep, but it changes across stages and differs from wakefulness.

*Therefore* expresses a result and, like *however*, cannot connect these independent clauses with a comma alone. The other options do not preserve the contrast between continuing activity and changing activity. The correct replacement is **but**.
