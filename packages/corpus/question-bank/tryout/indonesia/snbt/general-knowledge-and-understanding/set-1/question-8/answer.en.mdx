First, work out the relation *bobot : indeks harga konsumen* ("weight : consumer price index") from the reading. By sentence (2), the consumer price index is one number that sums up the prices of a basket of goods and services. By sentence (3), each item gets a weight, and that weight decides how far a price rise in that item moves the index. So the relation has **two features at once**:

1. the second word is one combined figure built from many parts,
2. the first word decides how much each part counts in that combined figure.

Now test each pair against both features. (*SKS* is the credit-unit count of a university course, and *IPK* is the cumulative grade point average.)

| Pair | Second word is a combined figure | First word weighs each part |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nilai mata kuliah : IPK (course grade : GPA) | yes | no, the grade is the part's content |
| berat badan : timbangan (body weight : scale) | no | no, this is the literal sense of *bobot* |
| SKS : mata kuliah (credits : course) | no, a course is only one part | yes |
| dosis : khasiat obat (dose : drug effect) | no | only similar, a dose sets how strong an effect is |
| SKS : IPK (credits : GPA) | yes | yes |

Only *SKS : IPK* meets both. The GPA is one number that sums up the grades of many courses. Each course has its credits, and the more credits it carries, the more its grade affects the GPA. That's the same pattern as the weight of an item in the consumer price index.

Here's why the other options are wrong.

- *Nilai mata kuliah : IPK* is a part and its combined figure, but the course grade matches an item's price, not its weight. What weighs that grade is the credit count.
- *Berat badan : timbangan* falls for the literal sense of *bobot*, which is physical weight. In the reading, *bobot* means how much something counts in a calculation, not a weight read off a scale.
- *SKS : mata kuliah* does contain the weighting, but its second word is only one part. The matching pair in the reading would be *bobot : barang* (weight : item), not *bobot : indeks*.
- *Dosis : khasiat obat* is about how big an effect is, but there's no combined figure built from many parts.

So the pair with the same relation as *bobot : indeks harga konsumen* is *SKS : IPK*: what weighs each part, and the combined figure it weighs.
