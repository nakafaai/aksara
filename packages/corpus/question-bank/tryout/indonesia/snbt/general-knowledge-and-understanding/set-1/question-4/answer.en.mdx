The word *tersebut* (the said, that) points back to something already mentioned. The referent is usually in **the sentence right before the phrase** and must be a rule.

Sentence (8) says that in some communities rice from the *leuit* may not be bought or sold and is taken only as the family needs it. It contains two provisions: a ban on selling the rice and a limit on taking it. Sentence (9) then gives the result: the food reserve stays intact. That result makes sense because the rice is neither sold nor taken in excess.

The other options do not fit:

- Storing rice in bundles on the stalk is a storage method from the first paragraph, not a community rule.
- Building on stilts is a feature of the building, not a rule about using the rice.
- The passage says the rice is not milled right away, so it mentions no duty to mill it.
- The passage mentions no distribution of rice to other residents. The rice is taken as the owner's family needs it.

So *aturan tersebut* (that rule) refers to the ban on selling rice from the *leuit* together with taking it only as the family needs it.
