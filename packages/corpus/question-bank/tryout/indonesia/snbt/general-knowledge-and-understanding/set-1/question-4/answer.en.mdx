# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="12" />) states that sugar still flows to the remaining fungus even though ***kirimannya*** ("its delivery") no longer matters. Sentence (<InlineMath math="13" />) adds that ***hal itu*** ("that") can make mycorrhizae burden rather than help the plant.

# Asked

What do *-nya* in *kirimannya* (sentence <InlineMath math="12" />) and *hal itu* (sentence <InlineMath math="13" />) refer to respectively?

# Solution

> Meskipun demikian, gula tetap mengalir ke jamur yang masih ada, padahal kirimannya tidak lagi berarti. Hal itu dapat membuat mikoriza lebih banyak membebani daripada membantu tanaman.

Resolve each referent separately using the **contrast word *padahal*** ("whereas") and the **cost-versus-benefit logic** of sentence (<InlineMath math="13" />).

## Resolving both referents in order

| Reference | Contextual clue | Exact referent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *-nya* in *kirimannya* | Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) pairs plant sugar with fungal nutrients, while <Highlight>padahal</Highlight> contrasts sugar with the other partner's supply | **The fungus (*jamur*)** |
| *hal itu* in sentence (<InlineMath math="13" />) | To <Highlight>lebih banyak membebani daripada membantu</Highlight>, ongoing sugar cost must pair with lost phosphorus benefit | **Sugar still flowing while fungal phosphorus no longer matters** |

The distractors either pick the wrong partner (*tanaman*) or capture only half of sentence (<InlineMath math="12" />). Losing benefit alone does not create a net burden without continuing sugar outflow.

# Answer

They refer respectively to **the fungus, and sugar continuing to flow to the fungus even though the phosphorus it delivers no longer matters** (*jamur, dan gula yang terus mengalir ke jamur padahal fosfor kiriman jamur tidak lagi berarti*).
