# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) states that frequent appearance in national media is not enough for a regional word to become established in Indonesian: it becomes established only once **speakers outside its home region** use it in **everyday conversation** **without feeling they are borrowing it**.

# Asked

Which paraphrase preserves the exact meaning of sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />)?

# Solution

> Kata daerah tidak menjadi kosakata bahasa Indonesia yang mapan hanya karena sering muncul di media nasional, tetapi baru setelah penutur di luar daerah asalnya memakainya dalam percakapan sehari-hari tanpa merasa sedang meminjam.

Verify **both logical halves**: media exposure is neither sufficient nor required, and actual establishment requires all three usage conditions.

## Checking every condition against the options

The accurate paraphrase states that <Highlight>sering muncul di media nasional belum cukup</Highlight> ("appearing often in national media is not enough") and preserves all three conditions intact: **speakers outside the region of origin**, **everyday speech**, and **no sense of borrowing**.

Each distractor alters one condition: treating media exposure as sufficient, switching to speakers inside the home region, turning media exposure into a mandatory joint condition (*sekaligus*), or replacing everyday conversation with formal writing.

# Answer

The exact paraphrase is: **Frequent appearance in national media is not enough, and a regional word becomes established only after speakers outside its home region use it in everyday conversation without feeling they are borrowing**.

(*Sering muncul di media nasional belum cukup. Kata daerah baru mapan setelah dipakai penutur dari luar daerah asalnya dalam percakapan sehari-hari tanpa merasa meminjam.*)
