Mark each statement Benar (true) or Salah (false) by comparing it with the sentence that covers it. Watch **limiting words** such as *semua* (all) and *beberapa* (some), because they change the scope of a claim.

| Statement | Value | Deciding sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rice is stored as milled rice | False | Sentence (4): rice is stored in bundles on the stalk, not as loose grain or milled rice. |
| The space under the floor reduces moisture | True | Sentence (3): air flows underneath and ground moisture is kept from seeping up. |
| All communities ban selling the rice | False | Sentence (8) mentions only **some communities**. |
| The husk protects against insects | True | Sentence (5): grains wrapped in husk resist insects and fungi better. |

The third statement is wrong because it widens *some* into *all*. The first statement is wrong because it directly contradicts sentence (4).

So the second and fourth statements are true, and the first and third are false.
