# Given

The **peatland** passage combines two complementary paragraphs:

- Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="6" />): natural peat stores water, yet drained peat catches fire easily, burns underground, and spreads thick smoke.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="7" /> to <InlineMath math="9" />): building and maintaining canal blocks to keep peat wet is cheaper and more effective than fighting fires.

# Asked

The main idea of the entire passage.

# Solution

A complete main idea links the **hazard in paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> with the prevention in paragraph <InlineMath math="2" />**: drained peat burns easily, making keeping it wet an essential preventive measure.

> Salah satu upaya pencegahan adalah <Highlight>membangun sekat di kanal agar air tertahan dan gambut kembali basah</Highlight>.

## Why the Other Four Options Contradict the Text

Each distractor directly **contradicts explicit facts**.

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="1" />) says plant remains do *not* decay fully and accumulate over thousands of years.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) says smoke harms human health as well as aviation.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) says underground peat fires are hard to extinguish even when surface flames vanish.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) stresses that canal blocks must be maintained so they do not break.

# Answer

**Drained peat catches fire easily, so keeping it wet is an important preventive measure**.

(*Gambut yang dikeringkan mudah terbakar, sehingga menjaganya tetap basah menjadi pencegahan penting.*)
