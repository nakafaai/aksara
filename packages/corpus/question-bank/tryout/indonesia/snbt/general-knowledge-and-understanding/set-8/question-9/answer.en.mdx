# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **lontar** passage directly states the goal of digitization:

> Karena itu, beberapa lembaga kini memotret dan mengetik ulang isi lontar <Highlight>agar pengetahuan di dalamnya tetap dapat dipelajari walaupun naskah aslinya rusak</Highlight>.

# Asked

The main purpose of institutions photographing and retyping lontar manuscripts according to the passage.

# Solution

The purpose clause beginning with *agar* (so that) in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) answers directly: **so the manuscript's contents can still be studied even if the original is damaged**.

## Checking the Other Options

Every distractor introduces **unmentioned motives** or confuses steps.

- Selling originals to foreign collectors is never mentioned.
- Photographing preserves text rather than stopping termites and mold from attacking leaves.
- The text does not ask residents to stop keeping manuscripts.
- Boiling and drying belong to leaf preparation (sentence <InlineMath math="2" />), unrelated to photographing.

# Answer

The main goal is **so the contents of the manuscript can still be studied even if the original manuscript is damaged**.

(*agar isi naskah tetap dapat dipelajari walaupun naskah aslinya rusak*)
