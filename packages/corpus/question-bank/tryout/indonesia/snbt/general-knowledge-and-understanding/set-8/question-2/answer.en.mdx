# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **Komodo dragon** passage states:

> Gigitannya mengandung <Highlight>bisa yang menghambat pembekuan darah</Highlight>, sehingga mangsa yang sempat lolos pun lama-kelamaan melemah.

# Asked

Why prey that escapes a Komodo dragon's bite gradually weakens according to sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

The result connector *sehingga* (so that) identifies the exact cause: **venom in the bite inhibits blood clotting**, causing prolonged bleeding that gradually weakens the escaped prey.

## Checking the Other Options

None of the other choices is **supported by the passage**.

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) says Komodo dragons ambush rather than chasing prey for days.
- Water sources, broken teeth, and prey getting lost in bushes are never mentioned.

# Answer

Escaped prey weakens because **venom in the bite inhibits blood clotting**.

(*bisa dalam gigitan menghambat pembekuan darah*)
