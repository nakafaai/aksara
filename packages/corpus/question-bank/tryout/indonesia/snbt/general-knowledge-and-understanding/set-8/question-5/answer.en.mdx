Sentence (8) names deer as the dragon's **prey**. So the relation *komodo : rusa* (Komodo dragon : deer) is **predator and prey**, with the predator first.

Cats prey on rats, so *kucing : tikus* (cat : rat) has the same relation in the same order.

The other pairs have different relations:

- *Ikan* (fish) lives in *air* (water), a creature and its habitat.
- *Burung* (bird) lives in a *sarang* (nest), a creature and its home.
- *Ayam* (chicken) produces *telur* (eggs), a producer and its product.
- *Petani* (farmer) works in a *sawah* (rice field), a worker and the workplace.

So the pair with the same relation is *kucing : tikus*.
