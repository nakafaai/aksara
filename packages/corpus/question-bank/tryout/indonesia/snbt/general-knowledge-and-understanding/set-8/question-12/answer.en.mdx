# Given

Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> of the **seaweed** passage describes how farmers cultivate seaweed by tying cuttings onto ropes in shallow coastal water until harvest.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) follows:

> <Highlight>Budi daya ini tidak memerlukan pupuk ataupun pakan</Highlight>, sehingga modalnya relatif kecil.

# Asked

The referent of *Budi daya ini* (this cultivation) in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

Both *ini* (this) and the detail *requires no fertilizer or feed* point squarely to **the rope-based seaweed cultivation described in paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />**.

## Checking the Other Options

All four distractors refer to **post-harvest processing or trading** rather than growing seaweed.

- Factory carrageenan processing, toothpaste and cosmetics manufacturing, sun-drying, and collector purchases happen after harvest and never involve fertilizer or feed.

# Answer

The phrase *Budi daya ini* refers to **the cultivation of seaweed on ropes described earlier**.

(*budi daya rumput laut pada tali yang diuraikan sebelumnya*)
