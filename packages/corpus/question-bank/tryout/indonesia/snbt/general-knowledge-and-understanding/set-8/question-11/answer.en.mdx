# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **seaweed** passage directly defines *karagenan* (carrageenan):

> Rumput laut kering diolah menjadi karagenan, yaitu <Highlight>bahan pengental yang dipakai dalam makanan, pasta gigi, dan kosmetik</Highlight>.

# Asked

The meaning of *karagenan* based on sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />).

# Solution

The definition cue *yaitu* (namely) in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) identifies *karagenan* directly as **a thickening agent processed from dried seaweed**.

## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor points to **a different element** mentioned elsewhere in the text.

- Clear shallow water is the farming habitat in sentence (<InlineMath math="1" />).
- Whitening and shedding describes a seaweed disease in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).
- Long ropes are the farming lines in sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />).
- Collectors (*pengepul*) are buyers in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Answer

Based on sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />), *karagenan* is **a thickening agent processed from dried seaweed**.
