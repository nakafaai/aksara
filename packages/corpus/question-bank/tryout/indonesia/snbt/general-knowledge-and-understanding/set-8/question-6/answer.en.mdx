The word *digoreskan* comes from *gores*, **a line made with a sharp object** (incised). Sentence (3) names the tool: a small pointed knife. So the letters are cut into the leaf with a sharp point.

Sentence (4) supports this: the incisions are rubbed with soot **afterwards** so the letters show black. So the letters are not written in ink to begin with.

The other options do not fit:

- The letters are not printed in ink. The black comes from soot rubbed in later.
- The reading does not mention a brush.
- Glue has nothing to do with making the letters.
- String is used to bind the sheets, not to form letters.

So *digoreskan* means cut in with a sharp point (*ditorehkan dengan ujung yang tajam*).
