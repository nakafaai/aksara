# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="3" />) and (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **lontar** passage describe how letters are made:

> Huruf-huruf <Highlight>digoreskan pada lembaran itu dengan pisau kecil yang runcing</Highlight>.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) adds that soot from burned candlenut is rubbed into the grooves afterward to darken them.

# Asked

The meaning of *digoreskan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />).

# Solution

Using a *small pointed knife* confirms that *digoreskan* means **incised or etched with a sharp tip** directly onto the palm leaf rather than written in ink from the start.

## Evaluating the Options

The **pointed knife** and the later soot step eliminate all four distractors.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *dicetak dengan tinta berwarna hitam* | Wrong, blackness comes later from soot in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) |
| *ditorehkan dengan ujung yang tajam* | Correct, matches etching with a sharp knife |
| *dilukis dengan kuas yang halus* | Wrong, no brush is used |
| *ditempelkan dengan lem dari getah pohon* | Wrong, no glue is used |
| *dijahit dengan benang berwarna* | Wrong, cord only binds the leaves (sentence <InlineMath math="5" />) |

# Answer

The word *digoreskan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) means **incised with a sharp tip** (*ditorehkan dengan ujung yang tajam*).
