The word *jebol* means **broken through, breached**, often said of embankments, walls, or dams. Sentence (9) gives the result: a *jebol* block **can no longer hold back water**, because water flows through the damaged part.

The other options do not fit:

- A block full of water can still hold water, so it does not match the stated result.
- Thick mud does not make a block fail to hold water.
- A freshly rebuilt block can hold water again.
- A block that is too tall still holds water.

So *jebol* means damaged and breached (*rusak dan berlubang*).
