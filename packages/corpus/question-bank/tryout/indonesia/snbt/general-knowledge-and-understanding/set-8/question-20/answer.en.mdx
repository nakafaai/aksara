# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **peatland** passage states:

> Meskipun demikian, sekat kanal perlu dirawat, sebab sekat yang <Highlight>jebol</Highlight> tidak lagi mampu menahan air.

# Asked

The meaning of *jebol* in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

# Solution

*Jebol* describes a dam, barrier, or wall that is **broken through and breached** so water pours past it. That matches the consequence stated in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): the block can no longer hold water.

## Comparing the Options

Only **damaged and breached** explains why a canal block stops holding water back.

| Option | Effect on Holding Water | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *penuh terisi air* | Still holds water | Wrong, not structural failure |
| *tertutup lumpur tebal* | Covered in mud, not breached | Wrong, not the meaning of *jebol* |
| *baru dibangun ulang* | Freshly rebuilt and functional | Wrong, opposite context |
| *rusak dan berlubang* | Damaged and breached | Correct, exact meaning of *jebol* |
| *terlalu tinggi* | Still blocks water | Wrong, does not leak |

# Answer

The word *jebol* in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) means **damaged and breached** (*rusak dan berlubang*).
