# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) of the **Komodo dragon** passage provide two time cues:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />): Komodo National Park was established in <InlineMath math="1980" />.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />): *Eleven years later*, UNESCO added it to the World Heritage list.

# Asked

The year UNESCO added Komodo National Park to the World Heritage list.

# Solution

Add the **eleven-year interval** to the <InlineMath math="1980" /> establishment date.

> <Highlight>Sebelas tahun kemudian</Highlight>, UNESCO memasukkan taman nasional itu ke dalam daftar Warisan Dunia.

## Computing the Listing Year

Adding the interval directly gives the **exact UNESCO year**:

<BlockMath math="1980 + 11 = 1991" />

The option <InlineMath math="1980" /> is the park establishment year, <InlineMath math="1969" /> subtracts <InlineMath math="11" /> by mistake, and <InlineMath math="1990" /> or <InlineMath math="1992" /> are off by one year.

# Answer

UNESCO added Komodo National Park to the World Heritage list in **<InlineMath math="1991" />**.
