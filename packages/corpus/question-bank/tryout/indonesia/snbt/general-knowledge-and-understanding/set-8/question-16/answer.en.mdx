# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />) of the **peatland** passage uses a figurative comparison:

> Dalam keadaan alami, <Highlight>lahan ini menyimpan banyak air, seperti spons raksasa</Highlight>.

# Asked

What the expression *seperti spons raksasa* (like a giant sponge) conveys about peatland.

# Solution

Placed right after *holds a great deal of water*, the image of a giant sponge means peatland **can absorb and store large amounts of water**.

## Evaluating the Options

Focusing on **water-holding capacity** eliminates distractors about color, portability, or dryness.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *berwarna kuning dan berpori besar* | Wrong, neither color nor soap foam is implied |
| *mudah dipindahkan* | Wrong, land is not portable |
| *dapat menyerap dan menyimpan banyak air* | Correct, matches storing huge amounts of water |
| *terbuat dari bahan buatan yang ringan* | Wrong, peat forms from plant remains (sentence <InlineMath math="1" />) |
| *selalu kering pada musim hujan* | Wrong, natural peatland stays wet |

# Answer

The simile shows that peatland **can absorb and store large amounts of water** (*dapat menyerap dan menyimpan banyak air*).
