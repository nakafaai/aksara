# Given

Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> of the **seaweed** passage identifies two risks for farmers:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): sudden shifts in water temperature and salinity can trigger disease causing seaweed to whiten and fall off.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): dried seaweed prices fluctuate with processing-factory demand.

# Asked

The situation most likely to cause losses for seaweed farmers based on the passage.

# Solution

A **sudden shift in seawater temperature right before harvest** matches sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) directly: disease whitens and strips the crop just before it can be sold.

> <Highlight>Perubahan suhu dan kadar garam air yang mendadak dapat memicu penyakit</Highlight> yang membuat rumput laut memutih dan rontok.

## Why the Other Four Situations Do Not Cause Losses

Each remaining option is **harmless or beneficial** according to the text.

- Higher fertilizer and feed prices do not affect farmers who use neither (sentence <InlineMath math="6" />).
- Stronger factory demand supports higher prices (sentence <InlineMath math="9" />).
- Clear, calm water is the ideal growing condition (sentence <InlineMath math="1" />).
- Collectors arriving early is not mentioned as a loss risk.

# Answer

**Seawater temperature changes suddenly right before harvest season**.

(*Suhu air laut berubah mendadak tepat menjelang musim panen.*)
