# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) of the **Komodo dragon** passage explains its hunting style directly:

> Komodo berburu dengan cara <Highlight>menyergap</Highlight>: ia diam menunggu di balik semak, lalu menerkam mangsa yang lewat.

# Asked

The meaning of *menyergap* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />).

# Solution

The explanation right after the colon describes **waiting motionless behind bushes and lunging suddenly** at passing prey. Thus *menyergap* means **attacking suddenly from hiding** (ambushing).

## Comparing the Options

Waiting still in cover clearly distinguishes **ambush** from the other four hunting methods.

| Option | Hunting Style | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *mengejar mangsa sampai jarak yang jauh* | Chasing over long distances | Wrong, it waits still |
| *membuntuti mangsa diam-diam* | Stalking from behind | Wrong, it waits in one spot |
| *memancing mangsa dengan umpan* | Luring with bait | Wrong, not mentioned |
| *menggali lubang jebakan* | Digging pit traps | Wrong, not mentioned |
| *menyerang tiba-tiba dari persembunyian* | Attacking suddenly from hiding | Correct, matches sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) |

# Answer

The word *menyergap* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) means **attacking suddenly from hiding** (*menyerang tiba-tiba dari persembunyian*).
