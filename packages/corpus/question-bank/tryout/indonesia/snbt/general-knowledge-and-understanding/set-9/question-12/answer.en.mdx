# Solution

*Ini* points directly to **fermentation of soybeans by mold**, introduced in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) and reinforced by over-fermentation signs in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

> Selama <Highlight>fermentasi, kapang menghasilkan enzim</Highlight> yang memecah sebagian protein dan lemak kedelai menjadi zat-zat yang lebih sederhana.

## Checking the Other Options

All four distractors refer to **earlier preparation steps** already finished before fermentation begins.

- Soaking, dehulling, and boiling occur in sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />).
- Wrapping in banana leaves happens in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />), not a biological process stopped before spore formation.

# Answer

The phrase *proses ini* refers to **fermentation of soybeans by mold**.

(*fermentasi kedelai oleh kapang*)
