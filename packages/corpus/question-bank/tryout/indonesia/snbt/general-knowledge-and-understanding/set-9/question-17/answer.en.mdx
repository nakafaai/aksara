# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="5" />) and (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **bamboo** passage explain its natural weakness:

> <Highlight>Pati menjadi makanan bagi kumbang bubuk, serangga kecil yang melubangi batang dari dalam sampai bambu menjadi rapuh</Highlight>.

# Asked

Why untreated bamboo can become brittle according to the passage.

# Solution

Sentences (<InlineMath math="5" />) and (<InlineMath math="6" />) establish a direct chain: **its starch attracts powderpost beetles (*kumbang bubuk*) that bore into the culm from inside** until it turns brittle.

## Checking the Other Options

Every distractor cites **strengths of bamboo** or the **preservation step itself**.

- Fast growth and a three-to-five-year harvest age are listed as advantages (sentences <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />).
- Dissolving starch by soaking is the *preventive treatment* (sentence <InlineMath math="7" />).
- Flexibility is a strength in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Answer

Untreated bamboo can become brittle because **its starch attracts powderpost beetles that bore holes into the stalk**.

(*patinya menarik kumbang bubuk yang melubangi batang*)
