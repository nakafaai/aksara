# Given

# Solution

The causal transition *Oleh sebab itu* (for that reason) directly links the lengthy, intricate work in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) to its **consequence** in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): a much higher price.

> Oleh sebab itu, <Highlight>kain tenun ikat yang diwarnai dengan bahan alami jauh lebih mahal</Highlight> daripada kain bermotif serupa yang dicetak dengan mesin.

## Checking the Other Options

Each distractor misstates the **cause-and-effect link**.

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) follows from rather than refutes sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).
- It compares prices rather than listing motif examples.
- The intricate steps were already explained in paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />.
- Introducing price adds new information rather than repeating sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).

# Answer

**Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) states the consequence of the detail in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />)**.
