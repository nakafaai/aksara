# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **tempeh** passage describes fungal growth:

> Dalam satu sampai dua hari pada suhu ruang, benang-benang kapang yang berwarna putih tumbuh di antara biji dan <Highlight>merekatkannya menjadi satu bongkahan padat</Highlight>.

# Asked

The meaning of *merekatkannya* in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />).

# Solution

*Merekatkan* comes from *rekat* (to stick or bond together), and *-nya* refers to the soybeans. Forming *one compact block* confirms it means **binding the soybeans together so they stick to each other**.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **making the soybeans stick together** explains turning loose beans into one solid block.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *memisahkan biji kedelai dari kulit arinya* | Wrong, hulling already happened in sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />) |
| *mengubah warna biji kedelai menjadi putih* | Wrong, white is the color of the fungal threads |
| *membuat biji-biji kedelai saling menempel* | Correct, forms one compact block |
| *melunakkan biji kedelai agar mudah dikunyah* | Wrong, not the meaning of *rekat* |
| *menutupi biji kedelai dengan lapisan daun* | Wrong, leaf wrapping happened in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) |

# Answer

The word *merekatkannya* means **making the soybeans stick to each other** (*membuat biji-biji kedelai saling menempel*).
