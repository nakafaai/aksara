The word *merekatkan* comes from *rekat*, meaning **sticky or stuck together**. The suffix *-nya* refers to the soybeans. Sentence (5) also gives the result: the beans become one solid block. So the mold threads make the beans stick to each other.

The other options do not fit:

- The skins were already removed in sentence (2), before the mold grows.
- The white color belongs to the mold threads, but *merekatkan* does not mean changing color.
- The reading does not say the mold softens the beans.
- The banana leaf is an outer wrapping, not something the mold threads make.

So *merekatkannya* means making the beans stick to each other (*membuat biji-biji kedelai saling menempel*).
