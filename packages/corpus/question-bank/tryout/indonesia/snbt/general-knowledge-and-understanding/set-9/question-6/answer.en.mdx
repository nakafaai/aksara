The word *cakupan* comes from *cakup*, **to cover or reach**. In sentence (8), *cakupan itu* (that coverage) refers to the advice in sentence (7): at least seventy percent of dogs vaccinated. So coverage is the size of the share, here of dogs, already reached by vaccination.

The other options do not fit:

- Vaccine prices are not mentioned.
- Vaccine types are not discussed either.
- The timing of doses is not what *cakupan* means.
- Vaccine storage has nothing to do with the percentage of dogs vaccinated.

So *cakupan* means the size of the share already reached (*besarnya bagian yang sudah terjangkau*).
