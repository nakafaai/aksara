# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="7" />) and (<InlineMath math="8" />) of the **rabies** passage state:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />): experts advise vaccinating at least seventy percent of dogs in an area.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): if that *cakupan* (coverage) is not reached, the virus can keep spreading from dog to dog.

# Asked

The meaning of *cakupan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).

# Solution

*Cakupan itu* refers directly back to the **at-least-seventy-percent vaccination share** in sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />), meaning **the proportion or share that has been reached**.

> Jika <Highlight>cakupan itu tidak tercapai</Highlight>, virus masih dapat terus berpindah dari satu anjing ke anjing lain.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **size of the share already reached** matches the percentage target in sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />).

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *harga vaksin untuk setiap dosis* | Wrong, vaccine price is not discussed |
| *jenis vaksin yang dipakai petugas* | Wrong, vaccine type is not discussed |
| *waktu pemberian vaksin dalam setahun* | Wrong, does not mean coverage |
| *tempat menyimpan vaksin* | Wrong, unrelated to vaccination share |
| *besarnya bagian yang sudah terjangkau* | Correct, matches the seventy-percent target |

# Answer

The word *cakupan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) means **the size of the share that has been reached** (*besarnya bagian yang sudah terjangkau*).
