# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **bamboo** passage names its mechanical qualities:

> Karena kuat, <Highlight>lentur</Highlight>, dan cepat tumbuh, bambu sering dijuluki *baja hijau*.

# Asked

The meaning of *lentur* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

*Lentur* means **flexible, able to bend without breaking**. Paired with *strong*, this quality explains why bamboo withstands bending loads in construction.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **able to be bent without snapping** defines *lentur*.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *keras dan tidak dapat ditekuk* | Wrong, opposite of flexible |
| *berat dan sulit untuk diangkat* | Wrong, refers to weight |
| *licin dan mudah tergelincir* | Wrong, refers to slipperiness |
| *dapat dibengkokkan tanpa patah* | Correct, exact meaning of *lentur* |
| *berongga di bagian dalamnya* | Wrong, hollowness is not flexibility |

# Answer

The word *lentur* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) means **able to be bent without breaking** (*dapat dibengkokkan tanpa patah*).
