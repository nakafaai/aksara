The word *lentur* means **able to bend without breaking**, then spring back. In sentence (4), it appears with *kuat* (strong) as a reason bamboo is called *baja hijau*. A material that is both strong and flexible does not snap easily under pressure or wind.

The other options do not fit:

- *Keras dan tidak dapat ditekuk* (hard and unbendable) is the opposite of *lentur*.
- Heavy and slippery have nothing to do with *lentur*.
- Bamboo stems are hollow, but that is not what *lentur* means.

So *lentur* means able to bend without breaking (*dapat dibengkokkan tanpa patah*).
