# Given

Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> of the **rabies** passage comprises sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) through (<InlineMath math="8" />):

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />): vaccinating dogs is the most effective way to stop rabies transmission.
- Sentences (<InlineMath math="7" />) and (<InlineMath math="8" />): at least seventy percent of dogs in an area must be vaccinated or the virus keeps spreading.

# Asked

# Solution

All three sentences center on one claim: **wide-coverage dog vaccination is the most effective way to stop rabies**.

> <Highlight>Cara paling efektif untuk menghentikan penularan rabies adalah memvaksin anjing</Highlight>.

## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor **contradicts the passage** or shifts back to paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />.

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) says rabies is almost always fatal once symptoms start.
- Wound washing is a first step in paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />, not enough alone to prevent *all* cases.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) calls dog vaccination the most effective method, not ineffective.
- Coverage applies across the local dog population, not solely to dogs that have bitten people.

# Answer

**Dog vaccination with sufficiently wide coverage is the most effective way to stop rabies**.

(*Vaksinasi anjing yang cakupannya cukup luas paling efektif menghentikan rabies.*)
