The same bridge has several dated changes. Keeping each event beside its year resolves the apparent conflict:

| Year | Event described in the sources |
| --- | --- |
| 1912 | Foundation work begins. |
| 1914 | The bridge opens for limited use. |
| 1916 | A public inauguration is held. |
| 1958 | The central span is substantially replaced. |

The main point is to tie **historical dates to the events they denote**. The central-span replacement and timeline display are details. Multiple dates do not make every fact unknowable or the earliest date always appropriate.
