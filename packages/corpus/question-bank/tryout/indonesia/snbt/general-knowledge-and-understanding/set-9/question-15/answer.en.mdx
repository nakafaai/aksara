# Given

After two days at room temperature, a maker finds the soybeans still loose with **no white threads** between them.

Sentences (<InlineMath math="4" />) and (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **tempeh** passage note that white mold threads normally bind beans within one to two days and that wrappers are perforated **because mold needs air to grow**.

# Asked

The most likely cause based on the passage.

# Solution

The absence of white threads after two days means **the mold failed to grow**. By sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />), air supply through wrapper holes is essential, so the most likely cause is that **the wrapper was sealed tight, depriving the mold of air**.

> Kedelai kemudian dibungkus dengan daun pisang atau plastik yang diberi lubang-lubang kecil, karena <Highlight>kapang memerlukan udara untuk tumbuh</Highlight>.

## Why the Other Options Do Not Fit

Every other option describes either **over-fermentation** or a **correct standard step**.

- Leaving tempeh too long means mold already grew and formed black spores (sentence <InlineMath math="9" />).
- Boiling thoroughly, removing hulls, and adding *Rhizopus* starter are all standard correct steps (sentences <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="3" />).

# Answer

The most likely cause is that **the wrapper was sealed tightly so the mold lacked air**.

(*bungkusnya tertutup rapat sehingga kapang kekurangan udara*)
