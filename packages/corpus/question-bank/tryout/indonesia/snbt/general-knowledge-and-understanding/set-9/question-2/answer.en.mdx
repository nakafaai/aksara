# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) and (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **ikat weaving** passage explain why threads are tied:

> Sebelum ditenun, benang-benang dibentangkan pada bingkai, lalu bagian-bagian tertentu <Highlight>diikat rapat dengan tali agar tidak terkena pewarna</Highlight>.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) confirms that tied parts keep their original color while open parts absorb dye.

# Asked

Why specific parts of the yarn are tied before dyeing according to sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) and (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

The purpose clause *so they do not touch the dye* directly establishes that sections are bound **so those parts do not absorb dye**, creating the patterned design.

## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor invents a reason **absent from the passage**.

- Tying is never linked to preventing thread breakage during weaving.
- Dye drying speed is never mentioned.
- Yarn is stretched on the frame *before* tying, not because of tying.
- Stopping others from copying motifs is not discussed.

# Answer

Specific parts of the yarn are tied **so those parts do not absorb dye**.

(*agar bagian itu tidak menyerap pewarna*)
