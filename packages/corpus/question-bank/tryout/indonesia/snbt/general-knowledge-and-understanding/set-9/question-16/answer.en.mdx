The nickname is a figure of speech. *Baja* (steel) points to **strength**, and sentence (4) does call bamboo strong and flexible. *Hijau* (green) points to where it comes from, **a plant** that grows fast. So bamboo is pictured as a plant material as strong as steel.

The other options do not fit:

- Bamboo is not a metal, and the reading says nothing about paint or rust.
- Sentence (4) calls bamboo strong, so *mudah patah* (breaks easily) contradicts the reading.
- Sentence (1) lists posts, walls, and bridges, so *hanya* (only) is wrong.
- Leaf color through the year is not discussed.

So *baja hijau* (green steel) pictures bamboo as a plant material as strong as steel.
