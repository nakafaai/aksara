# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **bamboo** passage states:

> Karena <Highlight>kuat, lentur, dan cepat tumbuh</Highlight>, bambu sering dijuluki *baja hijau*.

# Asked

What the nickname *baja hijau* (green steel) conveys about bamboo in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

The metaphor combines **steel** (strength for building) with **green** (a fast-growing plant origin), portraying bamboo as **a plant-based material as strong as steel**.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **a plant material strong like steel** captures both halves of the figure.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *logam yang dicat hijau* | Wrong, bamboo is not painted metal |
| *bahan dari tumbuhan yang kuat seperti baja* | Correct, unites *steel* and *green* |
| *tanaman hijau yang batangnya mudah patah* | Wrong, contradicts *strong* |
| *bahan yang hanya dipakai untuk jembatan* | Wrong, sentence (<InlineMath math="1" />) lists posts, walls, and bridges |
| *tumbuhan yang daunnya tetap hijau sepanjang tahun* | Wrong, evergreen leaves are not discussed |

# Answer

The nickname *baja hijau* portrays bamboo as **a plant material as strong as steel** (*bahan dari tumbuhan yang kuat seperti baja*).
