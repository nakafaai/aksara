# Given

The **bamboo** passage balances two paragraphs:

- Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="4" />): bamboo's strengths as a fast-growing, strong, flexible *green steel*.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="5" /> to <InlineMath math="9" />): its starch vulnerability to beetles and preservation via soaking and dry-season harvesting.

# Asked

The statement best matching the entire passage.

# Solution

The best overall statement pairs **bamboo's growth and strength with its need for preservation**: it grows fast and is strong, yet requires treatment to resist powderpost beetles.

> Untuk mencegah serangan kumbang itu, <Highlight>perajin biasanya merendam batang bambu di dalam air selama beberapa minggu agar sebagian pati larut dan terbuang</Highlight>.

## Why the Other Four Options Fail

Each distractor **stretches a metaphor or reverses a fact**.

- *Green steel* is figurative and never claims bamboo beats steel in every condition.
- Teak's decades-long growth contrasts bamboo's speed rather than praising teak as superior.
- Dry-season cutting aims for *lower* starch (sentence <InlineMath math="8" />), not attracting beetles.
- Soaking dissolves starch (sentence <InlineMath math="7" />), not making cut stalks grow taller.

# Answer

**Bamboo grows fast and is strong, yet needs preservation to resist powderpost beetles**.

(*Bambu cepat tumbuh dan kuat, tetapi perlu diawetkan agar tahan terhadap kumbang bubuk.*)
