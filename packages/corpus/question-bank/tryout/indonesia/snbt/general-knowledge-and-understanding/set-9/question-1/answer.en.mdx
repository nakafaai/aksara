# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="3" />) and (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **ikat weaving** passage describe dyeing yarn:

> Benang kemudian <Highlight>dicelup ke dalam pewarna</Highlight>, misalnya pewarna biru dari daun tarum atau pewarna merah dari akar mengkudu.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) adds that exposed parts absorb the dye.

# Asked

The meaning of *dicelup* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />).

# Solution

*Dicelup* means **dipped or immersed into a liquid** so the yarn absorbs the dye, matching sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) where untied sections take in color.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **placed into a liquid** fits absorbing liquid dye.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *dijemur di bawah sinar matahari* | Sun-dried | Wrong, drying rather than dyeing |
| *dimasukkan ke dalam cairan* | Immersed in liquid | Correct, exact meaning of *dicelup* |
| *dipukul-pukul dengan kayu* | Beaten with wood | Wrong, unrelated to dyeing |
| *digulung pada sebatang kayu* | Wound onto a stick | Wrong, does not dye yarn |
| *dijahit pada selembar kain* | Sewn onto cloth | Wrong, yarn is not cloth yet |

# Answer

The word *dicelup* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) means **immersed in liquid** (*dimasukkan ke dalam cairan*).
