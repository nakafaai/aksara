The word *dicelup* means **dipped into a liquid** and then lifted out. In sentence (3), the threads are dipped into the dye. Sentence (4) shows the result: the uncovered parts of the threads absorb color from that liquid.

The other options do not fit:

- *Dijemur* (dried in the sun) does not give color.
- *Dipukul-pukul* (beaten) has nothing to do with dyeing.
- *Digulung* (rolled) does not make the threads absorb color.
- *Dijahit* (sewn) does not fit, since the threads are not yet cloth at this stage.

So *dicelup* means dipped into a liquid (*dimasukkan ke dalam cairan*).
