# Given

The **bamboo** passage supplies four relevant details:

- Sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) and (<InlineMath math="3" />): *some* bamboo species grow nearly one meter a day and are harvested at three to five years, whereas teak takes decades.
- Sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />): starch feeds beetles, so soaking removes starch to prevent beetle attack.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): preserved bamboo lasts over a decade *provided it is not constantly exposed to rain and damp soil*.

# Asked

Whether each statement can be inferred (**Yes** / *Ya*) or not (**No** / *Tidak*) from the passage.

# Solution

Check the quantifier shift from **some to all**, the harvest comparison with teak, and the **provided** condition in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

> Catch shifts from *some* to *all* and respect the condition against continuous exposure to damp soil.

## Evaluating All Four Statements

Matching each claim to **textual evidence** gives these verdicts.

| Statement | Textual Evidence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All bamboo species grow nearly one meter per day | Contradicts sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />): *some* species only | **No** (*Tidak*) |
| Bamboo can be harvested much faster than teak | Matches sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) and (<InlineMath math="3" />): three to five years vs. decades | **Yes** (*Ya*) |
| Soaked bamboo stays durable even in constant damp soil | Contradicts the *provided* condition in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) | **No** (*Tidak*) |
| Reducing starch helps bamboo resist powderpost beetles | Matches sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) | **Yes** (*Ya*) |

# Answer

Statements <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> can be inferred (**Yes** / *Ya*), while statements <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="3" /> cannot (**No** / *Tidak*).
