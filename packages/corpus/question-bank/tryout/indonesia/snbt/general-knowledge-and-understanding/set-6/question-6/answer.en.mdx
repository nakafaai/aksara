# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **volcanic ash** passage explains the danger to jet engines:

> Suhu di dalam mesin jet lebih tinggi daripada titik leleh kaca gunung api, sehingga abu yang terisap dapat meleleh, lalu mengeras lagi dan menyumbat bagian mesin.

# Asked

The meaning of *terisap* in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />).

# Solution

*Terisap* comes from *isap* (to suck or draw in). Because a jet engine pulls air inward, airborne ash is **drawn inside** the engine, where it melts and clogs components.

## Evaluating the Options

Checking the **inward direction** of ash movement rules out the four distractors.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *terlempar keluar* | Thrown outward | Wrong, opposite direction |
| *tertarik masuk* | Drawn or sucked in | Correct, matches jet intake |
| *terbakar habis* | Burned away completely | Wrong, ash melts then hardens |
| *tersaring keluar* | Filtered out | Wrong, ash enters the engine |
| *tertiup menjauh* | Blown away | Wrong, stays outside the engine |

# Answer

The word *terisap* in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) means **drawn in** (*tertarik masuk*).
