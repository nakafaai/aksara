# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **karst** passage reads:

> Karena air cepat meresap ke celah-celah batu, <Highlight>permukaan kawasan karst sering tampak kering, padahal di bawahnya tersimpan banyak air</Highlight>.

# Asked

The statement that best matches the meaning of sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

The contrast marker *padahal* (whereas in fact) highlights a **gap between appearance and reality**. Although the surface looks dry, abundant water is stored below, so dryness on top does not mean a water shortage.

## Eliminating the Other Options

Each distractor alters the **cause or comparison** stated in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

- The surface looks dry because water seeps down quickly, not because rain is rare.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) never compares underground water volume to total rainfall.
- Limestone lets water enter cracks rather than trapping it on top.
- Rainwater acidity in sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) and (<InlineMath math="3" />) explains rock dissolution, not surface dryness.

# Answer

**A dry-looking surface does not mean a karst region lacks water**.

(*Permukaan yang tampak kering tidak berarti kawasan karst kekurangan air.*)
