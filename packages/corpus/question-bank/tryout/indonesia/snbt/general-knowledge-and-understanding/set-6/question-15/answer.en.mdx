# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="8" />) and (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **noken** passage state:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): the number of young artisans is falling, and cheap plastic bags are widely used.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): several communities in Papua hold noken-making workshops for young people so the craft is not broken off.

# Asked

The main purpose of the training mentioned in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

# Solution

The purpose clause *so the skill is not cut off* directed at *young people* directly addresses the shrinking pool of young artisans: the training aims to **pass noken-making skills on to the younger generation**.


> Sejumlah komunitas di Papua kemudian mengadakan <Highlight>pelatihan membuat noken bagi anak muda agar keterampilan itu tidak terputus</Highlight>.
## Checking the Other Options

Each distractor misses the **stated target and content** of the workshops.

- Replacing all plastic bags across Indonesia is never mentioned.
- Raising market prices for noken is not discussed.
- UNESCO registration already happened in <InlineMath math="2012" /> (sentence <InlineMath math="6" />).
- The workshops teach *making* noken, not how to carry babies.

# Answer

The training mainly aims to **pass on the skill of making noken to the younger generation**.

(*mewariskan keterampilan membuat noken kepada generasi muda*)
