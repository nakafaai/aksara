# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="4" />) and (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **biopore** passage explain how the infiltration hole works:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />): organic waste inside the hole feeds earthworms and other organisms.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />): the activity of those organisms forms small pores in the hole walls so rainwater enters the soil more easily.

# Asked

Why rainwater enters the soil more easily through a biopore hole according to the passage.

# Solution

The result connector *sehingga* (so that) in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) identifies the **direct mechanism**: organisms feeding on the waste **create small pores along the walls of the hole**.


> Kegiatan organisme itu <Highlight>membentuk pori-pori kecil di dinding lubang</Highlight> sehingga air hujan lebih mudah masuk ke dalam tanah.
## Checking the Other Options

Every distractor either **contradicts the passage** or invents an unsupported detail.

- The hole is not lined with concrete, which sentence (<InlineMath math="1" />) blames for flooding.
- The text never says organic waste absorbs *all* rainfall.
- The hole is only about <InlineMath math="10" /> cm wide and is never compared to street drains.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) says compost fertilizes plants rather than hardening soil.

# Answer

Rainwater enters more easily because **organisms eating the waste form small pores in the walls of the hole**.

(*organisme yang memakan sampah membentuk pori-pori kecil di dinding lubang*)
