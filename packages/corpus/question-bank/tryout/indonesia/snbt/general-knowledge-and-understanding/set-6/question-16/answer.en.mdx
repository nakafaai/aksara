# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **biopore** passage states:

> Sampah organik di dalam lubang perlu ditambah secara berkala, karena lama-kelamaan sampah itu berubah menjadi kompos.

# Asked

The meaning of the word *berkala* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

*Berkala* means **periodically or regularly at set intervals**. Because organic waste continually breaks down into compost, new waste must be added repeatedly on a regular basis.

## Comparing the Options

The need for **repeated refilling** as compost forms eliminates every other option.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *secara teratur pada waktu tertentu* | Regularly at certain times | Correct, exact meaning of *berkala* |
| *sekali saja ketika lubang dibuat* | Only once at creation | Wrong, contradicts ongoing refilling |
| *tanpa aturan dan sesuka hati* | Randomly without order | Wrong, opposite of regular |
| *hanya ketika lubang sudah penuh air hujan* | Only when filled with rainwater | Wrong, triggered by composting |
| *secara bersamaan oleh semua warga* | Simultaneously by all residents | Wrong, describes who rather than timing |

# Answer

The word *berkala* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) means **regularly at certain times** (*secara teratur pada waktu tertentu*).
