The word *berkala* means **done regularly at set times**, like a periodical that appears every month. Sentence (6) says organic waste must be added *berkala* because the old waste keeps turning into compost. Since that change never stops, adding waste must also be repeated regularly.

The other options do not fit:

- *Sekali saja* (only once) contradicts adding waste repeatedly.
- *Tanpa aturan* (without any rule) is the opposite of regular.
- The reading does not link adding waste to the hole filling with water.
- *Berkala* is about repeated times, not about how many people do it.

So *berkala* in sentence (6) means regularly at set times.
