# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) of the **noken** passage appear consecutively:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />): in <InlineMath math="2012" />, UNESCO placed noken on the list of intangible cultural heritage in need of urgent safeguarding.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />): *Status itu* (that status) shows that the continuity of noken is under threat.

# Asked

The referent of *Status itu* in sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />).

# Solution

The demonstrative *itu* points directly back to the **official UNESCO designation in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />)**: noken's status as cultural heritage requiring urgent protection.


> UNESCO memasukkan noken ke dalam <Highlight>daftar warisan budaya takbenda yang memerlukan pelindungan mendesak</Highlight>.
## Checking the Other Options

Tracking **sentence order** removes the four distractors immediately.

- Cheap plastic bags and fewer young artisans appear *after* sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />), in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />), as causes of the threat.
- Youth training workshops are introduced later in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).
- Carrying babies is one everyday use in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />), not a status.

# Answer

The phrase *Status itu* refers to **the status of noken as cultural heritage in need of urgent protection**.

(*status noken sebagai warisan budaya yang perlu dilindungi segera*)
