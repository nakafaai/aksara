# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="4" />) through (<InlineMath math="8" />) of the **volcanic ash** passage list harmful effects: engine damage, heavy roof loads, and eye or respiratory irritation.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) closes the passage:

> Meskipun demikian, <Highlight>dalam jangka panjang abu vulkanik menambah unsur hara tanah</Highlight>, dan inilah salah satu alasan lahan di lereng gunung api terkenal subur.

# Asked

The function of sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) in the passage.

# Solution

The contrast phrase *Meskipun demikian* (nevertheless) introduces a **balancing positive side**. After detailing immediate hazards, sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) adds the long-term benefit of enriching soil fertility.

## Checking the Other Options

Each distractor misstates the **balancing function** of sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

- It does not deny aviation hazards, since soil enrichment occurs over the long term.
- Adding soil nutrients is a benefit, not another harmful effect on land.
- Cleaning ash from rooftops is never discussed.
- It never claims volcanic ash is harmless overall.

# Answer

Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) functions to **present the beneficial side of volcanic ash after its harmful effects are explained**.

(*memberikan sisi baik abu vulkanik setelah dampak buruknya dijelaskan*)
