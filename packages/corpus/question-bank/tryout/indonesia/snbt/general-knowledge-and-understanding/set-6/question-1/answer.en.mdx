# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **karst** passage states:

> Karena air cepat meresap ke celah-celah batu, permukaan kawasan karst sering tampak kering, padahal di bawahnya tersimpan banyak air.

# Asked

The meaning of the word *meresap* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

*Meresap* means **to seep or soak into tiny openings**. Because rainwater quickly enters cracks in the limestone, the surface looks dry while large stores of water collect underground.

## Checking the Options

Tracking the **direction of water movement** confirms the exact match.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *menguap ke udara* | Evaporate into the air | Wrong, water moves downward |
| *mengalir di atas permukaan* | Flow on the surface | Wrong, water leaves the surface |
| *masuk ke dalam celah-celah* | Enter into cracks | Correct, matches sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) |
| *membeku menjadi es* | Freeze into ice | Wrong, unrelated to the passage |
| *menggenang di permukaan tanah* | Pool on the surface | Wrong, surface appears dry |

# Answer

The word *meresap* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) means **to enter into cracks** (*masuk ke dalam celah-celah*).
