The word *meresap* means **to seep, to soak gradually into something** through pores or gaps, as water soaks into cloth. In sentence (4), rainwater enters the cracks in the limestone. That is why the karst surface looks dry while the water is stored below.

The other options do not fit:

- *Menguap ke udara* (to evaporate into the air) means turning into vapor, not entering the rock.
- *Mengalir di atas permukaan* (to flow over the surface) contradicts the water being stored underground.
- *Membeku menjadi es* (to freeze into ice) has nothing to do with the reading.
- *Menggenang di permukaan tanah* (to pool on the ground) contradicts the surface looking dry.

So *meresap* in sentence (4) means entering the cracks (*masuk ke dalam celah-celah*).
