# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **volcanic ash** passage states:

> Suhu di dalam mesin jet lebih tinggi daripada titik leleh kaca gunung api, sehingga abu yang terisap dapat meleleh, lalu mengeras lagi dan menyumbat bagian mesin.

# Asked

The word closest in meaning to *menyumbat* in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />).

# Solution

*Menyumbat* means **to block or seal off a passage** so nothing can flow through. Melted volcanic glass that hardens again closes off internal engine passages.

## Comparing the Options

Checking the physical effect of **hardened ash inside the engine** singles out *menutup*.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *membakar* | Burn | Wrong, ash melts then re-hardens |
| *melumasi* | Lubricate | Wrong, opposite of obstructing |
| *mendinginkan* | Cool down | Wrong, unrelated to blocking |
| *melubangi* | Pierce or make holes | Wrong, opposite of closing off |
| *menutup* | Close or block off | Correct, closest synonym of *menyumbat* |

# Answer

The word *menyumbat* in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) is closest in meaning to **close off** (*menutup*).
