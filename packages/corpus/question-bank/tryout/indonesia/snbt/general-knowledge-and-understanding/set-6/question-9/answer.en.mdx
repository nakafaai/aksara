The word *menyumbat* means **to block a hole or passage** so that nothing can get through. In sentence (5), ash that melts and hardens again blocks parts of the engine, so air can no longer flow freely.

The other options do not fit:

- *Membakar* (to burn) does not fit, since the ash melts and hardens.
- *Melumasi* (to lubricate) means making something slippery so it moves easily, the opposite of what the ash does.
- *Mendinginkan* (to cool) has nothing to do with ash hardening inside the engine.
- *Melubangi* (to make a hole) is the opposite of blocking one.

So *menyumbat* is closest in meaning to *menutup* (to close, to block).
