The word *memadai* means **sufficient, meeting the need**. In sentence (9), an adequate number of holes means enough to take in the rain. Its opposite is *kurang* (insufficient, too few), falling short of what is needed, like the single hole in sentence (8).

The other options do not fit:

- *Mencukupi* (sufficient) is a synonym of *memadai*, not its opposite.
- *Banyak* (many) describes a large amount and is close in meaning to *memadai* in this sentence.
- *Tepat* (right) and *layak* (proper) are also close to suitable, not opposite.

So the opposite of *memadai* is *kurang*.
