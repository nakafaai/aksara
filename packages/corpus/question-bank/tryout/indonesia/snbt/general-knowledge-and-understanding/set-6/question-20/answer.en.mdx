Sentences (8) and (9) work as a pair. One biopore hole cannot take the rain from a whole yard, so the benefit only shows when the number of holes is *memadai*. **Memadai here means enough for the need**, and its opposite is not enough, *kurang*. Medical staff who are still *kurang* compared with the population uses the word in exactly that sense: the number falls short of the need.

Supplies that are *mencukupi* ("sufficient") are a synonym of memadai. Residents who came in *banyak* ("large") numbers sound like the opposite of few, but many is not automatically enough and few is not automatically short, because the yardstick is the need, not the raw count. An answer that is *tepat* ("right") and a house that is *layak* ("fit") to live in both mean suitable, so both agree with memadai.
