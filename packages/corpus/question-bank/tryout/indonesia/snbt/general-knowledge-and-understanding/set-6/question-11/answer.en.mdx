The word *ketekunan* comes from *tekun*, meaning **diligent, earnest, and persistent** at a task. Sentence (3) gives the reason: one large noken can take weeks to make. Such long work demands earnestness and patience.

The other options do not fit:

- *Kecepatan bekerja* (working speed) contradicts work that takes weeks.
- *Kekuatan tangan yang besar* (great hand strength) is not given as the reason and is not what *tekun* means.
- *Keberanian mencoba* (courage to try) is not the meaning of *tekun*.
- *Kecerdikan mencari cara* (cleverness at finding ways) means resourcefulness, not long, steady effort.

So *ketekunan* in sentence (3) means earnestness and patience (*kesungguhan dan kesabaran*).
