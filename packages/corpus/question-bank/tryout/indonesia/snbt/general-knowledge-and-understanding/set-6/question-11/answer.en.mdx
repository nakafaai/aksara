# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) of the **noken** passage states:

> Pembuatannya memerlukan ketekunan, sebab satu noken besar dapat dikerjakan selama berminggu-minggu.

# Asked

The meaning of the word *ketekunan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />).

# Solution

*Ketekunan* derives from *tekun* (diligent and persevering), meaning **earnestness and patience**. Spending weeks crafting a single large noken requires sustained care rather than speed or force.

## Comparing the Options

The clue *for weeks* (*berminggu-minggu*) confirms **earnestness and patience** against all distractors.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *kecepatan bekerja* | Speed of work | Wrong, conflicts with weeks of work |
| *kekuatan tangan yang besar* | Great hand strength | Wrong, not the meaning of *tekun* |
| *keberanian mencoba* | Courage to try | Wrong, unrelated to perseverance |
| *kesungguhan dan kesabaran* | Earnestness and patience | Correct, matches *ketekunan* |
| *kecerdikan mencari cara* | Cleverness in finding tricks | Wrong, refers to ingenuity |

# Answer

The word *ketekunan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) means **earnestness and patience** (*kesungguhan dan kesabaran*).
