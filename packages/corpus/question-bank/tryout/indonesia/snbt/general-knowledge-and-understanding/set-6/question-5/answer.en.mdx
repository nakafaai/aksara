# Given

Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> of the **karst** passage opens with fragility (sentence <InlineMath math="6" />), gives two subsurface threats in sentences (<InlineMath math="7" />) and (<InlineMath math="8" />), and closes in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) by urging management to pay attention to what happens beneath the surface.

# Asked

The author's purpose in writing paragraph <InlineMath math="2" />.

# Solution

Every sentence in paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> builds toward the **closing warning**: karst damage often happens underground, so stewardship must look below the surface as well as above it.


> Kawasan karst perlu dikelola dengan <Highlight>memperhatikan apa yang terjadi di bawah permukaannya</Highlight>, bukan hanya di atasnya.
## Ruling Out the Other Options

Each distractor points to **another paragraph** or introduces material absent from the passage.

- The formation of caves, dolines, and underground rivers is covered in paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />.
- The text makes no comparison with other rocky landscapes.
- The author never urges residents to stop using underground river water.
- No history of limestone mining in Gunung Sewu appears in the passage.

# Answer

The author's purpose is **to remind readers that karst damage can occur below the surface and must therefore be taken into account**.

(*mengingatkan bahwa kerusakan karst dapat terjadi di bawah permukaan sehingga harus diperhatikan*)
