# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="5" />), (<InlineMath math="6" />), and (<InlineMath math="10" />) of the **sasando** passage describe the palm-leaf resonator:

> Wadah inilah yang <Highlight>memantulkan dan memperkuat getaran dawai</Highlight> sehingga bunyinya terdengar lebih keras.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="10" />) confirms this role, since electric sasando replaces it with an amplifier.

# Asked

Why the palm-leaf resonator is attached to the sasando according to the passage.

# Solution

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) states its purpose directly: **the resonator amplifies and reflects the string vibrations** so the sound carries louder.

## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor is **unsupported** or confuses another part of the instrument.

- The text never links palm leaves to preventing broken strings.
- Pitch is set by small bridges (sentence <InlineMath math="4" />), not by the resonator.
- Ease of finding palm leaves on Rote Island is not given as the reason.
- The resonator is not described as a handle.

# Answer

The palm-leaf resonator is attached because **it makes the string vibrations stronger**.

(*wadah itu membuat getaran dawai lebih kuat*)
