The first two paragraphs explain how unequal retrieval can arise: errors in text recognition can enter the suggested metadata, while popular collections receive more corrections. The later paragraphs provide ways to inspect those weaknesses.

Follow the **error path from recognition to metadata and search results**. An empty search may reflect that path rather than a missing document. Original images, collection numbers, date ranges, and spelling variants provide other ways to investigate. Reviewing a sample of empty searches and auditing document groups test where failures occur.

The relationship is a diagnosed error chain followed by inspection and correction measures. Automation remains in use, and no search result is treated as a final description of the archive.
