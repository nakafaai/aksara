# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="4" />) and (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **proboscis monkey** passage specify its safe natural diet and hazards:

> <Highlight>Makanan utamanya adalah daun muda, ditambah buah yang belum matang</Highlight>.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) warns that sweet ripe fruit causes dangerous gas and bloating.

# Asked

The most appropriate zoo diet for a pair of proboscis monkeys based on the passage.

# Solution

Safe captive feeding must **match their natural diet in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />)** while avoiding high-sugar foods: **fresh young leaves and unripe fruit**.

## Checking the Other Four Diet Options

The other options are either **harmful** or **unrelated to their diet**.

- Sweet ripe bananas, papaya, sweet bread, and canned fruit contain abundant sugar that ferments rapidly and bloats the stomach (sentence <InlineMath math="6" />).
- Dry grains, roasted peanuts, fish, and shrimp are never listed as proboscis monkey food.

# Answer

The most suitable diet is **fresh young leaves and unripe fruit**.

(*daun muda segar dan buah yang belum matang*)
