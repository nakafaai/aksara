# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="4" />) and (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **proboscis monkey** passage explain its fruit diet:

> Namun, <Highlight>buah yang manis dan matang justru dapat membahayakan, karena gulanya cepat berfermentasi di lambung dan menghasilkan gas yang membuat perutnya kembung</Highlight>.

# Asked

Why unripe fruit is safer for proboscis monkeys based on sentences (<InlineMath math="4" />) through (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) gives the physiological reason directly: sugars in ripe fruit ferment rapidly in the chambered stomach, producing gas, so **sweet ripe fruit causes painful bloating**.
