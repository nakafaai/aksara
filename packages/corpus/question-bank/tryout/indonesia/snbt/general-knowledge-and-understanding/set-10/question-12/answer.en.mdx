# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="8" />) and (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **proboscis monkey** passage run consecutively:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): at night, *groups of proboscis monkeys* sleep in riverside trees.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): unfortunately, the forests where *they* (*mereka*) live are widely cleared for plantations and settlements.

# Asked

The referent of *mereka* (they) in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

# Solution

The plural pronoun *mereka* refers directly back to the **living subject of sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />)**: **those groups of proboscis monkeys** whose forest habitat is being cleared.

> Sayangnya, <Highlight>hutan tempat hidup mereka banyak dibuka</Highlight> untuk perkebunan dan permukiman.

## Checking the Other Options

None of the other choices fits as the **living inhabitants** of *the forests where they live*.

- Trees and mangrove/swamp forests are the habitat itself, not its residents.
- Plantations and settlements replace the cleared forests.
- Stomach bacteria were mentioned back in paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />.

# Answer

The word *mereka* refers to **those groups of proboscis monkeys** (*kelompok bekantan itu*).
