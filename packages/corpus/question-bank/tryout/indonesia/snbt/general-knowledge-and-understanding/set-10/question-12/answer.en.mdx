The pronoun *mereka* (they) refers to **the creatures just discussed**. Sentence (8) is about groups of proboscis monkeys sleeping in trees, and sentence (9) mentions the forest where *mereka* live. What lives in that forest is the monkeys.

The other options do not fit:

- Trees and forests are the habitat itself, not what lives there.
- Plantations and settlements are what the cleared forest becomes.
- The stomach bacteria are in the first paragraph and have nothing to do with forest clearing.

So *mereka* refers to the groups of proboscis monkeys (*kelompok bekantan itu*).
