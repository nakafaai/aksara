# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="6" />) and (<InlineMath math="7" />) of the **jamu** passage define the requirements for *fitofarmaka*:

> Kelompok ketiga adalah fitofarmaka, yang <Highlight>selain memenuhi syarat itu juga sudah diuji secara klinis pada manusia</Highlight>.

# Asked

What a product classified as *fitofarmaka* must certainly satisfy based on paragraph <InlineMath math="2" />.

# Solution

Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) requires *fitofarmaka* both to meet the second category's criteria (standardized ingredients and animal testing) and to have **already undergone clinical trials on humans**.

## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor either **contradicts sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />)** or goes beyond the text.

- Inherited family recipes define *jamu* (sentence <InlineMath math="5" />), not *fitofarmaka*.
- Prices are never discussed.
- *In addition to meeting those criteria* means animal testing was done, refuting *never tested on animals*.
- Turmeric and *temulawak* are merely sample jamu ingredients, not the *only* ingredients of *fitofarmaka*.

# Answer

A product classified as *fitofarmaka* has certainly **already been clinically tested on humans**.

(*sudah diuji secara klinis pada manusia*)
