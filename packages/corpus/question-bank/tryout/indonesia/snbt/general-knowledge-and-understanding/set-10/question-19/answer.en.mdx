Researchers observed that typed letters were **easier to retrieve** than handwritten notes and that historical spellings **often failed to appear**. These observations give concrete evidence about retrieval limits.

The usefulness of a correctable index is an evaluation. Displaying only readable text and conducting audits concern actions. Stopping all search does not follow from those observations.
