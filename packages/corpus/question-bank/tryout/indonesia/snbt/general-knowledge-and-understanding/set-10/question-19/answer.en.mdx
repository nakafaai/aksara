# Given

The **firefly** passage develops across two linked paragraphs:

- Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="5" />): fireflies are beetles that produce cold light to find mates.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="6" /> to <InlineMath math="9" />): populations are shrinking due to artificial night lighting and loss of moist larval habitats.

# Asked

The statement best matching the entire passage.

# Solution

The best summary combines **light-based mating (paragraph <InlineMath math="1" />) with both key threats (paragraph <InlineMath math="2" />)**: fireflies pair up through light yet face threats from artificial lamps and habitat loss.

> <Highlight>Kunang-kunang memakai kedipan cahayanya untuk mencari pasangan</Highlight>, dan setiap jenis kunang-kunang memiliki pola kedipan sendiri.

## Why the Other Four Options Fail

Each distractor **contradicts the passage** or overreaches.

- Fireflies are *beetles* emitting *cold* light (sentences <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />), not heat-producing flies.
- The text never recommends shutting off all city streetlights.
- Larvae live in *damp soil near water* (sentence <InlineMath math="9" />), not deep river water.
- Luciferase drives light production (sentence <InlineMath math="3" />) rather than population decline.

# Answer

**Fireflies find mates through light, yet are threatened by artificial lamps and habitat loss**.

(*Kunang-kunang berpasangan lewat cahaya, tetapi terancam lampu dan hilangnya habitat.*)
