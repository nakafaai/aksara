# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) and (<InlineMath math="3" />) of the **firefly** passage explain its light source:

> Pada ujung perutnya terdapat organ yang dapat <Highlight>memancarkan cahaya</Highlight>.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) adds that the light is generated internally by a chemical reaction between luciferin and oxygen.

# Asked

The meaning of *memancarkan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />).

# Solution

*Memancarkan* means **to emit or send light outward** from an internal source. Because fireflies generate their own light chemically, the exact match is **emit** (*mengeluarkan*).

## Evaluating the Options

Checking the **outward direction from an internal source** rules out the other four choices.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *menyerap* (absorb) | Draw inward | Wrong, opposite direction |
| *memantulkan* (reflect) | Bounce external light back | Wrong, fireflies make their own light |
| *mengeluarkan* (emit) | Send light outward | Correct, direct match for *memancarkan* |
| *memadamkan* (extinguish) | Put out light | Wrong, opposite of shining |
| *mengumpulkan* (gather) | Collect inward | Wrong, opposite direction |

# Answer

The word *memancarkan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />) means **to emit** (*mengeluarkan*).
