*Memancarkan* means **to give off or send out**, for example light or water. Sentence (3) shows the light is made by a chemical reaction inside the firefly's organ and then sent out.

The other options do not fit:

- *Menyerap* (absorb) and *mengumpulkan* (collect) mean taking in, the opposite of *memancarkan*.
- *Memantulkan* (reflect) means bouncing back light from another source, but fireflies make their own light.
- *Memadamkan* means putting a light out.

So *memancarkan* means to give off (*mengeluarkan*).
