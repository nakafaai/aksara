Rawa Village really was involved, but only one stage in the stated journey took place there:

| Stage | Information in the passage |
| --- | --- |
| Growing the cassava | Two districts. |
| Slicing | Rawa Village. |
| Frying | The city. |
| Packing | Another distributor handles this stage. The passage does not name its location. |

A reader who treats the village name as the origin of every stage adds a claim the label does not establish. A true place name can therefore mislead when understood as **the product's entire journey**.

The passage's solution keeps the front concise and makes fuller records accessible through a code. It does not require every stage on the package front, and the code still needs updated records.
