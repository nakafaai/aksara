# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **proboscis monkey** (*bekantan*) passage states:

> Lambung bekantan <Highlight>berbilik-bilik</Highlight> dan berisi bakteri yang membantu mencerna daun yang sulit dicerna.

# Asked

What *berbilik-bilik* indicates about the proboscis monkey's stomach in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />).

# Solution

The root *bilik* means **chamber or room**, and the reduplicated form *berbilik-bilik* means **divided into several chambers** that house leaf-digesting bacteria.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **divided into several chambers** matches the root *bilik*.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *terbagi menjadi beberapa ruang* | Correct, exact meaning of *berbilik-bilik* |
| *berukuran sangat kecil dan sempit* | Wrong, stomach size is not discussed |
| *dipenuhi oleh buah yang manis* | Wrong, sweet fruit is harmful (sentence <InlineMath math="6" />) |
| *mudah sekali terluka oleh daun* | Wrong, not mentioned |
| *tidak dapat mencerna daun muda* | Wrong, stomach bacteria aid leaf digestion |

# Answer

The term *berbilik-bilik* shows that the proboscis monkey's stomach is **divided into several chambers** (*terbagi menjadi beberapa ruang*).
