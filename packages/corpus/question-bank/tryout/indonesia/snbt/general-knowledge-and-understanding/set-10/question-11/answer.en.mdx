*Bilik* means **a room or compartment**, and the repeated form *berbilik-bilik* means having many compartments. So the proboscis monkey's stomach is divided into several chambers. Those chambers hold bacteria that help digest leaves.

The other options do not fit:

- Stomach size is not discussed.
- By sentence (6), sweet fruit is harmful, so the stomach is not full of it.
- The reading never says leaves injure the stomach.
- Sentence (5) says the bacteria in the stomach help digest leaves.

So *berbilik-bilik* shows that the stomach is divided into several chambers (*terbagi menjadi beberapa ruang*).
