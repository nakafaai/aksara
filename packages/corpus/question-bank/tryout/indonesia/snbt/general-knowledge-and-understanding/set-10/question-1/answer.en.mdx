# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **sasando** passage states:

> Di antara bambu dan dawai dipasang <Highlight>ganjal-ganjal kecil</Highlight>, sehingga setiap dawai menghasilkan nada yang berbeda.

# Asked

The phrase closest in meaning to *ganjal* in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

# Solution

*Ganjal* means a **small wedge or prop inserted for support** between two surfaces. Placed between the bamboo tube and each string, these bridges set the pitch of each string.

## Evaluating the Options

Being **inserted between bamboo and strings** singles out the correct definition.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *tali pengikat yang melilit tabung bambu* | Wrong, wrapping cord is not a wedge between surfaces |
| *lubang kecil pada dinding batang bambu* | Wrong, a hole is not an inserted object |
| *penutup kecil di ujung atas tabung* | Wrong, not placed between tube and strings |
| *benda kecil penyangga yang diselipkan* | Correct, exact meaning of *ganjal* |
| *alat pemetik yang dipasang di ujung jari* | Wrong, sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) says fingers pluck strings |

# Answer

The word *ganjal* is closest in meaning to **a small supporting wedge inserted between parts** (*benda kecil penyangga yang diselipkan*).
