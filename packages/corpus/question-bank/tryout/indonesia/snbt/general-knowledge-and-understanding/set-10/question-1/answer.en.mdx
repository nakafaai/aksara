A *ganjal* is **a small object slipped in to prop something up**. Sentence (4) says the *ganjal* sit between the bamboo and the strings, so they lift the strings off the bamboo. Where they sit gives each string its own note.

The other options do not fit:

- A binding cord or a hole is not placed *between* the bamboo and the strings.
- An end cap for the tube is not mentioned.
- Sentence (7) says the player plucks with the fingers, not with a pick.

So *ganjal* is closest in meaning to a small prop slipped in place (*benda kecil penyangga yang diselipkan*).
