A *substantial* effect here is large enough to **change the main outcome**. The meaning concerns the size of the impact, not merely whether a difference can be detected.

A tiny difference may be statistically detectable without being substantial. Small additional benefit, proportionate resources, and a foundational assumption concern other properties.
