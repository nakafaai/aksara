*Menenggelamkan* (to drown, to sink) is used here **figuratively**, not about water. The far brighter lamplight outshines the small firefly flashes, as if swallowing them up. As a result, by sentence (8), males and females struggle to find each other.

The other options do not fit:

- There is no water in sentence (8), so the literal meaning does not fit.
- The flashes do not get brighter, they are outshone.
- The reading does not say the flash speed changes.
- The street lamps do not dim, they are the ones that are too bright.

So the phrase means making the firefly flashes hard to see (*membuat kedipan kunang-kunang sulit terlihat*).
