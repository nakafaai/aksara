# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) of the **firefly** (*kunang-kunang*) passage states:

> Cahaya lampu jalan dan lampu rumah dapat <Highlight>menenggelamkan kedipan kunang-kunang</Highlight>, sehingga jantan dan betina sulit saling menemukan.

# Asked

The figurative meaning of *menenggelamkan kedipan kunang-kunang* (drowning out firefly flashes) in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).

# Solution

*Menenggelamkan* is figurative here: bright artificial lighting overpowers the tiny bioluminescent signals, **making firefly flashes hard to see** so males and females struggle to locate each other.

## Evaluating the Options

The consequence *hard to find each other* confirms the **figurative meaning**.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *membuat kunang-kunang jatuh ke air* | Wrong, literal drowning in water is absent |
| *membuat kedipan menjadi lebih terang* | Wrong, flashes are overpowered, not brightened |
| *membuat kunang-kunang berkedip lebih cepat* | Wrong, flash speed is not discussed |
| *membuat cahaya lampu jalan tampak redup* | Wrong, streetlights are overly bright |
| *membuat kedipan kunang-kunang sulit terlihat* | Correct, explains why mates cannot find each other |

# Answer

The phrase means **making firefly flashes hard to see** (*membuat kedipan kunang-kunang sulit terlihat*).
