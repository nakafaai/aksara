The word *dibakukan* comes from *baku*, meaning **a set measure or standard**. Raw materials that are *dibakukan* are made uniform to a set standard, so every batch has the same quality. The group's name, *obat herbal terstandar* (standardized herbal medicine), points to the same meaning.

The other options do not fit:

- Drying is just one way of processing, not what *dibakukan* means.
- The reading does not discuss synthetic chemicals.
- Price and packaging have nothing to do with *dibakukan*.

So *dibakukan* means made uniform to a set standard (*dibuat seragam menurut standar tertentu*).
