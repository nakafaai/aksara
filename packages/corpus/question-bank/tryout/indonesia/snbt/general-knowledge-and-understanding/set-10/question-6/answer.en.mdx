# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **jamu** passage describes the second category of natural medicine:

> Kelompok kedua adalah obat herbal terstandar, yang <Highlight>bahan bakunya sudah dibakukan</Highlight> dan khasiatnya sudah diuji pada hewan percobaan.

# Asked

The meaning of *dibakukan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

*Dibakukan* comes from *baku* (standard). Matching the category name *standardized herbal medicine* (*obat herbal terstandar*), it means **made uniform according to a set standard**.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **made uniform to a set standard** matches *baku* and *terstandar*.

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *dikeringkan lebih dulu di bawah sinar matahari* | Wrong, drying is not standardization |
| *dicampur dengan bahan kimia buatan pabrik* | Wrong, synthetic chemicals are not mentioned |
| *dibuat seragam menurut standar tertentu* | Correct, exact meaning of *dibakukan* |
| *dijual dengan harga yang lebih murah* | Wrong, refers to price |
| *dibungkus dalam kemasan yang rapat* | Wrong, refers to packaging |

# Answer

The word *dibakukan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) means **made uniform according to a specific standard** (*dibuat seragam menurut standar tertentu*).
