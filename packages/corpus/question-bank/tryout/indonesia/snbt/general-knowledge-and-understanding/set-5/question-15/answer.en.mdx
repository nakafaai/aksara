The **microplastics** passage develops across two paragraphs:

- Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="5" />): defines microplastics, lists multiple sources (cosmetics, broken-down plastics, clothing fibers), and notes uptake by small animals.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="6" /> to <InlineMath math="9" />): traces transfer through the food chain to fish and humans, notes ongoing health research, and describes regulatory and household limits.

# Asked

# Solution

A valid main idea must unite all **three core threads**: multiple sources, movement through the food chain, and emerging efforts to curb microplastics.


> Dari hewan kecil itu, <Highlight>mikroplastik dapat berpindah ke ikan yang memangsanya dan akhirnya sampai ke piring makan manusia</Highlight>.
## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor either **contradicts explicit facts** or exaggerates a single detail.

- Calling facial scrubs the *only* source contradicts sentences (<InlineMath math="3" />) and (<InlineMath math="4" />).
- Claiming disease is *already proven* contradicts sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />).
