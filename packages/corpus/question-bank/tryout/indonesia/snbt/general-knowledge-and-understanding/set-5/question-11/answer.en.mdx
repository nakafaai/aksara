# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) of the **microplastics** passage reads:

> Serat pakaian sintetis juga ikut terlepas ketika pakaian dicuci, lalu terbawa air limbah ke sungai.

# Asked

The word closest in meaning to *sintetis* in the phrase *pakaian sintetis*.

# Solution

*Sintetis* means **man-made or artificially produced** rather than natural. Because synthetic garments use artificial plastic fibers, washing them sheds microplastics into wastewater.

## Comparing the Options

Only **artificial** (*buatan*) matches the material origin expressed by *sintetis*.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *alami* | Natural | Wrong, antonym of *sintetis* |
| *tipis* | Thin | Wrong, describes thickness |
| *lembut* | Soft | Wrong, describes texture |
| *bekas* | Used or secondhand | Wrong, describes usage history |
| *buatan* | Artificial or man-made | Correct, direct synonym |

# Answer

The word *sintetis* is closest in meaning to **artificial** (*buatan*).
