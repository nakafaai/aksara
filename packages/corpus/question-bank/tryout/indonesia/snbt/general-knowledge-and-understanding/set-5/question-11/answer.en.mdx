The word *sintetis* (synthetic) means **made artificially**, usually from chemicals, rather than coming straight from nature. Synthetic clothes are made of man-made fibers such as polyester. That is why their fibers count as plastic and appear in a reading about microplastics.

The other options do not fit:

- *Alami* (natural) is the opposite of *sintetis*.
- *Tipis* (thin) describes thickness, not where the material comes from.
- *Lembut* (soft) describes the surface, not the origin of the material.
- *Bekas* (used, second-hand) means already worn and says nothing about the material.

So *sintetis* is closest in meaning to *buatan* (man-made).
