First name the relation in *petir : guruh* (lightning : thunder). By sentence (3), lightning heats the air and **produces** thunder. So the relation is **cause and effect**: the first word produces the second.

Fire produces smoke, so *api : asap* (fire : smoke) has the same relation.

The other pairs have different relations:

- *Payung* (umbrella) is a tool that protects against *hujan* (rain), not an effect of rain.
- *Awan* (cloud) is in the *langit* (sky), a thing and its place.
- A *guru* (teacher) works at a *sekolah* (school), a worker and the workplace.
- *Topan* (typhoon) is a very strong *angin* (wind), a matter of degree.

So the pair with the same relation as *petir : guruh* is *api : asap*.
