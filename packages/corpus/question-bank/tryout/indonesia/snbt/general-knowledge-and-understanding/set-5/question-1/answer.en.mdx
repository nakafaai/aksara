# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **subak** passage states that a *serentak* planting schedule across one area helps control pests.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) adds that after this synchronized harvest, all fields in the area are left dry for a while so pests lose their food source and decline.

# Asked

The meaning of the word *serentak* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

The word *serentak* means **happening at the same time**. In sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />), farmers across one area plant together so that harvest and drying also occur together.


> Planting at the same time lets harvesting and field drying happen together so pests lose their food supply.
## Context Proof and Option Check

Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) confirms **simultaneous timing** because drying all fields at once prevents pests from moving to neighboring plots.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *berganti-ganti* | Taking turns | Wrong, opposite of same time |
| *bersamaan* | At the same time | Correct, matches shared timing |
| *bertahap* | Step by step | Wrong, implies separate stages |
| *berulang-ulang* | Repeatedly | Wrong, refers to frequency |
| *beraturan* | Orderly | Wrong, does not require simultaneous action |

# Answer

The word *serentak* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) means **at the same time** (*bersamaan*).
