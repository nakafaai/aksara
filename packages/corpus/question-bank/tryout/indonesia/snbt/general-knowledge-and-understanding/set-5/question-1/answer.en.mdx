The word *serentak* means **happening at the same time**. In sentence (6), a *serentak* planting schedule means the farmers in one area plant at the same time.

This fits sentence (7). Since everyone plants together, everyone harvests together, so the whole area can be left dry at once and pests cannot move to another field.

The other options do not fit:

- *Berganti-ganti* (taking turns) is the opposite of at the same time.
- *Bertahap* (in stages) means little by little in several steps.
- *Berulang-ulang* (repeatedly) means done many times.
- *Beraturan* (orderly) means neat or regular, not necessarily at the same time.

So *serentak* in sentence (6) means simultaneous (*bersamaan*).
