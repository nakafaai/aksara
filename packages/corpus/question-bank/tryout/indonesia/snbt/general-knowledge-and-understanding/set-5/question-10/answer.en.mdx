# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **lightning** passage states:

> Jika guruh sudah terdengar, <Highlight>seseorang sudah berada cukup dekat untuk tersambar</Highlight>.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) immediately recommends taking shelter indoors.

# Asked

The most accurate interpretation of sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) treats **hearing thunder as a danger signal**. Once thunder is audible, the lightning storm is already close enough to strike.

## Checking the Other Four Options

Each distractor introduces a **logical or factual error** not supported by the passage.

- Lightning strikes people, whereas thunder is only the sound.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) sets no requirement that thunder be very loud.
- The text never guarantees safety when thunder is not heard.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) explicitly says distance can be estimated from the time gap between flash and thunder.

# Answer

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) is best interpreted as **hearing thunder signals that the danger of a lightning strike is already close**.

(*Terdengarnya guruh menandakan bahwa bahaya sambaran petir sudah dekat.*)
