# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="8" />) and (<InlineMath math="9" />) of the **pinisi** passage describe a shift in how the ships are used:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): most new pinisi ships are now built for marine tourism instead of cargo transport.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />): this shift opens a new market for craftsmen, yet timber suitable for the hull is increasingly difficult to obtain.

# Asked

The statement best supported by sentences (<InlineMath math="8" />) and (<InlineMath math="9" />).

# Solution

The contrast word *tetapi* (but) in sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) links **both sides of the shift**: marine tourism creates new demand for shipbuilders, while suitable hull wood becomes scarcer.


> Perubahan itu <Highlight>membuka pasar baru bagi para perajin, tetapi kayu yang cocok untuk lambung kapal dilaporkan makin sulit diperoleh</Highlight>.
## Checking the Other Options

The remaining options introduce **unsupported claims** or contradict sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />).

- The passage never says wood demand dropped, since tourism ships still need wooden hulls.
- Craftsmen gained a new market rather than stopping production for lack of buyers.
- No non-wood hull material is mentioned.
- The <InlineMath math="2017" /> UNESCO listing in sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) is never linked to timber scarcity.

# Answer

**Marine tourism opens a new market, but timber for the ships is becoming harder to obtain**.

(*Wisata bahari membuka pasar baru, tetapi bahan kayu untuk kapal makin sulit didapat.*)
