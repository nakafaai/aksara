# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) of the **lightning** passage explains why isolated trees are dangerous:

> Berteduh di bawah pohon yang berdiri sendiri justru berbahaya, sebab <Highlight>petir cenderung menyambar benda yang tinggi</Highlight> dan arusnya dapat melompat ke orang di dekatnya.

# Asked

What the word *cenderung* in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) shows about lightning.

# Solution

*Cenderung* expresses a **tendency or higher likelihood**, not an absolute rule. Lightning strikes tall objects more often, although not in every single case.

## Eliminating Overstated Options

The other options distort the **degree of certainty** or invent conditions absent from the text.

- *Always* turns a tendency into certainty.
- *Never* makes an absolute claim that low objects are completely safe.
- *Only isolated trees* mistakes one example for the sole target.
- *Only when raining* adds a weather condition not mentioned in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).

# Answer

The word *cenderung* shows that lightning **strikes tall objects more often, though not always**.

(*lebih sering menyambar benda tinggi, walaupun tidak selalu*)
