The word *cenderung* means **inclined to, more likely to**. It expresses a tendency, not a certainty. So lightning strikes tall objects more often, but not always.

The other options change the strength of the meaning:

- *Selalu* (always) turns a tendency into a certainty.
- *Tidak pernah* (never) is also too absolute. The reading does not say low objects are completely safe.
- A lone tree is only **an example** of a tall object, not the only target.
- The reading does not link the tendency to rainfall.

So *cenderung* shows that lightning strikes tall objects more often, though not always.
