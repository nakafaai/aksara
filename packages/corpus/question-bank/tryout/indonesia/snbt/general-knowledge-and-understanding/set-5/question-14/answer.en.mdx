# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="7" />) and (<InlineMath math="8" />) of the **microplastics** passage state:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />): researchers are still studying how large the impact is on human health.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />): *Meskipun begitu* (even so), many countries have already banned plastic beads in cosmetics.

# Asked

The logical relationship signaled by *Meskipun begitu* at the start of sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).

# Solution

*Meskipun begitu* is a **concessive contrast marker**. Even though health impacts are not yet settled in sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />), many nations have already enacted bans in sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />).


> <Highlight>Meskipun begitu, banyak negara sudah melarang butiran plastik</Highlight> dalam produk kosmetik.
## Eliminating the Other Relationships

Every other choice clashes with both the **contrast connector** and the facts of the two sentences.

- Cause and effect fails because sentence (<InlineMath math="7" />) says the impact is still being studied, not proven.
- Time sequence fails because the text never says bans preceded research.
- Elaboration fails because sentence (<InlineMath math="8" />) does not detail research results.
- Comparison fails because the passage does not rank bans as more important than research.

# Answer

*Meskipun begitu* indicates **contrast, because the ban was enacted even though the impact has not yet been confirmed**.

(*pertentangan, karena larangan dibuat walaupun dampaknya belum dipastikan*)
