*Meskipun begitu* (even so) is a connective that signals **contrast**: what follows happens despite a situation in the previous sentence that seems not to support it.

Sentence (7) says the health effects of microplastics are **still being studied**. Sentence (8) says many countries **have already banned** plastic beads. So the bans came even though the effects are not yet confirmed.

The other options do not fit:

- Sentence (7) says the effects are not yet proven, so there is no such cause and effect.
- The reading does not say the bans came before research began.
- Sentence (8) does not detail research results.
- The reading does not compare how important bans and research are.

So *Meskipun begitu* shows contrast: the bans were made even though the effects are not yet confirmed.
