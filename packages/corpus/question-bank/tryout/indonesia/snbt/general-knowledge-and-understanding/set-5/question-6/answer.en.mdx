# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) of the **lightning** (*petir*) passage explains how thunder forms:

> Saluran petir memanaskan udara di sekitarnya dalam waktu yang sangat singkat sehingga udara memuai dengan cepat dan menimbulkan bunyi guruh.

# Asked

The meaning of the word *memuai* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />).

# Solution

*Memuai* means **to increase in volume due to heating**. Sudden heating along the lightning channel makes the surrounding air expand rapidly, producing thunder.

## Comparing the Options

Testing each option against **rapid air heating** confirms *mengembang* as the exact match.

| Option | Meaning | Evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *menyusut* | Shrink or contract | Wrong, opposite of expanding |
| *mendingin* | Cool down | Wrong, the air is heated |
| *menguap* | Evaporate from liquid to gas | Wrong, air is already a gas |
| *mengembang* | Expand in volume | Correct, exact synonym of *memuai* |
| *menghilang* | Disappear | Wrong, the air remains and expands |

# Answer

The word *memuai* in sentence (<InlineMath math="3" />) means **to expand** (*mengembang*).
