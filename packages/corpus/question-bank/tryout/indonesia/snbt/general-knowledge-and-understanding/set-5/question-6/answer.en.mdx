The word *memuai* means **to grow in volume because of heat**. In sentence (3), the lightning channel heats the air very quickly, so the air expands rapidly and produces thunder.

The other options do not fit:

- *Menyusut* (to shrink) is the opposite of *memuai*.
- *Mendingin* (to cool) contradicts the air being heated.
- *Menguap* (to evaporate) means turning from liquid to gas, but air is already a gas.
- *Menghilang* (to vanish) does not fit, since the air is still there and actually grows in volume.

So *memuai* in sentence (3) means to expand (*mengembang*).
