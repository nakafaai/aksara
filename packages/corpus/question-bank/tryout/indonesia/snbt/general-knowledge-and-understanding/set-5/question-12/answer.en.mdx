The word *itu* (that, those) points to something **already mentioned**. The phrase *hewan kecil* (small animals) appears at the end of sentence (5): microplastics are easily eaten by small animals, **such as plankton and shellfish**. Sentence (6) continues from those small animals to the fish that eat them.

The other options do not fit:

- Fish prey on those small animals, so fish appear **after** the phrase as a different party.
- Scrub beads and clothing fibers are sources of microplastics, not animals.
- People are at the end of the food chain, not the small animals meant here.

So *hewan kecil itu* refers to the plankton and shellfish that eat microplastics.
