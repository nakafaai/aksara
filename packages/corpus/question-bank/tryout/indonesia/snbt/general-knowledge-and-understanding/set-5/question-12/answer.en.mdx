# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="5" />) and (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **microplastics** passage follow each other directly:

- Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />): microplastics are easily eaten by small animals, such as plankton and shellfish.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />): from *hewan kecil itu* (those small animals), microplastics move into fish that prey on them and finally to human plates.

# Asked

The referent of the phrase *hewan kecil itu* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

The demonstrative *itu* points back to the **nearest antecedent** at the end of sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />), namely **plankton and shellfish** that ingest microplastics.


> Karena ukurannya sangat kecil, mikroplastik tidak seluruhnya tertahan oleh instalasi pengolahan air limbah dan <Highlight>mudah termakan oleh hewan kecil, seperti plankton dan kerang</Highlight>.
## Why the Other Options Are Incorrect

Tracing the **food chain order** rules out the other four choices immediately.

- Fish are the predators introduced later in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).
- Scrubbing beads and clothing fibers are sources of microplastics, not animals.
- Humans stand at the end of the chain rather than being the small animals.

# Answer

The phrase *hewan kecil itu* refers to **plankton and shellfish that eat microplastics**.

(*plankton dan kerang yang memakan mikroplastik*)
