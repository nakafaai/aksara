# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) of the **pinisi** passage states:

> <Highlight>Pembuatan sebuah kapal besar dapat memakan waktu berbulan-bulan</Highlight> dan diiringi beberapa upacara adat.

# Asked

The meaning of the affixed word *pembuatan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Solution

*Pembuatan* combines the root *buat* with the circumfix **pe-an**, which denotes a **process or activity**. The predicate *taking months* and *accompanied by rituals* applies only to an ongoing process.

## Comparing Related Indonesian Forms

Checking neighboring derivations of *buat* confirms **process or activity** as the right sense.

- The *person* who builds is *pembuat* (prefix *pe-*), as seen in sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).
- The *product* or result is *buatan* (suffix *-an*).
- *Pembuatan* does not refer to a tool.
- A *place* cannot take several months to occur.

# Answer

The word *pembuatan* in sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />) means **the process or activity of building a ship**.

(*proses atau kegiatan membuat kapal*)
