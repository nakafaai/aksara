*Pembuatan* is formed from the root *buat* (make) with the circumfix *pe-an*. This affix often expresses **a process or act** of doing something, as in *pengiriman* (the process of sending). Sentence (6) says *pembuatan* a ship *takes months*, and only a process can last that long.

The other options do not fit:

- The person who makes is *pembuat*, with *pe-* alone, as in *para pembuat kapal* (the boatbuilders) in sentence (4).
- The result of making is better called *buatan*.
- A tool for making is not what *pe-an* expresses in this word.
- A place where boats are made does not fit *takes months*.

So *pembuatan* in sentence (6) means the process or activity of building a ship.
