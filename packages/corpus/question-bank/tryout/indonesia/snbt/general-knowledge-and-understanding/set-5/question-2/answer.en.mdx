# Given

Sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **subak** passage directly defines the term *awig-awig*:

> Semua ketentuan itu dituangkan dalam awig-awig, yaitu <Highlight>aturan adat yang disepakati bersama</Highlight> dan memuat sanksi bagi anggota yang melanggarnya.

Sentences (<InlineMath math="2" />) through (<InlineMath math="4" />) show that these provisions cover water allocation and planting schedules among subak members.

# Asked

The meaning of *awig-awig* based on sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />).

# Solution

The definition marker *yaitu* in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />) directly identifies *awig-awig* as **customary rules agreed upon together** by subak members.

## Checking Each Distractor

The other options confuse *awig-awig* with **irrigation channels**, a single part of the rules, or the organization itself.

- The water channel dividing water to each plot is the physical infrastructure in sentence (<InlineMath math="2" />).
- The planting schedule is set by the subak rather than the local government, and it is only one regulated topic.
- Sanctions are merely one component included inside *awig-awig*.
- The farmers organization sharing one water source is the *subak* itself in sentence (<InlineMath math="1" />).

# Answer

Based on sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />), *awig-awig* is **customary rules agreed upon jointly by subak members**.

(*aturan adat yang disepakati bersama oleh anggota subak*)
