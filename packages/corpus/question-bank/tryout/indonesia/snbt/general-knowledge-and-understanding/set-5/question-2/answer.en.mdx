Sentence (5) gives a direct definition with *yaitu* (that is): *awig-awig* is **a customary rule agreed on together** that includes sanctions for those who break it. The provisions it contains are the water sharing and planting schedules discussed in the sentences before.

The other options do not fit:

- Water channels are the means of sharing water in sentence (2), not *awig-awig*.
- The planting schedule is set by the subak, not the regional government, and it is only one part of the rules.
- Sanctions are only **part** of what *awig-awig* contains, not *awig-awig* itself.
- The organization of farmers who share a water source is the subak itself, according to sentence (1).

So *awig-awig* is a customary rule agreed on together by the subak members.
