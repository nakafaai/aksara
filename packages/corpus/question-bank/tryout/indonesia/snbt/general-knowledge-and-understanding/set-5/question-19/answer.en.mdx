The word *tradisional* (traditional) means **following ways or customs handed down for a long time**. Its opposite is *modern*, following present-day ways or technology. In the reading, the pinisi's traditional character shows in how it is built: led by a panrita lopi, without written plans, and from inherited knowledge.

The other options do not fit:

- *Kuno* (ancient) means from the distant past, close in meaning to *tradisional*, not its opposite.
- *Adat* (custom) relates to inherited customs and is also close in meaning.
- *Lokal* (local) means of a place. Its opposite is *global* or *asing* (foreign), not *tradisional*.
- *Klasik* (classic) means of lasting high value, not the opposite of *tradisional*.

So the opposite of *tradisional* is *modern*.
