# Given

**The word *tradisional*** in sentence (1) refers to building pinisi ships using inherited knowledge without written blueprints.

# Asked

The sentence whose italicized word is opposite in meaning to *tradisional* in sentence (1).

# Solution

> Anchor on the exact clue in sentence (4): **the opposite of inherited methods is present-day technology (*modern*), not *foreign* or *complex***.

The passage itself tells you what *tradisional* means in sentence (1). Sentence (4) explains that a pinisi is built by craftsmen led by a panrita lopi, without written plans, from knowledge handed down. So **traditional here means made by inherited methods**, and its opposite is the use of present-day technology, *modern*. The steel ships built with *modern* technology in a Surabaya shipyard are the right opposite.

| Clue in sentence (4) | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| led by a panrita lopi | the craft is kept by a customary master |
| no written design drawings | no present-day engineering method |
| knowledge handed down | a method inherited across generations |

The other choices tempt because they sit close to the passage's mood. A *kuno* ("ancient") method comes from the past, so it points the same way as traditional, not against it.

An *adat* ceremony ("customary") and a *lokal* product ("local") are only neighbours of traditional: custom is a practice kept alive, local means from the area, and their opposites are different, foreign or imported. A *sederhana* ("simple") tool can be perfectly modern, so simplicity is not the opposite of an inherited method.

# Answer

***Tradisional*** in sentence (1) is opposite in meaning to *modern* in the sentence about steel ships built with modern technology in a Surabaya shipyard.
