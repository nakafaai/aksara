# Given

The **subak** passage has two connected paragraphs:

- Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="5" />): farmers coordinate water sharing, irrigation turns, and planting schedules through *awig-awig*.
- Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> (sentences <InlineMath math="6" /> to <InlineMath math="9" />): synchronized planting curbs pests, while converting even one rice plot disrupts water flow to plots below.

# Asked

The statement that best fits the passage as a whole.

# Solution

The best overall statement captures the **shared theme of both paragraphs**: each subak plot succeeds only through collective management of water and planting schedules.


> Setiap subak mengatur pembagian air melalui saluran dan bendung kecil, lalu <Highlight>menetapkan jadwal tanam bagi anggotanya</Highlight>.
## Checking the Other Options

Each remaining choice introduces a **factual distortion** or unsupported extreme claim.

- Subak is a customary farmers organization rather than a government agency, and water is not always divided equally.
- Sentence (<InlineMath math="9" />) states that land conversion to buildings is still happening today.
- The passage never compares rice yields between upstream and downstream fields.
- Saying pests can *only* be controlled by drying fields exaggerates sentence (<InlineMath math="6" />).

# Answer

**The success of each subak rice plot depends on joint management of water and planting time**.

(*Keberhasilan setiap petak sawah subak bergantung pada pengaturan bersama atas air dan waktu tanam.*)
