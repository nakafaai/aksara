**Closure of most lending services** supplies an alternative explanation. Fewer late returns may result from fewer opportunities to borrow, even if users’ return behaviour is unchanged.

| Possible explanation | What changes? | What needs comparing? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reminders and restrictions help | Users return books more promptly | Returns for comparable groups and loan periods |
| Lending services close | Fewer loans can become overdue | The number of loans and the proportion returned late |

A lower count of late returns alone therefore cannot distinguish the explanations. Improvement after contact details are updated supports a reminder benefit, but other factors still need consideration. Card design, trial plans, and changed numbers do not offer as strong an alternative cause as the closure.
