*Turun-temurun* means **handed down from parents to children, then to grandchildren**, and so on. In sentence (5), boatbuilding knowledge is passed from one generation to the next.

This also fits sentence (4): the boats are built without written plans, so the knowledge passes through experience and teaching, not through notes.

The other options do not fit:

- *Secara cepat dan mendadak* (quickly and suddenly) contradicts a long process of inheritance.
- *Turun* (down) in this word does not mean from the top of the boat to the bottom.
- *Sedikit demi sedikit setiap hari* (little by little every day) describes a way of learning, not the meaning of *turun-temurun*.
- Sentence (4) says there are no written plans, so passing knowledge through notebooks does not fit.

So *turun-temurun* means from one generation to the next.
