# Given

Sentences (<InlineMath math="4" />) and (<InlineMath math="5" />) of the **pinisi** passage describe how shipbuilding knowledge is passed on:

> Ukuran dan bentuk kapal ditentukan berdasarkan pengalaman serta pengetahuan yang diwariskan turun-temurun.

Sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) also notes that the hull is assembled without written blueprints.

# Asked

The meaning of *turun-temurun* in sentence (<InlineMath math="5" />).

# Solution

The idiom *turun-temurun* means **passed down from one generation to the next**. Without written plans, pinisi shipbuilders hand down their craft directly across generations.

## Evaluating the Options

Only **generational transfer** matches both the dictionary idiom and the context of sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />).

| Option | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| *secara cepat dan mendadak* | Wrong, inheritance spans long periods |
| *dari bagian atas ke bagian bawah kapal* | Wrong, takes *turun* literally as physical direction |
| *dari satu generasi ke generasi berikutnya* | Correct, standard meaning of *turun-temurun* |
| *sedikit demi sedikit setiap hari* | Wrong, describes daily pacing |
| *dari satu buku catatan ke buku catatan lainnya* | Wrong, sentence (<InlineMath math="4" />) states there are no written designs |

# Answer

The word *turun-temurun* means **from one generation to the next** (*dari satu generasi ke generasi berikutnya*).
