Each figure must meet **both upper bounds**: at most two reflection axes and at most two matching positions during one full turn. Count the initial position once, via the <InlineMath math="360^\circ" /> turn.

| Figure | Reflection axes | Rotational symmetries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Right trapezoid | 0 | 1: only 360° |
| Regular pentagon | 5: each vertex to the opposite side midpoint | 5: multiples of 72° |
| Equilateral triangle | 3: each vertex to the opposite side midpoint | 3: 120°, 240°, 360° |
| Kite that is not a rhombus | 1: the diagonal through vertices where equal adjacent sides meet | 1: only 360° |

The kite below has **one reflection axis**. A 180° turn does not map it onto itself because the top and bottom vertices are at different distances from the diagonal intersection.
