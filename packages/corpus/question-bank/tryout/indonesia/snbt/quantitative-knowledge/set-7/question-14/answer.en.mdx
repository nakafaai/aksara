Rewrite the equation in centre and radius form by **completing the square** in <InlineMath math="x" /> and in <InlineMath math="y" />.

# Centre and radius

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(x^2-4x+4)+(y^2+2y+1)&=20+4+1\\
(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2&=25
\end{aligned}" />

The centre is <InlineMath math="(2,-1)" /> and **the radius is <InlineMath math="\sqrt{25}=5" />, not 25**. The first statement is false.

# Position of the points

A point lies on the circle when **its distance to the centre equals 5**, inside when it is less, and outside when it is more.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(5,3)&:\quad (5-2)^2+(3+1)^2=9+16=25\\
(0,0)&:\quad (0-2)^2+(0+1)^2=4+1=5<25
\end{aligned}" />

The point <InlineMath math="(5,3)" /> lies exactly on the circle, so the second statement is true. The origin lies inside the circle, so the third statement is false.

# Position of the line

The distance from the centre <InlineMath math="(2,-1)" /> to the horizontal line <InlineMath math="y=4" /> is <InlineMath math="4-(-1)=5" />. This distance **equals the radius**, so the line touches the circle exactly at <InlineMath math="(2,4)" />. The fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
