Compare the **reflection count and rotational order**. Count the full 360° turn once. A reflection axis must divide a shape into mirror-image halves.

| Figure | Reflection axes | Rotational symmetries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Isosceles trapezoid | 1: through the midpoints of its parallel sides | 1: 360° |
| Rectangle that is not a square | 2: through opposite side midpoints | 2: 180° and 360° |
| Equilateral triangle | 3: each vertex to the opposite side midpoint | 3: 120°, 240°, 360° |
| Kite that is not a rhombus | 1: the diagonal through vertices between equal adjacent sides | 1: 360° |

In the trapezoid below, **one vertical line** is a reflection axis. A 180° rotation swaps the long and short bases, so the trapezoid does not match its initial position.
