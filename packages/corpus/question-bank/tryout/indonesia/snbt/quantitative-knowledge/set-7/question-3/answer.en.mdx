A selected number must satisfy **both remainder conditions**. Check division by 5 and 4 for every candidate.

| Number | Division by 5 | Division by 4 | Satisfies both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | <InlineMath math="19=5\times3+4" /> | <InlineMath math="19=4\times4+3" /> | Yes |
| 29 | <InlineMath math="29=5\times5+4" /> | <InlineMath math="29=4\times7+1" /> | No |
| 39 | <InlineMath math="39=5\times7+4" /> | <InlineMath math="39=4\times9+3" /> | Yes |
| 49 | <InlineMath math="49=5\times9+4" /> | <InlineMath math="49=4\times12+1" /> | No |

Only 19 and 39 satisfy both conditions, namely **choices 1 and 3**.
