#### The axis of symmetry

The parabola opens to the left, so its axis of symmetry is a **horizontal line**. The two vertical-axis intercepts reflect into each other. The axis lies halfway between their vertical coordinates:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}y_p&=\frac{3+(-5)}2\\&=\frac{-2}2\\&=-1\end{aligned}" />

The axis is <InlineMath math="y=-1" />, not the vertical line <InlineMath math="x=-3" />. Therefore, **statements 1 and 3** are correct.
