Diketahui matriks:

<BlockMath math="A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 3 - x \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}" />

Untuk matriks berordo <InlineMath math="2\times2" />, determinan adalah hasil kali elemen diagonal utama dikurangi hasil kali elemen diagonal lainnya:

<BlockMath math="\det\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}=ad-bc" />

Hitung determinan matriks <InlineMath math="A" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\det(A)&=(5)(1)\\&\quad-(3-x)(-1)\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\det(A) = 5 - (-(3 - x))" />
  <BlockMath math="\det(A) = 5 + (3 - x)" />
  <BlockMath math="\det(A) = 8 - x" />
</MathContainer>

Diketahui <InlineMath math="\det(A) = 8" />, maka:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="8 - x = 8" />
  <BlockMath math="-x = 8 - 8" />
  <BlockMath math="-x = 0" />
  <BlockMath math="x = 0" />
</MathContainer>

Jadi, nilai <InlineMath math="x" /> adalah <InlineMath math="0" />.
