# Volume and lateral area

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
V&=\pi r^2t=\pi\cdot9\cdot8=72\pi\\
A_{\text{lateral}}&=2\pi rt=2\pi\cdot3\cdot8=48\pi
\end{aligned}" />

The first statement is **true**. The lateral area is <InlineMath math="48\pi" />, not <InlineMath math="24\pi" />, so the third statement is false. The value <InlineMath math="24\pi" /> appears when the factor 2 in the circumference of the base is forgotten.

# The greatest distance and a change of height

The two farthest points of the cylinder lie on the rim of the base and the rim of the top, on opposite sides of the axis. They form the diagonal of a rectangle measuring the 6 cm diameter by the 8 cm height, which is **the cross-section through the axis**.

<BlockMath math="d=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{100}=10" />

The second statement is true. The volume <InlineMath math="\pi r^2t" /> is proportional to <InlineMath math="t" /> to the first power, so doubling the height doubles the volume, not quadruples it. The fourth statement is false.
