Only water is added, so the **alcohol volume stays constant**. Alcohol has four parts in both ratios, meaning one part represents the same volume before and after the addition.

| Stage | Alcohol parts | Water parts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial | 4 | 3 |
| Final | 4 | 5 |
| Change | 0 | 2 |

The added 5 litres correspond to **two extra parts**, since <InlineMath math="5-3=2" />.

The volume of one part is:

<BlockMath math="5\,\mathrm{L}\div2=2.5\,\mathrm{L}" />

The final water volume contains five parts:

<BlockMath math="5\cdot2.5\,\mathrm{L}=12.5\,\mathrm{L}" />
