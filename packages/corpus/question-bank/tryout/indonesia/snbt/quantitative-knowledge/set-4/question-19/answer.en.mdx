- Initial ratio of alcohol : water is <InlineMath math="4 : 3" />
- Added <InlineMath math="5\text{ liters}" /> of water
- Final ratio of alcohol : water is <InlineMath math="4 : 5" />

#### Analyzing Ratio Changes

Since only water is added, the amount of alcohol remains constant.
Look at the alcohol ratio parts:

- Initial: <InlineMath math="4" /> parts
- Final: <InlineMath math="4" /> parts

Since the alcohol parts are already equal (<InlineMath math="4" />), we can directly compare the water parts.

#### Calculating Current Water Volume

Change in water parts:

- Initial: <InlineMath math="3" /> parts
- Final: <InlineMath math="5" /> parts
- Difference: <InlineMath math="5 - 3 = 2" /> parts

This difference of <InlineMath math="2" /> parts corresponds to the addition of <InlineMath math="5\text{ liters}" /> of water.

So, the value per part is:

<BlockMath math="5\,\mathrm{L}\div2=2.5\,\mathrm{L}" />

The current volume of water (<InlineMath math="5" /> parts) is:

<BlockMath math="5\cdot2.5\,\mathrm{L}=12.5\,\mathrm{L}" />

Therefore, the amount of water currently contained in the solution is <InlineMath math="12.5\text{ liters}" />.
