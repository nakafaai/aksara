# The intersection points

Solve <InlineMath math="\lvert x-2\rvert=4" />.

<BlockMath math="x-2=4\ \ \text{or}\ \ x-2=-4\quad\Rightarrow\quad x=6\ \ \text{or}\ \ x=-2" />

The vertices of the triangle are <InlineMath math="(-2,4)" />, <InlineMath math="(6,4)" />, and the vertex of the V at <InlineMath math="(2,0)" />. The <InlineMath math="y" />-axis **does not bound the region**, because the question does not mention it.

# Computing the area

The base of the triangle lies on the line <InlineMath math="y=4" /> with length <InlineMath math="6-(-2)=8" />. The height is the distance from <InlineMath math="(2,0)" /> to that line, which is 4.

<BlockMath math="A=\tfrac12\cdot8\cdot4=16" />

The value 32 forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\tfrac12" />. The value 8 counts only the half of the triangle on one side of the vertex. The values 14 and 6 cut the region with the <InlineMath math="y" />-axis, which is not part of the boundary.

So the area of the region is **16 square units**.
