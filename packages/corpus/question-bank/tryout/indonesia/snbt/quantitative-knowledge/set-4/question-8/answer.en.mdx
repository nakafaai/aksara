A figure must meet **both minimum counts**: at least two rotational symmetries and three reflection axes. Count the initial position as one rotational symmetry.

| Figure | Rotational symmetries | Reflection axes | Both conditions? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scalene triangle | 1: initial position | 0: no reflection axis | No |
| Parallelogram that is neither rectangle nor rhombus | 2: rotations of 0° and 180° | 0: no reflection axis | No |
| Ellipse that is not a circle | 2: rotations of 0° and 180° | 2: major and minor axes | No |
| Regular hexagon | 6: multiples of 60° from 0° to 300° | 6: three through opposite vertices and three through opposite side midpoints | Yes |

The diagram shows the **six reflection axes** of the regular hexagon. The ellipse has only two axes and falls short of the required three. Therefore, only one figure meets both conditions.
