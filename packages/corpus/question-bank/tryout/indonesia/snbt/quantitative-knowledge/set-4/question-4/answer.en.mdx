Given the functions <InlineMath math="f(x) = x^2 - x" /> and <InlineMath math="g(x) = 2x - 1" />.
We need the **composite function** <InlineMath math="(f \circ g)(x)" />.

<BlockMath math="(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))" />

We substitute <InlineMath math="g(x)" /> into the function <InlineMath math="f(x)" />.

<BlockMath math="(f \circ g)(x) = f(2x - 1)" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}(f\circ g)(x)&=(2x-1)^2\\&\quad-(2x-1)\end{aligned}" />

Next, we expand the square and **combine like terms**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
(f\circ g)(x)&=4x^2-4x+1 \\
&\quad{}-2x+1 \\
&=4x^2-6x+2
\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, <InlineMath math="(f \circ g)(x)" /> equals <InlineMath math="4x^2 - 6x + 2" />.
