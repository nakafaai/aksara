First find the total of every row and column. Each statement uses **a different reference group**, so pay attention to which group counts as the whole.

| Gender | Scouts | Basketball | Total |

| Girls | 20 | 10 | 30 |
| Total | 32 | 28 | 60 |

# Conditional probability and group sizes

The first statement chooses a student **only from the Scouts members**, so the sample space has 32 students, not 60. The probability is <InlineMath math="\frac{20}{32}=\frac58" />, so the first statement is true. There are 30 boys and 30 girls, so the fourth statement is true as well.

# The two percentage statements

Basketball has 28 of the 60 students, that is, <InlineMath math="\frac{28}{60}\approx46.7\%" />, less than half. The second statement is false.

Among the 30 boys, 18 play basketball, that is, <InlineMath math="\frac{18}{30}=60\%" />. This is **not more than 65%**, so the third statement is false. Note that the divisor is the 30 boys, not the 28 basketball players.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
