We need numbers that **fail at least one condition**. Every candidate leaves remainder 3 on division by 7, but each must also be odd.

| Entry | Number | Division by 7 | Division by 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 94 | <InlineMath math="94=7\cdot13+3" /> | <InlineMath math="94=2\cdot47+0" /> |
| 2 | 80 | <InlineMath math="80=7\cdot11+3" /> | <InlineMath math="80=2\cdot40+0" /> |
| 3 | 38 | <InlineMath math="38=7\cdot5+3" /> | <InlineMath math="38=2\cdot19+0" /> |
| 4 | 17 | <InlineMath math="17=7\cdot2+3" /> | <InlineMath math="17=2\cdot8+1" /> |

The numbers 94, 80, and 38 are **even**, so they fail the required remainder 1 on division by 2. The number 17 satisfies both conditions. The requested entries are therefore (1), (2), and (3).
