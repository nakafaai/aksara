The centroid of a triangle is **the mean of the coordinates of its three vertices**. So three times the coordinates of <InlineMath math="G" /> equal the sum of the coordinates of <InlineMath math="A" />, <InlineMath math="B" /> and <InlineMath math="C" />.

# The x and y coordinates

Let <InlineMath math="C(p,q)" />. Solve **each coordinate separately**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{-1+5+p}{3}&=2\;\Longrightarrow\;4+p=6\;\Longrightarrow\;p=2\\
\frac{4+(-2)+q}{3}&=3\;\Longrightarrow\;2+q=9\;\Longrightarrow\;q=7
\end{aligned}" />
