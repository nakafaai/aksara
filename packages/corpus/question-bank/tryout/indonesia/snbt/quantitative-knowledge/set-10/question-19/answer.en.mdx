Let <InlineMath math="S_I" /> and <InlineMath math="S_{II}" /> denote the semester totals, <InlineMath math="S" /> the overall total, <InlineMath math="\bar x_{II}" /> the second-semester mean, and <InlineMath math="\Delta" /> the increase in the mean.

The total score in Semester <InlineMath math="I" /> is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_I&=5 \times 7.5\\&=37.5\end{aligned}" />

The total score across both semesters is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S&=11 \times 8\\&=88\end{aligned}" />

Semester <InlineMath math="II" /> therefore contains <InlineMath math="11-5=6" /> exams with a combined score of:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}S_{II}&=88 - 37.5\\&=50.5\end{aligned}" />

The Semester <InlineMath math="II" /> average is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\bar x_{II}&=\frac{50.5}{6}\\&=\frac{101}{12}\\&\approx 8.4167\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the Semester <InlineMath math="I" /> average before rounding:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\Delta&=\frac{101}{12}-7.5\\&=\frac{11}{12}\\&\approx 0.9167\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="0.9167\approx0.92" />
</MathContainer>

Rounded to two decimal places, Hani's average increases by <InlineMath math="0.92" /> points.
