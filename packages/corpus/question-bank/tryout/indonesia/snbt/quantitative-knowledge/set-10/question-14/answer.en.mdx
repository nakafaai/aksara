Let <InlineMath math="a=1.\overline{54}" />. Because the repeating block has two digits, multiply by <InlineMath math="100" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="100a=154.\overline{54}" />
</MathContainer>

Subtracting <InlineMath math="a" /> from <InlineMath math="100a" /> cancels the repeating tail:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="100a-a=154.\overline{54}-1.\overline{54}" />
  <BlockMath math="99a=153" />
  <BlockMath math="a=\frac{153}{99}=\frac{17}{11}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, <InlineMath math="1.\overline{54}=\frac{17}{11}" />.
