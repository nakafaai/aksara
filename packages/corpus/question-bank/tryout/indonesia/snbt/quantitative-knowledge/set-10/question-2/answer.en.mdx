The angle between the two diagonal lines above the intersection is **vertically opposite** <InlineMath math="z^\circ" />. Vertically opposite angles are equal, so that upper angle also measures <InlineMath math="z^\circ" />.

The three adjacent angles <InlineMath math="x^\circ" />, <InlineMath math="z^\circ" />, and <InlineMath math="y^\circ" /> form a straight angle, so their measures add to <InlineMath math="180^\circ" />:

<BlockMath math="x^\circ + z^\circ + y^\circ = 180^\circ" />

Because <InlineMath math="x^\circ = y^\circ" />, replace <InlineMath math="y" /> with <InlineMath math="x" />:

<BlockMath math="x^\circ + z^\circ + x^\circ = 180^\circ" />

<BlockMath math="z^\circ + 2x^\circ = 180^\circ" />

<BlockMath math="z^\circ = 180^\circ - 2x^\circ" />

Thus, angle <InlineMath math="z" /> measures <InlineMath math="180 - 2x" /> degrees.
