For one fixed participant, <InlineMath math="20" /> of the <InlineMath math="3000" /> equally likely participant positions are winning positions. Let <InlineMath math="A" /> be the event that this participant wins. Then:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="P(A) = \frac{\text{number of prizes}}{\text{number of participants}}" />
  <BlockMath math="P(A) = \frac{20}{3000}" />
  <BlockMath math="P(A) = \frac{1}{150}" />
  <BlockMath math="P(A) \approx 0.0067" />
</MathContainer>

The decimal <InlineMath math="0.0067" /> is about <InlineMath math="0.67\%" />. Therefore, a particular participant wins with probability <InlineMath math="0.0067" />.
