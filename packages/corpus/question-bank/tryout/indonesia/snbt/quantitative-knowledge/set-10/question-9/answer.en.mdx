Define the events:

-   <InlineMath math="A" />: construction finishes on time
-   <InlineMath math="B" />: a protest occurs
-   <InlineMath math="B^c" />: no protest occurs

The cases <InlineMath math="B" /> and <InlineMath math="B^c" /> are mutually exclusive and cover every possibility. Use the law of total probability:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P(A)&=P(B)P(A\mid B)\\&\quad+P(B^c)P(A\mid B^c)\end{aligned}" />

Substitute the given probabilities:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P(B)&=0.7\\P(B^c)&=1 - 0.7 = 0.3\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P(A\mid B^c)&=0.8\\P(A\mid B)&=0.4\end{aligned}" />

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P(A)&=(0.7)(0.4)\\&\quad+(0.3)(0.8)\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P(A)&=0.28 + 0.24\\&=0.52\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, construction finishes on time with probability <InlineMath math="0.52" />.
