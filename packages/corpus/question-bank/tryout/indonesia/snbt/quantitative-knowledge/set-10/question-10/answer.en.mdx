Because Nina spends half of the total time on each activity, let both the running time and the walking time be <InlineMath math="t\text{ hours}" />. Distance is speed multiplied by time.

| Quantity | Run | Walk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed (km/h) | <InlineMath math="5" /> | <InlineMath math="1" /> |
| Time (h) | <InlineMath math="t" /> | <InlineMath math="t" /> |
| Distance (km) | <InlineMath math="5t" /> | <InlineMath math="t" /> |

The two distances add to <InlineMath math="3 \text{ km}" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="5t\text{ km}+t\text{ km}=3\text{ km}" />
  <BlockMath math="6t=3" />
  <BlockMath math="t=\frac{1}{2}\text{ hour}" />
</MathContainer>

The total time <InlineMath math="T" /> consists of two equal intervals:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}T&=2t\\&=2\left(\frac12\right)\text{ hour}\\&=1\text{ hour}\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="1\text{ hour}=60\text{ minutes}" />
</MathContainer>

The entire trip takes <InlineMath math="60\text{ minutes}" />.
