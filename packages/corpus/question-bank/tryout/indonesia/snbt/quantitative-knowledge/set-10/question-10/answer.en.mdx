Because Nina spends half of the total time on each activity, let both the running time and the walking time be <InlineMath math="t\text{ hours}" />. Use <InlineMath math="\text{distance}=\text{speed}\times\text{time}" />:

| | Speed | Time | Distance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Run | <InlineMath math="5 \text{ km/h}" /> | <InlineMath math="t \text{ hours}" /> | <InlineMath math="5t \text{ km}" /> |
| Walk | <InlineMath math="1 \text{ km/h}" /> | <InlineMath math="t \text{ hours}" /> | <InlineMath math="t \text{ km}" /> |

The two distances add to <InlineMath math="3 \text{ km}" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="5t\text{ km}+t\text{ km}=3\text{ km}" />
  <BlockMath math="6t=3" />
  <BlockMath math="t=\frac{1}{2}\text{ hour}" />
</MathContainer>

The total trip contains two equal time intervals:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{total time}=2t=2\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\text{ hour}=1\text{ hour}" />
  <BlockMath math="1\text{ hour}=60\text{ minutes}" />
</MathContainer>

The entire trip takes <InlineMath math="60\text{ minutes}" />.
