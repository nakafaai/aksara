Given the quadratic function:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="y = kx^2 + 8x + (k-1)" />
</MathContainer>

For a quadratic function <InlineMath math="Ax^2+Bx+C" />, the axis of symmetry is <InlineMath math="x=-\frac{B}{2A}" />. Here, the quadratic coefficient is <InlineMath math="A=k" /> and the linear coefficient is <InlineMath math="B=8" />.

Use the given axis <InlineMath math="x=4" /> to determine <InlineMath math="k" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="4 = -\frac{8}{2k}" />
  <BlockMath math="8k = -8" />
  <BlockMath math="k = -1" />
</MathContainer>

Substitute <InlineMath math="k=-1" /> and complete the square:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="y=-x^2+8x-2" />
  <BlockMath math="y=-(x^2-8x+16)+14" />
  <BlockMath math="y=-(x-4)^2+14" />
</MathContainer>

Because <InlineMath math="(x-4)^2 \ge 0" />, the term <InlineMath math="-(x-4)^2" /> is at most <InlineMath math="0" />. Equality occurs at <InlineMath math="x=4" />, so the maximum value is <InlineMath math="14" />.
