# Choices of even exponents

The even values of <InlineMath math="a" /> are 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, which is 7 choices. The even values of <InlineMath math="b" /> are 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, which is 5 choices. **The exponent 0 counts too**, because <InlineMath math="2^0=1" /> is also a perfect square.

# The multiplication rule

The choices of <InlineMath math="a" /> and <InlineMath math="b" /> are independent, so the counts multiply.
