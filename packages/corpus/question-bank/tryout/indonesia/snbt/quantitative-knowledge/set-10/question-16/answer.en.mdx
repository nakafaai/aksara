The mean changes because the total changes. Work with **the total of the data**, not directly with the mean.

# Totals before and after

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{total of 8 numbers}&=8\cdot15=120\\
\text{total of 10 numbers}&=10\cdot18=180
\end{aligned}" />

# The value of x

The difference between the totals is **the sum of the two new numbers**, <InlineMath math="x+2x=3x" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3x&=180-120=60\\
x&=20
\end{aligned}" />

Check: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{120+20+40}{10}=\tfrac{180}{10}=18" />.

The value 40 is the second new number, <InlineMath math="2x" />. The value 60 is the sum of the two new numbers, <InlineMath math="3x" />, and 30 is the mean of those two numbers.

So the value of <InlineMath math="x" /> is **20**.
