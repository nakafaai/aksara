Given:
-   Original price of math book is <InlineMath math="85{,}000" />
-   Price of math book after discount is <InlineMath math="28{,}900" />
-   Original price of biology book is <InlineMath math="40{,}000" />

Because both books receive the same discount, they retain the same fraction of their original prices. The mathematics book retains:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\frac{\text{discounted price}}{\text{original price}} = \frac{28{,}900}{85{,}000}" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{28{,}900}{85{,}000} = \frac{289}{850} = \frac{17}{50} = 34\%" />
</MathContainer>

The customer pays <InlineMath math="34\%" /> of the original price, which corresponds to a <InlineMath math="66\%" /> discount.

Apply the same retained-price fraction to the biology book:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{discounted biology price} = \frac{17}{50} \times 40{,}000 = 13{,}600" />
</MathContainer>

The discounted biology book therefore costs <InlineMath math="13{,}600" /> rupiah.
