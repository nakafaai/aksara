Equal percentage discounts leave the **same fraction of the original price** to pay. Compare the discounted mathematics-book price with its original price.

Let <InlineMath math="r" /> be the fraction of the original price paid and <InlineMath math="H" /> the discounted biology-book price in rupiah.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="r=\frac{28{,}900}{85{,}000}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\frac{28{,}900}{85{,}000}&=\frac{289}{850}\\&=\frac{17}{50}\\&=34\%\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

The customer pays <InlineMath math="34\%" /> of the original price, which corresponds to a <InlineMath math="66\%" /> discount.

Apply the same retained-price fraction to the biology book:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}H&=\frac{17}{50} \times 40{,}000\\&=13{,}600\end{aligned}" />

The discounted biology book therefore costs <InlineMath math="13{,}600" /> rupiah.
