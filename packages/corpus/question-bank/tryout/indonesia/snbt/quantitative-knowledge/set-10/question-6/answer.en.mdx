The parking fee consists of **a fixed charge for the first 2 hours** and an hourly charge after that. Let there be <InlineMath math="k" /> hours after the first 2 hours.

# Setting up the inequality

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
5{,}000+3{,}000k&\le20{,}000\\
3{,}000k&\le15{,}000\\
k&\le5
\end{aligned}" />

# Parking time

The largest value is <InlineMath math="k=5" />, so the longest parking time is **the first 2 hours plus 5 hours**, that is 7 hours. The fee is exactly <InlineMath math="5{,}000+5\cdot3{,}000=20{,}000" />. Parking longer than 7 hours, even by a few minutes, counts as 8 hours and costs 23,000 rupiah.

The answer 5 hours forgets to add the first 2 hours. The answer 6 hours divides all the money by 3,000 and rounds down, as if the first 2 hours had no special fee.

So the longest parking time is **7 hours**.
