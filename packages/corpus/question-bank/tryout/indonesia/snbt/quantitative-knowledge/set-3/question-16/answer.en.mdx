Count the **initial position** as one rotational symmetry. Compare positions that map the shape onto itself with reflection axes that divide it into mirror images.

| Figure | Rotational symmetries | Reflection axes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular octagon | 8: multiples of 45° from 0° to 315° | 8: four through opposite vertices and four through the midpoints of opposite sides |
| Rhombus that is not a square | 2: rotations of 0° and 180° | 2: both diagonals |
| Isosceles triangle that is not equilateral | 1: the initial position only | 1: the line from the apex to the base midpoint |
| Kite that is not a rhombus | 1: the initial position only | 1: the diagonal joining the vertices where the equal adjacent sides meet |

The rhombus below has **two reflection axes**, its diagonals. A 180° rotation exchanges opposite vertices and maps the shape onto itself.

The two counts are equal in every row. No figure has more rotational symmetries, so the answer is 0.
