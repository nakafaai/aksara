Number the boxes 1, 2, and 3 from left to right. Every **pair of boxes is equally likely**, so count pairs rather than the order in which the marbles are placed.

| Occupied boxes | Box 1 | Box 2 | Box 3 | Adjacent? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1, 2 | Filled | Filled | Empty | Yes |
| 2, 3 | Empty | Filled | Filled | Yes |
| 1, 3 | Filled | Empty | Filled | No |

There are three pairs. Only boxes 1 and 3 leave an **empty box between them**, so only that pair meets the condition.
