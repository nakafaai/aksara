The sign of <InlineMath math="x+1" /> is not known, so you **must not multiply both sides by <InlineMath math="x+1" /> straight away**. Move every term to one side and examine the sign of the resulting fraction.

# Forming a single fraction

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{x-3}{x+1}-2&\ge0\\
\frac{x-3-2(x+1)}{x+1}&\ge0\\
\frac{-x-5}{x+1}&\ge0\\
\frac{x+5}{x+1}&\le0
\end{aligned}" />

The last line multiplies by <InlineMath math="-1" />, so **the inequality sign flips**.

# Checking the signs

The numerator is zero at <InlineMath math="x=-5" />, and the denominator is zero at <InlineMath math="x=-1" />.

| Interval | Sign of <InlineMath math="x+5" /> | Sign of <InlineMath math="x+1" /> | Sign of the fraction |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| <InlineMath math="x<-5" /> | negative | negative | positive |
| <InlineMath math="-5<x<-1" /> | positive | negative | negative |
| <InlineMath math="x>-1" /> | positive | positive | positive |

The fraction is negative on <InlineMath math="-5<x<-1" /> and zero at <InlineMath math="x=-5" />. The point <InlineMath math="x=-1" /> is **excluded** because the denominator is zero there. Check <InlineMath math="x=-3" />: <InlineMath math="\frac{-6}{-2}=3\ge2" />.

The set <InlineMath math="x\le-5" /> appears when both sides are multiplied by <InlineMath math="x+1" /> as if it were positive. The set with <InlineMath math="-5<x" /> drops <InlineMath math="x=-5" />, although the left side is exactly 2 there. The set with <InlineMath math="x\le-1" /> includes the point where the denominator is zero. The set <InlineMath math="x\le-5" /> or <InlineMath math="x>-1" /> is the region with the opposite sign.

So the solution set is <InlineMath math="\{x\mid -5\le x<-1\}" />.
