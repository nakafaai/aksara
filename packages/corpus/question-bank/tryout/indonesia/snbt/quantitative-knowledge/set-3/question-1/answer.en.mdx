One-digit, two-digit, and three-digit numbers contribute different numbers of digits. So **group the numbers by how many digits they have**, then count the numbers in each group carefully.

# Counting by group

The number of integers from <InlineMath math="m" /> to <InlineMath math="n" /> is <InlineMath math="n-m+1" />, **not <InlineMath math="n-m" />**.

| Group | Count of numbers | Digits per number | Digits |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1 to 9 | <InlineMath math="9-1+1=9" /> | 1 | 9 |
| 10 to 99 | <InlineMath math="99-10+1=90" /> | 2 | 180 |
| 100 to 250 | <InlineMath math="250-100+1=151" /> | 3 | 453 |

<BlockMath math="9+180+453=642" />

# Checking the other options

The value 639 counts 150 three-digit numbers, and the value 640 counts 89 two-digit numbers. Both use <InlineMath math="n-m" />, and the value 637 makes both mistakes at once. The value 750 treats every number as **having three digits**.

So **642** digits are written.
