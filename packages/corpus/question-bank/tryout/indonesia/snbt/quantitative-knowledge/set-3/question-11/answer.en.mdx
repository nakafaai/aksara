| --- | --- | --- |

| <InlineMath math="\frac35>\frac56" /> | <InlineMath math="3\cdot6=18<25=5\cdot5" /> | False |
| <InlineMath math="\frac59>\frac34" /> | <InlineMath math="5\cdot4=20<27=3\cdot9" /> | False |
