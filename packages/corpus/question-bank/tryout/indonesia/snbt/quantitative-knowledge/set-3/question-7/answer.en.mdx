Several pairs <InlineMath math="(a,b)" /> satisfy the conditions, for example <InlineMath math="(7,5)" /> and <InlineMath math="(11,1)" />. Instead of trying them one by one, **factor <InlineMath math="P" />** as a difference of squares.

# Factoring the difference of squares

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P=a^2-b^2&=(a+b)(a-b)\\
&=12(a-b)
\end{aligned}" />

The last expression is **exactly <InlineMath math="Q" />**, for every pair that satisfies <InlineMath math="a+b=12" />.
