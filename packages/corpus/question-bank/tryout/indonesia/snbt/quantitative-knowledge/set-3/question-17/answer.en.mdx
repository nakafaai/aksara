Each number must satisfy **both remainder conditions**. Write it as divisor times integer quotient plus remainder.

| Number | Division by 8 | Division by 5 | Both conditions? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 29 | <InlineMath math="29=8\cdot3+5" /> | <InlineMath math="29=5\cdot5+4" /> | No |
| 37 | <InlineMath math="37=8\cdot4+5" /> | <InlineMath math="37=5\cdot7+2" /> | Yes |
| 44 | <InlineMath math="44=8\cdot5+4" /> | <InlineMath math="44=5\cdot8+4" /> | No |
| 77 | <InlineMath math="77=8\cdot9+5" /> | <InlineMath math="77=5\cdot15+2" /> | Yes |

Only 37 and 77 satisfy both conditions. Therefore, statements (2) and (4) are correct.
