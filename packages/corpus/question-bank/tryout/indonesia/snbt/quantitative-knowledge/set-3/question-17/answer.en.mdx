A statement is sufficient when it forces a definite yes or a definite no. The right angle could be at any vertex, so **check the angle opposite the longest side**. By the converse of Pythagoras' theorem, that angle is right when the square of the longest side equals the sum of the squares of the other two, and acute when it's smaller.

The choices "both statements together" or "not sufficient" usually come from <InlineMath math="a^4+b^4=c^4" /> **looking like Pythagoras' theorem**, so it seems the triangle could be right-angled.
