Given:
*   Initial jacket price is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}250{,}000.00" />
*   Discount is <InlineMath math="25\%" />
*   Tax is <InlineMath math="5\%" />

First, we calculate the price after the discount. Since the discount is <InlineMath math="25\%" />, Mrs. Kartika pays <InlineMath math="100\% - 25\% = 75\%" /> of the initial price.

Let <InlineMath math="H_0" /> be the original price, <InlineMath math="H_1" /> the discounted price, and <InlineMath math="H_2" /> the price after tax. All prices are in rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
H_1&=75\%\cdot H_0 \\
&=\frac{75}{100}\cdot250{,}000 \\
&=187{,}500
\end{aligned}" />

Next, a tax of <InlineMath math="5\%" /> is applied to the price after discount. Thus, the total to be paid is <InlineMath math="100\% + 5\% = 105\%" /> of the price after discount.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
H_2&=105\%\cdot H_1 \\
&=\frac{105}{100}\cdot187{,}500 \\
&=1.05\cdot187{,}500 \\
&=196{,}875
\end{aligned}" />

Directly, the calculation can be written as:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
H_2&=(100-25)\% \\
&\quad\cdot(100+5)\%\cdot H_0 \\
&=0.75\cdot1.05\cdot H_0 \\
&=0.7875\cdot250{,}000 \\
&=196{,}875
\end{aligned}" />

So, the price Mrs. Kartika has to pay is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}196{,}875.00" />.
