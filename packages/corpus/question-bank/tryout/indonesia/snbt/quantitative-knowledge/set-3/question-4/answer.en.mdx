# Finding the fixed fee and the hourly rate

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
a+5b&=38\\
a+3b&=26\\
2b&=12\quad\Rightarrow\quad b=6\\
a&=26-3(6)=8
\end{aligned}" />

The fixed fee is Rp8,000.00 and the hourly rate is Rp6,000.00. The first statement is **true**, and the third statement is false.

# Checking the other two statements

Renting for 10 hours costs <InlineMath math="8+6(10)=68" /> thousand rupiah, while twice the 5-hour cost is <InlineMath math="2(38)=76" /> thousand rupiah. They differ because **the fixed fee is paid only once** and is not doubled. The second statement is false.

With Rp50,000.00, the rental time must satisfy <InlineMath math="8+6t\le50" />, that is, <InlineMath math="t\le7" />. Exactly 7 hours cost <InlineMath math="8+42=50" /> thousand rupiah, and 8 hours cost 56 thousand rupiah. The fourth statement is true.
