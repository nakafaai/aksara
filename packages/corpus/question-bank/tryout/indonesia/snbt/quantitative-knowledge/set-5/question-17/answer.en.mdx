# Rewriting the question

Both sides are non-negative, and for two non-negative numbers, the smaller one also has the smaller square. So you can compare the squares.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
|x+y|<|x-y|\quad&\Longleftrightarrow\quad(x+y)^2<(x-y)^2\\
&\Longleftrightarrow\quad x^2+2xy+y^2<x^2-2xy+y^2\\
&\Longleftrightarrow\quad 4xy<0
\end{aligned}" />

So the question is the same as **whether <InlineMath math="x" /> and <InlineMath math="y" /> have opposite signs**.
