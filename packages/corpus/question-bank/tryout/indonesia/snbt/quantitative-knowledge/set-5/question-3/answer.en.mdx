The function <InlineMath math="h(x)=\sqrt{2-{}^{2}\log\left(x^2-2x\right)}" /> is real when two conditions hold: **the number inside the logarithm is positive**, and **the number under the square root isn't negative**. # The domain of h

The logarithm needs <InlineMath math="u>0" />. <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

x^2-2x-4\le0\quad&\Longleftrightarrow\quad 1-\sqrt5\le x\le1+\sqrt5
\end{aligned}" />

# The fourth statement
