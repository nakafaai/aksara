The values of <InlineMath math="a" /> and <InlineMath math="b" /> are not known, so compare the two quantities through **the sign of their difference** for every pair with <InlineMath math="0<a<b" />.

# Simplifying the difference

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
Q-P&=\frac{a+1}{b+1}-\frac ab\\
&=\frac{b(a+1)-a(b+1)}{b(b+1)}\\
&=\frac{b-a}{b(b+1)}
\end{aligned}" />

The numerator <InlineMath math="b-a" /> is positive because <InlineMath math="a<b" />, and the denominator <InlineMath math="b(b+1)" /> is positive because <InlineMath math="b>0" />. So **the difference is always positive**.

# Checking with an example

For <InlineMath math="a=1" /> and <InlineMath math="b=3" />: <InlineMath math="P=\tfrac13" /> and <InlineMath math="Q=\tfrac24=\tfrac12" />. Adding the same number to the numerator and denominator of a fraction below 1 **moves the fraction closer to 1**, so its value rises.

The option saying the information is not enough comes from <InlineMath math="a" /> and <InlineMath math="b" /> not being unique, although the sign of the difference is the same for every pair. So the correct relation is <InlineMath math="Q>P" />.
