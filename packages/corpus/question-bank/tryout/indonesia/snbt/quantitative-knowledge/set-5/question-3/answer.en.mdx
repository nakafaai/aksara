A number must leave remainder 2 in **both divisions**. Write each number as divisor times integer quotient plus remainder.

| Entry | Division by 4 | Division by 6 | Both remainders 2? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <InlineMath math="18=4\cdot4+2" /> | <InlineMath math="18=6\cdot3+0" /> | No |
| 2 | <InlineMath math="24=4\cdot6+0" /> | <InlineMath math="24=6\cdot4+0" /> | No |
| 3 | <InlineMath math="36=4\cdot9+0" /> | <InlineMath math="36=6\cdot6+0" /> | No |
| 4 | <InlineMath math="38=4\cdot9+2" /> | <InlineMath math="38=6\cdot6+2" /> | Yes |

Only 38 satisfies both conditions, so only statement (4) is correct.
