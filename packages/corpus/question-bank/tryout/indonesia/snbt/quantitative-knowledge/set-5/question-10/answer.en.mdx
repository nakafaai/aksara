Compare the **number of reflection axes and rotational symmetry order**. Count the initial position once, equivalent to the position after a 360° turn.

| Figure | Reflection axes | Rotational order | Equal? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regular hexagon | 6: three through opposite vertices and three through opposite side midpoints | 6: multiples of 60° | Yes |
| Isosceles right triangle | 1: from the right-angle vertex to the hypotenuse midpoint | 1: initial position only | Yes |
| Right trapezoid with exactly one parallel side pair | 0: no reflection axis | 1: initial position only | No |
| Non-square rectangle | 2: through the midpoints of opposite sides | 2: initial position and 180° | Yes |

The line in this triangle is its **only reflection axis**. A half turn does not map the triangle onto itself.
