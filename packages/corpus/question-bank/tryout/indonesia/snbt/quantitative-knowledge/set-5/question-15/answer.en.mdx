The two transformations happen in sequence, so **their order must be kept**: reflect first, then translate.

# Reflection in the line y equals x

Reflection in the line <InlineMath math="y=x" /> **swaps the two coordinates**, <InlineMath math="(x,y)\mapsto(y,x)" />.

<BlockMath math="A(4,-2)\ \mapsto\ A'(-2,4)" />

# Translation

The translation <InlineMath math="\begin{pmatrix}-3\\5\end{pmatrix}" /> adds <InlineMath math="-3" /> to the <InlineMath math="x" />-coordinate and 5 to the <InlineMath math="y" />-coordinate, matching **its top and bottom components**.

<BlockMath math="A'(-2,4)\ \mapsto\ A''(-2-3,\ 4+5)=A''(-5,9)" />

The option <InlineMath math="(3,1)" /> translates first and then reflects. The option <InlineMath math="(1,-1)" /> flips the signs of the translation. The option <InlineMath math="(1,7)" /> reflects in the <InlineMath math="x" />-axis, and the option <InlineMath math="(-1,1)" /> reflects in the line <InlineMath math="y=-x" />.

So the final image of <InlineMath math="A" /> is <InlineMath math="(-5,9)" />.
