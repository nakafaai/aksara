# The area of one segment

The segment beyond side <InlineMath math="BC" /> is the sector centred at <InlineMath math="A" /> minus triangle <InlineMath math="ABC" />. The sector's angle is the angle of an equilateral triangle, <InlineMath math="60^\circ" />, so **the sector is one sixth of the circle**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}" />

# The area of the whole region

**Add the triangle and the <InlineMath math="3" /> segments**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
