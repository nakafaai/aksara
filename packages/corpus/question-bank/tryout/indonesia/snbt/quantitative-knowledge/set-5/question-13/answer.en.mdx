The surface area of the pyramid is the base area plus the area of the four lateral faces. Each lateral face is a triangle with base 6 cm, and **the height of that triangle is the slant height**, not the height of the pyramid.

# The slant height

Draw the line from the apex <InlineMath math="T" /> to the midpoint of one base edge. This line, the height of the pyramid, and half a base side form **a right triangle** with legs 4 cm and 3 cm.

<BlockMath math="t_{\text{slant}}=\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5" />

# The surface area

Add **the base area and the four lateral triangles**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A&=6^2+4\cdot\tfrac12\cdot6\cdot5\\
&=36+60=96\text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}" />

The value 84 uses the pyramid height 4 cm as the slant height, which makes the lateral area 48. The value 60 is the lateral area without the base, and the value 48 is the lateral area with the wrong height and no base. The value 36 is only the base area.

So the surface area of the pyramid is **<InlineMath math="96\text{ cm}^2" />**.
