Let <InlineMath math="L" /> be the mean score of the male students and <InlineMath math="P" /> that of the female students. The stated difference gives:

<BlockMath math="P=L+1.75" />

The numbers of male and female students are in the ratio <InlineMath math="3:4" />. Write the group sizes as <InlineMath math="3k" /> and <InlineMath math="4k" />, where <InlineMath math="k>0" />. The class mean is the weighted mean of the two groups:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="7.5=\frac{3kL+4kP}{3k+4k}" />
  <BlockMath math="7.5=\frac{3L+4P}{7}" />
</MathContainer>

The factor <InlineMath math="k" /> cancels from the numerator and denominator. Now substitute the relationship between the means:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}7.5&=\frac{3L+4(L+1.75)}7\\&=\frac{3L+4L+7}7\\&=\frac{7L+7}7\\&=L+1\end{aligned}" />

The mean score of the male students is therefore:

<BlockMath math="L=7.5-1=6.5" />

Add the given difference to obtain the mean score of the female students:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P&=L+1.75\\&=6.5+1.75\\&=8.25\end{aligned}" />

Thus, the mean score of the female students is <InlineMath math="8.25" />.
