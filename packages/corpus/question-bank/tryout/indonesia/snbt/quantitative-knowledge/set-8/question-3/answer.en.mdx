Both shapes have the same area, so this compares **the perimeters of two shapes with equal area**. Compute both exactly, then compare.

# The two perimeters

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P&=2\pi\cdot7=14\pi\\
\text{area of the square}&=\pi\cdot7^2=49\pi\\
\text{side of the square}&=\sqrt{49\pi}=7\sqrt\pi\\
Q&=4\cdot7\sqrt\pi=28\sqrt\pi
\end{aligned}" />

# Comparison

Divide both quantities by <InlineMath math="14\sqrt\pi" /> to compare them easily.

<BlockMath math="P:Q=14\pi:28\sqrt\pi=\sqrt\pi:2" />

Since <InlineMath math="\pi<4" />, we have <InlineMath math="\sqrt\pi<2" />, so <InlineMath math="Q>P" />. In decimals, <InlineMath math="P\approx44" /> and <InlineMath math="Q\approx49.6" />. This matches the fact that among shapes of equal area, **the circle has the smallest perimeter**.

So the correct relation is **<InlineMath math="Q>P" />**.
