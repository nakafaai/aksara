Let:
- <InlineMath math="J_A" /> Distance traveled by Ali until they meet
- <InlineMath math="J_T" /> Distance traveled by Tika until they meet
- <InlineMath math="v_A" /> Ali's speed <InlineMath math="= 60\text{ km/h}" />
- <InlineMath math="v_T" /> Tika's speed <InlineMath math="= 70\text{ km/h}" />

Since Ali and Tika depart at the same time, they travel for the same duration. Let <InlineMath math="t" /> denote that duration.

Then:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} J_A &= v_A \times t = 60t \\ J_T &= v_T \times t = 70t \end{aligned}" />

Since the total distance is <InlineMath math="221\text{ km}" />, then:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} J_A + J_T &= 221 \\ 60t + 70t &= 221 \\ 130t &= 221 \\ t &= 1.7 \text{ hours} \end{aligned}" />

So the time required until they meet is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}1.7\text{ h}&=1\text{ h}+42\text{ min}\end{aligned}" />

Thus, they will meet at:

Add <InlineMath math="1\text{ hour}" /> and <InlineMath math="42\text{ minutes}" /> to <InlineMath math="09{:}15" />. They meet at <InlineMath math="10{:}57" />.
