# Height of the equilateral triangle

The altitude from <InlineMath math="P" /> to <InlineMath math="AB" /> bisects <InlineMath math="AB" />, giving a right triangle with hypotenuse 10 and base 5.

<BlockMath math="t_{ABP}=\sqrt{10^2-5^2}=\sqrt{75}=5\sqrt3\approx8.66" />

This is less than 10, so **<InlineMath math="P" /> does lie inside the square**.

# Area of triangle CDP

The sides <InlineMath math="AB" /> and <InlineMath math="CD" /> are parallel and 10 apart, so **the distance from point P to side CD** is <InlineMath math="10-5\sqrt3" />.

<BlockMath math="L_{CDP}=\tfrac12\cdot10\cdot\left(10-5\sqrt3\right)=50-25\sqrt3\approx6.7" />

The value <InlineMath math="100-50\sqrt3" /> forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\tfrac12" />. The value <InlineMath math="25\sqrt3" /> is the area of triangle <InlineMath math="ABP" />. The value 25 is a quarter of the square, which is only right if <InlineMath math="P" /> is at the centre.

So the area of triangle <InlineMath math="CDP" /> is **<InlineMath math="\left(50-25\sqrt3\right)\text{ cm}^2" />**.
