A statement is sufficient if **every pair of positive integers satisfying it gives the same value of <InlineMath math="a+b" />**. List the possible pairs.

# Statement (1) alone

Pairs with <InlineMath math="ab=12" /> include <InlineMath math="(1,12)" />, <InlineMath math="(2,6)" /> and <InlineMath math="(3,4)" />, together with their reverses. Their sums are 13, 8 and 7, so **the sum is not unique**. Statement (1) alone is not sufficient.

# Statement (2) alone

Factor it as <InlineMath math="(a-b)(a+b)=7" />. Since 7 is prime and <InlineMath math="a+b" /> is larger than <InlineMath math="a-b" />, **the only factor pair** is <InlineMath math="a-b=1" /> and <InlineMath math="a+b=7" />.

<BlockMath math="a=4,\quad b=3,\quad a+b=7" />

Check: <InlineMath math="16-9=7" />. Statement (2) alone is sufficient.

So **statement (2) alone is sufficient**, but statement (1) alone is not.
