Apply the definition of <InlineMath math="\odot" /> in the order fixed by the parentheses in <InlineMath math="(5 \odot 2) \odot 3" />. First evaluate <InlineMath math="5 \odot 2" /> with <InlineMath math="a = 5" /> and <InlineMath math="b = 2" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 5 \odot 2 &= 3(5)(2) - 2(1 + 2) \\ &= 30 - 2(3) \\ &= 30 - 6 \\ &= 24 \end{aligned}" />

The intermediate result <InlineMath math="24" /> now takes the place of <InlineMath math="a" />. With <InlineMath math="b = 3" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 24 \odot 3 &= 3(24)(3) - 2(1 + 3) \\ &= 216 - 2(4) \\ &= 216 - 8 \\ &= 208 \end{aligned}" />

Therefore, <InlineMath math="(5 \odot 2) \odot 3 = 208" />.
