With equal work rates, the **product of worker count and working time** stays constant. Fewer workers therefore need more days.

Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the required number of days.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 20\cdot12 &= 15x \\ x &= \frac{240}{15} \\ x &= 16 \end{aligned}" />

So, the time required to complete the order if only <InlineMath math="15\text{ people}" /> are working is <InlineMath math="16\text{ days}" />. Therefore, the additional time required is:

<BlockMath math="16 - 12 = 4 \text{ days}" />
