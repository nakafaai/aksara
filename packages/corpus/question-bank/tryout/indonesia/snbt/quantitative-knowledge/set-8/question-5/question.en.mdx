One ball is drawn from each box, and the probability that both are black is <InlineMath math="\frac{3}{10}" />. If the probability that both are white is <InlineMath math="\frac{m}{n}" /> in lowest terms, what is <InlineMath math="m+n" />?

Every ball in a box is equally likely to be drawn, and the draws from the two boxes are independent.
