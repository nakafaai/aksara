# Conditions on the factor pair

Since <InlineMath math="a" /> and <InlineMath math="b" /> are positive, <InlineMath math="a-b<a+b" />, so the smaller factor is <InlineMath math="a-b" />. Also <InlineMath math="a=\tfrac{(a+b)+(a-b)}{2}" />, so **both factors must be odd or both even**. Every factor of 45 is odd, so this always holds.
