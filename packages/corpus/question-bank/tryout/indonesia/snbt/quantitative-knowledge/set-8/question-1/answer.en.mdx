Let <InlineMath math="x = 0.323323323..." />. The repeating block <InlineMath math="323" /> has three digits, so multiplying by <InlineMath math="1000" /> shifts the decimal point by one full period:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="1000x = 323.323323..." />
</MathContainer>

Now subtract the original equation. The repeating decimal parts cancel:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 1000x &= 323.323323... \\ x &= 0.323323... \\ \hline 999x &= 323 \end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="x = \frac{323}{999}" />
</MathContainer>

The fraction is <InlineMath math="\frac{323}{999}" />.
