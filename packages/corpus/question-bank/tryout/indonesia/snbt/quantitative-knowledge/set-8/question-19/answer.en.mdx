Students who join **both activities are counted twice** if we simply add the Scouts and futsal totals. Subtract the overlap once. With <InlineMath math="P" /> denoting Scouts and <InlineMath math="F" /> denoting futsal, we obtain:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}n(P\cup F)&=n(P)+n(F)\\&\quad{}-n(P\cap F)\\&=12+15-7\\&=20\end{aligned}" />

The rest of the class joins neither activity:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}n\left((P\cup F)^c\right)&=30-20\\&=10\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, <InlineMath math="10" /> students join neither activity.
