Whether the sum of two numbers is divisible by 3 depends on **the remainder of each number when divided by 3**. Group the numbers 1 to 20 by remainder.

# Three remainder groups

| Remainder | Numbers | Count |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 0 | 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 | 6 |
| 1 | 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19 | 7 |
| 2 | 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20 | 7 |

The groups **are not equal in size**, because 20 is not a multiple of 3.

# Pairs whose sum is a multiple of 3

The sum is divisible by 3 if **both numbers leave remainder 0**, or **one leaves 1 and the other leaves 2**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{remainders 0 and 0}&:\quad \binom62=15\\
\text{remainders 1 and 2}&:\quad 7\cdot7=49\\
n(A)&=15+49=64,\qquad n(S)=\binom{20}{2}=190
\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="P(A)=\frac{64}{190}=\frac{32}{95}" />

The value <InlineMath math="\tfrac13" /> assumes the three groups are equal. The value <InlineMath math="\tfrac{3}{38}=\tfrac{15}{190}" /> counts only the pairs of multiples of 3, and <InlineMath math="\tfrac{49}{190}" /> counts only the pairs with remainders 1 and 2.

So the probability that the sum is divisible by 3 is **<InlineMath math="\tfrac{32}{95}" />**.
