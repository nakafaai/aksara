Let:
- <InlineMath math="S" /> be the set of all students
- <InlineMath math="P" /> be the students in Scouts
- <InlineMath math="F" /> be the students in futsal

First use inclusion-exclusion to count students in at least one activity:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} n(P \cup F) &= n(P)+n(F)-n(P\cap F) \\ &=12+15-7=20 \end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

The rest of the class joins neither activity:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="n\left((P\cup F)^c\right)=30-20=10" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, <InlineMath math="10" /> students join neither activity.
