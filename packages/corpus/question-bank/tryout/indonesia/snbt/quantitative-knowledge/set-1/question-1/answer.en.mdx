Use **elimination** on the two given operation results to determine <InlineMath math="k" /> and <InlineMath math="m" />. Then substitute the requested inputs into the operation rule.

From <InlineMath math="\triangle(2, 4) = 8" />, we get

<BlockMath math="\triangle(2, 4) = 2k + \frac{4}{m} = 8" />

Multiply both sides by <InlineMath math="m" />

<BlockMath math="2km + 4 = 8m \quad \ldots \text{(1)}" />

From <InlineMath math="\triangle(3, 5) = 11" />, we get

<BlockMath math="\triangle(3, 5) = 3k + \frac{5}{m} = 11" />

Multiply both sides by <InlineMath math="m" />

<BlockMath math="3km + 5 = 11m \quad \ldots \text{(2)}" />

Eliminate <InlineMath math="km" /> by multiplying equation (1) by <InlineMath math="3" /> and equation (2) by <InlineMath math="2" />

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="6km + 12 = 24m \quad \ldots \text{(1a)}" />
<BlockMath math="6km + 10 = 22m \quad \ldots \text{(2a)}" />
</MathContainer>

Subtract equation (2a) from equation (1a)

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&(6km+12)-(6km+10)\\&=24m-22m\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="2 = 2m" />
<BlockMath math="m = 1" />
</MathContainer>

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="2k(1) + 4 = 8(1)" />

</MathContainer>
