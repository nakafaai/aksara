Given that the perimeter of rhombus <InlineMath math="PQRS" /> is <InlineMath math="20\text{ units}" />, the length of each side is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}PQ&=\frac{20}{4}\\&=5 \text{ units}\end{aligned}" />

The diagonals of a rhombus **bisect each other at right angles**. The midpoint of <InlineMath math="PR" /> is <InlineMath math="O(4,0)" />, so <InlineMath math="PO=4" />. In right triangle <InlineMath math="POQ" />, the hypotenuse is <InlineMath math="PQ=5" />.

<BlockMath math="OQ=\sqrt{PQ^2-PO^2}=\sqrt{5^2-4^2}=\sqrt9=3" />

Since <InlineMath math="Q" /> lies above the horizontal axis, its coordinates are <InlineMath math="Q(4,3)" />. The opposite point is <InlineMath math="S(4,-3)" />.

Calculate the slope of line <InlineMath math="PS" />

<BlockMath math="m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}" />

With <InlineMath math="P(0, 0)" /> and <InlineMath math="S(4, -3)" />

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="m_{PS} = \frac{-3 - 0}{4 - 0}" />
<BlockMath math="m_{PS} = \frac{-3}{4}" />
<BlockMath math="m_{PS} = -\frac{3}{4}" />
</MathContainer>

Since the line we're looking for is perpendicular to <InlineMath math="PS" />, then

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1" />
<BlockMath math="-\frac{3}{4} \cdot m_2 = -1" />
<BlockMath math="m_2 = \frac{-1}{-\frac{3}{4}} = \frac{4}{3}" />
</MathContainer>

So the slope of the line passing through <InlineMath math="Q" /> and perpendicular to <InlineMath math="PS" /> is <InlineMath math="m = \frac{4}{3}" />.

Use **point-slope form** with <InlineMath math="Q(4,3)" /> to determine the required line:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}y-3&=\frac43(x-4)\\3y-9&=4x-16\\4x-3y&=7\end{aligned}" />

Option <InlineMath math="4x - 3y = 7" /> has slope

<BlockMath math="m = -\frac{4}{-3} = \frac{4}{3}" />

Verify that this line passes through <InlineMath math="Q(4, 3)" />

<BlockMath math="4(4) - 3(3) = 16 - 9 = 7" />

Correct. Therefore, the equation of the line passing through <InlineMath math="Q" /> and perpendicular to <InlineMath math="PS" /> is <InlineMath math="4x - 3y = 7" />.
