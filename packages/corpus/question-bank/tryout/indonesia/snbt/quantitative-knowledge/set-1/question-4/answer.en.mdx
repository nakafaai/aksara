Given map scale <InlineMath math="1 : 200{,}000" />, map distance <InlineMath math="12 \text{ cm}" />, and travel time <InlineMath math="12 \text{ minutes}" />.

Calculate the actual distance using the map scale

Let <InlineMath math="x" /> be the actual distance in centimeters. The ratio of map distance to actual distance is the map scale.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\frac{12 \text{ cm}}{x} = \frac{1}{200{,}000}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}x&=12 \times 200{,}000\\&=2{,}400{,}000 \text{ cm}\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Convert to kilometers

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}x&=2{,}400{,}000 \text{ cm}\\&=24 \text{ km}\end{aligned}" />

Convert time to hours

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}t&=12 \text{ minutes}\\&=\frac{12}{60} \text{ hours}\\&=\frac{1}{5} \text{ hours}\end{aligned}" />

Calculate average speed

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="v = \frac{x}{t}" />
<BlockMath math="v = \frac{24 \text{ km}}{\frac{1}{5} \text{ hours}}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}v&=24 \times 5\\&=120 \text{ km/h}\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the average speed of the airplane is <InlineMath math="120 \text{ km/h}" />.
