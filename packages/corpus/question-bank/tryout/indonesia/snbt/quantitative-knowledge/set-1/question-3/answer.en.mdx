Given the geometric sequence <InlineMath math="\frac{3}{2}, p, 6, q" /> with a positive ratio.

Let the first term be <InlineMath math="U_1 = a = \frac{3}{2}" /> and the ratio be <InlineMath math="r > 0" />.

From <InlineMath math="U_3 = 6" />, we get

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}U_3&=ar^2\\&=\frac{3}{2} \cdot r^2\\&=6\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}r^2&=6 \cdot \frac{2}{3}\\&=4\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="r = 2" />
</MathContainer>

Since <InlineMath math="r > 0" />, then <InlineMath math="r = 2" />.

**Statement (1): The ratio of the sequence is <InlineMath math="2" />.**

True. From the calculation above, we get <InlineMath math="r = 2" />.

Calculate the values of <InlineMath math="p" /> and <InlineMath math="q" />

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=U_2\\&=ar\\&=\frac{3}{2} \cdot 2\\&=3\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}q&=U_4\\&=ar^3\\&=\frac{3}{2} \cdot 2^3\\&=\frac{3}{2} \cdot 8\\&=12\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

**Statement (2): The value of <InlineMath math="pq" /> is <InlineMath math="72" />.**

False. From the calculation above, <InlineMath math="p = 3" /> and <InlineMath math="q = 12" />, so

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}pq&=3 \cdot 12\\&=36\end{aligned}" />

The calculation gives <InlineMath math="pq = 36" />. Therefore, the proposed value <InlineMath math="72" /> is incorrect.

Calculate the sixth term

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}U_6&=ar^5\\&=\frac{3}{2} \cdot 2^5\\&=\frac{3}{2} \cdot 32\\&=48\end{aligned}" />

**Statement (3): The difference between the sixth and second term is <InlineMath math="45" />.**

True. From the calculation above, <InlineMath math="U_6 = 48" /> and <InlineMath math="U_2 = 3" />, so

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}U_6 - U_2&=48 - 3\\&=45\end{aligned}" />

**Statement (4): Every term in the sequence is an even number.**

False. From the calculation above, <InlineMath math="U_1 = \frac{3}{2}" /> is not an integer, and <InlineMath math="U_2 = 3" /> is an odd number. So not all terms are even numbers.

Therefore, the true statements are (1) and (3).
