Given the expression

<BlockMath math="\frac{x + 2y}{3} + \frac{2x - y}{4} - \frac{3x + 2y}{6}" />

#### Finding the Common Denominator

To simplify this expression, we need to find the least common multiple (LCM) of <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="6" />.

Prime factorization:
- <InlineMath math="3 = 3" />
- <InlineMath math="4 = 2^2" />
- <InlineMath math="6 = 2 \times 3" />

The LCM of <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, and <InlineMath math="6" /> is <InlineMath math="2^2 \times 3 = 12" />.

#### Equalizing Denominators

Convert each fraction to have denominator <InlineMath math="12" />:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\frac{x + 2y}{3}&=\frac{4(x + 2y)}{12}\\&=\frac{4x + 8y}{12}\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\frac{2x - y}{4}&=\frac{3(2x - y)}{12}\\&=\frac{6x - 3y}{12}\end{aligned}" />
<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\frac{3x + 2y}{6}&=\frac{2(3x + 2y)}{12}\\&=\frac{6x + 4y}{12}\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

#### Simplifying the Expression

Call the expression <InlineMath math="E" />. Once the denominators match, combine the numerators. Keep the minus sign before the third fraction.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}E&=\frac{4x+8y}{12}+\frac{6x-3y}{12}\\&\quad-\frac{6x+4y}{12}\end{aligned}" />

Let <InlineMath math="N" /> be the combined numerator, so <InlineMath math="E=N/12" />. Expand the brackets and collect like terms:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}N&=(4x+8y)+(6x-3y)\\&\quad-(6x+4y)\\&=4x+8y+6x-3y\\&\quad-6x-4y\\&=(4x+6x-6x)\\&\quad+(8y-3y-4y)\\&=4x+y\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="E=\frac N{12}=\frac{4x+y}{12}" />

Therefore, the result of

<BlockMath math="\frac{x + 2y}{3} + \frac{2x - y}{4} - \frac{3x + 2y}{6}" />

is <InlineMath math="\frac{4x + y}{12}" />.
