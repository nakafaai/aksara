You're looking for every integer that makes the fraction zero or negative, and then adding them up. There are two traps: one factor **shows up in both the numerator and the denominator**, and one factor is a square. The plan: factor everything, note what the denominator forbids, study the signs, and then collect the integers.

# Factoring and noting the conditions

Factor each quadratic.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

The denominator can't be zero, so <InlineMath math="x\neq-3" />, <InlineMath math="x\neq-4" />, and <InlineMath math="x\neq9" />. You may cancel the factor <InlineMath math="x+3" />, but **the condition <InlineMath math="x\neq-3" /> still holds**, because the original fraction isn't defined there.
