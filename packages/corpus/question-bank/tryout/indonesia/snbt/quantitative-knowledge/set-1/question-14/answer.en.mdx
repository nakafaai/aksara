The first three statements use the basic lengths of a cube with edge <InlineMath math="a=9" />, namely **the face diagonal and the space diagonal**. The fourth statement is easiest to check with coordinates.

# Space diagonal and diagonal plane

The second statement is **true** because <InlineMath math="AG=9\sqrt3\text{ cm}" />. The diagonal plane <InlineMath math="ACGE" /> is a rectangle with sides <InlineMath math="AC=9\sqrt2" /> and <InlineMath math="AE=9" />, so its area is <InlineMath math="81\sqrt2\approx114.6\text{ cm}^2" />. The number 162 is <InlineMath math="AC^2" />, not the area of the plane, so the first statement is false.

# Angle between a line and a plane

The projection of <InlineMath math="AG" /> onto the base is <InlineMath math="AC" />, because <InlineMath math="G" /> lies directly above <InlineMath math="C" />. The required angle is angle <InlineMath math="GAC" /> in the right triangle <InlineMath math="ACG" />.

<BlockMath math="\tan\angle GAC=\frac{CG}{AC}=\frac{9}{9\sqrt2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}" />

An angle of <InlineMath math="45^\circ" /> needs a tangent of 1. Since <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}<1" />, **the angle is less than <InlineMath math="45^\circ" />** (about <InlineMath math="35.3^\circ" />). The third statement is false.

# Distance from a point to a plane

Place the cube at <InlineMath math="A(0,0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="B(9,0,0)" />, <InlineMath math="D(0,9,0)" />, <InlineMath math="E(0,0,9)" />, and <InlineMath math="G(9,9,9)" />. All three points <InlineMath math="B" />, <InlineMath math="D" />, and <InlineMath math="G" /> satisfy <InlineMath math="x+y-z=9" />, so that is **the equation of the plane <InlineMath math="BDG" />**. The distance from <InlineMath math="E" /> to this plane is

<BlockMath math="d=\frac{\lvert 0+0-9-9\rvert}{\sqrt{1^2+1^2+(-1)^2}}=\frac{18}{\sqrt3}=6\sqrt3" />

The result can also be seen geometrically. The space diagonal <InlineMath math="CE" /> is perpendicular to the plane <InlineMath math="BDG" />, the distance from <InlineMath math="C" /> to that plane is <InlineMath math="\frac{9}{\sqrt3}=3\sqrt3" />, and the rest is <InlineMath math="9\sqrt3-3\sqrt3=6\sqrt3" />. The fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
