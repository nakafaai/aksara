Find <InlineMath math="N" /> by **keeping both sides equal**. The starting equation is <InlineMath math="7 - N \times (-3) = 52" />.

#### Simplifying the Equation

First, notice the **subtraction of a negative term**. Simplify the form <InlineMath math="N \times (-3)" /> to <InlineMath math="N(-3)" /> or <InlineMath math="-3N" />.

<BlockMath math="7 - N(-3) = 52" />

Since <InlineMath math="-N(-3) = 3N" />, the equation becomes:

<BlockMath math="7 + 3N = 52" />

#### Solving the Equation

Subtract <InlineMath math="7" /> **from both sides**:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="7 + 3N - 7 = 52 - 7" />
<BlockMath math="3N = 45" />
</MathContainer>

Divide both sides by <InlineMath math="3" />:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="N = \frac{45}{3}" />
<BlockMath math="N = 15" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the value of <InlineMath math="N" /> is <InlineMath math="15" />.

Check by substituting <InlineMath math="N=15" /> into the original equation:

<BlockMath math="7-15(-3)=7+45=52" />
