The question has a yes or no answer. A statement is enough when **every <InlineMath math="n" /> that satisfies it gives the same answer**. Since <InlineMath math="12=2^2\cdot3" />, you need to know whether <InlineMath math="n" /> contains at least two factors <InlineMath math="2" /> and one factor <InlineMath math="3" />. # Writing the prime factors

When you raise <InlineMath math="n" /> to a power, **the exponent of each prime gets multiplied**.

So statement (1) alone is enough, and statement (2) alone is also enough to be sure that <InlineMath math="n" /> is divisible by <InlineMath math="12" />.
