A product of two factors is at most zero when **the factors have opposite signs or one of them is zero**. So find each factor's sign first, then combine the two cases.

# The sign of the exponential factor

Let <InlineMath math="u=3^x>0" />. The second factor becomes

<BlockMath math="u^2-28u+27=(u-1)(u-27)." />

This factor is <InlineMath math="\le0" /> exactly when <InlineMath math="1\le u\le27" />, that is <InlineMath math="0\le x\le3" />, and positive for <InlineMath math="x<0" /> or <InlineMath math="x>3" />. **The substitution isn't finished until you're back in <InlineMath math="x" />**.

# Two sign cases

- First factor <InlineMath math="\ge0" /> and second factor <InlineMath math="\le0" />: <InlineMath math="|x|\ge2" /> and <InlineMath math="0\le x\le3" />, so <InlineMath math="2\le x\le3" />.
- First factor <InlineMath math="\le0" /> and second factor <InlineMath math="\ge0" />: <InlineMath math="|x|\le2" /> and (<InlineMath math="x\le0" /> or <InlineMath math="x\ge3" />), so <InlineMath math="-2\le x\le0" />.

**The second case is the easiest to miss**, since there the exponential factor is positive. The number line below combines both cases.

# Checking the result

**The number <InlineMath math="1" /> really fails**: <InlineMath math="(1-4)(9-84+27)=(-3)(-48)=144>0" />. On the other hand, <InlineMath math="x=-1" /> gives <InlineMath math="(-3)\left(\tfrac19-\tfrac{28}{3}+27\right)" />, negative times positive, so it fits.

# The tempting wrong options

- <InlineMath math="2" /> uses only the first case, so **the negative numbers <InlineMath math="-2" />, <InlineMath math="-1" />, and <InlineMath math="0" /> get lost**.
- <InlineMath math="4" /> solves only the exponential factor, <InlineMath math="0\le x\le3" />.
- <InlineMath math="3" /> asks both factors to be <InlineMath math="\le0" />, that is <InlineMath math="0\le x\le2" />.
- <InlineMath math="6" /> takes all of <InlineMath math="[-2,3]" />, including <InlineMath math="x=1" />.

So the number of integers that satisfy the inequality is <InlineMath math="5" />.
