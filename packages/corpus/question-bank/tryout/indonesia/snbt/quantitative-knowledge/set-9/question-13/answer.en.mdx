The transformation applied to every data value is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="y=\frac{x-a}{b}, \qquad b>0" />
</MathContainer>

Subtracting the same number from every value shifts the data without changing the range. Dividing by the positive number <InlineMath math="b" /> divides both the mean and the range by <InlineMath math="b" />.

#### Finding <InlineMath math="b" /> from the Range

The transformed range is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="R_{\text{new}} = \frac{R_{\text{initial}}}{b}" />
  <BlockMath math="6 = \frac{12}{b}" />
  <BlockMath math="b = 2" />
</MathContainer>

#### Finding <InlineMath math="a" /> from the Mean

The transformed mean is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\bar{x}_{\text{new}} = \frac{\bar{x}_{\text{initial}} - a}{b}" />
  <BlockMath math="10 = \frac{24 - a}{2}" />
  <BlockMath math="20 = 24 - a" />
  <BlockMath math="a = 4" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, <InlineMath math="a=4" /> and <InlineMath math="b=2" />.
