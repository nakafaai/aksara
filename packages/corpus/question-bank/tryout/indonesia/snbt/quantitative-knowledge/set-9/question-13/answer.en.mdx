The lengthwise cross-section of the pool is a trapezium with parallel sides 1 m and 3 m and width 20 m between them. The volume is **the trapezium area times the width of the pool**.

# Volume of the pool

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
L_{\text{trapezium}}&=\frac{1+3}{2}\cdot20=40\text{ m}^2\\
V&=40\cdot10=400\text{ m}^3
\end{aligned}" />

# Converting units and finding the time

One cubic metre equals **1,000 litres**, so the volume is 400,000 litres.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{400{,}000}{1{,}000}=400\text{ min}\\
&=6\text{ h }40\text{ min}
\end{aligned}" />

The time 10 hours uses the depth 3 m for the whole pool, giving 600 cubic metres. The time 13 hours 20 minutes forgets to halve in the trapezium formula, giving 800 cubic metres.

So the time needed to fill the pool is **6 hours 40 minutes**.
