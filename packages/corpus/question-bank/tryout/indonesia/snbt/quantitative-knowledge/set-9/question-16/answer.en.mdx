The mean of grouped data is **the total of all scores divided by the number of students**, and the number of students also contains <InlineMath math="x" />.

# Setting up the equation

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{total score}&=3\cdot60+70x+5\cdot80+2\cdot90=760+70x\\
\text{number of students}&=3+x+5+2=10+x
\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{760+70x}{10+x}&=73\\
760+70x&=730+73x\\
30&=3x\\
x&=10
\end{aligned}" />

# Check

With <InlineMath math="x=10" />, the total score is 1,460 and there are 20 students, so the mean is <InlineMath math="1{,}460:20=73" />. This makes sense: a mean of 73 is close to 70, so **the score 70 must occur most often**.

A common mistake is dividing by 10 only, as if the students who scored 70 were not counted.

So the value of <InlineMath math="x" /> is **10**.
