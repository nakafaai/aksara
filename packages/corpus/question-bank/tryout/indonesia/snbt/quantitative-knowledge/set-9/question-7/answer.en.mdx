A statement is sufficient if **angle <InlineMath math="BAC" /> is unique** for every triangle that satisfies it.

# Statement (1) alone

Let <InlineMath math="\angle ACB=t" />, so <InlineMath math="\angle ABC=2t" /> and <InlineMath math="\angle BAC=180^\circ-3t" />. The value of <InlineMath math="t" /> **is still unknown**. For <InlineMath math="t=30^\circ" />, angle <InlineMath math="BAC" /> is <InlineMath math="90^\circ" />. For <InlineMath math="t=40^\circ" />, it is <InlineMath math="60^\circ" />. Statement (1) alone is not sufficient.

# Statement (2) alone

The sides <InlineMath math="AB" /> and <InlineMath math="BC" /> are equal, so the triangle is isosceles with apex <InlineMath math="B" />. **Angles opposite equal sides are equal**: the angle opposite <InlineMath math="BC" /> is <InlineMath math="\angle BAC" />, and the angle opposite <InlineMath math="AB" /> is <InlineMath math="\angle ACB" />.

<BlockMath math="\angle BAC=\angle ACB=40^\circ" />

Statement (2) alone is sufficient.

So **statement (2) alone is sufficient**, but statement (1) alone is not.
