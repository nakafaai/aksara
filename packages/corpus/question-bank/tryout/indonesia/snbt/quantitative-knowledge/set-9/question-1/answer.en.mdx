Because the circle is **tangent to all four square sides**, its diameter equals the side length of the large square. The small square is not needed to find the circumference.

The square area equals the square of its side length. A length must be positive, so:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}s^2&=256\\s&=\sqrt{256}=16\text{ cm}\\d&=s=16\text{ cm}\end{aligned}" />

Using the **diameter**, the circumference is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}C&=\pi d\\&=\pi\cdot16\\&=16\pi\text{ cm}\end{aligned}" />
