#### Expressing the Numbers on the Same Scale

To compare the five forms, we write each one as a decimal:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\frac{17}{14} \approx 1.214" />
  <BlockMath math="123\% = \frac{123}{100} = 1.23" />
  <BlockMath math="1.45 = 1.45" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{5}{3} \approx 1.667" />
  <BlockMath math="\sqrt{12} \approx 3.464" />
</MathContainer>

These values are sufficiently separated to determine their order. In particular, <InlineMath math="\frac{17}{14}<\frac{123}{100}" /> because <InlineMath math="1700<1722" />, and <InlineMath math="1.45<\frac53" /> because <InlineMath math="435<500" /> after cross multiplication.

#### Ordering the Numbers

From smallest to largest:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="1.214 < 1.23 < 1.45 < 1.667 < 3.464" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{17}{14} < 123\% < 1.45 < \frac{5}{3} < \sqrt{12}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the correct order is <InlineMath math="\frac{17}{14}; 123\%; 1.45; \frac{5}{3}; \sqrt{12}" />.
