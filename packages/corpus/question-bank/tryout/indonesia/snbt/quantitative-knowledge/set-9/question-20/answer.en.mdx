# Change and mean

The increase is measured against **the March value** as the starting value.

<BlockMath math="\frac{30-18}{18}\times100\%=\frac{12}{18}\times100\%\approx66.7\%" />

The increase is about 66.7%, not 50%. The first statement is false.

<BlockMath math="\bar x_{Q}=\frac{25+22+26+27+35}{5}=\frac{135}{5}=27" />

The mean is 27 million rupiah, so the second statement is false.

# Comparing the two shops

Compare month by month: shop P is higher in February (24 and 22) and April (30 and 27), and shop Q is higher in the other three months. That is **exactly two months**, so the third statement is true.

The total for shop P is <InlineMath math="20+24+18+30+33=125" />, while the total for shop Q is 135. The fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **false**, the third is **true**, and the fourth is **true**.
