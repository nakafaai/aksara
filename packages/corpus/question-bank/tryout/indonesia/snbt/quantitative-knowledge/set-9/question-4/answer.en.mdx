This function has two conditions. The number under the root **must not be negative**, and the denominator **must not be zero**.

# The root condition

<BlockMath math="x^2-5x+6=(x-2)(x-3)\ge0" />

A product of two factors is non-negative **outside its two zeros or exactly at them**, so <InlineMath math="x\le2" /> or <InlineMath math="x\ge3" />. Numbers between 2 and 3 make the radicand negative.

# The denominator and the domain

The denominator is zero at <InlineMath math="x=4" />, so that value is removed. The domain is **<InlineMath math="x\le2" /> or <InlineMath math="x\ge3" />, with <InlineMath math="x\ne4" />**.

| Value | Radicand | Denominator | In the domain? |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| <InlineMath math="x=3" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> | <InlineMath math="-1" /> | yes |
| <InlineMath math="x=2.5" /> | <InlineMath math="-0.25" /> | <InlineMath math="-1.5" /> | no |
| <InlineMath math="x=4" /> | <InlineMath math="2" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> | no |

For every <InlineMath math="x<0" />, both factors <InlineMath math="x-2" /> and <InlineMath math="x-3" /> are negative, so their product is positive, and the denominator is not zero. The fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
