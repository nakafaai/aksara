Let <InlineMath math="n_A" /> and <InlineMath math="n_B" /> be the numbers of students in classes <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" />.

The two class means are in the ratio <InlineMath math="9:10" />. Multiplying both parts by <InlineMath math="9" /> gives the equivalent ratio <InlineMath math="81:90" />. The combined mean and the mean of class <InlineMath math="A" /> are in the ratio <InlineMath math="84:81" />. We can therefore express all three means using the same factor <InlineMath math="k>0" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\bar X_A&=81k\\\bar X_B&=90k\\\bar X&=84k\end{aligned}" />

The combined mean is the total of all scores divided by the total number of students:

<BlockMath math="\bar X=\frac{\bar X_A n_A+\bar X_B n_B}{n_A+n_B}" />

Substitute the three means, then divide both sides by <InlineMath math="k" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="84k=\frac{81kn_A+90kn_B}{n_A+n_B}" />
  <BlockMath math="84=\frac{81n_A+90n_B}{n_A+n_B}" />
</MathContainer>

Multiply by <InlineMath math="n_A+n_B" /> and expand the left side:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}&84n_A+84n_B\\&\quad=81n_A+90n_B\end{aligned}" />

Subtract <InlineMath math="81n_A" /> and <InlineMath math="84n_B" /> from both sides:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="3n_A=6n_B" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{n_A}{n_B}=\frac63=\frac21" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the ratio of the numbers of students in classes <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" /> is <InlineMath math="2:1" />.
