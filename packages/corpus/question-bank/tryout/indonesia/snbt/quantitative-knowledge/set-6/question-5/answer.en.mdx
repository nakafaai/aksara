# The sum of eight terms

The fifth to twelfth terms also form an arithmetic sequence, so their sum is **the number of terms times the mean of the first and last**.

<BlockMath math="S=\frac{8}{2}\left(19+47\right)=4\cdot66=264" />

A check with a difference of sums: <InlineMath math="S_{12}-S_4=300-36=264" />.

The value 300 is the sum of the first twelve terms without subtracting anything. The value 245 is <InlineMath math="S_{12}-S_5" />, which also removes the fifth term. The value 297 counts nine terms, and the value 330 counts ten.

So the sum of the fifth to twelfth terms is **264**.
