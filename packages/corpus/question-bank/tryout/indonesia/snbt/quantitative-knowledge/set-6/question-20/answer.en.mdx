Read the eight values from the chart: Taman Kota (the city park) 80, 96, 72, 112 and Pantai (the beach) 60, 90, 108, 102. Each statement **compares a different quantity**, so compute those quantities one at a time.

# Means and weekly totals

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\bar x_{\text{Taman}}&=\frac{80+96+72+112}{4}=\frac{360}{4}=90\\
\bar x_{\text{Pantai}}&=\frac{60+90+108+102}{4}=\frac{360}{4}=90
\end{aligned}" />

The two means are **equal**, so the first statement is false.

The weekly totals of both locations are 140, 186, 180 and 214. The largest is in week four, so the second statement is true.

# Changes and ratios

A percentage decrease is measured against **the starting value, week two**.

<BlockMath math="\frac{96-72}{96}\times100\%=\frac{24}{96}\times100\%=25\%" />

The number 24 is the difference in bicycles, not the percentage. The third statement is false.

In week three, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{108}{72}=1.5" />, so the fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
