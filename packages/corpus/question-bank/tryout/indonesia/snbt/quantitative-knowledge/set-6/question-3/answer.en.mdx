Both quantities contain square roots, so rewrite them in **simplest radical form** before comparing.

# Expanding P

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P&=\left(\sqrt7\right)^2+2\sqrt7\sqrt3+\left(\sqrt3\right)^2\\
&=7+2\sqrt{21}+3=10+2\sqrt{21}
\end{aligned}" />

# Simplifying Q

Factor 84 so that the perfect square 4 appears.

<BlockMath math="Q=10+\sqrt{4\cdot21}=10+2\sqrt{21}" />

The two quantities are **exactly equal**, so <InlineMath math="P=Q" />. The tempting mistake is to take <InlineMath math="\left(\sqrt7+\sqrt3\right)^2=7+3=10" />, which forgets the middle term <InlineMath math="2\sqrt{21}" /> and makes <InlineMath math="Q" /> look larger.

So the correct relation is <InlineMath math="P=Q" />.
