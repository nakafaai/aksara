# Finding a and b

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}" />

The first statement is **true**. Because <InlineMath math="b=0" />, every term contains <InlineMath math="x" />, so <InlineMath math="x" /> is a factor. The third statement is true.
