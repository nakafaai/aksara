Given:

- Initial mass of the wet wood: <InlineMath math="90\text{ grams}" />.
- Final mass of the dry wood: <InlineMath math="60\text{ grams}" />.

The lost mass is the mass of the water that evaporated during drying:

<BlockMath math="90\text{ g}-60\text{ g}=30\text{ g}" />

The question asks for water as a percentage of the original wet mass, so the denominator is <InlineMath math="90\text{ grams}" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{30}{90}\cdot100\%&=\frac13\cdot100\% \\
&=33\frac13\%
\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, the water content in the wet wood is <InlineMath math="33\frac{1}{3}\%" />.
