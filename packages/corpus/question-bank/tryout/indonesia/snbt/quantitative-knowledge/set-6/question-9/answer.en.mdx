Compare **rotational symmetries and reflection axes**. Count the full <InlineMath math="360^\circ" /> turn once, as specified in the question.

| Figure | Rotational symmetries | Reflection axes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Isosceles right triangle | 1: 360° | 1: from the right angle to the hypotenuse midpoint |
| Right trapezoid that is not a rectangle | 1: 360° | 0 |
| Rectangle that is not a square | 2: 180° and 360° | 2: through the midpoints of opposite sides |
| Rhombus that is not a square | 2: 180° and 360° | 2: both diagonals |

In this rhombus, **both diagonals** divide the shape into mirror images. A 180° turn swaps opposite vertices.
