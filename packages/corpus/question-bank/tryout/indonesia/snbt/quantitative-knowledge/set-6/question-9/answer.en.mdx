The diagonals of a rhombus **cross at right angles and bisect each other**. They split the rhombus into four congruent right triangles with legs 5 cm and 12 cm.

# Side and area

The side of the rhombus is the hypotenuse of that triangle, and the area is half the product of the diagonals.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
s&=\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=13\\
A&=\tfrac12\cdot10\cdot24=120
\end{aligned}" />

The perimeter is <InlineMath math="4\cdot13=52" /> cm, so the first statement is **true**. The area is 120, not 240, so the second statement is false. The number 240 forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\tfrac12" />.

# Height and incircle

A rhombus is also a parallelogram, so its area equals **side times height**. This height is the distance between two parallel sides.

<BlockMath math="13t=120\quad\Rightarrow\quad t=\frac{120}{13}" />

The fourth statement is true. The incircle touches all four sides, so its diameter equals the distance between two parallel sides. Its radius is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{60}{13}" />, half the height, so the third statement is false.

So the first statement is **true**, the second is **false**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
