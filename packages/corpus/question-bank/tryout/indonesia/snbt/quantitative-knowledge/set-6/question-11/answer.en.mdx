Here's why: <InlineMath math="P(a)-P(b)" /> is a sum of terms <InlineMath math="c_k\left(a^k-b^k\right)" /> with integer <InlineMath math="c_k" />, and each <InlineMath math="a^k-b^k" /> factors.

<BlockMath math="a^k-b^k=(a-b)\left(a^{k-1}+a^{k-2}b+\dots+b^{k-1}\right)" />
