The cone volume formula needs the base radius and **the perpendicular height**, while the question gives the base circumference and the slant height. So convert those data first.

# Radius and height

The circumference <InlineMath math="2\pi r=12\pi" /> gives <InlineMath math="r=6" />. The radius, the height, and the slant height form a right triangle with **the slant height as the hypotenuse**.

<BlockMath math="t=\sqrt{10^2-6^2}=\sqrt{64}=8" />

# Volume

<BlockMath math="V=\tfrac13\pi r^2t=\tfrac13\pi(36)(8)=96\pi\text{ cm}^3" />

The value <InlineMath math="120\pi" /> **uses the slant height 10 as the height**. The value <InlineMath math="288\pi" /> forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\tfrac13" />, and the value <InlineMath math="360\pi" /> makes both mistakes. The value <InlineMath math="32\pi" /> uses the circumference <InlineMath math="12\pi" /> in place of the base area, as in <InlineMath math="\tfrac13\cdot12\pi\cdot8" />.

So the volume of the cone is **<InlineMath math="96\pi\text{ cm}^3" />**.
