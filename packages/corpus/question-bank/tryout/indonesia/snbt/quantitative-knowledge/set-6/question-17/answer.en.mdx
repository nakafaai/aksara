Each candidate must satisfy **both remainder conditions**. Write each number as the divisor times the quotient, plus the remainder.

| Number | Division by 7 | Division by 3 | Satisfies both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 52 | <InlineMath math="52=7\times7+3" /> | <InlineMath math="52=3\times17+1" /> | No |
| 59 | <InlineMath math="59=7\times8+3" /> | <InlineMath math="59=3\times19+2" /> | Yes |
| 42 | <InlineMath math="42=7\times6+0" /> | <InlineMath math="42=3\times14+0" /> | No |
| 38 | <InlineMath math="38=7\times5+3" /> | <InlineMath math="38=3\times12+2" /> | Yes |

Only 59 and 38 leave remainder 3 modulo 7 and remainder 2 modulo 3. Therefore, **statements 2 and 4** are correct.
