In triangle <InlineMath math="OAP" />, **the hypotenuse** is <InlineMath math="OP=13" /> and one leg is <InlineMath math="OA=5" />.

# The area of the quadrilateral

Each triangle has **legs 5 and 12**, so its area is <InlineMath math="\tfrac12\cdot5\cdot12=30" />.

<BlockMath math="[OAPB]=2\cdot30=60\text{ cm}^2" />

The value 30 counts only one triangle. The value 120 forgets the factor <InlineMath math="\tfrac12" />. The value 65 uses <InlineMath math="OP" /> as a leg, <InlineMath math="2\cdot\tfrac12\cdot5\cdot13" />, although <InlineMath math="OP" /> is the hypotenuse. So the area of quadrilateral <InlineMath math="OAPB" /> is **<InlineMath math="60\text{ cm}^2" />**.
