There are two conditions: every student gets a book, and the dictionary stays apart from the atlas. A clean way is to **count every distribution that meets the first condition, then subtract the ones that break the second**. Both counts use the inclusion-exclusion principle.

# All fair distributions

With no conditions at all, each book can go to any of the <InlineMath math="3" /> students, so there are <InlineMath math="3^5=243" /> ways. From that, remove the distributions that leave someone empty-handed. There are <InlineMath math="2^5" /> ways that leave one particular student empty, and <InlineMath math="3" /> choices for that student. Distributions that leave two students empty got removed twice, so **add them back once**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3^5-3\cdot2^5+3\cdot1^5 &= 243-96+3 \\
&= 150
\end{aligned}" />

# Distributions that keep the dictionary and atlas together

If the dictionary and the atlas go to the same student, **treat them as one bundle**. Now there are <InlineMath math="4" /> different items, the bundle and the other <InlineMath math="3" /> books, handed out so that every student gets something.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3^4-3\cdot2^4+3\cdot1^4 &= 81-48+3 \\
&= 36
\end{aligned}" />

# Subtracting the forbidden distributions

The distributions you want are the fair ones that don't put the dictionary and atlas together.

<BlockMath math="150-36=114" />

Check it by a direct route. The dictionary has <InlineMath math="3" /> possible receivers and the atlas has <InlineMath math="2" />, giving <InlineMath math="6" /> ways. The remaining three books are free, as long as **the third student, who has no book yet, gets at least one**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
6\times\left(3^3-2^3\right) &= 6\times(27-8) \\
&= 6\times19=114
\end{aligned}" />

Both routes give the same result.

# Why the other options fail

Each wrong option **ignores one of the conditions** or counts the wrong part.

- <InlineMath math="72=6\times3\times2^2" /> comes from making the third student get exactly one book, when the condition is at least one.
- <InlineMath math="162=6\times27" /> keeps the dictionary and atlas apart but forgets to make sure the third student gets a book.
- <InlineMath math="150" /> makes sure everyone gets a book but forgets the dictionary and atlas condition.
- <InlineMath math="36" /> is the number of forbidden distributions, the ones that put the dictionary and atlas together.

So the number of ways to hand out the books is <InlineMath math="114" />.
