# Statements that do not always hold

The statement <InlineMath math="x-2>0" /> fails for <InlineMath math="x=1.5" />, which lies in the interval but is less than 2. One **counterexample** is enough, so the first statement is false.

For <InlineMath math="1<x<4" />, taking reciprocals of positive numbers reverses the order: <InlineMath math="\frac1x>\frac14" />. The third statement claims the opposite, so it is false for every <InlineMath math="x" /> in the interval.

# Statements that always hold

Since <InlineMath math="0<x<4" />, both sides are positive and **squaring keeps the order**, so <InlineMath math="x^2<16" />. The second statement is true.

On the interval <InlineMath math="1<x<4" />, the factor <InlineMath math="x-1" /> is positive and the factor <InlineMath math="x-5" /> is negative. Their product is negative, so the fourth statement is true.

So the first statement is **false**, the second is **true**, the third is **false**, and the fourth is **true**.
