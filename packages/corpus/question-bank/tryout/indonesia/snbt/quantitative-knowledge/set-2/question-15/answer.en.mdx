We need an exact expression for the difference between two consecutive terms. From <InlineMath math="a_4 = 85" />, working backwards gives

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="a_3 = \frac{85 - 1}{4} = 21" />
  <BlockMath math="a_2 = \frac{21 - 1}{4} = 5" />
  <BlockMath math="a_1 = \frac{5 - 1}{4} = 1" />
</MathContainer>

#### Deriving the Difference Formula

Multiplying the recurrence by <InlineMath math="3" /> and adding <InlineMath math="1" /> gives

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="3a_{n+1} + 1 = 3(4a_n + 1) + 1" />
  <BlockMath math="3a_{n+1} + 1 = 4(3a_n + 1)" />
</MathContainer>

Since <InlineMath math="3a_1 + 1 = 4" />, it follows that <InlineMath math="3a_n + 1 = 4^n" />. The original recurrence then yields

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="a_{n+1} - a_n = 4a_n + 1 - a_n" />
  <BlockMath math="a_{n+1} - a_n = 3a_n + 1 = 4^n" />
</MathContainer>

For <InlineMath math="n = 2024" />,

<BlockMath math="a_{2025} - a_{2024} = 4^{2024} = 2^{4048}" />

This number has no prime factor other than <InlineMath math="2" />. Among <InlineMath math="3, 4, 5, 6," /> and <InlineMath math="7" />, only <InlineMath math="4 = 2^2" /> divides it. Therefore, the required divisor is <InlineMath math="4" />.
