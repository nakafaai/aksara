To calculate the area of region <InlineMath math="S" />, we need to determine the **vertices of the solution region** for the system of inequalities. This region is bounded by three lines:

1. Line <InlineMath math="1" />: <InlineMath math="y = 2x + 4" />
2. Line <InlineMath math="2" />: <InlineMath math="y = -x + 4" />
3. Line <InlineMath math="3" />: <InlineMath math="x = 4" />

The first two inequalities give <InlineMath math="-x+4\le y\le2x+4" />. The lower bound cannot exceed the upper bound, so <InlineMath math="-x+4\le2x+4" /> implies <InlineMath math="x\ge0" />. Together with <InlineMath math="x\le4" />, this places the region between the two lines for <InlineMath math="0\le x\le4" />.

Find the intersection points between these lines.

#### Finding the Three Vertices

Use **equal values of the vertical coordinate** at the intersection:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="2x + 4 = -x + 4" />
  <BlockMath math="3x = 0" />
  <BlockMath math="x = 0" />
</MathContainer>

Substitute <InlineMath math="x = 0" /> into one of the equations (e.g., Line <InlineMath math="2" />):

<BlockMath math="y = -0 + 4 = 4" />

So, the first intersection point is <InlineMath math="A(0, 4)" />.

Substitute <InlineMath math="x = 4" /> into the equation of Line <InlineMath math="1" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="y = 2(4) + 4" />
  <BlockMath math="y = 8 + 4 = 12" />
</MathContainer>

So, the second intersection point is <InlineMath math="B(4, 12)" />.

Substitute <InlineMath math="x = 4" /> into the equation of Line <InlineMath math="2" />:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="y = -(4) + 4" />
  <BlockMath math="y = 0" />
</MathContainer>

So, the third intersection point is <InlineMath math="C(4, 0)" />.

#### Calculating the Area

Region <InlineMath math="S" /> is a triangle with vertices <InlineMath math="A(0, 4)" />, <InlineMath math="B(4, 12)" />, and <InlineMath math="C(4, 0)" />.

We can calculate the area using the base and height.

- **Base**: The vertical line connecting point <InlineMath math="B" /> and <InlineMath math="C" /> (since both have <InlineMath math="x = 4" />).
  Length of base <InlineMath math="= y_B - y_C = 12 - 0 = 12" />.

- **Height**: The horizontal distance from point <InlineMath math="A" /> to the line <InlineMath math="x = 4" />.
  Height <InlineMath math="= x_{BC} - x_A = 4 - 0 = 4" />.

Let <InlineMath math="A" /> be the area, <InlineMath math="b" /> the base length, and <InlineMath math="h" /> the height of the triangle.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
A &= \frac12 bh \\
&= \frac12\cdot12\cdot4 \\
&= 6\cdot4 \\
&= 24
\end{aligned}" />

Thus, the area of region <InlineMath math="S" /> is <InlineMath math="24" /> square units.
