There are two restrictions: the committee can't be all one gender, and Andi can't be with Budi. **The order of members doesn't matter**, so you use combinations. The plan: count the mixed committees, then remove the mixed committees that contain both Andi and Budi.

# Mixed committees

From <InlineMath math="9" /> students, there are <InlineMath math="\binom94=126" /> committees of four. Remove **the committees with only one gender**, the all-boy and the all-girl ones.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\binom94-\binom54-\binom44&=126-5-1=120
\end{aligned}" />

# Committees with Andi and Budi

If Andi and Budi are both in, the other two members come from the remaining <InlineMath math="7" /> students: <InlineMath math="\binom72=21" /> committees. But <InlineMath math="\binom32=3" /> of them are all boys, namely Andi, Budi, and two of the other three boys. Those three committees were **already removed in the first step**, so don't remove them twice. The mixed committees with both Andi and Budi number <InlineMath math="21-3=18" />.

# The final count

<BlockMath math="120-18=102" />

Check it in the other order: **remove the Andi and Budi pair first**. Committees without the pair Andi and Budi number <InlineMath math="126-21=105" />. Among them, the all-boy committees without both of them number <InlineMath math="5-3=2" />, and there's <InlineMath math="1" /> all-girl committee. So <InlineMath math="105-2-1=102" />, the same.

# Traps in the other options

- <InlineMath math="120" /> forgets the Andi and Budi rule.
- <InlineMath math="108" /> only removes the Andi and Budi committees whose other two members are one boy and one girl, <InlineMath math="3\times4=12" /> committees, and **forgets the committees of Andi, Budi, and two girls**, which number <InlineMath math="\binom42=6" />.
- <InlineMath math="105" /> handles the Andi and Budi rule but forgets that there must be at least one boy and one girl.
- <InlineMath math="99" /> subtracts all <InlineMath math="21" /> committees with Andi and Budi, including the three all-boy committees that were already removed.

So there are <InlineMath math="102" /> ways to form the committee.
