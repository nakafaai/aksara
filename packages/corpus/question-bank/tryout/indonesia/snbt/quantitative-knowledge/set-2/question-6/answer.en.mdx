# The discriminant condition

With <InlineMath math="a=1" />, <InlineMath math="b=-2k" />, and <InlineMath math="c=k+6" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

A product of two factors is negative when the factors **have opposite signs**, which happens for <InlineMath math="-2<k<3" />.
