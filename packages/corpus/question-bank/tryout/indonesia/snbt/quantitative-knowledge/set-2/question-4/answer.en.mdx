To determine the passing percentage, we must check **two passing conditions** for each student:
1. The Science score must be higher than the median Science score of all students.
2. The mean of the two exam scores must exceed <InlineMath math="85" />. Add the Math and Science scores, then divide by <InlineMath math="2" />.

#### Determining the Median Science Score

The Science scores are: <InlineMath math="78, 82, 87, 85, 79, 88, 81, 80" />.

**Sort the data** from smallest to largest:
<InlineMath math="78, 79, 80, 81, 82, 85, 87, 88" />.

Since there are <InlineMath math="8" /> data points, the median is the average of the <InlineMath math="4" />th and <InlineMath math="5" />th data points:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\text{Median}&=\frac{81 + 82}{2}\\&=81.5\end{aligned}" />

Students who satisfy the first condition (Science Score <InlineMath math="> 81.5" />) are:
- Boni (<InlineMath math="82" />)
- Cindy (<InlineMath math="87" />)
- Dita (<InlineMath math="85" />)
- Fani (<InlineMath math="88" />)

The other students (Ayu, Erfan, Gilang, Herman) have Science scores below the median, so they automatically do not pass.

#### Checking the Average Score Condition

Now we check the second condition for students who passed the first condition:

Use <InlineMath math="\bar x" /> for the mean of a student’s two scores.

| Student | Mean | Meets second condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boni | <InlineMath math="\frac{92+82}2=\frac{174}2=87" /> | Yes, <InlineMath math="87>85" /> |
| Cindy | <InlineMath math="\frac{90+87}2=\frac{177}2=88.5" /> | Yes, <InlineMath math="88.5>85" /> |
| Dita | <InlineMath math="\frac{86+85}2=\frac{171}2=85.5" /> | Yes, <InlineMath math="85.5>85" /> |
| Fani | <InlineMath math="\frac{88+88}2=\frac{176}2=88" /> | Yes, <InlineMath math="88>85" /> |

All four meet **both conditions at once**. Therefore, four of the eight students pass.

#### Calculating the First Quantity

The denominator is **all eight students**, rather than only those above the median:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}P&=\frac{4}{8} \times 100\%\\&=50\%\end{aligned}" />

Given <InlineMath math="Q = 65\%" />.

Thus:

<BlockMath math="\begin{gathered}50\%<65\%\\P<Q\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad Q>P\end{gathered}" />
