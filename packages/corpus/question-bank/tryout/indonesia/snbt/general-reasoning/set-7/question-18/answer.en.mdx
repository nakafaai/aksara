# Given

Nadia (student) returned **two books <InlineMath math="10" /> days late**, Mr. Hadi (general) returned **one book <InlineMath math="20" /> days late**, and the **fine cap applies per book**.

# Asked

The **combined total fine** paid by Nadia and Mr. Hadi.

# Solution

> Calculate the fine for each individual book first so the per-book ceiling kicks in before you add everything together.

Work out the fine **per book** first, because the cap in rule 4 applies per book.

**Nadia (student, <InlineMath math="10" /> days)**. Days <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="2" /> are free. Days <InlineMath math="3" /> to <InlineMath math="7" /> are <InlineMath math="5" /> days, and days <InlineMath math="8" /> to <InlineMath math="10" /> are <InlineMath math="3" /> days.

<BlockMath math="5\times1{,}000+3\times2{,}000=11{,}000\text{ per book},\quad 2\times11{,}000=22{,}000" />

**Pak Hadi (general member, <InlineMath math="20" /> days)**. Days <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="7" /> are <InlineMath math="7" /> days, and days <InlineMath math="8" /> to <InlineMath math="20" /> are <InlineMath math="13" /> days.

<BlockMath math="7\times1{,}000+13\times2{,}000=33{,}000" />

That exceeds the cap, so Pak Hadi's fine becomes <InlineMath math="30{,}000" />.
