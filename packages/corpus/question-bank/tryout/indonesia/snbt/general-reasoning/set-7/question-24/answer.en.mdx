Measure rates in tankfuls per hour. While both pumps run at full capacity, their combined rate is:

<BlockMath math="\frac1{24}+\frac1{18}=\frac7{72}" />

After <InlineMath math="6" /> hours, the filled and remaining fractions are:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="6\times\frac7{72}=\frac7{12}" />
  <BlockMath math="1-\frac7{12}=\frac5{12}" />
</MathContainer>

In the next stage, A runs at <InlineMath math="75\%" /> of its original rate:

<BlockMath math="\frac34\times\frac1{24}=\frac1{32}" />

Divide the remaining fraction by the new rate to find the additional time:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}t&=\frac5{12}\div\frac1{32}\\&=\frac{40}{3}\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, the additional time is **<InlineMath math="40/3" /> hours**, or <InlineMath math="13" /> hours <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes.
