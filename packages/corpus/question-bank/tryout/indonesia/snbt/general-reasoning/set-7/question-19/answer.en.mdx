# Given

Three harbor rules link **Thousand Islands ferries, extra life jackets, and motorcycle cargo**, and **Kapal Bahari carries motorcycles**.

# Asked

The conclusion that is **certainly true** about Kapal Bahari tonight.

# Solution

> Walk backward from carrying motorcycles to rule out extra life jackets and then rule out sailing to the Thousand Islands.

Combine the statements with **contraposition**.

- Kapal Bahari carries motorbikes. No ship with extra life vests carries motorbikes, so Kapal Bahari does **not** carry extra life vests.
- Every ship sailing to the Thousand Islands tonight carries extra life vests. Its contrapositive: a ship without extra life vests does not sail there tonight. So Kapal Bahari does **not** sail to the Thousand Islands tonight.

The other options are uncertain or false:

- Kapal Bahari does not carry extra life vests.
- Ships carrying motorbikes have no extra life vests, so they cannot sail to the Thousand Islands tonight. That statement is actually false.
- Kapal Bahari may carry other cargo that is not mentioned.
- The second statement says no ship with extra life vests carries motorbikes.

# Answer

So the conclusion that must be true is that **Kapal Bahari does not sail to the Thousand Islands tonight**.
