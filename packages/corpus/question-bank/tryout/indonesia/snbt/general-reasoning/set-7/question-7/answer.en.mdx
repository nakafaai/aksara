Pak Ujang's data show that growing above <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> meters is **a shared feature** of every winner. That feature may be necessary, but the data do not show it is **sufficient**. Many other high farms may never win because of how the beans are processed, how the plants are tended, or the coffee variety.

For Pak Ujang to be sure his coffee will win just because his land is high, he must assume that altitude alone is enough.

The other options are not required assumptions:

- His belief does not depend on whether every high farm has entered the contest.
- The number of entrants next year is not part of his reason.
- Whether lowland coffee was ever entered does not make his coffee more likely to win.
- Land prices have nothing to do with the chance of winning.

So his belief depends on the assumption that altitude alone is enough to produce winning coffee.
