# Given

**Rina first four test scores** are **<InlineMath math="82, 88, 79, 90" />**, her sixth is projected at **<InlineMath math="95" />**, and her six-test average target is at least **<InlineMath math="85" />**.

# Asked

The **lowest score** Rina needs on her fifth test.

# Solution

> Multiply the target average by six to get the required point total, then subtract the five known and projected scores.

An average of at least <InlineMath math="85" /> over six tests means **the sum of the six scores** is at least <InlineMath math="6\times85=510" />.

The sum of the known or expected scores:

<BlockMath math="82+88+79+90+95=434" />

If the fifth score is <InlineMath math="x" />:

<BlockMath math="434+x\ge510\quad\Rightarrow\quad x\ge76" />

Check: with <InlineMath math="x=76" />, the sum is <InlineMath math="510" /> and the average is exactly <InlineMath math="85" />. So Rina needs at least <InlineMath math="76" /> on the fifth test.
