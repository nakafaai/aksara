Moving a member changes neither the total number of people nor the total score. Use the data **before** the move, when each group has <InlineMath math="9" /> members. Let <InlineMath math="S_A" /> and <InlineMath math="S_B" /> be the two score totals.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="S_A=9\times78=702" />
  <BlockMath math="S_B=9\times66=594" />
</MathContainer>

The combined mean is the total score divided by the total number of members:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\bar{x}&amp;=\frac{702+594}{9+9}\\&amp;=\frac{1296}{18}\\&amp;=72\end{aligned}" />

The transferred member's score is <InlineMath math="10\times68-594=86" />. You can follow the change in both groups in this table.

| Group | Members before | Score total before | Members after | Score total after |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 9 | 702 | 8 | <InlineMath math="702-86=616" /> |
| B | 9 | 594 | 10 | <InlineMath math="594+86=680" /> |
| Combined | 18 | 1296 | 18 | 1296 |

The combined total remains <InlineMath math="616+680=1296" />, so the overall mean stays **<InlineMath math="72" />**.
