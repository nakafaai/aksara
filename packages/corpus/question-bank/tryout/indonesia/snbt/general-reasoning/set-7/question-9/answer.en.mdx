A good sample must **represent all high school students in the city** and must not lean toward students who already love reading. Choosing classes at random from every school meets both conditions: every school is represented, and students in the chosen classes do not decide for themselves whether to take part.

The other options give biased samples:

- Book club members from one school are keen readers, and only one school is represented.
- Followers of the library's account who choose to answer tend to be students interested in books.
- Students met in a bookshop clearly read more than students in general.
- Principals give only estimates, not data from the students themselves.

So the best method is to choose some classes at random from every high school and interview all students in the chosen classes.
