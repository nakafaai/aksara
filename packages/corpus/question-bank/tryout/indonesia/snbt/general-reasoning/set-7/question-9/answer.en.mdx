The chart separates library and laboratory visits. To compare **combined totals**, add the two values for the same day, then rank the sums.

| Rank from highest | Day | Total visits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Monday | <InlineMath math="150+250=400" /> |
| 2 | Tuesday | <InlineMath math="135+210=345" /> |
| 3 | Thursday | <InlineMath math="140+170=310" /> |
| 4 | Friday | <InlineMath math="165+120=285" /> |
| 5 | Wednesday | <InlineMath math="100+140=240" /> |

Only Monday's total exceeds Tuesday's. The second-highest combined total is therefore on **Tuesday**, with <InlineMath math="345" /> visits.
