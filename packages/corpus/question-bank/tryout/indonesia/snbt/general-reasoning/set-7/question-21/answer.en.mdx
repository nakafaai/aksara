Separate the terms by **their positions**, rather than by whether their values are odd or even.

At odd positions, consecutive terms differ by <InlineMath math="7" />:

<BlockMath math="1,\ 8,\ 15,\ 22" />

<BlockMath math="a_9=22+7=29" />

At even positions, each term is <InlineMath math="3" /> times the preceding term:

<BlockMath math="2,\ 6,\ 18" />

<BlockMath math="a_8=18\times3=54" />

Substitute the two new terms into the requested expression:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}2a_8-a_9&amp;=2(54)-29\\&amp;=108-29\\&amp;=79\end{aligned}" />
