# Given

**A number sequence** begins with **<InlineMath math="3, 1, 2, 6, 9, 17, 32" />**.

# Asked

The **eighth term** of the sequence.

# Solution

> When differences look messy, check whether each new term equals the sum of the two or three terms right before it.

The sequence falls and then rises, and its differences, <InlineMath math="-2, 1, 4, 3, 8, 15" />, show no simple pattern. Check **how each term relates to several terms before it**. From the fourth term on, each term is the sum of the three terms before it:

<BlockMath math="3+1+2=6,\quad 1+2+6=9,\quad 2+6+9=17,\quad 6+9+17=32" />

The eighth term:

<BlockMath math="9+17+32=58" />

The chart below draws all eight terms. **From the fourth term on, the line climbs faster and faster**, because each term adds up the three before it.

# Answer

So the eighth term of the sequence is <InlineMath math="58" />.
