# Given

All five post-assignment rules apply together with **Ayu assigned to the center post**.

# Asked

The statement that is **certainly true** when Ayu takes the center post.

# Solution

> Since the center post holds only one guard, placing Ayu there fills it immediately and forces the rest into fixed posts.

If Ayu is at the middle post, the middle is **already full**. Trace the consequences one by one.

- Citra must go south, since the middle is full (rule 2).
- Eko cannot be with Citra, so Eko goes north (rule 5).
- Dani cannot go north, since rule 3 would then put Eko in the south, but Eko is north. So Dani goes south.
- Bima is left, and the one free place is in the north.

The diagram below draws that chain. **Each step leaves only one place**, so the assignment is unique.

The assignment: Bima and Eko north, Ayu in the middle, Citra and Dani south. Check rules 1 and 4: Ayu and Bima are at different posts, and Bima is not in the middle. Every rule holds.

The other options contradict this only assignment: Citra and Dani are south, Eko is north, and Bima and Dani are at different posts.

# Answer

So what must be true is that Bima and Eko serve at the north post.
