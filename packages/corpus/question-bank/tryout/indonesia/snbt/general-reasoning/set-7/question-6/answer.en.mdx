# Given

The free vaccine drive ran in March with **two doses spaced four weeks apart**, and April cat visits stayed far above February.

# Asked

The **most likely explanation** for why April cat visits remained much higher than February.

# Solution

> Cats given their first shot in March naturally come back four weeks later in April for their required second dose.

The passage says each cat in the program gets the vaccine **twice, four weeks apart**. Cats vaccinated first in March must come back about four weeks later, at the end of March or in April. That is why cat visits stayed high in April and then dropped in May once the second doses were done.

The other options do not explain it:

- Dog visits do not affect the number of cat visits.
- An extra vet in June does not explain April.
- The passage does not mention weather, and weather would not explain a jump that happened only for cats.
- The passage says dog fees did not change, and dog fees have nothing to do with cat visits.

# Answer

So the most likely explanation is that **cats vaccinated first in March came back for their second dose four weeks later**.
