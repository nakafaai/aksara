The rule applies to both lamps because they belong to the stated product series. Let <InlineMath math="B" />, <InlineMath math="D" />, and <InlineMath math="P" /> denote brightness, durability, and price. The subscript <InlineMath math="T" /> denotes the terrace lamp, while <InlineMath math="B" /> denotes the bathroom lamp.

The terrace lamp is brighter. The rule gives two consequences:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="B_T>B_B" />
  <BlockMath math="D_T>D_B" />
  <BlockMath math="P_T>P_B" />
</MathContainer>

Read the last two comparisons from the bathroom lamp's side:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="D_B<D_T" />
  <BlockMath math="P_B<P_T" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the bathroom lamp is **less durable and less expensive than the terrace lamp**. The other choices reverse at least one comparison or claim equality contrary to the rule.
