The comparison rule applies to these two lamps because both belong to the specified product series.

The terrace lamp is brighter. Therefore, according to the rule, it is also more durable and more expensive than the bathroom lamp:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="B_T > B_B \quad\Longrightarrow\quad D_T > D_B \;\land\; P_T > P_B" />
</MathContainer>

Reading the same comparisons from the bathroom lamp's side gives <InlineMath math="D_B < D_T" /> and <InlineMath math="P_B < P_T" />. Thus, the bathroom lamp is less durable and less expensive than the terrace lamp. The third choice is correct.
