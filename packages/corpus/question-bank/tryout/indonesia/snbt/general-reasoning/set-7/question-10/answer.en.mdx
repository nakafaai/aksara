To calculate the total, add the laboratory values from Monday to Friday:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Monday} = 250" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Tuesday} = 210" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Wednesday} = 140" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Thursday} = 170" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Friday} = 120" />
</MathContainer>

Thus, the total number of visitors is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Total} = 250 + 210 + 140 + 170 + 120" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{Total} = 890 \text{ people}" />
</MathContainer>

The laboratory therefore had **<InlineMath math="890\text{ visitors}" />** over the five days.
