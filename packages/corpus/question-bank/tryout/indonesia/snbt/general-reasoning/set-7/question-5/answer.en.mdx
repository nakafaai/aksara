Let:

- <InlineMath math="P" />: Budi completes every scheduled practice session this month.
- <InlineMath math="Q" />: Budi passes the long-distance assessment.
- <InlineMath math="R" />: Budi may enter the program's race.

The rules state <InlineMath math="P \rightarrow Q" /> and <InlineMath math="Q \rightarrow R" />. The assessment result gives <InlineMath math="\neg Q" />.

The first rule and the result form a direct modus tollens argument:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} P &\rightarrow Q \\ \neg Q &\\ \hline \therefore\ \neg P &\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, Budi did not complete **every** scheduled practice session this month. This does not mean that he did not practice at all, dislikes racing, or can never win another race.
