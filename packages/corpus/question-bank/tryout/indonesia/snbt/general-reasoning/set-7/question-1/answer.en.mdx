The statement about the greatest fullness after whole apple and lower energy intake reproduces both results in the passage:

- Of the four apple preparations, the whole apple produced the greatest fullness.
- Compared with no preload, it was associated with a <InlineMath math="15\%" /> lower total energy intake at lunch.

Those are results from this particular randomized crossover study. They do not establish that apples always prevent obesity, that all preparations work equally well, or that fiber alone explains the differences. Adding fiber to juice also does not establish that fiber explains the whole effect of an apple.

Therefore, the supported answer is that **whole apple produced the greatest fullness and lower total energy intake than no preload in this study**.
