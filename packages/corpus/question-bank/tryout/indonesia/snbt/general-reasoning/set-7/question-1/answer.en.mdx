The fourth statement reproduces both comparisons reported in the passage:

- Of the four apple preparations, the whole apple produced the greatest fullness.
- Compared with no preload, it was associated with a <InlineMath math="15\%" /> lower total energy intake at lunch.

Those are results from this particular randomized crossover study. They do not establish that apples always prevent obesity, that all preparations work equally well, or that fiber alone explains the differences. The abstract specifically notes that the effect of a whole apple could not be explained by fiber alone.

Therefore, the fourth statement is the only one fully supported by the passage.
