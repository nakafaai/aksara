# Given

In **Kenanga Park** bird species rose from <InlineMath math="8" /> to <InlineMath math="12" /> by late 2024, while **nest boxes were installed only in early 2025**.

# Asked

The conclusion **most strongly supported** by the timeline in the passage.

# Solution

> A change installed afterward cannot be the cause of an increase that had already happened before it arrived.

Look at **the order of events**. The nest boxes went up only at the start of 2025, but the number of bird species had already risen from <InlineMath math="8" /> to <InlineMath math="12" /> by the end of 2024. So most of the increase happened before there were any nest boxes.

The other options are not supported:

- The rise in 2024, <InlineMath math="4" /> species, is larger than the rise after the boxes went up, <InlineMath math="3" /> species. The boxes cannot be called the main cause.
- The passage counts **species** of birds, not individual birds.
- Taman Melati actually gained a species, from <InlineMath math="9" /> to <InlineMath math="10" />, so there is no sign of a loss.
- The biggest rise came right after the planting while the comparison park barely changed. These data do not support the trees having no effect.

# Answer

So the best supported conclusion is that **the rise in bird species at Taman Kenanga began before the nest boxes were installed**.
