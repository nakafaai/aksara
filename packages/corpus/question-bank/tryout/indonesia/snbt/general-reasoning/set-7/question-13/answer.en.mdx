"Not all campers brought a flashlight" (*Tidak semua ...*) denies "all campers brought a flashlight". That "all" statement already fails if **at least one** camper did not bring one. So the statement is equivalent to "**There is** a camper who did not bring a flashlight" (*Ada peserta kemah yang tidak membawa senter*).

The other options are not equivalent:

- "All campers did not bring a flashlight" is much stronger. The original stays true even if only one camper lacks a flashlight.
- "No camper brought a flashlight" is as strong as the first option.
- "Some camper brought a flashlight" cannot be concluded. Possibly no one did.
- "Most campers" adds an amount the original never mentions.

So the statement is equivalent to "There is a camper who did not bring a flashlight".
