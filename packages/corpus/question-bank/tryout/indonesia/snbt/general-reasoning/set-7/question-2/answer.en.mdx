The fourth statement changes a possible relationship into an exclusive universal claim.

The passage says that plaque **can** harden into tartar, tartar **can** irritate the gums, and swollen gums **can** be a sign of gingivitis. None of those statements says that plaque or tartar is the only possible cause of every swollen gum.

The other four statements stay within the limits of the passage. They preserve words such as "can," or they repeat the stated need for professional tartar removal.

Therefore, the fourth statement is the most likely false statement.
