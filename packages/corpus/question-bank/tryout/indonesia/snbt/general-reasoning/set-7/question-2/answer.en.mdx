The idea states **a cause**: the fruit trees attract new bird species. Evidence that strengthens it must link the newly arrived species to the **fruit** of those trees.

If most of the species first recorded in 2024 are fruit eaters, their arrival matches the new food supply from the local fruit trees. That link supports the idea most directly.

The other options do not strengthen it:

- Park size and the number of benches did not change with the planting, so they do not explain the rise in 2024.
- Visitor numbers do not show why bird species increased.
- Nest boxes used by species already present have nothing to do with new species in 2024.
- The change at Taman Melati is already given and has nothing to do with fruit trees.

So the statement that strengthens the idea most is that most of the species newly recorded in 2024 are fruit eaters.
