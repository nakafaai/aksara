Revenue depends on **cups and prices**, so the day with the most cups need not have the most revenue. Work out each day's revenue in thousands of rupiah:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Monday} &= 40\times9+25\times7+30\times6=360+175+180=715 \\
\text{Tuesday} &= 35\times9+30\times7+35\times6=315+210+210=735 \\
\text{Wednesday} &= 50\times9+20\times7+25\times6=450+140+150=740 \\
\text{Thursday} &= 30\times9+40\times7+30\times6=270+280+180=730 \\
\text{Friday} &= 45\times9+35\times7+20\times6=405+245+120=770
\end{aligned}" />

Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday all sold <InlineMath math="100" /> cups, but Friday sold more of the expensive drinks. So the highest revenue came on Friday (*Jumat*), <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}770{,}000" />.
