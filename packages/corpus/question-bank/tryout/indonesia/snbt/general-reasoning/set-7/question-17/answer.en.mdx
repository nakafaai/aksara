Let:

- <InlineMath math="P" />: The report passes the completeness check.
- <InlineMath math="Q" />: The report proceeds to analyst review.
- <InlineMath math="R" />: The report enters the final decision queue.

The procedure gives <InlineMath math="P \rightarrow Q" /> and <InlineMath math="Q \rightarrow R" />. By hypothetical syllogism, <InlineMath math="P \rightarrow R" />. Because Mira's report satisfies <InlineMath math="P" />, modus ponens gives <InlineMath math="R" />.

Mira's report therefore enters the final decision queue. Reaching that queue is not the same as already receiving approval, so the claims that Mira's application is already approved or that every queued report is automatically approved go beyond the rules.
