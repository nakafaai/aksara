Split the cases by **who takes the middle post**. Bima cannot be in the middle (rule 4), and Eko cannot be either (Eko in the middle forces Ayu and Bima together). So the middle post goes to Ayu, Citra, or Dani.

**Ayu in the middle**. As in the previous question, there is only one assignment: Bima and Eko north, Citra and Dani south. That is **1** assignment.

**Citra in the middle**. Ayu and Bima must be apart, so one is north and one is south. Dani and Eko fill the remaining place in the north and the remaining place in the south. If Dani is north, Eko must be south (rule 3), which is allowed. If Dani is south, Eko is north, also allowed. That is <InlineMath math="2\times2=4" /> assignments.

**Dani in the middle**. Citra must go south, so Eko goes north. Ayu and Bima are apart, one north with Eko and the other south with Citra. That is **2** assignments.

The total is <InlineMath math="1+4+2=7" /> assignments.
