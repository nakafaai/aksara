The two balls are selected without regard to order. From <InlineMath math="12" /> balls, the number of equally likely pairs is:

<BlockMath math="\binom{12}{2}=66" />

The payout for two red balls rises by <InlineMath math="50\%" />, from <InlineMath math="30" /> to <InlineMath math="45" /> thousand rupiah. The other payouts stay fixed:

| Outcome | Pairs | New payout |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Two red | <InlineMath math="\binom82=28" /> | <InlineMath math="45" /> |
| Different colours | <InlineMath math="8\times4=32" /> | <InlineMath math="15" /> |
| Two blue | <InlineMath math="\binom42=6" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

Let <InlineMath math="X" /> be the payout in thousand rupiah. Every payout in the table uses that unit. Weight each payout by its probability to obtain the expectation:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\mathbb E[X]&=45\frac{28}{66}+15\frac{32}{66}\\&\quad+0\frac6{66}\\&=\frac{1260+480}{66}\\&=\frac{290}{11}\end{aligned}" />

The new expected payout is therefore **<InlineMath math="290/11" /> thousand rupiah**.
