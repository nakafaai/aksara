Split into **two cases** by the two volumes, since they are always chosen together or not at all. Besides those two, there are <InlineMath math="5" /> other books.

**Both volumes chosen**. One more book comes from the <InlineMath math="5" /> others:

<BlockMath math="\binom{5}{1}=5" />

**Neither volume chosen**. All three books come from the <InlineMath math="5" /> others:

<BlockMath math="\binom{5}{3}=10" />

Choosing exactly one volume is not allowed, so that case is not counted. The total is <InlineMath math="5+10=15" /> ways.
