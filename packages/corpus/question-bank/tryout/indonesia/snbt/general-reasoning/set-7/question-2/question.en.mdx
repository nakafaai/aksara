Plaque is a sticky film of bacteria that continually forms on teeth. If plaque is not removed, it can harden below the gumline into tartar, which is more difficult to remove and can irritate the gums. Red, swollen, or bleeding gums can be signs of gingivitis, the early stage of gum disease. Once tartar has formed, a dental professional must remove it.

Sources: [National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research](https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/health-info/oral-hygiene) and [American Dental Association](https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/floss)
