Work backwards from the last statement with **modus tollens**.

- The audience is not limited, so by the second statement the show is **not** moved indoors.
- The show is not moved, so by the first statement "it rains or the wind blows hard" does **not** happen.

The negation of "rain or strong wind" is "**no rain and no strong wind**". Both must fail to happen.

The other options are not valid:

- Rain alone would move the show, so it cannot be raining today.
- The show is in fact not moved.
- An unlimited audience does not mean more than <InlineMath math="200" /> people. There may be fewer.
- Strong wind alone would also move the show.

So the valid conclusion is that today there is no rain and no strong wind.
