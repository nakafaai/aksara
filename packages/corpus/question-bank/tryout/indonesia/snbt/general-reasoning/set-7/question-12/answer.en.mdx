Because the ground is level and both shadows are formed at the same time, the sun angle is the same. Each person and their shadow form a right triangle. The two triangles are **similar**. The ratio of height to shadow length is equal. Let <InlineMath math="h_N" /> and <InlineMath math="s_N" /> be Nafis's height and shadow length, and <InlineMath math="h_M" /> and <InlineMath math="s_M" /> be Mirza's height and shadow length. All lengths are in meters.

Given:
- Nafis's height = <InlineMath math="1.5\text{ meters}" />
- Nafis's shadow = <InlineMath math="4.5\text{ meters}" />
- Mirza's shadow = <InlineMath math="6\text{ meters}" />

We set up the proportion equation:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\frac{h_M}{h_N}=\frac{s_M}{s_N}" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{h_M}{1.5} = \frac{6}{4.5}" />
  <BlockMath math="h_M = \frac{6 \times 1.5}{4.5}" />
  <BlockMath math="h_M = \frac{9}{4.5}" />
  <BlockMath math="h_M = 2" />
</MathContainer>

So, Mirza's height is **<InlineMath math="2.00\text{ meters}" />**.
