The project reports three variables: green land cover, land surface temperature, and airborne particle concentration. Flooding is not among the measured outcomes, and the passage supplies no rule from any measured variable to flooding.

That means the proposed causal conclusion is **not supported by the supplied information**. It does not mean the conclusion has been proved false. A dataset that did not measure flooding cannot establish either its occurrence or its absence.

The distinction matters:

- "Unsupported" means the premises do not justify the claim.
- "Definitely false" would require evidence that contradicts the claim.

Therefore, the fourth assessment is correct.
