Pak Ujang's mistake follows this pattern: **every member of group A has feature B**, and then something with feature B is taken to be **certainly** in group A. The shared feature is treated as a sufficient condition, though it is only shown to be something all members have.

The reasoning about Raka has the same pattern: every starting player is taller than <InlineMath math="180" /> cm, so tall Raka is assumed to be a starter. Yet many tall people are not starters.

The other options differ:

- The reasoning about Sinta is valid: a feature of all members is applied to one member.
- The reasoning about the field is valid too, modus tollens.
- The film reasoning generalizes from too small a sample.
- Bu Tini's choice rests on someone's report, not on a feature shared by a group.

So the reasoning with the most similar mistake is the one about Raka and the starting basketball players.
