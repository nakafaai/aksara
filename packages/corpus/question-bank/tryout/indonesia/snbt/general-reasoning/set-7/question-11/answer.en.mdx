Mark each conclusion Benar (true, it must follow) or Salah (false, it need not follow). A conclusion must follow when it holds in **every** situation that fits all three statements.

**Conclusion 1** is true. Every gamelan player can read kepatihan notation, and no one who reads it is under <InlineMath math="10" />. So no gamelan player is under <InlineMath math="10" />.

**Conclusion 2** is true. Some dancers are also gamelan players. By conclusion 1, those dancers are <InlineMath math="10" /> or older.

**Conclusion 3** is false. Only **some** dancers are known to be gamelan players. Other dancers may not read the notation.

**Conclusion 4** is false. The first statement runs one way only. Members who are not gamelan players, such as singers, may also read the notation.

So conclusions 1 and 2 are true, while conclusions 3 and 4 are false.
