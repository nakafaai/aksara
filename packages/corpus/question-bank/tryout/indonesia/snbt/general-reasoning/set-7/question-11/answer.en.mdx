# Given

Three premises link **gamelan players, kepatihan notation readers, and the minimum age of <InlineMath math="10" /> years**.

# Asked

Whether each conclusion is **True** (certainly true) or **False** (not guaranteed).

# Solution

> A conclusion holds with certainty only when every valid group of club members obeying all three rules satisfies it.

Mark each conclusion Benar (true, it must follow) or Salah (false, it need not follow). A conclusion must follow when it holds in **every** situation that fits all three statements.

The diagram below chains the three statements. **Dancers only join the chain through "some"**, so only the dancers who also play gamelan are surely <InlineMath math="10" /> or older.

<Mermaid
  title={<>The chain of statements about Sanggar Laras members</>}
  description={<>Every gamelan player can read kepatihan notation, and everyone who reads it is <InlineMath math="10" /> or older. Some dancers also play gamelan. Someone who reads the notation may not play gamelan.</>}
  chart={`flowchart LR
    G["Gamelan players"] -->|"all"| N["Can read kepatihan notation"]
    N -->|"all"| U["Aged 10 or older"]
    P["Dancers"] -.->|"some"| G`}
/>

**Conclusion 1** is true. Every gamelan player can read kepatihan notation, and no one who reads it is under <InlineMath math="10" />. So no gamelan player is under <InlineMath math="10" />.

**Conclusion 2** is true. Some dancers are also gamelan players. By conclusion 1, those dancers are <InlineMath math="10" /> or older.

**Conclusion 3** is false. Only **some** dancers are known to be gamelan players. Other dancers may not read the notation.

**Conclusion 4** is false. The first statement runs one way only. Members who are not gamelan players, such as singers, may also read the notation.

# Answer

So conclusions 1 and 2 are true, while conclusions 3 and 4 are false.
