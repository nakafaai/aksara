After the first water addition, the total volume is <InlineMath math="60+10=70" /> litres. The concentration at this stage is already given as <InlineMath math="45\%" />, so the proportions of the original two solutions need not be reconstructed.

Because the **mixture is uniform**, removing <InlineMath math="14" /> litres removes the same fraction of both solution volume and solute:

<BlockMath math="\frac{14}{70}=\frac15" />

The fraction of solute remaining is:

<BlockMath math="1-\frac15=\frac45" />

The replacement water adds no solute and restores the volume to <InlineMath math="70" /> litres. The new concentration is therefore:

<BlockMath math="45\%\times\frac45=36\%" />

Checking the solute volume gives the same result:

| Stage | Solute volume (litres) |
| --- | --- |
| Before removal | <InlineMath math="0.45\times70=31.5" /> |
| Removed with the mixture | <InlineMath math="0.45\times14=6.3" /> |
| Remaining | <InlineMath math="31.5-6.3=25.2" /> |

<BlockMath math="25.2/70=0.36=36\%" />
