# Given

A profit of **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}6.6" /> million** is split proportionally to **capital times investment duration**, with Cici investing <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3" /> million for <InlineMath math="12" /> months.

# Asked

**Cici share of the profit**.

# Solution

> Multiply each partner capital by their months invested first so both money and time get their fair weight in the split.

Each partner's weight is **capital times months invested** (millions of rupiah times months):

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Ani} &= 4\times12=48 \\
\text{Budi} &= 6\times8=48 \\
\text{Cici} &= 3\times12=36
\end{aligned}" />

The weights add up to <InlineMath math="132" />. Cici's share:

<BlockMath math="\frac{36}{132}\times6.6=\frac{3}{11}\times6.6=1.8\text{ million}" />

Check: Ani and Budi each get <InlineMath math="2.4" /> million, summing cleanly to <InlineMath math="6.6" /> million.

# Answer

Equal sharing would give <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2.2" /> million each, and sharing by capital alone would ignore that Budi invested for only <InlineMath math="8" /> months. So Cici's share of the profit is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}1.8" /> million.
