Measure every price in thousand rupiah. Let <InlineMath math="H" /> be the initial price. The increase, voucher, and tax give:

<BlockMath math="(1.15H-120)1.10=880" />

Undo the operations in reverse order: remove the tax, restore the voucher deduction, then divide by the increase factor.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="1.15H-120=\frac{880}{1.10}" />
  <BlockMath math="1.15H=800+120=920" />
  <BlockMath math="H=\frac{920}{1.15}=800" />
</MathContainer>

The initial price is <InlineMath math="800" /> thousand rupiah. The ratio of final to initial price is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{880}{800}=\frac{11}{10}" />

Therefore, the ratio in simplest form is **<InlineMath math="11/10" />**.
