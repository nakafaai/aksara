Represent the four statements as follows:

- <InlineMath math="P" />: The scheduled cleaning is skipped.
- <InlineMath math="Q" />: Residue remains on the sensor.
- <InlineMath math="R" />: The sensor develops a fault.
- <InlineMath math="S" />: The warning light turns on.

The protocol gives this implication chain:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="P \rightarrow Q \rightarrow R \rightarrow S" />
  <BlockMath math="P \rightarrow S" />
</MathContainer>

Today the warning light did not turn on, so <InlineMath math="\neg S" /> is true. Applying modus tollens to <InlineMath math="P \rightarrow S" /> gives:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} P &\rightarrow S \\ \neg S &\\ \hline \therefore\ \neg P &\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the scheduled daily cleaning was **not** skipped. This is the second choice.
