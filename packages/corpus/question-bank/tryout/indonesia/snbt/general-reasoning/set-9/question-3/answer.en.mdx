# Given

The passage reports **starting and ending caterpillar counts with traps (<InlineMath math="50" /> to <InlineMath math="20" />) and without traps (<InlineMath math="50" /> to <InlineMath math="30" />)**.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements is **True** or **False** from the passage.

# Solution

> Compute both the raw drop and percentage drop from fifty for each group before scoring the statements.

Mark each statement Benar (true) or Salah (false). Work out the drop in each group: the trap plots fell by <InlineMath math="50-20=30" />, the plots without traps by <InlineMath math="50-30=20" />.

The chart below puts the two groups' drops side by side. **The trap plots' bar is <InlineMath math="1.5" /> times the other bar**, not twice.

**Statement 1** is false. <InlineMath math="30" /> is <InlineMath math="1.5" /> times <InlineMath math="20" />, not twice.

**Statement 2** is true. That share is <InlineMath math="40\%" />.

**Statement 3** is true. <InlineMath math="30" /> is <InlineMath math="10" /> more than <InlineMath math="20" />, and <InlineMath math="50\%" />.

**Statement 4** is false. The passage only says it rained almost every day. Rain **may** have played a part, but the passage does not prove it, since there is no comparison without rain.

# Answer

So statements 2 and 3 are **true, while statements 1 and 4 are false**.
