Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> says that a floodplain **can retain sediment and nutrients** when a river deposits those materials during inundation. That directly matches the third option.

Notice the careful word **can**. The paragraph also says that floodplains are dynamic: material may accumulate through deposition, but erosion may carry it away. This rules out choices built around absolute words such as *always*, *only*, *never*, and *every*.

> The true statement is **floodplains can retain sediment and nutrients**.
