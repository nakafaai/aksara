Choose Mungkin (possible) or Tidak mungkin (impossible) for each situation. Remember two settled facts: Umar is on floor 1 gardening, and the cook lives **directly below** the painter.

**Situation 1**: impossible. The cook must live directly below the painter, so the cook cannot be on floor 4, the top floor.

**Situation 2**: possible. Umar on floor 1 gardening, Tika on floor 2 cooking, Sita on floor 3 painting, and Rani on floor 4 photographing meets every fact.

**Situation 3**: impossible. Sita is at least on floor 2, and Rani must be higher than Sita, so Rani is at least on floor 3.

**Situation 4**: possible. Umar on floor 1 gardening, Sita on floor 2 photographing, Rani on floor 3 cooking, and Tika on floor 4 painting meets every fact.

So situations 2 and 4 are possible, while situations 1 and 3 are impossible.
