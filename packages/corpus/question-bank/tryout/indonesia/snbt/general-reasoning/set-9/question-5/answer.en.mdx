The contradicted statement is:

> *"Standing water never provides breeding sites for mosquitoes."*

The passage says that standing water *can provide* mosquito breeding sites. The claim that standing water *never* provides breeding sites denies a possibility directly stated in the passage.

This does not mean every flood will produce a mosquito-borne disease outbreak. The passage explicitly separates a possible breeding habitat from the many local factors that determine whether transmission actually increases.
