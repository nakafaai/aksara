Paragraph <InlineMath math="2" /> describes a conditional balance. When deposition exceeds erosion, retained sediment and nutrients can support plant growth. When erosion is stronger, some of those materials may be carried away.

The statement **"Every seasonal flood always improves every soil"** removes that condition and turns a possible benefit into a universal guarantee. It is therefore the one statement that is not true. The other four options preserve the passage's distinction between deposition, retention, and erosion.

> The statement that is not true is **every seasonal flood always improves every soil**.
