The mayor's conclusion assumes public pools **cause** fewer drownings. It weakens if there is **another explanation** for the link in the survey.

If cities with many pools mostly have no large rivers, river drownings are few because **rivers are rare** there, not because of the pools. Building pools in Kota Tepian may not lower drownings if the city does have a large river.

The other options do not weaken it:

- Free swimming lessons give a reason why pools could reduce drownings, so they strengthen the conclusion.
- Construction cost does not show whether pools reduce drownings.
- Kota Tepian having fewer pools does not challenge the link in the survey.
- The season of the cases does not explain the differences between cities.

So the statement that weakens the conclusion most is that cities with many public pools mostly have no large rivers.
