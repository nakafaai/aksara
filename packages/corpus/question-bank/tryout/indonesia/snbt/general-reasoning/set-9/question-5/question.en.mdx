Standing water left after a flood can also provide breeding sites for mosquitoes. However, as [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/mosquitoes/response/index.html) explains, a flood does not automatically cause a mosquito-borne disease outbreak. Transmission risk is affected by the mosquito species present, locally circulating viruses, rainfall, and mosquito control.
