# Given

All floor and hobby rules apply with **Umar fixed on floor 1** and the extra condition **Sita loves photography**.

# Asked

The statement that is **certainly true** when Sita does photography.

# Solution

> Because the cook and painter must share two adjacent floors, testing their valid spots locks in where Sita must live.

Umar is certainly on floor 1, gardening. If Sita photographs, cooking and painting belong to Rani and Tika. By fact 1, the cook lives **directly below** the painter, so Rani and Tika are on **adjacent** floors.

The floors left for Rani, Sita, and Tika are <InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, and <InlineMath math="4" />. If Rani and Tika are adjacent, they are on floors 2 and 3 or on floors 3 and 4.

- If Rani and Tika are on floors 2 and 3, Sita is on floor 4. Rani is then lower than Sita, which breaks fact 2.
- So Rani and Tika are on floors 3 and 4, and Sita is on floor 2.

Two arrangements work: Rani on floor 3 cooking with Tika on floor 4 painting, or Tika on floor 3 cooking with Rani on floor 4 painting. In both, Sita lives on floor 2.

# Answer

**The other options hold in only one arrangement, so they are not certain. So what must be true is that Sita lives on floor 2**.
