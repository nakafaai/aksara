# Given

An alarm rings on a **fixed pattern at 03:00, 04:10, 05:30, and 07:00**.

# Asked

The **next ring time** after 07:00.

# Solution

> Measure the minutes between consecutive rings to see the gap growing by ten minutes at every single step.

Work out **the gaps** between consecutive alarms:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
03.00\to04.10 &= 70\text{ min} \\
04.10\to05.30 &= 80\text{ min} \\
05.30\to07.00 &= 90\text{ min}
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below draws the gaps between the alarms. **Each bar is ten minutes taller than the one before**, so the next gap is <InlineMath math="100" /> minutes.

  title={<>Gaps between consecutive alarms</>}

/>

The gap grows by <InlineMath math="10" /> minutes each time, so the next gap is <InlineMath math="100" /> minutes, <InlineMath math="1" /> hour <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes. From 07.00, the alarm rings again at 08.40.

08.30 comes from keeping the gap at <InlineMath math="90" /> minutes, and the other options do not follow the pattern of gaps.

# Answer

So **the alarm rings next at 08.40**.
