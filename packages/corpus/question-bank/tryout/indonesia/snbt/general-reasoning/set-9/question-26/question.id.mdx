| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| B | <InlineMath math="750" /> | <InlineMath math="30" /> | <InlineMath math="12" /> | <InlineMath math="5" /> |

<BlockMath math="I=\frac{q-d}{te}" />
