All prices below are in thousand rupiah. Reverse the tax by dividing the final price by <InlineMath math="111/100" />:

<BlockMath math="1{,}110\div\frac{111}{100}=1{,}000" />

Add the voucher back to recover the marked-up price:

<BlockMath math="1{,}000+200=1{,}200" />

A <InlineMath math="20\%" /> markup multiplies the initial price by <InlineMath math="6/5" />. The initial price is therefore:

<BlockMath math="1{,}200\div\frac65=1{,}000" />

The final price minus the initial price is:

<BlockMath math="1{,}110-1{,}000=110" />

Therefore, the net increase is **<InlineMath math="110" /> thousand rupiah**.
