# Given

Bus A leaves at **07:00 at <InlineMath math="40" /> km/h** and Bus B leaves from the opposite city at **07:30 at <InlineMath math="60" /> km/h**.

# Asked

The **time when the two buses meet**.

# Solution

> Subtract the head start Bus A covers in the first half hour first, then divide the remaining gap by their combined speed.

Bus A leaves **half an hour earlier**, so first find how far A has gone before B leaves.

<BlockMath math="40\times0.5=20\text{ km},\qquad\text{remaining distance }200-20=180\text{ km}" />

From 07.30 the buses approach each other, so the gap shrinks by <InlineMath math="40+60=100" /> km every hour.

<BlockMath math="\frac{180}{100}=1.8\text{ h}=1\text{ h }48\text{ min}" />

From 07.30, they meet at 09.18. Check: by then A has covered <InlineMath math="40\times2.3=92" /> km and B <InlineMath math="60\times1.8=108" /> km, and <InlineMath math="92+108=200" />.

# Answer

**09.00 comes from treating both buses as leaving together at 07.00. So the buses meet at 09.18**.
