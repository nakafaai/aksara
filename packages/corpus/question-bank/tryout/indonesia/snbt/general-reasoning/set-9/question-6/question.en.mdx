Floodplains can retain floodwater, sediment, and nutrients such as nitrogen and phosphorus. During inundation, rivers may deposit these materials on the floodplain. Floodplains are dynamic, however: they can both accumulate material through deposition and export it through erosion.

Where deposition exceeds erosion, retained sediment and nutrients can support plant growth and other ecosystem services. Where erosion is stronger, some sediment and nutrients may be carried away. Seasonal inundation can therefore benefit an ecosystem, but it does not make every flood beneficial to every soil.
