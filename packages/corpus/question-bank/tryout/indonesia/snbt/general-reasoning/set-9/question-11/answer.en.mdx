# Given

Contest rules split entrants into **categories with distinct Friday performance rules**, and **Lina did not perform on Friday**.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements about Lina is **True** (certainly true) or **False**.

# Solution

> Start from Lina skipping Friday and use contrapositives to cross out every category that requires a Friday performance.

Mark each statement Benar (true, it must follow from the facts) or Salah (false, it need not follow). Start from the facts about Lina, then use **contraposition** and **exactly one category**.

- Every poetry entrant performs on Friday. Lina does not perform on Friday, so Lina is **not** a poetry entrant.
- Every entrant is in exactly one category, poetry or short story. Since she is not in poetry, Lina must be a **short story** entrant.

**Statement 1** is true, by the reasoning above.

**Statement 2** is false. The facts say Lina does not perform on Friday.

**Statement 3** is false. The second fact runs one way only, and the fourth fact says short story entrants may perform on Friday. So a Friday performer need not be a poetry entrant.

**Statement 4** is true. Lina is a short story entrant, and every short story entrant submits before Thursday.

# Answer

So statements 1 and 4 are true, while statements 2 and 3 are false.
