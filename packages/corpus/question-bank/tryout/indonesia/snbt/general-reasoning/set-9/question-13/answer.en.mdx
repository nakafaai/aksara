Let <InlineMath math="n_M" />, <InlineMath math="n_O" />, and <InlineMath math="n_P" /> count students in only reading, only sports, and only coding.

Subtract both pairwise intersections from each club total. Students in all three were subtracted twice, so add them back once:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}n_M&=42-15-12+5\\&=20\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}n_O&=35-15-10+5\\&=15\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}n_P&=28-12-10+5\\&=11\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

These three groups are disjoint. The number of students in exactly one club is:

<BlockMath math="20+15+11=46" />
