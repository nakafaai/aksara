# Given

An item costs **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}600{,}000" />** with a **<InlineMath math="25\%" /> down payment** and interest-free monthly installments of **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}225{,}000" />**.

# Asked

How many **months** it takes to pay off the balance.

# Solution

> Subtract the twenty-five percent down payment first to find the remaining balance, then divide by the monthly payment.

First find **the remaining price** after the down payment, then divide by the installment.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{down payment} &= 25\%\times3{,}600{,}000=900{,}000 \\
\text{remaining} &= 3{,}600{,}000-900{,}000=2{,}700{,}000 \\
\text{months} &= \frac{2{,}700{,}000}{225{,}000}=12
\end{aligned}" />

# Answer

The division comes out whole, so the last installment is also exactly **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}225{,}000" />**. So Pak Tono pays in installments for **<InlineMath math="12" />** months.
