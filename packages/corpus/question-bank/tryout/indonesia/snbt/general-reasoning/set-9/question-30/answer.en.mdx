First find **the remaining price** after the down payment, then divide by the installment.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{down payment} &= 25\%\times3{,}600{,}000=900{,}000 \\
\text{remaining} &= 3{,}600{,}000-900{,}000=2{,}700{,}000 \\
\text{months} &= \frac{2{,}700{,}000}{225{,}000}=12
\end{aligned}" />

The division comes out whole, so the last installment is also exactly <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}225{,}000" />. So Pak Tono pays in installments for <InlineMath math="12" /> months.
