A gap between two groups can be linked to the traps only if the groups are **alike in everything except the traps**. If the variety, care, and choice of plots are the same or random, other explanations for the gap fall away, so the trap explanation gets stronger.

The other options do not strengthen it:

- The trap price does not show whether the traps work.
- Both groups had the rain, so it does not explain the gap between them, and the passage already says so.
- Caterpillars attacking chili plants have nothing to do with the onion plots in this test.
- Farmers in other villages have nothing to do with the result.

So the statement that strengthens the claim most is that both groups had the same variety, the same care, and plots chosen at random.
