After adding water, the solution volume is:

<BlockMath math="50+5=55\text{ L}" />

This solution has a concentration of <InlineMath math="50\%" />. Its solute volume is:

<BlockMath math="55\times\frac12=27.5\text{ L}" />

A homogeneous mixture has the same concentration throughout. Removing <InlineMath math="11" /> litres removes this much solute:

<BlockMath math="11\times\frac12=5.5\text{ L}" />

The replacement solution adds this much solute:

<BlockMath math="11\times\frac34=8.25\text{ L}" />

The final volume remains <InlineMath math="55" /> litres because equal volumes leave and enter. The new solute volume and concentration are:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}27.5-5.5+8.25&=22+8.25\\&=30.25\text{ L}\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{30.25}{55}\times100\%=55\%" />
</MathContainer>
