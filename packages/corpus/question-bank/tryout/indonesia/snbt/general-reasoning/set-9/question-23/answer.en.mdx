First count users of A or B. Users of both were counted twice, so subtract the intersection of A and B once:

<BlockMath math="80+70-29=121" />

Within that group, C users belong to the intersection of A with C or B with C. Users of all three belong to both intersections, so subtract them once:

<BlockMath math="24+20-10=34" />

Remove the C users from the users of A or B:

<BlockMath math="121-34=87" />
