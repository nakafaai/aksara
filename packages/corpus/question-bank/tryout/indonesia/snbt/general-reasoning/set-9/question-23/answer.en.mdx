# Given

The table reports **ticket prices and two-day sales** for VIP, Festival, and Tribun categories.

# Asked

The ranking of ticket categories from **highest to lowest two-day revenue**.

# Solution

> Add both days tickets inside each category first, then multiply by the ticket price to rank true revenues.

Each category's revenue is **tickets sold over both days times the price**. In millions of rupiah:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{VIP} &= (120+150)\times0.5=270\times0.5=135 \\
\text{Festival} &= (400+380)\times0.25=780\times0.25=195 \\
\text{Tribun} &= (600+720)\times0.15=1{,}320\times0.15=198
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below draws the three categories' revenue. **Tribun's bar is just above Festival's, and VIP's is the shortest**, even though VIP costs the most.

The order is Tribun <InlineMath math="(198)" />, Festival <InlineMath math="(195)" />, VIP <InlineMath math="(135)" />. VIP tickets cost the most, but so few were sold that VIP brought in the least.

# Answer

So the order is **Tribun, Festival, VIP**.
