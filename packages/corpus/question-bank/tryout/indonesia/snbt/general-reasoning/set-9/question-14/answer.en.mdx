Among the available choices, **D, A, C, E, B** satisfies all three conditions. C immediately follows A, D precedes B, and E is fourth.

The other choices fail because:

- A, C, B, D, E places D after B and E last.
- D, C, A, E, B places C before A.
- E, D, A, C, B places E first.
- A, D, C, B, E separates A and C and places E last.
