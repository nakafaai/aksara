# Given

Housing and hobby clues place **four residents across floors <InlineMath math="1" /> through <InlineMath math="4" />** under relative floor rules.

# Asked

The statement that is **certainly true** under all clues.

# Solution

> Cross out everyone who cannot live on the bottom floor until only one resident is left to anchor floor one.

Find who **can** live on floor 1.

- Tika does not live on floor 1 (fact 3).
- Sita does not live on floor 1 (fact 5).
- Rani lives higher than Sita, so Rani cannot be on floor 1 either (fact 2).

The diagram below sums up why each candidate for floor 1 drops out. **Only Umar is left**.

Only Umar is left, so Umar **certainly** lives on floor 1.

The other options are not certain. Umar on floor 1 gardening, Sita on floor 2 photographing, Rani on floor 3 cooking, and Tika on floor 4 painting meets every fact, so Rani is neither certainly a photographer nor certainly on floor 4. Umar on floor 1, Tika on floor 2 cooking, Sita on floor 3 painting, and Rani on floor 4 photographing also works, so Sita is not certainly on floor 2 and Tika is not certainly a painter.

# Answer

So what must be true is that Umar lives on floor 1.
