Every tomato in the stall is picked from plants in Pak Joko's garden, and no plant in that garden is sprayed with chemical pesticide. So some goods in the stall come from plants that are **not** sprayed with chemical pesticide.

- Goods other than tomatoes may come from elsewhere.
- Goods other than tomatoes may have been sprayed, since the statements only cover the tomatoes.
- The garden may also grow plants other than tomatoes.
- Every tomato in the stall comes from the unsprayed garden, so this option contradicts the statements.

So the conclusion that must be true is that some goods in Pak Joko's stall come from plants not sprayed with chemical pesticide.
