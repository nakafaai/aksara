# Given

Three premises state **no plant in Mr. Joko garden gets chemical pesticides, all tomatoes at his stall come from his garden, and some stall items are tomatoes**.

# Asked

The conclusion that is **certainly true** from those three statements.

# Solution

> Follow the stall items that are tomatoes back to the garden and straight into the zero-chemical-pesticide guarantee.

Start from the statement with **some** (*sebagian*). Some goods in the stall are tomatoes. Every tomato in the stall is picked from plants in Pak Joko's garden, and no plant in that garden is sprayed with chemical pesticide. So some goods in the stall come from plants that are **not** sprayed with chemical pesticide.

The other options are uncertain or false:

- Goods other than tomatoes may come from elsewhere.
- Goods other than tomatoes may have been sprayed, since the statements only cover the tomatoes.
- The garden may also grow plants other than tomatoes.
- Every tomato in the stall comes from the unsprayed garden, so this option contradicts the statements.

# Answer

So the conclusion that must be true is that **some goods in Pak Joko's stall come from plants not sprayed with chemical pesticide**.
