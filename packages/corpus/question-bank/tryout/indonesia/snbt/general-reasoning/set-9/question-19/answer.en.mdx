Use the **sum of all measurements**, rather than just the change in their mean. Eight measurements with mean <InlineMath math="72" /> have a total of:

<BlockMath math="8\times 72=576" />

Removing <InlineMath math="88" /> leaves the sum of seven measurements:

<BlockMath math="576-88=488" />

The replacement restores the count to eight. For a mean of <InlineMath math="75" />, the new total must be:

<BlockMath math="8\times 75=600" />

The value <InlineMath math="x" /> raises the total from <InlineMath math="488" /> to <InlineMath math="600" />:

<BlockMath math="x=600-488=112" />

You can check by replacing <InlineMath math="88" /> with <InlineMath math="112" />:

<BlockMath math="\frac{576-88+112}{8}=\frac{600}{8}=75" />
