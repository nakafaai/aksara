# Given

Day-three revenue must hit at least **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}300" /> million**, with **day-three VIP and Festival sales matching day two**.

# Asked

The **minimum number of Tribun tickets** that must be sold on day three.

# Solution

> Subtract the locked-in VIP and Festival revenue from three hundred million, divide by one Tribun ticket price, and round up to a whole ticket.

Work out the revenue **already fixed** from VIP and Festival, in millions of rupiah:

<BlockMath math="150\times0.5+380\times0.25=75+95=170" />

The rest of the target must come from Tribun tickets: <InlineMath math="300-170=130" /> million. If <InlineMath math="n" /> Tribun tickets are sold:

<BlockMath math="0.15\,n\ge130\quad\Rightarrow\quad n\ge866.67" />

Tickets are whole numbers, so **round up** to <InlineMath math="867" />. Check: <InlineMath math="866" /> tickets bring <InlineMath math="129.9" /> million, still short, while <InlineMath math="867" /> bring <InlineMath math="130.05" /> million.

# Answer

So at least <InlineMath math="867" /> Tribun tickets must be sold.
