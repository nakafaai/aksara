B initially has <InlineMath math="10" /> members and has <InlineMath math="11" /> after the move. Its score totals before and after the move are:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="10\times68=680" />
  <BlockMath math="11\times70=770" />
</MathContainer>

The difference is the moving member’s score:

<BlockMath math="770-680=90" />

<BlockMath math="11\times80=880" />

<BlockMath math="880-90=790" />
