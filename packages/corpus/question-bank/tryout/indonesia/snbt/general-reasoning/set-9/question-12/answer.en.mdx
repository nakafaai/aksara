This arrangement is called a **constructive dilemma**. There are two possible meeting times, each with its own consequence:

- If the meeting is in the morning, Andi attends.
- If the meeting is at midday, Budi attends.

Since the meeting is certainly held at one of these times, at least one consequence happens. The valid conclusion is **Andi attends or Budi attends**.

The other options are not valid:

- Both attending is not guaranteed, since the meeting may be only in the morning or only at midday.
- Andi attending is certain only if the meeting is in the morning, and the time is unknown.
- Budi attending is certain only if the meeting is at midday.
- Neither attending contradicts the conclusion that one of them must attend.

So the valid conclusion is that Andi or Budi attends the meeting.
