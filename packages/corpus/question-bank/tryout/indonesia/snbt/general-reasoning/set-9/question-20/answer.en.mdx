C and D always come together, with C the day before D. Since D is not on Monday, the pair can take Monday and Tuesday, Tuesday and Wednesday, or Wednesday and Thursday. In each case, A and B fill the other two days with A first.

- C and D on Monday and Tuesday, then A on Wednesday and B on Thursday.
- C and D on Tuesday and Wednesday, then A on Monday and B on Thursday.
- C and D on Wednesday and Thursday, then A on Monday and B on Tuesday.

If A is not on Monday, only the first arrangement works. In it, Tuesday has **activity D**.

So the activity held on Tuesday is activity D.
