The relationship is **instrument → what it indicates or measures**. A compass indicates direction, while a hygrometer indicates humidity. Keep the instrument and the observed property in that order.

| Other pair | Why it does not fit |
| :--- | :--- |
| Temperature : thermometer | Puts the quantity before the instrument |
| Distance : odometer | Puts the quantity before the instrument |
| Scale : speed | A scale does not measure speed |
| Microscope : sound intensity | A microscope does not measure sound intensity |

The matching pair is therefore **hygrometer : humidity**.
