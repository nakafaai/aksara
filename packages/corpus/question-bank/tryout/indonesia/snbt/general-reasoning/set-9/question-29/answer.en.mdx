The two balls are selected without regard to order. The number of equally likely pairs is:

<BlockMath math="\binom{12}{2}=\frac{12\times11}{2}=66" />

| Pair | Count | Prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Red and red | <InlineMath math="\binom62=15" /> | <InlineMath math="40" /> |
| Red and blue | <InlineMath math="6\times6=36" /> | <InlineMath math="20" /> |
| Blue and blue | <InlineMath math="\binom62=15" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

All prizes in the table are in thousand rupiah. Let <InlineMath math="X" /> be the payout from one draw. Its expected value is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\mathrm{E}[X]&=\frac{40\times15+20\times36}{66}\\&=\frac{600+720}{66}\\&=20\end{aligned}" />

A retry is awarded only after two blue balls. Its probability is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{15}{66}=\frac5{22}" />

Restoring the box makes the retry’s expected payout <InlineMath math="20" /> again. Add the first draw’s expected payout to the retry’s expected payout weighted by the probability of receiving it:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}20+\frac5{22}\times20&=20+\frac{50}{11}\\&=\frac{270}{11}\end{aligned}" />

The entry fee is paid once. The expected net gain is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{270}{11}-20=\frac{50}{11}" />

Therefore, the expected net gain is **<InlineMath math="50/11" /> thousand rupiah**.
