# Given

Five people sit in a row with **Ani and Budi required to sit side by side** and **Citra forbidden from either end seat**.

# Asked

The **number of valid seating arrangements**.

# Solution

> Tie Ani and Budi together as one pair first to count all adjacent seatings, then subtract the ones where Citra lands on an end.

First count all arrangements with Ani and Budi together, then **subtract** those that put Citra at an end.

Treat Ani and Budi as one block. The block with Citra, Dedi, and Eka makes <InlineMath math="4" /> units, which can be ordered in <InlineMath math="4!=24" /> ways. Inside the block, Ani and Budi can swap in <InlineMath math="2" /> ways.

<BlockMath math="24\times2=48" />

Arrangements with Citra in the leftmost seat: the other three units can be ordered in <InlineMath math="3!=6" /> ways, times <InlineMath math="2" /> for the block, that is <InlineMath math="12" />. With Citra in the rightmost seat there are also <InlineMath math="12" />.

<BlockMath math="48-12-12=24" />

So there are <InlineMath math="24" /> possible seating arrangements.
