Of the 66 pairs, 15 are red-red, 36 are mixed, and 15 are blue-blue. One draw has expected value <InlineMath math="[40(15)+20(36)]/66=20" />. A retry occurs with probability <InlineMath math="15/66=5/22" />, giving expected payout <InlineMath math="20+(5/22)(20)=270/11" />. After the entry fee, expected net profit is <InlineMath math="270/11-20=50/11" />.

Therefore, the requested value is <InlineMath math="\\frac{50}{11}" />.
