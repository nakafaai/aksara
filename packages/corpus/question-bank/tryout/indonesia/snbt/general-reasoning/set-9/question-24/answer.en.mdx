The combined rate in tankfuls per hour is:

<BlockMath math="\frac1{18}+\frac1{12}=\frac5{36}" />

During the first <InlineMath math="4" /> hours, the filled fraction is:

<BlockMath math="4\times\frac5{36}=\frac59" />

The unfilled fraction is:

<BlockMath math="1-\frac59=\frac49" />

After B stops, subtract the leak rate from A’s rate:

<BlockMath math="\frac1{18}-\frac1{36}=\frac1{36}" />

The additional time equals the remaining fraction divided by the net rate:

<BlockMath math="\frac49\div\frac1{36}=16" />

Therefore, the additional time is **<InlineMath math="16" /> hours**.
