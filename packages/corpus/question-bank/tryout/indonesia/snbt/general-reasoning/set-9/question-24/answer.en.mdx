# Given

The ticket table provides **day-one and day-two sales** along with **prices per category**.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements is **True** or **False** according to the table.

# Solution

> Compute day-one and day-two total revenues separately first so every comparison can be checked directly.

Mark each statement Benar (true) or Salah (false). Work out each day's revenue in millions of rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{day 1} &= 120\times0.5+400\times0.25+600\times0.15=60+100+90=250 \\
\text{day 2} &= 150\times0.5+380\times0.25+720\times0.15=75+95+108=278
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below splits both days' revenue by category. **Only Festival drops on day <InlineMath math="2" />**, while VIP and Tribun rise.

  title={<>Revenue of each category on both days in millions of rupiah</>}

On day <InlineMath math="2" />, they brought in <InlineMath math="75" />, <InlineMath math="95" />, and <InlineMath math="108" /> million, a total of <InlineMath math="278" />.</>}

**Statement 1** is true. Day <InlineMath math="1" /> sold <InlineMath math="1{,}120" /> tickets, day <InlineMath math="2" /> sold <InlineMath math="1{,}250" />.

**Statement 2** is true. VIP revenue rose from <InlineMath math="60" /> to <InlineMath math="75" /> million, and that is a <InlineMath math="25\%" /> jump.

**Statement 3** is false. Day <InlineMath math="1" /> revenue was <InlineMath math="250" /> million, not more than <InlineMath math="260" /> million.

**Statement 4** is false. Total revenue was <InlineMath math="528" /> million, and the Tribun share is <InlineMath math="37.5\%" />, less than <InlineMath math="40\%" />.

# Answer

So statements 1 and 2 are true, while statements 3 and 4 are false.
