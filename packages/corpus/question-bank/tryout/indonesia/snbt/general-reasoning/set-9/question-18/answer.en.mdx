# Given

Three parcels have **actual weight, box dimensions, zone A or B rate**, and a rule **billing the larger of actual or volumetric weight rounded up**.

# Asked

The **total shipping cost** for all three parcels.

# Solution

> Compare scale weight against volumetric weight for each box first, then round up to whole kilograms before applying the zone rate.

For each parcel, work out **the volume weight**, take the larger weight, round up, and multiply by the zone rate.

- **Parcel 1**: volume weight <InlineMath math="1" /> kg. The larger is the actual <InlineMath math="2.3" /> kg, rounded up to <InlineMath math="3" /> kg. The cost is <InlineMath math="3\times8{,}000=24{,}000" />.
- **Parcel 2**: volume weight <InlineMath math="5" /> kg, larger than <InlineMath math="1.2" /> kg. The cost is <InlineMath math="5\times12{,}000=60{,}000" />.
- **Parcel 3**: volume weight <InlineMath math="1" /> kg. The actual <InlineMath math="4" /> kg is larger. The cost is <InlineMath math="4\times8{,}000=32{,}000" />.

# Answer

**<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}80{,}000" />** comes from ignoring parcel 2's volume weight, and **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}110{,}400" />** from not rounding parcel 1 up. So the three parcels cost **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}116{,}000" />** in total.
