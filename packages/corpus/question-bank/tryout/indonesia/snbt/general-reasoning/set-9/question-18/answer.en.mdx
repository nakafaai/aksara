The two school records are mutually exclusive: a student is either in the completed-school category or in the withdrawal category, never both. The admission rule also says that anyone who enters University <InlineMath math="P" /> in this intake must come from the completed-school category.

It follows that a university entrant cannot belong to the withdrawal category. The first option states the opposite, so it directly contradicts both rules. The other options restate consequences that the passage guarantees.

> The definitely false statement is **students entering University <InlineMath math="P" /> belong to the withdrawal category**.
