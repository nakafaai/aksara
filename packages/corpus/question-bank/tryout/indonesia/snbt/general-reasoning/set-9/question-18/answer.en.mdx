Let <InlineMath math="A" /> mean the audit is complete, <InlineMath math="P" /> the report is approved, <InlineMath math="Q" /> quota is available, and <InlineMath math="R" /> the report is published. The rules and facts are:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="A\Rightarrow P" />
  <BlockMath math="(P\land Q)\Rightarrow R" />
  <BlockMath math="Q\land\neg R" />
</MathContainer>

Since **quota is available**, approval would lead to publication. Yet the report is not published, so it is **not approved**. A completed audit would require that approval under the first rule.

The contrapositive of the first rule gives:

<BlockMath math="\neg P\Rightarrow\neg A" />

Therefore, the audit is not complete. This does not imply cancellation, which the question does not mention.
