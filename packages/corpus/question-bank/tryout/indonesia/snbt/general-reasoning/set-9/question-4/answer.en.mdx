The true statement is:

> *"Warming has shifted the distributions of many marine species toward the poles."*

This is the population-level pattern described in paragraph <InlineMath math="3" />. The wording matters: *many* species is not the same as *every* tropical fish population.

A single fish moving in one direction also cannot prove that the ocean is warming. Evidence for climate-driven distribution shifts comes from repeated observations across populations, locations, and time. The passage likewise says that fisheries impacts differ by region, with larger projected losses in tropical and subtropical waters.
