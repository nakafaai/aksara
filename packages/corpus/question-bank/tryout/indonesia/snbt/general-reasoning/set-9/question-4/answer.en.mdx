This sequence uses the same recurrence as the Fibonacci sequence: from the third term onward, each term is the **sum of the previous two**. Checking the known terms gives:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 3 + 6 &amp;= 9 \\ 6 + 9 &amp;= 15 \\ 9 + 15 &amp;= 24 \\ 15 + 24 &amp;= 39 \end{aligned}" />

To find the next term after <InlineMath math="39" />, we add the last two known terms:

<BlockMath math="24 + 39 = 63" />

The two terms after the blank verify the result:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 39 + 63 &amp;= 102 \\ 63 + 102 &amp;= 165 \end{aligned}" />

Both results match the sequence, so the missing number is <InlineMath math="63" />.
