Choose the report from A and B that occupies an end, then choose the left or right end. This gives <InlineMath math="2\cdot2=4" /> choices. The other report from A and B must occupy one of four non-end positions, and the remaining four reports have <InlineMath math="4!" /> arrangements. Before excluding adjacent C-D, the count is <InlineMath math="4\cdot4\cdot4!=384" />.

For each report-and-end choice, treating C-D as one block with two internal orders gives <InlineMath math="2\cdot4!=48" /> arrangements in the remaining five positions. Of these, <InlineMath math="2\cdot3!=12" /> put the other report from A and B at the opposite end and are already invalid. Thus the number with adjacent C-D is <InlineMath math="4(48-12)=144" />.

There are <InlineMath math="384-144=240" /> arrangements in which C and D are not adjacent. Swapping C and D shows that half have C before D, giving <InlineMath math="240/2=120" /> valid arrangements.

Therefore, the requested value is <InlineMath math="\\frac16" />.
