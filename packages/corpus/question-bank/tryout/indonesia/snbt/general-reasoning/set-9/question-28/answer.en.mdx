Choose which of A and B occupies an end, then choose the left or right end:

<BlockMath math="2\times2=4" />

The other report from A and B fills one of four non-end positions. The remaining four reports can be arranged freely. Before checking C and D, the count is:

<BlockMath math="4\times4\times4!=384" />

For each report-and-end choice, combine C and D into one block with two internal orders. This block and three other reports form four objects occupying the remaining five positions:

<BlockMath math="2\times4!=48" />

Some of these block arrangements place the other report from A and B at the opposite end. They already violate the first condition. Fixing that report at the opposite end leaves three objects to arrange:

<BlockMath math="2\times3!=12" />

Subtract the already invalid arrangements, then multiply by the four report-and-end choices. The number with adjacent C and D that must be excluded is:

<BlockMath math="4(48-12)=144" />

Once C and D are nonadjacent, swapping them preserves every other condition. Exactly half of the remaining arrangements place C before D:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="384-144=240" />
  <BlockMath math="240\div2=120" />
</MathContainer>

There are <InlineMath math="6!=720" /> equally likely arrangements, so:

<BlockMath math="\frac{120}{720}=\frac16" />
