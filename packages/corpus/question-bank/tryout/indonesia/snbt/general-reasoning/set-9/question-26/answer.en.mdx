Let Dina's score be <InlineMath math="d" /> and Eko's <InlineMath math="e" />. There are **two unknowns**, so two equations are usually needed.

**Statement (1) alone**: not sufficient. <InlineMath math="d+e=160" /> fits many pairs, such as <InlineMath math="d=80, e=80" /> or <InlineMath math="d=90, e=70" />.

**Statement (2) alone**: not sufficient. <InlineMath math="e=d-6" /> also fits many pairs, such as <InlineMath math="d=70, e=64" /> or <InlineMath math="d=86, e=80" />.

**Both statements together**:

<BlockMath math="d+(d-6)=160\quad\Rightarrow\quad 2d=166\quad\Rightarrow\quad d=83" />

The chart below draws the Eko score that fits each statement for several Dina scores. **The two lines meet only at <InlineMath math="d=83" />**.
