B’s hourly energy use rises by <InlineMath math="20\%" /> from <InlineMath math="5" /> to:

<BlockMath math="5\times\frac{120}{100}=6" />

For each index, subtract defective products from output and divide by total energy use:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}I_A&=\frac{690-30}{12\times5}\\&=\frac{660}{60}=11\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}I_B&=\frac{750-30}{12\times6}\\&=\frac{720}{72}=10\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}I_C&=\frac{840-42}{14\times5}\\&=\frac{798}{70}=11.4\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, **machine C** has the highest index. Higher energy use lowers B’s index because its net output stays fixed.
