The two statements give the order <InlineMath math="N>M>K" /> with O lowest. The only unknown is **where L goes**, which can be anywhere above O: above N, between N and M, between M and K, or between K and O.

"L is lower than K" leaves L only one place, between K and O. The order becomes <InlineMath math="N>M>K>L>O" />, and it is certain.

The other options are not enough:

- L being taller than O is already known, since O is lowest.
- L below N still leaves three places: between N and M, between M and K, or between K and O.
- L below M still leaves two places: between M and K or between K and O.
- N being tallest still leaves L in one of three places below N.

So the information that fixes the order is that tree L is shorter than tree K.
