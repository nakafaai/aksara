Let the cooking times for lamb, beef, duck, rabbit, and chicken be <InlineMath math="t_L" />, <InlineMath math="t_B" />, <InlineMath math="t_D" />, <InlineMath math="t_R" />, and <InlineMath math="t_C" />, respectively. Each sentence gives one comparison:

| Cooking-time comparison | Notation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Beef takes longer than lamb | <InlineMath math="t_B>t_L" /> |
| Beef takes no longer than duck | <InlineMath math="t_B\le t_D" /> |
| Rabbit takes less time than chicken | <InlineMath math="t_R<t_C" /> |
| Rabbit takes longer than duck | <InlineMath math="t_R>t_D" /> |

Combine these comparisons from the shortest time to the longest:

<BlockMath math="t_L<t_B\le t_D<t_R<t_C" />

Lamb has the smallest cooking time, even if beef and duck take equally long. Therefore, **lamb** cooks fastest.
