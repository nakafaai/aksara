All <InlineMath math="8! = 40{,}320" /> arrangements are equally likely. Count those satisfying the conditions, then divide by this total.

#### Grouping adjacent reports

Treat C and D as one block. Its internal order can be CD or DC, giving <InlineMath math="2" /> possibilities. Together with the other six reports, there are seven objects to arrange:

<BlockMath math="N_0=2\times7!=10{,}080" />

#### Removing forbidden arrangements

Let <InlineMath math="N_A" /> count arrangements in which A and B are adjacent. Treat them as a second block. Both blocks have two internal orders, and there are now six objects:

<BlockMath math="N_A=2\times2\times6!=2{,}880" />

Let <InlineMath math="N_G" /> count arrangements with G at an end. Choose one of the two ends, choose the internal order of CD, and arrange the remaining six objects:

<BlockMath math="N_G=2\times2\times6!=2{,}880" />

Arrangements violating both conditions belong to both counts. For their intersection, choose an end for G, choose the internal orders of both blocks, and arrange the remaining five objects:

<BlockMath math="N_{AG}=2\times2\times2\times5!=960" />

Subtract both forbidden groups and add their intersection back once:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}N&=N_0-N_A-N_G+N_{AG}\\&=10{,}080-2{,}880\\&\quad-2{,}880+960\\&=5{,}280\end{aligned}" />

#### Applying the order condition

Swapping E and F preserves the adjacency conditions for A and B and for C and D, as well as G's position. Every arrangement with E before F therefore pairs with exactly one arrangement with F before E. Half satisfy the remaining condition:

<BlockMath math="\frac{5{,}280}{2}=2{,}640" />

The probability that all conditions hold is:

<BlockMath math="P=\frac{2{,}640}{40{,}320}=\frac{11}{168}" />
