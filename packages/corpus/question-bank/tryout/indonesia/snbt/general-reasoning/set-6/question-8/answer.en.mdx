Only the **four middle digits** can vary. The prefix and suffix are fixed, so they do not add any choices.

| Part of the number | Content | Number of choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First four digits | 0844 | 1 |
| First middle digit | One of 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 | 7 |
| Second middle digit | One of the same seven digits | 7 |
| Third middle digit | One of the same seven digits | 7 |
| Fourth middle digit | One of the same seven digits | 7 |
| Last four digits | 1221 | 1 |

Because **digits may repeat**, filling one position does not reduce the choices for the next. For example, both 2222 and 2345 are allowed in the middle.

By the multiplication rule, the number of different card numbers is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}N&amp;=1\times7\times7\times7\times7\times1\\&amp;=49\times49\\&amp;=2{,}401\end{aligned}" />

There are <InlineMath math="2{,}401" /> different card numbers. Digit order matters, so the middle sequences 2345 and 5432 produce two different numbers.
