"Only cooperative members may borrow harvest tools" (*Hanya ... yang boleh*) means **every borrower of harvest tools is a cooperative member**. Pak Harun borrowed harvest tools, so he must be a cooperative member.

The other options are not certain:

- The statements do not say where Pak Harun lives, so he may or may not be a farmer in Desa Mekar.
- For the same reason, saying he is not a farmer in Desa Mekar is not certain either.
- "Only members may borrow" does not mean every member borrows.
- Nothing says that any Desa Mekar farmer borrowed harvest tools.

So the conclusion that must be true is that Pak Harun is a cooperative member.
