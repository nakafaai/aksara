The key is to separate an event the report states as fact from plans, warnings, and hypothetical consequences.

- **Recorded fact:** independent farmers suffered losses after farm-gate prices fell.
- **Plan under consideration:** the government may relax imports. The imports have not necessarily occurred.
- **Warning:** imported supply could reduce demand for local chickens. The word "could" marks this as a possible future effect.
- **Conditional scenario:** export restrictions and a worsening trade balance depend on an explicit "if".

The report never mentions a trade ruling, and it does not say that retaliation alone determines the trade balance.

Therefore, the only statement guaranteed by the report is that **independent chicken farmers suffered losses**.
