Let <InlineMath math="P" /> mean "PT Batik opens the new branch this year" and <InlineMath math="Q" /> mean "PT Batik sells the new product this year."

The first premise states that at least one program will go ahead, while the permit condition rules out the branch opening this year:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="P\lor Q" />
  <BlockMath math="\neg P" />
</MathContainer>

By disjunctive syllogism, eliminating <InlineMath math="P" /> leaves <InlineMath math="Q" />:

<BlockMath math="Q" />

Therefore, **PT Batik sells the new product this year**. Canceling or postponing both programs contradicts the first premise. Not selling the new product contradicts the conclusion, while completing the permit this year contradicts the last premise.
