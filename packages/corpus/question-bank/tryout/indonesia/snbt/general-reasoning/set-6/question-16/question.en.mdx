A travel agency offers tickets to five tourist destinations. A father buys one ticket for each of his five children. Every destination must be assigned exactly once. Each child may receive only a destination on their wish list:

-   A wants to go to Yogyakarta or Lombok.
-   B wants to go to Yogyakarta, Lombok, or Bali.
-   C wants to go to Yogyakarta, Lombok, or Manado.
-   D wants to go to Yogyakarta or Padang.
-   E wants to go to Padang, Lombok, or Yogyakarta.

If **E is assigned Lombok**, which destination must A receive?
