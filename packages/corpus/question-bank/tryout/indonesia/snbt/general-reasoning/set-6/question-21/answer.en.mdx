Separate the terms by position. The **odd-position terms** form this arithmetic sequence:

<BlockMath math="6,\ 11,\ 16,\ 21,\ldots" />

Consecutive terms in this subsequence differ by <InlineMath math="5" />. The next odd-position term is therefore <InlineMath math="a_9=21+5=26" />.

The **even-position terms** form this geometric sequence:

<BlockMath math="3,\ 9,\ 27,\ldots" />

Each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term in this subsequence by <InlineMath math="3" />. Thus, <InlineMath math="a_8=27\times3=81" />.

Substitute both values into the requested expression:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}a_8-2a_9&amp;=81-2(26)\\&amp;=81-52\\&amp;=29\end{aligned}" />

The requested value is <InlineMath math="29" />.
