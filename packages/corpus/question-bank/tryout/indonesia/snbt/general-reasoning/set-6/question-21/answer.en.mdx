# Given

A number sequence follows **one fixed rule**: **<InlineMath math="3, 2, 3, 8, 31, 154" />**.

# Asked

The **next term** after <InlineMath math="154" />.

# Solution

> Numbers climbing faster and faster point to growing multiplication, so check multiplying each term by its step number minus one.

The sequence first falls, then rises faster and faster. Faster and faster growth points to **multiplication**, so check how each term relates to the one before.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
3\times1-1 &= 2 \\
2\times2-1 &= 3 \\
3\times3-1 &= 8 \\
8\times4-1 &= 31 \\
31\times5-1 &= 154
\end{aligned}" />

Each term is multiplied by its step number, then <InlineMath math="1" /> is subtracted. The factor <InlineMath math="1" /> in the first step is what makes the second term smaller than the first. The next step is step <InlineMath math="6" />:

<BlockMath math="154\times6-1=923" />
