# Given

The table of four farmer groups gives **individual harvests and land areas** totaling <InlineMath math="271" /> tonnes across <InlineMath math="45" /> hectares.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements is **True** or **False** from the table.

# Solution

> Combined productivity divides total tonnes by total hectares across all groups rather than averaging the four separate yields.

Mark each statement Benar (true) or Salah (false). First find the totals: <InlineMath math="271" /> tons and <InlineMath math="45" /> ha. Combined productivity is **total harvest divided by total area**, not the average of the four productivities.

<BlockMath math="\frac{271}{45}\approx6.02\text{ t/ha}" />

The chart below puts the combined productivity next to the four groups. **The combined bar is just above A's bar and above the <InlineMath math="6" /> line**.

**Statement 1** is true. The total is <InlineMath math="271" /> tons.

**Statement 2** is false. A's productivity is exactly <InlineMath math="6.0" />, slightly below <InlineMath math="6.02" />.

**Statement 3** is false. <InlineMath math="\frac{10}{45}\approx22.2\%" />, not <InlineMath math="25\%" />.

**Statement 4** is true. <InlineMath math="6.02" /> is more than <InlineMath math="6" />.

# Answer

So statements 1 and 4 are true, while statements 2 and 3 are false.
