Measure volume as a fraction of the tank's capacity. Pump A fills <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{14}" /> of a tank per hour, while B fills <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{21}" />. Their rates add while both pumps run:

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{14}+\frac{1}{21}=\frac{5}{42}" />

After the first <InlineMath math="6\text{ h}" />, the filled and remaining fractions are:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="6\times\frac{5}{42}=\frac{5}{7}" />
<BlockMath math="1-\frac{5}{7}=\frac{2}{7}" />
</MathContainer>

Once B is switched off, subtract the leak rate from A's rate:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{14}-\frac{1}{70}&=\frac{5-1}{70}\\&=\frac{2}{35}\end{aligned}" />

Divide the remaining volume by this net rate to find the additional time:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}t&=\frac{2/7}{2/35}\\&=\frac{2}{7}\times\frac{35}{2}\\&=5\text{ h}\end{aligned}" />

This time starts when B is switched off. The total time from the beginning is therefore <InlineMath math="6+5=11\text{ h}" />.
