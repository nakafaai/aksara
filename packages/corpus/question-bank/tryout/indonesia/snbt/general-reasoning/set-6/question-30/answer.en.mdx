Let <InlineMath math="x" /> and <InlineMath math="y" /> be the numbers of X and Y packages. Both are non-negative integers. Material and labour limits give:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}5x+2y&\le40\\x+3y&\le21\end{aligned}" />

The total profit to maximize is <InlineMath math="L=10x+8y" />. Since <InlineMath math="x\ge0" />, the labour limit gives <InlineMath math="y\le7" />. We therefore need only check integer values of <InlineMath math="y" /> from <InlineMath math="0" /> through <InlineMath math="7" />.

For each <InlineMath math="y" />, choose the largest integer <InlineMath math="x" /> satisfying both limits. This maximizes profit in that row because each additional X adds <InlineMath math="10" /> profit units.

| <InlineMath math="y" /> | <InlineMath math="x" /> largest | <InlineMath math="L" /> |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| <InlineMath math="0" /> | <InlineMath math="8" /> | <InlineMath math="80" /> |
| <InlineMath math="1" /> | <InlineMath math="7" /> | <InlineMath math="78" /> |
| <InlineMath math="2" /> | <InlineMath math="7" /> | <InlineMath math="86" /> |
| <InlineMath math="3" /> | <InlineMath math="6" /> | <InlineMath math="84" /> |
| <InlineMath math="4" /> | <InlineMath math="6" /> | <InlineMath math="92" /> |
| <InlineMath math="5" /> | <InlineMath math="6" /> | <InlineMath math="100" /> |
| <InlineMath math="6" /> | <InlineMath math="3" /> | <InlineMath math="78" /> |
| <InlineMath math="7" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> | <InlineMath math="56" /> |

For example, when <InlineMath math="y=5" />, the limits become:

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="5x+10\le40\Rightarrow x\le6" />
<BlockMath math="x+15\le21\Rightarrow x\le6" />
</MathContainer>

The table shows a unique maximum at <InlineMath math="x=6" /> and <InlineMath math="y=5" />, with profit <InlineMath math="100" />. Both resources are fully used: <InlineMath math="5(6)+2(5)=40" /> and <InlineMath math="6+3(5)=21" />.

There are <InlineMath math="6+5=11" /> packages. The mean profit per package at this solution is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{100}{11}" />

The requested mean is therefore <InlineMath math="\frac{100}{11}" /> profit units per package.
