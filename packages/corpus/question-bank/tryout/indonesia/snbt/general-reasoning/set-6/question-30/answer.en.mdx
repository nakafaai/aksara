# Given

On Monday and Tuesday Rudi each day spent **half his remaining money plus <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}000" /> for fare**, leaving **<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}10{,}000" />**.

# Asked

**Rudi starting money before Monday** expressed as rupiah divided by <InlineMath math="1{,}000" />.

# Solution

> Walk backward from the final leftover by reversing every move, turning subtract five thousand into add five thousand and half into double.

Work **backwards from the end**, undoing each step: a subtraction becomes an addition, and "spends half" becomes doubling.

- Before Tuesday's fare: <InlineMath math="10{,}000+5{,}000=15{,}000" />.
- Before Tuesday's meal: <InlineMath math="15{,}000\times2=30{,}000" />.
- Before Monday's fare: <InlineMath math="30{,}000+5{,}000=35{,}000" />.
- Before Monday's book: <InlineMath math="35{,}000\times2=70{,}000" />.

Check forwards: half of <InlineMath math="70{,}000" /> is <InlineMath math="35{,}000" />, minus <InlineMath math="5{,}000" /> gives <InlineMath math="30{,}000" />. Half of that is <InlineMath math="15{,}000" />, minus <InlineMath math="5{,}000" /> gives <InlineMath math="10{,}000" />. It matches.

# Answer

So Rudi had <InlineMath math="70" /> thousand rupiah before Monday.
