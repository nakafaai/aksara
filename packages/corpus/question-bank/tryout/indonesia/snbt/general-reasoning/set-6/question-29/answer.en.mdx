All pairs of balls are equally likely. From <InlineMath math="5+7=12" /> balls, the number of possible pairs is:

<BlockMath math="\binom{12}{2}=\frac{12\times11}{2}=66" />

Count the pairs for each outcome:

- Two red balls can be chosen in <InlineMath math="\binom{5}{2}=10" /> ways.
- One red and one blue ball can be chosen in <InlineMath math="5\times7=35" /> ways.
- Two blue balls can be chosen in <InlineMath math="\binom{7}{2}=21" /> ways.

Their sum is <InlineMath math="10+35+21=66" />, so these cases cover every possibility. All payouts below are in thousand rupiah.

Let <InlineMath math="X" /> be the payout in one round. Multiply each payout by its probability to obtain its expected value. The two-blue outcome contributes zero:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\mathrm{E}[X]&=60\frac{10}{66}\\&\quad+30\frac{35}{66}\\&=\frac{600+1{,}050}{66}\\&=25\end{aligned}" />

The box is restored for each round, so all three rounds have the same expected payout. The administration fee is paid only once:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\mathrm{E}[\text{net}]&=3(25)-10\\&=75-10\\&=65\end{aligned}" />

The expected net payout is <InlineMath math="65" /> thousand rupiah. Expected value describes the theoretical average over many repetitions of the game.
