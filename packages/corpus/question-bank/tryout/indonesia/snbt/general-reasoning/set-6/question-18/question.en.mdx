The World Health Organization explains that industrially produced trans-fatty acids can be formed when vegetable oils are partially hydrogenated. They may be present in baked and fried foods. Consuming trans fat raises LDL cholesterol, lowers HDL cholesterol, and increases the risk of heart attack and death from coronary heart disease.

Which conclusion is best supported by the information above?

Source: [World Health Organization, "Trans fat"](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/trans-fat/)
