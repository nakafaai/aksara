[The World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/trans-fat/) explains that industrially produced trans-fatty acids can be formed when vegetable oils are partially hydrogenated. They may be present in baked and fried foods. Consuming trans fat [raises LDL cholesterol and lowers HDL cholesterol](https://www.who.int/news-room/detail/14-05-2018-who-plan-to-eliminate-industrially-produced-trans-fatty-acids-from-global-food-supply), and increases the risk of heart attack and death from coronary heart disease.

Which conclusion is best supported by the information above?
