Write <InlineMath math="A > B" /> when month <InlineMath math="A" /> has heavier traffic than month <InlineMath math="B" />.

| Statement | Mathematical Analysis |
| :--- | :--- |
| December is busier than November | <InlineMath math="\text{December} > \text{November}" /> |
| October is busier than December | <InlineMath math="\text{October} > \text{December}" /> |
| January is not busier than November | <InlineMath math="\text{January} \le \text{November}" /> |
| January is busier than February | <InlineMath math="\text{January} > \text{February}" /> |

The comparisons form this order:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{October} > \text{December} > \text{November} \ge \text{January} > \text{February}" />
</MathContainer>

Only October appears before December. Therefore, **December** is second busiest, regardless of whether November and January are equal.
