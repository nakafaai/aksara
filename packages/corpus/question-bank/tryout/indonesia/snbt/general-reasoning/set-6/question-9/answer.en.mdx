A good design must show that any difference in milk comes from **the music**, not something else. It needs three things:

- **A comparison group** without music.
- **Random assignment**, so the two groups of cows are alike from the start.
- **The same observation period**, so weather, seasonal feed, and other factors apply to both groups.

Randomly splitting the cows between two similar barns and playing music in only one meets all three.

The other options are weak:

- Comparing with last year mixes the effect of music with everything else that changed in a year.
- Choosing the most productive cows makes the groups unequal from the start.
- Other farmers' opinions are not measurements.
- Without a comparison group, a rise in the first week cannot be traced to the music.

So the best design is to split the cows at random between two similar barns, play music in only one, and compare their milk over the same period.
