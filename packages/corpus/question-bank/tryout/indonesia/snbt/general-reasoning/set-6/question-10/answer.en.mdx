> Track the three features one by one, counting triangles, watching the quarter-turn rotation, and alternating solid with hollow.

Track **three features** in each figure: the number of triangles, their direction, and their fill (solid or hollow).

- **The number** grows by one each time: <InlineMath math="1, 2, 3, 4" />, so figure 5 has <InlineMath math="5" /> triangles.
- **The direction** turns clockwise: up, right, down, left, so figure 5 points up again.
- **The fill** alternates: solid, hollow, solid, hollow, so figure 5 is solid.
