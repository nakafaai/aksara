# Given

Four complete **first-through-sixth performance orders** must be checked against all five rules.

# Asked

Whether each lineup is **Possible** or **Impossible** under all five rules.

# Solution

> Breaking even a single rule immediately disqualifies a full lineup as impossible.

Choose Mungkin (possible) or Tidak mungkin (impossible) for each order. Test each against all five rules. One violation is enough to make an order **impossible**.

The chart below counts the rules each order breaks. **An order with a zero bar is a possible order**.

**Order 1**: impossible. U in third place comes just after R in second place, which breaks rule 4.

**Order 2**: possible. Q follows P directly, R comes before S, T is last, U in fifth place is not next to R in third, and S is fourth.

**Order 3**: impossible. S in fourth place comes before R in fifth place, which breaks rule 2.

**Order 4**: possible. Q follows P directly, R is first and before S, T is last, U in fourth place is not next to R, and S is fifth.

# Answer

So orders 2 and 4 are possible, while orders 1 and 3 are impossible.
