To check a change from Grade X to Grade XII, read one row from left to right. To compare hobbies within a grade, compare the numbers **in the same column**.

| Claim | Check against the data | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basketball participation always increases | It rises from 100 to 150, then falls to 120 | False |
| Dance always has fewer participants than singing | 75 > 57, 78 > 46, and 80 > 44 | False |
| Painting participation always increases | It falls from 68 to 54, then to 44 | False |
| Acting has the fewest participants in every grade | 40, 7, and 18 are the smallest numbers in the Grade X, XI, and XII columns, respectively | True |
| Singing has the fewest participants | Acting has fewer participants than singing in all three grades | False |

Acting therefore has **the fewest participants** in every grade. Its participation does not need to decrease from one grade to the next for this claim to hold. We compare acting with the other hobbies within each grade.
