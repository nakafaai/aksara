After one member moves, A has <InlineMath math="8-1=7" /> members and B has <InlineMath math="6+1=7" />. Use the relationship that the total score equals the number of members multiplied by their mean.

Let the moving member's score be <InlineMath math="x" />. The increase in B's total is exactly that score:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}x&=7(68)-6(70)\\&=476-420\\&=56\end{aligned}" />

A originally had a total score of <InlineMath math="8(77)=616" />. After removing the score of <InlineMath math="56" />, its new mean is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\bar{x}_A&=\frac{616-56}{7}\\&=\frac{560}{7}\\&=80\end{aligned}" />

B's new mean is already given as <InlineMath math="68" />. The difference between the new means is therefore <InlineMath math="80-68=12" />.
