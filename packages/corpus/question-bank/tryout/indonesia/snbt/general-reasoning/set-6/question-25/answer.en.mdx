# Given

All areas and harvests of A, B, and D stay fixed, while **combined productivity must reach <InlineMath math="6.4" /> tonnes per hectare** across <InlineMath math="45" /> hectares.

# Asked

How many **extra tonnes group C must harvest** to hit the target.

# Solution

> Multiply the target yield by total hectares to find the total harvest needed, then subtract the current combined harvest.

Combined productivity is **total harvest divided by total area**. The total area stays <InlineMath math="45" /> ha, so the harvest needed is:

<BlockMath math="6.4\times45=288\text{ tons}" />

The current harvest is <InlineMath math="271" /> tons. Since only group C changes, the increase is:

<BlockMath math="288-271=17\text{ tons}" />

# Answer

Check: C's harvest rises to <InlineMath math="101" /> tons, bringing the total to <InlineMath math="288" /> tons across <InlineMath math="45" /> ha for exactly <InlineMath math="6.4" />. So group C must harvest **<InlineMath math="17" />** tons more.
