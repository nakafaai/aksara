Let <InlineMath math="P" /> mean "a robbery occurred" and <InlineMath math="Q" /> mean "a theft occurred." The two premises are:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="P\lor Q" />
  <BlockMath math="\neg P" />
</MathContainer>

The second premise rules out robbery. For the first premise to remain true, theft must be the event that occurred:

<BlockMath math="Q" />

This is a disjunctive syllogism. Therefore, **a theft occurred in Kampung Bambu last Sunday**.
