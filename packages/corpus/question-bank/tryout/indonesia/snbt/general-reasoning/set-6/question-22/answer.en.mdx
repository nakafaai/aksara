The initial final mixture has 9 litres of solute in 45 litres. If <InlineMath math="y" /> litres of a 45% solution are added and the new concentration is 30%, then <InlineMath math="(9+0.45y)/(45+y)=0.30" />. Solving gives <InlineMath math="y=30" /> litres.

Therefore, the requested value is <InlineMath math="30" />.
