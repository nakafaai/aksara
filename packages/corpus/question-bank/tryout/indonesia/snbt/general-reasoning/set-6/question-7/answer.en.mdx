The village head concludes that the cameras **caused** the drop in theft. But the passage says night patrols were revived in February 2026, only a month after the cameras went up. Night patrols can also reduce theft. Because the two changes came almost together, the data **cannot separate** the effect of the cameras from that of the patrols.

The other options are not the main flaw:

- The village head does not treat the data as too little. He draws his conclusion straight from it.
- Camera prices do not decide whether cameras reduce theft.
- Both periods are equally long, January to October.
- Residents' approval has nothing to do with whether the causal conclusion is right.

So the main flaw is ignoring the night patrols, started almost at the same time, as another cause.
