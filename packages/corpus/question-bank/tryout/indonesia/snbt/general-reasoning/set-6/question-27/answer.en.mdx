All monetary values below are in thousand rupiah. Work backward by reversing the price changes in the opposite order.

The final price includes <InlineMath math="10\%" /> tax. The price after the voucher but before tax is therefore:

<BlockMath math="\frac{924}{1.10}=840" />

Add the voucher back to obtain the price after the markup:

<BlockMath math="840+60=900" />

This price is <InlineMath math="125\%" /> of the original price. Let the original price be <InlineMath math="H" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}1.25H&=900\\H&=\frac{900}{1.25}\\&=720\end{aligned}" />

The requested markup amount is:

<BlockMath math="0.25\times720=180" />

To check, <InlineMath math="720+180=900" />, the voucher reduces it to <InlineMath math="840" />, and tax raises it to <InlineMath math="924" />. The markup is therefore <InlineMath math="180" /> thousand rupiah.
