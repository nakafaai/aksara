We need to count **each participant exactly once**, even if they use several services. Inclusion-exclusion corrects the repeated counts as follows:

| Services used by one participant | Initial count from A + B + C | Pairwise correction | Three-service correction | Final count |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exactly one | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| Exactly two | 2 | −1 | 0 | 1 |
| All three | 3 | −3 | +1 | 1 |

A user of all three services is initially counted three times, then subtracted three times because they belong to every pairwise intersection. We must therefore **add them back once**.

Let <InlineMath math="U" /> be the number using at least one service:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}U&amp;=64+58+49\\&amp;\quad-24-19-17+8\\&amp;=171-60+8\\&amp;=119\end{aligned}" />

Participants using none are the remainder of the <InlineMath math="140" /> surveyed:

<BlockMath math="140-119=21" />

Therefore, <InlineMath math="21" /> participants use none of the services.
