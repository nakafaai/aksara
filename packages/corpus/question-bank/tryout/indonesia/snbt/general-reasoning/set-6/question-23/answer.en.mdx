# Given

The table lists **harvest output (tonnes) and farm area (hectares)** for four farmer groups A, B, C, and D.

# Asked

The ranking of farmer groups from **highest to lowest productivity**.

# Solution

> Productivity divides harvest by land area, so the largest harvest does not automatically mean the highest yield per hectare.

Productivity is **per hectare**, so divide each group's harvest by its land area.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{A} &= \frac{72}{12}=6.0 \\
\text{B} &= \frac{52}{8}=6.5 \\
\text{C} &= \frac{84}{15}=5.6 \\
\text{D} &= \frac{63}{10}=6.3
\end{aligned}" />

From highest: B <InlineMath math="(6.5)" />, D <InlineMath math="(6.3)" />, A <InlineMath math="(6.0)" />, C <InlineMath math="(5.6)" />.

The chart below draws the four groups' productivity. **C's bar is the shortest**, even though its harvest is the biggest.

The order C, A, D, B ranks **total harvest**, not productivity. C harvests the most because it has the most land, but its yield per hectare is the lowest.

# Answer

So the order is **B, D, A, C**.
