Use inclusion-exclusion to count participants using at least one service. Adding the A, B, and C counts directly counts users of two services twice, so subtract the three pairwise intersections. Then add the users of all three services once: they were initially counted three times and have just been subtracted three times.

Let <InlineMath math="U" /> be the number using at least one service:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}U&=64+58+49\\&\quad-24-19-17+8\\&=171-60+8\\&=119\end{aligned}" />

Participants using none are the remainder of the <InlineMath math="140" /> surveyed:

<BlockMath math="140-119=21" />

Therefore, <InlineMath math="21" /> participants use none of the services.
