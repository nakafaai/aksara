A yes or no question is answered when the answer is **always the same** in every situation the statement allows.

**Statement (1) alone**: not sufficient. Suppose the class has three students. Scores of <InlineMath math="80" />, <InlineMath math="80" />, and <InlineMath math="70" /> satisfy statement (1), with mean <InlineMath math="\frac{230}{3}\approx76.7" />, above <InlineMath math="75" />. Scores of <InlineMath math="76" />, <InlineMath math="76" />, and <InlineMath math="10" /> also satisfy statement (1), but the mean is <InlineMath math="54" />. The answer can be yes or no.

**Statement (2) alone**: sufficient. If the lowest score is <InlineMath math="80" />, **every** score is at least <InlineMath math="80" />, so the mean is also at least <InlineMath math="80" />. The answer is always yes.

So statement (2) alone is sufficient, but statement (1) alone is not.
