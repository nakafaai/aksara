Check the net-output requirement before comparing efficiency. For each machine, subtract defective units from total output:

| Machine | Net output |
| --- | ---: |
| A | <InlineMath math="660-20=640" /> |
| B | <InlineMath math="700-28=672" /> |
| C | <InlineMath math="800-32=768" /> |

A and B produce fewer than <InlineMath math="700" /> usable units, so neither qualifies. Only C meets the threshold.

C's efficiency index is its net output divided by its total energy use:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}I_C&=\frac{768}{16\times4}\\&=\frac{768}{64}\\&=12\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, **machine C** is the qualifying choice. A machine with a higher index would still be excluded if it failed the net-output requirement.
