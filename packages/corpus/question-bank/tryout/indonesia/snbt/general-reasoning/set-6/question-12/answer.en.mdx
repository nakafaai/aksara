Use **two steps** in turn.

- **Modus tollens**: if the meeting moves to the village hall, chairs must be borrowed from the school. The school's chairs cannot be borrowed, so the meeting does **not** move to the hall.
- **Disjunctive syllogism**: the meeting moves to the hall or is postponed. The first option is ruled out, so the meeting is **postponed**.

The other options are not valid:

- The meeting does not move to the hall, so it is not held there.
- The meeting does not move to the hall, so an option saying it moved fails.
- The second statement requires chairs from the school if the meeting moves. Chairs from elsewhere are not discussed and do not rescue the move.
- The first statement leaves only two possibilities, moving or postponing, so the meeting is not held at the original place on schedule.

So the valid conclusion is that the residents' meeting is postponed.
