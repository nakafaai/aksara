# Given

The passage reports **average food taken and average food wasted** both before and after switching plate sizes.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements is **True** or **False** according to the text.

# Solution

> Food actually eaten equals food taken minus leftovers, so subtract waste first before testing each statement.

Mark each statement Benar (true) or Salah (false), computing from the passage. Food eaten equals **food taken minus waste**.

**Statement 1** is true. Before the change it was <InlineMath math="620-70=550" /> grams and after it <InlineMath math="540-40=500" /> grams, a drop of <InlineMath math="50" /> grams.

**Statement 2** is true. Employees taking food more than once rose from <InlineMath math="15\%" /> to <InlineMath math="25\%" />, so those taking food only once fell from <InlineMath math="100\%-15\%=85\%" /> to <InlineMath math="100\%-25\%=75\%" />.

**Statement 3** is false. Waste fell by <InlineMath math="70-40=30" /> grams, which is <InlineMath math="\frac{30}{70}\approx43\%" />, less than half.

# Answer

So statements 1 and 2 are true, while statements 3 and 4 are false.
