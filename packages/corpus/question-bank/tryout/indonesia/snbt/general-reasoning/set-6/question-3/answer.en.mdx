Let <InlineMath math="p" /> mean “clean the drains” and <InlineMath math="q" /> mean “collect recyclable materials.” The announcement and the rain give two premises:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} p \lor q \\ \neg p \\ \hline \therefore q \end{aligned}" />

This is a disjunctive syllogism: **one activity must still take place**, and drain cleaning is impossible. Therefore, <InlineMath math="q" /> remains, so **recyclable materials are collected** on Sunday.

Rain rules out drain cleaning only. Canceling or postponing the entire event would contradict the premise that an activity takes place that Sunday.
