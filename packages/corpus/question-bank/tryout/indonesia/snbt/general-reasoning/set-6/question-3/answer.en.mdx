Let <InlineMath math="p" /> mean “clean the drains” and <InlineMath math="q" /> mean “collect recyclable materials.” The announcement and the rain give two premises:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} p \lor q \\ \neg p \\ \hline \therefore q \end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

This is a disjunctive syllogism: one of the activities must be chosen, and drain cleaning is impossible. Therefore, <InlineMath math="q" /> remains, so **recyclable materials are collected on Sunday**.
