Mark each conclusion Benar (true, it must follow from the rules) or Salah (false, it need not follow). Write both rules as implications. "May play in the final only if present at every session" means **plays in the final** <InlineMath math="\Rightarrow" /> **attended every session**. The second rule: **plays in the final** <InlineMath math="\Rightarrow" /> **gets a special shirt**.

**Conclusion 1** is true. Dodi did not attend every session. By the contrapositive of the first rule, Dodi does not play in the final.

**Conclusion 2** is false. Attending every session is a **necessary condition**, not a sufficient one. The coach may still leave Eka out.

**Conclusion 3** is true. Gita plays in the final, so by the first rule she attended every session, and by the second rule she gets a special shirt.

**Conclusion 4** is false. The second rule runs one way only. Someone who is not in the final may also get a special shirt.

So conclusions 1 and 3 are true, while conclusions 2 and 4 are false.
