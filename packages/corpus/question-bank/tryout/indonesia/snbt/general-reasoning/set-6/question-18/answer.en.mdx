# Given

The scoreboard for teams A through E applies **three tiered ranking rules** where the second and third rules act only as tiebreakers.

# Asked

The teams finishing **first, second, and third** in order.

# Solution

> Sort by the first rule first, and pull out the second or third rule only when two teams tie on the rule above.

Apply the rules **in order**. Rules 2 and 3 only break ties left by the rule before.

- **Rule 1**: the top total is <InlineMath math="90" />, held by B and D. Next is <InlineMath math="85" />, held by A and C. E, with <InlineMath math="80" />, is last.
- **B and D**: both have <InlineMath math="5" /> correct answers in the buzzer round, so use rule 3. D is faster (<InlineMath math="380" /> seconds against <InlineMath math="395" />), so D is first and B second.
- **A and C**: C has <InlineMath math="7" /> correct buzzer answers, more than A's <InlineMath math="6" />, so C is third. Time does not matter here.
