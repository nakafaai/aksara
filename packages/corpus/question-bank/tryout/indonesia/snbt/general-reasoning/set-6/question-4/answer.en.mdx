The passage describes the relationship in two steps:

1. High sodium intake is associated with increased blood pressure.
2. Increased blood pressure can contribute to cardiovascular disease.

The question asks for the first, most direct effect. That is **increased blood pressure**. The other choices either reverse the relationship or introduce effects that the passage does not mention.

This reasoning follows the [WHO healthy diet fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet).
