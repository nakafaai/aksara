Draw the houses as points on the grid. Put **Budi at the origin**.

- Ani is due north of Budi, so Ani is above Budi.
- Cici is due east of Ani, so Cici is to the right of Ani.
- Dedi is due south of Cici and due east of Budi, so Dedi is below Cici and to the right of Budi.

The four houses form the corners of a rectangle: Ani top left, Cici top right, Budi bottom left, and Dedi bottom right. Cici is up and to the right of Budi, that is, **northeast** of Budi's house.

The other options are false:

- Dedi is due east of Budi, not northeast.
- Dedi is southeast of Ani, not due south.
- Budi is due west of Dedi, not northwest.
- Ani and Dedi are not on the same north and south line, since Dedi is farther east.

So what must be true is that Cici's house is northeast of Budi's house.
