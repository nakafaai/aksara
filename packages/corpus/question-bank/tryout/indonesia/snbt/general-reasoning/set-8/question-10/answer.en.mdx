# Given

The combined number-and-letter sequence runs **2B, 5E, 10J, 17Q**.

# Asked

The **next term** in the sequence.

# Solution

> Notice that each letter alphabet number matches its front number exactly, while the numbers grow as squares plus one.

Each term is **a number** and **a letter**. Turn the letter into its position in the alphabet: B <InlineMath math="=2" />, E <InlineMath math="=5" />, J <InlineMath math="=10" />, Q <InlineMath math="=17" />. The letter always sits at the same position as the number.

The numbers grow by <InlineMath math="3, 5, 7" />, consecutive odd numbers, so next they grow by <InlineMath math="9" />: <InlineMath math="17+9=26" />. The <InlineMath math="26" />th letter is Z. The pattern can also be seen as <InlineMath math="n^2+1" />: <InlineMath math="2, 5, 10, 17" />, then <InlineMath math="5^2+1=26" />.
