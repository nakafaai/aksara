#### Translate the rules into implications

Let:

- <InlineMath math="P" />: the component passes the initial inspection,
- <InlineMath math="Q" />: the component has a blue seal,
- <InlineMath math="R" />: the component enters the durability test,
- <InlineMath math="S" />: the component is placed on rack C.

The three rules form one chain:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="P \rightarrow Q" />
  <BlockMath math="Q \rightarrow R" />
  <BlockMath math="R \rightarrow S" />
</MathContainer>

Starting with a blue seal gives <InlineMath math="Q \rightarrow R \rightarrow S" />. Therefore, every blue-sealed component is placed on rack C.

The other choices reverse a rule, deny an antecedent, or add the word “only.” None of those conclusions follows from the stated implications.
