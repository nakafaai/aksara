The table contains data for three machines.

| Machine | Output | Defects | Hours | Energy/hour |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 800 | 40 | 15 | 4 |
| B | 720 | 18 | 12 | 5 |
| C | 640 | 28 | 14 | 4 |

The efficiency index is defined as <InlineMath math="\frac{\text{output}-\text{defects}}{\text{hours}\times\text{energy per hour}}" />.

By what percentage must B's net output increase for its index to match A's, with B's hours and energy unchanged? Round to one decimal place.
