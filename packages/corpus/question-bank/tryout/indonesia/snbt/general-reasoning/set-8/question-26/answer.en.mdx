The net outputs of A and B equal total output minus defective products:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="800-40=760" />
  <BlockMath math="720-18=702" />
</MathContainer>

Both machines use the same total energy:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="15\times4=60" />
  <BlockMath math="12\times5=60" />
</MathContainer>

Since the index denominators are equal, B must reach A’s net output of <InlineMath math="760" />. The required increase is:

<BlockMath math="760-702=58" />

Calculate the percentage increase relative to B’s original net output of <InlineMath math="702" />:

<BlockMath math="\frac{58}{702}\times100\%\approx8.3\%" />

Therefore, B’s net output must increase by approximately **<InlineMath math="8.3\%" />**.
