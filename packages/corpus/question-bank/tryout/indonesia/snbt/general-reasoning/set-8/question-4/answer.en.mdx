<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}U_3&=1+4=5\\U_4&=4+5=9\\U_5&=5+9=14\end{aligned}" />

Writing <InlineMath math="U_n" /> for term <InlineMath math="n" />, this pattern is expressed for <InlineMath math="n\ge3" /> as:

<BlockMath math="U_n=U_{n-1}+U_{n-2}" />

The missing term is in the sixth position:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}U_6&=U_5+U_4\\&=14+9\\&=23\end{aligned}" />

This value also matches every subsequent term:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}U_7&=23+14=37\\U_8&=37+23=60\\U_9&=60+37=97\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, the missing value is **<InlineMath math="23" />**.
