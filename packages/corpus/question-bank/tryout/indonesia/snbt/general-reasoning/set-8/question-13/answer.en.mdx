The on-time submission rate rises from <InlineMath math="64\%" /> to <InlineMath math="86\%" /> after the app launches. This timing alone does not show that the app caused the increase.

Extending the deadline in the same week supplies a **competing explanation**. Extending the deadline from late afternoon to midnight makes timely submission easier at the same moment the app is introduced. The increase could therefore come from the longer submission window, the app, or both.

Imagine an assignment submitted at 6 p.m. on the due date. This example shows how its on-time status can change even when its submission time stays the same.

| Deadline | Submission time | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 p.m. | 6 p.m. | Late |
| Midnight | 6 p.m. | On time |

The other statements support the proposed mechanism, strengthen the comparison, or confirm that measurement stayed consistent. The simultaneous deadline change is the strongest weakening evidence because it directly confounds the before-and-after comparison.
