After adding water, the solution volume is:

<BlockMath math="50+5=55\text{ L}" />

At a concentration of <InlineMath math="20\%" />, the solute volume is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{20}{100}\times55=11\text{ L}" />

Evaporation removes only water. The solute remains at <InlineMath math="11" /> litres, while the solution volume becomes:

<BlockMath math="55-11=44\text{ L}" />

The new concentration is solute volume divided by solution volume. It rises because the volume decreases without losing solute:

<BlockMath math="\frac{11}{44}\times100\%=25\%" />
