The difference is the transferred member’s score, denoted by <InlineMath math="s" />:

<BlockMath math="s=544-483=61" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\bar{x}_A&=\frac{790-61}{9}\\&=\frac{729}{9}\\&=81\end{aligned}" />

<BlockMath math="\frac{s}{\bar{x}_A}=\frac{61}{81}" />
