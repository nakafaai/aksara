Let <InlineMath math="T" /> be the total valid votes. Each valid vote goes to one of the four candidates, so:

<BlockMath math="M+N+O+P=T" />

Statement 4 gives <InlineMath math="T=468" />. Statements 2 and 3 then determine the votes for O and P:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="O=\frac14(468)=117" />
  <BlockMath math="P=\frac23(117)=78" />
</MathContainer>

The remaining votes belong to M and N:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}M+N&=468-117-78\\&=273\end{aligned}" />

Statement 1 gives <InlineMath math="M=2N" />. Substitute this into the remaining-votes equation:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}2N+N&=273\\3N&=273\\N&=91\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="M=2(91)=182" />
</MathContainer>

Every statement has a necessary role:

- Without the total in statement 4, all counts can be scaled by the same factor.
- Without statement 2, the counts for O and P are not fixed.
- Without statement 3, the count for P is not fixed.
- Without statement 1, the combined votes for M and N cannot be split uniquely.

Therefore, **all four statements** are needed, giving <InlineMath math="M=182" /> votes.
