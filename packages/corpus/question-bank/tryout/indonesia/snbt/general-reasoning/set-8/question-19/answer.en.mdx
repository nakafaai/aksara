# Given

Three premises link **remote workers, Monday meeting attendees, and new hires**, noting some Monday attendees are new hires.

# Asked

The conclusion that is **certainly true** from those three statements.

# Solution

> Start with the real person guaranteed by the word some, then apply the rule that no new hire works remotely.

Start from the statement with **some** (*sebagian*), because only it guarantees that certain people exist. Some Monday meeting participants are new employees. By the third statement, new employees do **not** work remotely. So some Monday meeting participants do not work remotely.

The diagram below follows the new employees who attend the meeting. **The arrow from "new employee" to "not remote" holds for every new employee**, including those at the meeting.

The other options are uncertain or false:

- The new employees at the meeting do not work remotely, so not every participant works remotely.
- The second statement says some new employees attend the meeting.
- Only **some** participants are known to be new employees. Not every new employee need attend.
- The third statement says no new employee works remotely, so this option is false.

# Answer

So the conclusion that must be true is that some Monday meeting participants do not work remotely.
