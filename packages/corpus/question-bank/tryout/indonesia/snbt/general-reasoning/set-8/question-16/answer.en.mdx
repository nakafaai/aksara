Let <InlineMath math="T" /> mean that the building is a temple, <InlineMath math="D" /> that it has a domed roof, and <InlineMath math="H" /> its height in metres. The premises give necessary properties of a temple:

<BlockMath math="T \Longrightarrow (D \land H > 60\text{ m})" />

They do not give the reverse implication. A building that is <InlineMath math="100\text{ m}" /> tall is compatible with being a temple, but height alone does not establish that it is one. The missing roof and function data are directly relevant to the proposed conclusion.

The conclusion is therefore neither proved nor disproved. It is relevant to the information, but **cannot be assessed because the information is insufficient**.
