# Given

Four proposed **six-song setlists** must be tested against all four rules.

# Asked

Whether each setlist is **Possible** or **Impossible** under all four rules.

# Solution

> Scan for back-to-back slow songs or broken ordering pairs, because one single rule break eliminates the whole list.

Choose Mungkin (possible) or Tidak mungkin (impossible) for each order. Test each against all four rules. One violation makes an order **impossible**.

The chart below counts the rules each order breaks. **An order with a zero bar is possible**.

**Order 1**: possible. The slow songs S, T, and U are in places 2, 4, and 6, so none are adjacent, P comes before Q, T is fourth, and R comes right after S.

**Order 2**: possible. The slow songs are in places 1, 3, and 5, P comes before Q, T is fifth, and R comes right after S.

**Order 3**: impossible. T and U are sung back to back in places 3 and 4, which breaks rule 1.

**Order 4**: impossible. T is sung first, which breaks rule 3.

# Answer

So orders 1 and 2 are possible, while orders 3 and 4 are impossible.
