Statement <InlineMath math="2" /> gives the share received by O, but not the total vote count:

<BlockMath math="O=\frac14T" />

Statement <InlineMath math="4" /> gives the total:

<BlockMath math="T=468" />

Together, these two statements determine the votes for O:

<BlockMath math="O=\frac14\times468=117" />

Statement <InlineMath math="1" /> does not give the total. Statement <InlineMath math="3" /> only relates P to O. Adding them to statements <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="4" /> is unnecessary.
