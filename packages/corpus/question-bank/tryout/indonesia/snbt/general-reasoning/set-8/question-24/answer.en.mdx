The combined pump rate, in tankfuls per hour, is:

<BlockMath math="\frac1{20}+\frac1{30}=\frac1{12}" />

After <InlineMath math="8" /> hours, the filled fraction is:

<BlockMath math="8\times\frac1{12}=\frac23" />

The fraction still to be filled is:

<BlockMath math="1-\frac23=\frac13" />

A fills the remainder at <InlineMath math="1/20" /> tankful per hour. Its additional operating time is:

<BlockMath math="\frac13\div\frac1{20}=\frac{20}{3}" />

The two <InlineMath math="30" />-minute pauses add one hour without filling the tank:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\frac{2\times30}{60}=1" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{20}{3}+1=\frac{23}{3}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the additional elapsed time is **<InlineMath math="23/3" /> hours**, or <InlineMath math="7" /> hours <InlineMath math="40" /> minutes.
