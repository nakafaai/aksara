Statement A concerns task-practice routines. The survey measured no such data, so it neither strengthens nor weakens A.

Statement B concerns an association between high exam scores and final achievement. The survey reports that <InlineMath math="78\%" /> of final high achievers also had high exam scores. That observation is directly consistent with B and therefore strengthens it.

The survey supports an association; it does not by itself prove that high exam scores *cause* final achievement. The appropriate conclusion is therefore **strengthens statement B**. The evidence is too limited for “proves statement B.”
