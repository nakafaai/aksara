Let <InlineMath math="P" /> mean “River A exceeds the alert level,” <InlineMath math="Q" /> mean “District X enters flood status,” and <InlineMath math="R" /> mean “registered residents of District X receive an evacuation order.” The protocol gives:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="P \rightarrow Q" />
  <BlockMath math="Q \rightarrow R" />
  <BlockMath math="P" />
</MathContainer>

Because <InlineMath math="P" /> is true, the first rule gives <InlineMath math="Q" />. The second rule then gives <InlineMath math="R" />. These are two applications of modus ponens.

Therefore, the registered residents of District X receive an evacuation order. The rules say nothing about every other district or every person in the city, so those broader claims do not follow.
