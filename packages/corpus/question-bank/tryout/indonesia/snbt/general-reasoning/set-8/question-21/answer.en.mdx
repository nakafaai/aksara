The terms nearly double, so compare each term with **twice the term before it**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2\times2-1 &= 3 \\
2\times3+1 &= 7 \\
2\times7-1 &= 13 \\
2\times13+1 &= 27 \\
2\times27-1 &= 53
\end{aligned}" />

Each term is twice the previous one, with <InlineMath math="1" /> subtracted and added in turn. The next step adds <InlineMath math="1" />:

<BlockMath math="2\times53+1=107" />

Another route gives the same result: from the third term on, each term is the previous term plus twice the term two steps back, for example <InlineMath math="13+2\times7=27" /> and <InlineMath math="27+2\times13=53" />. The next term is <InlineMath math="53+2\times27=107" />.

So the next number is <InlineMath math="107" />.
