The odd-position terms are <InlineMath math="8" />, <InlineMath math="12" />, <InlineMath math="16" />, and <InlineMath math="20" />. Each increases by <InlineMath math="4" />:

<BlockMath math="a_9=20+4=24" />

The even-position terms are <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="8" />, and <InlineMath math="16" />. Each doubles the preceding term:

<BlockMath math="a_8=16\times2=32" />

Factor the two new terms. The least common multiple takes the highest power of each prime factor:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="24=2^3\times3" />
  <BlockMath math="32=2^5" />
  <BlockMath math="2^5\times3=96" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, the least common multiple is **<InlineMath math="96" />**.
