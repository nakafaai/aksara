The terms nearly double, so compare each term with **twice the term before it**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2\times5-2 &= 8 \\
2\times8-3 &= 13 \\
2\times13-4 &= 22 \\
2\times22-5 &= 39 \\
2\times39-6 &= 72
\end{aligned}" />

Each term is twice the previous term minus a number that grows by one each step. The next step subtracts <InlineMath math="7" />:

<BlockMath math="2\times72-7=137" />

Another route gives the same result: the differences are <InlineMath math="3, 5, 9, 17, 33" />, and each is twice the previous difference minus <InlineMath math="1" />. The next difference is <InlineMath math="2\times33-1=65" />, and <InlineMath math="72+65=137" />.

So the next number is <InlineMath math="137" />.
