To judge whether the music caused the rise, we need **a comparison**: a place that went through the same months **without** music. Another branch that played no music in September and October is such a comparison.

- If spending at that branch also rose by about <InlineMath math="15\%" />, the rise at Jalan Mawar was probably caused by something else, such as the season.
- If spending there stayed flat, the role of the music becomes more convincing.

The other questions help less:

- Bestseller prices since the shop opened do not show what changed from September to October.
- The song titles do not show whether music raised spending.
- How long the shop has existed has nothing to do with the two-month change.
- The manager's taste does not affect customer behavior.

So the most helpful question is whether other branches that played no music also saw spending rise from September to October.
