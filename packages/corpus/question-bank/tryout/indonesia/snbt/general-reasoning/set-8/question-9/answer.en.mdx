Let <InlineMath math="P" /> mean “manages time well,” <InlineMath math="Q" /> mean “studies consistently,” and <InlineMath math="R" /> mean “achieves a high exam score.” The rules give:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="P \rightarrow Q" />
  <BlockMath math="Q \rightarrow R" />
  <BlockMath math="P \rightarrow R" />
</MathContainer>

The last implication is equivalent to its contrapositive:

<BlockMath math="\neg R \rightarrow \neg P" />

Therefore, under these rules, a student who does not achieve a high exam score did not manage their time well. The other choices reverse an implication or claim an outcome for which the rules provide no guarantee.
