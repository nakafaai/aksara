The plan uses a test in **one city** to decide for **all of Indonesia**. Reasoning like this is strong only if the test participants represent consumers across the country.

If the taste for sweetness in many other regions is very different from that in Bandung, the Bandung result may not hold elsewhere. Many consumers there might tell the difference and dislike the less sweet recipe. So the plan is weakened.

The other options do not weaken it:

- A lower cost is an extra reason to change the recipe.
- Not telling participants which drink was which makes the test fairer, so it strengthens the test.
- Random selection is already in the passage and improves the sample within that city.
- Identical packaging has nothing to do with judging taste.

So the statement that weakens the plan most is that the taste for sweetness in many other regions differs greatly from that of Bandung residents.
