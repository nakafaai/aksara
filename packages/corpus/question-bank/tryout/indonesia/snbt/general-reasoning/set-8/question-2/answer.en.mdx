Shop B revenue rises from <InlineMath math="350" /> in year <InlineMath math="3" /> to <InlineMath math="385" /> in year <InlineMath math="4" />.

Let <InlineMath math="H_0" /> denote the previous year's revenue and <InlineMath math="H_1" /> the next year's revenue. The percentage increase <InlineMath math="p" /> uses the previous year as its base:

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{H_1-H_0}{H_0}\times100\%" />

Substitute the chart values:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{385-350}{350}\times100\%\\&=\frac{35}{350}\times100\%\\&=\frac1{10}\times100\%\\&=10\%\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, shop B revenue increased by **<InlineMath math="10\%" />**.
