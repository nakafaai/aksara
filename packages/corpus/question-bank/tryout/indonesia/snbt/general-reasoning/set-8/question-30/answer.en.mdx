# Given

A colony starts with **<InlineMath math="300" /> bacteria** and **doubles every <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes**, staying constant in between.

# Asked

The **minimum time (minutes)** until the bacteria count exceeds <InlineMath math="10{,}000" />.

# Solution

> Double the count every twenty minutes step by step until it crosses ten thousand for the first time.

The bacteria **double every <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes**, so work out the count at each multiple of <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
20\text{ min} &: 600 \\
40\text{ min} &: 1{,}200 \\
60\text{ min} &: 2{,}400 \\
80\text{ min} &: 4{,}800 \\
100\text{ min} &: 9{,}600 \\
120\text{ min} &: 19{,}200
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below draws the number of bacteria at each multiple of <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes. **The line only passes the limit at minute <InlineMath math="120" />**.

At minute <InlineMath math="100" /> there are <InlineMath math="9{,}600" /> bacteria, still fewer than <InlineMath math="10{,}000" />. At minute <InlineMath math="120" /> there are <InlineMath math="19{,}200" />, already more than <InlineMath math="10{,}000" />. Since the count doubles at the end of each <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes, the answer falls on the next multiple.

# Answer

So at least <InlineMath math="120" /> minutes are needed.
