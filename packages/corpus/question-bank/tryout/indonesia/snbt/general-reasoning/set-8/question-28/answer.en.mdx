All orders of the eight reports are equally likely. The total number of arrangements is:

<BlockMath math="8!=40{,}320" />

C and D can occupy the ends in two orders, with C on the left and D on the right or vice versa. This also ensures that neither A nor B occupies an end.

The other six reports fill the middle positions in <InlineMath math="6!" /> arrangements. Each arrangement with E before F pairs with exactly one arrangement obtained by swapping E and F. This swap preserves the positions of C and D, so exactly half of the middle arrangements place E before F:

<BlockMath math="\frac{6!}{2}=360" />

Including the two orders of C and D, the number satisfying every condition is:

<BlockMath math="2\times360=720" />

Divide the number satisfying the conditions by the total number of arrangements:

<BlockMath math="\frac{720}{40{,}320}=\frac1{56}" />
