# Given

Rina read a fraction of a book on day one, **one third of the remaining pages on day two**, and **<InlineMath math="40" /> pages on day three**.

# Asked

How many **unread pages remain** after day three.

# Solution

> Find what is left after day one first, because day two takes one third of the leftover pages rather than the whole book.

Note that the second day's fraction is taken of **the remaining pages**, not the whole book.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{day 1} &= \tfrac{1}{4}\times240=60,\quad \text{left } 240-60=180 \\
\text{day 2} &= \tfrac{1}{3}\times180=60,\quad \text{left } 180-60=120 \\
\text{day 3} &= 40,\quad \text{left } 120-40=80
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below draws the pages left after each day. **The second day also takes off <InlineMath math="60" /> pages**, because its third is taken from the remaining <InlineMath math="180" />, not from <InlineMath math="240" />.

Taking the second day's fraction of the whole book, <InlineMath math="80" /> pages, would leave <InlineMath math="60" /> pages. That does not match the question.

# Answer

So after the third day, **<InlineMath math="80" />** pages are still unread.
