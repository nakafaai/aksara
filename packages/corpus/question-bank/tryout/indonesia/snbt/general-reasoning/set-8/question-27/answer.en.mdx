Note that the second day's fraction is taken of **the remaining pages**, not the whole book.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{day 1} &= \tfrac{1}{4}\times240=60,\quad \text{left } 240-60=180 \\
\text{day 2} &= \tfrac{1}{3}\times180=60,\quad \text{left } 180-60=120 \\
\text{day 3} &= 40,\quad \text{left } 120-40=80
\end{aligned}" />

Taking the second day's fraction of the whole book, <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{3}\times240=80" />, would leave <InlineMath math="240-60-80-40=60" />. That does not match the question.

So after the third day, <InlineMath math="80" /> pages are still unread.
