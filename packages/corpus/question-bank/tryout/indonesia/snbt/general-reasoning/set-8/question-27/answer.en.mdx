All prices below are in thousand rupiah. The price after the markup and voucher deduction, before tax, is:

<BlockMath math="792\div\frac{11}{10}=720" />

Add the voucher back to recover the marked-up price. Divide by the markup factor to recover the initial price:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="720+60=780" />
  <BlockMath math="780\div\frac{13}{10}=600" />
</MathContainer>

Without the voucher, the marked-up price remains <InlineMath math="780" />. After tax:

<BlockMath math="780\times\frac{11}{10}=858" />

Compared with the final price using the voucher, the additional payment is:

<BlockMath math="858-792=66" />

We can also check directly. Without the voucher, the taxable amount increases by <InlineMath math="60" /> thousand rupiah. Including tax, the extra payment is <InlineMath math="60\times11/10=66" /> thousand rupiah.
