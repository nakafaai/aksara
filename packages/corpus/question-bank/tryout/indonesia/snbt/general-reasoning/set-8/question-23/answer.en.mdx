Efficiency is **per liter of fuel**, so divide each distance by the fuel used.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{P} &= \frac{360}{9}=40\text{ km/liter} \\
\text{Q} &= \frac{420}{12}=35\text{ km/liter} \\
\text{R} &= \frac{300}{6}=50\text{ km/liter} \\
\text{S} &= \frac{450}{10}=45\text{ km/liter}
\end{aligned}" />

The order is R <InlineMath math="(50)" />, S <InlineMath math="(45)" />, P <InlineMath math="(40)" />, Q <InlineMath math="(35)" />.

The order S, Q, P, R ranks **distance**, not efficiency. R covers the shortest distance yet is the most fuel efficient.

So the order is R, S, P, Q.
