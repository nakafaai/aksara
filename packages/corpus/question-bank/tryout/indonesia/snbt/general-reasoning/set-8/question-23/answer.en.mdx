Let <InlineMath math="n_B" /> and <InlineMath math="n_C" /> count users of only B and only C.

Subtract both pairwise intersections from each service total. Users of all three were subtracted twice, so add them back once:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}n_B&=79-32-22+11\\&=36\end{aligned}" />
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}n_C&=66-27-22+11\\&=28\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

<BlockMath math="36+28=64" />
