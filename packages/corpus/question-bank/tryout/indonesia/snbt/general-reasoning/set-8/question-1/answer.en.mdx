Shop C revenue rises from <InlineMath math="280" /> in year <InlineMath math="2" /> to <InlineMath math="320" /> in year <InlineMath math="3" />.

Let <InlineMath math="H_0" /> denote the previous year's revenue and <InlineMath math="H_1" /> the next year's revenue. The percentage increase <InlineMath math="p" /> uses the previous year as its base:

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{H_1-H_0}{H_0}\times100\%" />

Substitute the chart values:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{320-280}{280}\times100\%\\&=\frac{40}{280}\times100\%\\&=\frac17\times100\%\\&\approx14.29\%\end{aligned}" />

Rounded to the nearest whole percent, the increase is **<InlineMath math="14\%" />**.
