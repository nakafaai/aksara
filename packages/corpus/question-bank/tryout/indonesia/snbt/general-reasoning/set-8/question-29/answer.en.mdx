The two balls are selected without regard to order. The number of equally likely pairs is:

<BlockMath math="\binom{12}{2}=\frac{12\times11}{2}=66" />

The mixed-colour prize rises by <InlineMath math="20\%" />. In thousand rupiah, the new prize is:

<BlockMath math="30\times\frac{120}{100}=36" />

| Pair | Count | Prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Red and red | <InlineMath math="\binom42=6" /> | <InlineMath math="60" /> |
| Red and blue | <InlineMath math="4\times8=32" /> | <InlineMath math="36" /> |
| Blue and blue | <InlineMath math="\binom82=28" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

Let <InlineMath math="X" /> be the payout in thousand rupiah. The expected value adds the products of **prize and probability** for each pair:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\mathrm{E}[X]&amp;=\frac{60\times6+36\times32}{66}\\&amp;=\frac{360+1152}{66}\\&amp;=\frac{1512}{66}\\&amp;=\frac{252}{11}\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, the new expected payout is <InlineMath math="252/11" /> thousand rupiah.
