Gilang is **certainly** on the team, since without Gilang at most three athletes can be chosen. So we only pick three more athletes besides Gilang. Split the cases by **Indra**, because Indra triggers several rules.

**Case 1: Indra is chosen**. Indra forces Joko in, Joko rules out Kiki, Indra rules out Hana, and then Lusi must join. Gilang, Indra, Joko, and Lusi already make four. That is **1** team.

**Case 2: Indra is not chosen**. The other three come from Fani, Hana, Joko, Kiki, and Lusi, without Joko and Kiki together, and with at least one of Hana and Lusi. All possibilities:

| Other three athletes | Rule check |

| Fani, Hana, Joko | allowed |
| Fani, Hana, Kiki | allowed |
| Fani, Hana, Lusi | allowed |
| Fani, Joko, Lusi | allowed |
| Fani, Kiki, Lusi | allowed |
| Hana, Joko, Lusi | allowed |
| Hana, Kiki, Lusi | allowed |

The other triples fail: Fani, Joko, and Kiki, Hana, Joko, and Kiki, and Joko, Kiki, and Lusi all put Joko and Kiki together. That is **7** teams.
