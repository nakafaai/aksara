When both pumps run together, **add their filling rates**. One supplies <InlineMath math="1/16" /> of a tank per hour and the other supplies <InlineMath math="1/20" />.

During the first <InlineMath math="5" /> hours, both pumps fill this fraction of the tank:

<BlockMath math="5\left(\frac1{16}+\frac1{20}\right)=5\left(\frac5{80}+\frac4{80}\right)=\frac{45}{80}=\frac9{16}" />

Pump <InlineMath math="A" /> then runs alone for <InlineMath math="2" /> hours. The remaining fraction is:

<BlockMath math="1-\frac9{16}-\frac2{16}=\frac{16-9-2}{16}=\frac5{16}" />

The new rate of <InlineMath math="A" />, in tanks per hour, is:

<BlockMath math="\left(1+\frac{25}{100}\right)\frac1{16}=\frac54\times\frac1{16}=\frac5{64}" />

The rest takes <InlineMath math="\frac5{16}\div\frac5{64}=\frac5{16}\times\frac{64}{5}=4" /> hours. Total time, in hours:

<BlockMath math="5+2+4=11" />
