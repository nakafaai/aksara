The price per GB is the price divided by the number of GB. What you have to watch is **which GB go in the denominator**: all the quota, or only the quota that person can really use.

The first statement is false. A customer who never goes online during night hours can only use main quota, so the denominator is main quota alone.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Sedang package}&=\frac{45{,}000}{10}=4{,}500\text{ rupiah per GB}\\
\text{Besar package}&=\frac{70{,}000}{18}\approx3{,}889\text{ rupiah per GB}
\end{aligned}" />

The Besar package is cheaper. The statement looks true if you divide by all the quota, because <InlineMath math="45{,}000:20=2{,}250" /> really is smaller than <InlineMath math="70{,}000:30\approx2{,}333" />.

The second statement is true. This time the denominator is all the quota.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Malam package}&=\frac{15{,}000}{15}=1{,}000\text{ rupiah per GB}\\
\text{Half the Besar price}&\approx\frac{2{,}333}{2}\approx1{,}167\text{ rupiah per GB}
\end{aligned}" />

Since <InlineMath math="1{,}000<1{,}167" />, the statement is true.

The third statement is true. In the Sedang package, night quota is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{10}{20}=50\%" /> of all the quota. In the Besar package, the share is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{12}{30}=40\%" />. The Besar package has more night quota, but a smaller share of it.

The fourth statement is false. Two Sedang packages do give <InlineMath math="2\cdot10=20" /> GB of main quota, more than <InlineMath math="18" /> GB. But they cost <InlineMath math="2\cdot45{,}000=90{,}000" /> rupiah, which is more than <InlineMath math="70{,}000" /> rupiah. A combined statement is only true when both parts are true.

So the second and third statements are true, while the first and fourth are false.
