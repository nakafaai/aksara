The conclusion states a cause: riders at 06.30 to 08.00 fell **because** the early fare was cut. To judge it, we must rule out other causes, such as a seasonal pattern.

If riders in that slot fall from February to April every year even without a fare change, this year's drop is **not necessarily** due to the new fare. If such a drop never happened before, the operator's conclusion gets stronger. The answer to this question changes how we judge the conclusion.

The other questions do not help:

- Fares in other cities do not explain the change in corridor <InlineMath math="3" />.
- The number of buses does not show why riders in one slot fell, unless we learn that it changed.
- Next year's fare plans have nothing to do with the cause of a change that already happened.
- Trip length is the same for every slot and does not explain riders shifting.

So the most important question is whether a similar drop happened in earlier years, before the early fare was cut.
