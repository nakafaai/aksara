After adding water, the mixture volume is <InlineMath math="50+5=55\text{ L}" />. At <InlineMath math="50\%" /> concentration, the solute volume is:

<BlockMath math="0.5\times55=27.5\text{ L}" />

Removing <InlineMath math="11\text{ L}" /> from a homogeneous mixture leaves this fraction of each component:

<BlockMath math="\frac{55-11}{55}=\frac45" />

Thus, the remaining solute volume is:

<BlockMath math="\frac45\times27.5=22\text{ L}" />
