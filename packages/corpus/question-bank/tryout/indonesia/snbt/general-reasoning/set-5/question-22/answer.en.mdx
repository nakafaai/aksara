After adding water, the mixture volume is <InlineMath math="50+5=55\text{ L}" />. At <InlineMath math="50\%" /> concentration, the solute volume is:

<BlockMath math="0.5\times55=27.5\text{ L}" />

Removing <InlineMath math="11\text{ L}" /> from a **homogeneous mixture** removes water and solute in the same proportion as the mixture. The fraction of solute remaining is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{55-11}{55}=\frac45" />

Thus, the remaining solute volume is:

<BlockMath math="\frac45\times27.5=22\text{ L}" />

You can also check the result by calculating the solute removed: <InlineMath math="0.5\times11=5.5\text{ L}" />. The remaining solute is <InlineMath math="27.5-5.5=22\text{ L}" />. The starting volumes of the two solutions are unnecessary because the final concentration and mixture volume are already known.
