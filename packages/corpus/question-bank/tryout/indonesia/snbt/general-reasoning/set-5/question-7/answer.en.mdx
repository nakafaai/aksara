This is a classification task, so the conclusion must follow from the labels on the assigned card. General claims about nutrition or body weight do not determine the class.

The papaya card says:

- water: **high**;
- fat: **none**;
- carbohydrates: **low**.

The first, second, third, and fifth options restate those labels. The statement that the fruit **provides fat** directly contradicts the label **none**.

Therefore, the incompatible statement is **the card shows that the fruit provides fat**.
