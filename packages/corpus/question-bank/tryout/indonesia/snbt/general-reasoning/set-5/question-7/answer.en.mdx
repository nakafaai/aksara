On the papaya card, water is **high**, fat is **low**, and carbohydrates are **low**.

The statements about high water, low carbohydrates, low fat, and carbohydrates not being high agree with the card. **High fat** contradicts **low fat**.

Therefore, the incompatible statement is **the card shows high fat**.
