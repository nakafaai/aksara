Find a group that **must have both documents**. Use the rules in the problem to separate guaranteed ownership from what remains unknown.

| WNI group | Birth certificate | ID card (KTP) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| All ages | Guaranteed | Not guaranteed for everyone |
| Over <InlineMath math="17" /> | Guaranteed | Guaranteed |
| At most <InlineMath math="17" /> | Guaranteed | Not determined by the premises |

The premises explicitly say that **some Jakarta residents exceed that age limit**. Choose one such resident. They have a birth certificate because they are WNI, and they also have an ID card because they are over <InlineMath math="17" />. Thus some Jakarta residents must have both documents.

The rules do not guarantee an ID card for every Jakarta resident. For example, a <InlineMath math="10" />-year-old resident who has a birth certificate but no ID card would satisfy every premise. In contrast, any option asserting a resident without a birth certificate contradicts the rule for all WNI. The "exactly one document" claim also fails because the older group has both.
