**Analysis of Jakarta Residents:**
1.  **Jakarta Residents are WNI**: Therefore, *all* Jakarta residents must have a **Birth Certificate**.
2.  **Age of Jakarta Residents**: Some are **<InlineMath math="> 17\text{ years}" /> old**, so they have a Birth Certificate **and** an ID Card.

The existence of Jakarta residents over <InlineMath math="17\text{ years}" /> old is stated directly in the premises. Because they are WNI, this group must possess both documents.

Therefore, the valid conclusion is that **some Jakarta residents have a birth certificate and ID card (KTP)**.
