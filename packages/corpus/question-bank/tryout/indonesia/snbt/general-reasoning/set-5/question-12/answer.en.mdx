First identify the **actor and the thing being eaten**. In the target sentence, many cobras are the actor and a kitten is the object.

The example `yum ybeeppz! ong!meeongw` places the verb first, the object with `y-` second, and the actor with `ong-` last. Use the given plural form `beeppzpz!` for many cobras.

| Position | Role in the target sentence | Required form |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| First | Verb eats | `yum` |
| Second | Object kitten | `y-` + `!meeongw` gives `y!meeongw` |
| Third | Actor many cobras | `ong-` + `beeppzpz!` gives `ongbeeppzpz!` |

Join the three forms: `yum y!meeongw ongbeeppzpz!`, matching option E. Keep the object and actor prefixes distinct. The form `yum ybeeppzpz! ong!meeongw` would instead mean that the kitten eats many cobras.
