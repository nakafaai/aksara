# Given

Two recurring events with different periods coincide on **Monday, 2 March 2026**.

# Asked

How many times they coincide on **non-Monday days** before meeting again on a Monday.

# Solution

> Find the gap between joint meetings using the least common multiple, then count how many meetings pass before the weekday cycles back to Monday.

You need to line up three things in order: when the two events meet, when a meeting lands on a Monday, and how many meetings happen in between.

The two events meet whenever the number of days gone by is divisible by <InlineMath math="6" /> and by <InlineMath math="8" />. The smallest such number is the least common multiple of <InlineMath math="6" /> and <InlineMath math="8" />.

<BlockMath math="\operatorname{lcm}(6,8)=24" />

So they meet every <InlineMath math="24" /> days, not every <InlineMath math="6\cdot8=48" /> days.

Monday comes round every <InlineMath math="7" /> days. A meeting lands on a Monday again when the number of days gone by is also divisible by <InlineMath math="7" />. The numbers <InlineMath math="24" /> and <InlineMath math="7" /> share no factor other than <InlineMath math="1" />, so **the first meeting on a Monday comes after <InlineMath math="24\cdot7=168" /> days**.

Now count the meetings. In <InlineMath math="168" /> days there are <InlineMath math="168:24=7" /> meetings, on days <InlineMath math="24" />, <InlineMath math="48" />, <InlineMath math="72" />, <InlineMath math="96" />, <InlineMath math="120" />, <InlineMath math="144" /> and <InlineMath math="168" />. Meeting number <InlineMath math="7" /> is the Monday meeting itself, so it doesn't count.

<BlockMath math="7-1=6" />

Check those six meetings: the remainders of <InlineMath math="24" />, <InlineMath math="48" />, <InlineMath math="72" />, <InlineMath math="96" />, <InlineMath math="120" /> and <InlineMath math="144" /> on division by <InlineMath math="7" /> are <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="6" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="5" />, <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />. None is <InlineMath math="0" />, so none of the six falls on a Monday.

The chart below draws that remainder for all seven meetings. **The bar only hits zero at day <InlineMath math="168" />**, so the six meetings before it fall on days other than Monday.

You'd get <InlineMath math="7" /> times by counting the Monday meeting too. You'd get <InlineMath math="20" /> times or <InlineMath math="27" /> times by counting only the market days, <InlineMath math="168:8-1" />, or only the health post days, <InlineMath math="168:6-1" />.

# Answer

So the two events take place together **<InlineMath math="6" />** times on days other than Monday.
