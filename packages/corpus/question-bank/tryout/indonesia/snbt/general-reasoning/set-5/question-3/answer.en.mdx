The passage separates three ideas that should not be collapsed:

- **Definition:** stunting is low height for age.
- **Common pathway:** chronic or recurrent undernutrition.
- **Possible consequences:** reduced physical or cognitive potential, with outcomes that can differ between children.

That makes absolute statements such as "only genetic factors" and "always causes cognitive disability" unsupported. Low weight for height is also a different anthropometric condition, not the definition given here.

The supported conclusion is therefore: **stunting is low height for age and is commonly linked to chronic or recurrent undernutrition**.
