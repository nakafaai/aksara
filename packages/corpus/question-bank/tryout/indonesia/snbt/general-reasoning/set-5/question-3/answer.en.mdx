# Given

The passage and table provide **catch, price, and fuel figures for large and small boats** to evaluate four statements.

# Asked

Whether each statement **can be concluded** or **cannot be concluded** from the data.

# Solution

> A what-if claim about something that never happened cannot be proven unless the passage gives a real comparison group.

A statement can be concluded when the numbers in the text and the table are enough to settle it. A statement about what _would_ have happened needs a **comparison group**, meaning similar boats that actually went through that situation. If there's no such group, the statement can't be concluded.

The first statement can be concluded. All the tuna is sold at the price in the table, so add up the group's catch and multiply by the price after dividing prices by <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> rupiah.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{2024 season}&=(20\cdot10{,}000+30\cdot4{,}000)\cdot20=320{,}000\cdot20=6{,}400{,}000\\
\text{2025 season}&=(20\cdot12{,}800+30\cdot4{,}000)\cdot15=376{,}000\cdot15=5{,}640{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

The catch goes up, but sales fall from <InlineMath math="6{,}400" /> million to <InlineMath math="5{,}640" /> million rupiah.

The second statement can't be concluded. No large boat fished in the 2025 season without the rafts. Without that comparison, you don't know whether the catch would have stayed at <InlineMath math="100" /> kg, risen or fallen, because the amount of fish in the sea can change from season to season.

The third statement can't be concluded. The text says small boats can't reach the rafts, so there's no data for small boats out there. You can't simply move the large boats' <InlineMath math="160" /> kg onto a smaller boat.

The fourth statement can be concluded, even though the margin is thin.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Large boat}&=80\cdot160\cdot15-80\cdot500=152{,}000\\
\text{Small boat}&=100\cdot40\cdot15-100\cdot100=50{,}000\\
3\cdot50{,}000&=150{,}000<152{,}000
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below compares those two figures. **The large boat's bar is only slightly taller**, but it is taller.

# Answer

So the first and fourth statements can be concluded, while the second and third can't.
