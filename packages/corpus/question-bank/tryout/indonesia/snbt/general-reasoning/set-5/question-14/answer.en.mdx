A cylinder’s volume uses its **radius**, but the question gives its diameter. Match the length units to the flow rate, which is measured in cubic decimetres per minute.

#### Calculating the cylinder volume

One metre equals ten decimetres, so the height is <InlineMath math="4.2\times10=42\text{ dm}" />. The diameter is <InlineMath math="3\times10=30\text{ dm}" />, and the **radius is half the diameter**, giving <InlineMath math="30/2=15\text{ dm}" />.

#### Converting volume to time

At a **constant flow rate**, the same volume leaves the tank each minute. Divide the full volume by the leakage rate to obtain the time in minutes:

<BlockMath math="t\approx\frac{29{,}700}{12}=2{,}475\text{ min}" />

Separate the minutes into complete hours and a remainder:

<BlockMath math="2{,}475=41\times60+15" />

The approximate emptying time is <InlineMath math="41\text{ hours }15\text{ minutes}" />.
