Check what happens if Gilang is **not** chosen. By rule 1, Fani cannot be chosen either, since Fani always brings Gilang. The four athletes must then come from Hana, Indra, Joko, Kiki, and Lusi.

- At most one of Hana and Indra can be chosen (rule 2).
- At most one of Joko and Kiki can be chosen (rule 3).
- Lusi adds at most one more.

So without Gilang at most **three** athletes can be chosen, but the team needs four. Gilang is therefore **certainly** chosen.

No other athlete is certain. For example, the team Gilang, Hana, Joko, and Lusi leaves out Fani, the team Gilang, Indra, Joko, and Lusi leaves out Hana, and the team Fani, Gilang, Hana, and Kiki leaves out both Lusi and Joko. All three teams meet every rule.

So the athlete who is certainly chosen is Gilang.
