Convert the dimensions to decimetres. The height is <InlineMath math="h=42\text{ dm}" /> and the radius is <InlineMath math="r=15\text{ dm}" />. Using the given approximation, the water volume is:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="V=\pi r^2h" />

  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}V&\approx\frac{22}{7}\times15^2\times42\\&=29{,}700\text{ dm}^3\end{aligned}" />
</MathContainer>

Divide the volume by the constant flow rate. The time in minutes is:

<BlockMath math="t\approx\frac{29{,}700}{12}=2{,}475" />

Since <InlineMath math="2{,}475=41\times60+15" />, emptying takes approximately <InlineMath math="41" /> hours <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes.
