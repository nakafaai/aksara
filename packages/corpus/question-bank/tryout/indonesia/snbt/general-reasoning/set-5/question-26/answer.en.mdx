Sufficient.

**Statement (2) alone**:

<BlockMath math="3a-a=18{,}000\quad\Rightarrow\quad 2a=18{,}000\quad\Rightarrow\quad a=9{,}000" />

Both statements give the same answer, <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}9{,}000" />. A common mistake is to think the two statements must be combined, but the "three times" relation is already given in the question.

So statement (1) alone is sufficient, and statement (2) alone is sufficient too.
