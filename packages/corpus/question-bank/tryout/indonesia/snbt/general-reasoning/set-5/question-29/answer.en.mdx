There are <InlineMath math="\binom{12}{2}=\frac{12\times11}{2}=66" /> **equally likely** pairs. Group them by colour.

| Pair | Pair count | Payout |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Red-red | <InlineMath math="\binom72=\frac{7\times6}{2}=21" /> | <InlineMath math="60" /> |
| Mixed | <InlineMath math="7\times5=35" /> | <InlineMath math="30" /> |
| Blue-blue | <InlineMath math="\binom52=\frac{5\times4}{2}=10" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

Payouts are in thousand rupiah. The expected payout is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}E&amp;=\frac{60\times21+30\times35}{66}\\&amp;=\frac{1260+1050}{66}\\&amp;=\frac{2310}{66}=35\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the entry fee to find the expected net gain, in thousand rupiah:

<BlockMath math="35-30=5" />

An expected value is a **long-run average**, not a guaranteed payment in one game. A single game has a net gain of <InlineMath math="30" />, <InlineMath math="0" />, or <InlineMath math="-30" /> thousand rupiah, depending on the pair drawn.
