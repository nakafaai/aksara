There are <InlineMath math="\binom{12}{2}=66" /> equally likely pairs. Group them by colour.

| Pair | Pair count | Payout |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Red-red | <InlineMath math="\binom72=21" /> | <InlineMath math="60" /> |
| Mixed | <InlineMath math="7\times5=35" /> | <InlineMath math="30" /> |
| Blue-blue | <InlineMath math="\binom52=10" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

Payouts are in thousand rupiah. The expected payout is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}E&=\frac{60\times21+30\times35}{66}\\&=35\end{aligned}" />

Subtract the entry fee to find the expected net gain, in thousand rupiah:

<BlockMath math="35-30=5" />
