# Given

The six original rules combine with a seventh rule that **Danu and Cempaka cannot be picked together**.

# Asked

Whether each statement is **True** (must hold) or **False** across all teams satisfying all seven rules.

# Solution

> An extra constraint never adds new teams, and instead simply crosses out existing legal teams that pair Danu with Cempaka.

The seventh rule doesn't change the six old ones. It only throws out some teams that used to be allowed. So the safest way is to **list every team that meets the six rules**, then cross out the teams that hold both Danu and Cempaka.

Split on Gendis, because rule 4 and rule 6 both depend on Gendis.

- Gendis isn't chosen. Cempaka must be chosen (rule 4). The only physics member left is Haikal, so Haikal is chosen (rule 1), then Bening joins (rule 5), and Elvira is out (rule 3). The fourth member has to come from chemistry, so it's Danu or Fauzan.
- Gendis is chosen. Fauzan is out (rule 6). If Haikal joins, Bening joins, Elvira is out, and the chemistry member must be Danu. If Haikal doesn't join, the other three members come from Arka, Bening, Cempaka, Danu and Elvira while keeping rules 1, 2 and 3.

| Team that meets the six rules | Danu and Cempaka together? |
| --- | --- |
| Bening, Cempaka, Danu, Haikal | Yes, crossed out |
| Bening, Cempaka, Fauzan, Haikal | No |
| Bening, Danu, Gendis, Haikal | No |
| Arka, Bening, Danu, Gendis | No |
| Arka, Cempaka, Danu, Gendis | Yes, crossed out |
| Arka, Danu, Elvira, Gendis | No |
| Bening, Cempaka, Danu, Gendis | Yes, crossed out |
| Cempaka, Danu, Elvira, Gendis | Yes, crossed out |

Four teams are left. Now test each statement on those four teams.

- The first statement is true. Two teams have Arka without Haikal, and two have Haikal without Arka. No team has both or neither.
- The second statement is false. The team Bening, Cempaka, Fauzan, Haikal has no Gendis.
- The third statement is false. The team Arka, Danu, Elvira, Gendis has no Bening.
- The fourth statement is true. Elvira appears in only one team, Arka, Danu, Elvira, Gendis, and that team includes Arka.

# Answer

So the first and fourth statements are **true, while the second and third are false**.
