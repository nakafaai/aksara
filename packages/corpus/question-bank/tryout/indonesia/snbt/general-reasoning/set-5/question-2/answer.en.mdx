The conclusion says the fish near the devices **came from other waters**. Evidence that strengthens it must show that other waters lost fish.

If the catch per trip in waters far from the devices fell compared with the year before, part of the regency caught less exactly when the catch near the devices rose. Together with the unchanged total, this pattern **fits fish moving**.

The other options do not strengthen it:

- Fish prices say nothing about where the fish near the devices came from.
- If the fish are species never caught there before, they did not move from the regency's own waters. This statement weakens the conclusion.
- Maintaining the coconut leaves has nothing to do with where the fish come from.
- The depth of each device does not show whether fish moved or increased.

So the statement that strengthens the conclusion most is that the catch per trip in waters far from the devices fell compared with the year before.
