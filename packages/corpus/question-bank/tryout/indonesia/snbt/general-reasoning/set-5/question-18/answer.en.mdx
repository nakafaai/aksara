# Given

Four finalists make statements about their ranks, and **exactly one lies while the other three tell the truth**.

# Asked

The ranking statement that **MUST** be true across every consistent case.

# Solution

> Test each of the four suspects as the lone liar one by one, then see which ranking fact stays true in every scenario that works.

You don't know who's lying, so try the four possibilities one at a time. For each one, treat the other three statements as true and see whether the ranking can be built. Notice one thing first: Ilham says Juwita took rank <InlineMath math="1" />, while Juwita denies taking rank <InlineMath math="1" />. **They can't both be telling the truth**, so the liar must be Ilham or Juwita. Laksmi and Mahesa are certainly truthful.

From Laksmi and Mahesa you get a fixed order: Mahesa ranks better than Ilham, and Laksmi sits exactly one place below Ilham.

If Ilham is the liar, Juwita is truthful. Juwita isn't at rank <InlineMath math="1" />, and Mahesa ranks better than Laksmi. Juwita can't slip in between Ilham and Laksmi, so there are two arrangements.

If Juwita is the liar, Ilham is truthful, so Juwita is at rank <InlineMath math="1" />. The other three fill ranks <InlineMath math="2" /> to <InlineMath math="4" /> in the order Mahesa, Ilham, Laksmi.

| Liar | Rank <InlineMath math="1" /> | Rank <InlineMath math="2" /> | Rank <InlineMath math="3" /> | Rank <InlineMath math="4" /> |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ilham | Mahesa | Ilham | Laksmi | Juwita |
| Ilham | Mahesa | Juwita | Ilham | Laksmi |
| Juwita | Juwita | Mahesa | Ilham | Laksmi |

A statement that's certainly true has to hold in all three rows.

- Juwita is at rank <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="2" /> or <InlineMath math="1" />. Juwita is never at rank <InlineMath math="3" />, so this statement is certainly true.
- Laksmi at rank <InlineMath math="4" /> fails in the first row.
- Ilham as the liar fails in the third row.
- Mahesa at rank <InlineMath math="1" /> fails in the third row.
- Ilham at rank <InlineMath math="3" /> fails in the first row.

# Answer

So what's certainly true is that Juwita didn't take rank <InlineMath math="3" />.
