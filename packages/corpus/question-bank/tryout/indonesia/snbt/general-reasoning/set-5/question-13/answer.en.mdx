The statement has the form "every stall satisfies <InlineMath math="p \lor q" />", with <InlineMath math="p" />: accepts cashless payment and <InlineMath math="q" />: displays a price list. Negate it in **two steps**.

- The negation of "every" is "**there is**".
- The negation of <InlineMath math="p \lor q" /> is <InlineMath math="\lnot p \land \lnot q" />: does not accept cashless payment **and** does not display a price list.

So the negation is "There is a stall in Pasar Baru that does not accept cashless payment and does not display a price list" (*Ada kios ... yang tidak menerima pembayaran nontunai dan tidak memasang daftar harga*).

The other options are wrong:

- "Every stall" does not negate the quantifier. That statement is much stronger than the negation needed.
- *Atau* (or) after the negation is wrong. The negation of <InlineMath math="p \lor q" /> uses *dan* (and).
- "No stall ..." means every stall fails both, as strong as the first option.
- A stall that accepts cashless payment still satisfies the original statement, so it does not refute it.

So the negation is that there is a stall in Pasar Baru that does not accept cashless payment and does not display a price list.
