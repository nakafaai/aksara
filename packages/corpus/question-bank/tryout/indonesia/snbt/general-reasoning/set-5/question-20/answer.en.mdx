# Given

Four clues narrow down the **arrival order of six volunteers**, including exactly two arrivals between Naufal and Tiara.

# Asked

The **extra clue** that locks down the complete arrival order of all six volunteers.

# Solution

> List every order that survives the first four clues, then pick the extra fact that eliminates all but one single order.

An extra fact is enough when, once you add it, only **one** order is left. So list every order that fits the four facts first, then test each option.

Start with fact 3, because it's the tightest. Naufal and Tiara are exactly two volunteers apart, so their positions differ by <InlineMath math="3" />. Tiara arrives after Pandu and Ratri (fact 1), so Tiara is at position <InlineMath math="3" /> at the earliest. Naufal can't arrive after Tiara, because that would put Naufal at position <InlineMath math="6" />, and Olivia has to arrive after Naufal (fact 2). So Naufal arrives before Tiara, and there are three possibilities.

- Naufal at <InlineMath math="1" />, Tiara at <InlineMath math="4" />. Pandu and Ratri take positions <InlineMath math="2" /> and <InlineMath math="3" />. Satria and Olivia take positions <InlineMath math="5" /> and <InlineMath math="6" />.
- Naufal at <InlineMath math="2" />, Tiara at <InlineMath math="5" />. Pandu can't be first (fact 4), so Pandu and Ratri are at <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="4" />. Satria is at <InlineMath math="1" /> and Olivia at <InlineMath math="6" />.
- Naufal at <InlineMath math="3" />, Tiara at <InlineMath math="6" />. Only Satria can take position <InlineMath math="1" />. Pandu is at <InlineMath math="2" />. Ratri and Olivia take positions <InlineMath math="4" /> and <InlineMath math="5" />, and they can swap.

| Position | <InlineMath math="1" /> | <InlineMath math="2" /> | <InlineMath math="3" /> | <InlineMath math="4" /> | <InlineMath math="5" /> | <InlineMath math="6" /> |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First order | Naufal | Pandu | Ratri | Tiara | Satria | Olivia |
| Second order | Satria | Naufal | Pandu | Ratri | Tiara | Olivia |
| Third order | Satria | Pandu | Naufal | Ratri | Olivia | Tiara |
| Fourth order | Satria | Pandu | Naufal | Olivia | Ratri | Tiara |

Now test each extra fact.

- Satria arrives first: three orders still fit.
- Olivia arrives before Tiara: the third and fourth orders still fit.
- Olivia arrives before Ratri: **only the fourth order** fits.
- Ratri arrives fourth: the second and third orders still fit.
- Naufal arrives before Pandu: the first and second orders still fit.

# Answer

So the fact that's enough is that Olivia arrives before Ratri.
