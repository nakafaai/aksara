# Given

The table lists five letters in the **top row B, D, G, K, P** and **bottom row Y, V, T, Q, O**.

# Asked

The **<InlineMath math="7" />th letter** in the top row and in the bottom row respectively.

# Solution

> Convert every letter into its alphabet number from one to twenty-six so the step sizes stand out, then take two more steps past the fifth entry.

Turn each letter into its position in the alphabet, from A <InlineMath math="=1" /> to Z <InlineMath math="=26" />. That makes the jumps easy to see. Also remember that the question asks for position <InlineMath math="7" />, so you have to step **twice**, not once.

The top row moves forward with a jump that grows by one each time.

<BlockMath math="2\xrightarrow{+2}4\xrightarrow{+3}7\xrightarrow{+4}11\xrightarrow{+5}16\xrightarrow{+6}22\xrightarrow{+7}29" />

Number <InlineMath math="22" /> is V. Number <InlineMath math="29" /> has gone past Z, so subtract <InlineMath math="26" /> because the alphabet wraps around. That gives <InlineMath math="29-26=3" />, which is C.

The bottom row moves backward with a jump that alternates between <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="2" />.

<BlockMath math="25\xrightarrow{-3}22\xrightarrow{-2}20\xrightarrow{-3}17\xrightarrow{-2}15\xrightarrow{-3}12\xrightarrow{-2}10" />

Number <InlineMath math="12" /> is L, and number <InlineMath math="10" /> is J. So position <InlineMath math="6" /> holds V and L, while **position <InlineMath math="7" /> holds C and J**.

Each wrong answer has a clear source.

- V and L are what sits in position <InlineMath math="6" />. That's what you get if you stop one step too early.
- B or D in the top row comes from miscounting as you pass Z, subtracting <InlineMath math="27" /> or <InlineMath math="25" /> when the alphabet has <InlineMath math="26" /> letters.
- I in the bottom row comes from stepping back <InlineMath math="3" /> twice in a row, when the jumps alternate.

# Answer

So the letters in position <InlineMath math="7" /> are C in the top row and J in the bottom row.
