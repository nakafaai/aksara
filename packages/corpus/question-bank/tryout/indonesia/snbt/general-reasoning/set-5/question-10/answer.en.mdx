The words **not all** guarantee at least one Factory X bread that does not use high-protein flour. The rule for the remaining bread then determines the flour used by that bread.

| Premise | Guaranteed consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Some bread uses high-protein flour | The high-protein group is not empty. |
| Not all bread uses high-protein flour | At least one bread lies outside that group. |
| All remaining bread uses low-protein flour | Bread outside the high-protein group uses low-protein flour. |

Choose a bread whose existence is guaranteed by the second row. The third row tells you that it uses **low-protein flour**. Therefore, some Factory X bread uses low-protein wheat flour, as option D states.

The "all high-protein" option contradicts the second premise, while "none high-protein" contradicts the first. The carbohydrate premise also rules out claims about Factory X bread without carbohydrates. The passage provides no information about bread from other factories.
