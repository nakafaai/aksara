Focus on the two flour groups:

1. Some Factory X bread uses high-protein flour.
2. Not all Factory X bread uses high-protein flour.
3. Every bread outside the high-protein group uses low-protein flour.

The second premise guarantees that at least one Factory X bread lies outside the high-protein group. The third premise places every such bread in the low-protein group.

The carbohydrate premise also rules out any option claiming that a Factory X bread contains no carbohydrates.

Therefore, **some bread from Factory X uses low-protein wheat flour** must be true.
