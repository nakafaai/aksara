# Given

Four statements link **drama club, arts-festival committee, and photography club**, where some means at least one.

# Asked

The lone conclusion that is **NOT valid** (the exception) under the four statements.

# Solution

> Saying some members belong to another club guarantees at least one overlap, yet it never forbids every single member from overlapping too.

You're looking for the one conclusion the four facts **don't** guarantee. The other four are valid, so each of them can be proved. The key to any conclusion with _some_ is to point at one person who **must exist**.

Fact 4 guarantees there's a student, call them student X, who belongs to both the photography club and the theatre club. Fact 3 guarantees there's a student Y, a theatre club member who's on the arts night committee.

- Some exhibition participants are theatre club members. Valid, because student X is in the photography club, so X joins the exhibition (fact 1), and X is also in the theatre club.
- No photography club member is on the arts night committee. Valid, because every photography club member joins the exhibition (fact 1), and no exhibition participant is on the committee (fact 2).
- Some theatre club members don't join the exhibition. Valid, because student Y is on the committee, so Y isn't an exhibition participant (fact 2).
- Some theatre club members aren't photography club members. Valid, because student Y doesn't join the exhibition, while every photography club member does (fact 1).

One conclusion is left: some photography club members aren't theatre club members. This one isn't valid. Fact 4 only says at least one photography club member is in the theatre club. It doesn't stop **all** photography club members from being in the theatre club. For example, the photography club might have student X as its only member. All four facts still hold, yet no photography club member is outside the theatre club.

The tempting mistake here is reading _some_ as _only some, not all_. In logic, _some_ means at least one, and it may well be all.

# Answer

So the conclusion that isn't valid is that **some photography club members aren't theatre club members**.
