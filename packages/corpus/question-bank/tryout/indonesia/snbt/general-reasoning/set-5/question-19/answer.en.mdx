A good summary must preserve the paragraph's two central ideas:

1. change occurs in every society;
2. its sources can be internal or external.

The other options remove one source, make it exclusive, or reverse the stated research method.

Therefore, the best summary is **every society changes, and the sources of social change can be internal or external**.
