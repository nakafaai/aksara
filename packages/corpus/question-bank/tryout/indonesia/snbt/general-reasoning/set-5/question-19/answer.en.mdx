A summary must preserve the **scope of change** and the **sources of change**. The paragraph says that every society changes and that change can originate within or outside a society.

| Option | Fit with the passage |
| :--- | :--- |
| Only relationships between individuals | Too narrow: omits group relationships and outside influence. |
| External contact is the only source | Removes internal sources. |
| Every society changes, with internal or external sources | Preserves both central ideas. |
| Study all changes without identifying a primary one | Reverses the stated research approach. |
| Change occurs only through another society | Again excludes internal sources. |

Option C is therefore the best summary. The word **only** in the distractors turns several possible sources into an exclusive restriction that the paragraph does not impose.
