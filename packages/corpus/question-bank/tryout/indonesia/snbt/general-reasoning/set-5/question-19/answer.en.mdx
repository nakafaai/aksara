Start from the statement with **some** (*sebagian*), since only it guarantees that certain people exist. Some blue-badge wearers work in the second-floor gallery. By the third statement, second-floor gallery staff do **not** work at weekends. So those blue-badge wearers do not work at weekends, and the conclusion "some blue-badge wearers do not work at weekends" must be true.

The other options are not guaranteed:

- The first statement runs one way only. Blue-badge wearers may include people who never took the exam.
- The blue-badge wearers in the second-floor gallery need not be the ones who passed the Japanese exam.
- Guides who passed the exam and work elsewhere may well work at weekends.
- The second statement says only that some blue-badge wearers work in the second-floor gallery, not the reverse claim that all of that gallery's staff wear blue badges.

So the conclusion that must be true is that some blue-badge wearers do not work at weekends.
