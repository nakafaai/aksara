They are not constant, but **each difference is the sum of the two before it**: <InlineMath math="1+1=2" />, <InlineMath math="1+2=3" />, <InlineMath math="2+3=5" />. The next difference is <InlineMath math="3+5=8" />, so the next term is:

<BlockMath math="14+8=22" />

The same pattern shows in the terms themselves: from the third term on, each term is the sum of the two before it minus <InlineMath math="1" />.

<BlockMath math="2+3-1=4,\quad 3+4-1=6,\quad 4+6-1=9,\quad 6+9-1=14,\quad 9+14-1=22" />

Both ways give the same result. So the next number is <InlineMath math="22" />.
