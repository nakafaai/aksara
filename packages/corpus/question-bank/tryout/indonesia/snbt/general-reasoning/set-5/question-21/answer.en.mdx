# Given

A letter sequence has two blanks and each letter carries its **alphabet position from <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="26" />**.

# Asked

The **sum of the alphabet numbers** of the two missing letters.

# Solution

> Turn the visible letters into numbers first so the jump pattern reveals itself right away, then fill both missing slots.

Turn the letters you have into their numbers, then look at the jump from each letter to the next. Call the number of the first missing letter <InlineMath math="x" /> and the number of the last missing letter <InlineMath math="y" />.

<BlockMath math="26\xrightarrow{-1}25\xrightarrow{-3}22\xrightarrow{-2}20\longrightarrow x\longrightarrow13\xrightarrow{-5}8\longrightarrow y" />

The jumps you can see are <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, then two unknown jumps, then <InlineMath math="5" />. They don't keep growing, so try separating **the odd-numbered jumps from the even-numbered jumps**.

- Jumps <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="3" /> in the list are <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="2" />. The pattern grows by one, so jump <InlineMath math="5" /> is <InlineMath math="3" /> and jump <InlineMath math="7" /> is <InlineMath math="4" />.
- Jump <InlineMath math="2" /> in the list is <InlineMath math="3" />. If this pattern grows by one too, jump <InlineMath math="4" /> is <InlineMath math="4" /> and jump <InlineMath math="6" /> is <InlineMath math="5" />. Jump <InlineMath math="6" /> really is <InlineMath math="5" />, so the guess fits.

The chart below separates the two jump patterns. **Each pattern grows by one every time it shows up**, so jumps <InlineMath math="4" /> and <InlineMath math="7" /> are both <InlineMath math="4" />.

Now fill in the missing letters.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
x&=20-4=16\\
y&=8-4=4
\end{aligned}" />

Check with the letter M. From <InlineMath math="x=16" />, jump <InlineMath math="5" /> of size <InlineMath math="3" /> gives <InlineMath math="16-3=13" />, and number <InlineMath math="13" /> is indeed M. Number <InlineMath math="16" /> is P, and number <InlineMath math="4" /> is D.

<BlockMath math="16+4=20" />

You'd get <InlineMath math="21" /> by swapping the two missing jumps into <InlineMath math="3" /> then <InlineMath math="4" />, so <InlineMath math="x=17" />. Both orders land on M, so only the odd and even patterns above can settle the order.

# Answer

So the numbers of the two missing letters add up to **<InlineMath math="20" />**.
