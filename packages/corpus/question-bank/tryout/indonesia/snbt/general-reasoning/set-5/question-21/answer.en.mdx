Separate terms by **odd and even positions**, regardless of whether their values are odd or even.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="a_1,a_3,a_5,a_7:\quad4,10,16,22" />

  <BlockMath math="a_2,a_4,a_6:\quad5,10,20" />
</MathContainer>

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="10-4=16-10=22-16=6" />
  <BlockMath math="\frac{10}{5}=\frac{20}{10}=2" />
</MathContainer>

The odd-position sequence has common difference <InlineMath math="6" />, while the even-position sequence has common ratio <InlineMath math="2" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="a_9=22+6=28" />

  <BlockMath math="a_8=20\times2=40" />
</MathContainer>

Their arithmetic mean is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{28+40}{2}=\frac{68}{2}=34" />
