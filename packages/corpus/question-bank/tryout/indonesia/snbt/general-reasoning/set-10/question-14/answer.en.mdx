An order must satisfy **all conditions at once**. Before does not mean immediately before, but sessions E and B must be consecutive, with no session between them.

| Order | A before D | B immediately after E | C not adjacent to A | F before E |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="A,F,B,E,D,C" /> | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| <InlineMath math="E,B,F,A,C,D" /> | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| <InlineMath math="F,E,B,D,C,A" /> | No | Yes | No | Yes |
| <InlineMath math="A,C,D,F,E,B" /> | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| <InlineMath math="F,A,D,E,B,C" /> | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Only the last choice, <InlineMath math="F,A,D,E,B,C" />, passes all four checks.
