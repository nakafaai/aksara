The statement has the form **"p and q"**, which is true only when both parts are true. Its negation only needs to say that at least one part fails.

<BlockMath math="\sim(p\wedge q)\equiv\,\sim p\vee\sim q" />

With <InlineMath math="p" /> = registration opens on Monday and <InlineMath math="q" /> = registration closes on Friday, the negation is that registration does not open on Monday or does not close on Friday.

The other options do not fit:

- *Not ... and not ...* is too strong, since the negation already holds when just one part fails.
- *Opens on Monday or closes on Friday* denies nothing.
- An *if ..., then ...* form is not the negation of an *and* statement.
- Tuesday and Saturday are just one other case, not the negation.

So the negation is that registration does not open on Monday or does not close on Friday.
