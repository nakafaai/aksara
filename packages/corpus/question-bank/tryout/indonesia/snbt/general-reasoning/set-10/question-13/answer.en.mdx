Each pairwise intersection still contains the <InlineMath math="12" /> people in all three programs. To count people in **exactly two programs**, remove that group from each intersection.

| Group counted | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| A and B, without C | <InlineMath math="28-12=16" /> |
| A and C, without B | <InlineMath math="21-12=9" /> |
| B and C, without A | <InlineMath math="19-12=7" /> |

The remaining groups do not overlap. We can therefore add their sizes without counting anyone twice.

<BlockMath math="16+9+7=32" />

Exactly <InlineMath math="32" /> people join two programs.
