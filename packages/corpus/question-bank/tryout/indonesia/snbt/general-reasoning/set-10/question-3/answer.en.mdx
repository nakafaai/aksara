# Given

The data list **before-and-after visitor counts for both Cendana Market and Kenari Market**.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements is **True** or **False** from the market data.

# Solution

> Calculate the visitor change for each market and for their combined total before checking the four statements.

Mark each statement Benar (true) or Salah (false). Work out **the change at each market** first.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Cendana} &: 1{,}200\to1{,}800,\quad \frac{1{,}800}{1{,}200}=1.5\text{ times} \\
\text{Kenari} &: 1{,}500\to1{,}200,\quad \frac{300}{1{,}500}=20\%\text{ down} \\
\text{total} &: 2{,}700\to3{,}000,\quad \text{up }300
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below draws the change at each market and in total. **Kenari's bar is below zero**, so the total rises by only half of Cendana's rise.

  title={<>Change in visitors from before to after the renovation</>}

**Statement 1** is true. The total rose from <InlineMath math="2{,}700" /> to <InlineMath math="3{,}000" />.

**Statement 2** is false. Visitors to Kenari fell from <InlineMath math="1{,}500" /> to <InlineMath math="1{,}200" />.

**Statement 3** is false. The rise is <InlineMath math="1.5" /> times, not double.

**Statement 4** is true. A drop of <InlineMath math="300" /> from <InlineMath math="1{,}500" /> is <InlineMath math="20\%" />.

# Answer

So statements 1 and 4 are **true, while statements 2 and 3 are false**.
