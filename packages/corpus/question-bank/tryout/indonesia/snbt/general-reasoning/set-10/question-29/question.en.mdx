A box contains <InlineMath math="7" /> red balls and <InlineMath math="6" /> blue balls. Two balls are drawn together without replacement. The game pays <InlineMath math="50" /> thousand rupiah for two red balls, <InlineMath math="40" /> thousand rupiah for different colours, and nothing for two blue balls.

The operator pays <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of every stated prize. What entry fee in thousand rupiah is fair?

Each pair of balls is equally likely to be drawn. A fair fee makes the player’s expected net gain zero.
