The passage reports **one observed sequence**:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{intense rain} \longrightarrow \text{flooding} \longrightarrow \text{temporary vegetation}" />
</MathContainer>

That observation makes the proposed conclusion plausible. It gives us a reason to think the same result could occur again under similar conditions.

It does not make the result certain. Future vegetation can also depend on the duration and location of the rain, soil conditions, seeds, and temperature. One past event therefore supports **possibly true**. The available evidence is too limited for **definitely true**.
