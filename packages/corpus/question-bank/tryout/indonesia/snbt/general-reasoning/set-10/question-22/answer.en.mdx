# Given

A cross pattern adds one square per arm each step, with **figure <InlineMath math="2" /> using <InlineMath math="9" /> squares and a <InlineMath math="20\text{ cm}" /> perimeter**.

# Asked

The **perimeter** of the figure made of **<InlineMath math="41" /> small squares**.

# Solution

> Solve for the figure index from the square count first, then plug that index straight into the perimeter rule.

First find **the figure number**, then the perimeter.

Figure <InlineMath math="n" /> has one center square and four arms, each <InlineMath math="n" /> squares long.

<BlockMath math="4n+1=41\quad\Rightarrow\quad n=10" />

Each arm adds two long sides of <InlineMath math="n" /> cm and one end of <InlineMath math="1" /> cm to the perimeter.

<BlockMath math="\text{perimeter}=4\times(2n+1)=4\times21=84\text{ cm}" />

The chart below draws the squares and perimeter of figures <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="10" />. **At figure <InlineMath math="10" />, the squares reach <InlineMath math="41" /> and the perimeter <InlineMath math="84" /> cm**.
