# Given

On Saturday a group has **<InlineMath math="2" /> teachers, <InlineMath math="9" /> students with ID cards, and <InlineMath math="1" /> free <InlineMath math="4" />-year-old child** eligible for a group discount.

# Asked

The **total amount** the group must pay.

# Solution

> Count only paid tickets first to confirm the group qualifies for the bulk discount, then apply the discount to the combined subtotal.

Count **the paid tickets** first. The 4-year-old is free, so there are <InlineMath math="2+9=11" /> paid tickets, enough for the group discount.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{tickets} &= 2\times25{,}000+9\times15{,}000=185{,}000 \\
\text{Saturday extra} &= 11\times5{,}000=55{,}000 \\
\text{total} &= 185{,}000+55{,}000=240{,}000 \\
\text{after discount} &= 240{,}000-10\%\times240{,}000=216{,}000
\end{aligned}" />
