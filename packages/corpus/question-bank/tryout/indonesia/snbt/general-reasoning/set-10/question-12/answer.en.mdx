The condition in the first sentence has **two parts that must both hold**: the library is open and Rina has her card.

- Rina did not borrow, so by contraposition the two parts cannot both hold.
- The library is open, so the part that fails must be the second: Rina did not bring her card.

The other options do not fit:

- The facts say the library is open, so the first option contradicts them.
- Borrowing at another library is possible, but not certain.
- If Rina had her card, she would surely borrow, so the third option contradicts the facts.
- Rina's wishes are not discussed, so they cannot be certain.

So the conclusion that must be true is that Rina did not bring her library card.
