# Given

All six booth rules apply together with **booth XI-B placed in spot <InlineMath math="1" />**.

# Asked

Which **booth number XI-A must occupy**.

# Solution

> Once XI-B is pinned to spot one, the remaining rules lock the other booths down one by one until XI-A spot is forced.

Follow the rules **one at a time**, starting from booth 1.

- XI-B is at <InlineMath math="1" />, so X-A is at <InlineMath math="2" /> (rule 2).
- No two class X booths may be adjacent. With X-A at <InlineMath math="2" />, the other class X booths must be at <InlineMath math="4" /> and <InlineMath math="6" />.
- X-B must be at <InlineMath math="6" /> (previous question), so X-C is at <InlineMath math="4" />.
- XI-A is directly right of X-C, at <InlineMath math="5" /> (rule 5).
- XI-C takes booth 3, which is not an end and has a higher number than X-A.

The diagram below draws that chain of placements. **Each step leaves only one booth**, until XI-A lands at booth 5.

The order is XI-B, X-A, XI-C, X-C, XI-A, X-B, and every rule holds.

# Answer

So XI-A is at booth 5.
