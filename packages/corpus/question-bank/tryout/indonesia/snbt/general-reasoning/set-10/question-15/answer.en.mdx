The statement **some research collections** guarantees at least one research collection that is an encrypted archive. Follow what the rules imply for that collection.

| Applicable information | Consequence for the collection |
| --- | --- |
| The collection is an encrypted archive. | It requires a key. |
| No public dataset requires a key. | A collection requiring a key cannot be a public dataset. |

Therefore, some research collections are **not public datasets**. We do not know whether other research collections are encrypted or public. We cannot extend the conclusion to every research collection being encrypted or none being public. The rule that encrypted archives require a key also does not say that everything requiring a key is a research collection.
