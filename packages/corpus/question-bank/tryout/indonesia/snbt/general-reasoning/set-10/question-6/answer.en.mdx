Let <InlineMath math="s" /> be the number who like mathematics and <InlineMath math="t" /> the number who dislike it. The class total is <InlineMath math="s+t" />. The percentage is:

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{s}{s+t}\times100\%" />

Calculate each class. Non-integer results are rounded to one decimal place.

| Class | Total students | Percentage who like mathematics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MMM | <InlineMath math="23+40=63" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{23}{63}\times100\%\approx36.5\%" /> |
| NNN | <InlineMath math="18+20=38" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{18}{38}\times100\%\approx47.4\%" /> |
| PPP | <InlineMath math="10+15=25" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{10}{25}\times100\%=40\%" /> |
| QQQ | <InlineMath math="17+23=40" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{17}{40}\times100\%=42.5\%" /> |
| RRR | <InlineMath math="21+35=56" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{21}{56}\times100\%=37.5\%" /> |

Compare the **percentage of the whole class**, rather than the number who like mathematics alone. The classes have different sizes.

The lowest percentage is approximately <InlineMath math="36.5\%" /> in class <InlineMath math="\text{MMM}" />.
