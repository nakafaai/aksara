A good explanation has to cover **two things at once**: more visitors and fewer books per visitor. If many new visitors only read on the spot, they add to the visitor count without adding loans. So the average per visitor falls.

The other options do not explain both:

- A bigger collection and a higher loan limit would push people to borrow more.
- A bigger vehicle does not explain why the average loan fell.
- Villagers being busy on Tuesday mornings explains only the rise in visitors after the move to Saturday, not the lower average.

So the best explanation is that many new Saturday visitors come to read on the spot without borrowing.
