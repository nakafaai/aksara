#### Combine consecutive pairs

Blocks <InlineMath math="\text{AB}" /> and <InlineMath math="\text{CD}" /> have **fixed internal order**. Together with reports <InlineMath math="E,F,G,H" />, they form six movable units. The number of arrangements of these units is:

<BlockMath math="6!=720" />

#### Remove arrangements with adjacent blocks

When the two blocks are adjacent, treat them as **one combined unit**. There are then five units to arrange, with two internal orders for the combined unit: <InlineMath math="\text{ABCD}" /> or <InlineMath math="\text{CDAB}" />.

<BlockMath math="2\times5!=240" />

Subtract those arrangements from the total for six units. The remaining arrangements have at least one report between the two blocks.

<BlockMath math="720-240=480" />

#### Apply the order of E and F

**Swapping the positions** of <InlineMath math="E" /> and <InlineMath math="F" /> preserves both blocks and their separation. Each arrangement with <InlineMath math="E" /> before <InlineMath math="F" /> pairs with one having the reverse order. Exactly half of the remaining arrangements therefore meet the last condition.

<BlockMath math="480\div2=240" />

The random choice comes from all unrestricted arrangements of eight reports, rather than only the six-unit arrangements. The sample space has size <InlineMath math="8!=40320" />. The required probability is:

<BlockMath math="P=\frac{240}{40320}=\frac1{168}" />
