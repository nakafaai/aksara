# Given

The data-plan table lists **price, main quota, and night bonus quota** across all plans.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements is **True** or **False** according to the table.

# Solution

> Check carefully whether each statement counts only main data or also includes the night bonus.

Mark each statement Benar (true) or Salah (false). Recheck each against the table, and watch whether **the night bonus is counted**.

The chart below works out the price per GB when the night bonus counts. **Super's bar is shorter than Pelajar's**, so Super is cheaper per GB.

**Statement 1** is false. With the bonus, Pelajar costs <InlineMath math="70{,}000\div40=1{,}750" /> rupiah per GB, while Super costs <InlineMath math="170{,}000\div100=1{,}700" />. Super is cheaper.

**Statement 2** is true. Super has <InlineMath math="80+20=100" /> GB and Hemat <InlineMath math="20+5=25" /> GB, and <InlineMath math="100=4\times25" />.

**Statement 3** is false. The difference is <InlineMath math="170{,}000-120{,}000=50{,}000" />, while Hemat costs <InlineMath math="55{,}000" />.

**Statement 4** is true. The quota is <InlineMath math="60=2\times30" /> GB, while the price <InlineMath math="120{,}000" /> is less than <InlineMath math="2\times70{,}000=140{,}000" />.

# Answer

So statements 2 and 4 are true, while statements 1 and 3 are false.
