During the first <InlineMath math="8" /> hours, both pumps fill this fraction of the tank:

<BlockMath math="8\left(\frac1{30}+\frac1{20}\right)=\frac23" />

The remaining fraction is <InlineMath math="1-\frac23=\frac13" />. The new rate of pump <InlineMath math="A" />, in tanks per hour, is:

<BlockMath math="\left(1+\frac{50}{100}\right)\frac1{30}=\frac1{20}" />

The additional time, in hours, is:

<BlockMath math="\frac13\div\frac1{20}=\frac{20}{3}" />
