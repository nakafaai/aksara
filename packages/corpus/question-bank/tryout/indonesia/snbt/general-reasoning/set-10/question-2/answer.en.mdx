This guess needs evidence that some lane users **did not cycle at all before**. Users who used to ride a motorbike to work are new cyclists, not cyclists who only changed streets. That fact supports the guess directly.

The other options do not strengthen it:

- Roadworks on Cempaka give riders another reason to switch streets, so they weaken the guess.
- Rising bicycle prices tend to keep people from starting to cycle.
- Data from other cities do not show what happened in Lembayung.
- Equal weather only makes the two counts comparable, it does not show new cyclists.

So the strongest support is that some new lane users used to ride a motorbike to work.
