This guess needs evidence that some visitors **did not shop at either market before**. Visitors who always used to shop at a minimarket are new to the markets, not shoppers who only moved over from Kenari. That fact supports the guess directly.

The other options do not strengthen it:

- Roadworks on the way to Kenari give shoppers another reason to switch markets, so they weaken the guess.
- Rising prices tend to make people buy less, not bring in new shoppers.
- Data from other cities do not show what happened in Lembayung.
- Equal weather only makes the two counts comparable, it does not show new shoppers.

So the strongest support is that some new Cendana visitors always used to shop at a minimarket.
