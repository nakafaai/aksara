# Given

**A number sequence** starts with **<InlineMath math="4, 6, 9, 14, 21, 32" />**.

# Asked

The **seventh term** of the sequence.

# Solution

> Write down the step between consecutive terms to spot the sequence of consecutive prime numbers.

Work out **the differences between terms**:

<BlockMath math="6-4=2,\quad 9-6=3,\quad 14-9=5,\quad 21-14=7,\quad 32-21=11" />

The differences are consecutive primes: <InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="5" />, <InlineMath math="7" />, <InlineMath math="11" />. The next prime is <InlineMath math="13" />, so the seventh term is:

<BlockMath math="32+13=45" />
