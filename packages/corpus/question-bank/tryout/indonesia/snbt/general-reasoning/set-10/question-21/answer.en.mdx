Separate the terms by position.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="a_1,a_3,a_5,a_7:\quad10,12,14,16" />

  <BlockMath math="a_2,a_4,a_6:\quad3,9,27" />
</MathContainer>

The odd-position terms have common difference <InlineMath math="2" />, while the even-position terms have common ratio <InlineMath math="3" />. Thus:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="a_9=16+2=18" />

  <BlockMath math="a_8=27\times3=81" />
</MathContainer>

Using <InlineMath math="a_2=3" />, the requested result is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{a_8}{a_2}+a_9=\frac{81}{3}+18=45" />
