# Given

A family needs at least **<InlineMath math="150\text{ GB}" /> of monthly main data** and may stack multiple plans, including repeats.

# Asked

The **lowest monthly cost** (expressed as rupiah divided by <InlineMath math="1{,}000" />) to reach the target.

# Solution

> Cover most of the need with the cheapest price-per-GB plan first, then compare combinations for the remaining gigabytes.

Start from the package with **the cheapest price per GB of main quota**, Keluarga (<InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}000" /> per GB), then fill the gap as cheaply as possible.

- Two Keluarga packages give <InlineMath math="120" /> GB for <InlineMath math="240" /> thousand. That is <InlineMath math="30" /> GB short.
- The cheapest way to add <InlineMath math="30" /> GB is one Pelajar package for <InlineMath math="70" /> thousand.

<BlockMath math="240+70=310\text{ thousand rupiah for }150\text{ GB}" />

Compare with other mixes that reach <InlineMath math="150" /> GB:

- One Keluarga and three Pelajar: <InlineMath math="120+210=330" /> thousand.
- Two Super: <InlineMath math="340" /> thousand.
- Super, Keluarga, and Hemat: <InlineMath math="345" /> thousand.
- Five Pelajar: <InlineMath math="350" /> thousand.

# Answer

All cost more. So the lowest cost is <InlineMath math="310" /> thousand rupiah a month.
