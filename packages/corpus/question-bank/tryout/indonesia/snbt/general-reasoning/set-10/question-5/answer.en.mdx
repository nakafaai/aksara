# Given

The four-village table gives **per-village visitor and book-borrowing counts**.

# Asked

Whether each of the four statements is **True** or **False** based on the table.

# Solution

> Verify every more-than and less-than claim directly against the exact village rows in the table.

Mark each statement Benar (true) or Salah (false). Check each one against the table, and watch the words **lebih banyak** (more than).

The chart below puts the figures the four statements need side by side. **In Pasirjaya, the June visitor and loan bars are the same height**, and Mekarsari's visitors don't change.
