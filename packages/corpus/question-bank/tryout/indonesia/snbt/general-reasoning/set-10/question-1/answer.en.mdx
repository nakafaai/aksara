The number in the passage applies to the entire **agriculture, forestry, and other land use** sector, abbreviated AFOLU:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{AFOLU share in 2019} \approx 22\%" />
</MathContainer>

Deforestation is one source within that sector. It cannot simultaneously be the whole sector and account for almost all human-caused emissions. The second option therefore contradicts both the scope and the size of the estimate.

The remaining statements stay within the text: deforestation can worsen erosion, threaten habitats and forest-dependent communities, and forms only one part of the broader land-use sector.
