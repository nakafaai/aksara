The <InlineMath math="22\%" /> figure for <InlineMath math="2019" /> applies to the entire **agriculture, forestry, and other land use** sector, or AFOLU. It does not say that deforestation alone produced almost all human-caused emissions.

The claim about almost all emissions is incorrect because it changes both the scope and the size of the estimate in the passage. Deforestation is identified as one source within that sector.

The other statements agree with the passage: deforestation increases erosion risk, threatens wildlife habitats, and affects communities using firewood and forest products.
