# Given

Four colored keys each open one distinct door, with **red not the shed, blue garage or bedroom, and yellow kitchen or garage**.

# Asked

Which **key color opens the shed door**.

# Solution

> Cross out every key barred from the shed or locked onto two other doors until only one color remains for the shed.

Cross out the keys that **cannot** open the storeroom.

- The red key does not open the storeroom (first fact).
- The blue key opens only the garage or the bedroom (second fact).
- The yellow key opens only the kitchen or the garage (fourth fact).

The diagram below sums up why each key drops out for the storeroom. **Only the green key survives**.

Only the green key is left, and it may open the storeroom, since its only ban is the garage.

The facts do not settle the doors for the red and blue keys, since those two could swap between garage and bedroom. Still, the storeroom is certainly opened by the green key, so *tidak dapat dipastikan* (cannot be determined) is wrong.

# Answer

So the storeroom door is opened with the green key.
