It is easier to count **all teams, then subtract the teams with no girls**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{all teams} &= \binom{9}{3}=84 \\
\text{teams with no girls (all boys)} &= \binom{5}{3}=10 \\
\text{teams with at least 1 girl} &= 84-10=74
\end{aligned}" />

Order does not matter, since a team is being chosen.

So there are <InlineMath math="74" /> ways to choose the team.
