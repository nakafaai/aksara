There are <InlineMath math="\binom{13}{2}=78" /> equally likely pairs.

| Pair | Pair count | Listed prize |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Red-red | <InlineMath math="\binom72=21" /> | <InlineMath math="50" /> |
| Mixed | <InlineMath math="7\times6=42" /> | <InlineMath math="40" /> |
| Blue-blue | <InlineMath math="\binom62=15" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

Prizes are in thousand rupiah. The expected listed prize is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}E&=\frac{50\times21+40\times42}{78}\\&=35\end{aligned}" />

The actual payout is eighty percent of the listed prize. A fair entry fee equals the expected actual payout:

<BlockMath math="0.8\times35=28" />
