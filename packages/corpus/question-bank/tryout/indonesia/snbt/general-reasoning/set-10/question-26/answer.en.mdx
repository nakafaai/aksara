# Given

We want the total books on a shelf, given **(1) stacks of <InlineMath math="24" /> books each** and **(2) total weight <InlineMath math="36\text{ kg}" /> at <InlineMath math="300" /> grams per book**.

# Asked

Whether statements **(1)** and **(2)** are sufficient to find the book count.

# Solution

> Test each statement separately, since multiplying stacks or dividing total weight by one book weight can each pin down the exact count on its own.

Test **each statement on its own** first.

- Statement (1) alone: <InlineMath math="5\times24=120" /> books. Enough.
- Statement (2) alone: <InlineMath math="36{,}000\div300=120" /> books. Also enough.

The diagram below draws both roads to the answer. **Each statement alone already reaches <InlineMath math="120" /> books**.

# Answer

So statement (1) alone is sufficient, and statement (2) alone is also sufficient.
