Find the **combined unit counts** before calculating the percentage. The plants produce different numbers of units, so we cannot simply average their acceptable percentages.

| Count | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Total produced | <InlineMath math="200+150+250=600" /> |
| Defective units | <InlineMath math="10+12+15=37" /> |
| Acceptable units | <InlineMath math="600-37=563" /> |

Divide the acceptable count by all units produced, then round to one decimal place.

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{563}{600}\times100\%\approx93.8\%" />
