Mark each statement Benar (true, it must follow) or Salah (false, it need not follow). Join the facts into one chain, then use **contraposition**.

<BlockMath math="\text{cleanliness winner}\Rightarrow\text{trophy}\Rightarrow\text{may choose the trip}" />

**Statement 1** is false. Class XI-2 may not choose the trip, so by the third fact it certainly does not hold the trophy.

**Statement 2** is true. Without the trophy, Class XI-2 certainly did not win the cleanliness contest.

**Statement 3** is true. This is the chain above: a cleanliness winner gets the trophy, and a trophy holder may choose the trip.

**Statement 4** is false. The chain runs one way only. The second fact says the wall magazine winner also gets the trophy, so a class that may choose the trip need not be the cleanliness winner.

So statements 2 and 3 are true, while statements 1 and 4 are false.
