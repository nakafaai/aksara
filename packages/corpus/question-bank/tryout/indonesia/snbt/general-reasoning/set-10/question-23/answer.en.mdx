Summing the pairwise intersections counts each user of exactly two services once, but each user of all three three times. To count everyone using at least two once, subtract two extra copies of the all-three group.

<BlockMath math="40+33+29=102" />

<BlockMath math="102-2\times16=70" />

Divide by the total number of participants:

<BlockMath math="\frac{70}{220}=\frac7{22}" />
