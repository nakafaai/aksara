# Given

The internet-plan table lists **plan prices and main data allowances** for every option.

# Asked

The **plan name** with the lowest price per GB of main data.

# Solution

> Divide each plan price by its main data allowance alone so night bonuses do not distort the comparison.

Divide each price by **its main quota only**, in rupiah per GB.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Hemat} &: 55{,}000\div20=2{,}750 \\
\text{Pelajar} &: 70{,}000\div30\approx2{,}333 \\
\text{Keluarga} &: 120{,}000\div60=2{,}000 \\
\text{Super} &: 170{,}000\div80=2{,}125
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below draws the four packages' price per GB. **Keluarga's bar is the shortest**, just below Super.

Super has the largest quota, but its price per GB is still a little above Keluarga.

So the package with the cheapest price per GB of main quota is Keluarga.
