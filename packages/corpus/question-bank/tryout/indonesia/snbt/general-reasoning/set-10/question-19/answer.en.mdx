Follow the Grade VII students who are club members through **the chain of premises**.

- By premise 3, some Grade VII students are chess club members.
- By premise 1, those students have played in a tournament.
- By premise 2, those students are at least 12 years old.

So at least some Grade VII students are at least 12 years old.

The other options are not certain:

- Premise 3 covers only some Grade VII students, not all.
- Premise 1 says every member has played in a tournament, so the third option contradicts it.
- Premise 2 cannot be reversed: someone aged 12 need not be a club member.
- Grade VII members surely played in a tournament, so the fifth option contradicts the premises.

So the conclusion that must be true is that some Grade VII students are at least 12 years old.
