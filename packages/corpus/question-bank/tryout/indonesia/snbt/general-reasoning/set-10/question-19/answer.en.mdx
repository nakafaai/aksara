Convert the mean to the **sum of all scores**. Before removing one score, the twelve scores total:

<BlockMath math="12\times76=912" />

The remaining eleven scores total <InlineMath math="11\times74=814" />. The removed score is the difference between those totals, then receives an <InlineMath math="8" />-point correction downward.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="912-814=98" />
  <BlockMath math="98-8=90" />
</MathContainer>

Restoring the corrected score of <InlineMath math="90" /> brings the count back to twelve. The new mean is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{814+90}{12}=\frac{904}{12}=75\frac13" />

You can also check using the **decrease in the total**: just one score drops by <InlineMath math="8" />, so the mean falls by <InlineMath math="8/12=2/3" /> from <InlineMath math="76" />.
