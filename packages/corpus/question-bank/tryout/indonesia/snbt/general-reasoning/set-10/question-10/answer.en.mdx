The passage makes two claims that must be kept together:

- Potassium-rich foods **may help** manage high blood pressure by reducing sodium's effects.
- Increasing potassium is **not safe for everyone**.

The second option preserves both limits. It says that potassium-rich foods may support blood-pressure management without turning that possibility into a guarantee or a recommendation for everyone.

The other options go beyond the passage. It does not say that food can replace prescribed treatment, that potassium removes all sodium, that more potassium always produces a lower blood pressure, or that everyone should take supplements without professional advice.
