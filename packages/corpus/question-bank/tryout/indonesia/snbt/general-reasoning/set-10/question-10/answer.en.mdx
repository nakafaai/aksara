# Given

Fountain A sprays every **<InlineMath math="12" /> minutes**, fountain B every **<InlineMath math="18" /> minutes**, and their **first joint spray is at 16:00**.

# Asked

The **time of their third joint spray**.

# Solution

> Find the least common multiple of both intervals and jump forward twice from 16:00, since 16:00 already counts as the first match.

The two fountains spray together at every **least common multiple** of <InlineMath math="12" /> and <InlineMath math="18" /> minutes.

<BlockMath math="\text{lcm}(12,18)=36" />

The chart below marks the spray minutes since 16.00. **Both bars appear together only at minutes <InlineMath math="0" />, <InlineMath math="36" />, and <InlineMath math="72" />**, which are 16.00, 16.36, and 17.12.

# Answer

So they spray together for the third time at 17.12.
