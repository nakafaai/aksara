# Given

To print **<InlineMath math="1{,}200" /> sheets**, machine A starts at **07:00** (<InlineMath math="40" /> min alone) and machine B joins at **07:10** (<InlineMath math="60" /> min alone).

# Asked

The **time when printing finishes**.

# Solution

> Subtract the sheets machine A prints alone in the first ten minutes, then divide the remaining sheets by their combined speed.

First convert to **sheets per minute**: machine A prints <InlineMath math="1{,}200\div40=30" /> and machine B <InlineMath math="1{,}200\div60=20" /> sheets per minute.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{07.00 to 07.10 (A alone)} &: 30\times10=300\text{ sheets} \\
\text{left} &: 1{,}200-300=900\text{ sheets} \\
\text{A and B together} &: \frac{900}{30+20}=18\text{ minutes}
\end{aligned}" />

The chart below draws the sheets already printed. **The line gets steeper after 07.10**, because the two machines print <InlineMath math="50" /> sheets a minute together.

Printing ends <InlineMath math="18" /> minutes after 07.10, at 07.28. The answer 07.24 comes from assuming both machines start at 07.00.

# Answer

So printing finishes at 07.28.
