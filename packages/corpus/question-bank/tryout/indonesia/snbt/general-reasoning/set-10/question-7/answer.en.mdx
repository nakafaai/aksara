To determine which class has the highest percentage of students who like mathematics, compare the number who like the subject with the total number of students in each class.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{Percentage} = \frac{\text{Like}}{\text{Like} + \text{Dislike}} \times 100\%" />
</MathContainer>

Here are the percentage calculation results for each class:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\text{MMM} = \frac{23}{63} \approx 36.5\%" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{NNN} = \frac{18}{38} \approx 47.4\%" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{PPP} = \frac{10}{25} = 40.0\%" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{QQQ} = \frac{17}{40} = 42.5\%" />
  <BlockMath math="\text{RRR} = \frac{21}{56} = 37.5\%" />
</MathContainer>

Based on the comparison above, class <InlineMath math="\text{NNN}" /> has the highest percentage at approximately <InlineMath math="47.4\%" />. This indicates that the proportion of students who like mathematics in that class is greater than in the other classes.
