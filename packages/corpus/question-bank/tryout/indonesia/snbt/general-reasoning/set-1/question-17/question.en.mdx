Growth monitoring is one way to assess a child's nutritional status. The [World Health Organization growth standards](https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards) include length or height for age, weight for age, weight for length or height, and head circumference for age. These measurements are compared with age-appropriate reference curves and are most useful when followed over time. A single measurement does not describe every aspect of a child's health.
