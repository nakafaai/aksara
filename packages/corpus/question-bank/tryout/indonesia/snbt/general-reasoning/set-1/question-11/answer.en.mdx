Let <InlineMath math="F" />, <InlineMath math="M" />, and <InlineMath math="C" /> be the ages of Father, Mother, and their child.

#### Translate the age differences

Mother and Father differ in age by at most <InlineMath math="5\text{ years}" />, so

<BlockMath math="|F-M| \le 5" />

Father is <InlineMath math="35\text{ years}" /> older than the child, while Mother is <InlineMath math="x\text{ years}" /> older than the child:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} F-C &= 35 \\ M-C &= x \end{aligned}" />

Subtracting the second age difference from the first gives <InlineMath math="F-M=35-x" />. Therefore,

<BlockMath math="|35-x| \le 5" />

#### Solve the inequality

The absolute-value inequality means that <InlineMath math="35-x" /> lies between <InlineMath math="-5" /> and <InlineMath math="5" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} -5 &\le 35-x \le 5 \\ -40 &\le -x \le -30 \\ 30 &\le x \le 40 \end{aligned}" />

Thus, Mother's age difference from the child must satisfy <InlineMath math="30 \le x \le 40" />.
