The evidence shows a fairly consistent pattern: three of the four classes had a higher median after the new session was introduced, while several other factors were held constant.

Still, one limited trial cannot establish that every student's score will rise or that every school will obtain the same result. The unchanged class also shows that the result was not uniform.

Therefore, the careful conclusion is that **retrieval practice was usually associated with higher median scores under these trial conditions, but further testing is needed**.
