[UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-costumes-in-norway-craftsmanship-and-social-practice-02084?RL=02084) describes traditional costumes in Norway as closely connected to family life and local communities. Knowledge about how and when to wear them is passed on within families, while local craftspeople also provide guidance and practical training.

The costumes are worn at private and public celebrations and can symbolize personal identity and family history. When people gather in different traditional costumes, the practice can create a sense of recognition and belonging.
