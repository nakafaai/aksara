Instant noodles can be part of an occasional meal, but a packet of noodles alone does not provide a balanced diet. [World Health Organization guidance on a healthy diet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet) emphasizes dietary variety and limiting sodium. [WHO notes that many instant-noodle products contain excessive salt](https://www.who.int/westernpacific/newsroom/feature-stories/item/do-you-know-how-much-salt-is-in-your-instant-noodles). Their seasonings can contribute substantial sodium. Adding vegetables and a source of protein improves the variety of the meal, while using less seasoning can reduce its sodium content. Those changes do not make unlimited or daily consumption advisable.
