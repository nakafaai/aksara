The Cukurgondang research collection preserves mango genetic resources and supports the development of new varieties. Researchers use **selection** to identify promising accessions for particular purposes and **crossbreeding** to combine useful traits from different parents.

One result of crossbreeding is Agri Gardina 45, developed from Arumanis and Saigon mangoes. Another released variety, Denarum Agrihorti, is described by the Ministry of Agriculture as high yielding, low in fruit fiber, aromatic, and having an edible portion of about <InlineMath math="72.99\%" /> to <InlineMath math="80.32\%" />. These descriptions concern research varieties. Those statements do not establish that every accession is ready for export or that local farmers created the crosses.

*Sources: Ministry of Agriculture horticulture report and variety description, [Laporan Tahunan Puslitbang Hortikultura 2018](https://ppid.pertanian.go.id/doc/62/LaptahHorti2018.pdf) and [Agri Gardina 45](https://repository.pertanian.go.id/bitstreams/e8f31462-554d-49b2-9408-361835d7af23/download).*
