Distinguish **production figures from budget figures**. Percentages can measure different quantities even though they use the same percent sign.

| Figure | Quantity measured |
| --- | --- |
| More than <InlineMath math="11\%" /> | Increase in rice production |
| More than <InlineMath math="21\%" /> | Increase in corn production |
| <InlineMath math="70\%" /> | Budget share for production facilities and infrastructure |
| <InlineMath math="30\%" /> | Budget share for other items |

Thus, 70 percent refers to the production-facilities and infrastructure budget share, matching the third option. You can check the remaining share with:

<BlockMath math="100\%-70\%=30\%" />
