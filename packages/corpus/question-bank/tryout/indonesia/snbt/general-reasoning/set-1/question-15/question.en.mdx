Indonesia's Food and Drug Authority (BPOM) evaluates herbal products before issuing a marketing authorization. According to [BPOM guidance on herbal products](https://www.pom.go.id/penjelasan-publik/penjelasan-badan-pom-ri-tentang-produk-herbal-dan-suplemen-kesehatan-yang-digunakan-untuk-membantu-memelihara-daya-tahan-tubuh), an authorized product has been assessed for safety, claimed efficacy or benefit, and quality. Consumers are still advised to check the packaging, label, marketing authorization number, and expiry date, and to use the product only according to its approved claims. BPOM also [supervises products after they enter the market](https://www.pom.go.id/siaran-pers/temuan-obat-tradisional-dan-suplemen-kesehatan-yang-tidak-memenuhi-syarat-keamanan-dan-mutu) and may act against products that no longer meet the requirements.
