# Given

**Six ordering rules** arrange seven students across slots <InlineMath math="1" /> to <InlineMath math="7" />, while each option lists only slots <InlineMath math="3" /> through <InlineMath math="6" />.

# Asked

Which ordering is possible for slots <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="5" />, and <InlineMath math="6" />.

# Solution

> Four middle names only work if the remaining three students can still fill slots <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, and <InlineMath math="7" /> without breaking any rule.

Each option only fills turns <InlineMath math="3" /> to <InlineMath math="6" />. The remaining three students have to go into turns <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, and <InlineMath math="7" />. The four middle names are possible only if **the three outer turns can still be filled without breaking any condition**.

Among the four names in each option, no condition is broken directly. A problem only shows up when you ask where the other three students can present. So you have to complete each option first, and only then judge it.

Start with Eka, Bayu, Citra, Fani. Dimas, Galih, and Hesti are left. Citra is in turn <InlineMath math="5" />, so Dimas must be in turn <InlineMath math="7" /> (condition 2). Galih and Hesti get turns <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="2" />, which are next to each other. One full order is Galih, Hesti, Eka, Bayu, Citra, Fani, Dimas.

The diagram below draws that full order from the first turn to the last. **The three names outside the middle turns fit without breaking any condition**, so this option is possible.

| Condition | What happens in this order | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <InlineMath math="1" /> | Galih in turn <InlineMath math="1" />, Hesti in turn <InlineMath math="2" /> | consecutive |
| <InlineMath math="2" /> | Citra in turn <InlineMath math="5" />, Dimas in turn <InlineMath math="7" /> | exactly two turns apart |
| <InlineMath math="3" /> | Eka in turn <InlineMath math="3" />, Citra in turn <InlineMath math="5" /> | Eka goes first |
| <InlineMath math="4" /> | Eka in turn <InlineMath math="3" /> | not turn <InlineMath math="1" /> |
| <InlineMath math="5" /> | Turn <InlineMath math="7" /> goes to Dimas | not Galih or Hesti |
| <InlineMath math="6" /> | Bayu in turn <InlineMath math="4" />, an even number | this condition asks for nothing |

Condition 6 only applies when Bayu is in an odd-numbered turn. Bayu is in turn <InlineMath math="4" />, so Fani in turn <InlineMath math="6" /> breaks nothing.

The other four options all get stuck once you complete them, each because of a different condition.

- Eka, Galih, Citra, Bayu: Citra in turn <InlineMath math="5" /> forces Dimas into turn <InlineMath math="7" />. Hesti must sit right next to Galih, in turn <InlineMath math="3" /> or <InlineMath math="5" />. Both are taken, so condition 1 fails.
- Hesti, Fani, Citra, Bayu: Dimas is again in turn <InlineMath math="7" />. Galih must sit next to Hesti, and turn <InlineMath math="4" /> already belongs to Fani, so Galih takes turn <InlineMath math="2" />. That pushes Eka into turn <InlineMath math="1" />, which condition 4 forbids.
- Eka, Citra, Fani, Dimas: Bayu, Galih, and Hesti are left for turns <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, and <InlineMath math="7" />. Galih and Hesti must be side by side, so they take turns <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="2" />, and Bayu takes turn <InlineMath math="7" />. That turn is odd, so Fani would have to be in turn <InlineMath math="8" />, which doesn't exist. Condition 6 fails.
- Citra, Bayu, Dimas, Fani: Eka, Galih, and Hesti are left for turns <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="2" />, and <InlineMath math="7" />. Eka must come before Citra and can't be in turn <InlineMath math="1" />, so Eka takes turn <InlineMath math="2" />. Galih and Hesti end up split between turns <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="7" />, but they must be consecutive, and turn <InlineMath math="7" /> is off limits for them.

# Answer

So the order that can fill turns <InlineMath math="3" /> to <InlineMath math="6" /> is Eka, Bayu, Citra, Fani.
