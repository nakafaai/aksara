The best conclusion must combine ideas stated in the passage without adding an absolute claim.

The passage links several parts of the tradition:

- the physical costumes are used in family and community life;
- knowledge about wearing them is transmitted by families and local craftspeople;
- the practice can express identity, family history, recognition, and belonging.

Together, those details support the second option: the tradition includes garments, shared knowledge, and social practices that connect identity with community belonging.

The other options contradict explicit details. The passage does not require relatives to make every costume, says the costumes are worn at both private and public celebrations, gives local craftspeople an active role, and says different costumes can help people recognize one another.
