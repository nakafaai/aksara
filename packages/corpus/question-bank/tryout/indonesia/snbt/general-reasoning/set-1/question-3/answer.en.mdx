A statement can be concluded (*Dapat disimpulkan*) only when the data are enough to settle it. Two statements here need careful calculation, and the other two need **a comparison group the data don't have**.

Statement 1 can be concluded, even though it sounds false. In each row, the ponds that later got aerators were never worse in the earlier cycle: <InlineMath math="10\% = 10\%" /> in the low row and <InlineMath math="22\% < 26\%" /> in the high row. But the statement is about the average over all ponds, so weight by the number of ponds:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{Later aerated: } & \frac{5(10\%) + 15(22\%)}{20} = \frac{380\%}{20} = 19\% \\ \text{Never aerated: } & \frac{15(10\%) + 5(26\%)}{20} = \frac{280\%}{20} = 14\% \end{aligned}" />

That gives <InlineMath math="19\% > 14\%" />. The later-aerated group is higher because <InlineMath math="15" /> of its <InlineMath math="20" /> ponds are high density.

Statement 2 can't be concluded (*Tidak dapat disimpulkan*), even though it sounds reasonable. If you borrow the <InlineMath math="8" />-point rise of the aerated ponds, you get <InlineMath math="26\% + 8 = 34\%" />. But those five ponds never had aerators. There are no data on them with aerators, and nothing guarantees that the aerators' effect on other ponds carries over to them.

Statement 3 can't be concluded. The dry season did happen in the latest cycle, but every pond went through it. No pond in that cycle escaped the dry season, so there's no comparison group to separate the dry season's effect from anything else that changed between cycles.

Statement 4 can be concluded. The rise for each group is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{Low: } & 14 - 10 = 4 \text{ points, for both groups} \\ \text{High, aerated: } & 30 - 22 = 8 \text{ points} \\ \text{High, no aerator: } & 42 - 26 = 16 \text{ points} \end{aligned}" />

Twice the low-density rise is <InlineMath math="8" /> points. The high-density aerated group rose exactly <InlineMath math="8" /> points, so not more than twice. Only the high-density group without aerators, at <InlineMath math="16" /> points, rose more than twice as much.

So statements 1 and 4 can be concluded, while statements 2 and 3 can't.
