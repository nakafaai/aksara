The director claims a causal sequence:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\\text{relocation} \\longrightarrow \\text{employee resignations}" />
</MathContainer>

The strongest challenge is evidence that the supposed effect happened before the proposed cause. If most employees had already submitted their resignations before the relocation was announced, that relocation cannot explain those earlier decisions.

The first and third options strengthen the director's argument because they describe a more difficult commute. The fourth and fifth options explain why the company moved, but they do not explain when or why employees resigned.

Therefore, the second option most strongly weakens the argument.
