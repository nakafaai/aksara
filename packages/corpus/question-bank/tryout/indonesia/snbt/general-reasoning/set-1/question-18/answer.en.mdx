The director treats the office relocation as the cause of many resignations. To test that claim, compare the **order of events**: the announcement and the employees' decisions.

| Timing | Evidence in the fifth option |
| --- | --- |
| Earlier | Most resignation letters were submitted |
| Later | The relocation plans were announced |

Decisions made **before the announcement** weaken the claim that the announcement prompted those resignations. This evidence does not establish the actual cause, but it directly challenges the director's proposed causal connection.

A more distant office and a commute twice as long give reasons why relocation might encourage employees to leave. Lower rent and the expiry of the old lease explain why the company moved, not why employees resigned. The fifth option therefore most strongly weakens the director's argument.
