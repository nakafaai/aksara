# Given

**Organizers rent medium buses** holding <InlineMath math="31" /> passengers at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}2{,}100{,}000" /> each plus minibuses for at least <InlineMath math="96" /> people, requiring at least one medium bus.

# Asked

The lowest total rental cost satisfying capacity and the one-medium-bus requirement.

# Solution

> Since vehicles come in whole numbers, test each integer count of medium buses from <InlineMath math="1" /> upward and pick the cheapest valid mix.

You're looking for a mix of medium buses and minibuses that seats at least <InlineMath math="96" /> people, includes at least one medium bus, and costs as little as possible. The number of vehicles has to be whole, so a "cost per seat" rule alone won't settle it. The safe method is **checking every possible number of medium buses**, then adding just enough minibuses for the remaining passengers.

For each number of medium buses <InlineMath math="b" />, the passengers left over are <InlineMath math="96-31b" />. The number of minibuses is that remainder divided by <InlineMath math="14" />, rounded up, since you can't rent part of a minibus. The case <InlineMath math="b=0" /> isn't allowed, because at least one medium bus is required. With <InlineMath math="b=4" />, there are already <InlineMath math="124" /> seats, so a fifth bus would only add cost.

The lowest cost is <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}6{,}850{,}000" />, with one medium bus and five minibuses. Check that four minibuses wouldn't do: <InlineMath math="31+4\times14=87" /> seats, fewer than <InlineMath math="96" />.

The other options come from different traps:

- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}6{,}650{,}000" /> is the cost of seven minibuses (<InlineMath math="98" /> seats). It's the cheapest plan if you ignore the conditions, but it has no medium bus, so it's ruled out.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}7{,}250{,}000" /> comes from a greedy approach: a medium bus is slightly cheaper per seat (about <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}67{,}742" /> against <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}67{,}857" />), so you use as many as possible. That gives three medium buses and one minibus with <InlineMath math="11" /> empty seats. You still pay for those empty seats, so this plan costs more.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}8{,}400{,}000" /> is the cost of using medium buses only.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}8{,}750{,}000" /> comes from adding one medium bus to the seven-minibus plan without removing any minibuses.

# Answer

So the cheapest rental that meets every condition costs <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}6{,}850{,}000" />.
