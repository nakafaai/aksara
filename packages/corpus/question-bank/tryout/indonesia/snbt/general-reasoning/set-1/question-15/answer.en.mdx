A statement that must be true has to hold in **every order that meets all six conditions**. Finding one order that fits isn't enough. You need to know what never changes.

The best place to start is Citra, because three conditions pin her down at once. Eka must come before Citra (condition 3), Eka can't take turn <InlineMath math="1" /> (condition 4), and Dimas must come exactly two turns after Citra (condition 2).

Dimas sits two turns after Citra and can't go past turn <InlineMath math="7" />, so Citra is in turn <InlineMath math="5" /> at the latest. That leaves three candidates.
