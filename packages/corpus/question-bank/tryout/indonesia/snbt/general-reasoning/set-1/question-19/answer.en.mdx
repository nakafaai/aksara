A valid conclusion has to be true at every exhibition that obeys all three statements. If you can picture one exhibition that obeys all three but makes a conclusion false, that conclusion isn't valid.

First, recall what *sebagian* ("some") means. In reasoning questions, "some" means at least one, and possibly all. So "some ikat cloths aren't certified" does not mean that some ikat cloths are certified.

Follow one cloth that's guaranteed to exist.

1. Statement 2 guarantees at least one ikat cloth without a natural-dye certificate. Call it cloth Y.
2. Statement 1 says every cloth in the main hall is certified. Its contrapositive, the version that is flipped and negated at the same time, says: a cloth that isn't certified isn't displayed in the main hall. Cloth Y isn't certified, so cloth Y isn't displayed in the main hall.
3. Statement 3 says every ikat cloth is made on a *gedogan* loom (a traditional backstrap loom). Cloth Y is an ikat cloth, so cloth Y is made on a gedogan loom.

Cloth Y is made on a gedogan loom and isn't displayed in the main hall. So **at least one gedogan-made cloth isn't displayed in the main hall**, which is exactly what "some cloths made on a gedogan loom aren't displayed in the main hall" means.

The other conclusions aren't valid. For each one, there's an exhibition that obeys all three statements but makes the conclusion false.

- "No ikat cloth is displayed in the main hall" jumps from "some" to "all". Statement 2 only talks about the uncertified ikat cloths. Other ikat cloths that are certified may hang in the main hall.
- "Every cloth not displayed in the main hall is uncertified" is the inverse of statement 1, where both parts are negated without being swapped. An inverse isn't equivalent to the original. Statement 1 doesn't stop a certified cloth from hanging in another room, so a certified cloth in a side room is still possible.
- "Some cloths in the main hall aren't gedogan-made" reverses what was proven. You proved there's a gedogan cloth outside the main hall, not a non-gedogan cloth inside it. Every cloth in the main hall could be gedogan-made.
- "Some ikat cloths are certified" comes from reading "some aren't" as if the rest surely are. All the ikat cloths could be uncertified.

So the valid conclusion is that some cloths made on a gedogan loom aren't displayed in the main hall.
