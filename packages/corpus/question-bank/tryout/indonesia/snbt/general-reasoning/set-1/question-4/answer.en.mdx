The question asks for a measure stated explicitly in the passage. That means we should match an answer to the text rather than infer a new policy.

The passage lists the measures supported by the redirected budget: irrigation infrastructure, agricultural land, farm machinery, fertilizer, and improved seed. The second choice restates that list.

The other choices introduce claims that do not appear in the passage. It says nothing about replacing domestic crops with imports, spending the entire budget on rice, ending all imports through a law, or reducing cultivated land outside Java.

Therefore, the correct answer is **funding irrigation, agricultural land, machinery, fertilizer, and improved seed**.
