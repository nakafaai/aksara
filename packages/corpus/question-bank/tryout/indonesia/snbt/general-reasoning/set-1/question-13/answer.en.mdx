The negation (*ingkaran*) is the statement that's true exactly when the original is false. So first ask: when is the original false?

The original talks about one group: athletes who passed the regional selection and have never had a serious injury. For this group, it demands that every athlete meets at least one of two things: being on the provincial core team or holding a sports scholarship.

The original is false when at least one athlete in that group fails the demand. Failing "A or B" means not A and also not B. This rule is De Morgan's law: the negation of "or" becomes "and", and each part gets negated.

In symbols, let <InlineMath math="K(x)" />: athlete <InlineMath math="x" /> passed the regional selection and has never had a serious injury, <InlineMath math="T(x)" />: on the provincial core team, and <InlineMath math="B(x)" />: holds a sports scholarship.

<BlockMath math="\neg\,\forall x\,\big(K(x) \Rightarrow (T(x) \vee B(x))\big) \;\equiv\; \exists x\,\big(K(x) \wedge \neg T(x) \wedge \neg B(x)\big)" />

There are three steps, and only three.

1. "All" (*semua*) becomes "there is" (*ada*).
2. The group description, passed the regional selection and never seriously injured, **stays whole and isn't negated**. It only points to which athletes we're talking about.
3. The demand "core team or scholarship" is negated into "not on the core team and not a scholarship holder".

The result is "there is an athlete who passed the regional selection and has never had a serious injury, but who is not on the provincial core team and not a sports scholarship holder".

The other options fail because of these slips.

- The version with "not on the core team or not a scholarship holder" forgets to turn "or" into "and". An athlete in the group who holds a scholarship but isn't on the core team fits this option, yet doesn't contradict the original.
- The version starting with "all" isn't a negation. It's a much stronger claim. If the group holds one core-team athlete and one athlete with neither status, the original is false and this option is false too. A negation can't be false together with the original.
- The version with "didn't pass the regional selection or has had a serious injury" negates the group description. Such athletes sit outside the group, so whatever their status, they can't contradict the original.
- The version "there is an athlete who is not on the core team and not a scholarship holder" drops the group description. An athlete who never passed the regional selection can fit it without contradicting the original.

So the negation is that there is an athlete who passed the regional selection and has never had a serious injury, but who is not on the provincial core team and not a sports scholarship holder.
