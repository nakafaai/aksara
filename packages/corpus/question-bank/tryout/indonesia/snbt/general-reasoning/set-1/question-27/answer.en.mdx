Average speed is total distance divided by total time, not the direct average of the two speeds.

The times for the two parts are

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="t_1=\frac{180}{60}=3\text{ h}" />
  <BlockMath math="t_2=\frac{120}{80}=1.5\text{ h}" />
</MathContainer>

The total distance is <InlineMath math="300\text{ km}" /> and the total time is <InlineMath math="4.5\text{ h}" />. Therefore,

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="v_{\text{avg}}=\frac{300}{4.5}=66\frac{2}{3}\text{ km/h}" />
</MathContainer>

The average speed is **<InlineMath math="66\frac{2}{3}\text{ km/h}" />**.
