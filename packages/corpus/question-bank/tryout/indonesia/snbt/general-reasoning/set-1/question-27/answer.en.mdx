Calculate in thousand rupiah. Reverse the price changes in order: remove the tax, restore the voucher deduction, then remove the markup.

Before the <InlineMath math="10\%" /> tax, the price after the voucher was

<BlockMath math="605\div1.10=550" />

Restore the <InlineMath math="50" /> thousand rupiah voucher, then divide by the markup factor <InlineMath math="1.20" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 550+50 &=600 \\ P_0 &=600\div1.20=500 \\ 605-P_0 &=605-500=105 \end{aligned}" />

The final price therefore exceeds the initial price by <InlineMath math="105" /> thousand rupiah.
