Look for a rule that works for **all three examples at once**. First compare the arrangement of the consonants, then the change in the vowels.

Look at the consonants. In RAKIT, the last letter T moves to the front. In BOLAN, N moves to the front. In MUSIK, K moves to the front. So the first step is **moving the last letter to the first position**:

| Input | After moving the last letter | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RAKIT | TRAKI | TREKO |
| BOLAN | NBOLA | NBULE |
| MUSIK | KMUSI | KMASO |

Compare the middle column with the output. The consonants stay, but each vowel becomes **the next vowel** in the cycle A, E, I, O, U: A becomes E, I becomes O, O becomes U, and U becomes A. All four changes appear in the examples: A to E (RAKIT, BOLAN), I to O (RAKIT, MUSIK), O to U (BOLAN), and U to A (MUSIK).

Apply both steps to PITAR:

<BlockMath math="\text{PITAR} \;\rightarrow\; \text{RPITA} \;\rightarrow\; \text{RPOTE}" />

The other options come from an incomplete or wrong rule:

- RPITA only moves the last letter and keeps the vowels.
- POTER only changes the vowels and does not move a letter.
- OTERP moves the first letter to the end, while the examples move the last letter to the front.
- RPETU shifts the vowels backward, for example I to E.

So the machine turns PITAR into RPOTE.
