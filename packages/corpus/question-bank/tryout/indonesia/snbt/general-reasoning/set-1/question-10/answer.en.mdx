The draining time depends on two quantities: the **volume of water** in the full tank and the **combined flow rate** of the taps.

#### Find the water volume

The tank is a cube with **side length** <InlineMath math="4\text{ dm}" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} V &amp;= s^3 \\ &amp;= 4 \times 4 \times 4 \\ &amp;= 64\text{ dm}^3 \end{aligned}" />

Because <InlineMath math="1\text{ dm}^3 = 1\text{ liter}" />, the full tank contains <InlineMath math="64\text{ liters}" /> of water.

#### Add the flow rates

All three taps drain the tank **at the same time**, so their rates add:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} Q &amp;= Q_1 + Q_2 + Q_3 \\ Q &amp;= 4 + 3 + 1 \\ Q &amp;= 8 \text{ liters/minute} \end{aligned}" />

#### Find the draining time

Divide the **volume by the combined flow rate**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} t &amp;= \frac{V}{Q} \\ &amp;= \frac{64}{8} \\ &amp;= 8\text{ minutes} \end{aligned}" />

The tank will be empty after <InlineMath math="8\text{ minutes}" />.
