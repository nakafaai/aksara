Four officers working for three hours provide <InlineMath math="4\times3=12" /> person-hours. The checking rate per person-hour is

<BlockMath math="\frac{36}{12}=3\text{ forms}" />

Six officers working for five hours provide <InlineMath math="6\times5=30" /> person-hours. At the same rate, they check

<BlockMath math="30\times3=90" />

Therefore, the six officers can check **<InlineMath math="90" /> forms**.
