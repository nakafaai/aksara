There are <InlineMath math="\binom{10}{2}=45" /> equally likely pairs of balls. The numbers of red-red, mixed-colour, and blue-blue pairs are respectively

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \binom42 &=6 \\ 4\times6 &=24 \\ \binom62 &=15 \end{aligned}" />

Use thousand rupiah for every monetary amount. The expected prize payout before any fee refund is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} E &=30\times\frac6{45}+15\times\frac{24}{45} \\ &=4+8=12 \end{aligned}" />

Let the entry fee be <InlineMath math="c" />. Two blue balls have probability <InlineMath math="\frac{15}{45}=\frac13" />, and only that outcome refunds <InlineMath math="\frac{c}{4}" />. The entry fee is paid on every play.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 12-c+\frac13\times\frac c4 &=0 \\ 12-\frac{11c}{12} &=0 \\ c &=\frac{144}{11} \end{aligned}" />

An entry fee of <InlineMath math="\frac{144}{11}" /> thousand rupiah gives an expected net gain of zero.
