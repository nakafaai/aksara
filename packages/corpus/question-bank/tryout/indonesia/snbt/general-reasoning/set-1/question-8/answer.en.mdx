The question gives a **hypothetical total budget** and asks for the share represented by <InlineMath math="70\text{ percent}" />.

Convert the percentage to a fraction and multiply it by the total:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}A&amp;=\frac{70}{100}\times\text{Rp}100{,}000{,}000{,}000\\&amp;=\frac7{10}\times\text{Rp}100{,}000{,}000{,}000\\&amp;=\text{Rp}70{,}000{,}000{,}000\end{aligned}" />

Therefore, the allocation for agricultural production facilities and infrastructure would have been <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}70{,}000{,}000{,}000.00" />.
