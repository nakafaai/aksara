The passage makes two separate points:

1. Instant noodles should be occasional rather than an everyday complete meal.
2. Less seasoning, vegetables, and protein can improve the balance of that meal.

The second option preserves both points without promising more than the text supports. The other options make absolute claims. Changing the cooking water does not remove all sodium, olive oil does not make unlimited portions healthy, and vegetables do not cancel sodium already present in the seasoning.

Therefore, the second option is the best-supported argument.
