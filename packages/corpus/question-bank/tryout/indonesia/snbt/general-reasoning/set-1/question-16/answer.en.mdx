The key is to distinguish **improving a meal** from a guarantee that it can be eaten without limits. The passage supports these two adjustments.

| Adjustment | Benefit stated in the passage |
| --- | --- |
| Use less seasoning | Reduce sodium from the seasoning used |
| Add vegetables and a source of protein | Increase the variety of nutrients in the meal |

Both adjustments still come with the advice to eat instant noodles **occasionally**. The first option therefore preserves all the passage's limits.

The other options go further: a complete daily meal, removal of all sodium by changing the water, unlimited consumption after adding olive oil, or cancellation of sodium's effects by adding vegetables. The passage provides no basis for those absolute guarantees.
