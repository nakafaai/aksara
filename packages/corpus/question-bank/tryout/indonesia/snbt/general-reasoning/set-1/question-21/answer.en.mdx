Separate the odd and even positions. The odd-position terms form an arithmetic sequence with common difference <InlineMath math="4" />, while the even-position terms form a geometric sequence with ratio <InlineMath math="3" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 3,\ 7,\ 11,\ 15,\ 19 \\ 2,\ 6,\ 18,\ 54 \end{aligned}" />

The ninth term continues the odd-position sequence, and the eighth term continues the even-position sequence.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} a_9 &= 15+4=19 \\ a_8 &= 18\times3=54 \\ a_8+a_9 &= 54+19=73 \end{aligned}" />

The requested sum is <InlineMath math="73" />.
