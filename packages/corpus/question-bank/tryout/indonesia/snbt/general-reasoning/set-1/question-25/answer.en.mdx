Let

- <InlineMath math="p" /> mean the data audit passes,
- <InlineMath math="q" /> mean the report is published,
- <InlineMath math="r" /> mean the data files are archived.

The premises are <InlineMath math="p\rightarrow q" />, <InlineMath math="q\rightarrow r" />, and <InlineMath math="\neg r" />. The contrapositive of <InlineMath math="q\rightarrow r" /> gives <InlineMath math="\neg r\rightarrow\neg q" />. The contrapositive of <InlineMath math="p\rightarrow q" /> then gives <InlineMath math="\neg q\rightarrow\neg p" />.

Because the files were not archived, the report was not published and the audit did not pass. Therefore, **the data audit did not pass** must be true.
