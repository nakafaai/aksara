The statement that is definitely false is:

> *"Side sleeping guarantees that all snoring will stop and makes medical assessment unnecessary."*

The passage uses the qualified words *can help* and *some people*. Side sleeping may reduce snoring for part of the population, while others may see no benefit. It also explicitly recommends medical assessment when snoring is accompanied by breathing pauses, gasping, or choking.

The other choices preserve those qualifications and restate information given in the passage.
