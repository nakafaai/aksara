Admission requires both conditions:

- a completed bachelor's degree;
- a signed community-service commitment.

Let <InlineMath math="D" /> mean that the degree is complete and <InlineMath math="C" /> that the commitment is signed. The rule requires <InlineMath math="D \land C" />. Surya's situation is <InlineMath math="\lnot D \land C" />: he satisfies the second condition but not the first.

One missing mandatory condition is enough to make him ineligible. The conclusion that he will definitely be accepted is therefore **definitely false**.
