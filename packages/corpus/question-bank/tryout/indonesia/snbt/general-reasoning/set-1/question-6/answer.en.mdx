A sound conclusion must cover the passage's main point without turning an association into proof of a single cause.

The passage says that the ministry prioritized support for agricultural production. It then reports historical increases in rice and corn production and gives the 2017 budget share for production facilities and infrastructure. The fourth choice combines those points without claiming more than the passage establishes.

The other choices either contradict the figures or overstate the evidence:

- The ministry did not spend its entire budget on this support.
- Corn production was reported to have risen more than rice production.
- The figures do not prove that budget refocusing was the only cause.
- The remaining budget share was available for other items, so it could still be used.

Therefore, the fourth choice is the best-supported conclusion.
