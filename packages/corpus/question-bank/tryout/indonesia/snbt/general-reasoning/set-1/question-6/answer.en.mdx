A sound conclusion must cover the passage's main point without turning an association into proof of a single cause.

The passage says that the ministry prioritized support for agricultural production. It then reports historical increases in rice and corn production and gives the <InlineMath math="2017" /> budget share for production facilities and infrastructure. The conclusion about support priorities and production increases combines those points without claiming more than the passage establishes.

The other choices either contradict the figures or overstate the evidence:

- The ministry did not spend its entire budget on this support.
- Different lower bounds do not establish which actual increase was larger. The passage gives no exact percentages supporting the claim that corn rose less.
- The figures do not prove that budget refocusing was the only cause.
- The remaining budget share was available for other items, so it could still be used.

Therefore, the strongest conclusion combines the priority given to production support with the historical increases reported.
