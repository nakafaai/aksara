Treat the full tank as <InlineMath math="1" /> unit. Pump <InlineMath math="A" /> fills <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{12}" /> of the tank per hour, and pump <InlineMath math="B" /> fills <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{18}" /> per hour.

During the first <InlineMath math="4" /> hours, the filled fraction is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} v &=4\left(\frac{1}{12}+\frac{1}{18}\right) \\ &=4\times\frac{5}{36}=\frac{5}{9} \\ 1-v &=\frac{4}{9} \end{aligned}" />

Pump <InlineMath math="A" /> alone fills the remaining <InlineMath math="\frac{4}{9}" /> of the tank. The additional time is

<BlockMath math="t=\frac{4}{9}\div\frac{1}{12}=\frac{16}{3}" />

The question asks for the total time since both pumps started, so include the first <InlineMath math="4" /> hours.

<BlockMath math="4+\frac{16}{3}=\frac{28}{3}" />

The total time is <InlineMath math="\frac{28}{3}" /> hours.
