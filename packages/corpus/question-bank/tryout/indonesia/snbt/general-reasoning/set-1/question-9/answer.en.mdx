Treat the entire painting job as <InlineMath math="1" /> unit. A **work rate** tells us what fraction of that job someone completes in one hour.

#### Find the work rate of Uncle Udin

Father completes <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{20}" /> of the job per hour. Together, Father and Uncle Udin complete <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{4}" /> of the job per hour. Uncle Udin's rate is the **combined rate minus Father's rate**:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} r_U &amp;= \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{20} \\ &amp;= \frac{5}{20} - \frac{1}{20} \\ &amp;= \frac{1}{5} \end{aligned}" />

#### Find the work that remains

The **fraction of the job Father completes** during the first <InlineMath math="8\text{ hours}" /> is

<BlockMath math="8\times\frac1{20}=\frac8{20}=\frac25" />

Uncle Udin must finish the remaining <InlineMath math="1 - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3}{5}" />.

#### Find the time Uncle Udin needs

You find the time by dividing **remaining work by the work rate**:

<BlockMath math="t=\frac{\frac35}{\frac15}=\frac35\times5=3\text{ hours}" />

Uncle Udin needs <InlineMath math="3\text{ hours}" /> to finish the remaining work.
