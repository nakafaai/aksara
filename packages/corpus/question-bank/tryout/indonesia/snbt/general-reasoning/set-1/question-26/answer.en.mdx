Machine <InlineMath math="C" />’s hourly energy use rises from <InlineMath math="4" /> to <InlineMath math="4(1.25)=5" /> units. With operating hours and output unchanged, the indices become

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} I_A &= \frac{520-20}{10\times5} \\ &=\frac{500}{50}=10 \\ I_B &= \frac{480-12}{8\times6} \\ &=\frac{468}{48}=9.75 \\ I_C &= \frac{600-24}{12\times5} \\ &=\frac{576}{60}=9.6 \end{aligned}" />
