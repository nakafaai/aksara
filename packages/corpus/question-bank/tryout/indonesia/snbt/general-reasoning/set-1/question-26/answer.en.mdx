The data are **irrelevant to statements A and B**.

Evidence strengthens or weakens a statement only when it changes how well that statement is supported. Here, the data explain why public transport ridership increased: lower fares and better access. They do not report whether emissions changed, whether the air became cleaner, whether factories discharged waste, or whether river water quality changed.

Public transport may affect air quality in the real world, but that effect is not measured in the information given. We therefore cannot use these data to strengthen or weaken either statement.
