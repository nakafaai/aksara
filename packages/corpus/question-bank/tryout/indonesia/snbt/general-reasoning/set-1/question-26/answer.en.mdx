The second premise guarantees that at least one person has completed safety training and is also a volunteer. The third premise says that every volunteer is outside the paid staff.

That person is therefore someone with safety training who is not paid staff. No relationship between volunteers and laboratory assistants is given, so it does not follow that some assistants are volunteers.

Thus, **some people with safety training are not paid staff** must be true.
