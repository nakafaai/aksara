The statement that is **not true** is:

> *"More atmospheric carbon dioxide can only reduce crop growth and yield."*

Paragraph <InlineMath math="1" /> describes two different effects. More carbon dioxide can stimulate the growth of some crops, while also lowering the concentration of important nutrients. The word *only* removes the first possibility and therefore contradicts the passage.

The other choices restate information given in the paragraph: climate change already affects food security, the effects vary by crop and region, and temperature, rainfall, and extreme events all contribute to the risk.
