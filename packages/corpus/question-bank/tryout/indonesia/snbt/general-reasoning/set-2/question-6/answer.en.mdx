The "moved day" claim says that **the same people** used to come on other days and now come on Wednesday. The strongest support is evidence that shows those visitors are the same people.

A survey finding that most second-month Wednesday visitors had visited on another day in the first month provides exactly that evidence. It also explains why the other days lost visitors.

The other options do not support the claim:

- A new collection should raise visitors on every day, yet the days other than Wednesday fell. It does not explain a shift.
- More frequent rain is **another explanation** for fewer visitors on other days. It competes with the moved-day claim rather than supporting it.
- Students coming with their schools could be new visitors, so this does not show a shift.
- Higher parking fees are another explanation for fewer visitors, unrelated to the free tickets.

So the information that supports the claim most is the survey showing that most Wednesday visitors had come on another day in the first month.
