The percentage decrease uses **initial sales as the denominator**, not final sales. With initial sales <InlineMath math="a" /> and final sales <InlineMath math="b" />,

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{a-b}{a}\times100\%" />

| Item and years | Decrease | Percentage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shirts <InlineMath math="2011\text{-}2012" /> | <InlineMath math="50-35=15" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{50-35}{50}\times100\%=\frac{15}{50}\times100\%=30\%" /> |
| Shirts <InlineMath math="2012\text{-}2013" /> | <InlineMath math="35-20=15" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{35-20}{35}\times100\%=\frac{15}{35}\times100\%\approx42.86\%" /> |
| Suits <InlineMath math="2014\text{-}2015" /> | <InlineMath math="60-50=10" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{60-50}{60}\times100\%=\frac{10}{60}\times100\%\approx16.67\%" /> |
| Suits <InlineMath math="2012\text{-}2013" /> | <InlineMath math="55-45=10" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{55-45}{55}\times100\%=\frac{10}{55}\times100\%\approx18.18\%" /> |
| Pants <InlineMath math="2013\text{-}2014" /> | <InlineMath math="50-40=10" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{50-40}{50}\times100\%=\frac{10}{50}\times100\%=20\%" /> |

Three options each fall by <InlineMath math="10" /> units, but their percentages differ because their initial sales differ. The **smallest** decrease is for suits in <InlineMath math="2014\text{-}2015" />, approximately <InlineMath math="16.67\%" />. The third option is correct.
