A statement is Benar (true) only if it holds in **all four arrangements** that satisfy the conditions. Otherwise it is Salah (false).

| Arrangement | Top row (columns 1, 2, 3) | Bottom row (columns 1, 2, 3) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Beni, Ana, Eli | Caca, Doni, Fajar |
| II | Caca, Ana, Fajar | Beni, Doni, Eli |
| III | Beni, Eli, Ana | Caca, Fajar, Doni |
| IV | Caca, Fajar, Ana | Beni, Eli, Doni |

**Statement 1** is true. Ana and Doni fill one column and Caca and Beni fill column 1, so Eli and Fajar always fill the remaining column together. They are both in column 3 in I and II and both in column 2 in III and IV.

**Statement 2** is true. Caca and Beni are both in column 1, so they must be in different rows.

**Statement 3** is false. In arrangements I and II, Doni is in column 2.

**Statement 4** is false. Ana is always in the top row, while Eli is in the bottom row in arrangements II and IV.

So statements 1 and 2 are true, and statements 3 and 4 are false.
