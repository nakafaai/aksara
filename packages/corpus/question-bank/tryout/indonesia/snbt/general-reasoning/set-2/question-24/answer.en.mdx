Measure rates in **tankfuls per hour**. The combined rate is

<BlockMath math="\frac1{15}+\frac1{10}=\frac2{30}+\frac3{30}=\frac5{30}=\frac16" />

In <InlineMath math="4" /> hours, the pumps fill <InlineMath math="4\times\frac16=\frac23" /> of the tank. The remaining fraction is <InlineMath math="1-\frac23=\frac13" />.

A <InlineMath math="20\%" /> reduction leaves <InlineMath math="100\%-20\%=80\%=\frac45" /> of pump <InlineMath math="A" />’s original rate. Its new rate is

<BlockMath math="\frac45\times\frac1{15}=\frac4{75}" />

The question asks for the **additional time** after pump <InlineMath math="B" /> stops. Divide the unfilled fraction by pump <InlineMath math="A" />’s new rate:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}t&amp;=\frac{1/3}{4/75}\\&amp;=\frac13\times\frac{75}4\\&amp;=\frac{25}4\text{ hours}\end{aligned}" />
