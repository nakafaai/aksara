Subtract defective units from output to find the **good units**. Total energy use equals operating time multiplied by hourly energy use, <InlineMath math="te" />. Divide good units by total energy use to calculate each machine’s index.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}I_A&amp;=\frac{560-20}{10\times5}=10.8\\I_B&amp;=\frac{500-20}{10\times4}=12\\I_C&amp;=\frac{650-30}{13\times5}\\&amp;=\frac{620}{65}\approx9.54\end{aligned}" />

The ranking is <InlineMath math="I_B>I_A>I_C" />. Machine <InlineMath math="B" /> is best and machine <InlineMath math="A" /> is second.
