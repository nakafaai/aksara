Each pairwise intersection includes the <InlineMath math="10" /> people who use all three services. Subtract those people from each intersection to obtain the counts for exactly two services.

| Only these services | Number of users |
| :--- | :---: |
| <InlineMath math="A,B" /> | <InlineMath math="25-10=15" /> |
| <InlineMath math="A,C" /> | <InlineMath math="20-10=10" /> |
| <InlineMath math="B,C" /> | <InlineMath math="18-10=8" /> |

The resulting groups are disjoint, so add their counts.

<BlockMath math="15+10+8=33" />

Therefore, **<InlineMath math="33" /> participants** use exactly two services.
