Compare the first two expressions. Both contain **luma** and both meanings include "blue," so **luma** means "blue." The differing words show that **sari** means "book" and **peka** means "pen."

In the third expression, **naro** replaces **luma** while the object remains a pen. Therefore, **naro** means "red."

The invented language places the color before the object. Hence, "red book" is **naro sari**.
