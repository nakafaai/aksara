The machines' combined hourly rate is

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{12}+\frac{1}{18}=\frac{5}{36}" />

In three hours, they complete

<BlockMath math="3\times\frac{5}{36}=\frac{5}{12}" />

of the job. The remaining part is <InlineMath math="1-\frac{5}{12}=\frac{7}{12}" />. Machine <InlineMath math="B" /> works at <InlineMath math="\frac{1}{18}" /> of the job per hour, so the required time is

<BlockMath math="\frac{7}{12}\div\frac{1}{18}=\frac{7}{12}\times18=10.5\text{ hours}" />

Machine <InlineMath math="B" /> must therefore work for another **<InlineMath math="10.5\text{ hours}" />**.
