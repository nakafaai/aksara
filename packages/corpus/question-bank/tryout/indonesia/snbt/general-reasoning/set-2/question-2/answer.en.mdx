First, separate the premises:

1. **All residents** of Nelayan Village are fish farmers.
2. Residents who **have fish-farming land** <InlineMath math="\rightarrow" /> make **organic feed**.
3. Residents who **do not have fish-farming land** <InlineMath math="\rightarrow" /> make **non-organic feed**.

Every resident must fall into one of two conditions: either they have land or they do not have land.

- If a resident has land, they make organic feed.
- If a resident does not have land, they make non-organic feed.

These two conditions cover every resident. Therefore, every resident makes at least one of the two kinds of feed: organic feed **or** non-organic feed. Here, *or* does not require every resident to make both kinds. The premises also do not rule out a resident making both.

Thus, the conclusion that follows from all the premises is **all residents of Nelayan Village make organic fish feed or non-organic fish feed**.
