First, separate the premises:

1. **All residents** of Nelayan Village are fish farmers.
2. Residents who **have fish-farming land** <InlineMath math="\rightarrow" /> make **organic feed**.
3. Residents who **do not have fish-farming land** <InlineMath math="\rightarrow" /> make **inorganic feed**.

Every resident must fall into one of two conditions: either they have land or they do not have land.

- If a resident has land, they make organic feed.
- If a resident does not have land, they make inorganic feed.

These two conditions cover every resident. Therefore, every resident makes one of the two kinds of feed: organic feed **or** inorganic feed. Here, *or* does not mean that every resident makes both kinds.

Thus, the conclusion that follows from all the premises is **all residents of Nelayan Village make organic fish feed or inorganic fish feed**.
