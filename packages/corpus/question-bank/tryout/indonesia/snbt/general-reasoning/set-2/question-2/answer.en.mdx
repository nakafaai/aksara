Every resident either has fish-farming land or does not. These conditions **cover every resident**, so you can follow the two possibilities separately.

| Resident's condition | Consequence guaranteed by the premise |
| --- | --- |
| Has fish-farming land | Makes organic feed |
| Does not have fish-farming land | Makes non-organic feed |

In both rows, the resident makes **at least one** kind of feed. Therefore, every resident makes organic or non-organic feed, as stated in the first option.

Here, *or* does not require everyone to make both kinds. The premises also do not prohibit anyone from making both. They give neither the number of landowners nor a restriction on making the other kind of feed, so the remaining four options do not follow.
