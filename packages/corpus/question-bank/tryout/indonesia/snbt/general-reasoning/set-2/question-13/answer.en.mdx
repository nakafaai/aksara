The first sentence says that domestic rice procurement had been declining while rice imports could increase.

The two quantities are described as moving in opposite directions:

- Domestic procurement **falls**.
- Rice imports may **rise**.

The passage gives no equation that would justify the mathematical term *inversely proportional*. The precise conclusion is that **domestic procurement and rice imports are described as moving in opposite directions**.
