The residents' argument is: **The increase in motorcycle theft is caused by security guards not performing their duties properly.**

To weaken the argument, we need evidence that makes the guards' alleged negligence less plausible as the cause.

The statement that **security guards patrol regularly** supplies counterevidence: the guards are carrying out at least one central security duty. Their patrols do not prove that every duty is performed perfectly. They do, however, make the residents' explanation less convincing than the other options do.

Therefore, regular patrols are the strongest weakening statement.
