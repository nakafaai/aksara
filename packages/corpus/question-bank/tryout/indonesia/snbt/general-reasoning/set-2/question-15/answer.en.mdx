From the four possible arrangements, keep those that meet **the extra condition: Fajar in the top row**. Only arrangements II and IV do.

| Arrangement | Top row (columns 1, 2, 3) | Bottom row (columns 1, 2, 3) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| II | Caca, Ana, Fajar | Beni, Doni, Eli |
| IV | Caca, Fajar, Ana | Beni, Eli, Doni |

You can also derive this directly. If Fajar is on top, Eli, who shares Fajar's column, is below. Beni shares Eli's row, so Beni is also below, in column 1. Caca therefore takes the top locker in column 1.

In both arrangements, **Caca is in the top row**. This statement must be true.

The other options are not certain:

- Ana is in column 2 in arrangement II but in column 3 in arrangement IV.
- Eli is in column 3 in arrangement II but in column 2 in arrangement IV.
- In II, Doni is in column 2 and Fajar in column 3. In IV, Doni is in column 3 and Fajar in column 2. Doni is never directly below Fajar.
- Beni is always in the bottom row in both arrangements.

So if Fajar's locker is in the top row, Caca's locker must be in the top row.
