The government purchase-price line is **horizontal** at <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}3{,}700.00/\text{kg}" /> throughout all four months. The government price is therefore unchanged, as stated in the first option.

Farm-gate prices instead fall each month. Their March and April gaps above the government price also differ:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}4{,}604-3{,}700&amp;=904\\4{,}357-3{,}700&amp;=657\end{aligned}" />

Both gaps are in rupiah per kilogram. A fixed government price does not establish inverse proportionality with a changing farm-gate price. The other options therefore do not match the chart.
