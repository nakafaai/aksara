Low domestic rice procurement can weaken price stabilization at the farmer or producer level. Over time, this can erode farmers' purchasing power and welfare. Statistics Indonesia (BPS) recorded that the average farm-gate price of GKP (harvested dry unhusked rice) fell from <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}5{,}353.00" /> per kilogram in January to <InlineMath math="\text{Rp}4{,}357.00" /> per kilogram in April. The decline moved in the same direction as the NTP farmer terms-of-trade indicator, which is used to assess farmers' purchasing power and welfare. A BPS survey of <InlineMath math="2{,}431" /> grain-sale transactions in <InlineMath math="30" /> provinces in April <InlineMath math="2019" /> also found that the average farm-gate GKP price fell <InlineMath math="5.37\%" /> from the previous month. At the same time, prices for several necessities, including shallots, garlic, chilies, chicken eggs, and tomatoes, rose. Therefore, besides raising the government purchase price for grain, the government needed to expand distribution channels to increase rice procurement.

*Adapted from Kompas, May 3, 2019. Price data were checked against the official BPS release of May 2, 2019.*
