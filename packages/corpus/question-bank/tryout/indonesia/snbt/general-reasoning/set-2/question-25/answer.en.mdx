Let <InlineMath math="p" /> mean maintenance is underway, <InlineMath math="q" /> mean registration is suspended, and <InlineMath math="r" /> mean a notice appears.

The premises form the chain <InlineMath math="p\rightarrow q\rightarrow r" />. Since <InlineMath math="r" /> did not occur, contraposition gives <InlineMath math="\neg r\rightarrow\neg q" /> and then <InlineMath math="\neg q\rightarrow\neg p" />.

Therefore, **server maintenance is not underway**. The other options add facts that are not contained in the premises.
