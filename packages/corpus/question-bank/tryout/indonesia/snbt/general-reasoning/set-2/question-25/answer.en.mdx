Multiply the mean by the number of members to find the **total score**. Group <InlineMath math="B" /> initially has a total score of

<BlockMath math="9\times61=549" />

After one member joins, group <InlineMath math="B" /> has <InlineMath math="10" /> people. Its total score becomes

<BlockMath math="10\times63=630" />

The existing members’ scores stay unchanged, so the increase in the total is the **moving person’s score**:

<BlockMath math="630-549=81" />

The difference from group <InlineMath math="A" />’s original mean is

<BlockMath math="81-73=8" />
