The passage states that suboptimal procurement could leave the Government Rice Reserve (CBP) insufficient, especially during the lean season.

The relationship is direct: persistently low procurement reduces the government's ability to maintain its rice stock. The passage does not say that imports must occur, the planting season must change, or farmers must lack production inputs.

Thus, the supported conclusion is **government rice stocks, or the CBP, are at risk of declining**.
