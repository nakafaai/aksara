The example **o!miaw nmee!owi eng** places the object first and inserts the subject into **n...i eng**. Applying that same pattern gives the following mapping.

| Part | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **o!miaw** | Object: cat |
| **mee!ow inside n...i eng** | Subject: girl |

In the new sentence, the object is **mee!ow** and the subject is **o!miaw**. Inserting the subject into the frame gives **no!miawi eng**. The letter **i** is added after **w** without changing the base word.

The complete sentence is therefore **mee!ow no!miawi eng**.
