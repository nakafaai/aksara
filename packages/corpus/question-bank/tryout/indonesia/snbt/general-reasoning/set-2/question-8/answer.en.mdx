At a **constant rate**, output is proportional to operating time. Let <InlineMath math="N_5" /> and <InlineMath math="N_2" /> be the numbers of units made by machine <InlineMath math="A" /> in <InlineMath math="5" /> and <InlineMath math="2" /> hours.

Machine <InlineMath math="A" /> makes three times as many units as machine <InlineMath math="B" /> in the same time.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}N_5&amp;=3\times30\\&amp;=90\end{aligned}" />

Then apply the **time ratio**. Divide the five-hour output by five to get the hourly output, then multiply by two.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}N_2&amp;=\frac25\times90\\&amp;=2\times18\\&amp;=36\end{aligned}" />

Machine <InlineMath math="A" /> therefore produces <InlineMath math="36" /> units in <InlineMath math="2" /> hours.
