Given:
1. Bananas contain **vitamin B6** and **a small amount of fat**.
2. The child eats a banana.

Therefore, the child obtains vitamin B6 and **a small amount of fat** from that food.

The phrase *a small amount* still means that some fat is present. It does not mean zero. Therefore, the statement **"The child will not get any fat"** directly contradicts the premise.

Thus, the correct answer is **the child will not get any fat**.

[NIH lists bananas and several other foods as sources of vitamin B6](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/). The [USDA composition table for raw bananas](https://www.growables.org/information/TropicalFruit/documents/BananaNutrient.pdf) also records a small, nonzero amount of fat.
