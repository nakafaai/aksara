Given:
1. Bananas contain **a lot of vitamin B6** and **a small amount of fat**.
2. The child eats a banana.

Therefore, the child obtains a lot of vitamin B6 and **a small amount of fat** from that food.

The phrase *a small amount* still means that some fat is present. It does not mean zero. Therefore, the statement **"The child will not get any fat"** directly contradicts the premise.

Thus, the correct answer is **the child will not get any fat**.
