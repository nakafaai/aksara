Bananas contain vitamin B6 and **a small amount of fat**. A child eating a banana obtains both substances from that food.

A *small amount* is small but **not zero**. Therefore, the second option, “The child will not get any fat”, directly contradicts the premise. The options about children obtaining fat or vitamin B6 agree with the premise. The possibility of other vitamin B6 sources does not contradict it.

[NIH lists bananas and several other foods as sources of vitamin B6](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/). The [USDA composition table for raw bananas](https://www.growables.org/information/TropicalFruit/documents/BananaNutrient.pdf) also records a small, nonzero amount of fat.
