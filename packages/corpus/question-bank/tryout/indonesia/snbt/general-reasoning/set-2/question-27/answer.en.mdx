Express every price in thousand rupiah. Undo the price changes in **reverse order**. The <InlineMath math="10\%" /> tax multiplies the price by <InlineMath math="1.10" />, so divide to recover the price before tax:

<BlockMath math="\frac{792}{1.10}=720" />

Add back the voucher to recover the marked-up price.

<BlockMath math="720+80=800" />

Divide by the markup factor <InlineMath math="1+0.25=1.25" /> to obtain the original price <InlineMath math="p" />.

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{800}{1.25}=640" />

Use the **original price as the denominator** to calculate the effective increase to the final price, including the voucher and tax.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}r&amp;=\frac{792-640}{640}\times100\%\\&amp;=\frac{152}{640}\times100\%\\&amp;=23.75\%\end{aligned}" />
