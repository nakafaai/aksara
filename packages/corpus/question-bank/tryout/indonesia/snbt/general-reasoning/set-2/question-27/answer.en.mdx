Express every price in thousand rupiah. Reverse the price changes, starting by removing the tax.

<BlockMath math="\frac{792}{1.10}=720" />

Add back the voucher to recover the marked-up price.

<BlockMath math="720+80=800" />

Divide by the markup factor to obtain the original price <InlineMath math="p" />.

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{800}{1.25}=640" />

Calculate the effective increase from the original to the final price, including the voucher and tax.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}r&=\frac{792-640}{640}\times100\%\\&=\frac{152}{640}\times100\%\\&=23.75\%\end{aligned}" />
