Misalkan <InlineMath math="a" /> adalah penjualan awal dan <InlineMath math="b" /> penjualan akhir. Persentase penurunannya adalah

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{a-b}{a}\times100\%" />
| Barang | Tahun | Awal | Akhir | Perhitungan |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Baju | <InlineMath math="2011\text{-}2012" /> | <InlineMath math="50" /> | <InlineMath math="35" /> | <InlineMath math="30\%" /> |
| Baju | <InlineMath math="2012\text{-}2013" /> | <InlineMath math="35" /> | <InlineMath math="20" /> | <InlineMath math="\approx42{,}86\%" /> |
| Jas | <InlineMath math="2014\text{-}2015" /> | <InlineMath math="60" /> | <InlineMath math="50" /> | <InlineMath math="\approx16{,}67\%" /> |
| Jas | <InlineMath math="2012\text{-}2013" /> | <InlineMath math="55" /> | <InlineMath math="45" /> | <InlineMath math="\approx18{,}18\%" /> |
| Celana | <InlineMath math="2013\text{-}2014" /> | <InlineMath math="50" /> | <InlineMath math="40" /> | <InlineMath math="20\%" /> |

Contohnya, untuk jas pada perbandingan tersebut:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{60-50}{60}\times100\%\\&=\frac{10}{60}\times100\%\\&\approx 16{,}67\%\end{aligned}" />

Di antara pilihan yang diberikan, penurunan terkecil terjadi pada penjualan **jas dari tahun <InlineMath math="2014" /> hingga <InlineMath math="2015" />**, sekitar <InlineMath math="16{,}67\%" />.


**Baju <InlineMath math="2011" /> hingga <InlineMath math="2012" />:**

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{50-35}{50}\times100\%\\&=\frac{15}{50}\times100\%\\&=30\%\end{aligned}" />

**Baju <InlineMath math="2012" /> hingga <InlineMath math="2013" />:**

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{35-20}{35}\times100\%\\&=\frac{15}{35}\times100\%\\&\approx42{,}86\%\end{aligned}" />

**Jas <InlineMath math="2014" /> hingga <InlineMath math="2015" />:**

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{60-50}{60}\times100\%\\&=\frac{10}{60}\times100\%\\&\approx16{,}67\%\end{aligned}" />

**Jas <InlineMath math="2012" /> hingga <InlineMath math="2013" />:**

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{55-45}{55}\times100\%\\&=\frac{10}{55}\times100\%\\&\approx18{,}18\%\end{aligned}" />

**Celana <InlineMath math="2013" /> hingga <InlineMath math="2014" />:**

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{50-40}{50}\times100\%\\&=\frac{10}{50}\times100\%\\&=20\%\end{aligned}" />
