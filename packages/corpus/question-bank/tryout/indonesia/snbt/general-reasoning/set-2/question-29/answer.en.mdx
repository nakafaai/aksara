There are <InlineMath math="\binom{10}{2}=45" /> equally likely ball pairs in one round.

| Outcome | Pairs | Payout |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Two red | <InlineMath math="\binom52=10" /> | <InlineMath math="40" /> |
| Different colours | <InlineMath math="5\times5=25" /> | <InlineMath math="20" /> |
| Two blue | <InlineMath math="\binom52=10" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

Payouts in the table are in thousand rupiah. The expected payout for one round is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}E_1&=40\frac{10}{45}+20\frac{25}{45}\\&\quad+0\frac{10}{45}\\&=\frac{400+500}{45}\\&=20\end{aligned}" />

The box is restored before round two, so that round has the same expected payout. The total expected payout is

<BlockMath math="E_1+E_2=20+20=40" />

A fair fee equals the expected payout, making the expected net gain zero. The total fair fee is therefore **<InlineMath math="40" /> thousand rupiah**.
