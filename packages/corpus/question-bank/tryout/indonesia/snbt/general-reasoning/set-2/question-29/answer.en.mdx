There are <InlineMath math="9" /> balls, so the total number of possible pairs is

<BlockMath math="\binom{9}{2}=36" />

A same-color pair can be two red, two blue, or two green balls:

<BlockMath math="\binom{4}{2}+\binom{3}{2}+\binom{2}{2}=6+3+1=10" />

Therefore, the probability is

<BlockMath math="\frac{10}{36}=\frac{5}{18}" />

The probability of drawing two balls of the same color is **<InlineMath math="\frac{5}{18}" />**.
