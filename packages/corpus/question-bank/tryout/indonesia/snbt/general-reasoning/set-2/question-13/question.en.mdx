Domestic rice procurement had tended to decline for three years, which could lead to greater rice imports. Perum Bulog's procurement balance was considered worrying and could weaken its price-stabilization role. One proposed solution was to give Bulog priority in distributing rice through the BPNT non-cash food-assistance program, expanding its distribution channels and reducing business losses. Sapuan Gafar, a former Deputy Head of Bulog, argued that this solution would be relatively simple because it would change neither the budget allocation nor the program's name. It would, however, require a revision to Presidential Regulation Number <InlineMath math="63" /> of <InlineMath math="2017" /> on Non-Cash Social Assistance Distribution.
