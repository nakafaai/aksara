Domestic rice procurement had tended to decline for three years, which could lead to greater rice imports. Perum Bulog's procurement balance was considered worrying and could weaken its price-stabilization role. One proposed solution was to give Bulog priority in distributing rice through the BPNT non-cash food-assistance program, expanding its distribution channels and reducing business losses. Sapuan Gafar, a former Deputy Head of Bulog and Secretary to the State Minister for Food Affairs from 1993 to 1999, argued that this solution would be relatively simple because it would change neither the budget allocation nor the program's name. It would, however, require a revision to Presidential Regulation Number 63 of 2017 on Non-Cash Social Assistance Distribution.
