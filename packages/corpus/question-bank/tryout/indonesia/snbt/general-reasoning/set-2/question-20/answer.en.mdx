The destination values reported by Statistics Indonesia for <InlineMath math="2018" /> are:

- United States: <InlineMath math="\text{US\$}3.78\text{ billion}" />
- Japan: <InlineMath math="\text{US\$}740.9\text{ million}" /> (about <InlineMath math="\text{US\$}0.74\text{ billion}" />)
- Germany: <InlineMath math="\text{US\$}372.48\text{ million}" /> (about <InlineMath math="\text{US\$}0.37\text{ billion}" />)

The United States alone received <InlineMath math="51\%" /> of the total garment exports. The passage also identifies Japan as the **second-largest** destination. Together, those facts establish that the United States ranked first.

Therefore, **the United States was the main market for Indonesia's ready-made garments**.
