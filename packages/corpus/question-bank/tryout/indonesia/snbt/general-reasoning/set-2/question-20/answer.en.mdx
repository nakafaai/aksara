Compare **export values by destination**, rather than only their percentage changes. The largest value in the passage is for the United States.

| Destination | Export value (million US$) |
| --- | --- |
| United States | <InlineMath math="3{,}774.3" /> |
| Japan | <InlineMath math="715.3" /> |
| Germany | <InlineMath math="381.2" /> |

The United States accounts for **more than half** the total:

<BlockMath math="\frac{3{,}774.3}{7{,}284.8}\times100\%\approx51.8\%" />

The United States is therefore the main market in the year described. The two options claiming a decline contradict the increase stated in the passage. Japan ranks second, so it is not the main market and cannot be omitted from the two largest destinations.
