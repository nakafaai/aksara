Find every possible arrangement. Start from the tightest condition: **Ana and Doni fill one whole column**, Ana on top and Doni below.

Caca is in column 1, so Ana and Doni cannot use column 1. Their column is column 2 or column 3. After that, one column is still completely empty, and column 1 still has one free locker next to Caca.

Beni and Eli must share a row, with Eli to the right of Beni. Fajar cannot be in column 1. So the free locker in column 1 must go to Beni, and Eli and Fajar fill the empty column.

| Arrangement | Top row (columns 1, 2, 3) | Bottom row (columns 1, 2, 3) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Beni, Ana, Eli | Caca, Doni, Fajar |
| II | Caca, Ana, Fajar | Beni, Doni, Eli |
| III | Beni, Eli, Ana | Caca, Fajar, Doni |
| IV | Caca, Fajar, Ana | Beni, Eli, Doni |

In all four arrangements, **Beni is always in column 1**. This statement must be true.

The other statements hold only in some arrangements:

- Eli is in the top row in I and III but in the bottom row in II and IV.
- Fajar is in column 3 in I and II but in column 2 in III and IV.
- Ana is in column 2 in I and II but in column 3 in III and IV.
- Caca is in the bottom row in I and III but in the top row in II and IV.

So the statement that must be true is that Beni's locker is in column 1.
