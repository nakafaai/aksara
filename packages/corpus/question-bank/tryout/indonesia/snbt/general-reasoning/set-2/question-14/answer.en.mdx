The opening two sentences describe **possible consequences**, not a guarantee that they always occur. Follow the causal sequence given in the passage.

The fifth option follows this chain. A decline from year to year is not established by data for a few months. Raising the purchase price and revising regulations are policy proposals rather than the consequence asked for. The transaction option also misreads the percentage: it refers to the **GKP price**, not the number of sales transactions.
