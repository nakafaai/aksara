Focus on **only**: the claim rules out students choosing an expensive private university. The fourth option says students still choose a good university **even if it is expensive**. That directly contradicts the restriction to low-cost universities.

The options about facilities and lecturer quality merely identify factors students consider. Students could still want those qualities at a low price, so neither option directly contradicts the claim. The low-cost option supports it, while the definition of a good university does not state which university students choose.

The fourth option is therefore the strongest challenge.
