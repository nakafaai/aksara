There are <InlineMath math="\binom{10}{2}=45" /> equally likely pairs. The red-red, mixed, and blue-blue pair counts are

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \binom{6}{2}&=15 \\ 6\times4&=24 \\ \binom{4}{2}&=6 \end{aligned}" />

The expected listed payout, in thousand rupiah, is

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} E&=\frac{30(15)+15(24)+0(6)}{45} \\ &=18 \end{aligned}" />

The actual payout is <InlineMath math="90\%" /> of each prize, so its expectation is multiplied by the same factor:

<BlockMath math="0.9\times18=16.2=\frac{81}{5}" />
