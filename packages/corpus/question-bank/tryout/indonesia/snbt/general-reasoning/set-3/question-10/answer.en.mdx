Compare two neighboring groups letter by letter. You'll find **two rules working at the same time**: the order of the letters rotates, and every letter moves forward in the alphabet by a step that keeps growing.

Start with the rotation. Move the first letter of DIMA to the end and you get IMAD. Now compare IMAD with JNBE: <InlineMath math="\mathrm{I}\to\mathrm{J}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{M}\to\mathrm{N}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{A}\to\mathrm{B}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{D}\to\mathrm{E}" />. Every letter moves forward <InlineMath math="1" /> step.

Check that the rule continues. JNBE rotates to NBEJ, and comparing it with PDGL gives <InlineMath math="\mathrm{N}\to\mathrm{P}" />, with every other letter also moving forward <InlineMath math="2" /> steps. PDGL rotates to DGLP, and comparing it with GJOS shows every letter moving forward <InlineMath math="3" /> steps. The rotation stays the same, while the shift grows by one each time.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DIMA | IMAD | <InlineMath math="+1" /> | JNBE |
| JNBE | NBEJ | <InlineMath math="+2" /> | PDGL |
| PDGL | DGLP | <InlineMath math="+3" /> | GJOS |
| GJOS | JOSG | <InlineMath math="+4" /> | NSWK |

For the fifth group, rotate GJOS to JOSG, then move every letter forward <InlineMath math="4" /> steps: <InlineMath math="\mathrm{J}\to\mathrm{N}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{O}\to\mathrm{S}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{S}\to\mathrm{W}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{G}\to\mathrm{K}" />. The result is NSWK.

Here's a quick check. Four rotations of a four-letter group bring back the original order, and the total shift is <InlineMath math="1+2+3+4=10" />. So the fifth group must be DIMA with every letter moved forward <InlineMath math="10" /> steps: <InlineMath math="\mathrm{D}\to\mathrm{N}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{I}\to\mathrm{S}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{M}\to\mathrm{W}" />, <InlineMath math="\mathrm{A}\to\mathrm{K}" />. Same answer, NSWK.

Here's why the other options fail.

- JOSG is only rotated and never shifted.
- KNSW is shifted <InlineMath math="4" /> steps but never rotated.
- WKNS is rotated the wrong way, with the last letter moved to the front, and then shifted <InlineMath math="4" /> steps.
- MRVJ is rotated correctly but shifted <InlineMath math="3" /> steps again, as if the shift stayed fixed.

So the group that fills the blank is NSWK.
