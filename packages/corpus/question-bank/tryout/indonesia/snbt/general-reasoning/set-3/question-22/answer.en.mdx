Adding water increases the mixture volume to

<BlockMath math="50+5=55\,\text{L}" />

The solute amount remains unchanged during evaporation. Before evaporation, it is

<BlockMath math="0.20\times55=11\,\text{L}" />

Evaporation then returns the total volume to <InlineMath math="55-5=50\,\text{L}" />. The concentration after evaporation is therefore

<BlockMath math="\frac{11}{50}\times100\%=22\%" />
