Arrange the information as **chains of comparisons**. The sign <InlineMath math=">" /> means heavier.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
P &> S > R \\
P &> T > Q
\end{aligned}" />

The first chain comes from "S is lighter than P" and "R is lighter than S". The second comes from the statement about T and Q. P is at the top of both chains, so P is heavier than all four other boxes. So **P is certainly the heaviest box**.

The two chains are never compared with each other, so the order among S, R, T, and Q cannot be fixed:

- Q is not necessarily the lightest, because R might be lighter than Q.
- S and T are never compared, so S is not necessarily heavier than T.
- R and Q are never compared either.
- The second heaviest could be S or T, so T is not necessarily second.

So the statement that must be true is that P is the heaviest box.
