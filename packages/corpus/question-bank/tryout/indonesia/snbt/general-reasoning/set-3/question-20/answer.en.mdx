This question asks for the statement that's true in **every height order** that fits all four facts, not just in the first order you find.

Start with the fact that fixes a position, fact 4. Exactly three members are taller than Mira, so Mira is the fourth tallest. With six members, that means exactly two members are shorter than Mira.

Now look at who can be above Mira. Lusi is definitely taller than Mira, and Oki is definitely shorter (fact 1). So the three members taller than Mira are Lusi plus two of Kevin, Sekar and Niken.

Test Sekar with an assumption. Suppose Sekar were shorter than Mira. Niken is shorter than Sekar (fact 3), so Niken would be shorter than Mira too. Together with Oki, that's three members below Mira, but there's only room for two. The assumption is impossible, so **Sekar must be taller than Mira**.

To see the full picture, split every order into two cases by where Niken is. Orders are listed from tallest to shortest.

- Niken is taller than Mira. The top three are Lusi, Sekar and Niken. Kevin drops below Mira, and since Kevin is taller than Oki, the bottom two are Kevin and then Oki. For example: Lusi, Sekar, Niken, Mira, Kevin, Oki.
- Niken is shorter than Mira. The bottom two are Niken and Oki in either order. The top three are Lusi, Kevin and Sekar, with Lusi taller than Kevin. For example: Lusi, Kevin, Sekar, Mira, Oki, Niken.

In both cases, Sekar is above Mira.

Each other option has a valid order that makes it false.

- Lusi being the tallest fails for Sekar, Lusi, Kevin, Mira, Niken, Oki. The facts only put Lusi above Mira, Kevin and Oki, not above Sekar.
- Kevin taller than Mira fails for Lusi, Sekar, Niken, Mira, Kevin, Oki. This option traps you if you forget that Niken can move above Mira and push Kevin down.
- Oki being the shortest fails for Lusi, Kevin, Sekar, Mira, Oki, Niken. No fact compares Oki with Niken.
- Sekar taller than Kevin fails for Lusi, Kevin, Sekar, Mira, Niken, Oki. The facts never compare Sekar with Kevin.

So the statement that must be true is that Sekar is taller than Mira.
