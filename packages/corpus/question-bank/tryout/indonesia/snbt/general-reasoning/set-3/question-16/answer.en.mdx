Compare values for the **same portion size**, <InlineMath math="100\,\text{g}" />. Do not mix energy values with protein or fat values, because their units differ.

| Nutrient | Calculation or comparison | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | <InlineMath math="52-38=14\,\text{kcal}" /> | Jicama provides less energy |
| Protein | <InlineMath math="0.72-0.26=0.46\,\text{g}" /> | Jicama provides more protein |
| Fat | <InlineMath math="0.17>0.09" /> | Apple provides more fat |

The other two choices compare each value with one gram. Both protein values are below one gram, as are both fat values:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}0.26&amp;<0.72<1\\0.09&amp;<0.17<1\end{aligned}" />

The incorrect choice says that apple contains **less fat** than jicama. The table instead shows that apple has <InlineMath math="0.17-0.09=0.08\,\text{g}" /> more fat. Two values can still be compared even when both are below one gram.
