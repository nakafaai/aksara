A statement is Benar (true) only if it holds in **all four arrangements**. Otherwise it is Salah (false). For an "if" statement, check only the arrangements where the "if" part holds.

| Arrangement | Top shelf | Middle shelf | Bottom shelf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | K, L | M, O, P | N, Q |
| II | K, L | N, O, P | M, Q |
| III | M, P | K, L, O | N, Q |
| IV | M, P | N, O, Q | K, L |

**Statement 1** is true. P is on the middle shelf in I and II and on the top shelf in III and IV. P is never at the bottom, because it must be higher than Q.

**Statement 2** is false. In arrangement IV, the bottom shelf holds K and L, while Q is in the middle.

**Statement 3** is false. N is on the middle shelf in II and IV. In II, Q is at the bottom, but in IV, Q is in the middle.

**Statement 4** is true. K is on the top shelf in I and II, and in both P is on the middle shelf.

So statements 1 and 4 are true, and statements 2 and 3 are false.
