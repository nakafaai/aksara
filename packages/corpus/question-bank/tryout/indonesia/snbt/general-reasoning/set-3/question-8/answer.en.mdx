#### Reading the passage

The passage says that delirium:

- begins acutely and can fluctuate;
- can occur with many acute medical conditions;
- can be an atypical presentation of COVID-19 in some older adults; and
- requires assessment for an underlying cause.

#### Checking the choices

Four choices restate those points. The remaining choice says that **everyone** with delirium must be infected with SARS-CoV-2. That changes a possible association into a universal rule.

The passage explicitly says delirium is not specific to COVID-19. Therefore, delirium alone cannot establish a SARS-CoV-2 infection.

The conclusion least consistent with the passage is: **Everyone who experiences delirium must be infected with SARS-CoV-2.**
