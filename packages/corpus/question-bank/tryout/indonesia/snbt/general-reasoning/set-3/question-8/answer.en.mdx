#### Distinguish possibility from certainty

The passage describes **several possible causes** of delirium. COVID-19 is one possibility, particularly in some older and frail adults. This association does not mean that COVID-19 causes every case of delirium.

| Statement | Strength of the claim |
| --- | --- |
| Some older adults with COVID-19 may experience delirium | Describes a possibility in some people |
| Everyone with delirium must be infected with SARS-CoV-2 | Claims certainty for all people |

#### Compare the answer choices

The words **everyone** and **must** in the first choice turn a possibility into a universal rule. That choice contradicts the information that delirium can accompany other acute illnesses, making it the least consistent conclusion.

The other four choices remain consistent: delirium can accompany other acute illnesses, may occur in some older adults with COVID-19, develops acutely with fluctuating signs, and requires assessment for an underlying cause. Delirium alone cannot establish a SARS-CoV-2 infection.
