The question asks for the statement that **contradicts the premises**. A statement that is merely uncertain is still possible, so it is not the answer. OSIS is the student council.

Combine the first two premises. Every debate team member is in OSIS, and no OSIS member is on the futsal team. So **no debate team member is on the futsal team**. The statement "some debate team member is on the futsal team" directly contradicts this, so it cannot be true.

The other statements are possible:

- The premises do not limit the grade of debate members, so a grade XII debate member may exist.
- Every OSIS member could be in grade XII. Nothing forbids it, because the grade XII futsal players are outside OSIS.
- It is also possible that no debate member is in grade XII.
- "Some grade XII student is not in OSIS" is actually certainly true: the grade XII futsal players. A statement that must be true is certainly possible.

So the statement that cannot be true is that some debate team member is on the futsal team.
