Let <InlineMath math="v_P" />, <InlineMath math="v_Q" />, and <InlineMath math="v_R" /> be the cutting rates in **square metres per hour**.

Divide the cut area by working time to find mower <InlineMath math="R" />’s rate:

<BlockMath math="v_R=\frac{200}{4}=50" />

Since <InlineMath math="R" /> works at half <InlineMath math="P" />’s rate and <InlineMath math="P" /> works at three times <InlineMath math="Q" />’s rate,

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} v_P&amp;=2(50)=100 \\ v_Q&amp;=\frac{100}{3} \end{aligned}" />
