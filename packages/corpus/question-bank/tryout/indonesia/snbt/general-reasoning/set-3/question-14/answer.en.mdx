Find every possible arrangement. Start with **the pair K and L**, because they must share a shelf and take two places at once. The shelves are *rak atas* (top), *rak tengah* (middle), and *rak bawah* (bottom).

O already takes one place on the middle shelf, so the middle shelf has only two places left. K and L can be on the top, middle, or bottom shelf.

**K and L on the top shelf**. The top shelf is full. P must be higher than Q, so P is in the middle and Q at the bottom. M and N fill the last middle place and the last bottom place, in two possible ways.

**K and L on the middle shelf**. The middle shelf holds K, L, and O. The top and bottom shelves each have two places for M, N, P, and Q. N cannot be on top, so N is at the bottom. M must be on a different shelf from N, so M is on top. P must be higher than Q, so P is on top and Q at the bottom.

**K and L on the bottom shelf**. The top shelf and the two remaining middle places hold M, N, P, and Q. N cannot be on top, so N is in the middle. M must be on a different shelf from N, so M is on top. Q cannot be higher than P, so P is on top and Q in the middle.

| Arrangement | Top shelf | Middle shelf | Bottom shelf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | K, L | M, O, P | N, Q |
| II | K, L | N, O, P | M, Q |
| III | M, P | K, L, O | N, Q |
| IV | M, P | N, O, Q | K, L |

In all four arrangements, **K is never on the same shelf as M**. This statement must be true.

The other statements do not hold in every arrangement:

- P is on the top shelf in III and IV.
- Q is on the middle shelf in IV.
- N is on the middle shelf in II and IV.
- M is on the middle shelf in I and on the bottom shelf in II.

So the statement that must be true is that K is not on the same shelf as M.
