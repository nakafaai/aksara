The key is to preserve the quantifiers in the passage.

#### Premise Analysis

The passage gives three facts:

1. Every participant chose some drink.
2. Some, but not necessarily all, participants chose tea.
3. Within the tea-drinking group, at least one person did not add sugar.

#### Evaluating Conclusion Options

The first, second, and third choices contradict the second or third premise. The
fifth choice changes "every participant chose a drink" into "every participant
chose tea," which the passage does not say.

The fourth choice restates the third premise without adding anything. Therefore,
**at least one participant drank tea without added sugar** must be true.
