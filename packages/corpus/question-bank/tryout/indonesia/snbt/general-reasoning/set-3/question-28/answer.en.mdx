#### Place exactly one report at an end

Choose one report from <InlineMath math="A" /> and <InlineMath math="B" />, then choose the left or right end. There are <InlineMath math="2\times2=4" /> choices. For each choice, the other seven reports have <InlineMath math="7!" /> arrangements.

The condition is **exactly one**, so we must exclude the <InlineMath math="6!" /> arrangements with the other member of the pair at the opposite end. The remaining six reports freely occupy the six middle positions. Our initial count is

<BlockMath math="4(7!-6!)=4(5040-720)=17\,280." />

#### Exclude adjacent pairs

To count the forbidden arrangements, treat <InlineMath math="E" /> and <InlineMath math="F" /> as **one block**. The block can contain <InlineMath math="EF" /> or <InlineMath math="FE" />, giving a factor of <InlineMath math="2" />.

| Fixed positions | Objects still to arrange | Number of arrangements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One member of the pair at an end | The block and five reports | <InlineMath math="2\times6!=1440" /> |
| Both members at opposite ends | The block and four reports | <InlineMath math="2\times5!=240" /> |

The second row was already excluded in the previous step, so we must not subtract it twice. Across all four report-and-end choices, the number of forbidden arrangements still included is

<BlockMath math="4(1440-240)=4800." />

This leaves <InlineMath math="17\,280-4800=12\,480" /> arrangements satisfying the end-position and non-adjacency conditions.

#### Apply the order condition and calculate the probability

Swapping <InlineMath math="C" /> and <InlineMath math="D" /> preserves the previous two conditions. Every arrangement pairs with exactly one arrangement in which their order is reversed. Thus **half the arrangements** place <InlineMath math="C" /> before <InlineMath math="D" />.

<BlockMath math="N=\frac{12\,480}{2}=6240." />

All <InlineMath math="8!" /> arrangements are equally likely. Therefore,

<BlockMath math="P=\frac{6240}{8!}=\frac{6240}{40\,320}=\frac{13}{84}." />
