Choose the report from A and B that occupies an end, then choose the left or right end. This gives <InlineMath math="2\cdot2=4" /> choices. For each choice, the other seven reports have <InlineMath math="7!" /> arrangements, but the <InlineMath math="6!" /> arrangements that place the other member of A-B at the opposite end are invalid. Before excluding adjacent E-F, the count is therefore <InlineMath math="4(7!-6!)=17{,}280" />.

For each report-and-end choice, treating E-F as one block gives <InlineMath math="2\cdot6!=1{,}440" /> arrangements. Of these, <InlineMath math="2\cdot5!=240" /> also put the other member of A-B at the opposite end. Hence the number to exclude is <InlineMath math="4(1{,}440-240)=4{,}800" />, leaving <InlineMath math="17{,}280-4{,}800=12{,}480" /> arrangements before ordering C and D.

Swapping C and D pairs every arrangement with exactly one other arrangement, so half have C before D. The valid count is <InlineMath math="12{,}480/2=6{,}240" /> out of <InlineMath math="8!=40{,}320" />.

Therefore, the requested value is <InlineMath math="\\frac{13}{84}" />.
