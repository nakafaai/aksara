Both Indonesian sentences say that the speaker passed an examination. The shared words **saya** and **lulus** correspond to the shared codes **it** and **sit**, although their individual mappings remain unknown because order may change.

The only change in meaning is **SBMPTN** to **SIMAK**, matched by the code change **pit** to **nit**. Therefore, **SIMAK** is represented by **nit**.
