The second group initially has total score <InlineMath math="7\times64=448" />. After receiving one member, its total becomes <InlineMath math="8\times63=504" />.

The moving member’s score is therefore

<BlockMath math="504-448=56" />

The first group now has nine members, with mean

<BlockMath math="\frac{10\times74-56}{9}=76" />

The difference between the new means is <InlineMath math="76-63=13" />.
