| Part of the problematic choice | Relationship to the passage |
| --- | --- |
| Some provisions are more lenient than the Corruption Law | Matches the assessment in the passage |
| Corruption in Indonesia will decrease | Contradicts the prediction that corruption will become more widespread |

The choice predicting that corruption will **decrease** starts with supported information, then adds a contradictory effect. Because part of that conclusion contradicts the passage, it is the least consistent choice.

The other four choices mention lighter provisions, weaker deterrence, more widespread corruption, or more difficult recovery of state losses. Each idea appears in the passage. The task is to compare the choices with this historical opinion, rather than determine the current legal situation.
