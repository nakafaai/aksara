# Given

The chart shows **daily passengers from both ports**, while the passage gives the number of sailings and capacity per sailing each day.

# Asked

The day with the **highest overall occupancy percentage** across both directions.

# Solution

> Occupancy divides total passengers by total seats offered that day, so the busiest day is not always the fullest when extra sailings run.

The question asks for the day when the ferry's capacity is used the most. You need two things: the total passengers in both directions from the chart, and the number of departures each day from the text.

Here's the catch: **the daily capacity changes during the week**. From Monday to Friday there are three departures from each port, while on Saturday and Sunday there are four. Each departure carries at most <InlineMath math="200" /> passengers, and there are two ports of departure. So the daily capacity is:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Monday to Friday} &: 2 \times 3 \times 200 = 1{,}200 \\
\text{Saturday and Sunday} &: 2 \times 4 \times 200 = 1{,}600
\end{aligned}" />

Now add the two bars for each day and divide by that day's capacity.

The highest occupancy is on Friday. Check it against the closest rival, Saturday. To match Friday, Saturday would need about <InlineMath math="1{,}413" /> passengers. It only had <InlineMath math="1{,}300" />, so Saturday really does fall short.

Here's why the other options are tempting.

- Saturday has the most passengers, <InlineMath math="1{,}300" />. That's the officer's reason in the text, but Saturday's capacity is also bigger, <InlineMath math="1{,}600" />.
- Sunday has the tallest bar, <InlineMath math="690" /> passengers from Karang Mas. One bar doesn't describe the whole day, and Sunday's combined occupancy is only about <InlineMath math="76.9\%" />.
- Monday comes from looking only at the Karang Mas direction: <InlineMath math="560" /> out of a capacity of <InlineMath math="600" />, about <InlineMath math="93.3\%" />. The Batu Lintang direction was much quieter that day, so the combined occupancy is only about <InlineMath math="76.7\%" />.
- Thursday comes from looking only at the Batu Lintang direction: <InlineMath math="550" /> out of <InlineMath math="600" />, about <InlineMath math="91.7\%" />. Its combined occupancy is only <InlineMath math="77.5\%" />.

# Answer

So the combined occupancy of both directions is highest on Friday, at about <InlineMath math="88.3\%" />.
