This question asks you to sort four statements: which ones are **guaranteed by all four facts**, and which ones only might be true. A statement must be true (*pasti benar*) when no situation can satisfy all four facts and still make it false. Here *sebagian* means "some", that is, at least one.

The method is to chain the facts that talk about the same group of stalls. For a statement that isn't guaranteed (*tidak pasti benar*), one counterexample is enough: a set of stalls that meets all four facts but makes the statement false.

Statement 1 isn't guaranteed. Fact 2 only promises that some solar-lamp stall sells cold drinks. That stall doesn't have to be open tonight, because fact 1 only says every open stall uses a solar lamp, not the other way around. The solar-lamp group can be much bigger than the group of stalls open tonight.

Statement 2 must be true. Suppose a stall owned by the traders' association sold cold drinks. By fact 4, it would be in the north aisle. By fact 3, no cold-drink stall is in the north aisle. Those two clash, so no such stall exists.

Statement 3 must be true. Fact 2 guarantees at least one solar-lamp stall that sells cold drinks. Fact 3 says that stall isn't in the north aisle. So there really is a solar-lamp stall outside the north aisle.

Statement 4 isn't guaranteed. The only stall that's promised to be outside the north aisle is the cold-drink stall from fact 2, and that stall doesn't have to be open tonight.

A counterexample for statements 1 and 4 needs just two stalls, and neither belongs to the association.

| Stall | Open tonight | Solar lamp | Cold drinks | In north aisle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | yes | yes | no | yes |
| Y | no | yes | yes | no |

Check the four facts. The only open stall, X, uses a solar lamp, so fact 1 holds. Stall Y satisfies fact 2. Y isn't in the north aisle, so fact 3 holds. There's no association stall, so fact 4 isn't broken either. In this situation the only open stall is X, and X sells no cold drinks and sits in the north aisle. Statements 1 and 4 are both false here.

So statements 2 and 3 must be true, while statements 1 and 4 aren't guaranteed.
