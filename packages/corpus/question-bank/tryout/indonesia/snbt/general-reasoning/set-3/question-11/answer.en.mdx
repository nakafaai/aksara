Separate what is true of **every** ninth-grade student from what is true of
only **some** of them.

1. Ardian is a student in grade <InlineMath math="9" />. Therefore, the
   universal statement proves that he takes the School Exam.

2. The entrance-test premise concerns only some ninth-grade students. It does
   not identify Ardian as one of them.

Both situations are consistent with the premises:

- Ardian could be among the students who take an entrance test. In that case,
  the complete conclusion is true.
- Ardian could also be outside that group. In that case, only the School Exam
  part is true.

The premise that students continue to SMA/SMK does not by itself prove that
every school requires an entrance test. Adding that assumption would go beyond
the information given.

Therefore, the conclusion is **possibly true**, but not definitely true.
