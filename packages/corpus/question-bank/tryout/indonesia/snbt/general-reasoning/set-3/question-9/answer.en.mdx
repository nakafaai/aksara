The passage links sedimentation and disrupted channels to reduced river capacity. Lower capacity can increase flood risk during the rainy season.

The supported conclusion preserves that relationship: **mining-related sedimentation and disrupted channels can reduce river capacity and increase rainy-season flood risk**. The word *can* does not claim that every rainfall causes a flood or that mining is the only cause of flooding.

The other choices reverse the relationship. The passage does not say that stopping mining damages watersheds, open mining prevents floods, river damage poses no threat to communities, or sediment improves flow.
