Delirium is an acute confusional state that develops over hours or days and tends to fluctuate. It can affect attention, awareness, thinking, or perception and may accompany many acute medical conditions. During the COVID-19 pandemic, research identified delirium as a possible atypical presentation of COVID-19, especially in older and frail adults. Delirium is not specific to COVID-19, so a person with possible delirium needs clinical assessment for its underlying cause.

Sources: [NICE guidance on delirium](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg103) and [the study by Zazzara and colleagues](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10136845/).
