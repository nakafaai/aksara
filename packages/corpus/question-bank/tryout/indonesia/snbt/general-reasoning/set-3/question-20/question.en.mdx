Consider the following hypothetical language:

- **?hmmp**: fly, a fly
- **phipll**: mosquito
- **?hhmmp**: many flies
- **?hmmphng phipllowg cz**: a mosquito sees a fly

Assume that the word formation and word order shown in the example are used consistently. How would this language express "A fly sees many flies"?
