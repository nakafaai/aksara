The argument compares the **total monthly cost**, which combines rent with travel costs:

<BlockMath math="\text{total cost}=\text{rent}+\text{transport}" />

The conclusion assumes that the transport savings from living nearby are large
enough to make the nearby room cheaper overall.

The third statement attacks that assumption directly. If the rent premium near
the office exceeds the extra transport cost from farther away, then

<BlockMath math="\text{farther rent}+\text{extra transport}<\text{nearby rent}" />

In that situation, a more distant room is the cheaper overall choice. The
other statements either support a short commute, support low rent, or merely
describe how workers compare costs. Therefore, the third statement weakens the
argument most strongly.
