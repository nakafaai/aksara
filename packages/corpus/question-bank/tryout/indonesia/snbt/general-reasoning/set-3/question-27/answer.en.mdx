The post-voucher price is the **amount to which tax is applied**. Let that price be <InlineMath math="p" /> in thousand rupiah. After <InlineMath math="11\%" /> tax, the price becomes

<BlockMath math="p+0.11p=1.11p=888" />

The post-voucher, pre-tax price is therefore

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{888}{1.11}=800" />

Stop at the pre-tax price because that is the requested stage. There is no need to add the voucher back or undo the initial markup.
