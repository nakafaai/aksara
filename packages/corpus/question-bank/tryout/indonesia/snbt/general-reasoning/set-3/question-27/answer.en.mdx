# Given

The pharmacy opens every day except Sunday, five pharmacists rotate in fixed order, and the reference shift starts on **15 February 2028**.

# Asked

Which pharmacist is on duty on **8 March 2028**.

# Solution

> The roster moves forward only on open days, so count the working days between the two dates while watching for the leap day and Sundays.

The question asks which pharmacist is on duty on 8 March 2028. The rota only moves on days the pharmacy is open, so you need to count **how many open days** pass after 15 February, not how many calendar days. There are two traps along the way: the year 2028 and Sundays.

Step one: count the days. The year 2028 is divisible by <InlineMath math="4" /> and isn't a century year, so 2028 is a leap year and February has <InlineMath math="29" /> days.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{15 February to 29 February} &: 29 - 15 = 14 \text{ days} \\
\text{29 February to 8 March} &: 8 \text{ days} \\
\text{total} &: 14 + 8 = 22 \text{ days}
\end{aligned}" />

Step two: drop the Sundays. 15 February is a Tuesday, so the Sundays after it are 20 February, 27 February, and 5 March. That's <InlineMath math="3" /> Sundays, so the number of open days after 15 February up to 8 March is:

<BlockMath math="22 - 3 = 19" />

Also make sure 8 March isn't a Sunday. The gap is <InlineMath math="22" /> days (three full weeks plus one day), so 8 March falls one day after a Tuesday, on a Wednesday. The pharmacy is open that day.

Step three: the rota repeats every <InlineMath math="5" /> open days. Divide <InlineMath math="19" /> by <InlineMath math="5" />:

<BlockMath math="19 = 3 \times 5 + 4" />

Three full rounds bring the rota back to Laras, and the remaining <InlineMath math="4" /> steps go from Laras to Made, Nina, Oka, and Putri.

Check it another way. A full week has <InlineMath math="6" /> open days, so each week the rota moves <InlineMath math="6" /> places, which is the same as moving <InlineMath math="1" /> person. Three weeks after 15 February is Tuesday 7 March, and the rota has moved <InlineMath math="3" /> people, to Oka. The next day, Wednesday 8 March, it's Putri's turn.

Each other option comes from one counting slip:

- Laras comes from counting 15 February itself as a step, which gives <InlineMath math="20" /> steps.
- Made comes from forgetting 29 February and also not dropping Sundays, which gives <InlineMath math="21" /> steps.
- Nina comes from counting all <InlineMath math="22" /> calendar days without dropping Sundays.
- Oka comes from dropping Sundays but forgetting that 2028 is a leap year, which gives only <InlineMath math="18" /> open days.

# Answer

So the pharmacist on duty on 8 March 2028 is Putri.
