# Given

A number sequence follows a **fixed pattern** starting with <InlineMath math="2, 4, 5, 15, 17, 34, 37, 111" />.

# Asked

The first term in the sequence that exceeds **<InlineMath math="500" />**.

# Solution

> Check the jump from each term to the next and watch how multiplying and adding take turns in a repeating rhythm.

The question asks for the value of the first term that goes past <InlineMath math="500" />. So you need to find the pattern first, then keep the sequence going until it crosses that limit.

The differences between terms, <InlineMath math="2, 1, 10, 2, 17, 3, 74" />, don't form a pattern. Look at the step from each term to the next instead. The steps **alternate between multiplying and adding**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
2 \times 2 &= 4 \\
4 + 1 &= 5 \\
5 \times 3 &= 15 \\
15 + 2 &= 17 \\
17 \times 2 &= 34 \\
34 + 3 &= 37 \\
37 \times 3 &= 111
\end{aligned}" />

There are two smaller rules inside. The multiplier alternates <InlineMath math="2, 3, 2, 3, \ldots" />, while the number being added goes up by one each time: <InlineMath math="1, 2, 3, \ldots" />. After <InlineMath math="111" />, the next steps are add <InlineMath math="4" />, multiply by <InlineMath math="2" />, add <InlineMath math="5" />, then multiply by <InlineMath math="3" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
111 + 4 &= 115 \\
115 \times 2 &= 230 \\
230 + 5 &= 235 \\
235 \times 3 &= 705
\end{aligned}" />

Check the limit. The term <InlineMath math="235" /> is still below <InlineMath math="500" />, and the next term, <InlineMath math="705" />, is above it. Nothing sits between them, so <InlineMath math="705" /> really is the first term past the limit. It's the 12th term.

Two common slips give similar numbers:

- If you think the added number stays at <InlineMath math="4" />, you get <InlineMath math="115, 230, 234, 702" />, so you'd answer <InlineMath math="702" />.
- If you think the multiplier stays at <InlineMath math="3" /> after <InlineMath math="111" />, you get <InlineMath math="115, 345, 350, 1{,}050" />, so you'd answer <InlineMath math="1{,}050" />.

# Answer

So the first term of the sequence greater than <InlineMath math="500" /> is <InlineMath math="705" />.
