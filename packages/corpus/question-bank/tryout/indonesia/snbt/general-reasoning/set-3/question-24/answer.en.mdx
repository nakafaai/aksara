The combined filling rate, in tanks per hour, is

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{20}+\frac{1}{12}=\frac{2}{15}" />

After five hours, the filled and remaining fractions are

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 5\times\frac{2}{15}&=\frac{2}{3} \\ 1-\frac{2}{3}&=\frac{1}{3} \end{aligned}" />

Once the second pump stops and the leak starts, the net rate is

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{20}-\frac{1}{120}=\frac{1}{24}" />

The additional time required is

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{3}\div\frac{1}{24}=8\,\text{hours}" />

The total elapsed time is therefore <InlineMath math="5+8=13" /> hours.
