The combined filling rate, in tanks per hour, is

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{20}+\frac{1}{12}=\frac3{60}+\frac5{60}=\frac8{60}=\frac{2}{15}" />

After five hours, the filled and remaining fractions are

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} 5\times\frac{2}{15}&amp;=\frac{2}{3} \\ 1-\frac{2}{3}&amp;=\frac{1}{3} \end{aligned}" />

Once the second pump stops and the leak starts, **subtract the leak rate** from the rate of the pump still running. The net rate is

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{20}-\frac{1}{120}=\frac6{120}-\frac1{120}=\frac5{120}=\frac{1}{24}" />

The additional time required is

<BlockMath math="\frac{1}{3}\div\frac{1}{24}=\frac13\times24=8\,\text{hours}" />

The **total elapsed time** is therefore <InlineMath math="5+8=13" /> hours.
