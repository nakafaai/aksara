Test each statement against its own data. One depends on the capacity limit in the text, two depend on choosing the right denominator, and one needs you to add up the whole week.

Statement 1: Salah (false). On Monday, <InlineMath math="560" /> passengers left from Karang Mas. They were split over exactly three departures, and each departure carries at most <InlineMath math="200" /> people. Picture one departure being as empty as possible. The other two carry at most <InlineMath math="2 \times 200 = 400" /> people, so the emptiest departure still carries at least:

<BlockMath math="560 - 400 = 160 \text{ passengers}" />

So no Monday departure from Karang Mas carried fewer than <InlineMath math="160" /> people. If you only look at the average, <InlineMath math="560 \div 3 \approx 186.7" />, and assume one departure could be much emptier, you've forgotten **the capacity limit**.

Statement 2: Benar (true). Passengers from Batu Lintang went from <InlineMath math="530" /> on Friday to <InlineMath math="660" /> on Saturday. An increase is always measured against **the starting value**, which is Friday's.

<BlockMath math="\frac{660 - 530}{530} = \frac{130}{530} \approx 24.5\% > 20\%" />

If you divide by Saturday's value instead, you get <InlineMath math="130 \div 660 \approx 19.7\%" /> and wrongly mark this statement false.

Statement 3: Benar (true). Add up each direction over the seven days.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{Batu Lintang} &: 360+380+420+550+530+660+540 = 3{,}440 \\
\text{Karang Mas} &: 560+340+400+380+530+640+690 = 3{,}540
\end{aligned}" />

Karang Mas is ahead by <InlineMath math="100" /> passengers. The officer in the text got fooled because Batu Lintang does lead on four days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Saturday. Those leads are small, though: <InlineMath math="40 + 20 + 170 + 20 = 250" />. Karang Mas leads only on Monday and Sunday, but by a lot: <InlineMath math="200 + 150 = 350" />. On Friday the two are equal. Leading on more days doesn't mean leading in the total.

Statement 4: Salah (false). Saturday and Sunday together had <InlineMath math="1{,}300 + 1{,}230 = 2{,}530" /> passengers. The denominator has to be the whole week, <InlineMath math="3{,}440 + 3{,}540 = 6{,}980" />.

<BlockMath math="\frac{2{,}530}{6{,}980} \approx 36.2\% < 40\%" />

If you divide by the Monday to Friday passengers only, <InlineMath math="4{,}450" />, you get about <InlineMath math="56.9\%" /> and wrongly mark this statement true.

So the statements about the Friday to Saturday increase and the Karang Mas total are true, and the statements about the Monday departure and the weekend share are false.
