An analyst argues that Indonesia should maintain an education endowment and scholarship program. The analyst gives two reasons:

1. Part of the education budget allocation is not always spent within the same budget year, even as the allocation grows.
2. Only a small number of Indonesia's highest-achieving students can currently study at leading universities abroad.

Which statement most directly weakens the analyst's argument?

Context source: [LPDP, Selayang Pandang](https://lpdp.kemenkeu.go.id/tentang/selayang-pandang/) explains the constitutional education allocation and the establishment of the education endowment. The two numbered statements above are the premises to evaluate in this question.
