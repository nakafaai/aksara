A statement that is certainly false **directly contradicts one of the premises**. A statement that is merely uncertain could still be true.

The third premise says no teacher is on the organizing committee. "Some committee member is a teacher" means someone is both a teacher and a committee member, which contradicts that premise. This statement is certainly false.

The other statements could be true:

- Participants receive certificates, and some certificate holders are teachers. Those teachers might be participants.
- Nothing forbids every teacher from receiving a certificate.
- Nothing forbids certificate holders, such as those who are not teachers, from being on the committee.
- It is also possible that no committee member is a participant. The premises do not link the two.

So the statement that is certainly false is that some committee member is a teacher.
