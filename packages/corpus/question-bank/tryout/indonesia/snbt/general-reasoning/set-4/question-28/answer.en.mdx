# Given

Pump A repeats a **<InlineMath math="15" />-minute cycle with a <InlineMath math="5" />-minute rest**, while **Pump B drains <InlineMath math="15" /> liters per minute nonstop**.

# Asked

Whether each statement about the tank level and fill time is **True** or **False**.

# Solution

> Never check only the end of whole cycles, because the water can hit the top of the tank right before Pump A pauses to rest.

The pattern repeats every <InlineMath math="15" /> minutes, but **the tank can fill in the middle of a cycle**, so don't count only whole cycles.

Net rates in one cycle:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned} \text{A working (10 min)} &: 40 - 15 = 25 \text{ L/min} \Rightarrow +250 \text{ L} \\ \text{A resting (5 min)} &: -15 \text{ L/min} \Rightarrow -75 \text{ L} \\ \text{one cycle} &: +175 \text{ L} \end{aligned}" />

At the end of each cycle the volume is <InlineMath math="300 + 175k" /> liters: <InlineMath math="475" />, <InlineMath math="650" />, <InlineMath math="825" /> and <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> liters at minutes <InlineMath math="15" />, <InlineMath math="30" />, <InlineMath math="45" /> and <InlineMath math="60" />.

The chart below draws the volume at each point where pump A switches. **The line zigzags like a saw and touches the capacity at minute <InlineMath math="68" />**, in the middle of the fifth cycle.

Statement 1 is Benar (true). The highest volume in the fourth cycle comes at minute <InlineMath math="55" />: <InlineMath math="825 + 250 = 1{,}075" /> liters, not full yet. From minute <InlineMath math="60" />, the <InlineMath math="1{,}000" /> liters rise by <InlineMath math="25" /> liters per minute, so the missing <InlineMath math="200" /> liters take <InlineMath math="200 : 25 = 8" /> minutes. The tank is full at minute <InlineMath math="68" />. If you count only whole cycles, <InlineMath math="300 + 175k \ge 1{,}200" /> gives <InlineMath math="k = 6" /> and minute <InlineMath math="90" />, but the tank fills earlier.

Statement 2 is Salah (false). In the first <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes the volume goes from <InlineMath math="300" /> to <InlineMath math="650" /> liters, an increase of <InlineMath math="350" /> liters. The average is <InlineMath math="350 : 30 \approx 11.7" /> liters per minute, less than <InlineMath math="12" />.

Statement 3 is Salah (false). If pump B also stops, one cycle adds <InlineMath math="250" /> liters. At minute <InlineMath math="45" /> the volume is <InlineMath math="1{,}050" /> liters, and the missing <InlineMath math="150" /> liters take <InlineMath math="6" /> minutes. The tank is full at minute <InlineMath math="51" />, not minute <InlineMath math="68" />.

Statement 4 is Benar (true). At minute <InlineMath math="40" /> pump A has just finished working, so the volume is <InlineMath math="650 + 250 = 900" /> liters. During the rest up to minute <InlineMath math="45" />, it drops to <InlineMath math="825" /> liters.

# Answer

So statements 1 and 4 are true, and statements 2 and 3 are false.
