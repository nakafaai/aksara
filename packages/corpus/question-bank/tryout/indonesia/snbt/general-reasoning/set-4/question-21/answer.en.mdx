Separate terms in **odd and even positions**, because the two groups follow different rules.

  <BlockMath math="a_1,a_3,a_5,a_7:\quad7,9,11,13" />

  <BlockMath math="a_2,a_4,a_6:\quad2,8,32" />

<MathContainer>

  <BlockMath math="\frac82=\frac{32}{8}=4" />

The odd-position sequence has common difference <InlineMath math="2" />. The even-position sequence has common ratio <InlineMath math="4" />.

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}a_9&amp;=13+2=15\\a_8&amp;=32\times4=128\end{aligned}" />

  <BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}\frac{a_8}{a_9-a_7}&amp;=\frac{128}{15-13}\\&amp;=\frac{128}{2}=64\end{aligned}" />
