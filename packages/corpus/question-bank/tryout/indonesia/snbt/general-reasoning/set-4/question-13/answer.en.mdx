In the target sentence, the young bird does the watching. Thus, **the young bird is the subject**, while the fish is the object. The hypothetical language places the object first, even though the English sentence begins with the subject.

| Position | Required element | Written form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | Marker | `bo` |
| Second | Object, a fish | `bluwpz` |
| Third | Subject, a young bird, with its prefix | `mw-` + `ckeeck?` becomes `mwckeeck?` |

Join the three elements without changing their spelling: `bo bluwpz mwckeeck?`.

Notice **the prefix on the subject**. The form `mwbluwpz` would mark the fish as the actor, which does not match the requested sentence.
