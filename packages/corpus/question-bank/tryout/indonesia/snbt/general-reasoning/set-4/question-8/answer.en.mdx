The main weakness of the manager's conclusion is the lack of a comparison: sick leave could fall for another reason in the same period. The conclusion is strengthened most by **a comparison group** that went through the same period without plants.

If sick leave at a branch office without plants stayed the same over the same period, seasons or other general causes cannot explain the drop at the office with plants. The plants become the stronger explanation.

The other options do not strengthen the health conclusion:

- Lateness concerns discipline, not health.
- Easy-care plants do not show that plants improve health.
- How the survey was run has nothing to do with sick leave.
- Plants taken home are unrelated to sick leave at the office.

So the statement that strengthens the conclusion most is that sick leave at a branch office without plants did not change in the same period.
