A statement is Benar (true) only if it holds in every valid schedule. Otherwise it is Salah (false). Use the two groups of possible schedules, and for an "if" statement check **only the schedules where the "if" part holds**.

| Group | Friday | Saturday | Sunday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Q and R | P and T | U afternoon, S evening |
| B | T afternoon, S evening | P and U | Q and R |

**Statement 1** is true. U must perform on a later day than T, so U cannot perform on the first day, Friday.

**Statement 2** is false. In group A, T performs on Saturday with P and can take the evening session.

**Statement 3** is false. In group B, S performs on Friday.

**Statement 4** is true. Q performs on Friday only in group A, and in that group U always performs on Sunday.

So statements 1 and 4 are true, and statements 2 and 3 are false.
