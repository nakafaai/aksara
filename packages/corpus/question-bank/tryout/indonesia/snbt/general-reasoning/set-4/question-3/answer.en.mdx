The first premise applies to **every** dish. The second premise guarantees that at least one dish belongs to the group without raw vegetables.

Take any dish guaranteed by the second premise. It contains no raw vegetables. Because it is still a dish on the menu, the first premise also tells us that it tastes sour and spicy.

#### Check the Scope of the Other Options

- A dish that is not both sour and spicy would contradict the first premise.
- A dish that is neither sour nor spicy would also contradict the first premise.
- "Every dish contains raw vegetables" contradicts the second premise.
- "No dish contains raw vegetables" is stronger than the premises. The premises establish this for at least one dish. They give no information about all dishes.

Therefore, **at least one dish contains no raw vegetables and tastes sour and spicy**.
