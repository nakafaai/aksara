Dito is a student in this system, so **both rules apply to him**. We can work backward from the missing consequence, one step at a time.

| Step | Rule and fact | Conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check the practice test | If Dito takes the test, he receives a report. In fact, he received no report | Dito did not take the test |
| Check the prerequisite exercises | If Dito completes the exercises, he takes the test. The first step shows he did not take the test | Dito did not complete the exercises |

This reasoning is **modus tollens**: when a condition guarantees a consequence, the absence of that consequence rules out the condition. Let <InlineMath math="p" /> mean completing the exercises, <InlineMath math="q" /> taking the test, and <InlineMath math="r" /> receiving the report. The two steps are

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="q\rightarrow r,\quad\neg r\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\neg q" />
  <BlockMath math="p\rightarrow q,\quad\neg q\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\neg p" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, Dito **did not complete the prerequisite exercises**. His status as a student remains true. Not completing an activity does not mean he is not a student.
