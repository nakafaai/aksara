Mark each conclusion Benar (certainly true) or Salah (not certainly true). The key is the phrase **jika dan hanya jika** (if and only if). Rule 1 works in both directions: paying with a member card means earning bonus points, and earning bonus points means paying with a member card.

<BlockMath math="\text{bonus} \Leftrightarrow \text{member card},\qquad \text{member card} \Rightarrow \text{registered}" />

**Conclusion 1** is true. Rara earned bonus points, and by the direction "bonus means member card", Rara paid with a member card.

**Conclusion 2** is true. Rara paid with a member card, so by rule 2 Rara is registered.

**Conclusion 3** is true. The contrapositive of rule 2 is "not registered means not paying with a member card". Dimas is not registered, so Dimas did not pay with a member card, and by rule 1 Dimas earns no bonus points.

**Conclusion 4** is false. Rule 2 only says member-card payers are registered. A registered customer need not pay with a member card, so need not earn a bonus.

**Conclusion 5** is false. Without a member card, rule 1 rules out bonus points.

So conclusions 1, 2, and 3 are true, and conclusions 4 and 5 are false.
