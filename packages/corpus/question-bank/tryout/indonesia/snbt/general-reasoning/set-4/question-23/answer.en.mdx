We want **users of A only**, so we must remove A users who also use B or C. First separate the overlapping groups:

| Group of A users | Number of people |
| --- | --- |
| A and B, without C | <InlineMath math="35-15=20" /> |
| A and C, without B | <InlineMath math="28-15=13" /> |
| A, B, and C | <InlineMath math="15" /> |

These three rows do not overlap. Thus, <InlineMath math="20+13+15=48" /> users of A also use another service. The remaining number is

<BlockMath math="90-48=42." />

The same reasoning can be written using inclusion and exclusion:

<BlockMath math="90-35-28+15=55-28+15=27+15=42." />

Why add <InlineMath math="15" />? Users of all three services were **subtracted twice** through the two intersections, although they should be removed only once. Adding them back once corrects that double subtraction.
