Let <InlineMath math="p" /> be the **pre-tax price** in thousand rupiah. Adding <InlineMath math="8\%" /> tax makes the final price <InlineMath math="1.08p" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p+0.08p&amp;=1.08p\\1.08p&amp;=594\\p&amp;=\frac{594}{1.08}=550\end{aligned}" />

The tax paid, in thousand rupiah, is:

<BlockMath math="594-550=44" />

Tax is calculated from the pre-tax price, so the check is <InlineMath math="0.08\times550=44" />, not <InlineMath math="0.08\times594" />.
