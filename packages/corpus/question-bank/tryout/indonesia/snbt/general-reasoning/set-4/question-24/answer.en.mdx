The pumps work together for <InlineMath math="6" /> hours. The filled and remaining fractions are:

<BlockMath math="6\left(\frac1{18}+\frac1{24}\right)=\frac7{12}" />

<BlockMath math="1-\frac7{12}=\frac5{12}" />

Pump <InlineMath math="A" /> fills <InlineMath math="\frac1{18}" /> of the tank per hour. Its required operating time is:

<BlockMath math="\frac5{12}\div\frac1{18}=\frac{15}{2}" />

Add the <InlineMath math="30" />-minute pause, which is <InlineMath math="\frac12" /> hour. The total additional time, in hours, is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{15}{2}+\frac12=8" />
