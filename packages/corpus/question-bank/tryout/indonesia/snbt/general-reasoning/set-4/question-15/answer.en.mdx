If T performs on Friday, Q and R cannot use Friday, because together they need two sessions. So **Q and R perform on Sunday**. U must perform after Friday, and Sunday is full, so U performs on Saturday with P.

S is left and must fill the other Friday session. S has to perform in the evening, so **T performs in the afternoon**.

| Friday | Saturday | Sunday |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T afternoon, S evening | P and U, either session | Q and R, either session |

The other options are not certain:

- P can perform in the afternoon or evening, since the order of P and U on Saturday is open.
- R can also perform in either Sunday session.
- U can perform in either Saturday session.
- Q performs on Sunday, not Friday.

So if T performs on Friday, T must perform in the afternoon session.
