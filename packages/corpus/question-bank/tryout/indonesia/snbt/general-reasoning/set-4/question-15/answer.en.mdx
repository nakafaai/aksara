The question asks for a statement that's true in **every valid schedule**, not just in some of them. To prove a statement must be true, show that its opposite is impossible. To reject any other statement, one valid schedule that makes it false is enough.

Test the statement about the kuliner session. Suppose the opposite happens, so kuliner is held in a morning session.

- Condition 6 needs the batik day to come before the kuliner day, so kuliner can't be on Monday. Kuliner is on Tuesday morning or Wednesday morning.
- Kuliner is in a morning session, so condition 5 forces wayang onto Monday.
- Dongeng must be in a morning session (condition 4) and on a day after the wayang day (condition 3). So dongeng takes the other morning out of Tuesday morning and Wednesday morning.
- Now Tuesday morning and Wednesday morning hold kuliner and dongeng, and one Monday session holds wayang. The empty sessions are one Monday session, Tuesday afternoon and Wednesday afternoon.
- Batik and gamelan need two empty sessions in a row (condition 1). The session after Monday afternoon is Tuesday morning, the session after Tuesday afternoon is Wednesday morning, and both are taken. Wednesday afternoon is the last session, so nothing comes after it. Monday morning and Monday afternoon can't be used as a pair either, because wayang has one of them.

Batik and gamelan have no room, so the assumption is impossible. That means kuliner is always held in an afternoon session.
