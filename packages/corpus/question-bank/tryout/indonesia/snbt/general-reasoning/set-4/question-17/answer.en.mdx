#### Find vehicle A's speed

Speed is distance divided by time:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="v_A=\frac{20\text{ km}}{2\text{ hours}}=10\text{ km/hour}" />
</MathContainer>

#### Scale the speed and calculate the distance

Vehicle B travels at one-third of that speed:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="v_B=\frac13v_A=\frac{10}{3}\text{ km/hour}" />
  <BlockMath math="s_B=v_Bt=\frac{10}{3}\times3=10\text{ km}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, vehicle B travels **<InlineMath math="10\text{ km}" />**.
