#### Find vehicle <InlineMath math="A" />'s speed

Speed is distance divided by time:

<BlockMath math="v_A=\frac{20\text{ km}}{2\text{ h}}=10\text{ km/h}" />

#### Scale the speed and calculate the distance

Vehicle <InlineMath math="B" /> travels at one-third of that speed:

<MathContainer>
  <BlockMath math="v_B=\frac13v_A=\frac{10}{3}\text{ km/h}" />
  <BlockMath math="s_B=v_Bt=\frac{10}{3}\times3=10\text{ km}" />
</MathContainer>

Therefore, vehicle <InlineMath math="B" /> travels **<InlineMath math="10\text{ km}" />**.
