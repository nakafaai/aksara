The key comparison concerns **native range**, rather than every place where the species has been planted. The passage names native regions outside New Guinea, such as Sri Lanka, Java, and Sumatra. Even one such example refutes a claim that the species is native only to Papua.

| Aspect | Fact in the passage | Contradictory claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native range | Includes regions outside New Guinea | Native only to Papua or introduced everywhere else |
| Biome | Wet tropical | Dry temperate |
| Growth form | A tree in Sapindaceae | An herb rather than a tree |

Therefore, the native range of *Pometia pinnata* **extends beyond New Guinea**. This follows from the named regions without adding assumptions about the plant's distribution.
