Combine the first two premises. Every dictionary is a reference book, and **no reference book may be taken home**. So no dictionary may be taken home.

<BlockMath math="\text{dictionary} \Rightarrow \text{reference} \Rightarrow \text{cannot be taken home}" />

The other options are not certain:

- The third premise says only **some** history books may be taken home, not all.
- The premises do not link dictionaries with history books.
- The first premise cannot be reversed. A book that cannot be taken home may be some other reference book, not a dictionary.
- The history books that can be taken home are indeed not reference books, but other history books may be. So "none" is too strong.

So the conclusion that must be true is that no dictionary in that library may be taken home.
