#### Match each claim to the source

- *Pometia pinnata* is a tree species in Sapindaceae.
- It grows primarily in a wet tropical biome.
- Its native range includes many regions from Sri Lanka to the South Pacific.

New Guinea is only one part of that broad native range. Therefore, the supported statement is that **the species' native range extends well beyond New Guinea**.
