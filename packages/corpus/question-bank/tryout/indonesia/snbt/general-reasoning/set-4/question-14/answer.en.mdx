Let <InlineMath math="a" /> be the starting price and <InlineMath math="b" /> the ending price, both in rupiah. Let <InlineMath math="p" /> be the percentage decrease.

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{a-b}{a}\times100\%" />

The starting price is the denominator because the decrease is measured relative to the price before the change. The following results are rounded to one decimal place.

#### Noodle A, <InlineMath math="2017" /> to <InlineMath math="2018" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{3500-2500}{3500}\times100\%\\ &\approx28.6\%\end{aligned}" />

#### Noodle A, <InlineMath math="2019" /> to <InlineMath math="2020" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{3350-3000}{3350}\times100\%\\ &\approx10.4\%\end{aligned}" />

#### Noodle B, <InlineMath math="2017" /> to <InlineMath math="2018" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{4500-4250}{4500}\times100\%\\ &\approx5.6\%\end{aligned}" />

#### Noodle B, <InlineMath math="2018" /> to <InlineMath math="2019" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{4250-4000}{4250}\times100\%\\ &\approx5.9\%\end{aligned}" />

#### Noodle C, <InlineMath math="2019" /> to <InlineMath math="2020" />

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{2800-2650}{2800}\times100\%\\ &\approx5.4\%\end{aligned}" />
