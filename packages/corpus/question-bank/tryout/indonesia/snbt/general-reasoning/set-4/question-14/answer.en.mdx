Let <InlineMath math="a" /> be the starting price and <InlineMath math="b" /> the ending price, both in rupiah. Let <InlineMath math="p" /> be the percentage decrease.

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{a-b}{a}\times100\%" />

The **starting price** is the denominator because the decrease is measured relative to the price before the change. The following results are rounded to one decimal place.

| Product and period | Price decrease | Percentage decrease |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Noodle A, 2017 to 2018 | <InlineMath math="3500-2500=1000" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{1000}{3500}\times100\%\approx28.6\%" /> |
| Noodle A, 2019 to 2020 | <InlineMath math="3350-3000=350" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{350}{3350}\times100\%\approx10.4\%" /> |
| Noodle B, 2017 to 2018 | <InlineMath math="4500-4250=250" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{250}{4500}\times100\%\approx5.6\%" /> |
| Noodle B, 2018 to 2019 | <InlineMath math="4250-4000=250" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{250}{4250}\times100\%\approx5.9\%" /> |
| Noodle C, 2019 to 2020 | <InlineMath math="2800-2650=150" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{150}{2800}\times100\%\approx5.4\%" /> |

The largest decrease is approximately <InlineMath math="28.6\%" /> for Noodle A, <InlineMath math="2017" /> to <InlineMath math="2018" />.
