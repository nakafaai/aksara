#### Identify what the passage actually claims

The passage joins three ideas:

- school snacks are part of children's food intake;
- they can contribute nutrients;
- both safety and nutritional balance matter when choosing them.

That directly supports the conclusion that **safe, nutritionally balanced school snacks can contribute to children's nutrient intake**.

#### Reject the absolute alternatives

- It does not say that every school snack is unsafe.
- It does not tell children to replace main meals with snacks.
- It treats food safety as essential, so sufficient energy cannot excuse microbiological or chemical hazards.
- It considers nutritional balance, including more than energy alone.

Only the fifth option preserves all parts of the source without adding an unsupported absolute claim.
