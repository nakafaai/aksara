The passage gives **two considerations that must be assessed together**: food safety and nutritional balance. A snack's benefits do not remove either requirement.

| Claim in a choice | Relationship to the passage |
| --- | --- |
| Safe, nutritionally balanced snacks can contribute nutrients | Preserves both considerations and the stated benefit |
| Every school snack is unsafe | Turns the need for careful selection into a blanket rejection |
| Snacks should replace main meals | Adds a recommendation the passage does not make |
| Enough energy permits ignoring microbiological and chemical hazards | Removes the safety requirement |
| Energy is the only nutritional consideration | Removes nutritional balance |

The first conclusion is therefore best supported. The phrase **can contribute** describes a snack's contribution without promising that one snack meets all of a child's nutritional needs.
