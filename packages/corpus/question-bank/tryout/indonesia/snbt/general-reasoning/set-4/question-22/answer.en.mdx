Count the edge dots of a <InlineMath math="k\times k" /> arrangement carefully, because **each corner dot lies on two sides at once**.

Each side has <InlineMath math="k" /> dots. Adding the four sides directly counts every corner twice. There are <InlineMath math="4" /> corners, so

<BlockMath math="\text{edge dots}=4k-4=4(k-1)" />

Check with the table: <InlineMath math="4(2-1)=4" />, <InlineMath math="4(3-1)=8" />, and <InlineMath math="4(4-1)=12" />. For a <InlineMath math="15\times15" /> arrangement:

<BlockMath math="4(15-1)=56" />

The other options come from these mistakes:

- <InlineMath math="60" /> is <InlineMath math="4\times15" />, counting the corners twice.
- <InlineMath math="64" /> is <InlineMath math="4\times16" />, as if each side had <InlineMath math="16" /> dots.
- <InlineMath math="169" /> is <InlineMath math="13^2" />, the dots **inside** the square, not on the edge.
- <InlineMath math="225" /> is <InlineMath math="15^2" />, all the dots.

So there are <InlineMath math="56" /> dots on the edge.
