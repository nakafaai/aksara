After the first addition of water, the mixture has volume <InlineMath math="60+5=65\text{ L}" />. At <InlineMath math="30\%" /> concentration, the solute volume is:

<BlockMath math="0.3\times65=19.5\text{ L}" />

The second addition of water does not change the solute volume. The total volume becomes <InlineMath math="65+13=78\text{ L}" />. The new concentration is:

<BlockMath math="\frac{19.5}{78}\times100\%=25\%" />
