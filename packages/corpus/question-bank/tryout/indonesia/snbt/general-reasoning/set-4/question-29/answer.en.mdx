There are <InlineMath math="\binom{10}{2}=45" /> equally likely pairs.

| Outcome | Pair count | Payout (thousand rupiah) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Two red | <InlineMath math="\binom32=3" /> | <InlineMath math="30" /> |
| Different colours | <InlineMath math="3\times7=21" /> | <InlineMath math="15" /> |
| Two blue | <InlineMath math="\binom72=21" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

The expected payout for one attempt, in thousand rupiah, is:

<BlockMath math="E=\frac{30(3)+15(21)}{45}=9" />

The retry occurs with probability <InlineMath math="\frac{21}{45}=\frac7{15}" />. Restoring the box gives the retry the same expected payout of <InlineMath math="9" />. The total is:

<BlockMath math="9+\frac7{15}\times9=\frac{66}{5}" />
