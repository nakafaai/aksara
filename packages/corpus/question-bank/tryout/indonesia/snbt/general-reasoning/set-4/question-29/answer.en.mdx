# Given

Six people line up in a row under two rules: **Ayu and Banu must stand side by side** and **Candra cannot stand at either end**.

# Asked

The **number of valid lineup arrangements**.

# Solution

> Tie Ayu and Banu together as one block to count every adjacent lineup first, then subtract the cases where Candra lands on an end.

There are two conditions, so use two steps: first count the lineups that keep Ayu and Banu together, then **remove the lineups with Candra at an end**.

Step 1. Treat Ayu and Banu as one block. Now there are <InlineMath math="5" /> units: the AB block, Candra, Dewi, Eko and Fajar. Inside the block, the order can be AB or BA.

<BlockMath math="5! \times 2 = 120 \times 2 = 240" />

Step 2. Count the step 1 lineups with Candra at the left end. The other <InlineMath math="5" /> places hold the AB block, Dewi, Eko and Fajar, which is <InlineMath math="4" /> units.

<BlockMath math="4! \times 2 = 24 \times 2 = 48" />

The right end gives <InlineMath math="48" /> lineups too, so <InlineMath math="96" /> lineups have Candra at an end.

Step 3. Subtract.

<BlockMath math="240 - 96 = 144" />

Check another way: Candra may stand in position <InlineMath math="2" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="4" /> or <InlineMath math="5" />. Counting all those cases one by one also gives <InlineMath math="144" />.
