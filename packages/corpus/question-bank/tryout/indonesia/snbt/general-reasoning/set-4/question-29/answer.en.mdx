There are <InlineMath math="\binom{10}{2}=\frac{10\times9}{2}=45" /> **equally likely pairs**.

| Outcome | Pair count | Payout (thousand rupiah) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Two red | <InlineMath math="\binom32=\frac{3\times2}{2}=3" /> | <InlineMath math="30" /> |
| Different colours | <InlineMath math="3\times7=21" /> | <InlineMath math="15" /> |
| Two blue | <InlineMath math="\binom72=\frac{7\times6}{2}=21" /> | <InlineMath math="0" /> |

The expected payout for one attempt, in thousand rupiah, is:

<BlockMath math="E=\frac{30(3)+15(21)+0(21)}{45}=\frac{90+315}{45}=\frac{405}{45}=9" />

A retry occurs only after **two blue balls**, with probability <InlineMath math="\frac{21}{45}=\frac7{15}" />. Restoring the box gives the retry the same expected payout of <InlineMath math="9" />. The total is:

<BlockMath math="9+\frac7{15}\times9=9+\frac{21}{5}=\frac{45+21}{5}=\frac{66}{5}" />

Weight the extra expectation by the probability of receiving a retry. Do not simply double the one-attempt expectation, because not every player gets a second attempt. The second attempt cannot trigger a third.
