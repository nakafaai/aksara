**Mengubah tolok ukur dari sekadar kenaikan rata-rata menjadi kemampuan beragam kelompok memakai informasi secara mandiri.**

Under this definition, the team cannot ask only whether the overall figure rose. It must also ask who can find, understand, and use the directions without assistance. That is why results are separated by group and missing users remain an explicit limitation. The definition guides the evaluation of evidence rather than replacing that evidence.
