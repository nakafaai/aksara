**Katalog baru dan label lama dapat hidup berdampingan karena pembaruan sistem tidak harus menghapus jejak penggunaan sebelumnya.**

The thin pocket makes this possible. The 2026 card serves the current catalogue, while the 1998 label and alteration notes remain readable. Raka encounters both on the same coat. The story neither rejects renewal nor freezes the costume in its old condition. It places old and new records in one sequence that can still continue.
