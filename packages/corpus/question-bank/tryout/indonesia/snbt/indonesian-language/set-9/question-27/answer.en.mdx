**Wulan memperbaiki bagian yang mengganggu pemakaian, tetapi mempertahankan jahitan yang membawa riwayat, sehingga perawatan tidak disamakan dengan menghapus masa lalu.**

The loose end bothers Raka, so Wulan does not leave it untouched. She trims the irritating part and keeps the coat wearable. She does not remove the whole seam or its label. This very small choice holds the story's balance: an old object can be maintained and adapted without losing every trace of the people who used it before.
