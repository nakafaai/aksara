**Keduanya membuat keterbatasan pengamatan terlihat, sehingga ketidaklengkapan menjadi bagian jujur dari esai, bukan celah yang disamarkan.**

The blurred photograph shows that Laras's evidence is less polished than the posts she saw. The empty frame marks a place she never reached. She does not ask readers to treat either element as complete evidence. Instead, both expose the limits of what she gathered. That is how they support the story's concern with honest documentation.
