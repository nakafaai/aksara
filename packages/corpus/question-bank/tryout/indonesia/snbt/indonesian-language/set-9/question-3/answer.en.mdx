**Mengacak urutan pemasangan panel, mengulang nada pada beberapa posisi mikrofon dan beberapa ruang, lalu membandingkan tingkat bunyi serta waktu dengung pada setiap kondisi.**

This design addresses two central weaknesses in the first test. Randomising the order reduces the chance that measurement order drives the result. Testing several positions and rooms checks whether the pattern belongs to one location only. Keeping both sound level and reverberation time also makes the new evidence directly comparable with the original findings.
