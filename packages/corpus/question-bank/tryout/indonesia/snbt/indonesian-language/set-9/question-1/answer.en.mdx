**Pengujian awal menunjukkan panel gabus berkaitan dengan penurunan tingkat bunyi dan waktu dengung pada kondisi tertentu, tetapi bukti itu belum mewakili seluruh ruang atau frekuensi.**

Follow the report's full line of reasoning. Sound levels fall with the panels and return close to their starting values once the panels are removed. Reverberation time changes in the same direction. Yet the study covers one room, three frequencies, and only one microphone position for the reverberation reading. A sound summary must keep both the finding and those limits.
