**Pembaca dekat dengan kecemasan dan pertimbangan Laras, tetapi tidak boleh menganggap tafsir Laras atas pertanyaan penyunting sebagai fakta tentang niat penyunting.**

Internal focalisation opens Laras's thoughts to the reader. We understand why the clock, battery, and her friends' posts feel pressing to her. The narration never enters the editor's mind, however. We hear the editor's question only through Laras's experience. Her feelings are therefore close and knowable, while the editor's motive remains open.
