**Definisi itu membedakan ukuran lamanya bunyi bertahan dari tingkat bunyi pada tabel, sehingga pembaca memahami mengapa keduanya menjadi bukti yang saling melengkapi.**

The decibel figures describe sound levels while each tone is playing. Reverberation time measures something different: how long the sound takes to fade after its source stops. The definition keeps those measures from being confused. Because they capture different properties, they can complement one another, even though this study still measures them under limited conditions.
