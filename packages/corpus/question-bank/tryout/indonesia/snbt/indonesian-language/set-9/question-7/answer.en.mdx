Litter transfers carbon **from plants to soil**. Its direction stays the same, but its classification changes with the system's components.

| System counted | Plants | Soil | Litter transfer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plants only | Inside | Outside | Six units leaving the system |
| Plants and soil | Inside | Inside | Six units transferred internally |

For the combined system, those six units do not leave the total when they enter the soil. This does not let us ignore all soil flows. Decomposition to the atmosphere and movement to water still leave the combined system.
