**Serasah merupakan arus keluar jika hanya tumbuhan yang dihitung, tetapi menjadi perpindahan internal jika tumbuhan dan tanah ditempatkan dalam satu batas sistem.**

Picture a boundary drawn around the plants alone. Six litter units cross that boundary, so they leave the plant stock. Expand the boundary to include the soil, and the same flow now moves within the system rather than reducing its combined total. The number has not changed. Its role in the calculation changes because the question and boundary have changed.
