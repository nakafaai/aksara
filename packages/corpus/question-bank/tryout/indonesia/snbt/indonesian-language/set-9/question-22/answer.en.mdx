**Tindakan itu mengubah keterbatasan menjadi dokumentasi yang memiliki waktu dan tempat, sehingga Laras tidak perlu memakai bukti yang bukan pengalamannya.**

A sound recording alone is not automatically accountable. Laras also records where and when each clip was made, so readers can locate it within her actual experience. The choice gives her genuine material despite the closed path. She can now construct the essay without pretending to have reached a place she never visited.
