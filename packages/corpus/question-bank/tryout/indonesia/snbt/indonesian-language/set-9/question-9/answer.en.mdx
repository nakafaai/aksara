The system boundary determines **incoming, outgoing, and internal flows**. Including every listed flow gives these two balances.

| System | Incoming | Outgoing | Internal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plants | Photosynthesis 18 | Respiration 7 and litter 6 | No internal flow is shown |
| Plants and soil | Photosynthesis 18 | Respiration 7, decomposition 4, flow to water 3 | Litter 6 |

For plants alone:

<BlockMath math="\Delta C=18-(7+6)=18-13=+5" />

For plants and soil together:

<BlockMath math="\Delta C=18-(7+4+3)=18-14=+4" />

Both results give additional carbon units over one hypothetical year. The value 11 omits litter for plants, or decomposition and flow to water for the combined system. Correct arithmetic still describes a simulation, not measurements of real mangroves.
