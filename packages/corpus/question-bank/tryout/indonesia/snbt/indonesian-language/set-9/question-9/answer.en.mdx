**Menjelaskan mengapa arus yang sama dapat dihitung sebagai perpindahan keluar atau sebagai perpindahan internal, bergantung pada komponen yang dimasukkan ke dalam perhitungan.**

The definition shows why every flow crossing a boundary must be checked. The plant value of <InlineMath math="11" /> omits six units of outgoing litter, so the complete plant balance is five units. If soil is included, litter becomes an internal transfer, but decomposition and flow to water become outgoing flows of the combined system. That combined balance is four units, not eleven. The system boundary therefore determines which flows enter the calculation.
