**Memberi kriteria untuk menilai kesinambungan identitas mantel dan menjelaskan mengapa celah riwayat membatasi hubungan antara M-17 dan foto 2019.**

Provenance asks us to trace the object's history and custody rather than match its appearance alone. The maintenance ledger provides part of that chain, but it ends before 2019 and even contains missing pages. The definition explains why the photographic resemblance matters while remaining insufficient to close the gap in identity.
