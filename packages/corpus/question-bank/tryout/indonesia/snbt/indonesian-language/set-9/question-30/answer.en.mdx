**Benang Putih di Balik Mantel**

The white thread connects the conflict, Wulan's decision, and Raka's experience. Its place “behind the coat” also fits the labels and cards hidden inside yet carrying an important history. The other titles make the story too absolute, as if every change were forbidden, the entire history were complete, or moving buildings erased earlier traces.
