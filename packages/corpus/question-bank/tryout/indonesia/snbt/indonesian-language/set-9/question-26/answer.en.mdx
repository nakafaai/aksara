**Benang putih bergerak dari tanda benda yang hendak disingkirkan menjadi tanda hubungan yang dirawat tanpa menyembunyikan kerapuhannya.**

At first, the loose thread makes Wulan see the label as worn material that obstructs the new catalogue. After finding records from several periods, she preserves the seam while trimming its loose end. The thread remains fragile, so the old object is not made perfect. What changes is Wulan's way of caring for the link between earlier traces and present needs.
