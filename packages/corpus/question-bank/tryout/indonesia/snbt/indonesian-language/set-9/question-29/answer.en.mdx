The motif gains meaning through **repetition in changing contexts**.

| Moment | Action or response | Supported meaning of the thread |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginning | Wulan plans to remove the worn-looking label | Damage to be discarded |
| After reading old records | She preserves labels and adds card pockets | A trace worth maintaining alongside a new system |
| Rehearsal in the new building | Raka reads Mira's name, Wulan trims the loose end without removing the seam | Connection to the past survives a practical repair |

The definition directs you to compare this sequence. Whiteness alone or the number of mentions cannot establish the same meaning in every story.
