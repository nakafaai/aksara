**Mengarahkan pembaca menelusuri bagaimana pengulangan benang memperoleh arti berbeda melalui keputusan Wulan dan respons Raka.**

The definition reminds us that repetition alone does not create meaning. The white thread becomes a motif when its contexts are compared. It first prompts a plan to remove the label, then enters Wulan's decision to preserve it, and finally bothers Raka, prompting him to read the nearby label. That changing sequence requires interpretation, not the thread's colour in isolation.
