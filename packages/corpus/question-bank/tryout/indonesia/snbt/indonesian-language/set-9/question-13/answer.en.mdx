**Melibatkan kelompok pengguna yang belum terwakili, mengacak kondisi pada blok yang sebanding, lalu melaporkan hasil dan pengalaman setiap kelompok secara terpisah.**

This step broadens participation while retaining the random assignment of conditions already used in the initial trial. Previously absent groups can evaluate the sign through actual use, while comparable blocks preserve a meaningful comparison. Reporting each group separately also prevents a combined percentage from hiding or overstating the experience of a particular group.
