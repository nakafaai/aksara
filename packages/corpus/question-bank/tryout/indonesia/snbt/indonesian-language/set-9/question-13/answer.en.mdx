**Melibatkan kelompok pengguna yang belum terwakili, mengacak kondisi pada blok yang sebanding, lalu melaporkan hasil dan pengalaman setiap kelompok secara terpisah.**

This step addresses two weaknesses in the first trial. Previously absent groups can evaluate the sign through actual use, while randomised, comparable blocks preserve a meaningful comparison. Reporting each group separately also prevents a combined percentage from hiding or overstating the experience of a particular group.
