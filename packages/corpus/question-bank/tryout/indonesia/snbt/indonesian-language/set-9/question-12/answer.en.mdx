**Kenaikan gabungan terutama mencerminkan perbaikan pada kelompok berpenglihatan rendah; kelompok berpenglihatan biasa bertahan di sekitar 84 persen.**

Do not stop at the total row. Among visitors with usual vision, 42 out of 50 is 84 percent and 38 out of 45 is about 84.4 percent, so the change is tiny. The clear jump occurs in the low-vision group, from 20 percent to 70 percent. The combined increase therefore cannot be assigned equally to every group.
