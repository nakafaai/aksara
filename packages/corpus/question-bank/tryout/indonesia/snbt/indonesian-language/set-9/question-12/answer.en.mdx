Calculate **each group's proportion** before interpreting the combined result. Success counts alone are insufficient when group sizes differ.

| Group | Old sign | New sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Usual vision | <InlineMath math="\frac{42}{50}\times100\%=84\%" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{38}{45}\times100\%\approx84.4\%" /> |
| Low vision | <InlineMath math="\frac{4}{20}\times100\%=20\%" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{14}{20}\times100\%=70\%" /> |
| Combined | <InlineMath math="\frac{46}{70}\times100\%\approx65.7\%" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{52}{65}\times100\%=80\%" /> |

The low-vision group's rise is <InlineMath math="70-20=50" /> percentage points. The usual-vision group's rise is only about 0.4 points. Thus the combined increase of about 14.3 points does not describe the same change in each group. Group outcomes do not establish identical benefits for each individual either.
