**Laras beralih dari mengejar kesan lengkap menuju penyajian pengalaman yang dapat dipertanggungjawabkan, termasuk bagian yang tidak berhasil ia capai.**

At first, Laras judges her work by how complete it looks and how it compares with her friends' posts. That is why she deletes the blurred photograph and nearly borrows an archive image. By the end, she restores the photograph and marks the place she never reached. Her change is not from failure to perfection, but from concealing a limit to acknowledging it.
