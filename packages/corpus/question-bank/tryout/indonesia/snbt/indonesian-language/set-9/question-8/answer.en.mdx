**mengukur laju perpindahan dan lama penyimpanan karbon pada beberapa bagian ekosistem.**

A strong follow-up preserves the relation represented by the model while measuring it under real conditions with relevant comparisons controlled. More model units improve only internal precision; one matching case or many simultaneous changes do not test external applicability.
