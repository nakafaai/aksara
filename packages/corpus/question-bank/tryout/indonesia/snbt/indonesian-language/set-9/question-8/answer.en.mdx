**Cadangan bertambah 5 unit karena 18 unit masuk, sedangkan 7 unit respirasi dan 6 unit serasah keluar.**

Start with the plant-stock boundary. Photosynthesis brings 18 units in. Respiration returns 7 units to the atmosphere, and litter moves 6 units into the soil. The net change is therefore <InlineMath math="18 - 7 - 6 = 5" /> units. Flows from the soil to the atmosphere or water do not enter this calculation because they never cross the plant-stock boundary.
