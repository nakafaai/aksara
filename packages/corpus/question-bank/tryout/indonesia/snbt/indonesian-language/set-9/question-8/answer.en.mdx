**Cadangan bertambah <InlineMath math="5" /> unit karena <InlineMath math="18" /> unit masuk, sedangkan <InlineMath math="7" /> unit respirasi dan <InlineMath math="6" /> unit serasah keluar.**

Start with the plant-stock boundary. Photosynthesis brings <InlineMath math="18" /> units in. Respiration returns <InlineMath math="7" /> units to the atmosphere, and litter moves <InlineMath math="6" /> units into the soil. The net change is therefore <InlineMath math="18 - 7 - 6 = 5" /> units. Flows from the soil to the atmosphere or water do not enter this calculation because they never cross the plant-stock boundary.
