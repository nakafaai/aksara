**Simulasi menunjukkan bahwa hasil neraca karbon bergantung pada batas sistem dan kelengkapan aliran, tetapi angkanya belum dapat diperlakukan sebagai data mangrove nyata.**

The passage works on two levels. The model helps students see why the litter flow is treated differently when the system boundary changes. At the same time, a correct calculation inside the model does not turn its hypothetical values into field measurements. This option preserves both ideas, while the alternatives either erase the model's limits or misread the direction of a flow.
