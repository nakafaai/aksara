**Pada 1.000 Hz, selisih antara kondisi berpanel dan dua kondisi tanpa panel paling besar; temuan itu tetap terbatas pada susunan dan ruang yang diuji.**

Compare the values frequency by frequency. At 1,000 Hz, 61 dB is 13 dB below the initial condition and 12 dB below the reading after the panels are removed. Both gaps exceed those at 250 Hz and 4,000 Hz. The pattern is strong within this experiment, but the table cannot establish what will happen in another auditorium.
