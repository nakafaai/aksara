The period is **the time for one complete oscillation**. For the 60-centimetre string, the mean time for ten oscillations is 15.6 seconds. Divide that time by the number of oscillations:

<BlockMath math="T=\frac{t}{n}=\frac{15.6\,\mathrm{s}}{10}=1.56\,\mathrm{s}" />

The divisor is 10 oscillations, not five repetitions. The five repetitions provide the mean timing reported in the table. The result remains an estimate because the observer's reaction affects when the stopwatch starts and stops.
