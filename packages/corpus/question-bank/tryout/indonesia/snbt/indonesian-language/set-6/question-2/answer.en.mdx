**Rata-rata 15,6 detik untuk sepuluh ayunan pada tali 60 sentimeter memberi periode perkiraan 1,56 detik; ketelitiannya masih dipengaruhi pencatatan manual.**

A period is the time for one complete oscillation. The table gives the time for ten, so 15.6 divided by 10 gives an estimated period of 1.56 seconds. It remains an estimate because the observer's reaction determines when the manual stopwatch starts and stops.
