**Definisi *periode* menjelaskan mengapa waktu sepuluh ayunan perlu dibagi sepuluh sebelum hasil kedua panjang tali dibandingkan.**

The table does not display the period directly because it measures ten oscillations. The definition identifies the correct unit of motion: one complete oscillation. Dividing each value by ten gives 2.01, 1.56, and 2.00 seconds per oscillation for comparison.
