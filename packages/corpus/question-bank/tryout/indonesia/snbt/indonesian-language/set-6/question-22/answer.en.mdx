**Mengurangi bantuan pada tiap soal memberi Dimas ruang mengambil alih penalaran, sehingga Sari beralih dari pemberi langkah menjadi perancang dukungan sementara.**

If Sari keeps writing every step, Dimas only has something to copy. The sequence from example to prompts to independent work deliberately reduces dependence. When Dimas finds his own error through substitution, Sari's choice changes both the role of help and her view of success.
