**Kemampuan Dimas menemukan kesalahan melalui substitusi setelah bantuan dikurangi menunjukkan kemajuan pemahaman yang tidak terlihat dari jumlah soal selesai saja.**

Three problems are fewer than the original target of twelve. On the third problem, however, Dimas chooses a step, checks the result, and locates his own error. That sequence is evidence of control over learning that cannot be measured by the number of completed questions alone.
