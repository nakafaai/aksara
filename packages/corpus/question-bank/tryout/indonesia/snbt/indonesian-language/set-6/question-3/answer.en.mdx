**Kemiripan dua hasil tali 100 sentimeter dan waktu yang lebih singkat pada tali 60 sentimeter mendukung hubungan panjang dengan periode, tetapi panjang tambahan dan pencatat otomatis diperlukan untuk menentukan bentuk hubungannya.**

The 20.1- and 20.0-second results show that the 100-centimetre condition is reasonably stable before and after the 60-centimetre condition. Its difference from 15.6 seconds supports a relationship. Two length values cannot reveal whether that relationship is linear or has another form, and automatic timing would reduce observer reaction error.
