**Definisi *penyangga belajar* menjelaskan pola contoh, pertanyaan pemandu, dan kerja mandiri sebagai bantuan yang sengaja dikurangi ketika kendali Dimas meningkat.**

The meeting's three stages show the definition directly. The first problem receives the fullest help, the second only prompts, and the third independent work. Because the support decreases as Dimas gains control, scaffolding differs from continuously supplying answers.
