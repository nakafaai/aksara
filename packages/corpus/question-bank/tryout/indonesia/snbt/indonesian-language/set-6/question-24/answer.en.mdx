*Penyangga belajar*, or scaffolding, names **temporary support reduced as ability grows**. The three problems show how Sari adjusts her help.

| Problem | Sari's support | Dimas's role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | Full example and questions about reasons | Follows the example while discussing why steps work |
| Second | Guiding questions | Takes more responsibility for constructing steps |
| Third | Reminder to check by substitution after an error | Chooses steps, checks the result, and corrects the error |

Help does not disappear completely at once. Sari still gives a reminder on the third problem, but Dimas performs the check. That distinction separates scaffolding from taking over the answer.
