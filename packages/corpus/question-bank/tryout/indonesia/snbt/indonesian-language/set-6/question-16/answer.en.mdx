**Siswa menggabungkan label pameran dan kartu kondisi untuk menyusun provenans parsial lukisan, sambil membedakan lokasi, pemberi pinjaman, kepemilikan, dan celah dokumen.**

Labels and condition cards both provide time points, but they carry different information. A label names a collection or lender, while a card records location and physical condition. Together they trace Bandung, Surabaya, and then Makassar. The early transfer of ownership still lacks documentation.
