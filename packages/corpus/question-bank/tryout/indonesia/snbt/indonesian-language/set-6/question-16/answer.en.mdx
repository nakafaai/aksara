**Siswa menggabungkan label pameran dan kartu kondisi untuk menyusun provenans parsial lukisan, sambil membedakan lokasi, pemberi pinjaman, kepemilikan, dan celah dokumen.**

Labels and condition cards both provide time points, but they carry different information. A label names a collection or lender, while a card records location and physical condition. The labels associate the work with parties in Bandung and Makassar, while the cards establish inspection locations in Surabaya and Makassar. Whether and how ownership changed in the early part of the history remains undocumented.
