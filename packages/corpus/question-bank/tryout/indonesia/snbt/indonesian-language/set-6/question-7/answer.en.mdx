**Selisih yang muncul pada kedua ulangan mendukung dugaan bahwa penutup mengurangi tanah terbawa, tetapi model belum dapat memprediksi besarnya erosi pada lereng dengan akar, tanah, dan hujan yang berbeda.**

The same pattern appears twice: the covered trays lose much less soil than the open trays. That makes the proposed role of surface cover worth testing in the field. The values of 28 to 31 grams, however, cannot directly predict loss on a real slope because the model omits rainfall variation, soil structure, roots, and slope length.
