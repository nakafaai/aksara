Both trials show **less soil transported from covered trays**. Compare each covered tray with the open tray in the same trial.

| Trial | Open | Covered | Difference in soil mass |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 84 g | 28 g | <InlineMath math="84-28=56\,\mathrm{g}" /> |
| 2 | 79 g | 31 g | <InlineMath math="79-31=48\,\mathrm{g}" /> |

The direction is consistent, although the differences are not identical. This supports field testing of the proposed role of cover. The 28 to 31 grams remain results from small trays, not direct predictions for slopes with different roots, gradients, absorption, and rainfall.
