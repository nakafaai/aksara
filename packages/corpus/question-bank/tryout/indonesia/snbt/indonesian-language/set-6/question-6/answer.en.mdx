**Model baki menunjukkan bahwa penutup serat berkaitan dengan lebih sedikit tanah terbawa pada kondisi terkontrol, tetapi faktor penting pada lereng nyata belum terwakili.**

Across two trials, the open trays lose 84 and 79 grams of soil, while the covered trays lose only 28 and 31 grams. With water volume, angle, and soil mass held equal, surface cover is the meaningful difference inside the model. The result still omits roots, absorption, varying gradients, and changing rainfall in the field.
