**Definisi *provenans* menuntut riwayat kepemilikan, penguasaan, dan perpindahan dibedakan, sehingga satu catatan lokasi tidak langsung dianggap sebagai bukti hak milik.**

Provenance is not built from one kind of statement. A lender's name, an inspection location, and proof of a legal transfer answer different questions. The definition helps students assign each source its proper role and mark the parts that still require other documents.
