**Definisi *erosi* menjelaskan mengapa massa tanah yang berpindah bersama aliran menjadi hasil ukur, sekaligus membatasi penafsiran pada pemindahan partikel.**

The measured material is the soil actually carried out of each tray. That measure matches the definition of erosion as particle movement. The definition also prevents the result from being treated as evidence about every slope process, such as water absorption or root stability, which the model does not measure.
