**Exhibition labels help trace ownership, while condition cards record movements and damage; together they extend the work's history, but undocumented intervals must not be filled with assumptions.**

The two source types record different elements of provenance. Combining them enriches the verifiable history, but it does not guarantee that every movement was documented.
