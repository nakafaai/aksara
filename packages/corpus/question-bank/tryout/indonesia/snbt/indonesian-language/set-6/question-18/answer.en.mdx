**Kartu 1967 membuktikan lukisan berada di Surabaya saat diperiksa, tetapi tidak menjelaskan siapa pemiliknya atau bagaimana karya berpindah dari Bandung; celah itu tetap terbuka.**

The condition card can place the work in Surabaya in 1967 because the inspection was recorded there at the time. Location or physical custody, however, does not always equal ownership. Without a receipt, deed of gift, or loan record, the route from the Wiratama family to Surabaya remains unknown.
