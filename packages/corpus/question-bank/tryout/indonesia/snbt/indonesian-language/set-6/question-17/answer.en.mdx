A document's purpose shapes **what it supports**. The location of a named party is not necessarily the painting's physical location.

| Document | Recorded information | What that document does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1958 label | Wiratama family collection, Bandung | The subsequent chain of transfers |
| 1967 condition card | Fine crack, inspection in Surabaya | The owner or method of ownership transfer |
| 1974 label | Lent by Galeri Pesisir, Makassar | That the gallery owns it, or that the painting was then in Makassar |
| 1975 condition card | Frame repair in Makassar | A complete chain of ownership transfers |

Labels inform exhibition visitors, while condition cards support care. Both are useful, but they cannot be treated as interchangeable evidence.
