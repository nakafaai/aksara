**Membandingkan tanah terbawa dari petak lereng terbuka dan berpenutup yang memiliki kemiringan serta jenis tanah sebanding selama beberapa kejadian hujan.**

The field test should preserve the model's main contrast between open and covered ground. Matching slope and soil type makes the effect of cover easier to interpret. Several rain events are also needed so that the conclusion does not depend on one storm of a particular intensity.
