**Sari mengurangi bantuan dari contoh lengkap menjadi pemeriksaan mandiri, lalu mengukur keberhasilan melalui kemampuan Dimas menjelaskan dan memperbaiki langkahnya.**

The main change lies in both the amount of help and the measure of success. Sari moves from a full example to prompts and then to Dimas's own choice and check. By the end of three problems, Dimas can explain his steps and correct one error himself. That progress serves independence better than simply pursuing a target of twelve problems by copying steps.
