**Definisi *dukungan sebaya* menempatkan kartu sebagai alat percakapan antarpeserta yang setara, sehingga kartu tidak boleh mengubah satu peserta menjadi pengawas tetap bagi yang lain.**

The card helps partners agree on a direction and review progress together. Since their relationship is relatively equal, a goal must also remain open to revision through discussion rather than being imposed by one side. The definition explains why participant feedback matters when the card is redesigned.
