*Dukungan sebaya*, or peer support, describes **a relatively equal relationship between learning partners**. The card helps them set a direction for discussion and reflect on progress together.

Some participants also want to change goals during a meeting. The team responds by adding a revision column. The definition helps explain why participant experience informs the card's design. The passage does not assign either partner the role of permanent supervisor.
