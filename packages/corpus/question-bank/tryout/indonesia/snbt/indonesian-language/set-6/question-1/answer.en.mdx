**Percobaan membandingkan periode bandul bertali 60 dan 100 sentimeter dalam kondisi terkontrol, lalu membatasi simpulan karena baru dua panjang yang diuji dan waktu dicatat manual.**

Mass, release angle, and release method are held constant, so string length is the main observed difference. The two results for the 100-centimetre string are also nearly equal. Still, only two lengths are compared and timing is manual. The passage therefore supports an initial pattern that needs further testing, not a finished general rule.
