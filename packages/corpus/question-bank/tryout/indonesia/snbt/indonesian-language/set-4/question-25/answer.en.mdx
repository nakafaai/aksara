**Dari Lorong Sempit ke Usulan Akses Nisa**

The account moves from an inaccessible aisle to a sign proposal tested by the manager, with Nisa invited to evaluate it. **Dari Lorong Sempit ke Usulan Akses Nisa** captures both the initial barrier and the action that changes Nisa's role. The alternatives omit the evidence or her participation.
