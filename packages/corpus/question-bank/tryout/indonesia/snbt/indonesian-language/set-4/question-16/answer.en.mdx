**Siswa membandingkan dua jenis sumber tentang tur bangunan bersejarah untuk menyusun penjelasan yang terbatas dan dapat diperiksa.**

Plans from three periods show how the rooms changed, while two interviews provide residents' experiences. The students do not treat these kinds of evidence as interchangeable. They compare their functions, origins, and viewpoints to build a checkable account without assuming that every resident shared the same experience.
