**Serpihan biru menjadi petunjuk sejarah karena berulang di sepanjang retakan, sesuai dengan foto 1978, dan mengarah pada bidang pintu lama yang akhirnya dipertahankan.**

One flake could still be ordinary paint. Its meaning strengthens when the same colour repeats, forms an arch, and matches a photograph of the old reading room. Preserving the section at the end connects the early clue with fuller evidence.
