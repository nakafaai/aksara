**Repeating the model helps test whether water can move and change state without leaving a closed system. Because the lamp and ice reproduce only a small part of atmospheric conditions, the pattern does not establish the entire natural water cycle.**

The heating, condensation, and return of droplets can be inspected within the model. Its scale and omitted atmospheric conditions make the result useful as a mechanism hypothesis, not a complete replica of reality.
