Repetition lets students inspect **the sequence inside the box**: warming water, evaporation, droplets forming on the cold lid, and droplets falling back. Water changes state and location without leaving the closed box.

Repeating those steps does not add atmospheric conditions omitted by the lamp and ice. The model helps explain a principle and formulate ideas for field observations. Establishing that the same pattern describes every weather condition would still require real-world evidence.
