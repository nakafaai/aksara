**Definisi *triangulasi* memberi dasar untuk membandingkan posisi kedua sumber tanpa menganggap salah satunya sebagai catatan yang sepenuhnya netral.**

Triangulation explains why the plans and interviews are read together. The plans can check spatial changes described by the speakers, while the interviews add uses that the plans cannot show. The comparison strengthens points that converge and keeps the remaining gaps visible.
