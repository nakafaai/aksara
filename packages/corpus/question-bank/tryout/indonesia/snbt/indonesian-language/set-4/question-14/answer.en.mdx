**Definisi *pemangku kepentingan* menjelaskan alasan orang tua, pedagang, pengelola pasar, dan petugas kebersihan dilibatkan ketika data penunjuk arah dibahas.**

The four groups relate to the decision from different positions. For example, parents may use the room or accompany its users, traders and cleaners can offer insight into movement through the market, and managers can implement a change. The definition explains why the measurements should be discussed with their experience rather than replaced by opinion.
