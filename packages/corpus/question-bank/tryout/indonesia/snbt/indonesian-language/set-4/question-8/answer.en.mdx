**Mencatat suhu, kelembapan, penguapan, dan kondensasi pada beberapa kondisi cuaca nyata.**

The follow-up should measure evaporation and condensation outside the model while recording temperature, humidity, and weather conditions. Comparing several conditions lets students examine the limits of the pattern observed in the box. More boxes with the same simplifications cannot replace real weather data. One matching observation also cannot establish that a pattern always holds.
