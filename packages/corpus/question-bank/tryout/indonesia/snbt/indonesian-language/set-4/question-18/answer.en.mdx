**The plans trace spatial changes across periods, while the interviews describe residents' experiences; together they can test changes in function, but the memories of two interviewees do not represent every resident.**

The plans and interviews answer different kinds of questions. Comparing them strengthens an interpretation of the building's functions, while the small number of interviewees and the nature of memory limit the conclusion.
