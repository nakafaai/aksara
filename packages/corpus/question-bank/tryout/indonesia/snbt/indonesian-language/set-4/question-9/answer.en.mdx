**Definisi *kondensasi* menghubungkan proses pada model dengan proses yang diwakilinya sehingga kegunaan model dapat dibedakan dari batas penerapannya.**

The definition of condensation connects vapour touching the cold lid with the droplets that form. Without that link, students would see only a sequence of moving materials. The definition names the represented change of state but does not make the lamp and ice as complete as the real atmosphere.
