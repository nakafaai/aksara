*Advokasi diri*, or self-advocacy, names **how Nisa communicates her access needs**, as shown by the following actions.

| Nisa's action | Connection to self-advocacy |
| --- | --- |
| Records difficult routes | Makes the barriers she experiences concrete |
| Proposes a sign readable from a wheelchair | States the change she needs |
| Is invited to assess the test sign | Remains involved as a user in evaluation |

The term explains Nisa's participation, not just her feelings. The passage reports an invitation to evaluate, so we cannot assume that she has completed the assessment or that every access problem has been solved.
