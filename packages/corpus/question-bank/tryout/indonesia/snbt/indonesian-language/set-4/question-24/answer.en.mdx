**Definisi *advokasi diri* menjelaskan cara Nisa menyampaikan kebutuhan akses dengan bukti rute, mengusulkan perubahan, dan ikut menilai hasil uji.**

Self-advocacy appears when Nisa communicates an access need clearly and supports it with recorded experience. She also proposes a change and helps evaluate it. The definition names this sequence and shows that requesting a change can be a form of participation.
