**Nisa menghadapi hambatan dalam mengikuti latihan mandiri di pasar dan belajar melalui tindakan kecil yang bertanggung jawab.**

A complete central account must combine the subject, the decisive action or relationship, and the limit stated in the passage. The correct option satisfies all three conditions. The distractors isolate an outcome, definition, control, or limitation and therefore lose the passage's development.
