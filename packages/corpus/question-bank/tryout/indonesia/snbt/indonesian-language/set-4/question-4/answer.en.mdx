**Definisi *daya generalisasi* menjelaskan alasan hasil pada simulasi ini belum dapat langsung diterapkan pada hari berawan atau kondisi angin yang berbeda.**

Generalisability concerns how far a result extends beyond the tested conditions. The simulation records 25 degrees with the shade panel but does not include cloudy days or changing wind. The definition shows why the measured difference still needs testing in other settings.
