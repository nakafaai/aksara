**Pengelola ruang laktasi di pasar menilai penunjuk arah dari tiga pintu pasar melalui data pembanding dan masukan pihak terdampak.**

The team starts with a baseline of 18, tests signs from three entrances and obtains 29, then compares that result with 19. It also discusses the evidence with groups who use or manage the room. Because the night market remains unobserved, the evidence supports a limited extension rather than permanent adoption.
