**Model daur air dalam kotak transparan menyederhanakan proses agar dapat diperiksa sambil tetap memiliki batas representasi.**

The transparent box shows evaporation, cooling vapour, condensation, and water falling back. Students can repeat this sequence and observe changes of state more easily. The lamp and ice reproduce only a small part of atmospheric conditions, however. The model explains a principle in a closed system, not the entire natural water cycle.
