**Denah dan wawancara sama-sama membantu melacak fungsi bangunan, tetapi perubahan ruang tidak identik dengan pengalaman dua mantan penghuni.**

The correct option uses both similarity and difference. A shared subject connects the sources, while form, creator, date, and purpose determine what each source can support. The distractors dismiss one source too quickly or treat different perspectives as identical.
