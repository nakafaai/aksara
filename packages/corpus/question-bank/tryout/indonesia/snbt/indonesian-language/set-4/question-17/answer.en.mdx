Both sources concern the building's function, but **they provide different kinds of evidence**.

| Source | Available evidence | Interpretive limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Renovation plans from three periods | Changes in the arrangement of rooms | Do not directly explain every resident's experience |
| Interviews with two former residents | The speakers' experiences of using the building | Do not automatically represent all residents |

The shared subject allows comparison. A difference in form does not make either source useless. The creator, date, and purpose still need to be examined before drawing a conclusion.
