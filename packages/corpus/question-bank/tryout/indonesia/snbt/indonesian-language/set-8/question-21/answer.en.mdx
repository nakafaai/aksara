**Dito menelusuri penyebab angka menyimpang, mengulang pengukuran, mempertahankan jejak data, dan melaporkan 4,81 meter sebagai hasil dengan ketidakpastian.**

Dito neither discards the inconvenient value nor puts it straight into an average. He finds a testable technical explanation, repeats the measurement from one reference point, and marks the status of the old value. The median of the three repeats is 4.81 metres, but he still reports the tape's precision and possible water movement. This is the central sequence of decisions in the passage.
