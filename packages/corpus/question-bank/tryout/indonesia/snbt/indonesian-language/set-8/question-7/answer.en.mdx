**Repeating the model helps test whether material type can affect the rate of temperature change. Because lid thickness and air gaps are difficult to keep identical, a temperature difference cannot yet be attributed solely to the container material.**

Measurements at fixed intervals make cooling patterns comparable. Unequal lids and air gaps remain alternative explanations that must be controlled before the result is applied more broadly.
