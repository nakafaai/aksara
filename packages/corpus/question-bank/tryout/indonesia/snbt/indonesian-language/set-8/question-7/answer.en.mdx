**Laju pendinginan rata-rata sekitar 1,27°C per menit pada aluminium dan 0,73°C per menit pada polipropilena mendukung adanya perbedaan, tetapi rancangan belum mengisolasi bahan sebagai penyebabnya.**

For aluminium, the temperature falls by $70 - 51 = 19$°C in 15 minutes, giving an average of about $19/15 = 1.27$°C per minute. For polypropylene, the decrease is $11/15 \approx 0.73$°C per minute. The data clearly contain a difference, but its cause remains mixed with wall thickness, open tops, and other heat-transfer mechanisms. The calculation is sound while the causal claim must remain limited.
