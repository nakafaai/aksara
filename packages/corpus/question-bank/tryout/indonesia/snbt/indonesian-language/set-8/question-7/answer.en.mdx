Calculate **the temperature drop over 15 minutes**, then divide by elapsed time. These positive values express the magnitude of cooling.

| Container | Temperature drop | Mean cooling rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aluminium | <InlineMath math="70-51=19\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{19\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}}{15\,\mathrm{min}}\approx1.27\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}/\mathrm{min}" /> |
| Polypropylene | <InlineMath math="70-59=11\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}" /> | <InlineMath math="\frac{11\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}}{15\,\mathrm{min}}\approx0.73\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}/\mathrm{min}" /> |

Aluminium shows faster average cooling in this experiment. These are not necessarily constant rates for every minute. Wall thickness differs and several heat-transfer mechanisms remain active, so material is not isolated as the sole cause.
