**Membuat Catatan Sumur Dapat Dibandingkan tanpa Kehilangan Jejak**

The first part captures the new form's purpose: making units, reference points, and times clearer. "Without losing the trail" refers to keeping original values and units so conversions can be checked. The title also avoids promising that a format can erase genuine groundwater changes across different times and seasons.
