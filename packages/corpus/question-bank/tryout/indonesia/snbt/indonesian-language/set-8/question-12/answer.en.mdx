**Hasil 27 dari 30 menunjukkan formulir baru mengurangi kebutuhan menebak satuan dan titik acuan, tetapi pengukuran dari musim atau waktu berbeda tetap belum otomatis setara.**

Reviewers can interpret 27 pairs from the new form without guessing, compared with only 14 from the old form. This is evidence that the record structure helps. The form does not stop groundwater from changing, however. Morning and afternoon measurements, or rainy- and dry-season measurements, can differ even when written in the same format. Record comparability and equivalent measurement conditions must remain distinct.
