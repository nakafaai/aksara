The 0.28-gram value is much smaller than 0.82 and 0.74 grams, so the result does support the initial idea for this sample. The coating does not reduce mass loss to zero, however. The study also does not test other species or water movement in intact plants. We can therefore say that the evidence supports a hypothesis, but not that it establishes a rule for every leaf.
