**Menuliskan pertanyaan pada pembatas mengubah benda yang semula disembunyikan menjadi jejak penalaran yang Jati bawa menuju pembahasan berikutnya.**

At first, the bookmark is turned over so that others cannot see Jati's uncertainty. After he writes the question, it records an interpretation, supporting words, and a rejected reason. At the end, he deliberately leaves it visible and carries a new question into the next meeting. Its meaning changes through Jati's use of the object, not through its physical form.
