**Menuliskan pertanyaan pada pembatas mengubah benda yang sisinya semula dibalik menjadi jejak penalaran yang Jati bawa menuju pembahasan berikutnya.**

At first, the bookmark is turned over so that others cannot see Jati's uncertainty. Once he writes the question, the bookmark holds an idea for shared discussion. Jati then points to evidence in the text and crosses out a reason that no longer fits. At the end, he deliberately leaves it visible and carries a new question into the next meeting. Its meaning changes through Jati's use of the object, not through its physical form.
