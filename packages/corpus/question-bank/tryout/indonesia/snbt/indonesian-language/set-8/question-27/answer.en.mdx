**Buku tipis dan satu kalimat kebingungan mengubah kebiasaan menghindar Jati menjadi langkah baca yang kecil tetapi dapat dilanjutkan.**

The correct option connects the action, recurring object, and response at the ending. That sequence supports the interpretation. The distractors detach the object from the action, give the setting all causal weight, or claim an explicitly stated and fixed meaning.
