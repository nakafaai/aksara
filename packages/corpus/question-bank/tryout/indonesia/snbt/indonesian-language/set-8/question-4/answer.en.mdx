**Definisi *transpirasi* menghubungkan kehilangan massa dengan perpindahan uap air yang sedang dipelajari, tetapi perbandingan perlakuan tetap diperlukan untuk menafsirkan hasilnya.**

A balance reports only a change in mass. The definition of *transpiration* identifies the biological process that the measurement is intended to represent: water vapour moving from plant tissue to the air. The definition cannot replace the data. The uncoated, upper-coated, and lower-coated conditions are still needed to interpret how leaf surface relates to mass loss.
