Use the **uncoated group as the reference**. First calculate the difference in mass loss:

<BlockMath math="0.82\,\mathrm{g}-0.28\,\mathrm{g}=0.54\,\mathrm{g}" />

Then divide the difference by the reference group's mass loss, not the coated group's:

<BlockMath math="\frac{0.54\,\mathrm{g}}{0.82\,\mathrm{g}}\times100\%\approx65.9\%" />

The gram units cancel. The result is a relative reduction of about 65.9 percent, not 0.54 percent or 65.9 percentage points. This compares mean mass loss, not the number of leaves that stopped transpiring. The conclusion remains limited to the detached bean-leaf sample.
