**Definisi *integritas ilmiah* menjelaskan mengapa Dito menyimpan nilai menyimpang, mencatat alasan pengecualian, dan melaporkan ketidakpastian pengukuran.**

Scientific integrity appears in reviewable decisions, not in a declaration that Dito intends to be honest. The initial value remains available, its suspected error has a recorded reason, and the 4.81-metre result includes instrument precision and reading limits. The definition at the end unites these actions as transparent reporting practice.
