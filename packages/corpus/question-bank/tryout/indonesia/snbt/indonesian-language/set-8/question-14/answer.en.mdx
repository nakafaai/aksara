**Definisi *keterbandingan* menjelaskan mengapa satuan dan titik acuan perlu diselaraskan, tetapi konteks waktu juga harus cukup setara sebelum dua kedalaman dibandingkan.**

The definition provides a set of testable conditions. The new form addresses units, recording method, and reference points. It also records date and time, but recording time does not make groundwater conditions identical. The team therefore still needs a common schedule. *Comparability* connects the form's improvement to the reason further work remains necessary.
