Conduction names **heat transfer through material**, for example from the inner to the outer container wall. Distinguish it from the other processes in the passage.

| Mechanism | Example in the experiment |
| --- | --- |
| Conduction | Energy passes through the container wall |
| Convection | Moving water or air transports energy |
| Radiation | Energy travels through electromagnetic waves |
| Evaporation | Surface water becomes vapour and carries energy away |

The temperature table shows the combined outcome of the active processes. The definition identifies one mechanism without measuring its contribution or proving that the others are absent.
