**Definisi *konduksi* menjelaskan salah satu jalur perpindahan energi melalui dinding wadah, sekaligus mencegah seluruh pendinginan dianggap terjadi hanya melalui jalur itu.**

The water touches the container wall, and that wall touches the surrounding air. Conduction is relevant along this path. The water surface also contacts the air, however, and both containers are open. Convection, radiation, and evaporation can still carry energy away. The definition names one mechanism without erasing the others identified in the passage.
