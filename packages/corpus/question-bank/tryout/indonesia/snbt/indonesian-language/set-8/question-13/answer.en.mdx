**Proporsi pasangan yang dapat dibandingkan meningkat dari sekitar 46,7 persen menjadi 90 persen, yaitu sekitar 43,3 poin persentase pada uji 30 pasangan.**

Under the old form, the proportion is <InlineMath math="14/30 \approx 46.7\%" />. Under the new form, it is <InlineMath math="27/30 = 90\%" />. The difference is <InlineMath math="90 - 46.7 = 43.3" /> percentage points. This figure applies to the 30 tested pairs. It does not mean that 90 percent of the complete 40-well archive is ready for comparison because the table's unit of analysis is a tested pair.
