**Definisi *perkembangan tokoh* memberi dasar untuk membandingkan tindakan Jati pada awal dan akhir, tanpa mensyaratkan bahwa ia harus memahami seluruh bacaan.**

At the beginning, Jati chooses a seat near the door, hides the book, and turns the bookmark over. At the end, both objects are visible and he accepts responsibility for opening a discussion. Comparing these actions reveals a change in attitude. The definition prevents readers from using a false standard, such as requiring every question to be answered before a character can develop.
