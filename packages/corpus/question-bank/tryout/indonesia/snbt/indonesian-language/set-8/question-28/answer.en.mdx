**Perkembangan Jati ditandai oleh perubahan cara memperlakukan pertanyaan: dari sesuatu yang disembunyikan menjadi bahan penafsiran yang dapat diuji bersama.**

Jati does not develop because his questions disappear. In fact, he has two questions at the end. The difference lies in his actions: at first he turns the bookmark over and hides the book, then he reads the question, tests it against the paragraph, and carries it into the next meeting. This sequence shows a shift from avoiding uncertainty to working with it.
