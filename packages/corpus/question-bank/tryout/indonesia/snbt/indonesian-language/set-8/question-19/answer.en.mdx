**Definisi *kontekstualisasi* mengarahkan siswa membaca perubahan jadwal, peserta, tujuan pencatatan, dan kekosongan arsip sebelum menafsirkan pola peminjaman.**

The borrowing pattern does not exist in isolation. Meetings moved to the evening, young workers could begin attending, the available books may have changed, and conversation notes cover only some meetings. All of these conditions shape how the evidence should be read. Contextualisation lets students explain a possible relationship without reducing it to "the evening schedule caused better reading habits."
