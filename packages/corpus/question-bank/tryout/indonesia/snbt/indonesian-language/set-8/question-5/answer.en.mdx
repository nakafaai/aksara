**Permukaan Daun dan Kehilangan Air: Temuan Awal pada Daun Kacang**

The first part of the title captures the comparison between upper and lower leaf surfaces. *Initial finding* keeps the level of certainty appropriate, while *bean leaves* names the sample that was actually tested. The title neither turns reduced mass loss into stopped transpiration nor expands one experiment into a rule for all plants.
