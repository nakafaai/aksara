**Daftar peminjaman menunjukkan buku yang tercatat keluar, sedangkan catatan fasilitator memberi sebagian pengalaman peserta yang berbicara; keduanya menjawab pertanyaan yang berbeda.**

The administrative lists are useful for tracking titles and borrowing dates, but they do not show whether a book was finished or understood. The facilitator's notes contain conversations, but they favour people who spoke and cover only seven meetings. Comparing these functions keeps both sources useful without asking either one to answer a question it did not record.
