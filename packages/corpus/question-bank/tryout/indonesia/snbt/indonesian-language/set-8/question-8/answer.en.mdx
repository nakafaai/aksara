**Menguji beberapa wadah aluminium dan polipropilena dengan bentuk, ketebalan, penutup, volume air, dan suhu awal yang sama serta mengacak posisinya pada setiap pengulangan.**

The follow-up should make material the main difference between the groups. Matching thickness and shape reduces geometric effects, while identical lids reduce differences in evaporation. Multiple containers and randomised positions reduce the influence of one vessel or one location in the room. This design would test the link between material and cooling rate more precisely.
