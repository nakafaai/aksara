Radiocarbon dating answers *when* someone died, not *why*.

Sentences <InlineMath math="(12)" /> and <InlineMath math="(13)" /> place the South Asian-related group in the <InlineMath math="7" />th through <InlineMath math="10" />th centuries, while the other two groups date to the <InlineMath math="17" />th through <InlineMath math="20" />th centuries. Sentence <InlineMath math="(14)" /> adds that some date ranges within the older group do not overlap.

The remains therefore represent at least two phases separated by about <InlineMath math="1{,}000 \text{ years}" />, and possibly more than one event within the earlier phase. The dating does not prove a hailstorm as the cause of death, nor does it show that every member of the South Asian-related group died on the same day.

The strongest conclusion is therefore **Jasad-jasad tersebut tidak berasal dari satu peristiwa kematian**, meaning that the remains do not come from a single death event.
