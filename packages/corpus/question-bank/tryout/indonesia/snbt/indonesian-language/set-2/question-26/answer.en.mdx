Sentence <InlineMath math="(1)" /> gives the administrative hierarchy directly:

> “Lasem merupakan sebuah kecamatan di Kabupaten Rembang, pesisir utara **Jawa Tengah**.”

Lasem is the district, Rembang is the regency, and Jawa Tengah is the province. None of the other provinces in the choices is identified as Lasem's location.

Therefore, the answer is **Jawa Tengah**, or Central Java.
