The phrase *alat ini* contains the demonstrative *ini*, meaning "this." To identify its referent, we look back for the device introduced before sentence <InlineMath math="(25)" />.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(13)" /> introduces the GPS Collar as a collar fitted with a positioning device. Sentence <InlineMath math="(14)" /> explains that it records location. When sentence <InlineMath math="(25)" /> says that *this device* records positions and transmits location data, it continues the description of the same object.

The phrase does not refer to a camera trap, compass, handheld radio, or satellite receiver. The correct answer is **GPS Collar**.
