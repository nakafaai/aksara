A lexical meaning is a word's basic dictionary meaning. KBBI gives **tindakan mengurangi dampak bencana** as one meaning of *mitigasi*.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(17)" /> uses the word in the phrase *mitigasi interaksi negatif dengan gajah liar*. The context shows how mitigation is applied here: it aims to reduce risk and harmful effects. The question asks for that lexical meaning. Tracking movement and detecting an elephant's position are specific actions.

Therefore, the option that matches the dictionary meaning is **Tindakan mengurangi dampak bencana**.
