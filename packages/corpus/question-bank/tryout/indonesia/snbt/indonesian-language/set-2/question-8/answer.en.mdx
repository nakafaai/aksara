The wording *coba lakukan* is important. The question asks what Uwet attempted. The result of that attempt may still be incomplete.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(7)" /> states that he wanted to take a cherry. Sentence <InlineMath math="(11)" /> then says that he tried to reach it but got pricked by a thorn. The passage never says that he actually obtained the fruit.

So the precise answer is **Uwet mencoba mengambil buah ceri**.
