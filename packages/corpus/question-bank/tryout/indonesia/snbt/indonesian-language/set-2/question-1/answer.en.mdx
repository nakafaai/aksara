The main idea must cover the direction of the whole passage. One detail is too narrow. Sentences (1) and (2) introduce rice as a staple and versatile ingredient. Sentences (3) to (8) then focus on washing rice and its possible effects.

The passage centers on washing rice. The international dishes and Evangeline Mantzioris's identity provide context and evidence. *Pengolahan beras*, or rice processing, is too broad because it covers many practices.

Therefore, **mencuci beras sebelum dimasak**, washing rice before cooking, is the main idea.
