The Indonesian word *kosmopolitan* has more than one use. When it describes a person, it can refer to broad knowledge or outlook. In sentence <InlineMath math="(3)" />, however, it describes a *kawasan*, or area.

The sentence itself supplies the relevant meaning:

> “...kawasan yang tersusun dari orang atau unsur budaya yang berasal dari berbagai tempat.”

The coastal trade mentioned in sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" /> and the interregional connections in sentence <InlineMath math="(11)" /> reinforce this sense. The choices about residents' knowledge, modern development, or a coastal location do not define the word in this context.

The correct answer is **Tersusun dari orang atau unsur yang berasal dari berbagai tempat**, meaning “composed of people or elements from different places.”
