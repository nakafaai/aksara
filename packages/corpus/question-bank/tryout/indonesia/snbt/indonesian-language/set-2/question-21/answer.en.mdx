The question asks where the lake's nickname came from, not what caused the deaths of the people found there.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" /> states the cause-and-effect relationship directly:

> “Sisa kerangka beberapa ratus manusia tersebar di sekitar dan di dalam danau itu **sehingga** Roopkund dijuluki ‘Danau Tengkorak’.”

The word *sehingga* means “so” or “with the result that.” It links the hundreds of scattered skeletal remains to the nickname. The passage does not identify a war, an ancient settlement, or a group of pilgrims as the source of the name. The hailstorm hypothesis concerns a possible death event, not the naming of the lake.

Therefore, the correct answer is **Ratusan sisa kerangka manusia tersebar di sekitar danau**, meaning that hundreds of human skeletal remains are scattered around the lake.
