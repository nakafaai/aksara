A main idea must cover the passage as a whole. One supporting detail is too narrow.

The passage develops in four stages:

- Sentences (1) through (6) establish habitat loss and increasing interactions between people and elephants.
- Sentences (7) through (16) introduce movement research and the use of GPS Collars in Riau.
- Sentences (17) through (28) explain the device's purpose, installation, data, and role in early warning.
- Sentences (29) through (32) explain the technology's limits and the cooperation needed for it to help.

The population estimate, target identification, BBKSDA Riau's role, and community cooperation are supporting details. The idea broad enough to unite them is **Pemanfaatan GPS Collar untuk memitigasi interaksi negatif manusia dan gajah**.
