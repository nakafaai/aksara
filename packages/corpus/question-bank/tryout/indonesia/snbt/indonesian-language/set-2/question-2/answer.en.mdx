Sentence (5) says that washing removes some free starch from the surface of the rice grains. Sentence (6) gives visible evidence: surface starch left by milling makes the rinse water cloudy.

That removal must not be confused with a change in cooked texture. Sentence (7) warns that removing surface starch does not necessarily make cooked rice less hard or sticky. The passage also says nothing about damaging the grains, reducing flavour, or lowering quality.

Therefore, **pati bebas pada permukaan butiran beras berkurang**, the free surface starch decreases, is the stated effect.
