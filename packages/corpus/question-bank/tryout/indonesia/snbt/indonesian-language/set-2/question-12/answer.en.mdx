The key is the cause-and-effect relationship in sentence <InlineMath math="(22)" />:

> Karena kelompok keluarga gajah asia dipimpin oleh betina dewasa, pemantauan seekor pemimpin dapat mewakili pergerakan kelompoknya.

A GPS collar records the position of the individual wearing it. When that individual leads a family group, her route can indicate the movement of the group that follows her. The team selects her because she leads the group being monitored. Her sex alone would not explain that choice.

The correct answer is **Pergerakannya dapat mewakili kelompok keluarga yang dipimpinnya**.
