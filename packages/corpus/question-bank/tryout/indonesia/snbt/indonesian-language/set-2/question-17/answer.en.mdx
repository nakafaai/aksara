The question asks for **physical evidence**, not the archaeologists' conclusion. We therefore need to distinguish an observation from an interpretation.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(9)" /> describes small windows fitted with iron bars. Sentence <InlineMath math="(11)" /> adds that the production area had no door to the street and only one exit, which led into the atrium. Together, these observable features show that movement out of the work area was heavily restricted.

The label “bakery-prison” is the interpretation built from that evidence. Frescoes, an oven, and electoral inscriptions may belong to the same complex, but they do not directly establish restricted movement.

The strongest physical evidence is therefore **Jendela berjeruji dan satu-satunya jalan keluar menuju atrium**.
