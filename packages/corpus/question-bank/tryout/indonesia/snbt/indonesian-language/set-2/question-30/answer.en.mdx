A word's contextual meaning depends on what it describes and on the result stated in the sentence.

In sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" />, *strategis* describes Lasem's location. That location supported coastal trade and brought together people from different backgrounds. The word therefore refers to a position that is advantageous for a purpose. The sentence does not discuss military strategy.

The options about historical records and culture discuss the passage's subject matter, but they do not define *strategis*. The claim that Lasem was separated from trade routes directly contradicts the sentence.

Therefore, the correct answer is **Baik letaknya untuk mendukung suatu tujuan**, meaning “well-positioned to support a purpose.”
