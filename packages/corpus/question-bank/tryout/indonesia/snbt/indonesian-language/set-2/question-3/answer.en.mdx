A coherent continuation answers the question just introduced. Sentence (7) raises uncertainty about whether removing surface starch changes texture. Sentence (8) then introduces a study designed to test that issue.

The next paragraph would therefore most naturally report the study's findings about the hardness and stickiness of washed rice. Rice prices, consuming countries, recipes, and ways to remove all starch would interrupt the argument.

Thus, **hasil penelitian tentang pengaruh pencucian terhadap tekstur nasi**, research findings on washing and cooked rice texture, is correct.
