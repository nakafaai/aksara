Sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" /> states the purpose directly: enslaved people and donkeys were probably confined and exploited **to grind the grain needed to make bread**.

The floor marks in sentence <InlineMath math="(10)" /> support that explanation. The grooves around the mills guided the circular movement of the draft animals. The work described therefore belongs to the milling stage, before flour was turned into dough and baked.

Guarding the house, carrying crops, decorating the residence, and selling bread are not the functions assigned to this room in the passage.

The correct answer is **Menggiling biji-bijian yang diperlukan untuk membuat roti**.
