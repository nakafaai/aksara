**Perubahan terjadi ketika Raka memecah hambatan menjadi tindakan yang dapat diperiksa.**

At first, Raka delays because he imagines that the project must be finished all at once. Once that obstacle is divided into checkable steps, he begins and makes progress within a week. This relationship supports the conclusion that change starts when the problem becomes a measurable action.
