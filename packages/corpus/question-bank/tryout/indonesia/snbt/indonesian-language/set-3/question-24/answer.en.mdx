**Metacognition** names the way Raka examines and changes his learning strategy. Match the definition to actions actually described in the story.

| Thinking stage | Raka's action |
| --- | --- |
| Identifying the problem | Recognizing that expecting to finish the entire project at once makes him delay |
| Choosing a strategy | Making three small steps and asking a friend to check progress |
| Evaluating the result | Noting the first completed interview and revising the schedule after one week |

The definition explains **evaluation of one's own strategy**. It does not merely label a feeling or a setting. Actions and checks on their results show Raka's development. Simply using the word *metakognisi* would not demonstrate it.
