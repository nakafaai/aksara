The term **food web**, or *jaring-jaring makanan*, connects the model's objects to the feeding relationships they represent.

| Model component | What it represents |
| --- | --- |
| Plant, caterpillar, bird, and decomposer cards | Organisms in the garden |
| Strings between cards | Interconnected feeding relationships |
| A change to one component | A population change that may affect several feeding relationships |

The definition helps students read a **network of relationships**, not merely a collection of cards or one isolated chain. It does not add seasons, disease, or animal movement that the model omits. Understanding the term clarifies the model's purpose while preserving its limits.
