**Definisi *jaring-jaring makanan* menghubungkan proses pada model dengan proses yang diwakilinya sehingga kegunaan model dapat dibedakan dari batas penerapannya.**

The definition shows that the model represents a connected set of feeding relationships rather than a single isolated chain. A change to one card can therefore be read through its effect on several strings. This clarifies the model's purpose without adding the real-world factors that the cards omit.
