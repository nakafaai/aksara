**Memantau perubahan populasi serta beberapa hubungan makan di kebun pada musim yang berbeda.**

The follow-up should trace the relationship represented by the model in the actual garden. Comparing seasons while recording populations and feeding links lets students examine a factor omitted from the cards. Adding units with the same simplifications does not supply the missing field conditions. One matching observation also cannot establish that a pattern always holds.
