**Definisi *data dasar* menjelaskan alasan nilai 24 dipakai sebagai titik acuan ketika hasil uji 35 dan hasil pembanding 26 ditafsirkan.**

The value 24 was recorded before the short timetable was tested. It therefore provides a starting point for judging whether 35 differs from the earlier pattern, while 26 offers another comparison. A baseline helps interpret change but cannot establish its cause by itself.
