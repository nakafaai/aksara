**Definisi *simbol* menjelaskan cara lampu membawa makna undangan melalui perbedaan keadaan panggung sebelum dan sesudah tindakan Mira.**

The definition asks readers to find meaning beyond an object's literal function. The lamp illuminates the stage, but the story also places it in two contrasting scenes: empty chairs before Mira reads and a circle of listeners afterward. That contrast supports the meaning of an invitation.
