**Definisi *variabel kontrol* menjelaskan alasan jenis benih, volume air, dan ukuran wadah dijaga sama agar perbandingan pencahayaan lebih wajar.**

Seed type, water volume, and container size could also affect germination. Keeping them constant reduces alternative explanations when the lighting conditions are compared. The controls make the comparison more informative, but they do not prove that lighting is the sole cause.
