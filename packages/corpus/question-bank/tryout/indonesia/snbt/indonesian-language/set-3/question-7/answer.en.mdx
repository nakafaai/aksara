**Repeating the model helps test whether a change in one population can affect more than one feeding relationship. Because the cards omit seasons, disease, and animal movement, the pattern is a hypothesis for field observation rather than direct evidence about the whole ecosystem.**

The model makes the relationships among its parts repeatable and inspectable. Its deliberate omissions limit application to real ecosystems; it tests a mechanism and generates a field question, but does not replace field evidence.
