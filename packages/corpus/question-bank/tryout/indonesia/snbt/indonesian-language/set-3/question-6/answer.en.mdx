**Model jaring-jaring makanan di kebun sekolah menyederhanakan proses agar dapat diperiksa sambil tetap memiliki batas representasi.**

The cards and strings let students change, repeat, and compare feeding relationships. A change to one population can then be traced across several links. Seasons, disease, and animal movement are absent, however. The model can examine a mechanism and generate hypotheses, but it cannot replace the real garden.
