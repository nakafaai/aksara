**Keputusan Berbasis Bukti dalam Layanan Perpustakaan Keliling**

The passage focuses on how the service team evaluates a change through a baseline, a trial, a comparison, user input, and stated limitations. **Keputusan Berbasis Bukti dalam Layanan Perpustakaan Keliling** covers that full process without claiming that the new timetable must work everywhere.
