The lamp's meaning as an **invitation to listen together** is built through events, not through its physical shape alone.

| Story stage | Detail supporting the interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Beginning | The lamp is lit, but the chairs at the stage are empty |
| Mira's action | Mira begins reading the first page aloud |
| Response | The driver asks for more and the seller moves closer, forming a circle of listeners |

The same lamp now illuminates a **shared activity**. That change in the scene and the listeners' response support its symbolic meaning. The story neither changes the lamp's shape nor says that everyone in the terminal becomes a reader.
