**Lampu baca memperoleh makna sebagai undangan karena hadir saat panggung kosong, menyertai tindakan Mira, dan menerangi orang-orang yang akhirnya mendengarkan.**

The meaning does not come from the lamp alone. We must combine the empty stage, Mira's decision to read aloud, and the driver and seller's response. Together, those moments turn an ignored space into a shared activity.
