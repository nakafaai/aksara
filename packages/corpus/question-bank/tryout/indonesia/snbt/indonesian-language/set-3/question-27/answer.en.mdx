**Tindakan Mira membuat lampu beralih dari penanda panggung kosong menjadi undangan membaca, lalu permintaan sopir dan kedatangan penjual menguatkan perubahan itu.**

The lamp does not change physically. The events around it do: Mira begins reading, the driver asks her to continue, and the seller moves closer. Its symbolic meaning therefore comes from the connection between Mira's action and the closing response.
