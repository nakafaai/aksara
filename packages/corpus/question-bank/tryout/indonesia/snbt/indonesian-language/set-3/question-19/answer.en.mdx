**Definisi *sumber sezaman* memberi dasar untuk membandingkan posisi kedua sumber tanpa menganggap salah satunya sebagai catatan yang sepenuhnya netral.**

The term *sumber sezaman* establishes that the letter and photograph were created during the period being studied. That timing makes them relevant but does not make either source neutral or complete. The students must still consider the letter's purpose and the photograph's viewpoint before combining their information.
