**Kegunaan dan Batas Model Jaring-Jaring Makanan di Kebun Sekolah**

The passage gives equal weight to two sides of the model. It helps students trace feeding relationships in a controlled way, but it omits seasons, disease, and animal movement. **Kegunaan dan Batas Model Jaring-Jaring Makanan di Kebun Sekolah** captures both its value and its limits.
