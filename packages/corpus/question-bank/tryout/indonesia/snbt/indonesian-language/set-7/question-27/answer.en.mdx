**Pertanyaan pada kartu menahan tindakan otomatis pengunjung dan mengarahkannya menjadi percobaan, pembacaan diagram, serta percakapan.**

Before reading the card, the child is about to repeat the other visitors' routine: press the switch and leave. The question makes the hand pause. The child then changes the lamp's position, reads the diagram, and invites a friend to test an idea. The card works by changing the sequence of actions, not through its physical form or an answer supplied by Lila.
