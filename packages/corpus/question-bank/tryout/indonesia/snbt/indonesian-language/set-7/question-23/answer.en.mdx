**Alat bantu menjadi lebih aman ketika isinya diperiksa, batas penggunaannya dinyatakan, dan arahan manusia tetap tersedia.**

Three details support this conclusion. Staff members check the card's content, the nurse adds an exception, and the clinic treats it as a supplementary aid. Together, these details show that clear language alone is insufficient. The information must be accurate, its limits must be visible, and patients must still be able to ask for guidance when their situation does not fit the usual route.
