**Dalam tiga suhu yang diuji, 35°C menghasilkan keliling terbesar, tetapi suhu tambahan dan pengukuran gas langsung diperlukan sebelum 35°C disebut suhu optimum produksi gas.**

The data compare 20°C, 35°C, and 50°C, so the safe conclusion applies only to those choices. A temperature such as 32°C or 38°C might produce a different result. Circumference is also an indirect measure. Closer temperature intervals and direct gas-volume measurement are needed to test an optimum claim.
