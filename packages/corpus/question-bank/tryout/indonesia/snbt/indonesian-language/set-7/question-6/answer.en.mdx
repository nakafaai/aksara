Look at where the two numbers come from. Population A stops at <InlineMath math="16" /> and population B at <InlineMath math="24" />, **exactly matching the available food tokens**. The simulation therefore links the resource count directly to the population limit. However, the rule that removes excess population tokens was built into the model. The results explain the model's mechanism, but they do not establish that a natural population would stop at the same values.
