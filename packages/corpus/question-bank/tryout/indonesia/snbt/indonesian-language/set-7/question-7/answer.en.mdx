**Perbedaan titik henti konsisten dengan gagasan daya dukung, tetapi karena batas itu dimasukkan melalui jumlah keping makanan, model tidak dapat menjadi bukti mandiri bahwa populasi nyata akan berhenti dengan pola yang sama.**

In the simulation, every population token beyond the available food is removed. Under that rule, the two populations must stop at 16 and 24. This pattern represents carrying capacity inside the model, but it does not test what actually happens in nature. That would still require field data on populations, resources, and the other conditions that change over time.
