The model proposes a link between limited resources and a population limit. A useful follow-up must therefore record both quantities in a real population instead of merely adding more classroom tokens. **Births, deaths, migration, and age structure** are needed to account for changes in population size. Repeated observations can then test whether the apparent limit stays stable or shifts as environmental conditions change.
