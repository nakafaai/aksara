**Definisi *representasi* membatasi poster sebagai bukti tentang citra yang ingin dibangun penyelenggara, sehingga poster tidak disamakan dengan catatan lengkap pengalaman pengunjung.**

The posters remain useful, but their use must be precise. Because *representation* means how the exhibition presents itself, the slogans and images provide evidence about the message the organisers intended to convey. They do not record every visitor's reaction after attending. The definition prevents a jump from "the organisers wanted to appear inclusive" to "every visitor felt included."
