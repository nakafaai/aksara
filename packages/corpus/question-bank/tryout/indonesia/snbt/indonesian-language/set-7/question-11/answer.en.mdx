**Klinik memakai hasil perbandingan dan masukan aksesibilitas untuk memperbaiki penanda, lalu melanjutkan pengujian secara terbatas sebelum memutuskan penerapan tetap.**

The decision develops in three steps. First, 56 of 60 patients completed the route without returning under the new signs, compared with 42 of 60 under the old signs. Second, users identified a visibility problem with the floor arrows. Third, the team revised the signs and kept the trial limited because patient needs differed between shifts. The passage therefore combines numerical evidence with user experience instead of claiming that a permanent solution has already been proven.
