**Pesan Resmi dan Suara Pengunjung: Membaca Batas Arsip Pameran**

The phrase *official message* points to the five posters, while *visitor voices* refers to the voluntary guest-book entries. *Reading the archive's limits* matters because the surviving books cover only two cities and the total number of visitors is unknown. The title therefore captures both the source comparison and the caution at the centre of the passage.
