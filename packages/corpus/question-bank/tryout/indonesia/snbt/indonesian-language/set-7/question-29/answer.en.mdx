**Definisi *atmosfer* mengarahkan pembaca menghubungkan bunyi aula, gerak pengunjung, dan percakapan di meja untuk menafsirkan perubahan suasana.**

Atmosphere is not determined by one word such as *busy* or *quiet*. At the beginning, short sounds and quick movements create a hurried feeling. At the end, the hall remains noisy, but pausing, testing, reading, and speaking more softly create a focused mood. The definition helps readers judge that change through concrete language and events in the text.
