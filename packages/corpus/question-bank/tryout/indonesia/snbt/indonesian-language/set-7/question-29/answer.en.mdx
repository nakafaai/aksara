The term *atmosphere* directs attention to **sounds combined with actions**, not noise alone.

| Part of the story | Sounds and actions | Supported mood |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginning | Bells, buttons, loudspeaker, visitors press the switch and leave | Hurry and brief attention |
| Ending around the table | Hall noise remains, visitors test, read the diagram, and speak softly | Focus and shared investigation |

The change is limited to interaction at that table. Lila does not silence the whole hall or provide the scientific answer. Those details rule out interpretations based only on a reader's personal impression.
