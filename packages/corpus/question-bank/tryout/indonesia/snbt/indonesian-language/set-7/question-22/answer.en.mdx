**Meminta petugas memeriksa kartu membuat penyederhanaan Arum tetap sesuai alur umum dan mengungkap pengecualian yang sebelumnya ia lewatkan.**

Simplification can clarify a route, but it can also remove details that patients need. Staff review confirms that the card's four stages match the usual route. The nurse then identifies the missing exception: some patients need assessment first. The review does not turn the card into an absolute rule. It gives Arum a concrete reason to state the card's limits.
