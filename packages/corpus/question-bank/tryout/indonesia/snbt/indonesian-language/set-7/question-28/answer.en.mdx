**Keramaian fisik tetap ada, tetapi pilihan bunyi dan tindakan pada penutup memperlihatkan terbentuknya ruang perhatian di tengah keramaian itu.**

The ending deliberately repeats the hall's noise: the buttons are still sounding. The atmosphere therefore does not change because the hall suddenly becomes quiet. What changes is the pattern of action around the table. Visitors stop, test, read, and speak more deliberately. Together, these details show attention forming within the noise rather than after the noise disappears.
