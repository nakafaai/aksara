**Definisi *alur layanan* menunjukkan bahwa ukuran tanpa kembali hanya menilai kelancaran urutan, bukan seluruh mutu layanan seperti waktu tunggu, ketepatan tindakan, dan aksesibilitas.**

The team's measure is narrow: a patient counts as successful when they complete the stages without returning to an earlier desk. The definition of *service flow* explains why this measure is relevant because a flow concerns the order of activities. Yet an easy-to-follow order is not automatically fast, medically accurate, or accessible to everyone. The definition therefore clarifies both what the study measures and what it leaves unmeasured.
