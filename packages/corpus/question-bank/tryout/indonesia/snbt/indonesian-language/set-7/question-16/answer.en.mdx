**Siswa membandingkan citra inklusif pada lima poster dengan tanggapan sukarela dari dua kota, lalu membatasi simpulan karena tidak ada data seluruh pengunjung.**

The posters show a message deliberately created by the organisers: the exhibition presents itself as suitable for every family. The guest books add a wider range of experiences, but only from people who chose to write in two cities. Because the total number of visitors is also unknown, the 240 entries cannot be treated as a representative sample of all five cities. The correct option uses both sources while respecting that limit.
