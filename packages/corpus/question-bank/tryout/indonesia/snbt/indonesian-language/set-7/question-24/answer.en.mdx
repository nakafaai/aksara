**Definisi *literasi kesehatan* menyatukan tiga tindakan Arum: memperoleh informasi dari petugas, memahaminya melalui kartu yang diperiksa, dan menggunakannya untuk membantu nenek.**

The definition at the end is not an added label. Each part appears in Arum's actions. She *obtains* information by asking, *understands* it by drafting and checking the card, and *uses* it to prepare documents and find the next desk. This connection also explains why merely seeing the arrows at the beginning was not enough.
