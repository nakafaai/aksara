The definition of *health literacy* connects **obtaining, understanding, and using information** to Arum's actions.

| Part of the definition | Evidence from her actions |
| --- | --- |
| Obtaining | Asks staff about the sequence and documents |
| Understanding | Drafts the card, requests review, and corrects the exception |
| Using | Helps her grandmother prepare documents and find the next desk |

Desk numbers alone do not tell Arum about referral verification or exceptions to the route. Staff review and the note about changing patient conditions keep the card in its role as a supplementary aid.
