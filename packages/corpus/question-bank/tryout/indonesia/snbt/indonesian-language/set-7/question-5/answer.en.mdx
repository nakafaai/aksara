**Suhu, Pengembangan Balon, dan Hipotesis Produksi Gas Ragi**

Temperature is the compared variable, while balloon circumference is the actual observation. “Hypothesis” preserves the distinction between balloon expansion and the gas production it is meant to represent. The title captures the data, interpretive aim, and level of certainty.
