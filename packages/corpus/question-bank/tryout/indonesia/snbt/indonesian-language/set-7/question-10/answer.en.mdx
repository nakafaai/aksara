**Daya Dukung dalam Simulasi Keping: Aturan dan Batas**

This title names the central concept, carrying capacity, and captures both parts of the passage. The food-token rule creates a population limit that is easy to observe. At the same time, the passage warns that the values 16 and 24 come from that rule and should not be treated as natural population limits without field evidence.
