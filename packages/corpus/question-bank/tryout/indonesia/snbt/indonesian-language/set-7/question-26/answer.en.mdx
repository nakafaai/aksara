**Pertanyaan Lila mengubah interaksi di satu meja dari gerak tergesa menjadi penyelidikan bersama, meskipun keramaian aula tetap berlangsung.**

At the beginning, visitors press the switch and leave. After the card appears, one child pauses, moves the lamp, reads the diagram, and argues with a friend. That is the story's central change. The bell, buttons, and loudspeaker never disappear, so the whole hall does not become quiet. What changes is how a small group pays attention at one table.
