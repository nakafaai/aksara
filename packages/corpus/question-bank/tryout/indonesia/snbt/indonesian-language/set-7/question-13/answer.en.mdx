**Proporsi pasien yang tidak kembali meningkat dari <InlineMath math="70" /> persen menjadi sekitar <InlineMath math="93{,}3" /> persen, sehingga selisihnya sekitar <InlineMath math="23{,}3" /> poin persentase.**

Calculate both proportions using their common denominator. Under the old signs, the result is <InlineMath math="42/60 = 70\%" />. Under the new signs, it is <InlineMath math="56/60 \approx 93.3\%" />. The difference is therefore about <InlineMath math="23.3" /> percentage points. Calling this a <InlineMath math="23.3" /> percent increase would be inaccurate because a relative percentage increase uses <InlineMath math="70" /> percent as its base and gives a different value.
