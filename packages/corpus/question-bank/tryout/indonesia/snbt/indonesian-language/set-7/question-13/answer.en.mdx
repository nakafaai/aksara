**Proporsi pasien yang tidak kembali meningkat dari 70 persen menjadi sekitar 93,3 persen, sehingga selisihnya sekitar 23,3 poin persentase.**

Calculate both proportions using their common denominator. Under the old signs, the result is <InlineMath math="42/60 = 70\%" />. Under the new signs, it is <InlineMath math="56/60 \approx 93.3\%" />. The difference is therefore about <InlineMath math="23.3" /> percentage points. Calling this a 23.3 percent increase would be inaccurate because a relative percentage increase uses 70 percent as its base and gives a different value.
