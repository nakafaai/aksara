**Poster mendukung simpulan tentang citra yang ingin dibangun di lima kota, sedangkan buku tamu mendukung simpulan tentang sebagian pengalaman yang dicatat secara sukarela di dua kota.**

The key question is not which source wins, but which question each source can answer. The organisers created the posters before the event, so they are best suited to studying the official message. The guest books were written after visits and can reveal the writers' experiences. Those writers, however, chose whether to write, and the surviving books come from only two cities. Conclusions based on the guest books must therefore remain narrower.
