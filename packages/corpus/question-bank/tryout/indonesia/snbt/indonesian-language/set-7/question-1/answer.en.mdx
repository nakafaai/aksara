**Percobaan membandingkan keliling balon pada tiga suhu dengan faktor lain terkontrol, lalu membatasi simpulan karena keliling hanya menjadi petunjuk kasar jumlah gas.**

Four bottles at each temperature use the same mixture, vessel, and duration. The 23-centimetre circumference at 35°C exceeds the 14 and 4 centimetres at the other temperatures. Balloons can still differ in elasticity or leak. The passage therefore describes a temperature pattern through an indirect measure, not a precise gas quantity.
