In the model, the food supply determines how many individuals can remain. That is why the two supplies produce limits of <InlineMath math="16" /> and <InlineMath math="24" />. The definition of *carrying capacity* gives this relationship a name. The word *sustainably* also matters: **real environments can change**, so their carrying capacity need not remain one fixed number forever.
