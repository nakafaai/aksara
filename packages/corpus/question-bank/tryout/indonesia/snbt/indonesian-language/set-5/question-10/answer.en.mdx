**Kegunaan dan Batas Model Rangkaian Listrik Dua Lampu**

The passage moves from what the model can do to where its use stops. It helps compare series and parallel arrangements, but it does not represent household wiring. This title includes both ideas and names the subject precisely. The other choices focus on the term *rangkaian tertutup* alone or claim more certainty than the evidence allows.
