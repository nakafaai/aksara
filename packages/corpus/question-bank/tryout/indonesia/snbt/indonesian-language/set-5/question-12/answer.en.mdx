**Nilai 32 adalah kondisi pembanding untuk label tujuan besar; uji tersebut mengukur ketepatan tujuan, bukan keterlambatan akibat cuaca laut.**

The task separates an explicitly reported result from the claim that result can support. The correct option preserves both: it states the observed information and keeps the interpretation provisional. The distractors declare one cause, generalise beyond the passage, discard the result entirely, or remove a necessary comparison.
