**Jahitan merah lama, pilihan pola merah, dan tanggapan pelanggan membuat bekas reparasi terbaca sebagai kesinambungan perawatan pada blus warisan.**

The red thread does not gain meaning from colour alone. It first appears as the mother's old stitches and is then chosen again for the new repair. When the customer speaks of “two acts of care,” the ending strengthens the link between those two moments.
