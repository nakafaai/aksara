**Definisi *konflik* menjelaskan mengapa makna benang merah berkembang melalui pertentangan antara menyembunyikan kerusakan dan mempertahankan riwayat blus.**

The conflict appears in two wishes that cannot easily be satisfied together. The red thread gives the characters a concrete way to negotiate that tension. Its meaning therefore develops through the conflict and the closing response rather than remaining fixed from the start.
