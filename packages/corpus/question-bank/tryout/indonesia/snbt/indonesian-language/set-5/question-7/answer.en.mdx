**Repeating the model helps test whether component arrangement determines current paths and the behaviour of each bulb. Because the model uses low voltage and does not represent household wiring, its result cannot be applied directly to a building's electrical system.**

Comparing series and parallel circuits isolates the relationship between arrangement and bulb behaviour. The voltage and scale limits make the model suitable for testing a principle, not for inferring the safety or performance of household wiring.
