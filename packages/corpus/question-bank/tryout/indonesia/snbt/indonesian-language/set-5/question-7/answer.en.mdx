**Pengulangan model dapat menguji hubungan susunan dan perilaku lampu, tetapi hasilnya menjadi hipotesis untuk pengamatan nyata karena model bertegangan rendah dan bukan instalasi rumah.**

Comparing series and parallel circuits helps students focus on the effect of arrangement. The voltage and scale, however, differ from household conditions. The pattern is therefore worth testing in a real setting, but it is not yet evidence about the safety or performance of household wiring.
