The customer's change is visible in **the initial request and final choice**.

| Stage | Detail | Meaning for the customer's attitude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginning | Requests an invisible repair | Wants to preserve the garment's history without visible damage |
| Comparing samples | Recognises the mother's stitches and chooses red | Accepts a visible new repair |
| Ending | Refers to two acts of care | Connects earlier care with Ayu's repair |

The customer still wants to preserve the inherited blouse. What changes is acceptance of visible repair. Red gains meaning through the customer's choice and response, rather than merely serving as decoration.
