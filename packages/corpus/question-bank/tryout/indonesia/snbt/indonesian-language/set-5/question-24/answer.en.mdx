*Akuntabilitas*, or accountability, connects **explanation, consequences, and records** in Bima's actions.

| Action in the story | Aspect of accountability |
| --- | --- |
| Records task changes and their reasons | Provides an inspectable record |
| Holds two boxes with uncertain destinations | Accepts that the day's shipment falls to 16 boxes |
| Informs the receiving schools | Explains the decision to affected people |

The definition brings together actions already shown in the story. Naming the concept does not itself solve the problem. Two boxes still await the next shipment.
