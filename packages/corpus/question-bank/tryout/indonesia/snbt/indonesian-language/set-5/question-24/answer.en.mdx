**Definisi *akuntabilitas* menghubungkan catatan perubahan dan dua kotak yang ditahan dengan kesediaan Bima menjelaskan keputusan serta menerima akibatnya.**

The term is shown through actions rather than attached as a character trait. Bima keeps a record of changes, informs the recipients, and accepts that two boxes must be delayed. Together, those actions show a decision he can explain, a consequence he accepts, and evidence others can inspect.
