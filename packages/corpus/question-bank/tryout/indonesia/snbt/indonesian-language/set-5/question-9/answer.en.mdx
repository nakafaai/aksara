**Definisi *rangkaian tertutup* menghubungkan proses pada model dengan proses yang diwakilinya sehingga kegunaan model dapat dibedakan dari batas penerapannya.**

The term names the condition in which charge can move through a complete path. Once that condition has a clear name, readers can connect the model's arrangement to the electrical process it represents. The definition guides interpretation, but it does not make the model identical to household wiring.
