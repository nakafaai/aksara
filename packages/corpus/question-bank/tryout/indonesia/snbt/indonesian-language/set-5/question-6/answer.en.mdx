**Rangkaian listrik dengan dua lampu menyederhanakan proses agar dapat diperiksa sambil tetap memiliki batas representasi.**

The passage develops two sides of the model together. Using the same battery makes the series and parallel arrangements easier to compare, so students can examine how arrangement affects the bulbs. Yet the low voltage and simplified scale do not represent household wiring. This option captures both the model's use and its limit.
