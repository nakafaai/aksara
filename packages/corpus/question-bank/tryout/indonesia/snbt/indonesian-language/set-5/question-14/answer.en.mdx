**Definisi *logistik* menunjukkan bahwa ketepatan label hanya satu bagian dari perpindahan buku dari asal sampai penerima, sehingga keterlambatan akibat cuaca tetap relevan.**

The large labels were tested at the stage where boxes are assigned to destination vessels. *Logistik*, however, covers the whole movement from origin to recipient. The definition makes clear why improved routing accuracy does not yet explain delays during a sea journey.
