**Mengukur arus dan tegangan pada rangkaian seri serta paralel bertegangan rendah dengan kondisi komponen yang sebanding dan alat ukur terkalibrasi.**

The follow-up should test the same pattern: how circuit arrangement relates to current and voltage. Calibrated instruments and comparable component conditions provide quantitative data. These data clarify the relation in the experimental circuits, but do not establish the performance of household wiring. Adding units without measuring current and voltage does not answer the question. Changing many factors at once also makes differences hard to interpret.
