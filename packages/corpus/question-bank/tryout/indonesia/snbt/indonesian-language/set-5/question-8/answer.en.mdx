**Mengukur arus dan tegangan pada rangkaian seri serta paralel dengan komponen dan alat ukur yang terkalibrasi.**

The follow-up should test the same pattern: how circuit arrangement relates to current and voltage. Real components and calibrated instruments provide measurements that can be compared directly. Repeating the same simplification only strengthens the model's internal result, while changing many factors at once makes the cause of any difference unclear.
