**Definisi *indikator* menjelaskan bahwa persentase cahaya mewakili kejernihan visual, sehingga ukuran itu belum cukup untuk menyatakan air aman diminum.**

The percentage of transmitted light provides a measurable comparison of visual clarity. Water that looks clear may still contain microorganisms or dissolved substances. The definition of an indicator separates what the number represents from the untested claim of drinking safety.
