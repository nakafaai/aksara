Compare **the content and creation time of each source**, not just their shared subject.

| Source | Available content | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order book from 2004 to 2023 | Dates, repair types, and costs recorded at the transaction | Does not record customers' reasons |
| Interviews with three tailors in 2026 | Memories of customers extending garment life or repairing inherited clothes | Do not represent every customer's reason |

The order book helps identify repair patterns. The interviews offer possible explanations for those patterns. The sources complement each other by answering different questions.
