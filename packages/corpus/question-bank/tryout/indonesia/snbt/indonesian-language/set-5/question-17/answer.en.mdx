**Buku pesanan dan cerita penjahit sama-sama menunjukkan perubahan reparasi, tetapi jenis perbaikan dan alasan pelanggan merupakan bukti yang berbeda.**

The correct option uses both similarity and difference. A shared subject connects the sources, while form and purpose determine what each source can support. The distractors dismiss one source too quickly or treat different perspectives as identical.
