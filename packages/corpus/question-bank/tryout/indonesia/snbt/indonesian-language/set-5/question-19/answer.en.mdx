**Definisi *sejarah lisan* menempatkan wawancara sebagai rekaman pengalaman yang perlu dinilai menurut penutur dan waktu perekamannya, bukan disamakan dengan catatan transaksi.**

The interviews remain historical sources because they record experiences of the past. Their content, however, comes from the memories of three tailors in 2026 rather than from every transaction as it occurred. The definition explains both the interviews' value and why their speakers and recording date still matter.
