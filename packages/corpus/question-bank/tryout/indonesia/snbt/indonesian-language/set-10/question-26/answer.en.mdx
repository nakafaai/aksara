**Peta berubah dari daftar lokasi lampu terpasang menjadi catatan bersama yang membedakan data resmi, kondisi teramati, waktu, sumber, dan bagian yang belum diketahui.**

Start with the contrast between the office map and Nara's transparent layers. A dot on the office map says only that a lamp was installed. The new layers record the reach of its light, the observation time, and the source. Nara also keeps unresolved reports and an unexamined area. The map therefore becomes a shared record that can be checked and revised, rather than a final statement about conditions at every moment.
