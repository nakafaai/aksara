**Manfaat tampak paling besar pada kelompok mobilitas dan kebutuhan majemuk, sedangkan perubahan kecil pada kelompok sensorik menunjukkan ikon masih perlu diperbaiki.**

The mobility group rises from 8 to 16 out of 20, while the multiple-needs group rises from 3 to 8 out of 15. The sensory group moves only from 12 to 13 out of 20. The interviews suggest one explanation: the “quiet” icon is too broad. The evidence does not make sensory needs unimportant. It shows that the information design needs more precision.
