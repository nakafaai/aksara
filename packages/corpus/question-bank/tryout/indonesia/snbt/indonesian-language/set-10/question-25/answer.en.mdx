**Tari Mengubah Aturan setelah Mendengar**

The title captures the story's turning point. Tari does not immediately rewrite the rules from her own guess. She listens to participants, changes the game, and listens again when the table creates another barrier. The other titles preserve her initial assumption about identical rules or make one change seem to solve every problem.
