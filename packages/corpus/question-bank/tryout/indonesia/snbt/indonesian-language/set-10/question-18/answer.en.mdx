**Penerangan tampaknya lebih tersedia di sekitar pasar dan jalan utama daripada jalur sungai, tetapi tiga catatan belum cukup untuk mewakili seluruh rute atau memastikan kinerja setiap lampu.**

The map places more lamps near the market, and Sari describes a lit row there. Along the more sparsely marked riverside, Banu records four dark nights. This agreement supports a limited spatial pattern. It still cannot tell us about every route, every night, or every lamp because only three residents provide experiential evidence.
