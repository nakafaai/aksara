**Menjelaskan mengapa suhu antarputaran tidak boleh dibandingkan tanpa konteks energi matahari, sedangkan sudut reflektor dapat dibandingkan di dalam putaran yang sama.**

Irradiance rises from 580 to 720 watts per square metre, so all ovens can reasonably become warmer as more solar power is available. Comparing 66 degrees in the third run with 60 degrees in the second would mix angle and weather effects. A cleaner comparison stays within one row, where the ovens share the same irradiance context.
