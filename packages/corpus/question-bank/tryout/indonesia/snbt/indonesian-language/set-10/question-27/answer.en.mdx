**Kedua catatan berasal dari waktu yang berbeda dan sama-sama dapat memuat pengalaman yang benar; legenda menjaga konteks itu agar kondisi lampu tidak dianggap tetap.**

The key is the difference between 19.00 and 22.00. A lamp that works at the first time may be off at the second, so the two reports do not necessarily cancel each other out. The story gives no evidence that the lamp failed at one precise moment. By recording time and source, Nara preserves what is actually known without forcing a changing condition into one timeless answer.
