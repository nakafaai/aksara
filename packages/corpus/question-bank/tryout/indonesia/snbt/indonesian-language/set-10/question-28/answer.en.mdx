**Nara memperlakukan peta sebagai pengetahuan sementara: bagian yang belum diamati tidak boleh dianggap sudah diketahui, dan laporan yang berubah perlu tetap memiliki konteks.**

Put two of Nara's decisions together. First, she writes “unexamined” because she did not reach the alley; she does not turn missing data into proof that it was dark. Second, she keeps several layers so that differences in time and source remain visible. Both decisions show the same principle: the map may contain conclusions, but it must also show where those conclusions stop.
