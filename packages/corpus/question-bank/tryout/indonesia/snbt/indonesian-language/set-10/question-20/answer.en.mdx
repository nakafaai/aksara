**Membaca Peta Lampu dan Pengalaman Malam Warga**

This title names both sides of the evidence: the map's arrangement of lamps and residents' night-time experience. “Reading” fits because the passage examines the function, perspective, and limit of each source. The other titles turn three diaries into the voice of a whole village or treat installation marks as a guarantee of nightly operation.
