**Siswa menghubungkan lokasi administratif dengan pengalaman malam yang terikat rute dan waktu, lalu membatasi kesimpulan pada bagian kampung yang benar-benar tercakup.**

The map supplies locations and installation status. The diaries supply experiences tied to particular routes and times. Overlaying them links the different reports about the market, main road, and riverside to specific places. Three writers still cannot represent the whole village, and “installed” does not guarantee that a lamp worked every night.
