**Mewujudkan batas pengetahuan yang dimiliki para tokoh sekaligus membuka kemungkinan peta dilanjutkan tanpa memastikan siapa yang akan melengkapinya.**

The empty area is doing real work in the story. Nara had already marked the alley as unexamined. At the end, the story offers two possible people who might add to the map, an officer or a resident, but chooses neither. The blank space on the map thus becomes a blank in the plot as well. We know the work can continue, but not who will continue it or what they will find.
