**51 mililiter, karena 150 mililiter dikurangi 8 mililiter di permukaan dan 91 mililiter yang keluar.**

After 15 minutes, the water occupies three places: 8 millilitres remain on the surface, 91 millilitres have drained out, and the rest remains in the column. The stored amount is therefore <InlineMath math="150 - 8 - 91 = 51" /> millilitres. The 24-millilitre value belongs to minute five, not to the final state asked about.
