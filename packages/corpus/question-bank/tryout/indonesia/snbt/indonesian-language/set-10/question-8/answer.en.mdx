Use the state **at minute 15**. With no spill and evaporation ignored, the 150 mL is divided among surface water, stored water, and drainage.

Water that has entered:

<BlockMath math="150\,\mathrm{mL}-8\,\mathrm{mL}=142\,\mathrm{mL}" />

Subtract water that has already drained:

<BlockMath math="142\,\mathrm{mL}-91\,\mathrm{mL}=51\,\mathrm{mL}" />

Check the balance:

<BlockMath math="8\,\mathrm{mL}+51\,\mathrm{mL}+91\,\mathrm{mL}=150\,\mathrm{mL}" />

The stored amount is **51 mL**. The 24 mL figure is surface water at minute five, while 16 mL is the surface-water change between the readings. Neither is the amount remaining on the surface at minute 15.
