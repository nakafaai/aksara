**Peta Berlapis untuk Cahaya yang Berubah**

The title carries both central elements. “Layered map” points to Nara's way of preserving official data, observations, times, sources, and disagreements. “Changing light” captures the main problem: a lamp's presence on a map does not tell us whether a road is actually lit at a particular time. The other titles isolate one incident or claim a certainty that the story carefully avoids.
