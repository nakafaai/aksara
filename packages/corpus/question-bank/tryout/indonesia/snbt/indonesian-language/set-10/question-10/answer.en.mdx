**Membedakan Infiltrasi, Perkolasi, dan Penyimpanan Air**

Those three processes form the centre of the passage. The column data show why water entering, water draining, and water still stored must be tracked separately. The other titles repeat the very mistake the passage corrects or treat repacked columns as faithful copies of natural soil.
