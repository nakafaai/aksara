**Dalam setiap putaran yang memiliki iradiansi sama untuk semua oven, sudut 45 derajat unggul 2 derajat Celsius atas sudut 60 derajat, tetapi sudut di antara keduanya belum diuji.**

The gap between 45 and 60 degrees is exactly 2 degrees in every row. Against 30 degrees, the gaps are 8, 8, and 9 degrees. The 45-degree setting therefore leads consistently within matched rows. The data still say nothing about an untested angle such as 46 or 50 degrees, so they cannot identify the exact optimum.
