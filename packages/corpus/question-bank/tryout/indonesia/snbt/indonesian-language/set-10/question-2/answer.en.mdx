Compare **temperatures within the same run**. Each row calculates the 45-degree oven's temperature advantage over a comparison setting, in degrees Celsius.

| Irradiance | Versus 60 degrees | Versus 30 degrees | Versus no reflector |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 580 W/m² | <InlineMath math="58-56=2" /> | <InlineMath math="58-50=8" /> | <InlineMath math="58-45=13" /> |
| 650 W/m² | <InlineMath math="62-60=2" /> | <InlineMath math="62-54=8" /> | <InlineMath math="62-48=14" /> |
| 720 W/m² | <InlineMath math="66-64=2" /> | <InlineMath math="66-57=9" /> | <InlineMath math="66-51=15" /> |

The gap against 60 degrees is always 2 degrees Celsius. The no-reflector gap varies, so “always 13” is false. Angles of 46 or 50 degrees remain untested. The table identifies the highest tested setting, not a certain exact optimum.
