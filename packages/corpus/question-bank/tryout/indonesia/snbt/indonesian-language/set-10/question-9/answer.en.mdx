**Mencegah pembaca menyamakan air yang masuk dari permukaan dengan air yang telah bergerak sampai ke bawah kolom.**

This distinction directly corrects the student's error. All water has left the surface of the organic mixture, so infiltration has occurred. Much of it has not yet left the bottom, which means percolation through the full column has not produced as much drainage as in sand. The processes are connected, but their measured amounts are not identical.
