Distinguish **installation status from lighting experience**.

| Source | Recorded information | Interpretive limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June 1978 map | 24 installed and eight proposed lamps | Proposal is not installation, and installation does not guarantee nightly operation |
| Sari's July and August diary | Market lights on until about nine | Does not cover every route or the whole year |
| Banu's diary | Four dark nights after rain by the river despite a standing pole | Does not establish failure of every village lamp |
| Mina's diary | Brighter main road on weekly trips, while avoiding alleys | Experience of a particular route and time |

The sources can explain one another because their locations can be matched. “Inspection complete” does not turn the map into a record of nightly performance.
