**Peta menunjukkan status dan sebaran pemasangan, sedangkan buku harian menunjukkan bagaimana beberapa warga mengalami penerangan pada rute serta waktu tertentu.**

“Installed” on the map answers an administrative question about a pole or lamp's location. Banu's dark-night entry answers a different question about whether light was available when he walked after rain. The sources can be compared because they concern the same space, but they cannot be forced to act as if they recorded the same thing.
