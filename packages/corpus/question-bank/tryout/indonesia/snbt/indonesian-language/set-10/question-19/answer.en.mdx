**Menjelaskan mengapa tujuan administratif peta dan pengalaman terbatas para penulis harian menghasilkan bukti yang berbeda, bukan salah satunya otomatis keliru.**

The village office needed to record locations and installation status, so its map was not designed as a nightly performance report. Residents wrote from the routes and times they experienced, so their diaries do not cover the whole village. The perspective of each source determines what it can establish and where its coverage ends. Difference does not automatically mean error.
