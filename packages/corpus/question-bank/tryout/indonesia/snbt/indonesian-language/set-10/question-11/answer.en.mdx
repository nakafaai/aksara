The mobility and multiple-needs groups show clear gains. The sensory group changes very little, while communication and cognitive needs are not separated. The table also measures finding equipment, not using it. A child who locates an item may still be unable to use it. A sound summary must preserve the distinction between access to information and the play experience itself.
