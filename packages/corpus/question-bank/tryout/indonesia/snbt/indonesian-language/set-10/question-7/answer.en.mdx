Separate **water that entered from water that already drained**. Under the passage's assumptions, the organic mixture has this balance.

| Time | Surface water | Water that has entered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minute 5 | 5 mL | <InlineMath math="150-5=145\,\mathrm{mL}" /> |
| Minute 15 | 0 mL | <InlineMath math="150-0=150\,\mathrm{mL}" /> |

At minute 15, subtract bottom drainage to find stored water:

<BlockMath math="150\,\mathrm{mL}-62\,\mathrm{mL}=88\,\mathrm{mL}" />

Drainage of 62 mL does not mean that only 62 mL entered. Another 88 mL remains temporarily stored. These observations do not establish how much would remain after a longer period.
