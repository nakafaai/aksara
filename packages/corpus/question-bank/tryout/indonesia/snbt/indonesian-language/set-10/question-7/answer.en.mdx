**Repeating the model helps test whether pore size and particle arrangement can affect infiltration rate. Because the soil in the bottles has been disturbed and differs from natural layers, the pattern becomes a hypothesis for field observation.**

Columns with different materials make infiltration rates comparable. Natural soil structure, roots, and compaction are not fully represented, so the model result cannot be treated as a direct measurement of a real site.
