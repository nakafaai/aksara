**Hampir seluruh air sudah masuk setelah lima menit dan seluruhnya masuk setelah 15 menit, tetapi keluaran yang lebih kecil menunjukkan banyak air masih tersimpan dalam kolom.**

After five minutes, only 5 millilitres remain on the surface. After 15 minutes, none remains, so all 150 millilitres have infiltrated. With only 62 millilitres collected below, the remaining 88 millilitres are still in the column under the passage's assumptions. Low drainage therefore points to high retention here, not automatically to low infiltration.
