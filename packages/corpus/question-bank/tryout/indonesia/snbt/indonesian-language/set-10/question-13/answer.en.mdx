**Merancang ikon bersama kelompok yang belum terwakili, lalu mengukur keberhasilan menemukan alat dan pengalaman menggunakannya sebagai dua hasil terpisah.**

This step separates two questions that remain mixed. Co-design grounds the symbols in users' experience, including communication and cognitive needs. Separate measures then show whether the map helps people find equipment and whether that equipment can actually be used. A better map must not conceal a barrier in the equipment itself.
