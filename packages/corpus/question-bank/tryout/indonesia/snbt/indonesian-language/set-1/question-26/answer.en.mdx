The first paragraph describes daily life beside the sea. Sentence (1) places the reader in Werur, while sentences (2) to (5) add waves, coastal erosion, a seawall, and children playing nearby.

The passage gives no evidence that Werur is a tourist village or that the sea is a tourist attraction. The seawall project is present, but it is only one detail within the wider village setting.

Therefore, **perkampungan yang terletak di pesisir pantai**, a village located on the coast, is the best answer.
