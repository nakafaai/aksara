Sentence (1) contains two coordinated instructions: stop what you are doing and watch the sunset. A complete paraphrase must preserve **both** actions.

That is exactly what the option to **pause your activity** and **watch the sunset** expresses. The other choices keep only rest, reject concern for the surroundings, turn a brief pause into a permanent withdrawal, or introduce time calculation that is absent from the quotation.
