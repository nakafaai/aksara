A **compromise** is an agreement reached when the parties adjust their demands so that a dispute can be resolved, usually without violence. That matches **persetujuan yang dicapai melalui penyesuaian atau jalan damai**.

A compromise is an agreement reached through discussion and adjustment of demands. The two sides may still disagree on some points. This process differs from a vote or one side's complete surrender.

This agreement is the meaning used in sentence (6).
