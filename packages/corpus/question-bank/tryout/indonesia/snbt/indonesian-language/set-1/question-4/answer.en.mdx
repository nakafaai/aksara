The missing sentence must connect smoothly to sentence <InlineMath math="(22)" />:

> *Cuma fase saya saat itu datang lebih awal...*

Here, *cuma* means “only” or “the difference was merely that”. The next sentence contrasts the **timing** of Fei's experience with an experience that other people may also have.

That relationship becomes complete with **mungkin setiap orang akan mengalami fase ini juga**:

> Everyone may experience this phase too; only Fei experienced it earlier.

The other choices either repeat the idea of “earlier”, introduce an unrelated life metaphor, or do not provide the general statement that *cuma* needs to qualify.

Therefore, the best completion is **mungkin setiap orang akan mengalami fase ini juga**.
