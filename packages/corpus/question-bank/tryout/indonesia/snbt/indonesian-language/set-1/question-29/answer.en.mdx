The main setting should be the most specific place that contains the events in the passage. Sentence <InlineMath math="(1)" /> opens in Werur, and sentence <InlineMath math="(9)" /> locates that village within Bikar District, Tambrauw Regency, Southwest Papua.

Tambrauw and Southwest Papua are broader regions, while *pantai*, or beach, is too general. The seawall project is an activity at the location rather than the name of the setting.

Therefore, the main setting is **Kampung Pesisir Werur**. The fourth option is correct.
