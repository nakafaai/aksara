Sentence <InlineMath math="(10)" /> states that when pressure from human activity decreases, the environment gains room to recover. The answer must stay within that cautious claim.

Therefore, **lingkungan mendapat kesempatan untuk memulihkan diri** is correct. It describes an opportunity for recovery without promising that every environmental problem will disappear.

The other choices overstate or distort the passage:

- Returning **exactly** to past conditions is not stated.
- The disappearance of **all** air pollution is too absolute and covers only one type of environmental pressure.
- The certain creation of a new form of life is speculative.
- An uncontrolled Earth contradicts the recovery described in the passage.

The best answer is **lingkungan mendapat kesempatan untuk memulihkan diri**.
