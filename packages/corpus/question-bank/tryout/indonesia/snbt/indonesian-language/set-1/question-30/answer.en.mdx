Unlike Fera's profession, which the passage states directly, the narrator's profession must be inferred from what the narrator does. The narrator visits the festival, observes conditions in Werur, speaks with Fera as a source, and presents those findings in a field report.

That sequence of activities best matches the work of a reporter. Nothing in the passage suggests that the narrator teaches, leads the village or festival, or works as an extension officer.

Therefore, **Reporter**, the second option, is the strongest inference.
