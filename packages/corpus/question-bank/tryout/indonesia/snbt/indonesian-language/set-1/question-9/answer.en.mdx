Sentence <InlineMath math="(3)" /> defines the term directly. **Land-based debris** enters the sea through rivers or directly from coastal areas.

This definition concerns the route by which waste reaches the ocean. It does not mean every plastic item made on land, every nonorganic item, every hard-to-decompose material, or debris that merely washes ashore.

Therefore, the correct answer is **sampah yang masuk ke laut melalui sungai atau langsung dari pesisir**.
