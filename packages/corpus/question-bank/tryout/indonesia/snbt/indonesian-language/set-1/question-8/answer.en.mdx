The hypothetical removes only **terrestrial debris from Indonesia**. It does not remove debris from other countries, ships, or fisheries.

The conclusion most directly dependent on that source is sentence <InlineMath math="(8)" />: Indonesia is estimated to be the largest single source country for many long-floating terrestrial debris classes at Seychelles. If that input disappeared, this ranking would have to be recalculated.

The other options claim that all waste, all marine pollution, or seasonality would disappear. The passage provides no basis for those absolute conclusions.

Therefore, the answer is **Indonesia diperkirakan menjadi sumber tunggal terbesar sampah berbasis daratan di banyak lokasi Seychelles**.
