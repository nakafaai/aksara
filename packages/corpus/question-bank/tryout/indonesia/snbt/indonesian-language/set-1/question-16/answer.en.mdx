| Evidence in the passage | Connection to her film legacy |
| --- | --- |
| Sentence (2): AFI ranks Marilyn sixth among female American screen legends | A film institution recognizes her place in film history |
| Sentence (3): several of her films enter the National Film Registry | Those films are selected for preservation as part of American film heritage |

Choose the answer combining **AFI recognition and film preservation**. The alternatives describe events in Marilyn's life but do not provide these two indicators of her lasting place in film history.
