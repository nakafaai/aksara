A **denotative meaning** is the literal dictionary meaning of a word. In sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" />, *petuah* refers to a piece of good counsel or advice.

Among the choices, **nasihat** has that meaning. *Ilmu* means knowledge, *pengalaman* experience, *kesan* impression, and *perasaan* feeling.

Therefore, the correct answer is **nasihat**.
