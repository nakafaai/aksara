The capital letter in **Sekutu** matters. In the context of the Second World War, the word refers to the alliance of countries that fought the Axis powers, including Imperial Japan. It therefore names a union of several parties.

Among the available choices, **serikat** comes closest to that idea of a union or alliance. *Program* and *lembaga* do not fit the wartime context, while *negara* and *bangsa* are too narrow because each names a single political or social entity.

Therefore, the first option is correct.
