A factual sentence presents information that can be checked against evidence. Sentence (10) gives measurable data about Lake Semayang: an area of about <InlineMath math="13{,}000\,\text{ha}" />, meaning thirteen thousand hectares, and an average depth of about <InlineMath math="3.5\,\text{m}" />, meaning three and a half metres.

The other options have different functions:

- Sentence (1) is an invitation in a quotation.
- Sentence (3) interprets the quotation.
- Sentence (7) uses figurative description.
- Sentence (8) begins an imagined scene.

Therefore, the measurable fact is in **sentence (10)**.
