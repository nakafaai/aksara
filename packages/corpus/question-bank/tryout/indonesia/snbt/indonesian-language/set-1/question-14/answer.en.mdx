Literally, *berkubang* describes wallowing in mud or muddy water. Sentence <InlineMath math="(7)" /> uses the word figuratively in the phrase *berkubang dalam kemacetan*.

A person does not physically wallow in traffic. The image means being surrounded by and unable to escape an unpleasant traffic jam. That corresponds to **terjebak dalam keadaan yang tidak menyenangkan**.

The correct answer is therefore the fifth option.
