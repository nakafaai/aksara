A **demographic bonus** occurs when the productive-age population is proportionally larger than the dependent-age population. In common Indonesian demographic statistics, productive age covers <InlineMath math="15" /> to <InlineMath math="64" /> years.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(8)" /> links the term to the large number of people of productive age. That is why **banyak masyarakat Indonesia yang berusia produktif** is the closest option.

The other options name geography, agricultural products, an export ambition, or a possible economic result. None of them defines the population structure meant by *bonus demografi*.

Therefore, the correct answer is **banyak masyarakat Indonesia yang berusia produktif**.
