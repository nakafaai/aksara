A **demographic bonus** is an opportunity for economic growth arising from a large productive-age population relative to dependent age groups. The opportunity does not automatically become growth. Health, education, and access to work affect how it is used. In common Indonesian demographic statistics, productive age covers <InlineMath math="15" /> to <InlineMath math="64" /> years.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(8)" /> links the term to the large number of people of productive age. That is why **peluang pertumbuhan ekonomi karena besarnya proporsi penduduk usia produktif** is the closest option.

The other options name geography, agricultural products, an export ambition, or productivity without explaining the population’s age structure. They do not capture the relationship meant by *bonus demografi*.

Therefore, the correct answer is **peluang pertumbuhan ekonomi karena besarnya proporsi penduduk usia produktif**.
