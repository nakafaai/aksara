The question limits the evidence to the **first paragraph**. There, Fei leaves an established leadership role at a multinational company and enters community-based waste management, a field that is new to her and offers less certainty.

That action directly supports **berani mengambil risiko**: she is willing to accept uncertainty in order to pursue work she considers meaningful.

The other options are weaker:

- **Mengutamakan kenyamanan kerja** contradicts her decision to leave a secure position.
- **Sangat teliti dan ulet** may be positive traits, but the paragraph gives no evidence of them.
- **Sosok yang rapi** is not discussed.
- **Peduli terhadap kemanusiaan** becomes clearer later in the passage, but it is not the main trait established by the first paragraph.

Therefore, the best answer is **berani mengambil risiko**.
