Sentences <InlineMath math="(16)" /> to <InlineMath math="(18)" /> describe David Conover's role precisely. He photographed Norma Jeane at the Radioplane factory and suggested that she try modeling. The passage then links that suggestion to her modeling work and first film contract.

He did not cast her directly in a leading role, become her guardian, arrange her marriage, or teach at the Actors Studio.

Therefore, the correct answer is **memotretnya di pabrik dan menyarankan agar ia mencoba menjadi model**.
