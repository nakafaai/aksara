The lexical meaning of *senja* is the half-dark period **after sunset** and before night. The definition names a time of day. Colour and mood can appear as associations in other contexts.

That makes **waktu setengah gelap setelah matahari terbenam** the exact answer. “When the sun touches the horizon” describes sunset itself, while “calm and peaceful” is only an association that twilight may evoke.

Therefore, the fourth option is correct.
