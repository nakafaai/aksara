The phrase *cara pandang ini* means “this way of viewing the issue”. To find its referent, look for the complete idea immediately before it.

Sentence (10) presents that idea: war is a last resort, and its high cost pushes both sides to avoid it. Sentences (11) and (12) develop the consequence, while sentence (13) names the perspective.

Therefore, the phrase primarily refers to **sentence (10)**.
