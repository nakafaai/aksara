Unit (2) reads *Portraits of national figures too.* It is a noun phrase because it names additional portraits and contains no finite verb. It cannot stand as an independent clause.

The surrounding units each form a complete statement with a subject and a finite predicate. Therefore, the incomplete unit is **sentence (2)**.
