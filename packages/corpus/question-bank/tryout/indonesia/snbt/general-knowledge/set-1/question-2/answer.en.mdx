In sentence (9), *awake* means **not sleeping**. The opposite is being asleep, so the matching option is **fast asleep**. This describes a state of sleep, rather than the action of falling asleep.

*Woken up* also indicates wakefulness. *Cared for*, *maintained*, and *protected* concern care or protection, not sleeping.
