The core of a sentence keeps the subject and what is said about that subject, while omitting details that can be removed without losing the central statement.

In sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" />, *Visitors* is the subject and *were fascinated* is the predicate. The phrase *by the beauty of the grass covered in clear ice crystals* explains what fascinated them.

The shortest complete statement that preserves the core information is **Visitors were fascinated.**
