A sound conclusion combines the passage's central claims without turning a possibility into a proven fact.

The passage establishes three points:

- the sleeping brain remains active through different sleep stages;
- waking experiences and memory processing can contribute to dream content;
- the exact function of dreams is still being investigated.

The incorrect options make claims that are either too narrow or contradicted by the passage. Animal sleep does not reveal the exact content of an animal's subjective experience, dreams are not exact replays, and dreaming is not limited to a completely inactive brain outside REM sleep.

Therefore, the best conclusion is that **dreams draw on sleeping-brain activity and fragments of waking experience, but their precise function remains under study**.
