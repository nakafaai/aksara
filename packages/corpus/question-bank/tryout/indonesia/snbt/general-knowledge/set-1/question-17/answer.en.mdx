The passage says that Mardanu's uncle *fell in the struggle for independence*. In this context, *fell* does not describe losing one's balance. It is a euphemistic and more respectful way to say that a fighter died in battle.

The other options name people or groups directly. They do not soften an unpleasant idea. Therefore, **fell** is the expression whose connotation has been elevated.
