The inappropriate word occurs in sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" />:

> An international team of research analyzed viral DNA from those remains.

*Research* names the activity or body of study rather than the people who form the team. The phrase needs a plural noun for the members who performed the analysis:

> An international team of researchers analyzed viral DNA from those remains.

The words *recovered*, *date*, *samples*, and *circulated* all fit their scientific contexts. Therefore, the incorrect word is **research**, which should be **researchers**.
