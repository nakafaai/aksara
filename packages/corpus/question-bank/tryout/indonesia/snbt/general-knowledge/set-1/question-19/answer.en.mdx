Personification gives a nonhuman thing a human action or quality. Sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" /> says that *ten heavy shells would dance into the sky*.

Shells can travel, rise, or streak through the air, but they do not literally dance. The human action *dance* makes the shells appear animated and vivid. Therefore, the personification occurs in **sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" />**.
