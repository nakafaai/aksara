The noun phrase *local residents* in sentence (6) consists of the attributive adjective *local* followed by the head noun *residents*.

Only *high plateau* has the same adjective-plus-noun pattern: *high* modifies the head noun *plateau*. *Dew phenomenon* uses a noun as a modifier, *expanse of grass* contains a prepositional complement, and *frost* is a single noun. *Still located* is an adverb-plus-participle construction.

Therefore, the matching phrase is **high plateau**.
