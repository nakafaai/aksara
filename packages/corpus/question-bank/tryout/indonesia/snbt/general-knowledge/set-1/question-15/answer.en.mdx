The noun phrase *local residents* in sentence <InlineMath math="(6)" /> consists of the attributive adjective *local* followed by the head noun *residents*.

Only *high plateau* has the same adjective-plus-noun pattern: *high* modifies the head noun *plateau*. The other nominal options use a noun as a modifier or contain a prepositional complement, while *still located* is an adverb-plus-participle construction.

Therefore, the matching phrase is **high plateau**.
