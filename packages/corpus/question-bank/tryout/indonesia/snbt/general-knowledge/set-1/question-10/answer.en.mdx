In sentence <InlineMath math="(1)" />, *increased* means that participation became greater. Its direct opposite in the same grammatical form is *decreased*, meaning that participation became smaller.

*Growth*, *climbing*, and *rising* all describe an upward movement. *Progressive* also describes development or change. The opposite direction is expressed by **decreased**, so that is the correct antonym.
