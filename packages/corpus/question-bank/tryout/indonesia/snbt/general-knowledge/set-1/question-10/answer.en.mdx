In sentence <InlineMath math="(1)" />, *increased* means that participation became greater. Its direct opposite in the same grammatical form is *decreased*, meaning that participation became smaller.

*Growth*, *climbing*, and *rising* all point upward rather than downward. *Progressive* describes development or change, not the opposite direction. Therefore, the correct antonym is **decreased**.
