Sentence (7) follows the pattern Adverbial, Subject, Verb, Object:

- Adverbial: *On Saturday, July 25*
- Subject: *the number of visitors*
- Verb: *reached*
- Object: *<InlineMath math="1{,}200" />*

The sentence about air temperature has the same order:

- Adverbial: *After sunrise*
- Subject: *the air temperature*
- Verb: *reached*
- Object: *five degrees*

The verb *reached* takes an object naming the amount or value attained. The other choices begin with a subject or use *formed* followed by a phrase of place rather than an object. Therefore, the matching sentence is **After sunrise, the air temperature reached five degrees.**
