In sentence <InlineMath math="(4)" />, *confusion* describes a situation in which people cannot clearly distinguish reliable guidance from myths. The closest meaning is therefore **lack of clarity**.

*Certainty* and *uniformity* suggest clear and orderly information. *Timeliness* concerns when something happens, and *diversity* concerns variety. Neither describes unclear or conflicting information.
