In this passage, *evolutionary history* describes how related variola lineages changed and diverged across generations. The ancient genomes help researchers place an extinct sister clade in that history.

Evolution does not mean one dramatic event, rapid growth, or growth alone. It also need not proceed at one constant speed. In this scientific context, the most precise choice is **change in viral lineages across generations**.
