In sentence (8), *restricted* is a past participle used after the linking verb *remained*. It describes the continuing state of access. The sentence does not present *restricted* as a new action.

The same function appears in *The gate remained locked after sunset*: *locked* describes the state of the gate after *remained*.

- *locked* after *the guard* is a finite past-tense verb;
- *coldest* is a superlative adjective;
- *waiting* forms a progressive verb phrase;
- *read* belongs to a passive event with *was*.

Therefore, the matching word is **locked** in the first sentence.
