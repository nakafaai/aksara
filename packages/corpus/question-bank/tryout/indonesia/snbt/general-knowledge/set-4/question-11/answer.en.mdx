Digital retains an advantage for some groups, but not all transaction sizes. Signal and device barriers also remain.

The data therefore support **limited expansion, not mandatory digital payment for every transaction**. This recognises benefits without excluding other users. “Always faster” is too broad, while removing digital ignores the groups that benefit.
