One name serves administration, while the others retain local history and usage. The names need not have identical functions.

The conclusion is that **standardisation and preservation can coexist when name functions are distinguished**. The word *can* and the condition preserve the claim's limits. The primary index and revision rule are individual details rather than the combined conclusion.
