A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **Orderly mapping can use one official index while retaining other names with their evidence and context**, supported by the finding that **Signs, land letters, interviews, and coordinates show that all three names refer to the same location**.

**Coordinates show that one name actually refers to a different spring several kilometres away** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
