*Proportionate* means appropriate to the scale involved. **Increasing the sample and resources in line with the claim's scope** connects the effort to the reach of the intended conclusion.

Saving time and energy shows efficiency. A large effect concerns significance, a small added benefit concerns marginal benefit, and assessing several dimensions concerns breadth. Those choices do not directly express proportional allocation.
