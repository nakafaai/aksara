The team chooses one administrative name for official documents while retaining two others with their sources and contexts. Therefore, **choosing a primary name does not require deleting other names with a record of use**.

Alias storage does not automatically confer equal official status. Deleting local names or equating all legal statuses changes the decision's limits. Naming the primary index alone reports only one part of the decision.
