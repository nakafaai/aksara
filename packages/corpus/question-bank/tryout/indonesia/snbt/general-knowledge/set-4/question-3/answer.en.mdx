**Adjusting field procedures when the weather changes while preserving the main goal and measurement rules** explicitly meets both requirements: a response to new conditions and a retained objective.

Several implementation paths show flexibility. Abandoning the main goal after a complaint violates the requirement to preserve it. Maintenance before failure is preventive, and a planned collection sequence is systematic.
