A time comparison requires **equivalent measurement boundaries**. The correct additional finding uses different starting points.

| Payment method | Timing starts | Effect on the work counted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital | After the items have been scanned | Scanning time is excluded |
| Cash | When the first item is placed down | Time before and during scanning is included |

Digital can therefore appear faster because less work is being timed. Matching item counts alone does not fix unequal starting points. A new equivalent comparison that still favours digital would add support. Clearer signs with unchanged transaction times do not demonstrate a measurement error, while signal complaints and the next test are already mentioned in the passage.
