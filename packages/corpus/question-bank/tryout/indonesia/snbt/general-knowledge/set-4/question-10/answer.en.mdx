The comparison requires equivalent timing boundaries. **Starting digital timing after scanning but cash timing when the first item is placed down** measures different amounts of work.

That can make digital appear faster without establishing a real advantage. Matching item counts alone does not fix unequal timing boundaries. A new matched comparison still favouring digital adds support, while clearer signs do not explain this measurement difference.
