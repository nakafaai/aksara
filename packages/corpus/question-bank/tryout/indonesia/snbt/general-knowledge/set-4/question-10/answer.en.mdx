A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **Digital payment benefits must be judged with comparable data and user access, so adding it does not automatically justify removing cash**, supported by the finding that **After the data were separated, the time advantage nearly disappeared for transactions with many items**.

**Digital timing began after scanning, while cash timing began when the first item was placed down** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
