The first trial did not separate the poster's effect from volunteers and driver guidance. The next trial balanced those factors and found that the message worked when the majority figure was current.

The main point is therefore **that norm messages can influence behaviour, but their effects require testing and their claims must be accurate**. Observers and planned figures are details. The text neither guarantees change in every passenger nor equates complaints with all conversations.
