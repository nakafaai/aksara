Early notes from the student language exchange suggested that some users hesitated before the next step. The team did not assume a cause. It first counted the conversations lasting at least ten minutes each day and gathered short user comments over seven days.

The early evidence did not establish a cause, but it pointed to a testable idea. The team selected topic cards for starting a conversation as a small intervention. On other days, the old process remained in place, creating a comparison based on records rather than memory.

The daily figures were not read in isolation. A baseline of 19 was compared with 27 on trial days and 21 without the change. User responses helped explain the pattern but did not eliminate other explanations.

The available evidence supported further investigation, not unconditional adoption. Observation time was limited and the visitor mix varied. A longer repetition with the same measurement method was needed before a broader claim.
