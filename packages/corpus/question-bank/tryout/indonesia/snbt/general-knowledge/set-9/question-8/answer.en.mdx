The two trials differ in both the conditions they compare and what they count:

| Part of the investigation | Evidence and role |
| --- | --- |
| First trial | Complaints fall, but volunteer presence and driver guidance also differ. The poster's effect is not isolated. |
| Second trial | Volunteer schedules and guidance are balanced. Observers count loud conversations directly. This tests the proposed effect while controlling those other factors. |
| Final interpretation | The effect is found only with current majority figures. Accurate claims therefore limit how the message should be used. |

The progression is **hypothesis, controlled test, then limits on application**. Anonymous observers belong to the second trial. The text does not guarantee change in every passenger or treat complaints as a count of all conversations.
