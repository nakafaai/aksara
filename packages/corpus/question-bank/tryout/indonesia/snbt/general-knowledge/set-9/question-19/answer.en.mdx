The third paragraph distinguishes two tasks. Chronology orders events by time, while periodisation selects stage boundaries to explain change.

The paraphrase is **that periodisation chooses boundaries for an analytical purpose and chronology establishes time order**. Neither ignores later events nor always chooses the earliest date. The bridge replacement is an example, not a definition of the two concepts.
