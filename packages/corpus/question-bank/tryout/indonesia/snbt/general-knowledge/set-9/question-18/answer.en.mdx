The year 1912 can be accurate for the start of construction but not for opening or inauguration. Without the event type, a correct number can suggest an inaccurate account.

Thus, **numerical precision does not guarantee an accurate historical explanation**. This neither rejects facts nor favours the earliest date for every purpose. The 1958 replacement and timeline plan are supporting details.
