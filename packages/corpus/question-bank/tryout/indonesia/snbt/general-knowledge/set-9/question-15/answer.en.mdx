The years 1912, 1914, and 1916 refer to different events. The 1958 replacement adds another major change. The team keeps the dates together with their meanings.

The main point is **to tie historical dates to the events they denote**. The central-span replacement and timeline display are details. Multiple dates do not make every fact unknowable or the earliest date always appropriate.
