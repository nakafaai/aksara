The opening shows that construction, use, and inauguration have different dates. One unexplained number creates ambiguity.

The later section distinguishes chronology from periodisation and uses that distinction to improve the plaque. Thus, **the date problem is addressed through a conceptual distinction and a more accurate label**. The text neither rejects every fact nor makes the earliest date universally appropriate.
