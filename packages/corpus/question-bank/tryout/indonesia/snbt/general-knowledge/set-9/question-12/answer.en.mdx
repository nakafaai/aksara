A sound conclusion follows the evidence without adding new certainty. The passage shows that **In the first trial, buses with the new message also had more volunteer presence and drivers with extra guidance** and develops the central point that **Descriptive-norm messages can change behaviour, but their effect must be separated from other factors and their claims must be accurate**.

Those ideas support the conclusion **The initial fall in complaints was insufficient to prove the message mechanism because other treatments also differed**. This wording remains within the passage's conditions. Universal claims and reversed causal relationships are unsupported.
