*Marginal benefit* is the **gain from one additional unit**. The correct choice describes that unit adding a small amount to the benefit already obtained. The gain is small in this example, but *marginal* does not require every additional gain to be small.

| Quantity | What is counted? |
| --- | --- |
| Total benefit | The benefit accumulated from all units. |
| Marginal benefit | The increase when one more unit is added. |

A large total can therefore coexist with a small final addition. The other choices concern overall impact, proportional resources, or efficiency. None identifies the benefit contributed by one additional unit.
