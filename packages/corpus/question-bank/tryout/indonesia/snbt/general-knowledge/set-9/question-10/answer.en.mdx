An accurate paraphrase preserves both content and limits. The passage's central point is **Descriptive-norm messages can change behaviour, but their effect must be separated from other factors and their claims must be accurate**. **The force of a norm message depends on a credible picture of common behaviour, not merely an impressive-sounding number** restates that principle while retaining its relationship and conditions.

The wording neither removes an important qualification nor expands the claim into a universal rule. Its meaning therefore matches the writer's statement despite different wording.
