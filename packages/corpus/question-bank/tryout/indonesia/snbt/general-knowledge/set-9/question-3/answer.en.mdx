**Several implementation paths subject to the same outcome criteria** allow different methods without changing the goal. This is the form of flexibility requested.

Pausing and resuming an unchanged procedure provides no alternative method. Revision after complaints responds to a problem but does not specify multiple paths under fixed criteria. Group participation and coding rules concern inclusion and consistency.
