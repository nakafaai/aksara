A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **Descriptive-norm messages can change behaviour, but their effect must be separated from other factors and their claims must be accurate**, supported by the finding that **In the first trial, buses with the new message also had more volunteer presence and drivers with extra guidance**.

**Complete recordings show no change in loud conversations; complaints fell only because reporting channels failed on buses with the new poster** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
