*Periodisation* means **dividing time into stages to explain particular changes**. The structural change makes 1958 important for engineering history, while 1914 matters for the history of use.

Chronology only orders events. Choosing the earliest date for every purpose, forcing sharp boundaries, or supplying unsupported precision does not serve this analytical function.
