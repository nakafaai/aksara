**Repeated measurements staying within a narrow range at different times** demonstrate stability over the observation period. The relevant property is constancy of the values.

Coding rules and planned steps organise procedures without proving stable results. Simultaneous sensors concern timing, while multiple implementation paths concern flexibility.
