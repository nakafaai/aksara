A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **The museum improves the reliability of the account by distinguishing records, memories, and uncertainty rather than forcing one version to win**, supported by the finding that **The inventory book says evening market, while four former traders remember the main activity starting in the morning**.

**A newly found dated photograph clearly records the trading time and resolves the conflict between the two sources** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
