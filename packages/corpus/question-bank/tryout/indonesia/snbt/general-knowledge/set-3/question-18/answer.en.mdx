The literal version causes misunderstandings, while familiar wording and clearer order lead to more accurate route and time choices. The team still requires meaning, danger level, and action to remain intact.

Therefore, **a communicative translation can be more accurate than a literal one when those essential elements are preserved**. This does not guarantee success in every region. The options about message order and retesting report details rather than the overall conclusion.
