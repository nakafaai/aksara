An *equivalent* is **an expression in the target language with comparable meaning and function**. The team seeks familiar terms while preserving directions, deadlines, danger levels, and required actions.

Communicative function matters, so spelling and word order need not be identical. Removing instructions, adding a warning absent from the source, or providing pronunciation alone does not meet that purpose.
