A *consistent* procedure applies the same principles without contradiction. **Using the same coding rule for every group and measurement period** directly demonstrates that quality.

Similar final values do not prove that the procedure was the same. A planned sequence is systematic, observations provide an empirical basis, and ordering events by time is chronological.
