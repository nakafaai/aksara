*Cross-checking* means **comparing independent sources before settling a conclusion**. The museum compares the photograph, shadows, permit, and witness accounts so that one source can be checked against another.

Rechecking a single source or making the wording uniform adds no independent evidence. Giving all sources equal weight regardless of context also ignores the museum's concern with how each account was produced.
