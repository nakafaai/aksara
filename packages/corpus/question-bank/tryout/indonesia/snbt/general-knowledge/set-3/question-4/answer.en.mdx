*Explicit* information is stated directly. **Naming the assumptions, data limits, and decision criteria in the report** lets readers identify the decision's basis without inferring it for themselves.

Providing raw data alone does not explain that basis. Inferring the writer's position from details concerns implicit meaning. Checking provenance and assessing consequences have different purposes.
