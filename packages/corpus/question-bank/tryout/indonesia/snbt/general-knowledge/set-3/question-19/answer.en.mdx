An accurate paraphrase preserves both content and limits. The passage's central point is **An emergency message must preserve meaning and action in language residents understand, not merely copy official word order**. **Message accuracy depends on preserving intent and action, not on word-for-word sameness** restates that principle while retaining its relationship and conditions.
