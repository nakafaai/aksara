The final paragraph judges accuracy by preserved meaning, danger level, and action rather than matching words. The correct paraphrase is **that accuracy depends on preserved intent and action, not word-for-word sameness**.

Changing the danger level to make the words easier to remember violates that requirement. Mandatory word matching and guaranteed suitability everywhere are also unsupported. Placing action first is one revision step, not a paraphrase of the principle.
