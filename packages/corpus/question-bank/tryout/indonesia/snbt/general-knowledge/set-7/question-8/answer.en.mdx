The first two paragraphs use meeting records to reveal **participation barriers**. The final two respond with additional routes and checks on representation.

| Initial finding | Organisers’ response | What still needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Some residents leave before the decision because of transport | Daytime and evening meetings plus transport | Participant origins and reasons for absence |
| A Deaf participant has no sign-language interpreter | Interpretation and several ways to submit proposals | Each group’s opportunity to be considered |

The passage neither proves all groups are already represented nor compares fares to find the cheapest service. The organisers neither accept all proposals nor restrict decisions to nearby areas. Access improvements remain subject to checking rather than being treated as automatically successful.
