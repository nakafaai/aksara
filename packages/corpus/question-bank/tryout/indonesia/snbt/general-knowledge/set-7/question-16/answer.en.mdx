A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **A responsible adaptation may change a story's form while keeping its sources, changes, and diversity traceable**, supported by the finding that **Three oral recordings differ in characters, sequence, and ending**.

**An older dated document is found showing that all three recordings copied one identical script without community variation** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
