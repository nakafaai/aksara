In the passage, *adaptation* means **the reshaping of a work into a new form with changes suited to its purpose and medium**. Adaptation reshapes a work for a new purpose and medium while retaining a traceable relationship to its sources. Copying, title borrowing, unsourced blending, and purposeless shortening omit essential parts of that process.
