*Adaptation* is **reshaping a work to suit a new form, purpose, and medium**. The story becomes staged scenes, with the introduction and transitions revised after audience feedback.

Word-for-word copying does not make that transformation. Title borrowing, unsourced blending, and purposeless shortening also omit the relationship between source and process described here.
