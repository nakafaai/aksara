The first two paragraphs present diverse sources and a framing scene that displays their differences. The final two use **audience feedback** to refine the introduction and transitions, then explain documentation of sources and changes.

The design therefore responds to source diversity, while testing helps refine its presentation. Programme notes make the changes traceable. The group neither declares the written script original, hides sources, demands word-for-word copying, nor selects a version by majority vote.
