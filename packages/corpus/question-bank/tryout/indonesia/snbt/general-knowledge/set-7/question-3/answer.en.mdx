*Comprehensive* covers the dimensions important to a study's purpose. **Benefits, risks, costs, and effects on different groups** are considered together before a recommendation.

Group involvement focuses on who participates. Interacting factors, process transparency, and work sequence matter too, but do not cover the full range of evaluative dimensions in the correct choice.
