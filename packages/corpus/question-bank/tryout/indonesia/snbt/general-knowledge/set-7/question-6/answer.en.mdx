In the passage, *inclusive* means **providing practical opportunity to groups with different needs and barriers**. Inclusive asks whether groups facing different conditions have a genuinely usable route to participate. A uniform invitation, acceptance of every proposal, majority control, and attendance counts do not test practical access.
