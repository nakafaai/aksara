**Inspecting equipment and replacing worn parts before failure** meets the timing requirement of prevention. Maintenance addresses foreseeable damage before it occurs.

Revision after complaints responds to an identified problem. Weather adjustments, a collection sequence, and stable values do not explicitly describe preventing a failure before it happens.
