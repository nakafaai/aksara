The paragraph distinguishes a route for contributions to be considered from a guarantee of acceptance. Its paraphrase is **workable participation for different groups without guaranteeing every demand succeeds**.

Limiting contributions to in-person speech removes writing and representation. Open invitations or speaker numbers do not by themselves express that distinction.
