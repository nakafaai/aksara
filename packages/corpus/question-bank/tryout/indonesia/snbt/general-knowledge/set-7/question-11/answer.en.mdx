A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **Inclusive participation requires reducing practical barriers and examining representation, not merely issuing an open invitation**, supported by the finding that **Hill residents had to leave before the decision and the Deaf participant had no sign-language interpreter**.

**New data show that hill residents chose not to attend even though equivalent schedules, transport, information, and communication support had existed from the start** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
