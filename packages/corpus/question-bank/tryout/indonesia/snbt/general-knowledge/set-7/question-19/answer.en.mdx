The passage's central point is **A responsible adaptation may change a story's form while keeping its sources, changes, and diversity traceable**. **Changes can be acceptable when their origins and reasons are open and source diversity is not concealed** restates that principle while retaining its relationship and conditions.

The wording neither removes an important qualification nor expands the claim into a universal rule. Its meaning therefore matches the writer's statement despite different wording.
