The final sentence requires traceable changes, visible source diversity, and a coherent theatrical experience. **Acceptable change with open origins and reasons, visible diversity, and coherence** preserves all three.

Matching one script's words alone removes those conditions. Rejecting all change or ignoring sources also reverses the sentence's meaning.
