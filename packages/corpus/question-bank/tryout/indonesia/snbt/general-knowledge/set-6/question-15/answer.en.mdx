The second paragraph values traces from several generations. The third reports that the oldest paint was found in only a few rooms. Failure to find it elsewhere does not establish that it was never present there.

Together, they support the inference that uniformity can obscure history when it **conceals evidenced changes**. Sound timber concerns safety rather than directly establishing colour history. Mandatory uniform colour and retaining all material contradict these limits.
