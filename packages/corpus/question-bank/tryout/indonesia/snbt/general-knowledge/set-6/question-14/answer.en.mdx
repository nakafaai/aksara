The second paragraph mentions materials, techniques, changing uses, and care by several generations. Thus, **authenticity can include change across periods rather than imitation of one historical stage**.

Removing every later trace of care would erase some of that evidence. One colour, unsafe retention, and the paint finding in a few rooms do not express the requested account across periods.
