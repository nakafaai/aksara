A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **A deposit can reduce waste, but success must be judged alongside service access and costs for different groups**, supported by the finding that **After desk access improved, the return-rate gap across the site narrowed**.

**The waste reduction resulted from a simultaneous ban on single-use containers** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
