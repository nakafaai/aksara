The question tests whether the deposit caused the waste reduction. **A simultaneous single-use-container ban that actually caused the decline** supplies a direct alternative cause.

This does not prove the deposit had no influence. It means the decline alone no longer measures that influence. Continued high returns support the system, while costs, desk changes, and waiting times concern other service features.
