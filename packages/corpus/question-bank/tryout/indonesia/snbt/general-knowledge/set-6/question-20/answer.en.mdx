A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **Authentic restoration balances safety, material evidence, and visible change rather than merely making a building look new or old**, supported by the finding that **Mapping and samples show that some timber requires replacement while much can be strengthened**.

**New testing shows that all supposedly old material came from an undocumented modern renovation** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
