The question tests the assumed origins of materials attributed to several earlier periods. **If all were installed in one modern renovation**, they are not direct material evidence from those assumed periods.

Their dating and interpretation need revision. This does not mean modern renovation has no historical value or that all material should be discarded. Evidence confirming several periods adds support, while marker colours and documentation do not change provenance.
