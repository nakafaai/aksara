The problem is how to repair the hall without erasing history or ignoring danger. Investigation identifies what needs replacement and what can be strengthened.

The main point is **to balance safety, material evidence, and legible change in authentic restoration**. A paint layer and documentation of new parts are details. Retaining everything or imposing one colour ignores the evidence-based decision.
