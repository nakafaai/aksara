Evidence is an observed or recorded fact that can be used to test an evaluation. **Mapping and samples show that some timber requires replacement while much can be strengthened** reports a concrete finding from the passage.

**Authentic restoration balances safety, material evidence, and visible change rather than merely making a building look new or old** is an interpretation of the information. An option describing a plan remains a proposed action. The concrete finding therefore answers a question specifically asking for evidence.
