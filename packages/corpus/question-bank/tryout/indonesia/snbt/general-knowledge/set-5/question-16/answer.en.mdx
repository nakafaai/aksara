A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **A late-return policy should be judged by book availability and should distinguish levels of delay and user access**, supported by the finding that **The limited trial showed that reminders reduced brief delays and restrictions accelerated some long-overdue returns**.

**The decline in delays occurred because the library closed most lending services during the trial** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
