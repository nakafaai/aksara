**Involving groups with different mobility, language, and access needs** gives diverse users a part in the evaluation. That directly demonstrates inclusion.

Total costs and average benefits can hide different needs. Responding to a complaint, allowing several methods, or publishing data does not by itself show the participation of those groups.
