The second proposal is called more proportional because it distinguishes brief delays from those that harm other users. The matching paraphrase is **that consequences are matched to a delay's length and impact**.

Always using the same consequence removes that distinction. Revenue, phone numbers, and eliminating every fine do not express the reason requested.
