*Reconstruction* means **rebuilding an account of the past from available traces**. The team combines timetables, photographs, room-use records, and interviews to understand the move's stages.

The result remains open to reassessment. Copying one memory, adding concealed fiction, or merely listing dates does not perform that source comparison. Physical repair has a different purpose.
