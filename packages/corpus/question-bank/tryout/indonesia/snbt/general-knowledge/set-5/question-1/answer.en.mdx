*Significant* here means important to a practical decision. **A change that alters the decision even after measurement uncertainty is considered** meets that meaning.

A small change within the uncertainty range does not suffice. Added benefit, resource allocation, and checking an assumption have different focuses. The question concerns practical importance, not a statistical significance test.
