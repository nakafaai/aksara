The interpretation says classes moved in stages. **A verified letter from that period showing that every class moved on the same day** directly challenges it.

The letter should be assessed alongside other sources rather than used to discard them automatically. Separate move and opening dates remain compatible with distinct events. Caption placement and recording preservation do not establish whether all classes moved together.
