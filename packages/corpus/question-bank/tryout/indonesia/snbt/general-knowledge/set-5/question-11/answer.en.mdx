A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **Responsible historical reconstruction compares sources, explains viewpoints, and preserves originals for reassessment**, supported by the finding that **Timetables, photographs, room letters, and additional interviews show that the move occurred in stages**.

**A newly dated letter shows that every class actually moved on the same day** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
