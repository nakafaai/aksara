The issue combines delays of different lengths and impacts with users who miss reminders. The library judges the policy by whether books become available again.

The main point is **to distinguish delay levels and user access and assess success through availability**. Phone numbers and expansion plans are details. The trial does not prove all fines must disappear, and revenue is explicitly rejected as the success measure.
