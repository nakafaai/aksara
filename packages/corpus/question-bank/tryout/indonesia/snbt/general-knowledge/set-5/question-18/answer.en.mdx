A sound conclusion follows the evidence without adding new certainty. The passage shows that **The limited trial showed that reminders reduced brief delays and restrictions accelerated some long-overdue returns** and develops the central point that **A late-return policy should be judged by book availability and should distinguish levels of delay and user access**.

Those ideas support the conclusion **Graduated restrictions and reminders may be fairer, but communication channels and exceptions still require testing**. This wording remains within the passage's conditions. Universal claims and reversed causal relationships are unsupported.
