User corrections improve search quality but occur more often in popular collections. Concentrating their benefits there can widen differences in retrieval ease.

The inference is **that popular collections may become easier to find because they receive more corrections**. This is a possibility, not certainty for every document. Correction frequency and the audit plan are direct details, while stopping search and proving absence are unsupported.
