**Spikes occurring irregularly without fixed intervals** demonstrate sporadic occurrence. Rarity alone is insufficient when events still follow a schedule.

Simultaneous recording concerns shared timing. Chronological order, stable values, and systematic steps do not describe the requested irregular occurrence.
