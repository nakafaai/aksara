The hypothesis concerns systematic performance differences between document groups. **A complete audit finding identical accuracy and retrieval rates across all groups** directly weakens it.

Identical performance need not be perfect. Original images, zero-result review, and corrections may remain necessary. Missed documents and uneven corrections support the initial concern, while an audit plan is not a test result.
