Recognition errors can keep stored documents out of results. The passage even provides collection-number and date searches for unreadable text.

Therefore, **zero results are insufficient to prove a source absent**. This does not guarantee a hidden document behind every empty search. It limits the conclusion that can be drawn and supports further inspection.
