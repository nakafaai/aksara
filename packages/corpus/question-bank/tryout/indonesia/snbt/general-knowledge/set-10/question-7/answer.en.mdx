*Traceability* is **the ability to follow a product's origin, movement, and handling through records**. Those records connect ingredients and production stages to products requiring inspection.

One place name, a price, and alphabetical order do not provide that chain. Traceability helps identify faulty products, so it does not guarantee that every product is high quality.
