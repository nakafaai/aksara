The final paragraph defines traceability as following a product's journey and requires updating throughout the supply chain.

The paraphrase is **connecting the product to an updated record of that journey**. One location or the final distributor's address is insufficient. Codes do not remove updating, and printing every stage on the front is not part of the definition.
