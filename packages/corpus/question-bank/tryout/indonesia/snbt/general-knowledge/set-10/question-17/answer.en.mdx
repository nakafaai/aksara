Automated search broadens access, but some writing and spellings are harder to recognise. The team improves retrieval and provides inspection and correction.

The main point is **that automation is useful when its output remains a limited, inspectable, correctable index**. Popular-collection corrections and audits are details. The text neither ends all automation nor treats zero results as certain absence.
