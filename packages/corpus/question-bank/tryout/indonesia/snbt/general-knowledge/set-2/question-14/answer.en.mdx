The phrase *insect stings* combines two nouns: *insect* identifies the source, and *stings* names the events or injuries. Its pattern is therefore **noun + noun**.

The choices follow these patterns:

- *rapid changes*: adjective + noun
- *careful treatment*: adjective + noun
- *singing loudly*: verb form + adverb
- *bird calls*: noun + noun
- *gentle washing*: adjective + noun

Therefore, **bird calls** has the same word-class pattern.
