Hyperbole deliberately exaggerates beyond literal reality to intensify an impression.

Sentence <InlineMath math="(4)" /> says that the spark in Ben's eyes could light the entire shop. Eyes cannot literally illuminate a building. The exaggeration conveys how visibly excited and energized he looked, so sentence <InlineMath math="(4)" /> is the clear example of hyperbole.
