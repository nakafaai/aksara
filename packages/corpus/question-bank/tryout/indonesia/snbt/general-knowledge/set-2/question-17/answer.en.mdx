A complete declarative sentence needs an independent clause with a subject and a finite verb.

Expression <InlineMath math="(6)" />, *the weeks that followed*, is only a noun phrase. *The weeks* is its head, while *that followed* merely modifies that noun. Because the expression has no independent predicate, it cannot stand as a complete sentence. The fourth choice is correct.
