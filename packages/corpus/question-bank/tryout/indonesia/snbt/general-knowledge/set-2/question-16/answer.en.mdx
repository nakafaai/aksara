Writers can soften an uncomfortable description by choosing a milder expression. The meaning remains understandable, but the wording avoids sounding needlessly harsh.

**His frame had grown leaner** describes Ben's weight loss more gently than a blunt expression such as *he had become very skinny* or *he looked emaciated*. The other choices report hair, skin, eye, or grooming details directly. Therefore, the fourth choice is correct.
