The prefix *mal-* can indicate something faulty or inadequate, while *absorption* is the uptake of a substance by the body.

In sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" />, *malabsorption* therefore means **incomplete absorption** of lactose. This differs from complete digestion, rapid fermentation, excess enzyme production, and food preference. None of those choices describes impaired absorption.
