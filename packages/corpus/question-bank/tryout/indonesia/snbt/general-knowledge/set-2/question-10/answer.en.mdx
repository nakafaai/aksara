The prefix *mal-* can indicate something faulty or inadequate, while *absorption* is the uptake of a substance by the body.

In sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" />, *malabsorption* therefore means **incomplete absorption** of lactose. It names a digestive process and has nothing to do with discrimination or a dislike of milk.
