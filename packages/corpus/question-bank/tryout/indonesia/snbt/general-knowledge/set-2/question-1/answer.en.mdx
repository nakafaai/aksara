An antonym expresses the opposite meaning of another word.

In sentence <InlineMath math="(1)" />, *increase* means to make the residents' vulnerability greater. The choice *reduce* expresses the opposite action: making that vulnerability smaller. Therefore, **reduce** is the correct answer.
