Pronouns refer back to nouns that the reader can identify from the surrounding sentence.

The subject of sentence <InlineMath math="(1)" /> is **many bats**. Sentence <InlineMath math="(2)" /> keeps the same subject and contrasts their nighttime activity with their daytime rest. Therefore, *they* refers to **bats**.
