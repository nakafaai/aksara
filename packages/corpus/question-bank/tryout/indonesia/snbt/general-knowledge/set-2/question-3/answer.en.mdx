A sound conclusion captures the text's main claim without narrowing it to one example or adding a claim the text does not support.

Here, the first paragraph argues that disaster-risk reduction must shape development investment. The second paragraph shows why: much industrial, connectivity, and educational infrastructure was already located in hazard zones. The final paragraph names regulation and preparedness drills as parts of the response.

Therefore, the fourth choice best represents the whole text: **disaster-risk reduction should guide development investment because much critical infrastructure lies in hazard zones**.
