The passage rejects the idea that production can be improved safely through one isolated intervention. It links long-term output to people, data, management rules, habitats, and aquaculture practices.

The second choice is the only one that covers all those connected needs: **human capacity, science-based management, habitat protection, and suitable aquaculture technology**. Each other choice addresses only one factor or proposes greater fishing pressure without first protecting the resource base.
