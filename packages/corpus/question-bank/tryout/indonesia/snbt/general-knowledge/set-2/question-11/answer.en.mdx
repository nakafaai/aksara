Fermentation is a process in which microorganisms transform components of a food. In dairy production, selected bacteria may convert some lactose into lactic acid, changing the product's acidity, flavor, and texture.

Thus, *fermented dairy* means **dairy transformed by microorganisms through fermentation**. Sentence (9) also warns against assuming that every such product is lactose-free.
