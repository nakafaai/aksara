The reference sentence presents a condition first and then marks its consequence with *so*:

- cause: water quality affects fish health
- result: farms must monitor their effluent

Sentence <InlineMath math="(7)" /> uses the same relationship. The need for reliable data is the cause or basis, and decisions that can respond to local conditions are the result. The conjunction *so* makes that link explicit. Therefore, the third choice is correct.
