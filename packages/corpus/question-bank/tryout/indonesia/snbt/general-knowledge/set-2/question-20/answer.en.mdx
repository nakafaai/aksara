The reference sentence presents a condition first and then marks its consequence with *so*:

- cause: water quality affects fish health
- result: farms must monitor their effluent

Sentence <InlineMath math="(7)" /> uses the same relationship. Data that describes local conditions enables managers to make appropriate decisions. The conjunction *so* makes that link explicit. Therefore, the sentence about the benefit of reliable data is the answer.
