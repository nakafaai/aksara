A sound conclusion follows the evidence without adding new certainty. The passage shows that **Sites near the road had many reports and were also the easiest to visit** and develops the central point that **Community data can support mangrove monitoring when observation opportunity and classification accuracy are calibrated**.

Those ideas support the conclusion **An increase in reports does not by itself prove an equally large population increase**. This wording remains within the passage's conditions. Universal claims and reversed causal relationships are unsupported.
