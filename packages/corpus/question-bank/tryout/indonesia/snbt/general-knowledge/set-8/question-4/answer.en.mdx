The intended *construct* is the concept or attribute the instrument is designed to measure. The correct choice checks **whether the score represents that construct** for the stated purpose. A stable score does not answer that question by itself.

| What is checked? | What does the check establish? |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated measurements under comparable conditions give consistent scores. | Evidence of reliability. |
| The score reflects the intended attribute for its proposed use. | Evidence supporting validity. |

The same coding rules, defined units, and a rubric set in advance improve the measurement procedure. Independently checkable sources help assess a claim. None of those descriptions directly tests the score's connection to the intended construct, which is the issue in the question.
