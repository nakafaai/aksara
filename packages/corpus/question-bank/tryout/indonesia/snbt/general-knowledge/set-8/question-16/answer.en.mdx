*Calibration* means **checking or adjusting against a reference for appropriate interpretation**. Experts assess a sample for comparison with volunteer classifications.

More reports alone, deleting unwelcome results, and averaging without visit opportunity do not perform that check. Using experts as a reference does not make their judgement infallible.
