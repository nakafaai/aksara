In this passage, *calibration* refers to **checking results against a reference** so the reports can be interpreted appropriately. Ecologists assess a random sample, and their classifications are compared with the volunteers' classifications. Visit counts are considered separately because observation opportunity also affects the map.

More reports alone do not test classification accuracy. Deleting results that disagree with expectations would hide evidence, and averaging without accounting for visits would leave uneven coverage unresolved. Using experts as a reference also does not make their judgement infallible.
