Community reports provide broad coverage, but uneven access and classification errors affect the map. Checks preserve their value while clarifying limitations.

The main point is **that community data are useful when observation opportunity and accuracy are accounted for**. High-tide photos illustrate a limitation. Rejecting all data or equating no reports with no seedlings goes too far.
