A weakening finding must damage the link from evidence to decision. The passage relies on **Community data can support mangrove monitoring when observation opportunity and classification accuracy are calibrated**, supported by the finding that **Sites near the road had many reports and were also the easiest to visit**.

**Random surveys show that visit opportunity and classification accuracy were identical at every site from the beginning** weakens that basis by removing a necessary condition or providing a competing explanation for the pattern. A restated plan, additional support, or unrelated fact leaves the original reasoning intact.
