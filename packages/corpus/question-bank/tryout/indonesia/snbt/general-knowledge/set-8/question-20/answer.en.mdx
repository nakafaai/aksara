The hypothesis links the map pattern to differences in observation opportunity and accuracy between sites. **If both were equal everywhere from the start**, differences in those factors cannot explain the spatial pattern.

This does not prove all increased reports reflect growth or remove the need for validation. A survey supporting the adjusted map strengthens the approach, while extra reports on popular paths may increase imbalance.
