# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="-29.4\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" /> divides by <InlineMath math="0.050" /> mol, adding both ions, but they combine into only <InlineMath math="0.025" /> **mol of solid**.
- <InlineMath math="-1.47\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" /> is the heat in kilojoules, not yet divided by the moles.
- The mixture warmed up, so the reaction is exothermic and the sign is negative.
