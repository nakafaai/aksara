# Percentage uncertainties add when values are multiplied or divided

The pipette's share: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{0.06}{25.00} \times 100\% = 0.24\%" />. The titre is <InlineMath math="24.80 - 1.25 = 23.55\text{ cm}^3" />, built from two readings, so it is uncertain by <InlineMath math="2 \times 0.05 = 0.10\text{ cm}^3" />: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{0.10}{23.55} \times 100\% = 0.42\%" />.

The concentration comes from dividing one volume by the other, and for a product or a quotient the **percentage uncertainties add**: <InlineMath math="0.24\% + 0.42\% = 0.66\%" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Every instrument has an uncertainty, and the pipette's <InlineMath math="0.24\%" /> also reaches the answer.
- Uncertainties never cancel by subtraction, so the total cannot be **smaller** than either part.
- <InlineMath math="0.21\%" /> counts one burette reading, but the titre needs two.
