- <InlineMath math="30.5\%" /> divides the loss by the measured value, and <InlineMath math="10.6\text{ g}" /> uses the data book value. **The lamp delivers only <InlineMath math="1050\text{ kJ}" /> per mole**.
- <InlineMath math="76.6\%" /> is the share that reaches the water, not the share lost.
- <InlineMath math="-21.0\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" /> forgets to divide by the moles of ethanol burned, and so needs far too much ethanol.
