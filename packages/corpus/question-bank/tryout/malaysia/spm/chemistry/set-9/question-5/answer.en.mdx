# Each lead ion needs two iodide ions, so the iodide runs out, while the nitrate takes no part and is only diluted

The lead(II) ions are <InlineMath math="0.0100\text{ mol}" /> and the iodide ions <InlineMath math="0.0150\text{ mol}" />. **Each lead ion needs two iodide ions**, so <InlineMath math="0.0100\text{ mol}" /> of lead would need <InlineMath math="0.0200\text{ mol}" />, and the iodide runs out first. It makes <InlineMath math="0.00750\text{ mol}" /> of lead(II) iodide, <InlineMath math="0.00750\times461=3.46\text{ g}" />. **The nitrate ions are spectators**: all <InlineMath math="2\times0.0100=0.0200\text{ mol}" /> stay, now in <InlineMath math="55.0\text{ cm}^3" />, <InlineMath math="0.364\text{ mol dm}^{-3}" />.
