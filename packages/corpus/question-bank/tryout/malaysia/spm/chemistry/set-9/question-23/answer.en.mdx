# The charge fixes the copper that moves, so the rest of what the anode lost is sludge

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="100\%" /> assumes the anode lost only copper. **It lost <InlineMath math="0.30\text{ g}" /> more than the cathode gained**.
- <InlineMath math="109\%" /> divides the anode's loss by the cathode's gain, upside down.
- <InlineMath math="8.57\%" /> is the share of sludge, not of copper.
