# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="8.67\times10^{23}" /> counts only the oxygen's neutrons. **Each deuterium atom adds one more**.
- <InlineMath math="1.20\times10^{24}" /> uses <InlineMath math="M_r=18" />, the mass of ordinary water.
- <InlineMath math="2.16\text{ dm}^3" /> and <InlineMath math="360\text{ cm}^3" /> let all the heavy water react, but the sodium runs out first.
