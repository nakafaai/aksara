# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="9.20\text{ kJ}" /> and <InlineMath math="25.0\text{ cm}^3" /> count one hydrogen chloride per chlorine. **Each chlorine makes two**, which releases <InlineMath math="184\text{ kJ}" /> per mole and doubles the acid.
- <InlineMath math="23.0\text{ kJ}" /> lets the hydrogen decide, but <InlineMath math="0.125\text{ mol}" /> of it needs more chlorine than the <InlineMath math="0.100\text{ mol}" /> there.
- <InlineMath math="1000\text{ cm}^3" /> titrates all the hydrogen chloride, but only a <InlineMath math="25.0\text{ cm}^3" /> portion of the <InlineMath math="500\text{ cm}^3" /> goes into the flask.
