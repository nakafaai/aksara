# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="16\text{ s}" /> multiplies the time by <InlineMath math="0.4" />. **A lower concentration is slower, so the time grows**.
- <InlineMath math="0.096\text{ g}" /> lets all the thiosulfate react, but the acid runs out first, after <InlineMath math="0.00250\text{ mol}" />.
- <InlineMath math="40\text{ s}" /> keeps the time unchanged, but the diluted thiosulfate reacts more slowly.
