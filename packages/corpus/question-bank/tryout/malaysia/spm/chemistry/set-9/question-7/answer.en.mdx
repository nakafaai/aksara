# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="10.6\text{ g}" /> is anhydrous sodium carbonate. **The solid keeps one water molecule per formula unit**.
- <InlineMath math="20.0\text{ cm}^3" /> takes one acid molecule per carbonate, but the equation uses two.
- <InlineMath math="400\text{ cm}^3" /> titrates all the sodium carbonate, but only a tenth of the solution is used.
