A mass of <InlineMath math="0.280\text{ g}" /> of iron filings dissolves in excess dilute hydrochloric acid, giving iron(II) ions and hydrogen. Chlorine is then bubbled in until all the iron(II) ions are oxidised, <InlineMath math="2\text{Fe}^{2+}+\text{Cl}_2\rightarrow2\text{Fe}^{3+}+2\text{Cl}^-" />. Finally excess sodium hydroxide solution is added, and a precipitate forms.
