# Count three double bonds per oil molecule, add one hydrogen to each, keep that hydrogen in the fat, then make it from one zinc each

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="240\text{ cm}^3" /> and <InlineMath math="0.650\text{ g}" /> add one hydrogen per oil molecule. **Each of the three double bonds takes one**.
- <InlineMath math="8.78\text{ g}" /> keeps the mass unchanged, but the hydrogen added stays in the fat.
- <InlineMath math="1440\text{ cm}^3" /> and <InlineMath math="3.90\text{ g}" /> give two hydrogen molecules to each double bond, but one saturates it.
