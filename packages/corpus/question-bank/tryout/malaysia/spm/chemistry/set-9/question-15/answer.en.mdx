The oxide is <InlineMath math="5.00\div80=0.0625\text{ mol}" /> and the acid <InlineMath math="0.0500\text{ mol}" />. <InlineMath math="\text{CuO}+\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4\rightarrow\text{CuSO}_4+\text{H}_2\text{O}" /> is one to one, **so the acid runs out** and <InlineMath math="0.0125\text{ mol}" /> of oxide, <InlineMath math="1.00\text{ g}" />, is filtered off. # Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0\text{ g}" /> lets all the oxide react. **The acid is enough for only <InlineMath math="0.0500\text{ mol}" /> of it**.
- <InlineMath math="8.00\text{ g}" /> weighs anhydrous copper(II) sulfate, but the crystals carry five water molecules each.
- <InlineMath math="12.5\text{ g}" /> is the expected mass, but only <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of the crystals are collected.
