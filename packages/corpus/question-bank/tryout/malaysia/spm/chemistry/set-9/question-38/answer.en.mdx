# Count repeating units, give each one acid molecule, and release two water molecules for its two amide links

The repeating unit has <InlineMath math="M_r=226" />, so the nylon is <InlineMath math="2260\div226=10.0\text{ mol}" /> of units. **Each unit uses one acid molecule**, <InlineMath math="10.0\times146=1.46\text{ kg}" />. **Each unit has two amide links, and each link releases one water molecule**, so <InlineMath math="20.0\text{ mol}" /> of water, <InlineMath math="0.360\text{ kg}" />, is released. Check: <InlineMath math="1.46+1.16-0.36=2.26\text{ kg}" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="1.46\text{ kg}" /> and <InlineMath math="0.360\text{ kg}" /> go together. **One acid molecule per unit, and two water molecules for the two links**.
- <InlineMath math="0.180\text{ kg}" /> releases one water per unit, but each unit has two amide links.
- <InlineMath math="2.92\text{ kg}" /> uses two acid molecules per unit, but each unit holds one acid part and one diamine part.
