# Why the other options are wrong

- Propyl methanoate swaps the parts. **The alcohol gives the first word**, methyl, and the acid gives propanoate.
- <InlineMath math="9.90\text{ g}" /> starts the ester from <InlineMath math="0.150\text{ mol}" />, skipping the <InlineMath math="80\%" /> loss in the oxidation.
- <InlineMath math="75\%" /> is only the second step's yield. The overall yield also carries the <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of the first step.
