# Follow each sulfur atom into one acid molecule, lose four per cent in the converter, then dilute the pure acid to ninety-eight per cent

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="3.06" /> tonnes ignores the converter. **Only <InlineMath math="96\%" /> of the sulfur dioxide becomes sulfur trioxide**.
- <InlineMath math="2.88" /> tonnes multiplies by <InlineMath math="98\%" />, but the concentrated acid weighs more than the pure acid it holds.
- <InlineMath math="1.47" /> tonnes makes one acid molecule per two sulfur atoms, but each sulfur atom gives one.
