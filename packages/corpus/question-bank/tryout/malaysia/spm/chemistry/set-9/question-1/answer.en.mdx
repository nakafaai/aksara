- <InlineMath math="24.60\text{ cm}^3" /> averages all four titres. **The rough titre is never used, and only concordant titres are averaged**.
- <InlineMath math="0.0970\text{ mol dm}^{-3}" /> lets each acid molecule neutralise one hydroxide ion, but sulfuric acid gives two.
- <InlineMath math="24.37\text{ cm}^3" /> keeps the first accurate titre, which is <InlineMath math="0.30\text{ cm}^3" /> away from the others.
