It makes <InlineMath math="0.0200\text{ mol}" /> of magnesium chloride, <InlineMath math="0.0200\times95=1.90\text{ g}" />, and leaves <InlineMath math="0.0100\text{ mol}" /> of magnesium, <InlineMath math="0.240\text{ g}" />. In the bonds <InlineMath math="0.0400\text{ mol}" /> of electrons move from magnesium to chlorine. **Each formula unit carries two chloride ions**, so <InlineMath math="0.0400\text{ mol}" /> of silver chloride precipitates, <InlineMath math="0.0400\times143.5=5.74\text{ g}" />.
