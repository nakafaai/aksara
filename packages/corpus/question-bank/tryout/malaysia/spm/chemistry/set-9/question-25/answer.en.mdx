# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.254\text{ g}" /> and <InlineMath math="0.176\text{ g}" /> read the first equation as one to one. **One iodate gives three iodine molecules**.
- <InlineMath math="+7" /> misreads the iodate: three oxygens, not four, so iodine is <InlineMath math="+5" />.
- <InlineMath math="0.176\text{ g}" /> uses the iodate's moles for the ascorbic acid, but it is the iodine, three times as much, that oxidises it.
