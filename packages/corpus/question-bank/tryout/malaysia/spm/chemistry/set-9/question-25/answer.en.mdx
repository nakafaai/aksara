# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.254\text{ g}" /> and <InlineMath math="0.166\text{ g}" /> read the equation as one to one. **One iodate takes five iodide ions and gives three iodine molecules**.
- <InlineMath math="+7" /> misreads the iodate: three oxygens, not four, so iodine is <InlineMath math="+5" />.
- <InlineMath math="0.166\text{ g}" /> of potassium iodide uses one iodide ion per iodate, but the equation uses five.
