# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.762\text{ g}" /> and <InlineMath math="+5" /> go together. **Three iodine molecules form per iodate, and iodine in iodate is <InlineMath math="+5" />**.
- <InlineMath math="0.254\text{ g}" /> gives one iodine molecule per iodate, but the equation gives three.
- <InlineMath math="+7" /> misreads the iodate: three oxygens, not four, so iodine is <InlineMath math="+5" />.
