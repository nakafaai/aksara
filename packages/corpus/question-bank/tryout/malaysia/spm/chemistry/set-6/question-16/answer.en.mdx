# Excess alkali dissolves the amphoteric hydroxide, and each oxide holds two metal ions

Both ions first form hydroxides. **Aluminium hydroxide is amphoteric and redissolves in excess sodium hydroxide**, while iron(III) hydroxide does not, so only iron is in the first precipitate and only aluminium in the filtrate. Each formula unit of <InlineMath math="\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3" /> or <InlineMath math="\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3" /> comes from two metal ions.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="1.63\text{ g}" /> and <InlineMath math="1.34\text{ g}" /> take one metal ion per oxide unit. **Each oxide unit holds two**.
- <InlineMath math="1.34\text{ g}" /> of aluminium chloride applies the factor of two to iron but not to aluminium, though <InlineMath math="\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3" /> also holds two metal ions.
- <InlineMath math="6.50\text{ g}" /> and <InlineMath math="5.34\text{ g}" /> double the metal ions twice.
