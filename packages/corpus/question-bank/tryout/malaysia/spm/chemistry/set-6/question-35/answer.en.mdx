# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="32.0\text{ kg}" /> assumes all the methane's heat reaches the carbonate. **Only <InlineMath math="60\%" /> does**.
- <InlineMath math="440\text{ kg}" /> counts only the carbon dioxide from the carbonate. **Burning the methane releases more**.
- <InlineMath math="147\text{ kg}" /> counts only the carbon dioxide from the fuel, though the decomposition releases three times as much.
