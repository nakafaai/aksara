# The kiln must supply more heat than the decomposition absorbs, because part of it is lost

Ten thousand moles of carbonate absorb <InlineMath math="1.78\times10^6\text{ kJ}" />. **That is only <InlineMath math="60\%" /> of what the methane gives out**, so the methane must release <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1.78}{0.60}\times10^6\text{ kJ}" />. A positive <InlineMath math="\Delta H" /> means the products sit higher on an energy level diagram than the reactants.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="32.0\text{ kg}" /> assumes all the methane's heat reaches the carbonate. **Only <InlineMath math="60\%" /> does**.
- The decomposition absorbs heat, so its products have more energy, not less.
- <InlineMath math="80.0\text{ kg}" /> divides by the <InlineMath math="40\%" /> lost instead of the <InlineMath math="60\%" /> used.
