Poly(tetrafluoroethene), PTFE, is made by addition polymerisation of tetrafluoroethene gas, which is itself made from chlorodifluoromethane. Only <InlineMath math="95\%" /> of the tetrafluoroethene fed to the polymer reactor polymerises, and making it is complete. The hydrogen chloride formed is absorbed in water to give <InlineMath math="10.0\text{ mol dm}^{-3}" /> hydrochloric acid.

2\text{CHClF}_2&\rightarrow\text{CF}_2{=}\text{CF}_2+2\text{HCl}
\end{aligned}" />
