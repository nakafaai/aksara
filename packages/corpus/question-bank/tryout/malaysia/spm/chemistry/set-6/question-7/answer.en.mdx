# Yields in sequence multiply, and the titration samples only a tenth of the acid

Each step keeps only part of what reaches it, so the overall yield is <InlineMath math="0.90\times0.80=0.72" />. **The second yield applies to the ethanol that formed, not to the ethene**. Then the titration takes <InlineMath math="25.0\text{ cm}^3" /> of <InlineMath math="250\text{ cm}^3" />, one tenth of the acid, and ethanoic acid reacts with sodium hydroxide one to one.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="90.0\text{ cm}^3" /> uses only the first yield. **The oxidation keeps only <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of the ethanol**.
- <InlineMath math="80.0\text{ cm}^3" /> uses only the second yield and treats the ethene as if it all became ethanol.
- <InlineMath math="720\text{ cm}^3" /> titrates all the acid instead of the <InlineMath math="25.0\text{ cm}^3" /> portion.
