# Pick the gas by both conditions, count argon as single atoms, then spread the usable gas over the arc time

Only argon is both heavier than air and unreactive with hot aluminium. It is monatomic, so <InlineMath math="3.20\text{ kg}" /> is <InlineMath math="80.0\text{ mol}" />, <InlineMath math="1920\text{ dm}^3" />. **Only <InlineMath math="95\%" /> of that can be used**, <InlineMath math="1824\text{ dm}^3" />, which gives <InlineMath math="228" /> minutes of arc. **The arc burns for only <InlineMath math="40\%" /> of the working time**, so the cylinder covers <InlineMath math="570" /> minutes of work.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Nitrogen is slightly lighter than air and **forms aluminium nitride with the hot metal**, so it fails both conditions.
- Helium is far lighter than air and rises away from the weld.
- <InlineMath math="10.0" /> hours uses the whole cylinder. **The last <InlineMath math="5\%" /> cannot be used**.
