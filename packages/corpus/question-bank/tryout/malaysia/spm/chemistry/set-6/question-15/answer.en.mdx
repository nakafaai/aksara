# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="20.2\text{ g}" /> assumes all the salt crystallises. **Cold water still holds <InlineMath math="16.0\text{ g}" />**.
- <InlineMath math="0\text{ g}" /> takes the whole <InlineMath math="70.2\text{ g}" /> as water, which could hold <InlineMath math="22.5\text{ g}" /> at <InlineMath math="20^\circ\text{C}" />. **The solution's mass includes the salt**.
- <InlineMath math="16.0\text{ g}" /> is the mass that stays dissolved, not the mass that crystallises.
