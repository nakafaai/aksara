# Scale the solubility to the water that is left, at both temperatures

The neutralisation gives <InlineMath math="0.200\text{ mol}" /> of potassium nitrate. **The chart is per <InlineMath math="100\text{ g}" /> of water, but only <InlineMath math="50\text{ g}" /> remains**, so each solubility halves. At <InlineMath math="80^\circ\text{C}" /> all the salt stays dissolved. At <InlineMath math="20^\circ\text{C}" /> the water holds only <InlineMath math="16.0\text{ g}" />, and the rest crystallises.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="20.2\text{ g}" /> assumes all the salt crystallises. **Cold water still holds some of it**.
- <InlineMath math="0\text{ g}" /> uses the solubility for <InlineMath math="100\text{ g}" /> of water, <InlineMath math="32\text{ g}" />, which is more than the salt made. **Only half that water is left**.
- <InlineMath math="16.0\text{ g}" /> is the mass that stays dissolved, not the mass that crystallises.
