# Why the other options are wrong

- P and R is one reading of solvent X alone. **Solvent Y shows three dyes and nothing at R's <InlineMath math="0.40" />**.
- Q and S leave out the ink's spot at <InlineMath math="0.55" /> in Y, which only P gives.
- R would give a spot at <InlineMath math="0.40" /> in solvent Y, and there is none.
