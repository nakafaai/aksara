# One solvent can hide a dye behind another, so the second solvent decides

Every Rf value is measured from the baseline. In solvent X the two spots match two pairs of dyes, P or Q and R or S, so X alone cannot name them. **Solvent Y gives three spots, so the ink holds three dyes**, and two of them ran together in X. The three Rf values in Y match Q, P and S. Dye S explains the spot at <InlineMath math="0.70" /> in X, so R is absent.

# Why the other options are wrong

- P and R are one reading of solvent X alone. **Solvent Y shows a third dye and no spot at R's <InlineMath math="0.40" />**.
- Q and S leave out the spot at <InlineMath math="0.55" /> in solvent Y, which only P gives.
- R would give a spot at <InlineMath math="0.40" /> in solvent Y, and there is none.
