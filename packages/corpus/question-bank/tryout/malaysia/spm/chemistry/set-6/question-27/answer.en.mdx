# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="76.8\text{ dm}^3" /> is the carbon dioxide alone. **The nitrogen from the air leaves with it**.
- <InlineMath math="45.6\%" /> divides the petrol's mass by <InlineMath math="100\text{ g}" /> instead of the <InlineMath math="200\text{ g}" /> of crude oil.
- <InlineMath math="614\text{ dm}^3" /> counts the steam too, but it condenses at room conditions.
