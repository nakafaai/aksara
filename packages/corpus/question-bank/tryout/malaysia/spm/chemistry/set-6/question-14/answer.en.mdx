# Undo the dilution, convert to grams per cubic decimetre, then put the label in the same unit

The titration measures the diluted solution, <InlineMath math="0.0800\text{ mol dm}^{-3}" />. # Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="4.80\text{ g dm}^{-3}" /> is the diluted solution. **The vinegar is ten times stronger**.
- <InlineMath math="96.0\text{ g dm}^{-3}" /> lets each acid molecule neutralise only half a hydroxide ion. **Ethanoic acid reacts one to one**.
- The vinegar's <InlineMath math="48.0\text{ g dm}^{-3}" /> is <InlineMath math="4.8\text{ g}" /> per <InlineMath math="100\text{ cm}^3" />, under the <InlineMath math="5.0\text{ g}" /> the label promises.
