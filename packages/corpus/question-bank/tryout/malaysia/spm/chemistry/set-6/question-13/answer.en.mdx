# Count the ions in each formula unit, and count each transferred electron once

Two aluminium atoms make one formula unit of <InlineMath math="\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3" />, which holds two aluminium ions and three oxide ions, five ions in all. **Each electron that one aluminium atom loses is the same electron an oxygen atom gains**, so counting both the loss and the gain counts every electron twice. Each aluminium atom gives three.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="1.20\times10^{23}" /> ions counts formula units, or only two ions per unit. **Each unit has five ions**.
- <InlineMath math="7.22\times10^{23}" /> electrons adds the electrons gained by oxygen to those lost by aluminium, though they are the same electrons.
- <InlineMath math="20.4\text{ g}" /> forms one oxide unit per aluminium atom. **Each unit holds two aluminium atoms**.
