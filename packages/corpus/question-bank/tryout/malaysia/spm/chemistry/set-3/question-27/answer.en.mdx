Divide by the <InlineMath math="0.10" /> mol of Q: each molecule has <InlineMath math="4" /> carbon atoms and <InlineMath math="8" /> hydrogen atoms, so Q is <InlineMath math="\text{C}_4\text{H}_8" />. It fits <InlineMath math="\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n}" />, the alkenes, so it has a **carbon-carbon double bond** that adds bromine and decolourises bromine water.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="\text{C}_4\text{H}_{10}" /> would give <InlineMath math="0.50" /> mol of water, not <InlineMath math="0.40" />.
- <InlineMath math="\text{C}_8\text{H}_{16}" /> forgets to **divide by the moles of Q**: it would give <InlineMath math="0.80" /> mol of carbon dioxide.
- <InlineMath math="\text{C}_4\text{H}_8" /> is right, but an alkene is unsaturated.
