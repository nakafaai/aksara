# A metal displaces only metals below it

A more electropositive metal **gives electrons to the ions** of a less electropositive one, so a coating forms only when the solid metal sits higher in the series.

Q is displaced by both P and R, so Q is the lowest. R leaves P nitrate unchanged, so R cannot give electrons to <InlineMath math="\text{P}" /> ions: P is above R. The order is P, R, Q.

# Why the other options are wrong

- R above P would mean R displaces P, but R in P nitrate shows **no change**.
- Q is displaced in two tests, so it cannot be the most electropositive.
- Q below P is right, but R also displaces Q, so Q must be below R as well.
