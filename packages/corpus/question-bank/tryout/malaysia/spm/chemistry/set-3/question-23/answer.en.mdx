# Voltage measures the distance between two metals in the series

Each voltage shows how far a metal sits **above copper** in the electrochemical series. Magnesium is <InlineMath math="2.7" /> V above copper and zinc is <InlineMath math="1.1" /> V above it.

The gap between magnesium and zinc is <InlineMath math="2.7 - 1.1 = 1.6" /> V. Magnesium is the more electropositive metal, so it **releases electrons** and becomes the negative terminal, while zinc ions gain electrons at the positive terminal.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="3.8" /> V adds the two voltages, as if magnesium and zinc sat on **opposite sides** of copper. Both are above it.
- Zinc is less electropositive than magnesium, so it receives electrons and is the positive terminal.
- <InlineMath math="3.8" /> V is wrong for the same reason, even though magnesium is correctly the negative terminal.
