# Divide by the water actually formed, using the whole mass

Hydrogen ions: <InlineMath math="0.050 \times 1.0 = 0.050" /> mol. Hydroxide ions: <InlineMath math="0.030 \times 2.0 = 0.060" /> mol. The alkali is in excess, so only <InlineMath math="0.050" /> mol of water forms: **the acid limits the heat**.

Heat: the whole <InlineMath math="80\text{ cm}^3" /> warms up, so <InlineMath math="Q = 80 \times 4.2 \times 8.5 = 2856" /> J. Per mole of water: <InlineMath math="2856 \div 0.050 = 57\,120" /> J, and the reaction gives out heat, so <InlineMath math="\Delta H = -57.1\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" />.
