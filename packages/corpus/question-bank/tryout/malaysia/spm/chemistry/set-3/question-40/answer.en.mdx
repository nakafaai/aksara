# Match each additive to its job

Each additive on the label has one main job. Sodium benzoate **slows the growth of bacteria and fungi**, so it is a preservative. Ascorbic acid, vitamin C, reacts with oxygen before the fats do, so it is an antioxidant that stops the oil in the noodles turning rancid.

Monosodium glutamate strengthens the savoury taste, and tartrazine gives the noodles their yellow colour.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Ascorbic acid is an antioxidant, and monosodium glutamate is a **flavour enhancer**, not a colouring.
- Tartrazine is a yellow colouring. It does not change the taste.
- Monosodium glutamate enhances flavour. It does not protect fats from oxygen.
