# Why the other options are wrong

- P is **both precise and accurate**, so it is not "not accurate".
- R scatters widely and its mean is off, so it is neither precise nor accurate.
- S has the right mean, but its readings spread over <InlineMath math="0.011" />, so it is accurate on average yet not precise.
