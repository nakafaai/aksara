**Stage two**: ten moles of sodium give one mole of nitrogen, so <InlineMath math="2.0" /> mol of sodium gives <InlineMath math="0.20" /> mol. Total nitrogen: <InlineMath math="3.0 + 0.20 = 3.2" /> mol, or <InlineMath math="3.2 \times 24 = 76.8\text{ dm}^3" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="72.0\text{ dm}^3" /> stops after stage one and **misses the nitrogen** from the sodium.
- <InlineMath math="48.0\text{ dm}^3" /> takes one mole of nitrogen per mole of azide instead of three per two.
- <InlineMath math="4.80\text{ dm}^3" /> counts only the second stage.
