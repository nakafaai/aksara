Natural rubber is poly(isoprene), whose repeating unit is <InlineMath math="\text{C}_5\text{H}_8" /> with relative mass <InlineMath math="68" />. In a vulcanised sample every cross-link is a pair of sulfur atoms, <InlineMath math="-\text{S}{-}\text{S}-" />, joining two chains. Relative atomic mass of sulfur: <InlineMath math="32" />.
