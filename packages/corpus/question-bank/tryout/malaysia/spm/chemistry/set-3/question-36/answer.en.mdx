# Heat demand, fuel supply, and carbon dioxide from both

Calcium carbonate: <InlineMath math="1000 \div 100 = 10" /> mol, which needs <InlineMath math="10 \times 178 = 1780" /> kJ. Only half the fuel's heat arrives, so the coke must release <InlineMath math="1780 \div 0.50 = 3560" /> kJ.

Carbon: <InlineMath math="3560 \div 393.5 = 9.05" /> mol, or <InlineMath math="9.05 \times 12 = 109" /> g.

Carbon dioxide comes from **both reactions**: <InlineMath math="10" /> mol from the carbonate and <InlineMath math="9.05" /> mol from the coke, <InlineMath math="19.05" /> mol in all, or <InlineMath math="19.05 \times 24 = 457\text{ dm}^3" />.
