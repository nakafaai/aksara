# A temperature fall means heat is taken in

The water cools by <InlineMath math="28.0 - 22.0 = 6.0^\circ\text{C}" />, so it gave up <InlineMath math="50 \times 4.2 \times 6.0 = 1260" /> J, or <InlineMath math="1.26" /> kJ, to the dissolving salt. The salt is <InlineMath math="2.0 \div 80 = 0.025" /> mol.

<InlineMath math="\Delta H = +\tfrac{1.26}{0.025} = +50.4\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" />. The sign is positive because the process is **endothermic**: the products hold more energy than the reactants, and that energy came from the water.

# Why the other options are wrong

- A negative value describes an exothermic change, but the temperature **fell**.
- <InlineMath math="+25.2\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" /> uses <InlineMath math="0.050" /> mol, twice the amount of salt actually dissolved.
- <InlineMath math="+1.26\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" /> is the heat in kilojoules, not yet divided by the moles of salt.
