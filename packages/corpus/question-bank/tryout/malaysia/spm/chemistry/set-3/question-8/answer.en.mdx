# Three clues point to a transition element

Transition elements are recognised by **more than one oxidation number, coloured compounds and catalytic activity**. Check each clue against the data.

- I is correct: in <InlineMath math="\text{TCl}_2" /> T is <InlineMath math="+2" />, and in <InlineMath math="\text{TCl}_3" /> it is <InlineMath math="+3" />.
- II is correct: both solutions are coloured, pale green and yellow-brown.
- III is correct: T speeds up the manufacture of ammonia, so it is a catalyst. Iron plays this role in the Haber process.
- IV is wrong: a Group 2 metal forms only one chloride, <InlineMath math="\text{MCl}_2" />, and its solution is colourless. T's second chloride rules Group 2 out.

So I, II and III are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only misses III, **the catalyst clue in the stem**.
- II and IV only accepts IV, which reads one formula without the other.
- I, III and IV only accepts IV and drops II, although both solutions are coloured.
