As the reaction goes on, the hydrogen ions are used up, so the **acid concentration falls** and particles collide less often. The graph flattens and stops at <InlineMath math="150" /> s.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="132 \div 180 = 0.73" /> includes <InlineMath math="30" /> s after the reaction **had already stopped**. The reaction lasts <InlineMath math="150" /> s, so its average rate is <InlineMath math="132 \div 150 = 0.88" /> cm³ s⁻¹.
- The second minute gives <InlineMath math="38\text{ cm}^3" /> against <InlineMath math="88\text{ cm}^3" /> in the first, so its rate is lower.
- The chips get smaller as they react, and the total surface area shrinks, so they cannot grow larger.
