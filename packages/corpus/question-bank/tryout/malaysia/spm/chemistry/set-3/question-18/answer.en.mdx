The graph levels at <InlineMath math="132\text{ cm}^3" /> once **all the marble has reacted**. That is <InlineMath math="132 \div 24\,000 = 0.0055" /> mol of carbon dioxide. In <InlineMath math="\text{CaCO}_3 + 2\text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{CaCl}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{CO}_2" /> one mole of marble gives one mole of gas, so the marble was <InlineMath math="0.0055 \times 100 = 0.55" /> g.

# Why the other options are wrong

- From <InlineMath math="0" /> s to <InlineMath math="180" /> s the rate is <InlineMath math="132 \div 180 = 0.73\text{ cm}^3\text{ s}^{-1}" />. The value <InlineMath math="0.88" /> divides by <InlineMath math="150" /> s instead.
- <InlineMath math="0.275\text{ g}" /> halves the moles, as if **two moles of gas** came from each mole of marble.
- <InlineMath math="0.73\text{ cm}^3\text{ s}^{-1}" /> is the average over the whole <InlineMath math="180" /> s, not over the first <InlineMath math="60" /> s.
