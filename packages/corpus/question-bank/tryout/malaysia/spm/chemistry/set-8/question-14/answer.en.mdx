- <InlineMath math="0.100\text{ g}" /> stops after the first reaction. **The extra carbon dioxide dissolves some of the precipitate**.
- <InlineMath math="0\text{ g}" /> dissolves all the precipitate, but only <InlineMath math="0.00050\text{ mol}" /> of carbon dioxide is left for the second reaction.
- <InlineMath math="0.150\text{ g}" /> turns all the carbon dioxide back into calcium carbonate, but only <InlineMath math="0.00100\text{ mol}" /> of calcium hydroxide is there.
