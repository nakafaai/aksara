# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.10\text{ mol dm}^{-3}" /> is the salt's own concentration. **Each formula unit splits into five ions**.
- <InlineMath math="0.25" /> and <InlineMath math="0.15" /> divide by one cubic decimetre, but the solution is only <InlineMath math="0.500\text{ dm}^3" />.
- The total of <InlineMath math="0.50" /> is right, but there are three sulfate ions per formula unit, not one.
