# Count every bond, including the two oxygen molecules, the two double bonds in carbon dioxide and the four bonds in two water molecules

Breaking needs four <InlineMath math="\text{C}{-}\text{H}" /> bonds and **two** <InlineMath math="\text{O}{=}\text{O}" /> bonds: <InlineMath math="1656+996=2652\text{ kJ}" />. Forming gives two <InlineMath math="\text{C}{=}\text{O}" /> bonds in carbon dioxide and four <InlineMath math="\text{O}{-}\text{H}" /> bonds in the two water molecules: <InlineMath math="1608+1852=3460\text{ kJ}" />. **More energy is released in forming bonds than is used in breaking them**, so <InlineMath math="\Delta H=2652-3460=-808\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" />.
