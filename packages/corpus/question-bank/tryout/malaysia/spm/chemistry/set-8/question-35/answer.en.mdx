# Count every bond, including the carbon-carbon bond and the half oxygen molecule, then scale to the sample

Breaking needs six <InlineMath math="\text{C}{-}\text{H}" />, **one <InlineMath math="\text{C}{-}\text{C}" />** and three and a half <InlineMath math="\text{O}{=}\text{O}" />: <InlineMath math="2484+346+1743=4573\text{ kJ}" />. Forming gives four <InlineMath math="\text{C}{=}\text{O}" /> and six <InlineMath math="\text{O}{-}\text{H}" />: <InlineMath math="3216+2778=5994\text{ kJ}" />. So <InlineMath math="\Delta H=4573-5994=-1421\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" />. The <InlineMath math="1.50\text{ g}" /> is <InlineMath math="0.0500\text{ mol}" />, which releases <InlineMath math="71.1\text{ kJ}" />.
