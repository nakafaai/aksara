# Mass from volume, moles from mass, then molecules from moles

The drop weighs <InlineMath math="0.050\text{ g}" />, which is <InlineMath math="0.050\div18=2.78\times10^{-3}\text{ mol}" />. **Multiplying by the Avogadro constant turns moles into molecules**: <InlineMath math="1.67\times10^{21}" />. Each molecule has three atoms, so the drop holds three times as many atoms.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.78\times10^{-3}" /> is the number of moles. **Multiply by the Avogadro constant to count molecules**.
- <InlineMath math="5.02\times10^{21}" /> counts atoms, three per molecule, but the question asks for molecules.
- <InlineMath math="3.01\times10^{22}" /> multiplies the mass by the Avogadro constant without dividing by the molar mass first.
