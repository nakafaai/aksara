# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="1.28\text{ g}" /> treats all <InlineMath math="1.60\text{ g}" /> as oxide. **The water shows only <InlineMath math="1.20\text{ g}" /> was oxide**.
- <InlineMath math="100\%" /> counts all the hydrogen as reducing, but most of it only flushed and protected the tube.
- <InlineMath math="1.20\text{ g}" /> is the mass of copper(II) oxide, not the copper left at the end.
