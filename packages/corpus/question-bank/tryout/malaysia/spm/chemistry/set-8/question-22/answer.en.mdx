# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="22.3" /> days gives each zinc atom one electron. **It gives two**.
- <InlineMath math="0.232\text{ g}" /> pairs one silver(I) oxide unit with two zinc atoms, but the equation pairs them one to one.
