# Dehydration loses water, polymerisation keeps every gram, and the chain mass then counts the chains

The ethanol is <InlineMath math="1000\text{ mol}" />, and **each molecule loses a water molecule when it becomes ethene**, so the mass per mole falls from <InlineMath math="46" /> to <InlineMath math="28" />. At <InlineMath math="80\%" /> yield, <InlineMath math="800\text{ mol}" /> of ethene forms, <InlineMath math="22.4\text{ kg}" />, and addition polymerisation keeps all of it. Each chain weighs <InlineMath math="28\,000\text{ g mol}^{-1}" />, so <InlineMath math="22\,400\text{ g}" /> is <InlineMath math="0.800\text{ mol}" /> of chains.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="36.8\text{ kg}" /> applies the yield to the ethanol's mass. **Each molecule loses water, so the mass per mole falls**.
- <InlineMath math="28.0\text{ kg}" /> ignores the <InlineMath math="80\%" /> yield of the dehydration.
- <InlineMath math="4.82\times10^{20}" /> divides the mass in kilograms, not grams, by the chain mass.
