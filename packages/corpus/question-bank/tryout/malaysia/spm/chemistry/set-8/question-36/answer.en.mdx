- <InlineMath math="2220\text{ kJ}" /> is the heat from a whole mole, but the sample is only <InlineMath math="0.0250\text{ mol}" />.
- <InlineMath math="111\text{ kJ}" /> divides by <InlineMath math="22" />, half of propane's molar mass.
