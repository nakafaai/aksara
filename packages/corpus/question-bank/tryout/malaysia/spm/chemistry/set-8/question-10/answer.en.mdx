# Group 14 elements have a valency of four, so each germanium atom takes two oxygen atoms

Silicon's oxide is <InlineMath math="\text{SiO}_2" />, because Group 14 atoms have four valence electrons. **Germanium, in the same group, also forms <InlineMath math="\text{GeO}_2" />**, with molar mass <InlineMath math="73+32=105" />. The <InlineMath math="1.46\text{ g}" /> is <InlineMath math="0.0200\text{ mol}" />, which gives <InlineMath math="0.0200\text{ mol}" /> of oxide.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="\text{GeO}" /> gives germanium a valency of two, as in Group 2. **Group 14 elements have a valency of four**.
- <InlineMath math="\text{Ge}_2\text{O}" /> gives germanium a valency of one, as in Group 1.
- <InlineMath math="0.64\text{ g}" /> is only the oxygen gained, not the mass of the oxide.
