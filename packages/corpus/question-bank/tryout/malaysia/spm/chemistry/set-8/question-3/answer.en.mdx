# Use the molar volume at the steam's own temperature, then share one mole of molecules over that volume

The water is <InlineMath math="18.0\text{ g}" />, one mole. **At <InlineMath math="100^\circ\text{C}" /> one mole of gas fills <InlineMath math="30.6\text{ dm}^3" />, not the <InlineMath math="24\text{ dm}^3" /> of room conditions**, so the steam fills <InlineMath math="30\,600\text{ cm}^3" />, <InlineMath math="1700" /> times the liquid. The same <InlineMath math="6.02\times10^{23}" /> molecules are now spread over <InlineMath math="30\,600\text{ cm}^3" />, <InlineMath math="1.97\times10^{19}" /> in each cubic centimetre.
