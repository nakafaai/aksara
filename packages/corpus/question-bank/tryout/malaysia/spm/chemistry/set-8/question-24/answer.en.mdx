# Three quarters of the block dissolves, and every zinc atom gives two electrons

A quarter left means three quarters gone: <InlineMath math="975\text{ g}" />, <InlineMath math="15.0\text{ mol}" /> of zinc. **Each zinc atom gives two electrons**, so the charge is <InlineMath math="30.0\times96\,500=2.90\times10^6\text{ C}" />. At <InlineMath math="0.050\text{ A}" /> that takes <InlineMath math="5.79\times10^7\text{ s}" />, which is <InlineMath math="1.84" /> years.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.45" /> years waits for the whole block to dissolve. **It is replaced while a quarter remains**.
- <InlineMath math="0.92" /> years gives each zinc atom one electron, but it gives two.
- <InlineMath math="0.61" /> years uses the quarter that remains, not the three quarters that dissolve.
