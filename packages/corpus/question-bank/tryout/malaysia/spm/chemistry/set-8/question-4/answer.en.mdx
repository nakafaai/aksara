# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="\text{XY}" /> pairs the ions one to one. **<InlineMath math="\text{X}^{2+}" /> needs two <InlineMath math="\text{Y}^-" /> to balance its charge**.
- <InlineMath math="1.20\times10^{22}" /> counts formula units, but each one holds one positive and two negative ions.
- <InlineMath math="2.01\text{ g}" /> takes the <InlineMath math="10" /> electrons of <InlineMath math="\text{X}^{2+}" /> as its proton number, so it uses <InlineMath math="22" /> instead of <InlineMath math="24" /> for the nucleon number.
