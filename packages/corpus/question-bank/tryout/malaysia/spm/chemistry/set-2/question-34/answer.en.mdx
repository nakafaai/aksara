# A falling temperature means heat is being taken in

When a process absorbs heat, it takes that heat from the water around it, so **the thermometer reads lower**. This is an endothermic change, and <InlineMath math="\Delta H" /> is positive.

Dissolving ammonium nitrate absorbs about <InlineMath math="25.7\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}" />. The dissolved ions end up with more energy than the solid and water started with, which is why the products sit above the reactants on an energy level diagram.

# Why the other options are wrong

- An exothermic process releases heat and **raises the temperature**, the opposite of what is measured.
- The polystyrene cup keeps out most exchange with the air. The temperature falls because the dissolving takes heat from the water itself.
- In an endothermic change, more energy is absorbed to break bonds than is released when new bonds form, not less.
