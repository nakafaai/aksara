# Addition polymers keep every atom of their monomers

In addition polymerisation, **the double bonds open and the monomers join without losing any atoms**. The chain's mass is the mass of one monomer times the number of monomers.

/>

- Relative molecular mass of chloroethene, <InlineMath math="\text{C}_2\text{H}_3\text{Cl}" />: <InlineMath math="2(12) + 3(1) + 35.5 = 62.5" />.
- Chain of <InlineMath math="2000" /> monomers: <InlineMath math="2000 \times 62.5 = 125\,000" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="62\,500" /> counts only <InlineMath math="1000" /> monomers, **as if each repeating unit held two monomers**.
- <InlineMath math="250\,000" /> doubles the chain, counting <InlineMath math="4000" /> monomers.
- <InlineMath math="89\,000" /> leaves out chlorine and counts only carbon and hydrogen.
