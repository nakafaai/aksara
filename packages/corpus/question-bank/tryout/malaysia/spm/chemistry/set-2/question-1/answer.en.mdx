- <InlineMath math="0.400\text{ mol dm}^{-3}" /> skips the dilution, so the portion seems to hold <InlineMath math="0.00800\text{ mol}" /> of chloride, more than the silver can take.
- <InlineMath math="62.5\text{ cm}^3" /> titrates all the silver added, but **the chloride has already taken <InlineMath math="0.00200\text{ mol}" /> of it**.
- <InlineMath math="0.117\text{ g}" /> weighs the precipitate as sodium chloride, but it is silver chloride, <InlineMath math="M_r=143.5" />.
