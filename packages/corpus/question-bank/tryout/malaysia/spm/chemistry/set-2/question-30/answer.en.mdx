# Each decane gives one octane and one ethene, and only half of that ethene becomes ethanol

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.80\text{ g}" /> is the mass of the ethene, but the question asks for the octane.
- <InlineMath math="4.60\text{ g}" /> lets all the ethene react, but **only <InlineMath math="50.0\%" /> of it does**.
- <InlineMath math="14.2\text{ g}" /> keeps the mass of the decane, but part of it leaves as ethene.
