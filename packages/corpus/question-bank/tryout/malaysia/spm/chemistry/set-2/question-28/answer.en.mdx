# Oxidation adds oxygen, dehydration removes water

Acidified potassium dichromate(VI) is an **oxidising agent**, and heated porcelain chips act as a **dehydrating catalyst**. Each sends ethanol a different way.

- Reaction I: <InlineMath math="\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} + 2[\text{O}] \rightarrow \text{CH}_3\text{COOH} + \text{H}_2\text{O}" />. The orange dichromate turns green as ethanol is oxidised to ethanoic acid, so X is ethanoic acid.
- Reaction II: <InlineMath math="\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} \rightarrow \text{C}_2\text{H}_4 + \text{H}_2\text{O}" />. Water leaves the molecule and a double bond forms, so Y is ethene.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Ethene and ethanoic acid swaps the two reactions.
- Carbon dioxide comes from **burning ethanol completely**, and losing water gives ethene, not ethane.
- Ethyl ethanoate needs ethanol and ethanoic acid heated together with concentrated sulfuric acid.
