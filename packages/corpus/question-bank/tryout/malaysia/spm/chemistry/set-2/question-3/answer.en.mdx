# The dip below the plateau is supercooling, not the freezing point

The freezing point is **the temperature that stays constant while the liquid freezes**. A brief dip before freezing starts is supercooling: the liquid cools below its freezing point before the first crystals form.

- I and III only accepts I, **reading the supercooling dip as the freezing point**.
- I, II and IV only accepts I and IV, which both misread what happens during the plateau.
- III and IV only accepts IV and rejects II, which explains why the temperature holds.
