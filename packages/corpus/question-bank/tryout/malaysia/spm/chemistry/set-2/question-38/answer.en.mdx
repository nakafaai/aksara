- <InlineMath math="4.80\text{ dm}^3" /> and <InlineMath math="1.80\text{ g}" /> count only the PVC, but **the polyethene burns to carbon dioxide and water too**.
- <InlineMath math="2.80\text{ g}" /> is the mass of the polyethene, but the question asks for the PVC.
- <InlineMath math="6.30\text{ g}" /> turns all three hydrogens of each PVC unit into water, but one leaves as hydrogen chloride.
