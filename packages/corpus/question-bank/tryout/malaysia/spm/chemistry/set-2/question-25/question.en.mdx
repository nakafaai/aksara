At the anode the copper and the zinc dissolve, <InlineMath math="\text{Cu}\rightarrow\text{Cu}^{2+}+2e^-" /> and <InlineMath math="\text{Zn}\rightarrow\text{Zn}^{2+}+2e^-" />, while the silver falls as sludge. Only copper is deposited at the cathode. After some time the anode has lost <InlineMath math="7.00\text{ g}" />, the cathode has gained <InlineMath math="6.40\text{ g}" />, and <InlineMath math="0.650\text{ g}" /> of zinc has dissolved.
