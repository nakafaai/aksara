# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="10.0^\circ\text{C}" /> and <InlineMath math="0\text{ cm}^3" /> let all the copper(II) ions in Experiment II react, but **only <InlineMath math="0.0100\text{ mol}" /> of zinc is there**.
- <InlineMath math="10.0\text{ cm}^3" /> gives one hydroxide per copper(II) ion, but copper(II) hydroxide needs two.
- <InlineMath math="40.0\text{ cm}^3" /> precipitates all the copper(II) ions put in, but half of them were displaced by the zinc.
