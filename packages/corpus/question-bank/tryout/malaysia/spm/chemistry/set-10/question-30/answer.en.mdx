# The yield sets the glucose that ferments, each glucose gives two ethanol and two carbon dioxide, and both products carry on

The glucose is <InlineMath math="36.0\div180=0.200\text{ mol}" />, and **only <InlineMath math="75\%" /> ferments**, <InlineMath math="0.150\text{ mol}" />. Each gives two ethanol and two carbon dioxide, <InlineMath math="0.300\text{ mol}" /> of each, so <InlineMath math="0.300\times46=13.8\text{ g}" /> of ethanol. **Each carbon dioxide takes two hydroxide ions**, <InlineMath math="0.600\text{ mol}" />, <InlineMath math="0.600\text{ dm}^3" />, <InlineMath math="600\text{ cm}^3" /> of alkali. Burning <InlineMath math="0.300\text{ mol}" /> of ethanol releases <InlineMath math="0.300\times1370=411\text{ kJ}" />.
