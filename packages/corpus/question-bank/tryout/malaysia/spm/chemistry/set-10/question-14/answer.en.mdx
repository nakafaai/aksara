# The back titration finds the acid the tablet used, which fixes its carbonate, its gas and the acid it can neutralise

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="50.0\text{ cm}^3" /> gives each carbonate one acid, but **it takes two**, so the tablet neutralises <InlineMath math="0.0100\text{ mol}" />.
- <InlineMath math="20.0\%" /> treats the alkali's acid as the acid the tablet used, but the titration measures what the tablet left.
- <InlineMath math="100\%" /> uses all the acid added, forgetting the <InlineMath math="12.5\text{ cm}^3" /> back titration.
