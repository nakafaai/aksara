Aluminium is extracted by electrolysis of molten aluminium oxide, with <InlineMath math="\text{Al}^{3+}+3e^-\rightarrow\text{Al}" /> at the cathode and <InlineMath math="2\text{O}^{2-}\rightarrow\text{O}_2+4e^-" /> at the carbon anode. The oxygen burns the anode away, <InlineMath math="\text{C}+\text{O}_2\rightarrow\text{CO}_2" />. A cell runs at a current of <InlineMath math="1.00\times10^4\text{ A}" /> and makes <InlineMath math="2.70\text{ kg}" /> of aluminium.
