# Use the equation's ratio, three alcohol molecules to two dichromate ions

The balanced equation fixes the ratio: **two dichromate ions for every three alcohol molecules**. The orange dichromate(VI) ion is reduced to green chromium(III), so the mixture turns from orange to green.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="100\text{ cm}^3" /> reacts one dichromate ion with each alcohol molecule.
- Dichromate(VI) is orange and is reduced to green chromium(III). **The colour goes from orange to green, not back**.
- <InlineMath math="150\text{ cm}^3" /> turns the ratio upside down, three dichromate ions for two alcohol molecules.
