# The double bond has two different ends, so the OH group can go on either

Steam adds across the double bond: H on one carbon and OH on the other. **Propene's double bond joins an end carbon and the middle carbon**, so the OH can land on either, giving propan-1-ol or propan-2-ol. Both have the formula <InlineMath math="\text{C}_3\text{H}_7\text{OH}" />, so the mass does not depend on the mix.

Only the propan-1-ol in the mixture could later be oxidised to propanoic acid.

# Why the other options are wrong

- One alcohol misses that the OH can add to either carbon of the double bond.
- <InlineMath math="6.0\text{ g}" /> converts all the propene, but only <InlineMath math="80\%" /> reacted.
- <InlineMath math="3.4\text{ g}" /> takes <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of the propene's mass. **Adding water makes each molecule heavier, <InlineMath math="42" /> to <InlineMath math="60" />**.
