# Equal volumes hold equal numbers of molecules, so count molecules

At the same temperature and pressure, **volume follows the number of gas molecules**. Each two molecules of ammonia that break up give four, so the volume grows, and the acid then removes only the ammonia that is left.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="60\text{ cm}^3" /> ignores that decomposition makes more molecules than it uses.
- <InlineMath math="60\text{ cm}^3" /> after the acid subtracts the ammonia that decomposed, <InlineMath math="24\text{ cm}^3" />, instead of the ammonia that remained.
- <InlineMath math="96\text{ cm}^3" /> lets <InlineMath math="60\%" /> decompose. **<InlineMath math="40\%" /> broke up and <InlineMath math="60\%" /> stayed as ammonia**.
