# Check which reactant runs out, then place carbon in the reactivity series

Two zinc oxide units need only one carbon atom, so <InlineMath math="1000\text{ mol}" /> of zinc oxide needs <InlineMath math="500\text{ mol}" /> of carbon and the coke is in excess. **Carbon reduces the oxide of any metal below it in the reactivity series**, and it sits between aluminium and zinc.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Aluminium is above carbon in the reactivity series, so its oxide must be electrolysed.
- <InlineMath math="81\text{ kg}" /> is the mass of the oxide. **Removing the oxygen leaves less mass of zinc**.
- <InlineMath math="48.8\text{ kg}" /> pairs each carbon atom with one zinc oxide, but the equation uses one carbon for two.
