# Each negative test removes an anion until only nitrate is left

No gas rules out a carbonate, and the silver and barium tests rule out chloride and sulfate. **The brown ring is the test for nitrate**, and a precipitate that dissolves in excess ammonia to a dark blue solution identifies copper(II).

Copper(II) nitrate decomposes like the nitrates of other less reactive metals.

# Why the other options are wrong

- A carbonate would fizz with nitric acid, which the first test rules out.
- Iron(II) gives a green precipitate with ammonia that does not dissolve in excess.
- Heating a nitrate of copper gives brown nitrogen dioxide as well as oxygen. **The brown gas is what you see first**.
