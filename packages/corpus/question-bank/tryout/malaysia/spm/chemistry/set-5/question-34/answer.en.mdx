# Work back from the heat the soup needs to the heat the reaction must release

The soup needs <InlineMath math="40\text{ kJ}" />, but **only <InlineMath math="70\%" /> of what the reaction releases gets there**, so the reaction must release more than <InlineMath math="40\text{ kJ}" />. The negative <InlineMath math="\Delta H" /> means heat leaves the reacting mixture and warms the soup.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="34.5\text{ g}" /> assumes every joule reaches the soup.
- <InlineMath math="24.1\text{ g}" /> multiplies by <InlineMath math="0.70" /> instead of dividing. **Losses mean more oxide, not less**.
- A negative <InlineMath math="\Delta H" /> is an exothermic reaction, which releases heat into the soup.
