# Price each step in electrons

Vanadium's oxidation number is <InlineMath math="+5" /> in <InlineMath math="\text{VO}_2^+" />, <InlineMath math="+4" /> in <InlineMath math="\text{VO}^{2+}" />, <InlineMath math="+3" /> in <InlineMath math="\text{V}^{3+}" /> and <InlineMath math="+2" /> in <InlineMath math="\text{V}^{2+}" />, so **each colour change costs one electron per vanadium**. Going all the way to violet costs three, <InlineMath math="0.0300\text{ mol}" /> of electrons, and each zinc atom gives two, so at least <InlineMath math="0.0150\text{ mol}" /> of zinc, <InlineMath math="0.975\text{ g}" />. One zinc per vanadium, <InlineMath math="0.650\text{ g}" />, pays for only two of the three steps, and one electron per zinc doubles the bill to <InlineMath math="1.95\text{ g}" />.
