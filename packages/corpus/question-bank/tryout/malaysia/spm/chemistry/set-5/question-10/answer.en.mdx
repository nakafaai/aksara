# Work out vanadium's oxidation number in each ion, then balance the electrons

In <InlineMath math="\text{VO}_2^+" /> the two oxide ions carry <InlineMath math="-4" />, so vanadium is <InlineMath math="+5" />. Each step of colour is one electron gained, <InlineMath math="+5\rightarrow+4\rightarrow+3\rightarrow+2" />. **Every vanadium ion takes three electrons, and every zinc atom gives two**.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.65\text{ g}" /> pairs one zinc atom with one vanadium ion, though each needs three electrons and each zinc gives two.
- <InlineMath math="+3" /> misreads <InlineMath math="\text{VO}_2^+" /> by ignoring the oxygen. **Oxide counts <InlineMath math="-2" /> each**.
- <InlineMath math="1.95\text{ g}" /> forgets that each zinc atom gives two electrons.
