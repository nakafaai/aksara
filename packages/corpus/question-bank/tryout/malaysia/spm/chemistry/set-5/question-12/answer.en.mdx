# A hydrogen bond needs a hydrogen on oxygen on one side

Ethanol has an <InlineMath math="\text{O–H}" /> group, so its molecules hold each other by hydrogen bonds, which is why it boils <InlineMath math="102^\circ\text{C}" /> higher. **Methoxymethane has no hydrogen on its oxygen**, so its molecules cannot hydrogen-bond each other, but its oxygen can still accept a hydrogen bond from water, which is why it dissolves.

# Why the other options are wrong

- II gives methoxymethane hydrogen bonds between its own molecules, which needs an <InlineMath math="\text{O–H}" /> it does not have.
- IV mixes up boiling with decomposing. **Boiling separates whole molecules and breaks no covalent bond**.
