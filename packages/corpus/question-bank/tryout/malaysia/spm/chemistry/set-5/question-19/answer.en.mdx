# Temperature sets how fast each bit of surface dissolves, and a longer strip has more surface

From <InlineMath math="30" /> to <InlineMath math="50^\circ\text{C}" /> the rate doubles twice. **A strip twice as long has twice the surface but the same thickness**, so each part of it still dissolves in the same time: the longer strip does not take longer. It does give twice the hydrogen.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="60\text{ s}" /> doubles the rate only once, but <InlineMath math="20^\circ\text{C}" /> is two steps of <InlineMath math="10^\circ\text{C}" />.
- <InlineMath math="15\text{ s}" /> halves the time again for the longer strip, though its thickness, not its length, sets how long it lasts.
- <InlineMath math="48\text{ cm}^3" /> forgets the strip holds twice the magnesium. **More metal, more hydrogen**.
