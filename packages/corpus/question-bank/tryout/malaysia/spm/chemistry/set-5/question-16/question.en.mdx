A blue solid Y is one salt. A solution of Y gives these results.

- Dilute nitric acid: no gas.
- Acidified silver nitrate solution: no precipitate.
- Acidified barium chloride solution: no precipitate.
- Iron(II) sulfate solution, then concentrated sulfuric acid poured slowly down the side: a brown ring.
- Ammonia solution: a blue precipitate that dissolves in excess to give a dark blue solution.
