# The titration gives the molar mass, the molar mass gives the acid, and the acid names the ester

A monoprotic acid reacts one to one with sodium hydroxide, so the moles of alkali are the moles of acid. **Its molar mass, <InlineMath math="74" />, fits only <InlineMath math="n=2" />**, propanoic acid. With methanol it gives methyl propanoate, <InlineMath math="\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{COOCH}_3" />, whose name starts with the alcohol part.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}" /> has a molar mass of <InlineMath math="60" />, which does not match the titration.
- Propyl methanoate swaps the parts. **The alcohol, methanol, gives the methyl part of the name**.
- <InlineMath math="0.88\text{ g}" /> is the mass at a full yield and leaves out the <InlineMath math="75\%" />.
