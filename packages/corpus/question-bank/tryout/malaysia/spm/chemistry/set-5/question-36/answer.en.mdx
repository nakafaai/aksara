# Which gas runs out

The exhaust brings <InlineMath math="0.150\text{ mol}" /> of carbon monoxide and <InlineMath math="0.100\text{ mol}" /> of nitrogen monoxide, and the equation uses them two to two, that is one to one. **Nitrogen monoxide is used up first**, after <InlineMath math="0.100\text{ mol}" /> of each has reacted. That is only half of the equation as written, because <InlineMath math="\Delta H" /> belongs to two moles of nitrogen monoxide, so the heat is <InlineMath math="37.3\text{ kJ}" />. Multiplying <InlineMath math="746" /> by <InlineMath math="0.100" /> gives <InlineMath math="74.6\text{ kJ}" /> and quietly reads the value per mole of gas.
