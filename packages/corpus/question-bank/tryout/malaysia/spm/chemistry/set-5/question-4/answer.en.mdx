# The lost mass is oxygen, and only the chlorate made it

The catalyst comes out unchanged, so **the whole loss in mass is oxygen**. The equation turns the oxygen back into the chlorate that released it.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.96\text{ dm}^3" /> converts the moles of chlorate, <InlineMath math="0.0400" />, into gas instead of the moles of oxygen.
- <InlineMath math="27.4\%" /> is the oxygen's share of the mixture, not the chlorate's.
- <InlineMath math="35.0\%" /> pairs one chlorate with three oxygen molecules. **Two chlorate units give three oxygen molecules**.
