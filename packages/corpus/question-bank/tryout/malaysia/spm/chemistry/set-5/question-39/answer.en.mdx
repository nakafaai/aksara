# Count the paracetamol already taken, then check which limit is reached first

The cold-and-flu syrup is also a paracetamol medicine, so its two doses count, <InlineMath math="240\text{ mg}" /> and two of the four doses. The daily limit of <InlineMath math="1080\text{ mg}" /> leaves room for <InlineMath math="840\text{ mg}" />. **But only two doses remain, each at most <InlineMath math="270\text{ mg}" />**, so the dose count stops the child at <InlineMath math="540\text{ mg}" /> more, well under the daily limit.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="35.0\text{ mL}" /> fills the daily mass limit. **That would need more than four doses in all**, since one dose can hold at most <InlineMath math="270\text{ mg}" />.
- <InlineMath math="45.0\text{ mL}" /> forgets that the cold-and-flu syrup already contains paracetamol.
- <InlineMath math="3.58\times10^{-3}\text{ mol}" /> counts only the plain syrup and leaves out the <InlineMath math="240\text{ mg}" /> taken earlier.
