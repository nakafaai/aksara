# Dose by body mass first, then turn milligrams into millilitres

The dose depends on the child's mass: <InlineMath math="15\text{ mg}" /> for each of <InlineMath math="18\text{ kg}" />. **The syrup's strength converts milligrams to millilitres**: <InlineMath math="120\text{ mg}" /> in each <InlineMath math="5.0\text{ mL}" />. The daily limit is four doses, not one.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="11.3\text{ mL}" /> a day allows only one dose, though four are allowed.
- <InlineMath math="2.25\text{ mL}" /> divides milligrams by milligrams and forgets that each <InlineMath math="120\text{ mg}" /> comes in <InlineMath math="5.0\text{ mL}" />.
- <InlineMath math="67.5\text{ mL}" /> gives six doses a day. **The prescription allows four at most**.
