# The smaller amount decides the precipitate, and the larger one stays in the solution

The ions pair one to one, so **the carbonate, the smaller amount, runs out first**. The <InlineMath math="0.0020\text{ mol}" /> of copper(II) ions it leaves behind keep the filtrate blue.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.48\text{ g}" /> works from the copper(II) ions, though there is not enough carbonate to take them all.
- A colourless filtrate would need every copper(II) ion to precipitate. **Some are left over, and they colour the solution**.
- <InlineMath math="2.48\text{ g}" /> with a blue filtrate contradicts itself: if all the copper precipitated, none could colour the filtrate.
