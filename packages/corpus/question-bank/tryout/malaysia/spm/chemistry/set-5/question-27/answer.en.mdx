# List the chains, place the OH group, then test each position

Four carbon atoms make either a straight chain or a branched one. The straight chain takes the OH group at an end or on an inner carbon, and the branched chain at an end or on the central carbon. **Only an OH on an end carbon, joined to one other carbon, is oxidised to an acid**.

So there are four alcohols, and two of them give carboxylic acids.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Three alcohols misses one, usually 2-methylpropan-1-ol, the branched chain with the OH at its end.
- Four acids ignores the condition in the question. **An OH on an inner carbon cannot become a COOH without breaking the chain**.
- Three counts butan-2-ol as giving an acid, but its OH carbon is joined to two other carbons.
