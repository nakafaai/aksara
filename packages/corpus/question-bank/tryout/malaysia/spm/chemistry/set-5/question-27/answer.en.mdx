# Two propanoate ions on every calcium ion

The salt's formula carries two propanoate ions, so <InlineMath math="M_r=2\times73+40=186" /> and the <InlineMath math="0.0500\text{ mol}" /> that forms weighs <InlineMath math="9.30\text{ g}" />. The acid not used, <InlineMath math="0.0250\text{ mol}" />, is what the heat drives off, <InlineMath math="1.85\text{ g}" />. Pairing acid and base one to one leaves <InlineMath math="0.0750\text{ mol}" /> unused and sends <InlineMath math="5.55\text{ g}" /> into the air, but **a calcium ion's <InlineMath math="2+" /> charge needs two propanoate ions to balance it**.
