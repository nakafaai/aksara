# Same concentration, half the hydrogen

The hydrochloric acid holds <InlineMath math="0.00800\text{ mol}" /> of hydrogen ions, half of what the sulfuric acid carried. **Two hydrochloric acid molecules make each hydrogen molecule**, so the second flask gives <InlineMath math="0.00400\text{ mol}" />, <InlineMath math="96.0\text{ cm}^3" />, and iron is again left over. Expecting <InlineMath math="192\text{ cm}^3" /> a second time treats the two acids as if equal concentrations meant equal hydrogen.
