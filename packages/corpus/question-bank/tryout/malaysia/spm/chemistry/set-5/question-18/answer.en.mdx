# Copper(II) ions speed the reaction up, but they are not a true catalyst here

Zinc displaces copper from the copper(II) ions, and the copper that coats the zinc **makes a tiny cell that speeds up the release of hydrogen**. But the displacement uses <InlineMath math="0.0010\text{ mol}" /> of zinc that never reaches the acid, so flask II makes less hydrogen.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="240\text{ cm}^3" /> for both ignores the zinc that went into displacing copper.
- A slower II misses the effect of the copper on the zinc, which speeds the reaction up.
- <InlineMath math="264\text{ cm}^3" /> imagines that copper also gives hydrogen. **Copper is below hydrogen and does not react with the acid**.
