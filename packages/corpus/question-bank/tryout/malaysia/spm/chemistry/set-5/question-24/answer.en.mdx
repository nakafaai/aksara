# Charge gives electrons, electrons give zinc, and the reactivity series decides the scratch

The charge counts the electrons the zinc gave the hull, and **each zinc atom gives two**. Tin is below iron in the reactivity series: where the coat is broken, iron becomes the metal that gives electrons, so it rusts faster than bare steel would.
