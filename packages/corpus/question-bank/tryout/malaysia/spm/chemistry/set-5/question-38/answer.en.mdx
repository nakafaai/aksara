# What the dried solid gains

**Each converted group swaps one hydrogen atom for one sodium atom**, so it gains <InlineMath math="23-1=22" /> in mass, and the water formed is driven off when the product dries. Adding the whole sodium hydroxide, <InlineMath math="40" /> per group, gives <InlineMath math="20.4\text{ g}" /> and keeps the water that left. Converting all <InlineMath math="0.200\text{ mol}" /> gives <InlineMath math="18.8\text{ g}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
n&=\tfrac{14.4}{72}=0.200\text{ mol},\quad n(\text{NaOH})=0.750\times0.200=0.150\text{ mol}\ \Rightarrow\ V=\tfrac{0.150}{2.00}=0.0750\text{ dm}^3\\
m&=14.4+0.150\times(23-1)=17.7\text{ g},\quad 200\times17.7=3540\text{ g}=3.54\text{ kg}
\end{aligned}" />
