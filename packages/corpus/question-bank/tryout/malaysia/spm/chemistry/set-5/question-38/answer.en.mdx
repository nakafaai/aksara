# How many cross-links a polymer has decides how it behaves when heated or stretched

Melamine is a thermoset: **its chains are tied together by many covalent cross-links**, so heat cannot make them slide. Polyethene is a thermoplastic, its chains held only by weak forces between molecules. An elastomer sits between them, with a few cross-links that pull the stretched chains back.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I alone leaves out II, the reason a thermoplastic softens.
- III is wrong. **Without any cross-links, stretched chains would slide apart and not spring back**.
- IV is wrong: a thermoset cannot be melted down, which is why it is the hardest to recycle.
