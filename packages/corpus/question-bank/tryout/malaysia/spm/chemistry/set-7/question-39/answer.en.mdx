# Both additives carry sodium, and only the sodium share of each counts

MSG has a molar mass of <InlineMath math="169" />, of which <InlineMath math="23" /> is sodium, so <InlineMath math="5.0\text{ g}" /> carries <InlineMath math="0.68\text{ g}" />. **Table salt is sodium chloride and carries sodium too**: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{23}{58.5}" /> of <InlineMath math="3.0\text{ g}" />, <InlineMath math="1.18\text{ g}" />. Together they give <InlineMath math="1.86\text{ g}" />, just under the guideline.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.68\text{ g}" /> counts only the MSG. **The table salt also carries sodium**.
- <InlineMath math="1.18\text{ g}" /> counts only the table salt and forgets that MSG is a sodium salt.
- <InlineMath math="8.0\text{ g}" /> adds the whole masses of the additives instead of their sodium.
