# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="10.0\text{ cm}^3" /> uses one hydroxide ion per oxide unit. **The equation needs two**.
- <InlineMath math="60.0\text{ cm}^3" /> uses six hydroxide ions per oxide unit, more than the equation needs.
- Iron(III) oxide is only <InlineMath math="30\%" /> oxygen by mass and is basic, so it would not dissolve in the alkali.
