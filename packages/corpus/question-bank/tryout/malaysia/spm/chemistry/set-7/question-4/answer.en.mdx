# Each malachite unit holds two copper atoms, its water condenses, and every copper(II) oxide takes one hydrogen

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.80\text{ g}" /> gives one copper(II) oxide per malachite unit. **Each unit holds two copper atoms**.
- <InlineMath math="480\text{ cm}^3" /> of gas counts the water as a gas, but it condenses at room conditions.
- <InlineMath math="240\text{ cm}^3" /> of hydrogen reduces only one copper(II) oxide per malachite unit, but each unit gives two.
