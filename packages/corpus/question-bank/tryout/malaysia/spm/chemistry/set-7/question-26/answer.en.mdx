# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="14.3\text{ kg}" /> counts all the charge. **Only <InlineMath math="85\%" /> deposits copper**.
- <InlineMath math="24.4\text{ kg}" /> gives each copper ion one electron. **Copper(II) ions take two**.
- <InlineMath math="16.8\text{ kg}" /> divides by <InlineMath math="0.85" /> instead of multiplying, as if the waste added copper.
