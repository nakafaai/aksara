Magnetite, <InlineMath math="\text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4" />, is an ionic oxide that contains both iron(II) and iron(III) ions. A mass of <InlineMath math="4.64\text{ g}" /> of magnetite is heated in hydrogen until it is reduced completely to iron, and the only other product is water. All the iron formed is then dissolved in excess dilute hydrochloric acid.
