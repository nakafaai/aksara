# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="350\text{ cm}^3" /> is the pure ethanol needed. **The stock is only <InlineMath math="95\%" /> ethanol, so more is needed**.
- <InlineMath math="500\text{ g}" /> gives the whole sanitiser the density of water, but ethanol is lighter.
- <InlineMath math="409\text{ g}" /> counts only the added water and forgets the water already in the stock.
