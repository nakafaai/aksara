# The pure ethanol is the same before and after dilution, and only it counts for the mass

The sanitiser must hold <InlineMath math="0.70\times500=350\text{ cm}^3" /> of pure ethanol. **The stock is only <InlineMath math="95\%" /> ethanol**, so <InlineMath math="\tfrac{350}{0.95}=368\text{ cm}^3" /> of it supplies that, and the rest is water. The mass comes from the pure ethanol alone, <InlineMath math="350\times0.79" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="350\text{ cm}^3" /> is the pure ethanol needed. **The stock is not pure, so more of it is needed**.
- <InlineMath math="291\text{ g}" /> weighs all <InlineMath math="368\text{ cm}^3" /> of the stock as pure ethanol, though five per cent of it is water.
- <InlineMath math="350\text{ g}" /> treats cubic centimetres of ethanol as grams. **Its density is <InlineMath math="0.79\text{ g cm}^{-3}" />**.
