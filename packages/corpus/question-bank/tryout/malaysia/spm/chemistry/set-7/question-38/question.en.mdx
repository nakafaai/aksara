Poly(lactic acid), PLA, is a condensation polymer made from lactic acid, <InlineMath math="\text{C}_3\text{H}_6\text{O}_3" />, with one water molecule lost for every lactic acid unit that joins the chain, giving the repeating unit <InlineMath math="\text{C}_3\text{H}_4\text{O}_2" />. A <InlineMath math="1.00\text{ kg}" /> batch of PLA cups is later composted, and all its carbon becomes carbon dioxide and all its hydrogen becomes water.
