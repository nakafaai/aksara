An aluminium window frame is anodised: it is made the anode in dilute sulfuric acid, and a current of <InlineMath math="1.50\text{ A}" /> flows for <InlineMath math="30.0" /> minutes. All the charge builds a layer of aluminium oxide, <InlineMath math="\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3" />, on the frame, and each aluminium atom in the layer has lost three electrons. The aluminium in the layer comes from the frame itself.
