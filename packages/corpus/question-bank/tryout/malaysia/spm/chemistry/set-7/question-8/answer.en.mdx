# Only the metal gives hydrogen, so the gas measures the metal and the rest of the mass is oxide

The hydrogen is <InlineMath math="0.0300\text{ mol}" />, and two aluminium atoms give three hydrogen molecules, so the metal is <InlineMath math="0.0200\text{ mol}" />, <InlineMath math="0.54\text{ g}" />. **Aluminium oxide is amphoteric**: it also dissolves in the alkali, forming aluminate, but it has no metal left to oxidise, so it gives no gas. The remaining <InlineMath math="0.10\text{ g}" /> is the oxide.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="84.4\%" /> is the share of aluminium metal, not of the oxide.
- The oxide gives no hydrogen. **Its aluminium is already oxidised**, so only the metal releases gas.
- Aluminium oxide is amphoteric, so it does dissolve in sodium hydroxide.
