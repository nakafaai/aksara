# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.74\text{ g}" /> leaves all the hydroxide as solid. **The water dissolves <InlineMath math="0.17\text{ g}" /> of it**.
- <InlineMath math="12\text{ cm}^3\text{ s}^{-1}" /> divides the whole <InlineMath math="240\text{ cm}^3" /> by <InlineMath math="20\text{ s}" />, but only <InlineMath math="120\text{ cm}^3" /> forms in that time.
- <InlineMath math="0\text{ g}" /> assumes all the calcium hydroxide dissolves, but it is only sparingly soluble.
