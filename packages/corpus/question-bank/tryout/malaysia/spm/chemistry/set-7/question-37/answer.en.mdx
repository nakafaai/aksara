# The chain length does not change the mass of monomer needed, but the yield does

Each repeating unit has propene's molar mass, <InlineMath math="42" />, so a chain of <InlineMath math="84\,000" /> holds <InlineMath math="2000" /> units. **Whatever the chain length, one kilogram of polymer contains <InlineMath math="23.8\text{ mol}" /> of propene units**, and since only <InlineMath math="92\%" /> of the gas polymerises, <InlineMath math="25.9\text{ mol}" /> must be fed in.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="571\text{ dm}^3" /> assumes all the propene polymerises. **Only <InlineMath math="92\%" /> does**.
- <InlineMath math="1000" /> units divides by <InlineMath math="84" />, the mass of two propene units.
- <InlineMath math="526\text{ dm}^3" /> multiplies by <InlineMath math="0.92" /> instead of dividing. **The feed must exceed what polymerises**.
