- <InlineMath math="62.5\text{ kg}" /> and nothing escaping assume the scrubber catches all the sulfur dioxide. **It removes <InlineMath math="90\%" />**.
- <InlineMath math="13.5\text{ m}^3" /> is the sulfur dioxide removed, not the part that escapes.
