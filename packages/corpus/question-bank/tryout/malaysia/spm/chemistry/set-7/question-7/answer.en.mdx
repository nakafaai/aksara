# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0\text{ m}^3" /> lets the scrubber remove all the sulfur dioxide. **It removes only <InlineMath math="90\%" />**.
- <InlineMath math="13.5\text{ m}^3" /> of sulfur dioxide swaps the two gases: that is the carbon dioxide made, and only a tenth of the sulfur dioxide escapes.
- <InlineMath math="112.5\text{ kg}" /> takes two calcium carbonate per sulfur dioxide, but the equation is one to one.
