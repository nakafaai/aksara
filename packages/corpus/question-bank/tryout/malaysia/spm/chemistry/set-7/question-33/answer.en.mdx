# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="12.9\text{ kJ}" /> and <InlineMath math="2.40\text{ dm}^3" /> treat each mole of baking soda as one lot of the equation. **One lot uses two moles**.
- A positive <InlineMath math="\Delta H" /> means heat is absorbed, which is why the oven must supply it.
- <InlineMath math="2.40\text{ dm}^3" /> gives one carbon dioxide per baking soda unit. **Two units give one**.
