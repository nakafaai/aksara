# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="10\text{ J}" /> counts only the kinetic energy and forgets the **heat made by the sliding**.
- <InlineMath math="1.0\text{ m}" /> is how far the **belt** moves, not how far it slides under the parcel.
- <InlineMath math="30\text{ J}" /> with <InlineMath math="0.50\text{ m}" /> works out the heat over the belt’s whole <InlineMath math="1.0" /> m, but heat comes only from the **slip** between them.
