# Halve only the source’s share, then add the background back

The source alone gives <InlineMath math="820 - 20 = 800" /> counts per minute. A reading of <InlineMath math="45" /> means <InlineMath math="25" /> from the source, which is <InlineMath math="800 \div 25 = 32 = 2^5" />, so <InlineMath math="5.0" /> cm of lead.

With <InlineMath math="3.0" /> cm the source gives <InlineMath math="800 \div 8 = 100" />, and the counter still picks up the <InlineMath math="20" /> background, reading <InlineMath math="120" /> counts per minute. The lead **cannot stop the background**.

**Lead and decay together**: <InlineMath math="10.6" /> years is two half-lives, so the source itself gives <InlineMath math="800 \div 4 = 200" /> counts per minute. Two centimetres of lead halve that twice more, to <InlineMath math="50" />, and the counter adds the background: <InlineMath math="50 + 20 = 70" /> counts per minute.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="4.2\text{ cm}" /> and <InlineMath math="51" /> halve the raw readings, but the **background** neither passes through the lead nor decays.
- <InlineMath math="50" /> counts per minute is the source alone, forgetting the counter still **adds the background**.
- <InlineMath math="220" /> counts per minute allows for the lead but forgets that the source **decays** in <InlineMath math="10.6" /> years.
