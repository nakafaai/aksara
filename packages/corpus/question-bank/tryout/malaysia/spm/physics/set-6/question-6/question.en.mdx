On the Moon, where the gravitational field strength is one sixth of the Earth’s, an astronaut throws the same ball straight up with the speed it had when it hit the ground on the Earth. Ignore air resistance.

How long after the throw does the ball come back to the astronaut’s hand, and how fast is it moving if the astronaut lets it fall past that hand to the ground, <InlineMath math="1.8\text{ m}" /> below the hand?
