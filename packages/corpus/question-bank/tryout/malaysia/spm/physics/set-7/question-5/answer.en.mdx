# The momentum the foam must take away

From <InlineMath math="1.25\text{ m}" /> the egg lands at <InlineMath math="\sqrt{2gh}=5.0\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, carrying <InlineMath math="0.30\text{ N s}" /> of momentum. **Whatever the pad does, it has to remove exactly that** before the egg is at rest.

# The pad also holds the egg up

Removing <InlineMath math="0.30\text{ N s}" /> in <InlineMath math="0.080\text{ s}" /> takes a net upward force of <InlineMath math="3.75\text{ N}" />. **The pad has to supply that and carry the egg's <InlineMath math="0.60\text{ N}" /> weight as well**, so it pushes with <InlineMath math="4.35\text{ N}" />, about <InlineMath math="4.4\text{ N}" />. Stopping at <InlineMath math="3.8\text{ N}" /> forgets the weight, and <InlineMath math="3.2\text{ N}" /> subtracts it as if gravity were helping the foam.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
p&=0.060\times\sqrt{2\times10\times1.25}=0.30\text{ N s},\qquad F=\frac{0.30}{0.080}+0.60=4.35\text{ N}\\
t_{\min}&=\frac{0.30}{6.0-0.60}=0.056\text{ s},\qquad s=\frac{5.0}{2}\times0.056=0.14\text{ m}
\end{aligned}" />

# How deep the foam has to give

With a steady slowing, the egg's average speed during the stop is **half of its landing speed**, <InlineMath math="2.5\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, so in <InlineMath math="0.056\text{ s}" /> the foam squashes <InlineMath math="0.14\text{ m}" />. Multiplying the full <InlineMath math="5.0\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> by the time asks for <InlineMath math="28\text{ cm}" />, twice the foam really needed.
