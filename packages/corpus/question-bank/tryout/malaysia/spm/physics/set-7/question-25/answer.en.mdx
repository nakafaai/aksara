# Boyle's law gives the air's pressure, and that pressure gives the depth

The trapped air is at the same pressure as the water at the **water surface inside the glass**. For a uniform glass the air's volume is proportional to its length, so Boyle's law gives the new pressure. The pressure **above atmospheric** then fixes the depth through <InlineMath math="p-p_0=\rho gh" />.

<BlockMath math="p=\frac{(1.0\times10^{5})(20)}{18}\approx1.11\times10^{5}\text{ Pa},\qquad h=\frac{1.11\times10^{4}}{1000\times10}\approx1.1\text{ m}" />

<BlockMath math="p'=\frac{(1.0\times10^{5})(20)}{10}=2.0\times10^{5}\text{ Pa},\qquad h'=\frac{1.0\times10^{5}}{1000\times10}=10\text{ m}" />

Squeezing the air from <InlineMath math="20\text{ cm}" /> to <InlineMath math="18\text{ cm}" /> raises its pressure by about <InlineMath math="1.1\times10^{4}\text{ Pa}" />, which needs about <InlineMath math="1.1\text{ m}" /> of water. Halving its volume doubles the pressure, which needs a full extra atmosphere, <InlineMath math="10\text{ m}" /> of water.

<BlockMath math="L=20\times\frac{1.0\times10^{5}}{2.0\times10^{5}}\times\frac{280}{300}\approx9.3\text{ cm},\qquad 20-9.3\approx11\text{ cm}" />

At <InlineMath math="10\text{ m}" /> the pressure doubles, which alone would halve the air to <InlineMath math="10\text{ cm}" />. Cooling from <InlineMath math="300\text{ K}" /> to <InlineMath math="280\text{ K}" /> shrinks it further to about <InlineMath math="9.3\text{ cm}" />, so the water stands about <InlineMath math="11\text{ cm}" /> high, a little more than half the glass.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="1.0\text{ m}" /> takes the pressure rise as **<InlineMath math="10\%" /> of atmospheric** because the volume fell by <InlineMath math="10\%" />, but <InlineMath math="\dfrac{20}{18}" /> is an <InlineMath math="11\%" /> rise.
- <InlineMath math="10\text{ cm}" /> uses Boyle's law alone and **ignores the cooling** from <InlineMath math="27" /> °C to <InlineMath math="7" /> °C, which shrinks the air further.
- <InlineMath math="9.3\text{ cm}" /> is the length of the **trapped air**, not the height of the water in the glass.
