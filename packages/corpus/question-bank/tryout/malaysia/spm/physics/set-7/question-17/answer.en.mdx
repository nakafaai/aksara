# Where the spot starts

The beam leaves the mirror at <InlineMath math="30^\circ" /> to the normal, and the normal points straight at P. So **the spot starts <InlineMath math="3.0\tan30^\circ=1.73\text{ m}" /> from P**, already off to one side, and every later distance is measured from there.

# The beam turns twice as far as the mirror

Turning the mirror by <InlineMath math="5.0^\circ" /> turns its normal by <InlineMath math="5.0^\circ" /> and raises the angle of incidence to <InlineMath math="35^\circ" />, so the reflected beam swings from <InlineMath math="30^\circ" /> to <InlineMath math="40^\circ" /> on the other side. **The reflected beam always turns through twice the mirror's angle**. The spot lands <InlineMath math="3.0\tan40^\circ=2.52\text{ m}" /> from P and has moved <InlineMath math="0.79\text{ m}" />. Turning the beam by only <InlineMath math="5^\circ" /> gives <InlineMath math="0.37\text{ m}" />, and the arc estimate <InlineMath math="3.0\times0.175=0.52\text{ m}" /> only fits a spot right opposite the mirror, where the tangent still grows like a straight line.

# Aiming the spot three metres from P

A spot <InlineMath math="3.0\text{ m}" /> from P on a wall <InlineMath math="3.0\text{ m}" /> away needs the beam at <InlineMath math="45^\circ" />, since <InlineMath math="\tan45^\circ=1" />. The beam must turn <InlineMath math="15^\circ" /> from its start, so **the mirror turns only half of that, <InlineMath math="7.5^\circ" />**. Turning the mirror the full <InlineMath math="15^\circ" /> would sweep the beam out to <InlineMath math="60^\circ" />, more than <InlineMath math="5\text{ m}" /> from P.

# Timing the spinning mirror

At <InlineMath math="2.0" /> revolutions per second the mirror turns <InlineMath math="720^\circ" /> every second, so its <InlineMath math="7.5^\circ" /> takes <InlineMath math="0.0104\text{ s}" />. In that time **the spot covers <InlineMath math="3.0-1.73=1.27\text{ m}" />, an average of <InlineMath math="122\text{ m s}^{-1}" />**. The value <InlineMath math="2\omega D=75\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> is the speed of a spot sitting right at P. Out here the beam hits the wall at a slant, so the spot runs faster, and it keeps speeding up as it moves away from P.
