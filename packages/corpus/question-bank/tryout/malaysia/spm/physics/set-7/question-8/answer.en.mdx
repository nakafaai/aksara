# Balance the inverse-square pulls, then scale g by the square of the distance

Each pull is proportional to **mass divided by distance squared**. For the pulls to be equal, the distances must be in the ratio of the **square roots** of the masses, <InlineMath math="\sqrt{81}:1=9:1" />. Then the Earth's own pull at that distance follows from its surface value, scaled by <InlineMath math="(R/r)^2" />.

<BlockMath math="\frac{81M}{r_E^2}=\frac{M}{r_M^2}\Rightarrow\frac{r_E}{r_M}=9,\qquad r_E=0.9\times3.84\times10^{8}=3.46\times10^{8}\text{ m}" />

<BlockMath math="g=9.8\left(\frac{6.4\times10^{6}}{3.46\times10^{8}}\right)^2=9.8\times3.42\times10^{-4}\approx3.4\times10^{-3}\text{ m s}^{-2}" />

The point is <InlineMath math="0.9" /> of the way to the Moon, <InlineMath math="3.5\times10^{8}\text{ m}" /> from the Earth's centre. That is <InlineMath math="54" /> Earth radii out, where the Earth's pull is only <InlineMath math="1/2900" /> of its surface value.

<BlockMath math="g_M=3.4\times10^{-3}\times\left(\frac{3.84\times10^{7}}{1.74\times10^{6}}\right)^2=3.4\times10^{-3}\times487\approx1.6\text{ m s}^{-2}" />

At the balance point the Moon pulls exactly as hard as the Earth, <InlineMath math="3.4\times10^{-3}\text{ m s}^{-2}" />, from <InlineMath math="3.84\times10^{7}\text{ m}" /> away. Moving in to the Moon's surface, <InlineMath math="22" /> times closer, multiplies that by <InlineMath math="22^2" />, giving about <InlineMath math="1.6\text{ m s}^{-2}" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="3.8\times10^{8}\text{ m}" /> makes the distances proportional to the **masses**, <InlineMath math="81:1" />, instead of to their square roots, <InlineMath math="9:1" />.
- <InlineMath math="0.18\text{ m s}^{-2}" /> scales <InlineMath math="g" /> by the **ratio of the distances**, forgetting the inverse square, and repeats the slip at the Moon.
- <InlineMath math="0.074\text{ m s}^{-2}" /> gets the balance point right but scales to the Moon's surface by **<InlineMath math="22" /> instead of <InlineMath math="22^2" />**.
