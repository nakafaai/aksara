# Count the gaps, not the claps, and let the sound go there and back

From clap <InlineMath math="1" /> to clap <InlineMath math="31" /> there are **<InlineMath math="30" /> gaps**, not <InlineMath math="31" />. In each gap the sound of one clap travels **to the wall and back**, a path of <InlineMath math="100\text{ m}" />. That gives the speed of sound, and the same speed fixes the new interval for a <InlineMath math="160\text{ m}" /> round trip.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="340\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> divides <InlineMath math="9.0\text{ s}" /> by **<InlineMath math="31" /> claps instead of <InlineMath math="30" /> gaps**, making each interval too short.
- <InlineMath math="170\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> uses only the **one-way distance** to the wall, forgetting that the echo must come back.
- <InlineMath math="0.24\text{ s}" /> and <InlineMath math="33\text{ m}" /> take the sound's path at the new position as **one way only**.
