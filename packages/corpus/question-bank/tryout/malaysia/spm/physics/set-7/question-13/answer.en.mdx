# Count the gaps, send the sound there and back, then let temperature set the new speed

From clap <InlineMath math="1" /> to clap <InlineMath math="31" /> there are **<InlineMath math="30" /> gaps**. In each gap one clap's sound travels **to the wall and back**. That gives the speed of sound, the formula turns the speed into a temperature, and the midday temperature gives the new speed for a longer round trip.

The morning air is at <InlineMath math="20" /> °C, where sound travels at <InlineMath math="343\text{ m s}^{-1}" />. At midday sound is faster, <InlineMath math="352\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, so the <InlineMath math="240\text{ m}" /> round trip takes about <InlineMath math="0.68\text{ s}" />.

<BlockMath math="t_{\text{read}}=(t_{31}+0.15)-(t_1+0.15)=t_{31}-t_1=8.75\text{ s}" />

A delay at the start and the same delay at the stop **cancel**: the stopwatch still reads <InlineMath math="8.75\text{ s}" />, so the measured speed does not change at all.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="39" /> °C divides by **<InlineMath math="31" /> claps instead of <InlineMath math="30" /> gaps**, which gives an impossible morning speed of <InlineMath math="354\text{ m s}^{-1}" />.
- <InlineMath math="0.70\text{ s}" /> keeps the **morning speed** at midday, forgetting that sound is faster in warmer air.
- <InlineMath math="6\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> higher lets **only one end** of the timing be late, but the same delay at both ends cancels.
