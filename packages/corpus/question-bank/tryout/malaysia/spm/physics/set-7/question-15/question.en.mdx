A car waits at a traffic light on a straight road that leads to a tall building. Its radio is tuned to an FM station broadcasting at <InlineMath math="100\text{ MHz}" /> from far behind the car. Waves reflected from the building meet the waves arriving directly, so along the road the signal fades at points spaced half a wavelength apart, with a strong signal midway between neighbouring fades. The car has stopped exactly at one of the fades. Radio waves travel at <InlineMath math="3.0\times10^{8}\text{ m s}^{-1}" />.

When the light turns green, the car accelerates uniformly from rest towards the building at <InlineMath math="1.6\text{ m s}^{-2}" />. The radio smooths out any fading that happens more than <InlineMath math="4" /> times a second. How far apart are the fades, above what speed does the fading blur into a steady hiss, and how many separate fades does the driver hear after moving off, before the fading blurs?
