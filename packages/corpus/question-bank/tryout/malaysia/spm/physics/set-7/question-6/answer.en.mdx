Picture stepping stones for a child whose strides grow by the same amount each time. If one stone is missing, one jump looks far too long. # Back to the release

Think of a car passing two lamp posts. Its average speed between them is its speed halfway along. **Half a flash after image <InlineMath math="1" /> the ball moved at <InlineMath math="0.196/0.10=1.96\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, so at image <InlineMath math="1" /> itself it moved at <InlineMath math="1.96-9.8\times0.05=1.47\text{ m s}^{-1}" />**. From rest, gaining <InlineMath math="9.8\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> every second, it needed <InlineMath math="1.47/9.8=0.15\text{ s}" />. Pinning the <InlineMath math="1.96\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> average on image <InlineMath math="1" /> gives <InlineMath math="0.20\text{ s}" />, half a flash too late.
