# Nuclei now

Activity is proportional to <InlineMath math="N/T_{1/2}" />. **Equal activities now mean Y, with three times the half-life, has three times as many nuclei**, so X : Y <InlineMath math="=1:3" /> now.

# Twelve hours later

**X goes through <InlineMath math="12/2=6" /> half-lives and Y through <InlineMath math="12/6=2" />**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{activities: }&\frac{(1/2)^6}{(1/2)^2}=\frac{1}{16}\\
\text{nuclei: }&\frac{1\times(1/2)^6}{3\times(1/2)^2}=\frac{1/64}{3/4}=\frac{1}{48}
\end{aligned}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- **Nuclei <InlineMath math="1:16" /> treats the nuclei as if they followed the activities, forgetting that Y started with three times as many**.
- Nuclei <InlineMath math="1:3" /> is the ratio now, not after <InlineMath math="12\text{ hours}" />.
- <InlineMath math="16:1" /> and <InlineMath math="48:1" /> give Y's share first, the reverse of X : Y.
