# Store energy in both cords

Each cord stores the area under its own line, and **two cords side by side each stretch the full <InlineMath math="8.0\text{ cm}" />**, so the energy doubles.

<BlockMath math="E=2\times\tfrac12(16)(0.080)=1.28\text{ J},\qquad 0.80\times1.28=1.024\text{ J}" />

# Count the climb inside the cords

The pellet is released <InlineMath math="8.0\text{ cm}" /> below the point where it leaves the cords. **The <InlineMath math="1.024\text{ J}" /> lifts it from the release point**, so part of it is used before the pellet is free.

<BlockMath math="h_{\text{release}}=\frac{1.024}{0.025\times10}=4.10\text{ m},\qquad h=4.10-0.08=4.02\text{ m}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="4.10\text{ m}" /> **measures from the release point, not from where the pellet leaves the cords**.
- <InlineMath math="1.97\text{ m}" /> uses the energy of one cord only.
- <InlineMath math="5.04\text{ m}" /> ignores the <InlineMath math="80\%" /> efficiency.
