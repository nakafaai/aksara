# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="6.4\text{ N}" /> **ignores the height difference in the oil**.
- <InlineMath math="6.1\text{ N}" /> subtracts <InlineMath math="\rho gh" />, as if the small piston were the higher one.
- <InlineMath math="125" /> strokes divides by the lever ratio, but the lever changes the force, not the volume of oil moved.
