# Read the whole spring, then halve it

The gradient is <InlineMath math="4\pi^2/k" /> and the intercept divided by the gradient is <InlineMath math="m_s/3" />. **Cutting the spring in half changes two things**: each half stretches half as much under the same force, so its spring constant doubles, and it has half the mass.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{m_s}{3}&=\frac{0.008}{0.16}=0.050\text{ kg}\\
\text{half spring: gradient}&=\frac{0.16}{2}=0.08,\quad \frac{m_s}{3}\to0.025\text{ kg}
\end{aligned}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="1.95\text{ kg}" /> **doubles <InlineMath math="k" /> but keeps the whole spring's mass term of <InlineMath math="0.050\text{ kg}" />**.
- <InlineMath math="0.98\text{ kg}" /> halves the mass but keeps the old spring constant.
- <InlineMath math="0.95\text{ kg}" /> uses the whole spring as if it had never been cut.
