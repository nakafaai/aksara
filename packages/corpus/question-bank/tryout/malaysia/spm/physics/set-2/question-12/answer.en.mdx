# The first mixing measures the container

**The container is not negligible: the first mixing shows how much heat it takes**. The hot water gives up heat, and the cold water and the container take it in.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
0.10\times4200\times(90-40)&=(0.20\times4200+C)(40-20)\\
21\,000&=(840+C)\times20\ \Rightarrow\ C=210\text{ J}\,^\circ\text{C}^{-1}
\end{aligned}" />

# The second mixing uses all of it

**Now <InlineMath math="0.30\text{ kg}" /> of water and the container warm by <InlineMath math="5^\circ\text{C}" /> while the block cools by <InlineMath math="55^\circ\text{C}" />**.

<BlockMath math="0.20\times c\times55=(0.30\times4200+210)\times5=7350\ \Rightarrow\ c=670\text{ J kg}^{-1}\,^\circ\text{C}^{-1}" />
