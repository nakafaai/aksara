# Upthrust while the block hangs

**The block's volume is <InlineMath math="0.50/2500=2.0\times10^{-4}\text{ m}^3" />, so the upthrust is <InlineMath math="1000\times2.0\times10^{-4}\times10=2.0\text{ N}" />, and the spring balance reads <InlineMath math="5.0-2.0=3.0\text{ N}" />**.

# What the top pan feels once the thread is cut

**With the thread cut, nothing outside the beaker holds the block up any more**, so the top pan carries the whole weight of the block as well as the beaker and water.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="1.40\text{ kg}" /> **is the reading while the block still hangs from the thread**.
- <InlineMath math="1.50\text{ kg}" /> adds only the block's apparent weight of <InlineMath math="3.0\text{ N}" />, but the beaker now supports all of it.
- <InlineMath math="5.0\text{ N}" /> ignores the upthrust on the hanging block.
