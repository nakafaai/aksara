# Find the critical angle against water, not air

In air the critical angle is <InlineMath math="\sin^{-1}(1/1.50)=41.8^\circ" />, so <InlineMath math="45^\circ" /> would give total internal reflection. **Under water the critical angle uses the ratio of the two indices**, <InlineMath math="\sin c=\dfrac{1.33}{1.50}" />, which gives <InlineMath math="c=62.5^\circ" />. Now <InlineMath math="45^\circ" /> is below it, so the ray escapes.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Total internal reflection uses the critical angle against air. **Water lowers the change in index**, so the critical angle rises to <InlineMath math="62.5^\circ" />.
- <InlineMath math="39^\circ" /> swaps the indices, <InlineMath math="\sin r=\dfrac{1.33\sin45^\circ}{1.50}" />, as if the ray went from water into glass.
- <InlineMath math="45^\circ" /> ignores refraction, but the indices differ, so the ray bends.
