# The reactions must supply more than the output

**Only <InlineMath math="35\%" /> of the fusion energy becomes electrical energy**, so the reactions must release <InlineMath math="1.0/0.35=2.86\text{ GW}" />.

Each reaction uses one deuterium nucleus, so only its mass of <InlineMath math="2.01410\text{ u}" /> counts.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.10\text{ kg}" /> **ignores the efficiency, as if all fusion energy became electrical**.
- <InlineMath math="0.73\text{ kg}" /> counts the tritium as well as the deuterium.
- <InlineMath math="0.036\text{ kg}" /> multiplies by the efficiency instead of dividing.
