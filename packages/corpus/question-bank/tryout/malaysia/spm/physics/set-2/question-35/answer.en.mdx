# The load sets the ripple

Between peaks the capacitor discharges through the resistor. **A smaller resistance draws a larger current, so the capacitor loses its charge faster**, and the output dips further. **IV** is wrong.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only **rejects III, but <InlineMath math="20\text{ ms}" /> at <InlineMath math="10\text{ ms/div}" /> is exactly <InlineMath math="2" /> divisions**.
- Options with IV have the load the wrong way round, since a larger resistance gives the smoother output.
