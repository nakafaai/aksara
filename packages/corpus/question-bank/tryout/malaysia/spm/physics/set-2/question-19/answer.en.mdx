# Normal adjustment

**The objective forms its image at <InlineMath math="v" />, and for a final image at infinity that image sits at the eyepiece's focal point**.

<BlockMath math="\frac1v=\frac1{1.0}-\frac1{1.1}\ \Rightarrow\ v=11\text{ cm},\qquad d=11+5.0=16\text{ cm}" />

# Refocus for an image at <InlineMath math="25\text{ cm}" />

**The final image is virtual, on the same side as the object**, so its image distance is negative.

<BlockMath math="\frac1u=\frac1{5.0}-\frac1{-25}=\frac{6}{25}\ \Rightarrow\ u=4.17\text{ cm}" />

The objective's image stays put, so the eyepiece must come <InlineMath math="5.0-4.17=0.83\text{ cm}" /> closer to it, towards the objective.

# Why the other options are wrong

- **Moving <InlineMath math="0.83\text{ cm}" /> away would push the first image outside the focal point and give a real image**.
- <InlineMath math="1.25\text{ cm}" /> treats the final image as real, giving <InlineMath math="u=6.25\text{ cm}" />.
- <InlineMath math="11\text{ cm}" /> is the objective's image distance, not the separation of the lenses.
