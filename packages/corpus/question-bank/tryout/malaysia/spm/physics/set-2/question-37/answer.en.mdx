# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="3.3\text{ V}" /> is the swing the transistor would give with a larger supply. **The output cannot pass <InlineMath math="0" /> or <InlineMath math="6.0\text{ V}" />**.
- <InlineMath math="1.5\text{ V}" /> treats the collector current swing in milliamperes as a voltage, leaving out the resistor.
- <InlineMath math="6.0\text{ V}" /> takes the whole supply as the peak, but the steady point sits at <InlineMath math="3.0\text{ V}" />, halfway.
