# Power at the lower voltage

**The rating fixes the resistance, not the power**. With <InlineMath math="R" /> constant, power goes with the square of the voltage.

<BlockMath math="P=2000\times\left(\frac{220}{240}\right)^2=1681\text{ W},\qquad P_{\text{water}}=0.90\times1681=1513\text{ W}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="4.4\text{ minutes}" /> **uses the rated <InlineMath math="2.0\text{ kW}" /> at the lower voltage**.
- <InlineMath math="4.8\text{ minutes}" /> scales the power with the voltage instead of its square.
- <InlineMath math="4.2\text{ minutes}" /> divides the power by <InlineMath math="0.90" /> instead of multiplying, as if the losses added heat.
