The impulse is the triangle's area, <InlineMath math="\tfrac12(0.20)(10)=1.0\text{ N s}" /> east, and **the puck already carries <InlineMath math="0.60\text{ kg m s}^{-1}" /> west**.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.80\text{ m}" /> **east would put the puck beyond the wall**.
- <InlineMath math="0.20\text{ m}" /> east is how far the puck comes back from the wall, not its distance from where the push ended.
- <InlineMath math="0.80\text{ m}" /> west keeps the puck moving west, but the push reversed it.
