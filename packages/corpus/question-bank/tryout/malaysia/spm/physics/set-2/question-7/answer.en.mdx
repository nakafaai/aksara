# Compare the ball with the braking truck

The flight time comes from the vertical motion alone, <InlineMath math="t=\dfrac{2(8.0)}{10}=1.6\text{ s}" />. **Once released, the ball keeps the truck's <InlineMath math="15\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> forward**, but the truck slows down underneath it.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Back in her hand is true only if the truck keeps a constant velocity. **Braking slows her but not the ball**, so the ball gets ahead.
- <InlineMath math="2.56\text{ m}" /> behind her has the right size with the sign flipped, as if the truck had sped up.
- <InlineMath math="5.12\text{ m}" /> ahead leaves out the <InlineMath math="\tfrac12" /> in <InlineMath math="\tfrac12at^2" />.
