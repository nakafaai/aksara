A simple a.c. generator has a flat coil turning steadily in a uniform magnetic field, and it lights a lamp. Treat the coil and the lamp together as a fixed resistance, and ignore friction. The coil is then turned steadily at twice its original speed.

Which statements are correct?

I. The frequency of the output doubles.

II. The charge that flows through the lamp in each half-turn doubles.

III. The energy delivered to the lamp in each revolution doubles.

IV. The average torque needed to turn the coil doubles.
