# The loud sound is resonance: frequencies match, amplitudes do not matter

The air column sounds loudest when its natural frequency equals the fork’s <InlineMath math="512" /> Hz, so statement I is correct, and at **resonance** the fork passes energy to the air most effectively, so II is correct too.

Striking harder makes a larger amplitude at the **same** frequency, so the resonant length does not change, and III is wrong. A lower frequency means a longer wavelength, which needs a **longer** column, so IV is correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only leaves out IV, but a lower frequency does need a **longer** air column.
- I and III only accepts III, but the resonant length depends on the **frequency**, not the loudness.
- II, III and IV drops I, but resonance means the natural frequency **matches** the fork.
