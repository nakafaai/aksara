# Find the current, then use only the charge above the reserve

The two headlights take <InlineMath math="110" /> W together, so <InlineMath math="I = 110 \div 12 = 9.2" /> A.

The battery must keep <InlineMath math="30\%" /> for starting, so only <InlineMath math="70\%" /> of <InlineMath math="60" /> A h, <InlineMath math="42" /> A h, may be **used**. That lasts <InlineMath math="42 \div 9.17 = 4.6" /> h.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="4.6\text{ A}" /> counts only **one** headlight.
- <InlineMath math="6.5\text{ h}" /> drains the **whole** charge, leaving nothing to start the engine.
- <InlineMath math="2.0\text{ h}" /> uses the <InlineMath math="30\%" /> **reserve** as the charge that may be drained.
