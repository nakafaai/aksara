# Measure both depths down from the top face

The real depth is the thickness of the block, <InlineMath math="5.50 - 2.50 = 3.00" /> cm. The apparent depth runs from the top face down to the **image** of the mark, <InlineMath math="5.50 - 3.70 = 1.80" /> cm. So <InlineMath math="n = 3.00 \div 1.80 = 1.67" />.

The critical angle has <InlineMath math="\sin c = 1 \div n = 0.60" />, so <InlineMath math="c = 37^\circ" />.

**A ray at the top face**: leaving the glass would need <InlineMath math="\sin r = 1.67 \times \sin 40^\circ = 1.07" />, which is impossible. The <InlineMath math="40^\circ" /> ray is beyond the <InlineMath math="37^\circ" /> critical angle, so it is **totally reflected** back into the block.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.5" /> divides by the **rise** of the mark’s image, <InlineMath math="1.20" /> cm, instead of by its apparent depth.
- <InlineMath math="23^\circ" /> divides <InlineMath math="\sin 40^\circ" /> by the index, but light **leaving** glass bends away from the normal.
- Along the surface happens only **at** the critical angle, <InlineMath math="37^\circ" />, not beyond it.
