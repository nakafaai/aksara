# Measure both depths down from the top face

The real depth is the thickness of the block, <InlineMath math="5.50 - 2.50 = 3.00" /> cm. The apparent depth runs from the top face down to the **image** of the mark, <InlineMath math="5.50 - 3.70 = 1.80" /> cm. So <InlineMath math="n = 3.00 \div 1.80 = 1.67" />.

The critical angle has <InlineMath math="\sin c = 1 \div n = 0.60" />, so <InlineMath math="c = 37^\circ" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.5" /> divides by the **rise** of the mark’s image, <InlineMath math="1.20" /> cm, instead of by its apparent depth.
- <InlineMath math="53^\circ" /> is the **complement** of the critical angle.
- <InlineMath math="31^\circ" /> uses <InlineMath math="\tan c = 1 \div n" />, but the critical angle comes from the **sine**.
