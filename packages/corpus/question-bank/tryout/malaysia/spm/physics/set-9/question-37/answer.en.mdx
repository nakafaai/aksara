At first there were <InlineMath math="1.0 \times 10^{-3} \div 210 \times 6.02 \times 10^{23} = 2.87 \times 10^{18}" /> atoms. **Seven eighths** of them have decayed, each giving one alpha particle: <InlineMath math="2.5 \times 10^{18}" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="3.6 \times 10^{17}" /> counts the atoms **left**, not the ones that decayed.
- <InlineMath math="0.33\text{ mg}" /> divides by three, but three half-lives halve the sample **three times**.
- <InlineMath math="2.9 \times 10^{18}" /> counts every atom, but an eighth has **not yet decayed**.
