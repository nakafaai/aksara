# Why the other options are wrong

- RM <InlineMath math="240" /> charges every kWh at the cheaper rate and ignores the **second band**.
- RM <InlineMath math="288" /> charges every kWh at the dearer rate, which overcharges the first <InlineMath math="200" /> kWh.
- RM <InlineMath math="94" /> takes <InlineMath math="35\%" /> of the bill, but the energy saved all falls in the dearer band, so it saves more.
