# Count only the air above the reserve, then expand it to the pressure at depth

Only the pressure above the reserve can be used: <InlineMath math="12" /> L at <InlineMath math="20 - 5.0 = 15" /> MPa. At <InlineMath math="100" /> kPa that air would fill <InlineMath math="12 \times 15 \div 0.10 = 1800" /> L.

At <InlineMath math="20" /> m the surrounding pressure is <InlineMath math="100 + 1030 \times 10 \times 20 \div 1000 = 306" /> kPa, so the usable air fills only <InlineMath math="1800 \times 100 \div 306 = 588" /> L there. At <InlineMath math="20" /> L a minute it lasts <InlineMath math="29" /> minutes, because **each breath at depth** holds three times as much air.

# Why the other options are wrong

- About <InlineMath math="90" /> minutes breathes the air at **surface pressure**, but at <InlineMath math="20" /> m each breath is squeezed to <InlineMath math="306" /> kPa.
- About <InlineMath math="39" /> minutes uses the whole <InlineMath math="20" /> MPa and forgets the reserve that must be left.
- About <InlineMath math="120" /> minutes forgets both the reserve and the depth.
