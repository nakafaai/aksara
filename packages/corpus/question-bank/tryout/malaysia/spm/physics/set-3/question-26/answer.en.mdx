# Pressure difference from the tubes

<BlockMath math="\Delta P=\rho gh=1000\times10\times0.60=6000\text{ Pa}" />

# Continuity links the two speeds

**The same volume passes each section every second, so the throat, with half the area, carries water twice as fast**: <InlineMath math="v_2=2v_1" />.

<BlockMath math="6000=\tfrac12(1000)\left(4v_1^2-v_1^2\right)=1500\,v_1^2\ \Rightarrow\ v_1=2.0\text{ m s}^{-1}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="3.5\text{ L}" /> **treats the wide part as still water, using <InlineMath math="\Delta P=\tfrac12\rho v_2^2" />**.
- <InlineMath math="1.8\text{ L}" /> makes the throat speed <InlineMath math="4v_1" />, squaring the area ratio.
- <InlineMath math="8.0\text{ L}" /> multiplies the throat speed by the wide area.
