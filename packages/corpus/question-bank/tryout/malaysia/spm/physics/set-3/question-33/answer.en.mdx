# Induced currents in the copper

**The falling magnet changes the flux through each ring of the copper tube, which induces currents circling the tube**. By Lenz's law these oppose the motion, so the magnet falls more slowly. I is correct, and the currents heat the copper, so III is correct.

# The slit and the open coil

**A slit along the tube breaks the closed paths around it**, so the circling currents are much smaller and the magnet falls faster, not slower. II is wrong.

An e.m.f. comes from the changing flux whether or not a current can flow, so the open coil still has an induced e.m.f. across its ends. IV is correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and III only **misses IV, confusing an e.m.f. with a current**.
- Options with II have the slit's effect reversed.
