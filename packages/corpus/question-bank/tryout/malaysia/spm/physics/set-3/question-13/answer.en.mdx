# Equal temperature, not equal energy

**Thermal equilibrium means equal temperature and no net heat flow, not equal internal energy**. Internal energy also depends on the mass and the material, so I is wrong and II is correct.

# Energy bookkeeping

**The flask is insulated, so no heat leaves the pair: whatever the block gives up, the water takes in**. III is correct.

Steam at <InlineMath math="100^\circ\text{C}" /> holds the latent heat of vaporisation that water at <InlineMath math="100^\circ\text{C}" /> does not, so it has more internal energy at the same temperature. IV is correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- **Options with I treat equilibrium as equal energy**.
- II and IV only rejects III, but in an insulated flask no heat escapes.
