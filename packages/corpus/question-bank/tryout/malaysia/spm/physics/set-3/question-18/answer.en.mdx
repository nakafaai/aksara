# Why the image runs away

**Near the focal point, a small change in object distance makes a large change in image distance**. The object moved <InlineMath math="5\text{ cm}" /> toward the focus, and the real image moved <InlineMath math="40\text{ cm}" /> away from the mirror.

# Why the other options are wrong

- **Toward the mirror has the direction reversed: as the object nears the focus, the image recedes**.
- A magnification of <InlineMath math="2" /> is the value before the move.
- <InlineMath math="5\text{ cm}" /> assumes the image moves as much as the object.
