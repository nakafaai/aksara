# Work back from the side of the core to the end of the fibre

At the core-cladding boundary, <InlineMath math="\sin c = 1.40 \div 1.50 = 0.933" />, so <InlineMath math="c = 69^\circ" />. The cladding, **not air**, is the second medium here.

For total internal reflection the ray must hit the side at <InlineMath math="69^\circ" /> or more to the normal, so inside the core it makes at most <InlineMath math="90 - 69 = 21^\circ" /> with the axis. At the flat end, refraction from air: <InlineMath math="\sin \theta = 1.50 \sin 21^\circ = 0.54" />, so <InlineMath math="\theta = 33^\circ" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="42^\circ" /> is the critical angle for glass against **air**, but the core is surrounded by cladding.
- <InlineMath math="21^\circ" /> is the angle inside the core, before refraction at the end bends it out to <InlineMath math="33^\circ" /> in air.
- <InlineMath math="21^\circ" /> is the complement of the critical angle, not the critical angle itself.
