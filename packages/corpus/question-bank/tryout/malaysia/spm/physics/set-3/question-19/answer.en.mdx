# Which way the lens goes

**A nearer image needs the object farther from the lens**. The slide must sit <InlineMath math="10.53-10.26=0.27\text{ cm}" /> farther away, so the lens moves <InlineMath math="2.7\text{ mm}" /> away from the slide.

# Why the other options are wrong

- **Toward the slide reverses the direction, which would push the image farther out**.
- <InlineMath math="39" /> is the magnification before the screen moved.
- <InlineMath math="27\text{ mm}" /> slips a factor of ten in the shift, and <InlineMath math="20" /> divides the screen distance by the focal length instead of by the object distance.
