**Swapping the wires makes the two speakers push in antiphase**. The midpoint line is equally far from both, so the waves now arrive half a cycle apart and cancel. III is correct.

The spacing grows in proportion to <InlineMath math="D" />, so moving the line to <InlineMath math="20\text{ m}" /> doubles it to <InlineMath math="6.7\text{ m}" />. IV is wrong.
