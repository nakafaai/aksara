# Would it clear the wall?

Without the wall, the ball would fall for <InlineMath math="\sqrt{2(1.25)/10}=0.50\text{ s}" /> and land <InlineMath math="3.0\times0.50=1.5\text{ m}" /> away. **That is beyond the wall at <InlineMath math="1.2\text{ m}" />, so the ball meets the wall first**.

# Why the other options are wrong

- **The floor at <InlineMath math="1.5\text{ m}" /> ignores the wall standing in the way**.
- <InlineMath math="0.80\text{ m}" /> is how far the ball has fallen, not its height above the floor.
- The foot of the wall would need a range of exactly <InlineMath math="1.2\text{ m}" />, which the speed does not give.
