# The measured length misses the radius

The true length to the centre is <InlineMath math="L=l+r" />, so the graph is

<BlockMath math="T^2=\frac{4\pi^2}{g}\,l+\frac{4\pi^2}{g}\,r" />

**The gradient gives <InlineMath math="g" />, and the intercept divided by the gradient gives the radius, not the diameter**.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.0\text{ cm}" /> **is the radius, since the length was measured to the top of the bob**.
- <InlineMath math="39.5\text{ m s}^{-2}" /> multiplies the gradient by <InlineMath math="\pi^2" /> instead of dividing <InlineMath math="4\pi^2" /> by it.
- <InlineMath math="8.0\text{ cm}" /> reads the intercept as a length directly, without dividing by the gradient.
