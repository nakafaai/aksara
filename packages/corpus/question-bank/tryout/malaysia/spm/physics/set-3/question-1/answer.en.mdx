# Density from the cube of the diameter

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
V&=\frac{\pi(6.75\times10^{-3})^3}{6}=1.61\times10^{-7}\text{ m}^3\\
\rho&=\frac{1.26\times10^{-3}}{1.61\times10^{-7}}=7.82\times10^3\text{ kg m}^{-3}
\end{aligned}" />

**A small slip in <InlineMath math="d" /> grows threefold in the volume, which is why the options sit so close**.
