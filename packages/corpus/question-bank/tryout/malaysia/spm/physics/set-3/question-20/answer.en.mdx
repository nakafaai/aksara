# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="4.0\text{ cm}" /> **treats the whole <InlineMath math="16\text{ cm}" /> as water**.
- <InlineMath math="5.1\text{ cm}" /> uses the average of the two refractive indices for the whole depth.
- <InlineMath math="6.0\text{ cm}" /> treats the whole <InlineMath math="16\text{ cm}" /> as oil.
