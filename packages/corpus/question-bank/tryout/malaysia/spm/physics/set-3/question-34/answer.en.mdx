# Dark or light

**An LDR's resistance rises as the light fades**, which raises its share of the voltage. The base reaches <InlineMath math="0.7\text{ V}" /> once the LDR is above <InlineMath math="1.3\text{ k}\Omega" />, so the lamp lights in darkness.

# Why the other options are wrong

- **Bright light lowers the LDR's resistance and turns the transistor off**.
- <InlineMath math="76\text{ k}\Omega" /> solves for the LDR as if it were the upper part of the divider.
- <InlineMath math="1.2\text{ k}\Omega" /> takes <InlineMath math="0.7/6.0" /> of <InlineMath math="10\text{ k}\Omega" />, forgetting that the LDR is part of the total.
