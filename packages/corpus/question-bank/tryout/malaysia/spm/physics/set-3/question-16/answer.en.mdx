# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="680\text{ m}" /> **treats the car as standing still while the sound travels**.
- <InlineMath math="720\text{ m}" /> is the distance when the horn was sounded.
- <InlineMath math="600\text{ m}" /> subtracts the car's <InlineMath math="80\text{ m}" /> from the standing-car answer of <InlineMath math="680\text{ m}" />.
