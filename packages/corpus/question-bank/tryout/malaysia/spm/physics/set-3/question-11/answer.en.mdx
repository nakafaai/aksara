# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="6.68\times10^5" /> **divides by <InlineMath math="0.10\text{ kg}" />, half the mass**.
- <InlineMath math="8400" /> inverts the comparison of slopes: ice warms twice as fast per second as water does here, so its specific heat capacity is half, not double.
- <InlineMath math="1.67\times10^5" /> and <InlineMath math="1050" /> take the heater as <InlineMath math="100\text{ W}" />.
