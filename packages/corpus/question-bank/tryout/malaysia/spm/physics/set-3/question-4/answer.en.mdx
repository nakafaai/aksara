# When the gap is smallest

**The car brakes more gently, so it is still moving after the truck has stopped**, and the gap keeps shrinking until the car stops. The smallest gap is therefore reached at the very end, and the starting gap must cover the difference.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="17.5\text{ m}" /> **counts only the reaction distance, as if both braked equally hard**.
- <InlineMath math="69.6\text{ m}" /> is the car's whole stopping distance, as if the truck stopped instantly.
- <InlineMath math="13.0\text{ m}" /> compares the braking distances and forgets the car's reaction distance.
