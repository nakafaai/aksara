# Two changes to the resistance

<InlineMath math="R=V^2/P=240^2/1000=57.6\,\Omega" />. **Halving the length halves each piece's resistance, and putting the two halves in parallel halves it again**, so the new resistance is a quarter.

<BlockMath math="R'=\frac{57.6}{4}=14.4\,\Omega,\qquad P'=\frac{240^2}{14.4}=4000\text{ W}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2000\text{ W}" /> **counts only one of the two halvings**.
- <InlineMath math="4000\text{ W}" /> with the fuse holding misses that <InlineMath math="16.7\text{ A}" /> is above the fuse rating.
- <InlineMath math="250\text{ W}" /> multiplies the resistance by four instead of dividing.
