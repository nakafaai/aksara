# Work backwards from the slide

**Friction removes the block's kinetic energy over <InlineMath math="2.0\text{ m}" />, and the block with the bullet has a mass of <InlineMath math="1.00\text{ kg}" />**.

<BlockMath math="\tfrac12(1.00)v^2=4.0\times2.0\ \Rightarrow\ v=4.0\text{ m s}^{-1}" />

# Momentum, not energy, carries through the impact

**The bullet sticks, so kinetic energy is not conserved in the impact, but momentum is**.

<BlockMath math="0.010\,u=1.00\times4.0\ \Rightarrow\ u=400\text{ m s}^{-1}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="40\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> and <InlineMath math="0\%" /> **set the bullet's kinetic energy equal to the work against friction, as if the impact lost nothing**.
- <InlineMath math="1\%" /> is the share that survives the impact, not the share converted.
- <InlineMath math="396\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> uses only the block's <InlineMath math="0.99\text{ kg}" /> after the impact, leaving out the bullet inside it.
