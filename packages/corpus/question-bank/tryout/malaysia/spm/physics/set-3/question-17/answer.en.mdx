<BlockMath math="\sin c=\frac{1.40}{1.50}=0.933\ \Rightarrow\ c=69.0^\circ" />

# Refraction at the entry face

**The ray bends toward the normal as it enters, so outside the end it makes a larger angle**.

<BlockMath math="\sin\theta=1.50\sin21.0^\circ=0.538\ \Rightarrow\ \theta=33^\circ" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="21^\circ" /> **is the angle inside the core, before undoing the refraction at the end**.
- <InlineMath math="69^\circ" /> is the critical angle itself, measured from the normal to the side.
- <InlineMath math="90^\circ" /> uses the glass-air critical angle of <InlineMath math="42^\circ" />, as if there were no cladding.
