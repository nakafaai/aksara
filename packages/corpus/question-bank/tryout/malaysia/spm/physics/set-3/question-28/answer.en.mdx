# Two loads, two equations

**The same e.m.f. and internal resistance hold for both loads**, so <InlineMath math="E=I(R+r)" /> twice.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
E&=1.0(2.0+r)=0.50(5.0+r)\\
2.0+r&=2.5+0.50r\ \Rightarrow\ r=1.0\,\Omega,\quad E=3.0\text{ V}
\end{aligned}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.5\text{ V}" /> and <InlineMath math="0\,\Omega" /> **take the terminal voltage across the <InlineMath math="5.0\,\Omega" /> resistor as the e.m.f**.
- <InlineMath math="2.0\text{ W}" /> is the power in the external resistor, not inside the battery.
- <InlineMath math="4.0\text{ V}" /> and <InlineMath math="2.0\,\Omega" /> do not fit the second load, which would then carry <InlineMath math="0.57\text{ A}" />.
