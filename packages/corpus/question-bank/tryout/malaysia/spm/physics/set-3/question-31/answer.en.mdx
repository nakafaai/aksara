# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.30\text{ A}" /> **treats the transformer as ideal, with <InlineMath math="72\text{ W}" /> in**.
- <InlineMath math="0.27\text{ A}" /> multiplies by the efficiency instead of dividing, as if the input were less than the output.
- <InlineMath math="2.0\text{ A}" /> is the current in one lamp, not in the whole secondary coil.
