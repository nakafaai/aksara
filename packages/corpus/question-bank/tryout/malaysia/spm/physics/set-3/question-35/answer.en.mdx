- **Equal inputs are the two cases the gates block**.
- Both inputs at <InlineMath math="1" /> makes the NAND output <InlineMath math="0" />.
- A or B at <InlineMath math="0" /> includes <InlineMath math="00" />, where the OR output is <InlineMath math="0" />.
