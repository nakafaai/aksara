# Direction and size of the force

By Fleming's left-hand rule, reversing one of current or field reverses the force, so **reversing both reverses it twice and leaves it as it was**. I is correct.

The force is proportional to the current and to the field strength, so doubling one and halving the other leaves it unchanged. II is correct.

A wire lying along the field cuts no field lines across its length, and it feels no force. III is correct.

# Alternating current

**The force reverses every half cycle, so its average is zero, but the wire is pushed one way and then the other, and it vibrates at the supply frequency**. IV is wrong.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and III only **rejects II, but the force depends on the product of current and field**.
- Options with IV confuse a zero average with no force at any moment.
