# Momentum at the top

At the top the shell moves only horizontally, at <InlineMath math="v" />. **One half ends up at rest, so momentum makes the other half move at <InlineMath math="2v" />**.

<BlockMath math="mv=\tfrac{m}{2}(0)+\tfrac{m}{2}u\ \Rightarrow\ u=2v" />

# Same fall time, double the speed

**The top lies above <InlineMath math="60\text{ m}" />, and the fall from there takes half the full flight time, as in the unbroken flight**. In that time the unbroken shell would cover <InlineMath math="60\text{ m}" /> more, so the faster half covers <InlineMath math="120\text{ m}" /> more.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="240\text{ m}" /> **doubles the whole range instead of only the second half of the flight**.
- <InlineMath math="120\text{ m}" /> keeps the original landing point, ignoring the extra momentum.
- <InlineMath math="145\text{ m}" /> shares the kinetic energy instead of the momentum, giving <InlineMath math="u=\sqrt2\,v" />.
