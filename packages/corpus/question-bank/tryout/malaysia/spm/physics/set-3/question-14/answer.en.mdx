# Frequency stays with the source

<BlockMath math="f=\frac{v}{\lambda}=\frac{12}{1.2}=10\text{ Hz}" />

**The frequency is the same on both sides of the boundary**.

# Can the waves enter the deep region?

Refraction of water waves follows <InlineMath math="\dfrac{\sin i}{\sin r}=\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}" />.

<BlockMath math="\sin r=\sin40^\circ\times\frac{20}{12}=0.643\times1.67=1.07" />

**No angle has a sine above <InlineMath math="1" />, so no wave enters the deep region**. The critical angle is <InlineMath math="\sin^{-1}(12/20)=37^\circ" />, and <InlineMath math="40^\circ" /> is beyond it.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="23^\circ" /> **uses the speed ratio upside down, as if the waves entered slower water**.
- <InlineMath math="17\text{ Hz}" /> divides the deep-water speed by the shallow-water wavelength.
- <InlineMath math="6\text{ Hz}" /> divides the shallow-water speed by the wrong wavelength, <InlineMath math="2.0\text{ cm}" />.
