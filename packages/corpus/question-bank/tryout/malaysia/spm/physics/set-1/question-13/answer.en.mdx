Second, turn the number of gaps into a number of fringes. Six gaps between P and Q are bounded by **seven bright fringes**, just as six sections of fence need seven posts. So there are <InlineMath math="7" /> bright fringes from P to Q.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="6" /> is the number of gaps, but the question asks for **the number of fringes including P and Q**.
- <InlineMath math="10" /> uses the old spacing of <InlineMath math="3.0\text{ mm}" />, forgetting both changes.
- <InlineMath math="13" /> accounts for the nearer screen only, giving a spacing of <InlineMath math="2.25\text{ mm}" />.
