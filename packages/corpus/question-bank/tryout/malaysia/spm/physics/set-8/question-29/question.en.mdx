A washing machine has a metal case joined to earth by its earth wire. The resistance of the earth wire, including the path through the ground back to the supply's earth, is <InlineMath math="0.50\,\Omega" />. The plug holds a <InlineMath math="13\text{ A}" /> fuse in the live wire, and the supply is <InlineMath math="240\text{ V}" />. A fault inside the machine joins the live wire to the metal case, and the live wire from the supply to the fault has a resistance of <InlineMath math="0.30\,\Omega" />. At that moment a person standing barefoot on a wet floor is touching the case, and the path through their body and the floor to earth has a resistance of <InlineMath math="1000\,\Omega" />. Treat earth as being at <InlineMath math="0\text{ V}" /> and every resistance as constant.

What current flows through the fuse just after the fault occurs, what current flows through the person before the fuse melts, and if the earth wire had come loose instead, what current would flow through the person, and would the fuse melt?
