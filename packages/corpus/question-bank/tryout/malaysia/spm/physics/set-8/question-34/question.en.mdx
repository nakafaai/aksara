An aluminium ring rests over a vertical iron rod that passes through a coil. The coil is connected through a switch to a d.c. supply. The switch is closed suddenly. The experiment is then repeated with an aluminium ring that has a narrow cut across it, and then with the uncut ring after it has been cooled in liquid nitrogen, which lowers its electrical resistance.

What does the uncut ring do when the switch is closed, what does the ring with the cut do, and how does the cooled ring's jump compare with the jump at room temperature?
