A hiker stands on the flat floor of a straight canyon, between two parallel vertical walls, and claps once. The first echo is heard <InlineMath math="1.2\text{ s}" /> after the clap and the second <InlineMath math="2.0\text{ s}" /> after it. A little later the hiker hears a third echo, made by sound that has reflected once off each wall. Sound travels at <InlineMath math="340\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, and the hiker hears two sounds as one if they arrive less than <InlineMath math="0.10\text{ s}" /> apart.

How wide is the canyon, how long after the clap is the third echo heard, and how far from the point midway between the walls may the hiker stand so that the first two echoes are heard as one sound?
