# The lorry is done after two seconds

When a bus brakes hard, a bag on the seat slides forward, because the seat can only grip it so hard. The crate is that bag. # How hard the crate hits

A sledge on sand stops sooner than one on ice, because the sand keeps taking speed away. The crate loses <InlineMath math="4.0\times0.172=0.69\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> in the last stretch. **It meets the cab at <InlineMath math="12-0.69=11\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, and since the lorry is at rest that is also its speed relative to the lorry**.
