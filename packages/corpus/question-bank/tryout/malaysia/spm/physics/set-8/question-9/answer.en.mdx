# A see-saw in the sky

A grown-up and a small child can balance on a see-saw if the grown-up sits closer to the middle. C is the balance point of the two stars, so <InlineMath math="m_Ar_A=m_Br_B" />. **Star A circles at half B's radius, so A is the heavier star, with <InlineMath math="m_A=2m_B" />**. Picking the star on the bigger circle as the heavy one swaps the two masses.

# Two distances, two jobs

How hard two magnets pull on each other depends on how far apart they are, not on where you stand to watch. Gravity is the same, so the pull uses the full separation <InlineMath math="d=r_A+r_B=3.0\times10^{11}\text{ m}" />. The circle each star runs is a different matter, and it uses that star's own radius. **For star B, <InlineMath math="Gm_Am_B/d^2=m_B\cdot4\pi^2r_B/T^2" />, so <InlineMath math="m_A=4\pi^2r_Bd^2/(GT^2)=1.06\times10^{30}\text{ kg}" />**, and the see-saw gives <InlineMath math="m_B=5.3\times10^{29}\text{ kg}" />. Putting the own radius in both places treats each star like a planet round a fixed sun and gives <InlineMath math="4.7\times10^{29}" /> and <InlineMath math="5.9\times10^{28}\text{ kg}" />. Those cannot be right, because their ratio is eight, not two.

# How fast star B travels

On a spinning roundabout, a child at the edge and a child near the middle take the same time for a turn, but the one at the edge travels much further. **Star B covers its whole circle in one period, <InlineMath math="v_B=2\pi r_B/T=2\times3.14\times2.0\times10^{11}/(1.0\times10^{8})=1.3\times10^{4}\text{ m s}^{-1}" />**. Using <InlineMath math="r_A" /> there gives <InlineMath math="6.3\times10^{3}\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, which is star A's speed. As a last check, the two masses add up to <InlineMath math="4\pi^2d^3/(GT^2)=1.6\times10^{30}\text{ kg}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\frac{Gm_Am_B}{d^2}&=m_B\frac{4\pi^2r_B}{T^2}\ \Rightarrow\ m_A=\frac{4\pi^2r_Bd^2}{GT^2}=1.1\times10^{30}\text{ kg}\\
m_B&=\frac{m_Ar_A}{r_B}=5.3\times10^{29}\text{ kg},\qquad m_A+m_B=\frac{4\pi^2d^3}{GT^2}=1.6\times10^{30}\text{ kg}\\
v_B&=\frac{2\pi r_B}{T}=\frac{2\times3.14\times2.0\times10^{11}}{1.0\times10^{8}}=1.3\times10^{4}\text{ m s}^{-1}
\end{aligned}" />
