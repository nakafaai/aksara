Each rear spring takes <InlineMath math="628" /> N of passengers, sinking <InlineMath math="3.3" /> cm, and then <InlineMath math="491" /> N of luggage, sinking <InlineMath math="2.6" /> cm more. In all the rear has sunk <InlineMath math="5.9" /> cm, **more than the front**.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="1.6 \times 10^4\text{ N m}^{-1}" /> shares the passengers **equally over four springs**, but the front carries more.
- <InlineMath math="4.6\text{ cm}" /> spreads the luggage over all four springs, but only the **rear springs** are under the boot.
- <InlineMath math="2.6\text{ cm}" /> counts only the luggage and forgets the rear had **already sunk** under the passengers.
