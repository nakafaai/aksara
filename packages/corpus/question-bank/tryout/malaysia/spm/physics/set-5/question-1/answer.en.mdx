# Correct the reading, share it among the sheets, then build one sheet's volume

A negative zero error is subtracted, which **adds** to the reading: <InlineMath math="2.46 - (-0.02) = 2.48" /> cm. One sheet is <InlineMath math="2.48 \div 200 = 0.0124" /> cm, or <InlineMath math="0.124" /> mm.

One sheet fills <InlineMath math="21.0 \times 29.7 \times 0.0124 = 7.73\text{ cm}^3" /> and has a mass of <InlineMath math="2500 \div 500 = 5.0" /> g, so <InlineMath math="\rho = 5.0 \div 7.73 = 0.647\text{ g cm}^{-3}" />.

**Sheets in the box**: each sheet is <InlineMath math="0.0124" /> cm thick, so a <InlineMath math="5.0" /> cm box holds <InlineMath math="5.0 \div 0.0124 = 403" /> sheets, counting with the **corrected** thickness.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="0.657" /> and <InlineMath math="410" /> subtract the zero error instead of adding it, making each sheet **too thin**.
- <InlineMath math="0.0647" /> and <InlineMath math="40" /> slip a factor of ten between **millimetres and centimetres**.
- <InlineMath math="0.652" /> and <InlineMath math="407" /> ignore the zero error and use the **raw reading**.
