**Hearing it together**: the listener’s total delay is <InlineMath math="6.0" /> ms, and sound covers <InlineMath math="340 \times 6.0 \times 10^{-3} = 2.0" /> m in that time, so the presenter would have to stand about <InlineMath math="2.0" /> m from the loudspeaker, **as close as the listener sits to her radio**.

# Why the other options are wrong

- The presenter first counts only the **radio delay** and forgets the sound in the studio.
- About <InlineMath math="7.8\text{ m}" /> is how much **closer** the presenter must move, not the distance from the loudspeaker.
- About <InlineMath math="29\text{ ms}" /> ignores the <InlineMath math="2.0" /> m of **air between the radio and the listener**.
