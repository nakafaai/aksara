# Separate water taken up from water actually lost to the air

The potometer measures **water taken up** by the shoot. The balance measures **water lost** from the whole apparatus, and with the water surface sealed, the only way out is transpiration from the leaves. The two are not the same.

<BlockMath math="\text{transpiration rate}=\frac{4.2\text{ g}}{2\text{ h}}=2.1\text{ g per hour},\qquad \text{kept}=4.8-4.2=0.6\text{ g}" />

The <InlineMath math="0.6\text{ g}" /> taken up but not lost stays in the shoot. Some keeps the cells turgid, some is used as a raw material in photosynthesis, and some goes into growing cells.

So transpiration is <InlineMath math="2.1\text{ g}" /> per hour, and <InlineMath math="0.6\text{ g}" /> stayed in the shoot.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.4\text{ g}" /> per hour uses the potometer reading, **which measures uptake, not loss**.
- <InlineMath math="4.2\text{ g}" /> per hour is the loss over two hours, **not divided by the time**.
- Evaporation from the capillary tube is ruled out, **because the water surface is sealed with oil**.
