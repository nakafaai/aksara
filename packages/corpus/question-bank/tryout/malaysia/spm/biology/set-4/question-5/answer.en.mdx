# Ask what each enzyme finds when its turn comes

Protein is digested **in stages**, and each enzyme needs the right substrate and the right pH. So the order matters: a later enzyme can only work on what the earlier one left.

- I: P and Q differ only in pH. Pepsin digests at pH <InlineMath math="2" /> and does nothing at pH <InlineMath math="8" />. Statement I is correct.
- II: peptidase alone, in R, barely makes amino acids from whole protein. After pepsin has made polypeptides, in S, it releases a high level. Statement II is correct.
- III: S and T hold the same enzymes but give very different results, so the order matters. Statement III is wrong.
- IV: in T, peptidase acts first on whole protein and achieves little. Pepsin then makes polypeptides, but **no peptidase acts after it**, so the chains are never cut into amino acids. Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, II and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and III only ignores the order, **although tubes S and T differ tenfold**.
- I and IV only rejects II, **although peptidase works well only after pepsin**.
- II, III and IV only rejects the pH effect that P and Q show directly.
