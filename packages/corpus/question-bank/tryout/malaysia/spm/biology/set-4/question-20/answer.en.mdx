# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and III only accepts III, **although the men's curve never reaches the osteoporosis level**.
- I and IV only rejects the reserve from a higher peak and claims exercise lowers the peak, **when it builds bone**.
- II and IV only rejects the steep loss after menopause, **the largest drop in the women's curve**.
