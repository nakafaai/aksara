# Count only the activity Z really has in the duodenum, then round up to whole capsules

The rule counts the activity **at the pH of the duodenum**, not the <InlineMath math="25\,000" /> units printed for pH <InlineMath math="8" />. So work out what the meal needs, what one capsule really gives, and only then divide.

**Step 1: what the meal needs**.

<BlockMath math="36\text{ g}\times2000\text{ units per g} = 72\,000\text{ units}" />

**Step 2: what one coated capsule gives**. The coat stays shut at pH <InlineMath math="2" />, so the acid never touches Z, and all of it reaches the duodenum. There, at pH <InlineMath math="6" />, Z works at only <InlineMath math="40\%" /> of its activity at pH <InlineMath math="8" />:

<BlockMath math="25\,000\times0.40 = 10\,000\text{ units per coated capsule}" />

**Step 3: whole coated capsules**.

<BlockMath math="\frac{72\,000}{10\,000} = 7.2" />

Seven capsules give only <InlineMath math="70\,000" /> units, short of <InlineMath math="72\,000" />, and nobody can swallow <InlineMath math="0.2" /> of a capsule. So the patient needs <InlineMath math="8" />.

**Step 4: plain capsules**. A plain capsule opens in the stomach, and the test shows that an hour at pH <InlineMath math="2" /> leaves only <InlineMath math="5\%" /> of the activity, **even back at pH <InlineMath math="8" />**. The rest has been denatured for good. The survivors then work at <InlineMath math="40\%" /> in the duodenum:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
25\,000\times0.05\times0.40 &= 500\text{ units per plain capsule}\\
\frac{72\,000}{500} &= 144\text{ plain capsules}
\end{aligned}" />

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="3" /> coated or <InlineMath math="58" /> plain capsules divides by the activity at pH <InlineMath math="8" />, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{72\,000}{25\,000} = 2.88" />. **The rule counts the activity at the duodenum's pH <InlineMath math="6" />**, which is only <InlineMath math="40\%" /> of it.
- <InlineMath math="8" /> coated or <InlineMath math="8" /> plain capsules treats the acid as if it only paused Z. **The test shows that Z kept at pH <InlineMath math="2" /> stays dead even back at pH <InlineMath math="8" />**: only <InlineMath math="5\%" /> survives.
- <InlineMath math="7" /> coated capsules rounds <InlineMath math="7.2" /> down. **Seven capsules give <InlineMath math="70\,000" /> units, less than the <InlineMath math="72\,000" /> needed**, so the count must be rounded up.
