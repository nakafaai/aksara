Piece B is held under water in a syringe while the plunger is pulled, so that the air is drawn out of its air spaces and water takes its place. Piece B is then weighed. The density of water is <InlineMath math="1.00\text{ g cm}^{-3}" />.

| Piece | Mass (<InlineMath math="\text{g}" />) |
| --- | --- |
| A, untreated | <InlineMath math="0.80" /> |
| B, air spaces filled with water | <InlineMath math="2.06" /> |

Which of the following statements are correct?

I. The leaf stalk floats because its cell material is less dense than water.

II. Up to about <InlineMath math="95\%" /> of the air in the air spaces of piece A could be replaced by water before the piece sinks.

III. The air spaces make up about <InlineMath math="63\%" /> of the volume of the water hyacinth leaf stalk.

IV. With no stomata and a very thin cuticle, the leaves of *Hydrilla* sp. take in dissolved carbon dioxide and mineral salts directly across their whole surface.
