# Work out the share digested in each group, then split it between the termite and its flagellates

Cellulose that is digested does not come out in the faeces. So the share digested is **cellulose eaten minus cellulose in the faeces, divided by cellulose eaten**.

## Share of the cellulose digested

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{P, untreated:}\quad & \frac{400-80}{400}=\frac{320}{400}=80\%\\
\text{Q, no flagellates:}\quad & \frac{360-342}{360}=\frac{18}{360}=5\%\\
\text{R, flagellates returned:}\quad & \frac{380-95}{380}=\frac{285}{380}=75\%
\end{aligned}" />

Digestion and survival **rise and fall together**. Statement I is correct.

## Statement II

The termites in group S had the same heat treatment as group Q, yet <InlineMath math="90" /> of them are alive. So heating did not kill them. Fed on glucose, which they can use directly, they survive, while group Q on cellulose dies. Group R, given flagellates back, digests cellulose again and survives. The termites in Q die because **they cannot get energy from cellulose without the flagellates**. Statement II is correct.

## Statement III

The termite gives the flagellates food and a home, and the flagellates break down cellulose into sugars that the termite absorbs. Without them the termite starves. Both partners benefit, so this is **mutualism**, not parasitism. Statement III is wrong.

## Statement IV

The termite's own enzymes digest <InlineMath math="5\%" /> of the cellulose eaten, and by the assumption they do the same in untreated termites. The rest of the <InlineMath math="80\%" /> is **the flagellates' work**. As a share of everything digested:

<BlockMath math="\frac{80\%-5\%}{80\%}=\frac{75}{80}\approx94\%" />

Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, II and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only **stops at the difference <InlineMath math="80\%-5\%=75\%" />** and reads it as the flagellates' share. Those <InlineMath math="75" /> percentage points are a share of the cellulose eaten. As a share of the cellulose digested they are <InlineMath math="\tfrac{75}{80}\approx94\%" />.
- I, II, III and IV calls the flagellates **parasites**. A parasite harms its host, but here the termite dies without the flagellates, so both partners gain.
- II and IV only takes **the cellulose in the faeces as the cellulose digested**, so termites without flagellates seem to digest <InlineMath math="\tfrac{342}{360}=95\%" />. The faeces hold what passed through undigested.
