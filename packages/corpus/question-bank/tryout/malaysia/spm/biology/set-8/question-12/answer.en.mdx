# The times are running totals, so subtract first, then work out each speed and carry it forward

The last column is not the time spent in each stretch. It is the time since the SA node fired, a **running total**. So the time spent in a stretch is its end time minus the end time of the stretch before it. Only then can you divide length by time.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{atrial wall} &: 50-0 = 50\text{ ms}, & \tfrac{50\text{ mm}}{50\text{ ms}} &= 1.0\text{ m s}^{-1}\\
\text{AV node} &: 150-50 = 100\text{ ms}, & \tfrac{5\text{ mm}}{100\text{ ms}} &= 0.05\text{ m s}^{-1}\\
\text{bundle of His and Purkinje fibres} &: 180-150 = 30\text{ ms}, & \tfrac{120\text{ mm}}{30\text{ ms}} &= 4.0\text{ m s}^{-1}\\
\text{ventricle muscle} &: 210-180 = 30\text{ ms}, & \tfrac{12\text{ mm}}{30\text{ ms}} &= 0.4\text{ m s}^{-1}
\end{aligned}" />

One millimetre per millisecond is one metre per second, which keeps the units simple.

The slow AV node holds the impulse back for <InlineMath math="100\text{ ms}" />, so the atria finish contracting before the ventricles start. The fast Purkinje fibres then spread it over the ventricles almost at once, so they contract together from the apex upwards.

- I: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{0.05}{1.0} = \tfrac{1}{20}" />. Statement I is correct.
- II: <InlineMath math="\tfrac{4.0}{0.05} = 80" />, not <InlineMath math="20" />. Statement II is wrong.
- III: the new path starts where the Purkinje fibres start now, at the end of the AV node, so only the <InlineMath math="30\text{ ms}" /> of the bundle of His and Purkinje fibres is replaced:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

That is more than <InlineMath math="0.2\text{ s}" /> later. Statement III is correct.

- IV: halving the speed in the AV node doubles **only that stretch**, from <InlineMath math="100" /> to <InlineMath math="200\text{ ms}" />. At <InlineMath math="150" /> beats per minute, the SA node fires every <InlineMath math="\tfrac{60}{150} = 0.4\text{ s}" />:

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}

\text{next firing of the SA node} &: 400\text{ ms}
\end{aligned}" />

The impulse reaches the outer surface <InlineMath math="90\text{ ms}" /> before the next firing. Statement IV is wrong.

# Why the other options are wrong

- II and III only uses the **running totals as the time in each stretch**. Then the AV node seems to take <InlineMath math="150\text{ ms}" />, its speed is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{5}{150}\approx0.033\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, one thirtieth of the atrial speed, and the Purkinje fibres at <InlineMath math="\tfrac{120}{180}\approx0.67\text{ m s}^{-1}" /> look exactly <InlineMath math="20" /> times as fast.
- I, III and IV only doubles the **whole** <InlineMath math="210\text{ ms}" /> to <InlineMath math="420\text{ ms}" />, as if every stretch slowed down, so the impulse seems to arrive after the next firing at <InlineMath math="400\text{ ms}" />.
- I only compares the <InlineMath math="300\text{ ms}" /> through muscle with the whole <InlineMath math="180\text{ ms}" /> from the SA node to the inner surface, a delay of only <InlineMath math="120\text{ ms}" />. **The new path replaces only the <InlineMath math="30\text{ ms}" /> stretch**, and the first <InlineMath math="150\text{ ms}" /> stay as they are.
