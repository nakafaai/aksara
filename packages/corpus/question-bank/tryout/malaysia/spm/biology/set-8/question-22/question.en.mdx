The rate of photosynthesis of sunflower leaves is measured by the half-leaf method. At 6:00, discs are punched from the left half of each leaf, on one side of the midrib. At 14:00, after <InlineMath math="8" /> hours, discs of the same area are punched from the right half of the same leaf. The discs are dried and weighed. At 6:00, both halves of a leaf have the same dry mass per unit area.

Three groups of leaves are used.

| Group | Treatment of the leaves from 6:00 to 14:00 | Change in dry mass from 6:00 to 14:00 (<InlineMath math="\text{mg per dm}^2" />) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | left untreated, in sunlight | <InlineMath math="+48" /> |
| B | phloem of the leaf stalk killed with steam at 6:00, in sunlight | <InlineMath math="+96" /> |
| C | phloem of the leaf stalk killed with steam at 6:00, leaf covered with aluminium foil | <InlineMath math="-24" /> |

Killing the phloem of the leaf stalk stops all transport of sugar out of the leaf, but the xylem still supplies water. Assume that killing the phloem does not change the rates of photosynthesis or respiration, that the rate of respiration is the same in light and in the dark, and that all the dry mass made or used is carbohydrate. Every <InlineMath math="180\text{ g}" /> of carbohydrate made by photosynthesis uses <InlineMath math="264\text{ g}" /> of carbon dioxide.

What is the gross rate of photosynthesis of the leaves, given as carbon dioxide fixed, and what percentage of the carbohydrate made by photosynthesis do the untreated leaves export?
