# Compare each treatment with the untreated seeds, and follow the two hormones

Dormancy is a balance: **abscisic acid holds the seed back** and **gibberellin pushes it to grow**. Moist cold tips the balance by lowering abscisic acid, and added gibberellin can tip it from the other side. In a cold country, staying dormant until spring keeps the seedling from emerging just before winter.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and III only rejects IV, **although <InlineMath math="100" /> to <InlineMath math="20" /> is a fall of <InlineMath math="80\%" />**.
- II, III and IV only rejects I and **makes dormancy a way to germinate early**.
- I, II, III and IV accepts II, **the reverse of what dormancy is for**.
