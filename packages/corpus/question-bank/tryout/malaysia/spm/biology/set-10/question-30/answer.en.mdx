# Follow both curves to week <InlineMath math="6" />, and judge growth by the factor, not the number added

A pesticide can win the first round and lose the season: **resistant survivors** breed, and with their **predators killed too**, nothing holds them back. How fast a population multiplies is the **factor** by which it grows, not the number it adds. Predators settle at a balance, because they depend on their prey.

So statements I, II and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only rejects IV, **the balance that makes biological control last**.
- II, III and IV only rejects I and **reads the larger number added as faster multiplication**.
- I, III and IV only accepts III and rejects II, **ignoring the resistant aphids that rebound in P**.
