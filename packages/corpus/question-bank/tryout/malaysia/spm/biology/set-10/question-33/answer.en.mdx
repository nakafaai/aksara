# Compare the yields, then follow the gene by pollen and by food

The gene came from the virus, but a single **coat protein** is not a virus: it cannot copy itself or infect. The gene moves to other plants only by **pollen**, so distance limits it. Eating a plant digests its DNA and proteins, so the gene does not enter the eater's cells.

- I: <InlineMath math="45" /> tonnes against <InlineMath math="10" /> tonnes per hectare is <InlineMath math="4.5" /> times as much. Statement I is correct.
- II: a virus needs its genes to enter a cell and make copies of itself. The papaya makes only one protein of the virus, with none of its genes, so it cannot pass the disease on. Statement II is correct.
- III: the share of seeds with the gene falls from <InlineMath math="5\%" /> to <InlineMath math="0.1\%" />, a cut of <InlineMath math="4.9" /> out of <InlineMath math="5" />, which is <InlineMath math="98\%" />. Statement III is correct.
- IV: the DNA in food is broken down by digestion, so the gene does not enter the eater's cells, and humans are not infected by this plant virus anyway. Statement IV is wrong.

So statements I, II and III are correct.
