# Read the growth as a factor per year, and judge each cut against its own starting value

From <InlineMath math="2" /> to <InlineMath math="8" /> to <InlineMath math="32" /> is **multiplying by <InlineMath math="4" />** each year, so each extra year multiplies again. A percentage cut is the drop divided by the **starting value of that quantity**, so the snails and the seedling loss must each be judged on their own.

<BlockMath math="32\times4\times4=512,\qquad \frac{32-4}{32}=87.5\%,\qquad \frac{60-10}{60}\approx83\%" />

- I: the ducks cut the snails from <InlineMath math="32" /> to <InlineMath math="4" />, by <InlineMath math="87.5\%" />, and the seedling loss from <InlineMath math="60\%" /> to <InlineMath math="10\%" />, by about <InlineMath math="83\%" />. The snail cut is the larger. Statement I is correct.
- II: year <InlineMath math="4" /> would have <InlineMath math="32\times4=128" /> snails, and year <InlineMath math="5" /> would have <InlineMath math="128\times4=512" /> per square metre. Statement II is correct.
- III: the ducks are a natural predator used to keep a pest down, which is biological control. Statement III is correct.
- IV: the snails eat rice plants, so they are primary consumers. The rice plants are the producers. Statement IV is wrong.

So statements I, II and III are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and III only rejects II, **stopping one year short of year <InlineMath math="5" />**.
- II and IV only rejects I and III and **puts the snail at the bottom of the food web**.
- I, II, III and IV accepts IV, **although the snail feeds on the producers**.
