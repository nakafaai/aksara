# Read the extra growth each unit adds, and count the years of growth left

Each extra unit adds less than the one before, a **levelling response** as the target cells approach their full response. On <InlineMath math="2" /> units the boy grows <InlineMath math="9-4=5\text{ cm}" /> a year more than untreated, for the <InlineMath math="8" /> years until his plates close. Hormones work only where the **target can still respond**: closed plates cannot lengthen bones, though adult tissues still respond in other ways.

So statements I and II are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and IV only accepts IV, **blaming receptors instead of the closed growth plates**.
- II, III and IV only rejects I and **forgets that an adult's bones can no longer lengthen**.
- I, III and IV only rejects II, **although each unit adds less growth than the one before**.
