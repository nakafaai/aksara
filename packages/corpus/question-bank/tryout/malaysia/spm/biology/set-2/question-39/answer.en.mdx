# Two net rates, one after the other

What matters is the **net loss**, transpiration minus uptake, and it changes when the wind stops. The plant can spare <InlineMath math="300-270=30\text{ g}" /> before it wilts.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
1\text{ to }2\text{ pm}&: 35-20=15\text{ g per hour},\quad 30-15=15\text{ g left to lose}\\
\text{from }2\text{ pm}&: 25-20=5\text{ g per hour},\quad \frac{15}{5}=3\text{ hours}
\end{aligned}" />

So the plant starts to wilt <InlineMath math="3" /> hours after 2:00 pm, at 5:00 pm.

# Why the other options are wrong

- About 1:50 pm uses transpiration alone and forgets the water the roots take in.
- 3:00 pm keeps the windy rate of <InlineMath math="15\text{ g}" /> per hour all afternoon, but **the loss slows when the wind stops**.
- "Never" ignores that the plant still loses <InlineMath math="5\text{ g}" /> more than it takes in every hour.
