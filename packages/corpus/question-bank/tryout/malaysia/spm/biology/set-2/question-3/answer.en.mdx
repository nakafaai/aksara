# Sort the particles by size, then follow the water

A Visking tube is **semi-permeable**: small molecules pass through its pores, large molecules do not. Glucose, iodine and water are small. Starch is a large polysaccharide.

- Iodine diffuses **into** the tube and meets the starch there, so the contents turn blue-black. Statement I is correct.
- Starch cannot leave the tube, so the iodine in the beaker never meets starch and the water stays yellowish brown. Statement II is wrong.
- Glucose diffuses **out** into the beaker, down its concentration gradient, so the beaker water gives a brick-red precipitate with Benedict's solution. Statement III is correct.
- The solution inside the tube is far more concentrated than distilled water, so water moves **into** the tube by osmosis and the tube becomes heavier. Statement IV is wrong.

So only I and III are expected.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, III and IV accepts IV, but water enters the tube by osmosis, so **its mass increases**.
- II and III accepts II, which needs starch to cross the membrane, and rejects I.
- I and II accepts II and misses the glucose that leaves the tube.
