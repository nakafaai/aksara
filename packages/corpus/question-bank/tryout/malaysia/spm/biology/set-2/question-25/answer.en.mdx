# Same auxin, opposite effect, and something must sense gravity

An organ curves towards the side that grows less. Auxin only collects on one side if the plant **senses which way is down**, and the root cap does that.

- I is correct: in the shoot the auxin on the lower side speeds up elongation, so the lower side grows faster and the shoot curves up.
- II is correct: in the root the same auxin slows elongation, so the lower side grows more slowly and the root curves down.
- III is correct: on a klinostat every side takes turns being lowest, so auxin ends up evenly spread and neither organ curves.
- IV is wrong: without its root cap the root cannot sense gravity, so auxin no longer collects on the lower side and the root grows straight.

So I, II and III are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II misses III, **the even spread on a klinostat**.
- I, II and IV accepts IV, but auxin collects below only when the root cap senses gravity.
- II, III and IV rejects I and accepts IV.
