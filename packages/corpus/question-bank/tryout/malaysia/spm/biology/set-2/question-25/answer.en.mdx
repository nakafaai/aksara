# Same auxin, opposite effect

An organ curves **towards the side that grows less**, because the faster side gets longer and bends the organ around the slower one.

  chart={"flowchart LR\n  G[Gravity] --> A[Auxin collects on the lower side]\n  A --> S[Shoot: lower side faster] --> S2[Curves up]\n  A --> R[Root: lower side slower] --> R2[Curves down]"}
/>

- In the shoot, auxin collects on the lower side and speeds up elongation there. The lower side grows faster, so the shoot bends upwards, away from gravity.
- In the root, auxin also collects on the lower side, but in a root it slows elongation. The lower side grows more slowly, so the root bends downwards, towards gravity.

So the lower side of the shoot elongates faster and the lower side of the root elongates more slowly.

# Why the other options are wrong

- The second option puts the faster growth on the upper side, but **auxin collects on the lower side** in both organs.
- The third option swaps the effect of auxin in shoots and roots.
- The last option gives both organs the same response, which would curve both upwards.
