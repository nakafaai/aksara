# Sort by the nucleus first, then by cells and walls

The five kingdoms split first on whether a cell has a **nucleus with a membrane**, then on how many cells there are, then on the wall and the way the organism feeds.

- P has no nucleus with a membrane, so it is a prokaryote: Monera.
- Q is a single cell with a nucleus. It photosynthesises, but **one cell is not a plant**, so it belongs to Protista.
- R has a wall of chitin, no chlorophyll, and digests dead matter outside its body: Fungi.
- S is many-celled, with cellulose walls and chlorophyll: Plantae.

So P is Monera, Q is Protista, R is Fungi and S is Plantae.

# Why the other options are wrong

- The first option swaps P and Q, but **only P lacks a nucleus**.
- The second option puts Q in Plantae because it photosynthesises, but a single cell belongs to Protista.
- The third option puts R in Plantae because it has a wall, but a chitin wall and absorptive feeding make it a fungus.
