# The nucleus decides first, then the wall and the feeding

Sort first on whether there is a **nucleus with a membrane**, because that splits Monera from every other kingdom. Only then do chlorophyll, the wall and the way of feeding matter.

- P has chlorophyll but no nucleus with a membrane, so it is a prokaryote, a blue-green bacterium: Monera.
- Q is a single cell with a nucleus that can photosynthesise and also feed on particles, so it belongs to Protista.
- R is a single cell, but its chitin wall and its absorbing, fermenting way of feeding make it a yeast: Fungi.
- S is many-celled, with cellulose walls and chlorophyll: Plantae.

So P is Monera, Q is Protista, R is Fungi and S is Plantae.

# Why the other options are wrong

- The first option puts P in Plantae because of its chlorophyll, but **a cell without a nucleus is always Monera**.
- The second option puts R in Protista because it is one cell, but a chitin wall and absorbed food make it a fungus.
- The third option puts Q in Plantae because it photosynthesises, but a single cell that also eats particles is a protist.
