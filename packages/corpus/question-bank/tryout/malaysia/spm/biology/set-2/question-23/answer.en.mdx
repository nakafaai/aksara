<BlockMath math="\text{photosynthesis}=\text{net uptake}+\text{respiration}." />

At <InlineMath math="8" /> units: <InlineMath math="6+4=10\text{ mg}" /> per hour.

The compensation point is where photosynthesis just equals respiration, so the net uptake is **zero**. It lies between <InlineMath math="2" /> units, at <InlineMath math="-1" />, and <InlineMath math="4" /> units, at <InlineMath math="+2" />. The net uptake rises by <InlineMath math="3" /> over those <InlineMath math="2" /> units, so it reaches zero after rising by <InlineMath math="1" />:

<BlockMath math="2+\frac{1}{3}\times2=2+0.67\approx2.7\text{ units}." />

So photosynthesis fixes <InlineMath math="10\text{ mg}" /> of carbon dioxide per hour at <InlineMath math="8" /> units, and the compensation point is at about <InlineMath math="2.7" /> units.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="6\text{ mg}" /> per hour is the **net** uptake, which still has respiration taken away.
- <InlineMath math="4" /> units is the first reading where the net uptake is positive, but the zero lies before it.
- The last option has the right compensation point but uses the net uptake for photosynthesis.
