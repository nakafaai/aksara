# Area of the field of view, then stomata per square millimetre

Density is the number of stomata per unit area, so first find the area of the field of view. The formula uses the **radius**, which is half the diameter: <InlineMath math="r=\frac{0.40}{2}=0.20\text{ mm}" />.

<Mermaid
  title={<>From the Field of View to the Density</>}
  description={<>The diameter of <InlineMath math="0.40\text{ mm}" /> gives a radius of <InlineMath math="0.20\text{ mm}" /> and an area of about <InlineMath math="0.1257" /> square millimetres, so <InlineMath math="30" /> stomata give about <InlineMath math="239" /> per square millimetre.</>}
  chart={"flowchart LR\n  D[diameter 0.40 mm] -->|÷ 2| R[radius 0.20 mm]\n  R -->|π r²| A[area ≈ 0.1257 mm²]\n  A -->|30 ÷ area| N[≈ 239 stomata per mm²]"}
/>

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{area}&=\pi r^2=\pi\times0.20^2\approx0.1257\text{ mm}^2\\
\text{density}&=\frac{30}{0.1257}\approx239\text{ stomata per mm}^2
\end{aligned}" />

So the lower surface carries about <InlineMath math="239" /> stomata per <InlineMath math="\text{mm}^2" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- About <InlineMath math="60" /> uses the diameter <InlineMath math="0.40\text{ mm}" /> as the radius, **which makes the area four times too large**.
- <InlineMath math="750" /> drops <InlineMath math="\pi" /> and divides <InlineMath math="30" /> by <InlineMath math="0.20^2=0.04" />.
- About <InlineMath math="24" /> divides by the circumference, <InlineMath math="2\pi r" />, which is a length, not an area.
