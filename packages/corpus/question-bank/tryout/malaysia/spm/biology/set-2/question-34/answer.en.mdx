# Take away the mother's bands, then match what is left

Every band of the child came from the mother or from the father. So remove the bands the mother can explain, and the father must carry **all** the rest.

The child has bands <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="3" />, <InlineMath math="4" />, <InlineMath math="6" /> and <InlineMath math="7" />. The mother has <InlineMath math="1" />, <InlineMath math="4" /> and <InlineMath math="7" />, so bands <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="6" /> must come from the father. P has band <InlineMath math="3" /> but not <InlineMath math="6" />, R has <InlineMath math="6" /> but not <InlineMath math="3" />, and only Q has both. So man Q could be the biological father.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Man P shares band <InlineMath math="3" />, but **he lacks band** <InlineMath math="6" />, which the mother cannot explain either.
- Man R shares band <InlineMath math="6" /> and band <InlineMath math="7" />, but band <InlineMath math="7" /> is already the mother's, and he lacks band <InlineMath math="3" />.
- The last option forgets that a father must explain every band the mother cannot, and only Q does.
