# Classify by the features that define each kingdom, in order

The first split is the **nucleus**: no nuclear membrane means Monera. After that, the cell wall and the level of organisation decide. Plants and animals have cells organised into **tissues**. Organisms with a nucleus but no tissues are Protista, whether they have one cell or a chain of identical cells, unless their chitin wall marks them as fungi.

- I: W has cellulose and chloroplasts like a plant, and many cells, but every cell is the same and there are no tissues. A filament like this is an alga, which belongs to Protista. Statement I is correct.
- II: X has a nucleus, a wall of **chitin** and no chloroplasts, which marks a fungus. Yeast is a fungus made of one cell. Statement II is correct.
- III: Y has no nuclear membrane and a peptidoglycan wall, so it is a bacterium in Monera. Being one cell is not what puts an organism in Protista. Statement III is wrong.
- IV: Z has chloroplasts but no cell wall and swims with a flagellum, like *Euglena*, so it belongs to Protista. Chloroplasts alone do not make a plant. Statement IV is wrong.

So statements I and II are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and IV only lets chloroplasts decide, **but Z is a single cell with no tissues**.
- II and III only puts the many-celled W in Plantae and Y in Protista, **although W has no tissues and Y has no nucleus**.
- I and III only rejects X as a fungus because it is one cell, **but a chitin wall marks fungi, including yeast**.
