# Compare levels by ratios, not by differences

DDT does not break down and is stored in fat, so each feeder keeps the DDT from everything it eats. This is **biomagnification**, and it is measured by how many times the concentration is multiplied.

<BlockMath math="\frac{25}{0.00005}=\frac{25\times100\,000}{5}=500\,000" />

Now find the factor for each step:

<BlockMath math="\frac{0.04}{0.00005}=800,\qquad \frac{0.5}{0.04}=12.5,\qquad \frac{2}{0.5}=4,\qquad \frac{25}{2}=12.5" />

The plankton take DDT straight from the water and concentrate it <InlineMath math="800" /> times, far more than any later step. The largest **increase in parts per million** is from the large fish to the bird, but that is a difference, not a factor.

So the concentration in the bird is <InlineMath math="500\,000" /> times that in the water, and the largest step is from the water to the plankton.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="500\,000" /> times with the step from the large fish to the bird uses the **largest difference**, <InlineMath math="25-2=23" />, instead of the largest factor.
- <InlineMath math="50\,000" /> times drops a zero when dividing by <InlineMath math="0.00005" />, **which is five hundred-thousandths**.
- <InlineMath math="12.5" /> times looks only at the **last step** of the chain, not from the water all the way to the bird.
