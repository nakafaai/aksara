# Add the waiting time to the growing time

The tube must cover the **whole path**, the style and the part inside the ovary, and it starts growing only after the pollen germinates.

<BlockMath math="\text{total}=30\text{ min}+9\text{ h }20\text{ min}=9\text{ h }50\text{ min}" />

The tube carries two male gametes into the embryo sac. One fuses with the **egg cell** to form the diploid zygote. The other fuses with the **two polar nuclei** to form the triploid nucleus that grows into the endosperm. This is double fertilisation.

So the tube reaches the ovule <InlineMath math="9" /> hours <InlineMath math="50" /> minutes after landing, and one male gamete fuses with the egg cell while the other fuses with the two polar nuclei.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="8" /> hours <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes adds the <InlineMath math="30" /> minutes but stops at the end of the style, **leaving out the** <InlineMath math="2\text{ mm}" /> inside the ovary.
- <InlineMath math="9" /> hours <InlineMath math="20" /> minutes covers the whole <InlineMath math="14\text{ mm}" /> but forgets the **thirty minutes before germination**.
- Both gametes fusing with the egg has the right time, **but the second gamete forms the endosperm nucleus**.
