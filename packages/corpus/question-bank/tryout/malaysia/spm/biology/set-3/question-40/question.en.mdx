A sheep is cloned in three steps.

- The nucleus is taken from an udder cell of ewe A, which belongs to a breed with a white face.
- The nucleus is removed from an egg of ewe B, which belongs to a breed with a black face, and the udder nucleus is fused with this egg.
- The embryo that grows is placed in the uterus of ewe C, which also has a black face. Lamb D is born.

Which of the following statements about lamb D are correct?

I. Lamb D has a white face, like ewe A.

II. The DNA in the nuclei of lamb D is the same as the DNA in the nuclei of ewe A.

III. The DNA in the mitochondria of lamb D comes from ewe B.

IV. Lamb D inherits half of its chromosomes from ewe C.
