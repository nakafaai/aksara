# Use each antibiotic to answer one question

The ampicillin plate asks, **does this bacterium have a plasmid at all**? The tetracycline plate asks, **is the tetracycline gene still whole**? The insulin gene breaks that gene, so a recombinant plasmid fails the second test.

/>

- Bacteria without any plasmid die on ampicillin, so all <InlineMath math="120" /> colonies hold a plasmid.
- A plasmid that closed up without the insulin gene keeps a working tetracycline gene, so its <InlineMath math="90" /> colonies grow on tetracycline too.
- A recombinant plasmid has a broken tetracycline gene, so its colonies grow on ampicillin but not on tetracycline.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="90" /> colonies growing on both plates have a **whole tetracycline gene**, so they hold the plasmid without the insulin gene.
- <InlineMath math="120" /> colonies counts every bacterium that took up any plasmid, **recombinant or not**.
- <InlineMath math="30" /> colonies that grow on tetracycline but not on ampicillin cannot exist here, **because only colonies that grew on ampicillin were copied**.
