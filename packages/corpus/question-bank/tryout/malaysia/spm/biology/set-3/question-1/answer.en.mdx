# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only treats the best pH tested as the optimum, **but three points cannot pin down a peak**.
- I, II and III only accepts II alongside III, **although III itself shows a value above pH** <InlineMath math="7" /> could beat it.
- III and IV only keeps <InlineMath math="31\text{ cm}^3" /> in the mean, so the ratio becomes <InlineMath math="2.0" />, and reads a slower enzyme as a **dead enzyme**.
