A plasmid carries a gene for resistance to ampicillin and a gene for resistance to tetracycline. The human insulin gene is inserted into the middle of the tetracycline resistance gene, which then no longer works. Some plasmids take up the insulin gene and some close up without it.

The plasmids are mixed with bacteria that are resistant to neither antibiotic, and the bacteria are spread on a plate containing ampicillin. <InlineMath math="120" /> colonies grow. Each colony is then copied onto a plate containing tetracycline in the same position, and <InlineMath math="90" /> colonies grow there.
