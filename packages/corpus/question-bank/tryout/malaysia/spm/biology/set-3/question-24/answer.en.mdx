# Pair regions that differ in only one condition

Keeping the plant in the dark for <InlineMath math="48" /> hours uses up the starch already in the leaves, so any starch found afterwards was made during the <InlineMath math="6" /> hours of light. A fair comparison changes **one condition only**.

- P and S are both green and both in light. The only difference is carbon dioxide, which the potassium hydroxide removes from S. Comparing P with S tests carbon dioxide.
- P and R differ only in light, so that pair tests light. P and Q differ only in chlorophyll.
- Starch forms only where chlorophyll, light and carbon dioxide are **all present**, which is region P alone.

So P is compared with S, and only P turns blue-black.

# Why the other options are wrong

- P with R changes the light, **so it tests the need for light, not for carbon dioxide**.
- Q with S changes two conditions at once, and Q has no chlorophyll, **so it makes no starch**.
- P and R turning blue-black forgets that the plant was destarched and that R had **no light for six hours**.
