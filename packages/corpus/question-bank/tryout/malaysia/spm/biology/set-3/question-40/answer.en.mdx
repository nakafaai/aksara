# Trace the nucleus, the cytoplasm and the uterus separately

Each ewe gives the lamb something different. Ewe A gives the **nucleus**, ewe B gives the egg's **cytoplasm**, and ewe C gives only a uterus to grow in.

- I: face colour is controlled by genes in the nucleus, and the nucleus came from the white-faced ewe A. Statement I is correct.
- II: the udder nucleus held a full diploid set of ewe A's chromosomes, and every cell of lamb D was copied from it by mitosis. Statement II is correct.
- III: mitochondria hold a little DNA of their own and lie in the cytoplasm. The cytoplasm of the fused cell was ewe B's egg, so the lamb's mitochondrial DNA comes from ewe B. Statement III is correct.
- IV: ewe C only carried the embryo. No fertilisation took place, so she passed on **no chromosomes** at all. Statement IV is wrong.

So statements I, II and III are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only rejects III, forgetting that mitochondria carry **DNA of their own** from the egg.
- II and III only rejects I, **although face colour follows the nuclear genes from ewe A**.
- I, II and IV only treats the surrogate ewe C like a parent, **but she gave the lamb no chromosomes**.
