# Find the one part that explains every observation of each patient

A single damaged part must explain **all** of a patient's observations, so each clue has to fit the same part.

- K: falling over and failing the finger-to-nose test show poor balance and coordination of voluntary movement. That is the work of the **cerebellum**. Normal speech rules out the cerebrum as the damaged part.
- L: the medulla oblongata detects a rise in carbon dioxide and speeds up breathing. A breathing rate that ignores carbon dioxide points to the **medulla oblongata**.
- M: the hypothalamus controls body temperature and also makes ADH, the hormone that makes urine concentrated. Losing both at once points to the **hypothalamus**.

So K has a damaged cerebellum, L a damaged medulla oblongata and M a damaged hypothalamus.

# Why the other options are wrong

- K with the cerebrum blames the part for thought and speech, **but K's speech is normal** and only coordination fails.
- L with the hypothalamus and M with the medulla oblongata swaps the two, **although the medulla controls breathing and the hypothalamus controls temperature**.
- M with the pituitary gland explains the dilute urine, since ADH is released there, **but not the uncontrolled body temperature**.
