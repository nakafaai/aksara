# Trace each animal's path

A dichotomous key uses only **the feature written at each step**. Trace all three animals, then find the first step at which their paths split.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{bat}&:\ 1a\to2b\to3a\ (Q)\\
\text{turtle}&:\ 1a\to2b\to3b\to4b\ (S)\\
\text{frog}&:\ 1a\to2b\to3b\to4a\ (R)
\end{aligned}" />

The turtle and the frog both lack hair and do not feed young on milk, so they split only at step <InlineMath math="4" />. The turtle and the bat split at step <InlineMath math="3" />, because the bat has fur and feeds its young on milk. So the answer is step <InlineMath math="4" /> and step <InlineMath math="3" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Step <InlineMath math="2" /> for the bat takes wings to mean feathers, but **a bat's wings are skin, with no feathers**, so the bat also goes on to step <InlineMath math="3" />.
- Step <InlineMath math="3" /> for the frog is wrong, because the frog also has no hair and does not feed its young on milk, so it goes on to step <InlineMath math="4" />.
- The choice of steps <InlineMath math="3" /> and <InlineMath math="2" /> makes both of those mistakes at once.
