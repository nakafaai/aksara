# Growth time after the lag phase

Cells do not divide during the lag phase, so **only the time after the lag phase** produces divisions. Of the <InlineMath math="120" /> minutes, the first <InlineMath math="40" /> are the lag phase, which leaves <InlineMath math="80" /> minutes of growth.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{number of doublings}&=\frac{120-40}{20}=4\\
N&=100\times2^4=1600
\end{aligned}" />

So after <InlineMath math="2" /> hours there are <InlineMath math="1600" /> bacterial cells.
