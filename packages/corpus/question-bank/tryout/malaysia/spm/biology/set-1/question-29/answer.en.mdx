# Count the effective stomata on both surfaces

Leaf S loses water through both of its surfaces. The stomata of R are sunken, so **each stoma of R counts as half an ordinary stoma**.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
S&:\ 40+180=220\\
R&:\ 90\times\tfrac12=45\\
\frac{220}{45}&\approx4.9
\end{aligned}" />

R loses the least water, with few sunken stomata and a very thick cuticle, so R is the xerophyte. So S loses about <InlineMath math="4.9" /> times as much water and the xerophyte is R.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="2.4" /> times ignores the sunken stomata, but **each stoma of R loses only half as much water**.
- S as the xerophyte is wrong, because S wastes the most water, with a thin cuticle and many stomata.
- <InlineMath math="2.0" /> times compares only the lower surfaces and leaves out the <InlineMath math="40" /> stomata on the upper surface of S.
