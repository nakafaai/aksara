# Auxin on the shaded side decides the bend

The shoot tip makes auxin, and light from one side makes auxin move to **the shaded side**. The auxin then travels down and stimulates cell elongation on the shaded side, so the shoot bends towards the light. Bending therefore needs a tip that receives light and an unblocked downward path for auxin on the shaded side.

P has no tip, so there is no auxin. Q's tip receives no light, so auxin travels down evenly and the shoot grows straight. In R, the mica blocks the downward path on the shaded side, so that side does not elongate faster. In S, the mica is on the lit side, while auxin on the shaded side still travels down, so S bends towards the light. So only S bends towards the light.

# Why the other options are wrong

- R only is wrong, because the mica in R **blocks auxin on the shaded side**, the side that must elongate.
- Q does not bend, because a covered tip cannot detect the direction of light.
- R and S is wrong because it counts R, whose auxin path on the shaded side is blocked.
