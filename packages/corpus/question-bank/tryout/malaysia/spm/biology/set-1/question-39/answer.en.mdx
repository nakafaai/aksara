# Given

The field area is <InlineMath math="200\text{ m}^2" />. Ten square quadrats of **side <InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}" />** are placed at random.

In all <InlineMath math="10" /> quadrats combined, <InlineMath math="45" /> plants are counted, and the species appears in <InlineMath math="7" /> of the <InlineMath math="10" /> quadrats.

# Asked

Find the estimated total population of the plant across the whole field and its percentage frequency.

# Solution

Picture placing a square frame of side <InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}" /> inside a <InlineMath math="1\text{ m} \times 1\text{ m}" /> floor tile. Four of those small frames fit inside one tile, so each quadrat covers only <InlineMath math="0.25\text{ m}^2" />, not <InlineMath math="0.5\text{ m}^2" />.

> Quadrat area equals side squared, and percentage frequency counts the fraction of quadrats containing the species rather than the count of individuals.

Find the **total sampled area**, compute plant density per square metre, multiply by field area, and separately compute percentage frequency.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{area sampled} &= 10 \times (0.5 \times 0.5) = 2.5\text{ m}^2 \\
\text{density} &= \frac{45}{2.5} = 18\text{ plants m}^{-2} \\
\text{population} &= 18 \times 200 = 3600\text{ plants} \\
\text{frequency} &= \frac{7}{10} \times 100\% = 70\%
\end{aligned}" />

Slide the quadrat side length <InlineMath math="s" /> below to see how squaring <InlineMath math="s" /> makes the population estimate four times larger at <InlineMath math="s = 0.5\text{ m}" /> (<InlineMath math="3600" />) than at <InlineMath math="s = 1\text{ m}" /> (<InlineMath math="900" />).

Verify directly: <InlineMath math="2.5\text{ m}^2" /> fits into <InlineMath math="200\text{ m}^2" /> exactly <InlineMath math="80" /> times, and <InlineMath math="80 \times 45 = 3600" /> plants.

## Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="900" /> multiplies the mean per quadrat (<InlineMath math="4.5" />) directly by <InlineMath math="200" />, assuming **each quadrat covers <InlineMath math="1\text{ m}^2" /> instead of <InlineMath math="0.25\text{ m}^2" />**.
- <InlineMath math="45\%" /> uses the total plant count as frequency, whereas frequency counts the <InlineMath math="7" /> occupied quadrats out of <InlineMath math="10" />.
- <InlineMath math="1800" /> uses the side length <InlineMath math="0.5" /> as the quadrat area instead of squaring it to <InlineMath math="0.25\text{ m}^2" />.

# Answer

The estimated plant population in the field is **<InlineMath math="3600" />**, and its percentage frequency is **<InlineMath math="70\%" />**.
