# Dry mass rises when photosynthesis wins

Dry mass falls while respiration uses the seed's food store, and **starts to rise when photosynthesis exceeds respiration**. The lowest mass is on day <InlineMath math="6" /> and the first rise is between day <InlineMath math="6" /> and day <InlineMath math="9" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{gain from day }9\text{ to }12&=2.2-1.6=0.6\text{ g for }10\text{ seedlings}\\
\text{per seedling per day}&=\frac{0.6}{10\times3}=0.020\text{ g}
\end{aligned}" />

The leaves appear on day <InlineMath math="4" />, but they need time before their photosynthesis exceeds respiration. So the answer is day <InlineMath math="6" /> to day <InlineMath math="9" /> and <InlineMath math="0.020\text{ g}" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- Day <InlineMath math="9" /> to day <InlineMath math="12" /> is the largest gain, but **the first gain already happens from day <InlineMath math="6" />**.
- <InlineMath math="0.20\text{ g}" /> is not yet divided by the number of seedlings, but the data are for <InlineMath math="10" /> seedlings.
- <InlineMath math="0.060\text{ g}" /> is the gain per seedling over all three days, not per day.
