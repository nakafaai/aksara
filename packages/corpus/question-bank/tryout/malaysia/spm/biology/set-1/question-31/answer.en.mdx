# Correct the number still marked

The capture-recapture formula assumes every marked snail can still be recognised. Because <InlineMath math="20\%" /> of the marks are lost, **only <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of the <InlineMath math="60" /> snails are still marked** in the population.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{marked}&=0.80\times60=48\\
N&=\frac{48\times50}{10}=240\\
\text{density}&=\frac{240}{2.5}=96\text{ per hectare}
\end{aligned}" />

So the density is about <InlineMath math="96" /> snails per hectare.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="120" /> uses <InlineMath math="60" /> marked snails, but **<InlineMath math="12" /> of them can no longer be recognised**.
- <InlineMath math="600" /> multiplies the population by the area, but density is population divided by area.
- <InlineMath math="24" /> divides the number of snails marked by the area, but that is not a population estimate.
