# Given

In bright light, the potted plant releases oxygen at a **net rate** of <InlineMath math="8\text{ cm}^3" /> per hour. In the dark, it absorbs oxygen for **respiration** at <InlineMath math="4\text{ cm}^3" /> per hour.

Respiration continues at the same rate throughout all <InlineMath math="24" /> hours of the day.

# Asked

Find the minimum hours of bright light per day so the plant does not lose dry mass, and find its gross rate of photosynthesis.

# Solution

Picture a piggy bank that spends <InlineMath math="4" /> coins every hour, day and night. During sunny hours, <InlineMath math="8" /> spare coins remain after paying that hourly bill, so the shop actually earns <InlineMath math="12" /> coins per sunny hour.

> Gross photosynthesis equals net oxygen release plus respiration, because a leaf still respires while making food in the light.

First, add respiration back to the net release to get the **gross photosynthesis rate**. Then compare daily oxygen production during <InlineMath math="t" /> light hours against daily respiration over <InlineMath math="24" /> hours.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{gross rate} &= 8 + 4 = 12\text{ cm}^3\text{ h}^{-1} \\
\text{daily respiration} &= 4 \times 24 = 96\text{ cm}^3 \\
12t &\ge 96 \\
t &\ge 8\text{ hours}
\end{aligned}" />

As a quick check, <InlineMath math="8" /> bright hours give a net gain of <InlineMath math="8 \times 8 = 64\text{ cm}^3" />, which matches the dark loss over <InlineMath math="16" /> hours, <InlineMath math="4 \times 16 = 64\text{ cm}^3" />.

## Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="12" /> hours and <InlineMath math="8\text{ cm}^3" /> per hour treats **net release as gross photosynthesis**, forgetting that respiration already subtracted <InlineMath math="4\text{ cm}^3" /> per hour.
- <InlineMath math="24" /> hours and <InlineMath math="4\text{ cm}^3" /> per hour subtracts respiration twice instead of adding it back.
- <InlineMath math="16" /> hours and <InlineMath math="12\text{ cm}^3" /> per hour gives the dark duration, <InlineMath math="24 - 8 = 16" /> hours, rather than the light duration.

# Answer

The plant needs at least **<InlineMath math="8" />** hours of bright light per day, and its gross rate of photosynthesis is **<InlineMath math="12\text{ cm}^3" />** per hour.
