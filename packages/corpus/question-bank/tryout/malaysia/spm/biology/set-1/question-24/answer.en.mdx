# The net rate already has respiration taken off

The oxygen released in light is a net amount, photosynthesis minus respiration. **The gross rate is the net rate plus respiration**. Respiration goes on for all <InlineMath math="24" /> hours.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{gross rate}&=8+4=12\text{ cm}^3\text{ per hour}\\
12t&\ge4\times24=96\\
t&\ge8\text{ hours}
\end{aligned}" />

Another route gives the same result: the net gain <InlineMath math="8t" /> in light must cover the loss <InlineMath math="4(24-t)" /> in the dark, so <InlineMath math="t=8" />. So at least <InlineMath math="8" /> hours of light are needed and the gross rate is <InlineMath math="12\text{ cm}^3" /> per hour.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="12" /> hours uses the net rate <InlineMath math="8" /> as the gross rate, but **respiration in the light has already been taken off that figure**.
- <InlineMath math="24" /> hours subtracts respiration twice, which leaves a gross rate of only <InlineMath math="4" />.
- <InlineMath math="16" /> hours is the length of the dark period, not the light period needed.
