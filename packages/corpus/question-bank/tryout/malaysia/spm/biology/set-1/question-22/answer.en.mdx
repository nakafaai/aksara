# The chain of causes behind stomatal opening

Stomatal opening is a chain of causes. In daylight potassium ions are actively transported into the guard cells, so statement I is correct. As a result the water potential of the guard cells falls, so **water moves into the guard cells**, not out, and they become turgid. So statement II is wrong.

The guard cell wall next to the pore is thicker and less elastic, so when turgid the cells curve away from the pore and the stoma opens, which makes statement III correct. Statement IV is wrong, because at night there is no photosynthesis, so potassium ions leave, water follows, the cells become flaccid and the stoma closes. So only I and III are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and III accepts statement II, but the cells become turgid because **water enters**, not leaves.
- I, III and IV accepts statement IV, but there is no photosynthesis at all at night.
- II and IV rejects both correct statements and picks only the wrong ones.
