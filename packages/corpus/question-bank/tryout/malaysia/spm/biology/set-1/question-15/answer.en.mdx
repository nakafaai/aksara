# Time each neurone and each synapse

The two neurones conduct at different speeds, so **each neurone is timed separately**. The relay neurone makes two synapses in the spinal cord, sensory to relay and relay to motor.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
t_{\text{sensory}}&=\frac{0.8}{80}=0.010\text{ s}=10\text{ ms}\\
t_{\text{motor}}&=\frac{0.6}{40}=0.015\text{ s}=15\text{ ms}\\
t_{\text{synapses}}&=2\times0.5=1.0\text{ ms}\\
t&=10+15+1.0=26.0\text{ ms}
\end{aligned}" />

So the minimum time is <InlineMath math="26.0\text{ ms}" />.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="25.0\text{ ms}" /> ignores the synaptic delay, but **two synapses add <InlineMath math="1.0\text{ ms}" />**.
- <InlineMath math="24.3\text{ ms}" /> averages the two speeds to <InlineMath math="60\text{ m s}^{-1}" />, but each neurone covers its own length at its own speed.
- <InlineMath math="26.5\text{ ms}" /> counts three synapses, but there are only two synapses in the spinal cord.
