# Separate each route of water loss

Shoot C still loses <InlineMath math="0.04" /> with both surfaces covered, so **water is also lost through the stem and leaf stalks**. Shoot B loses water through the upper surfaces plus that stem route, so the loss through the upper surfaces is the difference between B and C.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{upper surfaces}&=0.10-0.04=0.06\text{ cm}^3\text{ h}^{-1}\\
\text{percentage}&=\frac{0.06}{0.50}\times100\%=12\%
\end{aligned}" />

So <InlineMath math="12\%" /> of the water is lost through the upper leaf surfaces.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="20\%" /> uses <InlineMath math="0.10" /> directly, but **that figure still includes <InlineMath math="0.04" /> from the stem**.
- <InlineMath math="8\%" /> is the share lost through the stem and leaf stalks, not through the upper surfaces.
- <InlineMath math="80\%" /> is the share lost through the lower surfaces, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{0.50-0.10}{0.50}" />.
