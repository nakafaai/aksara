# Bahagikan glukosa antara dua laluan

Oksigen yang ada menentukan **berapa banyak glukosa boleh dioksidakan secara aerob**. Bakinya ditapai.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
n_{\text{glukosa}}&=\frac{18}{180}=0.10\text{ mol}\\
n_{\text{O}_2}&=\frac{1.44}{24}=0.06\text{ mol}\ \Rightarrow\ 0.01\text{ mol glukosa aerob}\\
\text{ditapai}&=0.10-0.01=0.09\text{ mol glukosa}\\
n_{\text{CO}_2}&=6(0.01)+2(0.09)=0.24\text{ mol}=5.76\text{ dm}^3\\
E&=0.01(2898)+0.09(210)=28.98+18.9\approx47.9\text{ kJ}
\end{aligned}" />

Jadi, <InlineMath math="5.76\text{ dm}^3" /> karbon dioksida dan <InlineMath math="47.9\text{ kJ}" /> tenaga dihasilkan.
