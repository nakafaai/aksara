# Multiply the efficiency of each level, then scale the area

Energy passes through three levels, so **the three efficiencies are multiplied in turn**. Then turn the energy per square metre into energy per hectare.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
E_{\text{tertiary}}&=20{,}000\times0.10\times0.15\times0.20=60\text{ kJ m}^{-2}\\
\text{per hectare}&=60\times10{,}000=600{,}000\text{ kJ}\\
\text{number}&=\frac{600{,}000}{1200}=500
\end{aligned}" />

So one hectare can support at most <InlineMath math="500" /> tertiary consumers.
