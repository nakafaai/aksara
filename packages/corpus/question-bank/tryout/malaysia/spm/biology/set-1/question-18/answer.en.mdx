# Given

A woman has a regular **<InlineMath math="32" />-day menstrual cycle**, counting the first day of menstruation as day <InlineMath math="1" />. The luteal phase after ovulation through the final day always lasts exactly <InlineMath math="14" /> days.

Intercourse can lead to fertilisation from <InlineMath math="3" /> days before ovulation up to <InlineMath math="1" /> day after ovulation.

# Asked

During which range of cycle days can intercourse lead to fertilisation?

# Solution

Picture counting backwards from the finish line of a <InlineMath math="32" />-day calendar. Because the luteal phase after ovulation **always lasts <InlineMath math="14" /> days**, ovulation does not sit on day <InlineMath math="14" /> but <InlineMath math="14" /> days before day <InlineMath math="32" />.

> In any regular menstrual cycle, ovulation is always found by counting fourteen days back from the final day of the cycle.

## Ovulation and fertile window calculation

Subtract <InlineMath math="14" /> from <InlineMath math="32" /> to locate **ovulation day**, then apply the <InlineMath math="3" />-day before and <InlineMath math="1" />-day after bounds.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{ovulation day} &= 32 - 14 = \text{day } 18 \\
\text{earliest day} &= 18 - 3 = \text{day } 15 \\
\text{latest day} &= 18 + 1 = \text{day } 19
\end{aligned}" />

Quick check: counting day <InlineMath math="19" /> through day <InlineMath math="32" /> inclusive spans exactly <InlineMath math="14" /> luteal days.

## Why other options fail

- Day <InlineMath math="11" /> to day <InlineMath math="15" /> assumes ovulation always falls on day <InlineMath math="14" />, even though **this cycle lasts <InlineMath math="32" /> days**.
- Day <InlineMath math="18" /> to day <InlineMath math="21" /> counts <InlineMath math="3" /> days after ovulation instead of before ovulation.
- Day <InlineMath math="14" /> to day <InlineMath math="18" /> shifts the window one day early and omits day <InlineMath math="19" />.

# Answer

Intercourse can lead to fertilisation from day **<InlineMath math="15" />** to day **<InlineMath math="19" />**.
