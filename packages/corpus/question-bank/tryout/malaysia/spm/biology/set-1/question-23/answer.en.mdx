Which factor limits photosynthesis at light intensity <InlineMath math="1" /> unit, and at light intensity <InlineMath math="5" /> units with <InlineMath math="0.10\%\ \text{CO}_2" /> at <InlineMath math="25^\circ\text{C}" />?

# Solution

Picture a toy factory run by three workers: light, carbon dioxide, and warmth. Factory speed is always locked by the slowest worker, so **the limiting factor is whichever factor makes the rate jump when you increase it**.

> To identify the limiting factor at any point, compare two rows that differ in only one factor: the factor whose increase raises the rate is limiting.

## Testing one factor at a time

Compare pairs of rows that change **only one variable** at each target condition.

- At <InlineMath math="1" /> unit of light, raising <InlineMath math="\text{CO}_2" /> to <InlineMath math="0.10\%" /> or temperature to <InlineMath math="35^\circ\text{C}" /> leaves the rate stuck at <InlineMath math="10" />, whereas raising light to <InlineMath math="2" /> units doubles it to <InlineMath math="20" />, proving **light intensity** is limiting.
- At <InlineMath math="5" /> units (<InlineMath math="0.10\%\ \text{CO}_2" />, <InlineMath math="25^\circ\text{C}" />), the rate stays flat at <InlineMath math="40" /> from <InlineMath math="4" /> to <InlineMath math="5" /> units, then climbs to <InlineMath math="52" /> at <InlineMath math="35^\circ\text{C}" />, proving **temperature** is limiting.

## Why other options fail

- Light at <InlineMath math="5" /> units fails because the blue curve is already flat from <InlineMath math="4" /> to <InlineMath math="5" /> units, so **more light does not raise the rate**.
- Carbon dioxide at <InlineMath math="1" /> unit fails because raising <InlineMath math="\text{CO}_2" /> from <InlineMath math="0.04\%" /> to <InlineMath math="0.10\%" /> keeps the rate at <InlineMath math="10" />.
- Temperature at <InlineMath math="1" /> unit fails because warming to <InlineMath math="35^\circ\text{C}" /> also leaves the rate unchanged at <InlineMath math="10" />.

# Answer

The limiting factor is light intensity at **<InlineMath math="1" />** unit of light, and temperature at **<InlineMath math="5" />** units (**<InlineMath math="0.10\%\ \text{CO}_2" />**, **<InlineMath math="25^\circ\text{C}" />**).
