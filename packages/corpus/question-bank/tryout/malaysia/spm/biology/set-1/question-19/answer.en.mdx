# Separate the part that does not need respiration

With cyanide there are still <InlineMath math="2" /> units of uptake that do not depend on respiration. **That part must be taken off both conditions** before comparing the active transport.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{active in air}&=20-2=18\\
\text{active in nitrogen}&=12-2=10\\
\frac{10}{18}\times100\%&\approx56\%
\end{aligned}" />

Without oxygen, anaerobic respiration still supplies some ATP. So about <InlineMath math="56\%" /> of the active transport is maintained.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="60\%" /> divides <InlineMath math="12" /> by <InlineMath math="20" />, but **both figures still include <InlineMath math="2" /> units that are not active transport**.
- <InlineMath math="50\%" /> divides the active uptake in nitrogen by the total uptake in air.
- <InlineMath math="67\%" /> divides the total uptake in nitrogen by the active uptake in air.
