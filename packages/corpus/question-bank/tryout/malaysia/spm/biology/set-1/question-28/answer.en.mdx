# Trace where each tissue comes from

The female gametes come from the <InlineMath math="4n=48" /> plant, so the egg cell and each polar nucleus carry <InlineMath math="24" /> chromosomes. The male gametes from the <InlineMath math="2n=24" /> plant carry <InlineMath math="12" />. **The seed coat develops from the integument of the ovule**, so it is the mother's tissue and is not fertilised.

Run the pollen tube down and watch each nucleus pick up its chromosomes.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="36" /> for the endosperm counts three sets from the male gamete, but **the two polar nuclei come from the tetraploid mother**.
- <InlineMath math="36" /> for the seed coat equates it with the embryo, but the seed coat is the mother's tissue.
- <InlineMath math="72" /> for the endosperm assumes the male gamete also carries <InlineMath math="24" />, but the male parent is diploid.
