# Given

A teenager of mass <InlineMath math="64\text{ kg}" /> and fixed height <InlineMath math="1.60\text{ m}" /> wants to lower their **body mass index (BMI) to <InlineMath math="22.5" />**.

Daily energy need is <InlineMath math="11{,}000\text{ kJ}" />, daily intake is <InlineMath math="9000\text{ kJ}" />, and each <InlineMath math="1\text{ kg}" /> of mass lost supplies <InlineMath math="40{,}000\text{ kJ}" />.

# Asked

How many days are needed to reach the target BMI of <InlineMath math="22.5" />?

# Solution

Picture stored body energy like a piggy bank. Spending <InlineMath math="2000\text{ kJ}" /> more than you eat every day **drains exactly <InlineMath math="0.05\text{ kg}" /> of mass per day**.

> Because BMI divides mass by height squared, target mass always equals target BMI multiplied by the square of height.

## Target mass and days calculation

Find target mass using **the squared height**, convert mass loss into energy, then divide by the daily deficit.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
m_{\text{target}} &= 22.5 \times 1.60^2 = 22.5 \times 2.56 = 57.6\text{ kg} \\
\Delta m &= 64 - 57.6 = 6.4\text{ kg} \\
E_{\text{total}} &= 6.4 \times 40{,}000 = 256{,}000\text{ kJ} \\
\text{daily deficit} &= 11{,}000 - 9000 = 2000\text{ kJ day}^{-1} \\
\text{days} &= \frac{256{,}000}{2000} = 128\text{ days}
\end{aligned}" />

Quick check: starting BMI is <InlineMath math="25.0" />, a BMI drop of <InlineMath math="2.5" />, and <InlineMath math="2.5 \times 2.56" /> matches the <InlineMath math="6.4\text{ kg}" /> mass loss.

## Why other options fail

- Choosing <InlineMath math="560" /> days uses <InlineMath math="1.60" /> without squaring it, falsely giving a <InlineMath math="28\text{ kg}" /> mass loss.
- Choosing <InlineMath math="80" /> days multiplies the BMI drop <InlineMath math="2.5" /> by <InlineMath math="1.60" />, forgetting that **height must be squared**.
- Choosing <InlineMath math="50" /> days treats the BMI drop of <InlineMath math="2.5" /> directly as <InlineMath math="2.5\text{ kg}" />, mixing up different units.

# Answer

A total of <InlineMath math="128" /> days are needed to reach the target BMI of <InlineMath math="22.5" />.
