# Compare each treatment with the one that differs from it in one way

Each pair of treatments changes **one thing**. Compare tip on with tip off, auxin paste with plain paste, and tip on with tip on plus cytokinin.

- I: with the tip on no side shoots grow, and with it removed <InlineMath math="4" /> grow. The tip holds the side buds back, which is apical dominance. Statement I is correct.
- II: auxin paste on the cut stump keeps side shoots at <InlineMath math="0" />, just as the tip did. Statement II is correct.
- III: plain paste gives <InlineMath math="4" /> side shoots, the same as removing the tip with no paste. It shows that the paste itself does nothing, so the auxin is what matters. It does not show that removing the tip has no effect. Statement III is wrong.
- IV: with the tip still on, cytokinin on the buds lets <InlineMath math="2" /> side shoots grow, half as many as removing the tip. Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, II and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only rejects IV, **although cytokinin lets <InlineMath math="2" /> side shoots grow**.
- II, III and IV only rejects I and accepts III, **misreading the plain-paste control**.
- I, III and IV only accepts III and rejects II, **the auxin paste that replaces the tip**.
