# Divide the store by the hours it must last

A store that must last until dawn is spent at **the store divided by the hours of night**. Change the length of the night without changing the pace, and the store runs out early.

- I: <InlineMath math="60" /> units over <InlineMath math="12" /> hours is <InlineMath math="5" /> units an hour. Statement I is correct.
- II: at <InlineMath math="5" /> units an hour the starch lasts only <InlineMath math="12" /> hours, leaving the last <InlineMath math="4" /> hours of a <InlineMath math="16" />-hour night with none. Statement II is correct.
- III: to make <InlineMath math="60" /> units last <InlineMath math="16" /> hours, the plant must use them more slowly, <InlineMath math="3.75" /> units an hour, from the start of the night. Statement III is wrong.
- IV: sucrose made from the starch is exported in the phloem to the roots and other sinks, which need sugar through the night. Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, II and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only rejects IV, **the export of sugar that the night-time breakdown supports**.
- II, III and IV only rejects I and accepts III, **keeping the old pace for a longer night**.
- I, III and IV only accepts III and rejects II, **although <InlineMath math="60" /> units at <InlineMath math="5" /> an hour last only <InlineMath math="12" /> hours**.
