# Read what the cushion does to wind, and so to temperature

Sunlight warms the leaves, and wind normally carries that heat away. A dense cushion **slows the air inside it**, so the absorbed heat stays among the shoots.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and III only accepts a sixth, **dividing the wind speeds the wrong way or halving the answer**.
- III and IV only rejects I and **places the plant in deep shade**.
- I, III and IV only accepts IV, **although the slope is open and windy**.
