# Split each stool into water and dry matter before comparing

Stool mass alone hides two things. Multiply by the water percentage to get the **water**, and subtract it to get the **dry matter**. If the dry matter grows by less than the fibre added, some fibre has gone. Humans make no enzyme for cellulose, but **bacteria in the colon** ferment part of it.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only rejects III, **the longer stay in the colon that explains the drier low-fibre stool**.
- II and IV only rejects I and **credits human enzymes with digesting cellulose**.
- I, III and IV only accepts IV and rejects II, **although <InlineMath math="156\text{ g}" /> is three times <InlineMath math="52\text{ g}" />**.
