# Rule out the couple that cannot have a group O child

A group O child must receive <InlineMath math="I^O" /> from **both** parents. A parent with group AB carries no <InlineMath math="I^O" />, so couple <InlineMath math="2" /> can never have a group O child. Once the babies are sorted, the genotypes follow, and then the chances.

<BlockMath math="I^AI^O\times I^BI^O\to\tfrac14\,AB,\ \tfrac14\,A,\ \tfrac14\,B,\ \tfrac14\,O,\qquad \left(\tfrac34\right)^4=\tfrac{81}{256}\approx0.32" />

- I: the mother in couple <InlineMath math="2" /> is <InlineMath math="I^AI^B" /> and has no <InlineMath math="I^O" /> to pass on, so she cannot have a group O child. Baby X must be couple <InlineMath math="1" />'s. Statement I is correct.
- II: baby X is <InlineMath math="I^OI^O" />, so each parent of couple <InlineMath math="1" /> gave one <InlineMath math="I^O" />. The mother must be <InlineMath math="I^AI^O" /> and the father <InlineMath math="I^BI^O" />. Statement II is correct.
- III: couple <InlineMath math="2" />'s children get <InlineMath math="I^A" /> or <InlineMath math="I^B" /> from the mother and <InlineMath math="I^O" /> from the father, so they are <InlineMath math="I^AI^O" /> or <InlineMath math="I^BI^O" />, group A or B. A group AB child would need <InlineMath math="I^B" /> or <InlineMath math="I^A" /> from the father, who has neither. Statement III is wrong.
- IV: each child of couple <InlineMath math="1" /> has a <InlineMath math="\tfrac34" /> chance of not being group O, and four independent children give <InlineMath math="\left(\tfrac34\right)^4\approx32\%" />. Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, II and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only rejects IV, **the chance found by multiplying four independent births**.
- I, II and III only accepts III, **although the group O father cannot give a second codominant allele**.
- II, III and IV only rejects I, **although only couple <InlineMath math="1" /> can have a group O baby**.
