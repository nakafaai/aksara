# Match each lost function to the part of the brain that controls it

Each part of the brain has its own jobs. Find the job each patient has lost, and it names the damaged part. The **motor pathways cross over**, so each side of the cerebrum moves the opposite side of the body.

- I: the right arm and leg are moved by the left side of the cerebrum, because the nerve pathways cross in the brainstem. Statement I is correct.
- II: the cerebellum coordinates movement and balance. Memory depends on parts of the cerebrum, and nothing in P's symptoms points to memory loss. Statement II is wrong.
- III: the medulla oblongata sets the rhythm and depth of breathing, so damage there can make breathing fail, and a ventilator may be needed. Statement III is correct.
- IV: the hypothalamus compares blood temperature with its set point and triggers sweating or shivering, so its damage lets body temperature follow the room. Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, III and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and III only accepts II and rejects IV, **mixing up the cerebellum with memory and missing the hypothalamus**.
- II and IV only rejects I and III, **the crossed motor pathways and the medulla's control of breathing**.
- I, II, III and IV accepts II, **giving the cerebellum a memory role P's symptoms do not show**.
