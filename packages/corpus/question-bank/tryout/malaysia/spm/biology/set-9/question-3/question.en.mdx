Cells lining the small intestine have a carrier protein that takes in one sodium ion together with each glucose molecule. Oral rehydration solution, given to patients with severe diarrhoea such as cholera, contains both salt and glucose. A loop of intestine is filled with different solutions, and the water it absorbs is measured.
