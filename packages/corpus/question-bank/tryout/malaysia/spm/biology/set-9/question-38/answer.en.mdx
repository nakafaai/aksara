# Multiply number by mass, and keep mass apart from energy flow

The biomass at a level is the **number times the mass of each**. A pyramid of numbers can take odd shapes when one producer is huge or consumers are tiny, but biomass usually narrows upwards. Biomass is what is present at one moment, so it does not measure how much energy flows between levels over time.

Statement II is correct.
- III: <InlineMath math="300" /> out of <InlineMath math="2000" /> is <InlineMath math="15\%" />, but that compares masses present at one time. Energy transfer depends on how much is eaten, digested and turned into new tissue over a period, and caterpillars are replaced many times while birds live for years. Statement III is wrong.
- IV: the lice feed on the birds, which feed on the caterpillars, which feed on the producer, so the lice are fourth-level consumers. <InlineMath math="600" /> lice outnumber the <InlineMath math="10" /> birds. Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, II and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only rejects IV, **the parasites that sit one level above their hosts**.
- I, II and III only accepts III and rejects IV, **treating a ratio of masses present as an energy transfer**.
- II, III and IV only rejects I, **although one tree under thousands of caterpillars cannot make a pyramid of numbers**.
