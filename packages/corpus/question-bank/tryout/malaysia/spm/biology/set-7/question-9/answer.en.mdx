# Split the dive at the moment each store changes hands

The muscles live on myoglobin until it runs out, then go short of oxygen until the blood flow opens again at minute <InlineMath math="16" />. **From minute <InlineMath math="16" /> the blood feeds the muscles as well as the organs**, so it drains faster.

## How long each store lasts

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{myoglobin} &: \ \tfrac{10\text{ L}}{1.0\text{ L/min}}=10\text{ min} \\
\text{blood left at minute }16 &: \ 20-0.5\times16=12\text{ L} \\
\text{blood after minute }16 &: \ \tfrac{12\text{ L}}{0.5+1.0\text{ L/min}}=8\text{ min, so it runs out at minute }24
\end{aligned}" />

## Lactic acid formed in the muscles

In a dive of <InlineMath math="22" /> minutes, the muscles lack oxygen only **between minute <InlineMath math="10" /> and minute <InlineMath math="16" />**. After that the blood supplies them, and at minute <InlineMath math="22" /> the blood still holds <InlineMath math="12-1.5\times6=3\text{ L}" />.

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{with the flow opened} &: \ (16-10)\times1.0=6\text{ L lacked}\ \Rightarrow\ 6\times20=120\text{ g} \\
\text{with the flow kept closed} &: \ (22-10)\times1.0=12\text{ L lacked}\ \Rightarrow\ 12\times20=240\text{ g}
\end{aligned}" />

## Judging each statement

- I: the blood lasts until minute <InlineMath math="16+8=24" />. Statement I is correct.
- II: the muscles lack <InlineMath math="6\text{ L}" />, not <InlineMath math="12\text{ L}" />, so they form <InlineMath math="120\text{ g}" />. Statement II is wrong.
- III: with the flow kept closed they would lack <InlineMath math="12\text{ L}" /> and form <InlineMath math="240\text{ g}" />, which is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{240}{120}=2" /> times as much. Statement III is correct.
- IV: blood flows through the muscles again from minute <InlineMath math="16" />, while the dive lasts until minute <InlineMath math="22" />. **The lactic acid formed from minute <InlineMath math="10" /> is carried away in that blood** before the seal surfaces. Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, III and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- II only treats the muscles as cut off for the whole dive, **missing that the blood flow opens at minute <InlineMath math="16" />**. It then finds <InlineMath math="240\text{ g}" /> of lactic acid, no difference for statement III, blood lasting <InlineMath math="\tfrac{20}{0.5}=40" /> minutes, and no lactic acid in the blood during the dive.
- I and III only expects all the lactic acid to stay in the muscles until the seal surfaces. That holds for a dive with the flow closed, **not for one where blood reaches the muscles from minute <InlineMath math="16" />**.
- III and IV only divides the whole blood store by the combined use from the start, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{20}{1.5}\approx13" /> minutes, **forgetting that the muscles draw on the blood only from minute <InlineMath math="16" />**.
