| X, entered by an ant | <InlineMath math="0" />, <InlineMath math="40" />, <InlineMath math="65" />, <InlineMath math="80" />, <InlineMath math="140" />, <InlineMath math="150" />, <InlineMath math="175" />, <InlineMath math="230" /> |
| Y, hit by a falling twig that then lies still | <InlineMath math="0" />, <InlineMath math="20" /> |
