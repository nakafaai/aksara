# Compare the mass filtered each minute with the mass excreted each minute

Concentrations alone mislead, because water is taken back along the tubule. Work in **milligrams per minute**: the urine carries concentration times urine flow, and the filtrate carries plasma concentration times the filtration rate.

## The filtration rate from inulin

Inulin is neither taken back nor added, so **all the inulin filtered each minute leaves in the urine**.

<BlockMath math="\text{inulin excreted}=60\times2.0=120\text{ mg/min}\ \Rightarrow\ \text{filtration rate}=\tfrac{120}{1.0}=120\text{ cm}^3\text{ of plasma per minute}" />

## Filtered against excreted

<BlockMath math="\begin{aligned}
\text{urea} &: \ \text{filtered }120\times0.30=36\text{ mg/min},\quad \text{excreted }9.0\times2.0=18\text{ mg/min} \\
\text{penicillin} &: \ \text{filtered }120\times0.010=1.2\text{ mg/min},\quad \text{excreted }2.4\times2.0=4.8\text{ mg/min} \\
\text{glucose} &: \ \text{filtered }120\times1.0=120\text{ mg/min},\quad \text{excreted }0
\end{aligned}" />

**Urea loses half on the way, while penicillin gains**. Penicillin is not reabsorbed, so the extra <InlineMath math="4.8-1.2=3.6\text{ mg/min}" /> must be secreted by the tubules.

## Judging each statement

- I: the kidneys filter <InlineMath math="120\text{ cm}^3" /> per minute, and urea goes from <InlineMath math="36" /> to <InlineMath math="18\text{ mg/min}" />, so half is reabsorbed. Statement I is correct.
- II: of the <InlineMath math="4.8\text{ mg}" /> excreted each minute, <InlineMath math="3.6\text{ mg}" /> was secreted, which is <InlineMath math="\tfrac{3.6}{4.8}=\tfrac{3}{4}" />, not one quarter. Statement II is wrong.
- III: with secretion blocked, only the filtered <InlineMath math="1.2\text{ mg/min}" /> leaves, and <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1.2}{4.8}=\tfrac{1}{4}" /> of the present rate. Statement III is correct.
- IV: the mass excreted is the mass filtered plus the mass secreted, and neither depends on the urine flow. **More water only dilutes the same penicillin**, so its urine concentration halves. Statement IV is wrong.

So statements I and III are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I and II only takes the filtered share, <InlineMath math="\tfrac{1.2}{4.8}=\tfrac{1}{4}" />, as the secreted share. Then blocking secretion seems to leave three quarters, **swapping the two parts of the penicillin**.
- I, III and IV only thinks the mass excreted follows the urine flow, **forgetting that only filtration and secretion put penicillin into the tubule**.
- III only sees urea far more concentrated in the urine than in the plasma and decides none is reabsorbed, **judging from the concentration instead of the mass per minute**.
