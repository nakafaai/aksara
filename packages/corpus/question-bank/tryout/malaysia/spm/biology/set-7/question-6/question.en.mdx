A fungus makes the amino acid Z from a simple precursor in four steps. Each step is catalysed by a different enzyme, and the steps produce the intermediates P, Q and R in an order that is not given. The fungus cannot grow without Z.

Three strains of the fungus, X, Y and W, each lack one working enzyme of this pathway, and each strain lacks a different enzyme. Each strain is placed on a medium with no Z, with one substance added.

| Strain | P added | Q added | R added | Z added |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | grows | grows | no growth | grows |
| Y | no growth | grows | no growth | grows |
| W | no growth | no growth | no growth | grows |

When a step is blocked, the cells build up the substance that the missing enzyme would act on.

Which of the following statements are correct?

I. The pathway makes the intermediates in the order R, then P, then Q.

II. Strain Y builds up P.

III. A strain that lacks both the enzyme missing in strain X and the enzyme missing in strain Y would grow when P is added.

IV. The substance that strain W builds up would let both strain X and strain Y grow.
