A pancreatic cell secretes digestive enzymes by exocytosis. Each secretory vesicle fuses with the plasma membrane, so its membrane becomes part of the plasma membrane while its contents leave the cell. The cell takes membrane back by endocytosis, in small coated vesicles that pinch off from the plasma membrane and enclose some tissue fluid.

| Secretory vesicles that fuse with the plasma membrane each minute | <InlineMath math="10" /> |
| Diameter of one coated vesicle | <InlineMath math="0.10\,\mu\text{m}" /> |
