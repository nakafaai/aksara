# Multiply through each filter, from volume to useful sperm

Total sperm is volume times concentration. Only sperm that can swim and have a normal shape are likely to reach the oocyte, so multiply by **both shares** to get the useful number.

<BlockMath math="\text{Y}: 2\times15=30\text{ million},\quad 30\times0.60\times0.05=0.9\text{ million},\qquad \frac{2.4}{0.9}\approx2.7" />

- III: <InlineMath math="2.4" /> million against <InlineMath math="0.9" /> million is about <InlineMath math="2.7" /> times, which is less than three. Statement III is wrong.
- IV: most sperm never reach the oocyte, and many are needed to get even a few to the oviduct. Fewer sperm lower the chance of fertilisation. Statement IV is wrong.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and III only accepts III, but the ratio is about <InlineMath math="2.7" />, **not more than three**.
- II and IV only rejects I, **although X's total is three times the limit**.
- I, III and IV only rejects II and accepts III and IV, **missing Y's low count** and overstating the ratio.
