# Find the longest unbroken dark period, not the total dark

Both plants measure the **length of uninterrupted darkness**. A flash splits the night into two pieces, and only the longer piece counts. Where the flash falls therefore matters as much as whether there is one.

# Why the other options are wrong

- X in Q only treats **every flash as a night break**, missing that S still has <InlineMath math="14" /> unbroken hours after its flash.
- X in Q, R and S counts **total dark**, <InlineMath math="14" /> hours in R, ignoring the flash that splits it into <InlineMath math="7+7" />.
- X in P and R swaps the two plants, giving X the **short nights that Y needs**.
