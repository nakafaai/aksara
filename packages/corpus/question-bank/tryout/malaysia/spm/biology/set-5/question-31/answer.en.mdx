# Add stress only for the weeks that pass the threshold

The threshold is <InlineMath math="30\,^\circ\text{C}" />, but the stress in a qualifying week is measured from <InlineMath math="29\,^\circ\text{C}" />. A week at <InlineMath math="29.5\,^\circ\text{C}" /> is **below the threshold**, so it adds nothing at all.

- I: weeks <InlineMath math="2" /> to <InlineMath math="7" /> add a total of <InlineMath math="10" />, beyond the <InlineMath math="8" /> that usually causes widespread death. Statement I is correct.
- II: <InlineMath math="29.5\,^\circ\text{C}" /> is below <InlineMath math="30\,^\circ\text{C}" />, so weeks <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="8" /> add nothing. Taking <InlineMath math="29.5-29=0.5" /> ignores the threshold. Statement II is wrong.
- III: heat-stressed corals expel their symbiotic algae, which give them their colour and most of their food, leaving the white skeleton visible. Statement III is correct.
- IV: a bleached coral is weakened, not yet dead. If the water cools in time, the algae can recolonise its tissues. Statement IV is correct.

So statements I, III and IV are correct.

# Why the other options are wrong

- I, II and III only accepts II, which **skips the <InlineMath math="30^\circ\text{C}" /> threshold**, and rejects IV.
- II and IV only rejects the total of <InlineMath math="10" /> and the **loss of the algae**.
- I, II, III and IV adds <InlineMath math="0.5" /> for weeks <InlineMath math="1" /> and <InlineMath math="8" />, **although they are below the threshold**.
