# Raise the share of area to the power in the rule, and undo it for the reverse question

With <InlineMath math="S=cA^{0.25}" />, the constant cancels when two areas are compared, so the **share of species** kept is the share of area raised to the power <InlineMath math="0.25" />. To find the area for a chosen share of species, raise that share to the power <InlineMath math="4" />.

<MathContainer>
<BlockMath math="\frac{S_{\text{new}}}{S_{\text{old}}}=\left(\frac{A_{\text{new}}}{A_{\text{old}}}\right)^{0.25}=0.1^{0.25}\approx0.56" />
<BlockMath math="0.8=\left(\frac{A_{\text{kept}}}{A_{\text{old}}}\right)^{0.25}\ \Rightarrow\ \frac{A_{\text{kept}}}{A_{\text{old}}}=0.8^4\approx0.41" />
</MathContainer>

About <InlineMath math="56\%" /> of the species remain on <InlineMath math="10\%" /> of the area, and <InlineMath math="41\%" /> of the area would keep <InlineMath math="80\%" />. Species are lost much more slowly than habitat at first, but the loss speeds up as the last fragments go.

# Why the other options are wrong

- <InlineMath math="10\%" /> and <InlineMath math="80\%" /> assumes species fall **in proportion to area**, ignoring the power <InlineMath math="0.25" />.
- <InlineMath math="56\%" /> and <InlineMath math="80\%" /> gets the first part right but keeps <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of the area for <InlineMath math="80\%" /> of the species, **instead of raising <InlineMath math="0.8" /> to the power <InlineMath math="4" />**.
- <InlineMath math="32\%" /> and <InlineMath math="64\%" /> uses a **power of <InlineMath math="0.5" /> instead of <InlineMath math="0.25" />**, a square root and a square.
