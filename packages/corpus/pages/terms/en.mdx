## AI features

### How AI features work

If you use AI features, you submit prompts or messages and the Services generate outputs. Nakafa routes Nina's prompts and related context through Vercel AI Gateway to Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite or Gemini 3.7 Flash. The Gateway may select Google or Google Cloud Vertex AI, but no other language-model provider is permitted. Each request requires a route that Vercel identifies as disallowing prompt training. If no eligible Google or Vertex AI route is available, the request fails. No other language-model provider receives it. Research features may also use Google Search grounding to corroborate public information, and Firecrawl to search the web and read pages.

Our use of these services is subject to the [Vercel AI Product Terms](https://vercel.com/legal/ai-product-terms#ai-gateway), [Vercel Notices and License Information](https://vercel.com/legal/notices-and-license-information#ai-gateway), [Gemini API Additional Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms), and [Google Cloud Terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms), as applicable to the selected route.

### Answer scoring with Jev

Some try-out and School assessment answers are scored with Jev, a decision model from TypeSafe AI, Inc., which Nakafa calls through Vercel AI Gateway. Jev receives the answer text and the material needed to score it, without your name or account identifiers, and returns scores and probabilities. In try-outs outside Nakafa School, these scores are calculated automatically. In Nakafa School, a Jev score for an exam is a suggestion that a teacher reviews before any grade is released. Our Privacy Policy describes the providers that run Jev and how they handle the data.

### AI limitations and your responsibility

AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. You are responsible for verifying any output before relying on it, including in educational, professional, medical, legal, or financial contexts.

Scores from Jev can also be wrong. If you believe a score is wrong, contact your teacher in Nakafa School, or contact us at [nakafaai@gmail.com](mailto:nakafaai@gmail.com).

Do not include sensitive personal data in prompts or free-text answers (for example government IDs or payment card numbers). You are responsible for the content you submit.
