# About Nakafa

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Nakafa is a free place to learn, from primary school to university. You can read lessons in mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, and AI programming, practice with questions that come with worked explanations, and sit [try-outs](/en/try-out) that score you right away. If you get stuck, ask Nina, Nakafa's AI assistant, about the lesson or question you have open. Nina isn't always right, so check her answer against the lesson.

Public lessons are free and don't need an account. When you sign in, Nakafa saves your progress and opens Nina and the try-outs for you. For schools, Nakafa School offers exams, proctoring, and Nina for every class.

## How lessons are made

Every lesson is written in Aksara, Nakafa's content repository, which you can read on GitHub. AI writing tools help draft the lessons and translate them, and they work under a written editorial standard kept in the same repository. Each lesson names Nabil Akbarazzima Fatih as its author, and the articles are written by Shifna Zihdatal Haq.

The editorial standard sets these rules:

- Every language version carries the same facts, examples, exercises, and visuals, in the same order.
- Facts need a primary or official source, and each formula, example, and exercise is worked out again before it's published.
- Points on a graph are computed from the formula the graph draws.
- New or revised lessons, questions, worked answers, and visuals have to pass a full release check before they're published. Every lesson, article, question, and page also has to pass an automated language check.

When a lesson is published, Aksara signs it digitally, and Nakafa shows that signed version.

## Languages and curricula

Nakafa is in English, Indonesian, German, and Malay, and every lesson is written in all four. The lessons are matched to the curricula learners follow: Kurikulum Merdeka, Cambridge International, Singapore MOE, the United States Standards-Aligned Pathway, KSSM Malaysia, Gymnasiale Oberstufe, and Studienkolleg. The try-outs prepare you for SNBT and TKA in Indonesia, SPM in Malaysia, and the Aufnahmeprüfung of the Studienkollegs in Germany.
