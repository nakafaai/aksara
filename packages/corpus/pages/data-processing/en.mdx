# Data Processing Agreement

Version 2026-10. Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement (the "Agreement") is between PT NAKAFA TEKNO KREATIF ("Nakafa") and the School that uses Nakafa School. A School accepts this version when it opens a school on Nakafa, and Nakafa records the version with the opening. If a School signed a written agreement with Nakafa, that agreement names the version it accepts and prevails where the two differ.

Words defined in our [Terms of Service](/en/terms-of-service) and [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy) mean the same here.

## Roles

The School is the controller of School Data and decides why and how it is processed. Nakafa is the School's processor and handles School Data only to provide Nakafa School. Nakafa remains the controller of personal Nakafa accounts and of the data it needs to operate, secure, and bill the Services.

This Agreement applies Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection and, where it applies to the School, Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation.

## What Nakafa processes

| Part | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| People | Students, teachers, staff, and Guardians of the School |
| Data | The school's settings, units, periods, class groups, roster people and their placements, invitations, class work, exam answers and results, grades, report cards, Guardian consent records, and the audit record |
| Purpose | Providing Nakafa School to the School, with the features the School turns on |
| Duration | From the opening of the school until the data is deleted under this Agreement |

## The School's instructions

Nakafa processes School Data only on the School's documented instructions. The School gives them through this Agreement, through its settings and the actions of the people it authorizes, and through its integrations. Nakafa tells the School when it believes an instruction breaks the law.

Nakafa does not use School Data to train its own models, and it does not sell School Data.

## People and confidentiality

Nakafa limits access to School Data to the people who need it to provide Nakafa School, and they are bound to keep it confidential. Nakafa staff enter a school only on a visit that the School approves. A visit is temporary, it can be ended by the School, and it is recorded in the School's audit record.

## Security

Nakafa protects School Data with measures that fit its nature, including:

- Encrypted connections for all data in transit.
- A key for each School that seals free text, such as messages and essays, at rest.
- A decision on every request about what the caller's role allows, in the School's own space.
- An audit record of privileged changes that the School can read.

## Sub-processors

The School allows Nakafa to use the sub-processors below. Each one processes School Data under its own terms, which bind it to protections no weaker than this Agreement requires.

| Sub-processor | What it does | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Convex | Database, file storage, backend, and the AI gateway | United States |
| Cloudflare | Hosting and delivery | Global network, with workers in the United States |
| Resend | Email delivery, such as invitations | United States |
| Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, or Microsoft Azure | Running the language model when a School uses AI features | United States and other countries where they operate |
| OpenRouter | Routing AI requests for the Convex AI gateway | United States and other countries where it operates |

Nakafa tells the School at least 30 days before it adds or replaces a sub-processor. The School can object in that time. If the two cannot agree, the School can end this Agreement before the change takes effect. Nakafa stays responsible for what its sub-processors do with School Data.

## International transfers

Nakafa stores and processes School Data outside Indonesia, as the table above shows. The School, as controller, decides on these transfers by accepting this Agreement, and it is responsible for the legal basis they need. Nakafa protects transferred data as the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy) describes.

## Personal data breaches

Nakafa tells the School without undue delay, and no later than 48 hours after it becomes aware of a breach of School Data. The notice says what happened, which data and people are affected, and what Nakafa has done. Nakafa gives the School what it needs to meet its own duties to notify authorities and people.

## Help for the School

A request from a person about School Data goes to the School, and Nakafa does not answer it on the School's behalf. Nakafa helps the School answer such requests and carry out data protection impact assessments, as far as the nature of the processing allows.

## Evidence and audits

Nakafa gives the School the information it needs to show that this Agreement is met. The School can inspect the processing once a year, or after a breach, with 30 days' notice, in a way that protects other Schools' data.

## End of the Agreement

When the School's use of Nakafa School ends, the School can export its data for 30 days. Nakafa then deletes School Data within 30 more days, unless the law requires it to keep a part of it, and it tells the School when the deletion is done.

## Changes

Nakafa publishes each new version here with its own version label and tells the School before it applies. A School that keeps using Nakafa School after that date accepts the new version.
