# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PT NAKAFA TEKNO KREATIF ("Nakafa," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses Personal Data when you access or use our websites, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Your use of the Services is at all times subject to our [Terms of Service](/en/terms-of-service), which incorporates this Privacy Policy.

## What this Privacy Policy covers

This Privacy Policy covers how we treat Personal Data that we gather when you access or use the Services.

This Privacy Policy does not cover the practices of companies we do not own or control, or people we do not manage, including third-party services you may access through the Services. For information about third-party services' data practices, please refer to their respective privacy policies linked throughout this document.

## Key definitions

- Personal Data means any information that identifies or relates to a particular individual. Personal Data may also be referred to as "personally identifiable information" or "personal information" under applicable privacy laws.
- User Content means information you submit through the Services, such as chat messages, comments, and other text you provide.
- Service Providers means vendors that process Personal Data for us to provide a defined part of the Services. Their role and legal obligations depend on the service and applicable law.

## How third parties process data

Third parties receive Personal Data in different legal roles. Vercel, Convex, PostHog, Resend, Google, and Google Cloud generally provide defined technical services to Nakafa under their applicable service and data-processing terms. The precise role of each party depends on the processing activity and applicable law.

Polar acts as the Merchant of Record and authorized reseller for checkout transactions. Polar collects and processes checkout data under its own terms and privacy policy, while Nakafa receives the customer, checkout, and subscription information needed to provide and manage access to the purchased Service.

We limit disclosures to the data needed for the stated purpose. Where applicable law requires a data-processing agreement or an international-transfer safeguard, the required terms must be in place before the relevant processing occurs. The provider links below identify the terms that govern their processing and may be updated by those providers.

## AI processing

Nakafa routes AI requests through Vercel AI Gateway to Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite or Gemini 3.7 Flash. The Gateway may select Google or Google Cloud Vertex AI, but the application does not permit another model provider. Each request requires a route that Vercel identifies as disallowing prompt training. If no eligible route is available, the request fails. When you use an AI feature, the prompt, related conversation context, and technical metadata needed to process the request are sent to the selected providers. The generated response is returned to Nakafa and may be stored with the conversation so that the feature works and the conversation remains available to you.

Research features may use Google Search grounding to corroborate public information. That processing can include queries derived from your prompt and contextual information needed to retrieve grounded results. The no-prompt-training restriction is not a promise of zero retention. Provider-controlled security, abuse-prevention, and grounding logs remain subject to the applicable provider terms. Google currently documents that, for Vertex AI Grounding with Google Search, query and contextual logs that are not associated with a customer or end user may be stored for up to three days for debugging.

Do not include government identifiers, financial-account numbers, highly sensitive health information, or other sensitive Personal Data in an AI prompt. AI output can be inaccurate and must not be treated as professional advice or as the sole basis for an important decision.

The relevant provider terms include the [Vercel AI Product Terms](https://vercel.com/legal/ai-product-terms#ai-gateway), [Vercel Notices and License Information](https://vercel.com/legal/notices-and-license-information#ai-gateway), [Gemini API Additional Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms), [Google Cloud Terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms), [Google Cloud Service Specific Terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms), and [Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum](https://cloud.google.com/terms/data-processing-addendum).

## Cookies, local storage, and product analytics

We separate storage that is necessary to operate the Services from optional product analytics.

- Strictly necessary storage supports functions such as authentication, security, language preferences, and remembering your privacy choice. It is not used for product analytics.
- PostHog product analytics is disabled by default. We do not load its browser library, create its analytics identifier, or send product-usage events unless you first select "Accept" in the privacy controls.
- We do not use PostHog session replay, autocapture, heatmaps, surveys, product tours, browser performance capture, or automatic page-view and page-leave capture. For admitted analytics events, we disable geographic enrichment and restrict properties to reviewed event contracts.
- Rejecting analytics does not limit core features. You can later accept or withdraw consent through the privacy controls. Withdrawal stops future analytics collection and clears the browser analytics state controlled by Nakafa.
- An anonymous browser choice does not silently authorize identified analytics after sign-in. Identified analytics requires the consent recorded for the signed-in account.
- A new privacy-notice version requires a fresh decision before optional analytics can resume.
- If your browser signals that Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is enabled, optional analytics remains disabled. An existing account grant is replaced with a denial so backend product events also fail closed.

Vercel may still process request and security logs as our hosting provider, and Nakafa may report service exceptions without an advertising or product-analytics identifier. We process those limited records to operate and secure the Services, not to build a product-usage profile.

For information about PostHog's processing, review its [Privacy Compliance documentation](https://posthog.com/docs/privacy).

## Data retention

We retain Personal Data for as long as necessary to provide the Services and for legitimate business purposes, including compliance, dispute resolution, and enforcement of agreements.

Retention depends on the type and purpose of the data:

- Account, profile, and learning data is kept while your account is active. An account-deletion request starts deletion of account-owned data from our active systems.
- While an account exists, we retain its current analytics decision and decision history so we can enforce and demonstrate the choice. Account deletion removes both the current record and its history from Nakafa's active Convex data.
- Product analytics is not collected before consent. When collected, its retention by PostHog follows the provider-side project setting. Because that operational setting can change, this Policy does not state a fixed period; you can request the current setting at [nakafaai@gmail.com](mailto:nakafaai@gmail.com).
- For identified account analytics, account deletion starts deletion of the PostHog person and related historical events and repeats the request after a reconciliation delay. The browser is also reset to an analytics denial. Anonymous events are not linked to a Nakafa account and therefore cannot be selected by account identity for deletion.
- Subscription and billing records are retained for the period required by accounting, tax, payment-dispute, and other applicable legal obligations.
- Provider-controlled request and security logs, backups, and deletion queues may follow provider-side retention settings and legal obligations. Nakafa has not verified one fixed schedule that applies across those providers, so this Policy does not state one. Contact [nakafaai@gmail.com](mailto:nakafaai@gmail.com) for current information.

If you request deletion, we will delete or de-identify Personal Data unless we are required or permitted to retain it for legal or legitimate business reasons.
