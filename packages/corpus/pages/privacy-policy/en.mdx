# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PT. Nakafa Tekno Kreatif ("Nakafa," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses Personal Data when you access or use our websites, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Your use of the Services is at all times subject to our [Terms of Service](/en/terms-of-service), which incorporates this Privacy Policy.

## What this Privacy Policy covers

This Privacy Policy covers how we treat Personal Data that we gather when you access or use the Services.

This Privacy Policy does not cover the practices of companies we do not own or control, or people we do not manage, including third-party services you may access through the Services. For information about third-party services' data practices, please refer to their respective privacy policies linked throughout this document.

## Key definitions

- Personal Data means any information that identifies or relates to a particular individual. Personal Data may also be referred to as "personally identifiable information" or "personal information" under applicable privacy laws.
- User Content means information you submit through the Services, such as chat messages, comments, and other text you provide.
- Service Providers means vendors that process Personal Data for us to provide a defined part of the Services. Their role and legal obligations depend on the service and applicable law.

## Important notice about third-party data processing

This Privacy Policy covers how PT. Nakafa Tekno Kreatif acts as a data controller for Personal Data collected through the Services. However, we engage third-party service providers that act as data processors to provide core functionality, including hosting, database services, analytics, email delivery, subscription management, and AI processing.

These third parties have their own privacy policies and data processing practices. Our use of a provider is governed by the provider's contract and data-processing terms. Where applicable law requires a data-processing agreement or a transfer safeguard, we must have the required terms in place before that processing occurs. We share Personal Data only for the stated service purpose and limit it to what the provider needs for that purpose.

**This Privacy Policy provides general information.** For complete details about how third parties process your data, including their data locations, subprocessors, and specific practices, we encourage you to review their privacy policies directly via the links provided throughout this document.

The links provided in this Privacy Policy direct you to the third-party service providers' own privacy policies and data processing addendums. We make reasonable efforts to ensure these links remain accurate, but we cannot guarantee that third-party privacy policies will not change. You should periodically review third-party privacy policies for any updates.

**This Privacy Policy is not legal advice.** Privacy and data protection laws are complex and vary by jurisdiction. If you have specific questions about how your Personal Data is processed or your rights under applicable law, we recommend seeking independent legal advice.

## Cookies, local storage, and product analytics

We separate storage that is necessary to operate the Services from optional product analytics.

- Strictly necessary storage supports functions such as authentication, security, language preferences, and remembering your privacy choice. It is not used for product analytics.
- PostHog product analytics is disabled by default. We do not load its browser library, create its analytics identifier, or send product-usage events unless you first select "Accept" in the privacy controls.
- Rejecting analytics does not limit core features. You can later accept or withdraw consent through the privacy controls. Withdrawal stops future analytics collection and clears the browser analytics state controlled by Nakafa.
- An anonymous browser choice does not silently authorize identified analytics after sign-in. Identified analytics requires the consent recorded for the signed-in account.
- We respect supported Do Not Track signals and do not use product analytics when the signal requires the analytics library to opt out.

Vercel may still process request and security logs as our hosting provider, and Nakafa may report service exceptions without an advertising or product-analytics identifier. We process those limited records to operate and secure the Services, not to build a product-usage profile.

For information about PostHog's processing, review its [Privacy Compliance documentation](https://posthog.com/docs/privacy).

## Data retention

We retain Personal Data for as long as necessary to provide the Services and for legitimate business purposes, including compliance, dispute resolution, and enforcement of agreements.

Retention depends on the type and purpose of the data:

- Account, profile, and learning data is kept while your account is active. An account-deletion request starts deletion of account-owned data from our active systems.
- A current analytics-consent record is kept so we can enforce your choice. It is deleted with the account, subject to any record we must retain to demonstrate compliance.
- Product analytics is retained according to the configured PostHog project retention period and is not collected before consent.
- Subscription and billing records are retained for the period required by accounting, tax, payment-dispute, and other applicable legal obligations.
- Security, audit, and support records are retained for the operational window needed to investigate an incident or resolve a request, and longer only when a legal obligation or active dispute requires it.
- Processor backups and deletion queues expire under the relevant provider's documented retention and deletion schedule.

If you request deletion, we will delete or de-identify Personal Data unless we are required or permitted to retain it for legal or legitimate business reasons.
